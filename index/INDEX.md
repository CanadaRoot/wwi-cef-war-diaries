# WWI CEF War Diaries — Master Searchable Index

The single searchable index for the whole archive, merged daily by the digest task (`wwi-war-diary-daily-digest`) from the eight per-eighth indexes (`INDEX-A.md` … `INDEX-H.md`). Rebuilt from scratch each day, so corrections in the eighth indexes replace stale entries here.

- **Coverage:** 467 CEF unit war diaries with a diary line in the LAC finding aid (RG 9 III-D-3), transcribed from Library and Archives Canada digitized pages. (The ~1,587 figure in early planning counts diary volumes/folders; the guide's with-diary unit total is 467.)
- **Status (26 Oct 2026):** 208 dated entries from 1 unit(s) so far; 208 entries merged from 1 eighth index file(s).
- **Entries are sorted by date, then unit.** Each entry keeps: date, unit, location, people named, companies/platoons, casualties, movements, what happened — plus a pointer to the full transcript under `diaries/<unit-slug>/` (see Sources below).
- **Uncertain readings** in the transcripts are marked [uncertain]; nothing is guessed.
