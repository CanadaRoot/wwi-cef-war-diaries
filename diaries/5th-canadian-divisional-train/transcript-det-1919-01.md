## Transcriber's notes — January 1919

- Coverage: every day 1–31 January is accounted for. No appendices are listed on the contents page (diary text only, 4 pages); no appendix is cited in the diary text.
- Command: Major G. W. Cooper went on leave to Paris on 25 January and command passed to Captain F. F. Coste, who signs the month as "c/o.c. 5th Cdn. Divl. Train Det." The initials in the Hour column throughout the month read "G.A.C." — Capt. G. A. Coste's initials (his given names are not stated in the diary; the 25 January entry names him "Captain F.F. Coste", so the two initial sets differ in the original).
- The detachment remained at Lannesdorf, Germany, all month — no move. The Canadian Corps was moving back to Belgium (26 January) and the detachment was preparing to entrain from Beuel, with the exact hour not yet known by 31 January.
- Court martial: Dvr. J. C. Watts (512835), absent without leave 1–19 December 1918, was tried by court martial at Bonn on 10 January; Capt. Coste acted as prosecutor, CSM. Davidson and SSgt. Gibson were witnesses; the court martial was adjourned to 11 January. A set of long reins stolen by German civilians was recovered and proceedings taken against the civilians; the theft case was turned over to the R.N.W.M.P. [uncertain — as written].
- Sickness: Dvr. Drewes, C. (808536) was admitted to No. 2 C.C.S. with influenza on 3 January. No deaths are recorded this month.
- Administration: from 26 January the unit came under R.A. X Corps and its administration was taken over by H.Q. 5th C.D.A.C.; rations came from X Corps Troops M.T. Coy. from that date. Sgt. Webster (152000) was struck off strength on 2 January (Part II Order No. 58, 18-12-18).
- [uncertain] items:
  - 1 Jan: the service number before "Dvr. Munro, G.D." is partly overtyped; read as "187853".
  - 2 Jan: "152000 Sgt. Webster F.G." — the initials "F.G." are as written.
  - 10 Jan (contd.): "R.N.W.M.P." — the letters are as written; the force's full name is not given in the diary.
  - 12 Jan: "515311 Dvr. Louth W.E." (cf. "515311 Dvr. Louth W.R." on 15 Jan) — the middle initial differs in the original; transcribed as written.

  - 26 Jan: "ration X Corps Troops M.T. Coy." — the phrase is as written (apparently a fragment of a longer sentence).
  - 30 Jan: "879 Dvr. Wilson, W.W." — the service number is as written ("879"); "O.C. R.N.W.M.P. Troop" is as written.
