## Transcriber's notes

- This sheet is heavily inked in the left-hand columns; some weather entries for 1–7 January are recorded as ditto marks under "Clear - Frosty" and are transcribed as shown.
- The sheet heading reads "VOLUME 1." on both sheets; this appears to be a typing error for Volume 13 (the title page reads "VOLUME No 13"). Transcribed as printed.
- "RNO 441" (13 January) is not clear in the original; marked [uncertain].
- "Capt. [uncertain], 4th Cdn. Div. Train" (24 January): the captain's surname could not be read; it appears to begin "Misse-" but is not certain.
- "Sir. K. [uncertain]" (29 January): the lecturer's surname after "Sir. K." could not be read.
- The volume is signed by Major C. P. Ermatinger for the commanding officer, rather than by Lt.-Col. Shaw as in earlier months.
- No dates are missing: every day 1–31 January 1918 has an entry.
