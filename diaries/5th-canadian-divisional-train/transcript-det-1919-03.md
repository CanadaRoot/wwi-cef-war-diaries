## Transcriber's notes — March 1919

- Coverage: every day 1–31 March is accounted for. No appendices are listed on the contents page (diary text only, 5 pages); no appendix is cited in the diary text.
- Demobilization begins: Dvr. Smith, S. (874271) proceeded to England for demobilization on 4 March — "the first man of the company to be demobilized, an original member of the unit and an exceptionally good man in every respect". Capt. F. F. Coste was struck off strength on 17 March on proceeding to Canada (authority: O. i/c. C.A.S.C. Personnel, G.H.Q. 1st Echelon wire B 762, 12-3-19), after a farewell dinner on 17 March. CQMS. Speirs, J.M. (3628) was despatched to England on 27 March for return to Canada; Sgt. Messner, C.J. (4772) was appointed CQMS in his place.
- The proposed move to Andenne (28 March) was opposed: on 25 March Major Cooper and Lieut. Reed found the accommodation in the allotted area very poor and reported to Divisional Headquarters with a strong recommendation to postpone the move. No move took place this month; the detachment remained at Folx-Les-Caves.
- Other notable items: Lieut. Reed was confined to bed with influenza (5 March); a photograph of the officers and N.C.O.s was taken for Canadian War Records (12 March); an entertainment by "Doctor Seaton, conjurer" was held in the Y.M.C.A. (6 March) and London entertainer Mr. Fred Duxbury entertained the men (26 March); a unit dance was held (9 March); Drivers Cruickshank and Leslie were paraded for permission to marry (24 March); a meat shipment arrived from the railhead "in a deplorable condition" (31 March); a summary of evidence was taken in the case of Dvr. Weatherall on a charge of absence without leave (29 March).
