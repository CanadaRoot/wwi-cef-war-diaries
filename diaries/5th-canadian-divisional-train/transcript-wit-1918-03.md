## Transcriber's notes

- The sheet heading reads "VOLUME 3."; this appears to be a typing error for Volume 15 (the title page reads "VOLUME NO. 15."). Transcribed as printed.
- This is the final volume of the 5th Canadian Divisional Train (Witley) diary: on 7 March 1918 the whole Train (15 officers, 274 other ranks, 230 horses) was struck off strength to the C.A.S.C. Duty Depot, and its officers to Corps Depot, Shorncliffe, under the divisional demobilization instructions.
- No dates are missing: every day 1–7 March 1918 has an entry.
