## Appendices

### Appendix I — Order, 4 April 1919

Headed: "APPENDIX 1. 5th Canadian Div. Train Detachment."

> To:— Lieut. T. Love
> C. S. M.
> C. Q. M. S.
>
> 1. On the 5th inst., at 8.30 hours, the 5th Cdn. Div. Train Detachment will turn in to Corps Troops Ordnance Officer, Andenne, 25 G.S. wagons, 1 Limbered G.S. Wagon, 1 Two wheeled Cart, 25 double sets of harness, and all surplus equipment, stores, etc.
> 2. On the 8th inst., at 7.00 Hours, 3 lorries will report to these lines for the purpose of transporting all stores. CQMS. Messner will accompany these lorries with a fatigue party of 10 men and upon arrival at Andenne will report to Lieut. Love.
> 3. Lieut. Love will proceed on the 6th inst., to Andenne and arrange billets for horses and men with the Town Major.
> 4. At 7.00 hours on the 7th inst., the convoy of wagons as detailed above will proceed by road to Andenne in charge of Lieut. Love. Sgt. Young will be detailed to accompany this convoy. 12 teams will be used to move this transport.
> 5. If it is necessary to park wagons elsewhere than the Ordnance wagon park, sufficient guards will be placed on same the night of 5th–8th.
> 6. At 8.30 hours on the 8th inst., Lieut. Love will hand over all the equipment as designated in para. 1. Special receipts will be obtained for bicycles turned in.
> 7. Immediately on completion of this duty the 12 teams and all men will return by road to Folx Les Caves.
> 8. Sufficient rations for horses and men to accompany convoy.
>
> 4-4-19
>
> G. W. Cooper Major.
> O.C. 5th Canadian Div. Train Detachment.

[Note: Appendix I is dated 4-4-19 but its paragraph 1 says the turn-in will take place "on the 5th inst." while paragraph 5 refers to the night of "5th–8th"; the diary text for 7 April says the convoy left at 7 a.m. that day. Transcribed as written.]

## Transcriber's notes — April 1919

- Coverage: every day 1–30 April is accounted for. Appendices I–V are present and transcribed above. The diary text is 4 pages; the contents page lists Appendices 3 & 4 together as "Orders issued by 5th Cdn. Divl. Train Detachment dated 16-4-19" but Appendix III is in fact the Field Day programme (13 April) and Appendix IV is the order dated 16-4-19 — transcribed as written.
- Demobilization: the unit turned in its surplus wagons, harness and stores to the Corps Troops Ordnance Officer at Andenne on 5–8 April (Appendix I) and the balance on 17–18 April (Appendix IV). Horses were shipped to Bastogne, Neufchatel and Tournai (7 April) and the balance turned over to the Belgian Government on 26 April (6 Riders, 2 L.D. and 90 H.D.). The final shipment of the Division's horses was to leave Ramillies for Brussels on 29 April. From 17 April, four other ranks and three lorries from the C.M.T. Company delivered rations daily, replacing horse transport. Men were proceeding to England to return to Canada with their dependents (14, 15, 16, 18, 25, 29 April entries), and 27 other ranks were taken on strength from 14th Canadian Field Ambulance for demobilization on 24 April (Appendix V).
- Field Day: a unit sports day was held on 13 April (Appendix III) — football, mounted events, novelty races, running and jumping, boxing, tug-o-war and indoor baseball, followed by a supper and dance, "very much enjoyed by all". A farewell supper and dance had been planned for Sunday 13 April by the canteen committee (10 April).
- Court martial and inquiry: the court martial of Dvr. Weatherall was held on 5 April; a Court of Inquiry was held on 30 April into the illegal absence without leave of No. 645590 Dvr. McNair, M. (President: Capt. Cumming, 66th Battery; members: Lieut. Davidson, 60th Battery, and Lieut. A. W. Reed).
- No battle casualties are recorded this month. One H.D. horse was evacuated to the Canadian Veterinary Evacuating Station (8 April).
