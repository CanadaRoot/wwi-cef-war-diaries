## Transcriber's notes

- "SASKATCHEWAN votes taken." (13 October) is struck through in the original; transcribed here to record the deletion.
- "Lieut. PAFORN" (23 October) is spelled as typed; the same officer is spelled "FABORN" in the June and August sheets, so "PAFORN" appears to be a typing error in the original.
- "F FERGUSSON" (25 October): the initial before the surname is not clear; earlier sheets give this officer as "Capt. J.L. FERGUSSON". Marked [uncertain].
- "HENSON" (25 October): the surname is not fully clear and may be "STENSON" (an a/SSM Stenson appears in August). Marked [uncertain].
- No dates are missing: every day 1–31 October 1917 has an entry.
