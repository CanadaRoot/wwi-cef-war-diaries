## Page 2 — Vol. 4 Sheet 1

WAR DIARY
Lt. Col. J.A. SHAW. D.S.O
5th CAN. DIV. TRAIN
Army Form C. 2118 — 8-D-5 vol 4

[Printed:] Instructions regarding War Diaries and Intelligence Summaries are contained in F.S. Regs., Part II. and the Staff Manual respectively. Title pages will be prepared in manuscript.

**APRIL – 1917**

**Sunday – 1st.** Clear + fine – Regular Sunday inspection taken by Lt. Col. DEAN

**Monday – 2nd**
Weather – Windy – cold – heavy snowfall.
Strength – Lieut C.N. GALER reports
Parades – Lt. Col. SHAW held meeting of Officers + instructed them as to what he wanted.
Quarantine – Hut 25 (17 o.r.) quarantined for mumps.
Training – Commenced according to syllabus.

**Tuesday [day of week first written "Wednesday" and struck through] 3rd**
Weather – Cloudy – cold
Routine

**Wednesday [first written "Thursday" and struck through] 4th**
Weather – Showery – cold
C.O. inspected Railhead + Bakery

**Thursday [first written "Friday" and struck through] 5th**
Weather – Clear – mild
C.O. and O.C. #3 Co. inspect 14th Bde 1st Line Transport

**Friday 6th**
Weather – Clear – mild.
Capt. KEATING to BRAMSHOTT HOSPITAL

**Saturday 7th**
Weather – Fine – cold
M.O. reports unit completely vaccinated
Capt. KEATING operated on 4 p.m. – died 8.30 p.m., taken from hospital to his house midnight.

## Notes on this month

- No entries are missing: every day 1–30 April 1917 has an entry.
- The cover is typed (earlier months' covers were handwritten) and gives the closing date as "April 31, 1917"; April has 30 days and the diary ends on Monday 30th.
- The days of the week for 3, 4 and 5 April were first written one day later and then corrected in the original.
- Lt.-Col. J.A. Shaw, D.S.O., now commands; Lt.-Col. E.C. Dean took the regular Sunday inspection on 1 April.
- Casualty: Capt. P.F. Keating died on 7 April 1917 after an operation at Bramshott Hospital; his funeral service was held at Witley Church on 10 April and his body taken to the mortuary at Farnham.
- "Divarty" is the diary's own spelling for Divisional Artillery.
