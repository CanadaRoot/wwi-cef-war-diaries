## Page 3 — Diary text, p. 1

Army Form C. 2118. — WAR DIARY or INTELLIGENCE SUMMARY. (Erase heading not required.)

[Handwritten, top right:] Original. 5th Cdn. Divl. Train. Vol. I. Page I

[Printed, top left:] Instructions regarding War Diaries and Intelligence Summaries are contained in F. S. Regs., Part II. and the Staff Manual respectively. Title pages will be prepared in manuscript.

Columns: Place | Date 1917. | Hour | Summary of Events and Information | Remarks and references to Appendices

| Place | Date | Hour | Summary of Events and Information |
|---|---|---|---|
| Witley Camp, Surrey. | Aug. 17th | | Thirty eight wagons, with personnel and horses, attached to 5th Cdn. Divl. Arty. to proceed to France with that formation. G.M.C. [initials uncertain] |
| | 19th | | Received orders to proceed to France with 5th Cdn. Divl. Arty. in command of A.S.C. details. Accompanied by Lieut. F. A. Walker, and our batman. G.M.C. [initials uncertain] |
| | 20th | | Entrained MILFORD Station on train No. X 328 which left this point 3.50 a.m. Arrived SOUTHAMPTON 6.15 a.m. Leaving there same evening on H.M.T. "Archimedes" for France. G.M.C. [initials uncertain] |
| Havre, France. | 21st | | Arrived at docks in Le Havre at 12 a.m. and proceeded to Rest Camp 1A, with 13th Bde. H.Q. to which Unit we were attached for the time being. Transport with personnel still "on command" to respective Units. |
| Havre. | 22nd | | Busy nearly all day with Remounts where we exchanged 4 horses due to kicks etc. on passage over. G.M.C. [initials uncertain] |
| | 25th | | Left Camp 1A at 3.45 P.M. for point of entrainment, which we left at 9.32 P.M. with H.Q. 13th Bde. G.M.C. [initials uncertain] |
| | 26th | | Arrived at LILLERS 5.30 P.M. when orders were received to take over train duties. Wagons, horses & personnel of Detachment still "on command" to units. Motor Transport in use up to this day, supplying Batteries of 13th Bde. which were in billeting area previous to our arrival |

[Gap: no entries recorded for 18th, 23rd or 24th August.]

## Page 4 — Diary text, p. 2

Army Form C. 2118. — WAR DIARY or INTELLIGENCE SUMMARY. (Erase heading not required.)

[Handwritten, top right:] Original. 5th Cdn. Divl. Train. Vol. I Page 2.

| Place | Date | Hour | Summary of Events and Information |
|---|---|---|---|
| AMETTES, FRANCE. | Aug. 27th | | All Train Transport ordered to LILLERS Railhead to haul supplies for Artillery which were billeted as follows. 13th Bde. C.F.A. less 51st Batty. AMETTES. 14th Bde. C.F.A. AMES. D.A.C. Units — NEDON. T.M.B's to NEDONCHEL. H.Q. Divl. Arty. at FONTAINE les HERMINES. G.M.C. [initials uncertain] |
| | | | Weather extremely bad and trying. |
| | | | Refilling Point in 53rd Batty's wagon lines. No supply details with us yet. Supplies issued by a fatigue supplied by 51st Batty. in accordance with our figures etc. |
| | | | Transport remained with respective units over night, reporting to Railhead every morning. G.M.C. [initials uncertain] |
| | 28th | | Same routine, nothing of importance transpired. G.M.C. [initials uncertain] |
| | 29th | | Additional A.S.C. Details (21 Other Ranks 8 horses 2 G.S. wagons) arrived at AMETTES shortly after midnight 28th with 51st Batty. reporting to me this morning. |
| | | | All Train Transport recalled to Divl. Train billets, and are now "off command" as arranged verbally with H.Q. Divl. Arty. |
| | | | Railhead cleared very early every morning — delivery of Rations to units same day as drawn from Railhead. Weather continues bad. G.M.C. [initials uncertain] |

## Transcriber's notes — August 1917

- The diary text covers 17–29 August 1917 only; no entries for 18th, 23rd or 24th August appear on these pages.
- The 26th August entry runs to the foot of p. 1 and continues onto p. 2 with the 27th August entry; the first line of p. 2 ("All Train Transport ordered to LILLERS Railhead…") belongs to the 27th August entry at Amettes.
- Abbreviations as written: Divl. = Divisional; Cdn. = Canadian; Arty. = Artillery; Bde. = Brigade; Batty. = Battery; C.F.A. = Canadian Field Artillery; D.A.C. = Divisional Ammunition Column; T.M.B. = Trench Mortar Battery; A.S.C. = Army Service Corps; H.M.T. = His Majesty's Transport; G.S. = General Service; H.Q. = Headquarters.
- The initials closing most entries appear to read "G.M.C." but the handwriting is not fully clear; treated as [uncertain] where marked.
- No casualties are recorded in August 1917.
- People named: Lieut. F. A. Walker (accompanied the detachment to France).
