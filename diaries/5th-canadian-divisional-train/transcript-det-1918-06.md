## Transcriber's notes

- The diary is handwritten on Army Form C. 2118 ("War Diary or Intelligence Summary", the latter heading struck through; each page marked "Original").
- The diarist's initials, written after most entries, are read here as "G.H.C." — Major G. H. Coops, Officer Commanding the Detachment (identified by name in the Part II Orders quoted in the 18 June 1918 entry, and by his signature). From 28 June the entries are initialled "E.J.C." — Capt. E. J. Coste, acting Officer Commanding while Major Coops is on leave (see 28 June entry and the signature at the end of the month).
- Original spelling, punctuation, abbreviations and capitalisation are kept (e.g. "Divl.", "Cdn.", "bty.", "Dvr.").
- Words that could not be read with confidence are marked [uncertain]. The form's columns are Place / Date / Hour / Summary of Events and Information / Remarks and references to Appendices; the Hour and Remarks columns are blank throughout this month.
- Note on the 24th/25th June entries: the date numeral "25." stands beside the line "Nothing to report.", so the line "Weather, fine & windy. Went to bed in afternoon suffering from Influenza." is transcribed as part of the 24th entry.

## Page 3 — Diary text, p. 1 (det-page-0073-June_1918_p._3.jpg)

WAR DIARY — Original — Army Form C. 2118 — Vol. XI — 5th Cdn. Divl. Train Det. — Page 1.

Place: FREVIN CAPELLE, France.

**1st.** Weather — fine and warm. Visited O/C C.A.S.C. Personnel in morning at Cdn. Corps H.Q. PERNES. He was absent in England on duty. Saw German aeroplane in field nearby while there. It was brought down in flames last night the 3 occupants being burned to death. G.H.C.

**2nd.** Weather fine and warm. — Pack Train coming up short. Oats & part of hay had to be drawn from MONT ST ELOI Railhead. ⅔ Company allowed half day off for their general good work. Attended 1st Cdn. Divl. Artillery Gymkhana in afternoon.

**3rd.** Weather — fine and cooler. 511917. Pte. Alexander A.H. 512841. Pte. Bolingbroke E. 104561. Pte. Smith E.A. 426516 Pte. Weatherall M. 879. Pte. Wilson W. Jr. [uncertain] ordered taken on strength today. The first four arrived today. O.C. and S.S.O. 15th Divisional Train called on us today. Col. Johnston [uncertain] O.C. very much impressed. Held a confidential conference with A.C.O.'s of Unit 7.30 — 9 P.M. as to future policy of Unit. Very successful meeting. G.H.C.

**4th.** Weather — fair and somewhat cooler. Pay parade 5 P.M. Consultation with Genl. Dobbs [uncertain] C.R.A. 8 to 10 P.M. G.H.C.

**5th.** Weather — fine & warm. Nothing to record beyond fact that 2 H.D. horses arrived from Remounts today. *[One short line here is illegible.]*

**6th.** Weather — fine & warm. S/Sgts Speirs and Harker appeared before Interviewing Officer R.A.F. Nothing to record.

**7th.** Weather — fine and warm. Turnout look very well. Inspected 7 A.M. Nothing worth of recording. G.H.C.

## Page 4 — Diary text, p. 2 (det-page-0074-June_1918_p._4.jpg)

WAR DIARY — Original — Army Form C. 2118 — Vol. XI — 5th Cdn. Divl. Train Det. — Page 2.

Place: FREVIN CAPELLE, France.

**8th.** Weather warm. Visited O/C C.A.S.C. Personnel Cdn. Corps H.Q. in afternoon in reference to Reinforcements and Establishment.

**9th.** Weather — fine and moderately warm till about 5 P.M. Cloudy and a little rain fell. No 3 Section off for afternoon. Baseball game between D.A.C. and Company postponed on account of uncertain weather.

**10th.** Weather, Cloudy. Rain in early morning. Orders received to vacate a portion of our horse standings to make room for a Mobile Veterinary Section.

**11th.** Weather, fine and moderately warm. Nothing worth of note.

**12th.** Weather, fine & warm. In charge of ration Convoy leaving at 7 a.m. returning 1 P.M. Visited 13th Bde. H.Q. wagon lines, 51st and 55th Battery, W. R. effected a trade with a Heavy Artillery Battery, Graham big Chestnut being mated with one very similar. Made most satisfactory to all concerned. Men practising [uncertain — appears "Wrestling"] on horse back and other sports for an hour in afternoon, for 5th C.D.A. forthcoming sports.

**13th.** Weather — fine and warm. Capt. Coste and myself attended Baseball Game 6 to 7 P.M. 14th Bde. C.F.A. vs 5th Train. Score 22-2 favour 14th Bde. *[Text cut off at bottom of page image.]*

## Page 5 — Diary text, p. 3 (det-page-0075-June_1918_p._5.jpg)

WAR DIARY — Original — Army Form C. 2118 — Vol. XI — 5th Cdn. Divl. Train Det. — Page 3.

Place: FREVIN CAPELLE, France.

**14th.** Weather, fine, windy and moderately cool. Visited O/C C.A.S.C. Personnel Cdn. Corps H.Q. in morning, in reference to personnel and establishment of Unit. Pay Parade 2 P.M. Lieut. Paborn off parades. Sick.

**15th.** Weather, fine and warm. Took Convoy 7 a.m. Visited 14th Bde. C.F.A. H.Q. — Nothing further to report. Lieut. Paborn in bed today.

**16th.** Weather fine and warm. 510829 S/Sgt. HARKER A.H. leaves for England for Temporary Commission in the Royal Air Force. G.H.C.

**17th.** Weather — fine and moderately cool. Rain in evening. Visited 5th C.D.A. in connection with Establishment. G.H.C.

**18th.** Weather — fine and moderately cool. Visited 5th C.D.A. H.Q. in connection with establishment. 1 G.S. Baggage wagon on command to 61st Battery Condemned by D.O.M. No 4. G.H.C. Part II Orders No 36. dated 15-6-18 as follows. S.O.S. and Establishment: Major G. H. Coops on being reposted to C.A.S.C. Pool 4-6-18. Strength Increase. Capt. E. J. Coste taken on strength of Unit from C.A.S.C. Corps Depot, Shorncliffe, 25-2-18. Attachments. Major G. H. Coops (C.A.S.C. Pool) attached to Unit — for duty 4-6-18.

**19th.** Weather — cool with occasional rain. Outlined new organisation of Detachment into two sections. Visited 5th C.D.A. H.Q.

**20th.** Weather — dull and cool.

**21st.** Weather — dull and cool. Visited 57 C.C.S. to see Sgt. Ferguson A.B. G. H. Coops, Major.

## Page 6 — Diary text, p. 4 (det-page-0076-June_1918_p._6.jpg)

WAR DIARY — Original — Army Form C. 2118 — Vol. XI — 5th Cdn. Divl. Train Det. — Page 4.

Place: FREVIN CAPELLE, France.

**22nd.** Weather, windy & cool. Visited 57 C.C.S. to see Sgt. Ferguson who was discharged this morning prior to returning to England for Infantry Commission. G.H.C.

**23rd.** 4763 S/Sgt. Ferguson A.B. left for England. Lieut. T. Ross [uncertain] reported for duty from 2nd C.D. Train. Weather, fine & cool. G.H.C.

**24th.** Capt. Coste ill with Influenza. G.H.C. Weather, fine & windy. Went to bed in afternoon suffering from Influenza.

**25th.** Nothing to report. Weather — fine and cool.

**26th.** 4730 S/Sgt Gibson J.R. } reported from Base. 4772 Cpl. Messer [uncertain] } 7 other ranks attached from C.C.R.C. G.H.C.

**27th.** up this afternoon for first time. — Very weak. [uncertain] on leave in morning — (Dvr.) [uncertain]. G. H. Coops, Major, O.C. 5th Cdn. Divl. Train.

## Page 7 — Diary text, p. 5 (det-page-0077-June_1918_p._7.jpg)

WAR DIARY — Original — Army Form C. 2118 — Vol. XI — 5th Cdn. Divl. Train Det. — Page 5.

Place: FREVIN CAPELLE, France.

**28th.** Weather — fine and cool; Major Coops left "on leave" this morning. E.J.C.

**29th.** Visited 5th C.D.A. H.Q. for permission of C.R.A. to deliver rations twice tomorrow to enable personnel to visit Corps Sports July 1st. Weather — fine and cool. E.J.C.

**30th.** Made usual delivery of rations in morning then second delivery at 1 P.M. Weather — fine and warm. E.J.C.

E. J. Coste, Capt., a/O.C. 5th Cdn. Divl. Train.
