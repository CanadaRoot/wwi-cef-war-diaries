## Page 2 — Vol. 3 Sheet 1

WAR DIARY
Lt Col. E.C. DEAN
— FIFTH CANADIAN DIVISIONAL TRAIN —
Army Form C. 2118

[Printed:] Instructions regarding War Diaries and Intelligence Summaries are contained in F.S. Regs., Part II. and the Staff Manual respectively. Title pages will be prepared in manuscript.

**March 1st 1917, WITLEY CAMP, Thursday**

Weather – Mild – mostly clear.
Strength (Incr) – Lieuts McCABE and WALTER and 59 O.R. from SHORNCLIFFE
" 1 O.R. from CROWBOROUGH
Strength (Decr) – 29 O.R. To BRAMSHOTT – Lieut RAINVILLE to SHORNCLIFFE.
Horses Evacuated: Hq. Co. 3 – #2 Co., 1 – #3 Co., 6 – #4 Co., 1.
Duty – 37 waggons detailed
Discipline – Army Act. Sess 4 to 44 read to unit by Capt. MILLAR – Lt. HORNE.
Health – Hut 26 quarantined – Mumps. #4 Co. all in quarantine except some N.C.O's [uncertain]
Fuel – Reserve of about 1000 tons of coal in R.E. Yard.
Forage – Div. Board of Officers examine + condemn about 58 bales hay.

**March 2nd 1917, WITLEY CAMP, Friday**

Weather – Overcast – chilly.
Strength (Decr) – Lieut. GOGAY to SHORNCLIFFE.
Transport Weight carried during February 5258 tons – Av. 187.08 tons per diem

**March 3rd 1917, WITLEY CAMP, Saturday**

Weather – Fine – mild
Establishment – D.A.A.G. instructs – W.E. R VII amended 3D 2 d 3-9-15 to govern.
Personnel – All companies complete
Strength (Incr) – 2 O.R.
Horses Evacuated: – Hq. Co., 1 – #3 Co., 4 – #4 Co., 1.
Health – All contacts grouped in three huts. Inspected by A.A. + Q.M.G [uncertain]

**March 4th 1917, WITLEY CAMP, Sunday**

Weather – Fine – mild.
Progress – All new waggons greased, about half painted. All kits inspected – feet examined – underwear changed. O.C. institutes system of marking lines + stables by merit.

**March 5th 1917, WITLEY CAMP, Monday**

Weather – Cloudy – cold – slight snowfall during night 4/5
Horses Evacuated: – Hq., 2 – #3 Co., 2
General – Fatigues very irregular – Letter read to Officers re dress + conduct

## Page 3 — V3-S2

**March 6th 1917, WITLEY CAMP, Tuesday**

Weather – Clear – rain night 6/7
Strength (Decr) – 1 O.R. [remainder of line uncertain]
Supplies – Bread ration reduced to 14 oz.

**March 7th 1917, WITLEY CAMP, Wednesday**

Weather – Overcast – cold.
Health – Lieut GERRARD to BRAMSHOTT Hospital
Horses Evacuated: #3 Co. 4. Mallein test started.
Attachmts (Incr) – 1 O.R. – Decrease – Lieut HARRISON.

**March 8th 1917, WITLEY CAMP, Thursday**

Weather – Clear – bright – cold.
Attachmts (Incr) – 2 O.R. Decrease – Lieut SEAGO
Health – Officer – Lieut PABORN put in charge of quarantine.
Dental inspection of unit completed 1.15 P.M.
Transport – First line drivers withdrawn due to Bn. animals arriving. [uncertain] R.O. 953.

**March 9th 1917, WITLEY CAMP, Friday**

Weather – Cold – snow.
Strength (Incr) – 2 O.R. – Decrease – 1 O.R.
Attachmts (Incr) – 1 O.R. – " 2 O.R.
Horses – #3 Co. 1 Evacuated.
Boots – Routine Orders to take Overseas only 1 pair Can. or English

**March 10th 1917, WITLEY CAMP, Saturday**

Weather – Overcast – rain.
Strength (Incr) – 4 O.R.
Attachmts (Decr) – 3 O.R.

**March 11th 1917, WITLEY CAMP, Sunday**

Weather – Overcast.
Establishment – New one received from DST. London dated January 1917.
Horses – Mallein test Hq. Co. + #2 Co. – complete
Progress – Marked improvement in lines, horses + equipment.

Horses Evacuated: – Hq. Co., 3 – #3 Co., 1 – #4 Co., –1.

Weather – Mild – bright.
Documents – Instructions issued to Companies re preparation of documents ongoing Overseas.
Routine –

**Friday – March 16th**

Weather – Slightly overcast.
Routine –

**Saturday – March 17th**

Weather – Mild – bright.
Horses – Evacuated: – Hq. Co. 3
Routine.

Weather – Mild – bright
Progress – All harness fitted – waggons all painted + mostly marked regular inspections of kits – feet +c held. Unit pretty well settled down.

## Page 5 — V3-S4, MARCH 1917

**Monday 19th**

Weather – Overcast – cool.
Routine.

**Tuesday 20th**

Weather – Fine early – snow flurries late afternoon.
Horses – Evacuated: – #2 Co. 2 – #4 Co. 1 – All inspected by A.D.V.S.

**Wednesday 21st**

Weather – Cold – overcast.
Transport – First line inspected by A.A. + Q.M.G. – D.A.Q.M.G + O.C. Arranged for Train saddlers to supervise harness.

**Thursday 22nd**

Weather – Cold – snow flurries.
Parades – Muster – 7.30 A.M. new + zealous officer. [uncertain]
D.D. of S. + T. lectured all quartermasters on new ration [uncertain]

**Friday 23rd**

Weather – Cool – overcast.
Routine.

**Saturday 24th**

Weather – Mild – fine.
Strength (Decr) – 21 O.R. to #14 Field Ambulance
Transport – All [uncertain] harness inspected + made right.
Sports – Artillery sports – Lieut. McCABE [uncertain] 2nd in jumping
4.50 P.M. Period of special vigilance notified. Train instructed to supply deficiencies in 1st line transport.

**Sunday 25th**

Weather – Overcast.
Health – Inoculation complete.
Sent out horses to 15th Bde [uncertain]
Train ready to move at 5 P.M.
O.C. instructed to report to London

---

## Page 6 — V3-S5, MARCH 1917

**WITLEY CAMP, Monday 26th, 5 P.M.**

Weather – Cold – Snow.
Transport – First line commences drawing supplies.
Training – Train commences – foot + waggon drill + musketry.
Notice received to resume normal conditions.

**Tuesday 27th**

Weather – Cold – Snow.
Supplies – Varied ration commences.
Working pay restored from 1-1-16. [as written]

**Wednesday 28th**

Weather – Cloudy – cold – 11 degrees frost.
Rations – Shortage of food – 75 men no meat for supper.
Sports – Tug of war – Won from Engineers by default.
Health – Quarantine lifted – Hut 26.
O.C. returns – Ordered to Shorncliffe.

**Thursday 29th**

Weather – Overcast – showers.
Routine –

**Friday 30th**

Weather – Fine – cold.
Horses – Count checked – O.R. with A.D.V.S – 378 + 3 attached.
Tug of war – Won from 14th Field Ambulance
Lt Col. SHAW reported 8.30 P.M. to take Command

**Saturday 31st**

Weather – Overcast.
Strength Decrease – 28 O.R. to SHORNCLIFFE
Hut # 31 – out of quarantine.
Vaccination complete

DIARY (VOL. 3) ENDS

[Signed:] [signature] Lt. Col. Commanding 5th Can. Div Train.

---

## Notes on this month

- No entries are missing: every day 1–31 March 1917 has an entry.
- On 30 March, Lt.-Col. Shaw reported to take command, succeeding Lt.-Col. E.C. Dean (the signature at the foot of this sheet is not clearly legible).
- The move overseas was evidently imminent: the Train was put on "special vigilance" on 24 March, was ready to move at 5 p.m. on 25 March, and began drawing supplies and training in first-line work on 26 March.
