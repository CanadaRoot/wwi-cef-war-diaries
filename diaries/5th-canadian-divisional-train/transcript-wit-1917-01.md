## Notes on this month

- The diary proper begins 24 January 1917; there are no entries for 1–23 January 1917 (the Train was not yet mobilized; authority was granted 13 January 1917).
- The cover gives the month as 1–31 January 1917, but the diary itself (Volume No. 1) covers only 24–31 January.
- "REDFORD" in the 30 and 31 January entries is written as such but is almost certainly Lieut G.H. TEDFORD (named on 24 January); retained as written.
- The marginal signature on each sheet is not clearly legible; recorded as [uncertain].
