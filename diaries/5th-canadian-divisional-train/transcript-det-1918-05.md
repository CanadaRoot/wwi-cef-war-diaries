## Transcriber's notes

- The diary is handwritten on Army Form C. 2118 ("War Diary or Intelligence Summary", the latter heading struck through; each page marked "Original"). The appendices are handwritten on squared paper.
- The diarist's initials, written after most entries, are read here as "G.H.C." — Major G. H. Coops, Officer Commanding the Detachment (identified by name in the Part II Orders quoted in the 18 June 1918 entry, and by his signature at the end of the month).
- Original spelling, punctuation, abbreviations and capitalisation are kept (e.g. "Divl.", "Cdn.", "bty.", "Dvr.").
- Words that could not be read with confidence are marked [uncertain]. The form's columns are Place / Date / Hour / Summary of Events and Information / Remarks and references to Appendices; the Hour column is blank throughout this month. Remarks entries are noted where they occur.
- The place column spells the village "FRE VIN CAPELLE" / "FRE VIN-CAPELLE"; on diary pp. 5–6 it is written "FRAVEN CAPELLE" (sic).
- Appendix page numbering as written runs: App. I p. 1, (second page unnumbered), App. II p. 4, (continuation unnumbered), App. III p. 5, (continuation "Page 5. Continued").

## Page 3 — Diary text, p. 1 (det-page-0059-May_1918_p._3.jpg)

WAR DIARY — Original — Army Form C. 2118 — Vol. X — 5th Cdn. Divl. Train Det. — Page 1.

Place: LATARGET, France; HOUDAIN (5th).

**1st.** Company Canteen opened. Weather cold and dull — very muddy. G.H.C.

**2nd.** Went to H.Q. 5th C.D.A. in morning in reference to pending move of formation. Wrote O/C Base personnel Cdn. Corps in reference to Capt. Coste being absorbed into Establishment. Met O/C Base personnel at 4th Cdn. Divl. Train H.Q. in reference to reinforcements to C.A.S.C. in evening. Received warning 5th C.D.A. letter in reference to forthcoming move. Received two reinforcements. Weather fine and mild. G.H.C.

**3rd.** Compiled new establishment for this Detachment — forwarding same to O/C C.A.S.C. Personnel for his perusal and action. Wrote this Officer also in connection with S.A.A. Lieut. [uncertain]. Witnessed a German aeroplane brought down near the Camp. Two Germans in it — one killed (pilot) one badly wounded (Observer). Weather fine and fairly windy. G.H.C.

**4th.** Made a reconnaissance of new Billeting area around HOUDAIN with Capt. Bevill [uncertain] Staff Captain 5th C.D.A. and Capt. Coste leaving here 8 a.m. returning 4 P.M. Company preparing to move to HOUDAIN tomorrow — weather fine and mild. G.H.C.

**5th.** Moved to HOUDAIN today. Supply Lorries clearing railhead, dumping at new location. Weather — wet & mild. Called in to see D.D.S. & T. Staff 1st Army. Wagon lines of Company & Dump at J 32 C 60.30 Map 36 B [uncertain]. G. H. Coops, Major.

*[Remarks column, 5th: App I.]*

## Page 4 — Diary text, p. 2 (det-page-0060-May_1918_p._4.jpg)

WAR DIARY — Original — Army Form C. 2118 — Vol. X — 5th Cdn. Divl. Train Det. — Page 2.

Place: HOUDAIN, France.

**6th.** M.T. Cleared railhead ECURIE today. Delivered rations 8 A.M. to all units. Everybody comfortably settled. Weather fine and mild. Visited both Brigade Headquarters and all batteries in afternoon with Capt. Robertson Staff Captain 5th C.D.A. G.H.C.

**7th.** Railhead HOUDAIN — cleared with horse transport drawing through Cdn. Corps troops. Delivered rations to units 6.30 a.m. Wagons returned and cleared railhead loaded and parked ready to move off first thing in morning to Units. Lieut. Paborn and Sgt. Major Davidson [uncertain] visited batteries in afternoon. Weather raining in morning, fine and mild in afternoon. G.H.C.

**8th.** Nothing new to record. Pack train arrived at 10.30 spotted 11.30 a.m. Batt. parade held in afternoon. Weather, fine and mild. G.H.C.

**9th.** Wagons being painted. Visited 13th Bde. H.Q. and Batteries of that Brigade with Capt. Coste in late afternoon. Weather, fine and mild. G.H.C. S/Sgt. Harker returned from Hospital. G.H.C.

**10th.** Weather, dull and mild. Pack Train arrived rather later than usual. Refills 7.30 P.M. Went to conference with C.O.M.T. in morning. Now attached to D.A.D.O.S. 5th Cdn. [uncertain — line ends here]. G.H.C.

**11th.** Weather, dull in morning — fine in afternoon. Train arrived late refills 4.15 P.M. G.H.C.

**12th.** Weather, dull and cold. Forwarded S/Sgt. Speirs application for Commission in R.A.F. to Headquarters. *[Entry ends at the foot of the page.]*

## Page 5 — Diary text, p. 3 (det-page-0061-May_1918_p._5.jpg)

WAR DIARY — Original — Army Form C. 2118 — Vol. X — 5th Cdn. Divl. Train Det. — Page 3.

Place: HOUDAIN, France; May.

**13th.** Weather dull and cold with occasional rain in morning, and all afternoon. Nothing to record. G.H.C.

**14th.** Received proposed new establishment from O/C C.A.S.C. Personnel for the unit. Busy studying it. Weather fine and cool. Paraded S/Sgts. HARKER and SPEIRS to G.O.C. 5th C.D.A. in reference to Commissions in R.A.F. Replied to O/C C.A.S.C. Personnel letter A.S.C. 5-1 in reference to his new establishment. G.H.C.

**15th.** Weather — fine and mild. Visited Remount Depot with V.O. 13th Bde. C.F.A. Selected one Riding horse with 52nd Batt. C.F.A. [uncertain] for one officer's charger. Attended football match with Capt. Coste from 5.30 to 7 P.M. Match between officers of 13th & 14th Bdes. No Score. Went for a ride 7–8.30 a.m. with Capt. Coste. Bathing Parade.

**16th.** Weather — fine and warm. Wagons still be painted. Visited 13th Bde. H.Q. Pay parade held. G.H.C.

**17th.** Weather fine and warm. Went to Cdn. Corps H.Q. to interview O/C C.A.S.C. Personnel in regard to Establishment. Also consulted D.A.D.O.S. in regard to equipment.

**18th.** Weather — fine and warm. No. 784973 Driver DESSUREAULT, JOSEPH, of this unit accidentally killed this morning by being struck with motor car, midway between HOUDAIN and Division. Occupants of car Lt. Col. J. P. French D.S.O. [uncertain] and Major A. P. Shatford Cdn. Corps Chaplain. Driver of car Pte. J. H. Black No. 515038. Time about 11.15 a.m. Funeral held to HOUDAIN Church Cemetery 6.15 P.M. today, attended by all Officers and members of [uncertain — remainder of line cut off at bottom of page image]. G. H. Coops, Major.

## Page 6 — Diary text, p. 4 (det-page-0062-May_1918_p._6.jpg)

WAR DIARY — Original — Army Form C. 2118 — Vol. X — 5th Cdn. Divl. Train Det. — Page 4.

Place: HOUDAIN, France; FREVIN CAPELLE (22nd).

**19th.** Weather fine and warm. ⅓ of Company allowed afternoon off. Nothing more to record. G.H.C.

**20th.** Weather — fine and warm. Received verbal information that Divisional Artillery would move into billets now occupied by 4th C.D.A. on 22nd inst. Visited 4th Train H.Q. with Capt. Coste. Arrived back at 12 P.M. Arranged to clear pack train for each other at present location on 21st inst. and awaited lines. G.H.C.

**21st.** Moved Company to FREVIN CAPELLE. Railhead Houdain. Beautiful day. Left lines in HOUDAIN at 2.30 P.M. arriving FREVIN CAPELLE 6 P.M. Very hot day. Cleared pack train same as usual in morning, dumping, handing same over to S.D.O. 4th Cdn. Div. No. 1 Coy 4th Cdn. Div. carried out same plan in conjunction with arrangements. No lorries used for move and Company supply wagons moved empty. A Bomb dropped near 5th C.D.A. Mess tonight — while Capt. Coste and I were there. *[Remarks column: App II.]*

**22nd.** Weather fine and hot. Cleared railhead FREVIN CAPELLE 7 a.m. delivering to units afterwards. D.A.C. in [uncertain] 13th & 14th Bdes in and about Aubigny & Savy. French Mortars in Agnières. 5th C.D.A. H.Q. in Berles. Occupying same lines as when here before. Visited H.Q. for information regarding further immediate moves. G.H.C.

**23rd.** Weather — Fair turning cold in afternoon. Visited 15th Div. H.Q. with Capt. Bevill [uncertain] Staff Capt. 5th C.D.A. and [uncertain] 1st Coy 1st Cdn. Divl. Train in connection with move of tomorrow. Visited Cdn. H.Q. in reference to move, with Capt. Robertson & Capt. [uncertain] 5th C.D.A. *[Entry continues on next page.]*

Lorries were ordered off. Visited A.Q.M.G. 17th Corps for permission to use lorries on 26th and permission granted. G.H.C.

**26th.** 879. Dr. Wilson W. Jr. [uncertain] reports from C.C.R.C. Visited 15th Divl. Train H.Q. with Capt. Coste in evening [uncertain]. Moved Company to Frevin Capelle this a.m. Lorries cleared railhead dumping at old dump in Frevin Capelle. Men under canvas. Weather fair & mild.

**27th.** Weather — Fine & mild. Rode over to 5th C.D.A. to apply for Court Martial in charge against [name crossed out in original] "loaded" for neglecting to etc. Company Order #13 Riding on loaded wagon. Capt. Coste taking summary of evidence in evening. Enemy shelling in near vicinity.

**29th.** Weather fine & warm. Went to H.Q. 5th C.D.A. in morning. Nothing new transpired. G. H. Coops, Major.
