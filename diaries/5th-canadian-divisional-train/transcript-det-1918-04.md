## Transcriber's notes

- The diary is handwritten on Army Form C. 2118 ("War Diary or Intelligence Summary", the latter heading struck through; each page marked "Original").
- The diarist's initials, written after most entries, are read here as "G.H.C." — Major G. H. Coops, Officer Commanding the Detachment (identified by name in the Part II Orders quoted in the 18 June 1918 entry, and by his signature at the end of the month).
- Original spelling, punctuation, abbreviations and capitalisation are kept (e.g. "Divl.", "Cdn.", "bty.").
- Words that could not be read with confidence are marked [uncertain]. The form's columns are Place / Date / Hour / Summary of Events and Information / Remarks and references to Appendices; the Hour and Remarks columns are blank throughout this month unless noted.

## Page 3 — Diary text, p. 1 (det-page-0054-April_1918_p._3.jpg)

WAR DIARY — Original — Army Form C. 2118 — Vol. IX — 5th Cdn. Divl. Train Det. — Page 1.

Place: BRAY, France.

**1st.** Weather — fair & cool. System of supplies — Supply Column running. Clearing railhead wagon proceeding direct to units in early morning, returning to dump and loading whereupon they would park loaded for the night, ready for delivery first thing next morning. G.H.C.

**2nd.** Weather fine and windy. Enemy bty. busy firing up lines. Very conveniently billeted. G.H.C.

**3rd.** Weather mild and dull. Nothing worth of recording.

**4th.** Weather mild and dull. Visited the D.A.C. complete with Lt. Paborn. G.H.C.

**8th.** Weather during this period was mild and for the most part dull. G.H.C.

*[No separate entries for 5th, 6th or 7th April; covered by the 8th's "during this period".]*

**9th.** Drew from FREVIN CAPELLE railhead by horse transport the first time loaded at 7.45 a.m. Wagon cleared railhead first, then delivered to units. Heavy shelling over and about lines from 2 P.M. to 6.30 and intermittently through the night. 126778 Dvr. CLARK wounded left leg in afternoon at lines. His promotion to rank of [uncertain] appeared in 4th Cdn. Div. Routine Orders of today. Weather dull and cold. G.H.C.

**10th.** Occasional enemy shelling in near vicinity of camp. Convoy [uncertain] in charge of Lt. Paborn narrowly escaped, on way to railhead. All wagons loaded and parked for the night with exception of Railhead wagons. G.H.C.

2nd Lt. [uncertain] took over area in which BRAY is situated. Col. Corrigan [uncertain] O.C. 1st Train visited me today in reference to taking over lines. G.H.C. Weather dull and mild.

## Page 4 — Diary text, p. 2 (det-page-0055-April_1918_p._4.jpg)

WAR DIARY — Original — Army Form C. 2118 — Vol. IX — 5th Cdn. Divl. Train Det. — Page 2.

Place: FREVIN CAPELLE.

**11th.** Moved Company to FREVIN-CAPELLE today, taking over lines and Billets from No. 1 Coy 1st Cdn. Divl. Train. Wagons reported back to lines after delivering to their units. Supply Column cleared Railhead for us today. Lines in same field as No. 3 Coy 1st Cdn. Divl. Train. Supply Dump very handy to railhead. System used here as follows, Headquarters Section of Company, wagons, cleared railhead, all other wagons loaded and parked for the night, moving off to Units, who are still in same locations, early next morning. On passing dump on return journey they picked the Supplies up from dump, and proceeded to the wagon park nearby. Very handy indeed and everybody apparently well pleased with new surroundings. Weather dull and wet. G.H.C.

**12th.** Weather fine and clear. G.H.C.

**13th to 17th.** The weather for this period was cold, dull and windy. Held an inspection of men's equipment on 17th special attention being paid to field dressings, box respirators, steel helmets, pay books and ammunition. G.H.C.

**18th.** Lieut. Paborn away all day, inspecting Baggage wagons on command to the eight batteries and Divl. Arty. H.Q. Condition of same found to be generally fair. G.H.C.

**19th.** Showery. G.H.C.

**20th.** Fine and cool — nothing worth of recording, usual routine being carried out. G.H.C.

## Page 5 — Diary text, p. 3 (det-page-0056-April_1918_p._5.jpg)

WAR DIARY — Original — Army Form C. 2118 — Vol. IX — 5th Cdn. Divl. Train Det. — Page 3.

Place: FREVIN CAPELLE, France; LETARGET (26th).

**21st to 24th.** Weather during this period cold and dull. Commencing 21st men are allowed one hour off three days a week (Mondays Wednesdays and Fridays) and one Sunday afternoon per month, if exigencies of campaign permit as a reward for their good work. G.H.C.

**25th.** 4th Div. lorries go off road tomorrow necessitating change of railhead to ECURIE which would mean the company moving up near there. Went up to H.Q. Div. Arty. 4th Train & 4th Cdn. Div. to arrange matters. Weather, dull and cool. G.H.C.

**26th.** Moved Company to LETARGET. Drawing by horse transport today, from ECURIE Railhead. Wagons delivered to units first thing in morning, and upon being off loaded reported to ECURIE railhead, cleared pack train and reported to new lines. Same system employed here as at BRUAY and latterly at BRAY. Took over lines formerly occupied by 3rd Coy. Divl. Train. Very comfortable and conveniently settled here. Weather cool and dull. G.H.C.

**27th and 28th.** Nothing to record. Weather wet and cold.

**29th.** Received warning order that 5th C.D.A. would move shortly with Cdn. Corps. Weather for this day, dull and cool, raining in afternoon. Went up to H.Q. 5th C.D.A. to get any particulars of move. Nothing known. G. H. Coops, Major, 5th Cdn. Divl. Train.

**30th.** *[No entry recorded for 30th April.]*
