## Diary text

Army Form C. 2118. "Original". Vol. VIII. 5th Can. Divl Train Det.

Printed heading: "WAR DIARY or INTELLIGENCE SUMMARY" ("INTELLIGENCE SUMMARY" struck through). "Instructions regarding War Diaries and Intelligence Summaries are contained in F. S. Regs., Part II. and the Staff Manual respectively. Title pages will be prepared in manuscript."

Columns: Place | Date 1918 | Hour | Summary of Events and Information | Remarks and references to Appendices. The Hour column is blank throughout this month.

## Appendices

The appendices are handwritten on squared paper, signed by Capt. G. W. Cooper, O.C. 5th Cdn. Divl. Train Det. They are the movement and supply orders referred to in the diary text.

### Appendix I — Supply and Transport arrangements, 12 March 1918

Headed: "App. I Vol. VIII Page 1."

> Supply and Transport arrangements in accordance with 5th C.D.A. O 50/1/205 dated 9th March 1918.
>
> (1) The 5th C.D.A. will be relieved by the 2nd C.D.A. on 14th and 15th March 1918.
> (2) Batteries complete of 5th C.D.A. less personnel of one section and proportion of H.Q. part [uncertain] will proceed to BRUAY area on 15th March.
> - The Trench Mortar Brigade 5th C.D.A. will proceed to BRUAY area on 15th March.
> - The 5th D.A.C. will probably remain in BARLIN.
> (3) 13th Bde C.F.A. will take over wagon lines of 16th Bde. C.F.A. at MARLES MINES. 14th Bde C.F.A. will take over wagon lines of 5th Bde C.F.A. at HALLICOURT. Divisional Train Detachment will be in BRUAY.
> (4) The movement of H.Q. parties and remaining personnel of the 5th C.D.A. to the BRUAY area will take place on 16th March.
>
> Supply Arrangements.
>
> (1) Rations will be delivered as usual on morning of 14th March (for consumption 15th March)
> (2) Rations for H.Q. parties and remaining personnel of 5th C.D.A. moving on 16th March will be called for by units at [continues overleaf]

Headed: "App I Contd."

> later than 12 a.m. 14th inst. at Supply Dump. FOSSE 10. (for consumption 16th March)
> (3) Balance of Rations for issue on 15th for consumption 16th March, will be delivered to Units wagon lines in rear area on afternoon of 14th March instead of morning of 15th March. Units should therefore have a representative at their new lines to receive same.
> No delivery to Units will be made on 15th March.
> Rations for consumption 17th March will be delivered as usual on 16th March in new area for complete personnel.
>
> Transport Arrangements.
>
> (1) The 5th C.D.T. Detachment draws from SAINS-BOUVIGNY railhead for the last time on 14th March and for the first time from HOUDAIN on 15th March
> (2) The Detachment will move from FOSSE 10 on afternoon of 14th March taking up new lines in BRUAY same date.
> (3) Horse transport will be used throughout the move.
> (4) All Baggage wagons at present "On Command" to Units will report back to Divl Train lines immediately on completion of move.
>
> G. W. Cooper Capt. O.C. 5th C.D.T. Det.

### Appendix II — 5th Cdn. Divl. Arty. Moves, 12 March 1918

Headed: "5th Cdn. Divl. Arty. Moves. App. II Vol. VIII Page 1. Ref. Map. 36 B. Sw. 1/40,000."

> I The 5th Cdn. Divl. Train Detachment will move from its present lines to J 15 d 8.0.80 in BRUAY on afternoon 14th March.
> Starting Point — Cross Roads PETIT SAINS R 26.5.8
> TIME 1 P.M.
> Route. Hersin, Barlin, Hallicourt.
> Supplies will be delivered to units as usual on morning of 14th March, and drawn from SAINS-BOUVIGNY railhead for the last time same date.
> Rations and forage for personnel of 5th C.D.A. moving to rear area on 16th March will be called for by units themselves at Supply dump not later than 12 o'clock noon 14th March, with the exception of H.Q. 5th C.D.A. which rations and forage will be delivered to their present wagon lines by Train transport. Wagon reporting back to their lines immediately upon completion of this task.
> Rations and forage for remainder of 5th C.D.A. will be loaded into Train Wagons of respective units not later than 12 o'clock noon 14th March and parked.
> [uncertain] will have wagons cleaned.

Headed: "App. II Contd."

> Drivers will have their horses and harness touched up, and Convoy, in charge of Lieut. Paborn, will be ready to move off at 12.45 P.M. 14th March.
> Special attention to be paid to men's personal appearance.
> Company wagons will be spotted and loaded at various points (i.e. Cook House, Q.M. Stores etc.) as soon as available on morning of 14th and will be parked including water cart on Dump road ready to head Column.
> Postal Orderly wagon included in above.
>
> II. Billets & Lines.
>
> The Q.M.S. will be held responsible that all Billets and lines now occupied by this Unit are left in a clean and Sanitary condition, before hour of departure.
> Representatives of relieving Company will report during 13th March to take over lists of Billets and lines, and every assistance should be given them.
> One N.C.O. and one other rank, including Supply Detail of this Detachment will proceed on morning 13th March to J 15 d 80.80 in BRUAY to take over lists of Billets and lines now occupied by relieving Company.
> Accommodation for the night will be arranged by respective Units for this personnel.

Headed: "App. II Contd."

> III. Locations — wagon lines.
>
> 13th Bde C.F.A. — MARLES-LES-MINES.
> 14th [Bde C.F.A.] — HALLICOURT.
> 5th D.A.C. — BARLIN.
> 5th Divl Train — BRUAY.
> 5th C.D.A. — BRUAY.
> Trench Mortars — BRUAY.
> F. Post Office — BRUAY.
>
> Convoy in Charge of Lieut C. A. PABORN will leave Starting Point at 1 P.M. and proceed to BRUAY area delivering rations and forage to new wagon lines of respective Units there. Where representatives will be waiting to receive them.
> The convoy will be built up as follows.
> (1) Company (Headquarters section) wagons
> (2) Trench Mortar wagons.
> (3) 13th Bde. C.F.A. Convoy.
> (4) 14th [Bde. C.F.A.] Convoy.
> (5) D.A.C. Convoy.
>
> One Sergeant will be in Charge of D.A.C. and 14th Bde. Convoy, and one Sergeant in Charge of 13th Bde Convoy.
> Immediately upon wagons being off loaded in rear area, they will proceed to Cross Roads at J 10 d 8.9 at which point Lieut Paborn will be [continues overleaf]

Headed: "App. II Contd."

> stationed and receive further orders as to their despatch to new wagon lines of this Unit.
> Railhead commencing 15th March will be HOUDAIN.
> Reveille 14th March 5 A.M.
>
> As the Detachment will most likely come in close contact with other Divisions and most certainly with No. 1 Coy. 2nd Divl Train, during this move, the O.C. this Unit hopes we will make our usual favorable appearance, and every one is therefore asked to take a special interest in their equipment, so that when we do move off to new surroundings, our present favorable reputation will be lived up to.
>
> G. W. Cooper Capt.
> O.C. 5th Cdn. Divl Train Det.
>
> 12-3-18.

## Transcriber's notes — March 1918

- Coverage: every day 1–31 March is accounted for. The 9th–13th dates in the diary-text date column are smudged/illegible but are inferred from sequence (8th entry, then 14th). 31 March has no separate entry; the 30 March entry runs to the foot of diary-text page 3, followed by the signature.
- No battle casualties are recorded this month. The only death named is Major V. J. Kent, whose funeral Capt. Cooper attended on 5 March (no cause of death is given in the diary).
- Movements this month:
  - 14 March: FOSSE 10 to BRUAY (rest area), per 5th C.D.A. O.O. No. 26; railhead changed from SAINS-BOUVIGNY to HOUDAIN on 15 March.
  - 22 March: BRUAY to ESTREE CAUCHIE, loaded; pack train cleared by Supply Column.
  - 23 March: back to the former lines at FOSSE 10.
  - 30 March: FOSSE 10 to BRAY, near MONT ST. ELOI; railhead FREVIN-CAPELLE, drawing through 4th Canadian Division by lorry.
- [uncertain] items:
  - Contents page: "Volume 18-8" (cover gives Volume 8).
  - 8 March: "Submitted D. & T. arrangements" — the abbreviated words are read as "D. & T." but are not fully clear.
  - 30 March: "Capt Coste" — surname read as "Coste"; the same officer is written "Costi" in February (25th) and in Appendix IV. The lieutenant accompanying Cooper on the 30 March reconnaissance is not legible; the name appears to begin "Clen-" but is marked [uncertain] rather than guessed.
  - Appendix I (2): a short word after "proportion of H.Q. part" is not legible.
  - Appendix II (I): the final line reads "[uncertain] will have wagons cleaned" — the subject word (apparently "London" or similar) is not clear.
  - Appendix III: the movement table's Route/Destination columns run together for the 13th and 14th Brigades; the destinations are transcribed as written with [uncertain] flags. "5th Divl. Train [uncertain]" — a word after "Train" is not clear.
  - Appendix IV (II): two short phrases in the convoy route sentence are not legible.
  - Appendix V: the heading's O.O. date is written "29th March 1918" while the appendix itself is signed "24-3-18" — transcribed as written; the apparent inconsistency is in the original. "Lens 11" in the map references is not fully clear. "Cross Roads at PENDE" — the place name is not fully clear.
  - Appendix V (II): no move-off time is stated for the 13th Bde C.F.A. ration convoy (the 14th Bde's is 7 a.m.).
