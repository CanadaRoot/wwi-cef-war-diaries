## Page 3 — Vol. 5 Sheet 2, MAY 1917

**Wednesday 16**
Weather – Cloudy – mild – Test mobilization 2, 3 + 4 Coys – Message received by them at THURSLEY COMMON – 3.30 P.M. All packed + ready to move 6. P.M.

**Thursday 17**
Weather – Raining – cold – Test mob. #1 Co. Message received by them 6.45 A.M. Ready to move at 10.30 A.M.

**Friday 18**
" – Cloudy – Cool – ROUTINE.

**Saturday 19**
" – Cloudy – Cool – Gas practices commence.

**Sunday 20**
" – Clear – warm – C.R.A. + his Bde. Commanders surprise inspected stables 10 A.M. Inspection by A.A. + Q.M.G. at 11 A.M.

**Monday 21**
" – Cloudy – cool – Inr. Office report this unit all clear. [uncertain]

**Tuesday 22**
" – Fine – warm – Horses redistributed + all companies brought to strength.

**Wednesday 23**
" – Fine – warm – Gas practice in stables –

**Thursday 24**
" – Fine – warm – Holiday – Capt. E.M. COOPER reports

**Friday 25**
" – Fine – warm – Transport of units released from this camp inspected by C.O.

**Saturday 26**
" – Fine – hot – Fire apparatus tested – 3400 troops move O'seas. [Overseas]

**Sunday 27**
" – Fine – hot – 2100 new troops move in

**Monday 28**
" – Fine – warm – ROUTINE

**Tuesday 29**
" – Fine (a.m.) Showery (P.M.) violent thunderstorm 9.30 P.M. #2 Co. on Bivouac Cutmill Pond – horse injured

**Wednesday 30**
" – Cloudy – warm – #3 Co. Biv. at CUTMILL POND – #2 Co. at WAVERLEY ABBEY – Inspected by C.O. + Adjt. – D.A. of T. + D.D. of S. [uncertain]

---

## Page 4 — Vol. 5 S. 3, MAY 1917

**Thursday 31**
Weather – Fine – warm – Inspection by G.O.C + A.A. + Q.M.G. –
#3 Co. at CUTMILL POND 10.30 A.M.
#2 Co. Hq. section at cross road TILFORD
#2 Co. Supply section at R.P. – MILLBRIDGE [uncertain]
#1 Co. moved out to CUTMILL
#2 Co. reported in all correct
1 horse #341 – injured in storm – destroyed.
Officers parade re saluting
#3 Co. inspected at WAVERLEY ABBEY by C.O. + Adjt.

DIARY (Vol. 5) ENDS

[Signed:] [signature] J.A. Shaw, Lt. Col. Commanding 5th Can. Div. Train.

---

## Notes on this month

- No entries are missing: every day 1–31 May 1917 has an entry.
- "DIVARTY" is the diary's own spelling for Divisional Artillery.
- The "Inr. Office" entry on 21 May is as written; the abbreviation is not clearly legible.
- The horse injured in the 29 May thunderstorm was destroyed on 31 May (horse #341).
