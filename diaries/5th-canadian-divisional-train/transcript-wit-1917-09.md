## Transcriber's notes

- The title page reads "TO:- September 31, 1917" and Sheet 2 lists a "MONDAY 31" entry. September has only 30 days; both are transcribed exactly as written in the diary.
- "NONANDY COMMON" (22 September) is spelled as typed in the diary; it may be "Normandy Common". Marked [uncertain].
- No dates are missing: every day 1–30 September 1917 has an entry (plus the diary's extra 31st).
