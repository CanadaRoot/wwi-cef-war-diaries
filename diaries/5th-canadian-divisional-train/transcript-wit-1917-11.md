## Transcriber's notes

- The title page reads "TO:- November 31, 1917". November has only 30 days; transcribed exactly as written. The place line on the title page (where "WITLEY" appears in other months) is overwritten and cannot be read.
- "Capt. HORISON" (5 November) is spelled as typed in the diary.
- "Lieut MACABE" (17 November) is spelled as typed; the February 1918 sheet confirms the spelling "Macabe".
- No dates are missing: every day 1–30 November 1917 has an entry.
- Unlike other months, the November volume has no closing "DIARY ENDS" line or signature on its final sheet.
