## Diary text

Place: FREVIN CAPELLE, France (1–13 July); Sheet 51c. F20a 7.7, ECOIVRES AREA (14 July); Sheet 51c F20a 7.7, Bray, France (17–31 July).

### 1 July 1918

Made no delivery of rations to-day, off-loaded Pack, re-filled and packed wagons, majority of Personnel given leave to attend Corps Sports — weather — fine and very warm. visited Corps Sports in afternoon. The following O.R's. reported yesterday afternoon from the 5th M.T. detachment, attached for duty and discipline to act as loaders. No. 139539 Pte. CAPE J. No. 29 Pte. DOVE H. No. 510030 Pte. CULHAM J. No. 434775 Pte. GROSVENOR R.B. No. 3056 Pte. RESIDE G. G.F.C.

### 2 July 1918

Usual routine carried out, three new cases of "3 day fever" reported making seven cases in all at present time, weather — fine and very warm G.F.C.

### 3 July 1918

Usual Routine carried out, Inspection of Small Box Respirators 7 P.M. weather — fine + cool G.F.C.

### 4 July 1918

Inspection of equipment 2 P.M., Pay Parade .5 P.M. visited Can. Corps with Staff Capt. "A" re - leave allotment, saw asst. to O.i/c C.A.S.C. Personnel re-obtaining second rations for Supply Detail. weather - fine and cool. G.F.C.

### 5 July 1918

Men's bath - Parade 1.30 P.M. Pack Train left this morning, arrived 8.15 A.M. [?] Sgt. #80093 Neale E.W. i/c 5th Cdn. Divl. R.O. attached this unit ordered to report to 2nd Cdn. Div. replaced by 1/Sgt. #35125 Chagnon F. H., 1 O.R. attached from 5th M.T. Coy returned for duty to 5th M.T. Coy Weather — cloudy and cool G.F.C.

### 6 July 1918

39 lbs sugar and 4 tins milk reported stolen from T.M. Bde. Timber last night while under guard; ordinary routine carried out, weather - fine + cool. G.F.C.

### 7 July 1918

Conferred with A.P.M. 15th Division re. loss of rations [uncertain — remainder of entry cut off at foot of page] to search French Civilian's house tomorrow in Frevin [Capelle] — [uncertain] men ill with epidemic in unit [uncertain — last line(s) cut off at foot of page]. G.F.C. Capt. a/O.C. 5th Cdn. Divl. Train

### 8 July 1918

No Oats on Pack Train this morning had M.T. [uncertain] on and drew from F.S.D. Wavans, would not refill until 3 P.M.; with authority of Major and with French Gendarmes searched civilian's house at 2 P.M. suspected in connection with theft of rations night of 5th-6th, recovered sugar in similar quantity to that stolen also numerous other government stores. Twenty-three cases of epidemic in Unit today. Weather fine and very hot. G.F.C.

### 9 July 1918

Morning spent in investigation of ration theft, trying to identify soldier who turned over rations to civilians, not successful. Eighteen cases of Epidemic in Unit today. ordinary routine carried out. Weather fine in morning showers in afternoon, fine + cool in late evening, received [uncertain] Next Division administrative [uncertain] will change shortly. Weather alternately fine and squally with short showers of rain. Received wire from S.S.O. 1st Can. Div re-administering this formation for rations, drove to Villers-Chatel in afternoon, saw S.S.O. 1st Div Canadians who showed me an order from "Q" 1st Div. that we would be rationed by them [uncertain — last lines partly cut off at foot of page] 15th [uncertain] necessary arrangements [uncertain] in unit today. G.F.C. [?] Capt.

### 10 July 1918

[No separate entry — the 10th appears only as the date column for the continuation of the 9 July entry; see note above. No distinct 10 July entry is visible on the page.]

### 11 July 1918

Weather - intermittently fine and heavy showers. Drove to H.Q. 5th C.D.A. in morning, intimated by "Q" that we would come under 4th Cdn. Division from 13th inst. and not 1st Cdn. Div. Drove to ECOIVRES and VILLERS-CHATEL in evening saw S.S.O. 4th and 1st Divs. respectively, altering arrangement of previous day, will now be rationed through 4th Cdn. Div. but keep FREVIN-CAPELLE as railhead. Unit routine carried out as usual, fourteen cases of Epidemic in unit today G.F.C.

### 12 July 1918

One O.R. reported from 5th T.M.B to act as loader two O.R.'s reported from 5th M.T. Coy. attached as Railway loaders. Lieut. T. [uncertain — surname illegible] on strength from C.A.S.C. Pool to complete Establishment with effect 9-7-18. (Auth. Cdn. Corps A102-5-18 dated 10-7-18.) weather - uncertain alternating fine and showers, Company routine carried out as usual G.F.C.

### 13 July 1918

Weather - uncertain fine + showers. Received phone message from H.Q. 5th C.D.A. that we were to move to ECOIVRES area. Drove to H.Q. 4th Div. Train re move then went on to H.Q. 5th C.D.A. received definite orders to move, to be clear of FREVIN-CAPELLE by noon [uncertain — last lines cut off / overlapping at foot of page] necessary arrangements [uncertain] in evening with C.S.M. [uncertain] one of [uncertain] attacked. [uncertain] Capt. [?] 5th Cdn. Divl. Train Detachment.

### 14 July 1918

Mechanical Transport off-loading pack at 7.00 A.M. advance party consisting of [uncertain] + horse line picketing party left for ECOIVRES area 7.30 A.M., ration convoy as usual to Units, empty wagons reporting back to new location. Remainder section of the Company moved off from FREVIN-CAPELLE at 10.00 A.M. New refilling point at F20b 7.5, no refilling done as railhead will remain FREVIN-CAPELLE until 17-7-18, when it changes to MT. ST. ELOY this [uncertain] convoy of unit Ration wagons to railhead before delivering to units. Drove to H.Q. 5th C.D.A. to report move completed at 3.00 P.M. While this [uncertain] word that Major Cooper was at MT. ST. ELOY station returning from leave, drove there and brought Major Cooper to unit. Weather fine in early morning, rain commenced about 11.00 A.M. raining hard at night. G.F.C.

### 15 July 1918

Weather - fine and warm. Busy reviewing accumulated correspondence. Orders [uncertain] H. [?] Rother [uncertain] [inserted:] 1.50 [?] 645359 Dvr. Barrett R. transferred to 58th Battery, [uncertain] 10-7-18

### 16 July 1918

1.50 A.M. a terrible electrical storm accompanied by wind and rain broke over us. Visited 5th C.D.A. H.Q. in connection with leave for Lieut-Paterson, and other such questions. G. Cooper Major. O.C. 5th Cdn. Divl. Train.

### 17 July 1918

Weather - fine and warm in morning. Visited O i/c C.A.S.C. Personnel PERNES this morning in connection with Lieut-Paterson return to England, A/Cpl. Sdr. Buller being transferred, and question of promotion of N.C.O.'s. also consulted him re establishment and possibilities of obtaining an appointment on [uncertain] staff as a "learner". All very satisfactory to me. G.C.

### 18 July 1918

Weather fine and warm. Outside of general routine nothing worthy of note. Witnessed one of our aeroplanes "crash" in field nearby about 1.30 P.M. G.C. went to Pilot's assistance with Major [uncertain] 5th C.D. Train and Capt. Cole. G.C.

### 19 July 1918

Weather fine and warm. Visited H.Q. 5th C.D.A. with Capt. Cole also 14th Bde. H.Q. C.F.A. (near) on way to St. NICHOLAS (rear areas) for Brass P. [uncertain] G.C. #515799 Cpl. Swift J.W. admitted 13th Fd. Ambulance - Influenza. G.C.

### 20 July 1918

Weather - fine and moderately cool. Visited 14th Bde H.Q. with Capt. Cole Pay Parade 2 P.M. today. 515303 Pte Moore A.J. appointed a/Lcpl with out pay. G.C.

### 21 July 1918

Weather fine and moderately warm. Lt. Paterson given day off to visit Amiens. [uncertain — last lines partly cut off at foot of page] 515 [uncertain] Mercer C.A. attached from [uncertain] [uncertain] on charge neglect to prejudice of good order + military discipline. G. Cooper Major.

[Continued on det-page-0086:]

515748 Dvr. White F.H. and 4700 Dvr. Young L. appointed a/Lcpls without pay. [uncertain] detailed to take summary of evidence in case against Dvr. Mercer. [uncertain]

### 22 July 1918

Weather fine and warm Visited all the Batteries in afternoon with Capt. Cole. Felt rather stiff this morning as a result of our long ride. Usual routine being carried out in Camp. G.C.

### 23 July 1918

Weather dull and moderately cool. 51069 Cpl. Cole J.S. returned from leave. Nothing to report.

### 24 July 1918

Weather fine and warm. Engaged all morning in reorganizing sections etc. of Company. Visited 5th C.D.A. H.Q. in reference to leave in Paris for Capt. Cole Canteen opened. 510911 Buller A.E. (Saddler Cpl) despatched to H.Q. 1st Corps [uncertain] in accordance with O i/c C.A.S.C. Personnel wire. Nothing further to record. G.C.

### 25 July 1918

Weather fine & cool. Held daily inspection of transport 7.30 a.m. Heavy bombing in immediate neighborhood tonight by enemy machine. No damage or casualties caused to this unit. G.C.

### 26 July 1918

Weather cool and cloudy in morning. Rain at midday. Court Martial held in lines in case of 77053 Dvr. Mercer this morning — Acquitted. G.C.

### 27 July 1918

Weather fair and occasional showers. Mess dinner given in evening in honor of my brother's birthday and transfer to Royal Air Force. G.C.

### 28 July 1918

Weather fine occasionally numerous showers. Visited H.Q. 14th Bde. and 5th C.D.A. with Capt. Cole and Lt. Paterson. Capt. Cole attending sports meeting. All men available of Company allowed afternoon off. G.C.

### 29 July 1918

Weather - fine. Received word that Division would move in a day or so. Nothing definite. Arrangements made for a sudden move. G.C.

### 30 July 1918

Weather - fine and warm. Visited H.Q. 4th Cdn. Divn with S.S.O. 4th Div. to get information re move. Nothing to hand yet. Kept in close touch with 5th Cdn. D.A.Q. all day. Proceeded to PERNES to interview O i/c C.A.S.C. Personnel in connection with Establishment. Car broke down just outside of DIVION. Stopped lorries to PERNES and return arriving back at 6 P.M. Lunched at PERNES G. Mess. Car in workshop. Greatly inconvenienced at this important time as a result. Rations delivered to all units this evening instead of morning 31st Aug. Final arrangements made for entrainment. G.C.

[The entry as written says "31st Aug." — presumably 31st July; transcribed as written.]

### 31 July 1918

Weather fine and warm. Lorries cleared Pack Train MONT ST. ELOI this morning at 9 a.m. proceeding to new area with Capt. Cole and Supply details. Car still in workshop. Most inconvenient. Lieut. Paterson + Love visited batteries of 13th and 14th Brigades respectively this morning in connection with H.Q. orders (received 1.30 a.m.) to despatch 3 Divisional Train wagons to Brigade H.Q. and 1 wagon per Brigade back to Divl. Train (52nd and 66th Batteries affected in least) Lieut-Paterson and one other rank detailed for Billeting party, leaving by train No 1 from Savy. Remainder of Company consisting of 2 officers, 69 other Ranks, 34 horses, 10 G.S. wagons, 2-2 wheeled vehicles and 4 Bicycles moves from wagon lines BRAY marching to AUBIGNY Station arriving there at 3.45 P.M. Entrainment complete 5.00 P.M. Lieut. Love and self visited tea at Officers Club Aubigny. Left Aubigny at 7.13 P.M. on train No 1 with H.Q. [uncertain] for new area. Everything ran very smoothly throughout. Lt. Love and Self marched with men all the way, very sore all over as a result of our indulgence. G.C.

> G. N. Cooper Major.
>
> O.C. 5th Cdn. Divl. Train Det.

## Transcriber's notes — July 1918

- All 31 days are covered; there is no distinct 10 July entry — the 10th date column carries the tail of the 9 July entry. The 7, 9, 13, 15 and 21 July entries are partly cut off at the foot of the page in the images.
- Officers named: Major G. N. Cooper (O.C.); Capt. Cole (G. F. Cole, a/O.C. early in the month); Lieut. Paterson; Lieut. Love; Staff Capt. "A"; Lieut. T. [uncertain] (joined from C.A.S.C. Pool, 9 July).
- Other ranks named: Ptes. Cape J. (139539), Dove H. (29), Culham J. (510030), Grosvenor R.B. (434775), Reside G. (3056); Sgt. Neale E.W. (80093); 1/Sgt. Chagnon F.H. (35125); Dvr. Barrett R. (645359, transferred to 58th Battery); Cpl. Swift J.W. (515799, admitted 13th Field Ambulance, influenza); Pte. Moore A.J. (515303); Cpl. Cole J.S. (51069); Saddler Cpl. Buller A.E. (510911); Dvrs. White F.H. (515748), Young L. (4700), Mercer (77053, court-martialled and acquitted 26 July).
- Sickness: "3 day fever" (7 cases by 2 July); an epidemic (influenza) rising to 23 cases on 8 July, 18 on 9 July, 14 on 11 July.
- Notable incidents: theft of 39 lbs sugar and 4 tins of milk from T.M. Bde. Timber on the night of 5–6 July; house search with French gendarmes on 8 July recovering sugar and other government stores; enemy bombing near the unit on the night of 25 July (no damage or casualties); an aeroplane crash witnessed 18 July.
- Moves: Frevin-Capelle to Ecoivres area (Bray) on 14 July; on 31 July the detachment entrained at Aubigny for a new area, following the relief of the 5th C.D.A. by the 52nd D.A.
