WAR DIARY

[Printed:] Instructions regarding War Diaries and Intelligence Summaries are contained in F.S. Regs., Part II. and the Staff Manual respectively. Title pages will be prepared in manuscript.

**February 1st 1917, WITLEY CAMP, Friday**

Strength (Incr) 20 O.R. from #3 Depot C.A.S.C [as written]
Attachment Lieut E.S. HARRISON – Sub. [uncertain] 12th Inf Bde attached for duty + instruction
Discipline GUILDFORD out of bounds for O.R. without passes.
Passes of O.R. on leave in LONDON to be viséd in future.
Capt. MILLAR reports.

**February 2nd 1917, WITLEY CAMP, Saturday**

Strength (Incr) – 1 O.R. from Bramshott BRAMSHOTT.
" (Decr) – 1 O.R. To BRAMSHOTT
Lieut BOLDUC to SHORNCLIFFE
Horses (Decr) – 1 H-D – 2 L.D. to Hospital
Appts. – Capt. H. MILLAR to be Adjutant
– Lieut WHITEHEAD to be acting R.T.O
do HORNE " to be " O. i/c Forage
Fire Protection – Fire buckets to be kept filled + inside huts during cold weather. To be carefully watched by O.O. – Hydrants covered with straw.
[Remarks:] Authy DST 23.1.17 " O.C. " O.C.

**February 3rd 1917, WITLEY CAMP, Sunday**

Strength (Decr) – Lieut CAVERHILL to HASTINGS – 2 O.R to C.D.D. HASTINGS.
Attachmts (Incr) – Major STEPHENS + Capt. O'TESAN – 171st Bn. for quarters + rations.
Establishment – Endeavours made to get amended establishment officially.
Weather – Clear and cold.

**February 4th 1917, WITLEY CAMP, Monday**

Weather – Clear and cold.
Duties – Companies handed over to Company Commanders.
" Daily routine + detail of returns published.
Training – Return of men available for training called for – None available
Horses – Allotted to Cos. by colour – Hq. Co. Bay – #2 Co. Grey – #3 Co. Light bay – #4 Co. Dark brown and black. Counted by O.C. 436. by Capt MILLAR + R.S.M. 435.
Waggons – Allotted – Hq. # 1 to 35 – #2 Co, 36–82 – #3 Co, 83–109, #4 Co. 110 to 136.

## Page 3 — Vol. 2 Sheet 2

**February 5th 1917, WITLEY CAMP, Tuesday**

Strength (Incr) – Lieut GERRARD reports.
" (Decr) – 2 O.R. to C.D.D. Buxton + C.C.A.C. HASTINGS
Attachmts (Incr) – 3 Officers C.A.D.C. for quarters + rations.
" (Decr) – 6 O.R to C.G.D.D. – HASTINGS.
Appts. – Lieut FLETCHER to be O. i/c Messing
" WESTMORELAND to be O. i/c Transport.
Detail REDFORD [sic — TEDFORD] to Ration Board for ensuing week.
Transport – Duty Companies Roster provided for + instructions issued covering detailing of Transport.
Interior Economy – Books to be kept + returns required by Train Hqr. notified to Company Commanders
" – Nominal rolls of occupants of huts ordered to be prepared and hung in huts.
" – Instructions published regarding cleanliness of men.
" – Horses in sick lines to be cared for by Companies to which they belong
Fatigues – 4 N.C.O's and 72 men reported and detailed to duties.
[Remarks:] Authy – O.C. " .O.C.

**February 6th 1917, WITLEY CAMP, Wednesday**

Weather – Clear – frosty.
Transport – 2 trucks only instead of 4 report from BRAMSHOTT – at 2 P.M. instead of 9 A.M. Roads bad for H.T. horses slipping. Loads reduced from 3200 to 2860 lbs. Coal hauled from railhead due to breakdown of Imperial contractor. Two 3-Ton lorries requisitioned from London
All Transport worked till m/n [midnight]
Strength (Incr) – Capt. R.J. BENSON [initials uncertain] reported.
Attachmts (Decr) – 7 other ranks returned to C.C.A.C.
Horses – Count at 10.30 P.M. showed total 435

**WITLEY CAMP, Thursday Feb 7 [1917]**

Weather – Clear – very cold.
Strength (Incr) – 2 O.R. from BRAMSHOTT – Major [uncertain] reports.
Attachmts (Decr) – Lieut PARKINS and CARSON [uncertain]
Major MATTHEWS changed from "On strength" to "Attached"
Horses – [uncertain] Rolls revised and numbers allotted.
Transport – 2 trucks report from London. Indents for harness sent in [uncertain]
[Remarks:] Authy – O.C.

**Friday Feb 8**

Weather – Clear – milder.
Strength (Incr) – 17 other ranks, Lieut PATTERSON reports, + Lieut PABORN.
Attachmts (Incr) – 18 other ranks, Capt. McLEAN (V.C.) reports
Strength (Decr) – Lieut STEPHENSON struck off.
General – Serious complaints about coal shortage. Hq. advised that Train has nothing to do with supply – merely furnishes transport as called for by Barracks Officer.

**Saturday Feb 9**

Weather – Clear – cold.
Attachmts (Decr) – 2 O.R. To C.C.A.C. + C.D.D.
Health – Scarlet fever breaks out in Bakery

**Feb 10**

Weather – Clear – milder
Strength (Incr) – Capt HYNDMAN – Lieuts. [two names struck through — illegible] reports
Bakery – 27 O.R. quarantine. Arranged to draw from ALDERSHOT and [uncertain] ... commencing Wednesday next.
All [uncertain] for equipment completed + sent in.

**Feb 11**

Weather – Dull
Horses – Stable piquet count shows total 435
Interior Economy – Weekly report ordered covering kit inspections – health – changes of underwear + [uncertain]
General – Inspection by O.C. discloses much improvement in stables horses + harness, but huts + kits require improving
