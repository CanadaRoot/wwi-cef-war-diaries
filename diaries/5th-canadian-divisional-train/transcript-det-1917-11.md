## Page 3 — Diary text, p. 1

Army Form C. 2118. — WAR DIARY or INTELLIGENCE SUMMARY. (Erase heading not required.)

[Handwritten, top right:] Original. 5th Cdn. Divl. Train Det. Vol. IV. Page I.

Columns: Place | Date 1917. | Hour | Summary of Events and Information | Remarks and references to Appendices

| Place | Date | Hour | Summary of Events and Information |
|---|---|---|---|
| FOSSE 10, France. | Nov. 1st to 8th | | Nothing new or worthy of recording during this period. Weather was bad. Today, the 8th though is fine. Everything working nicely. G.M.C. |
| | 9th to 11th | | Weather fairly fine. Received word unofficially that reinforcements were coming up to us, as a result of my consultation regarding present Establishment, with Col. Wadmore. G.M.C. |
| | 12th | | Draft of reinforcements arrived in lines today. In accordance with G.H.Q. letter 6697 (Q.A.1) dated 8th Oct. 1917. it was composed as follows. 1 C.Q.M.S. — 1 Driver Wheeler. 2 Driver Saddlers — 1 Farrier Sgt. — 2 Driver Farriers — 18 Drivers [uncertain — one further word after "Drivers" is illegible] 1 Staff Sgt. (Clerk) — 1 Clerk — 3 Issuers — 1 Butcher. The letter referred to also called for one Subaltern, but up to the present he has not arrived. A letter from him states he is at Base doing Conducting duty, and is anxious to get up. Great rejoicing among all ranks meeting old friends etc. Things are now eased up considerably, and results should be much better. G.M.C. |
| | 13th | | New draft all settled and at various duties. Weather fine. G.M.C. |
| | 14th | | Nothing new — weather dull. |
| | 15th to 16th [second date uncertain — reads 16th or 17th] | | Weather fine — Expecting Lt. Paborn [uncertain] as a reinforcement any day, as he is in near neighbourhood disposing of a draft. G.M.C. |
| | 18 | | Lieut Paborn [uncertain] arrives. Lt Walker and myself pleased to see him. He is very pleased to be with us. Personnel of Detachment now complete. G.M.C. |

[Note: 19th and 20th November are covered on p. 2. No separate entry for 17th November.]

## Transcriber's notes — November 1917

- The diary text covers 1–30 November 1917, entirely at Fosse 10, in grouped entries: 1st–8th, 9th–11th, 15th–16th, 19th–20th and 22nd–30th. 17th November has no separate entry.
- The reinforcement draft foreshadowed by G.H.Q. letter 6697 (Q.A.1) arrived 12 November: 1 Company Quartermaster Sergeant, 1 driver wheeler, 2 driver saddlers, 1 farrier sergeant, 2 driver farriers, 18 drivers, 1 staff sergeant (clerk), 1 clerk, 3 issuers and 1 butcher. The letter also called for one subaltern, who had not yet arrived (still at Base on conducting duty).
- Lieut. Paborn [uncertain] arrived 18 November as a reinforcement officer; with him the detachment's personnel was complete.
- A Maxwell motor car, No. 45143, was taken on charge 21 November; Capt. Cooper notes it was very handy as he was doing the Supply Officer's duties as well as his own as O.C. Detachment.
- No casualties are recorded in November 1917.
- People named: Capt. G. M. Cooper (O.C. Detachment); Col. Wadmore (consulted on establishment; named as Lieut. Col. Wadmore, O.C. 1st Divl. Train, on 6 September); Lieut. F. A. Walker; Lieut. Paborn [uncertain].
- Abbreviations as written: Divl. = Divisional; Cdn. = Canadian; G.H.Q. = General Headquarters; C.Q.M.S. = Company Quartermaster Sergeant; Sgt. = Sergeant; O.C. = Officer Commanding.
