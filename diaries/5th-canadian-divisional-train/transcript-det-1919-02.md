## Transcriber's notes — February 1919

- Coverage: every day 1–28 February is accounted for. No appendices are listed on the contents page (diary text only, 12 pages); no appendix is cited in the diary text.
- The move to Belgium: the detachment entrained at Beuel on 2 February (after marching from Lannesdorf), detrained at Nameche on 3 February, staged one mile north of Leuze that night, and marched to its new area at Folx-Les-Caves on 4 February, where it remained for the rest of the month. From 6 February the detachment rationed the whole 5th Canadian Division.
- Horses: on 16 February notice was received that 582 horses would be handed over to the Belgian Government at Brussels on 23 February; Capt. Coste arranged the details (including 4 days' forage) and visited Brussels on 21 and 23 February. The detachment was also to feed 850 extra horses from 13 February (revised to 614 on 12 February).
- Command: Major G. W. Cooper returned from leave on 15 February and resumed command; Capt. Coste had kept the diary until then. On 18 February the establishment of the detachment was increased by 1 Major, 1 Batman and 1 Riding Horse (War Office letter 121/Overseas/1454 (S-D), 29-1-19), and Major Cooper was taken on strength 15-1-19.
- One horse died: 1 H.D. horse died and was struck off strength (4 February entry). No human casualties are recorded this month. Dvr. Kraft, W. (513038) was admitted to hospital on 5 February.
- Other notable items: the detachment visited the sandstone caves near Folx-Les-Caves (10 February), supposedly excavated by the Romans for road material; a recreation room was opened with games and periodicals from the Y.M.C.A. officer (13 February); a boxing tournament at 13th Brigade was attended on 15 February; a canteen was started in the company lines (21 February); confidential reports on the unit's officers were rendered for file with H.Q. O.M.F.C. (25 February); Dvr. McNaughton refused leave to Rome (28 February).
- [uncertain] items:
