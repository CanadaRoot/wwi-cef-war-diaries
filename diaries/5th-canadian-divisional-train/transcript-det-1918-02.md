## Diary text

Army Form C. 2118. "Original". Vol. VII. 5th Can. Divl Train Det.

Printed heading: "WAR DIARY or INTELLIGENCE SUMMARY" ("INTELLIGENCE SUMMARY" struck through). "Instructions regarding War Diaries and Intelligence Summaries are contained in F. S. Regs., Part II. and the Staff Manual respectively. Title pages will be prepared in manuscript."

Columns: Place | Date 1918 | Hour | Summary of Events and Information | Remarks and references to Appendices. The Hour and Remarks columns are blank throughout this month.

### Page 1

| Place | Date | Summary |
|---|---|---|
| FOSSE 10 France. | FEB. 1 | Word received that G.O.C. Cdn. Corps (Cdn) would inspect this Detachment tomorrow along with Batteries and [uncertain] Bdes. of 5th Cdn. Div. Arty. Every body working hard to make a good showing. |
| | 2 | No inspection. Lack of time. Every body very much disappointed. S/Cpl. Killik returns from Can. Corps Gas School. Passed. |
| | 3rd to 5th | Nothing to record. G.W.C. |
| | 6th | 470379 Driver Riley W. arrested on charge of Drunkenness. Weather - fine and mild. G.W.C. |
| | 8th | Driver Riley stands to be tried by F.G. C.M. on 3rd Charge of Drunkenness. Prisoner taking Summary of evidence. G.W.C. |
| | 9th to 11th | Nothing new. G.W.C. |
| | 12th | Court Martial in Case of Driver Riley held this afternoon. Capt. G. W. Cooper, Prosecutor. Members of Court. Major S. Clark [uncertain], C.F.A. Pres. Lieuts R. H. Babbage and H. B. Galpin [uncertain] C.F.A. Members. |
| | 13th | Sentence of Dr. Riley confirmed by G.O.C. 5th C.D.A. Sentence 42 days F.P. No 1. |

### Page 2

| Place | Date | Summary |
|---|---|---|
| FOSSE 10. | FEB. 1918 14th | Promulgation of Sentence against Dr. Riley. G.W.C. |
| | 15th | Ordinary Routine being carried out. Busy building Bombproof protections about Stables and other huts. G.W.C. |
| | 16th | Received word that the Corps Commander would inspect 1st line transport of 1st Cdn. Div. on 18th inst. the G.O.C. 5th C.D.A. request that the Detachment enter inspection. G.W.C. |
| | 17th | All arrangements made for participation in Inspection by G.O.C. Cdn. Corps. G.W.C. |
| | 18th | All ranks busy preparing for inspection Indications point to a very favorable appearance. G.W.C. |
| | 18th | Inspected by Lt. Gen. Sir A. Currie, Commanding Cdn. Corps. in the afternoon. Company looked very well and received very complimentary remarks from the G.O.C. Great credit due to all ranks. Received compliments from a number of 1st Divl Train Officers on our appearance. G.W.C. |
| | 19th to 23rd | Nothing to record beyond the fact that Painting of wagons Commenced day after inspection. G.W.C. |
| | 24th | 3348 [uncertain — number overwritten in the original] C.S.M. Davidson C.W. promoted to rank of Warrant Officer Class II auth. Part I order No 6 dated 22-2-18. G.W.C. |

## Transcriber's notes — February 1918

- No dates are missing: every day 1–28 February is covered, in grouped entries (3rd–5th, 9th–11th, 19th–23rd, 26th–28th). Note that 7 February has no entry of its own (the 6th and 8th entries are separate; 7th is not mentioned).
- No battle casualties are recorded this month. The only disciplinary case is Driver Riley W. (470379): arrested 6 February for drunkenness (his 3rd charge), tried by Field General Court Martial 12 February, sentenced to 42 days Field Punishment No. 1, confirmed by the G.O.C. 5th C.D.A. 13 February and promulgated 14 February.
- [uncertain] items:
  - 1 February: the word after "Batteries and" is read as "Bdes." (Brigades) but is not fully clear.
  - 12 February: "Major S. Clark" — surname read as "Clark" but not fully clear; "H. B. Galpin" — surname read as "Galpin" but not fully clear.
  - 24 February: the regimental number before "C.S.M. Davidson C.W." (read as 3348) is overwritten/corrected in the original and is not fully legible.
  - 25 February: "Capt. E. J. Costi" — surname read as "Costi"; the same officer is written "Coste" in the March diary text (30 March) and "Costi" in Appendix IV.
- Place: the detachment remained at FOSSE 10, France, for the whole month.
- People named: Lt. Gen. Sir A. Currie, Commanding Canadian Corps (inspected the detachment 18 February); S/Cpl. Killik (passed Canadian Corps Gas School); Driver Riley W. (470379); Capt. G. W. Cooper (prosecutor at the court martial and O.C.); Major S. Clark, C.F.A. (president of the court martial); Lieuts R. H. Babbage and H. B. Galpin, C.F.A. (members of the court martial); C.S.M. Davidson C.W. (promoted to Warrant Officer Class II); Capt. E. J. Costi (joined as Supply Officer from 2nd Canadian Divisional Train).
- Abbreviations used as written: G.O.C. = General Officer Commanding; C.D.A. = Canadian Divisional Artillery; C.F.A. = Canadian Field Artillery; F.G. C.M. = Field General Court Martial; F.P. No 1 = Field Punishment No. 1; C.S.M. = Company Sergeant-Major; C.D.T. = Canadian Divisional Train.
