| AMETTES, FRANCE. | Sep. 1st | | Weather slightly improved. Warning received Divl. Arty. would move to forward billets on 4th inst. Refilling Point moved to Divl. Train lines. All lines improving horse lines and wagon park. Men in comfortable billets. G.M.C. |
| | 2nd | | Weather bright. Busy checking over equipment etc. of personnel and transport. Engaged "breaking in" A. Co.'s and other personnel of Supply details as to [one word struck out, illegible] receipt, issue of supplies in field. G.M.C. |
| | 3rd | | Weather still continues nice. Same routine as yesterday being carried out. Preparing details for move of formation to forward billets tomorrow. Arrived to [uncertain — HOUPAIN?] le [uncertain] in accordance etc. to D.D.S. & T. and Accounts Dept. 1st Army G.M.C. |

## Page 4 — Diary text, p. 2

Army Form C. 2118. — WAR DIARY or INTELLIGENCE SUMMARY. (Erase heading not required.)

[Handwritten, top right:] Original. 5th Cdn. Divl. Train. Vol. II. Page 2.

| Place | Date | Hour | Summary of Events and Information |
|---|---|---|---|
| BARLIN, France. | Sep. 5th | | Drew from new Railhead, SAINS-BOUVIGNY, for first time. 1st Cdn. Divl. and Divl. Troops 2nd Cdn. Divl. drawing from same Railhead. Using same pack train as 1st Divl. Dump near Railhead. Still delivering to Units, same day as supplies are drawn. Busy putting lines at BARLIN into shape. G.M.C. |
| | 6th | | Consulted Lieut. Col. Wadmore [uncertain] O.C. 1st Divl. Train regarding present establishment of Detachment as despatched from England, and which is altogether inadequate. |
| | 7th | | Weather fair, becoming very warm. No change in routine. Pack train arriving in good time. G.M.C. |
| | 8th | | Weather dull. Same routine regards Railhead. 14th Bde. wagons remaining with Batteries over night. G.M.C. |
| | 9th | | Weather fine. Nothing to record. G.M.C. |
| | 10th to 15th | | Weather fine, with exception of 12th when it was dull. Detachment getting into the run of things fine, despite the fact we are poorly equipped. Received orders to move to FOSSE 10 tomorrow. G.M.C. |
| FOSSE 10. | 16th | | Moved Company to FOSSE 10. No billets whatever. Very comfortable and convenient billets. Company lines only a few hundred yards from our Railhead which make the operating of the Company much simpler. Dump area at Railhead |
| | 17th to 21st | | Weather lovely. G.M.C. |
| | | | Nothing new, with exception that word was received that the C.R.A. would inspect our canvas at Railhead. All ranks busily engaged preparing for same. Weather still fine. G.M.C. |

[Handwritten on squared paper. Headed, top right:] App. I. Vol. II Page I.

5th Cdn. Divl. Arty Moves [uncertain — word after "Arty" unclear]

In accordance with 5th Cdn. O.O. No 4. 3.9.17.

H.Q. Divl. Arty, D.A.C. and T.M.B. remain in present locations till the 5th inst.

Rations for all units of 5th Cdn. will be drawn same as usual from LILLERS Railhead and dumped as usual at present dump.

Rations for the latter group viz H.Q. Divl. Arty, D.A.C. and T.M.B. will be loaded and delivered same as usual. ## Transcriber's notes — September 1917

- The diary text covers 1–30 September 1917. Entries for 10th–15th, 17th–21st and 26th–30th are grouped; 18th–20th and 25th have no separate entries.
- The contents page states "Diary Text 3 Pages" and lists Appendix I as the order of 3-9-17; the appendix (pp. 6–8 of this month's images) is that order, giving the 4 September move to forward billets, routes, map references and billeting areas (Bully Grenay and Carency).
- Places: Amettes, Barlin, Fosse 10, Lillers, Bruay, Sains-Bouvigny (railhead), Houdain, Ferfay, Camblain-Châtelaine, Divion, Rebreuve, Gauchin-Légal, Les 4 Vents, Camblain-l'Abbé, Villers-au-Bois, Hallicourt, Hersin, Petit Sains, Aix-Noulette, Bully Grenay, Carency.
- People named: Capt. G. M. Cooper (O.C. Detachment); Lieut. F. A. Walker; Lieut. Col. Wadmore [uncertain] (O.C. 1st Divl. Train); Capt. Robertson (S/C, 5th Cdn.); Maj. Genl. MacDonell (G.O.C. 1st Cdn. Divl.); a Brigadier-General [name illegible/uncertain] (C.R.A. 5th Cdn.).
- No casualties are recorded in September 1917.
- The detachment was attached to 1st Canadian Divisional Train from 4 September, drawing rations from Sains-Bouvigny railhead.
- Abbreviations as written: Divl. = Divisional; Cdn. = Canadian; Arty. = Artillery; Bde. = Brigade; C.F.A. = Canadian Field Artillery; D.A.C. = Divisional Ammunition Column; T.M.B. = Trench Mortar Battery; C.R.A. = Commander Royal Artillery; G.O.C. = General Officer Commanding; O.C. = Officer Commanding; S/C = Staff Captain; N.C.O. = Non-Commissioned Officer; D.D.S. & T. = Deputy Director of Supplies and Transport; O.O. = Operation Order.
