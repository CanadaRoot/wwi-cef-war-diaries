## Diary text

Army Form C. 2118. "Original". Vol. XVII. 5th Canadian Divisional Train Det.

Printed heading: "WAR DIARY or INTELLIGENCE SUMMARY" ("INTELLIGENCE SUMMARY" struck through on some pages). "Instructions regarding War Diaries and Intelligence Summaries are contained in F. S. Regs., Part II. and the Staff Manual respectively. Title pages will be prepared in manuscript."

Columns: Place | Date | Hour | Summary of Events and Information | Remarks and references to Appendices. The Hour column carries the O.C.'s initials in place of times.

### Page 1

| Place | Date | Summary | App. |
|---|---|---|---|
| Hogne | 1st | Weather — Fine & Cold. Company moved from Jambes to Hogne moving off from Jambes at 7.30 a.m. Convoy in charge of Lieut. Love, Captain Coste and myself going through by Motor to billets. Horses arrived in fairly good shape considering distance travelled. Arrived Hogne 6 P.M. | |
| Cielle | 2nd | Weather — Overcast — Cold. Company moved from Hogne to Marcourt, moving off at 8.45 hours. Lieut Love in charge of convoy, Captain Coste and myself going through by motor. Marcourt filled up with Cavalry which necessitated our going on to La Roche to secure billets. Lieut. Love moved off from Marcourt and proceeded to Cielle on his own initiative where he obtained temporary billets for the night with the result that billeting party remained in La Roche for the night, being considerably inconvenienced. Crankshaft of motor car broke today. Captain Coste secured G.O.C's motor car and proceeded to Corps Troops M.T. Coy., to see about rations and fixing of motor car. Rations did not arrive today. Units considering requisitioning. This is the first day that rations have failed to arrive in due course since the 5th Cdn. Div. Artillery has been in France. 35374 Dvr. Copley proceeds on leave to Eng. | |
| LaRoche | 3rd | Weather — Dull. Company moved from Cielle to LaRoche, leaving Cielle [word struck through in the original] 7.30, arriving LaRoche 8.30. Captain Coste returned at 5.00 hours from a successful mission, having secured rations and a new crankshaft which is to be removed from a Ford being evacuated by Engineers M.T. company. Pte. Knowles, chauffeur busy all day taking down engine to proceed with same to Engineers M.T. Coy by returning Supply Lorries this date. 515529 Dvr. Nicholson, F.H. 152000 Sgt. Webster proceed on leave. | |
| Cherain | 4th | Weather — Wet. Company moved from LaRoche to Cherain. Exceptionally hard pull for horses just north-east of LaRoche. Latter part of trip quite easy. Arrived Cherain 6.P.M. Temporary billets secured here for the night, Mont-le-Ban, our original billeting town being unavailable owing to some Cavalry coming in. 105475 Dvr. McAuley, D. 470034 Dvr. McFarlane, W. admitted to Field Ambulance. | |
| Ourthe | 5th | Weather — Fine — Cold. Company moved from Cherain to Ourthe, moving off at 9.30. On arrival at Ourthe no accomodation found available owing to presence of Cavalry. Company pulled into open field for the night and men placed in best billets available which were poor. 120338 Dvr Brunet, O. 859746 Dvr. Saffin. M. return from leave. | |

## Transcriber's notes — December 1918

- Coverage: every day 1–31 December is accounted for. No appendices are listed on the contents page (diary text only, 5 pages); no appendix is cited in the diary text.
- The march to the Rhine: the detachment crossed the German border on 8 December at about 10.00 a.m. ("thus accomplishing our goal which we have been fighting for for four years and three months") and reached its final destination, Lannesdorf (near Godesberg/Bonn), on 16 December. The G.O.C. R.A. Canadian Corps inspected the column at Buschhoven on 16 December and complimented the horses' condition and the equipment's appearance.
- No battle casualties are recorded this month. Dvrs. McAuley and McFarlane were admitted to Field Ambulance on 4 December and returned from hospital on 17 December. Dvr. Watts was placed under open arrest on 20 December for being absent without leave 1–19 December. CQMS Speirs returned from leave on 25 December having been in No. 8 Canadian General Hospital, Paris, with Spanish Influenza.
- Christmas: the first Christmas in Germany — men's Christmas dinner held 25 December at 5 o'clock, "very successful and enjoyed by all"; the officers' Christmas dinner was held 24 December. The plum puddings and turkeys did not arrive; S/Sgt. Ball procured geese instead.
- Movements this month: 1 Dec — Jambes to Hogne; 2 Dec — Hogne to Marcourt, then on to Cielle (La Roche being full of cavalry); 3 Dec — Cielle to LaRoche; 4 Dec — LaRoche to Cherain; 5 Dec — Cherain to Ourthe; 7 Dec — Ourthe to Beho; 8 Dec — Beho to Wallerode, Germany (crossing the German border); 10 Dec — Wallerode to Hallschlag; 11 Dec — Hallschlag to Schmidtheim; 13 Dec — Schmidtheim to Mechernich; 14 Dec — Mechernich to Heimersheim; 16 Dec — Heimersheim to Godesburg/Lannesdorf.
- Personnel: on 12 December H.Q. 5th C.D.A. ceased to function and command of the column passed to Lieut-Col. Costigan, O.C. 5th C.D.A.C.; 30 other ranks and 26 horses of H.Q. 5th C.D.A. were attached to the detachment. Capt. Coste proceeded to Bonn on 12 December with Major Bovill, Capt. Robertson and Lieut. Garden. General Dodds left the Division on 30 December.
