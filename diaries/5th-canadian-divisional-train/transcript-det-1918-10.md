## Diary text

Place: Sheet 2.6 57c (1 Oct.); D.4a. 2.6 57C, W.28c. 6.6. 51B (2–3 Oct.); W.25C. 7.5. 51B (4–11 Oct.); W.30.d. 7.4, Sheet 51B (12–14 Oct.); S.29.A. 7.5. Sheet 51A, Morenchies (15–20 Oct.); I.1 B.1.7, Sheet 51A, Escaudain (21–31 Oct.).

### 1 October 1918

Weather — Overcast, cool. Lieut. A.F. Reed assumes duties as A/Supply Officer. Drew from Queant Railhead at 15 o'clock by M.T. off Cdn. Corps Troops Pack Train. Made a reconnaissance of Area east of Canal du Nord accompanied by Lieut. Reed with a view to moving company to this area in the morning. G.N.C.

[Note: the September diary gives this officer's initials as "A.K. Reed" (30 Sept.); the October diary gives "A.F. Reed". Both are transcribed as written.]

### 2 October 1918

Weather — Most part fine Cloudy in afternoon. Company moved from present location at 7.30 o'clock to W.28c. 6.6. Sheet 51B Lorries commenced loading at Railhead 8.30 o'clock, arriving Dump 12.30. Supply Dump located in lines. No cover for men or horses. H.Q. and No. 2 Sec. 5th C.D.A. and H.Q. Divarty moved to vicinity of Bourlon Wood. 808740 Dvr. Wanless, W. SOS. Part II. O.No.44. 2-10-18 G.N.C.

### 3 October 1918

Weather — Fine No. 874122 Dvr. Marshall, R. proceeds on 14 days leave to U.K. Battery wagon lines remaining in same location. Visited Headquarters 5th C.D.A. in reference to moving Company back a piece in the neighborhood of Inchy en Artois. Made a reconnaissance in afternoon accompanied by Lieut. Reed. G.N.C.

### 4 October 1918

Weather — Fine. Company moved from present location to W. 25C. 7.5. Sheet 51B. north of Inchy, west of Canal du Nord at 9.30 o'clock. Protection for the men secured here. Refilling Point same location as lines. In afternoon I visited R.S.O. Queant, 5th M.T. Company, Boyelles, and Officer i/c CASC Personnel, Cdn. Corps at Duisans. Enemy Aircraft active in vicinity of Camp through the night, bombs dropping in lines. 2 H.D. horses wounded. G.N.C.

### 5 October 1918

Weather — Fine Drew from Croisilles. I visited H.Q. 5th C.D.A. in afternoon in reference to forthcoming relief with 39th Div. Artillery. Wagon lines of 5th C.D.A shifting slightly. Moving of Company is not necessary at present. 874516 Dvr. MacIntosh, P. S.O.S. Part II. O. No.45. d/5-10-18 G.N.C.

### 6 October 1918

Weather — Cold and Dull with showers. 1 H.D. Horses destroyed by V.O. 13th Brigade, fractured shoulder caused by Enemy Aircraft. 186785 Dvr. Wade, W. proceeds on leave to U.K. 513107 Dvr. Mathews admitted to Hospital as result of fall from horse. Occasional enemy shelling in near vicinity of Camp. 515309 Dvr. Taggart, D.M wounded; sustained broken leg from enemy shell fire. G.N.C.

### 7 October 1918

Weather — Unsettled. 440824 Dvr. Wright, C.P. and 515035 Dvr. Theakston, A.L. return from leave. 2 stray horses returned to No. 3 M.V.S. Nothing further to record. G.N.C.

### 8 October 1918

Weather — Fair & cool 1 H.D. Horse evacuated to No. 3 M.V.S. Kick in head. 451932 Dvr. Faulknor, A.H. and 451833 Dvr. Faulknor, SOS. Pt.II O. No. 46. G.N.C.

[Note: the August nominal roll gives Faulkner J.W. as 451933; the number here is typed 451833. Transcribed as written.]

### 9 October 1918

Weather — Fine. Mens' committee elected for running of Canteen as follows:— 515102 Dvr. McConnell, A.G. 515529 Dvr. Nicholson, F.H. 187292 Dvr. Woodfield, C. G.N.C.

### 10 October 1918

Weather — Dull & Cold H.Q. 5th C.D.A. Rear and all Units move forward. Batteries 13th Brigade in vicinity of Sancourt; 14th Brigade, Sancourt and Tilloy. Visited HQ. 5th C.D.A. in forenoon. Made reconnaissance with Lieut. Reed of forward Area in expectation of moving Company forward in the immediate future. Location of Batteries this afternoon does not warrant moving of Company forward for the present. 513056 L/Cpl. Barnett, C. proceeds on leave to U.K. G.N.C.

### 11 October 1918

Weather — Unsettled—cold Lieut. Love and Section N.C.O's left 6 a.m. for batteries to ascertain if any moves forward had been made. Ration convoy held pending their return at 8.30 o'clock. Batteries in same location as last night. Convoy moved off at 9.30 in charge of myself. Capt. E.P. Coste returns from leave and assumes duties of S.O. Lieut. Reed placed in charge of No. 2 Section. Nos. 1 & 2 Sections, D.A.C. and several Batteries moved forward. While in charge of convoy made reconnaissance to move Company up tomorrow G.N.C.

[Note: "Capt. E.P. Coste" is typed here and elsewhere in October; the officer is "Capt. E. P. Cole" in the July–September diaries and in the August nominal roll. Transcribed as written.]

### 12 October 1918

Weather — Dull with showers. Company moved from W. 25.C. 7.5. intact to this location, ration convoys being despatched to Batteries from here. Lieut. Reed i/c Convoy. Fairly comfortable lines in sunken road. Men in dug-outs. Cover for horses. Trucks arrive late. 784356 Dvr. Zarfas, G.G. and 2 H D. Horses sent "On Command" to 53rd Battery. No. 3656 Dvr. Graham, H.E. returns off leave. G.N.C.

### 13 October 1918

Weather — Cloudy with showers. Lieut. Love i/c Convoy. Trucks arrive late in afternoon. Visited H.Q. 5th C.D.A. with Captain Coste in afternoon. 187356 Dvr. Wakefield, C.W. proceeds on leave to England. G.N.C.

### 14 October 1918

Weather — Fine. Lieut. Love and 152000 Sgt. Webster, P.G. proceed on course in Horse management at [uncertain] this date. Lieut. Reed i/c Convoy. Visited Cdn. Corps Veterinary Evacuating Station at 8.00 a.m. this morning in reference to my charger which was injured some time ago. 887643 Dvr. Miller, L.W. left with Mobile Station to look after same. Made a reconnaissance of country north of CAMBRAI with a view to moving Company forward in the morning. 124093 Dvr. Cant, R. S.O.S. Part II. O. No. 47 [uncertain] G.N.C.

### 15 October 1918

Weather — Unsettled, cool. Company moved from W. 30. d. 7.4. Sheet 51B. to MORENCHIES. Map Loc. S. 29. A. 7.5. Sheet 51A. Fairly comfortably situated. Men in bunkhouse built by Germans. Horse lines in Orchard. Very centrally located for Batteries. Visited H.Q. 5th C.D.A. and made a reconnaissance of area further north in the event of a further move. G.N.C.

### 16 October 1918

Weather — Rain. 135164 Cpl. R. Hoare proceeds on leave to U.K. Visited Railhead, Croisilles in morning with Captain Coste, afterwards proceeding to No. 1 Cdn. C.C.S. to have my ear examined and treated. Advised to enter C.C.S. for a couple of days treatment. Lunched at Officers Club in ARRAS. G.N.C.

### 17 October 1918

Weather — Cloudy & Raining. Making arrangements to enter C.C.S., for a couple of days treatment. No. 2207359 Dvr. Barclay reports from C.C.R.C. as M.T. Driver. Major. Cooper admitted to No. 1 Cdn. C.C.S., this date. G.N.C.

### 18 October 1918

Weather — Fine 35252 S/Sgt. Martell, C.F. Cdn. Postal Corps, Att'd. proceeds on leave in France. 802156 Dvr. Woods, B.E. returns off leave. Nothing further to record. G.N.C.

### 19 October 1918

Weather — Unsettled Major Cooper reports from C.C.S. 778851 Cpl. Brown, G.J. proceeds on leave to U.K. 515309 Dvr. Taggart, D.M S.O.S. Part II O. No. 48. No. 1260 Dvr. Donald, A. S.O.S. on transfer to Cdn. M.G.Corps, Part II. O. No. 48. G.N.C.

### 20 October 1918

Weather — Raining. Batteries moving to vicinity of ROMILY. Lieut. Mathewson, 53rd Battery proceeds to Railhead with Capt. Coste on way to Machine Gun Course having stopped the night with us. 1262264 Dvr. Brown, E.S. despatched to No. 4 MT. Company. Wagons return very late, Batteries moving much further than originally intended. 14th Brigade in vicinity of ESCAUDAIN; 13th Brigade in vicinity of DENAIN. Left 5 o'clock to make reconnaissance of this area with a view to moving Company forward in the morning. 145448 Dvr. Killick A.G. returns off leave. G.N.C.

### 21 October 1918

Weather — Cloudy with rains Proceeded to forward Area in vicinity of Denain, leaving present location at 4 A.M. accompanied by S/Sgts. Millard and Speirs. Located very comfortable quarters for men and fine stabling in ESCAUDAIN. Company moved off from present location at 8.00 a.m. proceeding intact to ESCAUDAIN. Wagons then proceeded to respective units. Batteries moved this afternoon to vicinity of Bellaing, Oisy and Hermin, wagons arriving back very late. G.N.C.

### 22 October 1918

Weather — Cloudy. 512979 Dvr. Booth, A. proceeds on leave to U.K. Nos. 1 and 2 Sections, DAC. move to vicinity of Haveluy. All wagons back 5.30 p.m. 52nd Bty. ration wagons despatched from lines on the 21st. report back to lines 3 p.m. this date some difficulty experienced in locating Battery. G.N.C.

### 23 October 1918

Weather — Fine. 874122 Dvr. Marshall, R. returns off leave. 703357 Dvr. Needham, H.V. S.O.S. Part II Order No. 49. Batteries in same location. Trucks arrive 4 P.M. Nothing of importance to record. G.N.C.

### 24 October 1918

Weather — Overcast Cold. 515092 Dvr. Cox, P.D. proceeds on leave to U.K. Assisting a few civilians to move personal effects from ESCAUDAIN to villages further behind the lines. Refilled 7.30 a.m. G.N.C.

### 25 October 1918

Weather — Fine. In charge of convoy and visited wagon lines of all Batteries. Nothing of importance transpired G.N.C.

### 26 October 1918

Weather — Fine. 186785 Dvr. Wade, W. returns off leave. Went to railhead with Capt. Coste. Drew from Marquoin. Trucks arrive 4 p.m. G.N.C.

### 27 October 1918

Weather — Fine No. 445558 Dvr. Jensen A.C. proceeds on 14 days leave to Eng. G.N.C.

### 28 October 1918

Weather — Fine. Lieut. Love and No. 152000 Sgt. Webster, P.G. return from Horse Management Course. G.N.C.

### 29 October 1918

Weather — Fine. 513056 L/Cpl. Barnett returns from leave. Back axle of car broken. No. 2688404 Dvr. Knowles, J.R. reports from C.C.R.C., as M.T. Driver. G.N.C.

### 30 October 1918

Weather — Fine. Took charge of convoy and visited Battery Wagon Lines. No. 510639 Cpl. Cole, J.S and 513056 L/Cpl. Barnett, C. admitted 13th Cdn. Fld. Amb. DENAIN. 152257 Dvr. Airey, J. proceeds on 14 days leave to Eng. G.N.C.

### 31 October 1918

Weather — Dull. No. 115168 Dvr. Ingram, J.W. proceeds on 14 days leave to Paris. 187356 Dvr. Wakefield returns from leave.

> ———— END ————

## Transcriber's notes — October 1918

- All 31 days are covered. The October entries are typewritten, unlike the handwritten July–September entries; the diary text is only 5 pages (det-page-0119 to det-page-0123).
- Officers named: Major G. N. Cooper (O.C.; admitted to No. 1 Cdn. C.C.S. 17 Oct. for ear treatment, reported back 19 Oct.); Capt. E. P. Cole, typed "Coste" throughout October (Supply Officer, returned from leave 11 Oct.); Lieut. A. F. Reed (acting Supply Officer from 1 Oct., then in charge of No. 2 Section); Lieut. T. Love; Lieut. Mathewson (53rd Battery).
- Casualties: 4 Oct. — 2 heavy draught horses wounded by enemy aircraft bombs; 6 Oct. — 1 horse destroyed (fractured shoulder, enemy aircraft) and Dvr. D. M. Taggart (515309) wounded with a broken leg from enemy shell fire (struck off strength 19 Oct.); Dvr. Mathews (513107) admitted to hospital after a fall from a horse; 30 Oct. — Cpl. J. S. Cole (510639) and L/Cpl. C. Barnett (513056) admitted 13th Cdn. Field Ambulance, Denain.
- Moves: D.4a 2.6 (Bourlon Wood area) to W.28c. 6.6 (2 Oct.), W.25C. 7.5 north of Inchy, west of Canal du Nord (4 Oct.), W.30.d. 7.4 (12 Oct.), Morenchies S.29.A. 7.5 (15 Oct.), and Escaudain I.1 B.1.7 (21 Oct.), following the advance past Cambrai toward Denain and Haveluy.
- Other notes: the 5th C.D.A. was to be relieved by the 39th Divisional Artillery (5 Oct.); the company helped French civilians move personal effects from Escaudain to villages behind the lines (24 Oct.).
