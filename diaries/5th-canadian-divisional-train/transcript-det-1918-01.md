## Diary text

Army Form C. 2118. "Original". Vol. VI. 5th Can. Divl Train Det.

Printed heading: "WAR DIARY or INTELLIGENCE SUMMARY" ("INTELLIGENCE SUMMARY" struck through). "Instructions regarding War Diaries and Intelligence Summaries are contained in F. S. Regs., Part II. and the Staff Manual respectively. Title pages will be prepared in manuscript."

Columns: Place | Date 1918 | Hour | Summary of Events and Information | Remarks and references to Appendices. The Hour and Remarks columns are blank throughout this month.

### Page 1

| Place | Date | Summary |
|---|---|---|
| FOSSE 10. France. | JAN. 1st | Concert held in Church Army Hut for the men. well attended and enjoyed by every one. weather - fine and cold. G.W.C. |
| | 2nd to 4th | Nothing to record. G.W.C. |
| | 5th | Kit inspection of all ranks, also inspection of Billets. Every thing in good order. G.W.C. |
| | 6th to 8th | Nothing of interest to record. G.W.C. |
| | 9th | Lieut. F.A. WALKER struck off strength on being granted leave to Canada. Auth Pt 2 orders #5 5-1-18. G.W.C. |
| | 10th | S/Sgt. Millar reports back from leave G.W.C. |
| | 11th to 16th | weather during this period dull and cold. Still at FOSSE 10 with no change in daily routine. G.W.C. |
| | 17th | 180736 L/Cpl. Cross, 515337 Dr. Harfield, 812195 Dr. Stollery W. are shown "On Command" to 5th Div Signals from this date. G.W.C. |
| | 17th | Gas Parade for D.49 [uncertain]. Results very satisfactory, the Detachment comparing very favorably with all other units of Division. G.W.C. |

### Page 2

| Place | Date | Summary |
|---|---|---|
| FOSSE 10 France. | JAN 1918 17th to 22nd | During this period weather remains for the most part, dull & cold. No change in routine, every thing running smoothly. Word received that we would draw through 1st Can. Div. on 23rd at same [uncertain] Railhead. G.W.C. |
| | 23rd | Drawing through 1st Can. Div. at SAINS-BOUVIGNY Railhead. Lines to remain in FOSSE 10. |
| | 24th to 31st | Routine the same as usual. Nothing to record. Every body wishing for a Change in conditions and surroundings. G.W.C. |

Signed at the foot of page 2:

> G. W. Cooper. Capt.
> O.C. 5th Can. Divl Train Det.

## Transcriber's notes — January 1918

- No dates are missing: every day 1–31 January is covered, in grouped entries (2nd–4th, 6th–8th, 11th–16th, 17th–22nd, 24th–31st).
- No casualties are recorded this month.
- [uncertain] items:
  - Contents page volume number reads "[uncertain] 26-1" (cover gives Volume VI).
  - "Gas Parade for D.49" — the reference after "Gas Parade for" is read as "D.49" but is not fully clear.
  - "at same [uncertain] Railhead" (17th–22nd entry) — the word before "Railhead" is not fully legible; it appears to read "Railhead" itself, possibly preceded by a short word.
  - Service numbers and surnames on 17 January (180736 L/Cpl. Cross, 515337 Dr. Harfield, 812195 Dr. Stollery W.) are transcribed as read; "Stollery" is the least clear of the three surnames.
- Place: the detachment remained at FOSSE 10, France, for the whole month; on 23 January it began drawing rations through 1st Canadian Division at the SAINS-BOUVIGNY railhead.
- People named: Lieut. F. A. Walker (struck off strength, leave to Canada); S/Sgt. Millar (returned from leave); L/Cpl. Cross, Dr. Harfield, Dr. Stollery W. (on command to 5th Divisional Signals); Capt. G. W. Cooper, O.C.
