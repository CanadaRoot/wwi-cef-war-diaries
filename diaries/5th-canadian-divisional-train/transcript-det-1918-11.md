## Diary text

Printed heading: "WAR DIARY or INTELLIGENCE SUMMARY" ("INTELLIGENCE SUMMARY" struck through on some pages). "Instructions regarding War Diaries and Intelligence Summaries are contained in F. S. Regs., Part II. and the Staff Manual respectively. Title pages will be prepared in manuscript."

## Transcriber's notes — November 1918

- Coverage: every day 1–30 November is accounted for. No appendices are listed on the contents page (diary text only, 6 pages), but the 15 November entry cites "APPEND. I" in the Remarks column; no Appendix I page is present among the images for this month.
- The Armistice: on 11 November the detachment entered Belgium (Marchelle) — its first entry into Belgium — and Capt. Cooper read the official wire to cease hostilities at 11 a.m. that day at 5th C.D.A. headquarters in Thulin.
- No battle casualties are recorded this month. Personnel changes are leave, returns from leave, transfers, invaliding to England (Cpl. Cole, Dvr. Mathews), and one horse evacuated (17 November).
- Movements this month: 4 Nov — Escaudain area to HERIN (D. 9.B.9.1, Sheet 51A); 8 Nov — to Anzin (D. 1. B.5.3); 9 Nov — to Quievrechain/Crespin area (S. 9. B. 2.8); 11 Nov — to Marchelle, Belgium (M. 24 B., Sheet 45); 20 Nov — remainder of company to Jemappes; 27 Nov — to Morlanwelz; 28 Nov — to Velaine; 29 Nov — to Jambes (suburb of Namur).
- The detachment's transport was broken up on 15–16 November: 13th Brigade and No. 2 Section D.A.C. attached to 1st Cdn. Div. Train; 14th Brigade and No. 1 Section attached to 2nd Cdn. Div. Train; leave for other ranks cancelled from 21 November because most of the unit was "On Command" to batteries.
- [uncertain] items:
  - 1 Nov place column: "I.1 B. 1.7 Escaudain" — the map reference is not fully clear.
  - 15 Nov: the 13th Brigade march was "cancelled until tomorrow"; the place "HAVRE" (16 Nov entry) is as written.
  - 16 Nov: "5th C.L.H." — the unit to which Dvr. Butler was transferred is read as "5th C.L.H." but is not fully clear. The middle of the 16 November entry is garbled in the original typewriting (overtyping and a repeated sentence) and is transcribed as written.
  - 16 Nov: "1 M. Horse evacuated" (17 Nov) — "1 M." is as written (one mule [uncertain]).
  - 19 Nov: "moved off at 8 to" — the hour is written "8 to" in the original.
  - 20 Nov: "Dvr Cruickshanks, P." — initial read as "P." (cf. "515575 Dvr. Cruickshanks" on 2 Nov, no initial given there).
  - 28 Nov: place column gives "Marlanwelz" (cf. "Morlanwelz" on 26–27 Nov); transcribed as written.
  - 29 Nov: "Departed Velaine 8.30, arrived Jambes 9.30" — the times are as written, though the distance makes the 1-hour journey surprising; transcribed as written.
  - "Jambes" is spelled "Jambes" throughout (the diary's own spelling); "Marchelle" (11 Nov) is as written.
- People named: Capt. G. W. Cooper (O.C.); Capt. Coste; Lieut. Reed; Lieut. Love; CQMS. Speirs (3628); CSM. Davidson, C.W. (7719); S/Sgt. Ball (30261); Capt. Robertson; Gen.
