# War Diary — 3rd Canadian Divisional Signal Company — November 1916

- **Unit:** 3rd Canadian Divisional Signal Company
- **Month:** November 1916 (Volume 12) — this volume covers 1 to 30 November 1916
- **Source:** Library and Archives Canada, Collection Search Item ID 2004905 (RG 9 III-D-3, vol. 5005, microfilm reel T-10853, file 692; finding aid 9-52)
- **Pages:** November 1916, p. 1 – p. 4 (image files page-0067 – page-0070 of the 307-page diary)
- **November 1916 — COMPLETE** (pages 67–70 of 307): cover, contents, "War Diary for Month of November 1916 — Original" divider, diary text 1–30 November, no appendices. Transcribed 2026-10-26.

## Transcription conventions
Original spelling, punctuation and abbreviations kept. Handwritten diary text throughout (Army Form C. 2118, "WAR DIARY or INTELLIGENCE SUMMARY"). `[uncertain]` marks words I could not read with confidence — never guessed. No map coordinates are given in the diary; places are recorded as written. Ditto marks ("do" / „) are not repeated verbatim; the entry they repeat is named. "Divis." = Divisional; "C.M.R" = Canadian Mounted Rifles (see December). A repeated mark in the Remarks column, read as "J.A." [uncertain] — apparently the diarist's initials — appears against most entries; it is noted here rather than repeated against every entry.

## Page 4 — Diary text (1–30 November 1916)

Army Form C. 2118, "WAR DIARY or INTELLIGENCE SUMMARY". Heading on the diary sheet: "3rd Canadian Divisional Signal Compy."

> **Aubigny, 1-Nov** — Usual office routine and patrol of Lines
>
> **1–6** [uncertain — reads "1–6", overlapping the 1 November entry; possibly "2–6"] — [ditto of 1 November]

> **8–17** — Usual Routine
