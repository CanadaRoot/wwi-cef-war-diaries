# War Diary — 3rd Canadian Divisional Signal Company, C.E. — October 1918

- **Unit:** 3rd Canadian Divisional Signal Company, C.E.
- **Month:** October 1918 (Volume 35) — this volume covers 1 to 31 October 1918
- **Source:** Library and Archives Canada, Collection Search Item ID 2004905 (RG 9 III-D-3, vol. 5005, microfilm reel T-10853, file 692; finding aid 9-52)
- **Pages:** October 1918, p. 1 – p. 6 (image files page-0251 – page-0256 of the 307-page diary)
- **October 1918 — COMPLETE** (pages 251–256 of 307): cover, contents, title page, diary text 1–31 October, no appendices. Transcribed 2026-10-26.

## Transcription conventions
Original spelling, punctuation and abbreviations kept. Typewritten diary text throughout (Army Form C. 2118, "WAR DIARY or INTELLIGENCE SUMMARY" — "INTELLIGENCE SUMMARY" struck through on each diary sheet). `[uncertain]` marks words I could not read with confidence — never guessed. Map references are recorded as typed (e.g. "V. 28. c. 9.5."). "C. I." = Canadian Infantry; "O.R." = Other Rank; "O.C." = Officer Commanding; "D.R." = Despatch Rider; "R.N.W.M.P." = Royal North-West Mounted Police; "C.D.A." = Canadian Divisional Artillery; "M.T." = Mechanical Transport; "Divn." = Division; "Hdqrs." = Headquarters; "Bde." = Brigade; "M.C." = Military Cross; "M.M." = Military Medal; "D.C.M." = Distinguished Conduct Medal. A repeated handwritten mark, read as "Kmc" [uncertain] — apparently the diarist's initials — appears in the Remarks column against every entry on pages 4–6; it is noted here rather than repeated against every entry. Each diary sheet also bears the rubber stamp "3RD CANADIAN DIVISION SIGNAL COY." with blank "No." and "Date" lines in the lower left of the Place column.

## Pages 4–6 — Diary text (1–31 October 1918)

Army Form C. 2118, "WAR DIARY or INTELLIGENCE SUMMARY" ("INTELLIGENCE SUMMARY" struck through). Heading on each sheet: "3rd Cdn. Divisional Signal Co'y." (p. 4) / "3rd Cdn. Div'l. Signal Co'y." (pp. 5–6).

### Page 4 — 1–9 October 1918

Place column (rotated): "Advanced Hqrs M. 11.0.0.9" at the top and "V. 28. d. 9.5." lower down; a rotated handwritten note beside the stamp reads "Rear Hqrs." [uncertain].

> **1st** — 8th C. I. Brigade in line west of CAMBRAI; 7th & 9th C. I. Brigades in Division reserve located in BOURLON area, close to Division Headquarters. Good communications to all Units. Salvaging pole and cable. Improving existing communications.
>
> **2nd** — Weather fine. 7th C. I. Brigade relieved by a Brigade of the 2nd Canadian Division. Salvaging and cleaning up all Company equipment. 1 O.R. wounded.
>
> **3rd** — Company employed at salvaging and putting all salvaged material into a usable condition for future operations.
>
> **4th** — Enemy bombed Divisional Headquarters very severely, casualties NIL. Material damage slight. Salvaging continued and much Signal Service equipment saved.
>
> **5th** — 8th C. I. Brigade pushed through CAMBRAI with slight opposition. Communications established to their Headquarters in CAMBRAI at 8.30 a.m. Brigades of 2nd Canadian Division and 57th British Division flanking the 8th C. I. Brigade closed over the front of the latter Brigade and continued the advance. 8th C. I. Brigade now in rest in CAMBRAI.
>
> **6th** — Brigade Sections re-organizing after strenuous period of active operations. Cleaning and overhauling all equipment. Salvaging continued by all Company units and a great deal of material has been secured for future operations.
>
> **7th** — Fitting up Office in Quarry and V. 28. c. 9.5. north west of INCHY, where proposed new Divisional Headquarters will be opened on 10th.
>
> **8th** — Salvaging war material. Large leave allotment to the Company for the 10th instant received. Inspection of gas helmets and rifles held at 9.00 a.m.
>
> **9th** — Nothing to report.

### Page 5 — 10–20 October 1918

Place column (rotated): "QUARRIES V. 28. c. 9.5." and "Headquarters".

> **10th** — Headquarters moved from M. 11. c. 0.0. to V. 28. c. 9.5. at 4.00 p.m. Traffic on time. Accommodation for billets quite inadequate. Division in rest.
>
> **11th** — O.C's. Inspection held at 9.30 a.m. after which large party detailed to salvage poles and wire.
>
> **12th** — Nothing to report.
>
> **13th** — Working all available men on salvaging wire and building new quarters.
>
> **14th** — An allotment for 8 O.Rs. leave vacancies received for 17th instant. Salvaging continued.
>
> **15th** — Weather misty and wet. An additional leave allotment of 9 vacancies received for 18th inst.
>
> **16th** — Weather wet and quite cold. Salvaging wire and poles.
>
> **17th** — Allotted 20 leave vacancies for 24th and 26th. Lieut. R. B. Gibson, M.C., M.M. proceeded on leave on 18th. Salvaging war material and disused telephone circuits.
>
> **18th** — Nothing to report.
>
> **19th** — Warning order received to be ready to move Off.
>
> **20th** — O.O. 193 received. We relieve the 1st Canadian Division. Units commence moving into 1st Canadian Division rear area. Lieut. W. M. Maxey with a party of linement and instruments left for LEWARDE at 8.00 a.m. to prepare communications. Cable detachments and Horse Transport under Lieut. F. Barber D.C.M. proceeded at 7.15 a.m. to new area by March route. Divisional Headquarters will close at V. 28. c. 9.5 at 12.00 noon 21st and open same hour at SOMAIN.

### Page 6 — 21–31 October 1918

Place column (rotated): "WALLERS" and "Headquarters", with small handwritten check marks beside several rows.

> **21st** — Div. Hdqrs. closed at V 28 C 9.5 (N of Queant) and opened at SOMAIN at 1000 hours. 7th. Bde. Hdqrs. Established West of Wandignies, 8th. Bde. at VILLERS-CAMPEAU, 9th. Bde. at HELESMES. Good communication to all Brigades. Billets exceptionally good.
>
> **22nd** — 7th. & 9th. Bdes. pushed through 1st. & 3rd. Bdes. respectively., 8th. Bde. relieved 2nd. Bde. in support. 7th. Bde. Hdqrs. moved forward to HASNON., 8th. Bde. relieved the 9th. Bde. at HELESMES. 9th. Bde. moved forward to V 11 C 3.5 RAISMES., Communication extended and all Bdes. kept in touch with Divn. Hdqrs.
>
> **23rd** — Divn. Hdqrs. closed at SOMAIN and opened at WALLERS at 1400 hours., 7th. Bde. Hdqrs. established at P 27 A 75.75., 8th. Bde. at AHEMBURG., 9th. Bde. at V 11 C 2.5., Sheet 44., Communication from new Hdqrs. to all units established by 12 hours noon.
>
> **24th** — Existing communications strengthened., Capt. S. B. Iler MC., rejoined from Hospital.
>
> **25th** — All available men salvaging wire ., 1 Officer and 20 O.Rs. of R.N.W.M.P. reported for D.R. duties ., 6 sent to 7th. and 9th. Bdes in the line.
>
> **26th** — Lieut F. S. McPherson MC., MM., rejoined from leave ., All communications temporily dislocated by balloon section , tearing down permanent routes., Stores at HERMAVILLE moved to present Hdqrs.
>
> **27th** — No.3 Section relieved No.2 Section in the line N of VALENCIENNES ., relief complete 1800 hours. Line run from Divn. Hdqrs. to 3rd. Cdn. M.T. Co'y. at ERRE., Line trouble normal ., Communication good.
>
> **28th** — Lieut F. S. McPherson MC., MM., left for 5th. C.D.A., Communications normal.
>
> **29th** — No.3 Section took over forward communications along the whole Divn. front , relieving No4 Section in right sector. No.4 Section relieved No.2 in reserve area., No.2 Section moved into new Hdqrs. in support area. Information received from Corps that new establishment had been sanctioned.
>
> **30th** — New airline complete from WALLERS to AHEMBURG thence to RAISMES., 10 miles D 8 Cable Salvaged.
>
> **31st** — Salvaging continued and much Signal equipment saved., Lieut L. A. C. Lee proceeded on leave.
>
> [Signed, bottom, partly cut off at the foot of the scan:] [uncertain — the signature is not legible; "Major" and part of a second line can be made out]

## Open questions / uncertain items (October 1918)
- The repeated mark in the Remarks column is read as "Kmc" [uncertain] — apparently the diarist's initials; it may instead be a flourish or a different set of initials.
- Page 4, Place column: the rotated handwritten note beside the stamp reads "Rear Hqrs." [uncertain].
- 19 October: "to be ready to move Off." — "Off." as typed (capital O); read as written.
- 20 October: "a party of linement" — "linement" as typed, apparently a typographical error for "linemen".
- 20 October: "O.O. 193 received." — "O.O." as typed; presumably Operation Order 193.
- 22 October: the words "extended" and "kept" in "Communication extended and all Bdes. kept in touch" appear overtyped in the typescript; read as written.
- 23 and 30 October: "AHEMBURG" as typed (twice) — the place name is not identified and may be a misspelling.
- 24 October: "Capt. S. B. Iler MC." — surname read as "Iler" as typed.
- 26 October: "temporily" as typed (for "temporarily"); "HERMAVILLE" as typed.
- 21 October: "Wandignies" and "VILLERS-CAMPEAU" as typed.
- The signature at the foot of page 6 is partly cut off by the scan edge and is not legible [uncertain].
- Names read as: Lieut. R. B. Gibson, M.C., M.M.; Lieut. W. M. Maxey; Lieut. F. Barber, D.C.M.; Capt. S. B. Iler, M.C.; Lieut. F. S. McPherson, M.C., M.M.; Lieut. L. A. C. Lee.
- Casualties: 1 O.R. wounded on 2 October (unnamed). Enemy bombing of Divisional Headquarters on 4 October caused no casualties ("casualties NIL") and only slight material damage. No deaths are recorded in this month.
