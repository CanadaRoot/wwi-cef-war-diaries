# War Diary — 3rd Canadian Divisional Signal Company — July 1918

- **Unit:** 3rd Canadian Divisional Signal Company, C.E.
- **Month:** July 1918 (Volume 32) — diary covers 1st to 31st July 1918
- **Source:** Library and Archives Canada, Collection Search Item ID 2004905 (RG 9 III-D-3, vol. 5005, microfilm reel T-10853, file 692; finding aid 9-52)
- **Pages:** July 1918, p. 1 – p. 12 (image files page-0216 – page-0227 of the 307-page diary)
- **July 1918 — COMPLETE** (pages 1–12): cover, contents, title page, diary text 1–31 July, no appendices. Transcribed 2026-10-26.

## Transcription conventions
Original spelling, punctuation and abbreviations kept. The diary text is typewritten throughout (Army Form C. 2118, "WAR DIARY or INTELLIGENCE SUMMARY"); the cover and title page are partly handwritten. `[uncertain]` marks words I could not read with confidence — never guessed. Struck-through words and overtyped characters are noted in square brackets rather than silently corrected. The diarist's initials "A.L." appear in the Remarks column against most entries; each diary sheet is signed at bottom right "A. Leavitt, Maj." [uncertain — see open questions]. The Place column on each sheet is given at the head of that sheet's section; it reads (rotated, top to bottom) "Rear Headquarters … Advanced …" for the first part of the month. The printed form imprint at the foot of each diary sheet reads "(A2925). Wt. W12839/M1293. 750,000. 1/17. D. D. & L., Ltd. Forms/C.2118/14." and is not repeated per page. The left margin of each sheet carries the printed note "Instructions regarding War Diaries and Intelligence Summaries are contained in F. S. Regs., Part II. and the Staff Manual respectively. Title pages will be prepared in manuscript." Abbreviations used in the diary: "C.F.A." = Canadian Field Artillery; "A.F.A." = Army Field Artillery; "C.I. Bde." = Canadian Infantry Brigade; "C.D.A." = Canadian Divisional Artillery; "O.P." = Observation Post; "O. i/c" = Officer in charge; "A. D. Signals" = Assistant Director of Signals; "G.S.O. I." = General Staff Officer, 1st grade; "C.R.E." = Commander Royal Engineers; "D.R.L.S." = Despatch Rider Letter Service; "C/W" = continuous wave (wireless); "O.R." = Other Rank; "G.H.Q." = General Headquarters. Daily "Traffic" figures are given in the diary's own abbreviated form — wireless messages, ordinary messages, registered despatches and specials — and are transcribed as written, including where the labels are dropped.

### Page 4 — 1–2 July 1918 (sheet 1)

Place column: "Rear Headqrs. PASSEUX. Advanced, BRETEUCOURT."

> **JULY 1st** — [Hour: 10 a. m.] Excellent weather; Two Signal Office shifts left LE CAUROY 6 p.m. June 30th to take over from 2nd Canadian Divisional Signal Co'y., at Passeux, VI Corps front, at 10 a. m. July 1st. Remainder of Company left LE CAUROY 7 a.m. this date by march route and Lorry. Relief complete; traffic running smoothely. Rear Divisional Headquarters at PASSEUX, advanced at BRETEUCOURT. 7th Brigade relieved the 6th Brigade of the 2nd Cdn. Div'n, 2.30 a. m. 29th Ultimo. Traffic on time and lines forward and rear reported OK. by Lieut. Auld. 8th Brigade relieved 5th Brigade of same Division night June 30th and July 1st; relief reported, "All correct". Signal office re-wired to suit instruments of incoming Brigade. 10th Artillery Brigade relieved 5th Brigade C.F.A., 3 p.m. afternoon June 30th. Lines patrolled and reported all correct. Visual and telephonic communication Brigade to Batteries. 9th Bde. C.F.A., left wagon lines BOESINGHE [uncertain] marching to MONCY-CAYEAU [uncertain], enroute to new front. It is reported only one watering place in line of march of 20 miles where animals could be watered. 6th Bde. CFA. still covering 8th Inf'y. Bde. Three brigades of Army Field Artillery in action this sector and various Heavy Artillery groups. 9th Cdn. Inf. Bde., in reserve at BRETEUCOURT. "QQ" Cable Section of Third Army Signals attached to us for construction of buried cable. Wireless communication established from Divisional Headquarters to Brigades; Power Buzzer and Amplifier sets from Brigades to Infantry Batt'ns. Also C/W sets from Artillery Headquarters to Artillery Brigades. Right Division 32nd Imperial Division; Left Division, 56th Imperial Division. Traffic for the day Wireless 13; Ordinary; Messages 1707; registered despatches 724; 4 specials.
>
> **2nd** — Officer Commanding and 2nd in Command made reconnaissances of routes and buried system of this Sector. Lt-Col. Lee Wright, A. D. Signals, VI. Corps held conference with Officers in connection with proposed new buried cable to be laid on this front by this Division. Weather very hot. Visual Established from 7th Bde. Headquarters to R. C. R.; and from there to right and left Battalion Headquarters in the line. Worked commenced buried cable from 8th Bde. Headquarters to "U" test-point (800) yards Night party of 230 men with insufficient tools dug 450 yards four feet deep in very hard chalky ground. 9th Bde. CFA., still enroute on road of march.
