# War Diary — 3rd Canadian Divisional Signal Company — March 1917

- **Unit:** 3rd Canadian Divisional Signal Company
- **Month:** March 1917 (Volume 16) — diary covers 1–31 March 1917
- **Source:** Library and Archives Canada, Collection Search Item ID 2004905 (RG 9 III-D-3, vol. 5005, microfilm reel T-10853, file 692; finding aid 9-52)
- **Pages:** March 1917, p. 1 – p. 4 (image files page-0084 – page-0087 of the diary)
- **March 1917 — COMPLETE** (pages 1–4): cover, contents, diary text 1–31 March, no appendices. Transcribed 2026-10-26.

## Transcription conventions
Original spelling, punctuation and abbreviations kept. Handwritten diary text throughout (Army Form C. 2118, "WAR DIARY or INTELLIGENCE SUMMARY"). `[uncertain]` marks words I could not read with confidence — never guessed. "do." = ditto, as written. No map coordinates are given in the diary; places are recorded as written. "Sig Office" = Signal Office; "H.Q." = Headquarters; "Divsn." = Division; "Stn." = Station; "Battr." = Battery; "Adv. H.Qrs." = Advanced Headquarters; "O.Ps" = Observation Posts. A repeated flourish, read as "do." [uncertain], appears in the Remarks column against most entries — apparently the diarist's initials or a ditto mark; it is noted here rather than repeated against every entry. The diarist's signature at the foot of each sheet reads "J. [uncertain] Capt., 3rd Can. Divisional Signal Coy" — the surname is an illegible flourish and is not guessed.

## Open questions / uncertain items (March 1917)
- 25 March: the new bury "commenced to. Divsn." — "Divsn." [uncertain]; the destination of the new bury is not clear.
- The repeated mark in the Remarks column is read as "do." [uncertain] — it may be the diarist's initials rather than a ditto.
- The diarist's signature surname is an illegible flourish [uncertain] on both sheets; the initial reads "J.".
- Names/places read as: Bruay; Villers au Bois; Gouy-Servins; Woodman Camp; Goodman Tunnel.
- No casualties are recorded in this month.
