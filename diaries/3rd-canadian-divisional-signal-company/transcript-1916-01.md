# War Diary — 3rd Canadian Divisional Signal Company — January 1916

- **Unit:** 3rd Canadian Divisional Signal Company, 3rd Canadian Division
- **Month:** January 1916 (Volume 2) — this volume covers 1 to 31 January 1916
- **Source:** Library and Archives Canada, Collection Search Item ID 2004905 (RG 9 III-D-3, vol. 5005, microfilm reel T-10853, file 692; finding aid 9-52)
- **Pages:** January 1916, p. 1 – p. 5 (image files page-0007 – page-0011 of the 307-page diary)
- **January 1916 — COMPLETE** (pages 7–11 of 307): cover, contents, title page, diary text 1–31 January, no appendices. Transcribed 2026-10-26.

## Transcription conventions
Original spelling, punctuation and abbreviations kept. Handwritten diary text throughout (Army Form C. 2118, "WAR DIARY or INTELLIGENCE SUMMARY" — "INTELLIGENCE SUMMARY" struck through). `[uncertain]` marks words I could not read with confidence — never guessed. Map references are given as written (Sheet 27 N.E.); no latitude/longitude coordinates are given. "N.C.O's" = Non-Commissioned Officers; "G.S. wagons" = General Service wagons; "O.C." = Officer Commanding; "R.E." = Royal Engineers. A repeated initialled mark appears in the Remarks column against most entries; it is noted in the open questions rather than repeated against every entry. The diary's own heading on the diary sheets is "3rd Canadian Division Signal Coy.", written in by hand at the top right. The "2" written beneath "Volume 2" on the cover is a microfilm/frame mark, not part of the volume number.

## Pages 4–5 — Diary text (1–31 January 1916)

Army Form C. 2118, "WAR DIARY or INTELLIGENCE SUMMARY". "Original" is written by hand at the top left of each sheet. The printed footer reads "T.2134. Wt. W708—776. 500000. 4/15. Sir J. C. & S."

### Page 4 — 1–24 January 1916 (Sheet No. 4)

> **MONTH OF JANUARY 1916.**
>
> **FLETRE, Jan. 1st to Jan. 4th.** — Organization, training and equipping of Company proceeded with. During this period one officer and fortytwo N.C.O's and men were attached to the Company.
>
> **FLETRE, Jan. 5th.** — No. 2 Section detached from Company and moved off at one thirty pm to join 7th Canadian Infantry Brigade; to be attached to that brigade to take care of its signal communications from this date. Headquarters of 7th Brigade being at Mont-des-Cats.
>
> **FLETRE, Jan. 5th to Jan. 23rd.** — Organization, training and equipping of the company proceeded with. During this period four officers, ninety-nine N.C.O's. and men were attached to the Company, also sixty horses, four cable wagons, eleven G.S. wagons, one water cart, twentyone motorcycles and nine bicycles were attached.
>
> **FLETRE, Jan. 24th.** — No. 3 Section, comprised of; one officer, twentysix N.C.O's and men, [entry continues on Sheet No. 5 — see Page 5].

### Page 5 — 24–31 January 1916 (Sheet No. 5)

> **MONTH OF JANUARY 1916.—contd. #2.**
>
> **FLETRE, Jan. 24th continued.** — one RE limber wagon and eight horses, posted to 8th Canadian Infantry Brigade, to take care of signal communications of that brigade, moved off to join 8th Brigade at METEREN, (Map location X17 C 3.4 sheet 27 NE) at eleven AM today.
>
> **FLETRE, Jan. 25th to Jan. 31st.** — Organization and training of Company proceeded with. During the period one officer and fortyfive N.C.O's. and men were attached to the Company pending transfer.
>
> [Signed, bottom right:] *S. E. Powers Major / O.C. 3rd Canadian Division Signal[s]* — the signature line is cut off at the right edge of the scan, so the final word is not fully visible.
