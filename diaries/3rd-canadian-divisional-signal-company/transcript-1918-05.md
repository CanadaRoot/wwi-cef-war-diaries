# War Diary — 3rd Canadian Divisional Signal Company, C.E. — May 1918

- **Unit:** 3rd Canadian Divisional Signal Company, C.E. (Canadian Engineers)
- **Month:** May 1918 (Volume 30)
- **Source:** Library and Archives Canada, Collection Search Item ID 2004905 (RG 9 III-D-3, vol. 5005, microfilm reel T-10853, file 692; finding aid 9-52)
- **Pages:** May 1918, p. 1 – p. 5 (image files page-0203 – page-0207 of the diary)
- **May 1918 — COMPLETE** (pages 1–5): cover, contents, title page, diary text 1–31 May, no appendices. Transcribed 2026-10-26.
- **Diary covers 5 December 1915 – 17 March 1919.**

## Transcription conventions
Original spelling, punctuation and abbreviations kept. The May diary is typewritten (Army Form C. 2118, "WAR DIARY or INTELLIGENCE SUMMARY"), like March and April. `[uncertain]` marks words I could not read with confidence — never guessed. No map coordinates are given in the diary; places are recorded as written. The diarist's initials "A.L." appear in the Remarks column against every entry and are not repeated against each entry below; the sheets are signed "A. Leavitt Maj" (O.C.). Typewriter conventions: the typewriter has no "1" key, so "1" is typed as "I" (e.g. "IIth" = 11th, "I9" = 19, "34I6" = 3416). Typos in the original ("Inspection s", "crrying", "whilelocated") are kept as typed. "H.Q." = Headquarters; "Hqrs" = Headquarters; "Bde" = Brigade; "Div" = Division; "Cdn" = Canadian; "C.I.B." = Canadian Infantry Brigade; "C.F.A." = Canadian Field Artillery; "Sigs" = Signals; "Oi/c" = Officer in charge; "C.W." = Continuous Wave (wireless); "M.M." = Military Medal; "Bde Pools" = brigade pools of trained signallers. "Rear HQ's Chateau dela Haie / Adv HQ's Sains-en-Gohelle" is the place written in the Place column for 1–6 May; "Norrent-Fontes" for 7–31 May.

## Pages 4–5 — Diary text (1–31 May 1918)

Army Form C. 2118, "WAR DIARY or INTELLIGENCE SUMMARY". Heading on each sheet: "3rd Canadian Divisional Signal Coy May 1918".

### Page 4 — 1–13 May 1918

> **1st** — Arrangements completed for relief by incoming Divns. Adv party of 18th Corps arrived at Chateau de la Haie. Orders issued re move to new area. Relief of 8th Bde Section to take place during night of 1st/2nd. Rainy weather. Slight enemy shelling, no damage to lines and communication maintained without interruption. Business Messages & Despatches 3920.
>
> **2nd** — Relief of 8th Bde Section effected without hitch. Rear Office handed over to 18th Corps and move to new area (Bryas) completed. Office opened up in Chateau at Bryas & lines obtained to Bdes. Weather fine. Business Messages & Despatches 2076
>
> **3rd** — Relief of 7th & 9th Bdes during course of night effected. Adv Office at Sains-en-Gohelle closed at 10. am and move made to Bryas. Weather fine. The move to new area made under good conditions. Business Messages & Despatches 855.
>
> **4th** — Inspections carried out of equipment and men's kit. Wireless Section increased by arrival of Artillery personnel manning C.W. Sets. These men will be trained in communication between Arty HQ's & Group HQ's. Weather remains fine. Business Messages & Despatches 983
>
> **5th** — Relief of Artillery in line effected and move made to new area (Fontain-les-Hermin.) All units of 3rd Cdn Divn now out of line. Usual routine carried on. Business Msges & Desp 799.
>
> **6th** — Inspection s of equipment etc carried out, exercising of horses. Usual routine maintained. Weather unsettled. Business 694. Orders received that Divn move to Norrent-Fontes. Bdes will be located in surrounding villages.
>
> **7th** — Move to Norrent-Fontes made without difficulty. Office opened in The Mairie Bldg and steps taken to establish communication with Bdes. Warning received that Boche may attack on this Front. Divn held as Army reserve. Arrangements made to carry out extensive training while located in this area. Business 850.
>
> **8th** — Training commenced. Sections formed under the following headings. Visual, Wireless and Power Buzzer, and Cable Section. Order from Divn that a daily report of training carried out by this unit to be forwarded to "G" Bch. 8th Bde warned to move forward on 8th inst and held as Corps reserve. Bde HQ's to be located at Ham-en-Artois. Divn held to move at 4 hours notice. Business Msgs & Despatches 1263.
>
> **9th to 11th** — Training carried on without interruption. Excellent progress made particularly with Cables Wagons. All day schemes in cable drill executed. Training of Signal Sections with Bdes carried out under supervision of Bde Signalling Officers.
>
> **12th** — Sunday. No training carried out. Inspections made of equipment etc. Morning spent in overhauling wagons etc.
>
> **13th** — Training continued. Definite schemes detailed for Wireless and Power Buzzer personnel. Communication established with Arty HQ's by means of Helio and Lucas lamp. Continued good weather greatly facilitating the crrying out of training.
>
> [Signed, bottom right:] *A. Leavitt Maj*

### Page 5 — 14–31 May 1918

> **14th** — Training continued by all Sections. The continued good weather proving a great help. As a result of continuous training men are showing splendid efficiency.
>
> **15th to 18th** — Sapper Watson, No 3 Section awarded the M.M. for bravery in the field. Training continued without cessation. All day schemes carried by Visual, Wireless and Cable Sections Attention given to sports and a number of games played with surrounding units.
>
> **19th** — Sunday. Voluntary church parade. No training carried out. Day given to overhauling equipment, inspections etc.
>
> **20th to 25th** — Training maintained during week. All sections show splendid progress and efficiency. Scheme embodying communication within the Divn and to flanking Divns carried out by Wireless & Power Buzzer Section. This section showing marked improvement in efficiency.
>
> **26th** — Sunday. Voluntary church parade. No training carried out. Morning given to inspection of equipment, overhauling cable wagons etc.
>
> **27th** — Manoeuvres carried out by 7th Bde and 9th Bde C.F.A. Detachment of Visual and Wireless Sections detailed to each Bde. Good results obtained by these methods of communication especially by the personnel manning C.W. sets with Artillery. Training continued by remainder of Sections.
>
> **28th** — Manoeuvres carried out by 9th Bde and 10th Bde C.F.A. Same tactical scheme as that carried out by 7th Bde. Sections detailed from Div to Bdes did good work tho' better results would have been obtained if more use had been made of these methods of communication. Fine weather continues and continuous training has been maintained. Great aerial activity maintained by both sides during nights.
>
> **29th** — Remainder of personnel from Bdes to complete establishment of Bde Pools for Power Buzzer-Amplifier reported to Div HQ's. Personnel who have received sufficient training in this method attached to the Visual Section for training. Competition arranged between Cdn Corps HQ's Cable Section and No 1 Section of this Coy, to take place on June 3rd. Training continued by all Sections.
>
> **30th** — Orders received that 8th Bde will be relieved by 9th Bde. 8th Bde to occupy 9th Bde HQ's at Bomy in manoeuvre area. Use was made of cable wagons during recent moves and a number of lines laid to units located in the Bomy area. Training maintained by all Sections. New personnel for Power Buzzer Section instructed in the principles of communication etc. Fine weather contd.
>
> **31st** — 9th Bde moved to Ham-en Artois relieving 8th Bde, who moved to Bomy area. Training carried out without cessation. The training carried out by this Company since arrival in this area has proved the most successful and satisfactory period from the formation of Company. All sections have shown interest in their work and splendid results have been obtained. Reports from the Sections attached to Bdes are also very satisfactory.
>
> [Signed, bottom right:] *A. Leavitt Maj*

## Open questions / uncertain items (May 1918)
- 5th May: "move made to new area (Fontain-les-Hermin.)" — read as typed; presumably Fontaine-lès-Hermans.
- 8th May: "to be forwarded to "G" Bch." — "Bch" read as typed (Branch).
- 9th–11th and 15th–18th and 20th–25th May are combined entries covering several days each; no separate business figures are given for those days, and none is given for 12th, 13th, 14th, 19th or 26th May.
- "Sapper Watson, No 3 Section awarded the M.M. for bravery in the field" (15th–18th May) — no first name or number given.
- The May diary text is only 2 pages for the whole month (the contents page states "Diary Text 2 Pages"), because the division was out of the line from 5 May and the month is a training record at Norrent-Fontes rather than a daily front-line log.
- Names read as: Sapper Watson (No 3 Section, M.M.).
- No casualties are recorded in May 1918. The division was relieved by XVIII Corps (20th & 24th Divisions) on 1–5 May, moved to Bryas and then to Norrent-Fontes, and spent the rest of the month training as Army reserve — the diary calls it "the most successful and satisfactory period from the formation of Company".
