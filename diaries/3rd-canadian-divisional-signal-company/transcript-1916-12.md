# War Diary — 3rd Canadian Divisional Signal Company — December 1916

- **Unit:** 3rd Canadian Divisional Signal Company
- **Month:** December 1916 (Volume 13) — this volume covers 1 to 31 December 1916
- **Source:** Library and Archives Canada, Collection Search Item ID 2004905 (RG 9 III-D-3, vol. 5005, microfilm reel T-10853, file 692; finding aid 9-52)
- **Pages:** December 1916, p. 1 – p. 4 (image files page-0071 – page-0074 of the 307-page diary)
- **December 1916 — COMPLETE** (pages 71–74 of 307): cover, contents, diary text 1–31 December, no appendices. Transcribed 2026-10-26.

## Transcription conventions
Original spelling, punctuation and abbreviations kept. Handwritten diary text throughout (Army Form C. 2118, "WAR DIARY or INTELLIGENCE SUMMARY"). `[uncertain]` marks words I could not read with confidence — never guessed. No map coordinates are given in the diary; places are recorded as written. Ditto marks ("do" / „) are not repeated verbatim; the entry they repeat is named. "Battns" = Battalions; "C.M.R" = Canadian Mounted Rifles; "C. I Bgde" / "Can I. Bde" = Canadian Infantry Brigade; "H Q" = Headquarters. A repeated mark in the Remarks column, read as "J.A." [uncertain] — apparently the diarist's initials — appears against most entries; it is noted here rather than repeated against every entry.

## Pages 3–4 — Diary text (1–31 December 1916)

Army Form C. 2118, "WAR DIARY or INTELLIGENCE SUMMARY"; "INTELLIGENCE SUMMARY" is struck through on both sheets. Each sheet carries the oval unit stamp "3rd CANADIAN DIVISION / SIGNAL COMPANY" — p. 3: No. 21, Date 12/16; p. 4: No. 31, Date 12/16. The sheets are headed "Original".

## Open questions / uncertain items (December 1916)
- The repeated mark in the Remarks column is read as "J.A." [uncertain] — apparently the diarist's initials.
- 18 December: "8th C. I Bgde" is written as shown; 20 December writes the same formation as "8th Can I. Bde".
- 22 December: the entry reads "Usual Office Routine and Patrol of lines" and is dittoed through 31 December.
- No casualties are recorded in December 1916.
