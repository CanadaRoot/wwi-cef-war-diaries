# War Diary — 3rd Canadian Divisional Signal Company — September 1917

- **Unit:** 3rd Canadian Divisional Signal Company
- **Month:** September 1917 (Volume 22) — 1 to 30 September 1917
- **Source:** Library and Archives Canada, Collection Search Item ID 2004905 (RG 9 III-D-3, vol. 5005, microfilm reel T-10853, file 692; finding aid 9-52)
- **Pages:** September 1917, p. 1 – p. 11 (image files page-0128 – page-0138 of the diary)
- **September 1917 — COMPLETE** (pages 128–138): cover, contents, title page, diary text 1–30 September, no appendices. Transcribed 2026-10-26.

## Transcription conventions
Original spelling, punctuation and abbreviations kept. Handwritten diary text throughout (Army Form C. 2118, "WAR DIARY or INTELLIGENCE SUMMARY"). `[uncertain]` marks words I could not read with confidence — never guessed. No map coordinates are given in the diary; places are recorded as written, including the map reference the diarist gives for Divisional Headquarters from 6 September ("Corps Line F.12.A.7.4, Sheet 51 N.E."). "A.L." in the Remarks column is the initials of the officer commanding, Major A. Leavitt (signature "A. Leavitt Maj" at the foot of each sheet); it is noted here rather than repeated against every entry. "H.Q." = Headquarters; "Bde." = Brigade; "Cdn" = Canadian; "Div" = Division; "Sig Coy" = Signal Company; "Imp" = Imperial; "Inf Bde" = Infantry Brigade; "C.R.E." = Commander Royal Engineers; "P.P.C.L.I." = Princess Patricia's Canadian Light Infantry; "D.S." = the cable type used for buried lines; "D II" and "D III" are cable types. The diary's own heading on the diary sheets reads "3rd CANADIAN Divisional Signal Coy".

## Pages 4–11 — Diary text (1–30 September 1917)

Army Form C. 2118, "WAR DIARY or INTELLIGENCE SUMMARY". Heading on each sheet: "3rd CANADIAN Divisional Signal Coy".

### Page 4 — 1–3 September 1917

> **Barlin, Sept 1st** — Work on cable burying with party from 9th Cdn Inf Bde. The Bde with so much other work on hand can only supply very small working parties for cable burying so progress is naturally slow. Party of 10 men from company salvaging cable. Over twenty five miles of wire picked up to day. Patrol of lines & usual routine
>
> **2nd** — Work continued on bury. Considerable difficulty experienced as we are obliged to dig through the CITE ST PIERRE which is constantly shelled. No casualties with working party. Party however obliged to quit work several times on account of shelling. Twenty miles of wire secured by salvaging party. Usual routine & patrol of lines
>
> **3rd** — Work on cable burying continued. 4450 yards completed laid, filled in and tested All lines tested out O.K. Enemy shelling fairly heavy. Orders received that 1st Canadian Division will relieve 3rd Cdn Div, the 3rd Cdn Div to relieve the 31st Imp Div. The Visited 31st Imp Div to look over lines and communications. Party of six linemen sent to new area to take over lines. 3rd Cdn Div Hqrs to be established at Chateau D'Acy and party of 4 men forwarded to get office in readiness

### Page 5 — 4–5 September 1917

> **Barlin, Sept 4** — Owing to the contemplated move of the Division obliged to discontinue work of burying cable. 1st Canadian Div made acquainted with work completed and proposed scheme. Major Blair of the 1st Sigs together with his lines officer shown over lines in our present area. Work on new Sig office at Chateau D'Acy continued also party of linemen putting lines through from Chateau D'Acy forward. No 3 Section 3rd Sigs take over from the Imperials in the left sector of our new area.
>
> **5th** — 3rd Cdn Div Arty move from BRACQUEMONT to new area coming temporarily under orders of the 31st Div. Work of establishing new office at Chateau D'Acy completed All lines tested and everything ready for move which takes place tomorrow. Owing to the number of offices and sub exchanges to be manned & the amount of work to be done in order to make the move a success the company taxed to the limit. All office reliefs working twelve hour shifts. Office relief for forward report centre in new area & office staff for new Hqrs sent forward by motor lorry at 11.30 P.M. Over 1700 messages & despatches handled to day

### Page 6 — 6–9 September 1917

> **Corps Line F12.A.7.4 Sheet 51 N.E., Sept 6th** — Forward office at SAINS EN GOHELLE relieved by 1st Cdn Div Sigs at 8 A.M. New Report Centre at Corps Line taken over from 31st Imp Div by 3rd Cdn Div Sigs at 8 A.M. Company with the exception of office staff still manning Office at Barlin move off at 9 A.M. Signal Office at Barlin relieved by 1st Cdn Div at 10 A.M. New Signal Office opened at Chateau D'Acy at 10 A.M. No 4 Section relieve Section of the Imp Bde in the right sector of new area. Communication never interrupted. All lines working well. Business above normal 1763 messages & despatches handled
>
> **7th** — Commenced work on new horse standings. Opened small Sub Exchange at AUX RIETZ. Ten line cordless installed and eight subscribers put on. Work of patrolling all lines in new area. Business light only 1521 messages & despatches handled. Enemy very quiet
>
> **8th** — Looking over ground to select route for cable which we propose to bury. Usual other work continued. Enemy quiet. Office business normal
>
> **9th** — Party of 15 men under Lt McPherson rebuilding forward lines. Locating fault in buried cable. Usual routine. Weather fine Enemy quiet

### Page 7 — 10–12 September 1917

> **Div Hqrs Corps Line F.12.A.7.4 Sheet 51. N.E., Sept 10** — Div Hqrs move from Chateau D'Acy to present Hqrs Corps Line. Working party secured from 7th Cdn Inf Bde for cable burying & work commenced under the supervision of Lt Smith. The P.P.C.L.I. Hqrs at Mt St Eloy furnished first party of 300. Party taken forward on Light Railway. Good progress made with first night's work Enemy quiet
>
> **11th** — Cable burying continued with party of 300 from 42nd Cdn Inf Batt. Good progress made. New lines of D S cable laid to 9th Cdn Inf Bde Hqrs at THELUS CAVE so old buried cable is badly grounded. Moved large Electric Lighting plant, property of the C.R.E 3rd Division from GOUY SERVINS to present Hqrs for the purpose of providing more light. Work on horse standings progressing satisfactorily. Weather fine and enemy quiet
>
> **12th** — Cable burying contd with party from the 49th Batt Lt McGill of No 3 Section granted 10 days leave of absence Installing new Electric Lighting plant. Sgt Shannon and 5 other men report from Wireless course Enemy quiet. Business normal.

### Page 8 — 13–18 September 1917

> **Div Hqrs Corps Line F.12.A.7.4 Sheet 51. N.E., Sept 13th** — Work on bury contd. Owing to the amount of work to be carried out & the limited length of time in which to get work completed and also owing to the shortage of officers with this Coy we are obliged to ask for an Officer from the Infantry to assist in connection with the buried cable work Lt Fletcher of the 42nd Cdn Inf Batt reports for duty to assist with the work. Work of jointing new cable, salvaging old wire, installing new electric lighting plant & horse standings carried on.
>
> **14** — All work in hand continued. Enemy quiet. Weather fine
>
> **15** — All work continued and good progress made. Orders having been received that Div will occupy THELUS CAVE as Adv Hqrs during forthcoming operations work commenced in fitting up Signal Office at this place. Weather fine. Enemy quiet
>
> **16** — All work continued. Buried cable reached the proposed new right Bde Hqrs. 1320 of 25 pair having been laid. Lines tested through & all O.K.
>
> **17** — All work contd & good progress made. Business normal. Weather fine Enemy quiet.
>
> **18** — Work continued. No unusual incidents. Enemy quiet Usual Routine

### Page 9 — 19–22 September 1917

> **Div Hqrs Corps Line F.12.A.7.4 Sheet 51. N.E., Sept 19** — Owing to Bde relief no working party available for buried cable work. 7th Bde take over from 8th and 9th Brigades. No 2 Section relieving both No 3 and 4 Sections
>
> **20th** — Working party of 200 secured & buried cable again gone on with. New Electric plant installed & running. Lt Beckett reports having completed course in Wireless at S. N. S. Enemy more active. Weather fine
>
> **21st** — A party of 60 men obtained from 3rd Cdn Entrenching Batt partly to work in daylight & in shifts of 30. Party to try to complete a portion of a lateral bury in front of VIMY STATION which the 31st Imp Division had failed to complete Lt Beckett supervising the work. Work completed at 4.30 P.M. Enemy active & party obliged to discontinue work several times on account of shelling. No casualties. All other work continued Weather fine
>
> **22** — Bury contd. Lt Beckett with party of linemen testing out lines in the lateral at VIMY STATION. Working party from company also sent out to fill in a number of shell holes & places on the lateral bury which required attention. Other work as usual. Enemy fairly active. Weather fine.

### Page 10 — 23–26 September 1917

> **Div. Hqrs Corps Line F.12.A.7.4 Sheet 51 N.E., Sept 23** — Work on buried cable continued. Working parties small as the one Bde at present in line owing to amount of work on hand are unable to supply more than 100 or 200 nightly. Party at work twisting cable both D II and D III. This for the use of Bdes & Batts during operations. Horse standings completed and horses moved in. Weather fine
>
> **24** — Looking over ground with a view to selecting site at which to establish a Div Visual Station for active operations. School of instruction under Lt Beckett opened. Power buzzer and Amplifier work being taught to the Bdes taking part in the attack. Buried, twisting & salvaging cable work carried on
>
> **25th** — New lines laid by Artillery detachments preparatory to the moving in of another Artillery group. School of instruction & all work in hand carried on with. Weather fine. Enemy quiet
>
> **26th** — All work continued. Classes also organized for brush up in Visual work Conference at 8th Bde Hqrs of Bde Sig Officers & Bde Intelligence & Scouts Officers & subject of communications during forthcoming engagements fully gone into. Weather fine. Enemy quiet

### Page 11 — 27–30 September 1917

> **Div Hqrs Corps Line F.12.A.7.4 Sheet 51 N.E., Sept 27** — All work continued. Good progress made with buried cable work. Cable has now reached proposed Left Bde Hqrs at Mont [uncertain — reads "Fres..."?] Quarries has been tested through and all 25 pairs O.K. Classes in visual & power buzzer work. Conference of Batt Sig Officers of the 8th Bde to discuss the subject of communication during the coming attack Enemy quiet. Weather fine
>
> **28** — Work in hand & classes of instruction carried on. Information obtained from aeroplane photo lead us to believe that some enemy buried cable exists in our area. As same would be of great assistance party under Capt Rees detailed to endeavour to trace same. No 117025 Sgt N. B. McCausland awarded the Military Medal for bravery & particularly good work in laying and maintaining communications in CITE ST. PIERRE area. Enemy quiet
>
> **29** — Work & instructional classes continued. Party under Capt Rees tracing German cable. Conference of Batt Signals Officers at 9th Bde to discuss Subject of best method of communication for coming attack Enemy quiet
>
> **30** — Work & classes continued. Small parties & extremely hard soil make progress with buried cable very slow. Weather fine. Enemy more active
>
> [Signed, bottom right:] *A. Leavitt Maj*

## Open questions / uncertain items (September 1917)
- 3 September: "The Visited 31st Imp Div to look over lines and communications" — read as written; the sense suggests "I visited" or "[someone] visited" the 31st Imperial Division.
- 27 September: the place after "Mont" is unreadable [uncertain] — it reads "Fres..." followed by "Quarries". Cf. 3 October, "a lateral bury also started from Quarries to connect with the Bde on our left."
- 28 September: Sgt McCausland's number is read as 117025 here, but the same award is recorded again on 3 October with the number 117095 — one of the two readings must be wrong; both are given as written.
- Casualties in September: none — the diary records "No casualties" on 2 and 21 September. One man was awarded the Military Medal: Sgt N. B. McCausland (28 September).
- Names read as: Major Blair (1st Sigs); Lt Smith; Lt McPherson; Lt Beckett; Lt McGill; Lt Fletcher (42nd Battalion); Sgt Shannon; Capt Rees; Sgt N. B. McCausland.
- The Divisional Headquarters map reference from 6 September is "Corps Line F.12.A.7.4, Sheet 51 N.E."; no other map coordinates are given.
