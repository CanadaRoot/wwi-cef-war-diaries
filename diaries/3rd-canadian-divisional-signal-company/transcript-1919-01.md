# War Diary — 3rd Canadian Divisional Signal Company — January 1919

- **Unit:** 3rd Canadian Divisional Signal Company
- **Month:** January 1919 (Volume 38) — this volume covers 1 to 31 January 1919
- **Source:** Library and Archives Canada, Collection Search Item ID 2004905 (RG 9 III-D-3, vol. 5005, microfilm reel T-10853, file 692; finding aid 9-52)
- **Pages:** January 1919, p. 1 – p. 9 (image files page-0284 – page-0292 of the 307-page diary)
- **January 1919 — COMPLETE** (pages 284–292 of 307): cover, contents, diary text 1–31 January, no appendices. Transcribed 2026-10-26.

## Transcription conventions
Original spelling, punctuation and abbreviations kept. Diary text is typewritten throughout (Army Form C. 2118, "WAR DIARY or INTELLIGENCE SUMMARY"). `[uncertain]` marks words I could not read with confidence — never guessed. No map coordinates are given in the diary; places are recorded as written. "O.C." = Officer Commanding; "O.Rs." = Other Ranks; "C.F.A." = Canadian Field Artillery; "C.I.B." = Canadian Infantry Brigade; "D.R.L.S." / "DRLS" = Despatch Rider Letter Service; "D.R." = Despatch Rider; "C.M.R." = Canadian Mounted Rifles; "C.D.A." = Canadian Divisional Artillery; "G.O.C." = General Officer Commanding; "C.O." = Commanding Officer. A handwritten initialled mark, read as "A.L." [uncertain], appears in the Remarks column against most entries; it is noted here rather than repeated against every entry. Signatures read as "A. Leavitt Maj." (Major A. Leavitt, who returned to the Company on 25 December 1918 and took command while Major K. M. Campbell was detached to Canadian Section G.H.Q., 1st Echelon, on 3 January 1919).
