# War Diary — 3rd Canadian Divisional Signal Company — August 1917

- **Unit:** 3rd Canadian Divisional Signal Company
- **Month:** August 1917 (Volume 21) — 1 to 31 August 1917
- **Source:** Library and Archives Canada, Collection Search Item ID 2004905 (RG 9 III-D-3, vol. 5005, microfilm reel T-10853, file 692; finding aid 9-52)
- **Pages:** August 1917, p. 1 – p. 9 (image files page-0119 – page-0127 of the diary)
- **August 1917 — COMPLETE** (pages 119–127): cover, contents, title page, diary text 1–31 August, no appendices. Transcribed 2026-10-26.

## Transcription conventions
Original spelling, punctuation and abbreviations kept. Handwritten diary text throughout (Army Form C. 2118, "WAR DIARY or INTELLIGENCE SUMMARY"). `[uncertain]` marks words I could not read with confidence — never guessed. No map coordinates are given in the diary; places are recorded as written. "A.L." in the Remarks column is the initials of the officer commanding, Major A. Leavitt (signature "A. Leavitt Maj" at the foot of each sheet); it is noted here rather than repeated against every entry. "H.Q." = Headquarters; "Bde." = Brigade; "Cdn" = Canadian; "Div" = Division; "Sig Coy" = Signal Company; "R.F.C." = Royal Flying Corps; "C.E.T.D." = Canadian Engineers Training Depot; "A.D.V.S." = Assistant Director of Veterinary Services; "A.D. Sigs" = Assistant Director of Signals; "S.N.S." = the wireless school (see 20 September); "D.R." = Despatch Rider; "N.C.O." = Non-Commissioned Officer; "P.P.C.L.I." = Princess Patricia's Canadian Light Infantry; "D.S." = the cable type used for buried lines. The diary's own heading on the diary sheets reads "3rd CANADIAN Div Sig Coy".

## Pages 4–9 — Diary text (1–31 August 1917)

Army Form C. 2118, "WAR DIARY or INTELLIGENCE SUMMARY". Heading on each sheet: "3rd CANADIAN Div Sig Coy". Place throughout: Barlin.

### Page 4 — 1–9 August 1917

> **Barlin, Aug 1** — Training continued. Weather conditions unfavourable for carrying on with much out door training so work confined to buzzer & sounder classes and lectures. Inoculation of company at 2. P.M.
>
> **2** — Training continued Weather again unfavourable
>
> **3** — " " "
>
> **4** — Training continued. Cpls GEE. D and NOBLE. E. S. leave for England to accept commissions in R.F.C.
>
> **5** — Church Parade 9 A.M. Company sports afternoon. 109146 2/Lt KEE. H awarded bar to Military Medal. No 110186 Sgt GIBSON R.B. awarded MILITARY MEDAL

>
> **8** — " " " "
>
> **9** — Inspection by A.D.V.S of company horses. All training carried on including Gas Helmet drill. Usual routine

### Page 5 — 10–16 August 1917

> **Barlin, Aug 10** — Training and all other work in hand carried on with. Conference with Bde Sig Officer 9th Bde and Battalion Sig officers in connection with manoeuvres to be carried out while Bde is in rest & training.
>
> **11** — Usual routine
>
> **12** — Usual routine No 108374 2/Cpl. MATHEW [uncertain — a "Y" is inserted above, possibly "MAYHEW"] H.B. for England for commission in R.F.C. LT BECKETT from C.E.T.D. Eng reports for duty

>
> **16** — Lt McPherson, while reconnoitring the ground in the manoeuvre area, slightly wounded by shell fire. Inspection of Hqrs & detachments with the 3rd Cdn Div Arty. Training & other work forwarded [uncertain] with

### Page 6 — 17–21 August 1917

> **Barlin, Aug 17** — Training and usual routine Detachments at work under Lt Beckett Lt Parker transferred to 1st Sig Coy. Lt Frampton of 1st Sigs transferred to this company and posted to No 2 Section. Brigade manoeuvres cancelled and Division to hold itself in readiness to move at short notice
>
> **18** — Training & usual routine. Lt Beckett proceeds to S. N. S. Wireless school for course of instruction. Conference of O.C.'s of all Signal Coys called by A. D. Sigs Cdn Corps. Looking over communications of 1st Div preparatory to relief of that Division.
>
> **19** — Church parade 9.30 A M. Warning order received to be ready at a moment's notice to take over from the 2nd Cdn Div. Looking over lines in 1st & 2nd Cdn Div areas
>
> **20** — One N.C.O reports to Corp. Gas School for course of instruction. Linemen sent forward to relieve men of the 1st Sig Coy. Work on horse standings continued
>
> **21** — Orders received that Div Hqrs will not move from Barlin to take over from 1st Div at Braquemont. Arranging for lines from Braquemont to Barlin Laid three pairs from LES BREBIES to BULLY GRENAY.

### Page 7 — 22–24 August 1917

> **Barlin, Aug 22** — Opened new office at LES BREBIES. Wireless stations installed at 7th and 8th Advanced Bde Hqrs. Amplifier installed at 7th Bde Advanced & power Buzzer installed at advanced Battalion Hqrs. All stations working well Relief of 1st Cdn Div completed at 10 A M. Opened new office at Bully Grenay. Owing to the large area taken over obliged to obtain six telegraph operators from Corps Signals and two telephone switchboard operators from 1st Cdn Sig Coy in order to carry on. Installed new switchboard at Barlin office
>
> **23** — Report Centre opened at SAINS-EN-GOHELLE. Relief of 2nd Cdn Div completed at 10 A.M. Patrolling and repairing lines. Laying new lines and making changes necessary to accomodate units in new area taken over. Enemy shelling fairly active
>
> **24** — Patrol & maintenance of lines. Arrangements made for [crossed-out word] extending buried cable. Brigades experiencing considerable trouble in maintaining forward communications as enemy shelling is heavy

### Page 8 — 25–28 August 1917

> **Barlin, Aug 25** — Power Buzzer installed at forward Batt Hqrs in 9th Bde area This station to work in connection with Amplifier station at 7th Bde Adv Hqrs. Station tested out and working well over a distance of 2000 yds. Patrol & maintenance of lines and usual routine. Enemy quiet
>
> **26** — Working party obtained & work commenced on buried cable Lt McKenzie of the 123rd Pioneer Batt attached to this company as Bde Sig Officer being in charge of the work Lt S. M. Smith from C.E.T.D reported for duty. Church parade at 9.30 a.m. Patrol of lines & usual routine
>
> **27** — 6th Imperial Div take over the portion of line as taken over by 3rd Cdn Div from 1st Cdn Division. No 4 Section of the 6th Sig Coy (Imp) relieves No 3rd Section of 3rd Div Sigs (Cdn) Relief completed at 6 A M. Signal Office closed at LES BREBIES at 2. P.M. Wireless stations in forward area relieved by wireless of the 6th Div Sigs (Imp). Buried cable continued Patrol of lines. Working party salvaging wire Usual routine
>
> **28** — Usual routine patrol maintenance & salvaging of wire Buried cable continued. Enemy quiet

### Page 9 — 29–31 August 1917

> **Barlin, Aug 29** — Usual routine. Lt Smith reports to 9th Bde to assist in buried cable work

> **30** — Burying cable, Patrol of lines, Salvaging wire & usual routine

## Open questions / uncertain items (August 1917)
- 12 August: "MATHEW" is written with a small "Y" inserted above — the name may be "MAYHEW". The regimental number is read as 108374.
- 16 August: "Training & other work forwarded [uncertain] with" — the word before "with" reads "forwarded" but the sense is unclear; it may be "proceeded".
- 18 August: the wireless school is read as "S. N. S." (cf. 20 September, "course in Wireless at S. N. S."); the August entry could read "S. N. &".
- 21 August: the place is written "Braquemont" here; in September and December the diarist writes the same place "BRACQUEMONT".
- 24 August: one word is crossed out before "extending buried cable"; the deleted word is illegible.
- Casualties in August: one wounded — Lt McPherson, slightly wounded by shell fire on 16 August. No men killed.
- Names read as: Cpl Gee D; Cpl Noble E. S.; 2/Lt Kee H (bar to Military Medal); Sgt Gibson R. B. (Military Medal); 2/Cpl Mathew [uncertain] H. B.; Lt Beckett; Lt Parker; Lt Frampton; Lt McPherson; Lt Maitland; Lt S. M. Smith; Lt McKenzie (123rd Pioneer Battalion).
- No map coordinates are given in the diary.
