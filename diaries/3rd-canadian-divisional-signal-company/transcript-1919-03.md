# War Diary — 3rd Canadian Divisional Signal Company — March 1919

- **Unit:** 3rd Canadian Divisional Signal Company
- **Month:** March 1919 (Volume 40) — this volume covers 1 to 17 March 1919; it is the final volume of the diary
- **Source:** Library and Archives Canada, Collection Search Item ID 2004905 (RG 9 III-D-3, vol. 5005, microfilm reel T-10853, file 692; finding aid 9-52)
- **Pages:** March 1919, p. 1 – p. 8 (image files page-0300 – page-0307 of the 307-page diary)
- **March 1919 — COMPLETE** (pages 300–307 of 307): cover, contents, diary text 1–17 March, no appendices. Transcribed 2026-10-26. The diary ends with the disbandment of the Company on 17 March 1919.

## Transcription conventions
Original spelling, punctuation and abbreviations kept. Diary text is typewritten throughout (Army Form C. 2118, "WAR DIARY or INTELLIGENCE SUMMARY"), except the cover (p. 1) and title page (p. 3), which are handwritten. `[uncertain]` marks words I could not read with confidence — never guessed. No map coordinates are given in the diary; places are recorded as written. "O.C." = Officer Commanding; "O.Rs." = Other Ranks; "O.M.F. of C." = Overseas Military Forces of Canada; "C.E.F." = Canadian Expeditionary Force; "C.E.R.D." = Canadian Engineer Reinforcement Depot [uncertain — expansion not given in the diary]; "S.O.S." = Struck Off Strength; "Y.M.C.A." = Young Men's Christian Association. A handwritten initialled mark, read as "K.H.M." [uncertain], appears in the Remarks column against most entries; it is noted here rather than repeated against every entry. Signatures read as "A. Leavitt Maj." (Major A. Leavitt, M.C.).

## Pages 4–8 — Diary text (1–17 March 1919)

Army Form C. 2118, "WAR DIARY or INTELLIGENCE SUMMARY". Heading on each sheet: "3rd Canadian Divisional Signal Co."

### Page 4 — 1–5 March 1919

> **Witley Camp, Mar. 1st.** — Leave: Lieut. R. B. Gibson M.C. M.M. and 2 O.R's. granted 8 days demobilization leave.
>
> Lieut. Maxey and 9 O.R's rejoined from leave.
>
> Hospital Admittances: 1 O.R. admitted to hospital.
>
> Strength Decrease: 2 O.R's. struck off strength.
>
> Y.M.C.A. Funds: A cheque for £12-5-10 has been received from the Y.M.C.A. and has been placed to the credit of the Company Regimental Funds.
>
> Weekend leave is now being granted to all ranks who apply for it, to enable them to visit relatives and friends before departure for Canada.
>
> **2/3/19.** — Leave: 18 O.R's rejoined from leave.
>
> Hospital admittances: 3 O.R's. admitted to hospital.
>
> Church Parade: Held at 09.15 hours. No. 4 Y.M.C.A. Hut this Camp.
>
> Since the arrival of the Company in England a severe epidemic of Influenza has been sweeping over the country. Large numbers of the men have suffered from attacks more or less severe, and most of the cases for admittance to hospital have been due to this epidemic.
>
> **3/3/19.** — Leave: 7 O.R's. rejoined from leave.
>
> Hospital Admittances: 1 O.R. sick admitted to hospital.
>
> Hospital discharges: 1 O.R. rejoined from hospital.
>
> Education: The Director of Educational Services of the Khaki University of Canada require teachers in Chemistry, Agriculture, Physics, and French to go to Ripon to teach in the concentration camp there until probably the end of June. 1 name has been submitted from this Company Viz: Sapper LEWIS R.M.
>
> **4/3/19** — Leave: 5 O.R's rejoined from leave.
>
> Hospital admittances: 2 O.R's.
>
> Hospital Discharges: Lieut. H. Pryde and 1 O.R. rejoined from hospital.
>
> Medical Boarding of Company Documentation, for demobilization being proceeded with
>
> Warning orders twice received have Company ready move Bramshott Camp no operation order yet received.
>
> **5/3/19** — Leave: Lieut Pryde granted 8 days leave..
>
> 8 O.R's. rejoined from leave.
>
> Major Leavitt M.C. granted 8 days demobilization leave.
>
> Capt. J. H. Leeson assumes Command.
>
> [Signed, bottom right:] *A. Leavitt Maj.*

### Page 5 — 6–11 March 1919

> **Witley Camp. 6/3/19** — Leave: L.A.C.Lee, Lieut. A. B. Rutherford and 22 O.R's. rejoined.
>
> Hospital discharges: 2 O.R's rejoined.
>
> Replicas of the Gold Medal presented by the city of Mons to the G.O.C. Canadian Corps were distributed by Capt. J.H.Leeson M.C. to all ranks of the Company who took part in the capture of Mons on November 11th last. This medal, given by the city of Mons, as a souvenir of their gratitude for the deliverance of the city from the Boche, bears the following inscription "LA VILLE DE MONS AUX SOLDATS DE LA 3eme DIVISION CANADIENNE, 11 9 bre 1918".
>
> **7/3/19** — Leave: Granted 2 O.R's, rejoined 15 O.R's.
>
> Hospital admittances: 1 O.R. admitted while on leave.
>
> **8/3/19** — Leave: 6 O.R's rejoined.
>
> Hospital discharges: 1 O.R. rejoined.
>
> Strength decrease: 14 O.R's struck off strength to C.E.R.D. Seaford.
>
> Company moves by road from "H" Wing Witley Camp under command J.H.Leeson M.C. to "A" Wing Bramshott Camp, passing the starting point 14.00 hours and arriving Bramshott 17.00 hours
>
> **9/3/19** — Leave: 7 O.R's rejoined.
>
> Strength decrease: Lieut W.D.Burden, Lieut. H.M. Bennett and 1 O.R. struck off strength.
>
> **10/3/19** — Leave: Lieut. H. D. Sharpe M.M. granted 3 days. 8 O.R's rejoined.
>
> Strength decrease: 2 O.R's struck off strength to C.E.R.D. Seaford, 2 O.R's struck off strength posted to C.E.R.D. Seaford to 2nd Canadian Discharge Depot London, 1 O.R. despatched to Khaki University Concentration Camp Ripon. posted C.E.R.D. Seaford. 1 O.R. admitted to hospital struck off strength.
>
> Hospital discharges : 1 O. R.
>
> **11/3/19** — Leave: No more leave can now be granted to the personnel of the Company owing to the urgency of completing and checking documentation and to the possibilities of sailing which will accomodate the entire Company in the very near future. One O.R. rejoined from leave.
>
> Strength Decrease: 3 O.R's struck off strength as being absent without leave, one since 26-1-19 and two 6-3-19.
>
> [Signed, bottom right:] *A. Leavitt Maj.*

## Open questions / uncertain items (March 1919)
- 8 March: "arriving Bramshott 17.00 hours" — the word "Bramshott" is struck through and rewritten in the typescript.
- 17 March: the Other Ranks draft figures (Montreal 29, Toronto 65, Winnipeg 38, Hamilton 7, Halifax 1) add to 140, but the diary's own typed total is "139." — discrepancy in the original.
- 17 March: "the drafts proceeding [struck-through word] as a body to their respective Dispersal Centre in Canada on the S. S. Cedric" — one word struck through and unreadable.
- The handwritten initialled mark in the Remarks column is read as "K.H.M." [uncertain].
- Names read as: Major A. Leavitt, M.C. (O.C.); Capt. J. H. Leeson, M.C.; Capt. S. B. Iler, M.C.; Lieut. H. Pryde; Lieut. R. B. Gibson, M.C., M.M.; Lieut. H. D. Sharpe, M.M. (also written "Sharp" on 10 March); Lieut. L. H. Miller; Lieut. A. B. Rutherford, M.M.; Lieut. L. A. C. Lee; Lieut. W. Maxey; Lieut. L. R. Peebles; Lieut. W. D. Burden; Lieut. H. M. Bennett; Sapper R. M. Lewis; Major T. E. Powers, M.C. (first O.C., December 1915).
- Casualties: none recorded in this month. Hospital admissions are almost all influenza: 1 O.R. (1 March), 3 O.Rs. (2 March), 1 O.R. (3 March), 2 O.Rs. (4 March), 1 O.R. while on leave (7 March), 1 O.R. (12 March, plus 1 detained in sick detention hut). No deaths are recorded in this month.
- The contents page says "Diary Text 5 Pages" and the diary text runs over pages 4–8 (5 diary pages), plus cover, contents and title page = 8 pages in total. No pages are missing. This is the last volume of the diary (Volume 40); the diary closes on 17 March 1919 with the Company's disbandment.
