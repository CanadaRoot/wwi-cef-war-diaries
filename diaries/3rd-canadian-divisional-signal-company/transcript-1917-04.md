# War Diary — 3rd Canadian Divisional Signal Company — April 1917

- **Unit:** 3rd Canadian Divisional Signal Company
- **Month:** April 1917 (Volume 17) — diary covers 1–30 April 1917
- **Source:** Library and Archives Canada, Collection Search Item ID 2004905 (RG 9 III-D-3, vol. 5005, microfilm reel T-10853, file 692; finding aid 9-52)
- **Pages:** April 1917, p. 1 – p. 5 (image files page-0088 – page-0092 of the diary)
- **April 1917 — COMPLETE** (pages 1–5): cover, contents, diary text 1–30 April, no appendices. Transcribed 2026-10-26.

## Transcription conventions
Original spelling, punctuation and abbreviations kept. Handwritten diary text throughout (Army Form C. 2118, "WAR DIARY or INTELLIGENCE SUMMARY"). `[uncertain]` marks words I could not read with confidence — never guessed. "do." = ditto, as written. No map coordinates are given in the diary; places are recorded as written. "Sig Office" = Signal Office; "H.Q." = Headquarters; "H.Qrs." = Headquarters; "Stn." = Station; "Battr." = Battery; "Adv. H.Qrs." = Advanced Headquarters; "O.Ps" = Observation Posts; "Battn." = Battalion. A repeated flourish, read as "do." [uncertain], appears in the Remarks column against most entries — apparently the diarist's initials or a ditto mark; it is noted here rather than repeated against every entry. The diarist's signature at the foot of each sheet reads "J. [uncertain] Capt., 3rd Can. Div Signal Coy" — the surname is an illegible flourish and is not guessed.

## Pages 3–5 — Diary text (1–30 April 1917)

Army Form C. 2118, "WAR DIARY or INTELLIGENCE SUMMARY". Heading on each sheet: "3rd Canadian Divisional Signal Coy".

### Page 3 — 1–10 April 1917

Place: Villers au Bois.

> **Villers au Bois, April 1** — Usual Routine. and patrol of lines. Burying of cable continued. Installing 30 pairs lead covered cable in Goodman Tunnel.
>
> **2** — Usual Routine. Goodman Tunnel cable completed. Bury from 1st test station to advanced H.Qrs at Fort George commenced. Bury from 5 test stn to Goodman Tunnel completed
>
> **3** — Bury from 1st station to Fort George continued. Usual Routine and patrol of lines.
>
> **4** — Fort George bury completed. Preparation of Fort George Signal Stn. commenced
>
> **5** — All buried systems completed and spliced through. Preparation of Fort George Stn. continued. Men from Battr. being trained in use of Buzzer. and their duties
>
> **6** — Power Buzzer Class Contd. Visual Signal Stn selected. [uncertain — "selected"?] and formation of Stn personnel
>
> **7** — Fort George Signal Stn. wiring completed. and working satisfactorily
>
> **8** — Preparation for occupation. of Adv. H.Qrs. Fort George. Power Buzzer class continued. Adv. H.Qr. Signal Office opened at 4. P.M. Contact aeroplane. Report centre. opened.
>
> **9** — Attack on Vimy Ridge. Communication intact throughout the day. Several important messages dropped by aeroplanes. and forwarded to Adv. H.Qrs.
>
> **10** — No breakdown in communications. Seven messages through Power Buzzer. Visual Stations little used but worked satisfactorily. Flag best for day work. and Lucas Lamp best for night work. Cable buried from E. end of Goodman Tunnel to Prince Arnulf Tunnel
>
> [Signed, bottom left:] *J. [uncertain] Capt. 3rd Can. Div Signal Coy*

## Open questions / uncertain items (April 1917)
- 6 April: "Visual Signal Stn selected." — "selected" [uncertain]; the word may read "erected".
- 9 April: the attack on Vimy Ridge — the diary records communications intact all day, several important messages dropped by aeroplanes and forwarded to Advanced Headquarters; seven messages went through by Power Buzzer on 10 April.
- 12 April: "Nuclear Stn installed at Vimy" — "Nuclear" [uncertain]; the station name is not clear from the handwriting.
- 21 April: "Salvage parties clearing forward area" — "area" [uncertain]; the last word of the entry is cut by the sheet edge.
- The repeated mark in the Remarks column is read as "do." [uncertain] — it may be the diarist's initials rather than a ditto.
- The diarist's signature surname is an illegible flourish [uncertain] on all three sheets; the initial reads "J.".
- Names/places read as: Villers au Bois; Fort George; Goodman Tunnel; Prince Arnulf Tunnel; Vimy Ridge; Gouy-Servins (March); Woodman Camp (March).
- No casualties are recorded in this month.
