- **Unit:** 3rd Canadian Divisional Signal Company

- **November 1917 — COMPLETE** (pages 151–159): cover, contents, title page, diary text 1–30 November, no appendices. Transcribed 2026-10-26.

## Transcription conventions
Original spelling, punctuation and abbreviations kept. Handwritten diary text throughout (Army Form C. 2118, "WAR DIARY or INTELLIGENCE SUMMARY"). `[uncertain]` marks words I could not read with confidence — never guessed. No map coordinates are given in the diary; places are recorded as written. "A.L." in the Remarks column is the initials of the officer commanding, Major A. Leavitt (signature "A. Leavitt Maj" at the foot of each sheet); it is noted here rather than repeated against every entry. "H.Q." = Headquarters; "Bde." = Brigade; "Cdn" = Canadian; "Div" = Division; "Sig Coy" = Signal Company; "Imp" = Imperial; "Inf Bde" = Infantry Brigade; "C.E.T.D." = Canadian Engineers Training Depot; "R.F.C." = Royal Flying Corps; "G.O.C." = General Officer Commanding; "O.C." = Officer Commanding. The diary's own heading on the diary sheets reads "3rd CANADIAN Div Sig Coy. CAN SIGS".

## Pages 4–9 — Diary text (1–30 November 1917)

Army Form C. 2118, "WAR DIARY or INTELLIGENCE SUMMARY". Heading on each sheet: "3rd CANADIAN Div Sig Coy. CAN SIGS".

### Page 4 — 1–3 November 1917

> **Canal Bank, Nov 1st** — Maintaining existing communication & laying new lines to connect new forward positions. The Divisional Visual doing good work and to day handled a large number of important messages. Enemy shelling heavy and using gas shells to a great extent.
>
> **2** — Orders received that 1st Cdn Div will relieve 3rd Cdn Div. Lt Parker lines officer of 1st Sig Coy with party of linemen shown over the area. No 4 Section of the 3rd Sigs relieved by No 4 Section 1st Sigs. Weather fine. Enemy shelling heavy
>
> **3rd** — Maintaining existing communications. Div Visual Station relieved by party from No 4 Sect 1st Signals. Owing to the number of Signal Officers obliged to be manned the number of linemen employed at the various test points and maintaining lines we were unable to provide relief for visual station & the men under Sgt Taylor have been on duty continuously for the past seven days. Their work has been of great importance & for the manner in which they carried this work on under the most trying conditions they are deserving of great praise. Weather fine. Enemy active

### Page 5 — 4–8 November 1917

> **Watou, Nov 4** — 3rd Cdn Div relieved by 1st Cdn Div. Relief completed at 11. A.M. Signal Office closed at CANAL BANK at 11. AM and opened at WATOU same hour.
>
> **5** — Inspection of all ranks not on duty at 9.30 A.M. Company allowed to knock off the remainder of the day to be a holiday. Arrangements made to take up training again.
>
> **6** — Gas Helmet & Box Respirator inspection & drill. Buzzer & Sounder classes started. A number of linemen that were employed in the forward area and the complete personnel of the visual station that were on duty reported sick. Doctor states men are suffering from effects of enemy gas. Cases not serious. Complete rest ordered. Weather wet
>
> **7** — Training carried on practically confined to indoor classes as weather conditions unfavourable for outdoor work. Usual routine
>
> **8** — Training continued. Warning order received that 3rd Cdn Div be prepared to relieve the 1st Cdn Div on morning of the 12th Usual routine

### Page 6 — 9–13 November 1917

> **Watou, Nov 9** — Training continued. No [number not given in original] Cpl SMARDEN awarded second bar to his Military Medal No [number not given] Cpl Burgess No [number not given] Sapper Young No [number not given] Spr BRAZIER No [number not given] Spr WILSON No [number not given] Spr BEATTEAY [uncertain] awarded the Military Medal for conspicuous bravery while maintaining communication during recent operations. Usual routine
>
> **10** — Party of linemen with instruments sent forward to relieve and take over from 1st Sigs. Training continued. Usual routine
>
> **11** — Training continued. All preparations made for move. Visited the 1st Sigs and shown over all lines etc.
>
> **Canal Bank, 12** — Office closed at WATOU at 10. A.M. & opened at CANAL BANK same hour. Relief of 1st Sigs completed at 9 A.M. and all lines working well. Visual Station established at KOREK also WIRELESS STN at same place. Considerable difficulty in maintaining forward lines on account of heavy enemy shelling. Weather fine
>
> **13** — Patrolling & maintaining existing communications. Enemy aircraft active & open lines frequently bombed out Weather fine

### Page 7 — 14–17 November 1917

> **Canal Bank, Nov 14** — Patrol & maintenance of lines. Two men of No. 3 Section wounded by shell fire. Enemy aircraft active. Weather fine
>
> **15th** — Patrol & maintenance of lines. No 2 Section 7th Bde relieves No 4 Sect 9 Bde. Orders received that 3rd Cdn Div will be relieved by 8th Imp Division. Maj Coom of the 8th Signals shown over the area.
>
> **16** — O.C. 8th Sigs again shown over communications. Party of linemen from 8th Sigs report in order to get acquainted with the lines. Party from 3rd Sigs proceed to new area to make ready new Sig Office
>
> **17** — Transport under Lt Smith starts for new area proceeding today as far as Castre. Our complete office, reliefs wireless, & report centre relief arrive from the 8th Sigs. The necessary changes of instruments switchboards etc completed at 9.15 P.M. Weather dull & foggy. Enemy fairly quiet

### Page 8 — 18–22 November 1917

> **Norrent Fontes, Nov 18** — New office at Norrent Fontes opened at 10. A.M. The relief of the 3rd Cdn Div Sigs by the 8th Imp Sig completed at 11. A.M. The remainder of company proceeded to Norrent Fontes by lorries arriving at 4. P.M.
>
> **19** — Company busy cleaning & fixing up billets & horse lines & transport
>
> **20** — Sgt Barker of No. 2 Section & Sgt Gibson of No. 3 Section (3rd Div Sigs) & Sgt CHALLACOMBE of 1st Div Sigs report to Hqrs for duty their applications for commissions having been approved. Instructional classes in visual, wireless, buzzer etc organized. Remainder of company busy getting horses, transport & harness again in shape
>
> **21** — Lt Pickles reports for duty from C.E.T.D. Eng. Lt MAXEY reports for duty (on loan from Cdn Corps Sigs) Lt McPherson left for England on 14 days leave. 3rd Cdn Div Arty arrive in rest area having been released in YPRES area. Training & cleaning up continued
>
> **22** — Corp WHEELER of the 2nd Sigs having received his commission reports for duty. Lt Pickles relieves Lt Craig at 10th Arty Bde. Lt Craig proceeding on leave to Eng. Capt McGrady also proceeds on leave.

### Page 9 — 23–30 November 1917

## Open questions / uncertain items (November 1917)
- 9 November: the diarist left the regimental numbers blank — "No" is written before each man's name but no number follows, for all six men (Cpl Smarden, Cpl Burgess, Sapper Young, Spr Brazier, Spr Wilson, Spr Beatteay). The spelling of "BEATTEAY" is uncertain.
- 14 November: two men of No. 3 Section wounded by shell fire — neither is named.
- 15 November: "Maj Coom of the 8th Signals" — read as written.
- 17 November: the transport proceeds "as far as Castre" — the diarist writes "Castre" (also the place name on 16 October); the intended place may be Castres.
- 23 November: the diarist wrote "compliments the company for the company for good work done" and struck through the repeated "for the company".
- Casualties in November: 2 wounded (two unnamed men of No. 3 Section, 14 November), plus a number of men sick from enemy gas (6 November, cases not serious). No men killed.
- Military Medals in November: Cpl Smarden (second bar to his Military Medal), Cpl Burgess, Sapper Young, Spr Brazier, Spr Wilson, Spr Beatteay (9 November).
- Names read as: Lt Parker; Sgt Taylor; Lt Smith; Lt Pickles; Lt Maxey; Lt McPherson; Lt Craig; Capt McGrady; Sgt Barker; Sgt Gibson; Sgt Challacombe; Corp Wheeler; Lt McKenzie (123rd Pioneer Battalion); Maj Coom (8th Signals); Cpl Smarden; Cpl Burgess; Sapper Young; Spr Brazier; Spr Wilson; Spr Beatteay [uncertain].
- No map coordinates are given in the diary.
