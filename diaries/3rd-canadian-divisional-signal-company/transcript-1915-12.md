# War Diary — 3rd Canadian Divisional Signal Company — December 1915

- **Unit:** 3rd Canadian Divisional Signal Company, 3rd Canadian Division
- **Month:** December 1915 (Volume 1) — this volume covers 5 to 31 December 1915
- **Source:** Library and Archives Canada, Collection Search Item ID 2004905 (RG 9 III-D-3, vol. 5005, microfilm reel T-10853, file 692; finding aid 9-52)
- **Pages:** December 1915, p. 1 – p. 6 (image files page-0001 – page-0006 of the 307-page diary)
- **December 1915 — COMPLETE** (pages 1–6 of 307): cover, contents, title page, diary text 5–31 December, no appendices. Transcribed 2026-10-26.

## Transcription conventions
Original spelling, punctuation and abbreviations kept. Handwritten diary text throughout (Army Form C. 2118, "WAR DIARY or INTELLIGENCE SUMMARY" — "INTELLIGENCE SUMMARY" struck through on Sheet No. 1). `[uncertain]` marks words I could not read with confidence — never guessed. Map references are given as written (Sheet 27 N.E.); no latitude/longitude coordinates are given. "N.C.O's" = Non-Commissioned Officers; "S.C. Sounders" = single-current sounders (telegraph sounders); "O.C." = Officer Commanding. A repeated initialled mark appears in the Remarks column against most entries; it is noted in the open questions rather than repeated against every entry. The diary's own heading on the diary sheets is the unit name written in by hand at the top right. The "3" written beneath "Volume 1" on the cover is a microfilm/frame mark, not part of the volume number.

## Pages 4–6 — Diary text (5–31 December 1915)

Army Form C. 2118, "WAR DIARY or INTELLIGENCE SUMMARY". "Original" is written by hand at the top left of each sheet. The printed footer reads "T.2134. Wt. W708—776. 500000. 4/15. Sir J. C. & S."

### Page 4 — 5–10 December 1915 (Sheet No. 1)

> **BAILLEUL, Dec. 5th.** — Major S.E. Powers reported to "Q" Branch of Canadian Corps and received instructions to report to General Mercer, also to proceed with the organization and training of a Third Canadian Division Signal Company.
>
> **BAILLEUL, Dec. 6th.** — Major Powers requested names of proposed personnel from troops in Canadian Corps command.
>
> **METEREN, Dec. 7th.** — Headquarters of new Company to be at METEREN (map ref. X17 C 3.4 Sheet 27 N.E.).
>
> **METEREN, Dec. 8th.** — District of new headquarters surveyed and provision made for communication to units of Canadian Corps troops by the use of Corps laid lines.
>
> **METEREN, Dec. 9th, 10 AM.** — Signal office at X17 C 3.4 (Sheet 27 N.E.) occupied by 2nd Canadian Infantry Brigade, 1st Canadian Division taken over by this Company at ten AM.
>
> **METEREN, Dec. 10th.** — Equipment, instruments and personnel drawn from Canadian Corps [entry continues on Sheet No. 2 — see Page 5].

### Page 5 — 10–16 December 1915 (Sheet No. 2)

> *continued from Sheet No. 1.*
>
> **METEREN, Dec. 10th.** — Signal Company to operate Corps lines to units of Canadian Corps Troops.
>
> **METEREN, Dec. 11th, 10 AM.** — Signal Office of Canadian Corps Troops opened at X17 C 3.4 (sheet 27 N.E.) at ten AM — Eleven N.C.O's and men and two riding horses attached to Company this date.
>
> **METEREN, Dec. 12th.** — System of office reliefs inaugurated this date.
>
> **METEREN, Dec. 13th, 14th, 15th.** — Organization, training and equipping of Company proceeded with. Six N.C.O's. and men attached to Company this date, nine on the 14th. and three on the fifteenth.
>
> **METEREN, Dec. 16th.** — Company routine laid down and class of instruction on S.C. Sounders and vibrators commenced.

### Page 6 — 17–31 December 1915 (Sheet No. 3)

> **METEREN, Dec. 17th to Dec. 22nd.** — Organization, training and equipping of Company proceeded with. During this period one officer, thirty-seven N.C.O's and men, and seven horses were attached to the Company.
>
> **METEREN, Dec. 23rd, 9.30 AM.** — Company moved to new headquarters at FLETRE, FRANCE, opened signal office and established communication with Canadian Corps and 7th and 8th Infantry Brigades.
>
> **FLETRE, Dec. 24th to Dec. 31st.** — Organization, training and equipping of Company proceeded with. During this period one officer, fifty N.C.O's. and men were attached to the Company.
>
> [Signed, bottom right:] *S. E. Powers Major / O.C. Signals 3rd Canadian Division*
