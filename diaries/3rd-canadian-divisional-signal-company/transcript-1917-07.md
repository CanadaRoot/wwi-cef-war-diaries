# War Diary — 3rd Canadian Divisional Signal Company — July 1917

- **Unit:** 3rd Canadian Divisional Signal Company
- **Month:** July 1917 (Volume 20) — diary covers 1–31 July 1917
- **Source:** Library and Archives Canada, Collection Search Item ID 2004905 (RG 9 III-D-3, vol. 5005, microfilm reel T-10853, file 692; finding aid 9-52)
- **Pages:** July 1917, p. 1 – p. 8 (image files page-0111 – page-0118 of the diary)
- **July 1917 — COMPLETE** (pages 1–8): cover, contents, title page, diary text 1–31 July, no appendices. Transcribed 2026-10-26.

## Transcription conventions
Original spelling, punctuation and abbreviations kept. Handwritten diary text throughout (Army Form C. 2118, "WAR DIARY or INTELLIGENCE SUMMARY"). `[uncertain]` marks words I could not read with confidence — never guessed. No map coordinates are given in the diary; places are recorded as written. "Sig Office" = Signal Office; "H.Q." = Headquarters; "H.Qrs." = Headquarters; "Adv." / "Advd" = Advanced; "Bde" = Brigade; "Bn" = Battalion; "Ent" = Entrenching; "C.F.A." = Canadian Field Artillery; "O.C." = Officer Commanding; "Sgt" = Sergeant; "Cpl" = Corporal; "Lt" = Lieutenant; "Capt" = Captain; "Maj" = Major; "recd" = received; "D V" = as written (twisted D V wire); "C.Y.D." and "C.Y.C.P." [uncertain] — as written, unexpanded. A repeated mark, read as "A.C.", appears in the Remarks column against most entries — apparently the diarist's initials; it is noted here rather than repeated against every entry. The diarist's signature at the foot of each sheet reads "A. Leavitt Maj." — the 15 July entry ("Maj Leavitt having gone on leave to England") confirms the surname Leavitt, resolving the May transcript's tentative "Witt" reading. From 15 July Capt Leeson signs in command during Maj Leavitt's leave; Maj Leavitt resumes command 25 July (the sheets are still signed "A. Leavitt Maj." throughout).

## Pages 4–8 — Diary text (1–31 July 1917)

Army Form C. 2118, "WAR DIARY or INTELLIGENCE SUMMARY". Heading on each sheet: "3rd Canadian Div Signal Coy".

### Page 4 — 1–5 July 1917 (diary sheet 3)

> **Fort George, July 1** — Operation Orders received 3rd Cdn Div to relieve 1st Cdn Div. Looking over lines in new area. Exchanged switchboards with C.Y.D. [uncertain] Details for relief arranged
>
> **" 2** — 7th Cdn Inf Bde relieves 12th Cdn Inf Bde in forward area. New line cut through to Bde in new area. Switchboard installed at new Advd Report Centre. No 3 Section relieved by No 2 Section of the 1st Div Sigs
>
> **" 3** — New report centre opened. All lines working well. Moving stores from present Hqrs to new Hqrs at Chateau De la Haie. Lt Nicholson of the 4th Entrenching Batt posted to No 4 Section as acting O.C. Lt McGie [uncertain] who had acted as Sig Officer with both 8th & 9th Bdes rejoined his own Section with the 8th Bde.
>
> **Chateau De La Haie, 4** — Hqrs moved from Fort George to Chateau De la Haie taking over from 1st Cdn Div. New line of twisted D V laid from Advd Report Centre to new Bde Hqrs in Red Trench.
>
> **" 5** — Cutting over new air lines to C.Y.C.P. [uncertain] Patrolling forward lines. Six [uncertain — "Six"?] wireless operators transferred from Corps Signals for new Divisional Wireless Section
>
> [Signed, bottom right:] *A. Leavitt Maj.*

### Page 5 — 6–10 July 1917 (diary sheet 4)

> **Chateau De La Haie, July 6** — Started to lay new armoured cable line from Advd Div Hqrs to Bde in line putting same in new communication trench and stapling well in to sides of trench. Party at work on new air lines. Building new horse lines and running through salvaged cable.
>
> **7th** — Party at work on armoured cable line. Work on horse lines continued. Permits obtained & work of trimming trees, where same were in contact with air lines, commenced. Good progress made with all work in hand.
>
> **8th** — Lt Parker placed in charge of wireless & power buzzer section. All work in hand continued.
>
> **9th** — Riding Horses (27) Light Draught (7) recd to complete Sub Sections of Arty Signals. Wireless station placed at Adv Bde Hqrs, Red Trench. Party at work salvaging cable. All other work previously in hand continued.
>
> **10th** — New armoured cable line completed & working satisfactorily. New horse lines completed. No 3 Section 8th Bde relieves No 2 Section 7th Bde in forward area. Sixteen other ranks returned to the Base as unfit
>
> [Signed, bottom right:] *A. Leavitt Maj.*

### Page 6 — 11–17 July 1917 (diary sheet 5)

> **Chateau De La Haie, July 11** — Rebuilding lines between Rear and Adv Bdes. Installing new cable at Rear Div Hqrs. Salvaging cable & trimming trees continued. Usual routine of patrolling lines etc.
>
> **12th** — Wireless station installed at Adv Div Hqrs in touch with wireless station at Adv Bde Hqrs. Good signals exchanged and a number of test messages sent. Lt Nicholson of 4th Ent Batt. receives orders to report to 87th Cdn Inf Batt.
>
> **13** — Usual routine. All work continued & good progress made
>
> **14th** — Lt McKenzie 123rd Pioneer Batt attached to this company and posted to No 4 Section as Bde Sig Officer 9th Cdn Inf Bde. Overland lines to forward Bde cut by shell fire and use made of wireless stations a number of messages handled. Usual routine.
>
> **15th** — Capt Leeson assumes command of company Maj Leavitt having gone on leave to England. Usual routine of patrolling lines salvaging cable etc continued.
>
> **16** — Usual routine.
>
> **17** — Usual routine.
>
> [Signed, bottom right:] *A. Leavitt Maj.*

### Page 7 — 18–24 July 1917 (diary sheet 6)

> **Chateau De La Haie, July 18th** — Commenced work on new trestle route between H.O. and S test box. Amplifier installed in forward area in order to police our own telephone lines and prevent indiscreet telephone conversations. No messages of any importance picked up. All other work in hand continued.
>
> **" 19** — Visual Station installed to act as central station & receive messages from any point in front line. A number of test messages received. New line laid from Report Centre to connect with Visual station. Other work carried on
>
> **20** — Wire salvaged being run through. Work on new trestle route continued. Lines to forward Bde again cut by shell fire. Visual & wireless both used with good results.
>
> **21st** — Usual routine all work in hand continued
>
> **22** — Usual routine. All work in hand continued
>
> **23** — Orders received 3rd Div Arty to come under orders of the 1st Cdn Div Arty and the necessary wires to new group and Battery positions being laid. Forward lines heavily shelled. Visual and wireless again used.
>
> **24** — 3rd Cdn Div Arty move to new positions communications established & working well
>
> [Signed, bottom right:] *A. Leavitt Maj.*

### Page 8 — 25–31 July 1917 (diary sheet 7)

> **Chateau De La Haie, July 25th** — Orders received for relief of 3rd Cdn Div by 4th Cdn Division and necessary arrangements made. Maj Leavitt returns from leave and assumes command. Usual routine. Showing Officers and men of 4th Div Sigs over lines.
>
> **Barlin, 26** — Relief completed at 10 A.M. and new office opened at BARLIN same hour
>
> **27** — Inspection of all ranks. Repairing & rebuilding horse standings and huts. All phones & instruments not in use called in from Bdes & Batteries for thorough overhaul while in rest.
>
> **28th** — Work of rebuilding huts & standings being carried on. Arrangements made for training and sports while company is in rest.
>
> **29** — Church parade of all ranks not on duty. Half holiday. Usual routine
>
> **30** — Inspection of all Bde Sections. Classes in Visual, buzzer, wireless and cable work formed and work of training commenced. Work on huts & standings also continued
>
> **31** — Training in all branches continued. Work on huts completed. Work on horse standings continued. Shale being drawn for use on horse lines. Usual routine
>
> [Signed, bottom right:] *A. Leavitt Maj.*

## Open questions / uncertain items (July 1917)
- 1 July: "Exchanged switchboards with C.Y.D." [uncertain] — the initials are read as written, unexpanded.
- 3 July: "Lt McGie" [uncertain] — the same name appears 27 June; it may read "McGee".
- 5 July: "Cutting over new air lines to C.Y.C.P." [uncertain]; "Six [uncertain] wireless operators transferred from Corps Signals for new Divisional Wireless Section" — the number before "wireless" is read as "Six" with low confidence.
- 12 July: "87th Cdn Inf Batt." — read as written; Lt Nicholson leaves the company to report to the 87th Battalion.
- 14 July: "Lt McKenzie 123rd Pioneer Batt" — read as written.
- 15 July: "Capt Leeson assumes command" — read as "Leeson"; the May transcript reads a similar name as "Lt Lesson [uncertain]", which may be the same man.
- 18 July: "between H.O. and S test box" — "H.O." as written (possibly H.Q.).
- Names read as: Maj A. Leavitt (O.C., on leave to England 15–25 July); Capt Leeson (in command 15–25 July); Lt Nicholson (4th Entrenching Batt, then to 87th Cdn Inf Batt); Lt McGie [uncertain]; Lt Parker (wireless & power buzzer section); Lt McKenzie (123rd Pioneer Batt, Bde Sig Officer 9th Cdn Inf Bde).
- No casualties are recorded in July 1917. Sixteen other ranks were returned to the Base as unfit (10 July).
- Places: Fort George (1–3 July); Chateau De la Haie (4–25 July); Red Trench (Bde Hqrs); Barlin (26–31 July, rest area).
- Moves: 3rd Cdn Div relieves 1st Cdn Div (1–4 July); 3rd Cdn Div Arty comes under 1st Cdn Div Arty orders (23–24 July); 4th Cdn Division relieves 3rd Cdn Div and the company moves to Barlin (25–26 July).
