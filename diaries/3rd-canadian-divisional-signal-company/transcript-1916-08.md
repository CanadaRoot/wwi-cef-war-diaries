# War Diary — 3rd Canadian Divisional Signal Company — August 1916

- **Unit:** 3rd Canadian Divisional Signal Company
- **Month:** August 1916 (Volume 9)
- **Source:** Library and Archives Canada, Collection Search Item ID 2004905 (RG 9 III-D-3, vol. 5005, microfilm reel T-10853, file 692; finding aid 9-52)
- **Pages:** August 1916, p. 1 – p. 8 (image files page-0049 – page-0056)
- **August 1916 — COMPLETE** (pages 1–8): cover, contents, a title sheet, diary text 1–31 August, no appendices. Transcribed 2026-10-26.

## Transcription conventions
Original spelling, punctuation and abbreviations kept. Handwritten diary text throughout (Army Form C. 2118, "WAR DIARY or INTELLIGENCE SUMMARY"). `[uncertain]` marks words I could not read with confidence — never guessed. No map coordinates are given in the diary; places are recorded as written. From August the diary is in a different, larger hand than May–July (a new diarist), and the Remarks column initials read clearly as "J.E.P."; they appear against most entries and are noted here rather than repeated against every entry. The diarist's spellings "RENINGHELST", "STEENVOORDE", "Dolls House" and "YEOMENRY" (June) are kept as written. "H.Q." = Headquarters; "Bde." = Brigade; "Infy." = Infantry; "C.F.A." = Canadian Field Artillery; "C.M.R." = Canadian Mounted Rifles; "D.R." = Despatch Rider; "O.C." = Officer Commanding; "O.R." = Other Ranks; "A.D.A.S." = Assistant Director of Army Signals (as written); "C.Y.C.R." and "K.S." are test-station / battle-headquarters code names used in the diary. "Helio" = heliograph.

## Pages 4–8 — Diary text (1–31 August 1916)

Army Form C. 2118, "WAR DIARY or INTELLIGENCE SUMMARY". The sheets are not headed with the unit name. Place: RENINGHELST until 24 August, then STEENVOORDE.

### Page 4 — 1–4 August 1916

> **RENINGHELST, 1st August** — Tactical scheme carried out with Cable Detachments, Mounted D.R.s and Visual Signallers, communication maintained with moving troops, fifteen miles from base, lateral communication by visual (Helio and Flag). Weather hot. Enemy quiet on our front.
>
> **2nd** — Work continued on forward buried cable. Inspection of Signallers at Divisional Sig School at Steenvoorde. Classes making good progress. Working party searching for fault on buried cable between K.S. and Dolls House. Weather hot. Enemy quiet.
>
> **3rd** — O.Cs. of the 1st 2nd and 3rd Sig Coys left to study methods and conditions of communications at the Somme. Trouble on faulty cable located and linesmen working on same. Cable Detachments hard at work training. Air raid by enemy planes at 8.45 P.M. Bomb dropped just outside billet, no damage done.
>
> **4th** — Company hard at work training. Working party replacing faulty cable. Classes opened for instruction of Artillery Signallers and linesmen. One officer and six other ranks attending. Course embraces, instruction in instruments, cable splicing + laying and maintenance of lines.

### Page 5 — 5–10 August 1916

> **RENINGHELST, Aug 5th** — Cleaning up all wires around Ramparts, old lines being buried. Party working replacing faulty cable. Company training. Considerable shelling near K.S. Several direct hits on cable route no damage. Party out filling in shell holes on cable route.
>
> **6th** — Work at Ramparts continued. Work of replacing faulty cable completed. [uncertain — "Linemen"?] at Dolls House being strengthened by working party from company.
>
> **7th** — Training continued. O.C. returned from the Somme. 7th Bde Machine Gun company consulted by wireless, a simple and secret code used, results excellent. Fairly heavy shelling around K.S.
>
> **8th** — Conference of A.D.A.S Corps and O Cs of Sig Coys. Training continued. Party filling in shell holes and mounding up buried cable route. Work continued at Ramparts.
>
> **9th** — Work on Cable route and at Ramparts continued also training. Gas attack on front of Div on our left. Gas strong at our Left Bde Hqrs and at K.S., lights at C.Y.C.R no damage. Relief of 1st Canadian by 3rd Canadian Division commenced.
>
> **10th** — Communications of 1st Canadian Divisional Sig Coy taken over by 3rd Canadian Div Sig Coy. New Bde Hqrs Office opened at Bedford House. Communication good. Work at Ramparts and on buried route continued. Weather hot. Enemy quiet.

### Page 6 — 11–14 August 1916

> **RENINGHELST, Aug 11th** — New Bde Hqrs Office opened at Railway Dugouts. Lecture on conditions of communications as observed at the Somme. Training continued. Work completed at Ramparts. Party from C.Y.C.R 3rd Canadian Entrenching Batt for burying cable from C.Y.C.R back to permanent pole route. [uncertain — "Real"?] covered cable used. Enemy attack on front of 1st C.M.R. and 60th Battalion. Buried lines though heavily shelled remained intact, overhead wires shot out.
>
> **12th** — Work continued with party from 3rd Canadian Entrenching Battalion in burying cable. Relief of 1st Canadian Artillery by 3rd Canadian Artillery taking place.
>
> **13th** — Centre Group Hqrs moved to Railway Dugouts and new office opened. Right Group to Belgian Chateau and new office opened. Weather fine. Enemy quiet.
>
> **14th** — Inspection of Signallers at Sig School. Eight men passed out, and others making good progress. Unable to get further working party from Entrenching Batt and work being carried on by men of company. Progress in burying very slow as only fifteen men available. Area party from Corps Signals under direction of 3rd Signals strengthening line forward of K.S.

### Page 7 — 15–23 August 1916

> **RENINGHELST, Aug 15th** — Signal School moved from Steenvoorde to Brandhoek. Training of company discontinued in order to supply men for completion of buried work started.
>
> **16th** — Work continued on burying. More men from battalions reporting to Signal School.
>
> **17th** — Completed work of burying. Bde Section of the 12th Canadian Inf Bde undergoing training with Bde Sig Section of 7th Canadian Inf Bde. Difficulty experienced in manning signal stations as in addition to the three Bde Hqrs, a Signal Station at Rest Bde, [uncertain — "Buseborn"?] and 5th Field Coy have to be maintained.
>
> **18th** — Visual Scheme carried out. A chain of stations out and Helio, Lamp, and flag used. New French Lamp used and found very satisfactory. Lamp could easily be read in daylight over three miles. Enemy quiet.
>
> **19th** — Visual work and training carried out.
>
> **20th** — Visual work carried on and route march in marching order carried out.
>
> **21st** — Party from 4th British Div Sig Coy taken over lines preparatory to taking over.
>
> **22nd** — Telephone exchange installed at Hooge Farm for use of Artillery observing officers. Communications good.
>
> **23rd** — Officers and men of 4th British Div Sig Coy again shown over lines and arrangements made for taking over. Enemy quiet. All circuits in perfect working order.

### Page 8 — 24–31 August 1916

> **RENINGHELST, Aug 24th** — Relief of 3rd Canadian Div Sig Coy by 4th British Div Sig Coy. One officer in charge of Artillery lines and one of Infantry lines together with three pack mules and six linesmen left behind to assist the 4th British Coy. 3rd Canadian Div Sig Coy move to Steenvoorde and office opened at 9. P.M.
>
> **STEENVOORDE, Aug 25th** — Company busy fixing up new billets. Arrangements made for carrying on training.
>
> **26th–27th** — Training commenced. Weather wet.
>
> **28th** — Central Station opened at MONT DES CATS for visual work. Communication opened with 7th 8th 9th Inf Bde Hqrs and 11th Battery Hqrs. Training continued. All phones from both Artillery and Infantry called in for a thorough overhauling.
>
> **29th** — Training continued.
>
> **30th** — Training continued. Weather wet.
>
> **31st** — Training continued. Weather fine. Good Helio work carried on.
>
> [Signed, bottom right:] *J. E. Powers Major*
>
> [Oval stamp:] 3rd CANADIAN DIVISION SIGNAL COMPANY — No. 96, Date 31/8/16

## Open questions / uncertain items (August 1916)
- The contents page says "Diary Text 6 Pages"; the diary sheets are the five Army Form C. 2118 sheets on pages 4–8, with the "War Diary Month of August / Original" title sheet on page 3 making up the sixth page.
- 6 August: "[uncertain — 'Linemen'?] at Dolls House being strengthened by working party from company." — the word before "at Dolls House" reads like "Linemen".
- 8 August: "Conference of A.D.A.S Corps and O Cs of Sig Coys." — "A.D.A.S" read as written.
- 9 August: "Gas strong at our Left Bde Hqrs and at K.S., lights at C.Y.C.R no damage." — "lights" read as written; the sense is unclear (possibly gas lights at C.Y.C.R).
- 11 August: "[uncertain — 'Real'?] covered cable used." — the word before "covered cable" reads like "Real"; it may instead be "Reel" or "Lead".
- 12th/13th: the date column carries both 12th and 13th; the 12th is assigned to "Work continued with party from 3rd Canadian Entrenching Battalion… Relief of 1st Canadian Artillery by 3rd Canadian Artillery taking place." and the 13th to "Centre Group Hqrs moved to Railway Dugouts and new office opened. Right Group to Belgian Chateau and new office opened. Weather fine. Enemy quiet." — the assignment follows the row alignment of the dates against the text.
- 17 August: "a Signal Station at Rest Bde, [uncertain — 'Buseborn'?] and 5th Field Coy have to be maintained." — the place name between "Rest Bde" and "5th Field Coy" could not be read with confidence.
- The Remarks column initials read clearly as "J.E.P." in this month's hand; they match the signature "J. E. Powers".
- No casualties are named in this volume; no map coordinates are given.
- Names read as: Maj. J. E. Powers; the O.Cs. of the 1st, 2nd and 3rd Signal Companies (3 August, unnamed).
