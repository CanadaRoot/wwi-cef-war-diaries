# War Diary — 3rd Canadian Divisional Signal Company — February 1916

- **Unit:** 3rd Canadian Divisional Signal Company, 3rd Canadian Division
- **Month:** February 1916 (Volume 3) — this volume covers 1 to 29 February 1916
- **Source:** Library and Archives Canada, Collection Search Item ID 2004905 (RG 9 III-D-3, vol. 5005, microfilm reel T-10853, file 692; finding aid 9-52)
- **Pages:** February 1916, p. 1 – p. 4 (image files page-0012 – page-0015 of the 307-page diary)
- **February 1916 — COMPLETE** (pages 12–15 of 307): cover, contents, title page, diary text 1–29 February, no appendices. Transcribed 2026-10-26.

## Transcription conventions
Original spelling, punctuation and abbreviations kept. Handwritten diary text throughout (Army Form C. 2118, "WAR DIARY or INTELLIGENCE SUMMARY" — "INTELLIGENCE SUMMARY" struck through). `[uncertain]` marks words I could not read with confidence — never guessed. No map references are given in this month's diary text. "N.C.O's" = Non-Commissioned Officers; "O.C." = Officer Commanding. A repeated initialled mark appears in the Remarks column against most entries; it is noted in the open questions rather than repeated against every entry. The diary's own heading on the diary sheet is "3rd Canadian Division Signal Coy.", written in by hand at the top right. The "1" written beneath "Volume 3" on the cover is a microfilm/frame mark, not part of the volume number.

## Page 4 — Diary text (1–29 February 1916, Sheet No. 6)

Army Form C. 2118, "WAR DIARY or INTELLIGENCE SUMMARY". "Original" is written by hand at the top left of the sheet. The printed footer reads "T.2134. Wt. W708—776. 500000. 4/15. Sir J. C. & S."

> **MONTH OF FEBRUARY 1916.**
>
> **FLETRE, Feb. 1st to Feb. 24th.** — Organization, training, and equipping of Company proceeded with. During the period forty men were taken on the strength of the Company on transfer and thirtyfive men were returned to battalions as unsuitable for signal work.
>
> **FLETRE, Feb. 24th.** — One officer, twentysix N.C.O's and men detached from the Company and posted to 9th Canadian Infantry Brigade, and moved off at two pm today to join that brigade, whose headquarters are at EECKE, FRANCE, to take care of the signal communications of that brigade and to be known as No 4 Section, 3rd Canadian Division Signal Company, C.S.C. [uncertain — "C.S.C."?]
>
> **FLETRE, Feb. 25th to Feb. 29th.** — Training of Company in mounted and dismounted work, cable laying and operating proceeded with. During this period sixty N.C.O's and men taken on strength of the Company.
>
> [Signed, bottom right:] *S. E. Powers Major / O.C. 3rd Canadian Division Signals* — the signature line is partly cut off at the right edge of the scan.
