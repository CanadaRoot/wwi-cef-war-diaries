## Transcription conventions
Original spelling, punctuation and abbreviations kept. The diary text is typewritten throughout (Army Form C. 2118, "WAR DIARY or INTELLIGENCE SUMMARY"); the "INTELLIGENCE SUMMARY" alternative heading is struck through on every diary sheet. `[uncertain]` marks words I could not read with confidence — never guessed. Passages struck through in the original are shown as [struck: …]; a handwritten correction is noted where it occurs. A repeated handwritten mark in the Remarks column, read as "K.M.C." [uncertain] — apparently the O.C.'s initials (cf. the signature at 30 September, which appears to read "K. W. Campbell" [uncertain]) — appears against most entries; it is noted here rather than repeated against every entry. Map references are given as written (e.g. "O. 29. a. 9.9."), with no coordinates supplied beyond the sheet reference "Sheet 51b". "H.Q." = Headquarters; "Hqrs" = Headquarters; "C. I. Bde." / "C.I.B." = Canadian Infantry Brigade; "C.D.A." = Canadian Divisional Artillery; "C.F.A." = Canadian Field Artillery; "D.M.G.C." = Divisional Machine Gun Commander; "D.R." = Despatch Rider; "O.C." = Officer Commanding; "O. i/c." = Officer in charge; "G.O.C." = General Officer Commanding; "G.S.O. 1" = General Staff Officer, 1st grade; "A.D.M.S." = Assistant Director Medical Services; "A.D.Signals" = Assistant Director of Signals; "N.C.O." = Non-Commissioned Officer; "O.Rs." = Other Ranks; "P.T." = Physical Training; "M.M." = Military Medal; "M.C." = Military Cross; "C.E." = Canadian Engineers; "M.N." = midnight; "CYC", "CRO", "CYCR", "CYB", "CRS" and "CF 3" are signal office call signs, as written. ### Page 4 — 1–3 September 1918

> **DUISANS, 1** — Divisional Artillery still in line, with Hqrs at O. 36 d 75.75 Sheet 51 b. Infantry Brigades at "Y" Camp, Hermaville and Etrun. Commanding Officer visited G.O.C., 8th C. I. Bde to talk over experiences of past month. A poled cable section is now formed to be used to bring up communications behind a rapid advance, as past experiences have shown that lines laid overland are unreliable. By poling cable it is hoped that the disadvantages of heavy horse transport and the striking of numerous camps, which in the past has added serious difficulties to communications, will be overcome.
>
> Warning order received to be ready to move on 4 hours notice.
>
> **2** — A Sergeant-Major Drill Instructor has been borrowed for instruction in "P.T." work; for training of N.C.Os., and drilling of men. N. C. Os. class of instructiion held each morning at 9.30 a.m; mens parade for "P.T." Drill at 2.00 p.m.
>
> **10.00 am.** Ordered to be ready to move on one hours notice. Cable Sections now packed in readiness, advanced party of Office Personnel ready at 10.30 a.m. to move off.
>
> **1.00 pm.** All brigades moving to ARRAS; M. G. Batt'n to ROMVILLE; Started to arrange communications for them via existing air-lines and buried systems.
>
> **2.30 pm.** Sgt. Dunbar, N.C.O. i/c Despatch Riders sent forward to make reconnaissances of roads of area likely to be occupied by the Division.
>
> 9th C. I. Bde. only Brigade to give proposed location in ARRAS, before moving. In my report on Communications of recent operations, great stress was laid on the advisability of beforehand information of proposed new headquarters. Such information very essential to the efficiency and prompt arrangement of Signal Services. An instructional pamphlet on, "The correct method of Exchange Operating", has been distributed throughout the Division, with a view to uniformity of operating all exchanges in use in throughout the Division, and increasing the general efficiency of the use of the telephone.
>
> **3, 10.00 am;** 9th C. I. Bde ordered to move from ARRAS to VIS-en-ARTOIS, exact location not yet known. An advanced Canadian Corps opening at N. 21 c. at 11.00 a.m.
>
> **12.35 pm.** Informed that Divisional Hqrs likely be located at O. 29. a. 9.9. Sheet 51b.
>
> **1.45 pm.** Lieut. J. D. Baker went forward to put through line to 9th C. I. Bde via ARRAS to VIS-en-ARTOIS.
>
> Parades: N.C.Os. class at 9.30 a.m. O.Rs. 2.00 p.m. "P.T." and Infantry Drill; 7th C.I.Bde located for night at No. 6 Rue de Simond, ARRAS.
