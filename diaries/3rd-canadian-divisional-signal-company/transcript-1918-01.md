# War Diary — 3rd Canadian Divisional Signal Company, C.E. — January 1918

- **Unit:** 3rd Canadian Divisional Signal Company, C.E. (Canadian Engineers)
- **Month:** January 1918 (Volume 26)
- **Source:** Library and Archives Canada, Collection Search Item ID 2004905 (RG 9 III-D-3, vol. 5005, microfilm reel T-10853, file 692; finding aid 9-52)
- **Pages:** January 1918, p. 1 – p. 9 (image files page-0168 – page-0176 of the diary)
- **January 1918 — COMPLETE** (pages 1–9): cover, contents, title page, diary text 1–31 January, no appendices. Transcribed 2026-10-26.
- **Diary covers 5 December 1915 – 17 March 1919.**

## Transcription conventions
Original spelling, punctuation and abbreviations kept. Handwritten diary text throughout (Army Form C. 2118, "WAR DIARY or INTELLIGENCE SUMMARY"). `[uncertain]` marks words I could not read with confidence — never guessed. No map coordinates are given in the diary; places are recorded as written. The diarist's initials "A.L." appear in the Remarks column against every entry and are not repeated against each entry below; the sheets are signed "A. Leavitt Maj" (O.C.). "H.Q." = Headquarters; "Hqrs" = Headquarters; "Bde" = Brigade; "Div" = Division; "Cdn" = Canadian; "Sigs" = Signals; "O.P." = Observation Post; "R.F.C." = Royal Flying Corps; "D.C.M." = Distinguished Conduct Medal; "S.S.O." = Senior Signal Officer; "A.D. Sigs" = Assistant Director of Signals; "O.C." = Officer Commanding; "C.C.R.C." = Canadian Corps Reinforcement Camp [uncertain — expansion not given in the diary]; "Gen Staff" = General Staff. "Bracquemont" is the place written in the Place column for 1–22 January (spelled "BRACQUEMONT" in the diary); "Auchel" for 23–31 January. "Ferfay" is a village near Auchel.

## Pages 4–9 — Diary text (1–31 January 1918)

Army Form C. 2118, "WAR DIARY or INTELLIGENCE SUMMARY". Heading on each sheet: "3rd Canadian Div Sig Coy. Cdn Signals" (variant spellings "Cdn Sigs", "Can Sigs").

### Page 4 — 1–7 January 1918

> **Bracquemont, Jan 1** — The day observed as far as possible as a holiday only the care & exercise of horses & the necessary office routine. Enemy quiet. Weather fine
>
> **2** — Lt H. H. Craig admitted to Field Ambulance. Patrol and maintenance of lines. Gas Helmet drill & inspection. Salvaging wire.
>
> **3** — Rifle inspection. Patrol & maintenance of lines. Salvaging old wire
>
> **4** — No 304611 Sgt Berry granted commission in the R.F.C. proceeds to England for training. Patrol & maintenance of lines. Salvaging wire. Weather fine
>
> **5** — Horse, Harness & Transport Inspection by Maj Grey of the Gen Staff Traffic in Signal Office increasing over 1300 messages & despatches handled during the last twenty four hours. Enemy fairly quiet. Weather fine
>
> **6** — Church Parade at 9.30 A.M. Usual routine patrol & maintenance of lines. Business handled in Signal Office 1347 messages & despatches
>
> **7** — Conference at Div Hqrs of Bde Intelligence & Div Intelligence and Bde & Div Signal Officers regarding the installing of O.P. telephone exchanges in the forward areas. Business handled 1425. Other routine as usual. Weather fine
>
> [Signed, bottom right:] *A. Leavitt Maj*

### Page 5 — 8–11 January 1918

> **Bracquemont, Jan 8** — Patrol & maintenance of lines. Arrangements made for the training of Battalion signal personnel at [uncertain — "Cant"?] 3rd Bde Div Reinforcement Camp the classes to open on the 13th inst and to be for continued indefinitely. Course of three weeks. Class fifty strong. Three instructors.
>
> **9** — Arranging for lines for new Div O.P. exchanges. When these are installed all O.Ps on the whole Div front will be in close touch with one another. Installing a system of communications for the Heavy & Medium Trench Mortar Batteries. Traffic in Signal Office still above normal 1523 messages & despatches handled
>
> **10** — Lt Barber of No 3 Section burying cable to H.17 65. This is a [word crossed out] tee off the main route & is to provide a safe means of communication for Observing Officers. Eight Hundred yards to be put in. Gas Helmet inspection and drill. Usual routine
>
> **11** — Patrol & maintenance of lines and usual signal routine
>
> [Signed, bottom right:] *A. Leavitt Maj*

### Page 6 — 12–15 January 1918

> **Bracquemont, Jan 12** — Horse, harness & transport inspection by the S.S.O 3rd Cdn Div. Patrol & maintenance of lines, salvaging cable, burying cable and usual routine
>
> **13** — Signalling class opened. Church parade at 9.30. Visit from Col Booth of the U.S. Forces for the purpose of gaining information re the establishment administration etc of a Signal Coy
>
> **14** — No 432850 Sgt Bennett H.M awarded the D.C.M for gallantry and consistent good work. Usual routine.
>
> **15** — Conference at Corps Hqrs with A.D. Sigs & O.C's Sig Companies subjects improvement in present system of communications, method of maintaining communication during an advance, precautions to be taken in the event of a retirement, better system of training Signal personnel, postings & promotions of Officers. Work on buried delayed on account of both snow & rain. Weather very wet
>
> [Signed, bottom right:] *A. Leavitt Maj*

### Page 7 — 16–21 January 1918

> **Bracquemont, Jan 16** — Patrol, maintenance, & usual routine.
>
> **17** — Patrol, maintenance & usual routine
>
> **18** — Progress on new buried cable route very slow as owing to the bad condition of trenches & other work to be done only very small working parties are available. Gas Helmet drill
>
> **19** — Inspection of horses & harness by Maj Gen Lipsett. No 2 Section 7th Bde relieved by Bde Section of the 4th Cdn Div Sigs.
>
> **20** — Church parade 9.30 A.M. Visited visual class at Ferfay. Class doing well but handicapped somewhat owing to lack of proper & sufficient equipment
>
> **21** — Snow precautions still on so [uncertain — "starts"?] to move stores etc by team to rest area. Officers of 1st Sigs. shown over area.
>
> [Signed, bottom right:] *A. Leavitt Maj*

### Page 8 — 22–27 January 1918

> **Bracquemont, Jan 22** — All arrangements complete for hand over to the 1st Signals. One officer relief from 1st Sigs arrived, and One complete relief from this company sent to new area.
>
> **Auchel, 23** — Relief completed at 10. A.M. Office opened at New Div Hqrs Auchel at the same hour. Transport reached new area at 2.30 P.M. remainder of day spent in seeing to horses & getting men in quarters
>
> **24** — Company inspection & cleaning up transport. Arrangements made for carrying training while in rest. Training of Battalion & Battery signallers being under the supervision of the Bde Sig Officers concerned. Classes in Wireless, Power Buzzer, Visual, Sounder & Buzzer work.
>
> **25** — Training continued. Visual class at L.C.R. [uncertain] Ferfay visited Class making good progress. Gas Helmet drill
>
> **26** — Horse, harness, & transport inspection. Training continued
>
> **27** — Church parade 9.30 A.M. Stable & Office duties remainder of day observed as holiday
>
> [Signed, bottom right:] *A. Leavitt Maj*

### Page 9 — 28–31 January 1918

> **Auchel, Jan 28** — All classes of instruction going in full swing. Repairing of horse lines
>
> **29** — Training continued. Repair of horse lines. Gas Helmet drill
>
> **30** — Training and repair of Horse lines Gas Helmet drill. Visual course at C.C.R.C Ferfay inspected & tested. Weather fine
>
> **31** — Usual routine of training etc.
>
> [Signed, bottom right:] *A. Leavitt Maj*

## Open questions / uncertain items (January 1918)
- 5 January: "Maj Grey of the Gen Staff" — "Grey" read as written.
- 8 January: "at [uncertain — 'Cant'?] 3rd Bde Div Reinforcement Camp" — the word before "3rd Bde" is unclear; it may be an abbreviation for a cantonment or camp name.
- 10 January: "burying cable to H.17 65." — read as written; "H.17 65" is presumably a map reference. One word before "tee" is crossed out and unreadable.
- 15 January: "Work on buried delayed" — read as written; presumably "work on buried [cable]".
- 19 January: "No 2 Section 7th Bde relieved by Bde Section of the 4th Cdn Div Sigs." — read as written.
- 21 January: "so [uncertain — 'starts'?] to move stores etc by team to rest area" — the verb is unreadable.
- 25 January: "Visual class at L.C.R. [uncertain] Ferfay visited" — the camp abbreviation is unclear; it may read "C.C.R.C." as on 30 January ("Visual course at C.C.R.C Ferfay inspected & tested") and as written clearly on 4 February ("Lt Gibson posted to C.C.R.C Ferfay").
- The signature on each sheet reads "A. Leavitt Maj"; the first initial is read as "A" (the Remarks-column initials are "A.L.").
- Names read as: Lt H. H. Craig; No 304611 Sgt Berry; Maj Grey; Lt Barber; Col Booth (U.S. Forces); No 432850 Sgt Bennett H.M.; Maj Gen Lipsett.
- No casualties are recorded in January 1918. The division was relieved by the 1st Canadian Division's signals on 22–23 January and the company moved from Bracquemont to Auchel (rest area) for training.
