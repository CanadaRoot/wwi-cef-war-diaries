# War Diary — 3rd Canadian Divisional Signal Company — January 1917

- **Unit:** 3rd Canadian Divisional Signal Company
- **Month:** January 1917 (Volume 14) — this volume covers 1 to 31 January 1917
- **Source:** Library and Archives Canada, Collection Search Item ID 2004905 (RG 9 III-D-3, vol. 5005, microfilm reel T-10853, file 692; finding aid 9-52)
- **Pages:** January 1917, p. 1 – p. 4 (image files page-0075 – page-0078 of the 307-page diary)
- **January 1917 — COMPLETE** (pages 75–78 of 307): cover, contents, diary text 1–31 January, no appendices. Transcribed 2026-10-26.

## Transcription conventions
Original spelling, punctuation and abbreviations kept. Handwritten diary text throughout (Army Form C. 2118, "WAR DIARY or INTELLIGENCE SUMMARY"). `[uncertain]` marks words I could not read with confidence — never guessed. No map coordinates are given in the diary; places are recorded as written. Ditto marks ("do" / „) are not repeated verbatim; the entry they repeat is named. "Jany" = January; "Sub exchange" = sub-exchange (telephone exchange). A repeated mark in the Remarks column, read as "J.A." [uncertain] — apparently the diarist's initials — appears against most entries; it is noted here rather than repeated against every entry.

## Open questions / uncertain items (January 1917)
- The repeated mark in the Remarks column is read as "J.A." [uncertain] — apparently the diarist's initials.
- 22 January: "As above" refers to the "Usual Office Routine and patrol of Lines" entry.
- 26–27 January: the "New Report Centre" is written as shown; the lines were cut over to it at Mont St Eloy at 1 P.M. on 27 January.
- No casualties are recorded in January 1917.
