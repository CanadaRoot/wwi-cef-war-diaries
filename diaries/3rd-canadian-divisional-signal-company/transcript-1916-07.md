# War Diary — 3rd Canadian Divisional Signal Company — July 1916

- **Unit:** 3rd Canadian Divisional Signal Company
- **Month:** July 1916 (Volume 8)
- **Source:** Library and Archives Canada, Collection Search Item ID 2004905 (RG 9 III-D-3, vol. 5005, microfilm reel T-10853, file 692; finding aid 9-52)
- **Pages:** July 1916, p. 1 – p. 4 (image files page-0045 – page-0048)
- **July 1916 — COMPLETE** (pages 1–4): cover, contents, diary text 1–31 July, no appendices. Transcribed 2026-10-26.

## Transcription conventions
Original spelling, punctuation and abbreviations kept. Handwritten diary text throughout (Army Form C. 2118, "WAR DIARY or INTELLIGENCE SUMMARY"). `[uncertain]` marks words I could not read with confidence — never guessed. No map coordinates are given in the diary; places are recorded as written. The July entries are written in a much smaller, denser hand than May–June, with several days grouped into single entries; the date column groups are given as written. The diarist's spellings "RENINGHELST", "Zillebeke", "STEENVOORDE" and "Brandhoek" are kept as written. "H.Q." = Headquarters; "Bde." = Brigade; "Infy." = Infantry; "O.C." = Officer Commanding. A repeated initialled mark in the Remarks column, read as "J.E.P." [uncertain], appears against most entries; it is noted here rather than repeated against every entry. The diary's own heading on the diary sheets reads "3rd Canadian Division Signal Coy."

## Pages 3–4 — Diary text (1–31 July 1916)

Army Form C. 2118, "WAR DIARY or INTELLIGENCE SUMMARY". Heading on each sheet: "3rd Canadian Division Signal Coy." Each sheet is marked "Original" at the top. Place throughout: RENINGHELST.

### Page 3 — 1–19 July 1916

> **RENINGHELST, July 1st** — Nothing to report. Usual routine of patrolling lines etc.
>
> **2nd–8th** — Buried cable towards Zillebeke continued with party from Corps Cavalry. Training in cable work and visual signalling.
>
> **9th** — Inspection of Headquarters and No. 1 Section wagons and lines, by Gen Staff.
>
> **10th–12th** — Continued burying of Zillebeke branch. Shelling fairly heavy during first of evening.
>
> **13th** — Began burial work forward of Dormy House toward Sanctuary Wood.
>
> **14th–16th** — Work on buried route through Dormy House Maple Lodge North House.
>
> **17th** — Work on buried cable to Zillebeke Bund completed. Very little reply to our firing. 3rd Canadian Divisional Artillery arrived.
>
> **18th** — Buried lines to Sanctuary Wood completed. All battalions working on same. Lines good. Buried lateral between Zillebeke Bund and Railway Dugouts commenced, Corps Cavalry employed on work.
>
> **19th** — Inspected Divisional Signal School at Steenvoorde. Classes making good progress. Started to remodel office of Left Group Artillery.

### Page 4 — 20–31 July 1916

> **RENINGHELST, July 20th** — Erected new Signal Office near Brandhoek in anticipation of move. Very quiet on our front.
>
> **21st** — Portion of old buried line through Ypres showing heavy earth. Working party sent out. Work completed on Left Group Artillery Office.
>
> **22nd** — Wiring commenced on new Signal Office at Brandhoek. Replaced cable in Ypres, as old cable was too badly damaged to repair. Heavy shelling on both sides during evening.
>
> **23rd** — Installed new office at Zillebeke Bund for Centre Group Artillery.
>
> **24th** — Installed new office at Zillebeke Bund for Right Group Artillery. Kite balloon shelled, some lines on permanent air line route broken.
>
> **25th** — Very Quiet. Nothing to report.
>
> **26th–28th** — Work on wiring of new office continued, telephone exchange and switchboard installed. Communications good.
>
> **29th** — Repaired cable near Lille Gate. Day quiet. Lines O.K.
>
> **30th** — Line laid to 3rd Canadian Divisional Ammunition Column. Weather very hot.
>
> **31st** — Fault on buried cable, working party out. Weather very hot.
>
> [Signed, bottom right, partly cut off at the page edge:] *J. E. Powers Major, O.C. 3rd Can. Div. Sig. Coy.* [uncertain — signature partly cut off]

## Open questions / uncertain items (July 1916)
- The date column on page 3 groups days as "2nd 6 8th", "10th & 12", "14th & 16th"; they are transcribed as ranges (2nd–8th, 10th–12th, 14th–16th). The 26th–28th group on page 4 is written "26th 28th".
- 9 July: "Inspection of Headquarters and No. 1 Section wagons and lines, by Gen Staff." — "Gen Staff" read as written; it likely means General Staff.
- 10th–12th: "Shelling fairly heavy during first of evening." — read as written.
- 14th–16th: "Work on buried route through Dormy House Maple Lodge North House." — read as written; "North House" is the diarist's place name as written.
- 21 July: "Portion of old buried line through Ypres showing heavy earth." — read as written; the sense is unclear (the line may have been exposed by shelling).
- The signature at the foot of page 4 is partly cut off at the page edge; it is read as "J. E. Powers Major, O.C. 3rd Can. Div. Sig. Coy." [uncertain].
- The repeated mark in the Remarks column is read as "J.E.P." [uncertain] — apparently the diarist's initials; it matches the signature "J. E. Powers".
- No casualties are recorded in this volume; no map coordinates are given.
- No personal names are given in this volume other than the signature.
