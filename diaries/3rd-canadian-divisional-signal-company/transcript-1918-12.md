## Transcription conventions
Original spelling, punctuation and abbreviations kept. Diary text is typewritten throughout (Army Form C. 2118, "WAR DIARY or INTELLIGENCE SUMMARY"). `[uncertain]` marks words I could not read with confidence — never guessed. No map coordinates are given in the diary; places are recorded as written. "O.C." = Officer Commanding; "O.Rs." = Other Ranks; "C.F.A." = Canadian Field Artillery; "C.I.B." = Canadian Infantry Brigade; "D.R.L.S." / "DRLS" = Despatch Rider Letter Service; "C.W." = Continuous Wave (wireless); "C.M.R." = Canadian Mounted Rifles; "M.G." = Machine Gun; "C.S.R.D." = Canadian Signal Reinforcement Depot [uncertain — expansion not given in the diary]; "C.C.R.C." = Canadian Corps Reinforcement Camp [uncertain — expansion not given in the diary]; "A.O.W." = Absent Without Leave [uncertain — expansion not given in the diary]; "G.O.C." = General Officer Commanding; "R.A." = Royal Artillery. A handwritten initialled mark, read as "K.H.M." [uncertain], appears in the Remarks column against most entries; it is noted here rather than repeated against every entry. Signatures read as "K. M. Campbell" (Major K. M. Campbell, O.C.).

### Page 7 — 13–15 December 1918

> **NIVELLES, Dec. 13th** — Office Traffic: No. 1, Section 136 DRLS 97; No. 2, 5, DRLS 100; No. 3; DRLS 70; No. 4, DRLS 89;
>
> Soldiers from the 7th C.I.B., Battalions and the 3rd M.G., Battalion parades the streets about 200 strong protesting against certain conditions, the chief of which was the carrying of packs, the long marches, bad and insufficient food. When the party reached Brigade HQ. the G.O.C., asked for spokesmen and four soldiers presented themselves to interview him. The men after declaring they would not go on parade to-morrow on existing conditions, dispersed to their billets.
>
> Division Headquarters moved to NIVELLES and the LA LOUVIERE report centre was brought in during the evening, communication with IV Corps was maintained by C.W. Wireless, and to other units by D.R. and runners, no lines being laid.
>
> Honours and Awards: 130291 Spr. New, D.A., of Canadian Signal Pool C.C.R.C., and attached to wireless section awarded the Military Medal.
>
> Lieut. H. Pryde, and 1 O.R., rejoined from leave.
>
> **14th** — Office Traffic: No. 1, Section 143; DRLS 136; No. 2, DRLS 110. No. 3, DRLS 120. No. 4, 3; DRLS 100.
>
> A mass meeting of soldiers of the 7th C. I. Brigade units was held on the square and mutinous speeches delivered. Two guardrooms were forced and prisoners released, some of whom returned to their Orderly Rooms, refusing to join the mob. The men declared they would not march out of the Town to-morrow morning, nor permit any soldier to march out carrying a pack. No. 2, Section did not participate in the mutiny, very few men even witnessed the events. The section obtained permission to choose its own route and time of departure for marching off to-morrow, carrying packs as usual.
>
> All units remained in present billets except 9th Bde. CFA. which moved from SENEFFE to JEMAPPE.
>
> 10 O.Rs. granted 14 days leave to U.K.
>
> A call for 1,000 coal miners to work in Nova Scotia mines has been made, and names of miners are being submitted to-day.
>
> **15th** — Office Traffic: No. 1, Section 134; DRLS. 151; No. 2, DRLS 68; No. 3, 76; No. 4, 6; DRLS, 57;
>
> No. 2, Section and transport left NIVELLES at 0730 hours by a circuitous route to avoid the main Square and the possibility of interference, taking again WATERLOO road [uncertain — word struck through] the Town.
