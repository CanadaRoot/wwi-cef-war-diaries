# War Diary — 3rd Canadian Divisional Signal Company — December 1917

- **Unit:** 3rd Canadian Divisional Signal Company
- **Month:** December 1917 (Volume 25) — 1 to 31 December 1917
- **Source:** Library and Archives Canada, Collection Search Item ID 2004905 (RG 9 III-D-3, vol. 5005, microfilm reel T-10853, file 692; finding aid 9-52)
- **Pages:** December 1917, p. 1 – p. 8 (image files page-0160 – page-0167 of the diary)
- **December 1917 — COMPLETE** (pages 160–167): cover, contents, title page, diary text 1–31 December, no appendices. Transcribed 2026-10-26.

## Transcription conventions
Original spelling, punctuation and abbreviations kept. Handwritten diary text throughout (Army Form C. 2118, "WAR DIARY or INTELLIGENCE SUMMARY"). `[uncertain]` marks words I could not read with confidence — never guessed. No map coordinates are given in the diary; places are recorded as written. "A.L." in the Remarks column is the initials of the officer commanding, Major A. Leavitt (signature "A. Leavitt Maj" at the foot of each sheet); it is noted here rather than repeated against every entry. "H.Q." = Headquarters; "Bde." = Brigade; "Cdn" = Canadian; "Div" = Division; "Sig Coy" = Signal Company; "Imp" = Imperial; "A/Capt" = Acting Captain; "G.O.C." = General Officer Commanding. The diary's own heading on the diary sheets reads "3rd CANADIAN Div Sig Coy. CAN SIGNALS".

## Pages 4–8 — Diary text (1–31 December 1917)

Army Form C. 2118, "WAR DIARY or INTELLIGENCE SUMMARY". Heading on each sheet: "3rd CANADIAN Div Sig Coy. CAN SIGNALS".

### Page 4 — 1–8 December 1917

> **Norrent Fontes, Dec 1st** — Nos 109329 Spr Kitchen T 431126 Spr Fleming T. A. 109396 Spr Davis C. J. awarded Military Medal and Sgt BALL C.W. awarded bar to Military Medal for gallant conduct in the field. During the recent engagements in the YPRES sector the above mentioned men laid & maintained telephone lines for five days under constant & extremely heavy shell fire

>

> **6** — Usual Routine & training

### Page 6 — 20–22 December 1917

> **Norrent Fontes, Dec 20** — Lt S. M. Smith promoted to the rank of A/Capt and reports to the 4th Div Sig Coy to act as officer in charge of Artillery Signals. Capt J. H. [uncertain] to hospital suffering from trench mouth. Lt Barker, Lt Gibson and Lt Stenhouse having finished course at Canadian Corps School report to this unit for duty. Operation Order for the relief of the 2nd Cdn Div by the 3rd Cdn Div cancelled
>
> **21** — Orders received that 3rd Cdn Div will relieve the 11th Imperial Division relief to be complete 10 A.M. 23rd Dec. Party of linemen sent forward to new area. Inspection of communications in new area preparatory to relief.
>
> **22nd** — Complete office relief for Report Centre and Div Hqrs Sig Office sent forward to be in readiness to take over the following day. Lt Gibson with Wireless personnel & equipment relieve the Wireless of the 11th Imp Division All in readiness for the move

### Page 7 — 23–26 December 1917

> **Bracquemont, Dec 23** — Sig Office closed at NORRENT FONTES at 10 AM and opened at BRACQUEMONT at the same hour. All instruments, switchboards, etc changed and complete relief of the 11th Div Sigs effected at 9 A.M. Lines and all communications in good working order
>
> **24** — No 2 Section 3rd Sig Coy relieve No 4 Section of the 1st Sig Coy the 3rd Cdn Div taking over a portion of the line held by the 1st Cdn Div. Patrol and maintenance of lines. Repair of horse lines. Sig Office business above normal over 1700 messages and despatches being handled.
>
> **25th** — Patrol & maintenance of lines. Sig Office business normal between 900 and 1000 messages & despatches handled. The day as far as possible observed as a holiday
>
> **26** — Patrol & maintenance of lines. Inspection of communications in the forward area of the 8th Bde Sector. Communications good as practically all lines are buried. Enemy very active with Trench Mortars. Office business normal. Weather fine and cold

### Page 8 — 27–31 December 1917

> **Bracquemont, Dec 27** — The usual Signal routine of patrolling & maintenance of lines, Gas Helmet drill & exercise of horses etc. Enemy very quiet. Weather fine and cold
>
> **28** — Usual routine. Visited 9th Bde and went over lines as far as Battalion Hqrs. Everything in good working order Working party detailed to salvage old lines not in use in forward area. Enemy quiet Weather clear & cold
>
> **29** — Usual routine. Visited 7th Bde & Ralstons Group. Enemy quiet
>
> **30** — Church parade 9 A.M. Usual office routine
>
> **31** — Horse, Harness & Transport inspection Gas Helmet drill. Lt Craig sick to Hospital. Lt Stenhouse reports to Cdn Ralstons Group to replace Lt Craig. Enemy quiet. Weather fine & cool
>
> [Signed, bottom right:] *A. Leavitt Maj*

## Open questions / uncertain items (December 1917)
- 11 December: no separate entry — the date "11" appears in the Date column, but the 10 December entry runs over the 11th row and the next entry is dated 12 December.
- 20 December: the name after "Capt J. H." is unreadable [uncertain] — it begins with "L" and the officer went to hospital suffering from trench mouth.
- 3 December: Cpl Flanigan's number is read as 113014 here, but the same man is recorded on 28 October with the number 113914 — one of the two readings must be wrong; both are given as written.
- 23 December: the place is written "BRACQUEMONT" here (and on 5 September); the same place is written "Braquemont" on 21 August.
- Casualties in December: none recorded; Lt Craig was sick to hospital on 31 December.
- Military Medals in December: Spr Kitchen T (No 109329), Spr Fleming T. A. (No 431126), Spr Davis C. J. (No 109396) and a bar to the Military Medal for Sgt Ball C. W. (1 December); Cpl Flanigan J. E. (No 113014) (3 December).
- Names read as: Spr Kitchen T; Spr Fleming T. A.; Spr Davis C. J.; Sgt Ball C. W.; Cpl Flanigan J. E.; Lt S. M. Smith; Lt Barker; Lt Gibson; Lt Stenhouse; Lt McPherson; Lt Craig.
- No map coordinates are given in the diary.
