# War Diary — 3rd Canadian Divisional Signal Company — February 1917

- **Unit:** 3rd Canadian Divisional Signal Company
- **Month:** February 1917 (Volume 15) — this volume covers 1 to 28 February 1917
- **Source:** Library and Archives Canada, Collection Search Item ID 2004905 (RG 9 III-D-3, vol. 5005, microfilm reel T-10853, file 692; finding aid 9-52)
- **Pages:** February 1917, p. 1 – p. 5 (image files page-0079 – page-0083 of the 307-page diary)
- **February 1917 — COMPLETE** (pages 79–83 of 307): cover, contents, diary text 1–28 February, no appendices. Transcribed 2026-10-26.

## Transcription conventions
Original spelling, punctuation and abbreviations kept. Handwritten diary text throughout (Army Form C. 2118, "WAR DIARY or INTELLIGENCE SUMMARY"). `[uncertain]` marks words I could not read with confidence — never guessed. No map coordinates are given in the diary; places are recorded as written. Ditto marks ("do" / „) are not repeated verbatim; the entry they repeat is named. "Hqrs" = Headquarters; "cwt" = hundredweight; "Inf Bde" = Infantry Brigade; "A.T." as written; "O.C." = Officer Commanding. A repeated mark in the Remarks column, read as "A.L." — the initials of the signing officer — appears against most entries; it is noted here rather than repeated against every entry.

## Pages 3–5 — Diary text (1–28 February 1917)

Army Form C. 2118, "WAR DIARY or INTELLIGENCE SUMMARY"; "INTELLIGENCE SUMMARY" is struck through on all three sheets. Each sheet carries the oval unit stamp "3rd CANADIAN DIVISION / SIGNAL COMPANY" — No. 28, Date 2/17. The sheets are headed "Original". The hand changes from the October–January sheets: these are signed by the Officer Commanding, not the Adjutant.

### Page 3 — 1–12 February 1917

> **Hermaville, Feb 1st** — Office at Hermaville closed at 10 A.M and new office opened at MT ST Eloy [uncertain — "sometime"?]
>
> **MT. ST. Eloy, 2nd** — New Hqrs and office in good running order. 3 cwt lorry run into by engine at Acq level crossing, No. 153947 Pte [uncertain — "Coom"?] from A.T. attached from 43 Bn Killed the two lorry drivers wounded.
>
> **3rd** — Usual routine. Preparations made for burying cable at new telephone exchange and telegraph office
>
> **4th** — Parties secured from 9th and 8th Canadian Inf Bde for buried cable work. Lead covered cable of 7 and 4 pairs being used.
>
> **5th** — Usual routine. Good progress with buried cable made
>
> **6th–12th** — [ditto of 5 February, repeated daily]
>
> [Signed, bottom right:] *A. [uncertain — "Levitt"?] Capt / O.C. 3rd Canadian Div Sig Coy*

### Page 4 — 13–20 February 1917

> **MT. ST. Eloy, Feb 13th** — During the move of Brigades working parties for cable burying not available. Men from company put at the work and good progress made.
>
> **14th** — Party from company finished cable burying. All lines tested and working perfectly
>
> **Bruay, 15th** — Hqrs moved from MT. ST. Eloy to BRUAY. 3rd Canadian Div Sig Coy relieved by 2nd Canadian Div Sig Coy. Relief completed at 10 A.M. New office opened at Bruay at 10 A.M.
>
> **16th** — Usual routine. Classes in telegraphy and visual signalling opened also Officers class in instrument repairing
>
> **17th** — Usual routine. Class of instruction opened on cable jointing and cable maintenance and repair
>
> **18th** — Church parade. Usual office routine. Half holiday afternoon
>
> **19th** — Usual office routine. Competition between cable detachments on cable laying. Classes on visual, instrument and cable being carried on
>
> **20th** — Usual office routine. Rifle + Gas Helmet inspection Good progress in instructional work
>
> [Signed, bottom right:] *A. [uncertain — "Levitt"?] Capt / O.C. 3rd Canadian Div Sig Coy*

## Open questions / uncertain items (February 1917)
- 1 February: the word after "MT ST Eloy" reads "sometime" [uncertain] — it may instead read "same time".
- 2 February: the casualty is No. 153947 Pte [uncertain — "Coom"?] from A.T., attached from 43 Bn — killed when a 3 cwt lorry was run into by an engine at Acq level crossing; the two lorry drivers were wounded. The surname is not legible with confidence and is not guessed.
- 24 February: "buried cable [uncertain — 'proposal'?] work" — the word reads as "proposal" but is not certain.
- 27 February: the word before "being carried on" reads as "Courses" [uncertain] — it may instead read "Classes".
- 28 February: "Good progress in visual" ends the entry as written (presumably "visual signalling" abbreviated).
- The signing officer's surname is read as "Levitt" [uncertain] — the signature is "A. [Levitt?] Capt, O.C. 3rd Canadian Div Sig Coy"; the Remarks-column mark "A.L." matches these initials.
- Casualties in February 1917: 1 killed (Pte [uncertain], No. 153947, attached from 43rd Battalion), 2 lorry drivers wounded (names not given).
