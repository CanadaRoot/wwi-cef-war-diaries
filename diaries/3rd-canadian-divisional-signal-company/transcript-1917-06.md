- **Unit:** 3rd Canadian Divisional Signal Company
- **Month:** June 1917 (Volume 19) — diary covers 1–30 June 1917
- **Source:** Library and Archives Canada, Collection Search Item ID 2004905 (RG 9 III-D-3, vol. 5005, microfilm reel T-10853, file 692; finding aid 9-52)
- **Pages:** June 1917, p. 1 – p. 9 (image files page-0102 – page-0110 of the diary)
- **June 1917 — INCOMPLETE as scanned** (pages 1–9): cover, contents, title page, diary text 6–30 June. The diary text DOES run to 30 June — sheet 8 ends with the 30 June entry — but the front of diary sheet 3 (1–5 June) is missing: p. 4 is only the reverse of that sheet, photographed from the back, so 1–5 June survive only as faint show-through (see Page 4). Transcribed 2026-10-26.
- **Note on the missing 10th June page:** the LAC manifest's 308th item ("June 1917", ecopy e011777720) is a DEAD LINK — the archive returns HTTP 404 "No record found for ID: e011777720", so no 10th June page could be fetched. No June p. 10 has been invented.

## Transcription conventions
Original spelling, punctuation and abbreviations kept. Handwritten diary text throughout (Army Form C. 2118, "WAR DIARY or INTELLIGENCE SUMMARY"). `[uncertain]` marks words I could not read with confidence — never guessed. No map coordinates are given in the diary; places are recorded as written. "Sig Office" = Signal Office; "H.Q." = Headquarters; "H.Qrs." = Headquarters; "Adv." / "Advd" = Advanced; "Bde" = Brigade; "Bn" = Battalion; "Ent" = Entrenching; "C.F.A." = Canadian Field Artillery; "A.F.A." = as written (179th A.F.A. Bde); "O.C." = Officer Commanding; "Sgt" = Sergeant; "Cpl" = Corporal; "Lt" = Lieutenant; "Capt" = Captain; "Maj" = Major; "recd" = received; "viz" = namely. A repeated mark, read as "A.C.", appears in the Remarks column against most entries — apparently the diarist's initials; it is noted here rather than repeated against every entry. The diarist's signature at the foot of each sheet reads "A. Leavitt Maj." — the July 15 entry ("Maj Leavitt having gone on leave to England") confirms the surname Leavitt, resolving the May transcript's tentative "Witt" reading.

## Pages 5–9 — Diary text (6–30 June 1917)

Army Form C. 2118, "WAR DIARY or INTELLIGENCE SUMMARY". Heading on each sheet: "3rd Canadian Divisional Signal Coy Cdn Sig Corps". Place throughout: Fort George.

### Page 5 — 6–10 June 1917 (diary sheet 4)

> **Fort George, June 6th** — Owing to Bde Relief no working party available. Work continued re-stringing Goodman Tunnel. Work commenced on air line to replace rear lines in the buried. Pole line of 12 pairs being put up. Twelve reinforcements arrive from Base. Lt Nicholson of the 4th Entrenching Batt attached to No 2 Section 7th Cdn Inf Bde for duty and instruction.
>
> **7th** — No party available for buried work. Work in Goodman and with air line continued. New lateral laid to 6th Cdn Inf Bde on our left. Enemy using a large number of Gas Shells.
>
> **8th** — A raid by 7th Cdn Inf Bde in conjunction with the 1st Cdn Div and 1st Corps made work in burying impossible. Party of 4 linesmen under Sgt Barber laid and maintained lines to enemy second line during raid. Air line work continued as well as work in Goodman. 28 reinforcements from Base.
>
> **9th** — Buried cable again started. Other work continued. Rear portion of air line completed. Arranged Special events for Arty Telephone Wagon in coming Div Sports. All six Arty Bdes entering viz 8th 9th 10th 11th and 3rd C.F.A and 179th A.F.A. Bde.
>
> **10th** — Cable burying continued and other work carried on with. Pole line forward of Adv Div Hqrs commenced. Enemy fairly quiet. Usual routine.
>
> [Signed, bottom right:] *A. Leavitt Maj.*

### Page 6 — 11–15 June 1917 (diary sheet 5)

> **Fort George, June 11th** — All work being carried on with and good progress made. 3rd Arty Bde C.F.A. rejoin their own Div. Transfer of personnel horses and equipment to Signals from 10th Arty Bde C.F.A. now complete. Lt Craig taken on as Sig officer of the 10th Arty Bde.
>
> **12th** — Relief of 7th Cdn Inf Bde by 8th Cdn Inf Bde. No party for work on buried cable available. Other work carried on with. Transfer of personnel, horses & equipment of 9th Arty Bde C.F.A. to Div Signals completed. No Signal officer available and section placed in charge of Cpl Ryan.
>
> **13th** — Bde relief no working party. Forward air line completed. Usual routine.
>
> **14th** — No party available. Div Horse Show. Lt Uglow of 9th Bde taking first and Third in officers charger class. 10th Arty Bde C.F.A. take first in Arty Telephone wagon tourney and 179th A.F.A second. Telephone and telegraph installed at Show grounds and results forwarded at once to forward Battalions & units in the line.
>
> **15th** — Lt C. A. McCreery [uncertain] reports for duty returning from Sick leave in Eng. Working party of 50 for buried cable work. All other work progressing favourably. Lt Uglow seriously wounded by sniper while looking over new route for extension of buried cable system
>
> [Signed, bottom right:] *A. Leavitt Maj.*

### Page 7 — 16–23 June 1917 (diary sheet 6)

> **Fort George, June 16th** — Only small working party for cable burying. Work commenced on installing new Sig Office at Adv Div Hqrs. Other work [one word struck through] of air line construction & rewiring tunnel progressing. Lt Uglow died of wounds at No 7 Casualty Clearing Station
>
> **17th** — Lt Uglow buried at Noeux le Mines. All other work in hand being carried on satisfactorily
>
> **18th** — Bde Relief no working party available. All other work continued. Usual routine
>
> **19th** — Working party obtained & work on bury continued. Canadian Corps Horse Show on. One cable wagon from No 1 Section entered for the competition. Usual routine
>
> **20th** — All work in hand being carried on. Usual routine. Enemy quiet
>
> **21st** — Work of rewiring & installing new test rack in Goodman tunnel completed. All other work progressing favourably. Cable Detachment from No 1 Section win driving competition at Corps Horse Show. Enemy quiet
>
> **22nd** — Party from company salvaging wire in tunnels. Work on new office, buried cable & air line construction progressing. Usual routine. Enemy quiet.
>
> **23rd** — Buried cable work completed, tested and found in perfect shape. Party at work laddering D I wire for use of batteries in coming attack. Laddered [uncertain — "Laddered"?] lines one mile in length with a ladder every fifty feet to be supplied. Usual routine.
>
> [Signed, bottom right:] *A. Leavitt Maj.*

### Page 8 — 24–28 June 1917 (diary sheet 7)

> **Fort George, June 24** — Work of salvaging wire, air line construction, and fitting of new office continued. Div Visual Station, for work with Battalions and companies in the front line during the coming attack, established & a number of test messages received.
>
> **25th** — Forward air line completed all other work continued. Army Horse Show. One cable wagon entered. Enemy quiet. Usual routine
>
> **26th** — Fault on old buried cable system found, party at work on same. New office completed and cut over made at 3.15 P.M. without a delay of any kind occurring. Work of salvaging wire continued. Thirty five miles of wire already salvaged. Usual routine.
>
> **27** — Attack made by 9th Bde. Lt McGie [uncertain] Sig Officer of the 8th Bde remained in the line carrying on for the 9th Bde. All communication during the attack worked perfectly. Lt McFarlane [uncertain] 1st Div Sigs taken over the lines preparatory to the 1st Div taking over when this Div moves into rest area
>
> **28th** — Further attack made by 9th Bde all communication remaining intact. Div Visual Station receiving a number of important messages from the forward troops. All other work as usual
>
> [Signed, bottom right:] *A. Leavitt Maj.*

### Page 9 — 29–30 June 1917 (diary sheet 8)

> **Fort George, June 29** — Work of salvaging continued. Shelling at 9.15 A.M. cut air lines forward of Div Hqrs. Lines quickly repaired & communication established. Officer of 2nd Div Sigs shown over lines. Schedule of training and sports, to be used when company moves to rest area, drawn up. Usual routine.
>
> **30th** — Orders to move to rest area cancelled and new orders for 3rd Cdn Div to relieve 1st Cdn Div issued. Inspecting & taking over lines from 1st Div Sig Coy. 7th Bde taking over from 12th Bde
>
> [Signed, bottom right:] *A. Leavitt Maj.*

## Open questions / uncertain items (June 1917)
- 1–5 June: the front of diary sheet 3 is missing (dead LAC link e011777720, HTTP 404); only the fragments listed under Page 4 could be recovered from the reverse's show-through. The month's text otherwise runs complete to 30 June.
- 8 June: "Party of 4 linesmen under Sgt Barber laid and maintained lines to enemy second line during raid" — read as written; the lines were laid forward during the 7th Cdn Inf Bde raid.
- 9 June: "All six Arty Bdes entering viz 8th 9th 10th 11th and 3rd C.F.A and 179th A.F.A. Bde." — six brigades as listed.
- 14 June: "tourney" = tournament (Arty Telephone wagon tourney at the Div Horse Show).
- 15 June: "Lt C. A. McCreery" [uncertain] — surname may read "M'Creery".
- 16 June: one word struck through before "of air line construction" (apparently "being").
- 17 June: burial place read as "Noeux le Mines".
- 23 June: "Laddered [uncertain] lines one mile in length with a ladder every fifty feet to be supplied."
- 27 June: "Lt McGie" [uncertain] and "Lt McFarlane" [uncertain] — the same "McGie" appears again 3 July; the name may read "McGee".
- Names read as: Lt Nicholson (4th Entrenching Batt); Sgt Barber; Lt Craig (10th Arty Bde); Cpl Ryan; Lt Uglow (9th Bde — wounded 15 June, died of wounds 16 June, buried 17 June); Lt C. A. McCreery [uncertain]; Lt McGie [uncertain]; Lt McFarlane [uncertain]; Maj A. Leavitt (O.C.).
- Casualty: Lt Uglow — seriously wounded by a sniper 15 June; died of wounds at No 7 Casualty Clearing Station 16 June; buried at Noeux le Mines 17 June. No other casualties are recorded in June 1917.
- Places: Fort George throughout; Goodman Tunnel; Noeux le Mines (burial); No 7 Casualty Clearing Station.
