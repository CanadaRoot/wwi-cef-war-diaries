# War Diary — 3rd Canadian Divisional Signal Company — October 1916

- **Unit:** 3rd Canadian Divisional Signal Company
- **Month:** October 1916 (Volume 11) — this volume covers 1 to 31 October 1916
- **Source:** Library and Archives Canada, Collection Search Item ID 2004905 (RG 9 III-D-3, vol. 5005, microfilm reel T-10853, file 692; finding aid 9-52)
- **Pages:** October 1916, p. 1 – p. 4 (image files page-0063 – page-0066 of the 307-page diary)
- **October 1916 — COMPLETE** (pages 63–66 of 307): cover, contents, diary text 1–31 October, no appendices. Transcribed 2026-10-26.

## Transcription conventions
Original spelling, punctuation and abbreviations kept. Handwritten diary text throughout (Army Form C. 2118, "WAR DIARY or INTELLIGENCE SUMMARY"). `[uncertain]` marks words I could not read with confidence — never guessed. No map coordinates are given in the diary; places are recorded as written. Ditto marks ("do" / „) are not repeated verbatim; the entry they repeat is named. "H.Q." = Headquarters; "Sig" = Signal; "No 1 Section" = No. 1 Section; "Y.M.C.A." = Young Men's Christian Association; "A + Q Branch" = Adjutant-General's and Quartermaster-General's Branch. A repeated mark in the Remarks column, read as "J.A." [uncertain] — apparently the diarist's initials — appears against most entries; it is noted here rather than repeated against every entry.

## Pages 3–4 — Diary text (1–31 October 1916)

Army Form C. 2118, "WAR DIARY or INTELLIGENCE SUMMARY". Heading on the diary sheets: "3rd Canadian Division" (p. 3) and "3rd Canadian Division Signal Co" (p. 4); "INTELLIGENCE SUMMARY" is struck through on p. 4.

### Page 3 — 1–26 October 1916

> **Albert, 1 Oct** — Usual company routine Headquarters Tara Hill
>
> **2–6** — [ditto of 1 October] and preparing new hut for H.Q. Sig Office
>
> **7** — Signal Office moved to new Hut.
>
> **8–10** — Usual routine
>
> **11th** — Advanced headquarters and No 1 section moved to Warloy for rest. Signal office established
>
> **12–14** — Company resting Signal Office carrying on usual duties
>
> **15** — Company moved to Canaples at 9 A.M. and established signal office
>
> **16** — Company moved to Bernaville at 9 A.M. Signal office taken over at 1. P.M.
>
> **17–18–19** — Company at Rest routine as usual
>
> **20** — Company moved to Frohen-le-Grande established office in Civil Exchange
>
> **21** — Company moved to Le Gouroy established Signal Office in Chateau.
>
> **22** — Company moved to Villiers Chatel and established Signal Office in Y. M. C. A.
>
> **23–24–25** — Usual Routine
>
> **26** — H.Q. + No 1 Section moved to Divisional H.Q Hermaville Signal Office taken over from 60th Division (British), Office at Villiers Chatel continued for A + Q Branch
>
> [Signed, bottom right:] *J. Agnew [uncertain]*

### Page 4 — 27–31 October 1916

> **Field, 27–28** — Both Offices Carrying on
>
> **29** — Office at Villiers Chatel moved to Aubigny Signal Office taken over from 19th Corps (British)
>
> **30** — Signal Offices at Hermaville and Aubigny Carrying on
>
> **31** — Hermaville Office closed, and Signal Office Staff moved to Aubigny, Carrying on
>
> [Signed, bottom right:] *J. Agnew [uncertain]*

## Open questions / uncertain items (October 1916)
- The repeated mark in the Remarks column is read as "J.A." [uncertain] — apparently the diarist's initials.
- The signature at the foot of each diary sheet is read as "J. Agnew" [uncertain] — the same name appears more legibly as "J. Agnew, Lieut. & Adjt." on the November sheet.
- Place names as written: "Tara Hill", "Warloy", "Canaples", "Bernaville", "Frohen-le-Grande", "Le Gouroy", "Villiers Chatel", "Hermaville", "Aubigny". No map coordinates are given.
- No casualties are recorded in October 1916.
