# War Diary — 3rd Canadian Divisional Signal Company — February 1919

- **Unit:** 3rd Canadian Divisional Signal Company
- **Month:** February 1919 (Volume 39) — this volume covers 1 to 28 February 1919
- **Source:** Library and Archives Canada, Collection Search Item ID 2004905 (RG 9 III-D-3, vol. 5005, microfilm reel T-10853, file 692; finding aid 9-52)
- **Pages:** February 1919, p. 1 – p. 7 (image files page-0293 – page-0299 of the 307-page diary)
- **February 1919 — COMPLETE** (pages 293–299 of 307): cover, contents, diary text 1–28 February, no appendices. Transcribed 2026-10-26.

## Transcription conventions
Original spelling, punctuation and abbreviations kept. Diary text is typewritten throughout (Army Form C. 2118, "WAR DIARY or INTELLIGENCE SUMMARY"), except the title page (p. 3), which is handwritten. `[uncertain]` marks words I could not read with confidence — never guessed. No map coordinates are given in the diary; places are recorded as written. "O.C." = Officer Commanding; "O.Rs." = Other Ranks; "D.S." = Divisional Signals; "W/T" = Wireless Telegraphy; "BF" = [uncertain — expansion not given in the diary]; "O i/c" = Officer in Charge; "GHQ" = General Headquarters. A handwritten initialled mark, read as "K.H.M." [uncertain], appears in the Remarks column against most entries; it is noted here rather than repeated against every entry. Signatures read as "A. Leavitt Maj." (Major A. Leavitt, M.C.), with the final sheet also signed "L. H. Miller, Lieut., For O. Commanding".

## Pages 4–7 — Diary text (1–28 February 1919)

Army Form C. 2118, "WAR DIARY or INTELLIGENCE SUMMARY". Heading on the sheets: "3rd Canadian Divisional Signal Co". The diary sheets are headed "SHEET 2" (p. 5) and "SHEET THREE" (p. 6).

### Page 4 — 1–11 February 1919

> **Tournai, Feb 1st** — Leave: 1 OR granted 14 days leave to UK,. Strength increase 1 OR.
>
> **Feb 2nd** — Hospital admittances: 1 OR sick.
>
> **Feb 3rd** — Leave; 1 OR granted 14 days leave to UK.
>
> **Feb 4th** — Leave; 1 OR granted 14 days leave to UK.
>
> **Feb 5th** — Equipment decrease: Sunbeam 4 seater no 17708 struck off strength upon being transferred to 3rd Canadian Divisional Pool.
>
> Appointment: 111271 Spr KIRK, R.E [uncertain] appointed L/Cpl with pay vice L/Cpl Gertsch, H reduced to ranks as from 5-2-19.
>
> Leave; 2 OR's granted 14 days leave to UK.
>
> **Feb 6th** — NOTHING TO REPORT
>
> **Feb 7th** — Leave; 2 OR's granted 14 days special leave to UK.
>
> **Feb 8th** — NOTHING TO REPORT
>
> **Feb 9th** — NOTHING TO REPORT
>
> **Feb 10th** — Leave: 3 OR's granted 8 days leave to UK to report on expiration of same to O i/c Records GHQ for duty
>
> Hospital admittances: 2 OR's.
>
> **Feb 11th** — Strength decrease: equipment on charge: P.M.L. set on charge to this company struck off strength upon being transported to Signal Park RE ABBEVILLE [uncertain].
>
> W/T stations were closed down and dismantled equipment and personnel being brought in to Divs Hqrs. Three 50 watt BF sets working for the three Infantry Brigade Hqrs, a Wilson set with Mark III tuner at Artillery Hqrs RENAIX working with the Division D.S. at TOURNAI have handled all message traffic during the period since the arrival of the Division in this area Dec 29th last. This has meant from 200 to 270 messages daily passing through Divisional D.S during the working hours, 07.00 to 22.00 hours.
>
> [Signed, bottom right:] *A. Leavitt Maj.*

### Page 5 — 12–19 February 1919 (Sheet 2)

> **Feb 12th** — NOTHING TO REPORT.
>
> **Feb 13th** — Representative Company Headquarters composed of Major A.Leavitt, MC with three officers and 26 OR's left TOURNAI at 0830 hours with all Company office equipment and records, baggage etc, arriving at BAISIEUX at 0930 hours. Entrained at BAISIEUX at 16.00 hours, officers with baggage in one box car, OR's in another box car, plenty of straw, wood, coal, rations and room for all.
>
> **Feb 14th** — Lt W.D. Burden and Lt L.H.Miller with 72 OR's proceeded from TOURNAI to UK on 8 days leave on expiration of which to report to MILFORD CAMP.
>
> **Feb 15th** — Train journey to LE HAVRE lasting 39 hours arriving LE HAVRE 08.00 hours 15-2-19 very comfortable journey throughout. Marched to Canadian Embarkation Camp (approx 2 miles) arriving 09.30 hours, men billetted, fed, extra blankets drawn by 12.00 hours, everybody fairly comfortable. All nominal rolls handed in by 13.00 hours, advised by O i/c Canadians Branch Records Office at 15.00 hours that Company nominal rolls were the most complete in detail of any unit to date. Rained throughout the day.
>
> **Feb 16th** — Inspection parade at 09.30 hours. Medical inspection at 10.00 hours. Embarkation tickets filled in and arrangements completed for unit to embark. Bathing parade at 10.30 hours.
>
> **Feb 17th** — Camp routine duties carried out whilst awaiting orders to proceed to ENGLAND.
>
> [Two lines heavily blacked out / crossed out — unreadable; see Open questions.]
>
> **Feb 18th** — Left Camp No 1 LE HAVRE at 20.30 hours and marched to docks. Embarked at 22.30 hours personnel and baggage being complete.
>
> **Feb 19th** — Left LE HAVRE at 03.00 hours. Arrived WEYMOUTH ENGLAND 08.00 hours after a very cold but very smooth passage. Disembarked 09.30 hours and proceeded to unload baggage etc. Hot meal served to all ranks in PALM COURT close to docks. Left WEYMOUTH by train at 13.00 hours arriving MILFORD 18.00 hours. Marched to WITLEY CAMP arriving 19.00 hours. Hot meal and good quarters awaiting men.
>
> [Signed, bottom right:] *A. Leavitt Maj.*

### Page 6 — 20–25 February 1919 (Sheet Three)

> **Feb 20th** — Company Headquarters established and orderly room opened. Men left at RENAIX with horses reported back from leave.
>
> **Feb 21st** — Information received that all sections of Company should report to Hqrs, Infantry and Artillery sections reported at 16.00 hours. One OR returned from leave.
>
> **Feb 22nd** — O.C's inspection 10.00 hours. Documentation proceeded with. 12 OR's reported back from leave. Lieut PRYDE and 5 OR's admitted to Hospital (FLU).
>
> **Feb 23rd** — O.C's inspection 09.30 hours. Camp Muster Parade 10.00 hours. Parade of all men going on leave 11.00 hours. Documentation proceeded with. 2 OR's reported back from leave. 3 OR's admitted to hospital influenza.
>
> **Feb 24th** — Documentation proceeded with 14 OR's rejoined from leave granted in FRANCE. Lieut H.M.Bennett attached to Canadian Corps Camp BRAMSHOTT for duty. 44 OR's reported back from leave. Capt J.H. Leeson, MC and Lt L.H.Miller rejoined from leave.
>
> **Feb 25th** — Capt S.B.Iler and Lt A C Lee granted 8 days demobilization leave. 60 OR's granted 8 days demobilization leave.
>
> [Signed, bottom right:] *A. Leavitt Maj.*

### Page 7 — 26–28 February 1919

> **Witley Camp., Feb. 26** — Hospital admittances. 1 O.R. admitted to hospital.
>
> Hospital discharges. 1 O.R. rejoined from hospital.
>
> Leave. 16 O.R's rejoined from leave.
>
> **Feb. 27** — A daily parade for all ranks is being held at 10.00 hours for O.C's inspection.
>
> Documentation preparatory to demobilisation is being carried on as rapidly as possible, the difficulty being that so many men are on leave.
>
> **Feb. 28** — Leave. Lieut. R. B. Gibson M.C., M.M., and 3 O.R's granted 8 days demobilization leave. 1 O.R. granted 14 days special leave. 1 O.R. granted 3 days special leave. 13 O.R's rejoined from leave. 1 O.R. granted permission to marry.
>
> [Signed:] *L. H. Miller*
>
> *Lieut.,*
>
> *For O. Commanding, 3rd Can. Div. Signal Company.*
>
> [Also signed, bottom right:] *A. Leavitt Maj.*
