# War Diary — 3rd Canadian Divisional Signal Company — May 1917

- **Unit:** 3rd Canadian Divisional Signal Company
- **Month:** May 1917 (Volume 18) — diary covers 1–31 May 1917
- **Source:** Library and Archives Canada, Collection Search Item ID 2004905 (RG 9 III-D-3, vol. 5005, microfilm reel T-10853, file 692; finding aid 9-52)
- **Pages:** May 1917, p. 1 – p. 9 (image files page-0093 – page-0101 of the diary)
- **May 1917 — COMPLETE** (pages 1–9): cover, contents, a typed divider page, diary text 1–31 May, no appendices. Transcribed 2026-10-26.
- **Note on the page count:** the contents page says "Diary Text 6 Pages" but there are 7 diary image files (pp. 3–9). Page 3 is a typed divider ("ORIGINAL / WAR DIARY / MAY. 1917 / 3rd CANADIAN DIVISIONAL SIGNAL COY. / CONFIDENTIAL"); the 6 diary sheets are pp. 4–9.

## Transcription conventions
Original spelling, punctuation and abbreviations kept. Handwritten diary text throughout (Army Form C. 2118, "WAR DIARY or INTELLIGENCE SUMMARY"), except the typed divider on page 3. `[uncertain]` marks words I could not read with confidence — never guessed. "do." and ditto marks (".") = ditto, as written; where a ditto entry is expanded the expansion is noted. No map coordinates are given beyond trench-map references (e.g. "S. 30. C 1.9") as written. "Sig Office" = Signal Office; "H.Q." = Headquarters; "H.Qrs." = Headquarters; "Bde" = Brigade; "Bn" = Battalion; "Stn." = Station; "Div." = Division; "Adv." = Advanced; "S.P." = [uncertain]; "Spr" = Sapper; "Sgt" = Sergeant; "Lt" = Lieutenant; "Capt" = Captain. A repeated mark, read as "A.C.", appears in the Remarks column against most entries — apparently the diarist's initials; it is noted here rather than repeated against every entry. The diarist's signature at the foot of each sheet reads "A. C. [uncertain] Maj., 3rd Can. Div Signal Coy" — the surname is an illegible flourish (possibly "Witt") and is not guessed. A new hand takes over the diary from May 1917 (the March–April diarist signed "J. [uncertain] Capt.").

## Pages 4–9 — Diary text (1–31 May 1917)

Army Form C. 2118, "WAR DIARY or INTELLIGENCE SUMMARY". Heading on each sheet: "3rd Canadian Divisional Signal Coy".

### Page 4 — 1–4 May 1917

Place: Fort George.

> **Fort George, May 1** — Work of burying cable from 1st Bn H.Q. to Battalion & Company H.Qrs commenced. The Bde Sig Officer & the 7th Bde taking charge of the work. for which 150 men are supplied by the Bde in line. Twenty five of [uncertain] Commenced cable to be buried in order to provide communication for the Field and Heavy Artillery. Salvaging of cable in rear areas contd Usual routine.
>
> **2** — Work continued on bury. Enemy shelling fairly heavy. Enemy receiving a large number of Gas Shells. Working party obliged to wear Gas masks. No casualties and good progress made with the work.
>
> **3** — Work on bury contd. Good progress made. Enemy fairly quiet. Preparation for moving Signal Office at Div H.Q. from Dug Out to above ground made. Salvaging of cable continued
>
> **4** — Work on new Sig Office commenced after relief and on duty being employed at the work. Bury continued in forward area. made. [uncertain — "made." appears to be the tail of a "Good progress made" phrase split across the line break] Enemy fairly lively & receiving number of Gas Shells.
>
> [Signed, bottom left:] *A. C. [uncertain] Maj.*

### Page 5 — 5–8 May 1917

Place: Fort George.

> **Fort George, May 5** — Div. Visual STN moved from S. 30. C 1.9 to S 25 C. 7.9 Shed 36 C 5.4 Vimy Shed. Station moved to get better view of Divisional front Station doing good work in receiving messages from Battalion and companies in front line. Work on new Sig Office completed and wiring of same commenced. Forward bury continued. Enemy active Three casualties to working party
>
> **6** — Wiring of new office completed and office opened. Cable burying continued. Good progress made. Enemy quiet. Lt Brown admitted to Hospital. Capt Agnew admitted to hospital. No party available for cable burying.
>
> **7** — Cable between Goodman & Schwaben Tunnel showing 20 deg of earth. Party detailed to locate fault. Trouble located in cable between tunnels. Cable supplied very poor quality & abandoned. Overland lines laid. S.P. Twin supplied for
>
> **8** — [uncertain — first word cut] wiring of Schwaben also getting showing faults and party locating trouble. Working party obtained and work on burying cable continued. Good progress made. Enemy fairly active.
>
> [Signed, bottom left:] *A. C. [uncertain] Maj.*

### Page 6 — 9–12 May 1917

Place: Fort George.

> **Fort George, May 9** — Lt C. A. M. Cready wounded. Cable burying continued. Good german dugouts discovered and some used as test points on buried cable. Fault on cable through Schwaben tunnel found to be caused by the Infantry billeted there lighting fires and candles causing the cable to short circuit.
>
> **10** — Lt Skinner, Corps Wireless Officer, attchd this unit wounded. Cable burying continued. Good progress made. Enemy quiet. Cable in Schwaben tunnel abandoned owing to continuous trouble and overland lines laid.
>
> **11** — Cable burying continued. Good progress made. Enemy quiet. Over fifty miles of cable salvaged & party at work running same through.
>
> **12** — Cable burying continued. Work nearing completion in forward area and route being selected for bury from Advanced Brigade H.Qrs to join up with system at Goodman tunnel. Enemy quiet
>
> [Signed, bottom left:] *A. C. [uncertain] Maj.*

### Page 7 — 13–18 May 1917

Place: Fort George.

> **Fort George, May 13** — Owing to Bde Relief no party available for Cable burying. Enemy active & overland wires to Adv Bde H.Qrs frequently cut by shell fire. A number of important messages handled by Wireless. Visual station also handled a number of messages.
>
> **14** — Working party supplied & cable burying continued. Overland wires again shot out & Wireless and Visual again handle several messages.
>
> **15** — Lt Fellows of Corps Sig Coy reported with cable detachment to assist in work of cable burying. Lt Uglow takes 9th Bde Section Lt Brown having been evacuated to England. Cable burying continued. Enemy quiet
>
> **16** — Cable burying continued. Usual company routine.
>
> **17** — Lt Lesson [uncertain] 1st Div Sig Coy reported as Second in command. Cable burying continued. Usual routine.
>
> **18** — Forward bury completed, lines tested, all lines working satisfactorily. Work of repairing & rewinding salvaged cable completed. Usual company routine. Enemy quiet
>
> [Signed, bottom left:] *A. C. [uncertain] Maj.*

### Page 8 — 19–24 May 1917

Place: Fort George.

> **Fort George, May 19** — Route selected, working party of 300 men provided and work on burying cable from Adv Bde H.Qrs to connect with buried system at Goodman tunnel commenced. Enemy fairly active, overland wires frequently cut by shell fire Wireless & Visual again prove of great assistance. Usual routine
>
> **20** — Burying cable continued. Good progress made. No 110106 Sgt GIBSON R.B No 108407 Spr McINTOSH. R.P awarded the Military Medal No 103416 [uncertain — rank before the name not legible] KEE. H. awarded Bar to Military Medal for bravery displayed during recent engagements on Vimy Ridge. Usual company routine.
>
> **21** — Cable burying continued. Test points X being refitted with new test racks. etc. Eleven men reported from Signal Pool as reinforcements Enemy quiet
>
> **22** — Cable burying continued. Work at X Test point carried on. Usual coy routine
>
> **23** — [ditto to 22 May] Cable burying continued. Work at X Test point carried on. Usual coy routine
>
> **24** — [ditto "Cable burying continued."] New rack at X Test point installed. Work commenced of stringing new lines through Schwaben Tunnel. 7 pr brass sheathed being used. 28 pairs being strung. Test rack installed at old German dugout which is to be used as a test point. Usual routine. Enemy quiet
>
> [Signed, bottom left:] *A. C. [uncertain] Maj.*

### Page 9 — 25–31 May 1917

Place: Fort George.

> **Fort George, May 25** — Cable burying continued. Work of wiring Schwaben Tunnel continued. Enemy quiet
>
> **26** — [ditto "Cable burying continued."] Wiring completed & lines tested. Enemy aircraft drops bombs causing casualties on party burying cable. Usual routine
>
> **27** — Cable burying continued. Overland lines again cut by shell fire. Fault in cable in Goodman Tunnel Party working on same
>
> **28** — No party for cable burying owing to Bde relief. Fault in cable in Goodman [uncertain — "Tunnel"?] discovered & repaired. Usual routine. Enemy quiet
>
> **29** — Bde relief no working party available. Usual routine. Enemy quiet
>
> **30** — Working party obtained and work on bury continued. German Cables were discovered in dugout by Lt Craig Sig Officer with Arty Group. Usual routine. Enemy quiet
>
> **31** — Inspection of Arty Telephone wagons and horses preparatory to transferring same to Signals. Work on cable burying to be now carried on by day and party obtained. Usual routine. Enemy quiet.
>
> [Signed, bottom left:] *A. C. [uncertain] Maj.*
