# War Diary — No. 3 Canadian General Hospital (McGill): June 1916

## Source
- **Unit:** No. 3 Canadian General Hospital (McGill), Canadian Army Medical Corps
- **Archival reference:** RG 9 III-D-3, vol. 5035, file 853; microfilm reels T-10924–T-10925
- **LAC Collection Search:** Item ID 2005099 — "War diaries - 3rd Canadian General Hospital, Basingstoke", 1915/03/05–1919/06/24
  - Record: https://recherche-collection-search.bac-lac.gc.ca/eng/Home/Record?app=fonandcol&IdNumber=2005099
- **Page images:** pages 88–92 of 470 in the LAC viewer (manifest labels "June 1916, p. 1–5"). Fetched from LAC via the IIIF manifest `https://digitalmanifest.bac-lac.gc.ca/DigitalManifest/1/2005099`; page images saved alongside this file.
- **Commanding officer:** Colonel H. S. Birkett (signs the volume "H.S.Birkett. Colonel. O.C., No. 3 Canadian General Hospital—McGill"); Lt. Col. J. M. Elder proceeded to England on two weeks' special sick leave on 7 June and returned from sick leave on 20 June.
- **Location:** Boulogne — the hospital is in the Jesuit College, Boulogne.

## Transcription conventions
- Faithful to the original: spelling, punctuation, abbreviations and capitalisation as written. Typewritten on Army Form C. 2118, "WAR DIARY or INTELLIGENCE SUMMARY", in columns: Place | Date | Hour | Summary of Events and Information | Remarks and references to Appendices.
- `[uncertain]` marks a word or name that could not be read with confidence — never guessed. No word in this month required an `[uncertain]` mark; every entry was legible (verified against zoom crops where noted).
- The Remarks column throughout is the handwritten initials "H.S.B." (Colonel H. S. Birkett).
- On all three diary-text pages the sub-heading "INTELLIGENCE SUMMARY" is struck through (the heading reads "WAR DIARY" with "INTELLIGENCE SUMMARY" crossed out); "(Erase heading not required.)" appears beneath it.
- The June pages are a different print run of Army Form C. 2118 from March: the printed footer reads "T2124. Wt. W708—776. 500000. 4/15. Sir J.C. & S." and the form carries a printed instructions block at top left ("Instructions regarding War Diaries and Intelligence Summaries are contained in F. S. Regs., Part II. and the Staff Manual respectively. Title pages will be prepared in manuscript.").
- The X-Ray report from Captain A. H. Pirie has no date or hour entries of its own; it is embodied in the diary at the end of diary-text p. 3, after the 30 June entries, and reports X-Ray figures for April and May 1916.
- June entries read "Nothing worthy of comment occurred" (as in March).

We now have installed at our own expense a Bergonie Electro Vibrator. By means of this we are able to localize foreign bodies and this represents considerable economy in the use of X-Ray plates. Captain Pirie has also ingeniously devised an instrument whereby one is able to localize a foreign body of iron, nickel, brass or copper to a depth of one inch, and of aluminium or lead to a depth of about one half inch. This apparatus, whilst not yet perfected, is in the experimental stage, and if further work with it establishes its efficiency, there can be no question about its usefulness in reducing very considerably the necessity of taking a second X-Ray plate. It works on the principle of the Telephone Probe and its probable cost would be twelve shillings......... | H.S.B. |

## Open questions / uncertain items (June 1916)
- 1 June: "temporary" is typed above the line in the McNevin entry ("reported for/duty" with "temporary" inserted above) — transcribed as "reported for temporary duty" (verified by zoom crop `zoom-jun-mcnevin1.jpg`).
- 3 June: "CentralMedical Inspection Room" is typed without a space between "Central" and "Medical" in the Graves entry; the Logie entries (5 and 27 June) give "Central Medical Inspection Room" with a space (verified by zoom crop `zoom-jun-mcnevin2.jpg`).
- 9 June: "taken on the strenth of this Unit" in the Harrison entry — "strenth" is as typed (the 'g' is missing); the Ridgeway entry the same day gives "strength" (verified by zoom crop `zoom-jun-strenth2.jpg`).
- 14 June: "Convoy of 94 Patients received" — "Patients" is capitalised as typed; every other convoy entry uses lowercase "patients".
- 24 June: Captain J.B.Gallagher "returned from temporary duty at No. 13 General Hospital" — as typed; McNevin (3 June) and Main (12 June) were posted to "No. 13 Stationary Hospital", so "General" here may be a slip in the original.
- 29 June: the Austin/McNaughton entry ends "taken on strength." — it omits "of this Unit" (unlike the other taken-on-strength entries); the trailing dots are partly obscured by the "H.S.B." initials in the Remarks column.
- Cover (p. 1): handwritten "3." below "VOLUME XIII."; a line of small type runs beneath "CONFIDENTIAL."; no Committee for the Medical History of the War stamp appears on this cover (unlike March).
- Contents page count matches the pages present: "Diary Text 3 Pages" and three diary-text pages are in the volume.
- Captain C.C.Main is spelled the same on 12 June (posted to No. 13 Stationary Hospital) and 25 June (returned from temporary duty there).
- Lt. Col. J. M. Elder: proceeded to England on two weeks' special sick leave 7 June and returned from sick leave 20 June — he had proceeded to Canada on six weeks' leave on 30 March, so he evidently returned to the unit in April or May.
- Colonel H. S. Birkett remains O.C.; the diary is signed by him as "H.S.Birkett. Colonel. O.C., No. 3 Canadian General Hospital—McGill."
- Convoy totals for June: 2,225 patients received in 38 convoys; no evacuations to a hospital ship are recorded this month. No casualties are named in this volume — patients are recorded only as convoy numbers.
- X-Ray figures reported (retrospective, for April and May): April — 936 plates of 703 patients; May — 728 plates of 565 patients. Captain A. H. Pirie's Bergonie Electro Vibrator and his experimental foreign-body localizer (Telephone Probe principle, probable cost twelve shillings) are reported in the undated note at the end of the month.
- No appendices are listed for this volume and none are filed in it.
