## Transcription conventions
- Faithful to the original: spelling, punctuation, abbreviations and capitalisation as written. Typewritten on Army Form C. 2118, "WAR DIARY or INTELLIGENCE SUMMARY", in columns: Place | Date | Hour | Summary of Events and Information | Remarks and references to Appendices.
- `[uncertain]` marks a word or name that could not be read with confidence — never guessed. No item in this month required an `[uncertain]` mark; every name and figure was read clearly and verified against zoom crops.
- The Remarks column throughout is the handwritten initials "H.S.B." (Colonel H. S. Birkett).
- On diary-text p. 1 the word "or" in the form heading is struck through with a row of X's (the heading reads "WAR DIARY" / "INTELLIGENCE SUMMARY." with "or" crossed out); on "Page Two" and "Page Three" the heading reads "WAR DIARY or INTELLIGENCE SUMMARY." with "INTELLIGENCE SUMMARY" struck through with X's. "(Erase heading not required.)" appears beneath the heading on all three diary-text pages.
- The printed footer on all three diary-text pages reads "T2134. Wt. W708—776. 500000. 4/15. Sir J.C. & S." (September's pages read "T2124") and the form carries the printed instructions block at top left ("Instructions regarding War Diaries and Intelligence Summaries are contained in F. S. Regs., Part II. and the Staff Manual respectively. Title pages will be prepared in manuscript.").
- No hours are recorded in October — the Hour column is blank throughout. The diary is kept as daily admission and discharge/evacuation totals, as in September; no convoy times are given this month. The word used for patients leaving is "discharged" on 1–3 October and "evacuated" from 4 October onward.
- As in September, ditto marks (") in the Place and Date columns are expanded to the repeated value in the transcription below (the date number is repeated on every row of a multi-entry date).
- This volume has no contents page: the cover is followed directly by the three diary-text pages.

- 10 October: "Lt.Colonels J.P.Dicarie and R. Rheaume, and Major Archambault" — "Dicarie" and "Rheaume" are as typed; Major Archambault is given with no initials (verified by zoom crops `zoom-oct-p2-mid.jpg` and `zoom-oct-p2-bot.jpg`).
- 12 October: Sgt.Major W.G.Hadley, W.O. Class 1, evacuated to England with Sciatica, ill since the third week of July; the O.C. records his appreciation of Hadley's service since the unit's inception (verified by zoom crop `zoom-oct-p2-bot.jpg`).
- 13 October: in the Bolster entry the word "strength" has its final letters overtyped, but it reads "strength" (verified by zoom crop `zoom-oct-bolster.jpg`).
- 14 October: "Lt.Colonel Toplitz, Inspector of Finance" — as typed, no initials given (verified by zoom crop `zoom-oct-p2-bot.jpg`).
- 21 and 30 October: Captain Barton is styled "C.E.Chaplain" on 21 October and "C.of E. Chaplain" on 30 October — both as typed (verified by zoom crops `zoom-oct-barton.jpg` and `zoom-oct-p4-mid.jpg`).
- 23 October: the D.D.M.S. letter is "No.3302/16" and the hospital's bed capacity for winter scale is placed at 1273 (verified by zoom crop `zoom-oct-p3-bot.jpg`).
- 27 October (second entry): the Leishmann visit entry is entered after the 28 October entries — out of chronological order — and it cites "(29-10-16)", a date after its own date, so it was written on or after 29 October (verified by zoom crop `zoom-oct-p4-mid.jpg`). In that entry the word "so" in "so far" and the word "that" before "up to the present time" appear overtyped; "P.U.O." is as written and is not expanded (verified by zoom crop `zoom-oct-leishmann.jpg`).
- The 1 October MacMillan entry records an event of 28 September 1916 ("evacuated from No. 14 Stationary Hospital to England 28-9-16") filed under 1 October.
- The printed footer on this volume's diary-text pages reads "T2134" where September's read "T2124"; the rest of the footer is identical.
- Patient totals for October: 4,514 admitted (31 daily figures); 527 discharged (1–3 October) and 3,892 evacuated (4–31 October), 4,419 out in total. The heaviest day was 27 October (303 admitted); the lightest was 15 October (11 admitted). Every date 1–31 October has a patient-figures line. No casualties are named in this volume — patients are recorded only as daily numbers.
- Colonel H. S. Birkett remains O.C.; the diary is signed by him as "H.S. Birkett. Colonel. O.C., No. 3 Canadian General Hospital—McGill."
- No appendices are listed for this volume and none are filed in it.
