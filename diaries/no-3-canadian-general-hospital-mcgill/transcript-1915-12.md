## Transcription conventions
- Faithful to the original: spelling, punctuation, abbreviations and capitalisation as written. Typewritten on Army Form C. 2118, "WAR DIARY or INTELLIGENCE SUMMARY", in columns: Place | Date | Hour | Summary of Events and Information | Remarks and references to Appendices.
- `[uncertain]` marks a word or name that could not be read with confidence — never guessed.
- The Remarks column on diary-text pp. 1–2 is the handwritten initials "H.S.B." (Lt. Col. H. S. Birkett); on diary-text p. 3 (23–31 December) the initials appear to be "J.M.E." (Lt. Col. J. M. Elder, acting O.C.).
- Ditto marks (") in the diary mean "as above" — here, "Nothing worthy of comment occurred" for the preceding days.
- On diary-text p. 1 the sub-heading "or INTELLIGENCE SUMMARY" is not struck through (the heading reads "WAR DIARY or INTELLIGENCE SUMMARY"); on pp. 2–3 it is struck through.
- December entries read "Nothing worthy of **comment** occurred" (as in November).

## Page 3 — Diary text, p. 1 (December 1915, p. 3)

> WAR DIARY or INTELLIGENCE SUMMARY — Army Form C. 2118
>
> | Place | Date | Hour | Summary of Events and Information | Remarks |
> | Camiers. | December 1. | | Captain Colin Russel of this Unit left for England to-day to report for duty as a Neurologist at the Canadian Special Hospital, Granville Hotel, Ramsgate, on the Authority of the A.D.M.S. Etaples Administrative District, D.512/15, dated 1/12/15. | H.S.B. |
> | " | 1. | | The following memorandum has been brought to my attention, "The Rontgen Society of London has six honorary members viz., Professor Rontgen the discoverer of X-Ray, Sir W. Crookes, who 20 years before Rontgen produced X-Ray's, but did not discover them. Sir Oliver Lodge, Sir Ernest Rutherford, formerly Professor of Physics at McGill University, Professor Holznecht, and Professor Stephen Leduc. A seventh member has just been elected, Captain A. Howard Pirie, M.D., B.Sc., now serving with No. 3 Canadian General Hospital—McGill in France". | H.S.B. |
> | " | 2. | | Nothing worthy of comment occurred. | H.S.B. |
> | " | 3. | | " " " " | H.S.B. |
> | " | 4. | | Continuous downpours of rain for the past three days rendering the lines almost impassable. | H.S.B. |
> | " | 5. | 8.00 p.m. | Instructions received from A.D.M.S., Etaples Administrative District, to detail thirty Nursing Sisters for temporary duty in England. | H.S.B. |
> | " | 6. | | Orders received from the A.D.M.S., E.A.D., for N.C.O's and men of the final year in Medicine to hold themselves ready to proceed to England en route to Canada in order to complete their Medical course in the Medical Faculty of McGill University, Montreal, Canada. | H.S.B. |
> | " | 7. | 11.45 a.m. | Three Non-Commissioned officers and ten Privates, Fifth year students of the Medical Faculty, McGill University, proceeded this day to England. Auth: A.D.M.S., E.A.D., D.498/15, 6-12-15. | H.S.B. |
> | " | 7. | | Wrote to the A.D.M.S., E.A.D., for a ruling regarding the Fourth year Students, vide A.D.M.S. E.A.D., C.1509/15, 1-12-15. | H.S.B. |
> | " | 8. | 8.30 a.m. | Thirty Nursing Sisters proceeded this day en route to England for temporary duty; fifteen to the Duchess of Connaught Hospital, Taplow, and fifteen to the Canadian Military Hospital, Shorncliffe. Auth: A.D.M.S. E.A.D. 5/12/15. | H.S.B. |
> | " | 9. | | Torrential rains continuous throughout the day. | H.S.B. |
> | " | 10. | | Nothing worthy of comment occurred. | H.S.B. |
> | " | 11. | | " " " " | H.S.B. |
> | " | 12. | 6.35 p.m. | Telephonic instructions received from A.D.M.S., E.A.D., to the effect that eight Officers should be selected to proceed to England for temporary duty in the Shorncliffe area. | H.S.B. |
> | " | 13. | | Nothing worthy of comment occurred. | H.S.B. |
> | " | 14. | 12.30 p.m. | In accordance with instructions received from the A.D.M.S., E.A.D., the following Officers, eight in number, proceeded en route to England for temporary duty in the Shorncliffe area:— Captains Howell W.B., Malone R.A., Reford L.A., McKim L.H., Henderson A.T., Hutchinson J.W., [continued on p. 2] | H.S.B. |
>

## Page 4 — Diary text, p. 2 (December 1915, p. 4)

> PAGE TWO. — WAR DIARY or INTELLIGENCE SUMMARY — Army Form C. 2118
>
> | Place | Date | Hour | Summary of Events and Information | Remarks |
> | Camiers. | Dec. 14 Continued. | | Wickham J.C. [uncertain — first letters clipped at the scan edge], Robertson R.B., Auth: D.C.M.S., D.C. 2733/6, dated 9-12-15, D.M.S., L. of C., M.2/1136/15, dated 11-12-15, A.D.M.S., Etaples, D.566/15, dated 13-12-15. | H.S.B. |
> | " | 15. | | To-day I visited Boulogne and interviewed the D.D.M.S. and the A.D.W. with estimates of original plans, regarding alterations at the Jesuit College, refused one half. As yet nothing has been started regarding the proposed alterations. | H.S.B. |
> | " | 16. | | Received the following telephonic communication from the A.D.M.S., Etaples, "Please inform me at once number of personnel, and weight of baggage to be conveyed to Boulogne, and when ready to move so that the trains may be arranged." | H.S.B. |
> | " | 16. | | To-day paid a visit to the A.D.W. at Boulogne to discuss the prospective plans and to point out the inefficiency of the proposed alterations and the possibility of retrenchment of expenditure in certain directions to greater advantage. | H.S.B. |
> | " | 17. | | Again visited the D.D.M.S. and A.D.W., Boulogne regarding alterations at the Jesuit College. | H.S.B. |
> | " | 18. | 8.30 a.m. | An advance party of six N.C.O's and eighteen Privates proceeded to Boulogne to start putting up our kitchens and altering the lavatories at the Jesuit College. | H.S.B. |
> | " | 19. | | Lt. Colonel H.B. Yates having been invalided to England on the 22nd November, 1915, is struck off the strength of this Unit from that date. | H.S.B. |
> | " | 20. | | Additional party of 4 Officers, 1 N.C.O., and 9 Privates detailed to precede this Unit to Boulogne to take over the Jesuit College. | H.S.B. |
> | " | 21. | | The Base Commandant, B.A.D., Colonel Graham Thomson, C.B., inspected the men of this Unit to-day at noon and expressed his regret at the departure of the Unit from his district. He congratulated them upon the excellent discipline which had been maintained and said that "there was no finer Medical Unit in this District". | H.S.B. |
> | " | 21. | 9.00 p.m. | Six privates of this Unit, Fourth Year Medical Students of McGill University, Montreal, to Canada proceeded to England with a view to discharge to enable them to return to Canada to complete their Medical studies. Auth: D.M.S., L.of C., M.1821/15, 9-12-15, War Office letter 121/Overseas/1109(A.M.D.1.) 24-11-15, A.G., A/1597, 14-12-15, A.D.M.S., Etaples, D.592/15. | H.S.B. |
> | " | 21. | | The following N.C.O's of this Unit — Sgt.-Major Kennedy G.L.D., Staff-Sergeant Wilkes A.B., Sergeant Wilson R.D., Sergeant Neilson H.K., Sergeant Baby G.R., — to be Captains, C.A.M.C., and posted to the C.A.M.C. Training School, 11/12/15. Auth: Corps Orders D.M.S., Canadian Contingents, No. 176, para 1213, dated 18-12-15. | H.S.B. |
> | " | 22. | 8.30 a.m. | In accordance with Authority D.C.2764/6 I proceed to-day on duty to England having placed Lt. Colonel J.M.Elder A/O.C. during my temporary absence. | H.S.B. |
>
> [Printed footer:] 1875 Wt. W993/826 1,000,000 4/15 J.B.C. & A. A.D.S.S./Forms/C. 2118.

## Open questions / uncertain items (December 1915)
- 1 December: "Captain Colin Russel" — as typed ("Russel", not "Russell"). "Granville Hotel, Ramsgate" — as typed.
- 1 December (Rontgen Society memorandum): "Professor Holznecht" and "Professor Stephen Leduc" — as typed (verified by zoom crop `zoom-dec-rontgen.jpg`). "Sir Oliver Lodge" — verified by zoom.
- 14 December: the eighth officer's name reads "Wickham J.C." but the first letters are clipped at the scan edge — flagged [uncertain].
- 29 December: the two leave authorities are both given as "A.40246" — as typed; presumably a typing error in the original.
- 31 December: "D.C.M.S. D.C.628/6, A.27-12-15" — as typed.
- Cover (p. 1): handwritten "No. 7" (top right) and "3." (below "VOLUME VII.") are uncertain.
- The move to Boulogne ordered in November was reversed: on 29 December the D.M.S., L. of C. decided to retain No. 3 Canadian General Hospital at Dannes-Camiers, and the unit re-opened there, taking over No. 22 General Hospital's quarters.
