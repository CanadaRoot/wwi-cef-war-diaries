- The Remarks column on all four diary-text pages is the handwritten initials "J.M.E." (Lt. Col. J. M. Elder, acting O.C.).
- On all four diary-text pages the sub-heading "or INTELLIGENCE SUMMARY" is not struck through (unlike December 1915, pp. 2–3, where it was).
- January entries read "Nothing worthy of comment occurred" (1 January only).
- The 2 January telegram number is given as "M.A-2874", but the 4 January confirmation quotes the same telegram as "M.R.2874" — the discrepancy is in the original.

## Page 3 — Diary text, p. 1 (January 1916, p. 3)

> WAR DIARY or INTELLIGENCE SUMMARY — Army Form C. 2118
>
> | Place | Date | Hour | Summary of Events and Information | Remarks |
> | Camiers. | January 1. | | Nothing worthy of comment occurred. | J.M.E. |
> | " | 2. | 11.00 p.m. | Received the following telegram from D.M.S., L. of C., through A.D.M.S., E.A.D.;— "M.A-2874 Cancel my C.R.M/2939/15 of 28-12-15. No. 3 Canadian General Hospital will move to Jesuit College, Boulogne. Letter follows" | J.M.E. |
> | " | 3. | 9.00 a.m. | Major A.C.P.Howard proceeded this day to England to report to the D.M.S., Canadian Contingents, London, with a view to resuming his professional duties in the University of Iowa. Authority: D.M.S., L.of C. M.2/1198/15, 29-12-15, A.D.M.S., E.A.D., D.617/15, 31/12/15. | J.M.E. |
> | " | 4. | 7.30 p.m. | Received the following message from D.M.S., L.of C., M.2939/15, d/3-1-16, through A.D.M.S., E.A.D., C.2664/6/15, 4-1-16, "In confirmation of my telegram No. M.R.2874, Dated 2nd January, 1916, No. 3 Canadian General Hospital will move to Boulogne at the earliest possible date. On arrival there it will occupy the buildings of the Jesuit College and the late No. 8 Convalescent Depot. Will you please cause an advance party to be detailed in readiness to proceed to Boulogne to prepare for the reception of the Hospital on its arrival there. Please notify this office at the earliest date possible strength of advance party:- Officers. Other Ranks. Strength of remainder of personnel. - Officers. Nursing Sisters. Other ranks. Weight of stores. The orders for the move will be issued through the Base Commandant as soon as the strength, etc., has been notified. All tentage will be left standing" | J.M.E. |
> | " | 4. | | Captains A.H.Pirie and W.W.Francis returned from leave of absence. | J.M.E. |
> | " | 5. | | Report from England that Lt.Colonel H. B. Yates is very ill. | J.M.E. |
> | " | 6. | 8.00 a.m. | Moved Headquarters into Boulogne. Left detail party in charge of Captain Turner at Camiers to look after loading of equipment as soon as transport can be arranged. | J.M.E. |
> | Boulogne. | 6. | 11.00 a.m. | Reported to Base Commandant and D.D.M.S., Boulogne Base, upon my arrival here. | J.M.E. |
> | " | 6. | | Major J.C.Meakins and Capt.H.M.Little proceeded on leave of absence, and Capt. J.A.MacMillan returned from leave of absence. | J.M.E. |
> | " | 7. | 3.00 p.m. | Colonel Kettle, No. 7 Officers' Hospital, Boulogne, visited us to-day in view of making report upon the possibility of adapting our premises for the use of Officers Hospital. | J.M.E. |
> | " | 7. | | 7th Engineers ordered to stop work on Hospital kitchen and other improvements, pending decision as to our remaining here or being turned into an Officers' Hospital. | J.M.E. |
> | " | 8. | | Captain H. C. Dixon proceeded on leave of absence. | J.M.E. |
> | " | 8. | | Received message from Surgeon-General Jones, C.M.G., D.M.S., Canadian Contingents, London, to meet him at the Folkestone Hotel, Boulogne, which I did and we conferred upon matters of interest to the Unit. | J.M.E. |
> | " | 9. | 11.00 a.m. | Surgeon-General Jones, C.M.G., visited and inspected our Hospital site to-day. | J.M.E. |

## Page 4 — Diary text, p. 2 (January 1916, p. 4)

> PAGE TWO. — WAR DIARY or INTELLIGENCE SUMMARY — Army Form C. 2118
>
> | Place | Date | Hour | Summary of Events and Information | Remarks |
> | Boulogne. | Jan. 10. | | Captains R.St.J.Macdonald and W.T.Ewing proceeded on leave of absence. | J.M.E. |
> | " | 10. | 5.00 p.m. | A meeting of Officers Commanding Medical Units in this neighbourhood was held in the Office of the D.D.M.S. The Camp Commandant laid before them a scheme for the establishment of an Officers' Club at Boulogne, which the Countess of Dudley was interested in having established. A plan was approved of in a general way and it was decided that Officers Commanding Units should bring before the members of their Mess the details of the proposed plan and find out how many Officers in their commands would be willing to become members of the proposed Club. General instructions regarding Camp discipline, the unauthorized use of Ambulances, etc., were given to the Commanding Officers by the D.D.M.S. for their information. | J.M.E. |
> | " | 10. | 7.30 p.m. | Telegram received from D.M.S., Canadian Contingents, London, asking for permission to keep Major J.C.Meakins there for a time. It was answered in the affirmative. | J.M.E. |
> | " | 11. | 3.00 p.m. | A loud explosion, apparently at sea, shook the whole premises. | J.M.E. |
> | " | 11. | | The D.D.M.S. informs me that our Nursing Sisters are to stay on at Camiers for the present. | J.M.E. |
> | " | 12. | 11.30 p.m. | Orders received by telephone that Ordnance train will arrive from Camiers to-morrow at 1.06 p.m. and must be unloaded by 4.30 p.m. | J.M.E. |
> | " | 13. | | The train with our ordnance and the loading party arrived at the Gare Central, Boulogne, about 2.30 p.m. The Railway authorities insisted upon its being emptied at once, and the same party of men who had loaded at Camiers at once proceeded to unload here. Lorries were sent by the Transport Officer and at one time 40 lorries were at work moving the 36 carloads of freight, and by 11.30 we had all our goods in our own quarters and most of it under cover. Fortunately for us, the weather, which was rainy in the morning, turned to fine at night. The Quartermaster and a small squad of men are still at Camiers cleaning up the camp and preparing to hand over what we have left there to our successors. | J.M.E. |
> | " | 13. | | The Matron and 30 Sisters in charge of Captain A.H.Pirie, with a detail of 14 other ranks, are remaining at Camiers. | J.M.E. |
> | " | 14. | | After the hard work of last night the men were not paraded to-day and were given a chance to make themselves comfortable in their new quarters. | J.M.E. |
> | " | 15. | | Miss K. O. McLatchey, Matron, has been awarded the Royal Red Cross Decoration (1st Class). Corps Orders 12, para 91, 15-1-16, D.M.S., Canadian Contingents, London. | J.M.E. |
> | " | 15. | | The day was spent in unpacking equipment. | J.M.E. |
> | " | 15. | | Captain W.C.Turner proceeded on leave yesterday and Capt.H.C.Dixon returned to-day. | J.M.E. |
> | " | 16. | | Made a round of the Camp with Captain Bryant, S.W.O., noting changes and improvements as per plans previously agreed upon. The Sisters quarters to be fitted up in the adjoining Convalescent Camp as soon as possible. | J.M.E. |
>

## Page 5 — Diary text, p. 3 (January 1916, p. 5)

> PAGE THREE. — WAR DIARY or INTELLIGENCE SUMMARY — Army Form C. 2118
>
> | Place | Date | Hour | Summary of Events and Information | Remarks |
> | Boulogne. | Jan. 17. | | Began fitting up the wards to-day with beds and furniture. | J.M.E. |
> | " | 17. | | Obtained permission from Major Campbell, No. 1 Convalescent Depot, to use the canteen on his premises temporarily, Camp Commandant permitting. | J.M.E. |
> | " | 18. | | Miserable wet day, not cold, but had to discontinue work upon the wards. Carpenters resumed work upon the Hospital kitchen assisted by our own carpenter. | J.M.E. |
> | " | 18. | | Orders from D.D.M.S. to send a Junior Officer to assist at No. 1 Convalescent Depot. Captain F. W. Tidmarsh sent. Auth: M.54/16, d.18-1-16. | J.M.E. |
> | " | 18. | | Captains R.St.J.Macdonald and W.T.Ewing returned from leave of absence. | J.M.E. |
> | " | 19. | | Very windy and showery. | J.M.E. |
> | " | 19. | | Lt.Colonel Cheever (Harvard Unit) dined with us and afterwards attended a lecture in the Y.M.C.A. "Bath" Hut, by John Baillie, M.A. (Edin), Lecturer in France for the British Red Cross. The subject of the lecture was "The Battle of the Marne" and very well handled. | J.M.E. |
> | " | 20. | | High winds, rain and hail. Wards inside the walls are now all complete with beds and furniture. | J.M.E. |
> | " | 20. | | Captain W.C.Turner returned from leave of absence. | J.M.E. |
> | " | 21. | | Sir A. Perry, Lt.Colonel Cheever and Major Heyburn (Harvard Unit), with Major Campbell, No. 1 Convalescent Depot, dined with us; afterwards attended concert in the "Bath" Hut, by the Princess Victoria's Concert Party, which was most enjoyable. | J.M.E. |
> | " | 22. | | All extremely saddened this morning by telegram from London announcing the death of Lt.Colonel H.B.Yates. | J.M.E. |
> | " | 23. | | Weather spring like, clear and balmy. | J.M.E. |
> | " | 23. | | In the afternoon attended Discussion at the Australian Hospital on "Treatment of Compound Fracture of the Femur, from the Front to Base Hospital in England". | J.M.E. |
> | " | 23. | | No. 14 Stationary Hospital, for infectious cases, burnt down last night with no loss of life. | J.M.E. |
> | " | 24. | | A fine day after misty morning. Matron and Sisters visited us and inspected their future quarters, being delighted with the outlook. | J.M.E. |
> | " | 25. | | Lt.Colonel Almond, Senior Chaplain Canadian Forces in France, lunched with us to-day. Went out to Wimille, and arranged with the Laundry there for days for the reception of our laundry. | J.M.E. |
> | " | 26. | | Hospital premises visited by Lt.Colonel J.V.Forrest, A.D.D.M.S. and also by Lt.Colonel Smith, A.D.W., both of whom noted with pleasure the preparations we are making to open the Hospital. | J.M.E. |
> | " | 27. | | Surgeon-General Sawyer, D.D.M.S., and Lt.Colonel J.V.Forrest, paid an official visit to the Hospital this morning and inspected and approved of what we have done. We were able to show them over 800 beds in position. | J.M.E. |
>

## Page 6 — Diary text, p. 4 (January 1916, p. 6)

> PAGE FOUR. — WAR DIARY or INTELLIGENCE SUMMARY — Army Form C. 2118
>
> | Place | Date | Hour | Summary of Events and Information | Remarks |
> | Boulogne. | Jan. 28. | | General Woodhouse, D.M.S., L.of C., Accompanied by Lt.Colonel Beveridge, Sanitary Officer, visited and inspected the Hospital to-day. The General was good enough to express approval of our arrangements. We received our first patients this afternoon - skin cases transferred to us from No. 14 Stationary Hospital which had been burnt. We cook for them on Men's Kitchen until Hospital Kitchen is ready. | J.M.E. |
> | " | 29. | | Received more patients of the same class as those of yesterday. We have now fifty patients in all, and until the kitchen is ready we cannot cook for more. | J.M.E. |
