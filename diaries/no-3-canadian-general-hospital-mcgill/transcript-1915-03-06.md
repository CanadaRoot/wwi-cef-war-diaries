## Transcription conventions
- Faithful to the original: spelling, punctuation, abbreviations and capitalisation as written. The diary is typewritten on Army Form C. 2118, "WAR DIARY or INTELLIGENCE SUMMARY", in columns: Place | Date | Hour | Summary of Events and Information | Remarks and references to Appendices.
- `[uncertain]` marks a word or name that could not be read with confidence — never guessed.
- Text struck through in the original is shown as `[struck through in original: "..."]`.
- The Remarks column throughout is the handwritten initials "H.S.B." (Lt. Col. H. S. Birkett).
- No map coordinates (latitude/longitude) are given in the diary; places are recorded as written.
- The LAC manifest labels all four pages of this volume "March 1915, p. 1–4", but the volume itself covers 5 March – 30 June 1915 (see cover and contents below).

## Page 3 — Diary text, p. 1 (March 1915, p. 3)

> WAR DIARY or INTELLIGENCE SUMMARY — Army Form C. 2118
>
> [Printed instructions, top left:] Instructions regarding War Diaries and Intelligence Summaries are contained in F. S. Regs., Part II. and the Staff Manual respectively. Title Pages will be prepared in manuscript.
>
> | Place | Date | Hour | Summary of Events and Information | Remarks |
> | Montreal | 5-3-15 | | No.3.Canadian General Hospital—McGill, mobilized under G.O.Headquarters, 4th Div; Canada, McGill University Buildings. | H.S.B. |
> | " | 6-3-15 | | Internal economy arranged and organized, stretcher and squad drill; instruction. | H.S.B. |
> | " | 1-4-15 | | Headquarters transferred to St.George's Home, No.5.Mansfield Street. | H.S.B. |
> | " | 20-4-15 | | Inspection by Major General Lessard. | H.S.B. |
> | " | 21-4-15 | | Inspection by Colonel Wilson, D.O.C. | H.S.B. |
> | " | 22-4-15 | | Inspection by H.R.H.The Duke of Connaught; Khaki Convocation to graduates; Mess Dinner to His Royal Highness. | H.S.B. |
> | " | 6-5-15 | 7.15 am. | Parade at the Barracks, establishment complete; embarked S.S."Metagama" 9-15 a.m. Departed from Montreal 11 a.m. Ship Hospital inspected, medical duties assumed by No.3.General Hospital, McGill. The smart bearing and discipline of the men was highly commented upon. | H.S.B. |
> | S.S. Metagama | 7-5-15 | | Parade of all troops 9.30 a.m. for inspection by the Brigadier. Colonel Denison. Colonel Birkett assumed duties as P.M.O. of the ship. Lt.Colonel Yates as O.C. of No.3.Can;Gen;Hosp.,-McGill. Orderly Officer of this unit to be the Medical Orderly Officer of the ship. Lt.Col;Anglin, No.5. Stationary Hospital—Queens, appointed Surgeon on duty. Major Meakins, Physician. Captain Henderson i/c of Ship Hospital. Captain MacMillan as Admitting Officer. Alarm Posts arranged. Colonel Denison comment,—"The discipline and deportment of all ranks is excellent". | H.S.B. |
> | " | 8-5-15 | | Pte Kerry, 21st Battalion admitted, Lobar Pneumonia. Pte Blythe, 21st Battalion—Acute appendicitis. Operation, Lt.Colonel Elder. Report sent to O.C. of troops, and O.C. of 21st Battalion. | H.S.B. |
> | " | 11-5-15 | | Case of measles; isolated with Pte Fry of same cabin. | H.S.B. |
> | " | 12-5-15 | | Life belt parade of all troops at 10-30. Belts were all carried up and put on at the quarters. All belts complete. Instructions given fully to all Medical Units about use of same, and to have them available for immediate use. | H.S.B. |
> | " | 13-5-15 | | All deck lights out in the evening. Men of No.3.General Hospital ordered to have clothes and life belts handy. Co.,Sgt Maj.,Lattion, 21st Battalion admitted, acute appendicitis. Captain Howell throughout has been keen on the discipline and deportment of officers and men. | H.S.B. |
> | " | 14-5-15 | | Co.,Sgt.,Maj.,Lattion operated upon; acute appendicitis. Lt.Col;Anglin, assisted by Lt.Colonel Etherington. Report to O.C. of troops and O.C. of 21st Battalion. About 7 p.m. the ship was picked up by her convoy, three destroyers. All lights shrouded. Convoyed to Plymouth roadstead, arriving 4 a.m. Proceeded up to Devonport after daylight; docked about 8 a.m. Wired D.M.S. London. Nursing Sisters entrained for London, c/o Lt.Colonel Elder and 4 Officers. | H.S.B. |
> | Devonport | [14-5-15] | | No.3.General Hospital—McGill entrained 4 p.m. for Shorncliffe, arriving about 2-30 a.m. with baggage and stores; marched to Moore Barracks, arriving 4 a.m. Officers and men billeted. | H.S.B. |
> | Shorncliffe | 17-5-15 | | Morning Parade of Unit. Majority of Nurses sent to France on duty; remainder to Cliveden. | H.S.B. |
>
