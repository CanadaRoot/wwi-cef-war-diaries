# War Diary — No. 3 Canadian General Hospital (McGill): September 1916

## Source
- **Unit:** No. 3 Canadian General Hospital (McGill), Canadian Army Medical Corps
- **Archival reference:** RG 9 III-D-3, vol. 5035, file 853; microfilm reels T-10924–T-10925
- **LAC Collection Search:** Item ID 2005099 — "War diaries - 3rd Canadian General Hospital, Basingstoke", 1915/03/05–1919/06/24
  - Record: https://recherche-collection-search.bac-lac.gc.ca/eng/Home/Record?app=fonandcol&IdNumber=2005099
- **Page images:** pages 106–110 of 470 in the LAC viewer (manifest labels "September 1916, p. 1–5"). Fetched from LAC via the IIIF manifest `https://digitalmanifest.bac-lac.gc.ca/DigitalManifest/1/2005099`; page images saved alongside this file.
- **Commanding officer:** Colonel H. S. Birkett (signs the volume "H.S. Birkett. Colonel. O.C., No. 3 Canadian General Hospital—McGill"); on 30 September he records that the Colonial Auxiliary Forces Officers' Decoration was awarded to him on 15 July 1916.
- **Location:** Boulogne — the hospital is in the Jesuit College, Boulogne.

## Transcription conventions
- Faithful to the original: spelling, punctuation, abbreviations and capitalisation as written. Typewritten on Army Form C. 2118, "WAR DIARY or INTELLIGENCE SUMMARY", in columns: Place | Date | Hour | Summary of Events and Information | Remarks and references to Appendices.
- `[uncertain]` marks a word or name that could not be read with confidence — never guessed. One item in this month required an `[uncertain]` mark (Captain Francis's first initial, 30 September).
- The Remarks column throughout is the handwritten initials "H.S.B." (Colonel H. S. Birkett).
- On all three diary-text pages the sub-heading "INTELLIGENCE SUMMARY" is struck through (the heading reads "WAR DIARY" with "INTELLIGENCE SUMMARY" crossed out); "(Erase heading not required.)" appears beneath it.
- The printed footer on all three diary-text pages reads "T2124. Wt. W708—776. 500000. 4/15. Sir J.C. & S." (the same June print run of Army Form C. 2118) and the form carries the printed instructions block at top left ("Instructions regarding War Diaries and Intelligence Summaries are contained in F. S. Regs., Part II. and the Staff Manual respectively. Title pages will be prepared in manuscript.").
- No hours are recorded in September — the Hour column is blank throughout. The diary is now kept as daily admission and discharge/evacuation totals rather than the timed convoy entries of June; no convoy times are given this month. The word used for patients leaving is "discharged" on 1–3 September and "evacuated" from 4 September onward.
- As in June, ditto marks (") in the Place and Date columns are expanded to the repeated value in the transcription below.

## Open questions / uncertain items (September 1916)
- 2 September: "taken on the strength of thig Unit" — "thig" is as typed (the 's' of "this" is typed as 'g'); verified by zoom crop `zoom-sep16-p3-sisters.jpg`.
- 5 September: Captain L.J.Rhea "received his promotion to that of a Majority" — "Majority" is as typed (for "Major"); verified by zoom crop `zoom-sep16-p3-rhea-top.jpg`.
- 12 September: "Professor J.H.N.Robertson" — initials read J.H.N. from the zoom crop `zoom-sep16-p3-visitors.jpg`.
- 13 September: "Geofrey H.Hamilton" is spelled "Geofrey" (not "Geoffrey") as typed; "W. Smith" and "W.Kershaw" are as typed (verified by zoom crop `zoom-sep16-p3-visitors.jpg`).
- 15 September: the sisters list ends "McArthur, Hannah J.," with a trailing comma; no further names follow — the next line is the Birkett Clark entry (verified by zoom crop `zoom-sep16-p4-top.jpg`).
- 15 September: "Nursing Sister M.Birkett Clark" — no comma or stop between "Birkett" and "Clark" as typed (verified by zoom crop `zoom-sep16-p4-top.jpg`).
- 18 September: two separate patient-figures lines are entered for the same date — "510 patients admitted and 190 evacuated" and "287 patients admitted and 370 evacuated" — both are transcribed as written; the second line's Date column is a ditto mark (verified by zoom crop `zoom-sep16-p4-mid.jpg`). No explanation is given in the diary.
- 18 September: "The Hon.W.H.Hurst, Prime Minister for Ontario" — "Hurst" is as typed (verified by zoom crop `zoom-sep16-p4-mid.jpg`).
- 19 September: the 19 September figures ("113 patients admitted and 249 evacuated") are entered after the 20 September reinforcements entry — the figures on this page are out of chronological order (verified by zoom crop `zoom-sep16-p4-low.jpg`).
- 20 and 21 September: two separate drafts of thirteen other ranks each arrived as reinforcements from the Canadian Base Depot, Havre — one on each day (verified by zoom crops `zoom-sep16-p4-low.jpg`).
- 21 September: "Fleet Surgeon F.S.Pleadwell of the United States Navy" — "Pleadwell" as typed (verified by zoom crop `zoom-sep16-p4-low.jpg`).
- 30 September: Captain Francis's first initial is overstruck/illegible and is marked [uncertain]; the visible overtyping looks like a "W" over another letter, so the name may be "W.W.Francis" but this is not certain (verified by zoom crops `zoom-sep16-p5-francis.jpg` and `zoom-sep16-p5-francis2.jpg`).
- 30 September: the interlineated reference below "243/3" in the decoration entry reads "MD4973/1" but the middle characters are crowded and it may be "MD49/3/1" (verified by zoom crops `zoom-sep16-p5-decor.jpg` and `zoom-sep16-p5-francis2.jpg`).
- 15 September is the only date with no patient figures at all — the day's entries are only the arrival of three nursing sisters and Sister Birkett Clark proceeding to England.
- Contents page count matches the pages present: "Diary Text 3 Pages" and three diary-text pages are in the volume.
- Colonel H. S. Birkett remains O.C.; the diary is signed by him as "H.S. Birkett. Colonel. O.C., No. 3 Canadian General Hospital—McGill."
- Patient totals for September: 5,441 admitted (30 daily figures); 382 discharged (1–3 September) and 5,091 evacuated (4–30 September), 5,473 out in total. The heaviest day was 18 September (797 admitted across the two figures lines); the lightest was 12 September (4 admitted). No casualties are named in this volume — patients are recorded only as daily numbers.
- No appendices are listed for this volume and none are filed in it.
