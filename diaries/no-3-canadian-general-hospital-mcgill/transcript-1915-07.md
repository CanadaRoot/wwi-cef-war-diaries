- **LAC Collection Search:** Item ID 2005099 — "War diaries - 3rd Canadian General Hospital, Basingstoke", 1915/03/05–1919/06/24
  - Record: https://recherche-collection-search.bac-lac.gc.ca/eng/Home/Record?app=fonandcol&IdNumber=2005099
- **Page images:** pages 5–8 of 470 in the LAC viewer (manifest labels "July 1915, p. 1–4"). Fetched from LAC via the IIIF manifest `https://digitalmanifest.bac-lac.gc.ca/DigitalManifest/1/2005099`; page images saved alongside this file.
- **Commanding officer:** Lt. Col. H. S. Birkett (initials "H.S.B." in the Remarks column throughout).

## Transcription conventions
- Faithful to the original: spelling, punctuation, abbreviations and capitalisation as written. Typewritten on Army Form C. 2118, "WAR DIARY or INTELLIGENCE SUMMARY", in columns: Place | Date | Hour | Summary of Events and Information | Remarks and references to Appendices.
- `[uncertain]` marks a word or name that could not be read with confidence — never guessed.
- The Remarks column throughout is the handwritten initials "H.S.B." (Lt. Col. H. S. Birkett).
- Ditto marks (") in the diary mean "as above" — here, "Nothing worthy of report occurred" for the preceding days.
