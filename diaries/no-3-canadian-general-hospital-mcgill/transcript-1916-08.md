## Source
- **Unit:** No. 3 Canadian General Hospital (McGill), Canadian Army Medical Corps
- **Archival reference:** RG 9 III-D-3, vol. 5035, file 853; microfilm reels T-10924–T-10925
- **LAC Collection Search:** Item ID 2005099 — "War diaries - 3rd Canadian General Hospital, Basingstoke", 1915/03/05–1919/06/24

- **Commanding officer:** Colonel H. S. Birkett (signs the volume "H.S.Birkett. Colonel. No. 3 Canadian General Hospital—McGill"); Captain A. H. Pirie, i/c X-Ray Department, proceeded to England on 21 days' sick leave on 8 August and returned from sick leave on 28 August.
- **Location:** Boulogne — the hospital is in the Jesuit College, Boulogne.

## Transcription conventions
- Faithful to the original: spelling, punctuation, abbreviations and capitalisation as written. Typewritten on Army Form C. 2118, "WAR DIARY or INTELLIGENCE SUMMARY", in columns: Place | Date | Hour | Summary of Events and Information | Remarks and references to Appendices.
- `[uncertain]` marks a word or name that could not be read with confidence — never guessed. No word in this month required an `[uncertain]` mark; every entry was legible (verified against zoom crops where noted).
- The Remarks column throughout is the handwritten initials "H.S.B." (Colonel H. S. Birkett).
- On all three diary-text pages the sub-heading "INTELLIGENCE SUMMARY" is struck through (the heading reads "WAR DIARY" with "INTELLIGENCE SUMMARY" crossed out); "(Erase heading not required.)" appears beneath it.
- The August pages are the same print run of Army Form C. 2118 as June: the printed footer reads "T2124. Wt. W708—776. 500000. 4/15. Sir J.C. & S." and the form carries a printed instructions block at top left ("Instructions regarding War Diaries and Intelligence Summaries are contained in F. S. Regs., Part II. and the Staff Manual respectively. Title pages will be prepared in manuscript.").
- Format change from June: instead of "Convoy of N patients received" entries, August records a daily "N patients admitted and N evacuated" figures line for most days. Rows with a blank Date cell are continuation rows of the last date given (e.g. the Williams-Taylor visit and the "11 patients" figures line belong to the 3rd, the McLeod entry and the "181 patients" line to the 8th, the Daru visit to the 30th).
- No "Nothing worthy of comment occurred" entries this month, and no X-Ray report is filed in this volume (unlike June).

## Open questions / uncertain items (August 1916)
- 2 August: two separate figures lines are recorded for this day — "125 patients admitted and 213 evacuated" (dated 2.) and, after the Wallace/McCullough entry, an undated "195 patients admitted and 147 evacuated". Both are transcribed as they stand; the 2nd is the only day with two figures lines, and the 1st and 28th have none. The 30 figures lines in the month cover 29 days (2–27, 29–31 August).
- 3 August: "11 patients admitted and 164 evacuated" — "11" is as typed, but it is far below every other day's admissions (next lowest is 71); a digit may have been dropped in the original typing. Transcribed as "11" (verified by zoom crop `zoom-aug16-p3-top.jpg`).
- 3 August: "Captain J.E.Gallagher" — as typed (verified by zoom crop `zoom-aug16-p3-top.jpg`); the June diary (24 June) gives "Captain J.B.Gallagher", so the initial differs between months.
- 12 August: "Captains Bâby, G.R." — "Bâby" is typed with a circumflex over the 'a' (verified by zoom crop `zoom-aug16-p3-bot.jpg`).
- 12 August: Captains McNeil, Wallace (H.C.) and Ireland reported for temporary duty, were taken on the strength, and the same day proceeded to A.D.M.S., Havre, and were struck off the strength — they passed through the unit in a single day. Note this is a different Wallace from Captain C.K.Wallace (2 August).
- 18 August: "Colonel Sir. William Leishman, A.M.S." — "Sir." carries a full stop as typed (verified by zoom crop `zoom-aug16-p4-mid.jpg`).
- 20 August: the figures line reads "259 patients admitted and 153 evacuated" as corrected — the typist first typed "and 153" after "259 patients" and struck it through before "admitted" (verified by zoom crop `zoom-aug16-p4-mid.jpg`).
- 28 August: the Harrison entry is typed "Corps for duty.and.is.struck.off.strength.of.this.Unit." — the words "and is struck off strength of this Unit" are separated by full stops rather than spaces, as typed (verified by zoom crop `zoom-aug16-p5-top.jpg`).
- 29 and 30 August: the figures lines use "patients received" rather than "patients admitted" — "171 patients received and 214 evacuated" and "111 patients received and 147 evacuated" (verified by zoom crop `zoom-aug16-p5-bot.jpg`).
- 30 August: the Daru visit has no date of its own; its Date cell appears to contain an overstruck "30." — it follows the 30 August figures line and precedes the 31st, so the visit is attributed to 30 August (verified by zoom crop `zoom-aug16-p5-bot.jpg`).
- Contents page (p. 2): the "D" of "Diary Text" appears overstruck/retyped.
- Cover (p. 1): handwritten "3." below "VOLUME XV."; a line of small type runs beneath "CONFIDENTIAL."; no Committee for the Medical History of the War stamp appears on this cover (unlike March).
- Contents page count matches the pages present: "Diary Text 3 Pages" and three diary-text pages are in the volume.
- Colonel H. S. Birkett remains O.C.; the diary is signed by him as "H.S.Birkett. Colonel. No. 3 Canadian General Hospital—McGill."
- Patient totals for August: 4,678 patients admitted and 4,682 evacuated in 30 daily figures lines (2–27, 29–31 August; no figures recorded for 1 or 28 August). The two totals are nearly balanced — the hospital was admitting and evacuating at almost the same rate through the Somme offensive. No casualties are named in this volume — patients are recorded only as daily numbers.
- Visitors this month: Sir Frederick Williams-Taylor, L.L.D., Manager of the Bank of Montreal (3 August); Sir William Peterson, K.C.M.G., Vice Chancellor & Principal of McGill University, on a six-day visit 16–21 August, who also called on Sir Arthur Sloggett (Director General Medical Services in France) and Surgeon-General Woodhouse (D.M.S., Lines of Communication) and visited other hospitals through the D.D.M.S., Boulogne Base (Surgeon-General R.H.S. Sawyer, C.M.G.); Colonel Sir William Leishman, A.M.S. (18 August); and Colonel Daru, Governor of Boulogne, with Lt-Colonel Marin, Chief of the Medical & Sanitary Services of Boulogne (30 August).
- Convalescent policy change, 7 August: convalescent patients from this hospital are henceforth transferred to No. 7 Convalescent Depot instead of No. 1 as formerly.
- No appendices are listed for this volume and none are filed in it.
