## Transcription conventions
- Faithful to the original: spelling, punctuation, abbreviations and capitalisation as written. Typewritten on Army Form C. 2118, "WAR DIARY or INTELLIGENCE SUMMARY", in columns: Place | Date | Hour | Summary of Events and Information | Remarks and references to Appendices.
- `[uncertain]` marks a word or name that could not be read with confidence — never guessed.
- The Remarks column on diary-text pp. 1–2 (1–21 February) is the handwritten initials "J.M.E." (Lt. Col. J. M. Elder, acting O.C.); on diary-text p. 3 (23–29 February) the initials are "H.S.B" (Col. H. S. Birkett). On diary-text p. 2 the 21 February "We have now about 550 patients" row has an initial crossed out with "J.M.Elder" written beneath, underlined; the long 21 February inspection entry is signed "H.S. Birkett" in the Remarks column.
- The sub-heading "INTELLIGENCE SUMMARY" is struck through on diary-text pp. 1–2; on diary-text p. 3 it reads "WAR DIARY or INTELLIGENCE SUMMARY" and is not struck through.
- February entries read "Nothing worthy of **comment** occurred" (as in November and December).

## Page 3 — Diary text, p. 1 (February 1916, p. 3)

> WAR DIARY — Army Form C. 2118 [sub-heading "INTELLIGENCE SUMMARY." struck through]
>
> | Place | Date | Hour | Summary of Events and Information | Remarks |
> | Boulogne. | Feb. 1. | | Made arrangements with Laundry for return of special Operating Room equipment. Well equipped Laundry at Wimille. | J.M.E. |
> | " | 2. | | His Eminence Cardinal Begin of Quebec, Canada, to-day visited and inspected the Hospital. | J.M.E. |
> | " | 3. | 10.00 a.m. | 29 Nursing Sisters and fatigue party, who were left at Camiers, returned for duty. | J.M.E. |
> | " | 3. | | Captain W.T.Ewing detailed for temporary duty as M.O. on Hospital Ship "St. David". | J.M.E. |
> | " | 4. | | Opened up new Wards. Weather good but showery. | J.M.E. |
> | " | 5. | | Fatigue parties put on to open up every Ward inside the walls. | J.M.E. |
> | " | 5. | | False rumour that Zeppelins were coming via Calais. | J.M.E. |
> | " | 6. | | Lt.Colonel J.V.Forrest, A.A.D.M.S., inspected the Hospital and was pleased with progress made. | J.M.E. |
> | " | 6. | | Nursing Sisters comfortably housed in new quarters. | J.M.E. |
> | " | 7. | | Engaged a fatigue party of charwomen to thoroughly scrub Wards. Gardener began work on the grounds to-day. | J.M.E. |
> | " | 8. | | Showers and hail storms and bright sun alternating. Owing to kitchen not being ready we are unable to receive patients to-day. | J.M.E. |
> | " | 9. | | A very beautiful day after frosty night. Progress in Ward fitting very satisfactory. | J.M.E. |
> | " | 10. | | Captain L.H.Thornton returned from leave of absence. | J.M.E. |
> | " | 10. | | General Sawyer, D.D.M.S., made thorough inspection of all parts of the Hospital to-day. Rather disappointed at backward state of kitchen arrangements; ptherwise we are ready for patients. | J.M.E. |
> | " | 11. | 10.00 a.m. | Captain L.J.Rhea proceeded to England on leave of absence. | J.M.E. |
> | " | 11. | 1.00 p.m. | 26 other ranks reported from the C.A.M.C. Training Depôt, Shorncliffe, and are taken on the strength of this Unit from this date. | J.M.E. |
> | " | 12. | | Reported to the D.D.M.S. that after Monday morning we would be ready to receive up to 500 patients. | J.M.E. |
> | " | 12. | | M.O's detailed for duty in the different Wards. | J.M.E. |
> | " | 13. | | Captain W.T.Ewing returned from temporary duty on Hospital Ship "St. David". | J.M.E. |
> | " | 14. | | Warm balmy weather. Got first mail for two days. | J.M.E. |
> | " | 14. | | Severe gale of wind began through night and kept up all day. Occasional showers. | J.M.E. |
> | " | 14. | | 12 Nursing Sisters from No. 1 Canadian General Hospital returned to-day from temporary duty. | J.M.E. |
> | " | 15. | 10.00 p.m. | Received a convoy of 86 patients. | J.M.E. |
> | " | 15. | | X-Ray machines began regular work this morning. | J.M.E. |
> | " | 15. | | Lt.Colonel Forrest and the E.M.O. visited the Hospital to-day giving valuable advice in the reception of patients. | J.M.E. |
>

> | " | 21. | | I have this day made a thorough inspection of the site at present occupied by this Unit, namely the Jesuit College. The alterations which I advised before proceeding to Canada on duty have been completed and are proving very satisfactory. I wish here to record my appreciation of the splendid work which Lt.Colonel J.M.Elder has done in connection with this work. His judgment, foresight and advice, have forwarded the work which I have found to be so satisfactory. I wish also to call attention to the very excellent results which have been obtained in the disposition of the ordnance and technical equipment, which is due entirely to the untiring efforts of my Quartermaster, Honorary Captain David Law. | H.S. Birkett |
> | " | 22. | | Nothing worthy of comment occurred. | H.S.B |

## Open questions / uncertain items (February 1916)
- 1 February: "Wimille" — as typed (village near Boulogne).
- 2 February: "Cardinal Begin" — as typed.
- 10 February: "ptherwise" — as typed; the "o" is missing in the original.
- 15 February: "the E.M.O." — as typed (verified by zoom crop `zoom-feb15-emo.jpg`).
- 16 February: "Honorary Captain R.W.Ridgeway" and "Honorary Captain G.C.Hepburn" — as typed.
- 18 February: "Princess Victoria Concert Party" — as typed.
- 21 February: the date of the final entry, "22.", appears corrected/overtyped in the original.
- 21 February: "We have now about 550 patients in the Hospital" — the Remarks initial was crossed out and "J.M.Elder" written beneath it, underlined.
- 21 February: "ordnance" — as typed; the middle of the word appears overtyped in the original.
- 23 February: "A.T.Henderson" — as typed (December gives "Henderson A.T.").
- 25 February: "Captain R.H.Malone" — as typed (distinct from "Malone R.A." in December).
- 29 February: "Major A.Campbell Geddis" — as typed.
- Cover (p. 1): handwritten "3." below "VOLUME IX." is uncertain; the stamp date reads "26.JUN. '19" with the first digit of the day partly obscured.
- Convoy arithmetic: the convoys recorded are 86 (15 Feb, 10.00 p.m.), 144 (15 Feb, 11.00 p.m.) and 116 (16 Feb, 11.45 p.m.), totalling 346, while the diary states a total of 386 in hospital on 16 February — as typed; the difference is not explained in the diary.
- The unit is now at Boulogne (Jesuit College), having moved from Dannes-Camiers; the hospital re-opened and received its first patients on 15 February, reaching about 550 patients by 21 February.
- No appendices are listed for this volume and none are filed in it.
