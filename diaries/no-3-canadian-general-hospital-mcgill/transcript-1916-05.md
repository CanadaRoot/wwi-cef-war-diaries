- **Archival reference:** RG 9 III-D-3, vol. 5035, file 853; microfilm reels T-10924–T-10925
- **LAC Collection Search:** Item ID 2005099 — "War diaries - 3rd Canadian General Hospital, Basingstoke", 1915/03/05–1919/06/24

- **Commanding officer:** Colonel H. S. Birkett (signs the volume "H. S. Birkett, Colonel, O.C., No. 3 Canadian General Hospital—McGill").
- **Location:** Boulogne — the hospital is in the Jesuit College, Boulogne.

## Transcription conventions
- Faithful to the original: spelling, punctuation, abbreviations and capitalisation as written. Typewritten on Army Form C. 2118, "WAR DIARY or INTELLIGENCE SUMMARY", in columns: Place | Date | Hour | Summary of Events and Information | Remarks and references to Appendices.
- `[uncertain]` marks a word or name that could not be read with confidence — never guessed.
- The Remarks column throughout is the handwritten initials "H.S.B." (Colonel H. S. Birkett).
- On all three diary-text pages the sub-heading "INTELLIGENCE SUMMARY" is struck through (the heading reads "WAR DIARY" with "INTELLIGENCE SUMMARY" crossed out); "(Erase heading not required.)" appears beneath it.
- Printed footer on all three diary-text pages: "T.2134. Wt. W708—776. 500000. 4/15. Sir J. C. & S." (March used the 1875/2353 Wt. W993 and W2514 forms; May uses this T.2134 form throughout).
- May entries read "Nothing worthy of **comment** occurred" (as in March, November and December).
- "Patients" replaces "cases" in the convoy entries this month: every convoy entry reads "Convoy of N patients received" (March read "Convoy of N cases received").

## Open questions / uncertain items (May 1916)
- Cover (p. 1): handwritten "3." below "VOLUME XII."; March had "6." below "VOLUME X." Top right corner carries a handwritten mark reading "NR" over "12" — meaning unknown, reading [uncertain] (verified by zoom crop `zoom-may-p1-corner.jpg` in /tmp, not saved with the diary).
- Cover (p. 1): the Committee stamp date is faint and only partly legible; it appears to read 26 JUN 1919, the same date as the March and December stamps, but this cannot be confirmed from this image.
- Contents (p. 2): cover says "VOLUME XII." while the contents page says "Volume 16-5" — the same dual numbering as March ("VOLUME X." / "Volume 16-3").
- Contents (p. 2): the line "Appendix" has no page count after it, and no appendix pages are included in this volume's five pages. The "Diary Text 3 Pages" count matches the three diary-text pages present.
- 9 May: "of the Hospitals visited" — the final "s" in "Hospitals" looks overtyped in the original; transcribed as "Hospitals".
- 9 May: nine officers taken on the strength — Captains Main, C.G., Neilson, H.E., Gallagher, J.B., Malloch, T.A., Wood, H.G., Martin, A.J., Campbell, A.D., Wallace, C.K., Graves, C.A. (verified by zoom crop).
- 11 May: Nursing Sister E.J.Stuart is taken on the strength, having reported for duty — yet on 8 May the diary records Nursing Sister Edith J. Stuart struck off the strength from 25-2-16 after three months' sick leave granted by a Medical Board. She appears to have returned to duty after the sick leave.
- 16 May: "Major E.W.Archibald to-day admitted to No. 7 Stationary Hospital" — as typed; no reason for the admission is given.
- 18 May: the promotion date reads "from January 4thp 1916" in the original — the character after "4th" looks like a "p" (possibly a mis-struck comma); transcribed as "from January 4th, 1916".
- 20 May: the Russian mission names are as read, but two spellings are uncertain — "Professor R. Orzeneki" (reads "Orzeneki", possibly "Orzenski") is marked [uncertain]; "Dr.A.Mokhuiloff" and "Professor Dr. I Tarassovitch" are transcribed as read. The mission is typed "Mission Médicale de l'Union des Zemstvos Russes".
- 24 May: "Princess Victoria" in "Princess Victoria Concert Party" appears underlined in the original [uncertain — may be a ruled line artefact].
- 11 May: Lt.Colonel J.M.Elder "returned from special leave to Canada" — March records him leaving on 30 March on six weeks' leave of absence to Canada, so the return is about six weeks later, as expected.
- 25–27 May: Lt.Colonel J. McCrae proceeded to England on special leave of absence on 25 May and returned on 27 May — a two-day absence, as typed.
- Convoy totals for May: 987 patients received in 17 convoys (1 May: 69 + 65; 3 May: 45; 4 May: 83; 7 May: 83; 8 May: 53; 10 May: 53; 12 May: 50; 14 May: 76; 16 May: 51; 18 May: 68; 20 May: 53; 22 May: 49; 24 May: 50; 26 May: 56; 28 May: 42; 30 May: 41). No evacuations to hospital ships are recorded this month, and no patients are named — convoy numbers only.
- No casualties are named in this volume. The only enemy action noted is the German aeroplane overflight on 21 May, driven off by the Batteries with no damage done.
- No appendices are filed in this volume, despite the "Appendix" line on the contents page.
- No blank registry-stamped final page this month (unlike December).
