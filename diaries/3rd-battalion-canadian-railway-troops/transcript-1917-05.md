# War Diary — 3rd Battalion, Canadian Railway Troops — May 1917

- **Unit:** 3rd Battalion, Canadian Railway Troops
- **Month:** May 1917 (Volume 3)
- **Source:** Library and Archives Canada, Collection Search Item ID 2004961 (RG 9 III-D-3, vol. 5012, folder 733; reels T-10861–T-10862)
- **Pages:** May 1917, p. 1 – p. 50 (image files page-0042 – page-0089 of the 400-page diary; May pp. 43–44 are absent from the LAC manifest)
- **May 1917 — COMPLETE** (pages 42–89 of 400, 48 physical pages): cover, contents, diary text 1–31 May, Appendices 1–8 (orders, plans and message forms). Transcribed 2026-10-26 (Eighth A, run 1).
- June 1917 onwards — not yet transcribed. Next run continues from page 90 (June 1917, p. 1).

## Transcription conventions
Original spelling, punctuation and abbreviations kept. Handwritten diary text; typed appendices where noted. `[uncertain]` marks words I could not read with confidence — never guessed. No map coordinates are given in the diary; places are recorded as written, with map sheet references as written. "C.M.R." = Canadian Mounted Rifles; "D.C.L.I." = Duke of Cornwall's Light Infantry; "K.O.Y.L.I." = King's Own Yorkshire Light Infantry; "R.W.S." = Royal West Surrey (Queen's); "A. + S.H." = Argyll and Sutherland Highlanders; "L.R.C.E." = Light Railway Construction Engineers (as written); "C.R.T." = Canadian Railway Troops; "O.R." = other ranks; "G.S." = General Service (wagons).

## Pages 3–17 — Diary text (May 1917, pp. 3–17)

Army Form C. 2118, "WAR DIARY or INTELLIGENCE SUMMARY". Place: Barlin throughout May.

### Page 3

> **1-5-17** — Advised to expect Detachment in Second Army area on 2nd inst. Arranged Billets and rations. Weather — fine + warm. Advised by S.S.O. 3rd Canadian Division that unit will be rationed in future by Canadian Corps Troops Supply Column at Houdain [uncertain]. Wired Canadian Corps regarding ordnance services. Tracklaying commenced by A Coy at Neuville St Vaast to connect existing tramway with transhipping yard at Standard Gauge. Attached labor 1st East Surrey Regt 500 OR. Reply received from Canadian [uncertain] regarding Ordnance Services.

> **2-5-17** — Weather fine + warm. Mechanical Transport of C+D Coys from Second Army area arrived Barlin at 5 pm. Advised that personnel of C+D Coys entrained at [place name omitted in original] at 7.59 am + proceeded via Hazebrouck and St Pol. Ballasting finished on Carency-Souchez line by "B" Coy. Labor attached to A Coy 13 officers 500 OR of 4th C.M.Rs. at Thelus.

### Page 9

> **15-5-17** — Weather fine. Iron rations issued today. Advised by O/C dump that this happens once a year. "A" Coy preparing to lay track East of Thelus. This part of line badly shelled. "C" Coy now organised into 13 parties for maintenance work. D Coy continued work but found it advisable to divide up into three parts each working 8 hours throughout the 24 hour day this on account of shelling so as to obtain that part of the day when no shelling was going on. "D" Coy labour attached 4 off 383 OR 19th (Queens) R.W.S. and 2 off 316 OR 4th Devons. C Coy. labour attached 4 off 263 OR D.C.L.I. Pioneers. A Coy labour attached 3 off 411 OR 27th Labour Coy. Arras shelled during night and grading party in forward area was withdrawn. Labour attached 4 off 398 OR 19th (Queens) R.W.S. and 2 off and 316 OR 4th Devons. A Coy. attached labour 4 off 411 or 27th Labour Coy. C Coy attached labour 4 off. 262 OR D.C.L.I. B. Coy. Commenced work on Lens Junction-Souchez line using slips + grabbers.

This unit urgently requires typewriter but all requisitions have so far received no answer. This unit has not been supplied with one.
