# Transcript — 3rd Battalion, Canadian Railway Troops — June 1917

- **Unit:** 3rd Battalion, Canadian Railway Troops (3rd Bn. Can. Rly. Troops)
- **Source:** LAC Collection Search Item ID 2004961 (RG 9 III-D-3, vol. 5012, folder 733; reels T-10861–T-10862)
- **Pages:** June 1917, p. 1 – p. 24 (image files page-0090 – page-0111 of the 400-page diary; June pp. 4–5 are absent from the LAC manifest)
- **June 1917 — COMPLETE** (pages 90–111 of 400, 22 physical pages): cover, document cover, contents, diary text 1–30 June, Appendices 1–3 (motor-cycle establishment messages). Transcribed 2026-10-26 (Eighth A, run 1).
- July 1917 onwards — not yet transcribed. Next run continues from page 112 (July 1917, p. 1).

## Conventions
Original spelling kept. `[uncertain]` marks words not read with confidence — never guessed. Struck-through original text shown as `[struck through in original: "..."]`. Attached-labour figures are transcribed as read from the daily tables; figures flagged `[uncertain]` where the handwriting is ambiguous. "off" = officers (e.g. "2 off 136 OR" = 2 officers, 136 other ranks). "A+S.H." = Argyll and Sutherland Highlanders; "KOYLI" = King's Own Yorkshire Light Infantry; "D.C.L.I." = Duke of Cornwall's Light Infantry.

## Open questions / uncertain items (June)
