# Transcript — 3rd Battalion, Canadian Railway Troops — April 1918

- **Unit:** 3rd Battalion, Canadian Railway Troops (3rd Bn. Can. Rly. Troops)
- **Source:** LAC Collection Search Item ID 2004961 (RG 9 III-D-3, vol. 5012, folder 733; reels T-10861–T-10862)
- **Pages:** April 1918, p. 1 – p. 13 (image files page-0259 – page-0269 of the 400-page diary; April pp. 4–5 are absent from the LAC manifest)
- **April 1918 — COMPLETE** (pages 259–269 of 400, 11 physical pages): cover, document cover, contents, diary text 1–30 April (pp. 6–13). Transcribed 2026-10-28 (Eighth A, run 10, in two working parts); merged and indexed 2026-10-28 (Eighth A, run 10).
- May 1918 onwards — not yet transcribed. Next run continues from page 270 (May 1918, p. 1).

## Conventions
Original spelling kept. `[uncertain]` marks words not read with confidence — never guessed.

> **READABILITY NOTE — April 1918:** The April diary text is written in the same cramped, hurried hand as August 1917–March 1918. Each entry ends with a signature flourish in the margin, rendered here as [signed]; the flourish appears to read "C.M.M" throughout April, the same reading December's and March's transcripts gave. Zoom crops of all eight diary-text pages (pp. 6–13) were made and re-read (`zoom-apr-p6-*.jpg` … `zoom-apr-p13-*.jpg` in this folder) to verify names, numbers and place names.

> **CONTENTS-PAGE NOTE:** The April contents page (p. 3) lists "Diary Text 8 Pages". The diary text is pp. 6–13 (8 pages), so the contents page is exact; no appendices are listed or present.

## Uncertain / open items

- **"Hayplock" (5 & 6 Apr)** — the camp/detachment site reads "Hayplock" in both April entries, flagged [uncertain]. February and March rendered the same word "Harptack" [uncertain] (27 Feb, 29 Mar) and it was never identified; the two spellings are not reconciled.
- **"Easton" (6 Apr)** — in "gun spur at Madagascar Corner Easton", flagged [uncertain]; sense unclear (place? person?).
- **"Chartier" (6 Apr)** — "blowouts at Chartier", flagged [uncertain]; no place of that name identified.
- **"Courcey" (10 Apr)** — "164 feet of track was blown out on the Courcey line", flagged [uncertain].
- **"Cam" (13 Apr)** — "A Coy all on maintenance on Cam [uncertain]"; only "Cam" is legible — possibly an abbreviation of Camblain (Camblain-Châtelain, near Barlin), but that is not asserted.
- **Full-rations authority, company letter (4 Apr)** — "Authority received for full rations for "D" Coy [uncertain]"; the letter reads "D" but the 1 Apr entry says "B" Coy was the one short of full rations, so it may be "B".
- **"Divion" (5 Apr)** — "Divion was selected as rendezvous in event of retirement" reads clearly here; March's 26 Mar entry flagged "Divion" [uncertain] on the Savy North Line route — the two readings agree.
- **Abbreviations (9 & 16 Apr)** — "ADLR(1)" and "ADGT(1)" (9 Apr) and "D.G.T." (16 Apr) transcribed as written; expansions not given in the diary.
- **"McKinnon" (14 Apr)** — camouflage erected "near McKinnon"; reads clearly but the point is not otherwise identified (a siding/junction name, or a person's name).
- **"Territorial" (13 & 30 Apr)** — "A Coy farm at Territorial" reads clearly; the farm/locality is not otherwise identified.
- **"W W Foot" (13 Apr)** — "blowout between Liege Dump St Nicholas and W W Foot" reads clearly; "W W Foot" not otherwise identified.
- **"Ecoures" (19 Apr, ×2)** — the farm plot's place name is read "Ecoures" but the hand is cramped; it may be "Ecurie" (the name written clearly on 22 Apr for the railway system). Flagged [uncertain] both times it occurs.
- **"Cape to Cairo" (21 Apr)** — the "C" Coy sentence ends at "Cape to Cairo" with the signature flourish overlapping the right margin — a following word (probably "line") may be hidden under the flourish. Flagged [uncertain].
- **28 Apr, "D" Coy sentence** — "D Coy reports that portion of track." ends abruptly as written — no verb follows in the original; transcribed as written, no word guessed.
- **28 Apr, "Bath parades at Coy"** — read as "Coy" (singular, as written); the phrasing is terse and may mean the companies generally.
- **Spelling confirmed:** the line name written "Beuvry" (20 and 24 Apr) is the same line March rendered "Bessy [uncertain]" — March's reading was almost certainly "Beuvry".
- **Month-end signature (p. 13):** a flourish reading "J. B. L. Macdonald", Lt. Col., O.C. 3rd Bn. Can. Rly. Troops — the initials follow the March transcript's reading of the same signature; the surname "Macdonald" is clear.

## Page 6 — Diary text (April 1918, p. 6)


Army Form C. 2118 — WAR DIARY or INTELLIGENCE SUMMARY. Place: Barlin throughout. Each entry ends with the signature flourish, rendered [signed]. This page covers 1–4 Apr.

### 1-4-18

> Weather fine. A Coy reports quiet in forward area. #229542 Spr C Arkwright was buried today in Aux Rietz Cemetery. "B" Coy instructed to take over maintenance of Cape to Cairo from Cairo Junction to Priory Junction. "C" Coy sent a detachment to Verquigneul to maintain forward lines in 1st Corps Area. Two captive balloons were brought down by German aeroplane tonight. Owing to change of Corps in "B" Coy area, "B" Coy is not receiving full rations until new authority is received.
>
> [signed]

### 2-4-18

> Weather fine and cool. "A" Coy reports heavy shelling at Vimy Village and 5 blowouts to track. One shell crater took 19 men 6 hours to fill and it took full force of detachment 16 hours to repair the 5 blowouts. "B" Coy reports that preliminary arrangements have been completed to grade new lateral line from Savy north. Artillery Corner was shelled today for first time with H.E. + gas shells. One blowout.
>
> [signed]

### 3-4-18

> Weather unsettled and dull. "A" Coy reports heavy shelling of Cardinal Spur and 3 blowouts. Many gun spurs are being constructed by A Coy. Grading was commenced on new lateral line Savy north with personnel of three O.R. B Coy and 120 Chinese Labour. "C" Coy detachment at Sailly were forced to evacuate by Enemy shell fire, moved to La Bourse and continued maintaining.
>
> [signed]

### 4-4-18

> Weather, dull + showery. Heavy shelling in Vimy Area there being 13 blowouts, requiring 20 sections of track for repairs. "B" Coy reports grading completed for new track at Gorre. "D" Coy reports four blowouts at Plateau Junction. Authority received for full rations for "D" Coy [uncertain].
>
> [signed]

## Page 7 — Diary text (April 1918, p. 7)


Army Form C. 2118 — WAR DIARY or INTELLIGENCE SUMMARY. Place: Barlin throughout. Each entry ends with the signature flourish, rendered [signed]. This page covers 5–8 Apr.

### 5-4-18

> Weather Cloudy + cool showery at intervals. "A" Coy reports 18 blowouts to track on Vimy section and enemy fire being kept up continuously. "B" Coy authorised to construct new line from Barlin to Cairo Junction. "D" Coy selected new site for a camp at Hayplock [uncertain] for a detachment of 50 OR to maintain lines from Villers-au-Bois to Laurel Junction at Mont St Eloy. 19 OR Reinforcements received today at Battalion H.Q. Divion was selected as rendezvous in event of retirement.
>
> [signed]

### 6-4-18

> Weather Cool + showery. Received instructions to draw Rifles + equipment and arm Battalion. "A" Coy reports enemy shelling in forward area and 7 blowouts. A Coy commenced grading an important gun spur at Madagascar Corner Easton [uncertain] and detachment proceeded to Hayplock [uncertain]. "D" Coy reports blowouts at Chartier [uncertain].
>
> [signed]

### 7-4-18

> Weather dull + cloudy. "A" Coy report quiet day no blowouts. "B" Coy reports that considerable damage was done to tracks by the Tanks crossing same last night, no advice having been received of their coming so that precautions might be taken. "D" Coy reports 9 OR back from leave and 8 OR reinforcements.
>
> [signed]

### 8-4-18

> Weather dull, heavy rain at night. "A" Coy reports 12 blowouts due to enemy shell fire, also very short rations issued today, meat being less than 45%. "B" Coy reports that Tanks again crossed tracks during night no warning having been given and damage occured. OC D Coy inspected line along north side of Scarpe River to Fampoux and found track blown out in many places. This with a view to taking over from third army.
>
> [signed]

## Page 8 — Diary text (April 1918, p. 8)


Army Form C. 2118 — WAR DIARY or INTELLIGENCE SUMMARY. Place: Barlin throughout. Each entry ends with the signature flourish, rendered [signed]. This page covers 9–12 Apr.

### 9-4-18

> Weather Cloudy + Cool. On instructions of ADLR(1) a despatch was sent to ADGT(1). A Coy reports a great deal of enemy shelling both in forward areas and back areas. Considerable damage was done to back area lines. "C" Coy reports very heavy enemy shelling in their forward area many blowouts, a number of OR were slightly gassed 3 being admitted to hospital. Capt Wilkinson and detachment were shelled out of ZELOBES and proceeded to HINGES. Artillery Corner was shelled today. No damage done.
>
> [signed]

### 10-4-18

> Weather Fair with a few showers. A Coy reports very heavy enemy shelling all over their lines. A Coy HQ was shelled and numerous blowouts to track are reported. Telephone communication between Battalion HQ and A + D Coys was cut all day. "B" Coy reports that 164 feet of track was blown out on the Courcey [uncertain] line being two direct hits of enemy shells of large calibre. "C" Coy reports their forward lines were ploughed up to look like a ploughed field owing to enemy shell fire. D Coy Head quarters was shelled with shrapnel today.
>
> [signed]

### 11-4-18

> Weather Fair + Fine. Received authority from Administrative Commandant First Army Railways to draw American equipment. "A" Coy today completes new line from Artillery Corner to Lindsay Junction. This line has already proved useful and carries a lot of traffic. Eleven blowouts to track are reported from forward area and one blowout near Ecoivres. "C" Coy reports ANNEQUIN line broken in many places. "D" Coy HQ was shelled today with shrapnel also during night. No casualties to Coy but 10 casualties to attached labour.
>
> [signed]

### 12-4-18

> Weather Fine. Enemy Aircraft very active in this vicinity at night. A Coy reports that all men were engaged on repairing damage to track through Enemy shellfire. "B" Coy turned over the maintenance of lines between Chateau Junction to Laurel Junction to D Coy. E.A. bombed Fouquereuil A Coy's former H.Q. D Coy H.Q. again shelled with shrapnel. "D" Coy building gun spurs.
>
> [signed]

## Page 9 — Diary text (April 1918, p. 9)


Army Form C. 2118 — WAR DIARY or INTELLIGENCE SUMMARY. Place: Barlin throughout. Each entry ends with the signature flourish, rendered [signed]. This page covers 13–16 Apr.

### 13-4-18

> Weather Fine. "C" Coy head quarters moved to 36 B K 4 a 29 near Vaudricourt. Light enemy shelling in forward areas. A Coy all on maintenance on Cam [uncertain] and 5 OR planting potatoes at A Coy farm at Territorial. "D" Coy reports a blowout between Liege Dump St Nicholas and W W Foot, this is a new area for enemy shell fire. Claim made for lost equipment (personal) which was lost during retirement from Bailleul.
>
> [signed]

### 14-4-18

> Weather Cloudy + Cool. General J.W. Stewart visited Battn H.Q. this morning. Rest day and bath parades. All on maintenance patrols. "C" Coy making new camp at Vaudricourt. "D" Coy sent in a complaint regarding camouflage erected alongside track near McKinnon. The Enemy appear to range on the camouflage and the track is constantly being blown up.
>
> [signed]

### 15-4-18

> Weather Cloudy and cool. A Conducting party was today sent to Boulogne to bring back eleven mules to complete establishment. "A" Coy reports rations very poor. Cape to Cairo line was broken in three places by enemy shell fire. "D" Coy reports four blowouts to track at Plateau.
>
> [signed]

### 16-4-18

> Weather Fine. Capt J.S. Lee reported back to Battalion from on command to D.G.T. Electric light was installed in the huts at Battalion H.Q. As a precaution in event of retirement a cache was selected for spare equipment at 36 B S.W. N 24 c 22. Received instructions regarding personnel of 1st Bn. C.R.T. at present with this unit. Heavy shelling in forward areas.
>
> [signed]

## Page 10 — Diary text (April 1918, p. 10)

Army Form C. 2118 — WAR DIARY or INTELLIGENCE SUMMARY. Place: Barlin throughout. Each entry ends with the signature flourish, rendered [signed]. This page covers 17–20 Apr.

### 17-4-18

> Weather Fine. A Coy reports heavy shelling in the forward area. The Dental personnel of 2 officers 6 sergts and 2 privates are now established at this Battalion H.Q. 8 reinforcements sent to A Coy today.
>
> [signed]

### 18-4-18

> Weather Fine Capt Lee J.S. posted to D Coy as 2nd in command. A Coy reports great improvement in rations. "C" Coy reports that Lieut Noble and detachment were compelled to leave La Bourse when they were billeted owing to Enemy shellfire. They returned later and were employed all afternoon repairing breaks.
>
> [signed]

### 19-4-18

> Weather Cloudy + fair. Heavy shelling of forward Areas today. "B" Coy entirely engaged in reballasting tracks the last few days. Lieut Noble's detachment of "C" Coy repaired 11 blowouts in his lines today. A plot of ground at Ecoures [uncertain] was allotted to this Battalion at Ecoures [uncertain] and farming commenced. 65 acres in all.
>
> [signed]

### 20-4-18

> Weather Fine. 9 other ranks of 1st Battn C.R.T. who had been received in exchange for men of this unit who volunteered for Palestine were today returned to 1st Battn. "C" Coy reports the Beuvry line broken in 13 sections and also heavy shelling at Mansion House.
>
> [signed]

## Page 11 — Diary text (April 1918, p. 11)

Army Form C. 2118 — WAR DIARY or INTELLIGENCE SUMMARY. Place: Barlin throughout. Each entry ends with the signature flourish, rendered [signed]. This page covers 21–24 Apr.

### 21-4-18

> Weather Fine with clear atmosphere. Maintenance patrols and unloading ballast took place today. Rest day and bath parades. Heavy shelling today of Vimy detachments. 4 blowouts reported. "B" Coy reports a new ammunition dump spur at Roclincourt completed. D Coy reports track blown out in 2 places at Roclincourt and also at McKinnon siding. "C" Coy repairing blowouts on Cape to Cairo [uncertain]
>
> [signed]

### 22-4-18

> Weather Cloudy and cool. Outskirts of Barlin were shelled with H.E. today. 22 other ranks of this unit who volunteered for service in Palestine with the 1st Battn C.R.T. returned to this unit today. Grading was commenced on a new line connecting up Ecurie system with Roclincourt line and will constitute a balloon line to serve batteries in Roclincourt district should St Catherine's line be destroyed.
>
> [signed]

### 23-4-18

> Weather fine. Barlin shelled at 6-45 pm. Capt and Adjutant C.M. Marpole proceeded to England today with a view to receiving leave to Canada. No shelling in forward area of A Coy reported today. Very light shelling reported in forward areas of "C" Coy. No blowouts reported.
>
> [signed]

### 24-4-18

> Weather Cool showers at night. Barlin shelled during night. Shipment of rifles and equipment received today. Very light shelling in forward areas of A Coy. Rifles were today distributed to the companies and instructions issued for daily drills and such training as the work will allow. "C" Coy reports the Beuvry - La Bassée Canal line open for traffic as far as Red Spur
>
> [signed]

Army Form C. 2118 — WAR DIARY or INTELLIGENCE SUMMARY. Place: Barlin throughout. Each entry ends with the signature flourish, rendered [signed]. This page covers 25–28 Apr.

### 25-4-18

> Weather fair but cloudy. C.S.M. C.B. Croasdale of D Coy granted a commission in this unit. Received notification of the award of the Distinguished Conduct Medal to Sergt J.A. >

### 26-4-18

> Weather dull + damp. Received word that all leave on compassionate grounds to Canada has been cancelled. Barlin shelled at intervals. Light shelling of forward area lines today. "C" Coy moved from Vaudricourt to Hesdigneul 36B E25 d 73.
>
> [signed]

### 27-4-18

> Weather dull and dry. Battalion H.Q. details paraded for the first time armed with rifles and bayonets. A lecture on the care of rifles was given by the R.S.M. D.O. II dated 24-4-18 shows Lieut W.M. Wallace on command to Audruicq Capt D.A. McColl on command to RCE(S) for duty.
>
> [signed]

### 28-4-18

> Weather Fair and cool. Barlin and vicinity shelled at intervals tonight. Rest day. Bath parades at Coy. "D" Coy reports that portion of track. D.O. II dated 24-4-18 shows Lieut D.B. Stillwell attached to HQ Canadian Troops Corps and Capt T.E. Poole detached to CRT depot England.
>
> [signed]

## Page 13 — Diary text (April 1918, p. 13)

Army Form C. 2118 — WAR DIARY or INTELLIGENCE SUMMARY. Place: Barlin throughout. Each entry ends with the signature flourish, rendered [signed]. This page covers 29–30 Apr and closes the month.

### 29-4-18

> Weather Cloudy and cool. HQ details received rifle drill. A Coy reports that all Russians in Coy were collected and sent to one detachment. This is an experiment. "C" Coy paraded to Dental Officer and 100 OR were treated. "B" Coy under instructions of A.D.L.R. made an experiment on a 30 metre curve in using tie plates instead of guard rail. They were a failure and every train derailed. Sergt J.A. McPhail D.C.M. was granted a commission this date in this unit.
>
> [signed]

### 30-4-18

> Weather Unsettled rain at intervals. Planting was completed on A Coy Territorial farm. "C" Coy received instructions to repair lines from HINGES to La Bassée Canal. This is within 1500 yds of front line. Posting notices for commissions in this unit were received today in favour of Sergt W.T. Fraser and Corpl T.F. Francis of B Coy.
>
> [signed]

> [Signed at the foot of the page] J. B. L. Macdonald, Lt. Col.
>
> O.C. 3rd Bn. Can. Rly. Troops.
