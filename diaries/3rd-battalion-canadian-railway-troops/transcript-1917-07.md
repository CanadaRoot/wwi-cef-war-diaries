## Pages 6–21 — Diary text (July 1917, pp. 6–21)

Army Form C. 2118 — WAR DIARY or INTELLIGENCE SUMMARY. Place: Barlin throughout. Each entry signed in the margin (initials). The July entries are narrative — no daily attached-labour tables (the labour distribution is given in Appendix I).

### 1-7-17

> Weather — fair and cloudy.
>
> Three officers reinforcements reported today for duty. 2 OR proceeded today to report to Port Construction Engineer for diving purposes.
>
> This being Dominion Day and the fiftieth anniversary of Confederation the personnel of this observed a holiday so far as was possible.
>
> "D" Coy reports that night working party on Bailleul line could not proceed with work owing to heavy enemy shelling between hours of 9 pm and 2 am.

### 2-7-17

> Weather — fair bright and warm.
>
> Material for light railways: owing to there being very few wooden ties now being used in this area, each company has now got a squad of men assembling steel, with steel sleepers clip bolts all fixed together in sections which are shipped to the different points ready for laying.
>
> "A" Coy observed Dominion Day today instead of yesterday except the necessary maintenance gangs.
>
> 4th Canadian Division moved into this area with H.Q. [uncertain].

### 3-7-17

> Weather — fair bright and warm
>
> One OR proceeded to school of Military Engineering Chatham with a view to obtaining a commission in the Royal Engineers
>
> Received aeroplane photographs showing part of light railway which has been camouflaged. Photos show that work is well hidden except that there is a continuity of line still showing and it is now intended to obviate this by painting the track different colours.
>
> The construction of the new line at Lestrem is now reported as complete as to grading and 6¾ miles of track has been laid.

### 4-7-17

> Weather — fair bright and warm with Thunderstorm.
>
> One OR today received commission in the R.E. and proceeded to Third Army to report for duty.
>
> Rations are reported by all companies as full and very good.
>
> This unit is being provided with cotton overalls and they are badly needed.
>
> Up to present have experienced difficulty in obtaining connection with Army Telephone lines but today received promise of connection with A.D.L.R. exchange to be put in at once

### 5-7-17

> Weather cool and dull
>
> Advised by H.Q. C.R.T. to indent for tents to complete an authorised establishment of 120 tents for battalion. Find that Ordnance will not supply tents at present, all tents being required in other army areas.
>
> Indents for full issue of cotton working clothing made on Ordnance today
>
> "A" Coy reports that the track is now completed and connections made from Thelus to Roclincourt Junction at 51B B19 C55.

### 6-7-17

> Cool and cloudy weather.
>
> Received wire from H.Q. C.R.T. to forward list of tools required for a battalion of Canadian Railway Troops engaged in grading tracklaying and maintaining light railways.
>
> "C" Coy are engaged on maintenance of operated track Ecoivres to Roclincourt and Scarpe River also building tracks at Artillery Corner Transhipment Yard. All Ballast now arrives at Artillery Corner by standard gauge and is unloaded for light railway at different points.

### 7-7-17

> Weather fair and bright.
>
> Grading has continued all week in the heavy cut and large fill on the Laurel A.D. Loop to King [uncertain] line. This is the heaviest work of its kind in the Army area so far as light railways are concerned and was necessitated by a line revision by which not only a much shorter line was obtained but the grade was reduced from [uncertain] to [uncertain].
>
> 44 teams of mules belonging to this unit were used with wheelers and scrapers in grading this cut + fill. "A" Coy reports the "North spur 3046 feet completed."

### 8-7-17

> Weather fair and bright.
>
> All Coy Commanders with their seconds in command met at Battalion H.Q. together with officers of H.Q. details and discussed matters for benefit of Battalion. These meetings necessitated owing to the unit being scattered over large area
>
> Coy Commanders were notified regarding Canadian Corps R.O. No. 1287 in reference to the returning to ordnance of Rubber sponge goggles. This having been necessitated by the enemy using a substance which is poisonous and goggles no longer afford protection.

### 9-7-17

> Weather Cloudy and showers.
>
> Owing to it being necessary for "B" Coy to have 12 camps it has been difficult to obtain good water for them, this has now been overcome by obtaining 100 gallon tanks to be distributed to the different camps.
>
> A spur is being put in by "B" Coy at Villers au Bois on light Railway to supply dump. It is expected that by use of this spur 30 lorries will be relieved of work.
>
> Forwarded a nil return of officers of this unit to H.Q. C.R.T. (as requested) who are not technically suited for work in this unit
>
> Team work on heavy work on Laurel-Portsmouth line completed today

### 10-7-17

> Weather Cloudy and cool.
>
> 7 OR proceeded on leave to England this date and one OR to Canada.
>
> 2 Sergeants and 64 corporals were today confirmed in their rank.
>
> Received urgent wire from Canadian Corps Q regarding completion of spur at Villers au Bois emphasising its importance.
>
> Made request to Canadian Corps for aeroplane photographs to illustrate result of camouflage and painting of track in 51B NW B19C.
>
> Requested A + B Coys to detail each one cook to attend cooking school at Bethune

### 11-7-17

> Weather cool cloudy showers.
>
> "B" Coy reported the spur to supply dump at Villers au Bois now complete and ready for traffic.
>
> "B" company reports a great improvement in rations this week.
>
> 1 OR reported last night from 72nd Battn. Canadian Infantry on transfer.
>
> "A" Coy reports that tracklaying on Laurel-Portsmouth line was completed this date also line between Thelus and Commandant's house was damaged and required new portions of track.

### 12-7-17

> Weather fair bright warm.
>
> Wrote to H.Q. C.R.T. requesting information as to whether we will be in order to requisition reinforcements in place of 19 Locomotive drivers who are on command to No. 1 Locomotive depot AUDRUICQ.
>
> Received aeroplane photographs from Canadian Corps as requested on the 10th inst.
>
> Requested three dozen road maps of First Army area from 1st Field survey company. These were received this date and are for the use of the mechanical Transport.

### 13-7-17

> Weather fair very warm.
>
> Survey party of this unit engaged on light railway work at Trouville rejoined unit. 2 OR proceeded to school of cookery Bethune.
>
> 10 a.m. Ecurie yard which is a main transhipping station from Broad gauge to light railways was shelled today for first time. Shelling delayed work in yard till noon. No damage to L.R. tracks and little damage to Broad gauge. Thelus district was shelled heavily today and SE line broken in two places
>
> Made request to XIII Corps that this unit be supplied with Corps orders

### 14-7-17

> Weather fair bright very warm. Thunder in afternoon.
>
> A Coy reports Laurel-Portsmouth line ready for operation. Ballasting having been completed.
>
> Rations this week reported by all companies as full and very good.
>
> "D" Coy is still working 24 hours on reliefs. Two platoons which are on night work are receiving rum issue. Night work is necessary on Gavrelle line owing to proximity to front line trenches
>
> D. Coy used their rifles today against German aeroplane which appeared over their camp.

### 15-7-17

> Weather dull warm Thunder.
>
> Canteens established at each coy Headquarters. Companies were today advised that the completion of putting distinguishing patches on tunics must be carried out at once. This patch is of red lining cloth sewn on 1" below the collar at the centre of the back [black square drawn in the original] denoting Canadian Railway Tps.
>
> Aeroplane Photo of Thelus Transhipping yard requested from Canadian Corps
>
> Wrote Company Commanders outlining proposed organisation of Engineering department of battalion under Chief Engineer.

### 16-7-17

> Weather cloudy with showers.
>
> Artillery Corner transhipping yard is now in full operation. All material ballast tools etc for Light Railways are in charge of Lieut. Williams and one NCO of this unit. Lieut. Williams has 100 OR attached labour for the purpose of loading and unloading
>
> Made request to A.D.L.R. to be supplied with Track motors for use on maintenance work. Suggested of Fairbanks Morse 4 cylinder track motors which could be converted to 60 cm gauge.

### 17-7-17

> Weather fair and warm
>
> Requested a dozen copies of regulations and instructions in the use of Gas Equipment. Arranging classes of instruction in Gas defensive measures as unit is being gradually moved further forward and more efficiency is required for defensive measures.
>
> First Army light railways are now divided for statistical purposes into the following four subdivisions: (1) Barlin–Savy (2) Maroeuil (3) Lestrem (4) Canadian Corps light railways.
>
> Requested two motor tractors for use of A Coy on tramways in forward area from A.D.L.R.

### 18-7-17

> Weather cloudy with showers + Thunder.
>
> Received enquiry from H.Q. C.R.T. regarding the provision of rifles for the personnel of this unit. Replied that the present issue of 25 rifles per company be increased to 50 rifles per company for use of men in most forward part of light railways and against aeroplanes. A full issue would be considered an encumbrance and a hindrance to the work.
>
> One officer admitted to F.A. suffering from gastritis.
>
> The attached Labour required by this unit in future is shown on Appendix I which does not include Lestrem work

### 19-7-17

> Weather Cloudy with showers.
>
> It was found necessary to again indent for tents required by maintenance parties. Canadian Corps advised that none were available so First Army was applied to and eventually today 30 tents were received from First Army Gun Park on authority of First Army. These tents were immediately distributed to companies for maintenance parties.
>
> Requested information from Divisional Gas Officer re box respirators
>
> Advised "B" Coy that they were to take over maintenance of line from Barlin to Verdrel on Monday 23rd inst. from A Coy and turn over portion of line from Camblain to Ecoivres to "C" Coy.
>
> [Margin: APP II]

### 20-7-17

> Weather Fair and bright.
>
> Lieut. H. G. Dinsdale 102nd Battn Canadian Infantry gave lectures during afternoon and evening on Box Respirators and P.H. helmets + gas defensive measures generally, to the officers and men of A Coy.
>
> Pay parades carried out at A, C, + D Companies today. Owing to pressure of work Pay parades are only being carried out once per month.
>
> Companies notified regarding extra rations allowed this unit in accordance with G.R.O. 1235 para 1(6).

### 21-7-17

> Weather Fair and bright.
>
> Redistribution of companies and labour took place today. This has been necessitated owing to the large amount of construction contemplated at Lestrem in XI Corps area. One platoon of B Coy returned to its company from Lestrem and was replaced by one platoon of "C" Coy. It is intended eventually to move the whole of "C" Coy to Lestrem area, the maintenance now being done by "C" Coy being taken over by "D" and "A" Coys.
>
> 1 officer and 21 OR took over dugouts in Thelus village belonging to Canadian Corps Tramways and got things in readiness for maintaining their lines

### 22-7-17

> Weather fair bright and warm
>
> Engineering department of this unit now organised under Chief Engineer of this unit and now in operation. Each coy has now its engineering staff composed of one Lieut. and 4 OR to carry out + be responsible to the Chief Engineer for the engineering work reports etc within the respective company areas.
>
> Received today from Canadian Corps ordnance 20 dixies extra to complement necessary owing to establishment of new camps and the unit being so much split up.
>
> [Appendices 3 & 4 (reports dated 22.7.17) — see below.]

### 23-7-17

> Weather fair and warm.
>
> [Hour column: "6 pm 22-7-17"]
>
> This unit took over from Canadian Corps Light Railways the maintenance of that portion of Canadian Corps tramways in the forward area from Vimy south towards XIII Corps area. This has necessitated establishing new camps in dugouts for "A" Coy at Farbus Wood and Vimy.
>
> Having received query from H.Q. C.R.T. regarding the employment of French civilian labour replied as shown in appendix
>
> Twenty cars on light railway system are now being allotted for construction purposes only. Chief Eng. of this unit returned from Audruicq having proceeded [uncertain] connected with [uncertain].
>
> [Margin: APP III]

### 24-7-17

> Weather fair and warm.
>
> Further work at Lestrem investigated and reported on by Chief Engineer of this unit to A.D.L.R. This scheme if approved will entail 67 miles of new construction work for this battalion not including all spurs and yards etc.
>
> Advised Veterinary officer to obtain Anti-gas horse respirators at once.
>
> Reference enquiry of gas officer regarding attachment to respirators he replied it was not issued in this army area pending result of experiments.

### 25-7-17

> Weather dull + rain.
>
> 4th Canadian Division moved out of Barlin for front line and 3rd Canadian Division moved in.
>
> The Canadian Corps tramways taken over by A Coy comprises approximately 35 Kilos of lines lying East and North east of Zivy. Two platoons were detailed from Coy Headquarters for this work as follows: Platoon #2 Thelus 2 officers 47 OR and Platoon #3 Chalk Pit 2 officers 51 OR. These parties are divided into 6 day and 6 night parties.
>
> Officers commanding companies were requested to forward nominal rolls showing nationality of all personnel in their companies.

### 26-7-17

> Weather dull + cool.
>
> The work authorised to date at Lestrem was completed today and is now in operation.
>
> Intermittent shelling of working area East of Territorial occurred also daily shelling of Lens-Arras road Thelus and vicinity. Vimy where one platoon of A Coy is, is shelled heavily, usually shrapnel, and especially at night.
>
> Cotton working clothing have now been issued to the Battalion and distributed.

### 27-7-17

> Weather fair and bright.
>
> All Russian subjects in this unit were brought to this Battalion H.Q. for the purpose of filling out forms giving general information regarding their origin. 33 OR reported.
>
> Ten teams of mules were lent to Rotos [uncertain] for grading work at Ecurie.
>
> Attached labour of A Coy has been changed from 92nd Labour Coy to 2nd Labour Coy. Chinese labour 30 OR are now used by this unit for maintenance work from Barlin to Verdrel. Special attention is being paid to getting line to Aix Noulette in such shape as to operate with steam, this line being now very important on account of Lens push.

### 28-7-17

> Weather fine and bright.
>
> Two courtsmartial pending the first since proceeding overseas.
>
> "A" Coy reports that Winchester Dump on Portigue [uncertain] line heavily shelled with H.E. on the 22nd. OC A Coy considers that enemy fire was drawn by steam Locomotive working there. Enemy shelling has been less this week on account of poor visibility.
>
> Russians were returned to Corps today

### 29-7-17

> Weather. Dull and violent thunderstorms.
>
> O.C. "D" Coy reports that Bailleul-Aireux line was damaged in several places by shell fire.
>
> 3 OR proceeded on leave to Paris today. Adjutant proceeded to G.H.Q. and returned.
>
> Received advice from OC A Coy complaining at the price of blue cotton pants for men in Forward Area. This matter being taken up with A.D.O.S. Canadian Corps.
>
> First Army Light Railways moved over 32000 tons of material this month.

### 30-7-17

> Weather. dull + showers.
>
> 1 OR casualty reported today Killed by shell at Vimy
>
> Received advice from "C" Coy complaining at not receiving clean underclothes from Baths. This unit being scattered over whole army area have great difficulty in being recognised by any Corps or Division when requiring clothes. Battalion H.Q. is situated in Canadian Corps area and receive ordnance services from Canadian Corps for all companies

### 31-7-17

> Weather. Dull cool and rain in evening.
>
> Effective strength of battalion as at the 29th inst — 38 officers 1035 OR not including 34 OR in hospital.
>
> Work completed during month on 60 cm light railways as per attached
>
> Escort proceeded to Paris to bring back 1 OR detained.
>
> Received advice regarding a proposed move from this area.
>
> [Margin: App IV]
>
> [Signed] J. B. Macdonald, Lt. Col.
>
> O.C. 3rd Bn. Can. Rly. Troops.

## Open questions / uncertain items (July)
- July pp. 4–5 are absent from the LAC manifest; the diary text begins at p. 6.
- The contents list describes Appendix 1 as a "Report issued by 3rd Battalion C.R.T. dated......" — the date is illegible in the contents page; Appendix I (the labour distribution table) is undated.
- Appendix 2 is described in the contents as an "Order issued to 3rd Bn. C.R.T. dated 1.8.17" — the page filed (p. 23) is a letter from the O.C. dated 1 August 1917 about S.B. respirators, not an order.
- The 7 July entry's "Laurel A.D. Loop to King [uncertain] line" and the grade figures are partly illegible.
- The 27 July entry's "Ten teams of mules were lent to Rotos [uncertain]" — the recipient name is unclear.
- The 28 July entry's "Winchester Dump on Portigue [uncertain] line" — the line name is unclear.
- "Territorial" is now confirmed as a line/place name (Appendix I: "Laurel Loop to Vimy via Portsmouth, Territorial, Thelus – Ecurie"), resolving the uncertainty noted in the May and June transcripts.
- Names Lieut. Williams, Lieut. H. G. Dinsdale, Major Barber, Major Stewart, Major Mackenzie, Major Duff — spellings as read.
