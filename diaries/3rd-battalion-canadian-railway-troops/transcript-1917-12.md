- **Source:** LAC Collection Search Item ID 2004961 (RG 9 III-D-3, vol. 5012, folder 733; reels T-10861–T-10862)
- **Pages:** December 1917, p. 1 – p. 19 (image files page-0192 – page-0208 of the 400-page diary; December pp. 4–5 are absent from the LAC manifest)
- **December 1917 — COMPLETE** (pages 192–208 of 400, 17 physical pages): cover, document cover, contents, diary text 1–31 December, Appendices 1–3 (congratulatory messages, 28 Nov – 24 Dec 1917). Transcribed 2026-10-27 (Eighth A, run 5).
- January 1918 onwards — not yet transcribed. Next run continues from page 209 (January 1918, p. 1).

## Conventions
Original spelling kept. `[uncertain]` marks words not read with confidence — never guessed.

> **READABILITY NOTE — December 1917:** The December diary text is written in the same cramped, hurried hand as August–November (each entry signed "C. M. M." [uncertain] in the margin). Many words could not be read with confidence and are marked [uncertain]; several sentences are only partly legible. Zoom crops of all diary-text pages (pp. 6–16) were made and re-read (`zoom-dec-p6-*.jpg` … `zoom-dec-p16-*.jpg` in this folder), with extra enlargements of the hardest lines — the transcription below records what could be read reliably and flags the rest. A future re-read with higher-resolution images may resolve the flagged passages.

> **CONTENTS-PAGE DISCREPANCY:** The December contents page (p. 3) lists "Diary Text 7 Pages" and "Appendices 1 to 3", but the diary text in fact runs 11 pages (pp. 6–16, 1–31 December) and the appendices are pp. 17–19 only. The contents page undercounts the diary text; the month is complete to 31 December, signed by Lt.-Col. J. B. L. Macdonald.

## Reconciliation notes — December 1917

- **Sgt Cooney casualty — two readings to reconcile:** 16 Dec (p. 11) reads "Pte Cooney [uncertain] No 279834 of 'A' Coy killed by enemy shell"; 30 Dec (p. 16) reads "No 22983 & [uncertain] Sgt Cooney J. D. awarded M.M. but He was unfortunately killed by shell fire 16th inst." Both are transcribed exactly as written; the rank (Pte vs Sgt), the number (279834 vs 22983) and the M.M. award differ between the two entries and are NOT reconciled here — the same man, Sgt J. D. Cooney, is the likeliest reading, but that is not asserted. A future re-read of pp. 11 and 16 may resolve it.
- **"Cape to Cairo" vs "Capt. to Cairo":** the line name is established as the "Cape to Cairo Line" on 8 Dec (p. 8). The recurring phrase on 20, 23, 27 and 28 Dec was read as "probing [uncertain] Capt. to Cairo Line" (pp. 13–15) — this is very likely the same "Cape to Cairo Line" formula misread in the cramped hand, but it is transcribed as read and flagged [uncertain], not silently corrected.
- **Election votes:** 18 Dec records "Votes Polled 938 out of Total 936 Eligible" — more votes than eligible, transcribed as written.
- **Place names:** "Marqueffles" (3, 12, 18 Dec) and "St. Zivy" (21, 26 Dec) are read as written and flagged [uncertain]; "Maroeuil" (23 Dec) as written.

### Page 17 — Appendix 1 (December 1917, p. 17)

> [Handwritten top left: "(Copy)" circled; "Appendix 1" underlined]
>
> Headquarters, Overseas Military Forces of Canada, Argyll House, 246 Regent Street, London, W. 1.
>
> November 28th, 1917.
>
> My dear General Stewart,
>
> I wish to take this opportunity of conveying to you my appreciation of the wonderful work being done by the troops under your command.
>
> It was a great pleasure for me to see the work they are doing, and I hope in the near future to be able to spend more time in visiting the various Companies.
>
> Please convey the above remarks to the Officers, N.C.O's and Men of the Canadian Railway Troops, and also state that in the work they are doing, they are making it possible for the Infantry and Artillery to carry out their work and bring the War to a victorious end.
>
> Yours Sincerely,
>
> (Sgd) R.E.W. Turner.
>
> Brig-General J.W. Stewart, C.M.G.
> G.O.C. & Dir. General of Transportation, (Construction), B.E.F., France.
>
> [Oval registry stamp, bottom left: "23017 / 18 JAN 1918" — surrounding stamp text illegible.]

**Appendix 1 — summary:** Letter dated 28 November 1917 from (Sgd) R. E. W. Turner, Headquarters Overseas Military Forces of Canada, London, to Brig-General J. W. Stewart, C.M.G., G.O.C. & Director General of Transportation (Construction), B.E.F., France, conveying appreciation of the Canadian Railway Troops' work and asking Stewart to pass the remarks to the Officers, N.C.O's and Men.
