- **April 1917 — COMPLETE** (pages 22–41 of 400): cover, contents, diary text 1–30 April, Appendices 1–10. Transcribed 2026-10-26 (Eighth A, run 1).
- April 1917 onwards — not yet transcribed. Next run continues from page 42 (May 1917, p. 1).

## Transcription conventions
Original spelling, punctuation and abbreviations kept. Handwritten diary text; typed appendices where noted. `[uncertain]` marks words I could not read with confidence — never guessed. Struck-through original text is shown as `[struck through in original: "..."]`. No map coordinates are given in the diary; places are recorded as written. The diary's "Bar" readings are barometer readings. "A.D.L.R." = Assistant Director of Light Railways; "L.R.C.E." = Light Railway Construction Engineers (as written); "C.R.T." = Canadian Railway Troops; "O.R." = other ranks; "G.S." = General Service (wagons).

## Pages 3–9 — Diary text (April 1917, pp. 3–9)

Army Form C. 2118, "WAR DIARY or INTELLIGENCE SUMMARY". Place: Barlin throughout April.

### Page 3

> **1-4-17** — Weather cold — showers in afternoon Bar 30.2. Transport etc as shown in app No 1 left Barlin at 1 pm with mules — 45 O.R. for Froissy with Tools for 1st Bn CRT. advised H.Q. C.R.T. advised G.O.C. Canadian Railway Troops regarding completion of tracklaying [uncertain — "See app 2" in margin].
>
> [Margin: "app 1." / "app 2."]
>
> **2-4-17** — Received wire from O.C. 1st Bn CRT, requiring Forges, replied by app. Weather — sleet — Heavy snow in afternoon — gale at night. Work proceeding as usual.
>
> [Margin: "app 2 A"]
>
> **3-4-17** — Weather — Snowing — rain in afternoon. Work continued as usual. Water Tank was completed by this unit in Barlin Yard.
>
> **4-4-17** — Weather — fair — cloudy all day — Advised by O.C. C+D Coys Second Army area, that they have orders to move to Dover camp, Ploegsteert. They are to hand over work to 7th Battn Canadian Railway Troops and will cease to be attached to 39th Division 2nd Corps. Wired O.C. 1st Battn C.R.T. requesting adzes & squares etc. to come by returning lorries. The operating department of Light Railways took over the line from construction dept.
>
> [Margin: "app. 3"]
>
> **5-4-17** — Weather — fine. Bar. 30.5. Transport for 1st Battn arrived at Froissy, handed over & received receipt. Light Railway Engines (Baldwin Type) arrived in Barlin Yard. General Stewart arrived at noon.

### Page 4

> **6-4-17** — Weather fine Bar. C + D Coys in Second Army area moved to Dover Camp Ploegsteert. 6 pm Lt. Morrison and 45 O.R. arrived back from Froissy after [sentence ends here in the original]. Reports of work for week ending 6th April for A + B Coys attached hereto. Board of enquiry for mules lost on night of 4th & 5th held today. Operating department appear to be having difficulty with Baldwin Engines. A.D.L.R.(1) has requested 4 locomotive Engineers from A Coy to be detailed for this work.
>
> [Margin: "app 4" / "app"]
>
> **7-4-17** — Major Macdonald with Major McHugh + Lt. Walpole proceeded to Dover Camp Ploegsteert to look over other portion of Battalion. Sgt Ward and 8 men proceeded to Audruicq to build Piledrivers [uncertain]. Weather fine Bar — 4 Loco Engineers of A Coy detailed to drive engines on M.G. Railway + help R.E. Engineers with Baldwin type of engine, at request of A.D.L.R.(1)
>
> **8-4-17** — Weather — fine. Bar. A Coy working in Barlin yard and Ballasting line between [uncertain] and Fosse 9. Major Macdonald returned from Dover Camp. Lorrie returned from Audruicq. Telegram received from + sent — H.Q. C.R.T. regarding Nominal roll of Locomotive Engineers. Four Loco Engrs employed on Engines (M.G.)
>
> **9-4-17** — Weather — fine strong wind. A Coy ballasting. Lorrie left at noon for Audruicq to obtain tools on order of A.D.L.R. Four Engineers (Loco) employed on Light Railway Engines pulling ammunition.
>
> **10-4-17** — Weather — wind & hail. Lieut. Stillwell and 1 O.R. proceeded to C+D Coys.

### Page 5

> **11-4-17** — Weather — cold + cloudy. 3 officers and 300 O.R. of Queens West Surrey Regt left this morning for Mont St Eloy and are no longer attached to this unit for duty.
>
> **12-4-17** — Weather — Blizzard + Rain. 125 O.R. of the Northamptonshire Regt reported for work today at 2 pm. They are to be utilised on maintenance work between Barlin + Verdrel Wood. They were in charge of 2nd Lieut. [uncertain]. Paymaster proceeded from Barlin to Ploegsteert to pay C+D Coys. Gouy yard complete.
>
> [Margin: "app 4 A"]
>
> **13-4-17** — Weather — fine. Major Macdonald, Major McHugh and Major McGregor proceeded as instructed by A.D.L.R. to look over ground at Neuville St Vaast with view to putting transhipping yard there. Received wire from H.Q. C.R.T. to send 21 cases of stationery to Audruicq. Lorrie proceeded [uncertain]. Reports for week ending 13th April for A+B Coys attached hereto. Received 87 O.R. Northamptonshire Regt attached for work who reported at 2 pm.
>
> [Margin: "App 5"]
>
> **14-4-17** — Weather — fine. Major Macdonald proceeded with L.R.C.E. to Carency valley to look over metre gauge Railway with reference to utilising same in conjunction with Barlin L.R. system. Lt. Hamilton and 30 O.R. of "A" Coy proceeded with tents to Neuville St. Vaast to make Survey of site for transhipping yard. Received 13 O.R. Northamptonshire Regt making a total of 100 O.R. Reported 7 am.
>
> [Margin: "App 6"]

### Page 6

> **15-4-17** — Weather — rain. Sent complete Battalion Nominal roll to H.Q. C.R.T. Lieut. McLeod and Lieut. Churchill left with 30 O.R. of A Coy to proceed to Neuville St. Vaast to grade sidings. "A" Coy. worked night shift last night and are ballasting between Barlin and Fosse 9 on account of no ammunition trains going over railway. Ration dump moved from Barlin to Villers au Bois.
>
> [Margin: "app. 7"]
>
> **16-4-17** — Weather — Rain at night — fine otherwise. Grading in progress at Neuville St. Vaast, Ballasting at Barlin. Received instructions from L.R.C.E.(1) regarding survey to be made at once from Neuville St. Vaast to Bailleul as soon as possible.
>
> **17-4-17** — Weather — rain — sleet. Major McGregor + Major McHugh proceeded to Neuville St. Vaast regarding survey of line to Bailleul. Grading completed at Neuville St. Vaast and men waiting steel to Bailleul. Advised by A.D.L.R. to load Ballast all day + night and arrange for same to be sent to "B" Coy for distribution on "B" Coy area. All arrangements for night-shift made.
>
> **18-4-17** — Weather — rain. "A" Coy loading Ballast for "B" Coy area. Major Macdonald proceeded to Souchez in reference to work to be done by "B" Coy. Arrangements made to ration men at Neuville St. Vaast at Mont-St-Eloy. Savy transhipping yard completed.
>
> [Margin: "app. 8"]

### Page 7

> **19th** — Weather — fine. Received instructions from H.Q. C.R.T. regarding organisation of transport section which is now to be separated from the Companies. 33 O.R. of A Coy proceeded to "B" Coy at Gouy for work on Carency-Souchez line. Detachment of this unit received orders to move camp to 28 SW T20 a 9.4. [uncertain — map reference] in Second Army Area. Lieut. Churchill relieved Lieut. Hamilton as O.I.C. at Neuville St. Vaast.
>
> **20th** — Weather — fine. Capt. Conroy Q.M. proceeded to Camp Berlette and loaded cars with equipment and consigned it to Audruicq. Lieut. Morrison W.A. proceeded from Barlin to join detachment of this unit in Second Army Area. Detachment of this unit in Second Army area has strength 12 officers 413 men having lost 30 other ranks since arriving in France. They have now 1000 men of other units attached for duty. Reports for week ending 20th attached.
>
> [Margin: "app 9"]
>
> **21st** — Weather — fine. "A" Coy received issue of small box respirators and received instruction from Gas officer of 3rd Canadian Division. 33 O.R. of A Coy proceeded to Souchez to work on Carency-Souchez line. These men have not yet been able to work on line being held up by Canadian Corps Light Railways.
>
> **22nd** — Weather — fine. Received announcement of gazetting of two promotions viz Capt. A.R. Mackenzie [uncertain] to be Major 20-1-17, Lieut. J.J. Sullivan [uncertain] to be Capt. 20-1-17.

### Page 8

> **23rd** — Weather — fine. Eleven Locomotive Engineers left Barlin for Audruicq to report to No. 1 Railway Depot. First casualty reported in Battalion Sapper Willership killed by shell near Ploegsteert. Consider Locomotive Engineers should be provided with overalls. Uniforms are getting spoilt with grease + oil of Engines. 13 ton Lorrie proceeded to Berlette to load track material for Neuville St. Vaast but returned empty owing to car being inaccessible.
>
> **24th** — Weather — fine. 2 Locomotive Engineers from Barlin and six from Second Army area proceeded to Audruicq to report to No. 1 Railway Depot. At Request of L.R.C.E. an N.C.O. was detailed for work on line from Bully-Grenay to Lievin. Our unit is not doing this work. 33 O.R. of A Coy at Souchez reported to A Coy at Neuville St. Vaast.
>
> **25th** — Weather — fine. 22 mule teams 13 G.S. Wagons 18 dump wagons proceeded to Neuville St. Vaast with 90 Other ranks and Ford lorry. An N.C.O. reported to R.E.s at Bully Grenay. Lieut. Hamilton proceeded to Neuville St. Vaast. 120 O.R. of R.E.s are attached for duty to A Coy at Neuville St. Vaast.
>
> **26th** — Weather — fine. First casualties reported from "B" Coy. Sapper A McDonald wounded by shrapnel in head at Souchez. Sapper Potts shell shock. Advised 7th Battn C.R.T. to send for equipment to be issued to them.

### Page 9

> **27** — Weather — fine. Reports for week ending 27th attached. Received orders to move the following transport to 4th Battn under H.Q. C.R.T. for move order for 16 O.R. 20 mules and 5 G.S. wagons. This move occasioned by a reduction in establishment of transport. 120 other ranks R.E. left A Coy. 50 more reported. The mules and slips were used at Neuville St. Vaast but owing to the ground being shell torn they were a failure. Grading was started. 620 O.R. of 116th Battn Canadians attached to A Coy. Lieut. [uncertain] and two N.C.O.s were detailed for work at St. Nicolas near Arras.
>
> [Margin: "app 10"]
>
> **28** — Weather — fine. General Stewart together with Col McDonell and Col Macdonald proceeded over the works of this unit at Neuville St. Vaast and Souchez. 11 am Lorrie which was lent to 1st Battn C.R.T. returned to Barlin. The officers and men of 116th Battn Canadians finished work at 3 pm as they had to report to another area. No others substituted.
>
> **29** — Weather — fine. Capt. Corkery + Lieut. Williams proceeded from Barlin to Audruicq in connection with equipment. A. Coy. at Neuville St. Vaast had no attached labour today.
>
> **30** — Weather — fine + 17 officers and 500 O.R. 1st East Surreys reported for work to "A" Coy at Neuville St. Vaast. Capt. Corkery returned from Audruicq. Lt. Williams stayed at Audruicq in charge of Canadian Railway Troops stores.
>
> [Signed] *J.B.L. Macdonald Lieut-Col*

Note on appendix numbering: the April volume files two reports under several appendix numbers (one from "A" Coy, one from "B" Coy). >
> Under authority First Army Q.705 you will despatch by road 1 Officer, 21 O.R., 80 mules, 20 G.S. waggons, 2 motor cycles, 2 trucks with tools, to FROISSY in Fourth Army area. Move will commence April 1st. Billets as follows, night 1-2 VALHUN to be arranged by 13th Corps. Night 2-3rd HUNCO. Night 3-4th DOULLENS. Night 4-5th VARENS. Arrive at Froissy evening of 5th.
