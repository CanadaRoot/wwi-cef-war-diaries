# Transcript — 3rd Battalion, Canadian Railway Troops — February 1918

- **Unit:** 3rd Battalion, Canadian Railway Troops (3rd Bn. Can. Rly. Troops)
- **Source:** LAC Collection Search Item ID 2004961 (RG 9 III-D-3, vol. 5012, folder 733; reels T-10861–T-10862)
- **Pages:** February 1918, p. 1 – p. 12 (image files page-0222 – page-0231 of the 400-page diary; February pp. 4–5 are absent from the LAC manifest)
- **February 1918 — COMPLETE** (pages 222–231 of 400, 10 physical pages): cover, document cover, contents, diary text 1–28 February (pp. 6–12). No appendices. Transcribed 2026-10-27 (Eighth A, run 7).
- March 1918 onwards — not yet transcribed. Next run continues from page 232 (March 1918, p. 1).

## Conventions
Original spelling kept. `[uncertain]` marks words not read with confidence — never guessed.

> **READABILITY NOTE — February 1918:** The February diary text is written in the same cramped, hurried hand as August 1917–January 1918. Each entry ends with a signature flourish in the margin, rendered here as [signed]. Most entries read clearly at full size; zoom crops of all diary-text pages (pp. 6–12) were made and re-read (`zoom-feb-p6-*.jpg` … `zoom-feb-p12-*.jpg` in this folder) to verify names, numbers and place names. The transcription below records what could be read reliably and flags the rest. A future re-read with higher-resolution images may resolve the flagged passages.

> **CONTENTS-PAGE NOTE:** The February contents page (p. 3) lists "Diary Text 7 Pages" and no appendices. The diary text is pp. 6–12 (7 pages), so the contents page is exact.

## Pages 6–7 — Diary text (February 1918, pp. 6–7)


Army Form C. 2118 — WAR DIARY or INTELLIGENCE SUMMARY. Place: Barlin throughout. Each entry ends with the signature flourish, rendered [signed]. Page ranges: p. 6 covers 1–4 Feb; p. 7 covers 5–8 Feb.

### 1-2-18

> Weather fine. Notified that tractors are exceeding the speed limit and damaging track.
>
> [A small circled mark precedes the number — see note below] 1039162 Sapper A.J. Borrows submitted designs for inventions in connection with Aeroplanes which have been forwarded to Board of Invention Admiralty.
>
> [signed]

### 2-2-18

> Weather fine and frosty. Notified that the following four other ranks were awarded the military medal for gallantry on the night 11th and 12th January
>
> No 668093 Spr. X. Brabant.
>
> 1039652 " A. Smith
>
> 1029623 " E. Watts
>
> 124366 " O. Sowler.
>
> [signed]

### 3-2-18

> Weather fine. Enemy planes heard overhead about 6 pm. Lights extinguished and cover taken. No bombs dropped. "B" Coy reports the completion of tracklaying on the portion of the Cape to Cairo line allotted to the Coy. A. Coy reports that ploughing operations are proceeding at their plot at Territorial [uncertain]
>
> [signed]

### 4-2-18

> Weather fine. Notified that the following honours have been awarded for gallantry on the night 11th and 12th January.
>
> Military Cross. Lieut. M. Helyer [uncertain]
>
> Distinguished Conduct Medal #192207 Cpl. Dors J. [uncertain]
>
> [signed]

### 5-2-18

> Weather fine. Major Baker O.C. "A" Coy this unit posted to 8th Battn Canadian Railway Troops to take over command of Battalion. D. Coy reports that arrangements are made with XIII Corps [XIII Corps — struck through in the original] that traffic on section between Maroeuil and Bray Jct. [uncertain] are to be suspended for 5 hours per day to allow relaying of steel.
>
> [signed]

### 6-2-18

> Weather fine and clear. "D" Coy reports moving 13 OR from St Aubin to Bray Junction [uncertain]. "C" Coy commenced grading operations on the line from Cape to Cairo line to La Marais dump [uncertain]. "B" Coy working on a line from Cape to Cairo line to same Headquarter Dump [uncertain] situated S. of Bethune.
>
> [signed]

### 7-2-18

> Weather dull with rain. Complained to A.D.L.R. regarding type of Engines being used on Light Railways (1200 and 1300 class) which owing to large wheel base and flangeless wheels in middle of drivers are causing damage to track on curves and to turnouts which are of 30 metre radius. "C" Coy are unloading metre gauge sleepers from Barge on La Bassee Canal.
>
> [signed]

### 8-2-18

> Weather fine. Lieut.-Col. Macdonald returned from Leave. Major H. J. Baker [uncertain] proceeded today to join 8th Battn Canadian Railway Troops. Applied to Administrative Commandant for authority to cultivate two plots of land containing 40 acres in accordance with G.R.O. 3123.
>
> [signed]

## Page 8 — Diary text (February 1918, p. 8)


Army Form C. 2118 — WAR DIARY or INTELLIGENCE SUMMARY. Place: Barlin throughout. Each entry ends with the signature flourish, rendered [signed]. This page covers 9–12 Feb.

### 9-2-18

> Weather fair cloudy no rain. 34 OR and 29 teams of mules proceeded to Marles-les-Mines to work on new Standard Gauge line - Lillers to Marles-les-Mines under supervision of 5th Battn Canadian Railway Troops. A. Coy reports heavy shelling at Vimy but no damage to track, this being the first shelling of consequence in this area for ten days.
>
> [signed]

### 10-2-18

> Weather fair. Experiments made with briquettes made of coal slack and clay manufactured by Canadian Corps Coal supply officer and found very satisfactory. A Coy reports 4 breaks in track at Vimy system due to Enemy shelling.
>
> [signed]

### 11-2-18

> Weather fair. This unit having Chinese labour allotted to it - the work was stopped today this date being Chinese New Year. Five breaks in Track due to Enemy shelling are reported from A Coy at Vimy though shelling is reported to be light. Lieut Fleming transferred from A Coy to B Coy. "D" Coy reports that Arleux line is being reballasted.
>
> [signed]

### 12-2-18

> Weather fine and bright. Quartermaster proceeded on leave. No. 826454 Sapper F. Hulme wounded today by shrapnel. This man belongs to "D" Coy. Received instructions that Cape to Cairo must be finished by end of this month. Three breaks in track due to Enemy shelling reported from A Coy detachments at Vimy.
>
> [signed]

## Pages 9–10 — Diary text (February 1918, pp. 9–10)


Army Form C. 2118 — WAR DIARY or INTELLIGENCE SUMMARY. Place: Barlin throughout. Each entry ends with the signature flourish, rendered [signed]. Page ranges: p. 9 covers 13–16 Feb; p. 10 covers 17–20 Feb.

### 13-2-18

> Weather dull, rain. Advised by H.Q. C.R.T. that all working tools in future will be supplied by A.D.L.R.
>
> A Coy reports no breaks in line. Lieut. Stilwell proceeded to Forestry Corps H.Q. with a view to obtaining transfer to Forestry Corps.
>
> [signed]

### 14-2-18

> Weather fair. A German Balloon was picked up today at 4.20 pm by Capt Wilkinson "C" Coy near Bethune. This was a red balloon such as is used by the Germans for scattering newspapers behind British & French lines. One newspaper was tied to balloon called Gazette des Ardennes
>
> [signed]

### 15-2-18

> Weather fine. Artillery drum fire heard all day from the direction of Arras. 3 OR reinforcements reported from C.A.S.C. pool for Mechanical Transport. Wrote A.A.G. 3rd Echelon regarding disposition. German balloon and paper found yesterday handed over to Intelligence 1st Corps.
>
> [signed]

### 16-2-18

> Weather fine and bright. Medical board (Colonel Brown) commenced examinations at this Battn H.Qrs reclassifying men in categories. 58 OR of this unit, 41 OR 7th Battn C.R.T., 30 OR 5th Battn C.R.T., and other details were examined. D Coy reports continuous enemy shelling during the day.
>
> [signed]

### 17-2-18

> Weather fine and bright. Medical board concluded its sittings.

>
> [signed]

### 18-2-18

> Weather fine and bright. A Coy reports heavy enemy shelling of Vimy detachments. The men of this unit's Mersey Trench detachment were forced to leave camp and take cover.
>
> A.F.B. 213 Strength of unit - 40 officers 963 other ranks
>
> [signed]

### 19-2-18

> Weather fine and bright. Colonel Macdonald accompanied A.D.L.R.(1) on inspection of Cape to Cairo. Received notification from H.Q. C.R.T. that the Transport officers C.R.T. are to be captains and Transport Sergt to have rank and pay as R.Q.M.S.
>
> Received a reinforcement of 48 other Ranks from England.
>
> [signed]

### 20-2-18

> Weather cloudy with rain. Sent out instructions to all Coys to turn into Quartermasters stores all P.H. Helmets. There have been ten breaks in track due to enemy shell fire reported by A Coy this week necessitating 164 Sections of rails to be replaced.
>

## Reconciliation notes — February 1918

- **Gallantry awards of 11–12 January — now fully accounted for:** the 12 January entry records "One officer and 5 OR were recommended for decorations on account of gallant conduct on the night of 11th + 12th." The February awards complete the set: Military Cross to Lieut. M. Helyer (4 Feb), Distinguished Conduct Medal to Cpl. Dors J. (4 Feb), and Military Medals to Sprs. Brabant, Smith, Watts and Sowler (2 Feb) — one officer and five other ranks, matching the January recommendation exactly.
- **Month signed by Lt.-Col. Macdonald, not Major Stewart:** the February diary text closes on p. 12 with the signature "J. B. L. Macdonald, Lt. Col., O.C. 3rd Bn. Can. Rly. Troops." Lt.-Col. Macdonald had returned from leave on 8 February and accompanied A.D.L.R.(1) on the Cape to Cairo inspection on 19 February. January was signed by Major J. [uncertain] Stewart in Macdonald's absence.
- **German propaganda balloons (14–15, 22 Feb):** a red balloon carrying the newspaper "Gazette des Ardennes" was picked up near Béthune by Capt. Wilkinson (C Coy) at 4.20 pm on 14 Feb; balloon and paper were handed to Intelligence, 1st Corps, on 15 Feb. A second German newspaper — an appeal to Irish soldiers to revolt — was dropped on 22 Feb and forwarded to the Administrative Commandant.
- **Medical board (16–17 Feb):** Colonel Brown's board reclassified men; 58 OR of this unit, 41 OR of the 7th Bn C.R.T. and 30 OR of the 5th Bn C.R.T. were examined. Eleven OR classed B iii were sent to the Canadian General Base Depot on 27 Feb; one OR classed B iii had already gone on 22 Feb.
- **Strength return (18 Feb):** A.F.B. 213 gives the unit strength as 40 officers and 963 other ranks.
- **Cape to Cairo nearly finished:** "C" Coy began ballasting their portion on 17 Feb; by 28 Feb only 1/2 mile of ballasting remained and the line was "urgently required by this date". B Coy had completed its allotted portion on 3 February.
- **Chinese New Year (11 Feb):** work by the Chinese labour allotted to the unit was stopped for the day, "this date being Chinese New Year" — transcribed as written; 11 February 1918 was indeed Chinese New Year.
- **Cape to Cairo deadline:** 12 Feb records instructions that the Cape to Cairo line "must be finished by end of this month" — the southern section's tracklaying was already reported complete on 28 January, and B Coy completed its allotted portion on 3 February.
- **"Bray" again:** "Bray Jct." (5 Feb) and "Bray Junction" (6 Feb) are read as written and flagged [uncertain], as in January ("Bray Junction", 3 and 5 Jan). The local place is more likely Bruay (Bruay-la-Buissière, near Barlin), but the writer spells it "Bray" throughout, so it is transcribed as written.
- **"Territorial" again:** "their plot at Territorial" (3 Feb) is read as written and flagged [uncertain], as in January ("to cultivate five acres of ground … at Territorial", 28 Jan). The sense of "Territorial" here (a place? a camp?) is still unclear.
- **"XIII Corps" struck through:** 5 Feb reads "arrangements are made with XIII Corps" — "XIII Corps" appears struck through in the original, as it did on 11 January. Transcribed as written with the strike noted; whether the deletion is the writer's correction or a later mark is not determined here.
- **Major Baker's move:** 5 Feb posts Major Baker (O.C. "A" Coy) to the 8th Battalion, Canadian Railway Troops, to take over command of that battalion; 8 Feb he proceeds to join it, the same day Lt.-Col. Macdonald returns from leave (he went on leave 22 January).
- **Borrows number:** the 1 February entry gives Sapper A.J. Borrows' number as 1039162, preceded in the original by a small circled mark (possibly a "No." or a tick). The number is transcribed without the mark; the mark's meaning is not determined here.
- **"La Marais" / "same Headquarter Dump" (6 Feb):** both read as written and flagged [uncertain] — "La Marais dump" (C Coy's grading line) and "same Headquarter Dump situated S. of Bethune" (B Coy's line). "Same" may mean the dump already named, or may be a misreading.
- **"Fosse No 7" (21 Feb):** four R.E. other ranks were attached for billets and rations to work on a steam shovel being installed at Fosse No 7 ballast pit. The January transcript noted "Fosse* 7" with an asterisk; here it is written "Fosse No 7" with no asterisk. Transcribed as written on both.
- **239th Battalion history (26 Feb):** the unit forwarded to H.Q. C.R.T. the historical record of the 239th Battalion, "which was the former designation of this unit" — the battalion was the 239th before redesignation as 3rd Bn. Canadian Railway Troops on 3 Feb 1917.
- **Russian subjects (26 Feb):** Russian subjects serving in the unit "may now proceed to France for leave" — transcribed as written; the reason for the restriction is not stated.
- **Transport officers (19 Feb):** C.R.T. transport officers were to become captains, and the Transport Sergeant to have the rank and pay of a R.Q.M.S.
- **P.H. helmets (20 Feb):** all P.H. (phenate-hexamine) gas helmets were ordered turned into Quartermasters stores — presumably on issue of a newer respirator, though the diary does not say so.
- **New line surveyed (22 Feb):** survey completed of a new light-railway line from Gorre to Lacouture.
- **Savy again a railhead (27 Feb):** the unit will now maintain the line from Harptack [uncertain] to Savy, "in view of Savy being again used as Railhead" — Savy was the railhead for the earlier lateral-line survey in January.
- **No casualties 13–28 Feb:** shelling damaged track repeatedly (ten breaks in the week to 20 Feb, needing 164 sections of rail; four places on 26 Feb, two on 28 Feb) and forced the Mersey Trench detachment to take cover on 18 Feb, but no killed or wounded are recorded in these entries. The month's only casualty is Sapper F. Hulme, wounded 12 February.
