## Pages 6–15 — Diary text (October 1917, pp. 6–15)

Army Form C. 2118 — WAR DIARY or INTELLIGENCE SUMMARY. Place: Barlin throughout. Each entry signed in the margin "C. M. M." [uncertain]. The 31 October entry is followed by the O.C.'s signature: "J. B. Macdonald [uncertain] Lt. Col., O.C. 3rd Bn. Can. Rly. Troops."

### 1-10-17

> Weather fine and clear.
>
> Difficulty being experienced in securing sufficient ballast.
>
> Slight enemy shelling near [uncertain]. Eight blowouts on St. Pierre Line and two Loos Line. Four seven [uncertain] ballast [uncertain] completed.

### 2-10-17

> Weather fine and clear.
>
> All Companies demanding ballast.
>
> Four blowouts St. Pierre Line. Two on Loos Line.
>
> Repairs made without serious delay traffic.
>
> Rations satisfactory.

### 3-10-17

> Weather Cloudy and Cool.
>
> "A" Coy taking over East Barlin-Gouy [uncertain].
>
> [uncertain] Coy men for forward area work in front of Lens.
>
> Two breaks repaired St. Pierre Line - Two Loos Line.
>
> Men returning from 1st Army Rest Camp much pleased with Rest Camp and treatment received.

### 4-10-17

> Weather Cloudy and Cool. Heavy Rain during afternoon.
>
> [uncertain] enemy shelling on St. Pierre and Loos Lines during evening necessitating relaying of 545' track.
>
> "A" Coy making Camp at Neuville St. Vaast & [uncertain] siding.

### 5-10-17

> Weather cool and windy.
>
> "B" Coy much interfered with on work at M.21.D.2.4. by enemy shelling.
>
> All Companies still demanding more ballast.

### 6-10-17

> Raining and cold.
>
> 838' of [uncertain] Line relaid with 30" steel.
>
> Supply of ballast still inadequate.
>
> All work progressing as usual. Shelling much below normal.

### 7-10-17

> Weather fine. Slight rain during afternoon.
>
> This being Sunday all men possible were allowed day off and bath parades arranged.
>
> Nothing of interest to report.

### 8-10-17

> Weather windy and heavy rain.
>
> [uncertain] commuting slow [uncertain] ballast.
>
> All Companies report Rations satisfactory.
>
> Nothing unusual to report. [uncertain] no improvements arrived from Rear.

### 9-10-17

> Weather fine [and] cool with rain in evening.

### 10-10-17

> Cool and Raining.
>

### 11-10-17

> Weather clear and cold.
>
