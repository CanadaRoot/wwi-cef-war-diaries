## Pages 6–10 — Diary text (November 1917, pp. 6–10)

Army Form C. 2118 — WAR DIARY or INTELLIGENCE SUMMARY. Place: Barlin throughout. Each entry signed in the margin "C. M. M." [uncertain]. Page ranges: p. 6 covers 1–3 Nov; p. 7 covers 4–5 Nov; p. 8 covers 6–8 Nov; p. 9 covers 9–11 Nov; p. 10 covers 12–14 Nov.

### 1-11-17

> Weather Fine and Cool.
>
> New Spur authorized Selliers-au-Bois [uncertain] Ration Dumps.
>
> Shelling much below normal. No break in track reported.
>
> Nothing of interest to report.

### 2-11-17

> Weather Foggy and Cool.
>
> Enemy artillery more active. 56' Track St. Pierre Line South of Lens blown out and replaced. Ballast situation not much improved. All Companies asking for larger supply [uncertain].
>
> Work grading Lens to Givry Line progressing favorably.

### 3-11-17

> Weather Cool and Cloudy.
>
> All work progressing satisfactorily. No damage caused by enemy shelling. Nothing unusual to report

### 4-11-17

> Weather Cloudy and Cool.
>
> With exception of Lens to Givry work (Being Sunday) all men possible to spare given day off & Bath parades arranged.
>
> Officers on invitation saw Major [uncertain] inspected Caves and Tunnels at Arras. No damage caused Track [uncertain] by enemy

### 5-11-17

> Weather Cool and Raining.
>
> Warning received to prepare to move "C" and "D" Coys. of this Unit South to Le Bouguiere [uncertain] (East of Bapaume) 3rd Army North.
>
> Arrangements made to distribute the work 1st Army between "A" and "B" Coys. and attached labor requisitioned for [uncertain] Coys being moved South.
>
> One Platoon of "D" Coy ordered to remain at Arras and take over L. R. work being done by Platoon of 6th Bn. Can. Ry. Troops which moved South.
>
> Considerable shelling by enemy of [Lievin?]-Loos Unit with [uncertain] Gas. Barlin also shelled slightly with H.E.'s

### 6-11-17

> Weather Cool and Raining.
>
> "C" and "D" left Artillery Camps [uncertain] for Lebuquiere [uncertain] by train at 4.30 P.M. Mule and M.T. Transport with these Coys. proceeded by road same morning to same destination.

### 7-11-17

> Weather Cool and Raining.
>
> A & B. Coys placing Detachments to take over work left by C & D Coys. who moved to Lebuquiere [uncertain] 3rd Army North. C & D. Coys. Engaged putting up huts and arranging Camp.

### 8-11-17

> Cold and Wet. - Grading Lens to Givry Line 52% Completed.
>
> 35' Track blown out St. Pierre - Loos Line. C & D Coys. 3rd Army North Engaged unloading Steel & Ballast.

### 9-11-17

> Cool and Raining. - Nothing of interest 1st Army area
>
