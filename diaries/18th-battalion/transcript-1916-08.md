## Transcription conventions

- Transcribed only from the LAC page images listed above. Spelling, punctuation, abbreviations and capitalisation are as written in the original.
- The diary text is on Army Form C. 2118, "War Diary or Intelligence Summary", with columns Place | Date | Hour | Summary of Events and Information | Remarks and references to Appendices. The form footer reads "2449 Wt. W14957/M90 750,000 1/16 J.B.C. & A. Forms/C.2118/12."
- `[uncertain]` marks any word or name that could not be read with confidence — names are never guessed.
- A dash or blank in the Place column means the place is unchanged from the row above; " (ditto marks) repeat the line above.
- "Battn" = Battalion, "Bn" = Battalion, "Div" = Division, "Bgde" = Brigade, "Coys" = Companies, "O.R." = other ranks, "S.O.S." = struck off strength, "D.R.O." = Divisional Routine Order, "A.D.M.S." = Assistant Director of Medical Services, "R.F.C." = Royal Flying Corps, "L.G.O." = Lewis Gun Officer, "M.O." = Medical Officer, "O.C." = Officer Commanding.
- The Remarks column carries a repeated flourished initial on every row that is not legible — [uncertain] — and is not transcribed row by row.
- Place names as written: Alberta Camp, La Clytte (also written LA CLYTTE / LACLYTTE), Vierstraat, Kenora Camp, Steenvoorde, Cassel, Noordpeene, Millaim [uncertain], Tournehem, Watten, Nordasques.

## Page 3 — Diary text, 1–7 August 1916

[Handwritten "Original" and "Confidential" at top, "18th Canadian Battalion" above the printed heading, "August 1916" at top right, and "12" at the far top right. "AUGUST 1916." is written across the top of the Date column. Place column reads "ALBERTA CAMP" for every day. Form C. 2118.]

### 1 August 1916 — Alberta Camp

> Battalion in reserve at ALBERTA CAMP. Pay and Bath parades. 20 o.r. arrived as reinforcements 16 o.r. returned from Base hospitals.

### 2 August 1916 — Alberta Camp

> Routine. Clothing parade 2 o.r. admitted to hospital. 2 o.r. returned from hospital. Lieut. D.S.J. WIGLE. S.O.S. of this Battalion on being invalided to England 30-7-16.

### 3 August 1916 — Alberta Camp

> Battalion route marching in heavy order. Sgt W.J. MORRIS granted a Commission as Temp Lieut. 1 o.r. sick admitted to hospital.

### 4 August 1916 — Alberta Camp

> Routine. Company parades 1 o.r. admitted to hospital 2 o.r. returned from hospital.

### 5 August 1916 — Alberta Camp

> Brigade on Route march and manoeuvres. Majors BARBER, FAIRBANK and Capt. GANDER arrived as reinforcements. Lieut T.H.O. MAYNARD proceeded on leave. 2 o.r. returned from hospital.

### 6 August 1916 — Alberta Camp

> Battalion Church parade 10 a.m. Part II order #30 dated 31-7-16 shows. 1 officer + 20 o.r.s struck off strength of the Battalion. 3 o.r. returned from hospital.

### 7 August 1916 — Alberta Camp

> Lieut. W.H. WILLARD returned from hospital, Lieuts. B.J. BATES and H.W. SMITH arrived as reinforcements 2 o.r. declared unfit and sent to Base. 3 o.r. returned from hospital.

## Page 4 — Diary text, 8–13 August 1916

### 8 August 1916 — La Clytte

> The 4th Canadian Infantry Brigade relieved 5th Canadian Infantry Brigade in VIERSTRAAT sector. 18th Canadian Battalion [uncertain — a word is struck out] moved into Brigade Reserve at LA CLYTTE. Lieut W.O. BROWN accidentally killed 14 o.r. wounded.

### 10 August 1916 — La Clytte

### 11 August 1916 — La Clytte

> Battalion Pay parades. Part II order #31 shows. 30 o.r. struck off strength of Battalion. Lieut H.D. DUNNETT admitted to hospital sick. 2 o.r. returned from hospital. Lieuts. J.P. CHRYSTA and A.E. COOK arrived as reinforcements.

### 12 August 1916 — La Clytte

> Battalion Pay and Bath parades. 3 o.r. returned from hospital. 1 o.r. admitted to hospital. D.R.O. #2648 shows 6 o.r. appointed to Commissions from 18th Can Bn.

### 13 August 1916 — La Clytte

> Battalion in Reserve. 3 o.r. admitted to hospital. Extract from London Gazette:— Lieuts. J.A. McINTOSH and D.E.A. RISPIN to be Temp CAPTAINS. 1 o.r. returned from hospital.

## Notes on this month

- Casualties: Lieut W. O. Brown accidentally killed and 14 other ranks wounded on 8 August, when the battalion moved into Brigade Reserve at La Clytte; 1 other rank killed in action on 16 August (name not given). No other battle casualties are recorded; the rest of the month's losses are sickness and hospital admissions.
- Movements: the battalion spends 1–7 August in reserve at Alberta Camp, 8–14 August in Brigade Reserve at La Clytte, and 15–21 August in the line at Vierstraat (C and D Companies in trenches M and N, A and B in battalion reserve). Relieved by the 46th Battalion on 22 August, it moves to Kenora Camp, then marches 24–28 August through Steenvoorde, Cassel, Noordpeene, Millaim [uncertain] and Watten/Nordasques to billets at Tournehem, where it remains at the end of the month.
- Officers named: Major H. L. Milligan (granted permission to wear the badges of rank of Lieutenant-Colonel, 20 August; signs the diary as commanding officer); Lieut W. O. Brown (accidentally killed, 8 August); Lieut D. S. J. Wigle (invalided to England, 2 August); Sgt W. J. Morris (granted a commission as Temporary Lieutenant, 3 August; proceeded on leave, 19 August); Majors Barber and Fairbank and Capt. Gander (arrived as reinforcements, 5 August); Lieut T. H. O. Maynard (proceeded on leave, 5 August); Lieut W. H. Willard (returned from hospital, 7 August); Lieuts. B. J. Bates and H. W. Smith (arrived as reinforcements, 7 August); Lieut H. D. Dunnett (admitted to hospital sick, 11 August); Lieuts. J. P. Chrysta and A. E. Cook (arrived as reinforcements, 11 August); Lieuts. J. A. McIntosh and D. E. A. Rispin (to be Temporary Captains, London Gazette, 13 August); Lieut T. Kirkham (admitted to hospital, 19 August); Lieut A. S. Allen (awarded the Military Cross, 20 August); Lieut R. H. Martin (struck off strength 24 August, posted to the R.F.C.); Lieut W. F. Cole (admitted to hospital while on course, 29 August).
- Awards: Pte A. Forrester (63648) awarded the Military Medal, 17 August; Lieut A. S. Allen awarded the Military Cross, 20 August.
- Notable: a general inspection by Major-General R. E. W. Turner and Brigadier-General Rennie on 9 August; on 30 August the battalion was issued Lee-Enfield rifles in place of the Ross Mark III (the sentence is underlined in the diary), and Watten was placed out of bounds for troops of the 2nd Canadian Division. Appendix I (Operation Order No. 26, dated 21 August) covers the relief by the 46th Battalion and the move to Kenora Camp.
- Cover/contents disagreement: the contents page dates Operation Order No. 26 "12st August", but the order itself is dated 21-8-16; the relief it orders took place on 22 August as the diary records.
- No date gaps: entries run 1–31 August without a break.
