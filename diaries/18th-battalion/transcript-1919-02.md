## Transcription conventions

- Transcribed only from the LAC page images listed above. Spelling, punctuation, abbreviations and capitalisation are as written in the original.
- The diary text is on Army Form C. 2118, "War Diary or Intelligence Summary", with columns Place | Date | Hour | Summary of Events and Information | Remarks and references to Appendices. The form footer reads "(4927) Wt W2215/P56 600,000 11/17 D. D. & L. Sch. 31a. Form C.2118/13." (variant spellings of the imprint occur across pages).
- `[uncertain]` marks any word or name that could not be read with confidence — names are never guessed. `[struck]` marks text struck through in the original.
- "do" is the diarist's ditto mark; "-do-" repeats the line above.
- "Battn" = Battalion, "Bn" = Battalion, "Cdn" = Canadian, "C.I.B." = Canadian Infantry Brigade, "O.R." = other ranks, "C.C.R.C." = Canadian Corps Reinforcement Camp, "M.T." = Mechanical Transport, "S.B.R." = Small Box Respirator, "H.Q." = Headquarters, "N.C.O." = non-commissioned officer, "W.O." = Warrant Officer, "R.C." = Roman Catholic, "B.O.S." = as written, "Q.M." = Quartermaster, "A.F.B." = Army Form B., "Y.M.C.A." = Young Men's Christian Association.
- Note: the cover and contents page of this volume read "1st CANADIAN DIVISION", although the battalion served in the 2nd Canadian Division in 1918; transcribed as written.
- Place names as written: Fosses, Namur, Godinne, Solayn, Brussels, Mons, Chapelle-lez-Herlaimont [Herlaimont], Paris, Nice, Italy, Hennef [uncertain], Germany, Canada, England, France.
- Filming order: the page images are not in printed order in the file sequence — pp. 4 and 5 precede pp. 3 and 2. This transcript follows printed page-number order. Pages 4 and 5 are duplicate filmings of the same map; pp. 13 and 14 are duplicate filmings of Appendix 2; pp. 25 and 26 are duplicate filmings of Appendix 13; p. 12 is the reverse (show-through) of Appendix 1.

## Page 6 — Diary text, 1–8 February 1919

[Typed. "36" at top right. Form C. 2118. "18TH. CANADIAN BATTALION, WESTERN ONTARIO REGIMENT. INTELLIGENCE SUMMARY." at top. "Map for Reference: Namur 8 1/100,000." Place column: "FOSSES I.35.75."]

### 1 February 1919 — Fosses, I.35.75.

> Battalion training as per Appendix No. 1. attached. In accordance with instructions re. Return to England of Supernumerary Officers, Lieut. C. D. Smith proceeded to England this date, Capt. C. F. Laing (Paymaster) and Capt. T. A. Girling (Veterinary Officer) and 16 O.R.s attached from 4th. C.I.B.

### 2 February 1919

> Church parades were held as per Syllabus attached. Lieut. C. J. Spencer returned from duty as Gas Officer with 6th. C.I.B. 3 O.R.s attached to 2nd. Divl. M. T. Coy. for Course of Instruction in Motor Mechanics. 1 O.R. proceeded to England for return to Canada on Compassionate grounds.

### 3 February 1919

> Battalion Training as per Appendix No. 3. attached.

### 4 February 1919

> Battalion training as per appendix attached (Appendix NO. 4.). 3 O.R.s proceeded to England for duty at demobilization Camp.

### 5 February 1919

> Battalion Training as per Appendix No. 5. attached. 1 O.R. returned from duty with 2nd. Divl. Train.

### 6 February 1919

> Commanding Officers Inspection was held this morning, on Battalion Parade Ground. Dress: Full Marching Order. 1 O.R. from leave. 1 O.R. to hospital. [uncertain] Ambulance driver. Lieut. J. McMillan returned from hospital. A very successful dance was given To-Night in the Hotel de Ville by the W.O.s, N.C.O.s and Men of Battalion "Hqrs." About 80 couples were present. The music was supplied by the Regimental Band. Suitable refreshments were provided by "Hq" Officers.

### 7 February 1919

> Battalion Training as per Appendix No. 7. attached. 1 O.R. returned from leave, 1 O.R. to hospital. Sergt. Drill Instructor attached from C.C.R.C. returns to his Unit, 27th. Cdn. Inf. Battn.

### 8 February 1919

> Battalion training as per Appendix No. 8. attached. The C.O. lectured to all N.C.O.s of the Battalion in the Town Hall this morning. The subject of the lecture was "Discipline". 5 O.R.s from leave. 2 O.R.s to hospital.

## Page 7 — Diary text, 9–15 February 1919

[Typed. "37" at top right. Form C. 2118.]

### 9 February 1919 — Fosses, I.35.75.

> Church Parades were held as per Appendix NO. 9. attached 1 O.R. returned from leave. 1 O.R. from duty at 4th. C.I.B.

### 10 February 1919

> Battalion training as per Appendix NO. 10 attached. Battalion Pay parades were held in the afternoon. 4 O.R.s returned from leave 4 O.R.s from 2nd. Cdn. Divl. Guard.

### 11 February 1919

> Battalion training and Educational Classes were as per Appendix NO. 11. attached. Lieut. C. Cook proceeded to PARIS for the purpose of purchasing books for the Battalion Library. Lieuts. G. J. Spencer and P. L. Goodman proceeded to England to continue their studies in the University there. 3 O.R.s attached 2nd. Divl. M.T. Coy. for one weeks course of instruction in Motor Mechanics. 8 O.R.s returned from leave.

### 12 February 1919

> Battalion training as per Appendix NO. 12. attached. Lieut. J. Morgan returned from leave. Capt. W. C. Michell arrived as a reinforcement. 1 O.R. to hospital. 5 O.R.s from leave.

### 13 February 1919

> Battalion training as per Appendix No. 13 attached. The Battalion Soccer team played the 20th. Battn. team on the Y.M.C.A. grounds this afternoon. The score was 2 - 0 in our favor. Lieut. S. G. Stokes proceeded to England to continue his studies in the University there. 1 O.R. attached as interpreter from Forestry Corps returned to his Unit. 1 O.R. from hospital and 16 O.R.s from leave.

### 14 February 1919

> Battalion training as per Appendix No. 14 attached. Capt. T. A. Girling ceases to be att'd from 4th. C.I.B. on evacuation to hospital. Capt. W. C. Michell assumes the duties of Town Major of FOSSES. 1 O.R. att'd. from 4th. Cdn. Sanitary Section. 3 O.R.s proceeded on and 9 O.R.s returned from leave. 1 O.R. arrived as reinforcement.

### 15 February 1919

> Battalion training and Educational Classes as per Appendix No. 15. attached. The "HQ" Stores were victors over "A" Co'y. in a football game played on the Y.M.C.A. grounds this afternoon. The score being 2 - 0. Lieut. J. R. Taylor and 44 O.R.s of "C" Co'y. proceeded to Solayn to relieve a 1st. Cdn. Divl. guard on the stores there. Capt. J. Leavitt and Lieut. J. McHammond proceeded on leave to NICE.

## Page 8 — Diary text, 16–22 February 1919

[Typed. "38" at top right. Form C. 2118.]

### 16 February 1919 — Fosses, I.35.75.

> Church Parades were held as per Appendix No. 16. attached. Lieut. J. Boyd and 8 O.R.s returned from leave. Lt. W. K. Rooney returned from Cdn. Inf. Base Depot. 3 O.R.s to hospital. 8 O.R.s to 4th. C.I.B. Hdqrs. as a guard. 16 O.R.s returned from leave.

### 17 February 1919

> Battalion and Educational work as per Appendix No. 17. attached. In connection with demobilization, Lt-Col. [uncertain] Jones addressed prospective farmers in the Battalion this morning. 2 O.R.s on Signalling Course. 3 O.R.s to hospital. 1 O.R. from leave. Lt. C. Cook returned from PARIS.

### 18 February 1919

> Battalion training and Educational work as per Appendix No. 18 attached. A Battalion Library and Reading room was opened to-day. 8 O.R.s from leave. 3 O.R.s attached to 2nd. Divl. M. T. Coy. for one weeks course of Instruction in Motor Mechanics.

### 19 February 1919

> Battalion training and Educational work as per Appendix No. 19. attached. Lt. A. H. Jones and an additional 20 O.R.s were despatched to Solayn for guard duty. 4 O.R.s proceeded on leave. 2 O.R.s from leave. 4 O.R.s to hospital.

### 20 February 1919

> C.O.'s Inspection was held at 09:00 hrs. this morning on the Battalion Parade Ground. Dress: Full Marching Order. In the afternoon the Battalion Soccer team played the 19th. Battn team on the Y.M.C.A. grounds. The score was 4 - 1 in our favor. 1 officer and 8 O.R.s att'd. from 4th. Cdn. Battn. C.S. [uncertain] for construction of Ovens. 8 O.R.s proceeded on and 1 O.R. returned from leave. 2 O.R.s returned from Signal Course. 2 O.R.s to hospital.

### 21 February 1919

> Battalion Training and Educational work as per Appendix No. 21 attached. A dance was given to-night by Battn. "HQ" at the Hotel de Ville. This dance was even more of a success than the inaugural one. Transport Details held a Euchre drive to-night. 8 O.R.s proceeded on and 6 O.R.s returned from leave. 4 O.R.s to hospital.

### 22 February 1919

> Battalion training and Educational work as per Appendix No. 22 attached. Officers of the Battalion played a game of Indoor Baseball on the Y.M.C.A. grounds this afternoon. Major J. S. Bell and 1 O.R. returned from leave. 2 O.R.s to hospital.

## Page 9 — Diary text, 23–28 February 1919

[Typed. "39" at top right. Form C. 2118.]

### 23 February 1919 — Fosses, I.35.75.

> Battalion Church Parades were held as per Appendix No. 23. attached. Voluntary Church service was held in the Cinema to-night. 1 O.R. returned from leave.

### 24 February 1919

> Battalion training and Educational work as per Appendix No. 24 attached. Pay parade was held in the afternoon. All public gathering places for troops were placed out of bounds from to-day on account of the epidemic of influenza. Heretofore a nightly entertainment has been given to the troops at the Battalion CINEMA. 3 O.R.s to hospital.

### 25 February 1919

> Battalion Training and Educational work as per Appendix No. 25 attached. "A" and "B" Co'ys. held Bath parade this afternoon. Good warm water showers and clean change of clothes were available for the men at the Public Baths FOSSES. Capt. R. C. Elliott and Lieut. J. MacRae proceeded on leave to ITALY. 1 O.R. on leave. Capt. J. Leavitt and Lt. J. McHammond returned from leave to NICE. 2 O.R.s from leave. Capt. C. F. Hodgins returned from duty at 5th. C.I.B.

### 26 February 1919

> Battalion training and Educational work as per Appendix No. 26 attached. Baths were open for "C" & "D" Co'ys. and "HQ" Details to-day. 1 O.R. att'd. Town Major, Fosses as clerk. 1 O.R. att'd. to Battalion from Belgian Mission as Interpreter. 8 O.R.s returned from Guard duty at 4th. C.I.B.

### 27 February 1919

> C.O.'s Inspection was held at 09:00 hrs. this morning on the Battalion parade ground. Dress: Full marching order. Educational work was carried on as usual. 8 O.R.s on leave. 4 O.R.s att'd. 2nd. Cdn. Divl. Train. 1 O.R. to hospital. 1 O.R. to prison.

### 28 February 1919

> Battalion training and Educational work as per Appendix No. 28. attached. The Battalion Soccer team played a team from the Canadian Engineers at GODINNE to-day. Four motor lorries were supplied to carry the team and supporters to and from the game. The score was 4 - 1 in our favor. During this month 3 days leave has been granted to men desiring to visit BRUSSELS and other towns in this vicinity where the Battalion was billeted on the march to Germany. During the latter part of December, in connection with the "Khaki University" educational classes were inaugurated while the Battalion was stationed at Hennef [uncertain] in Germany. These classes are for the purpose of preparing the men for entry into civilian life on return to Canada. They consist of courses in Agriculture, Business, Motor Mechanics, and Elementary Work. The Elementary class is designed especially for the benefit of men who have not had Common School Education and consists of classes in Primary Reading, Writing, and Arithmetic.
>
> [continued on page 10]

## Page 10 — Diary text, 28 February 1919 (continued)

[Typed. "40" at top right. Form C. 2118. Place column: "FOSSES I.35.75. (Cont'd.)"]

### 28 February 1919 (continued)

> The classes in Agriculture, Business, and Motor Mechanics give instruction, both in theory and practice, in the subjects named. The services of experienced teachers in the above subjects were available in the 4th. C.I.B. and valuable work has been carried on by them. One hour of each day is given over to Educational Work in the syllabus of training and men attending classes are excused all parades and duties which might prevent their attendance. So far, this scheme has received the enthusiastic support of all ranks and much valuable progress has been made. Lt-Col. L. E. Jones proceeded on leave. 4 O.R.s on leave. 2 O.R.s to hospital.

[Signed, in ink:]

> [signature — "James Bell" [uncertain]] Major
>
> O.C. 18th Cdn Inf Bn
