## Transcription conventions

- Transcribed faithfully from the LAC page images only; spelling, punctuation, abbreviations and capitalisation as written.
- `[uncertain]` marks any word or name that could not be read with confidence — never guessed.
- This month contains two separate diaries: the battalion diary (pages 2–4) and the Medical Officer's diary (pages 5–10, headed "M.O. 18th Infantry Battalion", covering 1–31 October). Both are handwritten on Army Form C. 2118, with columns Place | Date | Hour | Summary of Events and Information. The printed heading "INTELLIGENCE SUMMARY" is struck through and "WAR DIARY" used instead.
- Ditto marks in the original are kept as "— do —".
- Page 5 is the blank reverse of the battalion diary sheet, showing only show-through of the writing on the other side.

## Notes on this month

- The battalion's first full month in the line: "D" trenches at Dranoutre, then Vierstraat trenches, brigade reserve at Ridgewood, and La Brasserie/Bois Carré trenches, ending the month in divisional reserve at La Clytte.
- Killed in October: Pte Taggart (10 October, rifle grenade); Ptes. Unthank, Nelson, Nelson and Mundie (13 October, bombardment); Pte Ridgeley (20 October, shot in Poppy Lane while on a fatigue party); and one man on 2 October whose name could not be read. The Medical Officer's diary confirms Taggart, Unthank, the two Nelsons, Mundie and Ridgeley, and adds Pte H. J. [uncertain] (No 53476) and Pte W. W. [uncertain] (No 53227) killed at the end of September.
- The battalion diary and the Medical Officer's diary disagree on the date of the move to Ridgewood: the battalion diary records it on 14 October, the Medical Officer's on 15 October. The Medical Officer's diary also records the 8 October march through Locre and La Clytte to Hallebast, which the battalion diary does not mention.
- On 27 October representatives of the battalion attended a review by H.M. King George and the French [uncertain].
- People named: King George V; Capt. G. C. Hale (Medical Officer); Capt. Brothers (Bomb School); Lieut. F. G. Newton; Col. [uncertain] (brigade commander, 8 October).
