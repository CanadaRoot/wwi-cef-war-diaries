## Transcription conventions

- Transcribed only from the LAC page images listed above. Spelling, punctuation, abbreviations and capitalisation are as written in the original.
- The diary text is on Army Form C. 2118, "War Diary or Intelligence Summary", with columns Place | Date | Hour | Summary of Events and Information | Remarks and references to Appendices.
- `[uncertain]` marks any word or name that could not be read with confidence — names are never guessed. `[struck]` marks text struck through in the original.
- "do" is the diarist's ditto mark; " " (ditto marks) repeat the line above.
- "Battn" = Battalion, "Bn" = Battalion, "Cdn" = Canadian, "C.I.B." = Canadian Infantry Brigade, "O.R." = other ranks, "H.Q." = Headquarters, "N.C.O." = non-commissioned officer, "M.G." = Machine Gun, "R.A.P." = Regimental Aid Post, "C.C.R.C." = Canadian Corps Reinforcement Camp, "M.M." = Military Medal, "Bde." = Brigade, "L.G." = Lewis Gun, "O.P." = Observation Post, "R.S.M." = Regimental Sergeant Major, "Q.M." = Quartermaster, "S.B.R." = Small Box Respirator.
- Note on filming order: the page images were filmed out of printed order — printed p. 2 is image file 0209 and printed p. 3 is image file 0208, while printed p. 4 is image file 0202. This transcript follows printed page-number order.

## Page 10 — Diary text, 1–6 January 1919

[Typed. Form C. 2118. "30" at top right. "18TH CANADIAN BATTALION (WESTERN ONTARIO REGIMENT). JANUARY 1919." at top. The heading "INTELLIGENCE SUMMARY" is struck through. Form footer: "T4134. Wt. W798—775. 500000. 4/15. Sir J. C. & S." Place: ALLNER, 6-G. 90.90, Germany 2-1. The Remarks column carries the initials "bb" against each entry.]

### 1 January 1919 — Allner, 6-G. 90.90, Germany

> "New Year's Day"—— A most prosperous New Year to Everyone. The Battalion continued training as per Syllabi attached, appendix No. 1. The men of the Battalion have been specially directed against fraternising with the German population. 7 O.R's on leave. [struck: "Lieut. [uncertain] W.J. from C.C.R.C."] 1 O.R. attached to Prisoners of War Camp, Wahn. 1 O.R. admitted to Hospital and 1 O.R. returned from Hospital.

### 2 January 1919

> The Battalion continued training to-day as per Syllabi attached, appendix No. 2. Educational Classes are continuing as usual and the men are taking a very fair interest in all classes. 2 O.R's on leave. Lieut. C. Cook, returned from Hospital this date together with 6 O.R.

### 3 January 1919

> The Battalion continued training to-day as per Syllabi attached, appendix No. 3. Educational work is being continued as usual. Owing to the food shortage for the civilian population in the Occupied Territory and in Germany as a whole, the members of the Army of Occupation are forbidden to purchase any food stuffs whatsoever, with the exception of vegetables and fruit. 4 O.R's on leave and 1 O.R. returned from leave.

### 4 January 1919

> The Battalion continued training as per Syllabi attached, appendix No. 4. The Educational work continues and is showing a very fine progress.

### 5 January 1919

> The Battalion attended Church Service to-day and enjoyed one of the Padre's excellent sermons. 5 O.R's on leave to U.K. and 2 O.R's on leave to Paris. 1 O.R. returned from leave to U.K. Captain W. J. Baxter awarded the Military Cross. 2 O.R. returned from Hospital. Syllabus of training etc., for the day attached, appendix No. 5.

### 6 January 1919

> The Battalion continued training to-day as per Syllabi attached, appendix No. 6. Educational work continued as usual. 3 O.R's on leave to U.K. 1 O.R. attached to 2nd. Canadian Divisional Guard.

## Page 12 — Diary text, 11–15 January 1919

[Typed. Form C. 2118. "32" at top right. Form footer: "T4134. Wt. W798—775. 500000. 4/15. Sir J. C. & S." Place: ALLNER (continued).]

### 11 January 1919 (continued) — Allner

> On the evening of the 10th. of January, 1919, a Supper and Dance was given by the Officers of the Battalion, in the "Schloss" Allner. Over 100 Guests were invited including the Divisional Commander, Maj.-General H. Burstall and Officers of the different Brigades in the Division. The Nursing-Sisters from the C.C.S. in Bonn were also invited and were present to the number of about twenty. The early part of the evening was devoted to dancing and then Supper was served, after which the Nursing-Sisters and Officer Guests departed for their different stations in the area.

### 12 January 1919

> The Battalion attended Church Service to-day and as usual the padre was in excellent voice. The Battalion enjoyed an excellent sermon. The Syllabus for the day is attached, appendix No. 12.

### 13 January 1919

> The Battalion continued training as per Syllabi attached, appendix No. 13. Educational Classes carrying on as usual. During the evening a Supper and entertainment was given by the N.C.O's. of the Battalion. Sergeants, Senior N.C.O's and Warrant Officers being in attendance. Several guests, Warrant Officers and Senior N.C.O's. from the other Battalions in the Brigade being present. Also, Lt.-Colonel L. E. Jones and Lieut. C. Cook also were present. Lt.-Colonel Jones during the evening, complimented the N.C.O's upon their fine efforts in the past, expressing his sincere appreciation of the splendid co-operation with the Officers of the Battalion. Entertainment for the evening was provided by talent from the Battalion. Lt. J. E. Taylor of "C" Co'y. delighted the gathering with several songs of great popularity, giving the necessary colour and expression to the songs, as only Lieut Taylor can do. His efforts were vociferously appreciated. Bandmaster Thomas' efforts on the Piano were also greatly appreciated. Other varieties of entertainment were Clog-Dancing, Fancy Dancing etc. Altogether the evening was one of enjoyment.

### 16 January 1919

> The Battalion continued training as per Syllabi attached, appendix No. 16. Educational work was carried on as usual.
>
> DEFENCE SCHEME IN CASE OF RENEWAL OF HOSTILITIES OR ENEMY ATTACK, 18th Canadian Battalion, Western Ontario Regiment.
>
> (1) In case warning is received that hostilities are to be renewed, the Brigade Reserve Battalion will push forward to the Rheinbreitbach-Eudenbach-Muhleip-Eitorf line as soon as possible in order to prevent any enemy observation of the 2nd. Canadian Divisional Area, the 18th. Canadian Battalion holding itself in readiness to push forward and occupy line of resistance formed by forward Battalions.
>
> (2) In case the enemy attacks without previous warning, "B" Company will withdraw from Brohl area to the heights — Weisterschloss and Happerschoss. "D" Company will occupy the "Schloss" Allner, and occupying high ground to the North-east, connecting with "B" Company, destroying bridges over the river Sieg, and prevent enemy crossing river. "C" Company will occupy Hennef, covering Railway Lines and barricading all Railway Lines at present Outpost Line.
>
> "A" Company, in Battalion Reserve, will move to Weingarts-Gass, holding themselves in readiness to reinforce any part of this Battalion frontage immediately on receipt of instructions.
>
> (3) In case of attack, as in para. 2 above, Companies will act on receipt of the word "Positions". "A" Company will reinforce on receipt of words "Battle-positions" followed by [struck: "position"] location and will counter-attack on receipt of words "Drive Back" followed by location.
>
> Signed by:- C. Cook, Lieut., Adjt. 18th. Canadian Battalion. 16.1.19.
>

### 17 January 1919 — Allner

> The Battalion continued training as per Syllabus attached, appendix No. 18. Educational work being carried on as usual. Maj. J. J. Richardson returned from leave this date together with Capt. W. A. S. Porter and Lieut. C. D. Smith and 4 O.R.

### 18 January 1919

> The Battalion continued training as per Syllabi attached, appendix No. 19. Educational work being carried on as usual. 10 O.R's on leave.

### 19 January 1919

> The Battalion paraded for Church Service to-day, the last service they would attend as a Battalion in Germany. Service being in the Cinema at 09:30 hours. Syllabi for the day attached, appendix No. 20.

### 20 January 1919

> The Battalion was relieved, to-day, by the 2nd/4th. Queens (Imperial). All reliefs completed by 13:00 hours. The Battalion moved off at 13:30 hours, in full Marching Order, passing in review before Lt.-Col. L. E. Jones, D.S.O. and the Officer Commanding the 2nd./4th. Queens, and marched to Siegburg, arriving there about 17:30 hours and entrained at 18:00 hours. The Battalion spent the night on the train while enroute for destination, Fosses, Belgium. Move Order attached, appendix No. 21.

### 21 January 1919

> The day was spent on the train enroute from Germany. The Battalion arrived at Auvelais, Belgium at 18:00 hours and detrained and occupied billets in this town for the night.

### 22 January 1919

> The Battalion remained in Auvelais for the day, resting.

### 23 January 1919

> The Battalion moved off at 13:00 Hours, in full Marching Order and marched to Fosse, Belgium, where we were to remain for some time, arriving in Fosses about 15:00 hours. Move Order attached, appendix No. 22.

### 24 January 1919

> The Battalion continued training to-day as per Syllabi attached. Appendix No. 23. Preparations being made for a prolonged stay in this area, stoves, coal etc. were secured to provide against the expected cold weather.

> 18TH CANADIAN BATTALION, WESTERN ONTARIO REGIMENT.
>
> DEFENCE SCHEME IN CASE OF RENEWAL OF HOSTILITIES OR ENEMY ATTACK.
>
> 1. In case warning is received that hostilities are to be renewed, the Brigade Reserve Battalion will push forward to the RHEINBREITBACH - EUDENBACH - MUHLEIP - EITORF line as soon as possible in order to prevent any enemy observation of the 2nd Canadian Divisional Area, the 18th Canadian Battalion holding itself in readiness to push forward and occupy line of resistance formed by forward Battalions.
>
> 2. In case the enemy attacks without previous warning, "B" Company will withdraw from BROHL Area to the heights - WEISTERSCHLOSS and HAPPERSCHOSS. "D" Company will occupy the "Schloss" ALLNER and occupying high ground to the North-East, connecting with "B" Company destroying bridges over the river SIEG, and prevent enemy crossing river. "C" Company will occupy HENNEF, covering Railway Lines and barricading all Railway Lines at present Outpost Line.
>
> "A" Company, in Battalion Reserve, will move to WEINGARTS GASS holding themselves in readiness to reinforce any part of this Battalion frontage immediately on receipt of instructions.
>
> 3. In case of attack, as in para 2 above, Companies will act on receipt of the word "POSITIONS" "A" Company will reinforce on receipt of words "BATTLE POSITIONS" followed by location and will counter-attack on receipt of words "DRIVE BACK" followed by location
