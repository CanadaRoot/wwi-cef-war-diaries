## Transcription conventions

- Transcribed only from the LAC page images listed above. Spelling, punctuation, abbreviations and capitalisation are as written in the original.
- The diary text is on Army Form C. 2118, "War Diary or Intelligence Summary", with columns Place | Date | Hour | Summary of Events and Information | Remarks and references to Appendices. The form footer reads "2353 Wt. W2544/1454 700,000 5/15 D. D. & L. A.D.S.S./Forms/C. 2118."
- `[uncertain]` marks any word or name that could not be read with confidence — names are never guessed.
- "do" is the diarist's ditto mark; " " (ditto marks) repeat the line above.
- "Battn" = Battalion, "Bn" = Battalion, "O.R." = other ranks, "N.C.O." = non-commissioned officer, "R.E." = Royal Engineers, "T.M." = Trench Mortar, "C.I.B." = Canadian Infantry Brigade, "H.Q." = Headquarters, "R.T.O." = Railway Transport Officer, "G.S." = General Service.
- The Remarks column carries the same repeated initials on every row, apparently reading "C.A.K." — [uncertain] — and is not transcribed row by row.
- Place names as written: Villers-au-Bois (also written VILLERS AU BOIS), Villers Camp, Vimy Ridge, Chaudiere (also written CHAUDIERE), Frevillers (also written FREVILLERS), Ourton, Ligny-St.-Flochel (also written LIGNY-ST-FLOCHEL), Caestre (also written CAESTRE), Godewaersveldt.

## Page 3 — Diary text, 1–6 October 1917

[Handwritten "Confidential" at top left and "18th Canadian Battalion" and "October 1917" at top; "INTELLIGENCE SUMMARY." is struck through. A handwritten "A" stands at the top centre. Form C. 2118.]

### 1 October 1917 — Villers-au-Bois

> Battalion in billets at Villers Camp. Company parades and inspection of rifles & equipment. 2 O.R. admitted to Hospital.
>
> Maps for Reference. 36c. S.W. 36b. S.E. 51b. N.W. 51c. N.E.

### 2 October 1917 — Villers-au-Bois

> During the night of 2/3rd. 18th Battalion relieved the 28th Canadian Bn in reserve on Vimy Ridge, with Battalion H.Q. at S.23.a.4.5.

### 3 October 1917 — Vimy Ridge

> Position as yesterday. 'C' Coy, 1 Platoon of A Coy and 1 Platoon of B Coy (235 men) and 5 Officers attached to 185(T) Coy R.E. on working party, repairing trenches and building new dug-outs. 'D' Coy furnishes carrying parties for carrying material to front line. 5 O.R. admitted to Hospital.

### 4 October 1917 — Vimy Ridge

> Working parties and fatigues as yesterday. Nothing unusual occurred. 2 O.R. returned from Hospital.

### 5 October 1917 — Vimy Ridge

> Battalion furnishes working parties as yesterday. Situation in Reserve quiet. Lieut D. M. Northcombe returned from Hospital.

### 6 October 1917 — Vimy Ridge

> Carrying and working parties as yesterday. 3 O.R. returned from Hospital.

## Page 4 — Diary text, 7–12 October 1917

[Handwritten "18th Canadian Battalion" at top. Form C. 2118.]

### 7 October 1917 — Vimy Ridge

> Battalion still furnishing working parties. Nothing unusual to report.

### 8 October 1917 — Vimy Ridge

> During night of 8/9th the 18th Battalion relieved the 19th Canadian Bn in Front line in the Right Section of CHAUDIERE. 'D' Coy holding the Front line with details of A & B in close support. Working party with 185 Coy R.E. not being relieved.

### 9 October 1917 — Vimy Ridge

> Nothing unusual occurred. Position quiet. Battalion frontage patrolled by our Scouts during night, no enemy encountered. Lt J. Irons attached to 2nd Div Training Battalion as Instructor.

### 10 October 1917 — Villers Camp

> During night 10/11th 18th Canadian Bn was relieved by the 28th Bn. Battalion moved back to Villers Camp by light railway. 9 O.R. admitted to Hospital sick.

### 11 October 1917 — Villers Camp

> Battalion pay parades occupied the whole day. 11 O.R. rejoined from 4th Can T.M.By.

### 12 October 1917 — Villers Camp

> Bath and clothing parades. Lt H. L. Mitchell returned from Hospital.

## Page 5 — Diary text, 13–22 October 1917

[Handwritten "18th Canadian Battalion" at top. Form C. 2118.]

### 13 October 1917 — Villers Camp

> Coy parades and inspection. Recreational games during afternoon.

### 14 October 1917 — Villers Camp

> 11 O.R. arrived as reinforcements. 9 O.R. (Casuals) rejoined the Battalion.

### 15 October 1917 — Villers Camp, 8.30 a.m.

> 4th Canadian Infantry Brigade moved back into Ourton Area, 8.30am 18th Battalion marched to Frevillers, arriving at destination at 4 p.m.

### 16 October 1917 — Frevillers

> Company parades and Platoon Training. Recreational games in afternoon.

### 17 October 1917 — Frevillers

> Bath parades for Battalion. Capt W. C. Michell proceeded on leave. 7 O.R. admitted to Hospital.

### 18–22 October 1917 — Frevillers

> Company parades. Specialist training by Bombers, Lewis Gunners, and Grenadiers. Lieut J. F. Perkins arrived as reinforcement. 27 O.R. arrived as reinforcements. Lieut. W. S. Caldwell & Lieut W. H. Fenton proceeded on leave.

## Notes on this month

- Casualties: no battle casualties are recorded this month. All losses noted are sickness and hospital admissions — 2 O.R. admitted 1 October, 5 O.R. admitted 3 October, 9 O.R. admitted sick 10 October, 7 O.R. admitted 17 October, 2 O.R. admitted sick 25 October — with hospital returns of 2 O.R. on 4 October, 3 O.R. on 6 October and 5 O.R. on 27 October, and 11 O.R. rejoining from the 4th Canadian Trench Mortar Battery on 11 October.
- Movements: the battalion starts October in billets at Villers Camp, moves into reserve on Vimy Ridge on the night of 2/3 October, takes over the front line in the right section of Chaudiere from the 19th Battalion on the night of 8/9 October, is relieved by the 28th Battalion on the night of 10/11 October and returns to Villers Camp by light railway, then marches to Frevillers on 15 October (8.30 a.m. to 4 p.m.), and finally entrains at Ligny-St.-Flochel on 23–24 October for the Caestre area, detraining at Godewaersveldt and marching to camp at Caestre on 25 October, where it remains to the end of the month.
- Officers named: Lieut-Col. H. E. Jones (commanding, signed the diary 31 October); Lieut D. M. Northcombe (returned from hospital, 5 October); Lt J. Irons (attached to 2nd Division Training Battalion as instructor, 9 October); Lt H. L. Mitchell (returned from hospital, 12 October); Capt W. C. Michell (proceeded on leave, 17 October); Lieut J. F. Perkins (arrived as reinforcement, 18–22 October); Lieut W. S. Caldwell and Lieut W. H. Fenton (proceeded on leave, 18–22 October); Capt W. J. Sanders (rejoined from hospital, 29 October); Capt. V. H. Smallpiece (entraining officer, Order No. D.165); Lieut. F. J. Perkins (named in Order No. D.165, para. 12 — the initials are reversed from the diary's "J. F. Perkins"); Sgts. Rowlands, Callander and Nelson (Picquet, Order No. D.165); Capt. James S. Bell [uncertain] (Adjutant, signed Orders Nos. D.161 and D.165) and Lieut. Fisher [uncertain] (Adjutant, signed Order No. D.159).
- Notable: the month is quiet in the line — "Nothing unusual occurred" on 4 and 9 October, the position described as quiet, and the frontage patrolled by battalion scouts on the night of 8/9 October with no enemy encountered. Most of the month is working parties and fatigues under the 185th (T) Company, R.E., repairing trenches and building dug-outs on Vimy Ridge, followed by training, baths, clothing and pay parades, and recreational games at Villers Camp, Frevillers and Caestre. Reinforcements arrive on 14 October (11 O.R. plus 9 casuals rejoining) and in the 18–22 October block (27 O.R.).
- Contents/appendix disagreements: the contents page dates Order No. 161 to the 6th October, but the order itself is dated d/14.10.17; and the contents page calls the third order "Order D. 163", but the order itself is numbered D.165. The contents page also spells the destination "FRIVILLERS" where the diary and orders spell it "FREVILLERS".
- No date gaps: entries run 1–31 October without a break, except that 18–22 October are covered by a single bracketed block of text rather than day-by-day entries.
