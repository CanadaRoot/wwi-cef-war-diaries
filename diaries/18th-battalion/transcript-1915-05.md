## Transcription conventions

- Transcribed faithfully from the LAC page images only. Spelling, punctuation, abbreviations and capitalisation are as written in the original, including apparent errors (e.g. "form Course" for "from Course").
- `[uncertain]` marks any word or name that could not be read with confidence. Names are never guessed.
- The diary text is on Army Form C. 2118, headed "WAR DIARY or INTELLIGENCE SUMMARY" (the words "INTELLIGENCE SUMMARY" are struck through), with typed columns Place | Date | Hour | Summary of Events and Information | Remarks and references to Appendices.
- Ditto marks (") in the Place and Date columns repeat the entry above.
- The appendices are typed "Syllabus of Training" sheets for the weeks ending 22nd and 29th May 1915.

## Page 3 — Diary text, 29 April–27 May 1915

Army Form C. 2118. Handwritten at the top: "Apr 29-1915 to May 31-1915", "18th Bn. C.E.F." and "4th Inf Bde C.E.F." The form heading "INTELLIGENCE SUMMARY" is struck through.

### 29 April 1915 — Avonmouth

> 6.00 A.M. Arrived on R.M.S. GRAMPIAN.

### 29 April 1915 — W. Sandling Camp

> 3.00 P.M. Arrived at WEST SANDLING CAMP. and Allotted Quarters.

### 4 May 1915 — W. Sandling Camp

> 32 other Ranks detailed to attend course in Physical Training and Bayonet Fighting at NAPIER BARRACKS. SHORNCLIFFE.

### 7 May 1915 — W. Sandling Camp

> CAPTS. HALLAM, PERRY, SHUTTLEWORTH, and LOGHRIN, and 17 other Ranks to report to O.C. 17th. Batt for Course as above. LIEUT. W. J. FORBES-MITCHELL, LIEUT. RISPIN, and 6 other Ranks detailed for Course in M. G. ROSS BARRACKS, SHORNCLIFFE.

### 8 May 1915 — W. Sandling Camp

> 2 more other Ranks added to above Course.
>
> MAJORS L.E. JONES, A.C. PRINCE, R. [uncertain]MERTON, C.E. SALE,; LIEUTS. C.P. LAING, J.S. BELL, W.S. McKEOUGH, J.A. McINTOSH and 16 other Ranks detailed for Course in Musketry, to report to O.C. 20th. Batt. DIGGATE.

### 10 May 1915 — W. Sandling Camp

> Transport Detachments 19th. and 20th. Batts. (LIEUT HATCH and 33 other Ranks) attached for Duty and discipline.

### 26 May 1915 — W. Sandling Camp

> Detail of 8-5-15 Returned form Course at DIGGATE.
>
> Remarks column: Training syllabus Appendix 'A'.

### 27 May 1915 — W. Sandling Camp

> 19th and 20th. Detachments ceased to be attached. MAJOR J.T.F. MURPHY, CAPT. McKECHNIE, and CAPT. ROSS, A.M.C. attached to Officers Mess 18th. Batt.
>
> Remarks column: Training Syllabus Appendix B.

Form footer: "1577 Wt. W10791/1773 500,000 1/15 D.D. & L. A.D.S.S./Forms/C. 2118." A signature at the foot of the page is illegible: [uncertain].

Note: no diary entries are recorded for 28–31 May 1915; the month's remaining training is covered by Appendix B (Page 5).

## Page 4 — Appendix A: Syllabus of Training, week ending 22 May 1915

> Syllabus of Training — 18th Battalion, C. E. F.
>
> week ending May 22, 1915. Appendix A.

### Monday, May 17

> 6.30 – 7.00 squad drill without arms.
> 8.00 – 9.00 Physical training.
> 9.00 – 9.30 Extended Order.
> 9.30 – 10.30 Bayonet fighting.
> 10.00 & 10.30 Musketry (rapid loading).
> 10.30 – 11.00 Platoon Drill.
> 11.00 – 11.30 Company Drill.
> 11.30 – 12.00 Bayonet Fighting.
>
> afternoon — as for morning less ½ hour squad drill.

### Tuesday, May 18

> as for Monday.

### Wednesday, May 19

> Morning — as for Monday.
> Afternoon — Entrenching.

### Thursday, May 20

> as for Monday, less 2 hours.
> Night March 2 hours. (Cancelled by Maj. H.L.M.)

### Friday, May 21

> as for Monday.

### Saturday, May 22

> (morning only) as for Monday.

> Note:—
>
> About 700 Officers, N. C. O's and men are practising daily at Range "P" Hythe, and the above syllabus will be carried on with the remainder of the Battalion.
