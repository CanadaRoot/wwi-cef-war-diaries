## Transcription conventions

- Transcribed only from the LAC page images listed above. Spelling, punctuation, abbreviations and capitalisation are as written in the original.
- The diary text is on Army Form C. 2118, "War Diary or Intelligence Summary", with columns Place | Date | Hour | Summary of Events and Information | Remarks and references to Appendices. The form footer reads "T2134. Wt. W708—776. 500000. 4/15. Sir J. C. & S."
- `[uncertain]` marks any word or name that could not be read with confidence — names are never guessed.
- "do" is the diarist's ditto mark; " " (ditto marks) repeat the line above.
- "Battn" = Battalion, "Bn" = Battalion, "Bde" = Brigade, "Coys" = Companies, "O.R." = other ranks, "T.M." = trench mortar, "M.G." = machine gun, "N.C.O." = non-commissioned officer, "Lt-Col." = Lieutenant-Colonel.
- The Remarks column carries a repeated flourished initial on every row that is not legible — [uncertain] — and is not transcribed row by row.
- Place names as written: Calonne (also written CALONNE), Calonne II, Maroc, Bully.

## Page 4 — Diary text, 1–7 December 1916

[Handwritten "Original" at top left. "WAR DIARY 18th Canadian Battalion", with "INTELLIGENCE SUMMARY." struck through, and "December 1916" at top right. Form C. 2118.]

### 1 December 1916 — Calonne II, 8 pm

> 18th Canadian Battalion in front line at CALONNE II sector. On the night of December 1/2 a Minor operation was directed against enemy working party. 1 O.R. admitted to hospital.

### 2 December 1916 — Calonne II

> Position as yesterday, nothing unusual. 1 O.R. returned from Base Hospital.

### 3 December 1916 — Calonne II

> 18th Battalion relieved by the 20th Canadian Battalion. 18th Battalion moved into Brigade Support at CALONNE. Completion of relief in support notified by code word "SMITH".

### 4 December 1916 — Calonne II

> Battalion in Brigade support. Position quiet. Lieut H. R. GERRARD admitted to hospital sick. Major G. F. Morrison authorised to wear badges of rank of Lieut. Colonel.

### 5 December 1916 — Calonne II

> Position as yesterday. 1 O.R. returned from hospital.

### 6 December 1916 — Calonne II

> Do — Lieut HODGINS returned from leave. 2 O.R. returned from hospital.

### 7 December 1916 — Calonne II

> Position as yesterday. Nothing unusual. Lieuts A. G. Donaldson, G. E. Lucas, J. McHardy and A. A. McLean arrived as reinforcements

## Page 5 — Diary text, 8–14 December 1916

["WAR DIARY 18th Canadian Battalion", with "INTELLIGENCE SUMMARY." struck through. Form C. 2118.]

### 8 December 1916 — Calonne

> Position. Brigade support Calonne. Lieut. J. McAlmond [uncertain] admitted to hospital.

### 9 December 1916 — Calonne

> Church Service in Chapel Calonne. Major L. E. Jones returned from leave.

### 10 December 1916 — Maroc, 11 am

> 18th Canadian Battalion relieved 20th Canadian Battalion in the MAROC Sector. Completion of relief notified by Code word "LAW". 2 O.R. returned from Base Hospital.

### 11 December 1916 — Maroc

> Position as yesterday. Capt W. J. Baxter and Lieut W. S. McClinton received their orders to Canada. Enemy quiet. Our T.Ms shelled enemy wire with good effect

### 12 December 1916 — Maroc

> Position quiet. Patrol reports enemy frontage all quiet. 2 O.R. admitted to hosp.

### 13 December 1916 — Maroc

> Position as yesterday. Enemy aircraft active. Pte de Monbey [uncertain] claims 1 Hun. Our artillery did heavy damage among enemy wire entanglements.

### 14 December 1916 — Maroc, 5.30 am

> Position same. Our artillery very active, doing extensive damage to enemy trenches. Sheets [uncertain] of an explosion fell in Maroc, 1 O.R. admitted to hospital.

## Page 6 — Diary text, 15–21 December 1916

["WAR DIARY 18th Canadian Bn.", with "INTELLIGENCE SUMMARY." struck through. Form C. 2118.]

### 15 December 1916 — Maroc

> Position unchanged. Sgt Hunt shot one Hun who was observing with Field glasses. Our T.Ms opened two wiring parties. Enemy aircraft active. 2 O.R. admitted to hospital.

### 16 December 1916 — Maroc

> 18th Canadian Bn relieved by 20th Battalion in Calonne II sector. (Maroc) 18th Bn moved into Brigade reserve at BULLY. Lieut. E. R. V. Lloyd and H. B. Dunnett proceeded on leave.

### 17 December 1916 — Bully

> Battalion in Brigade Reserve. Bath and Clothing parades. Routine. Lieut B. J. Bates [uncertain] previously reported "missing" is now reported "Killed in Action". Capt E. E. Gwyn arrived as reinforcement.

### 18 December 1916 — Bully

### 19 December 1916 — Bully

> Position as yesterday. Lieut. G. S. Brooks arrived as reinforcement. Lieut J. J. Richardson D.S.O. returned from leave.

### 20 December 1916 — Bully

### 21 December 1916 — Bully

> — do — Lieuts. G. V. Irwin and L. V. Gay arrived as reinforcements. Major J. A. McIntosh to be Adjutant vice Capt. W. J. Baxter to Canada.

## Page 7 — Diary text, 22–26 December 1916

["WAR DIARY 18th Canadian Battalion", with "INTELLIGENCE SUMMARY." struck through. Form C. 2118. The Place column is blank for 22 December.]

### 22 December 1916 — 9.30 am

> 18th Canadian relieved the 20th Canadian Bn. in the Calonne II sector. Right Front A Coy, Right support B Coy, Left Front C Coy, Left Support D Coy. Major G. F. Morrison from 19th Battalion to be Lt-Col. and command 18th Battalion. Position quiet.

### 23 December 1916 — Calonne II

> Position as shown yesterday. Enemy busy repairing his trenches. Patrol under Sgt Dougall crossed frontage and reported all quiet.

### 24 December 1916 — Calonne II

> Position as yesterday. 2 O.R. reported wounded. Patrols crossed Battalion frontage and reported all quiet.

### 25 December 1916 — Calonne II

> Position same. Lieut T. C. Might [uncertain] admitted to hospital. Enemy seems to have very much water in his trenches. Patrol reports all quiet.

### 26 December 1916 — Calonne II, 9 pm

> Minor operation was carried out. Operation order attached. Report:— Raiding party under Lieut Eastwood entered enemy line and worked down the trench. No enemy were encountered at all. A rifle was found in one unoccupied bay. Several dug outs were bombed but no Boches were seen. Our casualties were nil. Lieut H. N. Garner arrived as reinforcement.

## Page 8 — Diary text, 27–31 December 1916

### 27 December 1916 — Calonne II

> Position as shown. Our Artillery shelled Enemy wire doing considerable damage. Enemy very nervous, using M. Gs. frequently. 1 O.R. admitted to hospital.

### 28 December 1916 — Calonne II

> 18th Battalion relieved by 20th Battalion in Calonne II sector. 18th Battalion moved into Brigade support at Calonne. 1 O.R. admitted to hospital sick.

### 29 December 1916 — Calonne

> Battalion in Brigade Support. Lt Col. T. J. Johnston and Major. R. P. Watt attached for instruction. 5 O.R. admitted to hospital.

### 30 December 1916 — Calonne

> Position as yesterday. 1 O.R. Wounded and 3 O.R. sick admitted to hospital.

### 31 December 1916 — Calonne

> Position as yesterday, nothing unusual. 6 O.R. sick admitted to hospital. 1 O.R. reported "Died of wounds".

## Page 10 — Appendix I, Report on Minor raid, night of 1/2 December

[Typed.]

> Report on Minor raid, Night of Dec.1/2.
>
> 18th. Canadian Battalion.
>
> Our patrol advanced within short distance of enemy wire but no party were there, so we lay in wait for several hours hoping it would appear.
>
> Information:—shell holes were signs of occupation by enemy covering parties as foot prints and cylindrical sticks were found. Enemy was very nervous and put up many flares and fired twice on our patrol.
>
> Seeing that enemy would not come out and the wire was too thick to enter his trench our patrol came in.
>
> Signed. H. D. Dunnett Lieut.
>
> O.C. Special Service Platoon.
>
> 18th. Canadian Battalion.

## Notes on this month

- Casualties: Lieut B. J. Bates [uncertain] — previously reported "missing", now reported "Killed in Action" on 17 December; 2 O.R. wounded 24 December; 1 O.R. wounded 30 December; 1 O.R. reported "Died of wounds" on 31 December (name not given). No other battle casualties are recorded; the rest of the month's losses are sickness and hospital admissions.
- Movements: the battalion rotates between the front line at Calonne II, Brigade Support at Calonne, the Maroc sector, and Brigade Reserve at Bully, relieved in turn by the 20th Battalion — reliefs on 3, 10, 16, 22 and 28 December, with code words "SMITH" (3 December) and "LAW" (10 December).
- Officers named: Major G. F. Morrison (authorised to wear the badges of rank of Lieut-Colonel 4 December; from the 19th Battalion, to be Lt-Col. and command the 18th Battalion 22 December, and signs the month's diary as commanding officer); Major J. A. McIntosh (to be Adjutant vice Capt. W. J. Baxter, 21 December); Capt W. J. Baxter and Lieut W. S. McClinton (ordered to Canada, 11 December); Lieut H. R. Gerrard (to hospital sick 4 December, returned 18 December); Lt Col. T. J. Johnston and Major R. P. Watt (attached for instruction, 29 December); Major L. E. Jones (returned from leave 9 December); Lieut J. J. Richardson D.S.O. (returned from leave 19 December); Lieuts Donaldson, Lucas, McHardy and McLean, Capt E. E. Gwyn, Lieut G. S. Brooks, Lieuts G. V. Irwin and L. V. Gay, and Lieut H. N. Garner (arrived as reinforcements).
- Notable: two minor operations against the enemy at Calonne II — the night of 1/2 December, a 25-man patrol against enemy working parties that found no enemy, waited hours, and came in under flares and fire (Appendix I); and the night of 26/27 December, a raid under Lieut Eastwood that entered the enemy trench, found it empty, bombed several dugouts and took no casualties (Appendix II). Sgt Hunt shot a German observing with field glasses on 15 December, and Pte de Monbey [uncertain] is credited with claiming 1 Hun on 13 December. Christmas Day passed quietly in the trenches at Calonne II, the enemy's trenches reported full of water.
- No date gaps: entries run 1–31 December without a break.
- Anomalies: page 2 is a duplicate filming of the cover, and page 12 is a duplicate filming of Appendix II (Operation Order No. D-4); the diary text pages are numbered 2–6 on the forms, while the contents page lists "Diary Text 5 Pages", which agrees. The Place column is blank for the 22 December entry. The contents list agrees with the appendices present.
