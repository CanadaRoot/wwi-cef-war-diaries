## Transcription conventions

- Transcribed only from the LAC page images listed above. Spelling, punctuation, abbreviations and capitalisation are as written in the original.
- The diary text is on Army Form C. 2118, "War Diary or Intelligence Summary", with columns Place | Date | Hour | Summary of Events and Information | Remarks and references to Appendices. The form footer reads "(A998) Wt. W2544/M2253 75,000 1/17. D. D. & L., Ltd. Forms/C.2118/14."
- `[uncertain]` marks any word or name that could not be read with confidence — names are never guessed.
- "do" is the diarist's ditto mark; " " (ditto marks) repeat the line above.
- "Battn" = Battalion, "Bn" = Battalion, "Cdn." = Canadian, "Coy" = Company, "O.R." = other ranks, "G.O.C." = General Officer Commanding, "L.G." = Lewis Gun, "R.A.P." = Regimental Aid Post, "S.B.R." = Small Box Respirator, "P.T." = Physical Training, "B.F." = Bayonet Fighting, "C.T." = Communication Trench, "H.Q." = Headquarters, "Q.M." = Quartermaster.
- The Remarks column on the diary pages carries a repeated mark on every row that reads like "C.P.L." — [uncertain] — and is not transcribed row by row.
- The syllabus sheets are typed, each on its own leaf, and the reverse of each leaf was filmed as the following page image. The reverses carry only show-through from the front and the handwritten word "Paymaster" (in pencil, top right), and are noted as such rather than transcribed in full.
- Place names as written: Febvin-Palfart (also written FEBVIN-PALFART), Auchel, Camblain L'Abbe, Neuville St Vaast, Avion, Lens, Arras, La Coulotte, Nedonchelle, Amettes, Ferfay, Cauchy-à-la-Tour, Divion, Houdain, Gauchin Legal, Villers-au-Bois, Le Pendu, Mont-St-Eloy, La Targette.

## Page 4 — Diary text, 1–6 January 1918

[Handwritten "January 1918" at top and "18th Canadian Infantry Battalion" at top right. Place column reads "Febvin Palfart". Form C. 2118.]

### 1 January 1918 — Febvin Palfart, 9 am to 10.30

> Company Parades and Inspection. Physical Training until 10.30 when Battalion was dismissed for the day Being New Years Day

### 2 January 1918 — Febvin Palfart, am

> Training as per Syllabus attached. A & B coys proceeded to Ranges for Musketry in the morning and C & D coys in afternoon. Lt. J. McAmmon [uncertain] proceeded on leave to England.

### 3 January 1918 — Febvin Palfart, 9.15 a.m.

> Battalion parade in marching order for Route march, returning to Billets about 1.30 pm.

### 4 January 1918 — Febvin Palfart, 9 am

> Bathing Parades occupied the whole of the day. Major T. A. McIntosh proceeded to England for Senior Officers Course. Capt. J. S. Bell taking over command of the Battalion. 2 O.R. admitted to hospital sick.

### 5 January 1918 — Febvin Palfart, 1.30

> Company Parades and Inspection of Arms and Equipment. Training as per Syllabus attached. Capt. C. P. Laing proceeded to A.G.'s office [uncertain] for tour of duty. 4 O.R. to hospital sick.

### 6 January 1918 — Febvin Palfart, 9 am

> Battalion Parade for Divine Service. Inspection of Companies by Commanding Officer after which Battalion was dismissed for the day.

## Page 5 — Diary text, 7–12 January 1918

[Handwritten "18th Canadian Infantry Battalion" at top. Place column reads "Febvin Palfart". Form C. 2118.]

### 7 January 1918 — Febvin Palfart, 8.30 to 11 am

> Training in Bayonet Fighting and Platoon in attack 11.30. Battalion Parade in Full marching order for inspection by Commanding Officer.

### 8 January 1918 — Febvin Palfart, 7.30

> Battalion parade in Heavy marching order for inspection by Divisional Commander. Officers and men were complimented on the clean and smart turnout of the Battalion. Notification of award of M.C. to Capt G. W. F. Hawkins and R.S.M. Garrod [uncertain].

### 9 January 1918 — Febvin Palfart

> A and C coys proceeded to Ranges for firing practice. B and D coys Training as per Syllabus attached. 2 O.R. admitted to hospital sick.

### 10 January 1918 — Febvin Palfart, 8.30 am to 12.30

> Training in Assault and attack. 2 pm to 4 pm Recreational Training 1 O.R. returned from Hospital

### 11 January 1918 — Febvin Palfart

> Clothing Parades and cleaning Kit, owing to wet weather no training being carried out. 2 O.R. returned from hospital.

### 12 January 1918 — Febvin Palfart

> Battalion Parade in Fighting order for inspection by the G.O.C. Canadian Corps. Battalion again being complimented on its smart and efficient turnout. Lieut J. M. Fisher proceeded to England on Special duty.

## Page 6 — Diary text, 13–18 January 1918

[Handwritten "18th Canadian Infantry Battalion" at top. Place column reads "FEBVIN PALFART" for 13–14 January, then "Move" for 15 January. Form C. 2118.]

### 13 January 1918 — Febvin Palfart, 9.30

> Battalion Parade for Divine Service, after which the men were dismissed for the day. 2 O.R. returned from Hospital.

### 14 January 1918 — Febvin Palfart, 8.30

> Company inspections and Training in Live Bomb Throwing.

### 15 January 1918 — Move, 9 a.m.

> Battalion Parade in Full marching order for move to AUCHEL, passing via Nedonchelle, Amettes, Ferfay, and Cauchy à la Tour, arriving at Auchel about 3 p.m. Rain all day.

### 16 January 1918 — Move, am

> Battalion marched from Auchel to Camblain L'Abbe via Divion, Houdain, Gauchin Legal, arriving at Camblain about 3 p.m. Battalion billeted in Huts for one night.

### 17 January 1918 — Move

> Battalion marched from Camblain L'Abbe into Reserve at HILL CAMP (Neuville St Vaast) arriving there about 1 p.m. 6 O.R. admitted to hospital sick.

## Page 7 — Diary text, 18–22 January 1918

[Handwritten "18th Canadian Infantry Battalion" at top. Place column reads "Contd." for 18 January, then "Front Line Avion Sector". Form C. 2118.]

### 18 January 1918 (continued) — Contd.

> Outposts, 34 in number. 16 of them being occupied by left company and 18 by the right coy. Owing to cloudy and misty weather, the Battalion was able to move along the LENS-ARRAS road to Communication trenches in daylight, completing relief by 7.35 p.m.

### 19 January 1918 — Front Line Avion Sector

> Position as shown yesterday. Trenches being in poor condition owing to recent rainstorms and bad weather. Situation quiet. 19 O.R. attached to 1st Cdn Tunnelling Coy. 1 O.R. slightly wounded.

### 20 January 1918 — Front Line Avion Sector

> Position as shown. Nothing unusual occured. 2 O.R. admitted to hospital sick.

### 21 January 1918 — Front Line Avion Sector

> During night of 21/22 inter company relief was completed, disposition now being, "C" coy left front, "D" coy right front. "A" coy left support and "B" coy right support. Relief completed 10.05 p.m.

[Remarks column: "See maps attd."]

### 22 January 1918 — Front Line Avion Sector

> Position as shown yesterday. Situation quiet, 4 O.R. admitted to hospital sick.

## Notes on this month

- Casualties: 1 O.R. slightly wounded 19 January; 2 O.R. wounded 23 January. No men killed in action are recorded in January 1918; the rest of the month's losses are sickness and hospital admissions (2–6 O.R. at a time), plus 19 O.R. attached to the 1st Canadian Tunnelling Company on 19 January.
- Movements: the battalion trains at Febvin-Palfart 1–14 January, then marches in three stages — Febvin-Palfart to Auchel (15 January), Auchel to Camblain L'Abbe (16 January), Camblain L'Abbe to Hill Camp, Neuville St Vaast (17 January) — and relieves the 19th Battalion in the front line in the Avion Sector on the night of 18/19 January. An inter-company relief swaps the front and support companies on the night of 21/22 January. The 20th Battalion relieves the 18th in the front line on the night of 23/24 January and the 18th moves into right support, where it works on the communication trenches until relieved by the 29th Battalion on the night of 28/29 January, when it moves back into reserve at Hills Camp.
- Officers named: Major T. A. McIntosh (to England for the Senior Officers Course, 4 January); Capt. J. S. Bell (takes command of the Battalion, 4 January; signs the orders as Adjutant, then as Commanding Officer); Capt. C. P. Laing (to the A.G.'s office for a tour of duty, 5 January); Capt. G. W. F. Hawkins and R.S.M. Garrod [uncertain] (notified of Military Cross awards, 8 January); Lieut. J. M. Fisher (to England on special duty, 12 January); Lieut. D. S. Wigle [uncertain] (joined the Battalion, 25 January); Lieut. H. L. Connolly [uncertain] (arrived as reinforcement, 27 January); Lieut. Col. L. E. Jones (returned from leave, 24 January; signs the diary); Lieut. J. C. Brookes [uncertain] (admitted to hospital sick, 30 January); Lieut. J. McAmmon [uncertain] (on leave to England, 2 January); Lieut. Donaldson (Lewis Gunners); Lieut. Spencer (scouts, patrolling instruction); Sgt. Borthwick (signallers); Sgt. Smith (scouts); C. Cook, Lieutenant (Assistant Adjutant, signs the later syllabuses).
- Notable: the battalion is inspected by the Divisional Commander on 8 January and by the G.O.C. Canadian Corps on 12 January, and complimented on its smart and efficient turnout on both occasions; medal ribbons are presented after the Corps inspection on 12 January. The diary is signed by Lt.-Col. L. E. Jones. The month is dominated by training at Febvin-Palfart (musketry, bayonet fighting, bombing, assault courses, gas drill) and then by trench work in support — cleaning and improving the Cyril, Beaver, Clucas, Adept, Mersey and Red communication trenches, with map references recorded in the work reports.
- No date gaps: entries run 1–31 January without a break, except that 24–28 January are covered by a single bracketed block, and there is no training syllabus for Friday 11 January in the filmed set.
- Anomalies and disagreements:
  - The contents page (p. 3) describes Orders 178 and 179 as "14th Battalion may go home" and "18th Battn. may go over the Top when fit and ready", but the orders as filed are the march order (Order 178, p. 37) and the relief of the 47th Battalion in the front line (Order 179, p. 38).
  - The 2 January syllabus (p. 12) is headed "January 2nd 1917" although it is dated 1.1.18 at the foot.
  - The 14 January syllabus (p. 35) has three lines under "A" Company heavily struck through and illegible.
  - Order 182's para. 7 is filmed (p. 46) before sheet II carrying paras. 3–6 (p. 47), so the order's paragraphs appear out of sequence in the filmed pages.
  - Pages 39 and 40 are duplicate filmings of the same reverse side of Order 179, both carrying the same two pencil note lines naming Pte J. Douglas (B Coy) and one other private (A Coy) [uncertain].
  - The 31 January work report (p. 55) is dated at the foot "1.2.18".
  - The diary's 21 January entry refers to maps attached; the two maps (pp. 57–60) are filed at the end of the month's appendices.
