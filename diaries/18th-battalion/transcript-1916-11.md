## Transcription conventions

- Transcribed only from the LAC page images listed above. Spelling, punctuation, abbreviations and capitalisation are as written in the original.
- The diary text is on Army Form C. 2118, "War Diary or Intelligence Summary", with columns Place | Date | Hour | Summary of Events and Information | Remarks and references to Appendices. The form footer reads "2449 Wt. W14957/M90 750,000 1/16 J.B.C. & A. Forms/C.2118/12."
- `[uncertain]` marks any word or name that could not be read with confidence — names are never guessed.
- "Do" is the diarist's ditto mark; " " (ditto marks) repeat the line above.
- "Battn" = Battalion, "Bn" = Battalion, "Infy" = Infantry, "Bgde" = Brigade, "Coys" = Companies, "O.R." = other ranks, "S.O.S." = struck off strength, "R.F.C." = Royal Flying Corps, "G.O.C." = General Officer Commanding, "N.C.O." = non-commissioned officer, "L/Cpl" = Lance Corporal, "M.G." = machine gun.
- The Remarks column carries a repeated flourished initial on every row that is not legible — [uncertain] — and is not transcribed row by row.
- Place names as written: BULLY, CALONNE, CALONNE II, Maroc Sector, Front line, Move.

## Page 3 — Diary text, 1–5 November 1916

[Handwritten "Original" and "Confidential" at top left; "18th Canadian Battalion" handwritten over the struck-through "INTELLIGENCE SUMMARY"; "November 1916" handwritten at top right. Form C. 2118.]

### 1 November 1916 — Bully

> 18th Canadian Battalion in Brigade reserve, Company and platoon drill. Units special Training. Extract from [uncertain — the order prefix is unclear] D.R.O 2976 (To be Acting Staff Captain 4th Canadian Infy Brigade. Capt. D.E.A. RISPIN 18th Can. Bn." Lieut Col. H. L. MILLIGAN. Taken on the strength of C.T.D and posted to General List. from 9-10-16. 2 o.r. returned from hospital.

### 2 November 1916 — Do

> Battalion Pay and Clothing Parades. Major C P LAING to be Hon Capt and Paymaster with the 18th Canadian Bn from 29-9-16.

### 3 November 1916 — Do

> Foot inspection by Company Commanders Inspection of N.C.Os by G.O.C. 4th Can Infy Brigade Marching order without packs. Inspection of all rifles by Brigade Armourer. Lieut H S ALLEN. M.C S.O.S on account of being Transfered to the R.F.C.

### 4 November 1916 — Move, 9 am

> 18th Battalion relieves 20th Battalion in front line CALONNE II sector. A and C coys in front line B and D coys in support. Companies moved off by platoons at 200 yds interval. Completion of reliefs wire to Battalion H.Q. Code. H.C. 1 o.r accidentally wounded. 3 o.r admitted to hospital.

### 5 November 1916 — Front Line

> Position as shown yesterday. Nothing unusual. Lieut. G. S Brooks to England sick and S.O.S. from 12:10.16.

## Page 4 — Diary text, 6–10 November 1916

[Handwritten "Confidential" at top left; "18th Canadian Battalion" handwritten over the struck-through "INTELLIGENCE SUMMARY". Form C. 2118.]

### 6 November 1916 — Front line, Calonne II

> Position as yesterday. Lieut. S.C. KIRKLAND admitted to hospital (sick) 4 o.r. sick admitted to hospital. Nothing unusual, line all quiet.

### 7 November 1916 — Do, 10.30 pm

> Position as yesterday. Violent Trembling of Southern Crassier was felt for 10 sec at 9am. Two patrols under Sgt. [uncertain] Dougall and L/Cpl Wallis covered frontage. Sergt Dougall noticed an enemy wiring party. He returned and got a Lewis Gun out to the lip of Crater M.4.C.6.64 [uncertain — the map reference is unclear] and fired upon the party, who were only 20 yds away. Many were heard to cry out, and the Enemy immediately opened up with his M.Gs British M.Gs from Crassier also opened up. 1 o.r. (Accidently wounded) to hospital.

### 8 November 1916 — Do

> Position as yesterday. Two patrols covered Battalion frontage and reported all quiet. 1 o.r. returned from hospital.

### 9 November 1916 — Do

> Position as yesterday. Nothing unusual. Cpl. HUNT, sniper. shot 1 Hun. 2 patrols covered frontage and reported all quiet. 1 o.r. admitted to hospital, 1 o.r. returned from hospital. Major G.F. Morrison proceeded on leave.

### 10 November 1916 — Do

> 18th Canadian Battn relieved by 20th Canadian Bn in front line 18th Battalion moved into Brigade support at CALONNE. Lieut. S.C. Kirkland returned from hospital.

## Page 6 — Diary text, 19–24 November 1916

["18th Canadian Battalion" handwritten over the struck-through "INTELLIGENCE SUMMARY". Form C. 2118.]

### 19 November 1916 — Calonne II

> Position same. Lieut F.E. Boekel [uncertain] arrives as reinforcement. 2 o.r. returned from hospital. Enemy very alert near the Crassier. Our patrol was unable to get close owing to numerous flares used by enemy. The enemy was reported to be very alert on the whole front.

### 20 November 1916 — Do

> Position as yesterday. Nothing unusual. 4 o.r. admitted to hospital 1 o.r. returned from hospital.

### 21 November 1916 — Do

> Position quiet. Four patrols covered whole frontage and a Protective patrol remained out until Dawn. Enemy working party was dispersed by our Lewis guns. 1 o.r. wounded admitted to hospital.

### 22 November 1916 — [Place column blank]

> 18th Battn relieved by 20 Can Bn in Calonne II Sector; 18th Battalion moved into Brigade Reserve at BULLY. Enemy very busy repairing wire entanglements.

### 23 November 1916 — Bully

> Battalion in Brigade Reserve. Company & Platoon drill and Instruction Maj. J. A. McIntosh returned from hospital. 2 o.r. returned from hospital.

### 24 November 1916 — Do

> Brigade Reserve. Routine. 1 o.r. returned from hospital.

## Notes on this month

- Casualties: no men are recorded killed in action this month. 1 other rank accidentally wounded 4 November; 1 other rank accidentally wounded 7 November; 1 other rank wounded 21 November. On 14 November, 8 other ranks previously reported wounded were reclassified as "Wounded and Missing". The rest of the month's losses are sickness and hospital admissions.
- Movements: the battalion rotates between Brigade Reserve at Bully, the front line in the Calonne II sector, and Brigade Support at Calonne, relieved in turn by the 20th Battalion. On 29 November it took over the front line in the Maroc Sector from the 20th Battalion.
- Officers named: Capt. D. E. A. Rispin (appointed Acting Staff Captain, 4th Canadian Infantry Brigade, 1 November); Lieut-Col. H. L. Milligan (posted to the General List, 1 November); Major C. P. Laing (Hon. Captain and Paymaster, 2 November); Lieut H. S. Allen, M.C. (transferred to the Royal Flying Corps, 3 November); Lieut G. S. Brooks (to England sick, 5 November); Lieut S. C. Kirkland (to hospital 6 November, returned 10 November); Major G. F. Morrison (proceeded on leave 9 November; signed the diary as commanding officer); Lieut R. G. Elliot (returned from leave, 13 November); Major J. A. McIntosh (to hospital 16 November, returned 23 November); Lieut F. E. Boekel [uncertain] (reinforcement, 19 November); Sgt. C. J. Jackson (commissioned as Lieutenant, 26 November); Major L. E. Jones (proceeded on leave, 27 November); Lieuts Dupuy and Rowe (reinforcements, 28 November); Capt. J. W. Baxter (proceeded on leave, 30 November).
- Notable: on 7 November Sgt. Dougall's patrol fired a Lewis gun on an enemy wiring party at a crater only 20 yards away, drawing enemy machine-gun fire answered by British machine guns from the Crassier; a violent trembling of the Southern Crassier was felt the same morning. On 18 November a patrol under Sgt. Dougall examining a crater was spotted and fired on with bombs and rifle fire. The enemy was reported very alert near the Crassier, using numerous flares (19 November), and an enemy working party was dispersed by Lewis guns (21 November). Cpl. Hunt, a sniper, shot one enemy soldier on 9 November. Relief completions were signalled by code words — "XTRA" on 28 November and "SPESHUL" on 29 November.
- Cover/contents disagreement: the cover reads "Volume 16" with a "5" written below it, while the contents page gives Volume 15; the "5" appears to be a correction to 15.
- No date gaps: entries run 1–30 November without a break.
