## Transcription conventions

- Transcribed only from the LAC page images listed above. Spelling, punctuation, abbreviations and capitalisation are as written in the original.
- The diary text is on Army Form C. 2118, "War Diary or Intelligence Summary", with columns Place | Date | Hour | Summary of Events and Information | Remarks and references to Appendices. The form footer reads "(Apagn. Wt. W2544/M299 75,000 1/17. D. D. & L., Ltd. Forms/C.2118/14." (variant spellings of the imprint occur across pages).
- `[uncertain]` marks any word or name that could not be read with confidence — names are never guessed. `[struck]` marks text struck through in the original.
- "do" is the diarist's ditto mark; " " (ditto marks) repeat the line above.
- "Battn" = Battalion, "Bn" = Battalion, "Cdn" = Canadian, "C.I.B." = Canadian Infantry Brigade, "C.M.R." = Canadian Mounted Rifles, "O.R." = other ranks, "R.T.O." = Railway Transport Officer, "S.B.R." = Small Box Respirator, "H.Q." = Headquarters, "N.C.O." = non-commissioned officer, "M.G." = Machine Gun, "A.D.S." = Advanced Dressing Station.
- The Remarks column carries a repeated flourished initial on every row, resembling "C.P.H." — [uncertain] — and is not transcribed row by row except where it carries an appendix reference or other note.
- Place names as written: Hill Camp, Neuville St. Vaast, Camblain L'Abbe, Mont St. Eloy, Mericourt Sector, Canada Trench, Vancouver Camp, Chateau de La Haie, Zivy, Lens Sector.
- Map references are trench-map sheet references as written, e.g. T.26.a.35.75.

## Page 4 — Diary text, 1–6 February 1918

[Handwritten "18th CANADIAN BATTALION" and "INTELLIGENCE SUMMARY." at top, "February 1918" at top right, "Confidential" at top left. Form C. 2118.]

### 1 February 1918 — Hill Camp (Neuville St Vaast)

> Battalion in Reserve at Hills Camp near Neuville St Vaast. Company parades and training. Specialist training of Lewis gunners, bombers, and grenadiers. Four ranks rejoined from hospitals.

### 2 February 1918 — Relief, 11 a.m.

> 18th Battalion relieved at Hills Camp by 21st Canadian Battalion. On relief 18th Battalion moved to Billets at CAMBLAIN L'ABBE, passing via Mont St Eloy. 2 O.Rs rejoined from Hospitals.

### 3 February 1918 — Camblain L'Abbe, 10 a.m.

> Battalion parade for Divine Service in Cinema Hut. During afternoon A and C Coys proceeded to 12 Motte Ranges [uncertain] for firing practice. 3 O.Rs admitted to hospital. Lieut. G. A. Brookin [uncertain] (F.A.) [uncertain] rejoined from hospital.

[Remarks column: "App. I." written vertically.]

### 4 February 1918 — Camblain L'Abbe

> Company parades and Training as per Syllabus attached.

### 5 February 1918 — Camblain L'Abbe

> Pay and Bathing parades occupied the whole of the day. 7 O.Rs arrived as reinforcements. 5 other ranks admitted to hospital [uncertain — the line ends with an illegible word].

### 6 February 1918 — Camblain L'Abbe

> Parades and Training as per Syllabus attached. Notification of Award of Belgian Croix de Guerre to Lieut. F. Morgan, Sgt. J. Faulkner, Ptes. H. W. J. Clarke and F. K. Showler.

### 7 February 1918 — Camblain L'Abbe

> Parades and inspection of Arms and Equipment as per Syllabus attached. 2 O.R. admitted to hospital [uncertain — the line ends with an illegible word].

## Page 6 — Diary text, 9–17 February 1918 (continued)

[Handwritten "18th Canadian Battalion" and "INTELLIGENCE SUMMARY." at top. Form C. 2118.]

### 9–15 February 1918 (continued) — Support

> Light Railway runs. Rations for Bn H.Q. and C Coy were brought up by this Railway and those for A, B and D Coys by horse transport to junction of Canada Trench & New Brunswick Rd. Trench wardens were detailed from Companies and the whole Communication and support trenches in the area were cleaned and minor repairs effected. 9 O.Rs (wounded) rejoined from hospitals.

### 16 February 1918 — Relief; 36C S.W. Battalion Frontage N.17.d.75.70 to N.11.C.00.10.

> During night of 15/16th 18th Battalion relieved the 20th Canadian Battalion in Right subsection with H.Q. at T.16.C.9.3. Disposition of companies being A and B Coys Right and left front line with D and C Coys in Right and left support. Relief being completed without casualty at 3.15 p.m.
>
> During night 16/17th 4 patrols of 18th Bn under Lieuts Spencer + McCrae [uncertain — one further word illegible] patrolled No man's land on Battalion frontage, no enemy were encountered and there was nothing unusual to report.

### 17 February 1918

> During afternoon an inter company relief was completed. D and C Coys relieving A and B Coys in front line, A + B Coys taking up positions vacated by D and C Coys in support. Relief being completed at 3.30 p.m.

## Page 7 — Diary text, 18–22 February 1918

[Handwritten "18th Canadian Battalion" and "INTELLIGENCE SUMMARY." at top. Form C. 2118.]

### 18 February 1918 — Front line, Mericourt Sector

> Training as shown. Considerable enemy activity. Planes patrolling both lines. Situation quiet, nothing unusual occurred.

### 19 February 1918

> Battalion relieved in front line by 4th C.M.R. Battalion. Relief being completed at 9 p.m. 18th Battalion being conveyed by Light Railway from Zivy Station to Vancouver Camp at Chateau de La Haie. The first train leaving Zivy about midnight. 16 O.R. rejoined from 1st Cdn Tunnelling Coy.

### 20 February 1918 — Vancouver Camp

> Bath and clothing parades. Inspection of arms and equipment. 2 O.R. returned from hospital.

### 21 February 1918 — Vancouver Camp

> Battalion pay parades occupied the whole of the day. Lieut. C. E. Oglesby rejoined Battalion from 4th Div. M. G. Coy.

### 22 February 1918 — Vancouver Camp

> Company parades and inspections. Training in Bayonet fighting and attack. Recreational training in afternoon. 2 O.R. admitted to hospital.

## Page 8 — Diary text, 23–27 February 1918

[Handwritten "18th Canadian Battalion" and "INTELLIGENCE SUMMARY." at top. Form C. 2118.]

### 23 February 1918 — Vancouver Camp

> Training and Musketry as per Syllabus attached. 6 O.R. arrived as Reinforcements. 5 O.R. returned from hospital. Lieut W. K. Rooney admitted to hospital. Major General W. B. Hodgins [uncertain] who was A.O.C. M.D. No.1 LONDON ONTARIO when the 18th Bn. was mobilized inspected the Battalion and its Transport lines.

[Remarks column: the initials "C.P.H." with a further word below, illegible — [uncertain].]

### 24 February 1918 — Vancouver Camp, 9.30

> Battalion Parade for Divine Service in 2nd Div. Theatre. after which the men were dismissed for the day.

### 25 February 1918 — Vancouver Camp

> Training and instruction of specialists. Battalion furnished working party of 3 Officers and 165 O.R. digging Communication Trench. Capt T. H. O. Hayward proceeded to C.C.R.C. as an Instructor.

### 26 February 1918 — Vancouver Camp

> Battalion (by companies) passed through Cloud Gas in order to test our respirators. Working party of 3 Officers and 150 O.R. worked 4 hours on new Communication Trench. Lieut A. B. Yeo and 1 O.R. being wounded on this fatigue.

### 27 February 1918 — Relief

> During night of 27/28th. 18th Canadian Battalion relieved 25th Battalion in Right section of Lens Sector.

>
> 6. Order of March: "A" "B" "C" "D" "HQ".
>
> 7. Battalion to be ready to move off by 11.00 a.m.
>
> C. Cook Lieutenant.
