## Filming order note

The pages were not filmed in numerical order. Files `2005910-page-0001` through `2005910-page-0009` carry diary pages 1–9 (cover, contents, title page, and the six diary-text pages for 1–31 March). Files `2005910-page-0010` through `2005910-page-0019` carry diary pages 31–40 (Appendix II orders, the W.3121 recommendation form, and the Appendix III work report). Files `2005910-page-0020` through `2005910-page-0040` carry diary pages 10–30 (the Appendix I training syllabuses for 8–22 March, interleaved with their blank reverses). This transcript follows the manifest/file order: pages 1–9, then pages 31–40, then pages 10–30.

## Transcription conventions

- Transcribed only from the LAC page images listed above. Spelling, punctuation, abbreviations and capitalisation are as written in the original.
- The diary text is on Army Form C. 2118, "War Diary or Intelligence Summary", with columns Place | Date | Hour | Summary of Events and Information | Remarks and references to Appendices.
- `[uncertain]` marks any word or name that could not be read with confidence — names are never guessed. `[struck]` marks text struck through in the original.
- "do" is the diarist's ditto mark; " " (ditto marks) repeat the line above.
- "Battn" = Battalion, "Bn" = Battalion, "Cdn" = Canadian, "C.I.B." = Canadian Infantry Brigade, "O.R." = other ranks, "R.T.O." = Railway Transport Officer, "S.B.R." = Small Box Respirator, "S.B." = Small Box Respirator, "H.Q." = Headquarters, "N.C.O." = non-commissioned officer, "L.G." = Lewis Gun, "P.T." = Physical Training, "B.F." = Bayonet Fighting, "R.G." = Rifle Grenade.
- The Remarks column carries a repeated flourished initial on every row, resembling "C.P.H." — [uncertain] — and is not transcribed row by row except where it carries an appendix reference or other note.
- Place names as written: Neuville St Vaast, St. Lawrence Camp, Chateau de la Haie, Le Pendu, Bois des Alleux, Villers au Bois, Roelincourt, Stewart Camp, Bailleulval, Agny, Bellacourt, Neuville Vitasse, Telegraph Hill, Bouraines, Mercatel.
- Map references are trench-map sheet references as written, e.g. M.30.a.40.75.

## Page 4 — Diary text, 1–7 March 1918

[Handwritten "18th Canadian Battalion" and "INTELLIGENCE SUMMARY." at top, "March 1918" at top right, "Confidential" at top left. Form C. 2118. Place column: "MAP Sheet 36 C. S. E."]

### 1 March 1918

> Battalion in front line, Right sub-section of [uncertain] sector. H.Q. being located at M 30 a. 40. 75. Enemy artillery active over whole front. 7 O.Rs arrived as reinforcements, 6 O.Rs rejoined from Hospital.

### 2 March 1918

> Position as yesterday. 6 O.Rs wounded by shrapnel, desultory shelling by enemy over whole area.

### 3 March 1918

> During night of 3/4th. Battalion was relieved in front line by the 20th Canadian Bn. 18th Canadian Bn moved back into Right support. with H.Q. at M 23. C. 50. 60. See O.O. D 191 attached.

### 4 March 1918

> Position as shown. Battalion in support. Nothing unusual occured. Battalion furnished working parties, repairing trenches and carrying material to front line.

### 5 March 1918

> Position as yesterday. Nothing unusual to report.

### 6 March 1918 — St. Lawrence Camp

> 18th Battalion relieved in support during day by 21st Canadian Bn. On relief 18th Battalion moved into Reserve billets at St. LAWRENCE CAMP. CHATEAU de la Haie. See O.O. d. 193.

### 7 March 1918

> Battalion pay & bathing parades occupied the whole of the day.

## Page 5 — Diary text, 8–13 March 1918

[Form C. 2118. Place column: "St. LAWRENCE CAMP" for 8th–11th, "Le PENDU" for 12th.]

### 8 March 1918 — St. Lawrence Camp

> Company Parades and inspections of Rifles & equipment. Platoon Training by Platoon Officers. Syllabus attached

### 9 March 1918

> Company Parades. Training in Bayonet Fighting and Close order Drill. Recreational training in afternoon.

### 10 March 1918, 9 a.m.

> Battalion parade for Divine service, after which the men were dismissed for the day.

### 11 March 1918, 12 noon

> Battalion moved from Chateau de la Haie to Le Pendu Huts. Bois des Alleux. 1 O.R. returned from Hospital. Lieut C. J. Jackson to be acting Captain from 22 January 1918.

### 12 March 1918 — Le Pendu

> Company Parades commencing at 9. a.m. Physical Training & Bayonet fighting. During afternoon Recreational Training arranged by Sports Officer Capt W. C. Mitchell.

### 13 March 1918, 9 a.m.

> Company Parades. Training in Bombing & Lewis Gun instruction. 2 O.Rs. returned from Hospital. Recreational training in afternoon.

## Page 6 — Diary text, 14–19 March 1918

[Form C. 2118. Place column: "Le PENDU. (Bois des ALLEUX)".]

### 14 March 1918, 8.30 a.m.

> Company Parades. Instruction in Lewis Gun & Bombing. Respirator drill Physical Training and Bayonet fighting. 2 O.Rs rejoined from Hospital. Recreational Training during afternoon.

### 15 March 1918, 8.30 a.m.

> Company inspection of rifles and equipment. Summarized Training as per syllabus attached. 91 O.Rs. arrived from 5th Canadian [uncertain] Division as reinforcements. 2 O.Rs returned from Hospital. Recreational training, i.e. Football Baseball etc during afternoon.

### 16 March 1918

> Parades and Training as yesterday. 8 O.Rs. arrived as reinforcement. 2 O.Rs. returned from Hospital.

### 17 March 1918, 10 a.m.

> Battalion Parade for Divine Service. Bathing Parades from 1.30 p.m. to 5.00 p.m. 3 O.Rs returned from Hospital.

### 18 March 1918

> Company training in Bombing, Lewis Gun, Musketry and Wiring. Recreational training in afternoon. 1 O.R. rejoined from Hospital.

### 19 March 1918

> Battalion Pay parades occupied greater part of the day. Recreational Training & games during afternoon.

## Page 7 — Diary text, 20–26 March 1918

[Form C. 2118. Place column: "Le PENDU Huts" for 20th–23rd, "MAP ROELINCOURT." for 24th, "MAP. LENS. II." for 26th.]

### 20 March 1918 — Le Pendu Huts

> Training and Parades as per syllabus attached. 1 O.R. admitted to and 1 O.R. returned from Hospital.

### 21 March 1918

> Parades and Training as per syllabus attached.

### 22 March 1918

> Battalion "Standing to" prepared to move under orders at 1 hour notice. Training carried on as usual. 10 O.Rs. arrived as reinforcements.

### 23 March 1918

> Battalion still "Standing to" but under 1 hour notice, to any place required. 2 O.Rs. admitted to Hospital.

### 24 March 1918, 9.30 — Roelincourt

> Battalion moved by Bus to Stewart Camp (A 2.7.c) near ROELINCOURT, see O.O. No D 200. 2 O.Rs returned from and 1 O.R. admitted to Hospital. 675 O.Rs on working party clearing trenches on GREEN LINE between Junction Post and Ridge Post.

### 25 March 1918

> Battalion "Standing to" in expectation of Hun attack. Reconnaissance of GREEN LINE and approaches made by Officers and H.Q. Coy.

### 26 March 1918, 9 a.m.

> 600 O.Rs again working on GREEN LINE. Battalion marched to BAILLEULVAL a distance of 14 1/2 miles, the men being billeted in Barns and out houses.

## Page 8 — Diary text, 27–30 March 1918

[Form C. 2118. Place column: "BAILLEULVAL." for 27th–28th, "Neuville Vitasse Map 51B. S.E." for 29th.]

### 27 March 1918 — Bailleulval

> Battalion "Standing to" in Billets. Reconnaissance of roads forward made by Officers. 2 O.Rs admitted to Hospital.

### 28 March 1918

> Battalion "Standing to" all day ready to move at 1 hours notice. Received orders to move and Battalion marched from BAILLEULVAL at 9 p.m. and proceeded to AGNY a distance of 13 1/2 Kilometres, being in support to 3rd Imperial Division. Each man now carrying 170 rounds of ammunition instead of 120. Transport lines located in BELLACOURT.

### 29 March 1918 — Neuville Vitasse

> No enemy attacks developed and the 4th Canadian Infantry Brigade relieved the 3rd Imperial Division in front line, in front of Neuville Vitasse. 18th Battalion taking up position in Left support to Brigade at Telegraph Hill Switch from Railway cutting to BOURAINES - Mercatel Road. Battalion in position by 9.30 p.m. 2 O.Rs slightly wounded.

### 30 March 1918

> Battalion in support. Situation quiet. Further adjustment of Companies made so as to obtain better command of ground. 1 O.R. accidentally wounded.

## Notes

- **Out-of-order filming:** diary pages 31–40 (Appendix II orders, the W.3121 form, and the Appendix III work report) were filmed before diary pages 10–30 (the Appendix I training syllabuses). The transcript follows manifest/file order, as noted in the header.
- **Duplicates:** Order No. 195 (10 March 1918) appears twice, on pages 36 and 37, with identical text. The reverse of the duplicate (page 38) is blank except for a handwritten "War Diary" note.
- **Contents-page discrepancies:** the contents page (page 2) describes Order D.191 as "20th Bn. will relieve 18th Bn. on the 3rd and 4th March" (correct) but describes Order D.193 as "18th Bn. to be relieved by 26th Bn. on the 6th March" — the order as filmed (page 32) says the battalion will be relieved by the **21st** Canadian Battalion, which matches the diary entry for 6 March. The contents page also lists Order D.204 (8 March, "18th Bn. will relieve 31st Bn. on the 9th and 10th April"), but no copy of D.204 appears among the 40 page images.
- **Missing order:** the diary entry for 24 March refers to "O.O. No D 200" (the move to Stewart Camp near Roelincourt); no copy of D.200 appears among the 40 page images.
- **Illegible pages:** the W.3121 recommendation form (page 33, and its reverse on page 11) is a faint carbon copy whose entries cannot be read; the names and actions it records are therefore not transcribed. The reverses of the training syllabuses (pages 13, 15, 20, 24, 26, 28, 30) and of the W.3121 form (page 35) and of the work report (page 40) carry only faint show-through from the other side, plus the handwritten "Paymaster" / "War Diary" filing notes recorded above.
- **Date coverage:** the diary text covers every day from 1 to 31 March 1918 with no gaps. Training syllabuses cover 8–22 March only; no syllabus pages for 1–7 March or 23–31 March are present.
- **Commanding Officer:** the diary is signed on 31 March by Lieut.-Col. H. E. Jones [uncertain — signature], Commanding 18th Canadian Battalion. The appendices are signed throughout by C. Cook, Lieutenant, Assistant Adjutant.
- **Casualties recorded in the diary text:** 2 March — 6 O.Rs wounded by shrapnel; 29 March — 2 O.Rs slightly wounded; 30 March — 1 O.R. accidentally wounded; 31 March — 1 O.R. killed in action, 2 O.R. wounded.
