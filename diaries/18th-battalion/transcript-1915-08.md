## Transcription conventions

- Transcribed faithfully from the LAC page images only; spelling, punctuation, abbreviations and capitalisation as written.
- `[uncertain]` marks any word or name that could not be read with confidence — never guessed.
- The diary for this month is typed on Army Form C. 2118, with columns Place | Date | Hour | Summary of Events and Information. The printed heading "INTELLIGENCE SUMMARY" is struck through and "WAR DIARY" used instead. Handwritten corrections to the typed text are noted as [handwritten].
- Ditto marks in the original are kept as "— do —" where they occur.
- The month covers 2–30 August 1915 only; no entries for 1 or 31 August appear, and several dates within the month have no entry.

## Notes on this month

- The battalion spent the whole month in training in England at West Sandling Camp, Kent: divisional reviews, brigade attacks, trench warfare practice, bivouacs, route marches and divisional manoeuvres. No front-line service and no casualties are recorded in this month's diary.
- No entries are recorded for 3, 5, 7, 9, 15, 17, 19, 21, 26, 27, 28 or 29 August, or for 1 and 31 August.
- People named: Major General Hughes, Mr. Bonar Law, Col. de Prey (G.S.O.).
