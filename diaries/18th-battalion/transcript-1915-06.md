## Transcription conventions

- Transcribed faithfully from the LAC page images only. Spelling, punctuation, abbreviations and capitalisation are as written in the original.
- `[uncertain]` marks any word or name that could not be read with confidence. Names are never guessed.
- The diary text is on Army Form C. 2118, headed "WAR DIARY or INTELLIGENCE SUMMARY" (the words "INTELLIGENCE SUMMARY" are struck through), with typed columns Place | Date | Hour | Summary of Events and Information | Remarks and references to Appendices.
- Ditto marks (") in the Place and Date columns repeat the entry above.
- The appendices are typed "Syllabus of Training" sheets. Appendix references in the diary text are given as written ("Appendix D", "appendix E"); the contents page and the syllabus sheets themselves use the A2–G2 numbering, so "Appendix D" of 28 June is Appendix D2 and "appendix E" of 29 June is Appendix E2.

## Page 3 — Diary text, 1–29 June 1915

Army Form C. 2118. Handwritten at the top: "June-1-1915 to June-30-1915", "18th Bn. C.E.F." and "4th Inf. Bde C.E.F." The form heading "INTELLIGENCE SUMMARY" is struck through. A stamp at the top right reads: "PAY & RECORD OFFICE JUL 22 1915 CANADIAN CONTINGENTS".

### 1 June 1915 — W. Sandling Camp

> SERGT. INSTRUCTOR ECCLES attached as Instructor in Gymnasia.

### 3 June 1915 — W. Sandling Camp

> CAPT. ROBINSON and LIEUT D. St.J WIGLE Detailed to attend Course at Staff College CAMBERLEY.
> Routine in Camp, First Post, Last Post and Lights out ½ hour later. ie 9.30-10.00-10.15 P.M.
>
> Remarks column: Training Syllabus Appendix A2.

### 5 June 1915 — W. Sandling Camp

> Range Finders appointed. 5 in Battalion.

### 7 June 1915 — W. Sandling Camp

> Field Conduct Sheets placed in use from 1st June 1915.
> CAPT. C.B. STOVER ordered to report to Chief Paymaster, LONDON.
> LIEUTS. F.G. NEWTON and J.S. BELL detailed to attend Machine Gun Course at ROSS BARRACKS, SHORNCLIFFE.

### 8 June 1915 — W. Sandling Camp

> CAPT. SHUTTLEWORTH takes command of Base Company during absence of CAPT. ROBINSON.

### 14 June 1915 — W. Sandling Camp

> 18th. Battalion, Duty Battalion for Division.
> LIEUT R.G. ELLIOTT and L/SERGT. D. COLE detailed to attend course on Field Telephones.
>
> Remarks column: Training Syllabus Appendix B2.

### 28 June 1915 — W. Sandling Camp

> Training as per Appendix D.
>
> Remarks column: Training Syllabus Appendix C2.

### 29 June 1915 — W. Sandling Camp

> Training as per appendix E.
>
> Remarks column: Training Syllabus Appendix D2.

The Remarks column also lists, below the dated rows, "Training Syllabus Appendix E2", "Training Syllabus Appendix F2" and "Training Syllabus Appendix G2", corresponding to the daily syllabi for 28th–30th June and 1st–3rd July (Pages 8–10).

Form footer: "1577 Wt. W10791/1773 500,000 1/15 D.D. & L. A.D.S.S./Forms/C. 2118." A signature at the foot of the page is illegible: [uncertain].

## Page 6 — Appendix C2: Syllabus of Training, week ending 19 June 1915

> Syllabus of Training — 18th Battalion, C.E.F.
>
> week ending June 19, 1915. Appendix C2

### Monday, June 14

> 6.30 – 7.00 a.m. ½ hour squad drill (without arms).
> Commencing 8.00 a.m. 1 " each Co. Physical training.
> During the period 8.00 – 9.00, Companies not in Physical Training Class will practice Bayonet Fighting and Rapid loading.
> 9.00 – 12.00 a.m. 3 " Co. work in the field.
> 1.30 – 2.00 p.m. ½ " Close order drill.
> 2.00 – 3.00 p.m. 1 " Trench warfare.
> 3.00 – 5.00 p.m. 2 " Co. work in the field.
>
> Total 8 hours.

### Tuesday, June 15

> as for Monday, less 4 hours Company Field work, that period being taken for entrenching.

### Wednesday, June 16

> as for Monday, and including 3 hours night work.

### Thursday, June 17

> as for Tuesday.

### Friday, June 18

> as for Monday, and including 3 hours night work.

### Saturday, June 19

> Route March.

> Notes:—
>
> (a) Squad drill, physical training, musketry, bayonet fighting, and close order drill are practised on Battalion Parade ground.
> (b) Entrenching is practised on TOLSFORD HILL.
> (c) Work in the field is practised on Battalion training area.
> (d) The Battalion has completed a two weeks' musketry course at Hythe.
> (e) Company work in the field will include attack and defence, outposts, action of advanced and Rear guards, Fire Discipline, March Discipline, reconnaissance, Patrolling, Map reading, wood and village fighting.
> (f) Work in the field and Route Marching in Marching Order.
> (g) O. C. Companies will prepare their scheme of training in conformity with the above syllabus.
>
> Physical training — as for week ending June 12, /15.
