## Transcription conventions

- Transcribed only from the LAC page images listed above. Spelling, punctuation, abbreviations and capitalisation are as written in the original.
- The diary text is on Army Form C. 2118, "War Diary or Intelligence Summary", with columns Place | Date | Hour | Summary of Events and Information | Remarks and references to Appendices. The form footer reads "2353 Wt. W2544/1454 700,000 5/15 D. D. & L. A.D.S.S./Forms/C. 2118."
- `[uncertain]` marks any word or name that could not be read with confidence — names are never guessed.
- "do" is the diarist's ditto mark; " " (ditto marks) repeat the line above.
- "Battn" = Battalion, "Bn" = Battalion, "Divnl" = Divisional, "Bgde" = Brigade, "Coys" = Companies, "O.R." = other ranks.
- The Remarks column carries a repeated abbreviated entry on every row that is not legible — [uncertain] — and is not transcribed row by row.
- Place names as written: La Clytte, Vierstraat, Ridgewood.

## Notes on this month

- No casualties are recorded in the November diary text. The month is a routine rotation: the battalion alternates between the trenches at Vierstraat, Brigade Reserve at Ridgewood, and Divisional Reserve at La Clytte, relieved in turn by the 19th Battalion.
- Training at La Clytte, 18–22 November, covers equipment inspection, foot inspection and bathing, gas (smoke) helmet drill, company drill in the attack, Divine Service, and company and platoon drill.
- No commanding officer is named in the November text; the diary is signed with a flourished signature (initial "J.") that could not be read.
- No date gaps: entries run 1–30 November without a break.
