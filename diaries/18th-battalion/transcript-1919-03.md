## Transcription conventions

- Transcribed only from the LAC page images listed above. Spelling, punctuation, abbreviations and capitalisation are as written in the original.
- The diary text is on Army Form C. 2118, "War Diary or Intelligence Summary", with columns Place | Date | Hour | Summary of Events and Information | Remarks and references to Appendices. The form footer reads "(A9173) Wt W2135/P260 600,000 12/17 D. D. & L. Sch. 81a. Forms/C2118/15" (variant spellings of the imprint occur across pages).
- `[uncertain]` marks any word or name that could not be read with confidence — names are never guessed. `[struck]` marks text struck through in the original.
- "do" is the diarist's ditto mark; " " (ditto marks) repeat the line above.
- "Battn" = Battalion, "Bn" = Battalion, "Cdn" = Canadian, "C.I.B." = Canadian Infantry Brigade, "C.M.R." = Canadian Mounted Rifles, "O.R." = other ranks, "R.T.O." = Railway Transport Officer, "S.B.R." = Small Box Respirator, "H.Q." = Headquarters, "N.C.O." = non-commissioned officer, "C.E." = Canadian Engineers, "M.T." = Mechanical Transport, "R.C." = Roman Catholic, "Y.M.C.A." = Young Men's Christian Association, "D.R.O." = Divisional Routine Order, "D.A.D.O.S." = Deputy Assistant Director of Ordnance Services, "S.S.O." = Senior Supply Officer, "Q.M." = Quartermaster, "R.S.M." = Regimental Sergeant-Major, "C.F.A." = Canadian Field Artillery, "G.O.C." = General Officer Commanding, "M.M." = Military Medal, "Inst." = instant (this month).
- Place names as written: Fosses (Fosses-la-Ville), Auvelais, Le Havre, Mons, Brussels, Montreal, Bramshott, Namur.
- The diary is the FINAL month of the 18th Battalion war diary: it ends with the 31 March 1919 entry, signed by the Commanding Officer, as the battalion entrains for Le Havre on the return journey to Canada.
- Filming note: the first eight printed pages were filmed out of order (file sequence p. 1, p. 4, p. 5, p. 6, p. 7, p. 8, p. 3, p. 2). Seven appendix pages are duplicate filmings of the same document (pp. 18, 20, 22, 24, 26, 38 and 48); each is noted in place and its text is not repeated. Pages 4–8 are five overlapping photographs of the same large folded map; see the note under Page 4.

## Page 13 — Diary text, 31 March 1919 (final entry)

[Typed. "45" at top right. "CONFIDENTIAL." stamped at top. Form C. 2118. The printed heading "INTELLIGENCE SUMMARY." is struck through with rows of "X".]

### 31 March 1919 — Fosses, I.35.75.

> "C" and "D" Companies and Battalion Headquarters formed up in the square in front of the Hotel de Ville at 08:45 hours in Full Marching Order and moved off at 09:00 hours for Auvelais where they entrained for Le Havre at 11:00 hours together with three companies of the 19th. Canadian Battalion. The train was made up of box cars and each car was equipped with a stove and sufficient straw to ensure the comfort of each man. The men were furnished with blankets and everything possible was done to make comfortable the journey. The Y.M.C.A. provided free tea at the station and had a cars allotted theirs use as a canteen. They also provided games and sporting goods of all kinds for the use of the men during the stops of the train. Reading matter also was provided. The Corps and Divisional Commanders were at the station for a short period before the train left. The train pulled away from the station at 12:30 hours— the first stop was made at Mons at 18:00 hours, for supper and recreation. The day was ideal as to weather conditions and the first day of the return journey to Canada found everyone in the best of spirits. Syllabus for the day attached, appendix No. 29.

[Signed, in ink:]

> [signature — "L. E. Jones"] Lieut.-Colonel,
>
> Comdg. 18th. Canadian Battalion,
>
> Western Ontario Regiment.

[The diary ends here: this is the last page of the 18th Canadian Battalion war diary. No closing statement beyond the 31 March entry and the Commanding Officer's signature. Note the companies named do not match the 30 March entry and Order No. D.276 exactly: the order sends "A" and "B" Companies on the 30th and the rest of the battalion on the 31st, while the 30 March diary entry names "A" "C" & "B" Companies and the 31 March entry names "C" and "D" Companies and Battalion Headquarters.]
