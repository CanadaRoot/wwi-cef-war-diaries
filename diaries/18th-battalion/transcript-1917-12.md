## Transcription conventions

- Transcribed only from the LAC page images listed above. Spelling, punctuation, abbreviations and capitalisation are as written in the original.
- The diary text is on Army Form C. 2118, "War Diary or Intelligence Summary", with columns Place | Date | Hour | Summary of Events and Information | Remarks and references to Appendices. The heading "INTELLIGENCE SUMMARY" is struck out on the diary pages and "18th Canadian Battalion" is written in by hand. The form footer reads "(A2991 Wt. W11133/M1293 75,000. 1/17. D. D. & L., Ltd. Forms/C.2118/14.)"
- `[uncertain]` marks any word or name that could not be read with confidence — names are never guessed.
- "do" is the diarist's ditto mark; " " (ditto marks) repeat the line above.
- "Battn" = Battalion, "Bn" = Battalion, "Cdn." = Canadian, "O.R." = other ranks, "Bde" = Brigade, "Coy" = Company, "T.M." = Trench Mortar, "A.A." = Anti-Aircraft, "I.O." = Intelligence Officer, "O.P." = Observation Post.
- The Remarks column carries a repeated initialled mark on every row ("C.P.L." [uncertain]) and is not transcribed row by row.
- Map references are sheet 36, S.W. / 36c, 1:20,000 (and Rouvroy 1/10,000 for 10 December).
- Place names as written: Vancouver Road, Winnipeg Road, Acheville Sector, Rouvroy Trench, New Brunswick Trench, Ottawa-Hudson, Villers-au-Bois, Suburban Camp, Febvin-Palfart, Willerval.

## Page 4 — Diary text, 1–5 December 1917

[Handwritten "18th Canadian Battalion" and "December 1917" at top. "INTELLIGENCE SUMMARY" struck out. Map 36c. 1/20000. Form C. 2118.]

### 1 December 1917 — Vancouver Road, T.28.d.5.2.

> Battalion in support area at Vancouver Rd. Situation quiet, nothing unusual occurred. Major J. J. Richardson rejoined Battalion from England. 5. O.Rs. admitted to hospital.

### 2 December 1917 — Vancouver Road

> Position as shown yesterday. Wiring parties of 2 officers and 30. O.R. put out 60 yds of single apron wire in front of trench from T.24.C.90.50 to T.24.C.80.70. 20 O.R. arrived as reinforcements.

### 3 December 1917 — Winnipeg Road, 1 p.m.

> 18th Battalion relieved 19th Battalion in front line. Acheville Sector. Relief completed at dusk without casualty. 58 O.Rs arrived as reinforcements. 7 O.Rs rejoined from hospital.

### 4 December 1917 — Winnipeg Road

> Battalion in front line. Wiring parties put out barbed wire in front of trenches during night. Nothing unusual occurred.

### 5 December 1917 — Winnipeg Road

> Position same. Wiring parties out at night and fatigue parties improving trenches. 2 O.Rs returned from hospital.

## Page 5 — Diary text, 6–10 December 1917

[Handwritten "18th Canadian Battalion" at top. "INTELLIGENCE SUMMARY" struck out.]

### 6 December 1917 — Winnipeg Road

> Situation quiet. Considerable Aeroplane activity on both sides patrolling the lines. 50 O.R. arrived as reinforcements. Lieut C. E. Ogilvy attached to 4th Can [uncertain] Mortar By. 1 O.R. wounded.

[Transcriber's note: the word before "Mortar By." on 6 December reads like "French", though the context suggests "Trench" (Trench Mortar Battery).]

### 7 December 1917 — Winnipeg Road

> Situation unchanged. Enemy working parties observed by our observers working on Rouvroy Trench. 2 O.R. admitted to hospital. 1 O.R. wounded.

### 8 December 1917 — Winnipeg Road

> Position same. Considerable enemy artillery activity on rear country throughout the day. 2 O.R. returned from Base.

### 9 December 1917 — Winnipeg Road

> Enemy working parties seen by observers working on support trenches, and artillery notified. 2 O.Rs. attached to Brigade as messengers.

### 10 December 1917 — Map Rouvroy 1/10000

> Battalion relieved in front line by the 19th Battalion. 18th Battalion moved back into support area. Bn H.Q. being in Vancouver Road at T.28.d.5.2. A Coy in New Brunswick Trench. B Coy. in Ottawa-Hudson and Canada. D. New Brunswick. 1 O.R. wounded.

## Notes on this month

- Casualties: no men killed in action are recorded in December 1917; 3 other ranks were wounded (1 on 6 December, 1 on 7 December, 1 on 10 December). The rest of the month's losses are hospital admissions and returns.
- Movements: the battalion begins December in support at Vancouver Road, relieves the 19th Battalion in the front line in the Acheville Sector on 3 December, is relieved by the 19th on 10 December back into support, is relieved in support by the 20th Battalion on 13 December, moves back to Suburban Camp near Villers-au-Bois, then on 19 December moves by motor lorry to the rest area at Febvin-Palfart, where it remains for the rest of the month training.
- Officers named: Major J. J. Richardson (rejoined from England, 1 December); Lieut C. E. Ogilvy (attached to 4th Canadian [uncertain] Mortar Battery, 6 December); Capt C. P. Laing (supervised Canadian Election polling, 11 December); Lieut R. C. Sheridan (rejoined from hospital, 15 December); Lieut H. K. Bawden (rejoined from Training School, 22 December); Lt. Col. L. E. Jones (proceeded on leave to England, 23 December); Major J. A. McIntosh (took command, 23 December; signed the diary); Lieut A. B. Yeo (arrived as reinforcement, 30 December); Lieut G. J. Spencer (Intelligence Officer, signed the 1 December Intelligence Summary).
- Notable: the battalion voted in the 1917 Canadian federal election while in the support area (polling 11–12 December, with parades for voting on 14 December); a German gas-shell bombardment of the Willerval area is reported in the 1 December Intelligence Summary; Christmas Day 1917 was spent at Febvin-Palfart with a turkey and pork dinner arranged by the officers; the month ends with the battalion in training at the rest area.
- Appendix anomalies: the 10 December Intelligence Summary p. 1 and Order No. D174 were each filmed twice (pages 36/37 and 49/50); seven pages are blank reverses of order/syllabus sheets carrying only the handwritten word "Paymaster"; training syllabi survive only for 15, 16, 17, 18, 21, 22, 24, 25, 27, 28, 29 and 31 December — none for 19, 20, 23, 26 or 30 December, though the 21 December syllabus refers to a 20 December syllabus for the pay parade. The contents page misstates the 3 December move order as "A.B.D.D." (the order reads A, B, C, D) and lists the Intelligence Summaries out of date order.
- No date gaps: entries run 1–31 December without a break (the 31 December entry has no separate text; see the note under 30 December).

## Appendix A — gaps and anomalies

- Duplicate filmings: page 37 is a second filming of the 10 December Intelligence Summary p. 1 (page 36); page 50 is a second filming of Order No. D174 (page 49).
- Reverses: pages 41, 52, 55, 57, 59, 66 and 68 are the blank reverses of order/syllabus sheets, carrying only the handwritten word "Paymaster" and show-through; no text was transcribed from them beyond "Paymaster".
- Missing syllabi: the diary refers to a syllabus for every day from 15 December, but the file set contains syllabi only for 15, 16, 17, 18, 21, 22, 24, 25, 27, 28, 29 and 31 December. No syllabus sheets are present for 19, 20, 23, 26 or 30 December (the 21 December syllabus refers to a pay parade "as detailed in syllabus for 20.12.17", so a 20 December syllabus existed but is not in this set).
- The 28 December syllabus (page 64) has no visible signature or date line; the foot of the sheet is cut off in the image.
- The contents page lists the move order in the 3 December order as "A.B.D.D. and H.Q."; the order itself (Order No. D174, page 49) reads "A, B, C, D, and H.Q."
- The contents page lists the Intelligence Summaries in the order "1st, 3rd, … 5th, 6th, 2nd, 7th, 8th & 9th, 10th … 11th, 12th, 13th December" — the summaries are filed out of date order in the appendix (the 2 December summary follows the 6 December one).
