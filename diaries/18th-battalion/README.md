# 18th Battalion (Western Ontario) — war diary

## Identity

Wigle; mobilised London, Ontario; embarked Halifax 18 April 1915 aboard GRAMPIAN; landed England 28 April 1915; landed France 15 September 1915, 2nd Canadian Division, 4th Canadian Infantry Brigade)
- **Diary:** 29 April 1915 – 31 March 1919 (LAC guide says to 3 March 1919; the last LAC record runs to 31 March 1919)
