## Transcription conventions

- Transcribed faithfully from the LAC page images only. Spelling, punctuation, abbreviations and capitalisation are as written in the original.
- `[uncertain]` marks any word or name that could not be read with confidence. Names are never guessed.
- The diary text is on Army Form C. 2118, headed "WAR DIARY or INTELLIGENCE SUMMARY" (the words "INTELLIGENCE SUMMARY" are struck through), with typed columns Place | Date | Hour | Summary of Events and Information | Remarks and references to Appendices. The form is marked "ORIGINAL" (underlined).
- Ditto marks (") in the Place and Date columns repeat the entry above.
- The appendices are typed "Syllabus of Training" sheets for the weeks ending 10th, 17th, 24th and 31st July 1915.

## Page 3 — Diary text, 1–29 July 1915

Army Form C. 2118. Handwritten at the top: "July 1st 1915 July 31st 1915", "18th Bn. C.E.F." The form heading "INTELLIGENCE SUMMARY" is struck through and "ORIGINAL" is written below it and underlined.

### 1 July 1915 — West Sandling Camp

> Dominion Day. Half holiday for Sports.

### 2 July 1915 — West Sandling Camp

> noon. Lecture on entrenching by Major Battye.

### 3 July 1915 — West Sandling Camp

> Lieut. C.P. Laing detailed to attend course at Scarborough

### 5 July 1915 — West Sandling Camp

> R.S.M. White promoted to Warrant Officer.

### 5 July 1915 — West Sandling Camp

> 5 P.M. Lecture on Entrenching etc. by Major Battye.

### 10 July 1915 — West Sandling Camp

> Extract D.R.O. 4-7-15,— No change to be made in stores without authority of D.A.D.O.S.
>
> Remarks column: App. A 3 training 5-10 July.

### 12 July 1915 — West Sandling Camp

> Capt. F.S. Robinson and Lieut D.St.J Wigle return from course at Camberley.

### 15 July 1915 — West Sandling Camp

> 2 P.M. 20th. Reserve Battalion allotted to 18th. Battalion as reserve Unit.

### 16 July 1915 — West Sandling Camp

> 9 A.M. Muster Parade.

### 16 July 1915 — West Sandling Camp

> 2.30 P.M. Divisional Ceremonial at Beachborough.
>
> Remarks column: App. B 3 10-17 July.

### 17 July 1915 — West Sandling Camp

> 9.30 A.M. Brigade March Past at Base of Tolsford Hill.

### 20 July 1915 — West Sandling Camp

> Divisional Review by Sir Robt. L. Borden and Major Gen. S. Hughes, at Beachborough.
>
> Remarks column: App. C 3 17-24 July.

### 21 July 1915 — West Sandling Camp

> Brigade marched to Ashford and worked out Billeting Scheme and returned same night.

### 22 July 1915 — West Sandling Camp

> 18th. Bn. took up defensive position at Hythe Hill and attack was carried out by 19th. Bn.

### 23 July 1915 — West Sandling Camp

> Brigade in attack field day, from STONE HILL.

### 24 July 1915 — West Sandling Camp

> Battalion fought a pursuing action to [uncertain]Lymchurch and bivouaced.

### 25 July 1915 — West Sandling Camp

> Returned from Bivouac.
>
> Remarks column: App. D 3 24-31 July.

### 27 July 1915 — West Sandling Camp

> Brigade route march and Billeting on active service on Stone Street near six mile Cottages.

### 28 July 1915 — West Sandling Camp

> Companies marching by map, rendezvous near Elham — Bivouaced for night.

### 29 July 1915 — West Sandling Camp

> Returned from Bivouac and Muster Parade in afternoon.

Form footer: "1577 Wt. W10791/1773 500,000 1/15 D.D. & L. A.D.S.S./Forms/C. 2118." A signature at the foot of the page is illegible: [uncertain].
