## Transcription conventions

- Transcribed only from the LAC page images listed above. Spelling, punctuation, abbreviations and capitalisation are as written in the original.
- The diary text is on Army Form C. 2118, "War Diary or Intelligence Summary", with columns Place | Date | Hour | Summary of Events and Information | Remarks and references to Appendices. The form footer reads "2353 Wt. W2544/1454 700,000 5/15 D. D. & L. A.D.S.S./Forms/C. 2118."
- `[uncertain]` marks any word or name that could not be read with confidence — names are never guessed.
- "Do" is the diarist's ditto mark; " " (ditto marks) repeat the line above.
- "Battn" = Battalion, "Bn" = Battalion, "Divl" = Divisional, "Bgde" = Brigade, "Coys" = Companies, "O.R." = other ranks, "M.O." = Medical Officer.
- A repeated flourished initial at the end of every diary row is not legible — [uncertain] — and is not transcribed row by row.
- Place names as written: Vierstraat, Ridgewood, La Clytte (also written LA CLYTTE), Bois Carré.

## Page 3 — Diary text, 1–13 February 1916

[Handwritten "18th Canadian Battalion" at top, "WAR DIARY or INTELLIGENCE SUMMARY. (Erase heading not required.)" and "Original" at top right. Form C. 2118. The Place column is blank for 2 and 8 February; the places in the headings below are those named in the entries. A signature at the foot of the page — [uncertain].]

### 1 February 1916 — Vierstraat

> "B" and "D" Coys in trenches "M" + "N". Lt Col. E.S. WIGLE acting Brigadier 4th Can Infy Bgde. Lieut Rispin [uncertain] went to 1st Infy Bgde on Staff. 2 other ranks admitted to hospital. 2 other ranks reported missing. Failed to return from patrol.

### 2 February 1916 — Ridgewood, 5 pm

> Relieved by 19th Can Bn. 18th Bn went to Brigade Reserve at RIDGEWOOD. 2 other ranks admitted to hospital. 1 other rank returned from hospital.

### 3 February 1916 — Ridgewood

> Routine — 4 other ranks struck off strength of Battalion.

### 4 February 1916 — Ridgewood

> Do — 2 other ranks admitted to hospital. 70 Reinforcements arrived.

### 5 February 1916 — Ridgewood

> Do — 2 other ranks returned from hospital.

### 6 February 1916 — Ridgewood

> Do — Communion service was held at 11 a.m. — Capt. HALE proceeded on leave.

### 7 February 1916 — Ridgewood

> Routine. Lieuts. T. LAWSON and R.H. MARTIN arrived as reinforcements. Capt. KENNY of 5th Field Ambulance attached to relieve Capt HALE (M.O.)

### 8 February 1916 — Vierstraat, 5 pm

> Relieved 19th Can Bn at VIERSTRAAT. "A" + "C" Coys in trenches "M" + "N". Lieut C.P. LAING promoted to Captain to date from 30th Dec 1915. Capt. T.C. LAMB admitted to hospital.

### 9 February 1916 — Vierstraat

> Nothing unusual. 4 other ranks admitted to hospital. 1 other rank returned from hospital.

### 10 February 1916 — Vierstraat

> Major NELSON returned from leave. 2 other ranks admitted to hospital.

### 11 February 1916 — Vierstraat

> "B" + "D" Coys relieved "A" + "C" Coys in trenches at 6.30 p.m.

### 12 February 1916 — Vierstraat

> "B" + "D" Coys in trenches. 4 other ranks struck off strength of Battalion.

### 13 February 1916 — Vierstraat

> Church Parade was held at 9.45 A.M. at VIERSTRAAT for Companies in Reserve. Major JONES and Capt Lochrin [uncertain] proceeded on leave.

## Page 4 — Diary text, 14–27 February 1916

[Handwritten "18th Canadian Battalion" at top, "WAR DIARY or INTELLIGENCE SUMMARY. (Erase heading not required.)" Form C. 2118. The Place column is blank for 14, 25 and 27 February; the places in the headings below are those named in the entries. A signature at the foot of the page — [uncertain].]

### 14 February 1916 — La Clytte, 11 pm

> Battalion relieved by 19th Can Bn. Relief was delayed on account of heavy shell fire in Bois Carré Communication Trench. Battalion in Divl Reserve at LA CLYTTE.

### 15 February 1916 — La Clytte

> 2 other ranks returned from hospital. Major NELSON admitted to hospital. Coys held clothing, bath, + pay parades. — Capt. HALE returned from leave.

### 16 February 1916 — La Clytte

> Do — 6 p.m. Fatigue party for wire laying of 500 men. 20 other ranks arrived as reinforcements. 2 other ranks admitted to hospital 2 other ranks returned from hospital.

### 17 February 1916 — La Clytte

> Battn received inoculation against Para. typhoid. 5 other ranks returned from hospital.

### 18 February 1916 — La Clytte

> Inoculation completed. 3 other ranks returned from hospital.

### 19 February 1916 — La Clytte

> Battalion resting after inoculation. Capt. T.C. LAMB returned from hospital.

### 20 February 1916 — Vierstraat

> Battalion relieved 19th Can Bn in trenches "M" + "N". "A" + "C" Coys in trenches. Capt EMERY and Capt C.P. LAING proceeded on leave. Major EMMERTON admitted to hospital.

### 21 February 1916 — Vierstraat

> Position of Battn as yesterday. 2 other ranks admitted to hospital.

### 22 February 1916 — Vierstraat

> Do — 2 other ranks returned from hospital.

### 23 February 1916 — Vierstraat, 6 pm

> "B" + "D" Coys relieved "A" + "C" Coys. Lieut GWYN arrived as reinforcement. Major JONES + Capt LOCHRIN returned from leave. 3 other ranks admitted to hospital.

### 24 February 1916 — Vierstraat

> Position as yesterday. Major JONES takes over duties as Commandant of Trench Warfare School.

### 25 February 1916 — Ridgewood, 6 pm

> Battn relieved by 19th Can Bn. 18th Bn in RIDGEWOOD in Brigade Reserve.

### 26 February 1916 — Ridgewood

> Lieut. DILLON (wounded) admitted to hospital. 6 other ranks struck off strength of Battn.

### 27 February 1916 — Ridgewood

> Position as yesterday. Do — Lieut. G. WEBB transferred to artillery. Lieut W. FORBES-MITCHELL transferred to Bgde Machine Gun Coy.

## Notes on this month

- Casualties: 2 other ranks reported missing on 1 February, having failed to return from patrol; Lieut Dillon (wounded) admitted to hospital 26 February; 1 other rank killed and 2 other ranks wounded on 28 February. No names are given for the missing, killed or wounded other ranks. The rest of the month's losses are sickness and hospital admissions.
- Movements: the battalion rotates between the trenches at Vierstraat (trenches "M" + "N"), Brigade Reserve at Ridgewood, and Divisional Reserve at La Clytte, relieved in turn by the 19th Battalion. The relief on 14 February was delayed by heavy shell fire in the Bois Carré Communication Trench. On 28 February the battalion relieved the 27th Battalion in the S.P. Trenches, with "A" Company in reserve at Ridgewood.
- Officers named: Lt Col E. S. Wigle (acting Brigadier, 4th Canadian Infantry Brigade, 1 February); Lieut Rispin [uncertain] (to 1st Infantry Brigade staff, 1 February); Capt Hale, Medical Officer (on leave 6 February, returned 15 February), relieved by Capt Kenny of 5th Field Ambulance (7 February); Lieuts T. Lawson and R. H. Martin (arrived as reinforcements, 7 February); Lieut C. P. Laing (promoted Captain, to date from 30 December 1915); Capt T. C. Lamb (admitted to hospital 8 February, returned 19 February); Major Nelson (returned from leave 10 February, admitted to hospital 15 February); Major Jones and Capt Lochrin [uncertain] (on leave 13 February, returned 23 February; Jones took over as Commandant of the Trench Warfare School, 24 February); Capt Emery (on leave 20 February, returned 29 February); Major Emmerton (admitted to hospital 20 February); Lieut Gwyn (arrived as reinforcement, 23 February); Lieut Dillon (wounded, 26 February); Lieut G. Webb (transferred to artillery, 27 February); Lieut W. Forbes-Mitchell (transferred to Brigade Machine Gun Coy, 27 February).
- Notable: the battalion received inoculation against paratyphoid on 17 February, completed 18 February, and rested on 19 February. Reinforcements arrived in two drafts — 70 on 4 February and 20 on 16 February. A 500-man fatigue party went out for wire laying on 16 February. A communion service was held 6 February and a church parade at Vierstraat on 13 February.
- No date gaps: entries run 1–29 February without a break (1916 was a leap year).
- Anomalies: the contents page lists "Diary Text 3 Pages" and no appendices — the month's five page images contain no appendices and no separate "Original for War Office" sheet, unlike December 1915. The Place column is blank for 2, 8, 14, 25 and 27 February; the places for those days are taken from the entries themselves.
