# War Diary — 17th Canadian Reserve Battalion (Nova Scotia)

## Source
- **Unit:** 17th Canadian Reserve Battalion (Nova Scotia)
- **Archival reference:** RG 9 III-D-3, vol. 4951, file 479, part 2; microfilm reel T-10767
- **LAC Collection Search:** Item ID 2004685 — "War diaries - 17th Reserve Battalion"
- **Page images:** pages 140–151 of the LAC record, saved alongside this file (`page-0140-...jpg` … `page-0151-...jpg`).
- **Commanding officer:** Lieut.-Colonel W. H. Muirhead (re-assumed command 2 January 1918; signs the month's diary 4 February 1918).
- **Place:** Bramshott, Hants., England, throughout the month.

## Transcription conventions
- Faithful to the original: spelling, punctuation, abbreviations and capitalisation as written.
- `[uncertain]` marks a word or name that could not be read with confidence — never guessed.
- The diary-text pages are Army Form C. 2118, "WAR DIARY or INTELLIGENCE SUMMARY", typewritten in columns: Place | Date | Hour | Summary of Events and Information | Remarks and references to Appendices.
- "do." in a column means "ditto" (same as the entry above).
- N.S.R.D. = Nova Scotia Regimental Depot; B.R.D. = [uncertain — as written] (Base Regimental Depot?); O.R. = Other Rank(s).
- No map coordinates (latitude/longitude) are given in the diary; places are recorded as written.

## Page 148 — Order No. 19, page 2 (Tactical Scheme — General Idea)

> General Idea.
>
> Tactical Scheme.
>
> Altonia is at war with Guilfordia. Valuable stores at the town of Haslemore on the frontier of Guilfordia, at an important road junction. The loyalty of the people of Haslemore is doubtful and it is considered that by a demonstration of force, the people of the Town will hand over all stores willingly.
>
> Special Idea. O.C. Troops occupying the Bramshott Hills, detached 17th Battalion of Foot, to occupy Haslemore as soon as possible, seize stores and return.

## Page 149 — Operation Order No. 19, page 3 (18 January 1918)

> 17th Canadian Reserve Battalion.
>
> Operation Order No. 19.
>
> January 18th, 1918.
>
> 1. Information. Valuable stores are at Haslemore. Hostile cavalry are advancing from Godalming to seize them and were reported 4 miles N. of Haslemore at 10.00 today, [uncertain — "bivouacked"].
>
> 2. Intention. The Battalion will occupy Haslemore, collect stores and return to Bramshott.
>
> 3. Distribution. "C" Company under Major [uncertain] will act as advance guard and will occupy Haslemore, pushing out on far as road junction N. east of Haslemore on North Chapel Road. >

## Page 151 — Order No. 20, page 2 (Tactical Scheme, 25 January 1918)

> 17th Canadian Reserve Battalion. Order No. 20.
>
> Copy No. 8.
>
> 24.1.18.
>
> Map Reference — ALDERSHOT (SOUTH)
>
> 1" — 1 Mile.
>
> 1. Information. Block has declared war on Red. The BLACKS have taken possession of GUILDFORD and their patrols have been encountered at GODALMING. We have strong advance parties of all arms at HINDHEAD. The BLACKS are reported to have cavalry parties moving southwards in the direction of the Punch Bowl.
>
> 2. INTENTION. To march to HINDHEAD to pick up supplies via SHOTTERMILL, HINDHEAD HOTEL and PORTSMOUTH ROAD.
>
> 3. DISTRIBUTION. O.C. "HQ" Company will form the ADVANCE GUARD until HINDHEAD is reached. O.C. "C" Company will detail 1 Platoon as REAR GUARD. Orders for the return to Bramshott will be issued later.
>
> 4. REPORTS. Will be sent to head of MAIN BODY.

## Notes and gaps
- No dates are missing from the January diary sheets: 1–31 January 1918 are all covered across the five sheets (pages 141–144).
- The diary records no casualties, deaths or hospital admissions in January; the only adverse entries are 5 Other Ranks struck off as deserters (4 on 17 January, 1 on 31 January).
- The month is dominated by routine training at Bramshott, daily strength movements to and from N.S.R.D. and B.R.D., and four Friday route marches (4, 11, 18 and 25 January), the last two run as tactical exercises against imaginary enemies (Altonia/Guilfordia; "Block" vs "Red"/"BLACKS").
- Unit strength at 31 January 1918: Officers 36; Other Ranks 1177 (per Routine Order No. 999, 27 March 1917).
