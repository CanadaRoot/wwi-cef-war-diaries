# War Diary — 17th Canadian Reserve Battalion

## Source
- **Unit:** 17th Canadian Reserve Battalion (Nova Scotia) — formed 23 January 1917 at Bramshott by the amalgamation of the 17th, 193rd and 219th Battalions; part of the 5th Canadian Reserve Brigade, reinforcing the 85th and 185th Battalions
- **Archival reference:** RG 9 III-D-3, vol. 4951, file 479, part 2; microfilm reel T-10767
- **LAC Collection Search:** Item ID 2004685 — "War diaries - 17th Reserve Battalion", 293 pages
  - Record: https://recherche-collection-search.bac-lac.gc.ca/eng/Home/Record?app=fonandcol&IdNumber=2004685
- **Page images:** pages 80–82 of 293 in this file (`page-0080-23-01-1917_cover.jpg` … `page-0082-23-01-1917.jpg`)
- **Commanding officer:** Lieut. Colonel D. D. Cameron (in command from 23 January 1917); Lieut. Colonel W. H. Muirhead signed the intelligence summary as the incoming commanding officer — see note below
- **Note on the record:** this LAC record also contains an unrelated 1914 diary of the 17th Battalion on pages 1–79; those pages are NOT transcribed here. This chunk covers only the Reserve Battalion diary, January–December 1917 (pages 80–139).

## Transcription conventions
- Faithful to the original: spelling, punctuation, abbreviations and capitalisation as written.
- `[uncertain]` marks a word or name that could not be read with confidence — never guessed.
- The diary-text pages are Army Form C. 2118, "WAR DIARY or INTELLIGENCE SUMMARY", typed in columns: Place | Date | Hour | Summary of Events and Information | Remarks and references to Appendices.
- The "Hour" column is blank throughout this volume — no hours are recorded.
- "do." in the Place column means "ditto" (same place as the entry above).
- The Remarks column is initialled on each line (initials not legible — see per-page notes).
- No map coordinates (latitude/longitude) are given in the diary; places are recorded as written.
- "Part II Orders" and "Routine Order paras." are cited by number only; the orders themselves are not in this record.

## Page 81 — Intelligence summary (history of the amalgamation)

Typed, headed "WAR DIARY or INTELLIGENCE SUMMARY of the 17th Canadian Reserve Battalion". Handwritten "2/1" circled at top left. Signed at the foot by Lieut. Colonel W. H. Muirhead, "COMDG. 17th RES. BATTN." — although the text states Lieut. Colonel D. D. Cameron had been appointed to command the battalion by Headquarters, London, Order dated 2 January 1917.

> The 17th Canadian Reserve Battalion is composed of the old 17th Battalion, which came overseas in the early days of the war and which, through unfortunate circumstances, did not get an opportunity of going into the 1st Division as a unit; the 193rd and the 219th Battalions.
>
> Headquarters, Canadians, Routine Order No.95 of the 29th, regarding the reorganization of Canadian Infantry Battalions, stated that these three Battalions would be amalgamated to reinforce the 25th and the 85th Battalions.
>
> The 193rd and the 219th Battalions had been part of the Nova Scotia Highland Brigade, which was broken up and the two senior battalions — 85th and 185th — were retained as Units, the 85th Battalion going overseas to the 4th Division and the 185th Battalion remaining as a Unit in the newly formed 5th Division.
>
> Before the 85th went over, the only Nova Scotia Battalion in the field was the 25th Battalion. The initial idea therefore to the 17th Reserve Battalion, was that it was to reinforce the two Nova Scotia Battalions in the field; viz. 25th and 85th.
>
> At the moment this order was promulgated, the 17th Battalion, which had swallowed many another Battalion, was at East Sandling, and the 193rd and 219th were at Witley. On December 29th the order came for the 193rd and the 219th Battalions to move to Bramshott, which they did, and carry on as Battalions till January 23rd, when they were amalgamated with the 17th Battalion.
>
> The C.O. of the 193rd Battalion was Lieut. Colonel R.J.S. Langford, now with the 185th Battalion at Witley; and the C.O. of the 219th Battalion was Lieut. Colonel W.H. Muirhead, now in command of the 17th Reserve Battalion.
>
> On the 10th January the 17th Reserve Battalion moved to Bramshott, but on account of the 154th being in lines 6–9, they were for some days in the lines 2-A. For a time there was a hitch in the organization, but on January 23rd, 1917, "The 17th, 193rd and 219th Battalions having amalgamated, will issue part II Orders as the 17th Canadian Reserve Battalion from 23-1-17." (BrB.19-1-2(A.B.)22117).
>
> Lieut. Colonel D.D.Cameron had been appointed to command the 17th Reserve Battalion by Headquarters, London, Order dated January 2nd, 1917. On January 20th 1917, Headquarters, London, Order, under heading "Reorganization of Canadian Infantry Battalions" therein it was designated that the 17th Reserve Battalion, to be formed from the 17th, 219th and 193rd Battalions, would form part of the 5th Canadian Reserve Brigade, stationed at Bramshott Camp, and would reinforce the 85th and 185th Battalions, which with the 219th and the 193rd had formerly formed the Nova Scotia Highland Brigade.

## Page 82 — Diary sheet, 23 January–3 February 1917

Army Form C. 2118. Heading: "WAR DIARY or INTELLIGENCE SUMMARY." Printed note at top left: "Instructions regarding War Diaries and Intelligence Summaries are contained in F. S. Regs., Part II. and the Staff Manual respectively. Title pages will be prepared in manuscript." Every line is initialled in the Remarks column (initials not legible).

### 23 January 1917 — Bramshott

> "The 17th, 193rd and 219th Battalions, having amalgamated will issue Part II Orders as the 17th Reserve Battalion as from January 23, 1917." (B.H.19-1-2(A.B.)22117). Lieut. Colonel D.D.Cameron in command. Part II Order No.19. Routine Order paras. 230–239. Muster Parade of three battalions at 9.00 a.m.

> Part II Orders No.20; 6 Officers proceed overseas; routine Order para.240–254.

### 27 January 1917 — Bramshott

> Part II Orders No.22; Routine Orders paras 287–295.

### 28 January 1917 — Bramshott

### 29 January 1917 — Bramshott

### 30 January 1917 — Bramshott

### 31 January 1917 — Bramshott

## Notes and gaps
- The January volume (cover p. 80) runs 23 January–31 March 1917, but this diary sheet (p. 82) already runs to 3 February; the February and March sheets follow on pages 83–88.
- "Part II Orders No.20" is given for both 24 and 25 January, as typed — not corrected.
- Routine Order paragraph numbers cited have small gaps (e.g. 307 to 310; 325 to 327; 332a to 335), as typed.
- The summary (p. 81) says Lieut. Colonel W. H. Muirhead was "now in command of the 17th Reserve Battalion", and Muirhead signs it; the diary sheet (p. 82) says Lieut. Colonel D. D. Cameron was in command from 23 January. Both statements are transcribed as written.
