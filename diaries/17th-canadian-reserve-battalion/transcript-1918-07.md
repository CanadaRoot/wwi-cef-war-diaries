- **Commanding officer:** Lt.-Col. [signature illegible] (signs the 31 July strength return as "LT.-COL. COMDG. 17TH BATTALION O.I.").

## Transcription conventions
- Faithful to the original: spelling, punctuation, abbreviations and capitalisation as written.
- `[uncertain]` marks a word or name that could not be read with confidence — never guessed.
- The diary-text pages are Army Form C. 2118, "WAR DIARY", handwritten in columns: Place | Date | Hour | Summary of Events and Information | Remarks and references to Appendices. The printed sub-heading "OR INTELLIGENCE SUMMARY" is struck through on every sheet. The "Hour" column is blank throughout.
- The Remarks column carries a handwritten initial on each row; the initials are illegible and are not transcribed.
- No map coordinates (latitude/longitude) are given in the diary; places are recorded as written.
- "do." means "ditto". "N.S.R.D." = Nova Scotia Regimental Depot. "O.R." / "Other Rank" = Other Rank. "C.B.D." = Canadian Base Depot. "R.C.R." = Royal Canadian Regiment.
- The monthly report (pp. 201–202) and the operation orders (pp. 203–206) are landscape sheets, filed sideways in the LAC sequence.

Place: BRAMSHOTT. HANTS. ENGLAND. Date: 1918. July.

> **1st. Mon.** "Dominion Day." Declared General Holiday. Sports carried on all day. Morning. Baseball — Americans versus Canadians (21st. Can. Res. Bn. Team), resulting in score of 14 to 1 for Canadians. Afternoon General Sports held on 15th. Reserve Battalion Parade Ground. Evening — Boxing, Massed Band Concert and Military Tattoo.
>
> **2nd. Tue.** Weather — Very warm, with local showers at night. Training carried on as usual. Reinforcements No. 236 called this date. Routine Orders 1744 to 1754. Part II. Daily Order 154. One Other Rank Taken on strength from desertion. Four Other Ranks (6th. Casualty Draft) taken on strength on arrival from Canada 21-6-18. One Other Rank taken on strength from N.S.R.D.
>
> **3rd. Wed.** Weather — Very warm. Training carried on as usual. Organised Sports held in afternoon. B.A.A.A. Baseball 17th [uncertain — word overtyped] versus Can. Gen. Hospital, resulting in score of 6 to 2 for home team. Reinforcements No. 234 proceeded this date, and No. 235 warned to proceed on 6-7-18. Routine Orders 1755 to 1764. Part II. Daily Order 155. One Other Rank taken on strength from N.S.R.D. One Other Rank taken on strength from R.A.F. Cadet Wing. Three Other Ranks (1 from 25th. Bn.; 1 from 85th. Bn.; 1 from R.C.R.;) taken on strength on exchange. Ten Other Ranks struck off strength to N.S.R.D.
>
> **4th. Thur.** Weather — Very fine and warm. Training carried on as usual all day. Routine Orders 1765 to 1774. Part II. Daily Order 156. One Other Rank struck off strength having died in No. 12 Can. Gen'l. Hospital 3-7-18. Ten Other Ranks struck off strength on proceeding overseas to 25th. Battalion in the Field. Nineteen Other Ranks struck off strength on posting to N.S.R.D. having been lowered in category. One Officer taken on strength on reporting from Canadian Training School, Bexhill-on-Sea.
>
> **5th. Fri.** Weather — Very fine and warm. Training carried on as usual. Route March, as per copy of Operation Order No. 34 attached, carried out in evening. Three hundred and forty-six (346) Other Ranks proceeded on "Landing Leave" this date. Twenty-two Other Ranks (Tug-of-War Teams, heavy and medium weights) proceeded to Dinton this date to attend Australian Sports. Advice received of three (3) Officers and three hundred and fifty-one (351) Other Ranks due to arrive at Segregation Camp, Frensham Pond, shortly, from Canada. Routine Orders 1775 to 1788. One Other Rank taken on strength from N.S.R.D. One Other Rank struck off strength to Quebec Reg't. 'A' [uncertain]. One [uncertain — word overtyped] Other Rank struck off strength to N.S.R.D.
