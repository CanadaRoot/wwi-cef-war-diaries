# War Diary — 17th Canadian Reserve Battalion (Nova Scotia)

## Source
- **Unit:** 17th Canadian Reserve Battalion (Nova Scotia)
- **Archival reference:** RG 9 III-D-3, vol. 4951, file 479, part 2; microfilm reel T-10767
- **LAC Collection Search:** Item ID 2004685 — "War diaries - 17th Reserve Battalion"
- **Page images:** pages 183–193 of the LAC record, saved alongside this file (`page-0183-...jpg` … `page-0193-...jpg`).
- **Commanding officer:** Lieut.-Colonel W. H. Muirhead (signs the month's diary 1 July 1918).
- **Place:** Bramshott, Hants., England, throughout the month.

## Transcription conventions
- Faithful to the original: spelling, punctuation, abbreviations and capitalisation as written.
- `[uncertain]` marks a word or name that could not be read with confidence — never guessed.
- The diary-text pages are Army Form C. 2118, "WAR DIARY or INTELLIGENCE SUMMARY", typewritten in columns: Place | Date | Hour | Summary of Events and Information | Remarks and references to Appendices.
- "do." in a column means "ditto" (same as the entry above).
- N.S.R.D. = Nova Scotia Regimental Depot; O.R. = Other Rank(s); C.A.P.C. = Canadian Army Pay Corps; C.E.T.D. = Canadian Engineers Training Depot.
- The 3 June entry reads "C.A.C.S." as written (the typist's rendering; C.A.S.C. appears elsewhere in this diary).
- The route place-name "SHUFFLESHEEPS" in Orders No. 30, 31 and 33 is as read, but the faint type makes its exact spelling uncertain.
- Each diary row bears a handwritten mark in the Remarks column (illegible — appears to be a check or initial); the marks are not transcribed. The printer's imprint at the foot of each diary sheet is not transcribed.
- A catchword at the foot of each diary sheet gives the date beginning the next sheet (13th., 20th., 26th.).
- No map coordinates (latitude/longitude) are given in the diary; places are recorded as written.
