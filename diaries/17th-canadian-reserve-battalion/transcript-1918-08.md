- **Note on the images:** each page image carries the LAC microfilm target labels at the foot ("Militia and Defence / Milice et Défense — RG 9, III D 3, Vol. 4951" and "National Archives of Canada / Archives nationales du Canada"). These are archival labels, not diary content, and are not transcribed per page.

## Transcription conventions
- Faithful to the original: spelling, punctuation, abbreviations and capitalisation as written.
- `[uncertain]` marks a word or name that could not be read with confidence — never guessed.
- The diary-text pages are Army Form C. 2118, "WAR DIARY" (the printed "INTELLIGENCE SUMMARY" heading is struck through on each sheet, with "(Erase heading not required.)"), typewritten in columns: Place | Date | Hour (struck through — no hour entries are made; the Date column carries the day and weekday) | Summary of Events and Information | Remarks and references to Appendices.
- The Place column is filled in only on the first diary sheet (BRAMSHOTT, Hants., England) and left blank on the later sheets; the unit remained at Bramshott throughout the month unless an entry says otherwise.
- The Remarks column carries a handwritten word against most entries, apparently reading "original" [uncertain]; a few entries are blank or carry only a mark.
- The number at the foot of each diary sheet ("7th.", "12th.", "16th.", "26th.", "30th.") is the date of the first entry on the next sheet.
- "N.S.R.D." = Nova Scotia Regimental Depot; "R.C.R." = Royal Canadian Regiment; "O.M.F. of C." = Overseas Military Forces of Canada; "S.O.S." = struck off strength; "do." = ditto.

## Page 208 — Diary text, 1–6 August 1918 (August 1918, p. 2)

Army Form C. 2118. Heading: "WAR DIARY" ("INTELLIGENCE SUMMARY" struck through). Place: BRAMSHOTT, Hants., England. Date: 1918 AUGUST. (Sheet number not visible in the image.)

### 1 August 1918 — Bramshott, Hants., England

> Weather. Fine and very warm throughout the day. Rain commenced about 7.00 p.m. Training carried on as usual. Routine Orders 2035 to 2044. Part II D.O. 180. Four Other Ranks taken on strength from Nova Scotia Regimental Depot.

### 2 August 1918 — Bramshott, Hants., England

> Weather. Steady rain throughout the day, clearing about 5.00 p.m. Training carried on as usual in morning. No training in afternoon, time being spent in issuing equipment. Route March in evening as per Operation Order No. 37 attached. Reinforcements No. 242 (12 Signallers and one prisoner - absentee from 25th Battalion) proceeded overseas this date. Routine Orders 2045 to 2052. Part II D.O. 181. Four Other Ranks taken on strength from N.S.R.D. Four other ranks struck off strength to N.S.R.D. Two Other Ranks struck off strength as "Deserters".

### 3 August 1918 — Bramshott, Hants., England

> Weather. Very dull throughout the day. Rain at night. Training carried out as usual for half day. Routine Orders 2063 to 2068. Part II D.O. 182. Nineteen Other Ranks taken on strength from N.S.R.D. Twelve Other Ranks struck off strength on proceeding overseas: (Two Signallers for 25th Battalion; five signallers for 85th Battalion; five signallers for Royal Canadian Regiment). Eight Other Ranks struck off strength on posting to N.S.R.D.
>
> [Note: Routine Orders jump from 2052 on 2 August to 2063 on 3 August; transcribed as written.]

### 4 August 1918 — Bramshott, Hants., England

> Weather. Very fine and warm. Usual Divine Services held in morning. "Special Anniversary" Service in commemoration of the outbreak of War, at Liphook in afternoon. This Unit "Duty Unit" week commencing this date. 326 Other Ranks reported off command from Frensham Pond Segregation Camp this date.

### 5 August 1918 — Bramshott, Hants., England

> Weather. Steady rain throughout the day, clearing in evening, but with slight showers. Training as usual in morning. Holiday in afternoon. Routine Orders 2069 to 2078. Part II D.O. 183. Two Other Ranks struck off strength on posting to N.S.R.D. One Other Rank struck off strength as a "Deserter".

### 6 August 1918 — Bramshott, Hants., England

> Weather. Fine and warm. Training carried on as usual. Routine Orders 2079 to 2089. Part II D.O. 184. Three Other Ranks struck off strength on posting to N.S.R.D. One officer struck off strength on proceeding overseas to Royal Canadian Regiment in the Field 1-8-18.

[At foot of sheet: "7th."]

## Page 209 — Diary text, 7–11 August 1918 (August 1918, p. 3)

Army Form C. 2118. (Sheet number not visible in the image.)

### 7 August 1918 — Bramshott, Hants., England

> Weather. Fine but cooler. Training carried on as usual in morning. Organised sports in afternoon. Reinforcements 246 warned to proceed overseas on 8-8-18. New Schedule in B.A.A.A. Baseball League started this week. This Unit's team defeated the 13th Reserve Battalion in their first game by score of 7-5. Routine Orders 2089 to 2099. Part II D.O. 185. Four Other Ranks taken on strength from N.S.R.D. One other rank taken on strength from Canadian General Depot, Shorncliffe. Four Other Ranks struck off strength on posting to N.S.R.D. & Eleven other ranks lowered in category struck off strength on posting to N.S.R.D. One Other Rank struck off strength on transfer to 2nd Canadian Engineers Reserve Battalion, Seaford.

### 8 August 1918 — Bramshott, Hants., England

> Weather. Very fine throughout day. Reinforcements No. 246 (15 Other Ranks 25th Battalion; 79 Other Ranks 85th Battalion; 10 Other Ranks to Royal Canadian Regiment) proceeded overseas this date at 11.00 p.m. Training carried on as usual, all day. Lt. Col. C.L.E. Willetts DSO Officer Commanding Royal Canadian Regiment in the field visited this unit this date, inspecting training and troops. 103 Other Ranks reported from and 113 Other Ranks proceeded to Mytchett Musketry Camp this date. Routine Orders 2100 to 2110. Part II D.O. 186. 4 Other Ranks struck off strength to N.S.R.D.

### 9 August 1918 — Bramshott, Hants., England

> Weather. Very fine and warm. All day. Training carried on as usual in the morning. Route March in afternoon as per copy of Operation Order No. 38 attached. Routine Orders 2111 to 2115 Part II D.O. 187. 10 Other Ranks struck off strength on proceeding overseas to Royal Canadian Regiment. 15 Other Ranks struck off strength on proceeding overseas, to 25th Battalion. 7 Other Ranks struck off strength on proceeding overseas to 85th Battalion.

### 10 August 1918 — Bramshott, Hants., England

> Weather. Fine and very warm throughout the day, Training carried on as usual for morning. Routine Orders 2116 to 2136. Part II D.O. 188. 38 Other Ranks taken on strength from N.S.R.Depot (Reporting from 2nd C.C.Depot) 1 Other Rank taken on strength on transfer from No. 1 Detachment, Canadian Ordnance Corps, Ashford. 1 Officer struck off strength on transfer to 13th Canadian Reserve Battalion.

### 11 August 1918 — Bramshott, Hants., England

> Weather Fine. No training carried on. Tour of duty as Duty Battalion ended this date, and taken over by 13th Canadian Reserve Battalion. Divine Services held in morning.

### 12 August 1918 — Bramshott, Hants., England

> Weather. Fine and very warm. Training carried on as usual. Tug-of-war team of this unit qualified to compete at O.M.F. of C. ### 13 August 1918 — Bramshott, Hants., England

> Weather. Fine and very warm. Training carried on as usual. Heavy demands for Reinforcements for 26th, 85th Battalions and R.C.R. Received this date. Twelve Other Ranks from this unit proceeded on command to Aldershot this date to act as Camp Details in opening up new Segregation Camp for troops expected to arrive from Canada shortly. Routine Orders 2150 to 2154. Part II D.O. 190. One Other Rank taken on strength from N.S.R.D. Two Other Ranks (Category "D" 1) struck off strength on posting to N.S.R.D. ### 14 August 1918 — Bramshott, Hants., England

> Weather. Fine and very warm. Training carried on as usual in morning. Organized Sports in afternoon. Five Officers and about seventy other ranks proceeded to Stamford Bridge, London S.W. this date to witness O.M.F. of Canada. Sports being held there to-day. Routine Orders 2155 to 2165. Part II D.O. 191. One Other Rank taken on strength on posting to Permanent Cadre of this Unit as P.T. & B.F. Instructor from N.S.R.D. Six Other Ranks proceeded on command to new Canadian Air Force. R.A.F. Depot, Blandford, this date, pending transfer. Two Other Ranks (Category "D"1) struck off strength on posting to N.S.R.D.

### 15 August 1918 — Bramshott, Hants., England

> Weather. Very fine and warm. Training carried on as usual. Reinforcements No. 258 (12 Other Ranks for 25th Battalion; 4 Other Ranks for 85th Battalion; 15 Other Ranks for R.C.R.) proceeded Overseas this date. Reinforcements No 259 warned to proceed Overseas any time after Reveille 21/8/18. Team from this Unit won tug-of-war competition at O.M.F. of C. Sports held at Stamford Bridge on 14-8-18. Routine Orders 2166 to 2178. Pte II D.O. 192. One Other Rank in Category "B" II struck off strength on posting to N.S.R.D. Twenty-one Other Ranks (Coloured Troops) struck off strength on transfer to Canadian Forestry Corps, Sunningdale.
