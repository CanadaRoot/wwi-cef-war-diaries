# War Diary — 17th Canadian Reserve Battalion (Nova Scotia)

## Source
- **Unit:** 17th Canadian Reserve Battalion (Nova Scotia Regiment), Bramshott, Hants., England
- **Archival reference:** RG 9 III-D-3, vol. 4951, file 479, part 2; microfilm reel T-10767
- **LAC Collection Search:** Item ID 2004685 — "War diaries - 17th Reserve Battalion"
- **Page images:** pages 232–238 of the LAC record (October 1918 volume, Volume 20)
- **Commanding officer:** a Lieut-Colonel whose signature on the diary sheets and monthly report is illegible.

## Transcription conventions
- Faithful to the original: spelling, punctuation, abbreviations and capitalisation as written, including the typist's errors.
- `[uncertain]` marks a word, figure or name that could not be read with confidence — never guessed.
- The diary-text pages are Army Form C. 2118, "WAR DIARY or INTELLIGENCE SUMMARY", typewritten in columns: Place | Date | Hour | Summary of Events and Information | Remarks and references to Appendices. The "Hour" column is struck out on these sheets; no hours are recorded.
- The "INTELLIGENCE SUMMARY" alternative heading is struck out on each sheet.
- The Place column is only filled in on the first sheet of the month ("OCTOBER 1918. BRAMSHOTT HANTS ENGLAND"); later sheets leave it blank.
- In October the diary switches from "Part II Daily Orders" to "Part 11 D.O." (as typed) for the daily orders numbers.
- The Remarks column carries the same short illegible handwritten mark against each entry.
- "N.S.R.D." = Nova Scotia Regimental Depot. "R.C.R." = Royal Canadian Regiment.
- The 14 October entry is split across two sheets: it breaks off at the foot of page 234 and continues at the top of page 235.

## Page 233 — Diary text, 1–6 October 1918 (October 1918, Sheet 1)

Army Form C. 2118. Heading: "WAR DIARY or INTELLIGENCE SUMMARY. 17th Canadian Reserve Battalion." Place column: "OCTOBER 1918. BRAMSHOTT HANTS ENGLAND".

### 1 October 1918 — Bramshott, Hants., England — Tues.

> Weather Fair. Local Showers in early morning. Training carried on as usual. 98 Other Ranks proceeded to Longmoor Ranges this date to fire Part 1 G.M.C. returning about 4.30 p.m. Routine Orders 2627 to 2635. Part 11 D.O. 232. 2 Other Ranks Taken on Strength from N.S.R.D. 4 Other Ranks Struck off Strength to N.S.R.D. 1 Other Rank Struck off Strength on transfer to Y.S. Battalion. 2 Officers Struck off strength on proceeding Overseas to 25th Battalion. 1 Officer Struck off Strength on proceeding Overseas to Royal Canadian Regiment. 1 Officer Struck off Strength on proceeding Overseas to 1st Battalion.

### 2 October 1918 — Bramshott, Hants., England — Wed.

> Weather cold with showers in morning. Afternoon Fair and cold. Training carried on as usual. Routine Orders 2636 to 2645. Part 11 D.O. 233. 6 Other Ranks Taken on Strength from N.S.R.D. 4 Other Ranks struck off strength to N.S.R.D. 1 Other Rank Struck off strength on transfer to C.R.T.D. Seaford.

### 3 October 1918 — Bramshott, Hants., England — Thurs.

> Weather Fair and cold. Training carried on as usual. Class 10 on Area Route March in afternoon. Reinforcements No. 287 proceeded this date parading at 2045 o'clock. Canteens and Messes in Area closed owing to Epidemic of Influenza. Routine Orders 2646 to 2654. Part 11 D.O. No. 234. 1 Officer Taken on strength from N.S.R.D. having reported from Officers Casualty Company Bexhill-on-Sea.

### 4 October 1918 — Bramshott, Hants., England — Fri.

> Weather Fair and Warm. Training carried on as usual. Routine Orders No 2655 to 2666. Part 11 D.O. No. 235. 1 Other Rank Taken on strength on transfer from Canadian Forestry Corps, Sunningdale. 1 Other Rank struck off strength to N.S.R.D. 53 Other Ranks struck off strength on proceeding Overseas to Royal Canadian Regiment. 41 Other Ranks struck off strength on proceeding Overseas to 25th Battalion. 4 Other Ranks struck off strength on proceeding Overseas to 85th Battalion. 2 Other Ranks struck off strength to N.S.R.D. having proceeded to Royal Air Force. 3 Officers taken on strength from N.S.R.D. 6 Officers taken on strength on reporting from 10th. Officers Training Class, Bexhill-on-Sea.

### 5 October 1918 — Bramshott, Hants., England — Sat.

> Weather, Fair in morning, slight showers in afternoon and evening. Training carried on as usual for half day. Camp quarantined this date and all towns and villages in vicinity placed "Out of Bounds," owing to Epidemic of Influenza breaking out. Routine Orders 2667 to 2676. Part 11 D.O. No. 236. 26 Other Ranks taken on strength on posting from N.S.R.D. 1 Other Rank taken on strength on transfer from Canadian Record Office, London. Four Other Ranks struck off strength on posting to N.S.R.D. 1 Officer taken on strength on posting from N.S.R.D. 1 Officer struck off strength on posting to N.S.R.D. having been patient in Hospital for 21 days.

### 6 October 1918 — Bramshott, Hants., England — Sun.

> Weather Fine and warmer. Divine Service cancelled owing to Influenza epidemic. Range Party scheduled to proceed to Mytchett Ranges this date cancelled owing to Influenza epidemic. 106 Other Ranks (Range Party) reported Off Command from Mytchett Ranges this date.
