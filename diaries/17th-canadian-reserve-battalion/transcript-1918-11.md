## Transcription conventions
- Faithful to the original: spelling, punctuation, abbreviations and capitalisation as written.
- `[uncertain]` marks a word or name that could not be read with confidence — never guessed.
- The diary-text pages are Army Form C. 2118, "WAR DIARY or INTELLIGENCE SUMMARY", typewritten in columns: Place | Date | Hour | Summary of Events and Information | Remarks and references to Appendices. The "Hour" column is blank throughout this volume.
- The "Remarks and references to Appendices" column carries the same handwritten word against each day's entry, [uncertain] (appears to read "certified"); it is not repeated entry by entry below.
- "T.O.S." = Taken on Strength; "S.O.S." = Struck off Strength; "N.S.R.D." = Nova Scotia Regimental Depot.
- The diary sheets are numbered in print "(Page 2)" to "(Page 6)"; the first diary sheet (1–6 November) carries no printed page number.

### Friday 1 November 1918 — Bramshott

> Weather Fair, but with slight showers throughout the day. Training carried on as usual. Routine Orders 2900 to 2904 Part II Daily Order 259 — Sixty-five Other Ranks Taken on Strength on posting from Nova Scotia Regimental Depot. One Other Rank (Category "Bi") Struck off Strength on posting to Nova Scotia Regimental Depot. One Other Rank having died in Bramshott Military Hospital (Pleurisy) Struck off Strength with effect 31-10-18. One Officer Taken on Strength on posting from Nova Scotia Regimental Depot.

### Saturday 2 November 1918 — Bramshott

> Weather Fair throughout the day. Rain at night. Training carried on as usual for half-day. Routine Orders 2905 to 2912 Part II Daily Order 260 — Eight Other Ranks Taken on Strength on posting from Nova Scotia Regimental Depot. One Other Rank Struck off Strength on transfer to 10th Canadian Reserve Battalion (Quebec Regiment "Q"). Eleven Other Ranks (lowered in Category) Struck off Strength on posting to Nova Scotia Regimental Depot. Two Officers Taken on Strength on posting from Nova Scotia Regimental Depot.

### Sunday 3 November 1918 — Bramshott

> Weather — Heavy rain throughout the day. Divine Services held this date. Medical Inspection of Battalion also held this date.

### Monday 4 November 1918 — Bramshott

> Weather — Heavy rain and wind storm all day. Training (in huts) carried on as usual throughout the day. Range Party (197 Other Ranks) reported Off Command from Mytchett this date. Routine Orders 2913 to 2919 Part II Daily Order 261 — Two Other Ranks Taken on Strength on posting from Nova Scotia Regimental Depot. One Officer Taken on Strength on transfer from 2nd Canadian Engineers Reserve Battalion, Seaford, with effect 28-10-18.

### Tuesday 5 November 1918 — Bramshott

> Weather — Fair. Training carried on as usual throughout the day. Routine Orders 2920 to 2926, Part II Daily Order 262 — Two Other Ranks Taken on Strength on posting from Nova Scotia Regimental Depot. Ten Other Ranks (lowered in Category) Struck off Strength on posting to Nova Scotia Regimental Depot. Two Officers (one for 25th Battalion and one for 85th Battalion) Struck off Strength on proceeding Overseas this date.

### Wednesday 6 November 1918 — Bramshott

> Weather — Clear and cold. Training carried on as usual. 78 Other Ranks (Contacts) reported from Segregation Camp, Frensham Pond Camp. Routine Orders 2927 to 2933 — One Other Rank Taken on Strength on posting from Nova Scotia Regimental Depot. One Other Rank Struck off Strength to Nova Scotia Regimental Depot for invaliding to Canada. One Other Rank classified
>
> [Sentence continues on page 241.]

## Page 241 — Diary text, 6–11 November 1918 (November 1918, p. 3)

### Wednesday 6 November 1918 (contd.) — Bramshott

> "Bii" Struck off Strength to Nova Scotia Regimental Depot.

### Thursday 7 November 1918 — Bramshott

> Weather — Fine and clear all day. Training carried on as usual all day. Class 7 carried out Route Marching in afternoon. Parades dismissed at 1530 o'clock in order that All Ranks could witness Bands and Party attending Lord Mayor's Show March Past. Reinforcements No.310 proceeded Overseas this date. Routine Orders 2934 to 2944 Part II Daily Order 264 — 1 Other Rank Struck off Strength to Nova Scotia Regimental Depot (Classified "Biii"). One Other Rank Struck off Strength to Nova Scotia Regimental Depot for despatch to Royal Air Force. Three Officers Taken on Strength from Nova Scotia Regimental Depot.

### Friday 8 November 1918 — Bramshott

> Weather — Heavy rain and wind, occasional sleet. 133 Other Ranks proceeded to Mytchett Ranges this date to fire Part III C.M.C. Training carried on in huts owing to the inclemency of the weather. Band and party to attend Lord Mayor's Show in London left the Area this date. Routine Orders 2945 to 2949 Part II Daily Order 265 — 2 Other Ranks Taken on Strength from Nova Scotia Regimental Depot. 134 Other Ranks Struck off Strength on proceeding Overseas to 25th Battalion. 35 Other Ranks Struck off Strength on proceeding Overseas to 85th Battalion. 114 Other Ranks Struck off Strength on proceeding Overseas to Royal Canadian Regiment.

### Saturday 9 November 1918 — Bramshott

> Weather — Very fine and warm. Training carried on as usual. Routine Orders 2950 to 2957, Part II Daily Order 266 — 27 Other Ranks Taken on Strength from Nova Scotia Regimental Depot. 1 Other Rank Taken on Strength on transfer from Witley Regimental Depot Group. 7 Other Ranks (lowered in Category) Struck off Strength to Nova Scotia Regimental Depot. 3 Other Ranks Struck off Strength to Nova Scotia Regimental Depot for transfer to Royal Air Force.

### Sunday 10 November 1918 — Bramshott

> Weather — Fine and very clear. Divine Service held in morning as usual.

### Monday 11 November 1918 — Bramshott

> Weather — Cold with rain. Armistice was signed at 0500 this date and fighting ceased at 1100, 1559th day of the War. Training carried on as usual throughout the morning. Afternoon declared a holiday by General Officer Commanding and all training dispensed with. Canteens and Messes were opened for troops all afternoon, Smoker and Concert in Sergeants' and Officers' Messes in evening. Routine Orders 2958 to 2965, Part II Daily Order 267 — 2 Other Ranks Taken on Strength from Nova Scotia Regimental Depot. 1 Officer Struck off Strength on proceeding Overseas to Royal Canadian Regiment.

Routine Orders 2986 to 2993, Part II Daily Order 270. 1 Other Rank Taken on Strength from Nova Scotia Regimental Depot. 1 Other Rank Struck off Strength to Nova Scotia Regimental Depot. 1 Other Ranks Struck off Strength on transfer to Manitoba Regiment, Seaford.

### Friday 15 November 1918 — Bramshott

> Weather — Fine and very clear. Muster Parade of Unit was held at 0830 this date. Inspection of Reinforcements No. 312 was held at 0900 this date. 49 Other Ranks on transfer to Canadian Machine Gun Depot, Seaford, and 74 Other Ranks (Colored Troops) on transfer to Canadian Forestry Corps, Sunningdale, proceeded this date.
