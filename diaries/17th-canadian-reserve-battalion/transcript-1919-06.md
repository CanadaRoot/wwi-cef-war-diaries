## Note on the end date
- The LAC record and the task brief give the diary's end as 26 June 1919, and the cover and title page of this volume say "1 June 1919 — 28 June 1919". The diary text in fact runs to **28 June 1919**: the 26 June entry continues onto Sheet 6, and entries for 27 and 28 June follow, the last recording the battalion's departure for Southampton. No diary pages for 29–30 June exist in this record.

## Page 288 — Diary text, 1–6 June 1919 (June 1919, Sheet 1)

Army Form C. 2118. Heading: "Sheet 1. / WAR DIARY / or / INTELLIGENCE/SUMMARY/ / 17th Canadian Reserve Battalion, (Nova Scotia Regiment)."

### 1 June 1919 — Block 28, South Camp, Ripon, Yorkshire.

> Weather — fine. Divine Services held as usual this date.

### 2 June 1919 — Block 28, South Camp, Ripon, Yorkshire.

> Weather — fine. Routine Orders No.726 to 729. Part II Daily Order No.126. 1 Other Rank Taken on Strength from Headquarters, Sub-Staff, Canadian Troops, Ripon, with effect 2-6-19. 5 Other Ranks Struck off Strength on transfer to 2nd Canadian Command Depot, Ripon, with effect 31-5-19. 39 Other Ranks (attending Classes at Khaki University) struck off strength on transfer to 2nd Canadian Command Depot, with effect 28-5-19. 54 Other Ranks, (while on command to various Units, etc.) struck off strength to Nova Scotia Regimental Depot, with effect 1-6-19. 42 Other Ranks (Patients in various Hospitals) Struck off strength to Nova Scotia Regimental Depot, with effect 1-6-19. 1 Officer Struck off strength to Nova Scotia Regimental Depot on admission to Granville Canadian Special Hospital, Buxton, with effect 2-6-19.

### 3 June 1919 — Block 28, South Camp, Ripon, Yorkshire.

> Weather — fine. Routine Orders No. 730 to 734. Part II Daily Order No.127. 4 Other Ranks Struck off Strength to Nova Scotia Regimental Depot, whilst patients in various Hospitals, with effect 1-6-19. 1 Other Rank Struck off Strength to Nova Scotia Regimental Depot with effect 3-6-19.

### 4 June 1919 — Block 28, South Camp, Ripon, Yorkshire.

> Weather — rain fell at intervals. Routine Orders No.735 to 738. Part II Daily Orders No.128. 77 Other Ranks (Rear Parties of the 13th, 15th, 21st and 23rd Reserve Battalions) attached to this Unit for quarters, rations, and discipline, with effect 4-6-19. 3 Other Ranks having reported, are Taken on Strength from Nova Scotia Regimental Depot, with effect 4-6-19.

### 5 June 1919 — Block 28, South Camp, Ripon, Yorkshire.

> Weather — fine. Routine Orders No. 739 to 742. Part II Daily Orders No.129.

### 6 June 1919 — Block 28, South Camp, Ripon, Yorkshire.

> Weather — fine and very warm. Routine Orders No.743 to 745. Part II Daily Orders No.130. The Brass Band of this Unit (26 Other Ranks) reported off Command from Kinmel Park Camp, 4-6-19. 3 Other Ranks (on command to 2nd Canadian Discharge Depot, London,) struck off strength to Nova Scotia Regimental Depot, with effect 1-6-19. 1 Other Rank struck off strength on transfer to 2nd Canadian Command Depot, Ripon, with effect 26-5-19.
