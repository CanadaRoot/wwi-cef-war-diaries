## Page 280 — Diary text, 1–6 May 1919 (May 1919, Sheet 1)

Army Form C. 2118. Heading: "SHEET 1. / WAR DIARY / or / INTELLIGENCE/SUMMARY/ / 17th Canadian Reserve Battalion, (Nova Scotia Regiment)."

### 1 May 1919 — Block 28, South Camp, Ripon, Yorkshire.

> Weather — Raining. Training carried on as per Syllabus. Routine Orders 597 to 605. Part II Daily Order No.100. 2 Other Ranks Taken on Strength from Nova Scotia Regimental Depot. Two Other Ranks Struck off Strength to Nova Scotia Regimental Depot. Two Other Ranks Struck off Strength to 85th Canadian Infantry Battalion, Bramshott. Three Other Ranks Struck off Strength on proceeding to Canada, 18-1-19.

### 2 May 1919 — Block 28, South Camp, Ripon, Yorkshire.

> Weather — Raining at intervals throughout the day. Training carried on as usual. Routine Orders 606 to 610. Part II Daily Order No.101. 17 Other Ranks Taken on Strength from the Nova Scotia Regimental Depot. 1 Other Rank Struck off Strength to Canadian Record Office, London.

### 3 May 1919 — Block 28, South Camp, Ripon, Yorkshire.

> Weather — Raining at intervals throughout the day. Training carried on as usual for half day. First game in Ripon Amateur Baseball League played this date. Routine Orders 611 to 618. Part II Daily Order No.102. 6 Other Ranks Taken on Strength from Nova Scotia Regimental Depot. Two Officers Taken on Strength from Nova Scotia Regimental Depot. Party of 1 Officer & 43 Other Ranks proceeded On Command to Normald Green Ranges this date to act as Permanent Party at that Camp.

### 4 May 1919 — Block 28, South Camp, Ripon, Yorkshire.

> Weather — Rain in morning, fine in evening. Divine Services held as usual this date.

### 5 May 1919 — Block 28, South Camp, Ripon, Yorkshire.

> Weather — Dull and overcast. Training carried on as usual. Routine Orders 616 to 618. Details of A.D.M.S., A.D.D.S., and various Sick Detention Hut Patients attached to this Unit for all purposes, this date. Part II Daily Order No.102. 5 Other Ranks Taken on Strength from Nova Scotia Regimental Depot.

### 6 May 1919 — Block 28, South Camp, Ripon, Yorkshire.

> Weather — Raining. Training carried on as per Syllabus. Routine Orders 619 to 624. Part II Daily Order No.104. 60 Other Ranks Struck off Strength M.D.C. Wings, Kinmel Park, for return to Canada, with effect this date.

[Note: the Routine Orders on 5 May are given as "616 to 618", overlapping the "611 to 618" of 3 May, and the Part II Daily Order numbers jump from No.102 (5 May) to No.104 (6 May) — as written in the original.]

## Page 281 — Diary text, 7–11 May 1919 (May 1919, Sheet II)

### 7 May 1919 — Block 28, South Camp, Ripon, Yorkshire.

> Weather — Heavy rainfall. Routine Orders 625 to 628. Band of this Unit held dance at "Winter Gardens", Harrogate, this date. Part II Daily Order No.105. 8 Other Ranks Taken on Strength from Nova Scotia Regimental Depot.

### 8 May 1919 — Block 28, South Camp, Ripon, Yorkshire.

> Weather — rain in morning, clearing in afternoon. Training carried on as usual. Routine Orders 629 to 631. Part II Daily Order No.106. 1 Other Rank Taken on Strength from Nova Scotia Regimental Depot. 4 Other Ranks Taken on Strength from 25th Canadian Infantry Battalion, Witley. 1 Officer Taken on Strength from 25th Canadian Infantry Battalion, Witley.

### 9 May 1919 — Block 28, South Camp, Ripon, Yorkshire.

> Weather — rain in morning, clearing in afternoon. Training carried on as usual. Routine Orders 632 to 637. Part II Daily Order No.107. 1 Other Rank Taken on Strength from Nova Scotia Regimental Depot. 1 Other Rank Struck off Strength to Nova Scotia Regimental Depot for invaliding to Canada. 6 Other Ranks cease to be shown on command to 2nd Canadian Discharge Depot, London, and are Struck off Strength of this Unit upon discharge in the British Isles. 1 Other Rank Struck off Strength to C.A.D.C., Ripon. 1 Officer Taken on Strength from 25th Canadian Infantry Battalion, Witley.

### 10 May 1919 — Block 28, South Camp, Ripon, Yorkshire.

> Weather — fine and warm during day; thunder storm with heavy rain in evening. Training carried on as usual for half day. Routine Orders 638 to 640. Part II Daily Order No.108. 13 Other Ranks Taken on Strength from Nova Scotia Regimental Depot. 10 Other Ranks (Married) having proceeded to Canadian Discharge Depot, Buxton, for return to Canada, struck off strength of this Unit to Nova Scotia Regimental Depot. 25 Other Ranks Struck off Strength to M.D.C.Wings, Kinmel Park for return to Canada, with effect this date. 1 Officer Taken on Strength from Nova Scotia Regimental Depot. 1 Officer Struck off Strength on transfer to the C.E.F. in Canada with effect 3-5-19.

### 11 May 1919 — Block 28, South Camp, Ripon, Yorkshire.

> Weather — fine and very warm in morning. Thunder storm with heavy rain in afternoon. Divine Services held as usual this date.

## Page 282 — Diary text, 12–15 May 1919 (May 1919, Sheet III)

### 12 May 1919 — Block 28, South Camp, Ripon, Yorkshire.

> Weather — very dull. Training carried on as per usual. Routine Orders 641 to 648. Part II Daily Order No.109. 6 Other Ranks Taken on Strength of this Unit from Nova Scotia Regimental Depot. 1 Other Rank Taken on Strength from 25th Canadian Infantry Battalion, Witley. 1 Other Rank Taken on Strength from Headquarters Sub Staff, Canadian Troops, Ripon. 2 Other Ranks having been declared illegally absent by Courts of Inquiry held on 7-5-19 and 8-5-19 respectively Struck off Strength of this Unit as deserters. 1 Officer Taken on Strength of this Unit from the 25th Canadian Infantry Battalion, Witley. 3 Officers Struck off Strength of this Unit to 65th Canadian Infantry Battalion, Bramshott.

### 13 May 1919 — Block 28, South Camp, Ripon, Yorkshire.

> Weather — fine and very warm. No training carried on, Battalion Sports being held this date. Routine Orders 649 to 650. Part II Daily Order No.110. 2 Other Ranks Taken on Strength from Nova Scotia Regimental Depot. 1 Other Rank Taken on Strength from 25th Canadian Infantry Battalion, Witley. 1 Other Rank Taken on Strength from the C.A.M.C. Casualty Company. 4 Other Ranks Struck off Strength to M.D.C.Wings, Kinmel Park, for return to Canada, with effect this date.

### 14 May 1919 — Block 28, South Camp, Ripon, Yorkshire.

> Weather — fine and very warm. Training carried on as usual. Commencing this date Reveille sounds at 0630, and Retreat at 1700. Routine Orders No.651 to 657. Part II Daily Order No.111. 2 Other Ranks Taken on Strength from Nova Scotia Regimental Depot. 1 Other Rank Taken on Strength from "G" Wing, Canadian Corps Camp, Witley. 19 Other Ranks cease to be shown On Command at Kinmel Park Camp, Rhyl, N.Wales, and are Struck off Strength of this Unit, having proceeded to Canada on various dates enumerated in said order, No.111. 25 Other Ranks Struck off Strength to M.D.C.Wings, Kinmel Park, for return to Canada, with effect this date. 1 Other Rank Struck off Strength to Unit Group No.12, 4th Canadian Division, Canadian Corps Camp, Bramshott. 1 Officer Struck off Strength to Nova Scotia Regimental Depot, having proceeded to Canada this date.

### 15 May 1919 — Block 28, South Camp, Ripon, Yorkshire.

> Weather — fine and very warm. Training carried on as usual. Routine Orders 658 to 662. Part II Daily Order No.112. 6 Other Ranks Taken on Strength from Nova Scotia Regimental Depot. 1 Other Rank Struck off Strength on transfer to the C.E.F. in Canada, with effect 12-1-19. 2 Other Ranks Struck off Strength on transfer to the C.A.D.C., Ripon.

## Page 283 — Diary text, 16–20 May 1919 (May 1919, Sheet IV)

### 16 May 1919 — Block 28, South Camp, Ripon, Yorkshire.

> Weather — fine and very warm. Training carried on as usual for half day. Holiday in afternoon to allow men to attend Ripon Race Meet. Draft of 2 Officers, and 113 Other Ranks proceeded Overseas for service with Canadian War Graves Detachment, France, with effect this date. Routine Orders 663 to 668. Part II Daily Order No.113. 5 Other Ranks Taken on Strength from Nova Scotia Regimental Depot. 4 Other Ranks cease to be shown On Command to 2nd Canadian Discharge Depot, London, and are Struck off Strength of this Unit upon discharge in the British Isles.

### 17 May 1919 — Block 28, South Camp, Ripon, Yorkshire.

> Weather — dull, with slight rainfall in afternoon. Ceremonial Parade held this a.m., as many men as possible being on parade. Routine Orders 669 to 673. Part II Daily Order No.114. 7 Other Ranks Taken on Strength from Nova Scotia Regimental Depot. 113 Other Ranks Struck off strength on proceeding overseas to Canadian War Graves Detachment, France. 32 Other Ranks Struck off Strength to M.D.C.Wings, Kinmel Park, for return to Canada, with effect this date. 1 Officer Taken on Strength from Nova Scotia Regimental Depot. 2 Officers Struck off Strength on proceeding overseas to Canadian War Graves Detachment, France, with effect 16-5-19.

### 18 May 1919 — Block 28, South Camp, Ripon, Yorkshire.

> Weather — Overcast and chilly. Divine Services held as usual this date.

### 19 May 1919 — Block 28, South Camp, Ripon, Yorkshire.

> Weather — Very fine. Monthly Muster Parade of this Battalion held this date. Training carried on as usual after Muster Parade. Holiday granted in the afternoon by G.O.C., Canadian Troops, Ripon. Routine Orders 674 to 681. Part II Daily Orders 115. 1 Officer and 28 Other Ranks proceeded On Command — Educational Tour visiting Large Fish Houses in the British Isles, with effect this date. 3 Other Ranks Taken on Strength from Nova Scotia Regimental Depot. 1 Other Rank Struck off Strength to No.1 Detachment, C.A.P.C., London. 1 Officer Struck off Strength on transfer to the C.E.F. in Canada, with effect 10-5-19.

### 20 May 1919 — Block 28, South Camp, Ripon, Yorkshire.

> Weather — Very fine. 29 Officers and 630 Other Rank from this Area proceeded to Sheffield this date for Review by His Majesty King George V. Dental Boarding of this Unit, prior to return to Canada, commenced this date. Routine Orders No.682. Part II Daily Orders No.116. 5 Other Ranks Taken on Strength from Nova Scotia Regimental Depot.

## Page 284 — Diary text, 21–26 May 1919 (May 1919, Sheet V)

### 21 May 1919 — Block 28, South Camp, Ripon, Yorkshire.

> Weather — fine. Training carried on as usual for half day. Routine Orders 683 to 686. Part II Daily Order No.117. 3 Other Ranks Taken on Strength from Nova Scotia Regimental Depot. 1 Other Rank Struck off Strength to Headquarters Sub Staff, Canadian Troops, Ripon.

### 22 May 1919 — Block 28, South Camp, Ripon, Yorkshire.

> Weather — fine and warm. Training carried on as usual. Routine Orders 687 to 690. Part II Daily Order No.118. 2 Other Ranks Taken on Strength from Nova Scotia Regimental Depot. 8 Other Ranks cease to be shown to 2nd Canadian Discharge Depot, London, and are struck off Strength of this Unit upon discharge in the British Isles.

### 23 May 1919 — Block 28, South Camp, Ripon, Yorkshire.

> Weather — fine and very warm. Medical Boarding of this Unit, prior to return to Canada, commenced this date. Routine Orders 691 to 694. Part II Daily Order No.119. 9 Other Ranks Taken on Strength from Nova Scotia Regimental Depot. 1 Other Rank Struck off Strength on posting to Nova Scotia Regimental Depot. 1 Other Rank Struck off Strength on transfer to No.9 Section, Canadian Salvage Corps, Ripon. 1 Officer Struck off Strength on transfer to the C.E.F. in Canada, with effect 14-5-19.

### 24 May 1919 — Block 28, South Camp, Ripon, Yorkshire.

> Weather — fine and very warm. General holiday this date. Area Sports held on Ripon City Cricket Grounds this date, same being largely attended by civilians and soldiers. No Battalion Orders issued this date.

### 25 May 1919 — Block 28, South Camp, Ripon, Yorkshire.

> Weather — dull, sultry, and rainy. Divine Services held as usual this date.

### 26 May 1919 — Block 28, South Camp, Ripon, Yorkshire.

> Weather — fine. Routine Orders 695 to 700. Part II Daily Orders No.120. 1 Other Rank Taken on Strength, having surrendered himself from desertion, with effect 7-5-19. 7 Other Ranks Taken on Strength from Nova Scotia Regimental Depot. 1 Other Rank Struck off Strength on transfer to 85th Canadian Infantry Battalion, Bramshott. 16 Other Ranks (Married) having proceeded to Canadian Discharge Depot, Buxton, for return to Canada, Struck off Strength of this Unit to Nova Scotia Regimental Depot, with effect 24-5-19. 1 Officer Struck off Strength to 85th Canadian Infantry Battalion, Bramshott.

## Page 285 — Diary text, 27–31 May 1919 (May 1919, Sheet VI)

### 27 May 1919 — Block 28, South Camp, Ripon, Yorkshire.

> Weather — fine. Permanent Party reported off Command from Normald Green Musketry Camp, with effect this date. Routine Orders 701 to 706. Part II Daily Order No.121. 1 Other Rank Taken on Strength from Nova Scotia Regimental Depot. 1 Other Rank Struck off Strength on posting to Nova Scotia Regimental Depot. 2 Officers having been Patients in Hospital for a period of 21 days, Struck off Strength on posting to Nova Scotia Regimental Depot.

### 28 May 1919 — Block 28, South Camp, Ripon, Yorkshire.

> Weather — fine and very warm. Routine Orders 707 to 709. Party of 1 Officer and 28 Other Ranks reported off Command from Educational Tour visiting large Fish houses this date. Part II Daily Order No.122. 1 Other Rank Taken on Strength from Nova Scotia Regimental Depot. 1 Other Rank having proceeded to Canadian Discharge Depot, Buxton, for return to Canada on compassionate grounds, Struck off Strength of this Unit on posting to Nova Scotia Regimental Depot, with effect 24-5-19.

### 29 May 1919 — Block 28, South Camp, Ripon, Yorkshire.

> Weather — dull. Routine Orders 710 to 713. Part II Daily Order No.123. 1 Other Rank Taken on Strength from Nova Scotia Regimental Depot. 10 Other Ranks cease to be shown on Command to Kinmel Park Camp, Rhyl, N.Wales, and are Struck off Strength of this Unit to the various M.D.C.Wings, Kinmel Park, and on dates enumerated in said Order — No.123.

### 30 May 1919 — Block 28, South Camp, Ripon, Yorkshire.

> Weather — fine. Routine Orders 714 to 717. Part II Daily Order No.124. 1 Officer Taken on Strength from Nova Scotia Regimental Depot with effect 8-5-19. 1 Officer, a patient in Ripon Camp Military Hospital, struck off Strength to Nova Scotia Regimental Depot, with effect this date.

### 31 May 1919 — Block 28, South Camp, Ripon, Yorkshire.

> Weather — dull. Routine Orders 718 to 725. Part II Daily Order No.125. 17 Other Ranks reported off command from Normald Green Musketry Camp, with effect 30-5-19. 2 Other Ranks Struck off Strength on transfer to C.A.D.C., London, with effect 19-5-19. 1 Officer Struck off Strength on transfer to the 2nd Canadian Command Depot, Ripon, with effect 31-5-19. Strength of Unit at this date — OFFICERS 63. OTHER RANKS 1476.
