## Page 254 — Diary text, 1–8 January 1919 (January 1919, p. I)

Army Form C. 2118. Heading: "17TH CANADIAN RESERVE BATTALION. / JANUARY, 1919. / WAR DIARY / or / INTELLIGENCE SUMMARY. / PAGE I."

### 1 January 1919 — Bramshott, Hants.

> New Years Day. Holiday all day, and no training carried on. Weather showery. No Daily Orders this date.

### 2 January 1919 — Bramshott, Hants.

> Weather fair, rain at night. Training carried on as usual. Routine Orders 1 to 4. Part II D.O. No.1. 5 Other Ranks Taken on Strength from Nova Scotia Regimental Depot. 5 Officers Struck off Strength to Nova Scotia Regimental Depot for embarkation to Canada.

### 3 January 1919 — Bramshott, Hants.

> Weather fair, rain at night. Training carried on as usual. Routine Orders 5 to 12. Part II D.O. No.2. 1 Other Rank Taken on Strength on transfer from 13th Reserve Battalion, Witley. 1 Other Rank Taken on Strength from Royal Air Force. 3 Other Ranks taken on Strength from Nova Scotia Regimental Depot. 148 Other Ranks Struck off Strength on transfer to C.E.F. in Canada.

### 4 January 1919 — Bramshott, Hants.

> Weather fair, rain at night. Training carried on as usual for half-day. Routine Orders 13 to 21. Part II D.O. No.3. 6 Other Ranks Taken on Strength from Nova Scotia Regimental Depot.

### 5 January 1919 — Bramshott, Hants.

> Weather fair with showers. 100 Other Ranks proceeded to Kinmel Park this date for return to Canada. Divine Services held as usual.

### 6 January 1919 — Bramshott, Hants.

> Weather showery all day. Training carried on as usual. Routine Orders 22 to 28. Part II D.O.4. 2 Other Ranks Taken on Strength from Nova Scotia Regimental Depot. 31 Other Ranks Struck off Strength to C.E.F. in Canada.

### 7 January 1919 — Bramshott, Hants.

> Weather fair with showers. Training carried on as usual. Routine Orders 29 to 35. 35 Part II D.O.5. 25 Other Ranks Taken on Strength from Nova Scotia Regimental Depot. 1 Other Rank Struck off Strength on transfer to C.E.F. in Canada.

### 8 January 1919 — Bramshott, Hants.

> Weather fair and cold, showers at night. 50 Other Ranks proceeded to Kinmel Park this date. Training carried on as usual. Routine Orders 36 to 41. Part II D.O.6. 15 Other Ranks Taken on Strength from Nova Scotia Regimental Depot. 1 Other Rank Struck off Strength to 25th Battalion, returned for trial by Field General Court Martial.

## Page 255 — Diary text, 9–15 January 1919 (January 1919, p. II)

### 9 January 1919 — Bramshott, Hants.

> Weather showery. Training carried on as usual. Routine Orders 42 to 49. Part II D.O.7. 38 Other Ranks Taken on Strength from Nova Scotia Regimental Depot. 1 Other Rank Struck off Strength on transfer to No.2 Section, Canadian Salvage Corps. 1 Other Rank Struck off Strength on transfer to Canadian War Records Office, London.

### 10 January 1919 — Bramshott, Hants.

> Weather fair with showers at night. Training carried on as usual. 50 Other Ranks proceeded to Kinmel Park this date for return to Canada. Routine Orders 50 to 56. Part II D.O.8. 5 Other Ranks Taken on Strength from Nova Scotia Regimental Depot. 2 Other Ranks Struck off Strength to Nova Scotia Regimental Depot. 2 Other Ranks Struck off Strength as Deserters. 5 Officers Taken on Strength from Nova Scotia Regimental Depot.

### 11 January 1919 — Bramshott, Hants.

> Weather fair. Training carried on as usual for half day. Routine Orders 57 to 66. Part II D.O.9. 5 Other Ranks Taken on Strength from Nova Scotia Regimental Depot. 1 Other Rank Struck off Strength to Nova Scotia Regimental Depot. 4 Officers Taken on Strength from Nova Scotia Regimental Depot.

### 12 January 1919 — Bramshott, Hants.

> Weather showery in morning, clearing in afternoon. This Unit took over Area Duties with effect Reveille this date.

### 13 January 1919 — Bramshott, Hants.

> Weather fair and cold. 60 Other Ranks proceeded to Kinmel Park this date for return to Canada. Training carried on as usual. Routine Orders 67 to 71. Part II D.O.10. 3 Other Ranks Taken on Strength from Nova Scotia Regimental Depot. 1 Officer Taken on Strength from Nova Scotia Regimental Depot.

### 14 January 1919 — Bramshott, Hants.

> Weather foggy with rain all day. Training carried on as usual. Routine Orders 72 to 74. Part II D.O.11. 33 Other Ranks Taken on Strength from Nova Scotia Regimental Depot. 1 Other Rank Struck off Strength to Nova Scotia Regimental Depot. 26 Other Ranks (Cadets) cease to be shown on Command to Cadets Segregation Camp and are Struck off Strength to C.E.F. in Canada.

### 15 January 1919 — Bramshott, Hants.

> Weather — heavy rain throughout the day. Training carried on as per Syllabus. Routine Orders 75 to 78. Battalion warned this date to prepare for move from this Area to Ripon Camp, Yorks. Part II D.O.12. 3 Other Ranks Taken on Strength from Nova Scotia Regimental Depot. 8 Officers Taken on Strength from posting from Nova Scotia Regimental Depot. 1 Officer Taken on Strength having reported from Royal Canadian Regiment, France.

## Page 256 — Diary text, 16–20 January 1919 (January 1919, p. III)

### 16 January 1919 — Bramshott, Hants.

> Weather — slight showers throughout the day. Training carried on as usual. Routine Orders 79 to 84. Part II D.O.13. 56 Other Ranks Taken on Strength from Nova Scotia Regimental Depot. 1 Other Rank Struck off Strength on posting to Nova Scotia Regimental Depot. 3 Officers Struck off Strength on posting to Nova Scotia Regimental Depot, having proceeded to Headquarters, O.M.F.C., for embarkation instructions for Canada.

### 17 January 1919 — Bramshott, Hants.

> Weather — fair. Training carried on as usual in morning. Battalion paraded to No.1 Y.M.C.A. at 1430 o'clock for Lecture by Lieut. Colonel Frank Adams, McGill University, on "The Canadian Government Land Policy for Soldiers". Advance Party, consisting of 2 Officers and 21 Other Ranks proceeded to Ripon Camp, Yorks., this date. 29 Other Ranks (Married Men) proceeded to Canadian Discharge Depot, Buxton, for return to Canada, this date. Routine Orders 85 to 89. Part II D.O.14. 8 Other Ranks Taken on Strength on posting from Nova Scotia Regimental Depot. 1 Other Rank Struck off Strength on posting to Nova Scotia Regimental Depot.

### 18 January 1919 — Bramshott, Hants.

> Weather — fair, but with slight showers throughout the day. Training carried on as usual for half day. Routine Orders 90 to 97. Part II D.O.15. 60 Other Ranks Struck off Strength on proceeding to Canada from Kinmel Park Camp, Rhyl, N.Wales, (1 with effect 12-12-18; 11 with effect 15-12-18; 48 with effect 21-12-18). 8 Other Ranks Struck off Strength on posting to Nova Scotia Regimental Depot. 1 Officer Taken on Strength on posting from Nova Scotia Regimental Depot.

### 19 January 1919 — Bramshott, Hants.

> Weather — fair. Divine Services held as usual. 55 Other Ranks proceeded to Kinmel Park for return to Canada this date.

### 20 January 1919 — Bramshott, Hants.

> Weather — heavy rain all day. Battalion Muster Parade for January held this date. Training carried on as usual after Muster. Routine Orders 98 to 106. Part II D.O.16. 3 Other Ranks Taken on Strength on posting from Nova Scotia Regimental Depot. 1 Other Rank Taken on Strength having surrendered himself from desertion. 160 Other Ranks (1 with effect 4-1-19; 159 with effect 9-1-19) Struck off Strength on proceeding to Canada from Kinmel Park Camp, Rhyl, N.Wales. 1 Other Rank Struck off Strength on transfer to Headquarters Sub-Staff, Canadians, Bramshott. 1 Other Rank Struck off Strength having died in Bramshott Military Hospital. 18 Cadets cease to be shown on command at Cadet Segregation Camp, 10th Canadian Reserve Battalion, and Struck off Strength on proceeding to Canada 18-1-19. 3 Officers Taken on Strength on posting from Nova Scotia Regimental Depot.

## Page 257 — Diary text, 21–27 January 1919 (January 1919, p. IV)

### 21 January 1919 — Bramshott, Hants.

> Weather — rain all day. Training carried on as usual. Routine Orders 107 to 109. Part II D.O.17. 2 Other Ranks Struck off Strength on transfer to Corps of Canadian Military Police, Kinmel Park. 3 Other Ranks Struck off Strength on posting to Nova Scotia Regimental Depot. 9 Officers Struck off Strength on posting to Nova Scotia Regimental Depot, having proceeded to Canadian Discharge Depot, Buxton, pending embarkation instructions for Canada.

### 22 January 1919 — Bramshott, Hants.

> Weather fine and warmer. Battalion moves to Ripon Camp, Yorkshire, this date, entraining on three trains, viz. 2210, 2310, and 0010, 23rd inst. Routine Orders 110 to 115. Part II D.O.18.

### 23 January 1919 — South Camp, Ripon, Yorks.

> Weather fair and very cold. Battalion arrived at Ripon as follows — 1st Train 23 Officers, 513 Other Ranks at 0920; 2nd Train 24 Officers, 506 Other Ranks at 0840; 3rd Train 13 Officers and 511 Other Ranks at 1040. No Daily Orders published this date owing to move.

### 24 January 1919 — South Camp, Ripon, Yorks.

> Weather fine and cold. Routine Orders 116 to 124. Part II D.O.19. 2 Other Ranks Taken on Strength from Nova Scotia Regimental Depot. 64 Other Ranks cease to be shown on command at Kinmel Park, and are Struck off Strength to C.E.F. in Canada with effect 12-1-19. 1 Officer Taken on Strength from Nova Scotia Regimental Depot.

### 25 January 1919 — South Camp, Ripon, Yorks.

> Weather fine and cold. Training carried on as usual for half day. Routine Orders 125 to 143. Part II D.O.20. 3 Other Ranks Taken on Strength from Nova Scotia Regimental Depot. 2 Other Ranks Struck off Strength on transfer to Canadian Army Pay Corps.

### 26 January 1919 — South Camp, Ripon, Yorks.

> Weather very fine and warm, during the day, turning very cold at night. Divine Services held as usual.

### 27 January 1919 — South Camp, Ripon, Yorks.

> Weather fair and cold. Snow fell during the day. Routine Orders 144 to 153. Part II D.O.21. 1 Other Rank Taken on Strength from Nova Scotia Regimental Depot. 1 Other Rank Struck off Strength to Nova Scotia Regimental Depot.

## Page 258 — Diary text, 28–31 January 1919 (January 1919, p. V)

### 28 January 1919 — South Camp, Ripon, Yorks.

> Weather fair and cold. Training carried on as usual. Routine Orders 154 to 156. Part II D.O.22. 2 Officers Taken on Strength from Nova Scotia Regimental Depot. 3 Officers Struck off Strength to Nova Scotia Regimental Depot. 2 Other Ranks Taken on Strength from Nova Scotia Regimental Depot. 3 Other Ranks Struck off Strength to C.E.F. in Canada. 2 Other Ranks Struck off Strength to Nova Scotia Regimental Depot.

### 29 January 1919 — South Camp, Ripon, Yorks.

> Weather fair with showers at night. Training carried on as usual. Routine Orders 157 to 164. Part II D.O.23. 15 Other Ranks Taken on Strength from Nova Scotia Regimental Depot. 186 Other Ranks Struck off Strength to Canada with effect 18-1-19. 3 Officers Taken on Strength from Nova Scotia Regimental Depot.

### 30 January 1919 — South Camp, Ripon, Yorks.

> Weather fair. Training carried on as usual. Routine Orders 165 to 172. Part II D.O.24. 1 Officer Struck off Strength on transfer to C.E.F. in Canada. 32 Other Ranks Struck off Strength to Canadian Army Medical Corps Reserve & Training Depot on transfer, having proceeded to Granville Special Hospital, Buxton, for training. 2 Other Ranks Struck off Strength to Nova Scotia Regimental Depot. 5 Other Ranks Taken on Strength from Nova Scotia Regimental Depot.

### 31 January 1919 — South Camp, Ripon, Yorks.

> Weather fine and cold. Training carried on as usual. Route March held in afternoon. Routine Orders 173 to 177. Part II D.O.25. 21 Other Ranks Taken on Strength from Nova Scotia Regimental Depot. 1 Other Rank Struck off Strength to Nova Scotia Regimental Depot. 3 Other Ranks Struck off Strength on transfer to Canrecords, London. Strength of Unit in accordance with Headquarters, Canadians, Routine Order 939, dated 29-3-19. Officers 71. Other Ranks 2170.

Signed at foot of page: [uncertain — signature illegible] LT.-COL., COMDG. 17th Res. BATTALION O.I.
