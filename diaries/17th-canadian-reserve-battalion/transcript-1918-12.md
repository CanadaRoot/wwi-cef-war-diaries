## Transcription conventions
- Faithful to the original: spelling, punctuation, abbreviations and capitalisation as written.
- `[uncertain]` marks a word or name that could not be read with confidence — never guessed.
- The diary-text pages are Army Form C. 2118, "WAR DIARY or INTELLIGENCE SUMMARY", typewritten in columns: Place | Date | Hour | Summary of Events and Information | Remarks and references to Appendices. The "Hour" column is blank throughout this volume.
- The "Remarks and references to Appendices" column carries the same handwritten word against each day's entry, [uncertain] (appears to read "certified"); it is not repeated entry by entry below.
- "T.O.S." = Taken on Strength; "S.O.S." = Struck off Strength; "N.S.R.D." = Nova Scotia Regimental Depot; "C.T.S." = Canadian Training School; "C.C.D." = Canadian Command Depot.
- The December diary sheets carry no printed page numbers.

## Page 249 — Diary text, 1–7 December 1918 (December 1918, p. 3)

Army Form C. 2118. Heading: "WAR DIARY or INTELLIGENCE SUMMARY. (Erase heading not required.)" The printed heading "INTELLIGENCE/SUMMARY/" is struck through, with "or" written above it. ### Sunday 1 December 1918 — Bramshott

> Weather fair with showers. Divine Service held as usual.

### Monday 2 December 1918 — Bramshott

> Weather very foggy and rain at night. Training carried on as usual. 99 Other Ranks proceeded to Kinmel Park for embarkation to Canada. Routine Orders 3102 to 3110. Pt.II D.O's 285. 117 Other Ranks T.O.S. from N.S.R.D. 4 O.Ranks T.O.S. from C.T.S., Bexhill. 1 Officer SOS to NSRD.

### Tuesday 3 December 1918 — Bramshott

> Weather fair and clean. Training carried on as usual. 76 Other Ranks proceeded to Kinmel Park, Rhyl, for return to Canada. Routine Orders 3111 to 3114. Pt.II D.O's 286. 1 Other Rank Taken on Strength from N.S.R.D. 13 Other Ranks (lowered in category) Struck off Strength to N.S.R.D. 2 Officers Struck off Strength to N.S.R.D.

### Wednesday 4 December 1918 — Bramshott

> Weather fair with showers. Training carried on as usual. Routine Orders 3115 to 3121. Part II Daily Order 287. 25 Other Ranks (Category "A") Taken on Strength from N.S.R.D. 1 Other Rank Taken on Strength from C.T.S., Bexhill. 2 Other Ranks Struck off Strength on transfer to C.A.P.C., London.

### Thursday 5 December 1918 — Bramshott

> Weather fair—showers at night. Training carried on as usual. In afternoon, ceremonial parade carried out on 15th. Reserve Battn. parade ground. Routine Orders 3122 to 3127. Part II Daily Orders 288. 6 Other Ranks Taken on Strength from N.S.R.D. 1 Other Rank Taken on Strength from C.T.S., Bexhill. 38 Other Ranks (Category "A") reporting from 2nd. C.C.D. Taken on Strength from N.S.R.D. 2 Other Ranks (Raised in Category) Taken on Strength from N.S.R.D. 2 Officers Taken on Strength from N.S.R.D.

### Friday 6 December 1918 — Bramshott

> Weather foggy and damp. Training for day cancelled. Ceremonial Parade and March Past held on 15th. Reserve Battn. parade ground in honour of visit by Rt. Hon. Sir R.L. Borden, K.C.M.G. (Premier of Canada) and Sir R.B.W. Turner, V.C., K.C.M.G., D.S.O., (G.O.C. Canadians in British Isles.) Investiture held at 1100. Routine Orders 3128 to 3138. Part II Daily Order 289. 40 Other Ranks (Category "A") Taken on Strength from N.S.R.D., and shown "On Command" at Kinmel Park, Rhyl, for return to Canada.

### Saturday 7 December 1918 — Bramshott

> Weather fine and clear. 48 Other Ranks (Category "A" Casualties) proceeded at 0500 for Southampton, for return to Canada. Training carried on as usual. Routine Orders 3139 to 3145. Part II Daily Order 290. 19 Other Ranks Taken on Strength from N.S.R.D. 48 Other Ranks Struck off Strength on transfer to C.E.F. in Canada, 1 Other Rank Str.Off Str. to NSRD.
