## Page 273 — Diary text, 1–7 April 1919 (April 1919, Sheet 1)

Army Form C. 2118. Heading: "SHEET 1. / WAR DIARY / or / INTELLIGENCE/SUMMARY/ / 17th Canadian Reserve Battalion, (Nova Scotia Regiment)."

### 1 April 1919 — Block 28, South Camp, Ripon, Yorkshire.

> Weather — fair. Training carried on as usual. Routine Orders 466 to 468. Pt.II.D.O.76. Three Other Ranks Taken on Strength from Nova Scotia Regimental Depot. One Other Rank Struck off Strength as deserter.

### 2 April 1919 — Block 28, South Camp, Ripon, Yorkshire.

> Weather — fine. Training carried on as usual. Routine Orders 469 to 476. Pt.II.D.O.77. Eleven Other Ranks Taken on Strength from Nova Scotia Regimental Depot. Two Other Ranks Struck off Strength to Permanent Cadre of M.D.C.Wing 2, Kinmel Park.

### 3 April 1919 — Block 28, South Camp, Ripon, Yorkshire.

> Weather — fine and warm. Training carried on as usual. Routine Orders 477 to 484. Pt.II.D.O.78. Seven Other Ranks Taken on Strength from Nova Scotia Regimental Depot. Twenty-five Other Ranks Struck off Strength to M.D.C. Concentration Wings, Kinmel Park, for return to Canada. Three Officers Taken on Strength from Nova Scotia Regimental Depot.

### 4 April 1919 — Block 28, South Camp, Ripon, Yorkshire.

> Weather — fine. Training carried on as usual. All available men attended C.O's. Parade 1400 this date. Routine Orders 485 to 488. Part II D.O.79. Five Other Ranks Taken on Strength from Nova Scotia Regimental Depot. One Cadet Struck off Strength having proceeded to M.D.C.Wing 6, Canada. One Officer Taken on Strength from Nova Scotia Regimental Depot.

### 5 April 1919 — Block 28, South Camp, Ripon, Yorkshire.

> Weather — fair. Training carried on as usual for half-day. Routine Orders 489 to 493. Part II D.O.80. Two Other Ranks Taken on Strength from Nova Scotia Regimental Depot. Forty-seven Other Ranks Struck off Strength to M.D.C. Concentration Wings, Kinmel Park, for return to Canada. One Officer Struck off Strength on retirement from Overseas Military Forces of Canada in British Isles.

### 6 April 1919 — Block 28, South Camp, Ripon, Yorkshire.

> Weather — fine and warm. Divine Services held as usual this date.

### 7 April 1919 — Block 28, South Camp, Ripon, Yorkshire.

> Weather — fair. Training carried on as usual. Routine Orders 494 to 498. Part II.D.O.81. Six Other Ranks Taken on Strength from Nova Scotia Regimental Depot. Two Other Ranks Struck off Strength to Canadian Army Dental Corps, Ripon.

## Page 274 — Diary text, 8–13 April 1919 (April 1919, Sheet 2)

### 8 April 1919 — Block 28, South Camp, Ripon, Yorkshire.

> Weather — fine and warm. Training carried on as usual. Routine Orders 499 to 503. Part II D.O.82. One Other Rank Taken on Strength from Nova Scotia Regimental Depot.

### 9 April 1919 — Block 28, South Camp, Ripon, Yorkshire.

> Weather — fine and warm. Training carried on as usual. Team from this Unit won Cross-Country Race held by Canadian Units of Ripon Division, this date. Routine Orders 504 to 508. Part II D.O.83. Five Other Ranks Taken on Strength from Nova Scotia Regimental Depot. One Other Rank Struck off Strength to Nova Scotia Regimental Depot. Fifty-one Other Ranks Struck off Strength to M.D.C.Concentration Wings, Kinmel Park, for return to Canada.

### 10 April 1919 — Block 28, South Camp, Ripon, Yorkshire.

> Weather — fine and warm. Training carried on as usual. Routine Orders 509 to 514. Part II D.O.84. Two Other Ranks Taken on Strength from Nova Scotia Regimental Depot. Two Other Ranks Taken on Strength from M.D.C.Concentration Wings, Kinmel Park. Two Other Ranks Struck off Strength to Nova Scotia Regimental Depot. One Officer Taken on Strength from Nova Scotia Regimental Depot. One Officer Struck off Strength to Nova Scotia Regimental Depot, having been a patient in Ripon Camp Hospital since 12-3-19.

### 11 April 1919 — Block 28, South Camp, Ripon, Yorkshire.

> Weather — fine in the morning, rain in afternoon, clearing in the evening. Training carried on as usual. Routine Orders 515 to 518. Part II D.O.85. Four Other Ranks Taken on Strength from Nova Scotia Regimental Depot. Seven Other Ranks, having proceeded to Canadian Discharge Depot, Buxton, for return to Canada, Struck off Strength to Nova Scotia Regimental Depot.

### 12 April 1919 — Block 28, South Camp, Ripon, Yorkshire.

> Weather — fine. Training carried on as usual for half-day. Routine Orders 519 to 523. Part II D.O.86. Eight Other Ranks Taken on Strength from Nova Scotia Regimental Depot. Two Other Ranks Taken on Strength from M.D.C.Concentration Wings, Kinmel Park. One Other Rank Taken on Strength from Canadian Corps Camp, Bramshott. Forty Other Ranks Struck off Strength to M.D.C.Concentration Wings, Kinmel Park, for return to Canada.

### 13 April 1919 — Block 28, South Camp, Ripon, Yorkshire.

> Weather — fine and warm throughout the day. Rain at night. Divine Services held as usual this date.

## Page 275 — Diary text, 14–19 April 1919 (April 1919, Sheet 3)

### 14 April 1919 — Block 28, South Camp, Ripon, Yorkshire.

> Weather — Heavy rain all day. Battalion Muster Parade detailed for this date cancelled. Training suspended. Routine Orders 524 to 533. Part II.D.O.87. Five Other Ranks Taken on Strength from Nova Scotia Regimental Depot. One Other Rank Struck off Strength to Nova Scotia Regimental Depot.

### 15 April 1919 — Block 28, South Camp, Ripon, Yorkshire.

> Weather — slight showers throughout the day. Muster Parade postponed on Monday, 14-4-19, held this date. Training carried on as usual. Routine Orders 534 to 541. Part II D.O.88. One Other Rank Taken on Strength from Nova Scotia Regimental Depot. One Other Rank Taken on Strength on apprehension from desertion. Six Other Ranks Struck off Strength on discharge in the British Isles.

### 16 April 1919 — Block 28, South Camp, Ripon, Yorkshire.

> Weather — rain in the early morning, generally mild, but gusty and cloudy. Routine Orders 542 to 546. Battalion paraded to Palladium Theatre, Ripon, this date, to witness Film depicting the work being done by the Department of Soldiers Civil Re-Establishment in Canada. Part II D.O.89. Two Other Ranks Taken on Strength from Nova Scotia Regimental Depot. Thirty-four Other Ranks Struck off Strength to M.D.C. Concentration Wings, Kinmel Park, for return to Canada. Two Officers Taken on Strength from Nova Scotia Regimental Depot. One Officer Struck off Strength on retirement from Overseas Military Forces of Canada in British Isles.

### 17 April 1919 — Block 28, South Camp, Ripon, Yorkshire.

> Weather — dull and wet. Training carried on as usual. Routine Orders 547 to 551. Part II D.O.90. Four Other Ranks Taken on Strength from Nova Scotia Regimental Depot. One Other Rank Struck off Strength to Permanent Cadre, M.D.C.Wing 5, Kinmel Park. Two Officers Taken on Strength from Nova Scotia Regimental Depot.

### 18 April 1919 — Block 28, South Camp, Ripon, Yorkshire.

> "Good Friday". Holiday. Sunday routine carried out. No Battalion Orders issued this date. Weather — fine and warm.

### 19 April 1919 — Block 28, South Camp, Ripon, Yorkshire.

> Weather — dull throughout the day, fine in the evening. Sunday Routine carried out. Routine Orders 552 to 557. Part II D.O.91. One Other Rank Taken on Strength from Nova Scotia Regimental Depot. Twenty-three Other Ranks (Category "B") cease to be attached and Taken on Strength from Nova Scotia Regimental Depot. One Other Rank Struck off Strength as a deserter. Fifteen Other Ranks Struck off Strength to M.D.C. Concentration Wings, Kinmel Park, for return to Canada. One Officer Taken on Strength from Nova Scotia Regimental Depot.

## Page 276 — Diary text, 20–29 April 1919 (April 1919, Sheet 4)

### 20 April 1919 — Block 28, South Camp, Ripon, Yorkshire.

> "Easter Sunday". Weather — dull in morning, but fine for remainder of day. Special Divine Services held this date.

### 21 April 1919 — Block 28, South Camp, Ripon, Yorkshire.

> "Easter Monday". Holiday. Sunday Routine carried out. No Battalion Orders issued this date. Weather — very dull in morning, but fine for remainder of day.

### 22 April 1919 — Block 28, South Camp, Ripon, Yorkshire.

> Weather — rain during the day; fine in evening. Training carried on as usual. Routine Orders 558 to 561. Part II D.O.92. Six Other Ranks Taken on Strength from Nova Scotia Regimental Depot. Five Other Ranks Struck off Strength to 25th Canadian Infantry Battalion, Canadian Corps Camp, Witley.

### 23 April 1919 — Block 28, South Camp, Ripon, Yorkshire.

> Weather — very fine. Training carried on as usual. Routine Orders 562 to 565. Part II D.O.93. Fourteen Other Ranks Taken on Strength from Nova Scotia Regimental Depot. Seven Other Ranks Struck off Strength on discharge in the British Isles.

### 24 April 1919 — Block 28, South Camp, Ripon, Yorkshire.

> Weather — rain throughout the day. Training suspended. Routine Orders 566 to 569. Part II D.O.94. One Other Rank Taken on Strength from Nova Scotia Regimental Depot.

### 25 April 1919 — Block 28, South Camp, Ripon, Yorkshire.

> Weather — cloudy. Training carried on as usual. Routine Orders 570 to 576. Part II D.O.95. Three Other Ranks Taken on Strength from Nova Scotia Regimental Depot. One Officer Taken on Strength from Nova Scotia Regimental Depot.

### 26 April 1919 — Block 28, South Camp, Ripon, Yorkshire.

> Weather — very wet, snowing occasionally. Training suspended. Routine Orders 577 to 588. Part II D.O.96. Nine Other Ranks Taken on Strength from Nova Scotia Regimental Depot. Forty-five Other Ranks Struck off Strength to M.D.C.Concentration Wings, Kinmel Park.

### 27 April 1919 — Block 28, South Camp, Ripon, Yorkshire.

> Weather — snow and rain throughout the day. Divine Services held as usual. This Unit assumed Divisional Duties for week commencing Reveille, this date.

### 28 April 1919 — Block 28, South Camp, Ripon, Yorkshire.

> Weather — rain throughout the day. Training suspended. Routine Orders 583 to 588. Part II D.O.97. Two Other Ranks Taken on Strength from Nova Scotia Regimental Depot.

[Note: the Routine Orders on 26 April are given as "577 to 588" and on 28 April as "583 to 588" — the ranges overlap as written in the original.]

## Page 277 — Diary text, 29–30 April 1919 (April 1919, Sheet 5)

### 29 April 1919 — Block 28, South Camp, Ripon, Yorkshire.

> Weather — fine and warmer. Training carried on as usual. Routine Orders 589 to 595. Part II D.O.98. Six Other Ranks Taken on Strength from Nova Scotia Regimental Depot. One Other Rank Struck off Strength on transfer to Canadian Army Dental Corps, Ripon. One Other Rank Struck off Strength as a deserter.

### 30 April 1919 — Block 28, South Camp, Ripon, Yorkshire.

> Weather — rather dull throughout the day, but fine in evening. Training carried on as usual. Routine Order 596. Part II D.O.99. Seven Other Ranks Taken on Strength from Nova Scotia Regimental Depot. One Other Rank Taken on Strength from M.D.C.Wing 6, Kinmel Park. One Other Rank Struck off Strength on discharge to Canada from Hospital. One Other Rank Struck off Strength to Nova Scotia Regimental Depot. Two Officers Taken on Strength from Nova Scotia Regimental Depot. Strength of this Unit as at 30th April, 1919, in accordance with Headquarters, Canadians, Routine Order 939, dated 29-3-17:— Officers — 68. Other Ranks — 1715.

[Note: the Routine Order date is given as "29-3-17" as written in the original; the January, February and March strength statements cite the same order dated 29-3-19.]
