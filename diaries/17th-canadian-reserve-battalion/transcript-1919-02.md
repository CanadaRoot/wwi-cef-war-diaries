## Page 260 — Diary text, 1–6 February 1919 (February 1919, p. 1)

Army Form C. 2118. Heading: "17TH CANADIAN RESERVE BATTALION. / WAR DIARY / or / INTELLIGENCE/SUMMARY (FEBRUARY, 1919) (PAGE 1)."

### 1 February 1919 — South Camp, Ripon, Yorkshire.

> Weather — Fair and Cold. Training carried on as usual for the half day. Routine Orders 178 to 184. Part II Daily Order 26. One Other Rank taken on strength from Nova Scotia Regimental Depot. Five Other Ranks cease to be shown on command to Rhyl, and are struck off strength to C.E.F. in Canada. Twenty-three Other Ranks struck off strength to Concentration Wings, Kinmel Park, Rhyl, for return to Canada. One Other Rank struck off strength to Nova Scotia Regimental Depot. Two Officers taken on strength from Nova Scotia Regimental Depot.

### 2 February 1919 — South Camp, Ripon, Yorkshire.

> Weather — Fair and Mild. This Unit commenced Divisional Duties at Reveille this date. Divine Services held as usual.

### 3 February 1919 — South Camp, Ripon, Yorkshire.

> Weather — Fine and Mild. Training carried on as usual. Routine Orders 185 to 190. Part II Daily Order 27. Twelve Other Ranks taken on strength from Nova Scotia Regimental Depot. One Other Rank struck off strength to C.E.F. in Canada. One Other Rank struck off strength to Nova Scotia Regimental Depot. One Officer taken on strength from Nova Scotia Regimental Depot. One Officer struck off strength to 25th Battalion in the Field, having been returned to stand trial by F.G.C.M. One Officer struck off strength to Nova Scotia Regimental Depot.

### 4 February 1919 — South Camp, Ripon, Yorkshire.

> Weather — fine with local snow flurries. Training carried on as usual. Rear Party, (2 Officers; 81 Other Ranks) reported from Bramshott Camp this date. Routine Orders 191 to 197. Part II Daily Order 28. Ten Other Ranks taken on strength from Nova Scotia Regimental Depot. 2 Officers taken on strength from Nova Scotia Regimental Depot. Ten Officers struck off strength to C.E.F. in Canada.

### 5 February 1919 — South Camp, Ripon, Yorkshire.

> Weather — Fair and Mild. Training carried on as usual. Routine Orders 198 to 210. Part II Daily Order 29. Twenty-one Other Ranks taken on strength from Nova Scotia Regimental Depot. One Officer taken on strength from Nova Scotia Regimental Depot.

### 6 February 1919 — South Camp, Ripon, Yorkshire.

> Weather — Fine and Mild. Training carried on as usual. Routine Orders 211 to 219. Part II Daily Order 30. One Officer taken on strength from N.S.R.D. One Other Rank struck off strength on transfer to Headquarters, Canadians, Ripon. Two Other Ranks struck off strength to Nova Scotia Regimental Depot. Four Other Ranks struck off strength to C.E.F. in Canada. One Other Rank struck off strength to C.O.C., Bramshott. Fifteen Other Ranks taken on strength from Nova Scotia Regimental Depot.

## Page 261 — Diary text, 7–13 February 1919 (February 1919, p. 2)

### 7 February 1919 — South Camp, Ripon, Yorkshire.

> Weather — Fine and Cold. Training carried on as usual. Routine Orders 220 to 224. Part II Daily Order 31. Five Other Ranks taken on strength from Nova Scotia Regimental Depot. One Other Rank struck off strength to Nova Scotia Regimental Depot. Twenty-five Other Ranks struck off strength to Concentration Wings, Kinmel Park.

### 8 February 1919 — South Camp, Ripon, Yorkshire.

> Weather — Fine and very cold. Training carried on as usual. Routine Orders 225 to 231. Part II Daily Order 32. Two Officers taken on strength from Nova Scotia Regimental Depot. Six Other Ranks taken on strength from Nova Scotia Regimental Depot. Thirteen Other Ranks struck off strength to C.E.F. in Canada. Two Other Ranks struck off strength as "Deserters." One Other Rank struck off strength, having died in No. 12 Can. General Hospital.

### 9 February 1919 — South Camp, Ripon, Yorkshire.

> Weather — Fine and very cold. Divine Services held as usual. Divisional Duties ceased at Reveille this date.

### 10 February 1919 — South Camp, Ripon, Yorkshire.

> Weather — Fine and very cold. Training carried on as usual. Routine Orders 232 to 238. Part II Daily Order 33. Thirteen Other Ranks taken on strength from Nova Scotia Regimental Depot. One Officer taken on strength from Nova Scotia Regimental Depot.

### 11 February 1919 — South Camp, Ripon, Yorkshire.

> Weather — Fair and Mild. Training carried on as usual. Routine Orders 239 to 246. Part II Daily Order 34. One Other Rank taken on strength from Canadian Army Gymnastic Staff. Six Other Ranks, (Cadets), struck off strength on transfer to C.E.F. in Canada. One Officer struck off strength to Nova Scotia Regimental Depot.

### 12 February 1919 — South Camp, Ripon, Yorkshire.

> Weather — Fine and Cold. Training carried on as usual. Sports held in afternoon. Routine Orders 247 to 254. Part II Daily Order 35. Three Other Ranks taken on strength from Nova Scotia Regimental Depot. Two Other Ranks taken on strength from desertion. Fifty-nine Other Ranks struck off strength to Concentration Wings, Kinmel Park.

### 13 February 1919 — South Camp, Ripon, Yorkshire.

> Weather — Fine and Cold. Training carried on as usual. Routine Orders 255 to 260. Part II Daily Order 36. Thirty-four Other Ranks taken on strength from Nova Scotia Regimental Depot. Thirty Other Ranks struck off strength on transfer to C.E.F. in Canada. Five Other Ranks struck off strength to Canadian Postal Corps, London. Four Officers taken on strength from Nova Scotia Regimental Depot.

## Page 262 — Diary text, 14–20 February 1919 (February 1919, p. 3)

### 14 February 1919 — South Camp, Ripon, Yorkshire.

> Weather fair and cold. Training cancelled for the day to allow for the compilation of Questionnaires by all Other Ranks. Routine Orders 261 to 263. Part II Daily Orders 37. 3 Other Ranks Taken on Strength from Nova Scotia Regimental Depot. 40 Other Ranks Struck off Strength to Concentration Wings, Kinmel Park. 1 Officer Taken on Strength from Nova Scotia Regimental Depot. 1 Officer Struck off Strength to Nova Scotia Regimental Depot.

### 15 February 1919 — South Camp, Ripon, Yorkshire.

> Weather fair and mild, showers at night. Training carried on as usual for half day. Routine Orders 264 to 266. Part II Daily Orders 38. 2 Other Ranks Taken on Strength from Nova Scotia Regimental Depot.

### 16 February 1919 — South Camp, Ripon, Yorkshire.

> Weather fair and mild with showers. Divine Services not held today owing to Influenza.

### 17 February 1919 — South Camp, Ripon, Yorkshire.

> Weather fair and mild, local showers. Muster Parade of Unit held at 0900 this date. Band returned from Royal Academy, Piccadilly. Training carried on as usual. Routine Orders 269 to 276. Part II Daily Orders 39. 6 Other Ranks Taken on Strength from Nova Scotia Regimental Depot. 1 Other Rank Taken on Strength from Military District 6, Concentration Wing, Kinmel. 8 Other Ranks Struck off Strength to C.E.F. in Canada. 6 Other Ranks Struck off Strength to Nova Scotia Regimental Depot. 1 Officer Taken on Strength from Nova Scotia Regimental Depot.

### 18 February 1919 — South Camp, Ripon, Yorkshire.

> Weather fair and cold. Training carried on as usual. Routine Orders 277 to 284. Part II Daily Orders 40. 3 Other Ranks Taken on Strength from Nova Scotia Regimental Depot.

### 19 February 1919 — South Camp, Ripon, Yorkshire.

> Weather fair and mild. Training carried on as usual for half day. Organised sports carried on in afternoon. Routine Orders 285 to 290. Part II Daily Orders 41. 57 Other Ranks Struck off Strength on transfer to C.E.F. in Canada.

### 20 February 1919 — South Camp, Ripon, Yorkshire.

> Weather mild and showery. Training carried on as usual. Routine Orders 291 to 294. Part II Daily Orders 42. 20 Other Ranks Taken on Strength from Nova Scotia Regimental Depot. 1 Other Rank Struck off Strength on transfer to Royal Canadian Regiment, Concentration Camp, Bramshott. 1 Officer struck off strength to Royal Canadian Regiment.

## Page 263 — Diary text, 21–27 February 1919 (February 1919, p. 4)

### 21 February 1919 — South Camp, Ripon, Yorkshire.

> Weather fair and very mild, showers at night. Training carried on as usual. Routine Orders 295 to 299. Part II Daily Orders 43. 7 Other Ranks Taken on Strength from Nova Scotia Regimental Depot. 3 Other Ranks Struck off Strength on transfer to C.E.F. in Canada. 6 Other Ranks Struck off Strength to Canrecords, London. 1 Other Ranks Struck off Strength to Nova Scotia Regimental Depot.

### 22 February 1919 — South Camp, Ripon, Yorkshire.

> Weather fair and mild. Training carried on as usual for half day. Routine Orders 300 to 307. Part II Daily Orders 44. 15 Other Ranks Taken on Strength from Nova Scotia Regimental Depot. 77 Other Ranks Struck off Strength to Concentration Camps, Kinmel Park.

### 23 February 1919 — South Camp, Ripon, Yorkshire.

> Weather fine and mild. No Divine Services held this date owing to the prevalence of Influenza.

### 24 February 1919 — South Camp, Ripon, Yorkshire.

> Weather fine and mild. Training carried on as usual. Routine Orders 308 to 312. Part II Daily Orders 45. 2 Officer Taken on Strength from Nova Scotia Regimental Depot. 14 Other Ranks Taken on Strength from Nova Scotia Regimental Depot. 1 Other Rank Struck off Strength to Nova Scotia Regimental Depot. 9 Other Ranks Struck off Strength on transfer to C.E.F. in Canada.

### 25 February 1919 — South Camp, Ripon, Yorkshire.

> Weather fine and mild. All Parades cancelled owing to Influenza. Routine Orders 313 to 317. Part II Daily Orders 46. 11 Other Ranks Taken on Strength from Nova Scotia Regimental Depot. 1 Other Ranks Struck off Strength to Nova Scotia Regimental Depot.

### 26 February 1919 — South Camp, Ripon, Yorkshire.

> Weather fair and mild. No training owing to Influenza. Routine Orders 318 to 322. Part II Daily Orders 47. 6 Other Ranks Taken on Strength from Nova Scotia Regimental Depot. 1 Other Rank Struck off Strength to Nova Scotia Regimental Depot.

### 27 February 1919 — South Camp, Ripon, Yorkshire.

> Weather fair and mild. No training. Routine Orders 323 to 330. Part II Daily Orders 48. 43 Other Ranks Taken on Strength from Nova Scotia Regimental Depot. 1 Officer Struck off Strength to Nova Scotia Regimental Depot.

## Page 264 — Diary text, 28 February 1919 (February 1919, p. 5)

### 28 February 1919 — South Camp, Ripon, Yorkshire.

> Weather very mild all day. No Training. Routine Orders 331 to 335. Part II Daily Orders 49. 2 Other Ranks Taken on Strength from Nova Scotia Regimental Depot. 7 Other Ranks Struck off Strength on transfer to C.E.F. in Canada. 3 Other Ranks Struck off Strength to Nova Scotia Regimental Depot. 40 Other Ranks Struck off Strength to Military District Concentration Wings, Kinmel Park. 2 Officers Struck off Strength on proceeding Overseas to 85th Battalion.
>
> Strength of Unit in accordance with Headquarters, Canadians, Routine Order No.939, dated 29-3-19. Officers 80. Other Ranks 1982.
