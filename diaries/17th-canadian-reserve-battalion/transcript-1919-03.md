## Page 266 — Diary text, 1–6 March 1919 (March 1919, p. 1)

Army Form C. 2118. Heading: "WAR DIARY / or / INTELLIGENCE/SUMMARY/ (MARCH, 1919) (Page 1)."

### 1 March 1919 — South Camp, Ripon, Yorkshire.

> Weather — Fair and Mild. Training cancelled owing to Influenza. Routine Orders 336 to 341. Part II D.O.No.50. Three Other Ranks Taken on strength from Nova Scotia Regimental Depot. One Other Rank struck off strength on transfer to C.E.F. in Canada. One Other Rank struck off strength to Nova Scotia Regimental Depot. One Other Rank struck off strength on transfer to Canadian Record Office, London.

### 2 March 1919 — South Camp, Ripon, Yorkshire.

> Weather — Fair and Mild. Voluntary Divine Services held this date.

### 3 March 1919 — South Camp, Ripon, Yorkshire.

> Weather — Very mild. Training cancelled owing to Influenza. Routine Orders 342 to 348. Part II D.O.No. 51. Four Other Ranks taken on strength from N.S.R.D. One Other Rank struck off strength on transfer to C.E.F. in Canada. One Other Rank struck off strength on transfer to Concentration Wing, Kinmel Park. One Other Rank struck off strength, having died in Ripon Camp Military Hospital. One Officer taken on strength from N.S.R.D. One Officer struck off strength to Nova Scotia Regimental Depot.

### 4 March 1919 — South Camp, Ripon, Yorkshire.

> Weather — Fair. Training cancelled owing to Influenza. Pipe Band proceeded Overseas this date to 25th Battalion. Routine Orders 349 to 351. Part II.D.O.No.52. Two Other Ranks taken on strength from Nova Scotia Regimental Depot. One Other Rank taken on strength from Canadian Record Office, London. Twenty Other Ranks (Pipe Band) struck off strength on proceeding Overseas to 25th Battalion. Two Officers taken on strength from N.S.R.D.

### 5 March 1919 — South Camp, Ripon, Yorkshire.

> Weather — Fine and Mild. Training cancelled. Routine Orders 352 to 354. Part II.D.O.No.53. Three Other Ranks taken on strength from Nova Scotia Regimental Depot. Three Other Ranks struck off strength to Kinmel Park. One Other Rank struck off strength to C.E.F.in Canada. One Other Rank struck off strength to Nova Scotia Regimental Depot. One Officer struck off strength to Nova Scotia Regimental Depot.

### 6 March 1919 — South Camp, Ripon, Yorkshire.

> Weather — Fine; showers at night. Training cancelled. Routine Orders 355 to 358. Part II.D.O.No.54. Twelve Other Ranks taken on strength from Nova Scotia Regimental Depot. One Other Rank taken on strength from "G" Wing, Canadian Corps Camp, Bramshott. Three Other Ranks struck off strength to Nova Scotia Regimental Depot. One Other Rank struck off strength, having died in Ripon Camp Hospital. Two Officers taken on strength from Nova Scotia Regimental Depot. Seven Officers struck off strength on transfer to C.E.F. in Canada. (Cont'd.)

## Page 267 — Diary text, 7–14 March 1919 (March 1919, p. 2)

### 7 March 1919 — South Camp, Ripon, Yorkshire.

> Weather — Fair & Mild. No Training. Routine Orders 359 to 364. Part II.D.O.No.55. Fourteen Other Ranks taken on strength from Nova Scotia Regimental Depot. Two Officers taken on strength from Nova Scotia Regimental Depot.

### 8 March 1919 — South Camp, Ripon, Yorkshire.

> Weather — Fair & Mild. No training. Routine Orders 365 to 370. Part II.D.O.No.56. Four Other Ranks taken on strength from Nova Scotia Regimental Depot. Seventy-seven Other Ranks struck off strength to Kinmel Park. Two Other Ranks struck off strength to Nova Scotia Regimental Depot.

### 9 March 1919 — South Camp, Ripon, Yorkshire.

> Weather — Mild with local showers in morning. No Divine Services held this date.

### 10 March 1919 — South Camp, Ripon, Yorkshire.

> Weather — Fair with showers. New N.C.O's Class started this date. Routine Orders 371 to 372. Part II.D.O.No.57. One Officer struck off strength to C.E.F.in Canada. Five Other Ranks taken on strength from Nova Scotia Regimental Depot. One Other Rank struck off strength to Hqrs., O.M.F.of C., London. One Other Rank struck off strength to Nova Scotia Regimental Depot.

### 11 March 1919 — South Camp, Ripon, Yorkshire.

> Weather — Fair in morning; rain at night. Routine Orders 373 to 379. Part II.D.O.No.58. Three Other Ranks taken on strength from Nova Scotia Regimental Depot. One Other Rank struck off strength to No.6 Det., C.A.P.C., Seaford.

### 12 March 1919 — South Camp, Ripon, Yorkshire.

> Weather — Rain; clearing at night. Routine Orders 380 to 382. Part II.D.O.No.59. Six Other Ranks taken on strength from Nova Scotia Regimental Dept.

### 13 March 1919 — South Camp, Ripon, Yorkshire.

> Weather — Fair. Routine Orders 383 to 387. Part II.D.O.No.60. Eight Other Ranks taken on strength from Nova Scotia Regimental Depot. One Other Rank struck off strength to Nova Scotia Regimental Depot. Two Other Ranks struck off strength to C.E.F.in Canada. One Other Rank struck off strength to Canadian Corps Camp, Bramshott. One Officer taken on strength from Nova Scotia Regimental Depot.

### 14 March 1919 — South Camp, Ripon, Yorkshire.

> Weather — Fine and warm. Routine Orders 388 to 393. Part II.D.O.No.61. One Officer struck off strength, having proceeded Overseas to 85th Battalion. Eight Officers struck off strength to Concentration Wings, Kinmel Park. Fifteen Other Ranks taken on strength from Nova Scotia Regimental Depot. One Other Rank (absentee from France) taken on strength from R.C.R. One Other Rank struck off strength as a deserter.

## Page 268 — Diary text, 15–21 March 1919 (March 1919, p. 3)

### 15 March 1919 — South Camp, Ripon, Yorkshire.

> Weather — Fair and Mild. Routine Orders 394 to 399. Part II.D.O.No.62. Twenty-five Other Ranks Taken on strength from Nova Scotia Regimental Depot. One Other Rank taken on strength from M.D.C.Wing 6 Kinmel Park. Fifty-four Other Ranks struck off strength to M.D.C.Wings, Kinmel Park, for return to Canada.

### 16 March 1919 — South Camp, Ripon, Yorkshire.

> Weather — Very fine and warm. This Unit took over Divisional Duties from Reveille this date.

### 17 March 1919 — South Camp, Ripon, Yorkshire.

> Weather — Fine and warm. Routine Orders 400 to 404. Part II.D.O.No.63. Eighteen Other Ranks taken on strength from Nova Scotia Regimental Depot. Two Other Ranks struck off strength on transfer to C.E.F.in Canada.

### 18 March 1919 — South Camp, Ripon, Yorkshire.

> Weather — Fine; showers at night. Training was resumed this date. Routine Orders 405 to 407. Part II.D.O.No.64. Two Other Ranks Taken on strength from Nova Scotia Regimental Depot. One Officer struck off strength to 85th Battalion Overseas.

### 19 March 1919 — South Camp, Ripon, Yorkshire.

> Weather — Rain and snow all day, and very cold. Training cancelled owing to the inclemency of weather. Routine Order 408 to 412. Part II.D.O.No.65. Three Other Ranks taken on strength from Nova Scotia Regimental Depot. One Other Rank taken on strength from desertion. One Other Rank struck off strength on transfer to C.E.F.in Canada. Forty-eight Other Ranks struck off strength to M.D.C.Wings, Kinmel Park, for return to Canada. One Officer struck off strength to Nova Scotia Regimental Depot.

### 20 March 1919 — South Camp, Ripon, Yorkshire.

> Weather — Clear and Cold. Training carried on as usual. Routine Orders 413 to 417. Part II D.O.No.66. Nine Other Ranks taken on strength from Nova Scotia Regimental Depot.

### 21 March 1919 — South Camp, Ripon, Yorkshire.

> Weather — Fair and Mild. Training carried on as usual. Routine Orders 418 to 420. Part II D.O.No.67. One Officer struck off strength to Nova Scotia Regimental Depot. Five Other Ranks taken on strength from Nova Scotia Regimental Depot. One Other Rank taken on strength from Canadian Corps Camp, Bramshott. One Other Rank (Absentee) taken on strength from R.C.R. One Other Rank struck off strength on transfer to C.E.F.in Canada. Three Other Ranks struck off strength to Nova Scotia Regimental Depot, for invaliding to Canada.

## Page 269 — Diary text, 22–28 March 1919 (March 1919, p. 4)

### 22 March 1919 — South Camp, Ripon, Yorkshire.

> Weather — Fine and Warm. Training carried on as usual for half day. Routine Orders 421 to 426. Part II.D.O.No.68. Seven Other Ranks taken on strength from Nova Scotia Regimental Depot. One Other Rank taken on strength from M.D.C.Wing 3, Kinmel Park. One Other Rank struck off strength to Nova Scotia Regimental Depot. One Other Rank struck off strength to M.D.C.Wing 5, Kinmel Park.

> Weather — Fair and Mild. Divine Services held as usual. Tour of duty as Duty Battalion ceased at Reveille this date.

> Weather — Fine and warm. Muster Parade held at 0900 this date. Training carried on as usual. Routine Orders 427 to 430. Part II.D.O.No.69. Twenty-one Other Ranks [a small "x" is typed above the word "taken"] taken on strength from Nova Scotia Regimental Depot. One Other Rank taken on strength from Canadian Corps Camp, Bramshott. Seven Other Ranks struck off strength to Kinmel Park for return to Canada. Six Other Ranks struck off strength on transfer to C.E.F.in Canada. One Other Rank struck off strength to H.Q.S.S., Canadian Troops, Ripon. Two Other Ranks struck off strength to Nova Scotia Regimental Depot.

### 25 March 1919 — South Camp, Ripon, Yorkshire.

> Weather — Fine and Warm. Routine Orders 431 to 434. Part II.D.O.No.70. Nine Other Ranks taken on strength from Nova Scotia Regimental Depot. Two Officers taken on strength from Nova Scotia Regimental Depot. Four Officers struck off strength to 85th Battalion Overseas.

### 26 March 1919 — South Camp, Ripon, Yorkshire.

> Weather — Fine and warm. Training carried on as usual. Routine Orders 435 to 440. Part II D.O.No.71. Six Other Ranks taken on strength from Nova Scotia Regimental Depot. One Officer taken on strength from Nova Scotia Regimental Depot.

### 27 March 1919 — South Camp, Ripon, Yorkshire.

> Weather — Fine and warm. Training carried on as usual. Routine Orders 441 to 446. Part II D.O.No.72. Eleven Other Ranks taken on strength from Nova Scotia Regimental Depot. One Other Rank struck off strength on transfer to C.E.F.in Canada.

### 28 March 1919 — South Camp, Ripon, Yorkshire.

> Weather — Fine and warm. Training carried on as usual. Routine Orders 447 to 454. Part II D.O.No.73. Six Other Ranks taken on strength from Nova Scotia Regimental Depot. One Officer taken on strength from Nova Scotia Regimental Depot.

## Page 270 — Diary text, 29–31 March 1919 (March 1919, p. 5)

### 29 March 1919 — South Camp, Ripon, Yorkshire.

> Weather — Fine and Warm. Training carried on as usual. Routine Orders 455 to 459. Part II D.O.No.74. Four Other Ranks taken on strength from Nova Scotia Regimental Depot. Five Other Ranks struck off strength to Nova Scotia Regimental Depot, for return to Canada. One Other Rank struck off strength to Nova Scotia Regimental Depot. Six Officers struck off strength to M.D.C.Wings, Kinmel Park.

> Weather — Fair and Mild. Showers at night. Divine Services held as usual. "Summer Time" came into effect at 0200 this date.
