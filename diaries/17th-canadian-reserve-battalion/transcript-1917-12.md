# War Diary — 17th Canadian Reserve Battalion

## Source
- **Unit:** 17th Canadian Reserve Battalion (Nova Scotia) — formed 23 January 1917 at Bramshott by the amalgamation of the 17th, 193rd and 219th Battalions; part of the 5th Canadian Reserve Brigade, reinforcing the 85th and 185th Battalions
- **Archival reference:** RG 9 III-D-3, vol. 4951, file 479, part 2; microfilm reel T-10767
- **LAC Collection Search:** Item ID 2004685 — "War diaries - 17th Reserve Battalion", 293 pages
  - Record: https://recherche-collection-search.bac-lac.gc.ca/eng/Home/Record?app=fonandcol&IdNumber=2004685
- **Page images:** pages 133–139 of 293 in this file (`page-0133-01-12-1917_cover.jpg` … `page-0139-monthly_report.jpg`)
- **Commanding officer:** Major A. W. P. Weston (assumed command 9 November 1917, vice Lieut. Colonel W. H. Muirhead, on duty in London; signs the 31 December sheet and the efficiency report, dated Bramshott Camp, 4 January 1918)
- **Note on the record:** this LAC record also contains an unrelated 1914 diary of the 17th Battalion on pages 1–79; those pages are NOT transcribed here. This chunk covers only the Reserve Battalion diary, January–December 1917 (pages 80–139).

## Transcription conventions
- Faithful to the original: spelling, punctuation, abbreviations and capitalisation as written.
- `[uncertain]` marks a word or name that could not be read with confidence — never guessed.
- The diary-text pages are Army Form C. 2118, "WAR DIARY or INTELLIGENCE SUMMARY", typed in columns: Place | Date | Hour | Summary of Events and Information | Remarks and references to Appendices.
- From December the Date column also gives the day of the week ("1st. Sat.", "2nd. Sun.", etc.), and the typist writes "Part II Daily Order" (Roman numerals) instead of "Part 11 Orders"; almost every entry also notes the weather. Transcribed as typed.
- The "Hour" column is blank throughout this volume — no hours are recorded.
- The Place column gives "BRAMSHOTT. HANTS. ENGLAND." on the first entry of each sheet and is blank (ditto) thereafter.
- The Remarks column is initialled on each line (initials not legible — see per-page notes).
- No map coordinates (latitude/longitude) are given in the diary; places are recorded as written.
- "Part II Daily Orders" and "Routine Orders" are cited by number only; the orders themselves are not in this record.

## Notes and gaps
- Strength: 59 officers and 1,052 other ranks at 30 November; 51 officers and 1,156 other ranks at 31 December.
- 12 December: the battalion subscribes £200 to Halifax Relief "for use of families of soldiers" — Officer's Mess £100, Sergeant's Mess £50, Men's Mess £50 — six days after the Halifax Explosion of 6 December 1917.
- 18 December: "Report re Election this date. Soldier's Vote not yet heard from." 19 December: "General Holiday all day for all troops. No training carried out." — the 1917 Canadian federal election (polling day 17 December 1917).
- 25 December: Christmas Day dinner in the Men's Mess at 12 noon inspected by Brigadier-General Meighen, C.M.G., and Mrs. Meighen, accompanied by Colonel J. A. Gunn, D.S.O., and Mrs. Gunn. 26 December: Boxing Day concert in the Men's Mess by local talent assisted by London ladies, attended by all officers, N.C.O.s and men.
- 24 December: 6 other ranks (Minors) struck off strength to the Young Soldier's Battalion — under-age soldiers removed from the draft pool. 80 other ranks proceed on Christmas leave 23 December, 38 more on 24 December.
- 14 December: divisional inspection and march past by Major-General W. S. Hodgins, C.M.G., at 2.15 p.m.; route march Portsmouth Road towards Liphook to Cold Ash Hill, Hewshott Hill House and return to camp.
- 18 December: inspection by General Sir Archibald Murray, G.C.O. [as typed], of Aldershot Command, accompanied by Sir Francis Howard, Inspector of Infantry, Aldershot Command.
- 5 December: inspection by Lieut. Colonel Montague, A.A. & Q.M.G., 2nd Div., France. 7 December: Major-General Hodgins' visit cancelled; the route march is cancelled owing to inclement weather.
- Deserters: 11 December 2 other ranks struck off as deserters; 13 December 1; 24 December 2. 24 December also records 1 other rank taken on strength "from Desertion" (a returned deserter).
- 17 December: 1 officer struck off strength "for absence without leave".
- Routine Order No. 4210 is not cited (14 December cites 4202–4209; 15 December cites 4211–4224).
- The efficiency report (page 139) is for much smaller drafts than November's (1 / 21 / 11 men); the R.C.R. figures are internally consistent (8 passed + 3 failed = 11 strength). The Corporals row shows a faint mark in the R.C.R. column that could not be read as a number — recorded as blank.
- From December the typist reverts to "Part II Daily Order" (Roman numerals) after using "Part 11 Orders" in October and November — transcribed as typed in each month.
- No individual names of other ranks are given anywhere on these sheets; only numbers and the units they join or leave.
