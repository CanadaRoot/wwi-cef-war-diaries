# War Diary — 17th Canadian Reserve Battalion (Nova Scotia)

## Source
- **Unit:** 17th Canadian Reserve Battalion (Nova Scotia)
- **Archival reference:** RG 9 III-D-3, vol. 4951, file 479, part 2; microfilm reel T-10767
- **LAC Collection Search:** Item ID 2004685 — "War diaries - 17th Reserve Battalion"
- **Page images:** pages 152–158 of the LAC record, saved alongside this file (`page-0152-...jpg` … `page-0158-...jpg`).
- **Commanding officer:** Lieut.-Colonel W. H. Muirhead (signs the month's diary 4 March 1918).
- **Place:** Bramshott, Hants., England, throughout the month.

## Transcription conventions
- Faithful to the original: spelling, punctuation, abbreviations and capitalisation as written.
- `[uncertain]` marks a word or name that could not be read with confidence — never guessed.
- The diary-text pages are Army Form C. 2118, "WAR DIARY or INTELLIGENCE SUMMARY", typewritten in columns: Place | Date | Hour | Summary of Events and Information | Remarks and references to Appendices.
- "do." in a column means "ditto" (same as the entry above).
- N.S.R.D. = Nova Scotia Regimental Depot; B.R.D. = [uncertain — as written]; O.R. = Other Rank(s).
- No map coordinates (latitude/longitude) are given in the diary; places are recorded as written.

## Notes and gaps
- No dates are missing from the February diary sheets: 1–28 February 1918 are all covered across the five sheets (pages 153–157).
- Operation Orders Nos. 27, 28, 29 and 30 are cited in the diary (route marches on 1, 8, 15 and 22 February) but are NOT filed with this month's pages — they are not in the page sequence 152–158, so they could not be transcribed here.
- One death is recorded: an unnamed Other Rank who died in Bramshott Military Hospital on 24 February 1918 (entry of 26 February; no name given in the diary).
- Notable event: on 5 February Mrs. Ellen Stevenson was presented at a special parade with the Military Medal won by her deceased son, Sergeant [uncertain] (No. 477109, M.M.), by Brigadier-General F. S. Meighen, D.S.O., C.M.G., G.O.C. Canadians, Bramshott.
- The 185th Battalion (Witley) was absorbed in large numbers during the month: 11 officers and 25 Other Ranks arrived 23 February, 1 officer and 88 Other Ranks on 24 February, and 1 officer and 82 Other Ranks taken on strength with effect 28 February.
- Drafts proceeding overseas in February: 2 officers and 50 Other Ranks to the 25th Battalion; 2 officers and 30 Other Ranks to the 165th Battalion (plus the 30-strong Royal Canadian Regiment draft in the efficiency report).
- The unit strength figure at 28 February is partly illegible: "72 Officers" is readable, but the Other Ranks figure could not be read — marked [uncertain].
