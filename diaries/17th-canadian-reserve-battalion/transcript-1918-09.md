# War Diary — 17th Canadian Reserve Battalion (Nova Scotia)

## Source
- **Unit:** 17th Canadian Reserve Battalion (Nova Scotia Regiment), Bramshott, Hants., England
- **Archival reference:** RG 9 III-D-3, vol. 4951, file 479, part 2; microfilm reel T-10767
- **LAC Collection Search:** Item ID 2004685 — "War diaries - 17th Reserve Battalion"
- **Page images:** pages 221–231 of the LAC record (September 1918 volume, Volume 19)
- **Commanding officer:** a Lieut-Colonel whose signature on the diary sheets and monthly report is illegible (see "Uncertain items" in the report). Major R. J. S. Langford was Field Officer for the unit's Duty Week beginning 22 September 1918.

## Transcription conventions
- Faithful to the original: spelling, punctuation, abbreviations and capitalisation as written, including the typist's errors (e.g. "On Officer" for "One Officer", "Retimental" for "Regimental", "19th Other Ranks" for "19 Other Ranks").
- `[uncertain]` marks a word, figure or name that could not be read with confidence — never guessed.
- The diary-text pages are Army Form C. 2118, "WAR DIARY or INTELLIGENCE SUMMARY", typewritten in columns: Place | Date | Hour | Summary of Events and Information | Remarks and references to Appendices. The "Hour" column is struck out on these sheets; no hours are recorded.
- The "INTELLIGENCE SUMMARY" alternative heading is struck out on each sheet.
- The Place column is only filled in on the first sheet of the month ("SEPTEMBER, 1916. BRAMSHOTT HANTS.. ENGLAND." — the year is typed "1916." in the original, evidently a clerical error for 1918); later sheets leave it blank.
- The Remarks column carries the same short illegible handwritten mark against each entry.
- A date printed at the foot of each diary sheet (e.g. "6th", "11th") is the first date of the following sheet.
- "N.S.R.D." = Nova Scotia Regimental Depot. "R.C.R." = Royal Canadian Regiment. "O.R." / "Other Ranks" as written.

## Page 222 — Diary text, 1–5 September 1918 (September 1918, Sheet 1)

Army Form C. 2118. Heading: "WAR DIARY or INTELLIGENCE SUMMARY. 17th Canadian Reserve Battalion." Place column: "SEPTEMBER, 1916. BRAMSHOTT HANTS.. ENGLAND." — the year is typed "1916." in the original, a clerical error for 1918.

### 1 September 1918 — Bramshott, Hants., England — Sun.

> Weather. Very cool with strong wind blowing. Divine Services held as usual in morning. 161 Other Ranks reported off and 145 Other Ranks proceeded on command (Range Parties) Mytchett Ranges this date.

### 2 September 1918 — Bramshott, Hants., England — Mon.

> Weather. Warmer. Training carried on as usual. Routine Orders 2332 to 2342. Part II Daily Orders 207. Two Other Ranks (Category "D1") struck off strength on posting to N.S.R.D. One Officer struck off strength on proceeding overseas to 25th Battalion, 1-9-18.

### 3 September 1918 — Bramshott, Hants., England — Tues.

> Weather. Very fine and warm. Training carried on as usual. Routine Orders 2343 to 2352. Part II Daily Orders 208. Two Other Ranks taken on strength on transfer from 13th Reserve Battalion (New Brunswick Regiment) with effect 30-8-18. On Officer taken on strength on posting from Nova Scotia Regimental Depot.

### 4 September 1918 — Bramshott, Hants., England — Wed.

> Weather. Very dull. Training as usual for half day. Organized Sports carried on in afternoon. General Staff Inspection of Reinforcements 269 held this date. Routine Orders 2353 to 2361. Part II Daily Orders 209. One Other Rank taken on strength on transfer from Canadian Army Service Corps, Shorncliffe. Two Other Ranks boarded for invaliding to Canada struck off strength on posting to Nova Scotia Regimental Depot. Two Category "D1" and one Category "B11" Other Ranks struck off strength on posting to Nova Scotia Regimental Depot.

### 5 September 1918 — Bramshott, Hants., England — Thurs.

> Weather. Fair and warmer. Training carried on as usual. Reinforcements Number 269 (5 Other Ranks for 25th Bn; 5 Other Ranks for 65th Bn.; 110 Other Ranks for R.C.R.) warned to proceed Overseas this date. Route March as per Operation Order Number 42 attached, scheduled for this date, cancelled, instructions having been received from Headquarters, Bramshott, that until further notice Route Marches in this Area are discontinued and training to be carried on as usual. Reinforcements Number 269 as detailed above proceeded Overseas this date. Routine Orders 2362 to 2372. Part II Daily Orders 210. Two Other Ranks taken on strength on posting from Nova Scotia Regimental Depot. Eight Other Ranks (6 Category "D1" and 2 Category "B11") struck off strength on posting to Nova Scotia Regimental Depot. Four Other Ranks (Category "B11") struck off strength on posting to Nova Scotia Regimental Depot and are attached for duty. Two Other Ranks (Category "D1") despatched to 2nd Canadian Command Depot from hospital, struck off strength on posting to Nova Scotia Regimental Depot. One Officer struck off strength on transfer to 13th Canadian Reserve Battalion. One Officer struck off strength on posting to Nova Scotia Regimental Depot.

[At foot of sheet: "6th".]
