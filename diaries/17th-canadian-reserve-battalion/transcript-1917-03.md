# War Diary — 17th Canadian Reserve Battalion

## Source
- **Unit:** 17th Canadian Reserve Battalion (Nova Scotia) — formed 23 January 1917 at Bramshott by the amalgamation of the 17th, 193rd and 219th Battalions; part of the 5th Canadian Reserve Brigade, reinforcing the 85th and 185th Battalions
- **Archival reference:** RG 9 III-D-3, vol. 4951, file 479, part 2; microfilm reel T-10767
- **LAC Collection Search:** Item ID 2004685 — "War diaries - 17th Reserve Battalion", 293 pages
  - Record: https://recherche-collection-search.bac-lac.gc.ca/eng/Home/Record?app=fonandcol&IdNumber=2004685
- **Page images:** pages 86–88 of 293 in this file (`page-0086-08-03-1917.jpg` … `page-0088-27-03-1917.jpg`)
- **Commanding officer:** Lieut. Colonel W. H. Muirhead (in command since 12 February 1917; signs the 31 March sheet)
- **Note on the record:** this LAC record also contains an unrelated 1914 diary of the 17th Battalion on pages 1–79; those pages are NOT transcribed here. This chunk covers only the Reserve Battalion diary, January–December 1917 (pages 80–139).

## Transcription conventions
- Faithful to the original: spelling, punctuation, abbreviations and capitalisation as written.
- `[uncertain]` marks a word or name that could not be read with confidence — never guessed.
- The diary-text pages are Army Form C. 2118, "WAR DIARY or INTELLIGENCE SUMMARY", typed in columns: Place | Date | Hour | Summary of Events and Information | Remarks and references to Appendices.
- The "Hour" column is blank for most entries; where an hour is given (Divine Service times, drafts proceeding overseas, the General Mobilization) it is transcribed.
- The Place column gives "BRAMSHOTT" on the first entry of each sheet and is blank (ditto) thereafter.
- The Remarks column is initialled on each line (initials not legible — see per-page notes).
- No map coordinates (latitude/longitude) are given in the diary; places are recorded as written.
- "Part II Orders" and "Routine Order paras." are cited by number only; the orders themselves are not in this record.

## Page 86 — Diary sheet, 8–18 March 1917

> Routine Orders paras 926–935. 1 other rank taken on the strength from the 85th Battalion; 1 other rank struck off the strength to the 85th Battalion.

> Routine Orders paras 936–944. Appendix thereto showing establishment of W.O.'s and N.C.O.'s. 5 Officers struck off strength to the C.C.A.C.

## Notes and gaps
- The January volume closes on this sheet: strength of the unit as at 31 March 1917 was 66 officers and 1,148 other ranks.
- 16 March: the largest single draft of the month — 125 other ranks proceeded overseas to the 85th Battalion. Further drafts followed on 19 March (15), 21 March (9 other ranks, 4 officers) and 28 March (55).
- 24–26 March: "General Mobilization" (24 March, 5 p.m.), "still on" 25 March, "Normal Conditions resumed" 26 March at 4 p.m. — no further explanation is given.
- 29 March: the battalion football team qualified for the final of the Bramshott Divisional Championship.
- 17 March: Lieut. J. S. Roper appointed Adjutant, backdated to 1 February 1917.
- Part II Order numbers cited skip several numbers (No.60, No.61, No.64, No.71, No.76 are not cited on these sheets), and No.75 is cited for both 29 and 30 March — transcribed as written.
- 28 March: "with effect 12-3-18" is as typed; the year is presumably a typist's error for 1917.
- 23 March and the signature block both read "C.I." — transcribed as written.
- No individual names of other ranks are given anywhere on these sheets; only numbers and the units they join or leave.
