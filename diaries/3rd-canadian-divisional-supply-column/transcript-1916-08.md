# War Diary — 3rd Canadian Divisional Supply Column — August 1916

- **Unit:** 3rd Canadian Divisional Supply Column, Canadian Corps
- **Month:** August 1916 (Volume 7) — 1 to 31 August 1916
- **Source:** Library and Archives Canada, Collection Search Item ID 2005012 (RG 9 III-D-3, vol. 5021, file 776, microfilm reel T-10905; finding aid 9-52)
- **Pages:** August 1916, p. 1 – p. 4 (image files page-0029 – page-0032 of the 102-page diary)
- **August 1916 — COMPLETE** (pages 1–4 of 102): cover, contents, diary text 1–31 August, no appendices. Transcribed 2026-10-27.

## Transcription conventions
Original spelling, punctuation and abbreviations kept. Diary text is typewritten throughout on Army Form C. 2118, "WAR DIARY or INTELLIGENCE SUMMARY" (the printed heading "or INTELLIGENCE SUMMARY" is not struck through). `[uncertain]` marks words I could not read with confidence — never guessed. Ditto marks (") under "Issued Supplies as usual" and under weather notes are expanded to the repeated text. Every entry is initialled in ink at the end of its line — "H.O.L." (Major H. O. Lawson, Officer Commanding) throughout this month; the initials are not repeated after every entry below. Map references are given as written (Sheet 27, Sheet 28); no latitude/longitude coordinates are given. "N.C.Os." = Non-Commissioned Officers; "A.G." = Adjutant General; "D.D.S.T." = Deputy Director of Supplies and Transport; "A.Q.M.G." = Assistant Quartermaster General; "R.F.C." = Royal Flying Corps; "M.T." = Motor Transport. The Place column is headed by the handwritten place name "Steenvoorde", partly cut at the left edge of the frame. The cover gives the volume as "Volume 7", while the contents page gives "Volume 16 - 8" — both are transcribed as written.

### Page 4 — 26–31 August 1916 (Sheet No. 2)

> **Steenvoorde, Aug. 26th.** — The Supply Section and Advanced Park moved from G.21.a.1.6., Sheet 28 to Column at K.31.d.3.3. Sheet 27. Refilling points were formed at the following locations:— 7th. Inf. Bde. at J.17.b. 2.8., 8th. Inf. Bde. at K.17.b.6.7., 9th. Inf. Bde. at K.32.d.6.3., Divnl. Troops at K.32.d.8.6. all Sheet 27. Railhead moved from Reninghelst Siding to Caestre. ## Open questions / uncertain items (August 1916)
- 10 August: the authority cited for Lieut. Cameron's transfer reads "(GC, A-52-72, 6-8-16, …)" — "GC" [uncertain]; it may read "GOC".
- 11 August: Lieut. MacKinnon's entry ends "(Do— do—" — apparently a ditto of the previous entry's references, left unfinished on the sheet; transcribed as written.
- 25 August: the entry opens "at J.17.b.2.8. Sheet 27." with no verb, before "Weather conditions — fine." and the parenthetical giving the brigades' locations; transcribed literally. It apparently states the Column's own location.
- 1 August: "Changed Horse' rations for 5800 Horses for Standard Pack Train" is transcribed as written; the sense of "Changed" (presumably the ration scale was changed) is the diary's own wording.
- Names read as: Lieut. W. Stratton; Lieut. F. A. Gibson; Lieut. D. I. Cameron; Lieut. C. G. MacKinnon; Major H. O. Lawson.
- No casualties are recorded in this month. 16 N.C.Os. and men left for England on 15 August and 21 more on 30 August, to be discharged under the Adjutant General's ruling on cessation of working pay — these are discharges, not casualties.
