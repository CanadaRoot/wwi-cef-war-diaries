# War Diary — 3rd Canadian Divisional Supply Column — April 1917

- **Unit:** 3rd Canadian Divisional Supply Column, Canadian Corps (C.A.S.C.)
- **Month:** April 1917 (Volume 15) — this volume covers 1 to 30 April 1917
- **Source:** Library and Archives Canada, Collection Search Item ID 2005012 (RG 9 III-D-3, vol. 5021, file 776, microfilm reel T-10905; finding aid 9-52)
- **Pages:** April 1917, p. 1 – p. 3 (image files page-0056 – page-0058 of the 102-page diary)
- **April 1917 — COMPLETE** (pages 1–3 of 102): cover, contents, diary text 1–30 April, no appendices. Transcribed 2026-10-27.

## Transcription conventions
Original spelling, punctuation and abbreviations kept. The diary text is typewritten throughout (Army Form C. 2118, "WAR DIARY or INTELLIGENCE SUMMARY"). `[uncertain]` marks words or figures I could not read with confidence — never guessed. The diary gives no map references and no latitude/longitude coordinates. "O.C." = Officer Commanding. The Place column gives the Column's location for the whole sheet ("OLHAIN"). The Remarks column carries a repeated handwritten initialled mark against most entries; it is noted in the open questions rather than repeated against every entry. Note the layout point recorded below: the sheet carries 31 lines of diary text for the 30 days of April — an extra, undated ration-issue line sits between the 16 April narrative entry and the 17 April row.

## Open questions / uncertain items (April 1917)
- 16–17 April: the sheet has 31 lines of diary text for the 30 days of April. The line "Issued 28720 men's and 6712 horses' rations. Weather conditions — rain and very cold." sits between the 16 April narrative entry and the 17 April row and carries no date of its own; the date labels 17–30 April each sit against their own rows, so the line is an extra, undated line transcribed here in its sheet position. It is most likely 16 April's ration issue (the 16th has no other ration line) or 17 April's, with the date label displaced. It is not assigned to a date in the transcript; the index carries it under 16 April with this note.
- 8 April: the horses' figure reads 6355, but the first digit is overwritten and may read 8 (8355); marked [uncertain].
- 15 April: there is a small mark before "rain" in the weather note; transcribed as "rain."
- The railhead is written "Frevin Capelle" here, where the February sheet writes "Provin-Capelle Railhead" (see transcript-1917-02.md). Both are recorded as written.
- The repeated mark in the Remarks column is read as "W.T." [uncertain] — apparently the checking officer's initials.
- The signature at the foot of the sheet, written vertically, is not legible; the printed appointment is O.C. 3rd Canadian Divisional Supply Column.
- The contents page gives the volume as "Volume 17 — 4." while the cover gives "VOLUME 15"; both are recorded as written.
- Names read as: Captain Ellard.
- No casualties are recorded in this volume. The 16 April entry records a change of railhead (Bruay to Frevin Capelle) during the Battle of Vimy Ridge period; the diary itself does not mention the battle.
