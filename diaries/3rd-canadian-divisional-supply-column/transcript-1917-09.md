# War Diary — 3rd Canadian Divisional Supply Column — September 1917

- **Unit:** 3rd Canadian Divisional Supply Column, 3rd Canadian Division
- **Month:** September 1917 (Volume 20) — this volume covers 1 to 30 September 1917
- **Source:** Library and Archives Canada, Collection Search Item ID 2005012 (RG 9 III-D-3, vol. 5021, file 776, microfilm reel T-10905, finding aid 9-52)
- **Pages:** September 1917, p. 1 – p. 4 (image files page-0074 – page-0077 of the 102-page diary)
- **September 1917 — COMPLETE** (pages 74–77 of 102): cover, contents, title page, diary text 1–30 September, no appendices. Transcribed 2026-10-27.

## Transcription conventions
Original spelling, punctuation and abbreviations kept. The diary text is a single typewritten Army Form C. 2118 sheet ("WAR DIARY or INTELLIGENCE SUMMARY" — the printed instruction "(Erase heading not required.)" appears beneath the heading; neither heading is struck through). `[uncertain]` marks words or figures I could not read with confidence — never guessed. The Place column gives "Olhain" against 1 September only; the remaining rows are blank (ditto). "men's" and "horses'" are as typed throughout. A repeated initialled mark appears in the Remarks column against most entries; it is noted in the open questions rather than repeated against every entry. The sheet is signed at the foot "O.C., 3rd Canadian Divisional Supply Column." with "Captain," beside it; the signature itself is [uncertain]. No map references or latitude/longitude coordinates are given.

## Open questions / uncertain items (September 1917)
- 21 September: the horses' figure (4204) is well below the ~5,100 of surrounding days; transcribed as written — the diary gives no explanation.
- The cover gives the volume as "VOLUME. 20." while the contents page gives "Volume 17 – 9." — both are as written.
- The contents page ends "Diary Text 1 Page.:" — the colon is as typed.
- No moves, no named people and no casualties are recorded in this volume; every day's weather is recorded as "fine".
