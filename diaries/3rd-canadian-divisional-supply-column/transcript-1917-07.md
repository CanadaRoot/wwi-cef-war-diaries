# War Diary — 3rd Canadian Divisional Supply Column — July 1917

- **Unit:** 3rd Canadian Divisional Supply Column, Canadian Corps (C.A.S.C.)
- **Month:** July 1917 (Volume 18) — this volume covers 1 to 31 July 1917
- **Source:** Library and Archives Canada, Collection Search Item ID 2005012 (RG 9 III-D-3, vol. 5021, file 776, microfilm reel T-10905; finding aid 9-52)
- **Pages:** July 1917, p. 1 – p. 4 (image files page-0066 – page-0069 of the 102-page diary)
- **July 1917 — COMPLETE** (pages 1–4 of 102): cover, handwritten title page, contents, diary text 1–31 July, no appendices. Transcribed 2026-10-27.

## Transcription conventions
Original spelling, punctuation and abbreviations kept. The diary text is typewritten throughout (Army Form C. 2118, "WAR DIARY or INTELLIGENCE SUMMARY"). `[uncertain]` marks words or figures I could not read with confidence — never guessed. The diary gives no map references and no latitude/longitude coordinates. "O.C." = Officer Commanding. The Place column gives the Column's location for the whole sheet ("Olhain"). The Remarks column carries a repeated handwritten initialled mark against most entries; it is noted in the open questions rather than repeated against every entry. One narrative line on the sheet carries no date of its own; it is transcribed in its sheet position and noted below.

## Open questions / uncertain items (July 1917)
- 30–31 July: the line about Major H.O. Lawson proceeding on leave to England carries no date; it is transcribed between the 30 and 31 July rows and carried under 30 July in the index.
- The signature at the foot of the sheet reads "J. E. Ellard" [uncertain] — the initials are not fully legible; the surname is Ellard, the same officer named as Captain Ellard in the February, April and May entries. The printed rank is Captain, O.C. 3rd Canadian Div'l. Supply Column.
- The repeated mark in the Remarks column is read as "W.T." [uncertain] for most of the month, changing to "J.H.B." [uncertain] for the 30 and 31 July rows — apparently the checking officer's initials.
- The contents page gives the volume as "Volume 17 — 7." while the cover gives "VOLUME 18"; both are recorded as written.
- The ration figures fall sharply in the last week of the month — about 20,000 men's rations from 26 July, and about 17,700 men's and 2,700 horses' rations on 30–31 July, against about 24,000–25,000 men's and 5,500–6,000 horses' rations earlier in the month. The diary gives no reason for the fall; it is recorded here as read, not explained.
- Names read as: Major H. O. Lawson.
- No casualties are recorded in this volume.
