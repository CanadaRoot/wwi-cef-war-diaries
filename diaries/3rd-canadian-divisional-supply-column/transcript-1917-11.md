# War Diary — 3rd Canadian Divisional Supply Column — November 1917

- **Unit:** 3rd Canadian Divisional Supply Column, 3rd Canadian Division
- **Month:** November 1917 (Volume 22) — this volume covers 1 to 30 November 1917
- **Source:** Library and Archives Canada, Collection Search Item ID 2005012 (RG 9 III-D-3, vol. 5021, file 776, microfilm reel T-10905, finding aid 9-52)
- **Pages:** November 1917, p. 1 – p. 3 (image files page-0082 – page-0084 of the 102-page diary)
- **November 1917 — COMPLETE** (pages 82–84 of 102): cover, contents, diary text 1–30 November, no appendices. There is no separate title page in this month's volume. Transcribed 2026-10-27.

## Transcription conventions
Original spelling, punctuation and abbreviations kept. The diary text is a single typewritten Army Form C. 2118 sheet ("WAR DIARY or INTELLIGENCE SUMMARY" — the printed instruction "(Erase heading not required.)" appears beneath the heading; neither heading is struck through). `[uncertain]` marks words or figures I could not read with confidence — never guessed. The form's first column is headed "Hour, Date, Place" and the place is given as a ditto mark (") after its first appearance. "men's" and "horses'" are as typed throughout; the weather is written with a capital "Weather Conditions" in the 5 November entry, as typed. A repeated initialled mark appears in the Remarks column against most entries; it is noted in the open questions rather than repeated against every entry. The sheet is signed at the foot "O.C., 3rd. Canadian Divisional Supply Column." with "Captain," beside it; the signature itself is [uncertain]. No map references or latitude/longitude coordinates are given.

## Open questions / uncertain items (November 1917)
- 19 November: the place changes from Poperinghe to Auchel with no move recorded in the diary text — the change is in the Place column only.
- 18 and 19 November: both days record identical figures (12631 men's, 1700 horses' rations); transcribed as written.
- 12–16 November: the figures jump to ~30,000–38,000 men's and ~11,700–12,800 horses' rations, against ~11,000–12,000 men's on surrounding days; transcribed as written — the diary gives no explanation.
- 30 November: the horses' figure is read as 3902 [uncertain] — the first digit is overstruck.
- The cover gives the volume as "VOLUME 22" while the contents page gives "Volume 17 – 11." — both are as written.
- No named people, no moves and no casualties are recorded in this volume.
