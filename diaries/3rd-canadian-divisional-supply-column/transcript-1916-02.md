# War Diary — 3rd Canadian Divisional Supply Column — February 1916

- **Unit:** 3rd Canadian Divisional Supply Column, 3rd Canadian Division (styled "3rd (Temp.) Canadian Divisional Supply Column" from 1 February 1916)
- **Month:** February 1916 (Volume 1) — this volume covers 1 to 29 February 1916
- **Source:** Library and Archives Canada, Collection Search Item ID 2005012 (RG 9 III-D-3, vol. 5021, microfilm reel T-10905, file 776; finding aid 9-52)
- **Pages:** February 1916, p. 1 – p. 5 (image files page-0001 – page-0005 of the 102-page diary)
- **February 1916 — COMPLETE** (pages 1–5 of 102): cover, title page, contents, diary text 1–29 February, no appendices. Transcribed 2026-10-27.

## Transcription conventions
Original spelling, punctuation and abbreviations kept. Diary text is typewritten throughout (Army Form C. 2118, "WAR DIARY or INTELLIGENCE SUMMARY" — "INTELLIGENCE SUMMARY" struck through on the printed heading). `[uncertain]` marks words I could not read with confidence — never guessed. Ditto marks in the original are written out as the repeated text, with "(ditto)" noted. A repeated handwritten initial mark appears at the end of most entries; it is not repeated against every entry here. Map references are given as written (Sheet 27, Sheet 28); no latitude/longitude coordinates are given. "C.C.T.S. Column" = Canadian Corps Troops Supply Column; "C.A.S.P." = Canadian Army Service Park (as written); "T.S. Col." = Transport and Supply Column (as written); "O.C." = Officer Commanding; "Cwt." = hundredweight. The diary's own heading on the diary sheets is the unit name written in by hand at the top right. The signature at the foot of the February sheets is read as [uncertain] (see open questions).

### Page 4 — 1–21 February 1916 (Sheet No. 1)

> **BAILLEUL, 1/2/16.** — Canadn. Corps R.O. no. 303 dated 30/1/16, published in Column Orders. Designation of Unit is changed to 3rd. (Temp.) Canadn. Divisional Supply Column.
>
> **BAILLEUL, 2/2/16.** — 4—3ton lorries transferred to Canadian Corps Troop Supply Col. for Corps H.Q. Motor Omnibusses (attached) H.Q. car drivers etc. are taken on the strength of C.C.T.S. Column they having taken over all work of the Canadian Corps.
>
> **BAILLEUL, 3/2/16.** — Supplies of the 3rd. Canadian Division only are drawn by this Column.
>
> **BAILLEUL, 4/2/16.** — Supplies as usual are drawn from railhead at Bailleul. Received from 2nd. Army T.S. Col. two light cars for the 3rd. Candn. Divnl. Train. Auth:—Candn. Corps, Q.26/12.
>
> **BAILLEUL, 5/2/16.** — Supplies as usual. Made arrangements with 3rd. Div. H.Q. as regards replacement and supplying light car drivers for their H.Q.
>
> **BAILLEUL, 6/2/16.** — Drew supplies as usual.
>
> **BAILLEUL, 7/2/16.** — Drew supplies as usual. (ditto)
>
> **BAILLEUL, 8/2/16.** — Drew supplies as usual. (ditto)
>
> **BAILLEUL, 9/2/16.** — Four 3Ton lorries returned from Canadian Cavalry Bde., these lorries were sent with this Bde. in January when they moved out of Corps area.
>
> **BAILLEUL, 10/2/16.** — Drew supplies from railhead as usual.
>
> **BAILLEUL, 11/2/16.** — Drew supplies from railhead as usual. (ditto)
>
> **BAILLEUL, 12/2/16.** — Exchanged all Imperial car drivers at 3rd. Candn. Div. H.Q. and replaced them with Canadian; Lieut. C. P. McCallum supply Officer leaves to join 3rd. Candn. Train. Supplies as usual.
>
> **BAILLEUL, 13/2/16.** — Drew supplies from railhead at Bailleul as usual.
>
> **BAILLEUL, 14/2/16.** — Drew supplies from railhead at Bailleul as usual. (ditto)
>
> **BAILLEUL, 15/2/16.** — Drew supplies from railhead at Bailleul as usual. (ditto)
>
> **BAILLEUL, 16/2/16.** — Drew supplies from railhead at Bailleul as usual. (ditto)
>
> **BAILLEUL, 17/2/16.** — Drew supplies from railhead at Bailleul as usual. (ditto)
>
> **BAILLEUL, 18/2/16.** — Drew supplies from railhead at Bailleul as usual. (ditto)
>
> **BAILLEUL, 19/2/16.** — Transferred to 3rd. (Temp.) C. A. S. P. 6—3Ton lorries, these still remained attached as this Unit was only in process of formation. Auth. Candn. Corps—C.C.14/150. dated 10/2/16.
>
> **BAILLEUL, 20/2/16.** — Drew rations from Bailleul railhead as usual. Twelve lorries proceeded direct to Lederzeele with supplies for the Candn. Div. Artillery, from which point arrangements have been made for the Train to take over these rations at 6.30am.
>
> **BAILLEUL, 21/2/16.** — Supply lorries arrived from Lederzelle at 9.30am. Drew supplies from Bailleul railhead at usual time 10am. This new dump makes five dumps we are drawing to at present—X.24-A.8-9., Q.36-A.O-3., Q.21-C.2-0., sheet 27, T.7-D. and Lederzelle. (Sheet 28.)

### Page 5 — 21–29 February 1916 (Sheet No. 2)

> **BAILLEUL, 21/2/16.** — Drew supplies from Bailleul railhead as usual. Received 3, 30 Cwt lorries from 2nd. Army T.S. Col.
>
> **BAILLEUL, 22/2/16.** — Drew supplies from Bailleul railhead as usual. (ditto) Lieut. Holding workshop Officer (Imperial) left for G.H.Q. D.D.S.&T.—P.2229. This Column is now without a Workshop Officer.
>
> **BAILLEUL, 23/2/16.** — Supplies as usual. Sent off strength 2—3Ton lorries to 2nd. Army T.S. Col. and 2—3Ton to 3rd. (T) C. A. S. P.
>
> **BAILLEUL, 24/2/16.** — Received one 30Cwt lorry from 2nd A. T. S. Col. Sent Studabaker light car from the Third Train to Base as casualty. Mechanically this car was proved unsatisfactory.
>
> **BAILLEUL, 25/2/16.** — Drew supplies from Bailleul railhead as usual etc.
>
> **BAILLEUL, 26/2/16.** — Drew supplies from Bailleul railhead as usual etc. (ditto)
>
> **BAILLEUL, 27/2/16.** — Received 6—30 Cwt. lorries.
>
> **BAILLEUL, 28/2/16.** — Supplies as usual. Difficulty is being experienced by the Transport Officer in taking the lorries drawing supplies to Lederzelle up and down Cassell Hill, skid chains have no effect on the paved or cobbled road in the weather now, and, altho there is little snow the rubber will not grip, I have therefore ordered these lorries to go by St. Omer, the only other route open for heavy traffic, so long as this weather continues. This adds somewhat to mileage but obviates the danger of serious damage to the lorries.
>
> **BAILLEUL, 29/2/16.** — Supply lorries came in on time from Lederzelle via St. Omer. Drew supplies as usual. Received replacement of light car for 3rd. Candn. Div. Train.
>
> [Signed, bottom right:] *[signature — [uncertain]] Major, / O.C. 3rd. Canadian Divisional Supply Column.*

## Open questions / uncertain items (February 1916)
- The cover reads "From 1st February to 29th February 1916" and "Volume 1", but the contents page reads "From 1st February to 28th February, 1916" and "Volume 16 — 2." — both as written; the contents page appears to be a later typed summary and disagrees with the cover on the end date and volume number.
- 21 February appears twice, once at the foot of Sheet No. 1 (page 4) and once at the head of Sheet No. 2 (page 5), with different entries; both are transcribed as written.
- The place name is spelled "Lederzeele" on 20 February and "Lederzelle" on 21, 28 and 29 February — both spellings as written.
- The O.C.'s signature at the foot of the February sheets is not read with confidence; it appears to read "J. O. Lawson" [uncertain] but is recorded here as [uncertain] and not used as a name.
- The repeated handwritten initial mark at the end of most entries is not read with confidence and is not transcribed against every entry.
- Names read as: Lieut. C. P. McCallum; Lieut. Holding (workshop officer, Imperial).
- No casualties are recorded in this volume. No map coordinates (latitude/longitude) are given; dump locations are given as sheet references only.
