# War Diary — 3rd Canadian Divisional Supply Column — January 1917

- **Unit:** 3rd Canadian Divisional Supply Column, Canadian Corps
- **Month:** January 1917 (Volume 12) — 1 to 31 January 1917
- **Source:** Library and Archives Canada, Collection Search Item ID 2005012 (RG 9 III-D-3, vol. 5021, file 776, microfilm reel T-10905; finding aid 9-52)
- **Pages:** January 1917, p. 1 – p. 3 (image files page-0047 – page-0049 of the 102-page diary)
- **January 1917 — COMPLETE** (pages 1–3 of 102): cover, contents, diary text 1–31 January on a single sheet, no appendices. Transcribed 2026-10-27.

## Transcription conventions
Original spelling, punctuation and abbreviations kept. Diary text is typewritten throughout on Army Form C. 2118, "WAR DIARY or INTELLIGENCE SUMMARY" (the printed heading "or INTELLIGENCE SUMMARY" is not struck through). `[uncertain]` marks words I could not read with confidence — never guessed. Every entry is initialled in ink at the end of its line — "H.O.L." (Major H. O. Lawson, Officer Commanding) throughout this month; the initials are not repeated after every entry below. The 23 and 26 January entries each carry two lines: a move, then the day's ration issue; both lines are transcribed under the one date. The Date column is headed "1917." and the dates are written "1-1-17" and so on. Map references are given as written (Sheet 51C, Sheet 36B); no latitude/longitude coordinates are given. The Place column reads "Tilloy-Les-Hermaville" for 1–22 January, "Mingoval" for 23–25 January and "Olhain" for 26–31 January. The printed footer on this month's diary sheet reads "1875 Wt. W593/826 1,000,000 4/15 J.B.C. & A. A.D.S.S./Forms/C. 2118."

## Open questions / uncertain items (January 1917)
- 23 January: the starting map reference reads "D 27 p.4-9 Sheet 51C" — the middle token "p.4-9" [uncertain]; it may read "P.4-9".
- 26 January: the destination reference reads "P 24 b5-9 Sheet 36B" as written.
- No names other than the diarist's initials appear this month.
- No casualties are recorded in this month. The Column moved twice: Tilloy-les-Hermaville to Mingoval on 23 January, then Mingoval to Olhain on 26 January, both moves given by map reference only.
