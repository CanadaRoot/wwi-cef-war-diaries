# War Diary — 3rd Canadian Divisional Supply Column — May 1917

- **Unit:** 3rd Canadian Divisional Supply Column, Canadian Corps (C.A.S.C.)
- **Month:** May 1917 (Volume 16) — this volume covers 1 to 31 May 1917
- **Source:** Library and Archives Canada, Collection Search Item ID 2005012 (RG 9 III-D-3, vol. 5021, file 776, microfilm reel T-10905; finding aid 9-52)
- **Pages:** May 1917, p. 1 – p. 4 (image files page-0059 – page-0062 of the 102-page diary)
- **May 1917 — COMPLETE** (pages 1–4 of 102): cover, contents, diary text 1–31 May on two sheets, no appendices. Transcribed 2026-10-27.

## Transcription conventions
Original spelling, punctuation and abbreviations kept. The diary text is typewritten throughout (Army Form C. 2118, "WAR DIARY or INTELLIGENCE SUMMARY"). `[uncertain]` marks words or figures I could not read with confidence — never guessed. The diary gives no map references and no latitude/longitude coordinates. "O.C." = Officer Commanding; "A/O.C." = Acting Officer Commanding; "C.F.A.S.C." is transcribed as written (see open questions). The Place column gives the Column's location for the whole month ("Olhain"). The Remarks column carries a repeated handwritten initialled mark against most entries; it is noted in the open questions rather than repeated against every entry. Several narrative lines on the sheets carry no date of their own; each is transcribed in its sheet position and noted below.

## Open questions / uncertain items (May 1917)
- 4 May: the undated ration line "Issued 25827 men's and 6824 horses' rations. Weather conditions — fine." sits between the 4 May bombardment entry and the 5 May row; it is transcribed in its sheet position and carried under 4 May in the index with this note. It may instead be 5 May's issue, with the date label displaced.
- 9–10 May: the line about Major Lawson assuming command of the Canadian Corps Supply Column in Major Harris's absence carries no date; it is transcribed between the 9 and 10 May rows and carried under 9 May in the index. "C.F.A.S.C." is transcribed as written; it may read "C.F.A. & S.C." or similar — [uncertain].
- 19–20 May: the line about Major Lawson resuming command of 3rd Cdn. Div. Sup. Col. on Major Harris's return from leave carries no date; it is transcribed between the 19 and 20 May rows and carried under 19 May in the index.
- 23 May: the Labyrinth Railhead renaming line carries no date; it is transcribed after the 23 May row and carried under 23 May in the index. The authority is given as "Authy. 1st A. R.O.1006, d/21-5-17" — "1st A." is transcribed as written.
- 25–26 May: the line about Major H.O. Lawson proceeding on thirty days' leave to England carries no date; it is transcribed between the 25 and 26 May rows and carried under 25 May in the index.
- 1 May: "Horse Transport drawing." is transcribed as written; the sentence appears abbreviated in the original.
- The repeated mark in the Remarks column reads "W.T." [uncertain] for 1–6 May and changes to "A.R.A." [uncertain] from 7 May — apparently the checking officer's initials, changing with the signing officer (the sheet is signed by Lieut. A. R. Anderson, A/O.C.).
- The contents page gives the volume as "Volume 17 — 5." while the cover gives "VOLUME 16"; both are recorded as written.
- Names read as: Captain Ellard; Major H. O. Lawson; Major Harris, E.M.; Lieut. A. R. Anderson.
- Casualties: 4 May — one driver (unnamed) wounded by bomb fragments in the aerial bombardment of Ecurie Railhead; Lorry W.D. No. 12976 damaged. No other casualties are recorded in this volume.
