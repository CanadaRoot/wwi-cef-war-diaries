# War Diary — 3rd Canadian Divisional Supply Column — October 1916

- **Unit:** 3rd Canadian Divisional Supply Column, Canadian Corps
- **Month:** October 1916 (Volume 9) — 1 to 31 October 1916
- **Source:** Library and Archives Canada, Collection Search Item ID 2005012 (RG 9 III-D-3, vol. 5021, file 776, microfilm reel T-10905; finding aid 9-52)
- **Pages:** October 1916, p. 1 – p. 4 (image files page-0037 – page-0040 of the 102-page diary)
- **October 1916 — COMPLETE** (pages 1–4 of 102): cover, contents, diary text 1–31 October, no appendices. Transcribed 2026-10-27.

## Transcription conventions
Original spelling, punctuation and abbreviations kept. Diary text is typewritten throughout on Army Form C. 2118, "WAR DIARY or INTELLIGENCE SUMMARY" (the printed heading "or INTELLIGENCE SUMMARY" is not struck through). `[uncertain]` marks words I could not read with confidence — never guessed. Ditto marks (") under "Issued Supplies as usual" and under weather notes are expanded to the repeated text. Entries are initialled in ink at the end of each line — "H.O.L." (Major H. O. Lawson, Officer Commanding) for 1–28 October, and "C.McK." (Capt. C. G. MacKinnon) for 29–31 October; the initials are not repeated after every entry below. The October sheets are signed at the foot by Capt. C. G. MacKinnon "for O.C. 3rd. Canadian Divisional Supply Column", indicating Major Lawson was absent at the month's end. Map references are given as written; no latitude/longitude coordinates are given. The Place column reads "Rubempre" for 1–17 October, "Bernaville" for 18 October and "Maisonleblond" for 19 October on Sheet No. 1, and "Tinques" for 22 October and "Tilloy" for 27 October on Sheet No. 2. The figure "2" appears at the top right of the Summary of Events column on Sheet No. 1 and "3" on Sheet No. 2.

## Pages 3–4 — Diary text (1–31 October 1916)

### Page 3 — 1–19 October 1916 (Sheet No. 1)

> **Rubempre, Oct. 1st.** — Issued 18651 men's and 4834 horses' rations. Weather conditions — fine and warm.
>
> **Rubempre, Oct. 2nd.** — Issued Supplies as usual. Weather conditions — rain.
>
> **Rubempre, Oct. 3rd.** — Issued 17996 men's and 4799 horses' rations. Lieut. Gibson and 20 lorries of No. 2 Section relieved Lieut. McDowall and 20 lorries of No. 1 Section on Engineers' Work at Advanced Park. Weather conditions — rain.
>
> **Rubempre, Oct. 4th.** — Issued 18264 men's and 4756 horses' rations. Weather conditions — rain. Railhead moved from Belle Eglise to Acheux.
>
> **Rubempre, Oct. 5th.** — Issued Supplies as usual. Weather conditions — light showers.
>
> **Rubempre, Oct. 6th.** — Issued 18072 men's and 4774 horses' rations. Lieut. Gibson and 20 lorries of No. 2 Section at Advanced Park returned to Column. The entire Column is now working on Supplies, the Divisional Train having discontinued drawing from Railhead. Weather conditions — fine.
>
> **Rubempre, Oct. 7th.** — Issued 17530 men's and 4836 horses' rations. Weather conditions — rain.
>
> **Rubempre, Oct. 8th.** — Issued 17381 men's and 4782 horses' rations. Weather conditions — dull.
>
> **Rubempre, Oct. 9th.** — Issued 17813 men's and 4817 horses' rations. Weather conditions — dull. Issued 6989 men's and 3930 horses' rations for 2nd Canadian Divisional Troops.
>
> **Rubempre, Oct. 10th.** — Issued 16003 men's and 4812 horses' rations. Weather conditions — fine.
>
> **Rubempre, Oct. 11th.** — Issued 15379 men's and 4799 horses' rations. Weather conditions — dull and wet.
>
> **Rubempre, Oct. 12th.** — Issued 19777 men's and 7679 horses' rations. Lieut. McDowall and 15 lorries of No. 1 Section proceeded to Huleux to accompany Lahore Division Artillery, and will draw from Frevent Railhead, October 13th. Weather conditions — dull.
>
> **Rubempre, Oct. 13th.** — Issued 20592 men's and 5560 horses' rations. Weather conditions — dull.
>
> **Rubempre, Oct. 14th.** — Issued 21591 men's and 5411 horses' rations. Weather conditions — dull.
>
> **Rubempre, Oct. 15th.** — Railhead moved from Acheux to Canaples. Issued 21797 men's and 5402 horses' rations. Weather conditions — dull.
>
> **Rubempre, Oct. 16th.** — Issued supplies as usual. Weather conditions — dull.
>
> **Rubempre, Oct. 17th.** — The Headquarters and Workshops of Column moved to Bernaville via the following route:— Rubempre to Villers Bocage, Flesselles, Havernas, Canaples to Bernaville. The supply details with 20 lorries under Capt. McKinnon and Lieut. Gibson went direct to railhead and issued 12780 men's and 1868 horses' rations. Weather conditions — slight showers.
>
> **Bernaville, Oct. 18th.** — Issued supplies as usual. Weather conditions — heavy rain.
>
> **Maisonleblond, Oct. 19th.** — The Headquarters and Workshops of Column moved to Maisonleblond via the following route:— Bernaville to Le Meillard, Frohenlegrand, Villers C'Hopital, Bonniere to Maisonleblond. The Supply Details and 20 lorries under Lieut. Gibson went to Railhead and issued 12903 men's and 2013 horses' rations. Weather conditions — very wet.

### Page 4 — 20–31 October 1916 (Sheet No. 2)

> **Maisonleblond, Oct. 20th.** — Railhead moved from Canaples to Frevent. Issued supplies as usual. Weather conditions — fine with cold winds.
>
> **Maisonleblond, Oct. 21st.** — Railhead moved from Frevent to St Pol. Issued 12977 men's and 2022 horses' rations. Weather conditions — fine and heavy frost.

## Open questions / uncertain items (October 1916)
- 9 October: a stray mark follows "for 2nd Canadian Divisional Troops." in the original; it is not transcribed as text.
- 12 October: "proceeded to Huleux to accompany Lahore Division Artillery" is transcribed as written.
- 17 October: Lieut. C. G. MacKinnon, who joined the Column as a lieutenant in August, is now styled "Capt. McKinnon" — the diary's own spelling here is "McKinnon", against "MacKinnon" in the August entry and in the signature.
- 22 October: the move is written "moved from Maisonleblond to Tinque" in the text and "St Pol to Tinques" in the route; both forms are transcribed as written.
- 27 October: "moved from Tinques to Tilloy-les, Hermanville Direct." is transcribed as written; "Direct" appears to be the diarist's word for the direct route, and the place is the Tilloy-les-Hermaville of the November and December sheets.
- The initials change from "H.O.L." to "C.McK." on 29 October, and Capt. MacKinnon signs the month's sheets for the Officer Commanding.
- Names read as: Lieut. F. A. Gibson; Lieut. McDowall; Capt. C. G. MacKinnon (McKinnon); Major H. O. Lawson.
- No casualties are recorded in this month. The Advanced Park's Engineers' Work ended on 6 October, when the whole Column returned to supply work; a 15-lorry detachment under Lieut. McDowall went to accompany Lahore Division Artillery on 12 October. Ration issues drop sharply from about 18,000 men's rations to about 13,000 on 17–18 October as the Column moves, then rise again to about 18,500 by 29 October.
