- **August 1917 — COMPLETE** (pages 70–73 of 102): cover, title page, contents, diary text 1–31 August, no appendices. Transcribed 2026-10-27.

## Transcription conventions
Original spelling, punctuation and abbreviations kept. The diary text is a single typewritten Army Form C. 2118 sheet ("WAR DIARY or INTELLIGENCE SUMMARY" — the printed instruction "(Erase heading not required.)" appears beneath the heading; neither heading is struck through). `[uncertain]` marks words or figures I could not read with confidence — never guessed. The Place column gives "Olhain." against 1 August only; the remaining rows are blank (ditto). "men's" and "horses'" are as typed throughout. A repeated initialled mark appears in the Remarks column against most entries; it is noted in the open questions rather than repeated against every entry. The sheet is signed at the foot "Captain, O.C., 3rd Cdn. Div'l. Sup. Column."; the signature itself is [uncertain]. No map references or latitude/longitude coordinates are given.

## Open questions / uncertain items (August 1917)
- 9 August: the horses' figure is read as 2513 [uncertain] — the figure is smudged/overstruck.
- 31 August: both figures (23018 men's, 5715 horses') are [uncertain] — the printed form footer is overprinted across the bottom line of the sheet, partly obscuring the figures.
- 26 August: the entry spells the destination "Sains Bouvigny" for the Railhead and Supply Section and "Sains Bovigny" for the Postal move — both spellings are as typed; not corrected.
- 12 August: the horses' figure jumps to 4773 (from ~2,500 on surrounding days) and 22–25 August the figures jump to ~25,000–33,000 men's and ~8,000–13,000 horses' rations; transcribed as written — the diary gives no explanation.
- The cover gives the volume as "VOLUME. 19" while the contents page gives "Volume 17 – 8." — both are as written; the "17 – 8" numbering appears on every contents page in this diary (see the other months).
- No casualties are recorded in this volume.
