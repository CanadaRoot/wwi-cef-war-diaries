# War Diary — 3rd Canadian Divisional Supply Column — February 1918

- **Unit:** 3rd Canadian Divisional Supply Column, 3rd Canadian Division
- **Month:** February 1918 (Volume 25) — this volume covers 1 to 28 February 1918
- **Source:** Library and Archives Canada, Collection Search Item ID 2005012 (RG 9 III-D-3, vol. 5021, file 776, microfilm reel T-10905, finding aid 9-52)
- **Pages:** February 1918, p. 1 – p. 4 (image files page-0092 – page-0095 of the 102-page diary)
- **February 1918 — COMPLETE** (pages 92–95 of 102): cover, title page, contents, diary text 1–28 February, no appendices. Transcribed 2026-10-27.

## Transcription conventions
Original spelling, punctuation and abbreviations kept, including the typist's error "sand" for "and" in the 9 February entry. The diary text is a single typewritten Army Form C. 2118 sheet ("WAR DIARY or INTELLIGENCE SUMMARY" — the printed instruction "(Erase heading not required.)" appears beneath the heading; neither heading is struck through). `[uncertain]` marks words or figures I could not read with confidence — never guessed. The form's first column is headed "Hour, Date, Place" and the place is given as a ditto mark (") after its first appearance. A repeated initialled mark appears in the Remarks column against most entries; it is noted in the open questions rather than repeated against every entry. The sheet is signed at the foot "O.C., 3rd. Canadian Divisional Supply Column." with "Captain," beside it; the signature itself is [uncertain]. No map references or latitude/longitude coordinates are given.

## Open questions / uncertain items (February 1918)
- 20 and 28 February: an "x" stands in the Hour column; the footnote at the foot of the sheet reads "x Railhead HOUDAIN to this date; After ECURIE." — so the railhead was at Houdain until 28 February and at Ecurie thereafter.
- 9 February: the diary reads "Issued 11468 men's sand 2597 horses rations." — "sand" is the typist's error for "and", transcribed as written.
- 11 and 12 February: both days record 2631 horses' rations; transcribed as written.
- 18–21 February: the figures rise sharply (from ~11,700 men's and ~2,600 horses' to ~23,200 men's and ~5,000 horses' rations); transcribed as written — the diary gives no explanation.
- The cover gives the volume as "VOLUME 25" while the contents page gives "Volume 18 – 2." — both are as written.
- No named people and no casualties are recorded in this volume.
