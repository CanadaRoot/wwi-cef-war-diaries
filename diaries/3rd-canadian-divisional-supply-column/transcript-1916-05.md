# War Diary — 3rd Canadian Divisional Supply Column — May 1916

- **Unit:** 3rd Canadian Divisional Supply Column, 3rd Canadian Division (styled "3rd (Temp.) Canadian Divisional Supply Column")
- **Month:** May 1916 (Volume 4) — this volume covers 1 to 31 May 1916
- **Source:** Library and Archives Canada, Collection Search Item ID 2005012 (RG 9 III-D-3, vol. 5021, microfilm reel T-10905, file 776; finding aid 9-52)
- **Pages:** May 1916, p. 1 – p. 4 (image files page-0015 – page-0018 of the 102-page diary)
- **May 1916 — COMPLETE** (pages 15–18 of 102): cover, title page, contents, diary text 1–31 May on a single sheet, no appendices. Transcribed 2026-10-27.

## Transcription conventions
Original spelling, punctuation and abbreviations kept. Diary text is typewritten throughout (Army Form C. 2118, "WAR DIARY or INTELLIGENCE SUMMARY" — "INTELLIGENCE SUMMARY" struck through on the printed heading). `[uncertain]` marks words I could not read with confidence — never guessed. Ditto marks in the original are written out as the repeated text, with "(ditto)" noted. A repeated handwritten initial mark appears at the end of most entries; it is not repeated against every entry here. Map references are given as written; no latitude/longitude coordinates are given. "Divnl. H.Q." = Divisional Headquarters (as written); "O.C." = Officer Commanding; "Cwt." = hundredweight. The diary's own heading on the diary sheets is the unit name written in by hand at the top right. The signature at the foot of the May sheet is read as [uncertain] (see open questions).

> **ST SYLVESTRE-CAPPEL, 29/5/16.** — Started the construction of a screen around motor Ambulances to protect them from shrapnel, this consists of expanding lath screen placed on three strips of wood on the body of the ambulance which holds the screen about two inches off the body. This arrangement does not render occupants immune from shrapnel but is a considerable protection against spent pieces of shell or shrapnel and adds but little extra weight. Supplies as usual.

## Open questions / uncertain items (May 1916)
- 18–20 May: the three dates are bracketed together on the sheet but no entry text is written against the bracket; the next entry text belongs to the 21–24 May bracket.
- 27 May and 30 May: the rows are blank — no entry text is recorded for those dates.
- 31 May: the feeding-strength line ("21176 men and 5097 horses") is partly overprinted by the signature and the printed form footer, but is legible and is transcribed as written.
- The cover reads "Volume 4" but the contents page reads "Volume 16 — 5." — both as written.
- The O.C.'s signature at the foot of the May sheet is not read with confidence (it appears to read "J. O. Lawson" [uncertain]) and is recorded here as [uncertain].
- The handwritten "266" in the left margin of the title page is a file/frame mark, not part of the diary text.
- No personal names are given in this volume. No casualties are recorded in this volume. No map coordinates (latitude/longitude) are given.
