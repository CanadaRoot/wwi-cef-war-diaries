# War Diary — 3rd Canadian Divisional Supply Column — January 1918

- **Unit:** 3rd Canadian Divisional Supply Column, 3rd Canadian Division
- **Month:** January 1918 (Volume 24) — this volume covers 1 to 31 January 1918
- **Source:** Library and Archives Canada, Collection Search Item ID 2005012 (RG 9 III-D-3, vol. 5021, file 776, microfilm reel T-10905, finding aid 9-52)
- **Pages:** January 1918, p. 1 – p. 3 (image files page-0089 – page-0091 of the 102-page diary)
- **January 1918 — COMPLETE** (pages 89–91 of 102): cover, contents, diary text 1–31 January, no appendices. There is no separate title page in this month's volume. Transcribed 2026-10-27.

## Transcription conventions
Original spelling, punctuation and abbreviations kept. The diary text is a single typewritten Army Form C. 2118 sheet ("WAR DIARY or INTELLIGENCE SUMMARY" — the printed instruction "(Erase heading not required.)" appears beneath the heading; neither heading is struck through). `[uncertain]` marks words or figures I could not read with confidence — never guessed. The form's first column is headed "Hour, Date, Place" and the place is given as a ditto mark (") after its first appearance. "men's" and "horses'" are as typed throughout. A repeated initialled mark appears in the Remarks column against most entries; it is noted in the open questions rather than repeated against every entry. The sheet is signed at the foot "O.C., 3rd. Canadian Divisional Supply Column." with "Captain," beside it; the signature itself is [uncertain]. No map references or latitude/longitude coordinates are given.

## Open questions / uncertain items (January 1918)
- 12 January: the weather entry is a ditto mark under 11 January's "Thaw – Wet"; transcribed as "[ditto — Thaw – Wet]".
- 22 January: an "x" stands in the Hour column; the footnote at the foot of the sheet reads "x Railhead up to this date,— SAINS BOUVIGNY. After HOUDAIN." — so the railhead was at Sains Bouvigny until 22 January and at Houdain thereafter.
- 19 and 20 January: both days record 6443 horses' rations; transcribed as written.
- 23 January: the figures drop sharply (11975 men's, 2596 horses') from 21266 men's and 6394 horses' on 22 January, and stay at that lower level for the rest of the month; transcribed as written — the diary gives no explanation.
- A stray mark appears in the Hour column against 20 January; it carries no text and is not transcribed as content.
- The cover gives the volume as "VOLUME 24." while the contents page gives "Volume 18 – 1." — both are as written.
- No named people and no casualties are recorded in this volume.
