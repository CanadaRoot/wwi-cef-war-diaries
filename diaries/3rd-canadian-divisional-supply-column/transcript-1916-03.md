# War Diary — 3rd Canadian Divisional Supply Column — March 1916

- **Unit:** 3rd Canadian Divisional Supply Column, 3rd Canadian Division (styled "3rd (Temp.) Canadian Divisional Supply Column")
- **Month:** March 1916 (Volume 2) — this volume covers 1 to 31 March 1916
- **Source:** Library and Archives Canada, Collection Search Item ID 2005012 (RG 9 III-D-3, vol. 5021, microfilm reel T-10905, file 776; finding aid 9-52)
- **Pages:** March 1916, p. 1 – p. 5 (image files page-0006 – page-0010 of the 102-page diary)
- **March 1916 — COMPLETE** (pages 6–10 of 102): cover, title page, contents, diary text 1–31 March, no appendices. Transcribed 2026-10-27.

## Transcription conventions
Original spelling, punctuation and abbreviations kept. Diary text is typewritten throughout (Army Form C. 2118, "WAR DIARY or INTELLIGENCE SUMMARY" — "INTELLIGENCE SUMMARY" struck through on the printed heading). `[uncertain]` marks words I could not read with confidence — never guessed. Ditto marks in the original are written out as the repeated text, with "(ditto)" noted. A repeated handwritten initial mark appears at the end of most entries; it is not repeated against every entry here. Map references are given as written (Sheet 27, Sheet 28); no latitude/longitude coordinates are given. "C.A.S.P." = Canadian Army Service Park (as written); "A.T.S. Col." = Army Transport and Supply Column (as written); "R.C.R." = Royal Canadian Regiment; "O.C." = Officer Commanding; "Cwt." = hundredweight. The diary's own heading on the diary sheets is the unit name written in by hand at the top right. The signature at the foot of the March sheets is read as [uncertain] (see open questions).

> **ST SYLVESTRE-CAPPEL, 23/3/16.** — Supply lorries proceeded to dumps as usual. Drew reserve rations from railhead at Bailleul. Column moved from S.13.d.2.8. sheet 28. to P.23.c.0.3. sheet 27. [Remarks column: SEE APPENDIX]
>
> **ST SYLVESTRE-CAPPEL, 24/3/16.** — Supply Sec. and six lorries under Lieut. Cameron move to new railhead L.28.C.4.3. sheet 27. Transferred off one three ton and two 30 Cwt. lorries. Received one 3 Ton. lorry. Completed the details of the new camp i/e—Have erected a mens mess with sufficient seating capacity to allow all men to have their meals at table at the same time. This Column is now off the road the 3rd. Candn. Div. Train draw direct from railhead the supply Sec. of this Column issuing the rations direct from the train. Coal, wood etc. being taken to the dumps by the six lorries with the Supply Sec.

## Open questions / uncertain items (March 1916)
- 8 March: the new Officer Commanding records taking over command from Capt. N. J. Lindsay; the O.C.'s signature at the foot of the March sheets is not read with confidence (it appears to read "J. O. Lawson" [uncertain]) and is recorded here as [uncertain].
- 23 March: the Place column changes from Bailleul to "St Sylvestre-Cappel" on this date, and the Remarks column against the move entry reads "SEE APPENDIX" — the appendix referred to is the "Organization of 3rd Cdn. Divisional Supply Column" appendix filed with the July 1916 diary, which dates the move to the morning of 23 March.
- The handwritten "262" in the left margin of the title page is a file/frame mark, not part of the diary text.
- Names read as: Capt. N. J. Lindsay; Lieut. J. D. Lawson; Lieut. C. H. Rogers; Lieut. R. J. McDowell; Lieut. W. W. Stratton; Lieut. Cameron.
- No casualties are recorded in this volume. No map coordinates (latitude/longitude) are given; dump and railhead locations are given as sheet references only.
