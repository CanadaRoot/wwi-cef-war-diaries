# War Diary — 3rd Canadian Divisional Supply Column — March 1917

- **Unit:** 3rd Canadian Divisional Supply Column, Canadian Corps (C.A.S.C.)
- **Month:** March 1917 (Volume 14) — this volume covers 1 to 31 March 1917
- **Source:** Library and Archives Canada, Collection Search Item ID 2005012 (RG 9 III-D-3, vol. 5021, file 776, microfilm reel T-10905; finding aid 9-52)
- **Pages:** March 1917, p. 1 – p. 3 (image files page-0053 – page-0055 of the 102-page diary)
- **March 1917 — COMPLETE** (pages 1–3 of 102): cover, contents, diary text 1–31 March, no appendices. Transcribed 2026-10-27.

## Transcription conventions
Original spelling, punctuation and abbreviations kept. The diary text is typewritten throughout (Army Form C. 2118, "WAR DIARY or INTELLIGENCE SUMMARY"). `[uncertain]` marks words or figures I could not read with confidence — never guessed. The diary gives no map references and no latitude/longitude coordinates. "O.C." = Officer Commanding; "A/O.C." = Acting Officer Commanding. The Place column gives the Column's location for the whole sheet ("OLHAIN."). The Remarks column carries a repeated handwritten initialled mark against most entries; it is noted in the open questions rather than repeated against every entry.

## Open questions / uncertain items (March 1917)
- 9 March: the original reads "men's saand 5693 horses'" — apparently a typing error for "men's and"; transcribed as written.
- 17 March: the weather is written "Fine." with a capital F; transcribed as written.
- The repeated mark in the Remarks column reads "W.T." [uncertain] for 1–18 March and changes to "A.R.A." [uncertain] from 19 March — apparently the checking officer's initials, changing with the signing officer.
- The signature at the foot of the sheet, written vertically, reads "A. R. Anderson" [uncertain] — consistent with the May sheet, which is signed in full "A. R. Anderson, Lieutenant, A/O.C., 3rd Canadian Divisional Supply Column" (see transcript-1917-05.md).
- The contents page gives the volume as "Volume 17 — 3." while the cover gives "VOLUME 14"; both are recorded as written.
- No people are named in this volume and no casualties are recorded.
