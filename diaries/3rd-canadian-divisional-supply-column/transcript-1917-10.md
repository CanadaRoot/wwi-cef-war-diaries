# War Diary — 3rd Canadian Divisional Supply Column — October 1917

- **Unit:** 3rd Canadian Divisional Supply Column, 3rd Canadian Division
- **Month:** October 1917 (Volume 21) — this volume covers 1 to 31 October 1917
- **Source:** Library and Archives Canada, Collection Search Item ID 2005012 (RG 9 III-D-3, vol. 5021, file 776, microfilm reel T-10905, finding aid 9-52)
- **Pages:** October 1917, p. 1 – p. 4 (image files page-0078 – page-0081 of the 102-page diary)
- **October 1917 — COMPLETE** (pages 78–81 of 102): cover, title page, contents, diary text 1–31 October, no appendices. Transcribed 2026-10-27.

## Transcription conventions
Original spelling, punctuation and abbreviations kept. The diary text is a single typewritten Army Form C. 2118 sheet ("WAR DIARY or INTELLIGENCE SUMMARY" — the printed instruction "(Erase heading not required.)" appears beneath the heading; neither heading is struck through). `[uncertain]` marks words or figures I could not read with confidence — never guessed. From this month the form's first column is headed "Hour, Date, Place" (previously "Place | Date | Hour") and the place is given as a ditto mark (") after its first appearance; the weather is now written "Weather conditions–fine" (hyphen, no spaces around the dash) in most entries. "men's" and "horses'" are as typed throughout. A repeated initialled mark appears in the Remarks column against most entries; it is noted in the open questions rather than repeated against every entry. The sheet is signed at the foot "O.C., 3rd Canadian Divisional Supply Column." with "Captain," beside it; the signature itself is [uncertain]. No map references or latitude/longitude coordinates are given.

## Open questions / uncertain items (October 1917)
- 5 October: the place the Supply Section and Railhead moved from is read as "Bourie" [uncertain] — the word is not clearly legible.
- 15 October: the new place is written "Casstre." [uncertain] — the spelling is unclear (possibly Caestre); transcribed as it appears.
- 22 October: the issue figures (59823 men's, 21296 horses') are far above the rest of the month; transcribed as written — the diary gives no explanation. The 23–31 October figures are likewise much higher than 1–21 October.
- 23 and 26 October: the railhead destination is written "Edwaertshoek" — transcribed as typed.
- The cover gives the volume as "VOLUME 21." while the contents page gives "Volume 17 – 10." — both are as written.
- No named people and no casualties are recorded in this volume.
