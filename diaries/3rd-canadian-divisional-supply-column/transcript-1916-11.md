# War Diary — 3rd Canadian Divisional Supply Column — November 1916

- **Unit:** 3rd Canadian Divisional Supply Column, Canadian Corps
- **Month:** November 1916 (Volume 10) — 1 to 30 November 1916
- **Source:** Library and Archives Canada, Collection Search Item ID 2005012 (RG 9 III-D-3, vol. 5021, file 776, microfilm reel T-10905; finding aid 9-52)
- **Pages:** November 1916, p. 1 – p. 3 (image files page-0041 – page-0043 of the 102-page diary)
- **November 1916 — COMPLETE** (pages 1–3 of 102): cover, contents, diary text 1–30 November on a single sheet, no appendices. Transcribed 2026-10-27.

## Transcription conventions
Original spelling, punctuation and abbreviations kept. Diary text is typewritten throughout on Army Form C. 2118, "WAR DIARY or INTELLIGENCE SUMMARY" (the printed heading "or INTELLIGENCE SUMMARY" is not struck through). `[uncertain]` marks words I could not read with confidence — never guessed. Ditto marks (") under the weather note for 2 November are expanded to the repeated text ("wet and dull"). Entries are initialled in ink at the end of each line — "C.McK." (Capt. C. G. MacKinnon) for 1–12 November, and "H.O.L." (Major H. O. Lawson, Officer Commanding) from 13 November; the initials are not repeated after every entry below. The whole month's entries are ration issues and weather only, except 5 and 18 November. Map references are not given this month; no latitude/longitude coordinates are given. The Place column reads "Tilloy-Les-Hermaville" throughout the month. The contents page reads "From 1st BNovember to 30th November, 1916" — the "B" before "November" is a typewriter over-strike in the original, transcribed as written.

## Open questions / uncertain items (November 1916)
- 5 November: "returned to Column from detached duty with 2nd. C.D.S.C." is transcribed as written — C.D.S.C. here reads as Canadian Divisional Supply Column, i.e. the 2nd Canadian Divisional Supply Column. This is the return of the 15-lorry detachment sent to accompany Lahore Division Artillery on 12 October.
- The initials change from "C.McK." to "H.O.L." on 13 November, marking Major Lawson's resumption of the diary after Capt. MacKinnon initialled 1–12 November.
- Ration issues jump from about 18,800 men's rations on 17 November to 22,537 on 18 November, and again to 25,620 on 29 November, with horse rations rising from about 2,500 to about 5,400 and then 8,300 — the diary gives no reason for the increases.
- Names read as: Lieut. McDowall; Capt. C. G. MacKinnon; Major H. O. Lawson.
- No casualties are recorded in this month, and the Column did not move.
