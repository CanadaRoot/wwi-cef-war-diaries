# War Diary — 3rd Canadian Divisional Supply Column — June 1916

- **Unit:** 3rd Canadian Divisional Supply Column, 3rd Canadian Division (styled "3rd (Temp.) Canadian Divisional Supply Column")
- **Month:** June 1916 (Volume 5) — this volume covers 1 to 30 June 1916
- **Source:** Library and Archives Canada, Collection Search Item ID 2005012 (RG 9 III-D-3, vol. 5021, microfilm reel T-10905, file 776; finding aid 9-52)
- **Pages:** June 1916, p. 1 – p. 4 (image files page-0019 – page-0022 of the 102-page diary)
- **June 1916 — COMPLETE** (pages 19–22 of 102): cover, title page, contents, diary text 1–30 June on a single sheet, no appendices. Transcribed 2026-10-27.

## Transcription conventions
Original spelling, punctuation and abbreviations kept. Diary text is typewritten throughout (Army Form C. 2118, "WAR DIARY or INTELLIGENCE SUMMARY" — "INTELLIGENCE SUMMARY" struck through on the printed heading). `[uncertain]` marks words I could not read with confidence — never guessed. Ditto marks in the original are written out as the repeated text, with "(ditto)" noted. A repeated handwritten initial mark appears at the end of most entries; it is not repeated against every entry here. Map references are given as written (Sheet 27); no latitude/longitude coordinates are given. "C.A.S.C. T.D." = Canadian Army Service Corps Training Depot (as written); "A.T.S. Col." = Army Transport and Supply Column (as written); "L.G.O.C." = London General Omnibus Company (as written); "O.C." = Officer Commanding; "Cwt." = hundredweight. The diary's own heading on the diary sheets is the unit name written in by hand at the top right. The signature at the foot of the June sheet is read as [uncertain] (see open questions).

## Open questions / uncertain items (June 1916)
- 8 June: the typed phrase "replace casualties" is struck through and "complete establishment" written above it in the typescript; both readings are recorded. The entry states the Divisional Ambulances "having had a number of casualties" — no number of casualties is given.
- 24–26 June: the Column's Imperial lorries and drivers are replaced by 22 Daimler 3-ton lorries with 126 Canadian men from C.A.S.C. T.D. Shorncliffe; 40 Imperial drivers are returned to 2nd A.T.S. Col. and 22 lorries with Imperial personnel transferred off.
- 29 June: "cutting down the number of different makes to nine" — the word "different" is written above the line in the typescript.
- The cover reads "Volume 5" but the contents page reads "Volume 16 — 6." — both as written.
- The O.C.'s signature at the foot of the June sheet is not read with confidence (it appears to read "J. O. Lawson" [uncertain]) and is recorded here as [uncertain].
- The handwritten "268" in the left margin of the title page is a file/frame mark, not part of the diary text.
- Names read as: Lieut. C. P. Colville (Paymaster).
- No named casualties are recorded in this volume. No map coordinates (latitude/longitude) are given; the new dump is given as a sheet reference only.
