# War Diary — 3rd Canadian Divisional Supply Column — June 1917

- **Unit:** 3rd Canadian Divisional Supply Column, Canadian Corps (C.A.S.C.)
- **Month:** June 1917 (Volume 17) — this volume covers 1 to 30 June 1917
- **Source:** Library and Archives Canada, Collection Search Item ID 2005012 (RG 9 III-D-3, vol. 5021, file 776, microfilm reel T-10905; finding aid 9-52)
- **Pages:** June 1917, p. 1 – p. 3 (image files page-0063 – page-0065 of the 102-page diary)
- **June 1917 — COMPLETE** (pages 1–3 of 102): cover, contents, diary text 1–30 June, no appendices. Transcribed 2026-10-27.

## Transcription conventions
Original spelling, punctuation and abbreviations kept. The diary text is typewritten throughout (Army Form C. 2118, "WAR DIARY or INTELLIGENCE SUMMARY"). `[uncertain]` marks words or figures I could not read with confidence — never guessed. The diary gives no map references and no latitude/longitude coordinates. "O.C." = Officer Commanding. The Place column gives the Column's location for the whole sheet ("Olhain"). The Remarks column carries a repeated handwritten initialled mark against most entries; it is noted in the open questions rather than repeated against every entry. Two narrative lines on the sheet carry no date of their own; each is transcribed in its sheet position and noted below.

## Open questions / uncertain items (June 1917)
- 9–10 June: the line about Lieut. C.H. Rogers joining the Column from England, vice Lieut. A.B. Anderson to England, carries no date of its own but ends "9-6-17"; it is transcribed between the 9 and 10 June rows and carried under 9 June in the index.
- 23–24 June: the line about Major H.O. Lawson returning from leave to England carries no date; it is transcribed between the 23 and 24 June rows and carried under 23 June in the index.
- 14 June: the original reads "rations.." (double full stop); transcribed as written.
- 12 June: the date is written "12-6-17." with a trailing full stop; transcribed as written.
- The repeated mark in the Remarks column reads "A.R.A." [uncertain] for 1–8 June, "J.H.B." [uncertain] for 9–23 June, and "W.T." [uncertain] from 24 June — apparently the checking officer's initials.
- The contents page gives the volume as "Volume 17 — 6." while the cover gives "VOLUME 17"; both are recorded as written (here the two agree on the number).
- Names read as: Lieut. C. H. Rogers; Lieut. A. B. Anderson; Major H. O. Lawson.
- No casualties are recorded in this volume.
