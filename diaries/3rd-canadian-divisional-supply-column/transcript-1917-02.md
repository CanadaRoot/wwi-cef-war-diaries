# War Diary — 3rd Canadian Divisional Supply Column — February 1917

- **Unit:** 3rd Canadian Divisional Supply Column, Canadian Corps (C.A.S.C.)
- **Month:** February 1917 (Volume 13) — this volume covers 1 to 28 February 1917
- **Source:** Library and Archives Canada, Collection Search Item ID 2005012 (RG 9 III-D-3, vol. 5021, file 776, microfilm reel T-10905; finding aid 9-52)
- **Pages:** February 1917, p. 1 – p. 3 (image files page-0050 – page-0052 of the 102-page diary)
- **February 1917 — COMPLETE** (pages 1–3 of 102): cover, contents, diary text 1–28 February, no appendices. Transcribed 2026-10-27.

## Transcription conventions
Original spelling, punctuation and abbreviations kept. The diary text is typewritten throughout (Army Form C. 2118, "WAR DIARY or INTELLIGENCE SUMMARY"). `[uncertain]` marks words or figures I could not read with confidence — never guessed. The diary gives no map references and no latitude/longitude coordinates. "C.D.S.C." = Canadian Divisional Supply Column; "O.C." = Officer Commanding. The Place column gives the Column's location for the whole sheet ("Olhain."). The Remarks column carries a repeated handwritten initialled mark against most entries; it is noted in the open questions rather than repeated against every entry. Rows for 1–5 February end "rations;" (semicolon) in the original; later rows end "rations." — both reproduced as written.

## Open questions / uncertain items (February 1917)
- 6 February: the men's figure reads 25837, but the fourth digit may be 9 (25937); marked [uncertain].
- 13 February: the horses' figure reads 5810, but the second digit may be 5 (5510); marked [uncertain].
- 24 February: the men's figure reads 25739, but the fourth digit may be 9 (25799); marked [uncertain].
- 16 February: the railhead is written "Provin-Capelle Railhead" — the April sheet writes the same place "Frevin Capelle" (see transcript-1917-04.md). The two readings are recorded as written; which is correct is not resolved here.
- The repeated mark in the Remarks column is read as "W.T." [uncertain] — apparently the checking officer's initials.
- The signing officer's signature at the foot of the sheet is not legible beyond an apparent "W. T." [uncertain]; the printed rank is Major, O.C. 3rd Canadian Divisional Supply Column.
- The contents page gives the volume as "Volume 17 — 2." while the cover gives "Volume 13"; both are recorded as written.
- Names read as: Captain Ellard; Lieut. Driver.
- No casualties are recorded in this volume.
