# War Diary — 3rd Canadian Divisional Supply Column — April 1918

- **Unit:** 3rd Canadian Divisional Supply Column, 3rd Canadian Division
- **Month:** April 1918 (Volume 27) — this volume covers 1 to 14 April 1918; the diary ends on 14 April 1918
- **Source:** Library and Archives Canada, Collection Search Item ID 2005012 (RG 9 III-D-3, vol. 5021, file 776, microfilm reel T-10905, finding aid 9-52)
- **Pages:** April 1918, p. 1 – p. 3 (image files page-0100 – page-0102 of the 102-page diary)
- **April 1918 — COMPLETE** (pages 100–102 of 102): cover, contents, diary text 1–14 April, no appendices. There is no separate title page in this month's volume. Transcribed 2026-10-27.

## Transcription conventions
Original spelling, punctuation and abbreviations kept, including the typist's errors "artions" for "rations" in the 12 April entry and "AERLES" in the 3 April move entry (see open questions). The diary text is a single typewritten Army Form C. 2118 sheet ("WAR DIARY or INTELLIGENCE SUMMARY" — the printed instruction "(Erase heading not required.)" appears beneath the heading; neither heading is struck through). `[uncertain]` marks words or figures I could not read with confidence — never guessed. The form reverts to the earlier layout with the first column headed "Place | Date | Hour". "men's" and "horses'" are as typed throughout. A repeated initialled mark appears in the Remarks column against most entries; it is noted in the open questions rather than repeated against every entry. The sheet is signed at the foot "3rd. Canadian Divisional Supply Column." with "Captain," beside it; the signature itself is [uncertain]. No map references or latitude/longitude coordinates are given.

## The end of the unit
On 14 April 1918 the 3rd Canadian Divisional Supply Column was absorbed, together with the 3rd Canadian Divisional Ammunition Sub-Park, into No. 3 Canadian Divisional Mechanical Transport Company (No. 3 Canadian Divisional M. T. Coy.), under the command of Major E. M. Harris, on the reorganization of Canadian Corps Mechanical Transport ordered by C.H.Q. letter No. 6712 (QA1) dated 28 March 1918. The cover of this volume carries the handwritten note: "This unit ceased to exist on 14–4–18; see entry in Diary for that date." This is the last diary in the set (page 102 of 102).

## Open questions / uncertain items (April 1918)
- 3 April: the move entry reads "Unit moved from HAILLICOURT to AERLES" [uncertain] — the destination is written "AERLES", while the Place column from 4 April gives "BERLES". The two spellings are as written; the destination may be Berles, but it is not corrected here.
- 6 April: the men's figure (34611) is well above the ~25,000–29,000 of surrounding days; transcribed as written — the diary gives no explanation.
- 11 April: the whole entry is struck through and no rations figures are legible; transcribed as "[Entry struck through — no rations figures legible] [uncertain]".
- 13–14 April: the dates on the last rations rows are struck through and overwritten. Two rations entries appear to be dated 13 April (32410 men's / 7284 horses', Dull; and 32083 men's / 7327 horses', Rain), and one dated 14 April (32163 men's / 7587 horses', Dull); the word "Issued" in the 14 April rations entry is overstruck. All are transcribed as read, with [uncertain] against the dates and the overstruck word.
- 14 April: the reorganisation entry spells the new unit "No. 3 Canadian Divisional M. T. Coy." — i.e. the 3rd Canadian Divisional Mechanical Transport Company named in the task brief.
- The cover gives the volume as "VOLUME 27" while the contents page gives "Volume 18 – 4." — both are as written.
- No casualties are recorded in this volume.
