# War Diary — 5th Canadian Reserve Battalion (Central Ontario) — July 1917

- **Unit:** 5th Canadian Reserve Battalion (Central Ontario)
- **Month:** July 1917 (Volume No. 4)
- **Source:** Library and Archives Canada, Collection Search Item ID 2004673, "War diaries - 5th Reserve Battalion" (RG 9 III-D-3, vol. 4950, folder 475; finding aid 9-52)
- **Pages:** July 1917, p. 1 – p. 3 (image files page-0012 – page-0014 of the 23-page diary)
- **July 1917 — COMPLETE** (3 pages): cover, diary text 1–31 July, no appendices. Transcribed 2026-10-27.

## Transcription conventions

Original spelling, punctuation and abbreviations kept. Typed diary text throughout (Army Form C. 2118, "WAR DIARY or INTELLIGENCE SUMMARY"). `[uncertain]` marks words I could not read with confidence — never guessed. See transcript-1917-04.md for the abbreviation glossary. "R & Q" / "Q & R" = as written (presumably Rations and Quarters — not verified). The Remarks column on every diary sheet carries a repeated handwritten initial mark; it is noted here rather than repeated against every entry.

## Notes

- **Every day in July has an entry** — no date gaps.
- **9 July:** "from O.C.C. [uncertain] Bexhill" — the typed letters read "O.C.C."; the 7 July entry reads "O. T. C. Bexhill", so this may be the same body with a faintly typed "T" — flagged [uncertain] rather than corrected.
- **17 and 22 July:** "attached for R & Q" / "detached for Q & R" — transcribed as written; the expansion (Rations and Quarters) is not verified.
- **Command:** the July diary is signed by the same officer who signed June as Major, now as Lt-Col. (signature reads "J. D. McCrimmon" [uncertain]).
- **Strength check:** 1 July strength (102 / 1,609) matches the 30 June strength in transcript-1917-06.md.
- **No casualties** are recorded in July 1917.
