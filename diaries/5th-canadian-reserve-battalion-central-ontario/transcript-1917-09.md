## Transcription conventions

Original spelling, punctuation and abbreviations kept. Typed diary text throughout (Army Form C. 2118, "WAR DIARY or INTELLIGENCE SUMMARY"). `[uncertain]` marks words I could not read with confidence — never guessed. See transcript-1917-04.md for the abbreviation glossary. The Remarks column on every diary sheet carries a repeated handwritten initial mark (read as "app." [uncertain]); it is noted here rather than repeated against every entry.

## Notes

- **Every day in September has an entry** — no date gaps.
- **5 September:** six officers revert from Captain to Lieutenant (reversion in rank — no names given).
- **14 September:** battalion reorganized to a 5-company basis (from 6 companies on 7 August — see transcript-1917-08.md); 4 signallers struck off strength to the 2nd Battalion, France.
- **23 September:** 20 snipers struck off strength on proceeding to France.
- **26–28 September:** the battalion (36 officers, 675 other ranks) went on brigade manoeuvres and returned.
- **29–30 September:** air raid actions over West Sandling on two successive nights (18:10 on the 29th to 01:00 on the 30th; 19:00–22:00 on the 30th) — no casualties in this battalion either night. These are the first air raids recorded in this diary.
- **Command:** the September diary is signed by a Major (signature reads "R. T. Lane" [uncertain]) as O.C., not by Lt-Col. McCrimmon [uncertain] who signed July and August.
- **No strength return** is given for 30 September, and no casualties are recorded in September 1917.
