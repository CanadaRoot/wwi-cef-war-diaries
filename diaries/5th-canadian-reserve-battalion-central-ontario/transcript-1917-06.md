# War Diary — 5th Canadian Reserve Battalion (Central Ontario) — June 1917

- **Unit:** 5th Canadian Reserve Battalion (Central Ontario)
- **Month:** June 1917 (Volume No. 3)
- **Source:** Library and Archives Canada, Collection Search Item ID 2004673, "War diaries - 5th Reserve Battalion" (RG 9 III-D-3, vol. 4950, folder 475; finding aid 9-52)
- **Pages:** June 1917, p. 1 – p. 3 (image files page-0009 – page-0011 of the 23-page diary)
- **June 1917 — COMPLETE** (3 pages): cover, diary text 1–30 June, no appendices. Transcribed 2026-10-27.

## Transcription conventions

Original spelling, punctuation and abbreviations kept. Typed diary text throughout (Army Form C. 2118, "WAR DIARY or INTELLIGENCE SUMMARY"). `[uncertain]` marks words I could not read with confidence — never guessed. See transcript-1917-04.md for the abbreviation glossary. The Remarks column on every diary sheet carries a repeated handwritten initial mark (read as "app." [uncertain]); it is noted here rather than repeated against every entry.

## Notes

- **Every day in June has an entry** — no date gaps.
- **3 June:** the word "One" at the start of the entry is overtyped (struck and re-typed) in the original.
- **7 June:** "Sea Bathing for Units in this Brigade, commences" — sea bathing for the brigade begins; the same entry records 168 other ranks transferred to the 164th Battalion, Witley.
- **11 June:** "C.O.M.F." is transcribed as written; "Auth. A.O.22-2-12." is transcribed as written (the reference is not expanded — its meaning is not verified).
- **Command:** the June diary is signed by a Major (signature reads "J. D. McCrimmon" [uncertain]) as O.C., not by Lt-Col. G. T. Chisholm who signed April and May — Chisholm was presumably absent; the same signature, now as Lt-Col., signs July and August.
- **Strength check:** 1 June strength (133 / 1,804) matches the 31 May strength in transcript-1917-05.md; 30 June strength (102 / 1,609) matches the 1 July strength in transcript-1917-07.md.
- **No casualties** are recorded in June 1917.
