# War Diary — 5th Canadian Reserve Battalion (Central Ontario) — April 1917

- **Unit:** 5th Canadian Reserve Battalion (Central Ontario)
- **Month:** April 1917 (Volume No. 1)
- **Source:** Library and Archives Canada, Collection Search Item ID 2004673, "War diaries - 5th Reserve Battalion" (RG 9 III-D-3, vol. 4950, folder 475; finding aid 9-52). Record date range 1917/04/01–1918/02/15; the digitized manifest for this record contains only Volumes 1–7 (April–October 1917) — see Notes.
- **Pages:** April 1917, p. 1 – p. 5 (image files page-0001 – page-0005 of the 23-page diary)
- **April 1917 — COMPLETE** (5 pages): false docket, cover, diary text 1–30 April, no appendices. Transcribed 2026-10-27.

## Transcription conventions

Original spelling, punctuation and abbreviations kept. Typed diary text throughout (Army Form C. 2118, "WAR DIARY or INTELLIGENCE SUMMARY"). `[uncertain]` marks words I could not read with confidence — never guessed. "do" = ditto. "O/Ranks" = Other Ranks. "Res: Bn:" = Reserve Battalion. "C.O.R.D." = as written (not expanded). "C.C.D." = as written. "C.A.P.C." = as written. "C.M.G. Depot" = Canadian Machine Gun Depot (Crowborough). "Bde. O." = Brigade Order. "R.O." = Routine Order. "D.O." = Divisional Order [uncertain]. "O.T.C." = Officers Training Corps. The Remarks column on every diary sheet carries a repeated handwritten initial mark (read as "Jas." [uncertain]); it is noted here rather than repeated against every entry. No map coordinates are given in the diary; places are recorded as written.

## Notes

- **Dates with no diary entry (genuine gaps in the original):** 6th, 8th and 29th April.
- **5 April:** the Capt. A. R. Robertson appointment line is struck through in full in the original — transcribed as struck through, not as a live entry. "Usual Sunday Routine carried on, on Good Friday" is dated the 5th in the diary, but Good Friday 1917 fell on 6 April, not 5 April — transcribed as written.
- **27 April:** no date appears in the date column for the "Ninety Other Ranks" entry; assigned to the 27th on the strength of its position between the 26th and 28th entries.
- **28 April:** "Crowboro." is truncated at the right margin in the original (for Crowborough) — transcribed as written.
- **Strength check:** 1 April strength (115 officers, 1,360 other ranks) to 30 April strength (98 officers, 935 other ranks) — the month's drafts to France (15th and 20th Battalions) account for the fall. The 30 April strength (98 / 935) matches the 1 May strength in transcript-1917-05.md.
- **No casualties** are recorded in April 1917; the two "returned casualty" officers (24th, 25th) are officers returning from France after recovering, not new casualties.
