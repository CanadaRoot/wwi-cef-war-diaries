## Pages 2–3 — Diary text (1–31 May 1917)

Army Form C. 2118, "WAR DIARY or INTELLIGENCE SUMMARY". Heading on each sheet: "ORIGINAL 5th Canadian Reserve Battalion." Place throughout: West Sandling.

> **West Sandling, 1st** — Strength. Officers 98. Other Ranks. 935.
>

>
> **4th** — One Officer proceeded to the 3rd Res: Battalion. West Sandling. 50 Other Ranks proceeded to the 15th Battalion. France. Muster Parade held.
>

>
> **7th** — One Officer transferred from 2nd Reserve Battalion. West Sandling. Two Officer Cadets taken on strength. One Officer transferred to the 160th Battalion. Witley.

## Notes

- **Dates with no diary entry (genuine gaps in the original):** 3rd, 5th, 10th, 13th, 14th, 17th, 18th, 22nd, 24th, 25th and 30th May.
- **9 May:** the battalion absorbed the 253rd Battalion (17 officers, 459 other ranks) and the 241st Battalion (21 officers, 625 other ranks) — the two drafts that lift the strength from 98 / 935 to 133 / 1,804 by 31 May. This matches the LAC guide history (absorbed 241st Battalion 7 May 1917 and 253rd Battalion in May 1917).
- **20 May:** the 253rd and 241st drafts were inspected by the Officer Commanding Troops, Shorncliffe.
- **26 May:** "One Officer (casualty) posted from 1st C.O.R.D." — a returned casualty posted in, not a new casualty.
- **Strength check:** 1 May strength (98 / 935) matches the 30 April strength in transcript-1917-04.md; 31 May strength (133 / 1,804) matches the 1 June strength in transcript-1917-06.md.
- **No casualties** are recorded in May 1917.
