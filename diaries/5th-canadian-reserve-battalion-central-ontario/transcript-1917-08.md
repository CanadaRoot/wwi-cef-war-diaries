- **Pages:** August 1917, p. 1 – p. 3 (image files page-0015 – page-0017 of the 23-page diary)
- **August 1917 — COMPLETE** (3 pages): cover, diary text 1–31 August, no appendices. Transcribed 2026-10-27.

## Transcription conventions

Original spelling, punctuation and abbreviations kept. Typed diary text throughout (Army Form C. 2118, "WAR DIARY or INTELLIGENCE SUMMARY"). `[uncertain]` marks words I could not read with confidence — never guessed. See transcript-1917-04.md for the abbreviation glossary. The Remarks column on every diary sheet carries a repeated handwritten initial mark; it is noted here rather than repeated against every entry.

## Notes

- **Every day in August has an entry** — no date gaps.
- **7 August:** battalion re-organized from an 8-company basis to a 6-company basis (re-organized again to a 5-company basis on 14 September — see transcript-1917-09.md).
- **16 August:** the date column shows both "16" and "15" for the "15 Other Ranks taken on strength" entry — one of the two dates is a correction; indexed as 16 August with the ambiguity noted here.
- **19 August:** an Honorary Captain and Chaplain was taken on strength — the only chaplain mentioned in this diary; no name is given.
- **20 August:** 16 officers returned from the Officers Training Battalion, Seaford, to proceed to France.
- **30 August:** one officer struck off strength to the R.F.C. (Royal Flying Corps).
- **Strength check:** 1 August strength (95 officers, 1,640 other ranks) does not follow from the 1 July strength (102 / 1,609) — the July diary gives no month-end strength return, so the change over July (net −7 officers, +31 other ranks) cannot be reconciled from the diary text alone; transcribed as written.
- **No casualties** are recorded in August 1917.
