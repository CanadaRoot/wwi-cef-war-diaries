## Transcription conventions

Original spelling, punctuation and abbreviations kept. Typed diary text throughout (Army Form C. 2118, "WAR DIARY or INTELLIGENCE SUMMARY"). `[uncertain]` marks words I could not read with confidence — never guessed. See transcript-1917-04.md for the abbreviation glossary. The Remarks column on every diary sheet carries a repeated handwritten initial mark; it is noted here rather than repeated against every entry.

## Notes

- **Date with no diary entry (genuine gap in the original):** 23rd October.
- **Air raids:** 1, 2, 6 and 9 October — air raid actions over West Sandling; the 1 October raid ran 16:45–23:30 with no casualties in this battalion. Together with 29–30 September, this diary records six air-raid nights in just over a month.
- **17 October:** one officer struck off strength to the Imperial Salvage Corps, France.
- **No strength return and no signature** appear on the October sheets — unlike April–September, the month ends without a signed strength statement.
- **No casualties** are recorded in October 1917.
- **End of the digitized record:** Volume 7 (October 1917) is the last volume in the LAC manifest for Item ID 2004673. The record's date range runs to 1918/02/15 and the false docket (April 1917, p. 1) reads "APRIL, 1917 TO JANUARY, 1918", so volumes for November 1917 – January/February 1918 presumably exist in the archive but are NOT in this digitized record — see the diary README.
