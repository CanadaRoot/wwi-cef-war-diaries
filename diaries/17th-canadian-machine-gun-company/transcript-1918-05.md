## Notes on May 1918 — CENSORED PAGES
- The May volume is Volume 3 of the new France numbering, covering 1–31 May 1918.
- **The diary text for May 1918 is not readable in the LAC images.** On pages 106–107 every line of typed text — including the Place, Date and Hour columns — has been blacked out with heavy black ink (military censorship of operational content, applied before the diary was filed). The stamps (Orderly Room 31 May 1918; Camp Commandant 31 May 1918), the form headings and the initialled remarks column survive, but no entry can be read.
- Page 108 (the last diary page) is worse: the text has faded or been erased to near-invisibility, leaving only faint specks; only Capt. G. Black's signature and the blacked-out "17th Canadian Machine Gun Company" line survive.
- Contrast enhancement of the images was tried and confirms the text is not recoverable from the digitized files — the blacking-out is in the source images, not a download or rendering fault.
- **No index entries can be extracted for May 1918.** Nothing has been invented or guessed.
- A few words at the ends of lines on page 107 escaped the blacking-out and are partially legible, but their dates are blacked out so they cannot be attributed to any day: "Church Parade and Drill."; a passage mentioning "the Officer Commanding Canadian Corps Machine Gun Companies" allowing "all available" men "to attend the Corps Sports"; and a line ending "Drill and … Parade." These fragments are recorded here only as evidence of what survived, not as dated entries.
