## Notes on November 1917
- November is kept as a terse day-by-day summary table by Capt. G. Black (signed "G. Black, Capt."), not the usual timed programme. Both diary-text pages bear an "ORDERLY ROOM 17th CANADIAN MACHINE GUN COY 4 DEC. 1917" stamp, and every row is initialled in the remarks column (illegible).
- The abbreviations "F.T.T." and "P.T.T." are as written (presumably field training and physical training).
- 15–16 November: firing/practice at Marehill Ranges. 23 November: barrage practice on Thursley Common. 27 November: march to Longmoor Ranges, with practice there 28–30 November.
- "Holliday" is spelled as written throughout November.
