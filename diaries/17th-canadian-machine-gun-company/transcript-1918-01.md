- **No February 1918 diary exists in this file** — the next pages (95–98) are March 1918. See note below.
- March 1918 onwards — not yet transcribed. Next: page 95 (March 1918, p. 1).

## Notes on January 1918
- January continues the terse day-by-day summary style of November–December, kept by Capt. G. Black (signed "G. Black, Capt."). Both diary-text pages bear an Orderly Room stamp dated February 1918 (14 Feb. on p. 3, 2- Feb. on p. 4), and every row is initialled in the remarks column (illegible).
- The date "2" in the date column is written in a form resembling "v" — transcribed as 2 January by sequence.
- 1 January: New Year's Day holiday. 4 January: demonstrations of the effect of gas on a dugout. 9 and 30 January: the company was paid. 29 January: Divisional Route March.
- **February 1918 is missing from the diary.** The January volume ends 31 January 1918 and the next volume (pp. 95–98) is March 1918. The filed HQ Canadian Troops extract (see `transcript-1917-02.md`, p. 3) explains why: the 17th, 18th and 19th Machine Gun Companies were disbanded early in February 1918 and no war diary could be obtained from them.
