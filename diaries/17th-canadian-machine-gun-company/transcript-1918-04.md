## Notes on April 1918
- April 1918 is typed (Volume 2 of the new France numbering). Each day's entry is split into "A" Battery, "B" Battery and Rear H.Qrs. sub-entries. The "or INTELLIGENCE SUMMARY" heading is struck through on every page.
- The company is in the line in France (Vimy Ridge / Lens sector, then Liévin). Rear H.Qrs. is at Ecurie until 13 April, then at Les Brebis.
- 1 April: two enemy aeroplanes brought down four of our observation balloons in flames; the company fired 125 rounds at the planes without result. Our troops projected gas at 3.00 a.m.
- 5 April: No. 650044 Pte. T. G. Brown slipped during drill and ruptured himself; sent to hospital.
- 11 April: both batteries moved from the Vimy sector to the Lens sector (Liévin) to relieve the 46th Imperial Machine Gun Battalion; relief completed 11.30 p.m.
- 13 April: a direct enemy hit on "B" Battery's gun position destroyed a tripod, a box of 24 Mills bombs, 2 belt boxes and a condenser bag; no casualties. The 13 April entry also records Capt. Black ordered to report elsewhere (the typed destinations are struck through and corrected by hand to "for 2 days" in each case).
- 14 April: a gas bombardment slightly gassed 2 officers and 3 other ranks of the 42nd Battalion, 3rd Division, and broke one N.C.O.'s leg.
- 22 April: gas alarm at 2.00 a.m.; a tripod and 5 boxes of ammunition hit by shrapnel; no casualties.
- 26–30 April: the company is out of the line at Les Brebis, running a machine-gun training course (10 officers and 60 other ranks attached on 26 April, 25 more on 27 April; a special instruction class under Lt. Radford, Lt. Goodwin and 15 N.C.O.s from 28 April).

## Page 101 — Diary text, 1–6 April 1918 (April 1918, p. 3)

Army Form C. 2118 (typed form 2449). Stamped: ORDERLY ROOM 30 APR 1918; CAMP COMMANDANT 30 APR 1918 19-00 CANADIAN CORPS. The "or INTELLIGENCE SUMMARY" heading is struck through.

### 1 April 1918 — Ecurie — 10.00 a.m.

> "A" and "B" Batteries still up the line, in reserve positions. "A" Battery reports more or less quiet on its front, with occasional shelling. Anti/air Craft positions selected. Our troops projected gas at 3.00 a.m. "B" Battery reports large quantity of pamphlets in foreign language, presumably German, drifted overhead towards enemy line. At 5.55 p.m. two enemy aeroplanes attacked and brought down in flames four of our observation balloons. Fired 125 rounds at planes but no results observed. Nothing further of special interest to report. Rear H. Qrs. Short periods of drill was carried out during the day. An inspection of the Coy. lines by the A/O.C. 5th Cdn. M. G. Bn. was made.

### 2 April 1918 — Ecurie — 10.00 a.m.

> "A" Battery — O. C. Company and O. C. "A" Battery reconnoitred machine gun positions on Vimy Ridge line between railway embankment and Goulet wood. No. 7 Gun occupied new position. Ammunition supply sufficient to make each supply approximately 11,000 rounds was carried up. The day was particularly quiet. "B" Battery — All men at work improving quarters and digging trenches. Rear H. Qrs. Two hours drill was carried out during the day. The remainder of the day being occupied fatigue work & Coy. Transport lines.

### 3 April 1918 — Ecurie — 10.00 a.m.

> "A" Battery — No. 7 Gun position was completed at B2 b09.40 [uncertain]. The front was quiet. One gun was moved from No. 7 position to V15 position without incident. "B" Battery — Work was continued on improving the quarters and digging the trenches. Rear H. Qrs. — Fatigue party of all available ranks was sent to Advanced H. Qrs.

### 4 April 1918 — Ecurie — 10.00 a.m.

> "A" Battery — A heavy bombardment took place on the Grand Trunk trench, near Railway embankment. Shelling lasted three quarters of an hour and was intense. A surprisingly large number of shells sent over by the enemy were "duds". No gas was used. Nothing else of importance to report. "B" Battery — Heavy [uncertain] firing took place south of our position at 4.45 a.m. and continued till 7.00 a.m. Rear H. Qrs. — Fatigue party of all available ranks was sent to Advanced H. Qrs.

### 5 April 1918 — Ecurie — 10.00 a.m.

> "A" Battery — In complying with D.M. G. O's order No. D.304-2-4-18 rapid bringing of guns into action was practised. During this drill No. 650044 Pte. Brown T. G. slipped and ruptured himself and was sent to hospital. Grand Trunk trench was again heavily shelled. "B" Battery — Everything quite on our front; nothing of importance to report. Rear H. Qrs. — Drill was carried out throughout the day.

### 6 April 1918 — Ecurie — 10.00 a.m.

> "A" Battery — Preparations were made for relief of "A" Battery by "B" Battery. Guides were despatched through the barrier at 7.00 p.m. One officer reported to "B" Battery H. Qrs. RED MILL locality at 6.00 p.m. to take over from O. C. Battery. Relief completed at 9.30 a.m. "B" Battery — Prepared for intercompany relief. Party sent to meet tunnelling officer to work on his orders. Relief of "A" Battery completed at 9.55 a.m. Rear H. Qrs. — The A/O. C. 5th Corps M. G. Corps inspected lines of both transport and company. The day was spent in a general clean up.

Signed at foot of page: G. Black, Capt.

## Page 102 — Diary text, 7–14 April 1918 (April 1918, p. 4)

### 7 April 1918 — Ecurie — 10.00 a.m.

> "A" Battery — "A" Battery at RED MILL locality. The day being spent in cleaning equipment and repairing dugouts. "B" Battery — Heavy artillery fire from the north of our position 1.00 a.m. Received orders re gas projection all precautions taken. Rear H. Qrs. — Church parade held at 10.00 a.m.

### 8 April 1918 — Ecurie — 10.00 a.m.

> "A" Battery — Spent the day in repairing shelters and dug outs and several reliefs were supplied to a Tunnelling Coy. "B" Battery — All quiet on our front. Nothing to report. Rear H. Qrs. — Period of drills was carried out during the day.

### 9 April 1918 — Ecurie — 10.00 a.m.

> "A" Battery — The day was spent in continuing the repair of shelters and dugouts, relief being sent to the Tunnelling Company. "B" Battery — Heavy shelling at and to the right of our position. Numerous tear gas shells being sent over by the enemy. Rear H. Qrs. — The day was devoted to fatigues; Company lines and Transport.

### 10 April 1918 — Ecurie — 10.00 a.m.

> "A" Battery — Continued the repairing of shelters and dug outs throughout the day. "B" Battery — All quiet on our front, nothing of special interest to report. Rear H. Qrs. — Bathing parade for all ranks in reserve party was held. Drill and fatigues balance of day.

### 11 April 1918 — Ecurie — 10.00 a.m.

> "A" Battery — Battery received orders to proceed to Lievin front to relieve the 46th Imperial Machine Gun Battalion. The day was spent in moving from Vimy to LENS Sector. Relief was completed at 11.30 p.m. "A" Battery taking up the right sector of this defence. "B" Battery — Left RED MILL locality at 4.00 p.m. and marched to LIEVIN where we relieved the 46th Imperial Mach. Gun Bn. at 11.30 p.m. Rear H. Qrs. — Limbers were sent to meet "B" Battery in order to effect relief. Four limbers were sent to O. C. Coy to effect the relief of "A" Battery.

### 12 April 1918 — Ecurie — 10.00 a.m.

> "A" Battery — A few shells were sent over by enemy, apart from this the front was quiet. "B" Battery — Lewis Guns in our vicinity engaged three hostile planes without results. Rear H. Qrs. — The day was devoted to cleaning of transport and transport lines.

### 13 April 1918 — Ecurie — 10.00 a.m.

> "A" Battery — All positions in the right defence were reconnoitred, also ammunition and water supply dumps and dressing stations. All was quiet on our front. Adv.H.Q. — Capt. Black O.C. ordered to report to [struck through in original] for 2 days, [struck through in original] for 2 days. "B" Battery — Heavy intermittent shelling. Direct enemy hit on our gun position, destroying one tripod, one box of 24 Mills bombs, 2 belt boxes and 1 condenser bag. No casualties. Rear H. Qrs. — On orders received, Rear H. Qrs. was moved from Ecurie to LES BREBIS, arriving at destination about 12.00 noon and were comfortably quartered.

### 14 April 1918 — Les Brebis — 10.00 a.m.

> "A" Battery — Enemy shelled our area intermittently all day, with occasional gas shell. During the evening night firing was indulged in on targets allotted by group commander. "B" Battery — Large number of Gas shells was put over by enemy. About 4.15 p.m. resulted in 2 officers and 3 other ranks of the 42nd Batt. 3rd Div., being slightly gassed. 1 N.C.O. having a leg broken.

Signed at foot of page: G. Black, Capt.

## Page 103 — Diary text, 14 (continued)–23 April 1918 (April 1918, p. 5)

### 14 April 1918 (continued) — Les Brebis

> Rear H. Qrs. — The day was devoted to cleaning up quarters and stables taken over the day previous.

### 15 April 1918 — Les Brebis — 10.00 a.m.

> "A" Battery — The day was quiet. Night firing from 8.30 to 11.30. "B" Battery — The day was quiet, nothing unusual to report. Rear H. Qrs. — The day was spent in fatigue work and the cleaning of transport.

### 16 April 1918 — Les Brebis — 10.00 a.m.

> "A" Battery — was reinforced by 1 officer from reserve at LES BREBIS. Harassing fire on enemy's rear areas was carried out by 3 guns of "A" Battery. Shelling was fairly heavy during the day but no damage was done. "B" Battery — Conditions normal on our front. Rear H. Qrs. — Cleaning of transport, erection of kitchen and the gathering of fuel.

### 17 April 1918 — Les Brebis — 10.00 a.m.

> "A" Battery — The relieving officers were taken over the various positions and a system of relief was decided upon. Guns from 3 positions brought harassing fire to bear on enemy refilling centres in their rear area. "B" Battery — Enemy artillery inactive. Our own artillery put on an intense bombardment of enemy position from 3.30 to 5.30 a.m. Carried harassing fire this p.m. on special target fired 5000 rounds. Rear H. Qrs. — The day was devoted to drill and fatigues.

### 18 April 1918 — Les Brebis — 10.00 a.m.

> "A" Battery — Enemy exceptionally quiet throughout the day. Guide from each position were at Company H. Qrs. at 8.00 p.m. to guide in relieving crews. Relief completed without incident. E. & F. Battery 3rd M.G. Bn. "B" Battery — Received orders that Battery would be relieved this P.M. 4 of "B" Battery guns on relief returned to Coy. Rear H. Qrs. LES BREBIS. The 4 remaining guns quartered at CITE ST PIERRE under Lieut. Goodwin and McLennan, with crews from Coy. Rear H. Qrs. Rear H. Qrs. — All available ranks sent to relieve "B" Battery.

### 19 April 1918 — Les Brebis — 10.00 a.m.

> "A" and "B" Batteries with 12 guns returned to Coy. Rear H. Qrs. LES BREBIS, and went into billets. The 4 guns remaining in reserve at CITE ST PIERRE under Lieuts. Goodwin and McLennan are in reserve at H. Qrs. of 5th M. G. Coy. Lts. Goodwin & McLennan reconnoitred for gun positions. Rear H. Qrs. — The day was spent in re-organizing and re-equiping of men and straightening out of billets.

### 20 April 1918 — Les Brebis — 10.00 a.m.

> Lt. Goodwin reports all quiet from his reserve position. Rear H. Qrs. — The company was inspected by the Officer Commanding and drill carried out throughout the day.

### 21 April 1918 — Les Brebis — 10.00 a.m.

> Lt. Goodwin reports all quiet from his reserve position. Rear H. Qrs. — Drill was carried on throughout the day. The men being paid in the afternoon.

### 22 April 1918 — Les Brebis — 10.00 a.m.

> Lt. Goodwin reports from reserve position, gas alarm at 2.00 a.m. billets shelled intermittently all day. One tripod and 5 boxes of ammunition hit by enemy shrapnel. No casualties. Rear H. Qrs. Bathing parade and drill carried on with.

### 23 April 1918 — Les Brebis — 10.00 a.m.

> Lt. Goodwin reports from his reserve position all quiet. Rear H. Qrs. Drill and general routine.

Signed at foot of page: G. Black, Capt.

## Page 104 — Diary text, 24–30 April 1918 (April 1918, p. 6)

### 24 April 1918 — Les Brebis — 10.00 a.m.

> 4 Guns under Lieut. Goodwin, in reserve at CITE ST PIERRE relieved by 4 Lewis Guns. Officers and gun crews joined remainder of Battery at Rear H. Qrs. Rear H. Qrs. [one line struck through in original, illegible] Company Route March was held in the morning and drill in the afternoon.

### 25 April 1918 — Les Brebis — 10.00 a.m.

> Coy. H. Qrs. FOSS 2, Capt. Black O. C., 6 Officers 12 N. C. O's left for reconnoitering of gun positions on the left flank of the Cdn. Corps., the support positions occupied by the 3rd M. G. Bn. Company Drill, Musketry, Physical Training, Anti-Gas Drill and Immediate Action was held during the day.

### 26 April 1918 — Les Brebis — 10.00 a.m.

> Drill was carried out the day in Company lines. 10 Officers and 60 other ranks were attached to this Company for a course in Machine Gun Training.

### 27 April 1918 — Les Brebis — 10.00 a.m.

> Company Route March and Drill was carried on throughout the day. 25 other ranks reported for instruction in Machine Gun Training.

### 28 April 1918 — Les Brebis — 10.00 a.m.

> Drill as laid down by Company syllabus. The special class of instruction on Machine Gun Training commenced at 8.00 a.m. under Lt. Radford, Lt. Goodwin and 15 N.C.O's.

### 29 April 1918 — Les Brebis — 10.00 a.m.

> Drill as laid down by Company syllabus. All billets occupied by this unit and grounds were thoroughly cleaned.

### 30 April 1918 — Les Brebis — 10.00 a.m.

> Drill as laid down by Company syllabus, including rifle practice on ranges.

Signed at foot of page: G. Black, Captain, Officer Commanding, 17th Canadian Machine Gun Company.
