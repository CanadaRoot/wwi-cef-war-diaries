## Transcription conventions
- Faithful to the original: spelling, punctuation, abbreviations and capitalisation as written.
- `[uncertain]` marks a word or name that could not be read with confidence — never guessed.
- The diary-text pages are Army Form C. 2118, "WAR DIARY or INTELLIGENCE SUMMARY", handwritten in columns: Place | Date | Hour | Summary of Events and Information | Remarks and references to Appendices.
- The "Hour" column is the hour the entry was written up (9 p.m. on weekdays, 1 p.m. on Saturdays/Sundays).
- No map coordinates (latitude/longitude) are given in the diary; places are recorded as written.
- "do." in the Place column means "ditto" (same place as the entry above).

## Page 4 — Diary text, 6–15 February 1917 (February 1917, p. 4)

Army Form C. 2118. Heading: "WAR DIARY or INTELLIGENCE SUMMARY. 17th Canadian Machine Gun Company."

### 6 February 1917 — Witley Camp, Surrey — 9 p.m.

> The Company 53 strong — 9 officers and 44 other ranks — left Crowborough, Sussex at 8.30 a.m. Reported at WITLEY, SURREY, same day and were attached:— 6 Officers to 202nd batt'n [and] [uncertain] ranks to 189th batt'n, for rations and quarters. Taken on strength of 5th Can. Division, (Authy. Hd. Qrs. WITLEY. R.O. No. 680.)

### 7 February 1917 — Witley Camp, Surrey — 9 p.m.

> Company paraded at 9 a.m. in full marching order for Route March under CAPT. GIBSON and returned at noon. The afternoon was occupied in lectures by LT. LAURIE, CAPT. GIBSON, LT. GOODWIN.

### 8 February 1917 — Witley Camp, Surrey — 9 p.m.

> Company paraded at 9 a.m. in full marching order for Route March under CAPT. GIBSON, returning at noon. In the afternoon LTS. WILSON and CHIPMAN lectured, while CAPT. GIBSON gave P.T.

### 9 February 1917 — Witley Camp, Surrey — 1 p.m.

> Company paraded at 8 a.m. in full marching order for Route March under CAPT. GIBSON, returning at noon and finishing for the day.

### 10 February 1917 — Witley Camp, Surrey — 1 p.m.

> Company paraded for Divine Service with 202nd batt'n, under LT. LAURIE

### 11 February 1917 — Witley Camp, Surrey — 9 p.m.

> Company paraded in full marching order for Route March under CAPT. GIBSON. In the afternoon lectures by LT. LYON on "Relief of Trenches", and LT. LAURIE on "Range Cards", and one hour P.T.

### 12 February 1917 — Witley Camp, Surrey — 9 p.m.

> Company paraded for Route March under CAPT. GIBSON. At 2 p.m. the Coy paraded for pay at [uncertain] after which, all ranks moved into new quarters.

### 13 February 1917 — Witley Camp, Surrey — 9 p.m.

> Officers proceeded to Digging area and mapped out Emplacements and Dugout Scheme, while other ranks drew Barrack [uncertain] etc. In the afternoon all ranks commenced the construction of Dugouts and Emplacements in Digging area.

### 14 February 1917 — Witley Camp, Surrey — 9 p.m.

> The whole day was occupied in construction of Dugouts and Emplacements in Digging area. The work was inspected in afternoon by GEN. SIR A. HOWARD K.C.B.

### 15 February 1917 — Witley Camp, Surrey — 9 p.m.

> Company paraded at 8 a.m. under CAPT. GIBSON for Entrenching at THURSLEY COMMON, returning for dinner. The same work in afternoon. Marching order always to be worn to Digging Area.

Signed at foot of page: T. C. Bamfield.

## Page 5 — Diary text, 16–26 February 1917 (February 1917, p. 5)

### 16 February 1917 — Witley Camp, Surrey — 1 p.m.

> Company paraded at 8.30 a.m. and proceeded to digging area under CAPT. GIBSON — returning at 12.30 noon

### 17 February 1917 — Witley Camp, Surrey — 1 p.m.

> Coy paraded for Divine Service with 202nd Batt'n, under LT. WILSON. Attention was again drawn to the fact that no photographs might be taken of troops, military works etc. unless the photographer was in possession of a pass signed by A.P.M. All former passes being cancelled. (Ref:- C.R. a.c. No. 9620. (A))

### 18 February 1917 — Witley Camp, Surrey — 9 p.m.

> The whole day was spent at digging area under CAPT. GIBSON. (Troops marching along roads at night must be preceded by advance party carrying white light and followed by a rear party carrying red light —) [Ref J.C.R. a.c. Q.2675/4 (A)]

### 19 February 1917 — Witley Camp, Surrey — 9 p.m.

> Coy paraded at 8.30 a.m. and 2 p.m. to proceed to Entrenching area.

### 20 February 1917 — Witley Camp, Surrey — 9 p.m.

> Coy paraded at 8.30 a.m. and 1.30 p.m. and proceeded to THURSLEY COMMON, to continue Dugouts etc. returning at 4.30 p.m.

### 21 February 1917 — Witley Camp, Surrey — 9 p.m.

> Coy paraded at 8.30 a.m. and 1.30 p.m., in Marching Order, and proceeded to Entrenching area under CAPT. GIBSON, returning at 4.30 p.m.

### 22 February 1917 — Witley Camp, Surrey — 9 p.m.

> The same work as on the preceding day was carried out, and orders for Anti-gas measures posted.

### 23 February 1917 — Witley Camp, Surrey — 9 p.m.

> The day was occupied digging emplacements etc at Entrenching Area. — returning at 4.30 p.m.

### 24 February 1917 — Witley Camp, Surrey — 1 p.m.

> Coy paraded for Kit inspection, after which the Coy quarters were thoroughly cleaned. Orders from Div. Hqrs [uncertain] regarding Preservation of Boots, was posted.

### 25 February 1917 — Witley Camp, Surrey — 1 p.m.

> Coy paraded for Divine Service with 128th batt'n, under LT. WILSON

### 26 February 1917 — Witley Camp, Surrey — 9 p.m.

> Coy proceeded to Entrenching area and carried on with emplacements and dugouts. In the afternoon the same work as in forenoon.

Signed at foot of page: T. C. Bamfield.

## Page 6 — Diary text, 27 February–5 March 1917 (February 1917, p. 6)

### 27 February 1917 — Witley Camp, Surrey — 9 p.m.

> At 9 a.m. there was a Barrack Room inspection by 2nd in Command, and at 10 a.m. the Coy paraded, in full marching order, for inspection by G.O.C. on their parade ground. In the afternoon the Coy paraded to digging area to construct dugouts etc.

### 28 February 1917 — Witley Camp, Surrey — 9 p.m.

> The day was spent at Entrenching area carrying on with emplacement construction

### 1 March 1917 — Witley Camp, Surrey — 9 p.m.

> The work was identical with that of preceding day.

### 2 March 1917 — Witley Camp, Surrey — 9 p.m.

> At 8.30 a.m. the Coy paraded to Entrenching area under CAPT. GIBSON and at 1.20 p.m. paraded to area Gas School for short lecture and went through gas chamber.

### 3 March 1917 — Witley Camp, Surrey — 9 p.m.

> All available men were used for Stable fatigues

### 4 March 1917 — Witley Camp, Surrey — 1 p.m.

> Coy paraded at 9.45 a.m. for Divine Service with 128th batt'n.

### 5 March 1917 — Witley Camp, Surrey — 9 p.m.

> All available men were used for Barrack Fatigues. At 8 a.m. all men detailed for Drivers Course proceeded to 52nd batt'y, 13th bde, C.F.A. to commence the course.

Signed at foot of page: T. C. Bamfield.
