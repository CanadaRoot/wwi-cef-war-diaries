## Notes on October 1917
- The October cover is written on a different form (Form X 4071, a signal/message pad sheet) and is headed "8-X-17". The diary text returns to the unlined Army Form C. 2118 (A5834), kept by Capt. G. Black (signed "G. Black, Capt.").
- 1 October: the company marches from Bisley Common to Witley Camp, completing the six-day route march begun 26 September.
- 8–12 October: machine-gun firing practice at Mytchett Camp ranges — application at 600 and 300 yards with box respirators, observation of fire by signals, loose and rigid holdings, rapid laying out of gun positions, night firing at 600 yards, and locating positions by reckoning and firing with given bearings and elevations. A margin note reads "Full details sent to D.H.Q."
- 11–13 October: the details left in camp moved all stores and equipment from A1 Lines to F1- and E1 Lines, ready for the company's return at noon on 13 October.
- 22 October: two other ranks left for Mytchett Camp for a course in sniping, 22–27 October. Margin note: "Full details sent to D.H.Q."
- 21 October: the Divisional Machine Gun Officer inspected the company lines and found them in a clean condition.

## Page 73 — Diary text, 7–8 October 1917 (October 1917, p. 5)

### 7 October 1917 — Witley Camp — [hour not given]

> Roman Catholics paraded at 9 am & attended Mass at 9.15 am. Other denominations paraded at 9.30 am & attended Divine Service at 10.00 am, at No 3 Y.M.C.A. Hut.

### 8 October 1917 — Witley Camp — [hour not given]

> The Company paraded at 7.30 am & marched to Mytchett Camp ranges, for practice in Machine Gun fire. The following practices were carried out: Oct 9–10–11 & 12th Application. 600 yds with Box Respirators " 300 " Observations of fire by means of signals Tests of loose & rigid holdings Rapid laying out of gun positions Night firing. 600 yds. Selecting reference objects & swinging number of degrees on to target: Locations of positions by reckoning & firing with given bearings & elevations

[Margin note: "Full details sent to D.H.Q." — initialled, illegible.]

Signed at foot of page: G. Black, Capt., 17th Canadian Machine Gun Company.
