## Notes on April 1917
- April is kept in a new, different hand from February–March, and the entries are signed in the margin "Haw. major" — the 30 April sign-off identifies the officer as **Major H. A. Cobb, O.C. 17th Can. M.G. Coy**; the marginal signature is his initials H.A.C. written in flowing script. Major T. C. Bamfield (O.C. in February–March) does not sign in April.
- The April entries for 10–13 April are written as a programme in the future tense ("The company will parade...").
- The company was still training at Witley Camp, Surrey, throughout April — no move to France yet.

## Page 15 — Diary text, 1–2 April 1917 (April 1917, p. 3)

Army Form C. 2118. Entries signed in the margin "Haw. major" [H. A. Cobb — see Notes].

### 1 April 1917 — Witley Camp, Surrey — 9.0 PM

> The Company attended Divine Service. The Roman Catholics attended Mass at 9-0 AM at No 2 YMCA Hut. Other Denominations paraded with the 128th Battn and proceeded to the mens mess Room for Service.

### 2 April 1917 — Witley Camp, Surrey — 9.0 PM

> The Company paraded at 8.0 AM for Physical Training until 9-0 AM. — 9-0 AM until 10-0 AM Lecture on Visual Training and Judging Distance — 10.0 AM until 11-0 AM practical Visual Training and Judging Distance in the field; 11-0 AM till 12-0 Noon Lecture on Machine Gun Company Organization 2-0 PM till 3-0 PM Lecture on Indication and recognition of targets 3-0 PM till 4-0 PM practical Indication and Recognition of Targets in the Field. 4-0 PM till 4-30 PM Physical Training.

## Page 16 — Diary text, 3–4 April 1917 (April 1917, p. 4)

### 3 April 1917 — Witley Camp, Surrey — 9.0 PM

> The Company paraded at 8-0 AM for Physical Training which was carried on till 9-0 AM. — 9-0 AM till 10-0 AM Lecture on Machine Gun Company Organization. 10-0 AM till 11-0 AM Lecture on Choice of Gun positions and Creation of Belts of Fire 11-0 AM till 12-0 Noon Fire orders and Signals. 1-30 PM till 4-30 PM Marched to Digging Area and received Practical Illustration of Gun positions Belts of Fire, and Principles of Construction of Dug Outs and Emplacements for Machine Gunners.

### 4 April 1917 — Witley Camp, Surrey — 9.0 PM

> The Company paraded at 8-0 AM and marched to Digging Area to have Types of Emplacements and Dug Outs shown to groups Explanation of Belts of Fire. Principles underlying Construction of Splinter proof and Shell proof Dug Outs. and Choose Gun positions for a Defensive Scheme. 1-30 PM Muster Parade, followed by Lecture on Range Cards, Attack and Defence and draw Range Cards in the Field

## Page 17 — Diary text, 5–6 April 1917 (April 1917, p. 5)

### 5 April 1917 — Witley Camp, Surrey — 9-0 PM

> The Company paraded at 8-0 AM and marched to Digging Area to construct Emplacements and Dug Outs. Officers selected fresh Sites for a Support Line and Defence of Flanks 1-30 PM till 2-30 PM Lecture on the Training of Machine Gunners. 2-30 PM till 3-30 PM Practical Indication and Recognition of Targets in the Field 3-30 PM till 4-30 PM Making Range Cards, Attack and Defence in the Field

### 6 April 1917 — Witley Camp, Surrey — 9-0 PM

> The Company paraded at 8-0 AM for Physical Training until 9-0 AM. 9-0 AM until 10.30 AM Clothing Inspection. 10.30 AM to 12-0 noon Marching Order Parade. 1.30 PM to 2.30 PM Lecture on Machine Gun Characteristics. 2.30 to 3.30 Lecture Fire Direction Part I 3.30 to 4.30 Lecture Military Vocabulary.
