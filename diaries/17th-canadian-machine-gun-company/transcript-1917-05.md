## Notes on May 1917
- The 3 May entry repeats "Nos. 3 and 4 sections" for both the miniature-range party and the party carrying on — transcribed as written; likely a slip for Nos. 1 and 2 in one of the two places (compare 2 May, where Nos. 1 and 2 went to the ranges and Nos. 3 and 4 carried on).

- The month is signed off by Lieut. Chipman (for the O.C.); the April O.C., Major H. A. Cobb, does not sign in May.

## Page 27 — Diary text, 1–2 May 1917 (May 1917, p. 3)

Army Form C. 2118. Entries signed in the margin "H.A.C. major" [Major H. A. Cobb — see April notes].

### 1 May 1917 — Witley Camp, Surrey — 9-0 PM

> The company paraded at 8-0 to 9-0 am for Drill. Combined Aiming and Holding. 9-0 am to 10-0 am Mechanism. 10-0 am to 11-0 am Adjustment and Repairs. 11-0 am to 12-noon Automatic Tapping and Vertical Searching 1.30 PM to 2.30 PM Immediate Action. 2.30 PM to 3.30 PM Belt Filling by Hand and Machine. 3.30 to 4-0 PM Lecture Points Before, During and After Firing. 4-0 PM to 4.30 PM Stripping and Cleaning.

### 2 May 1917 — Witley Camp, Surrey — 9-0 PM

> The company paraded at 8-0 am Nos. 1 and 2 Sections proceeded to the miniature ranges for firing. Nos 3 and 4 Sections carried on as follows: — 8-0 to 9-0 am Drill Combined Aiming and Holding. 9-0 am to 10-0 am Mechanism 10-0 am to 11-0 am Adjustment and Repairs 11-0 to 12-noon Automatic Tapping and Vertical Searching. 1.30 PM to 2.30 PM Immediate Action. 2.30 PM to 3.30 PM Belt Filling by Hand and Machine. 3.30 PM to 4-0 PM Lecture: Points Before During and After Firing. 4-0 to 4.30 PM Stripping and Cleaning.

## Page 28 — Diary text, 3–5 May 1917 (May 1917, p. 4)

### 3 May 1917 — Witley Camp, Surrey — 9-0 PM

> The company paraded at 8-0 am. Nos. 3 and 4 sections paraded to the miniature ranges for firing. Nos. 3 and 4 sections carried on as follows: — 8-0 am to 9-0 am Drill. 9-0 am to 10-0 am Mechanism. 10-0 am to 11-0 am Immediate action. 11-0 am to 12-noon Fire Orders and Signals 1.30 PM to 2.30 PM Spare Parts Box. 2.30 PM to 3.30 PM Belt Filling by hand and machine. 3.30 PM to 4-0 PM Lecture Faults on Firing. 4-0 PM to 4.30 PM Cleaning

### 4 May 1917 — Witley Camp, Surrey — 9-0 PM

> The company, having supplied fatigues, guards and picquets during the absence of the four battalions in the 13th Infantry Brigade, and being on duty practically all night, were dismissed until 12-noon when the following programme of training was carried out. From 1.30 PM to 2.30 PM Spare Parts Box. 2.30 to 3.30 PM Belt Filling by hand and machine. 3.30 PM until 4-0 PM Lecture Faults in Firing. 4-0 to 4.30 PM Cleaning

### 5 May 1917 — Witley Camp, Surrey — 1-0 PM

> The company paraded at 8-0 am to 9-0 am for Drill. 9-0 am to 10-0 am for Mechanism. 10-0 am to 11-0 am Immediate action. 11-0 am to 11.30 am Spare Parts Box. 11.30 to 12 noon Cleaning

## Page 33 — Diary text, 18–23 May 1917 (May 1917, p. 9)

### 18 May 1917 — Witley Camp, Surrey — 9-0 PM

> The company paraded at 8-0 am to 9-0 am Drill Aiming & Holding. 9-0 am to 10-0 am Mechanism and Immediate Action 10-0 am to 11-0 am Automatic Tapping & Vertical Searching. 11-0 am to 12-noon Minor Tactics Practical. 1.30 PM to 2.30 PM Range Cards Attack & Defence. 2.30 to 3.30 Judging Distance. Use of Range Finder. 3.30 to 4-0 PM Lecture on Night Fire. 4-0 to 4.30 PM Stripping and Cleaning.

### 19 May 1917 — Witley Camp, Surrey — 9-0 PM

> The company paraded in full marching order at 8-0 am and proceeded to Mytchett Ranges for the purpose of firing Classification practices. The men stood the march remarkably well and reached Mytchett at 2.30 PM. The Transport Section accompanied the column and dinner was served on the road. The remainder of the day was spent in occupying quarters and cleaning guns preparatory to firing.

### 20 May 1917 — Mytchett Ranges — 9-0 PM

> No. 4 Range at Mytchett was allotted to the Company and firing was carried on. The shooting for the most part was very good.

### 21–23 May 1917 — Mytchett Ranges / Witley Camp, Surrey — 9.0 PM

> For the marginally noted three days firing was carried on at Mytchett Ranges On the afternoon of the 23rd the company marched back to Witley. The shooting during our stay at Mytchett was exceptionally good.

## Page 35 — Diary text, 28–31 May 1917 (May 1917, p. 11)

### 28 May 1917 — Witley Camp, Surrey — 9-0 PM

> The company paraded at 8-0 am to 9-0 am for Physical Training. 9-0 am to 9.30 am Lecture on Machine Gun Field Work. 9.30 am Muster Parade for all ranks. This day being Whit Monday was declared a half holiday

### 29 May 1917 — Witley Camp, Surrey — 9-0 PM

> Parade was called at 8-0 to 9-0 am for Physical Drill 9-0 to 10-0 am Indication & Recognition of Targets. Practical. 10-0 am to 11-0 am Adjustment & Repairs 11-0 am to 12-noon Lecture on Indirect & Overhead Fire. 1.30 PM to 4.30 PM Pay Parade & Medical Inspection.

### 30 May 1917 — Witley Camp, Surrey — 9-0 PM

> The company paraded at 6.45 am and carried out a tactical scheme in conjunction with the 160th Battalion in the vicinity of Elstead.

### 31 May 1917 — Witley Camp, Surrey — 9-0 PM

> The company paraded at 6.45 am and moved off at 7-0 am. They carried out another scheme with the 160th Battalion in the vicinity of Thursley.
