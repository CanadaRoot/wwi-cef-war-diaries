## Notes on July 1917
- July is kept in a new, cramped hand. The month is signed by **Capt. G. Black, a/O.C.** (acting Officer Commanding) — the Yukon officer Captain George Black. Major H. A. Cobb does not sign in July.
- 1 July: Divine Service conducted by Pte (Rev) Buck of the Company. 2 July: Dominion Day divisional holiday.
- 6 July: the entry for a move to Mytchett Ranges is struck through and replaced with "Route march to Camp"; the move to Mytchett actually takes place on 7 July, with firing on the ranges 8–10 July.
- 24–26 July: night tactical relief scheme on Hankley Common — the company moved off at 9.45 p.m. on 24 July and the relief was completed at 1 a.m. on 27 July.
- The hour column is mostly left blank in July.

## Page 44 — Diary text, 1–10 July 1917 (July 1917, p. 3)

Army Form C. 2118.

### 1 July 1917 — Witley Camp, Surrey

> The Company attended Divine Service in Hut in Company lines Service conducted by Pte (Rev) Buck of the Company. Roman Catholics attended Mass at No 2 YMCA Hut

### 2 July 1917 — Witley Camp, Surrey

> Divisional Holiday (Dominion Day)

### 3 July 1917 — Witley Camp, Surrey

> 6.30 am No. 3 section proceeded to Entrenching area for Field Engineering 12.15 PM [uncertain] No 2 and 4 Sections 7–8 am Range finding — 8–10 Scouting 10–11 Semaphore 11–12 Mechanism & Immediate action — No 4 Sec. 1–2 PM Drill light tripod 2–3 Immediate action with Box respirators 3–4 Indication and Recognition 4–5 Discussion of Tactical Scheme No. 10.
