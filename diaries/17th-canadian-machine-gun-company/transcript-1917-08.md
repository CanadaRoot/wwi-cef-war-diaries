## Notes on August 1917
- The August cover originally reads "1916" for both dates; "1916" is circled and "1917" written above in each case — transcribed as 1917.
- August is kept by Capt. G. Black (signed "G. Black, Capt." at the foot of most pages), on lined Army Form C. 2118 sheets (T2134) rather than the usual unlined form.
- **14 August — the company's first fatality:** Lieut J. R. Chute was killed when a motor ambulance collided with the marching column near Hindhead; Lieut P. S. Wilson and two men were slightly injured, and two horses had to be destroyed. The company attended Lieut Chute's funeral on 17 August.
- The entry dated 24 August says "Saturday morning", although 25 August 1917 was the Saturday (26 August, with church parades, was the Sunday) — the writer appears to have slipped a day; transcribed as written.
- 30 August has two different programmes, one at the foot of p. 10 and one at the head of p. 11 — transcribed as written; the duplication is in the original.
- 5–7 August: three-day tactical scheme with the 13th Infantry Brigade "under actual active service conditions". 19–22 August: continuous firing at Mytchett Ranges (long range, searching, indirect and night firing).

## Page 51 — Diary text, 1–3 August 1917 (August 1917, p. 3)

Army Form C. 2118 (lined form T2134).

### 1 August 1917 — Witley Camp — 9-0 PM

> No 2 Section proceeded to the entrenching area to relieve No 1 Section in carrying on the work of constructing a deep dug out

### 2 August 1917 — Witley Camp — 9-0 PM

> No 3 Section proceeded to the entrenching area to relieve No 2 Section in the work of constructing a deep dug out. No 2 Section arranged for guides to conduct No 3 in its proper relief manner. Additional emplacements were constructed and repairs made to dug out etc.

### 3 August 1917 — Witley Camp — [hour not given]

> The company with the exception of No 3 Section paraded for Physical Drill from 7-0 to 8-0 am. 8-0 to 12 noon there was a demonstration of Gas on Machine Guns & Lewis guns & how to protect against same 1–2 PM Immediate Action. 2–3 Anti Gas Drill. 3–4 Semaphore 4–5 Adjustments & Repairs. Stripping & Cleaning.

Signed at foot of page: G. Black, Capt.

## Page 52 — Diary text, 4–9 August 1917 (August 1917, p. 4)

### 4 August 1917 — Witley Camp — 9-0 PM

> The company parades for Divine Service at 8.30 am under the Orderly Officer with the 160th Battalion. Roman Catholics paraded to No. 2 YMCA at 9-am to attend Mass.

### 5–7 August 1917 — Witley Camp — 9-0 PM

> The company took part in a tactical scheme with the 13th Infantry Brigade which was conducted under actual active service conditions. This scheme lasted for three days corresponding to the dates which appear in the margin.

### 8 August 1917 — Witley Camp — 4-0 PM

> Owing to the fact that the company did not return till an early hour in the morning there was no early morning parade and the afternoon from 1-0 to 5-PM was employed in cleaning Quarters & Equipment

### 9 August 1917 — Witley Camp — 9-0 PM

> Nos 1 & 2 Section proceeded to Entrenching area at 8-0 am. No 3 & 4 Section 7–8 PM Physical Drill 8–9 Immediate Action. 9–10 Semaphore. 10–11 Judging Distance and Ranging 11–12 Noon Map Reading & Compass.

Signed at foot of page: G. Black, Capt.

## Page 53 — Diary text, 9 (continued)–12 August 1917 (August 1917, p. 5)

### 9 August 1917 (continued) — Witley Camp, Surrey — 9-0 PM

> 1-0 to 2-PM Lecture on Indirect Fire 2–3 PM Combined drill. 3–4 Lecture on Venereal Disease. 4–5 Adjustment & Repairs. Stripping & Cleaning.

### 10 August 1917 — Witley Camp, Surrey — 9-0 PM

> Nos. 3 & 4 Section did not parade until 11.30 PM when they proceeded to the Entrenching area to carry on the construction of a deep dug out. Nos. 1 & 2 Sections paraded at 7–8 am Physical Drill. 8–9 am Combined Drill 9–10 am Semaphore, 10–12 noon Cleaning Quarters.

### 11 August 1917 — Witley Camp, Surrey — 10 PM

> The company parades for Divine Service at 9.40 am under the Orderly Officer with the 164th Battalion. Roman Catholics paraded at 9-0 am to No. 2 YMCA Hut to attend Mass.

### 12 August 1917 — Witley Camp, Surrey — 9-0 PM

> No 1 & 2 Sections paraded at 8.30 am and proceeded to the Entrenching area to continue the construction of a Deep Dugout. Nos 3 & 4 Sections paraded at 7 am – 8 am for Physical Drill 8–9 Section Drill

Signed at foot of page: G. Black, Capt.

## Page 54 — Diary text, 12 (continued)–14 August 1917 (August 1917, p. 6)

### 12 August 1917 (continued) — Witley Camp, Surrey — 9-0 PM

> 9-0 to 10 am Loading & Unloading Limbers. 10–11 Bombing. 11–12 noon Use of Ground & Cover. 1–2 PM Anti Gas Drill. 2–3 PM Mechanism 3–4 Stripping & Cleaning

### 13 August 1917 — Witley Camp, Surrey — 9-0 PM

> The company paraded at 7–8 am Physical Training 8–9 Use of Ground & Cover 9–10 Semaphore 10–11 Immediate Action 11–12 noon Cleaning Quarters. 1–5 PM Sports.

### 14 August 1917 — Witley Camp, Surrey — 9-0 PM

> The company paraded at 7.40 am for a route march and proceeded in the direction of Hindhead, where a most unfortunate accident occurred in that a motor ambulance collided with the column resulting in the death of Lieut J. R. Chute and minor injuries to Lieut P. S. Wilson and two men. It was also necessary to kill two horses who were badly injured.

Signed at foot of page: G. Black, Capt.

## Page 55 — Diary text, 15–17 August 1917 (August 1917, p. 7)

### 15 August 1917 — Witley Camp, Surrey — 1-0 PM

> The company paraded at 7–8 am for Physical Drill. 7–9 am Immediate Action 9–12 noon Pay Parade & Cleaning Quarters

### 16 August 1917 — Witley Camp, Surrey — 9-0 PM

> No. 3 Section paraded at 11.30 pm on the night of Aug 15th & proceeded to Thursley Common for construction of a Deep Dug out and were relieved by No. 4 Section at 6.am on the morning of Aug. 16th No. 1 Section relieved No 4. at 12 noon No. 2 relieved No 1 at 6 pm. who were relieved by the 19th M.G. Coy.

### 17 August 1917 — Witley Camp, Surrey — 9-0 PM

> The company paraded at 7–8 am Physical Drill. 8–10.30 am Company Ceremonial Drill. 10.30 to 12 noon Cleaning equipment. The Coy moved off at 1-PM to attend the funeral of the late Lieut. J. R. Chute.

Signed at foot of page: G. Black, Capt.

## Page 56 — Diary text, 18–24 August 1917 (August 1917, p. 8)

### 18 August 1917 — Witley Camp, Surrey — 9-0 AM

> The company paraded at 7–8 am for Physical Drill. 8–9 am Semaphore 9–10 am Anti Gas Drill 10–11 am Cleaning Quarters 11–12 noon Kit Inspection. At 1.40 PM the company fell in and marched to Mytchett Ranges for night firing.

### 19–22 August 1917 — Mytchett Ranges — 9-0 PM

> During Aug 19th 20th 21st 22nd firing was carried out at Mytchett Ranges almost continually. Long range, searching fire, Indirect fire and night firing practices were carried out.

### 23 August 1917 — Witley Camp, Surrey — 8.30 AM

> The company Marched from Mytchett Ranges arriving at Witley Camp 11–30 a.m. The rest of day spent in thoroughly cleaning and overhauling guns.

### 24 August 1917 — Witley Camp, Surrey — 7-30 AM

> No 2 Section paraded & proceeded to Entrenching area to relieve 19th Machine Gun Coy & continued work on Deep Dug out — No. 3 Section relieved No 2 Section at Entrenching Area at 2 P.M. No 4 Section relieved No 3 Section

Signed at foot of page: G. Black, Capt.

## Page 57 — Diary text, 24 (continued)–27 August 1917 (August 1917, p. 9)

### 24 August 1917 (continued) — Witley Camp — [hour not given]

> At 8.00 AM Saturday morning Medical Inspection paraded. 7.am to 8 am Physical Training & anti gas Belt filling by Hand 9 am to 10 am Semaphore 10 am to noon Lecture on Bombs & Bomb throwing by the Divisional Bombing Officer. No 1 Section 1 PM to 2 PM Bomb throwing with dummies 2.0 PM to 3 PM Anti Gas drill. 3 PM to 4 PM Naming contents of Spare Parts Box.

### 25 August 1917 — Witley Camp — 7 am

> No 3 Section paraded. 7 am to 8 am Physical Training 8 am to 9 am Belt Filling by Hand. 9 am to 10 am Semaphore. 10 am to 12 noon Nos 1, 2 & 4 Sections Cleaning quarters. No. 2 Section paraded at 7-30 am & relieved No 1 Section at 8.0 PM & continued work on Deep dug out.

### 26 August 1917 — Witley Camp — [hour not given]

> Usual Church parades were held.

### 27 August 1917 — Witley Camp — [hour not given]

> No. 1 Section paraded at 7.30 am & proceeded to Entrenching Area & continued work on Deep dug out. No 4 Section relieved No 1 Section at 2 pm at Entrenching Area. No. 2 Section relieved No 4 Section at Entrenching Area at 8 pm. No 2 Section attended

Signed at foot of page: G. Black, Capt.
