## Page 38 — Diary text, 1–4 June 1917 (June 1917, p. 3)

Army Form C. 2118. Entries signed in the margin "H.A.C. major" [Major H. A. Cobb].

### 1 June 1917 — Witley Camp, Surrey — 9-0 PM

> The company paraded at 8-0 am to 9-0 am for Physical Training. At 9-0 am the Company proceeded to Hankley Common to participate in a tactical scheme with the 161st Battn

### 2 June 1917 — Witley Camp, Surrey — 9-0 PM

> The company paraded at 8-0 am to 9-0 am for Physical Drill. 9-0 am to 10-0 am Minor Tactics Practical 10-0 am to 11-0 am Indication & Recognition Practical 11-0 am to 11.30 am Stoppages. 11.30 am to 12-noon Stripping & Cleaning

### 3 June 1917 — Witley Camp, Surrey — 1-0 PM

> The company attended Church parade with the 164th Battn on their Battalion parade grounds at 10-0 am. Roman Catholics attended Mass in No 2 YMCA Hut at 9-0 am

### 4 June 1917 — Witley Camp, Surrey — 9-0 PM

> The company paraded at 8-0 am to 9-0 am for Physical Training 9-0 am to 10-0 am Combined Drill. 10-0 am to 11-0 am Indication and Recognition Practical 11-0 am to 12-noon Lecture on Open Warfare. 1.30 to 2.30 PM Indirect and Overhead Fire Practical. 2.30 to 3.30 PM Judging Distance and Use of Range Finder. 3.30 to 4.0 PM Lecture on Machine Gun Tactics. 4-0 to 4.30 PM Stripping and Cleaning

Signed at foot of page: H. A. Cobb, Major, 17th Canadian Machine Gun Company.

## Page 39 — Diary text, 5–7 June 1917 (June 1917, p. 4)

### 5 June 1917 — Witley Camp, Surrey — 9-0 PM

> The Company paraded at 8-0 am to 9-0 am for Physical Training. 9-0 am to 10 am Parade to Hut F6. for an issue of Box Respirators 10-0 am to 11-0 am Judging Distance Practical. 11-0 am to 12 noon Fire orders and Signals. 1.30 PM to 2.30 PM Indirect & Overhead Fire. 2.30 PM to 3.30 PM Judging Distance & Use of Range Finder. 3.30 PM to 4-0 PM Lecture on Machine Gun Tactics. 4-0 to 4.30 PM Stripping & Cleaning.

### 6 June 1917 — Witley Camp, Surrey — 9-0 PM

> The company paraded at 8-0 to 8.30 am for Physical Drill. 8.30 the company proceeded to Merries Ranges for Revolver Practice. Section Officers saw to the proper issue of Ammunition

### 7 June 1917 — Witley Camp, Surrey — 9-0 PM

> The company paraded at 8-0 am to 9-0 am for Physical Training 9-0 am to 4.30 PM a route march of sixteen miles. The field Kitchen accompanied the company and the men were fed on the road. The full transport also paraded The men at the finish were in excellent condition

Signed at foot of page: H. A. Cobb, Major, 17th Canadian Machine Gun Company.
