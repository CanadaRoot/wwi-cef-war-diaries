## Notes on September 1917

- 5–7 September: tactical scheme with the 13th Brigade against the 14th Brigade, marching to Normandy Common via Milford, Shackleford and Puttenham. 6–8 September are also described as a Brigade Group Scheme — the two accounts overlap on 7 September; transcribed as written.

## Page 62 — Diary text, 1–3 September 1917 (September 1917, p. 3)

Army Form C. 2118 (lined form T2134).

### 1 September 1917 — Witley Camp — [hour not given]

> Physical Training was carried out under assistant instructors. From 8 am to 9 am the company was practised in Company Drill and Tactical movements. The balance of the morning was employed in Machine Gun Training consisting of — Immediate Action Blindfolded — Loading and unloading Limbers — and Semaphore. The afternoon was observed as a holiday as is customary on Saturday Afternoon.

### 2 September 1917 — Witley Camp — [hour not given]

> The Company paraded at 9.45 am and proceeded to No 3 Y.M.C.A. to attend Church Service. The lines & huts in charge of this unit were inspected by the Divisional Machine Gun Officer and favourably reported on.

### 3 September 1917 — Witley Camp — [hour not given]

> The usual training routine adhered to. Syllabus carried out consisted of the following — Physical Training — Combined Drill — Immediate Action — Mechanism — Semaphore — Anti Gas Drill (Contd)

Signed at foot of page: G. Black, Capt., 17th Canadian Machine Gun Company.

## Page 63 — Diary text, 3 (continued)–5 September 1917 (September 1917, p. 4)

### 3 September 1917 (continued) — Witley Camp

> — Indirect Fire, theoretical — and Stripping & Cleaning.

### 4 September 1917 — Witley Camp — [hour not given]

> The training as carried out consisted of — Company Drill — Use of Ground and Cover — practical — Practice Bomb Throwing, Dummies — Immediate Action — Semaphore — Mechanism & Stripping & Cleaning.

### 5 September 1917 — Witley Camp — [hour not given]

> The Company paraded at 7.00 AM in heavy marching order and proceeded on route march to Normandy Common via Milford, Shackleford, Puttenham operating in conjunction with the 13th Brigade on a tactical scheme against the 14th Brigade. The scheme lasted till Sept. 7th on which date the Company returned to camp at 2.30 p.m. and were then dismissed to allow the men to get some rest.

Signed at foot of page: G. Black, Capt., 17th Canadian Machine Gun Company.

## Page 64 — Diary text, 8–13 September 1917 (September 1917, p. 5)

### 6–8 September 1917 — Witley Camp — 9-0 PM

> Sept 6th 7th 8th were devoted to a Brigade Group Scheme and returned to camp on the afternoon of Sept 8th

### 9 September 1917 — Witley Camp — 1-0 PM

> The Company paraded for Divine Service to No 3 YMCA hut at 10-0 am. Roman Catholics paraded for mass at 9.0 am

### 10–12 September 1917 — Witley Camp — [hour not given]

> Sep. 10th 11th 12th were devoted to another Brigade Group Scheme in which the 13th Brigade were opposed to the 15th Brigade. The Company returned to camp on the afternoon of Sep. 12th 1917.

### 13 September 1917 — Witley Camp — [hour not given]

> The company paraded at 7–8 am for Physical Drill 8–9 am Company Drill 9–10 am Semaphore. 10–11 Use of Spare Parts Boxes 11–12 noon Belt filling by hand. 1–2 pm Lecture on Indirect Fire 2–3.5 Box Respirators Drill 3–4 pm Adjustments & Repairs.

Signed at foot of page: G. Black, Capt., 17th Canadian Machine Gun Company.

## Page 65 — Diary text, 14–17 September 1917 (September 1917, p. 6)

### 14 September 1917 — Witley Camp, Surrey — 9-0 PM

> The company paraded at 7–8 am for Physical Training 8–9 Company Drill. 9–10 Revolver Drill. 10–11 Immediate Action Blindfolded. 11–12 noon Dummy Bomb Throwing. 1–2 Indirect Fire. 2–3 P.H Gas Helmet Drill 3–4 Stripping & Cleaning.

### 15 September 1917 — Witley Camp, Surrey — 9-0 PM

> The company paraded at 7–8 Physical Training 8–9 Combined Drill 9–10 Mechanism 11–12 noon Adjustments & Repairs Stripping & Cleaning.

### 16 September 1917 — Witley Camp, Surrey — 9-0 PM

> The company paraded at 9.45 am for Divine Service at No 3 YMCA hut. Roman Catholics paraded to No 2 YMCA hut for mass. At 1.45 PM Pay Parade was held.

### 17 September 1917 — Witley Camp, Surrey — 9-0 PM

> The company paraded at 7–8 AM for Physical Drill. 9–10 Lecture on Stokes Gun 10–12 Demonstration of Stokes Guns. 2–4 PM Lecture on Theory of Stokes Gun & Demonstration of firing.

Signed at foot of page: G. Black, Capt., 17th Canadian Machine Gun Company.

## Page 68 — Diary text, 26–30 September 1917 (September 1917, p. 9)

### 26–30 September 1917 — Witley Camp, Surrey — 9-0 PM

> The company paraded at 7-0 am for a six day route march marching on an average of ten miles per day. The company halted for the first night at Cobham Park. and continued during the remaining five days The weather was excellent and the men stood the trip remarkably well the only casualties being two men who were kicked by horses.

Signed at foot of page: G. Black, Capt., 17th Canadian Machine Gun Company.
