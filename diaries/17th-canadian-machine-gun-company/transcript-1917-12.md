- January 1918 onwards — not yet transcribed. Next: page 91 (January 1918, p. 1).

## Notes on December 1917
- December continues the terse day-by-day summary style of November, kept by Capt. G. Black (signed "G. Black, Capt."). Both diary-text pages bear an "ORDERLY ROOM 17th CANADIAN MACHINE GUN COY 3- JAN. 1918" stamp, and the "or INTELLIGENCE SUMMARY" heading is struck through.
- 1–12 December: barrage practice schemes and demonstrations at Longmoor Ranges, with route marches between Longmoor and Witley Camp (3, 5 and 12 December).
- 4 December: "Company rested. Dominions Elections" — the word reads "Dominions" as written.
- 25 December: Christmas Day holiday; 26 December: Boxing Day holiday. 30 December: church parade and inspection of company lines by the D.M.G.O. (Divisional Machine Gun Officer).
