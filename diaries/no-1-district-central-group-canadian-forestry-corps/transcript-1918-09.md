## Coverage notes

- **Dates with entries:** all 30 days of September 1918. Days with no change of billets are entered as "Billets unchanged." (ditto marks in the original, expanded here) — no date has no entry.
- **Dates with no entry:** none.
- **Pages that are covers/contents rather than diary text:** LAC p. 52 (cover, Volume 14), p. 53 (contents — "Diary Text 2 Pages"). Diary text is on LAC pp. 54–55 (2 sheets, Army Form C. 2118, headed "H.Q., No.1 District, CANADIAN FORESTRY CORPS, B.E.F." and "for the month of September, 1918."; the heading "INTELLIGENCE SUMMARY" is struck through on both sheets).
- **Casualties:** none recorded in September 1918.
- **Weather:** none recorded in September 1918.
- **Movements:** 4 September — No. 45 Company commenced laying a standard gauge railway siding to the mill site. 12 September — 28 Russian O.R's struck off strength on transfer to Bramshott Regimental Depot Group, and 31 O.R's each to No. 5 District and No. 9 District, C.F.C., on account of surplus strength in No. 1 District. 26 September — No. 20 Company commenced hauling to le Mesle from their Scotch Mill. 28 September — a detachment of No. 1 Northern Non-Combatant Company moved from No. 30 Company to their unit at Perseigne. 30 September — Headquarters and half company of No. 2 Eastern Non-Combatant Company removed from Perseigne to a new camp site near Belleme.
- **Other notes:** 16 September — a new record for a day's cut by the District, 402,066 f.b.m. in one ten-hour day. 17 September — No. 54 Company awarded the diploma of Honour by the Société d'Horticulture et Viticulture de l'Eure-et-Loire, Chartres, for camp lines, discipline and good behaviour. 18 September — 26 O.R's of the 4th Division Concert party began a series of concerts at H.Q. and companies. 20 September — Quarterly Audit Board convened at 9.00 a.m. to audit Mess and Canteen Accounts. 21 September — Brig.-Gen. Atkinson visited Senonches operations. 28 September — a new record for a month's production and shipment (period 4 weeks, 2–29 September): 25,884 tons produced; 6,652,962 f.b.m. of sawn material; 23,822 tons shipped; 1,210 trucks shipped.
