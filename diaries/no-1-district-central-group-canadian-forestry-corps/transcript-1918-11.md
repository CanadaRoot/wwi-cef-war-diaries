## 7 November 1918 — LAC p. 62 (diary Sheet No. 1)

Place: Alençon. Hour: —

> Lieut.-Gen. Sir R. Turner, V.C., Col. J. B. White, D.S.O., Lieut.-Col. W. F. Cooke, D.S.O., Lieut.-Gen. [uncertain — words struck through in the original] and Capt. Nichol (A.D.C.), arrived at H.Q. 1600 o'clock, and inspected H.Q. Orderly Room, Camp and Q.M. Dept. After the inspection Gen. Turner complimented Col. White, who conveyed similar remarks to the Acting O.C. of the District. Dined at H.Q. Officers' Mess, where a very enjoyable evening was spent. Weather rainy.

## 8 November 1918 — LAC p. 62 (diary Sheet No. 1)

Place: Alençon. Hour: —

> The party proceeded to 41 and 54 companies at Senonches. The camps and mills were inspected. Gen. Turner was very pleased with the operations and progress, and was much taken with the kitchens, messing and accomodation at these two companies. He was also impressed with the salvage of solder. Visited light railways and forest operations. On return lunched at the joint mess of the two companies. After lunch Gen. Turner and Col. White addressed the men of 41 and 54 companies, who had been assembled. His remarks were very complimentary. He told them how much their work was appreciated by the men in the line. He also thanked Col. White for the pleasant trip through the Forestry units and expressed his pleasure with the operations of each unit visited. Gen. Turner and Col. White proceeded to Rouen and Col. Cooke to H.Q., Central Group. Weather unsettled.

## 11 November 1918 — LAC p. 62 (diary Sheet No. 1)

Place: Alençon. Hour: —

> Weather fine. Advice received by telephone from Paris at 1130 that Germany had accepted the terms of the armistice with the Allies. Advice received from O.C., Central Group that a general holiday was to be observed throughout the District on November 12 for the purpose of celebrating the temporary cessation of hostilities as laid down in the terms of the armistice.
