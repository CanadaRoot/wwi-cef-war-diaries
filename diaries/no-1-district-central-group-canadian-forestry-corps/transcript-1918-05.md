## Coverage notes

- **Dates with entries:** all 31 days of May 1918. Days with no change of billets are entered as "Billets unchanged." (ditto marks in the original, expanded here) — no date has no entry.
- **Dates with no entry:** none.
- **Pages that are covers/contents rather than diary text:** LAC p. 33 (cover, Volume 10), p. 34 (contents — "Diary Text 1 Page"). Diary text is on LAC p. 35 (1 page, Army Form C. 2118).
- **Casualties:** none recorded in May 1918.
- **Weather:** none recorded in May 1918.
- **Movements:** 19 May — advance parties of No. 41 Company to Foret de Bourse and No. 43 Company to Foret de Senonches. 23 May — No. 38 Company from Foret de Perseigne to Foret de Belleme. 24 May — No. 54 Company from Bois Pelay to Foret de Senonches; No. 102 P. of W. Company arrived at Foret Andaine. 25 May — detachment of No. 2 Construction Company re-attached from No. 54 Company to No. 42 Company on moving to Foret de Senonches. 26 May — No. 44 Company and No. 38 P. of W. Company moved to a new site in Foret Andaine.
- **Other notes:** 11 May — 24 O.R's attached from the Canadian Labour Pool and 18 Russian reinforcements arrived; 31 May — 20 more O.R's Russian reinforcements arrived. 17 May — orders that each company devote two half days a week and 3 hours on Sunday morning to Military Training.
