## 1 October 1917 — LAC p. 11 (diary p. 1)

Place: ALENCON. Hour: —

> Billets unchanged. Major Carew went to Headquarters Central Group, to go with O.C. Cent. Group to Headquarters, Paris Plage.

## 2 October 1917 — LAC p. 11 (diary p. 1)

Place: ALENCON. Hour: —

> Billets unchanged.

## 3 October 1917 — LAC p. 11 (diary p. 1)

Place: ALENCON. Hour: —

> Billets unchanged.

## 4 October 1917 — LAC p. 11 (diary p. 1)

Place: ALENCON. Hour: —

> Billets unchanged. Major Carew returned from Paris Plage. Capt. Harper C.A.P.C. attached as in War Establishment reported for duty at Headquarters.

## 5 October 1917 — LAC p. 11 (diary p. 1)

Place: ALENCON. Hour: —

> Billets unchanged. Lieut. H.F. Goodwin Acting O.i/c Records went to H.Q. Central Group for a conference in regard to re-organization of keeping records in the Group. Audit board at No. 30 Company.

## 6 October 1917 — LAC p. 11 (diary p. 1)

Place: ALENCON. Hour: —

> Billets unchanged. Audit board at Nos. 20 and 54 Companies.

## 7 October 1917 — LAC p. 11 (diary p. 1)

Place: ALENCON. Hour: —

> Billets unchanged.

## 8 October 1917 — LAC p. 11 (diary p. 1)

Place: ALENCON. Hour: —

> Billets unchanged. Audit Board went to Foret d'Andaine to audit accounts of Nos. 38, 42 & 44 Coys.

## 9 October 1917 — LAC p. 11 (diary p. 1)

Place: ALENCON. Hour: —

> Billets unchanged. Advance party of No. 54 Company entered Pelay Forest.

## 10 October 1917 — LAC p. 11 (diary p. 1)

Place: ALENCON. Hour: —

> Billets unchanged.

## 11 October 1917 — LAC p. 11 (diary p. 1)

Place: ALENCON. Hour: —

> Billets unchanged. No. 78 Company, Major Craig O.C. arrived at Almeneches, 6 Officers 172 O.R's including 7 C.A.S.C. and 1 C.A.M.C. attached. Col. Jones O.C. Central Group visited H.Q. Col. Therton [uncertain] and Commandant Granger dined at H.Q.

## 12 October 1917 — LAC p. 11 (diary p. 1)

Place: ALENCON. Hour: —

> Billets unchanged. Col. Jones and Major Carew went to Le Mans.

Place: ALENCON. Hour: —

> Billets unchanged.

## 14 October 1917 — LAC p. 11 (diary p. 1)

> Billets unchanged.

Place: ALENCON. Hour: —

> Billets unchanged. Field General Court Martial held at Headquarters, Alencon, on Pte. Harrison J.R. No. 30 Company.

## 16 October 1917 — LAC p. 11 (diary p. 1)

Place: ALENCON. Hour: —

> Billets unchanged. O's C. Companies held conference at Headquarters.

## 17 October 1917 — LAC p. 11 (diary p. 1)

Place: ALENCON. Hour: —

> Billets unchanged. O's C. Companies and their bush officers inspected operations in the State forest of Perseigne.

## 18 October 1917 — LAC p. 11 (diary p. 1)

Place: ALENCON. Hour: —

> Billets unchanged. A party of technical N.C.O's inspected the mill operations in the Forest of Dreux.

## 19 October 1917 — LAC p. 11 (diary p. 1)

Place: ALENCON. Hour: —

> Billets unchanged.

## 20 October 1917 — LAC p. 11 (diary p. 1)

Place: ALENCON. Hour: —

> Billets unchanged.

## 21 October 1917 — LAC p. 11 (diary p. 1)

Place: ALENCON. Hour: —

> Billets unchanged. Advance party of one Officer and 50 other ranks, of No. 42 Company moved from Bagnoles to Surdon Forest, near Almeneches.

## 22 October 1917 — LAC p. 11 (diary p. 1)

Place: ALENCON. Hour: —

> Billets unchanged. No. 78 Auxiliary Petrol Company, (979 M.T. Company) A.S.C. arrived at H.Q. for duty in No. 1 District, Strength as follows:- O.C., Capt. H.R. Hood Barrs and four second lieutenants. 141 O.R's, 48 lorries, 4 motor cycles, 1 motor car.

## 23 October 1917 — LAC p. 12 (diary p. 2)

Place: Alencon. Hour: —

> Billets unchanged.

## 24 October 1917 — LAC p. 12 (diary p. 2)

Place: Alencon. Hour: —

> Billets unchanged. No. 54 Company completed move from Bois L'Eveque to Pelay Forest. Brig. Gen. A. MacDougall, Director of Timber Operations, Colonel B. Hepburn, Deputy Director of Timber Operations Canadian Forestry Corps and Col. Jones, O.C. Central Group, C.F.C. visited H.Q.

## 25 October 1917 — LAC p. 12 (diary p. 2)

Place: Alencon. Hour: —

> Billets unchanged. General MacDougall, Col. Hepburn, Col. Jones accompanies by Major Carew visited companies in the District.

## 26 October 1917 — LAC p. 12 (diary p. 2)

Place: Alencon. Hour: —

> Billets unchanged. F.G. Court Martial held at Headquarters on No. 1048142 Pte McIntyre D.E. No. 54 Company.

## 27 October 1917 — LAC p. 12 (diary p. 2)

Place: Alencon. Hour: —

> Offices moved from 4 Rue Julien to 32 Rue de Bretagne. Instructions received from H.Q. Conches to warn No. 78 Company to prepare to move to Bordeaux. To return No. 53 Company men attached to No. 54 Company to Conches.

## 28 October 1917 — LAC p. 12 (diary p. 2)

Place: Alencon. Hour: —

> Billets unchanged. No. 53 Company men, (43 all told) went to Conches by rail from Almeneches and by lorry from Alencon.

## 29 October 1917 — LAC p. 12 (diary p. 2)

Place: Alencon. Hour: —

> Billets unchanged.

## 30 October 1917 — LAC p. 12 (diary p. 2)

Place: Alencon. Hour: —

> Billets unchanged.

## 31 October 1917 — LAC p. 12 (diary p. 2)

Place: Alencon. Hour: —

> Billets unchanged. No. 42 Company completed move from Foret d' Andaine to Surdon Forest.

## Coverage notes

- **Dates with entries:** all 31 days of October 1917.
- **Dates with no entry:** none.
- **Pages that are covers/contents rather than diary text:** LAC p. 9 (cover — "CONFIDENTAL" as written), p. 10 (contents — "Diary Text 2 Pages"). Diary text is on LAC pp. 11–12 (2 pages, Army Form C. 2118).
- **Casualties:** none recorded in October 1917.
- **Weather:** none recorded in October 1917.
- **Movements:** 9 October — advance party of No. 54 Company entered Pelay Forest. 11 October — No. 78 Company (Major Craig, O.C.; 6 Officers, 172 other ranks, including 7 C.A.S.C. and 1 C.A.M.C. attached) arrived at Almeneches. 21 October — advance party of one Officer and 50 other ranks of No. 42 Company moved from Bagnoles to Surdon Forest, near Almeneches. 22 October — No. 78 Auxiliary Petrol Company (979 M.T. Company), A.S.C., arrived at H.Q. for duty in No. 1 District (O.C. Capt. H. R. Hood Barrs and four second lieutenants, 141 other ranks, 48 lorries, 4 motor cycles, 1 motor car). 24 October — No. 54 Company completed its move from Bois L'Eveque to Pelay Forest. 27 October — District offices moved from 4 Rue Julien to 32 Rue de Bretagne, Alencon; No. 78 Company warned to prepare to move to Bordeaux; No. 53 Company men attached to No. 54 Company to return to Conches. 28 October — the 43 No. 53 Company men went to Conches by rail from Almeneches and by lorry from Alencon. 31 October — No. 42 Company completed its move from Foret d'Andaine to Surdon Forest.
- **Other notes:** Courts martial: a Field General Court Martial was held at Headquarters, Alencon, on Pte. Harrison J. R., No. 30 Company (15 October), and on No. 1048142 Pte. McIntyre D. E., No. 54 Company (26 October). An audit board toured the District 5–8 October (Nos. 20, 30, 54 Companies, then Foret d'Andaine for Nos. 38, 42 and 44 Companies). Brig. Gen. A. MacDougall, Director of Timber Operations, with Colonel B. Hepburn, Deputy Director of Timber Operations, Canadian Forestry Corps, and Col. Jones, O.C. Central Group, visited H.Q. on 24 October and toured the companies on 25 October. The Hour column on each diary sheet carries handwritten initials rather than times; no times are recorded. The place column reads "ALENCON" on diary p. 1 and "Alencon" on diary p. 2.
