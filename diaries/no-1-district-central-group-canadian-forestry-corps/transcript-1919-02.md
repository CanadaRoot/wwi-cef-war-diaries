## Coverage notes

- **Dates with entries:** all 28 days of February 1919.
- **Dates with no entry:** none.
- **Pages that are covers/contents rather than diary text:** LAC p. 76 (cover, "CONFIDENTIAL"), p. 77 (contents — "Diary Text 1 Page"). Diary text is a single sheet on LAC p. 78 (Army Form C. 2118). The sheet is headed "WAR DIARY or INTELLIGENCE SUMMARY" for "No. 1 District, Canadian Forestry Corps. For the month of February 1919." — the printed month word "January" is struck through and "February" typed above it, a leftover from the January form.
- **Casualties:** none recorded in February 1919.
- **Weather:** recorded every day — wet 1–2, 4, 17, 19, 22–24 February; fair 3, 18, 21, 25–26 February; fair and cold 5–10, 12–16 February; fair, cold 11 February; fair and wet 20 February; fine 28 February.
- **Movements:** 15 February — 3 Officers and 74 Other Ranks of No. 42 Company and Headquarters despatched on draft to Havre for demobilization (ordered 10–12 February as a draft of 2 Officers and 75 Other Ranks to England). 28 February — orders received to despatch a draft of 3 Officers and 81 Other Ranks to Havre for demobilization on 1 March.
- **Other notes:** the month is the winding-up of the District: mills sold to French purchasers — 1 February, 2 Canadian Mills at Senonches; 20 February, Nos. 20 and 38 Scotch mills; 26 February, 30 Company's Scotch Mill; 27 February, 20 and 43 Company's Canadian Mills. 14 February — the Departmental Questionnaire for all Other Ranks in the District was completed and signed. 1 February notes "Billets unchanged during the month." The month's signatory is a Major signing for the O.C.; the signature is illegible (see the [uncertain] flag at the foot of the month). The place column reads "Alençon, Orne." throughout.
- **Count of [uncertain] flags in this file:** 2.
