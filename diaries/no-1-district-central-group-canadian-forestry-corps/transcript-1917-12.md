## Coverage notes

- **Dates with entries:** all 31 days of December 1917.
- **Dates with no entry:** none.
- **Pages that are covers/contents rather than diary text:** LAC p. 16 (cover), p. 17 (contents — "Diary Text 2 Pages"). Diary text is on LAC pp. 18–19 (2 pages, Army Form C. 2118).
- **Casualties:** none recorded in December 1917.
- **Weather:** none recorded in December 1917.
- **Movements:** 13–19 December — Major F. J. Carew proceeded on Command to Headquarters, Canadian Forestry Corps, France, in the Zone of the Armies, returning 19 December. 18 December — the District's Dominion Election ballots were taken by Major J. J. H. Fee from Alencon to the Commissaire du General du Canada in France, 17–19 Boulevard des Capucines, Paris. 21–27 December — Col. J. Grove White, Inspector of Camps, Canadian Forestry Corps, visited the District. 24 December — 100 H.D. horses arrived from Bordeaux District for distribution, quartered temporarily in French Cavalry Barracks. 26 December — No. 30 Company began to move to Belleme Foret. 28–30 December — Major Carew and Capt. Waldron, Q.M., went to Orleans to take over Nos. 41 and 43 Companies from No. 4 District; the companies were taken on strength of No. 1 District on 30 December. 31 December — No. 2 Construction Company (Black) arrived in the District.
- **Other notes:** 1 December — the poll opened for the Dominion of Canada elections, with Major Carew as Deputy Presiding Officer. 25 December — officers of the District at Headquarters for Christmas Dinner. The second diary sheet (LAC p. 19) opens with a struck-through line repeating the 1 December election entry — an abandoned duplicate, not a dated entry — and its printed heading is heavily overtyped. The 31 December entry is a handwritten addition in the original, not typed. The Hour column on each diary sheet carries handwritten initials rather than times; no times are recorded. The company number "43" in the 28 and 30 December entries appears overtyped in the original (see the [uncertain] flags).
