## Coverage notes

- **Dates with entries:** all 28 days of February 1918.
- **Dates with no entry:** none.
- **Pages that are covers/contents rather than diary text:** LAC p. 23 (cover), p. 24 (contents — "Diary Text 1 Page"). Diary text is on LAC p. 25 only (1 page, Army Form C. 2118, carrying all 28 days).
- **Casualties:** none recorded in February 1918.
- **Weather:** none recorded in February 1918.
- **Movements:** 9–12 February — No. 38 Company moved from Foret d'Andaine to Foret de Perseigne, finishing the move on 12 February; its mill began cutting on 17 February. 9 February — Lieut. E. C. Foote, Headquarters Central Group, arrived in the District on special duty. 27 February — advance party of No. 42 Company moved to Foret de Ecouves. 25–27 February — Major Carew visited the Orleans area, returning 27 February.
- **Other notes:** 17 February — Lt-Col. Donnelly (A.D.T.O.), Lt-Col. Williams and Lt-Col. Alhusen of Headquarters, Canadian Forestry Corps, visited Headquarters; a name before "Alhusen" is struck through in the original (see the [uncertain] flag). 20 February — Captain Graham, S.M.O., visited companies in the District. The Hour column on the diary sheet carries handwritten initials rather than times; no times are recorded.
- **Count of [uncertain] flags in this file:** 1.
