## Coverage notes

- **Dates with entries:** 6 to 31 August 1917 (26 days). The diary opens on 6 August, when Headquarters billets were first taken at Alencon.
- **Dates with no entry:** 1 to 5 August 1917 — no entries; the District was authorised on 26 July 1917 but Headquarters billets were not taken until 6 August.
- **Pages that are covers/contents rather than diary text:** LAC p. 1 (cover), p. 2 (contents — "Diary Text 2 Pages"). Diary text is on LAC pp. 3–4 (2 pages, Army Form C. 2118).
- **Casualties:** none recorded in August 1917.
- **Weather:** none recorded in August 1917.
- **Movements:** 10 August — Major F. J. Carew, O.C., and Staff completed moving from Conches to Alencon. 12 August — detachment of No. 54 Company left Conches for Lambel-Camors, Brittany, to take out piles for the American Disembarkation Port. 30 August — No. 38 Prisoner of War Company transferred from Rouvray Forest to Foret d'Andaines, detraining at Juvigny. Shipping of company product began during the month: No. 44 Company from Juvigny Station (20 August), No. 38 Company from Juvigny Station (24 August), No. 20 Company from Alencon Station (25 August), No. 30 Company from Almeneches Station (26 August), No. 42 Company from Bagnoles Station (27 August). No. 54 Company was shown in Group Orders as attached to No. 1 District (31 August).
- **Other notes:** The Remarks column on LAC p. 3 records that No. 1 District, Central Group, Canadian Forestry Corps — consisting of Nos. 20–30, 38–42 and 44 Companies, Headquarters at Alencon — was authorised on 26 July 1917, and that No. 44 Company mill began operations in the Forest de Andaines on 31 July 1917. Mill openings in August: No. 30 Company at Bellaere (13 August), No. 38 Company in Foret d'Andaines and No. 20 Company in Foret de Perseigne (14 August). No. 44 Company sawed 61,113 F.B.M. with a Scotch mill on 16 August, "making a record for the Central Group". The Hour column on each diary sheet carries handwritten initials rather than times; no times are recorded. The 23 August entry reads "No.43 Company", although No. 43 is not among the companies listed in the Remarks note (Nos. 20–30, 38–42 and 44) — see the [uncertain] flag.
