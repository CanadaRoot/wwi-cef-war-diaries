## 1 October 1918 — LAC p. 58 (diary sheet 1)

Place: Alencon. Hour: 7 A.M.

> Weather wet.

## 2 October 1918 — LAC p. 58 (diary sheet 1)

Place: Alencon. Hour: 7 A.M.

> Urgent message received from the D.T.O. to despatch as soon as possible 150 men and 6 N.C.O's (All "A" Category) to Can. Corps Reinforcement Camp as Reinforcements. Message received at midnight. Despatch Riders sent immediately to companies with orders. Weather. Fine.

## 3 October 1918 — LAC p. 58 (diary sheet 1)

Place: Alencon. Hour: 7 A.M.

> Lorries arrived from Companies with required reinforcements. Weather fine.

## 4 October 1918 — LAC p. 58 (diary sheet 1)

Place: Alencon. Hour: 7 A.M.

Weather fine. Capt. K. A. Morrison. O.C. Detachment No. 2 Construction Company recalled to Headquarters C.F.C. Paris Plage. Colonel Wilson, S.M.O. Corps visited Headquarters re hospital. Capt. R. Clarkson Headquarters visited District re despatch of draft of Reinforcements.

## 11 October 1918 — LAC p. 58 (diary sheet 1)

Place: Alencon. Hour: 7 A.M.

> Authority granted to enter Serie V Coupes D and E Foret de Ecouves, to be operated by No. 42 Coy. Weather -Wet. Colonel White D.T.O. visited Senonches operations. Brig-Gen. Ross J.G. Paymaster General, C.M.F.C. Colonel J.B. White D.T.O. Colonel E. Gagnon Paymaster Canadian Troops in France, Lt-Col. G. Todd i/c Pay and Administration, G.H.Q. France, Major C.E. Read D.T.O's Staff and Major Wolff, Canadian Record Office, London, visited the District. Met at Senonches by Lt-Col. Carew O.C. District and inspected all operations. Were greatly surprised by the size of the operation, and thought the work was marvellous. Proceeded to Alencon in the afternoon and inspected Pay Records. Everything was found to be exceedingly satisfactory.

## 24 October 1918 — LAC p. 59 (diary sheet 2)

Place: Alencon. Hour: 7 A.M.

> Weather-Fine. Colonel L. A. Dowey, Auditor General, Colonel McKinnon and Mr. McLelan visited No. 1 District Headquarters Camp and Ramp which they found in the best of condition. They dined with the Officers of No. 1 District H.Q. in the evening and proceeded the following morning to inspect the camps of Nos. 43 Coy. in the Foret de Bourse and 41 and 54 in the Senonches Forest. Lunch was taken at Senonches and the party proceeded to Paris. They were greatly impressed with the immensity of the operations in this District, especially in the Senonches Forest. This is the largest operation they have viewed.

## Coverage notes

- **Dates with entries:** all 31 days of October 1918. October is the first month in this range to record the weather every day.
- **Dates with no entry:** none.
- **Pages that are covers/contents rather than diary text:** LAC p. 56 (cover, Volume 15), p. 57 (contents — "Diary Text 2"). Diary text is on LAC pp. 58–59 (2 sheets, Army Form C. 2118, headed "H.Q. No. 1 District. CANADIAN FORESTRY CORPS B.E.F." and "For the month October 1918."; the heading "INTELLIGENCE SUMMARY" is struck through on both sheets).
- **Casualties:** none recorded in October 1918, but 15 October records 11 O.R's "Unsuitable for Forestry" evacuated to C.G.B.D. Etaples.
- **Weather:** recorded daily — wet 1, 9, 10, 17 October; very wet 20 and 21 October; unsettled 16 October; fair and cool 14 and 18 October; fine on all other days (2–8, 11–13, 15, 19, 22–31 October).
- **Movements:** 2–4 October — urgent reinforcement draft: 150 men and 6 N.C.O's (all "A" Category) despatched to the Canadian Corps Reinforcement Camp, leaving 4 October with four officers conducting; 50 O.R's brought in to "stand to" at Headquarters in case of a further call. 4 October — Capt. K. A. Morrison, O.C. Detachment No. 2 Construction Company, recalled to Headquarters C.F.C., Paris Plage. 21–23 October — Lt-Col. F. J. Carew proceeded to Evreux on duty, then on to Paris Plage. 11 October — authority granted to enter Serie V Coupes D and E, Foret de Ecouves, to be operated by No. 42 Company. 25 October — authority given by Col. Thirion to enter Serie 1 Coupes C2, C3, D3 and Serie 2 Coupes A3, B3, C2 in the Forest de Bourse.
- **Other notes:** 8 October — Major Moore, Corps Q.M., arrived to inspect Q.M. stores of all companies. 9 October — letter of congratulation from the D.T.O. on the prompt despatch of the reinforcement draft; telephone connections made between Headquarters Camp, Q.M. Stores, Headquarters and the Officers' Mess. 11 October — visit by Brig.-Gen. Ross (Paymaster General, C.M.F.C.), Col. J. B. White (D.T.O.), Col. E. Gagnon, Lt-Col. G. Todd, Major C. E. Read and Major Wolff; they inspected the Senonches operations and the pay records, "thought the work was marvellous". 13 October — Colonel Toney, Capt. Oliver and Capt. McKinnon visited to investigate establishing the "Khaki College" educational scheme in the area. 15 October — Major A. T. Hill (A/O.C. Central Group) and Col. Hayes (S.M.O. Central Group) visited re the intended hospital site. 19 October — record shipment of wood for the District, 598 trucks; new hospital site secured at No. 32 Rue Bercail, Alencon. 24 October — visit by Colonel L. A. Dowey (Auditor General), Colonel McKinnon and Mr. McLelan; they called the Senonches Forest operation "the largest operation they have viewed". 28 October — owing to the serious nature of the influenza epidemic, Alencon was placed out of bounds to British troops except those passing through on regimental duties. 30 October — Major A. M. Hill (Second in Command, O.C. C.F.C.) and Lieut-Col. Hayes D.S.O. (CAMC) visited re the new hospital. 31 October — record production and shipments for the month: 30,428 tons produced, 32,496 tons shipped, 76 complete trains of 2,314 wagons despatched.
