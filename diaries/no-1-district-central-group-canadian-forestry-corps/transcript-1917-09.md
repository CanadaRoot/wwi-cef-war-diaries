## 7 September 1917 — LAC p. 7 (diary p. 1)

Place: ALENCON. Hour: —

> Billets unchanged. When No. 30 Company men felled a poplar tree it was found that it contained four nests of bees which swarmed out and stopped operations for two hours.

## 9 September 1917 — LAC p. 7 (diary p. 1)

Place: ALENCON. Hour: —

> Billets unchanged. International Sports in Alencon. Canadian Forestry Corps, Central Group, including C.A.S.C. and A.S.C. attached won the following prizes, 1st, Tug of War. 1st, 145 lb. boxing. 1st 125 lb boxing, 2nd High Jump. 3rd, Relay Race. Conference of Officers Commanding Companies at Headquarters.

## 10 September 1917 — LAC p. 7 (diary p. 1)

Place: ALENCON. Hour: —

> Billets unchanged. Lieut. Martin and detachment of No. 54 Company operating at Lambel-Camors Brittany for the American Army returned to Alencon. Major Utton, A.G's Staff Rouen visited all companies in the District. Revised sawing instructions sent to Companies. No. 54 Company began shipping product of Nos. 20 & 30 Companies from Sees.

## 11 September 1917 — LAC p. 7 (diary p. 1)

Place: ALENCON. Hour: —

> Billets unchanged. Lieut. Hocey and Lambel-Camors detachment proceeded to Almeneches to rejoin No. 54 Company.

## 12 September 1917 — LAC p. 7 (diary p. 1)

Place: ALENCON. Hour: —

> Billets unchanged. Major Peacock, Headquarters Paris-Plage visited Headquarters. New shipping instructions for product received.

## 13 September 1917 — LAC p. 7 (diary p. 1)

Place: ALENCON. Hour: —

> Billets unchanged. Commandant Jaubier and Lieut. Roux, French Forestry Officers, visited H.Q. No. 54 Company began shipping their own product from Sees Station.

Place: ALENCON. Hour: —

Place: ALENCON. Hour: —

> Billets unchanged. Major Garratt and Capt. Mason, A.S.C. visited Nos. 30 & 54 Companies to inspect Transport. Major Carew went to Conches for a conference with the O.C. Central Group.

## 28 September 1917 — LAC p. 8 (diary p. 2)

Place: ALENCON. Hour: —

> Billets unchanged. Major Garratt and Capt. Mason, A.S.C., inspected Transport at Nos. 38, 42 and 44 Companies. Major Carew returned from Conches. Capt. Graham, Medical Officer, Central Group, visited Headquarters in connection with the organization of Medical Services for the District.

## 29 September 1917 — LAC p. 8 (diary p. 2)

Place: ALENCON. Hour: —

> New Camp ground rented near the Ramp, Alencon. Erection of huts begun. General Faurie, Commander of the Fourth Region, Army Corps, at Le Mans, Sarthe, visited Nos. 30 & 54 Companies with Major Carew. Colonel Jones, O.C. C.G., visited Headquarters.

## 30 September 1917 — LAC p. 8 (diary p. 2)

Place: ALENCON. Hour: —

> Billets unchanged. Colonel Jones left Headquarters and proceeded to the Foret d'Andaine.

## Coverage notes

- **Dates with entries:** all 30 days of September 1917.
- **Dates with no entry:** none.
- **Pages that are covers/contents rather than diary text:** LAC p. 5 (cover), p. 6 (contents — "Diary Text 2 Pages"). Diary text is on LAC pp. 7–8 (2 pages, Army Form C. 2118).
- **Casualties:** none recorded in September 1917.
- **Weather:** none recorded in September 1917.
- **Movements:** 10 September — Lieut. Martin and the No. 54 Company detachment at Lambel-Camors, Brittany (working for the American Army) returned to Alencon. 11 September — Lieut. Hocey and the Lambel-Camors detachment proceeded to Almeneches to rejoin No. 54 Company. 17–20 September — No. 38 Company stopped operating on its old site and moved to a new site, Series 2 Forest d'Andaine, five miles from La Ferte Mace; its mill began cutting timber there on 20 September. 19 September — Lieut. W. Dippie, A.S.C. (M.T.), with ten lorries and 26 other ranks, reported for duty at No. 54 Company to haul product. A new camp ground was rented near the Ramp, Alencon, on 29 September, and hut building began there (begun 19 September at Alencon Station).
- **Other notes:** 7 September — four nests of bees in a felled poplar stopped No. 30 Company operations for two hours. 9 September — International Sports at Alencon; Canadian Forestry Corps, Central Group (including attached C.A.S.C. and A.S.C.) won 1st in Tug of War, 1st in 145 lb. boxing, 1st in 125 lb. boxing, 2nd in High Jump and 3rd in the Relay Race. Additional billets were taken on Rue Bretaine, Alencon, on 16 September. The Hour column on each diary sheet carries handwritten initials rather than times; no times are recorded.
