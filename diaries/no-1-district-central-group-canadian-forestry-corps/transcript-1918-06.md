## Coverage notes

- **Dates with entries:** 29 of the 30 days of June 1918. Days with no change of billets are entered as "Billets unchanged." (ditto marks in the original, expanded here).
- **Dates with no entry:** 10 June — the Summary column for that date appears blank in the original [uncertain].
- **Pages that are covers/contents rather than diary text:** LAC p. 36 (cover, Volume 11), p. 37 (contents — "Diary Text 1 Page"). Diary text is on LAC p. 38 (1 page, Army Form C. 2118).
- **Casualties:** none recorded in June 1918.
- **Weather:** none recorded in June 1918.
- **Movements:** none recorded in June 1918.
- **Other notes:** 2 June — instructions for an "A" Class of 150 Other Ranks drawn from companies every ten days for a course in Infantry Training; the first class reported to Headquarters 8 June. 11 June — instructions received re organisation and mobilisation of an Infantry Battalion from the District. 29 June — No. 30 Company commenced operating a Scotch Mill in conjunction with the Canadian Mill. 30 June — representatives of all companies attended sports at Alencon to celebrate "Dominion Day" (the sports programme and newspaper report are filed as appendices to the July diary). The diary-text page is headed "From 1.5.18 to 31.5.18" in the original — a misprint, as the dates entered run 1.6.18 to 30.6.18. The Hour column carries a handwritten entry on every line, read throughout as 7 A.M. [uncertain]. The signature at the foot of the month is a scrawl, read as F. J. Carew, Major [uncertain].
- **Count of [uncertain] flags in this file:** 6.
