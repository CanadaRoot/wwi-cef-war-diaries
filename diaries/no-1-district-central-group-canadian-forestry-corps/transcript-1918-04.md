## Coverage notes

- **Dates with entries:** all 30 days of April 1918.
- **Dates with no entry:** none.
- **Pages that are covers/contents rather than diary text:** LAC p. 29 (cover), p. 30 (contents — "Diary Text 2 Pages"). Diary text is on LAC pp. 31–32 (2 pages, Army Form C. 2118).
- **Casualties:** none recorded in April 1918.
- **Weather:** none recorded in April 1918.
- **Movements:** 2 April — No. 14 [uncertain] P. of W. Company arrived (3 Officers, 111 escort, 392 prisoners). 22 April — No. 38 Company moved from Perseigne to Belleme Forest. 28 April — 55 Russian reinforcements arrived from the Canadian General Base Depot. 29 April — Lord Lovat, Col. Williams, Col. White (D.T.O., D.S.O.) and Lieut-Col. Jones, O.C. Central Group, arrived in the District for a tour of inspection.
- **Other notes:** 3 April — orders received to compile a District Return showing all ranks according to Medical Category and Military Training. 6 April — meeting of Officers Commanding Forestry Companies and Prisoner of War Companies at District Headquarters. 17 April — orders received to discontinue infantry training except musketry instruction for other ranks not engaged on production; 23 April — orders received to resume infantry training in time men are not on production. 30 April — Major T. T. Kirkby appointed acting Adjutant. Major Carew signs this month as Lieut-Colonel — his promotion appears in the signature block; earlier months he signed as Major. The Hour column on each diary sheet carries handwritten initials rather than times; no times are recorded.
