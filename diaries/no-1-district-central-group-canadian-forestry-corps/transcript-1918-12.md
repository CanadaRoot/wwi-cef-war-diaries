## 1 December 1918 — LAC p. 66 (diary Page 1)

## Coverage notes

- **Dates with entries:** all 31 days of December 1918.
- **Dates with no entry:** none.
- **Pages that are covers/contents rather than diary text:** LAC p. 64 (cover, "CONFIDENTIAL"), p. 65 (contents — "Diary Text 2 Pages"). Diary text is on LAC pp. 66–67 (2 pages, Army Form C. 2118). The diary sheets are headed "WAR DIARY or INTELLIGENCE SUMMARY" for "No. 1 District, Canadian Forestry Corps, For month of December, 1918."
- **Casualties:** none recorded in December 1918.
- **Weather:** recorded most days — wet 1–2, 4–5 and 16–31 December; bad 6–14 December; fine 15 December only. No weather is recorded for 3 December.
- **Movements:** demobilization drafts dominate the month — 1–2 December, 5 Officers and 196 Other Ranks to Conches en route to Etaples for drafting to Canada; 4 December, 1 Officer and 135 Other Ranks of No. 2 Canadian Construction Company (coloured) left for Etaples, and the Rear Party at No. 43 Company left Orleans to rejoin its Unit; 7 December, 1 Officer and 108 Other Ranks arrived from No. 2 District to be entrained, and 3 Officers and 142 Other Ranks were sent to C.G.B.D. for return to Canada; 13 December, 200 prisoners sent from 38 P.O.W. Company to 43 Company for attached duty.
- **Other notes:** 11–13 December — a fire broke out at No. 20 Company caused by spontaneous combustion; 50 men from Headquarters, 50 from 42 Company and 20 teams from 42 and 43 Companies, then 50 men from 30 Company, were sent to fight it; the fire was got under control and no damage was done to stock or mill. 15 December — a football match between Nos. 1 and 2 District, C.F.C., played in Alencon, ended in a draw with no scoring. 25 December — Christmas Day observed as a holiday throughout the District. 27 December — a dinner of all officers of No. 1 District was held in the evening. The month's signatory is a Major signing for the O.C.; the signature is illegible (see the [uncertain] flag at the foot of the month). The place column reads "Alençon, Orne." throughout.
