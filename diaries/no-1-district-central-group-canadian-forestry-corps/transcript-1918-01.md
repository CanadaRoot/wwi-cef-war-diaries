## Coverage notes

- **Dates with entries:** all 31 days of January 1918.
- **Dates with no entry:** none.
- **Pages that are covers/contents rather than diary text:** LAC p. 20 (cover — "CONFIDENTAL" as written), p. 21 (contents — "Diary Text 1 Page"). Diary text is on LAC p. 22 only (1 page, Army Form C. 2118, carrying all 31 days).
- **Casualties:** none recorded in January 1918.
- **Weather:** none recorded in January 1918.
- **Movements:** 1 January — two Officers and 180 other ranks of No. 2 Construction Company attached to the District. 5 January — No. 106 German P. of W. Company arrived in the District. 11 January — 85 reinforcements reported from the Labour Pool. 17 January — No. 30 Company mill started cutting in Belleme Forest. 21 January — an extra tract of ground was rented at the Camp and erection of a Machine Shop started there. 26 January — Major F. J. Carew proceeded to Evreux for a conference with Brig-General MacDougall.
- **Other notes:** 13 January — Major Carew attended a conference in Paris. 16 January — conference of Officers Commanding Companies at Headquarters, with Colonel Jones, O.C. Central Group, present. The Hour column on the diary sheet carries handwritten initials rather than times; no times are recorded.
