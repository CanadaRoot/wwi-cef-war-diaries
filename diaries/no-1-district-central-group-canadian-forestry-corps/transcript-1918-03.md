- **Casualties:** none recorded in March 1918.
- **Weather:** none recorded in March 1918.
- **Movements:** 10 March — 50 other ranks reinforcements arrived from the Labour Pool. 14 March — Major W. S. Waldie arrived for duty from England as O.C. 54 Company. 22 March — No. 42 Company completed its move to Foret d'Ecouves and began sawing. 23 March — General Phillips, Lt-Col. Jones (O.C. Central Group) and Major Carew proceeded from Alencon to visit Nos. 41 and 43 Companies operating near Orleans. 25–27 March — Russian reinforcements arrived from the Canadian Corps (40 on 25 March, 24 on 26 March, 50 on 27 March); on 27 March orders were received for companies to be ready to move up to the Armies Area. 29 March — Nos. 13 and 31 German P. of W. Companies arrived at H.Q. for duty in the District.
