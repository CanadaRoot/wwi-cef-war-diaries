## Manifest structure (note for readers)

The LAC manifest runs chronologically, Aug 1917 – Mar 1919 (pages 1–83), then contains a SECOND set of September 1918 pages (pages 84–128, "Septembre 1918, p. 1" – "p. 45"). The main September 1918 diary text is on pages 52–55; the second set is the original handwritten field diary — Army Book 136, Vol. 2, "#1 District, Sept 1918 – March 1919" — the rough day book kept at District Headquarters from which the typed monthly diaries (LAC pp. 52–83) were compiled. Its wording and figures differ in places from the typed fair copies, and it contains entries and details not in the typed versions. Transcribed as `transcript-1918-09-to-1919-03-army-book-136.md`; its entries are indexed separately in INDEX-G.md under "(Army Book 136 field diary)" sections.
