## Coverage notes

- **Dates with entries:** all 31 days of August 1918. Days with no change of billets are entered as "Billets unchanged." (ditto marks in the original, expanded here) — no date has no entry.
- **Dates with no entry:** none.
- **Pages that are covers/contents rather than diary text:** LAC p. 49 (cover, Volume 13), p. 50 (contents — "Diary Text 2 Pages"). Diary text is on LAC p. 51 (1 page, Army Form C. 2118, headed "H.Q., No 1 District, Canadian Forestry Corps, B. E. F." and "AUGUST, 1918.").
- **Casualties:** none recorded in August 1918.
- **Weather:** none recorded in August 1918.
- **Movements:** 1 August — narrow gauge railway commenced operations at 41 Company. 2 August — one Officer, 27 Other Ranks, ten lorries and one motor-cycle attached from the 118th Auxiliary Petrol A. S. C. 15 August — one Officer and 31 Other Ranks with ten steam tractors, trailers and box wagons attached from the 117th Auxiliary Steam Company. 24 August — 2nd Eastern Company, Non-Combatant Corps (1 Officer, 96 Other Ranks) attached. 29 August — Sir Geo. Perley and Colonel White, D.T.O., visited the District. 30 August — Sir Robert Faulkner and Colonel Fallis, A.D.C.S., visited the District.
- **Other notes:** 4 August — special church service on the 4th anniversary of the Declaration of War. The contents page says "Diary Text 2 Pages" but only one diary-text page (LAC p. 51) is in this page range; the whole month's 31 entries fit on that single page. The Hour column carries a handwritten entry on every line, read throughout as 9.20 [uncertain]. The signature at the foot of the month is a scrawl, read as J. C. Oliver, Capt., Adjt. [uncertain].
- **Count of [uncertain] flags in this file:** 4.
