## Coverage notes

- **Dates with entries:** all 30 days of November 1917.
- **Dates with no entry:** none.
- **Pages that are covers/contents rather than diary text:** LAC p. 13 (cover), p. 14 (contents — "Diary Text 1 Page"). Diary text is on LAC p. 15 only (1 page, Army Form C. 2118, carrying all 30 days).
- **Casualties:** none recorded in November 1917.
- **Weather:** none recorded in November 1917.
- **Movements:** 5 November — No. 78 Company moved from No. 1 District to Leon les Landes, Bordeaux District. 8 November — new camp at the Ramp, Alencon, opened and other ranks moved to quarters in huts. 16 November — Major J. J. H. Fee went to England on Command, returning 24 November. 18 November — Major C. A. Low went to England on Command. 19 November — 52 other ranks of No. 24 Company (transferred from combatant units) attached to No. 1 District units pending transfer.
- **Other notes:** Sawing records: No. 38 Company cut 62,957 F.B.M. with a Scotch mill on 1 November; No. 44 Company cut 93,546 F.B.M. with a Scotch mill on 16 November. 28 November — ballots received for the Dominion Elections for six companies and the nominal roll of voters completed. 30 November — a Medical Board arrived in the District to examine clerks, stenographers, batmen and C.A.S.C. M.T. drivers. The Hour column on the diary sheet carries handwritten initials rather than times; no times are recorded. The signature at the foot of the sheet is partly cut off at the page edge.
