## Page 132 — Diary text (May 1917, p. 4)

> Place: VIMY | Date: 1917, May 1–2
>
> **1 May (contd)** — Two new Officers reported for duty — Lt [uncertain — Soulsby or Coulsby] attached to C Section + Lt Doutille [uncertain] to D Section. One gun mounted at Transport lines for firing at enemy aircraft. During the day 500 rounds were fired at hostile aircraft + an enemy plane was turned back towards its own lines. Enemy Artillery active during the day especially in the afternoon — LA CHAUDIERE, the Railway embankment + STATION receiving much attention. — Enemy Artillery very active on our right.
>
> **2 May** — From 7 am. hostile Artillery quieter but much air activity. Enemy shelled the whole area intermittently throughout the day with occasional bursts of heavy fire — VIMY STATION, the Railway embankment, CHAUDIERE and ANDROS Corner all received special attention. A.E.B.
>
> 5.25 a.m. Our artillery put on practice barrage — Enemy retaliated on our front + support lines. On the night Enemy machine guns were active sweeping roads + approaches to front line. A.E.B.

## Page 133 — Diary text (May 1917, p. 5)

> Date: 1917, May 2–4
>
> **2 May (contd)** — 9.30 to 11 p.m. Enemy machine guns sweeping embankment. A.E.B.
>
> **3 May** — Our heavy + Divisional Artillery very active all day. Enemy shelling on front lines light — Usual targets shelled in back areas. — LENS ARRAS road between PETIT VIMY and CHAUDIERE + the area west of CHAUDIERE was shelled all afternoon by 5.9" + 8" guns. — One of our M.G. positions heavily shelled with gas shells. A.E.B.
>
> 8.20 p.m. Enemy aeroplane crossed low over one of our positions + was turned back with 200 rounds of our M.G. fire. A.E.B.
>
> **4 May** — Enemy artillery activity considerable during the morning. VIMY, CHAUDIERE, LENS-ARRAS road, the Railway + QUARRIES were heavily shelled. During the night some of our gun positions shelled with gas shells. During the day 2,000 rounds were fired at enemy aircraft with good results. Pte Whitley a Company runner wounded. A.E.B.

## Page 138 — Diary text (May 1917, p. 10)

> Date: 1917, May 13–14
>
> **13 May (contd)** — Two guns T20 b 10.85. One gun T16 d 0.0. Two guns T21 a 26 77. Two guns T14 d 18. Enemy shelled the support trench in front gun positions. VIMY STATION and railway embankment. Heavy shelling on our right from 9 p.m. to 10 p.m. — Much work is being done to strengthen our positions. The weather remains fine + warm. A.E.B.
>
> **14 May** — Enemy Artillery fairly active — Many shells were fired at VIMY STATION and VIMY mostly 4.2s and also at Railway embankment. Shells [uncertain] fired from T16 b 2 8½. Enemy planes fairly active — One plane came quite close over the QUARRIES but was driven back by our Machine Gun fire. A.E.B.

## Page 139 — Diary text (May 1917, p. 11)

> Date: 1917, May 15–17
>
> **15 May** — The day was quiet in this Sector of the Front — Enemy planes normal. Artillery fire below normal on both sides during the day. During the night owing to an expected German relief our guns reached the roads + over land [uncertain] roads expending about 6000 rounds — Targets mostly in and around MERICOURT. A.E.B.
>
> **16 May** — Aeroplane activity below normal — Artillery more normal. Enemy Artillery quiet. A.E.B.
>
> **17 May** — Our Artillery fired on working parties and movement during the day + during the night on enemy roads in MERICOURT. AVION + MERICOURT also received attention from our heavies. The Enemy seems to be strengthening his positions with new trenches + wire. Aircraft were active on both sides — During the early morning we fired 1700 rounds at enemy planes who rarely ventured more than a short distance over the trenches. A.E.B.

## Page 140 — Diary text (May 1917, p. 12)

> Date: 1917, May 18–20
>
> **18 May** — Our heavy Artillery very active against AVION + MERICOURT. Enemy Artillery more active. VIMY + MONT FORET QUARRIES receiving more attention than for some days past. — Aircraft active on both sides. We expended 1000 rounds on Enemy Aircraft. A.E.B.
>
> **19 May** — Seven Enemy planes crossed our lines on the whole Corps front + one was brought down in flames near MERICOURT. Our Artillery active. Enemy Artillery active. We are doing a lot of work at the gun positions digging deep dugouts + generally making as strong defence as possible. We are also bringing up about 10,000 rounds of ammunition per night. A.E.B.
>
> **20 May** — Both our own + Enemy Aircraft were active during the day. Many Enemy planes over our lines — One dropped bombs at S.27 a 8.5. about 2.00 p.m. An enemy plane was forced to land by one of our triplanes [uncertain]. A.E.B.
