## Page 68 — Diary text (October 1916, p. 3)

Army Form C. 2118, "WAR DIARY or INTELLIGENCE SUMMARY" (heading struck through). Handwritten; headed "Original", "October 1st to October 31st 1916".

> Place: BOUZINCOURT | Date: 1916, October 1–6
>
> **1 October** — 10.30 am Church Parade with 60th Bn.
>
> **2 October** — 9.am Parades as usual in morning. In afternoon the Coy. moved to Brickfields, Albert where they bivouaced.
>
> **3 October** — 5.00 am 2 Officers sent to No. 2, 9 [uncertain] 4th & 6th C.M.G. Co's in the line to arrange relief. 4.30 pm 2 Sections marched off and took up positions as follows: 3 guns under Lieut Walker near M26 A 86; [uncertain] Whitaker near R36 A 97; [uncertain] R36 B 36; 2 [guns under] Drinkwater with 43rd Bn. Relief completed at 10.30 pm. Ref. maps:— Le Sars 1/10000.
>
> **4 October** — Quiet during the day & heavy shelling during evening.
>
> **5 October** — Our artillery fairly active. "C" Section took up reserve positions. Evacuated positions held by Lieuts Walker & Drinkwater (as shown above), and took up new positions near R.36 A 97. 1 Officer wounded. 1 O.R. killed.
>
> **6 October** — A & B Sections came under orders of Col Brutinel [uncertain] for [uncertain] operations. 1 O.R. wounded.

## Page 69 — Diary text (October 1916, p. 4)

> Place: BAILIFF WOOD | Date: 1916, October 7–14
>
> **7 October** — "C" Section moved to front line with 43rd Bn. 1 Officer & 1 O.R. wounded. Artillery on both sides very active.
>
> **8 October** — Attack on REGINA TRENCH took place at 4.50 A.M. Machine Guns fired intensely until 6.00 A.M. and at intervals during day & night (about 60,000 rds). Attack was unsuccessful owing to enemy's wire not being cut. Our C.O. Capt MacKinnon was killed. 1 O.R. killed. 1 Officer wounded. 2 O.R. missing. 1 gun destroyed by shell fire.
>
> **9 October** — A, B, & C Sections & Headquarters relieved. D Section came under orders of 8th C.M.G. Co. 2. O.R. wounded.
>
> **10 October** — Reached Tara Hill at 1 a.m. At 1.20 p.m. Company marched to the Brickfields, Albert. 1 O.R. evacuated sick.
>
> **11 October** — Company rested.
>
> **12 October** — Moved from Brickfields to Warloy.
>
> **13 October** — " " Warloy to Beauval.
>
> **14 October** — " " Beauval to Fieffe.

## Page 71 — Diary text (October 1916, p. 6)

> Place: ELBE TRENCH | Date: 1916, October 25–31
>
> **25 October** — Front all quiet. Improvements on dug outs & emplacements commenced.
>
> **26 October** — Enemy trench mortars active on right front. Artillery on both sides quiet.
>
> **27 October** — Enemy trench mortars very active. Improvement work continued and several emplacements completed.
>
> **28 October** — Trench mortars active. Front otherwise quiet. Working party observed and dispersed by indirect fire from our guns. D Section relieved "A" Section who went into reserve at BRAY.
>
> **29 October** — All quiet on front. Work continued.
>
> **30 October** — Trench mortars on both sides very active. Two working parties dispersed by indirect fire.
>
> **31 October** — Usual trench mortar activity. Noticeable increase in enemy machine gun fire at night.
>
> [signed] *A. S. Page Lieut*
> *for O.C. 9th C. M. G. Co.*
