## Page 79 — Diary text (December 1916, p. 3)

Army Form C. 2118, "WAR DIARY or INTELLIGENCE SUMMARY" (heading struck through). Handwritten; headed "December 1st to 31st 1916".

> Place: ELBE TRENCH | Date: 1916, December 1–6
>
> **1 December** — Considerable trench mortar activity in left subsection. Our artillery retaliated in the afternoon. Our guns fired on selected targets at night.
>
> **2 December** — Mutual trench mortar activity in left subsection. Right subsection was quiet. Our guns active at night.
>
> **3 December** — Usual trench mortar activity in left subsection. "A" Section relieved "B" Section in right subsection. Our guns fired on selected targets during day & night.
>
> **4 December** — Little trench mortar activity. Our aeroplanes were active. Our artillery active with no retaliation. Our guns firing as usual at night.
>
> **5 December** — Mutual trench mortar activity in both subsections. Our artillery still active. Our guns active during day and night.
>
> **6 December** — About 5.15 am. a party of 52nd Can. Bn. raided the enemy trenches from A16 9/95 [as written] to A16 a 9985 [as written]. Casualties were inflicted [continued on next page].

> Place: ELBE TRENCH | Date: 1916, December 6–9
>
> **6 December (contd)** — and prisoners taken. Artillery, trench mortars and machine guns co-operated in putting a barrage in rear and flanks.
>
> **7 December** — Usual trench mortar activity in both subsections. Enemy machine guns very active during night. Our guns active during day and night.

They, however, bombed the trench and inflicted [continued on next page].

## Page 81 — Diary text (December 1916, p. 5)

> Place: ELBE TRENCH | Date: 1916, December 9–12
>
> **9 December (contd)** — casualties. Artillery, trench mortars & machine guns put on a barrage in rear of objective. Casualty:— 1 O.R. wounded.
>
> **10 December** — Front comparatively quiet. Slight trench mortar activity on right. About 6 p.m. a party from the 58th Can Bn. raided the enemy trenches at A4 d 3300 to A4 d 3525. Casualties were inflicted but no prisoners taken. We co-operated with artillery and trench mortars in barrage work. Owing to a suspected relief by the enemy we fired heavily on communication trenches and roads behind enemy lines.
>
> **11 December** — Enemy put trench mortars & shrapnel on support line in right subsection. Left subsection was normal. Our guns fired as usual at night. "C" Section relieved "D" Section in the overhead fire positions.
>
> **12 December** — Usual mutual trench mortar activity in both subsections. Enemy artillery fairly active. Our artillery retaliated with [continued on next page].

## Page 82 — Diary text (December 1916, p. 6)

> Place: ELBE TRENCH | Date: 1916, December 12–15
>
> **12 December (contd)** — good effect. Our guns more active than usual during the day and as usual at night.
>
> **13 December** — Usual trench mortar activity. Artillery of both sides fairly active. Enemy machine guns very active at night. Our firing as usual.
>
> **14 December** — Considerable trench mortar and rifle grenade activity on right subsection. Quiet on left. Mutual machine gun activity at night.
>
> **15 December** — Artillery of both sides active during the day. [uncertain] party from 43rd Bn attempted a raid on enemy trench near THELUS ROAD. The explosion of an enemy bomb was mistaken for the explosion of the ammonal tube and the artillery, machine guns & trench mortars opened fire prematurely. The fuse of the tube was out of order and the party had to retire without blowing the enemy wire. We dispersed a [continued on next page].

## Page 83 — Diary text (December 1916, p. 7)

> Place: ELBE TRENCH | Date: 1916, December 15–19
>
> **15 December (contd)** — working party at A11 b 6580 during the afternoon. One man was seen to fall and was later carried off. A number of enemy shells falling in our right subsection were "blinds".
>
> **16 December** — Trench mortars of both sides quite active. Our artillery was active in afternoon. Our aircraft active both morning & afternoon. Our guns firing on selected targets at night.
>
> **17 December** — Mutual artillery and trench mortar activity at intervals during day. Fairly quiet at night. Enemy machine guns very active during early part of evening. Our guns were firing as usual. "A" Section relieved "B" in the left subsection.
>
> **18 December** — Usual mutual artillery & trench mortar activity. Enemy machine guns quiet. Our firing as usual.
>
> **19 December** — Trench mortars were active. Front otherwise quiet. Our guns firing as usual. "A" [uncertain] Section relieved "B" in the left subsection.

## Page 84 — Diary text (December 1916, p. 8)

> Place: ELBE TRENCH | Date: 1916, December 20–22
>
> **20 December** — Our artillery active with light retaliation. In the afternoon (3.15 p.m.) The 1st Cdn. Bn. attacked enemy lines at A22 b 3345 to A16 c 9600. Under cover of a barrage by artillery, trench mortars & machine guns they succeeded in entering the front line & supports and held them for 1½ hours, when, upon a given signal, they withdrew. 2 officers & 56 O.R. German prisoners. 1st Cdn. Bn. lost 6 O.R. killed & 18 O.R. wounded, all of which were evacuated. Three of our guns took part in barrage work. [inserted] Our casualties:— 2 [uncertain] killed & 2 O.R. wounded. Firing as usual at night.
>
> **21 December** — Slight trench mortar on left. Enemy machine guns active at night. "B" Section relieved "C" Section in the overhead fire positions.
>
> **22 December** — Usual trench mortar activity, especially heavy in left subsection during afternoon. At night enemy shelled our support line in the right subsection.

## Page 85 — Diary text (December 1916, p. 9)

> Place: ELBE TRENCH | Date: 1916, December 23–29
>
> **23 December** — Whole section comparatively quiet. Enemy threw a few trench mortars into front line in left subsection. "B" Section relieved "C" Section in the overhead fire positions.
>
> **24 December** — Mutual artillery & trench mortar activity throughout the day. Quiet at night. Our guns firing as usual.
>
> **25 December** — Very quiet all day & night. Our guns firing as usual.
>
> **26 December** — Our artillery very active. No retaliation. At night enemy put in about 20 "whiz-bangs" near ELBE TRENCH in right subsection. Enemy trench mortars active on our left. Our guns active at night.
>
> **27 December** — Our artillery, especially the heavies, active all day. Mutual trench mortar activity. Enemy especially active. "C" Section relieved "D" Section in the right subsection.
>
> **28 December** — Artillery normal. Both sides trench mortars very active. Our guns firing as usual.
>
> **29 December** — Our artillery active. Enemy more active than usual [continued on next page].

## Page 86 — Diary text (December 1916, p. 10)

> Place: ELBE TRENCH | Date: 1916, December 29–31
>
> **29 December (contd)** — and using heavier guns. Trench mortars of both sides very active especially so in right subsection.
>
> **30 December** — Our artillery and light trench mortars active. Enemy was exceptionally quiet all day. Our guns very active at night.
>
> **31 December** — Artillery and trench mortars rather quiet. Enemy was walking overland in several places. Our snipers claim several hits. Our guns quiet until midnight, when they all fired heavily for a few minutes. Our artillery did likewise. "D" Section relieved "A" in the left subsection.
>
> [signed] *A. S. Page Lieut*
> *for O.C. 9th Canadian Machine Gun Coy.*
