## Page 108 — Diary text (April 1917, p. 3)

Army Form C. 2118, "WAR DIARY or INTELLIGENCE SUMMARY" (heading struck through). Handwritten; headed "Ref. Map VIMY. 1/10000 & 36. B." "Instructions regarding War Diaries and Intelligence Summaries are contained in F. S. Regs., Part II. and the Staff Manual respectively. Title Pages will be prepared in manuscript."

> Place: 1917 GOUY-SERVINS, LA FOLIE SECTION | Date: 1917, April 1
>
> **1 April** — We relieved the 7th C. M. G. Co in the LA FOLIE SECTION with 7 guns at the following positions:— (No 6) S 27 b.6.8. (No 7) S 21 d 4½ 4½. (No 8) S 27 b 6½ 9½ (No 9) S 21 d 4½ 5.3. (No 16) S 26 b.6.3 (Nos 7+18) M.G. FORT S 27 b 1.6. Two guns were also relieved at X 19 a 3595 (2 [uncertain] b). The latter are mounted for anti-aircraft purposes. Relief was completed by 11.45 pm. Company strength (in trenches) 11 officers & 141 O.R. Company Hdqrs are in M.G. FORT. L.S.P. [Remarks: O.O. No 49 attached]
>
> **2 April** — Our artillery put on a barrage at 6.30 am which lasted for 15 minutes. Enemy shelled NEUVILLE ST. VAAST during the morning. L.S.P.
>
> **3 April** — Our artillery continues their destructive bombardment and harassing fire at night. Enemy artillery was very active between 9 & 10 am especially on LA SALLE Tr. and "P" LINE. "B" Section were relieved at [uncertain] anti-aircraft positions [uncertain] at X 019 a 3595 and moved into the line to "K" Battery (S 27 a 5 4 12). "B" Section (2 guns) were relieved by "A" at anti-aircraft positions. L.S.P. [Remarks: O.O. No 50 attached]
>
> **4 April** — Bombardment continued at intervals throughout the [day and night — continued on next page]

## Page 109 — Diary text (April 1917, p. 4)

> Place: LA FOLIE SECTION | Date: 1917, April 4–8
>
> **4 April (contd)** — day and night. Enemy 'plane brought down by our machine guns and landed behind enemy lines. Our guns active at night on artillery targets within range. Casualty 1 O.R. killed L.S.P.
>
> **5 April** — Bombardment continued on enemy positions. Enemy artillery retaliating strongly especially in vicinity of NEUVILLE ST. VAAST & M.G. FORT and LA SALLE AVE. Our guns active at night on selected targets and in anti-aircraft work during day. L.S.P.
>
> **6 April** — Bombardment continued. Enemy retaliating near M.G. FORT and GRANGE TRENCH. Our guns active as usual. "A" Section moved into line and took up positions at "K" Battery (S 27 a 5 4 12). L.S.P. [Remarks: O.O. No 51 attached]
>
> **7 April** — Bombardment continued at intervals. Enemy retaliated strongly on GRANGE and "P" LINE. Our No 6. Position was damaged and entrance to dugout blown in. 1 O.R. killed. Gun moved from No 6. Position to M.G. FORT. 1 O.R. wounded L.S.P.
>
> **8 April** — Our artillery quiet except for two fierce bombardments of a few minutes each. Enemy quiet. Our men are walking overland behind our support line in daylight [continued on next page]

## Page 111 — Diary text (April 1917, p. 6)

> Place: LA FOLIE SECTION | Date: 1917, April 9–11
>
> **9 April (contd)** — forward slightly in advance of the artillery barrage. The infantry met with small resistance from the enemy except at one or two points where they were held up for a short time. When the final objectives had been reached we put a barrage on for half an hour. From that time we put an intermittent barrage across our front and later on our left flank which was "in the air" for some hours before the division on our left attained its final objective and connected with our line. In the 24 hours after zero the 3 batteries fired approximately 334,000 rounds. Casualties: 2 O.R. wounded, both slightly L.S.P. Two guns of "B" were sent to S 22 C under Lieut Johnson. [Remarks: O.O. 52 attached]
>
> **10 April** — Barrage work carried on at intervals. Enemy seems very demoralized and incapable of concerted retaliation. L.S.P.
>

## Page 112 — Diary text (April 1917, p. 7)

> Place: LA FOLIE | Date: 1917, April 11–14
>
> **11 April (contd)** — 7th C. M. G. Coy along ridge in S 22 b.d & S 28 b. Relief complete by 5. p.m. Enemy shelled along the ridge between 5 & 6 p.m. Patrols of 49th Can. Bn. reports that there are no enemy immediately in front of us. Casualties 2 O.R. wounded. L.S.P. [Remarks: O.O. 53 attached]
>
> **12 April** — Advanced. Coy Hdqrs moved to FANNY TRENCH (S 23 a 28). Enemy shelled ridge with "whizbangs" and shrapnel between 9 and 10.30 am. Our aircraft active over our lines. >
> **13 April** — Advanced patrols have reached LENS-ARRAS ROAD and VIMY outskirts. Sniping by enemy seems to have ceased. Our planes quite active. Two enemy planes over our lines about 7. am L.S.P.
>
> **14 April** — The guns were moved to the following positions:— 7 guns to NORTHERN VIMY DEFENCES; 7 guns on line from VIMY STN. through 13 d central to CHAUDIERE. Coy Hdqrs [continued on next page]

>
> **14 April (contd)** — is now at S 29 d 8050. Enemy shelling VIMY and top of ridge between 7 and 9 p.m. Casualties:— Lieut Johnson [uncertain] Wounded. 3 O.R. killed. 2 O.R. wounded [Remarks: O.O. [uncertain] attached] L.S.P.

>

[Remarks: O.O. 54 attached]
