## Page 313 — Diary text (February 1918, p. 4)

> Place: MARQUEFFLES FME. R 26 L 2.8. SHEET 36 B. S.E. FRANCE. | Date: 1918, Feb 1–3
>
> **1 February** — Strength of Company 10 Officers — 178 Other Ranks + 1 O.R. attached — The morning was spent with Squad Drill; Gun Stoppages and remedies for same, Care and cleaning of guns and Gas Helmet Drill — In the afternoon the gun teams made different types of M.G. emplacements — Several O.R's went on courses of instruction. Cpl PETTIGREW + Pte STEWART. R.B. returned from 3rd Can. Divl School FERFAY —

## Page 318 — Diary text (February 1918, p. 9)

> Place: MERICOURT SECTOR | Date: 1918, Feb 18–20
>
> **18 February** — A.M. Fair and cold. Company in reserve positions in and around VIMY. Company Hdqr. in SUNKEN ROAD S.23 b 63.50. Company Strength in the line Five officers and one hundred O.R. P.M. Sections employed on making full reconnaissance of positions and rendering reports to Hdqr. required on taking over new positions.
>
> **19 February** — A.M. to P.M. Fair and Cold. All sections employed on work on dugouts and emplacements. These are nearly all good but as usual it is found that improvements can be made and work of upkeep carried out. These positions are [uncertain] if it is not possible to work during the hours of daylight [uncertain] worked on during darkness.
>
> **20 February** — A.M. to P.M. Cloudy with showers throughout the day. Orders issued to Sections showing groups of alternative and battle positions to be reconnoitred. These positions are to be known by all ranks. Emplacements of various patterns have been built or are being built in these positions which will be occupied in certain emergencies.

## Page 319 — Diary text (February 1918, p. 10)

> Place: MERICOURT SECTOR | Date: 1918, Feb 21–23
>
> **21 February** — A.M. to P.M. Cloudy with occasional showers. Low visibility and Sections able to send their personnel to various groups to familiarize them with the locations. Work done on dugouts and emplacements to improve and repair Anti-gas apparatus.
>
> **22 February** — A.M. Fair and Cold. Sections at work on improving overhead cover on emplacements and dugouts. P.M. Enemy aeroplane seen to come down in flames. Reported brought down by Machine Gun fire. Report not as yet confirmed.
>
> **23 February** — A.M. to P.M. Dull and Cloudy. Sections at work on improving overhead cover and wire on emplacements and dugouts. Reports called for from Section O.C. with suggestions for improvements in the way of wire and Camouflage round all emplacements and dugouts.

## Page 320 — Diary text (February 1918, p. 11)

> Place: MERICOURT SECTOR | Date: 1918, Feb 24–27
>
> **24 February** — A.M. Fair in the A.M. with clouds and rain in the P.M. Sections worked on dugouts and emplacements improving overhead cover, wire and camouflage.
>
> **25 February** — A.M. Heavy rain in morning but afternoon turned out fine. New bunks and stoves put in some of the dugouts. P.M. Continued work on dugouts and emplacements with particular attention to improving camouflage.
>
> **26 February** — A.M. to P.M. Fair and sunny. Visibility too good for much work during day. After dark work carried on at emplacements as usual.
>
> **27 February** — A.M. to P.M. Cloudy with high wind from W.S.W. Rain in evening. Work carried on as usual on emplacements and dugouts. Div. M.G.C. went over all positions in rear sector and declared himself satisfied with work done.

> II Guides
>
> (a) Guides for A. C. + D Sections will be at T 19 d 9.4 (C. Sec Hdqr.) at 4 P.M.
>
