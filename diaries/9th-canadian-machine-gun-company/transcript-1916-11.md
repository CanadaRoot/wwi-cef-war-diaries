## Page 74 — Diary text (November 1916, p. 3)

Army Form C. 2118, "WAR DIARY or INTELLIGENCE SUMMARY" (heading struck through). Handwritten; headed "from Nov. 1st to Nov. 30th 1916".

> Place: ELBE TRENCH | Date: 1916, November 1–13
>
> **1 November** — "A" Section relieved "B" Section in the left subsection. Nothing of importance to report.
>
> **2 November** — Trench mortars and machine guns active. Artillery active.
>
> **3 November** — Bombardment by enemy trench mortars & artillery of left subsection. Nothing of importance to report from other positions.
>
> **4 November** — Mutual trench mortar activity.
>
> **5 November** — " " " " and artillery activity. "B" relieved "C" Section in overhead [uncertain] positions.
>
> **6 November** — Quiet. 1 O.R. wounded.
>
> **7 November** — Usual trench mortar activity.
>
> **8 November** — Quiet.
>
> **9 November** — "C" Section relieved "D" Section in right subsection. Quiet.
>
> **10 November** — Quiet.
>
> **11 November** — Slight trench mortar activity.
>
> **12 November** — " " " "
>
> **13 November** — " " " " "D" Section relieved "A" Section in left subsection.
