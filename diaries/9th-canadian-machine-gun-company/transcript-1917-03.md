## Page 102 — Diary text (March 1917, p. 4)

> Place: GOUY-SERVINS / LA FOLIE SECTION | Date: 1917, March 11–15
>
> **11 March (contd)** — preparing for the trenches.
>
> **12 March** — Three Sections, with 12 guns, moved into the line and relieved part of the 5th C.M.G. Coy. in the LA FOLIE Section. Company Hdqs are in the Machine Gun Fort. Relief was complete at 10.30 p.m. The guns are posted at the following positions:— Ref. Maps:— 36 C. S.W.3 and 51 B. N.W.1. [uncertain] (1) A 4 a 2.7½ — (2) S 28 c 1½.7½ — (3) S 28 a 1½.½. (4) S 27 b. 1.8. (5) S 21 d 4½.4½ (6) S 27 b 6½. (7) S 21 d 4½.3. (12) A 3 b 2¾.3. (13) A 3 b 4.5. (14 & 15) A 3 a. 0.½. (16) S 26 b 6.3.
>
> **13 March** — Section on right front report considerable trench mortar activity. Front otherwise fairly quiet. Enemy machine guns quite active at night. Owing to continued rainfall the trenches are very muddy.
>
> **14 March** — Our artillery is becoming more active, firing on enemy support and communication trenches. Enemy artillery active on our left front.
>
> **15 March** — At 3.05 am. enemy artillery opened on our left front at junction of GRANGE TR. and "P" LINE and searched down GRANGE and FRENCH TR. past the QUARRIE LINE. [continued on next page]

## Page 103 — Diary text (March 1917, p. 5)

> Place: LA FOLIE SECTION | Date: 1917, March 15–19
>
> **15 March (contd)** — Our No 9. Position (near GRANGE) was hit, the emplacement and dugout door being damaged and tripod put out of action. The enemy raided a Lewis gun post near COMMONS CRATER and wounded two men. Artillery of both sides fairly active.
>
> **16 March** — Artillery still active. Considerable activity in the air during the afternoon. Enemy using a very fast RED 'plane. Our guns did considerable anti-aircraft firing. Enemy shelling LA TARGETTE in the afternoon.
>
> **17 March** — Our artillery shelled enemy support lines. Front otherwise quiet. One of our planes was shot down south of FONT ST. about 4. p.m. Both pilot & observer were killed. Our guns turned back several enemy planes.
>
> **18 March** — Artillery of both sides fairly active. Considerable aircraft activity. Trench mortars fairly active.
>
> **19 March** — Front normal. Increased activity in air. Large fires seen behind enemy lines both north and south.

## Page 104 — Diary text (March 1917, p. 6)

> Place: LA FOLIE SECTION | Date: 1917, March 20–28
>
> **20 March** — Increased activity of artillery and trench mortars of both sides. Front lines and support trenches receiving most attention. In conjunction with artillery programme our m. g. fire at night has been considerably increased.
>
> **21 March** — Our artillery continue to shell enemy positions. Enemy shelled GRANGE TRENCH and LA TARGETTE in the afternoon. And Stokes guns shelled enemy front line. Our guns active in anti-aircraft work. One of our planes brought down in NEUVILLE ST. VAAST.
>
> **22 March** — Artillery activity continued. We were relieved by 8th C.M.G. Coy in right subsection. Relief complete at 11.15 p.m. We were relieved by 7th C.M.G. Co in left subsection. Relief complete at 1.30 am.
>
> **23 March** — Marched out from GOUY-SERVINS at 9.30 am. and reached BRUAY at 1.15 p.m. where we went into billets.
