## Page 95 — Diary text (February 1917, p. 3)

Army Form C. 2118, "WAR DIARY or INTELLIGENCE SUMMARY" (heading struck through). Handwritten.

> Place: ELBE TRENCH | Date: 1917, February 1–9
>
> **1 February** — Artillery and Trench mortars of both sides fairly active. "D" Section relieved "A" Section in the right subsection.
>
> **2 February** — Our artillery active in retaliation for enemy trench mortar fire.
>
> **3 February** — All quiet.
>
> **4 February** — Our artillery active during morning. Enemy trench mortars active in late afternoon.
>
> **5 February** — Usual trench mortar activity. "A" Section relieved "B" Section in left subsection.
>
> **6 February** — All quiet. Enemy machine guns active at night.
>
> **7 February** — All quiet.
>
> **8 February** — Our artillery active at intervals throughout the day.
>
> **9 February** — Enemy trench mortars active during morning until silenced by our artillery. "B" Section relieved "C" Section in the overhead fire positions. Officers of the 4th C.M.G. Co came to look over the area in preparation for [continued on next page].

## Page 96 — Diary text (February 1917, p. 4)

> Place: ELBE TRENCH | Date: 1917, February 9–15
>
> **9 February (contd)** — relief.
>
> **10 February** — All quiet.
>
> **11 February** — Fairly quiet. Our artillery slightly active.
>
> **12 February** — All quiet.
>
> **13 February** — Trench mortars of both sides active. Our artillery also active in afternoon. One man per crew, with guns and equipment, of 4th C.M.G. Co relieved one man per crew and our guns.
>
> **14 February** — All quiet on our front. What appeared to be a small bombing raid on our right, developed into an artillery duel of considerable size, lasting about one hour. Personnel of 4th C.M.G. Co. relieved personnel of 9th C.M.G. Co. about noon. Relief was completed by 1.30 p.m.
>
> **15 February** — At 12.00 noon we marched out from ECOIVRES and reached HOUDAIN at 5.15 p.m. The [Company] billeted here for the night.

## Page 97 — Diary text (February 1917, p. 5)

> Place: HOUDAIN / MARLES-les-MINES | Date: 1917, February 16–25
>
> **16 February** — We marched out at 10.00 am and reached MARLES-les-MINES at 2.30 p.m. We found some of our billets occupied by refugees but eventually found other billets.
>
> **17 February** — Company Parade at 9.00 am. Morning spent in overhauling equipment. Received orders to prepare to move to AUCHEL. Company was paid in afternoon.
>
> **18 February** — Moved to AUCHEL in afternoon arriving about 5. p.m.
>
> **19 February** — The Company was divided into two classes for training purposes, based on general knowledge & efficiency. Parade hours are from 8.30 am to 12.00 am and 1.30 pm to 3.30 pm.
>
> **20 February** — Parades as usual.
>
> **21 February** — Parades as usual in morning. Afternoon devoted to recreation.
>
> **22 February** — Parades as usual.
>
> **23 February** — do
>
> **24 February** — do
>
> **25 February** — Church parade in morning. Afternoon devoted to recreation.
