## Page 209 — Diary text (September 1917, p. 4)

Army Form C. 2118, "WAR DIARY or INTELLIGENCE SUMMARY" (heading struck through). Handwritten.

> Place: BOUVIGNY; VIMY, Map Ref. 36c S.W.3 1/10,000 [4 September] | Date: 1917, September 1–4
>
> **1 September** — Usual training parades. Five O.R. Reinforcements.
>
> **2 September** — Usual training parades.
>
> **3 September** — Usual training parades + preparing to leave BOUVIGNY.
>
> **4 September** — The Company paraded at 7.00 am. + moved to new TRANSPORT LINES near AUX RITZ Corner (A8c 6.2) arriving at 12.00 noon. The rest of the day was spent in preparing for the trenches. On the night of the 4th/5th the 9th Can. M.G. Coy relieved the 92nd Machine Gun Company (Imperial unit) as follows: Four guns A Section + 1 gun B Section 9th Can. M.G. Coy relieved four guns No 2 Section 92nd M.G. Coy. at AINTREE (T9c 35.12) one gun. ASCOT (T15a 55.95 one gun. AUK (T15b 45.60) one gun + BUFFALO (T14d 15.75) two guns. (One of A Section + one of B Section)
>
> B Section 9th Can. M.G. Coy less two guns occupied reserve positions at CONDOR (S23b 60.55) relieving two crews of No 4 Section 92nd M.G. Coy.

## Page 210 — Diary text (September 1917, p. 5)

> Place: VIMY, Map Ref. 36c S.W.3 1/10,000 | Date: 1917, September 4–5
>
> **4 September (contd)** — Four guns C Section 9th Can. M.G. Coy relieved two guns No 4 Section 92nd M.G. Coy at BADGER (T14d 1.0) + BULLDOG (T20a 25.90). Four guns D Section 9th Can. M.G. Coy occupied reserve positions at COYOTE (S23b 85.70) two guns + COBRA (S24a 67.43) two guns. One gun B Section of the 9th Can. M.G. Coy relieved one gun of No 1 Section 92nd M.G. Coy in ANTELOPE (T16a 9.1) + came under the command of the 15th Can. M.G. Coy. — The relief was completed by 12 midnight. Between 12 + 1. o'clock the enemy fired a considerable number of gas shells causing quite a few casualties among the infantry during a relief. — The enemy targets were chiefly our reserve trenches — VIMY + Railway track. — Our guns answered when they thought was an S.O.S. from the front line. We had two O.R. wounded gassed. Gas shells had a mustard odour. Strength of Company at time of going into the line 10 Officers + 178 O.R. 44 O.R. attached.

## Page 211 — Diary text (September 1917, p. 6)

> Place: VIMY, Map Ref. 36c S.W.3 | Date: 1917, September 5–7
>
> **5 September** — The day was very quiet. During the night the enemy shelled our reserve trenches. VIMY lightly shelled with gas shells. Two O.R. were sick [uncertain].
>
> **6 September** — The day was very quiet except for four heavy shells on the LENS ARRAS road at 2.15 pm.
>
> 5.30 pm — Enemy struck a dump away to our left (1500 yds to left of LENS ARRAS Road) which burned for about an hour.
>
> 7.30 pm — Our Artillery on left started a bombardment which quietened down in only five minutes.
>
> 9.55 pm — Enemy Artillery opened up on Targets near AVION — The fire was heavy at first but soon slackened + died away about an hour later. — During the night scattered shelling near our batteries.
>
> **7 September** — The day was very quiet.
>
> 5.30 pm — Enemy shelled our Anti aircraft battery (13 pr.) in S18c then shortening his range put a few in T17d + T13a. At 9.00 + 9.30 pm. about 15 H.E. Shells between S24a 65 + S. 32a 50.

>

>

>

>

>
