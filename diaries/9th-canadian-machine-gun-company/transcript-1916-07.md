## Page 32 — Diary text (July 1916, p. 3)

Army Form C. 2118, "WAR DIARY or INTELLIGENCE SUMMARY" (heading struck through). Handwritten; headed "Original", "July 1st to July 31st 1916", unit stamp "9th CANADIAN INFANTRY BRIGADE, MACHINE GUN COMPANY".

> Place: ZILLEBEKE BUND | Date: 1916, July 1–4
>
> [Remarks column: the word "Annex" appears against each entry on this page — reading uncertain]
>
> **1 July** — Our batteries shelled quite heavily throughout the day. Aeroplanes very active on both sides. [Hour: 4.45 P.M.] Five of our planes returning [inserted above the line] from reconnaissance met two German planes, and one was [inserted above the line] seen to fall. [Hour: 4 P.M.] One M.G. gun at CULVERT knocked out by German shell, and 3 O.R. were wounded.
>
> **2 July** — Hostile shelling on LILLE GATE RD, SHRAPNEL CORNER and transport routes in retaliation for shelling our artillery activity. [Hour: 11 P.M.] Inter-section relief took place. Enemy very quiet but very nervous especially North and South. 1 Offr & 4 O.R. reported back from Vickers course
>
> **3 July** — Quiet during most of day. [Hour: 3 P.M.] Our artillery bombarded MOUNT SORREL, with very little retaliation. The increase in Machine Gun fire and rifle was very marked.
>
> **4 July** — Quiet during day but Machine Gun and rifles very active at night. Work on new DORMY LANE position started. [Hour: 7 P.M.] Area South of ZILLEBEKE BUND very heavily shelled. Second Canadian Bde on our right and 60th Division on our left

## Page 33 — Diary text (July 1916, p. 4)

> Place: ZILLEBEKE BUND | Date: 1916, July 5–8
>
> [Remarks column: the word "Annex" appears against each entry on this page — reading uncertain]
>
> **5 July** — Very quiet. BUND shelled lightly at 7 P.M. Reconnaissance of DORMY HOUSE position carried out
>
> **6 July** — [Hour: 11 A.M.] Very quiet. ZILLEBEKE RD shelled heavily at two minute [inserted above the line] Intervals. Brigade on our right. 1st Canadian Brigade. New Yeomanry [inserted above the line] Post position laid out
>
> **7 July** — [Hour: 4 P.M.] Trenches 66–74 shelled during the afternoon. YPRES and forward batteries also shelled. Work started on New Yeomanry [inserted above the line] post position. 1 O.R. wounded
>
> **8 July** — Quiet in the morning. In the afternoon [uncertain — appears "Guns"] batteries were shelled. Heavy bombardment of MOUNT SORREL by our artillery for fifteen minutes at [Hour: 7.15 P.M.] We opened an intensive bombardment at 11.45 followed by an attack. [Hour: 11.15 P.M.] 5 O.R. casualties, including one sergeant killed. Lieut. [one initial struck through in original] L. ELLIS reported for duty from Machine Gun base.
>
> [Note: the hour column reads 11.15 P.M. while the text reads 11.45 — as written.]

## Page 34 — Diary text (July 1916, p. 5)

> Place: ZILLEBEKE BUND | Date: 1916, July 9–12
>
> [Remarks column: the word "Annex" appears against each entry on this page — reading uncertain]
>
> **9 July** — Heavy artillery retaliation by the enemy, for our activity on the night 8/9. "A" section was relieved by one section of the 7th Bde M.G. Coy and went to [struck through in original: MACHINE GUN] Camp
>
> **10 July** — Quiet throughout the day. This company is doing no firing until our front line trenches are improved. The Division on our left made raids accompanied by a discharge of smoke and also by heavy bombardment. "D" section was relieved by a section of 7th Can Bde M.G. Coy. First Canadian Bde on our right relieved the 2nd Can Bde. Lt. H. D. BROWNE killed in action
>
> **11 July** — Quiet. The enemy threw some Gas shells of a new type at ZILLEBEKE BUND. These shells had the effect of making one very sick at the stomach. The 9th Can Inf Bde was relieved in the front line by the 7th Can. Inf. Bde. Divs sections [as written] of this company were held in Reserve and came under O.C 7th Bde M.G. Coy.
>
> **12 July** — Counter battery work. Otherwise very quiet.

## Page 35 — Diary text (July 1916, p. 6)

> Place: MACHINE GUN CAMP "ERIE" | Date: 1916, July 13–19
>
> [Remarks column: the word "Annex" appears against each entry on this page — reading uncertain]
>
> **13–14 July** — During these two days the two sections at MACHINE GUN CAMP carried on general training, most of the time being spent on Mechanism and Immediate action.
>

>
> **16 July** — Company moved to rest billets at STEENVOORDE, leaving MACHINE GUN CAMP at 2.30 P.M. and arriving at [word struck through in original — appears "billets"] at J 35 d at 7 P.M. (sheet 27).
>
> **17 July** — Usual parades.
>
> **18 July** — Parades as follows. 6.15–7.15 Physical drill. 8.30–9.30 Stripping. 9.30–10.30 Limber drill. 10.30–11.30 Immediate action. 11.30–12.30 Advanced drill. 9.30–10.30 Range takers class under C.S.M.
>
> **19 July** — Parades as usual 7.30–9 Musketry parade. 9–10 Mechanism. 10–11 Company drill under Guards Instructor. 11–12 Gun drill.

## Page 37 — Diary text (July 1916, p. 8)

> Place: REST BILLET STEENVOORDE | Date: 1916, July 22–24
>
> [Remarks column: the word "Annex" appears against each entry on this page — reading uncertain]

## Page 38 — Diary text (July 1916, p. 9)

> Place: MACHINE GUN CAMP | Date: 1916, July 25–26
>
> [Remarks column: the word "Annex" appears against each entry on this page — reading uncertain]
>
> **25 July** — [Hour: 7.15] The company less "A" & "D" sections moved from STEENVOORDE AREA to MACHINE GUN CAMP ERIE.
>
> **26 July** — The 9th Bde M.G. Coy relieved the 8th Can Bde M.G. Coy on the night of July 26/27. Relief complete by 12.30 midnight. Our frontage has been increased by taking over trenches 46–52 in addition to what we held from 52 to MENIN Rd. This additional frontage requires in support six guns placed as follows.
>
> 1 gun. I 29 b 8½ 8.
> 1 gun I 24 c 1 3
> 1 gun I 24 c 3, 3½ [uncertain]
> 1 gun I 24 c 3 5½
> 1 gun I 24 c 6½ 2½
> 1 gun I 24 d 3 3
>
> These guns all fire towards the GAP, MOUNT SORREL, and HILL 62.

## Page 39 — Diary text (July 1916, p. 10)

> Place: ZILLEBEKE BUND | Date: 1916, July 27–31
>
> [Remarks column: the word "Annex" appears against each entry on this page — reading uncertain]
>
> **27 July** — Very quiet. Work on new deep [inserted above the line] dugouts commenced on OBSERVATORY RIDGE
>
> **28 July** — Very quiet throughout the day. Heavy hostile artillery on YPRES. MACHINE GUN fire very active at night
>
> **29 July** — Very quiet. Enemy artillery exceptionally quiet. This company engaged different enemy targets during the night. Our fire did not seem to increase enemy's Machine Gun fire
>
> **30 July** — Very quiet. This company again carried out indirect fire.
>
> **31 July** — Very quiet. Indirect fire on enemy tram-lines, roads and dumps carried on. 10 O.R. reported for duty with the company
>
> [signed] *J. M. Hunter [uncertain], Lieut, O.C. 9th M.G. Coy*
