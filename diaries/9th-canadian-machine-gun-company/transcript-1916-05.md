## Page 8 — Diary text (May 1916, p. 3)

Army Form C. 2118, "WAR DIARY or INTELLIGENCE SUMMARY" (heading struck through). Handwritten; headed "9th Can. Inf. Bde. M.G. Coy.", "Duplicate", "for May 1916", "Vol. 2", "1/5/16 to 31/5/16" [as written; the cover and contents say to 30th May]. Marginal initials appear at the end of most entries; they are not transcribed.

> Place: HOOGE | Date: 1916, May 1–5
>
> **1 May** — Night. Shelling fairly regular but not intense. Machine gun fire very heavy along CHINA WALL, MAPLE COPSE and YEOMANRY POST. Men in front line replaced by men in reserve.
>
> **2 May** — Fairly quiet day. Reconnaissance was possible by day. Plans for new M.G. Emplacements surveyed with Engineer Officer. MENIN ROAD heavily shelled. Night M.G. fire along CHINA WALL is getting more intense.
>
> **3 May** — YEOMANRY POST is registered by 4.9" Have decided that pending strengthening of M.G. Emplacement in that neighbourhood guns will be [uncertain: hidden] in undergrowth instead of buildings
>
> **4 May** — YPRES is consistently shelled by day and by night. Transport routes are shelled. Machine Gun fire of Germans is very intense by night. Dugouts in Front line complete.
>
> **5 May** — Enemy drop a few shells behind the lines MENIN ROAD and YEOMANRY POST. Relieved by 8th Canadian Inf Bde M.G. Coy.

## Page 9 — Diary text (May 1916, p. 4)

Army Form C. 2118, "WAR DIARY or INTELLIGENCE SUMMARY" (second heading struck through). Handwritten; headed "9th Can. Inf. Bde. M.G. Coy."

> Place: B CAMP | Date: 1916, May 6–14
>
> **6 May** — 3.30 AM Arrived B Camp. Muster parade called and bathing completed
>
> **7 May** — Kit inspection. Found considerable slackness in discipline of Sections B and D. B was conspicuous in loss of equipment. Repayment was ordered in nearly all cases. 2 PM Constructing range for M.G. practice. Anti — Air — Craft mounts constructed.
>
> **8 May** — 8 AM Physical drill. 9 " 2 Sections at range construction. Two Sections at Mechanism and stripping. 2 PM 2 Sections at range construction. Two Sections at Mechanism and stripping
>
> **9 May** — Same as previous day.
>
> **10 May** — 9 AM Range practice and gun drill for all Sections.
>
> **11 May** — Range practice and limber packing
>
> **12 May** — Range practice with condemned ammunition (B 15 G 15) and limber packing
>
> **13 May** — Clinometer firing and limber drill.
>
> **14 May** — Preparation for march to line. Take over from 7th Canadian Inf Bde M.G. Coy.

## Page 10 — Diary text (May 1916, p. 5)

Army Form C. 2118, "WAR DIARY or INTELLIGENCE SUMMARY" (second heading struck through). Handwritten; headed "9th Can. Inf. Bde. M.G. Coy."

> Place: ZILLEBEKE, RIGHT SECTOR | Date: 1916, May 15–18
>
> **15 May** — 2 AM Relief complete with 4 guns in front line. Sniping is active throughout the day. 4 AM One casualty sustained. One man wounded in MAPLE COPSE
>
> **16 May** — Enemy sniping is galling in Maple Copse. Enemy Artillery quiet. Night Machine Guns harass our men. Our M.Gs register on the line and range we calculate, but were not successful in silencing them.
>
> **17 May** — Enemy Artillery becoming fairly active registering on front line by day. Enemy Aeroplanes becoming active also. All Machine Guns receive orders to engage them whenever possible. Indirect fire on Enemy supports and reserve trenches is getting more satisfactory results
>
> **18 May** — Enemy Artillery has been very heavy on the front and support lines in Sanctuary Wood. Our M.G's are very active in retaliation for German M.G. fire. Enemy saps are kept under fire all night. Front line guns relieved.

## Page 11 — Diary text (May 1916, p. 6)

Army Form C. 2118, "WAR DIARY or INTELLIGENCE SUMMARY" (second heading struck through). Handwritten; headed "9th Can. Inf. Bde. M.G. Coy."

> Place: ZILLEBEKE | Date: 1916, May 19–22
>
> **19 May** — Enemy Artillery heavy on communication trenches. Enemy Air Craft very active. M.G. Emplacements complete in trench 53 and dugout begun.
>
> **20 May** — Enemy Artillery very heavy on front and rear positions. Our M.G. Emplacements seems to be calling for special attention from them. Enemy M.G's very active. Ours retaliate and saps are engaged with heavy fire. Enemy Rifle Grenades give trouble in front line
>
> **21 May** — Concentrated Artillery fire on front line and strong points. Aeroplanes active. Our Artillery is endeavouring to retaliate with 18 pounders but do not have much effect. Enemy Rifle Grenades frequent. Our M.G's do observation firing on support lines by day. Enemy search for our M.G's with shrapnel at night. Their M.G's also search the neighbourhood of our Guns.
>
> **22 May** — Concentrated Artillery fire on HILL 61 Communication trench and S.Ps. Enemy subject our M.G's to heavy shrapnel fire. Had to shift Guns.

## Page 12 — Diary text (May 1916, p. 7)

Army Form C. 2118, "WAR DIARY or INTELLIGENCE SUMMARY" (second heading struck through). Handwritten; headed "9th Can. Inf. Bde. M.G. Coy."

> Place: ZILLEBEKE | Date: 1916, May 23–25
>
> **23 May** — Front line shelled heavily. Machine Gun emplacements and dugouts are most heavily shelled. Enemy M.G's are less active, also his rifle fire, as we are putting heavier fire on their front line
>
> **24 May** — Enemy Artillery is again heavy. MAPLE COPSE is heavily shelled daily, also communication trenches in that neighbourhood
>
> **25 May** — Artillery fire heavy throughout the day. M.G. Emplacement in trench 59 blown in. 1 Casualty. Enemy trench mortars are active. Infantry casualties pretty heavy. Aeroplanes are active on both sides. Enemy Aeroplanes [uncertain] our whole front. Work consisting in repairing the wrecked Emplacement

## Page 13 — Diary text (May 1916, p. 8)

Army Form C. 2118, "WAR DIARY or INTELLIGENCE SUMMARY" (second heading struck through). Headed with a stamp: "9th CANADIAN INFANTRY BRIGADE, MACHINE GUN COMPANY."

> Place: ZILLEBEKE | Date: 1916, May 26–30 [as written; see note below]
>
> **26 May** — One casualty from M.G. fire from Maple Copse. Our M.G. fire on German front line is restricted by patrols and wiring parties. Enemy saps have advanced 15 yds in front of Trench 53. Aeroplanes active on both sides. Work: Tunnelled Dugout + Emplacement in Trench 59. Completed Emplacement in S.P. 14 completed. Emplacement in Maple Copse completed.
>
> **27 May** — Two casualties from German heavy trench mortar. Enemy shell communication trenches and routes by Aeroplane Observation. S.P. 11.12.13.14 receive heavy shell fire and Maple Copse is almost untenable. There is too much movement here at all times
>
> **30 May** — [as written; out of sequence — this entry precedes the 29 May entry on the next page, and there is no entry dated 28 May] Three casualties from shell fire in front line. New emplacement and one dugout wrecked in Trench 59. Hedge Street M.G. emplacement wrecked. Work: Rebuilding the wrecked emplacements. Zillebeke is shelled.

## Page 14 — Diary text (May 1916, p. 9)

Army Form C. 2118, "WAR DIARY or INTELLIGENCE SUMMARY" (second heading struck through). Headed with a stamp: "9th CANADIAN INFANTRY BRIGADE, MACHINE GUN COMPANY."

> Place: ZILLEBEKE | Date: 1916, May 29–30
>
> **29 May** — Aircraft of enemy very active. Concentrated shell fire on front line, communication trenches, routes and all dugouts. Zillebeke Village shelled but front line is much quieter.
>
> **30 May** — Two casualties in front line today. Enemy heavy trench mortar very active all day. Front line is badly wrecked. This looks as if an attack were to be launched on this frontage. Our M.G's are very active on German saps. Frontage is extended today to the south as far as trench 66.
>
> Place: B. CAMP | Date: 1916, May 31
>
> **31 May** — In B Camp. Inspection of Kit & Equipment.
>
