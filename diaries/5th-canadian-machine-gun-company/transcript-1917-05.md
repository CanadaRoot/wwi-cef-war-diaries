### 1/5/17 — FARBUS-VIMY R.R. EMB.

> During the night 30th/1st and early morning, 32,000 rounds of Indirect Fire were fired on ACHEVILLE [uncertain] road F24.B.5.5 to U19.C.4.3. and wire in front of enemy's support line. The day passed quietly with heavy enemy shelling of R.R. Embankment. An S.O.S. went up about dusk in answer to which our artillery and M.G.s opened up. It proved to have been put up by the enemy, due probably to his extreme nervousness. Sgt. F. G. Hopkins and PTE. Apt [uncertain] of 'B' Section were killed by a shell which landed in the trench at No 1 Battery.

### 2/5/17 — (do)

> During the night 1st/2nd our usual indirect fire was carried out on the usual targets, 24,000 rounds being expended. Enemy retaliated with H.E. & Gas Shell doing no damage. The enemy heavily shelled R.R. embankment and vicinity by Coy. Hdqrs. between 12 m.n & 2 A.M. with H.E. causing 1 Casualty at Coy. Hdqrs. The 6th Cdn. Inf. Bde relieved the 24th Bn in the right half of the Divisional sector during the night 2nd/3rd. Preparatory to this relief and to the attack due to take place at dawn on the 3rd Major WATSON issued Preliminary Order. M.G. 461 (attached). As Coy. Hdqrs were required for a 6th Bde Battalion Hdqrs. a new Hdqrs. was established in the R.R. Embankment at T26.A.6.2. In addition to the 8 mobile guns of the Coy. the following guns were detached to the 5th Bde. and, reporting during the evening, took up their positions as follows:— 2 guns 4th Cdn. M.G. Coy & 6 Guns 14th Cdn. M.G. Coy on FARBUS VIMY R.R. Embankment, 6 guns 4th Cdn. M.G. Co. in Observation Line, VIMY RIDGE. At 7.30 P.M. Major WATSON received orders to assume command of the Barrage Guns of the Division, Major EASTHAM having been evacuated sick. Major WATSON immediately issued the instructions to all Battery Commanders contained in M.G. 469 (attached) and established his H.Q. [uncertain] the O.C. 6th Bde in THELUS MILL CAVE. Fine

### 3/5/17 — (do)

> [uncertain] assuming command of the Mobile Guns of the 5th Bde. Intermittent shelling of the R.R. embankment continued all night. Casualties 1 O.R wounded

### 3/5/17 — (do) [continued]

> Indirect Fire carried out between 12 M.N and dawn on streets & cross roads in ACHEVILLE also enemy's support line wire, 10,000 rds. being expended. At 3.45 A.M. the 6th Bde, in conjunction with operations of 1st Cdn. Div & XIII Corps, on our right, attacked the German trench system to the south of ACHEVILLE. The attack was not entirely successful, being partly held up by machine guns in a reinforced concrete emplacement on the high ground at U19.C.0.4. Our Bde (5th) did not take part in this attack, but held the ground they already occupied. Two of our sections 'C' & 'D' were employed on barrage work and known as 7 Battery. The day, after the artillery quieted down in the morning, was normal. A counter attack was expected at 8.15 A.M, but, on our Guns opening up, it came to naught. Transport pack train delivered 30,000 rds. ammunition to No 7 Battery during the evening. One gun of 'C' section was put out of action at 9.30 P.M. by a shell bursting close to the emplacement. The No 1, PTE. D W. GRAHAM was killed and 2. O.R wounded. one of whom died at C.C.S.

### 4/5/17

> The usual indirect fire was carried out during the past night. The damaged 'C' section gun was replaced during the day from the advanced Armourer's Repair Shop at AUX R.EITZ and also the crosshead off of the tripod which was damaged at the same time. ### 6/5/17 — (do)

Enemy displayed considerable aerial activity during the morning and our A.A. guns at the Quarries & R.R embankment were kept busy. 1800 rds. being expended. The rest of the day passed uneventfully. Weather much cooler. Health of the men good. Casualties Nil. Lieut. D. H. ROSS arrived as a reinforcement this date.

### 7/5/17 — (do) [continued]

> gas shells on the whole Divisional front from the tops of VIMY RIDGE to forward of the VIMY-FARBUS R.R. Embankment. No 7 Battery ('C' & 'D' sections) were withdrawn at dusk and proceeded to rest camp at AUX REITZ. Fortunately only one casualty occured during this withdrawal, although several men were slightly gassed. Arrangements were completed and guides were provided for the relief of 'A' & 'B' Sections, by 1 section of the 14th Cdn. M.G Coy and 1 section of the 4th Cdn. M.G. Coy.

### 8/5/17 — (do)

> During the night of the 7th/8th A & B sections were relieved by 1 section each of 4th & 14th Cdn. M.G. Coy. But the relief was hampered by the gas shelling by the enemy which continued the whole night without cessation right up to 5 a.m this morning. Upon completion of relief, which was at 2 a.m, A. & B. sections proceeded [uncertain] to AUX REITZ, the last section arriving there at 8 a.m. Fortunately both sections, were clear of forward area before the counter attack which the enemy made at dawn & in which he recaptured FRESNOY from the 5th British Div., and the portion of the OPPY-MERICOURT line held by this division. The remainder of the day was spent in resting. The men are fatigued, several are suffering from the effects of the Gas shells. The Company bathed at NEUVILLE-ST VAAST during the evening. Two guns were mounted at dawn this morning for A.A work at Divisional Hdqrs. AUX REITZ & another has been mounted at the Camp.

### 9/5/17 — AUX RIETZ

> Physical training was carried out for 1 hour in the morning. The remainder of the day was spent in G.M. parades, cleaning guns, equipment etc. Weather is fine.

## Notes

- **Coverage:** Entries run 1–31 May 1917 with no date gaps. Diary text is 11 pages (Sheets I–XI), matching the contents sheet ("Diary Text — 11 Pages"); appendices 1–6 are the orders M.G. 461, M.G. 469, O.O. No. 4, O.O. No. 5, O.O. No. 6 and O.O. No. 7. The contents sheet lists the appendices as "Orders issued by 5th Cdn. M.G. Co., 2-5-17, 13-5-17 to 28-5-17".
- **Officers named:** Major S. M. Watson (O.C., signs the diary and the orders); Major Eastham (O.C. 6th Cdn. M.G. Co., evacuated sick 2 May); Lieut. McBurney (commanded the T.16.d.6.1 barrage battery); Lieut. D. H. Ross (arrived as a reinforcement 6 May); Lieut. Sinclair (attached to 'A' Section, then assumed command of it); Lieut. Creighton (left for the Machine Gun School, Camiers, 20 May); Lieut. Grantham, Lieut. Taylor, Lieut. Young, Lieut. Hobson, Lieut. Ketcheson (admitted to hospital 28 May), Lieut. McCorkell (acting Q.M.), Lieut. Gray, Lieut. Tucker and Lieut. Carter (6th Cdn. M.G. Co.); L. G. Francis, Lieut. for Lieut.-Col. Corp. M.G. Officer (quoted report on the company, 5 May).
- **Other ranks named:** Sgt. F. G. Hopkins (killed 1 May); Pte. Apt [uncertain] (killed 1 May); Pte. D. W. Graham, No. 1 of a 'C' Section gun crew (killed 3 May); Sgt. Cooper (left for the Machine Gun School, 20 May); Sgt. Chamberland (in charge of 'D' Section during Lieut. McBurney's absence); Sgt. Morrow ('D' Section, first leave granted to the company since January); Sgt. Milns and Cpl. (now Sgt.) Woods (returned with a draft of 10 O.R., 28 May).
- **Equipment:** Vickers machine guns in four sections (A–D), grouped into barrage batteries (Nos. 4–7); anti-aircraft guns at the Quarries and the R.R. Embankment and at Divisional Hdqrs.; pack animals and limbers for transport; tripods (one crosshead replaced 4 May); S.A.A. (small arms ammunition) expended in large quantities on indirect fire (32,000 rounds on the night of 30 April/1 May alone).
- **Movements:** The company held the Farbus–Vimy R.R. Embankment and Vimy Ridge positions at the start of May; 'C' and 'D' Sections withdrew to rest camp at Aux Reitz on the night of 7/8 May and 'A' and 'B' Sections followed the same night; the company moved back into the line on 10–11 May (Neuville-St. Vaast, Vimy Ridge, Observation Line); relieved again 13–14 May and 19–20 May, resting at Aux Reitz 21–25 May; back into the line 26–27 May; an advance party under Lieut. Ross left for the camp at Gouy Servins on 31 May ahead of a relief by the 2nd Cdn. M.G. Co.
- **Discipline:** None recorded in May 1917.
- **Casualties:** Killed — Sgt. F. G. Hopkins and Pte. Apt, 1 May; Pte. D. W. Graham, 3 May; two O.R. of 'D' Section, 14 May; one O.R. of 'B' Section, 20 May. Died of wounds — one of the two O.R. wounded on 3 May (died at a Casualty Clearing Station) and one O.R. of 'D' Section, 14 May. Wounded — one O.R., 3 May; one O.R. slightly wounded, 4 May; one casualty at Coy. Hdqrs., 2 May; one casualty during the 7 May withdrawal, with several men slightly gassed; several men suffering gas effects, 8 May; one O.R. of 'D' Section, 15 May; two casualties in 'A' Section on the way in, 27 May; one O.R., 30 May.
- **Anomalies:** (1) Operation Order No. 6 (Appendix 5) is dated "May 18th. 1915." as typed, although it orders reliefs on the nights of 19th/20th and 20th/21st May 1917. (2) Preliminary Order M.G. 461 is cut off at the foot of page 14, mid-paragraph 3; page 15, headed "Observation Line", is otherwise blank. (3) Pages 20 and 24 are the reverses of typed orders; most of each page is carbon-copy bleed-through and not legibly readable — only the distribution lists and the order continuation (page 24) are transcribed. (4) The 31 May entry ends mid-sentence ("to take over the positions,") immediately before the signature. (5) The spelling of the rest camp varies between "AUX REITZ" and "AUX RIETZ" in the diary. (6) The 28 May entry's "GOUY SERVINS" is read from the same camp name written clearly on 31 May. (7) "O.C. Clay" is named as the addressee for completion reports in O.O. No. 4 and O.O. No. 5 — apparently a code name, as elsewhere completion is wired to named code words (BRONZ, MARTINI, CLOVER, DAISY, SILVER FIZZ). (8) On the 3 May entry the opening line ("[uncertain] assuming command of the Mobile Guns of the 5th Bde.") is a fragment, apparently the tail of a sentence about a change of command; the name is illegible. (9) Map references use "α" in places (e.g. "T22 α 8.6") and fractional figures (e.g. "T. 24. C.8½.4.") as written.
