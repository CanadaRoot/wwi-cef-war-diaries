## Page 10 — War Diary (Army Form C. 2118), Sheet No. 8

Heading: WAR DIARY or INTELLIGENCE SUMMARY [the words "INTELLIGENCE SUMMARY" are struck through] — 5th Canadian Machine Gun Coy. "JANUARY 1917" is written across the heading.

Remarks column: each entry is initialled "C.V.G."

### 16/1/17 — Field

> During the minor operations carried out by the 26th Can Inf Bn. on our front 12 of our guns assisted by 4 guns of 1st M.M.G. BDE & 2 guns of 1st M.G. SQDN. placed in position for barrage of Indirect Fire & Direct Fire around the front of the enemy's line attacked. The idea carried out was to place an intense box barrage close around the objective of the 26th This was carried out by eight guns operating from well on the flanks. The remaining 10 guns were used in placing another barrage further back of the first and concentrating especially on C.Ts & Hqrs and main C.T's. During the half hour that the operations lasted 80,750 rds S.A.A. were fired. All guns worked well and the Coy. suffered no casualties. The operation took place at 4.30 P.M. and all positions were occupied for several hours after.

### 17/1/17 — (ditto)

> All guns and crews were again in position at 7.00 A.M. in order to assist in the 4th Bde operations. Fire was opened at 7.45 A.M. and maintained until 8.45 A.M. 90.500 rds S.A.A.

## Notes

- **Coverage:** Entries run 1–31 January 1917 with no date gaps. 15 images in total: cover, typed contents sheet, and 12 diary sheets (Sheets No. 1–12), plus a scan of the verso of Sheet No. 12 as the 15th image. The contents sheet's "Diary Text — 12 Pages" matches the 12 diary sheets; the verso scan is extra.
- **Form change:** "INTELLIGENCE SUMMARY" is struck through on Sheets No. 1–9 but left standing on Sheets No. 10–12. The heading on Sheet No. 9 reads "5th Canadian Machine Gun Company" in full. A new reference map line appears on Sheet No. 10: "REF. MAP. 36 B. FRANCE."
- **Officers named:** Major S. W. Watson (O.C., signs the month as Major — promoted from Captain since December); Lieut C. V. Grantham (continues to initial the entries "C.V.G."); Lieut Hobson (returned from a map reading course at Pernes 23 Jan); Lieut McBurney (left for a map reading course at Pernes 25 Jan; the McBurney emplacement on the slag pile was finished 9 Jan).
- **Other ranks named:** None by name in January 1917. Eight men were returned to the Machine Gun Pool at Camiers for further training on 23 Jan; an advance party of 1 officer and 10 other ranks from the 2nd Canadian Machine Gun Company arrived 18 Jan.
- **Equipment:** New indirect-fire positions built at R 24 C.1. 4 1/2 (1 Jan), R 24 C.1. 3 1/2 (4 Jan), R 24 C.5.4 (chosen 1 Jan), R.35. b.5 1/2.4 (5 Jan) and R 24 C.1.3 1/2 (11 Jan); an alternative emplacement for the Cap du Pont and Angres guns (5 Jan); a temporary gun position on the slag pile at Fosse 10 and another in Corons d'Aix trench at M 25. B. 5.5 (6 Jan); the McBurney emplacement on the slag pile finished 9 Jan; Very pistols and lights installed at each emplacement (5 Jan); 16 feet of covered trench put in at the McBurney emplacement (8 Jan).
- **Movements:** The company stayed in the line at Angres–Bully until relieved by the 2nd Canadian Machine Gun Company on 19 Jan, moved by sections to billets at Fosse 10, Sains-en-Gohelle, then marched to Bruay on 20 Jan (via Pt. Sains, Sains-en-Gohelle, Hersin, Barlin, Ruitz) with the 5th Canadian Trench Mortar Battery following, and remained in billets at Bruay for the rest of the month, training, route-marching and bathing at Houdain. Inter-company reliefs in the line on 2 and 12 Jan.
- **Discipline:** None recorded in January 1917.
- **Casualties:** None recorded in January 1917. The company fired 80,750 rounds of small-arms ammunition in the 26th Battalion's minor operation on 16 Jan and 90,500 rounds in the 4th Brigade operation on 17 Jan, with no casualties in the company or the attached sections. Enemy shelling damaged the ammunition shelter at Bully Alley (a dud shell went through the roof, 11 Jan) and the Link Alley dugout roof, but no casualties are reported.
- **Anomalies:** (1) The 1 Jan target list gives Targets A, C and D only — Target B is not listed (it had been defined in December as M26 b 7.6). (2) The two new indirect-fire positions named on 1 Jan have their position numbers left blank: "(POSITION NO [blank])". (3) The 15 Jan entry has a word struck through before "CAP DU PONT". (4) The 17 Jan entry gives the ammunition expended as "90.500 rds S.A.A." (as written). (5) The 25 Jan entry gives the starting-point map reference as "1.24 d 9 1/2. 8", transcribed as read; the figures are hard to read. (6) The 26 Jan entry ends without a full stop. (7) The 16 Jan entry says the operation was "carried out by the 26th Can Inf Bn." while the 17 Jan entry says the guns assisted "the 4th Bde operations". (8) Watson signs as Major, his promotion since December.
