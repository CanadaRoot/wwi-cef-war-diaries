## Page 3 — War Diary (Army Form C. 2118), Sheet No. 1

Heading: WAR DIARY or INTELLIGENCE SUMMARY [the words "INTELLIGENCE SUMMARY" are struck through] — July 1916 — 5th Cdn Bde Machine Gun Company. "~ORIGINAL~ CONFIDENTIAL".

Remarks column: each entry is initialled "J.E. McC."

### 1.7.16 — Field

> REF MAP. SHEET 28 OF BELGIUM SCALE 1:40,000. TRENCH MAP VOORMEZEELE. PART OF SHEET 28, SCALE 1:10,000.
>
> G.S.O.3 from 2nd Canadian Division inspected Hqrs and emplacements. 1 Officer and 3 O.R. left for Vickers M.G. Course at CAMIERS. Lack of material still exists at SPOIL BANK. 2 Officers and three O.R. expected back from course at CAMIERS.

### 2.7.16 — do

> Demolished entire wall on building at Headquarters for additional overhead cover. Cellars pumped out and cleaned. Work commenced on renovating emplacement at ECLUSE 8. New dug-out sited in building in rear of Y.4. emplacement and work commenced to make it very strong. Indirect fire carried out by two guns for three hours.

### 3.7.16 — do

> The parapet at S.P.8 which was in a very poor condition was repaired, this work required some 300 sandbags. At LOCK POST the sap was cleaned out and advanced a distance of 4 feet. Work continued on emplacement at ECLUSE 8 and over 200 sand bags filled and put in position. Frames for holding gas blankets are being erected at all emplacements and dug-outs. Progress on all work very slow for want of material. Indirect fire carried out by one gun for 2 hours

### 4.7.16 — do

> Dug out at HILLTOP was completed and sap at A.P.1 advanced a distance of five feet. Work was carried out as far as possible at ECLUSE 8, and [uncertain] dug-out at Y.4., but owing to shortage of material this work is slow.

### 5.7.16 — do

> The dug out and emplacement at ECLUSE 8 was finished. This is now a very strong place, having over 6 feet of sand bags on the roof, and all sides. The dug out at CONVENT SCHOOL has been lowered two feet and 100 sand bags have been filled and placed on the roof. Gas blankets were procured and placed over the doors
>
> [Entry continues on Sheet 2.]

Heading: WAR DIARY or INTELLIGENCE SUMMARY [the words "INTELLIGENCE SUMMARY" are struck through] — JULY 1916 — 5th CDN INF BDE M.G. COY. "~ORIGINAL~ CONFIDENTIAL".

The 5.7.16 entry continues from Sheet 1 (no new date line):

> of all dug-outs and emplacements, this required frames being made for the doors which was done. Work at all other points was held up through lack of material

Remarks column: each entry is initialled "J.E. McC."

### 6.7.16 — Field

> The floor of dug-out at Y4 was dug down 18 inches, and a brick floor was put in. Twenty I beams were carried up but owing to lack of lumber, work had to be stopped. Emplacement CONVENT WALL was excavated and a new pedestal was erected. The sap at HILL TOP was advanced a distance of 4 feet and timbered

### 7.7.16 — do

> Twenty I beams were put in position at Y4. dugout and frame work completed and sides reinforced by sand bags. Work on new dug out at SARDINE BOX was commenced. A site for an indirect fire emplacement was found at Hdqrs, and lines layed out. A barricade was built at Hdqrs as protection to transport from machine gun fire.

### 8.7.16 — do

> Work continued on dug-out at Y.4, and sap at A.P.1 was advanced 4 feet and timbered Indirect fire emplacement at SUNKEN ROAD was pushed into the bank a distance of 6 feet and well sand bagged. A slit was also made as protection against shell fire. Work on Hdqrs indirect fire emplacement was commenced and dug down a distance of three feet and communication trench made. Reinforcements of 1 Officer arrived.

### 9.7.16 — do

> Relieved at SPOIL BANK by 6th Cdn BDE M.G. Coy. Work continued on dug out at Y4, and SARDINE BOX. The walls at Hdqrs were strengthened by timber. Work continued on Indirect fire emplacement at Hdqrs. 1 officer and 3 O.R reported for VICKERS GUN course at CAMIERS, and 1 officer and 3. O.R reported back
>
> [Entry continues on Sheet 3.]

## Page 5 — War Diary (Army Form C. 2118), Sheet No. 3

Heading: WAR DIARY or INTELLIGENCE SUMMARY [the words "INTELLIGENCE SUMMARY" are struck through] — July 1916 — 5th CDN BDE M.G. COY. "~ORIGINAL~ CONFIDENTIAL".

The 9.7.16 entry continues from Sheet 2 (no new date line):

> from VICKERS GUN course at CAMIERS.

Remarks column: each entry is initialled "J.E. McC."

### 10.7.16 — Field

> Indirect fire position at Hdqrs finished, and dug-out at SARDINE BOX finished, work continued at Y.4, and on dug out 20 I. beams were put in position and sand bagged New emplacement at Y.1 commenced and 600 sand bags filled.

### 11.7.16 — do

> Indirect fire carried out by 5 guns of this company, covering enemy's feed lines, Two indirect fire positions completed in left sector near SEPTEMBER POST. Indirect fire position at sunken Road completed. 20,000 rounds of S.A.A. sent to MIDDLE FARM.

### 12.7.16 — do

> The roof and walls at Coy Hdqrs collapsed the debris was put into sand bags and put on top strengthen top of dug-outs. Work continued on emplacements at Y.1 SARDINE BOX and DEAD HORSE. Ammunition depot commenced at VOORMEZEELE SWITCH. Instructional work carried out from 2.0 PM to 4.0 PM on VICKERS GUNS.

### 13.7.16 — do

> Emplacement and dug-out at SARDINE BOX completed. Two more indirect fire positions in this sector. Frame erected in left sector making a total of 4 of these positions in this sector. Frame work and loop hole made for DEAD HORSE emplacement and material carried over. Emplacement at Y.1 completed, and dug-out for this emplacement commenced, work continued on Ammunition depot at VOORMEZEELE SWITCH.

### 14.7.16 — do

> Ammunition depot in VOORMEZEELE SWITCH completed. This depot is very strong and will hold a large quantity of Ammunition. All material for dug-out at Y.1 Carried in. Indirect fire was carried out by 5 guns of this company, firing 2,000 rounds per gun, and covering ST ELOI - HILLE ROAD. and ST ELOI - HIELE FARM and territory in between to MARTINS FARM, and judging by the retaliation this fire must have been very effective.

### 25.7.16 — Field

> Material for new emplacement was carried to emplacement and excavation continued. Two indirect fire positions were made and indirect fire was used from these emplacements to the YIERSTRAATE - WYTSCHAETE road and LOUWAEGE - MARTINS FARMS. The section in Company reserve carried on a scheme in open warfare. The section at Rest Billets carried on with training and excavation for a field oven

### 26.7.16 — do

> Two more indirect fire emplacements were made and indirect fire by four guns was used on the Yierstraate-Wytschaete road and HOUWAEGE FARM, and MARTINS FARM, Rear support lines and enemies communication trenches. Excavation completed and frames put in position at GRANTHAM. [uncertain] in Company Reserve carried on advanced training. Trench stores at all emplacements were completed.

### 27.7.16 — do

> Front wall of GRANTHAM completed and loop hole put in place, twenty I beams put on roof and covered over, screen raised and rodded. Indirect fire carried on with four guns covering the YIERSTRAATE - WYTSCHAETE road, HOUWAEGE FARM - MARTINS FARM, reserve lines and communication trenches in rear. An alarm scheme was carried out by the section in Company reserve. Limbers renovated for to suit Vickers Machine Guns, and frames built for new limbers.

### 28.7.16 — do

> Indirect fire carried on with 4 guns covering the YIERSTRAATE - WYTSCHAETE road, HOUWAEGE - MARTINS FARMS, reserve lines and communication trenches in rear. 10,000 rds were expended and apparently gave good results, the enemy increasing his M.G. fire. Field oven at Rest Billets completed. Advanced training carried on by section in Company reserve. Rear wall of GRANTHAM emplacement completed Sand bags and Corrugated iron was placed on roof, and side walls revetted and sandbagged.

## Notes

- **Coverage:** Entries run 1–31 July 1916 with no date gaps. Diary text is 6 pages (Sheets 1–6); no appendices this month. Total 8 pages transcribed (cover, contents sheet, 6 diary sheets).
- **Officers named:** Capt. S. W. Watson (O.C.; signs the month at the foot of Sheet 6); Lieut. J. E. McCorkell (initials the diary sheets "J.E. McC."); Brigadier General MacDonnell (inspected transport and camp, 22 July); G.S.O.3, 2nd Canadian Division (inspected Hqrs and emplacements, 1 July). Officers on the Vickers courses at Camiers are unnamed.
- **Other ranks named:** None by name. One officer reinforcement arrived 8 July; the one O.R. wounded on 29 July is unnamed.
- **Equipment:** The changeover from Colt to Vickers guns is completed this month — all Colt guns and equipment were recalled on 17 July and the sections issued with Vickers guns; limbers were renovated to suit the Vickers guns and the G.S. limbered wagons extended (27–30 July); a machine gun was mounted for anti-aircraft work at advanced Hqrs (30 July). Gas blankets and frames fitted to dug-outs and emplacements; corrugated iron used on the Grantham emplacement roof. Indirect fire is heavy in the second half of the month — 10,000 rounds expended on 28 and 29 July.
- **Movements:** In the line at Voormezeele (St Eloi sector) at the start of July; relieved by the 4th Bde M.G. Coy on 15 July and back to rest billets at Reninghelst; into the Ypres–Yserstraat (Yierstraate) sector on 23 July, relieving sections of the 6th Bde M.G. Coy, with the 150th Bde M.G. Coy on the right and the 4th Cdn Bde M.G. Coy on the left; on 31 July the 6th Cdn Inf Bde relieved the 4th Cdn Inf Bde on the company's left.
- **Discipline:** None recorded in July 1916.
- **Casualties:** 29 July — 1 O.R. wounded. No deaths recorded in July.
- **Anomalies:** (1) The 5 July entry begins on Sheet 1 and continues onto Sheet 2 without a new date line; the same for the 9 July entry (Sheets 2–3) and the 24 July entry (Sheets 4–5). (2) The opening word of the 9 July entry ("Relieved") appears partly struck through; transcribed as written. (3) In the 4 July entry a word before "dug-out at Y.4." has been blacked out with ink; flagged [uncertain]. (4) In the 26 July entry a word before "in Company Reserve" is struck through; flagged [uncertain]. (5) The place is spelled "YIERSTRAATE" (also "YIERSTRAAT" on 29 and 31 July) and "LOUWAEGE" (25 and 29 July) against "HOUWAEGE" (26–28 and 30 July); the emplacement is spelled "GRANTHAM" except on 29 July, where it is "GRATHAM"; all transcribed as written. (6) The 27 July entry reads "Limbers renovated for to suit Vickers Machine Guns"; transcribed as written. (7) The unit heading on Sheet 2 reads "5th CDN INF BDE M.G. COY." and on Sheet 6 "5th Cdn Bde In G. Coy." (sic); transcribed as written. (8) The 11 July entry says the indirect fire covered "enemy's feed lines"; transcribed as written. (9) The 14 July entry gives the roads as "ST ELOI - HILLE ROAD" and "ST ELOI - HIELE FARM"; transcribed as written.
