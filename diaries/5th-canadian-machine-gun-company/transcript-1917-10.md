### 1/10/17 — Field

> MAISNIL BOUCHE. Ref. Map 36.b.FRANCE.
>
> The Company paraded at 8.30. A.M. and the O.C., Carried out a test of each section for fire control and indication of targets. The reminder of the morning was spent in overhauling guns, S.A.A., belts etc. A Canteen committee consisting of Lt: Creighton as President and three N.C.O's and three O.R.'s selected by the company wa formed to supervise the expenditure of the profits of the company canteen. This canteen was first started during May of this year and, after buying Polish and Brasso for the transport and Company and fresh vegetable for the men etc., out of the profits, the net balance on hand at this date is approximately 2000 francs.

### 2/10/17 — (ditto)

> In the morning the Officers, N.C.O's numbers one and two of gun crews and the scouts paraded to VILLER AU BOIS to view plasticene model of our Division front. A programme of Company sports has been arranged by Lieut. Gibson to be held in the near future and entries have been freely made in all events. Corporal Hickman D.C.M. returned from 30 days Prob. N C.O's Course and resumed his duties with "C" Section.

### 3/10/17 — (ditto)

> Nothing of particular importance occurred during the day. Parades were as usual.

### 4/10/17 — (ditto)

> Lieut. G.W.H.Millican reported today on being reposted from the 15th Cdn. M.G.Coy.

### 4/10/17 — Field (continued)

> The company sports were to have been held during the afternoon but were called off on account of rain. The Officers, N.C.O's and number one and two paraded to VILLERS AU BOIS at 5 P.M. to view the plasticene model of the front.

### 5/10/17 — (ditto)

> Parades were held as usual in the morning but wet weather in the afternoon again interfered with the sports. All the attached carriers were returned during the day to their units, Great trouble being experienced in getting sufficient stores to complete their shortages so as to return them completely outfitted. The containers in the Box Respirators were replaced by the new issue by the M.G.Bttn., Gas N.C.O. and all helmets tested.

### 6/10/17 — (ditto)

> The usual parades were carried on with. The weather again interfered with the company sports.

### 7/10/17 — (ditto)

> "C" & "D" Sections paraded at 8 A.M. for the long range at MARQUETTES, "A" & "B" Sections supplying the fatigue parties for sentries. The morning was spent in practicing direct and indirect fire. A rather unfortunate incident occurred— one of the sentries was struck in the foot by what must have been a ricochet. The wound was very trivial.

### 8/10/17 — Field

> The usual parades were held in the morning. During the forenoon it rained at intervals. At 4 P.M. it again commenced to rain and continued until 10. P.M. Lieut. C.V.Grantham left this company today the, take over command of the 6th Cdn. M.G.Coy. Lieut. Maclaren returned from the base where he was in hospital.

### 9/10/17 — (ditto)

> Day fine till 4 P.M. when it commenced to rain, continued till 9 P.M. Company paraded at 8.30. A.M. for inspection and instruction on how gas helmets and respirators are to be worn. Sports were held during the morning. In the evening a company concert was held in the Y.M.C.A. Concert Hut, At Gouy.

### 10/10/17 — (ditto)

> The company paraded and was paid at 8.30 A.M. in accordance with instructions received from the D.M.G.O., the company fell in at 0.45 A.M. and moved off with complete transport for the AUX RITZ Area. The weather was very disagreeable and it rained continuously through out the morning. The company arrived at AUX RITZ at 12.30. P.M. and went into billets. The transport took over covered standings.

### 12/10/17 — Field

> Rain at intervals during day and night. Both our own and enemy artillery quiet. Our scout planes active in forenoon. No enemy aircraft were seen. We carried on the following night firing from 7 P.M. to 11 P.M.
>
> 2500 rds. S.A.A., at Cross roads T.5.a.10.85
>
> 2500 rds. S.A.A., at MERICOURT Square. T.5.b.15.05
>
> During the day Capt. McCorkell called and explained the disposition of the companies guns to the B.M. 6th Cdn. Inf. Bde., Colonel Bell 31st Cdn. Battn., and to the O.C., 28th Battn. Left Maclaren reported to the O.C., 31st Cdn. Battn., and Lt. McBurney reported to O.C., 28th Battn. Casualties, "Nil"
>
> 32 Reinforcements arrived at Transport this date.

### 13/10/17 — (ditto)

> Rain during the early morning and again at 4 P.M. lasting for half an hour. The artillery on both sides was more active than usual today. During the afternoon the enemy shelled BOIS de CHAUDIERE, the front line and TEDDY GERRARD at Approximate T.15.b. 40.60
>
> From 7 P.M. to 11 P.M. we carried out the following night firing.
>
> 2800 rds. S.A.A., at Cross Roads T.5.a.10.85
>
> 2500 rds. S.A.A., at MERICOURT SQUARE T.5.b.15.05
>
> During the day Capt. McCorkell and Lt. Maclaren reported to the O.C., 31 Battn. and Lt. McBurney to O.C., 28th Battn. Casualties, "Nil".

### 14/10/17 — Field

> Day cool with occasional showers. Both artilleries active. During afternoon the enemy fired about 150, 4.1 on our trenches between T.7.b.90.05 and T.7.b.72.25 No damage done. Following night firing carried out.
>
> 2500 rds. S.A.A. Cross Roads T.5.a.10.85.
>
> 2500 rds. S.A.A., MERICOURT SQUARE. T.5.b.15.05
>
> "B" Section re-built parapet at BLUENOSE (T.7.b.90.15) also firing step using 150 sand bags. Casualties Nil.

### 15/10/17 — (ditto)

> Day cool with showers. Both enemy and our own artillery active. Enemy bombarded right sub-sector of LA CHAUDIERE sector heavily, from 4.30. A.M. to 5.A.M., but no infantry action took place. Our artillery retaliated, ceasing fire at 5.30. A.M. Following night fire was carried out.
>
> 2500 rds. S.A.A., cross roads T.5.a.10.85
>
> 2500 rds. S.A.A., at MERICOURT SQUARE. T.5.b.15.05
>
> Casualties "Nil".

### 16/10/17 — (ditto)

> Day cool and clear. No Rain. Both our own and enemy artillery quiet. In accordance with 2nd Cdn. Divl. M.G. Battn., O.O. No. 52 the company was relieved by the 144th Bde. M.G.Coy., of the 48th (Midland) Division. The 144th Bde. M.G.Coy., only relieved 10 of our guns, our remaining six were withdrawn. The relief was reported complete and correct at [uncertain] P.M. On completion of relief the sections moved independently to billets at AUX REITZ where a hot meal was served. O.O.NO.27 att.

### 17/10/17 — Field

> Day fine. The commanding officer held an inspection company at 10 A.M. Men were free for remainder of day.

> Day fine. The commanding officer held inspection of company at 10 A.M.

### 20/10/17 — (ditto)

> Company paraded at 9 A.M. Standing orders and extracts from censorship Regulations were read. At 9.30. A.M. Muster Roll was called. 10.30. to 11 A.M. Gas Helmet and Box Respirator drill. The company paraded at 12.45. P.M. and marched to SAVY for a bath and clean change of clothes.

### 21/10/17 — (ditto)

> DAY FINE, NO parades held.

### 22/10/17 — Field

> Day fine. Commanding Officer's inspection held at 9 A.M. Gas Drill 10. to 10.30 A.M. Capt. J.E.McCorkell proceeded on leave to England this date.

### 23/10/17 — (ditto)

> Day fine. Company paraded 10 A.M. full marching order, for inspection. Company paraded at BETHENCOURT at 7 P.M. and marched to the station at TINQUES. The men marched onto the train as soon as we arrived at the station. The Transport, vehicles and animals completed entraining at 9.30. P.M. and train moved off at 10.40. P.M. from TINQUES and proceeded by ST.POL, outskirts of BETHUNE, LILLERS, AIRE AND HAZEBROUCK detraining at CAESTRE at 4 P.M.

### 24/10/17 — (ditto)

> Early morning cool. Detraining was completed at 5.30. A.M. Owing to there being no guide to meet us we were unable to move off for our billets till 8 A.M. At 8.30. A.M. we started on the march for billets which we arrived at, at 9 A.M. The company is billet in two large barns at LA BREAIRIE [uncertain] (Map ref. Sheet. BELGIUM AND PART OF FRANCE. NO. 27 1/40,000 Location V.5.c.4.1). The men had a hot meal at 1 P.M.

### 25/10/17 — (ditto)
