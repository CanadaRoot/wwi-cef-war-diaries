## Page 3 — War Diary (Army Form C. 2118), Sheet No. 1

Heading: WAR DIARY or INTELLIGENCE SUMMARY [the words "INTELLIGENCE SUMMARY" are struck through] — JUNE 1916 — 5th CANADIAN BRIGADE MACHINE GUN COY. "ORIGINAL CONFIDENTIAL".

Remarks column: each entry is initialled "J.E. McC. Lt."

### 1.6.16 — In the Field

> REF. MAP:- SHEET 28 OF BELGIUM. SCALE 1:40.000. TRENCH MAP. VOORMEZEELE, PART OF SHEET 28, SCALE 1:10.000.
>
> The 5th BDE. M. G. Coy standing to in VOORMEZEELE as supports to the 6th BDE M G Coy.

### 2.6.16 — do

> The work at A.P.1, HILL TOP, LOCK POST, and SPOIL BANK, continued, and good progress is being made there. The 26th BN are supplying a work party of 8 men each night - so as to hurry these positions along. A sentry box was started and partly completed at CONVENT SCHOOL. All guns, spare parts, ammunition, gone over and thoroughly cleaned

### 3.6.16 — do

> The saps at A.P.1, HILL TOP, LOCK POST, and SPOIL BANK were advanced a distance of 3'-0" and timbered. The work party promised by the 26th BN, at these points, was unable to come owing to a stand to, so the work had to be carried on by our own men that were able to be spared from the teams. A trench was dug from CONVENT SCHOOL emplacement, connecting it up with the main trench. At M.G. HQRS, a surface dugout was started, and the roof was braced up with timber.

### 4.6.16 — do

> Good progress has been made at A.P.1, HILL TOP, LOCK POST and SPOIL BANK, but owing to the unrest in this part of the line, we were unable to obtain any work parties from the BNS, but every available man in the company was used.

### 5.6.16 — do

> The sap at HILL TOP which is 4'x3' has been advanced 12', and gradually lowered a distance of 6', the whole was timbered through out. At LOCK POST the sap which is 5'x3' has been advanced 14' and completely timbered. At SPOIL BANK, two dugouts 4'x3'x6' have been completed, these had to be sapped and timbered. Improvements are still in progress on all dug outs at HQRS. In the evening the company was relieved by the 9th BDE. M.G. Coy Imperials, who have taken over this portion of the line. The company returned to REST BILLETS.

## Page 4 — War Diary (Army Form C. 2118), Sheet No. 2

Heading: WAR DIARY or INTELLIGENCE SUMMARY [the words "INTELLIGENCE SUMMARY" are struck through] — JUNE 1916 — 5th CDN BDE M.G. COY. "ORIGINAL CONFIDENTIAL".

Remarks column: each entry is initialled "J.E. McC. Lt."

### 6.6.16 — Field

> All men returned to camp REST BILLETS at 3.0 AM. and rested all day.

### 7.6.16 — do

> General clean up of the camp, all tents struck and floor boards taken up. Bath and Pay parades in the afternoon. The Company moved to the trenches again at ZILLEBEKE at 7.0 P.M.

### 8.6.16 — do

> Two sections returned to REST BILLETS, at 5.0 AM, not having been required last night. Rested all day, and moved off again at 7.0 PM for ZILLEBEKE Casualties:- 3 O.R. wounded.

### 9.6.16 — do

> General reconnaissance made of the ZILLEBEKE area, by all officers of this Company.

### 10.6.16 — do

> The trench stores at each emplacement completed up to the following:- 10,000 S.A.A. 20 Iron Rations, 9 gallons of fresh water, 24 Mills Bombs. Intense bombardment by our artillery from 6.30 PM until 7.0 PM on the enemy's front line, very heavy come back by the enemy.

### 11.6.16 — do

> Intense bombardment carried out by our artillery on the Germans front line from 2.0 AM to 2.30 AM, practically no come back by the enemy. The 5th BDE M.G. Coy relieved in the evening by the 2nd BDE M G Coy Lieut. Thomas wounded at 7.0 PM this date by shrapnel at Piccadilly Square.

### 12.6.16 — do

> Arrived at CAMP A AREA 3 at 5.0 AM, had breakfast, and then rested the remainder of the day. The Company moved off again at 5.0 PM to their REST BILLETS at RENINGHELST.

### 13.6.16 — do

> Guns, ammunition and spare parts, thoroughly cleaned, and belts refilled. Kit inspection held and deficiencies made up. Foot inspection held.

## Page 5 — War Diary (Army Form C. 2118), Sheet No. 3

Heading: WAR DIARY or INTELLIGENCE SUMMARY [the words "INTELLIGENCE SUMMARY" are struck through] — JUNE 1916 — 5th CDN BDE M.G. COY. "ORIGINAL CONFIDENTIAL".

Remarks column: each entry is initialled "J.E. McC. Lt."

### 14.6.16 — Field

> All guns overhauled and tried out on the range. 5th BDE M.G. Coy relieved the 2nd BDE. M.G. Coy at the ZILLEBEKE area.

### 15.6.16 — do

> About 1.0 AM Sergeant Duffett of No 2 Section, captured 7 Germans who were in dugouts in HALIFAX trench, three had been wounded by our bombardment, the others were in good health, they were handed over to Major Alexander of the 24th BN, who sent them back to BDE HQRS. No casualties this date.

### 16.6.16 — do

> Area around RED COTTAGE was inspected and found to be suitable for an emplacement, so same was built and gun put in. This gun was taken from reserve in ZILLEBEKE. Trench stores at all emplacements replenished.

### 17.6.16 — do

> Pedestal was erected on the old S.P.13 emplacement, and gun brought from reserve at FOSSEWAY, and put in this position. Two Germans M.G's and a quantity of ammunition found deeply buried in mud, in SANCTUARY WOOD, these were dug out and carried to BDE. Casualties 1. O.R. (SHELL SHOCK)

### 18.6.16 — do

> Nothing of any note took place this date. Artillery and machine gun fire practically nil. Casualties 1 O.R. Wounded.

### 19.6.16 — do

> Two sections of the 5th BDE M.G. Coy were relieved by the 2nd BDE M.G. Coy and proceeded to rest Billets at RENINGHELST.

### 20.6.16 — do

> The two sections being relieved the evening before, and arrived in camp about 4.0 AM, and rested all day. The two remaining sections and HQRS were relieved by the remainder of the 2nd BDE M.G. Coy, when they proceeded to rest Billets.

## Page 6 — War Diary (Army Form C. 2118), Sheet No. 4

Heading: WAR DIARY or INTELLIGENCE SUMMARY [the words "INTELLIGENCE SUMMARY" are struck through] — JUNE 1916 — 5th CDN INF BDE M.G. COY. "ORIGINAL CONFIDENTIAL".

Remarks column: each entry is initialled "J.E. McC. Lt."

### 21.6.16 — Field

> The company rested all day. The two sections that were relieved the evening previous arrived in camp at 4.0 A.M.

### 22.6.16 — do

> The camp was thoroughly cleaned, and all tents struck, to allow the air to get at the ground under the tent bottoms. The guns were thoroughly cleaned, ammunition overhauled, and belts refilled. In the afternoon the company had a pay parade.

### 23.6.16 — do

> The company had a bath parade in the morning, and general instructional work in the afternoon.

### 24.6.16 — do

> Lieuts J E McCorkell and A.C Bowles and 3 O.R. left at 5.0 AM for machine gun course in Vickers Guns at CAMIERS at 5.0 AM. General instructional work during the morning for the company Received 16 Vickers guns and equipment during the afternoon from Ordnance.

### 25.6.16 — do

> Church Service held in Camp ground at 11.0 AM.

### 26.6.16 — do

> All guns cleaned and tried out on the range, limbers packed in readiness to move up to VOORMEZEELE in the evening, but relief cancelled.

### 27.6.16 — do

> General instructional work carried out all day on Vickers guns.

### 28.6.16 — do

> All guns tried out on the range, limbers packed, and everything got [inserted above the line] ready to leave at 9.0 PM. The 5th CON BDE M.G. Coy went into the trenches in the St Eloi sector.

### 29.6.16 — do

> All emplacements and dugouts cleaned as far as possible, and all deficiencies made up in trench stores, all water cans emptied and refilled. Ammunition 10 R [uncertain]

### 30.6.16 — do

> The saps at SPOIL BANK were started again, and work is progressing very fast, as two sappers were obtained from the 25th BN. Everything in this sector very quiet all day.

Signed at foot of page: "S.W. Watson Capt O.C. 5th Con. BDE. M.G. Co."

## Notes

- **Coverage:** Entries run 1–30 June 1916 with no date gaps. Diary text is 4 pages (Sheets 1–4); no appendices this month. Total 6 pages transcribed (cover, contents sheet, 4 diary sheets).
- **Officers named:** Capt. S. W. Watson (O.C.; signs the month at the foot of Sheet 4); Lieut. J. E. McCorkell (initials the diary sheets "J.E. McC. Lt." and leaves for the Vickers course at Camiers on 24 June); Lieut. A. C. Bowles (also to Camiers, 24 June); Lieut. Thomas (wounded by shrapnel at Piccadilly Square, 11 June); Major Alexander, 24th Battalion (receives the seven German prisoners, 15 June).
- **Other ranks named:** Sergeant Duffett, No. 2 Section (captured seven Germans in Halifax Trench, 15 June). All casualties are unnamed other ranks.
- **Equipment:** Sixteen Vickers guns and equipment received from Ordnance on 24 June, replacing the Colt guns in use earlier in the year; two captured German machine guns and ammunition dug out of the mud in Sanctuary Wood (17 June); trench stores standard per emplacement — 10,000 rounds S.A.A., 20 iron rations, 9 gallons of fresh water, 24 Mills bombs (10 June).
- **Movements:** In the line at Voormezeele at the start of June; relieved by the 9th Brigade M.G. Coy (Imperials) on the evening of 5 June and back to rest billets; into the Zillebeke trenches on the evening of 7 June; relieved by the 2nd Bde M.G. Coy on the evening of 11 June, back to Camp A Area 3, then to rest billets at Reninghelst on 12 June; back into the Zillebeke area relieving the 2nd Bde M.G. Coy on 14 June; sections relieved by the 2nd Bde M.G. Coy on 19–20 June and back to rest billets; into the trenches in the St Eloi sector on the evening of 28 June.
- **Discipline:** None recorded in June 1916.
- **Casualties:** 8 June — 3 O.R. wounded; 11 June — Lieut. Thomas wounded by shrapnel at Piccadilly Square; 17 June — 1 O.R. shell shock; 18 June — 1 O.R. wounded. No deaths recorded in June.
- **Anomalies:** (1) The 24 June entry gives "5.0 AM" twice ("left at 5.0 AM ... at CAMIERS at 5.0 AM"); transcribed as written. (2) The 29 June entry ends mid-sentence with "Ammunition 10 R" — the figure appears abbreviated or cut off at the page edge; flagged [uncertain]. (3) The unit heading on Sheet 4 reads "5th CDN INF BDE M.G. COY." (with "INF" added), as on the last May sheet. (4) In the 28 June entry the word "got" is inserted above the line ("everything got ready to leave"). (5) The 15 June entry says the seven Germans were captured in Halifax Trench; three had been wounded by the bombardment. (6) The diary spells the school "CAMIERS" here and "CAMIERES" in May; both transcribed as written.
