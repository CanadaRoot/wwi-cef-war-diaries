## Page 3 — War Diary (Army Form C. 2118), Sheet No. 1

Heading: WAR DIARY or INTELLIGENCE SUMMARY [the words "INTELLIGENCE SUMMARY" are struck through] — 5th Canadian Machine Gun Coy. "NOVEMBER 1916" is written across the heading.

Remarks column: each entry is initialled "J.H.McC."

### 1/11/16 — Field

> The material having been obtained, work was resumed on the Horse Standings. A draft of 10. O.R. was received from the Machine Gun Depot France. They are all very likely looking men. All work being done in the line progressed favourably.

### 2/11/16 — (ditto)

> The two sections in reserve relieved the two sections in the line Relief being completed at 1.15 P.M. Two indirect fire positions were located and built at R 23 d 6 1/2. 2 for firing at M 26 b 7.9 to M 21 C 6.1 The other at M 13 d 9.0 1/2 for firing at M 21 C 8.8 to M 21 d 7.2 3000 rds S.A.A. were fired from each position between the hours of 8.30 P.M. to 12.00 Midnight the targets are both trench tramways and paths. There was no retaliation.

### 3/11/16 — (ditto)

> The two sections in reserve were both bathed during the day. One Section spent the morning at the M.G. RANGE. On range practices during the afternoon both sections in reserve and Headquarters Staff were fitted with the new box respirators and were tested in the Brigade Gas Chamber. There were no faulty ones found [continued on next sheet]

## Page 4 — War Diary (Army Form C. 2118), Sheet No. 2

Heading: WAR DIARY or INTELLIGENCE SUMMARY [the words "INTELLIGENCE SUMMARY" are struck through] — 5th Canadian Machine Gun Coy. "NOVEMBER 1916" is written across the heading.

Remarks column: each entry is initialled "J.H.McC."

### 4/11/16 — Field

> Indirect fire was carried on with during the past night between the hours of 8.00 P.M. and 12.00 P.M. Two guns were used the targets and gun positions being the same as the previous night 4000 rds S.A.A. per gun were fired. There was no retaliation. The indirect fire positions was altered and considerably improved the gun being sunk down to the level of the ground.

### 5/11/16 — (ditto)

> Indirect fire was continued last night as usual 4000 Rds. per gun being fired. The Hun retaliated to the fire of the left gun with two guns firing on the top of the Slagheap at Liege No 15 (M 20 a) evidently thinking that our fire was from that point. Church Parade was held during the day for all men in reserve at BULLY. On account of the high wind work has had to be discontinued on the Horse Standings.

### 6/11/16 — (ditto)

> Indirect Fire was carried on as usual last night 4000 Rounds S.A.A. per gun being used. The enemy retaliated strongly at both guns with M. G. fire, but failed to locate them.

## Page 5 — War Diary (Army Form C. 2118), Sheet No. 3

Heading: WAR DIARY or INTELLIGENCE SUMMARY [the words "INTELLIGENCE SUMMARY" are struck through] — 5th Canadian Machine Gun Coy. "NOVEMBER 1916" is written across the heading.

Remarks column: each entry is initialled "J.H.McC."

### 6/11/16 — Field (continued)

> Work on the new dugout at LEMCO ALLEY is being seriously impeded by cave-ins of the roof. In LINK TRENCH both mattresses [uncertain] and laid and one of the gun platforms widened. Work on the new Horse Standings is progressing favourably, the roof being nearly completed. During the day two "duds" fell about 50 yards from the RESERVE LINE Emplacement. A new Indirect Fire position was built in CHARCOAL TRENCH between BOVRIL ALLEY and BAJOLLI LINE. (M 19 b. 5. 9 1/2).

### 7/11/16 — (ditto)

> Three Guns of this Unit used Indirect Fire on the following targets (M. 26. B. 7.9. to M 21. C. 6.1.) (M. 21. C. 8.8. to M. 21. D. 7.2) and new target (M. 26. D. 3.8. to M 33. O. 9.) all of these guns received heavy retaliation but were not found. Each Gun expended 4000. Rounds S.A.A. firing from 8 P.M. to 12 P.M. We feel confident that we are well on our target in each case, or else we would not get the retaliation. The caving in of LEMCO Dugout has been remedied. CAPT. S. W. WATSON reported back to duty and assumes Command of this Unit.

## Page 6 — War Diary (Army Form C. 2118), Sheet No. 4

### 8/11/16 — Field

> An inter-company relief was carried out. The two sections in reserve relieving the two sections in the line, relief being completed at 12 Noon. The new Horse Standings were today completed and the Horses moved from the Old Standings. A new position in the BAJOLLI LINE. was chosen for the CROSS ROADS gun and the work of construction was begun. The Ammunition Dugout in CAP DU PONT TRENCH is being torn down and constructed into a dugout.

### 9/11/16 — (ditto)

> Two new emplacements in the RESERVE LINE were commenced and excavation completed. These emplacements are part of the Defence Scheme and are to be known as MECHANICS I and MECHANICS II. The following work including the two Emplacements just mentioned is under way, TRENCH AT LINK ALLEY, TRENCH AT BAJOLLI NO I. Dugout at LEMCO ALLEY, and emplacement at SUNKEN ROAD. Indirect fire was also carried out by three guns on the usual targets and again the retaliation was very noticeable.

### 10/11/16 — Field

> Indirect fire was used by 3 guns on the previously mentioned targets, but the time was changed and made a little earlier, fire commencing at 6 P.M. and ceasing at 10 P.M. 4000 Rounds per gun was used, and apparently with good success. The usual Indirect fire with three guns was continued and we received more retaliation to night than usual.

## Notes

- **Coverage:** Entries run 1–30 November 1916 with no date gaps. 16 pages in total: cover, typed contents sheet, and 14 diary sheets (Sheets No. 1–14), matching the contents sheet's "Diary Text — 14 Pages".
- **Form change:** Unlike September and October, the November sheets are on lined paper (imprint "T.1134. Wt. W708—776. 500000. 4/15. Sir J. C. & S." at the foot of each sheet) and the heading "INTELLIGENCE SUMMARY" is struck through on Sheets No. 1–7 but left standing on Sheets No. 8–14.
- **Officers named:** Capt. S. W. Watson (reported back to duty and assumed command of the unit 7 Nov); Capt. J. H. McCorkell (continues to initial the entries and signs the month "for O.C."); Major Foss V.C. D.S.O. of the General Staff (inspected every position 13 Nov, points rectified 14 Nov).
- **Other ranks named:** None by name in November 1916.
- **Equipment:** New box respirators fitted to reserve sections and Headquarters staff and tested in the Brigade Gas Chamber 3 Nov, to A and B Sections 15 Nov, and to Transport personnel 19 Nov; gas blankets placed at dugout entrances; three anti-aircraft positions erected 29 Nov; 50 rolls of barbed wire used for a barrier across the Sunken Road 11 Nov; 40 steel rails carried to Headquarters at Mechanics Dump 29 Nov; 400 sand bags used revetting Morocco South trench 27 Nov.
- **Movements:** The company remained in the Angres sector throughout November, in the line and in reserve at Bully with transport lines at Fosse 10, Sains-en-Gohelle; inter-company reliefs (reserve sections relieving line sections) on 2, 8, 14, 20 and 26 Nov.
- **Discipline:** None recorded in November 1916.
- **Casualties:** None recorded in November 1916. The nightly indirect fire drew regular enemy retaliation, but no casualties are reported.
- **Anomalies:** (1) Capt. S. W. Watson assumes command 7 Nov, yet the entries keep being initialled "J.H.McC." and the month is signed by McCorkell "for O.C." (2) The 28 Nov entry gives the same time for opening and ceasing fire ("opened fire at 9.30 P.M. Ceased fire at 9.30 P.M."). (3) The 16 Nov entry has the word "carried" inserted above the line in "the artillery carried out a strafe". (4) Several map-reference figures are faint and are transcribed as read. (5) The 30 Nov entry ends without a full stop.
