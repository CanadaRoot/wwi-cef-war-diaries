### 1/4/17 — PETIT-SERVINS

> Being Sunday, church parade was held at 11.00 A.M. Remainder of day was spent in rest. Lieut. Robson and working party arrived out of the line.

### 2/4/17 — (ditto)

> "C" Section paraded to the practice trenches. The remainder of the Company carried on with individual training. Lieut. McBurny lectured to the Officers N.C.O's and Nos. 1's on Indirect Fire between 5 and 6 P.M.

### 3/4/17 — (ditto)

> The Plan for the Barrage scheme was perfected. On account of the weather "C" Section did not go to practice trenches and the O.C's parade was cancelled. The Coy. was paid during the day. Lieuts. Eller and Creighton visited O.C 6th Cdn. M.G. Coy. at Neuville St. Vaast during the day to make arrangements for relieving them on the night of the 4th and 5th.

### 4/4/17 — (ditto)

> Orders received to relieve the 6th CDN. M.G. Co. at Neuville St. Vaast. "B" + "D" Sections moved off from PETIT SERVINS at 3 P.M. under Lieut. Bowles. Bivouaced for tea at MONT. ST. ELOY. O.C. and Lieut. Robson proceeded at 5 P.M and arrived at NEUVILLE ST. VAAST 7.35 P.M. "B" Section relief complete at 10.15 P.M. and "D" Section 12.15 A.M. Night passed off quietly except for the continual bombardment of our guns of all calibres.

### 5/4/17 — (ditto)

> Enemy very quiet and our bombardment continues. Two trial barrages put over during the day. The 15" inchers shelled THELUS at 1.30 A.M. One man of this Co. Killed and two wounded by Trench Mortar this afternoon. Work on new dug-out at Parallel I for the offensive proceeding satisfactorily. Battery positions Y and Y.S. [uncertain] nearing completion. Commenced to put in "Bladder" Telephone system for communication with Batteries from Bde. Hdqrs. Six thousand rounds expended on Indirect fire. Targets open country and overland routes in rear of SWISCHEN STELHUNG Trench. "A" and "C" Sections proceed to the line, to make final preparations for Zero hour.

### 6/4/17 — (ditto)

> Quiet day with the few odd shells into Neuville St. Vaast. "C" Section carried out Indirect fire on open country and overland routes in rear of SWISCHEN STELHUNG Trench. 8,000 rds. being expended. Lieut. O.B. ELLER was evacuated sick during the day.

### 9/4/17 — NEUVILLE ST. VAAST (continued)

> by a subsection in each. On its arrival the whole section, under Lieut Creighton's guidance, immediately started to work to help the engineers complete the strongpoints. Major Watson visited all of "A" & "C" Section's guns in their positions between 12 noon & 3 P.M. Lieut. Grantham reconnoitred forward area between 4 P.M. & 8 P.M. Capt. J. E. A total of 50,000 rounds being fired by this battery during the day's operations. They were withdrawn during the evening to their dugouts for a night's rest.

### 10/4/17 — (ditto)

> Nothing of importance happened during the night or morning. Rations and water were got up to all sections. About 11.30 A.M. "C" Section, from the Head of BONVAL RAVINE, observed a party of 100 strong of the enemy between PETIT VIMY and VIMY. Fire was immediately opened on them and they were dispersed with casualties, as was a second party numbering about 50, 15 minutes later. Retaliation immediately followed by the enemy who shelled the head of the ravine with about 38 77 mms and swept it with M.G. fire. At 3.40 P.M. word was received that a counter attack was expected and for "F.S." battery to stand-to. At 4. P.M. "F.S." Battery was ordered to prepare to move forward and Capt. McCorkell took over command from Lieut. Bowles, who was ordered to the horse lines being sick. Moving forward at 4.30 P.M. to relieve guns of the 14th Cdn. M. G. Co. in "F.1" position at A5 A 0.1, Capt. McCorkell found that these guns were not at that location, so he moved his Battery to the correct location at 5.35 P.M. Command of this battery passed from Major Watson to Major Basevi, O.C. 14th Can. M. G. Co, leaving Major Watson with no batteries under him. This order was changed at 6.40 P.M., command of "D.1," "E.1" and "F.1" batteries passing to Major Watson who was ordered at the same time to have them take up entirely new positions further forward so as to engage entirely new targets from those ordered previously. Casualties were light, the total for the two days now being 1. O.R Killed & 7. O.R Wounded.

### 11/4/17 — VIMY RIDGE

> after the moves of last night, "F.1" Battery is now located in FUSTY trench at A.5 D 7.6. "B" section guns on right and "D" section on left. Bn. taking over dug-outs at PARALLEL IX. All arrangements for rations and water are planning out well and the men are keeping in good health despite the bad weather. One crosshead belonging to "C" section was damaged by shell fire during the day and 1. O.R Killed and 1. O.R. wounded. Lieut. Robson was called into the line from the detail camp and held in reserve at Hdqrs in case of casualties among the section commanders.

### 12/4/17 — (ditto)

> The old section Hdqrs dug out in WATLING STREET was found to be unoccupied this morning so Advanced Coy. Hdqrs was moved to it. The control of "F.1" battery has passed from Major Watson to Major Forbes Mitchell, O.C. 4th Cdn. M.G. Co. The day passed quietly with only occasional shelling around the gun positions. The weather still continued very bad, snow flurries and showers of rain being frequent. The health of the men continues good, one or two cases of trench feet developed but dry socks are being issued. Lieut. A.C. BOWLES was evacuated sick during the day. Owing to the extra rum ration the men are not suffering with the cold.

### 14/4/17 — VIMY-FARBUS RY. LINE

> At 5.40. A.M. "D" Section was ordered to move forward and take up protective positions covering our Brigade front on a line in advance of MOUNT FORÉT quarries. By 6.30. A.M. the disposition of the Company was as follows. "D" Section 4 guns between T15b 9.2 & T16 D 3.3 Hdqrs in MOUNT FORÉT quarries. "A" Section 4 guns as follows, T26 b 3.1, T26 a 3.7, T26 c 7.7 and T26c 8, 8 1/2 [uncertain]. "C" Section 4 guns in line between T20 c B.5. & T26 a 5.4, along the R. R. embankment. ### 15/4/17 — (ditto)

> Rations and water were brought up by pack animals to the MILL at T26a 8.1 during the morning. The 6th Cdn. M. G. Coy. relieved us during the afternoon relief being completed at 3.45 P.M. On completion of relief the Company moved by sections to dugouts in NEUVILLE ST. VAAST and remained there for the night. The weather continues very wet & cold.

### 16/4/17 — AUX RIETZ

> During the morning the Company moved by sections to the 5th Cdn. Inf. Bde. camp, about 300 yds. west of AUX. REITZ. Tents were drawn & pitched and the details from YUKON CAMP rejoined during the day. The Company bathed during the late afternoon at the BOIS DE BRAY baths.

### 17/4/17 — (ditto)

> Owing to the wet, cold weather there were no parades. The day was spent completing shortages. Capt. McCorkell was placed in charge of the transport & Q. M. work of the Company.

### 28/4/17 — VIMY-FARBUS R.R. EMBT. (continued)

> ARLEUX EN GOHELLE in conjunction with operations being carried out by the 3rd Army further south. Nos 4, 5, 6, and 7 M.G. Batteries under Major S. W. Watson, O.C 5th Cdn. M. G. Coy. formed a supporting and protective barrage in conjunction with the artillery. At the same time the 25th Cdn. Inf. Bn. advanced their line so as to connect up with the 26th Bn, who had earlier in the night dug in in advance of the 25th Bn's left flank, and to conform with the ground gained by the 1st Cdn. Div. on their right, the operation was successful and all objectives were reported as having been taken. A total of 162,000 rds. S.A.A were expended by the four M.G. Batteries between 4.25 A.M and 6.55 A.M. A gun and crew of "B" section at T23 d 5.9 1/2 was buried by the German counter barrage at 4.30 A.M., 2. O.R being killed and 1. O.R wounded. The remainder of the crew Cpl. RONAYNE and 1. O.R. were dug out unhurt 3 hours later by PTE. BROWN of the other gun crew. The gun was slightly damaged but not put out of action. During the early morning these two guns had caught a few fleeting parties of the enemy in the open and inflicted casualties. By noon the situation was normal but, as the enemy were reported to be massing between ROUVROY and the BOIS BERNARD and also at U21 and U20c, all batteries were kept standing to, laid on their protective barrages. At 8.30 P.M the enemy put up a S.O.S on our right flank. All batteries opened up fire, 17,000 rds. being expended. One Gun of the Borden Battery was put out of action by shrapnel during the day, but was replaced immediately from the Div. Repair Centre at AUX RIETZ. Casualties among attached Units 1. O.R killed and 2 O.R wounded.

### 29/4/17 — (ditto)

> During the early morning one gun of "A" section was moved to a newly constructed strongpoint at T22c 4.5. 50 [uncertain]. In answer to a S.O.S at 4.30 A.M all Batteries opened up fire, 2,5000 rds being expended. 112,000 rds. S.A.A. were delivered at forward dumps at Battery positions during the evening, by our transport, pack animals being used forward of VIMY STATION. The enemy heavily shelled the rear areas at R.R. Embankment during the evening with Gas shells. Our pack train was caught in this gas and the horses slightly gassed.

### 30/4/17 — VIMY-FARBUS R.R. EMBT.

> In answer to a S.O.S. at 4.30 A.M. all M.G. batteries opened fire on their protective barrage for a short time, 25,000 rds. S.A.A. being expended. The remainder of the day passed quietly with the usual enemy's shelling of our forward area. Orders were received in the afternoon for Nos. 5 & 6 Batteries (14th CON. M.G. CO.) to be withdrawn during the evening to rest. This necessitated alterations in the protective barrage, which was amended accordingly. Later word was received that No. 4 Battery (1st CON. M.M.G.) would also be withdrawn. This cancelled any further attempt to form a protective barrage owing to the extensive front to be covered by so few guns. Therefore No. 7 Battery was dissolved and "C" section was ordered to man positions in the sunken road at T28 a 5.5 for the purpose of carrying out indirect fire on the enemy's trenches and wire, which had been damaged by our artillery fire, to prevent repairing of same. "D" section was ordered to carry out Indirect fire on the streets and crossroads in ACHEVILLE. The enemy maintained an intensive bombardment on MT. FORÊT QUARRIES and our forward area from 5.30 P.M. onwards, evidently suspecting the relief of the 25th Bn. by the 24th Bn. which was actually in progress.

> 11. (1) CRITICISM.
>
> The only criticisms I can make of the organization and arrangements, are that I think the Battery Positions, Targets, and Group Commanders were unnecessarily switched and changed about too often. In some cases I think by the actual situation not being clearly understood.
> The changing of Group Commanders seemed to cause a lot of confusion amongst Battery Commanders as they did not know where to send reports etc. In my own case my Signal Corporal commenced linking up the Batteries to me by telephone twice, on each occasion he was recalled owing to the switching of Group Commanders.
