## Page 3 — War Diary (Army Form C. 2118), Sheet No. 1

Heading: WAR DIARY or INTELLIGENCE SUMMARY [the words "INTELLIGENCE SUMMARY" are struck through] — August 1916 — 5th Canadian Machine Gun Company. "~ORIGINAL~ CONFIDENTIAL".

Remarks column: each entry is initialled "J.E. McC."

### 1.8.16 — Field

> Indirect fire carried out by four guns of this Company on the YIERSTRAATE-WYTSCHAETE road, LOUWAEGE - MARTINS FM, reserve lines and communication trenches in rear. A thorough reconnaissance of the BRIGADE front was made by the C.O. of this company, with the idea of locating new sites for emplacements to further the protection of our support line. Three sites were located, and work will commence at once. One emplacement will be built by the 26th BN, and two will be built by this Company.

### 2.8.16 — do

> 1500 Sandbags and material was carried in for new emplacement in left sector Indirect fire was carried out as usual, by 4 guns of this Company, covering the same ground as previous night. Germans retaliation very quiet for the past 24 hours.

### 3.8.16 — do

> Excavation commenced at DUCK POND and POPPY LANE emplacements, and forty I Beams carried up to each of these emplacements. Four guns of this Company used indirect fire covering the same ground as on previous nights.

### 4.8.16 — do

> At 9.00 P.M. a bomb was dropped from hostile aeroplane, and exploded in the Rest Billets of this Company, damaging the Q.M. Stores, and a great deal of clothing and equipment, 2 VICKERS MACHINE GUNS, storeman wounded and five horses wounded, one serious. Material for frame for DUCK POND, emplacement carried up and assembled, Excavation continued on POPPY LANE emplacement. Indirect fire carried out as usual by 4 guns, covering same area as previous.

### 5.8.16 — do

> Frame work at DUCK POND emplacement erected and loophole put in position, and 500 sand bags placed on the walls. ## Page 5 — War Diary (Army Form C. 2118), Sheet No. 3

Heading: WAR DIARY or INTELLIGENCE SUMMARY [the words "INTELLIGENCE SUMMARY" are struck through] — August 1916 — 5th Cdn In G. Co. "~ORIGINAL~ CONFIDENTIAL".

The 17.8.16 entry continues from Sheet 2 (no new date line):

> by the 6th CON. M.G. Co. at HILL TOP LOCK POST and A.P.1 was carried on with. Between 5.30 PM to 6.30 P.M. enemy bombarded our front line with shrapnel & H.E. Our artillery retaliated. The O.C & 2nd in Command of the 11th Cdn. In. G. Co. came up during the day to arrange about their company coming in. Capt Watson went to hospital tonight sick, suffering from Pyrexia, cause unknown. Lieut J. E Mc Corkell assumed command, & Lieut Grantham 2nd I/C.

Remarks column: each entry is initialled "J.E. McC."

### 18.8.16 — (")

> Capt Clark, Lieut. Ward, Samson, Murray, Burden, & junior, with two 1 & 2 [uncertain] of 11th Cdn In G. Co arrived for instruction this afternoon, were distributed to sections. Frames, 25 I beams, & other material carried to ARUNDEL, for new dugout. Roof at Y.4. dugout removed & rebuilt. 100 sandbags & 25 brick bags used in strengthening roof at Y.1 dugout. Work in left sector held up for lack of material. Lieut W H F. Ketcherson went to hospital tonight with PYREXIA.

### 19.8.16 — (")

> Very uneventful day. Four shells fell in VOORMEZEELE during the day. Frames erected and excavation started at ARUNDEL dugout. HILL TOP and LOCK POST saps advanced 2 feet & timbered. Casualties 1 Sgt of 11th Cdn In G Co wounded at HILL TOP

### 20.8.16 — (")

> Quiet. Work again held up in the left sector for lack of material. Excavation completed for ARUNDEL dugout. Y.1 dugout. 50 sandbags & 100 brick bags filled and placed completing same. Bricks obtained at MOATED GRANGE. Frames for new emplacement at N.6. b. 4.9. carried over from Hdqrs. Wall at Convent Wall emplacement propped up. Y.4 gun moved back to old position. Ten shells fell near Duck Pond. The junior section Officer with the remaining N. C. O. & men of the 11th Cdn In. G. Co arrived up to relieve the others.

## Page 6 — War Diary (Army Form C. 2118), Sheet No. 4

Heading: WAR DIARY or INTELLIGENCE SUMMARY [the words "INTELLIGENCE SUMMARY" are struck through] — August 1916 — 5th Cdn. In. G. Co. "~ORIGINAL~ CONFIDENTIAL".

Remarks column: each entry is initialled "J.E. McC."

### 21.8.16 — Field

> The I Beams for ARUNDEL all down there. LOCK POST and HILL TOP saps advanced 3 feet & timbered. At MIDDLESEX LANE emplacement 700 sand bags well filled, the frames put together, and corrugated iron carried in. Ten shells fell at LOCK POST two small ones within 10 feet of M.G. E. No damage. Otherwise all quiet. A gas alarm was sounded about 8.00 P.M some distance to the left. It was taken up by a town in VOORMEZEELE. 'Gas alert' was put into effect. Word received that Sgt SOSNOWSKI of 11th Cdn In G Co wounded on Aug 19th had died.

### 22.8.16 — (")

> The BURNHAM (ARUNDEL) dugout completed & two open air platforms built for gun. HILL TOP, LOCK POST, & A.P.1 advanced about 2 feet. At the MIDDLESEX LANE emplacement the frames were put in place, loopholes placed, I beams carried from dump & put into place. All four sides finished to roof. 600 sand bags used. No artillery near In G. E. Front line crumpled severely during day. The O.C. officer & men attached from 11th Cdn In. G. Co returned to their camp tonight during the afternoon 8 or 10 shells fell in VOORMEZEELE - 4.1. & 5.9. used. 80 men bathed at LACLYTE during the day.

### 23.8.16 — (")

> Word received from Capt Watson at Boulogne that he is being evacuated to England. The MIDDLESEX LANE emplacement is completed. 22 I beams were carried down & placed & the roof completed & sand bagged over. The new emplacement just in front of CONVENT WALL is also completed with the exception of pedestal. The 11th Cdn In G. Co arrived in at 10.00 P.M with guns & personnel, & took over from us. Our guns were sent out but our personnel remained in until tomorrow. One man wounded by rifle bullet in forehead, whilst on guard at LOWER OOSTHOEK.

### 25.8.16 — (")

> Busy day preparing for move tomorrow. Billets of Co billeted at RENINGHELST left in morning. Clothing parade held for men, & all Guns etc packed on limbers. Billeting party consisting of Lieut Grantham & 2 O.R left at 3.0 PM to arrange billets.

### 26.8.16 — (")

> First day of march to 2nd Army Training Area. Company moved off parade ground at 4.30 AM, and arrived at starting point (G.32.d. 6 1/2. 3 1/2) (SHEET 28) at 5.21 AM. Arrived at billets in STEENEVORDE at 9.00 AM (on the EECKE-STEENEVORDE road 1 mile south of STEENEVORDE, east side of road. - (SHEET 5A of HAZEBROUCK.) Accomodation at this place is very good. Health of men at finish of march excellent, and no casualties on march.

### 27.8.16 — (")

> The Company left billets for starting point at 6.00 AM, and arrived there at 7.30 AM (Road crossing immediately south of N in STEENEVORDE sheet 5A HAZEBROUCK), and arrived in billets (farm 1/4 m immediately south of second E in OEHTEZEELE, sheet 5A HAZEBROUCK) at 1.30 P.M. Accomodation here is all that can be desired. Officers, men & horses, are in the pink of condition. There were no casualties on the line of march.

### 28.8.16 — (")

> The company left billets at 6.00 A.M, and arrived at starting point (road crossing on the CASSEL - WATTEN road 1/2 m south of the last E in LEDERZEELE) at 8.40 AM. Arrived in billets (1 1/2 north of M in MOULLE, sheet. 5A HAZEBROUCK) at 2.20 P.M. Accomodation for men, horses & officers poor. The health of the officers, men & horses is of the best. There were no casualties on the line of march.

### 29.8.16 — Field

> The company proceeded in a down pour of rain to the training area at AUDENTHUN, and carried on with syllabus of training until 3.0 P.M. Arrived back at billets at 5.00 PM. All ranks thoroughly wet. The remainder of the time up to 12.00 midnight was taken up in drying clothes & belts, cleaning S.A.A. and guns.

### 30.8.16 — (")

> Owing to continual rain, todays training was carried on in billets

### 31.8.16 — (")

> The Company paraded from 6.00 AM until 6.20 AM for physical training. It paraded again at 7.45 AM and proceeded to training area at 8.00 AM, arriving there at 9.45 AM. Returned to billets at 4.00 P.M. The Ross rifle was taken from this unit, and we are now armed with the Short LEE-ENFIELD.

## Notes

- **Coverage:** Entries run 1–31 August 1916 with no date gaps. Diary text is 6 pages (Sheets 1–6); no appendices this month. Total 9 pages transcribed (cover, contents sheet, 6 diary sheets, plus the blank reverse of Sheet 6 as page 9).
- **Officers named:** Capt. S. W. Watson (O.C. until 17 August, when he went to hospital at the front with pyrexia of unknown cause; evacuated from Boulogne to England, 23 August); Lieut. J. E. McCorkell (assumed command 17 August; signs the month); Lieut. Grantham (2nd in command from 17 August); Lieut. W. H. F. Ketcherson (to hospital with pyrexia, 18 August); Lieut. Sinclair (left up in the line until the brigade was relieved, 24 August); of the 11th Cdn Inf Bde M.G. Coy — Capt. Clark, Lieut. Ward, Samson, Murray and Burden (arrived for instruction, 18 August), and the O.C. and 2nd in Command of the 11th (visited 17 August).
- **Other ranks named:** Sgt Sosnowski, 11th Cdn Inf Bde M.G. Coy (wounded 19 August, died of wounds, reported 21 August); one sergeant of the 11th wounded at Hill Top, 19 August (unnamed). All other casualties are unnamed.
- **Equipment:** Two Vickers machine guns damaged and a storeman and five horses wounded when a hostile aeroplane bomb hit the rest billets on 4 August; the Ross rifle was withdrawn from the unit on 31 August and replaced by the Short Lee-Enfield; Duck Pond emplacement completed with a solid concrete top (6 August); Middlesex Lane emplacement completed (23 August).
- **Movements:** In the line in the Ypres–Yserstraat (Yierstraate) sector at the start of August; relieved by the 4th Cdn M.G. Coy on 7 August and back to rest billets at Reninghelst; back into the line on the Voormezeele front on the night of 16/17 August, relieving the 6th Bde M.G. Coy; relieved by the 11th Cdn Inf Bde M.G. Coy on the night of 23/24 August; then a three-day march (26–28 August) to the 2nd Army Training Area via Steenevorde, Oehtezeele and Moulle, training at Audenthun on 29–31 August.
- **Discipline:** None recorded in August 1916.
- **Casualties:** 4 August — 1 storeman wounded and 5 horses wounded (1 seriously) by an aeroplane bomb in the rest billets; 19 August — 1 sergeant of the 11th Cdn Inf Bde M.G. Coy wounded at Hill Top (Sgt Sosnowski, who died of his wounds, reported 21 August); 23 August — 1 man wounded by a rifle bullet in the forehead while on guard at Lower Oosthoek. No deaths among the 5th's own men recorded in August.
- **Anomalies:** (1) The 5 August entry ends mid-sentence ("Indirect fire by four guns, covering same ground, as") with no continuation on Sheet 2, which opens with the 6 August entry; transcribed as written. (2) The 17 August entry begins on Sheet 2 and continues onto Sheet 3 without a new date line; the same for the 24 August entry (Sheets 4–5). (3) In the 24 August entry the word "returned" is struck through and replaced by "withdrew" ("Our personnel withdrew during afternoon"); transcribed with the correction. (4) The 18 August entry's "with two 1 & 2 of 11th Cdn In G. Co" is not clearly readable; flagged [uncertain]. (5) The signing line at the foot of Sheet 6 reads "O.C. 5-7th Con. M.G. Coy." — transcribed as written; it appears to be a slip for "5th". (6) The 22 August entry reads "No artillery near In G. E." and "The O.C. officer & men attached from 11th Cdn In. G. Co returned to their camp tonight during the afternoon 8 or 10 shells fell in VOORMEZEELE - 4.1. & 5.9. used." — transcribed as written. (7) The 21 August entry says the gas alarm "was taken up by a town in VOORMEZEELE"; transcribed as written. (8) The unit is styled "5th Canadian Machine Gun Company" on the cover and "5th Cdn In G. Co." on the diary sheets; both transcribed as written. (9) The 25 August entry reads "Billets of Co billeted at RENINGHELST left in morning"; transcribed as written.
