## Page 3 — War Diary (Army Form C. 2118), Sheet No. 1

Heading: WAR DIARY or INTELLIGENCE SUMMARY [the words "INTELLIGENCE SUMMARY" are struck through] — 5th Canadian Machine Gun Coy. "DECEMBER 1916" is written across the heading.

Reference map line at the head of the sheet: "REFERENCE MAP — FRANCE, SHEETS 36c S.W. AND 36B S.E."

Remarks column: each entry is initialled "C.V.G."

### 1/12/16 — Field

> Anti-Aircraft mount and rest were erected and placed near the CROSS ROADS position. Laying of trench mats from COOKER ALLEY through LINK TRENCH to gun emplacement, completed this date. A shelter was also built at this emplacement for ammunition tripod etc. Work was carried on an stairway at left Section Hqrs. Frames were made and put into position. Five men worked ten hours at this work. A platform for the ammunition box was built into the post at the anti-aircraft position. Work was commenced on new M.G. Emplacement at M.13 d 4.7 this date. 150 sandbags filled and frames put together. Indirect Fire by three guns was carried out as usual from 5.30 P.M. to 9.30 P.M. 4000 rounds per gun being fired. Scarcely any hostile M.G. Fire. Capt J.H. McCorkell left for LONDON at 2 P.M. to proceed to CANADA on leave. Progress in N.C.O's class. Machine Gun mounted at H.Q.RS. for use against hostile aircraft. Work continued at M.G. Emplacement at M.13 d 4.7, 100 sandbags

## Notes

- **Coverage:** Entries run 1–31 December 1916 with no date gaps. 16 pages in total: cover, typed contents sheet, and 14 diary sheets (Sheets No. 1–14), matching the contents sheet's "Diary Text — 14 Pages".
- **Form change:** Unlike November, where "INTELLIGENCE SUMMARY" was left standing on Sheets No. 8–14, in December it is struck through on every sheet. The reference map for the month is given on Sheet No. 1: France, sheets 36c S.W. and 36B S.E.
- **Officers named:** Capt. S. W. Watson (O.C., signs the month; cut the nightly indirect fire to 2,000 rounds per gun on 4 Dec); Capt. J. H. McCorkell (left for London on 1 Dec to proceed to Canada on leave); Lieut C. V. Grantham (initials the entries "C.V.G."; returned from the Machine Gun Course at Camiers on 16 Dec with two other ranks); Lieut W. H. F. Ketcheson (returned to the company 13 Dec); Lieut Creighton (completed the kitchen 3 Dec; fired a belt at an enemy aeroplane 15 Dec); Lieut Boa (bombing instruction to "D" Section 4 Dec and "C" Section 5 Dec); Lieut Taylor (practised stoppages during indirect fire 12 Dec); Lieut Hobson (supervised the new M.G. emplacement work 15 Dec); Lieut McBurney (finished the work on Section Headquarters 10 Dec).
- **Other ranks named:** None by name in December 1916. Two unnamed other ranks returned with Lieut Grantham from Camiers on 16 Dec.
- **Equipment:** New M.G. emplacement built at M.13 d 4.7 (begun 1 Dec, near completion 9 Dec); a second new indirect-fire emplacement at M.13 d 4.2 (in use by 21 Dec); anti-aircraft mounts erected at the Cross Roads position, at Headquarters and in the left sector, with a machine gun mounted at Headquarters against hostile aircraft; new box respirators already in use (gas blankets installed at dugout entrances and Section Headquarters); 20 steel rails and 24 steel rails used in emplacements; hundreds of sandbags filled and placed daily; corrugated iron and 5 ft and 9-course brick roofing on emplacements; one gun barrel worn out after firing 11,000 rounds (11 Dec); a gun mounted on the slag pile at Fosse 5 on 25 Dec.
- **Movements:** The company remained in the Angres–Bully sector throughout December, in the line and in reserve at Bully; inter-company reliefs on 20 and 26 Dec. No move out of the sector.
- **Discipline:** None recorded in December 1916.
- **Casualties:** None recorded in December 1916. The company was shelled repeatedly (including gas shelling of Bully and Corons d'Aix on the night of 27/28 Dec, with gas prevalent around company headquarters and billets), a "Toffee Apple" landing 15 ft from a dugout door on 3 Dec, and a fishtail landing 15 ft in front of Cap du Pont emplacement on 22 Dec, but no casualties are reported.
- **Anomalies:** (1) The 11 Dec entry has one word before "installed at LEMCO and BULLY" that is illegible, marked [uncertain]; the word "Trench" in the same entry is struck through. (2) The 16 Dec entry has the word "re" inserted above the line in "has been rebuilt", and the word "nil" after "Enemy retaliation" is written small. (3) The 24 Dec entry has the word "rounds" inserted above the line after "1000". (4) The 21 Dec entry gives the indirect-fire hours as "from 11 P.M. to 1 P.M." (as written). (5) The 30 Dec entry ends without a full stop ("considerable work will be needed"). (6) Capt. Watson signs as O.C., but the entries are initialled "C.V.G." (Lieut Grantham) throughout, as in November when McCorkell initialled while Watson commanded.
