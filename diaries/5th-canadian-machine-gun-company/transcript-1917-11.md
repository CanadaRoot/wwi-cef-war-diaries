### 1/11/17 — Field

> Day was fine. Parades were carried out as follows:— 9. to 10. A.M. Physical Training. 10 to 11 A.M. Squad Drill. 11 A.M. to 12.30 P.M. Route March. At 8 P.M. G.S.Wagon and four gun limbers left for POTIJZE under Lt. MacLaren. O.O. No. 51 Attached.

### 2/11/17 — Field

> Day fine. Reveille 4 A.M. Breakfast 4.30 A.M. In accordance with 2nd Can. Divl. M.G.Battn. O.O. No. 53 the company moved from LABREARDE to the POTIJZE Area. The Transport with Lt. Creighton moved off at 6.30 A.M. and arrived at the new area at 4 P.M. The Company less transport, had a haversack lunch and fell in in full marching order at 11.30 A.M. At 11.45 A.M. Company moved off arriving at CAESTRE at 12.45 P.M. where we entrained. The four companies of the 2nd Cdn. M.G.Battn. were on this train. Train moved off from CAESTRE at 1.45 P.M. and proceeded via GODEIWAERSVELDE and POPERINGHE to the ASYLUM at YPRES where we detrained and marched through YPRES to lines at 1.3.d.1.4. arriving at 3.15 P.M. We took over lines of 16th Cdn. M.G.Coy. who left following day for rest area. Camp consisted 8 Bell tents, 16 Bivouacks and various other shelters horses being in the open. At 5 P.M. "D" Sect. consisting of 1 sergeant, 1 corporal and 24 men under Lt. H.A.McBurney moved off for the line where they relieved four barrage guns of 16th Cdn. M.G.Coy. under Lieut. Stinson at SEINE CORNER. (D.16.d.0.80) relief was reported complete by 9.45 P.M. and reported to Capt. Grantham, barrage group commander at TYNE COT. (D.17.a.45.20) Casualties "NIL" Company O.O.No.31 attached.

### 3/11/17 — Field

> Day bright. At 5.05. A.M. an S.O.S. went up. "D" Sect. 4 barrage guns opened fire at once at 5.45 A.M. and 6.15 A.M. the S.O.S. was again sent up by the battalions of the 4th Cdn. Inf. Bde. holding the front line, On both these occasions our guns fired on S.O.S. lines. Several of our guns had fired 350 rds. S.A.A. before the S.O.S. rocket had reached the ground. S.A.A. expended 13000 rds. Enemy shelling was very severe during the early morning slackening at 8 A.M. About 75 — 8" shells fell in the vicinity of SEINE CORNER Enemy aircraft were continuously flying low over our lines during the afternoon. Between 5 P.M. and 7 P.M. enemy placed down a heavy barrage and no direct hits were made on trench occupied by barrage guns. During this bombardment 1018356 Pte Douville G. and 1018413 Pte Davies T.E. attached carriers, were hit and both died of their wounds while being carried to the dressing station. During the night 4000 rds. S.A.A. were fired on PASSCHENDAELE CROSS ROADS. Casualties 2 men killed.

### 4/11/17 — Field

> Rain fell during the early morning. At 5.A.M. Lt. Creighton left transport lines with a pack train of 35 animals loaded with 50.000 rds. S.A.A. which was dumped at SEINE CORNER. Train returned at 8.15 A.M. having suffered no casualties, although enemy shelled road in the vicinity of ZONNEBEK continuously
>
> One crew of "C" Sect. under Acc. Cpl. Berrick was sent up with orders to report to Lieut. McBurney. This crew was placed at D.16.d.55.85 and was used for Anti Aircraft work, firing a thousand rounds at Enemy aircraft during the day. One gun of "D" Sect. fired 500 rds. S.A.A. at an enemy plane which appeared to land in rear of PASSCHENDAELE.

### 4/11/17 — Field (continued)

> During the day in accordance with orders received from Capt. Grantham, Lt. McBurney took a party of 8 men to D.11.c.90.30 and commenced work on a new 8 gun battery position. The party was only at work 15 minutes before being forced to stop owing to enemy shelling. Between 9 P.M. and 12 P.M. 4000 rds. S.A.A. were fired on PASSCHENDAELE CROSS ROADS.
>
> In accordance with 2nd Cdn. Divl. M.G.Battn. O.O. No. 55 para. 6 "B" Section less 1 gun under Lt. Hobson relieved 3 guns of the 4th Cdn. M.G.Coy. as follows :— 1 gun at D.12.a.45.00; 2 Guns at D.12.c.30.70; 1 gun of "A" Sect. took up position at D.12.a.30.90 This gun came under the command of Sgt. Tingley, 6th Cdn. M.G.Coy. Relief was completed at 10 P.M. All of these guns are Mobile and come under the command of Major Pearce, Mobile Group Commander.
>
> During the afternoon enemy fired 8-5.2" high velocity shells in an about our transport lines. Casualties "Nil"

### 5/11/17 — Field

> Day fine. Between 11.30 P.M. 4th inst. and 4.30 this morning enemy fired about 70 rds. of high velocity 5.2" in and about our transport lines. During this shelling the following casualties occurred, 1051152 Pte. Larson A.P. Wounded and died later at dressing station. 288990 Pte. Campbell E. wounded. 240078 Pte. Dills C.E. wounded. 415658 Pte. Landry J. wounded.

### 5/11/17 — Field (continued)

> 70022 Pte. Wallace A.G. wounded remained on duty. Two saddle and two light draught horses were killed and one saddle and eleven light draught horses wounded. Enemy aircraft was very active commencing at daybreak and remaining so throughout the hours of daylight. Pack train of 25 animals under S-t. Christie carried 50,000 rds. S.A.A to SEINE CORNER. Enemy shelled the whole area heavily from 5 A.M. to 8 A.M. and during the day shelled area about "B" Section with heavies. In "D" Section two men were wounded, viz:— 240653 Pte. Coaton A.C. 401498 Pte. Barrett H.
>
> Lce. Cpl. Sillers and 8 men of "D" Section worked all day on new battery position, while the remainder, except those on duty at the guns carried 33000 rds. S.A.A. to new positions.
>
> At 4 P.M. Lt. Gibson left the transport lines with two crews of A section, and one crew of the 6th Cdn. M.G.Coy. These crews took up their positions with the 26th Battn. "D" Section under Lt. McBurney moved and took up new position at D.11.c.30.30 Capt. McCorkell went up to advanced 5th Cdn. Inf. Bde. Hqrs., this afternoon, to act as M.G.Liaison Officer, during these operations.

The 26th Cdn. Inf. Battn. of 5th Cdn. Inf. Bde. attacked on the right. The 3 crews under Lt. Gibson advanced with the first wave of the 26th Battn. and took up positions as follows:—

### 6/11/17 — Field (continued)

> (cont) 2 guns at D.6.d.50.65; 1 gun at D.6.d.55.05
>
> These guns suffered no casualties in reaching their objectives, but shortly afterwards Lt. Gibson was wounded by shrapnel in the jaw and over the eye. Sgt. Chamberland then took charge and did excellent work till relieved.
>
> The enemy fire was intense and continued throughout the day. In "D" Section 1051072 Pte. Green H. was killed, and 721105 Pte. Cook P.R. was wounded. During the day barrage guns responded to 3 S.O.S. calls. These guns fired 60,000 rds. S.A.A. during barrage and S.O.S. calls. At night 6000 rds. S.A.A. was expended in harassing fire. From 8 P.M. to midnight enemy artillery fire was very heavy. "B" section on the right was heavily shelled but escaped casualties till 3 P.M. when the right position was practically destroyed, both guns being blown out of the trench. 175819 Pte. Osborn A.J. being killed. Two of the crew were buried but were soon dug out by the splendid work of 175336 Pte. Wilton R.J. and 706378 Pte. Bruce R.B. who deserve great credit.
>
> The left crew suffered heavily in casualties. 70318 Pte. Butler T. was killed and 238095 Pte. McGrouther R. and 922821 pte. Drysdale were wounded.

### 7/11/17 — Field

> Day fine. Pack train of 25 animals under Sgt. Christie carried 50,000 rds. S.A.A. to SEINE CORNER in the early morning. Heavy shelling of our positions by the enemy artillery, especially his retaliation for our protective barrage at 5.30 A.M. 171161 Pte. Forrester H.S. of "D" section was wounded and 1051474 Pte. Doobie [uncertain] H.J. & 1018532 Pte. Humphrey of "A" Section were wounded in advance of PASSCHENDAELE. 1051391 Pte. Bills H.W. an attached man was killed at TYNE COT.

### 7/11/17 — Field (continued)

> (con) of "A" Section were wounded in advance of PASSCHENDAELE. 1051391 Pte. Bills H.W. an attached man was killed at TYNE COT.

### 8/11/17 — Field

> Day fine. Pack train of 25 animals under Lt. Creighton carried 50,000 rds S.A.A. to SEINE CORNER. Enemy shelling was heavy and "D" Section battery position was hit several times and 204244 Pte. Pattison S. and 542459 Pte. Collypitts G were killed. During the night Lt. MacLaren with two guns of "C" Section and 1 gun of 6th Cdn. M.G.Coy. relieved the 3 mobile guns of "B" Section under Lt. Hobson. On the way in the relieving crews were caught in an enemy barrage and the two guns of "C" Section were destroyed and 175364 Pte. Watkins H. was severely wounded. Relief was completed by 6 P.M. [uncertain] and "B" Section moved back to transport lines. The 3 guns under Sgt. Chamberland were relieved by 3 guns of the 14th Cdn. M.G.Coy. Relief was complete by 8 P.M. and these crews moved back to the transport lines.

### 9/11/17 — Field

> Day cool and misty. Orders were issued for "D" Section to move forward a thousand yards and take up new battery positions at D.11.b.30.30 During the day Lt. McBurney went forward, chose a position and had a party dig a trench 4 and emplacements. The guns guns were put in positions at dusk. During the night S.A.A. was taken up. The guns under Lt. MacLaren were shelled continuously and the following men became casualties. Killed:— 175181 Pte. Hutchings. W.J. 141917 Pte. Hardiment S.P. 123051 [uncertain] Pte. Miles T.C.

### 9/11/17 — Field (continued)

> (cont) Wounded:— 739736 Pte. Tobicoe W. 636597 Pte. Gilroy G. 1001087 Pte. Herter E.F. 636920 Pte. Hindle O.A.

### 10/11/17 — Field

> Rain during the day. At 6.05 the 4th Cdn. Inf. Bde. of 2nd Cdn. Div. in conjunction with 1st Cdn. Div. and 1st Imperial Division under cover of an intense barrage attacked the enemy's position. The 1st and 2nd Cdn. Div. gained all their objectives while the 1st Imperial Division on the left was held up. The 4th Cdn. Inf. Bde. did not meet with any resistance but the 1st Cdn. Div. met with considerable. Our barrage guns fired 14,000 rds. S.A.A. during attack and 4,500 rds. S.A.A. on the S.O.S. call at 3 P.M. During the afternoon rain fell heavily and positions were nearly washed away.

### 11/11/17 — Field

> Day fine. The 4 crews "D" Sect. were withdrawn at 7 A.M. and reported back to the transport lines. Our guns, tripods and 22 belts and boxes were taken back by mule train. The 3 guns under Lt. MacLaren were relieved by three guns of the 11th Cdn. M.G.Coy. and withdrew to Transport Lines. Arriving back about midnight. The 16th Cdn. M.G.Coy., moved into our transport lines this afternoon. The 4th Cdn. Div. is relieving the 2nd Cdn. Div. in the line. Relief to be completed by morning of the 13th instant.

### 12/11/17 — Field

> In accordance with 2nd Cdn. Divl. M.G.Battn. O.O.No.60 table "A" & "B". The transport left the POTIJZE AREA at 6 A.M. and proceeded via VLAEMERTINGHE, BUSSEBOOM, ABEELE, GODEWAERSVELDE to EECKE where they parked for the night. The company less transport moved off from the POTIJZE Area at 11.45 A.M. and entrained at YPRES STATION at 12.30 P.M. and proceeded by train to BRANDHOEK where we detrained and marched to camp of tents at H.14.c.10 (On POPERINGHE & RENNINGHELST ROAD.)

### 13/11/17 — Field

> Day fine Transport moved off from EECKE at 8 A.M. and proceeded via HAZEBROUCK TO ST. FLORIS arriving there at 1.30 P.M. The company fell in at 8.30 A.M. and at 9 A.M. moved off to the Embussing point which was near OUDERDOM on the VLAEMERTINGHE—RENNINGHELST ROAD. We embussed at 11.30 A.M. and convoy moved off at 12.40 P.M. We proceeded to ST. FLORIS, via RENNINGHELST, BAILLEUL, HAZEBROUCK and ST. VENANT, debussing at 4.30 P.M. Lt. Hobson and Sgt. Chamberland had gone forward as a billetting party and secured very good billets for the company.

### 14/11/17 — Field

> Day fine. Transport moved off from ST. FLORIS at 8 A.M. and arrived at LAPOUGNAY at 12.30 P.M. The company embussed at 9.30 A.M. and proceeded to LAPOUGNAY when we debussed at 12 NOON. Muster parade was held at 3 P.M. From 3.30 to 5 P.M. sections cleaned guns etc., and repacked limbers.

### 15/11/17 — Field

> Day fine. Transport moved off from LAPOUGNAY at 8 A.M. and arrived at MAISNIL BOUCH E at 1.30 P.M. Company embussed at 2 P.M. and arrived at MAISNIL BOUCH at 5 P.M. Route followed MARLES LE MINES, HULLICOURT, BARLIN, HERSIN, AND GRAND SERVINS.

### 16/11/17 — Field

> At MAISNIL BOUCHE. 9 to 10 A.M. Physical Training. 10 to 11 A.M. Cleaning of guns and equipment. Company was supposed to have moved to AUX RIEZ but this was cancelled till further order.

### 17/11/17 — Field

> Received orders to move to AUX RIEZ in the afternoon but this was again cancelled owing to the 27th Cdn. Battn. still being in our billets. Guns, equipment and limbers were cleaned and all the limber were repacked.

### 18/11/17 — Field

> Orders received to move to camp at AUX RIEZ. Company paraded at 1 P.M. with transport and moved off at 1.15 P.M. arriving at AUX RIEZ at 4.30 P.M. Camp in a very filthy condition and dug-outs falling down. A fatigue was immediately detailed to put the camp into a sanitary condition.

### 19/11/17 — Field

> Clothing parade was held for the whole company in the morning and a muster parade and roll at 2.30 P.M. Company was then detailed off in small working parties to re-build the dugout and get the camp in good condition.

### 20/11/17 — Field

> All guns and equipment were inspected by the Divl. Armourer and five tripods were condemned and replaced by new ones. The 30 carriers that were attached to this unit from the C.C.R.C. were returned. An inspection was held by the commanding officer of all harness and saddlery which was found clean and in good condition.

### 21/11/17 — Field

> The company carried on with instructional work as per the following table:— Physical training 6.30 A.M. to 7 A.M. Care of Arms 9 to 10 A.M. Gun drill 10 to 11 A.M. Limber packing 11 A.M. to 12 NOON. Pay parade was held in the afternoon.

### 22/11/17 — Field

> In accordance with 2nd Cdn. Divl. M.G.Battn. O.O.No. [uncertain] this unit is now in battalion reserve and "C" Section moved forward into position as shewn on O.O. No. 55 [uncertain] copy attached. > A new emplacement was completed at T.20.c.20.50 and 10,000 rds. S.A.A. carried over and put in position and the whole completely camouflaged. In accordance with 2nd Cdn. Divl. M.G.Battn. O.O.No. 63 "C" Section of the company was relieved as per operation order No. 56 copy attached and this company comes into divisional reserve.

### 27/11/17 — Field

> The company paraded to the baths after which "C" Section paraded for Xmas pay. Owing to heavy rain no other parades were held.

### 28/11/17 — Field

> The following syllabus of training was carried out:— 6.30 to 7 A.M. Physical Training. 9 A.M. Company parade. 9.30 to 10.30 A.M. Lecture on "Indirect Fire" 10.30 A.M. to 12 NOON Fire direction and fire control. 2 P.M. Company parade. 2.30 P.M. to 4 P.M. Lecture on "Discipline and the Army Acts"

### 29/11/17 — Field

> The stables occupied by the horses of the company were all closed in by corrugated iron and a brick fire place was built in the officers' mess. 2 new dug outs were also built and the driveway from the road to the transport line was repaired.

### 30/11/17 — Field

> In accordance with 2nd Cdn. Divl. M.G.Battn. O.O.No.63 This company relieved the 14th Cdn. M.G.Coy., as per O.O.No. 57 copy attached. The company paraded for the trenches at 7.15 P.M. and moved off at 8.30 P.M. Relief being completed at 7.30 P.M. Relief would have been completed much sooner only "B" Section came under enemy artillery fire and two gun teams were put out of faction, 2 men being killed instantly and 5 seriously wounded, so I borrowed two guns with complete equipment and one gun team from the 14th Cdn. M.G.Company and completed relief. The guns equipment and gun team were returned to the 14th Cdn. M.G.Coy the following night.
