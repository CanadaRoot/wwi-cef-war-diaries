## Notes

- **Coverage:** Entries run 1–31 January 1916 with no date gaps. The contents sheet states "Diary Text — 4 Pages", which matches the four diary-text pages (pages 3–6).
- **Officers named:** Lt A. J. S. Mills (Adjutant, 5th Canadian Inf. Bde. M. G. Coy; signs the diary on page 6). No other officer is named in January.
- **Other ranks named:** None named in January. Two Canadian Army Medical Corps men (attached) are mentioned on 27 January, unnamed, detailed as water detail.
- **Equipment:** Three general service limbers and nine light draught horses received 3 January; two riding horses received 6 January; two riding horses changed at the Cavalry Depot 21 January; three general service limbers, one cook's cart, one water cart and nine light-draught horses received 27 January. Machine gun emplacements reconnoitred and ranged from 14 January; an indirect-fire position was picked 15 January, decided upon 22 January and prepared 28–29 January. A new telephone line to 5th Canadian Infantry Brigade Headquarters was laid 26 January.
- **Movements:** The company moved on 5 January to billets lately occupied by the 49th Canadian Battalion (map reference M.23.d.6.8, Sheet No 28, Series "B", Belgium and France, scale 1:40,000). It remained in the Locre / Westoutre area for the rest of the month: baths at Westoutre, church parades at Locre, instructional firing at Scherpenberg. Huts were erected 7 January and demolished 8 January, the lumber being returned to 5th Canadian Infantry Brigade Headquarters.
- **Discipline:** None recorded in January 1916.
- **Casualties:** None recorded in January 1916.
- **Anomalies:** (1) The diary is a training record for the whole month — no front-line action; nearly every entry is "General instructional work" and/or work on horse lines. (2) The Place column reads "Field" on every page, with no more specific place given; Locre, Westoutre and Scherpenberg appear only inside the entry text. (3) The remarks-column initials are "A. J. S. M. Lieut" — the initials and surname initial of Lt A. J. S. Mills, Adjutant; his hurried signature on page 6 at first glance reads "A. E. J. Mill", but the February diary signature and the Appendix A signature (February) both read clearly "A. J. S. Mills". (4) The spelling "accomodation" (6 January) and "reconnoisance" (15 January) are as written in the original. (5) Page 7 is the reverse of the final diary sheet, used as a back cover; its text is show-through from page 6 plus the handwritten title and "Original for War Office".
