### 13/2/17 — Field (continued)

> The Transport lines etc. are located in ECOIVRES. The relief was carried out in good order. On the whole the dugouts in this section of the line are very good. They are deep dugouts each having two or more entrances.

### 14/2/17 — (ditto)

> The day passed off quietly. One O.R. was slightly wounded in the arm by a bit of shell. One officer (LT. BOA) was taken sick & sent out. There are 103 O.R.'s attached from the different Bns. in the Brigade for working party at the M.G. FORT. One officer is attached from 7th Cdn M.G. Coy to assist in supervising the work at that place.

### 15/2/17 — (ditto)

> Nothing of unusual interest occurred during the day. while retaliating for T.M. fire, the enemy dropped two whizz-bangs in succession on to roof of dugout for No.3 position partly wrecking the dugout. Rations are rather poor.

### 16/2/17 — (ditto)

> The day passed uneventfully a few whizz-bangs dropped in the vicinity of NOS. 1 & 3 positions. One gun was started on Indirect Fire 1000 rds being fired between 7 P.M. & 10 P.M. on PETIT. VIMY. There was no retaliation.

## Notes

- **Coverage:** Entries run 1–28 February 1917 with no date gaps. 13 pages in total: cover, typed contents sheet, and 11 diary sheets (Sheets No. 1–11), matching the contents sheet's "Diary Text — 11 Pages".
- **Form change:** Unlike January, where "INTELLIGENCE SUMMARY" was left standing on Sheets No. 10–12, in February it is struck through on every sheet. The Place column is left blank on Sheets No. 8 and 9. New reference map lines appear: "REF MAP 1/10,000 LENS. 11." on Sheet No. 1, and "REF MAPS VIMY 36 C S.W. 3, 1/10,000. ROLLENCOURT 51 B N.W. 1 1/10,000" on Sheet No. 4.
- **Officers named:** Major S. W. Watson (O.C.; on 10 days' leave in England 3–17 Feb, resumed command 17 Feb; inspected dugouts and emplacements with Lieut Boa on 28 Feb); Lieut C. V. Grantham (continues to initial the entries "C.V.G." and signs the month "for O.C."); Lieut Boa (taken sick and sent out 14 Feb; back on duty inspecting with Watson 28 Feb); Lieut Taylor (reported back from the map reading course at Pernes 20 Feb); Lieut Hobson (began work on Right Section Headquarters 26 Feb, rushing the dugout 27 Feb); Lieut McBurney (left 26 Feb to attend the Machine Gun Course at Camiers); the Divisional Commander (inspected the company and transport at Bruay 7 Feb); the Brigadier (inspected the transport 5 Feb).
- **Other ranks named:** None by name in February 1917. One other rank was slightly wounded in the arm by a shell fragment on 14 Feb — the month's only recorded casualty. 103 other ranks from the brigade's battalions were attached for the working party at the Machine Gun Fort (14 Feb).
- **Equipment:** Anti-aircraft guns mounted at company headquarters and at No. 14 Position in Chassery Trench (19–20 Feb), with an anti-aircraft mount installed at No. 2 Position (27 Feb); several new indirect-fire positions built in the La Folie sector since the takeover; the Machine Gun Fort in the left sector under construction, checked by the withdrawal of its working party and the Pioneers on 25 Feb; 10 guns positioned for the contemplated 4th Canadian Division raid (24 Feb), with 4 guns attached from the 4th Canadian Machine Gun Company.
- **Movements:** The company stayed in billets at Bruay until 12 Feb, marched to Ecoivres (via Houdain, Rebreuve, Gauchin-Legal, Chamblain-l'Abbé), and on 13 Feb relieved the 7th Canadian Machine Gun Company in the La Folie sector of the line, taking over 16 gun positions (Nos. 1–16) with company headquarters at Neuville St Vaast and transport lines at Ecoivres. Inter-company reliefs in the line on 19 and 22 Feb.
- **Discipline:** None recorded in February 1917.
- **Casualties:** One other rank slightly wounded in the arm by a shell fragment on 14 February 1917. No other casualties recorded, despite frequent enemy trench-mortar and shell fire and 15–20 rifle grenades falling near No. 5 gun on 19 Feb.
- **Anomalies:** (1) The 1 Feb entry gives the starting point as "([uncertain] Cross road 1/2 mile east of N. in DIVION)" — the same crossroads is written "Main Cross Roads" in the 9 Feb entry; the first word is hard to read on 1 Feb. (2) The 1 Feb entry gives the billets' arrival time as "1.30 A.M." and the starting-point time as "9.30 1/2 A.M." (as written). (3) The 3 Feb entry ends without a full stop ("left for 10 days leave in ENGLAND"). (4) The 6 Feb entry ends mid-sentence ("and") and continues on the next sheet; the 9 Feb entry also ends mid-sentence. (5) The 10 Feb entry reads "The situation was presumed the unusual." (as written). (6) The 17 Feb entry repeats "The" ("The The right boundary"). (7) The 13 Feb position table gives no map references for Positions No. 12 and No. 13, and the Reserve Section map references are faint. (8) The 23 Feb entry repeats the total ("Total 8000 rounds expended between 6 P.M. and 11. P.M. 8000 rds retaliation nil."), although the individual gun totals do add to 8,000. (9) The 28 Feb entry has two words after "occupied by the Coy." that are hard to read, marked [uncertain]. (10) Lieut Grantham signs the month "for O.C." rather than Major Watson.
