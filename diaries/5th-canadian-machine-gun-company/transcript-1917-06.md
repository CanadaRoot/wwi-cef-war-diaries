### 1/6/17 — VIMY STATION

> The day was exceptionally quiet. Work was done, deepening trenches and on latrines at Gun positions. The 5th Bde was relieved by the 3rd Cdn. Inf. Bde. during the evening, but this Company remained in the line another day. Officers & O.R's of the 2nd Cdn. M. G. Coy. arrived in the trenches with rations, in order to spend a day at gun positions, before taking over the line

### 2/6/17 — (do)

> Some Aerial activity by enemy planes about 8.30 P.M. our guns (B. sect.) engaged them, expending 2,000 rds. The 2nd Cdn. M.G. Co. arrived in the line to relieve this Coy. during the evening according to 5th Cdn. M. G. Coy. O.O. No. 8 (see Appendix 1 — attached).

## Notes

- **Coverage:** Entries run 1–30 June 1917 with no date gaps. Diary text is 9 pages (Sheets I–IX), matching the contents sheet ("Diary Text — 9 Pages"); appendices 1–2 are Operation Order No. 8 and the training syllabus. The contents sheet describes the appendices as "Orders issued by 5th Cdn. M.G. Co., (undated)".
- **Officers named:** Major S. W. Watson (O.C.; awarded the Distinguished Service Order, London Gazette supplement dated 2 June 1917; detailed as acting D.M.G.O. 3–11 June; left 29 June for indirect-fire demonstrations at Camiers and a 17-day course at the M.G. School there); Major Goodfellow (D.M.G.O., returned from leave 11 June, when Watson resumed command); Lieut. Grantham (assumed command of the company 3 June and signed the diary as O.C. on 30 June); Lieut. Creighton; Lieut. McCorkell (Quartermaster); Lieut. Gray; Maj. Gen. Burstall, C.B., C.M.G., A.D.C. (Divisional Commander, inspected the company 26 June); the G.O.C. 5th Brigade (addressed the officers of the unit and the 5th Cdn. T.M.B. on 13 June).
- **Other ranks named:** Sgt. Cooper (returned from the M.G. School, Camiers, 9 June); Sgt. Young (left for the M.G. School, 9 June); Sgt. Major Lindsay (returned from course, 30 June); Ptes. Moss and McKay J., both scouts (won the map-reading cross-country race at the 5th Bde. Sports Meeting, 20 June).
- **Equipment:** Machine guns and gun equipment (inspected 7 June); limbers (two entered in the M.G. Coy limber turn-out competition, winning 1st and 3rd at the Divisional Horse Show, 14 June); revolvers (drill and range practice); Box Respirators (tested 29–30 June); Very lights (syllabus item 75).
- **Movements:** The company was relieved in the line by the 2nd Cdn. M.G. Co. on the night of 2/3 June and moved to rest billets at Gouy Servins (via Villers-aux-Bois) on 3 June, remaining there for the rest of the month. Route march of its own on 13 June via Bouvigny, Boyeffles, Coupigny, Petit Servins and Gouy Servins.
- **Discipline:** None recorded in June 1917.
- **Casualties:** None recorded in June 1917.
- **Anomalies:** (1) The contents sheet describes the appendices as undated; Operation Order No. 8 carries no date in the copy. (2) Page 13 is the reverse of Operation Order No. 8; most of it is carbon-copy bleed-through and not legibly readable — only the distribution list is transcribed. (3) The training-syllabus date/hour grid (pages 14–16) is not transcribed cell by cell: the handwritten hour blocks are small and only partly legible. (4) Page 17 is a loose handwritten sheet, apparently a football team list, most of which is not legibly readable. (5) The diary is signed by Lieut. C. V. Grantham as O.C., Major Watson being away at Camiers. (6) The spelling of the rest camp varies between "AUX REITZ" and "AUX RIETZ" in the diary. (7) In the 3 June entry the word "reached" is inserted above the line. (8) Syllabus item 60 ("Care of gas helmets and anti-gas appliances") is struck through in the original.
