Remarks column: each entry is initialled "J. McCorkill Lt".

### 1/7/17 — LIEVEN SECTOR

> Lieut. J. D. Grantham acting O.C. of the unit proceeded to the trenches in the LIEVEN SECTOR to interview the O's. C. 137th & 138th Imperial Machine Gun Coy's and make arrangements for their relief. The Company paraded at 10.15 A.M. under the command of Lieut. J. E. McCorkill, and moved off at 10.30 A.M. arriving at Brigade Starting Point, [uncertain] Cross Roads at 10.45 A.M. and took their position in rear of 22nd French Canadian Battalion and arrived at billets in Aix-Noulette at 2. P.M. Company headquarters are located at R 22.3.5.3 Sheet 36 B 1/4 [uncertain] 2 O.Rs of this unit proceeded to FIRST ARMY REST CAMP. Billets in this area are very comfortable but the horse standings are not favorable. Lieut. Grantham reported back to this unit at 7. P.M.

### 2/7/17

> Lieut. Grantham proceeded to the trenches, to complete arrangements for the relief which takes place tonight as per Operation Order #9 attached

## Notes

- **Coverage:** Entries run 1–31 July 1917 with no date gaps. Diary text is 13 sheets (Sheets 1–13); the month also contains two appendices (Operation Order No. 9, 2 July 1917, and Operation Order No. 16, 17 July 1917).
- **Officers named:** Lieut. J. D. Grantham (acting O.C., then on leave 7–22 July); Lieut. J. E. McCorkill (assumed command 7 July; diarist); Lieut. B. A. Taylor (O.C. "C" Section, seriously wounded 3 July, died of wounds 4 July); Lieut. D. H. Rose (wounded 23 July; reported to Coy. advanced Hqrs. 3 July); Lt. Sinclair (took command of "A" Section 20 July; went to hospital sick 22 July); Lt. Creighton (attended Taylor's funeral 5 July; went on leave 20 July); Lieut. Malcolm (reported for duty 8 July; led working parties 27–28 July); Major J. [uncertain] Watson D.S.O. (former O.C. of the company, transferred to 1st Canadian Division as D.M.G.O., 27 July).
- **Other ranks named:** Sergt. Trudway (slightly wounded 3 July); Lance Sergt. Clark (killed 3 July); Pte. Pettit (died of wounds 7 July); Sergt. Mills (led a working party 27 July); three [uncertain] sergeants placed under Lt. Sinclair's command 3 July.
- **Equipment:** Machine guns (16 guns in four sections, plus 3 anti-aircraft guns erected 4 July and further AA emplacements 13 and 18 July); Mills bombs, iron rations, range cards, standing orders and S.A.A. held as trench stores at each gun (24 Mills bombs, 4 gallons water, 48 iron rations per gun); limbers and pack horses for transport; indirect (barrage) fire a nightly routine, with S.A.A. expenditure of 3,000–32,000 rounds a night.
- **Movements:** Aix-Noulette (billets 1–2 July) → Lens/Liéven sector trenches (relief of 137th & 138th Imperial Machine Gun Companies, night 2/3 July) → relieved by 4th Canadian M.G. Coy. 9/10 July → billets Aix-Noulette, then Bouvigny Huts 11 July → trenches again to relieve 4th Canadian M.G. Coy. (night 17/18 July) → relieved 23/24 July → billets Bully Grenay → training area work forming "K" Battery 27–29 July → ordered 31 July to relieve the 16th Canadian M.G. Coy. in the Lens sector that night.
- **Discipline:** None recorded in July 1917.
- **Casualties:** 3 July — Lieut. B. A. Taylor seriously wounded, Sergt. Trudway slightly wounded, Lance Sergt. Clark killed; "D" Section 1 O.R. killed, 2 O.R. wounded; day's totals given as 1 officer wounded, 3 O.R. killed, 2 O.R. wounded (the diary's own figures do not fully reconcile — see Anomalies). 4 July — Lieut. Taylor died of wounds; 1 O.R. wounded. 5 July — 2 O.R. wounded. 6 July — 2 O.R. wounded; Lieut. Grantham slightly abraded on the left temple. 7 July — Pte. Pettit died of wounds. 21 July — 1 O.R. killed, 1 O.R. died of wounds, 2 O.R. wounded. 23 July — Lieut. D. H. Rose wounded. 28 July — 2 O.R. wounded. All other days "NIL".
- **Anomalies:** (1) The contents sheet dates Appendix 1 as "2-8-17", but Appendix 1 itself is headed "JULY 2, 1917" (Operation Order No. 9); transcribed as written in each place. (2) The 3 July casualty figures as written do not reconcile: "D" Section alone reports 1 killed and 2 wounded, while the day's totals are given as 3 O.R. killed and 2 O.R. wounded, and the narrative names only Lance Sergt. Clark killed; transcribed as written. (3) On 3 July the phrase about sergeants put under Lt. Sinclair's command contains an illegible word, and the final word of the entry is cut off at the foot of the page. (4) The 7 July entry's final lines run off the bottom of Sheet 5 and the command-change names there are illegible; the entry continues on Sheet 6. (5) The 28 July entry has the words "to Complete" written in the left margin, read as part of the sentence "16 men proceeded to 'K' Battery to complete the gun emplacements." (6) The 16 July entry's opening word is illegible ("By [uncertain] Sections..."). (7) Appendix 2 (Operation Order No. 16) is a badly blurred carbon copy on both pages; only clearly readable words are transcribed, the rest marked [uncertain]. (8) The diary spells the sector "LIEVEN" (and "Lieven Square") throughout, while the operation orders spell it "Lens"; transcribed as written in each place. (9) The 9 July entry cites "operation order No. 15. 9.7.17" but no copy of Order No. 15 is included in this month's appendices.
