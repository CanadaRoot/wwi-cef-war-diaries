# War Diary — 3rd Canadian Divisional Trench Mortar Group — January 1918

- **Unit:** 3rd Canadian Divisional Trench Mortar Group (3rd C.D.T.M.B.), 3rd Canadian Division
- **Month:** January 1918 (Volume 21; contents page reads "Volume 18-1")
- **Source:** Library and Archives Canada, Collection Search Item ID 2004819 (RG 9 III-D-3, vol. 4980, file 592, reel T-10809, finding aid 9-52)
- **Pages:** January 1918, p. 1 – p. 67 (image files page-0185 – page-0251)
- **January 1918 — COMPLETE** (pages 1–67): cover, contents, diary text 1–31 January (5 pages), Appendix 1 firing reports 1–31 January, Appendix 2 targets for 3rd T.M. Batteries commencing 9-1-18 and O.O. No. 1 orders for destructive shoot on 19-1-18, and targets for 3rd Cdn. T.M. Battery commencing 20-1-18. Transcribed 2026-10-27.

## Transcription conventions
Original spelling, punctuation and abbreviations kept. Diary text is handwritten throughout (Army Form C. 2118, "WAR DIARY or INTELLIGENCE SUMMARY"). `[uncertain]` marks words I could not read with confidence — never guessed. The Remarks column on the diary sheets carries a repeated short entry on most lines that I could not read with confidence; it is recorded as [uncertain] in each entry's note rather than in the text. "T.M." = Trench Mortar; "H.T.M." = Heavy Trench Mortar; "C.D.T.M.B." = Canadian Divisional Trench Mortar Batteries; "D.T.M.O." = Divisional Trench Mortar Officer; "O.R." = other ranks; "C.M.R." = Canadian Mounted Rifles; "C.D.A.C." = Canadian Divisional Ammunition Column; "O.O." = Operation Order; "do" = ditto. Map references (e.g. N3a 6.5, H33c 1.4) are transcribed as written; they are trench-map sheet/square references, not latitude/longitude. The signature at the foot of the diary sheets and of each firing report reads as initials resembling "J. O. B." followed by a surname initial resembling "H."; it is recorded as [signature — [uncertain]] and not used as a name. The diarist's rank is given as "Capt." and office as "D.T.M.O., 3rd Canadian Division".

Page 58 names the brigade commander as Capt. H. O. Bennett M.C., Commdg. 3rd Can Div. T.M. Brigade — the signature may be his, but this is not asserted here.
- The diary's monthly total is 2,709 rounds 6" Newton and 304 rounds 9.45" H.T.M. The firing-report round counts mostly agree with the diary, but not always: 1 January (report rows sum to 93 vs 87 in the diary); 5 January (report rows sum to 31 6" rounds vs 71 in the diary); 9 January (report rows sum to 105 6" rounds vs 100 in the diary); 21 January (report rows sum to 99 6" rounds vs 94 in the diary); 23 January (report rows sum to 170 6" rounds vs 140 in the diary). All figures are transcribed as written; the discrepancies are not resolved.
- 12 January has two firing reports (pp. 19–20): the 81-round report (raid support, 6th [uncertain] raid) appears before the 80-round report (2.10–3.05 a.m. raid support, 5th [uncertain] Bn.), although the diary lists the 80-round entry first.
- The 25 January firing report (p. 33) is headed "5.am Jan 25 1918 / 5 am " 26 1918" and its 6" total (148) matches the diary's 25 January entry exactly.
- The 19 January firing report (p. 27) notes "5th C.D.T.M.B's fired 67 rounds medium" and "8th & 9th Bde Stokes fired 4750 rounds 3" Stokes"; the bombardment report (p. 59) gives the 5th C.D.T.M. Bde figure as 62 rounds medium. Both as written.
- There are two documents stamped "Order No. 5": one dated 12/1/18 (p. 50, pre-arranged shoots repeated 13–14 January) and one dated 19/1/18 (p. 60, shoots for 20 January). Both as written.
- Order No. 7 (p. 63) refers to "order No 6 dated 21/1/18"; the Order No. 6 stamp (p. 62) is dated 20/1/18.
- The contents page describes O.O. No. 1 as "Orders for destructive shoot on 19-1-18"; the O.O. No. 1 itself (p. 54) is dated 17 January 1918, while the separate "Order No. 1" stamp (p. 42) is dated 8/1/18 and orders a counter-mortar shoot commencing 9 January.
- Page 40 is a Messages and Signals form ("A" Form, Army Form C. 2121) used as a divider headed "Appendices to T.M. War Diary", addressed to 3 Cdn Div.
- The word "Rally" appears repeatedly in issue lists ("Rally Right T.M. Commander", "Rally Left", "Rally Forward") — transcribed as written; whether it is a place, a sector name or a person's name is not clear from the diary.
- Names read as: Capt. H. O. Bennett M.C. (Commdg. 3rd Can Div. T.M. Brigade, p. 58); R.A. [uncertain] (Captain C.F.A., D.T.M.O. 1st Canadian Div., p. 65). No other personal names appear in the January diary text or appendices.
- No human casualties are recorded in January 1918. The only casualty-related note is on the 25 January firing report: "Casualties [uncertain] by Btn observer" (p. 33) — the wording is unclear and no numbers are given.
- No map coordinates (latitude/longitude) are given; all locations are trench-map references (sheets 36B, Farm Road, Hill 70, Lens/Loos, 1/10,000 and 1/40,000 scales). Places named: Bully Grenay (H.Q.), Crucifix, North Dumps, Ferme des Mines de Lens, Hill 70, Nun's Alley, Humbug Alley, Nelson Trench, "No" and "Nell" trenches.
- Batteries named in O.O. No. 1 (p. 54): X.3.C., Y.3.C. and Z.3.C. Medium and V.3.C. Heavy Trench Mortar Batteries. The 5th C.D.T.M. Bde. and the 8th and 9th Cdn. Inf. Bdes.' Stokes guns co-operated in the 19 January bombardment.
