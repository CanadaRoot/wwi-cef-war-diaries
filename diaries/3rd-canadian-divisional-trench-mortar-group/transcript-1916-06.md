# War Diary — 3rd Canadian Divisional Trench Mortar Group — June 1916

- **Unit:** 3rd Canadian Divisional Trench Mortar Group, 3rd Canadian Division (Trench Howitzer Brigade, 3rd Canadian Division — as stamped on the final sheet)
- **Month:** June 1916 (Volume 2) — 1 June to 30 June 1916
- **Source:** Library and Archives Canada, Collection Search Item ID 2004819 (RG 9 III-D-3, vol. 4980, file 592, reel T-10809)
- **Pages:** June 1916, p. 1 – p. 6 (image files page-0008 – page-0013)
- **June 1916 — COMPLETE** (pages 1–6): cover, contents, diary text 1–30 June, no appendices. Transcribed 2026-10-27.

## Transcription conventions
Original spelling, punctuation and abbreviations kept. Diary text is handwritten throughout (Army Form C. 2118, "WAR DIARY or INTELLIGENCE SUMMARY" — "INTELLIGENCE SUMMARY" struck through on the printed heading). `[uncertain]` marks words I could not read with confidence — never guessed. The Place column reads "Field" on the first diary sheet (1–4 June) and is blank on the remaining sheets; the Hour column and the "Remarks and references to Appendices" column are blank throughout. "Lieut." = Lieutenant; "Sgt" = Sergeant; "Cpl" = Corporal; "L/Cpl" = Lance Corporal; "Bdr" = Bombardier; "Gr" = Gunner; "Dr" = Driver; "N.C.O." = Non-Commissioned Officer; "O.R." = other ranks; "C.C.A.C." is written "CCAC" in the diary (Canadian Casualty Assembly Centre, as written — not expanded); "S.P.13" = as written; "T.M's" = Trench Mortars; "O.C." = Officer Commanding. The unit stamp on the final sheet reads "TRENCH HOWITZER / BRIGADE, / 3rd CANADIAN DIV." The printed footer on each diary sheet reads "2449 Wt. W14957/M90 750,000 1/16 J.B.C. & A. Forms/C.2118/12."

## Pages 3–6 — Diary text (1–30 June 1916)

Army Form C. 2118, "WAR DIARY or INTELLIGENCE SUMMARY". The printed footer reads "2449 Wt. W14957/M90 750,000 1/16 J.B.C. & A. Forms/C.2118/12." "ORIGINAL / CONFIDENTIAL" is written in ink at the top left of the first diary sheet.

### Page 3 — 1–4 June 1916

Place: Field.

> **June 1st.** — Fired 12 Rounds, Machine Gun Emplacement at request of Infantry.
>
> **2nd.** — 1 N.C.O. Returned from Hospital (Sgt Norton). Lieut:- Burnstead L.B. addmitted to Hospital wounded.
>
> **3rd.** — Lieut:- Newton + DAVIDSON in Trenches with X.3.C. Battery. 3 P.M. Enemy attack on our Front Line Sanctuary Wood etc, after intense Bombardment.
>
> **4th.** — after Bombardment of 2nd June, the following N.C.O's + men reported missing:— No 300448 Sgt Aminder E. (now officially reported Killed) No. 433040 Cpl Ockenden E. (since reported in Hospital) 300540. Bdr Carter (now officially reported Prisoner of war) 404. Bdr Scott W. (now officially reported, wounded + prisoner of war) 476675 Bdr Wright R.E. 348884 L/Cpl Herron J. 89324 Gn D'Aoust J.C. 300161. Williams W. (now officially reported prisoner of war) 104722. Sigsworth T.C. 348146. Thompson F. (now officially reported prisoner of war) 300341. Carnahan V. 40473. Powell C.H. 6508 Lauder W. (now Reported to be at Can Base Depot) 89258 Brown G (unofficially reported Prisoner of war) 91825 Ridsdale R.H. 304275 Lepper B.R. (officially wounded at CCAC) 96. Bastien A.G. (officially wounded at CCAC) 43153 Mansfield P.F.
>
> Very little news filtered through from our Batteries, + all guns behind front line Trenches, reported out of action. No record of No. of Rounds fired on this date is available, as nearly all the Gun Crews were either wounded or missing, + it was not possible to get into Communication with

### Page 4 — 4 June (continued)–9 June 1916

> **June 4th (continued).** — the Batteries.
>
> The Gun crews of X.3.C + Z.3.C. batteries, are deserving of the highest praise for the splendid way in which they stood to their Guns, during the Heavy Bombardment which our front line + supports were subjected to from May 27th to June 2nd. When the enemy made his attack on June 2nd these crews stood to their Guns firing till the last moment + only 4 Gunners were able to report out of the crews of the whole 7 guns in action. Special mention should be made of the conduct of Gunner Heaps T. who alone remained unwounded of the crew of B.4. Gun + served it himself until available ammunition was exhausted, when he obtained a rifle + joined with the Infantry, also helping to carry out wounded even after being wounded himself.
>
> **5th.** — Lieut. NOTMAN W.M. Killed by Shell Fire, working party 27 N.C.O's. + men + 1 new 2" Howitzer sent to Trenches. No 513. Gr Whitehead F.C. Returned from Hospital.
>
> **6th.** — No. 300035. Gr CLAIRMONT reported missing (now officially reported wounded at CCAC) Y.3.C. Battery sent to Trenches to relieve X.3.C. Battery. Working Party to try + recover buried Guns, Ammunition etc.
>
> **7th, 8th, 9th.** — Working Parties carrying up ammunition to Guns at MAPLE COPSE. A large quantity of Ammunition had to be carried up to the Copse every night—

### Page 6 — 25–30 June 1916

> **June 25th, 26th, 27th.** — Y.3.C Battery with assistance of working Parties, completed building + placing gun in position on Vigo Street. Gun + ammunition were taken from our old Emplacement off Romers Line, between Sackville St + Pinch Gut which the enemy left in fairly good condition. Work was also started on a new Emplacement behind the Front Line Trenches on Warrington Ave.
>
> **28th.** — Continued work on Warrington Ave. emplacement.
>
> **29th.** — X.3.C. Battery relieves Y.3.C in Trenches + carried on work on Warrington Ave. emplacement. Received Reinforcement of 10 other Ranks from Base as follows:— No 85264. Gr Alexander B.G. 90202 Gr Peck L.A. 10173. Dr Phillips A. 348264 Dr Phillips G.W. 5578 Dr Wilson H.W. 63 Dr Merritt A. 303604 Dr Forster H.G. 41765 Gr Cunningham L. 127 Gr Edwards L.W. 42154 Gr Kett. F.

Work was well started on new Gun Emplacement + Magazine on Trench 65.R.

## Open questions / uncertain items (June 1916)
- The cover reads "Volume 2" but the contents page reads "Volume 16-6" — both as written; the contents page appears to be a later typed summary and disagrees with the cover on the volume number. The contents page's "Diary Text 4 Pages" matches the 4 diary sheets (pages 3–6). There is an unclear mark beneath the "2" on the cover, possibly a "4", recorded here as [uncertain] and not read as a volume number.
- 2 June: "Lieut:- Burnstead L.B. addmitted to Hospital wounded." — "addmitted" is transcribed with the double "d" as written; the diarist's spelling of "admitted" varies (see 11 June).
- 3 June: "Sanctuary Wood" is the place of the enemy attack on the front line; the entry is dated 3 June but describes the attack of 2 June in the previous day's context — the 4 June entry then gives the missing list "after Bombardment of 2nd June".
- 4 June: the missing list of 21 N.C.O's and men is given with later updates added in brackets ("now officially reported Killed", "Prisoner of war", "wounded at CCAC", etc.) — these parenthetical updates are in the diary text as written and are transcribed as written; they were evidently added after the event. The list is: Sgt Aminder E. (300448, now officially reported Killed); Cpl Ockenden E. (433040, since reported in Hospital); Bdr Carter (300540, now officially reported Prisoner of war); Bdr Scott W. (404, now officially reported, wounded + prisoner of war); Bdr Wright R.E. (476675); L/Cpl Herron J. (348884); Gn D'Aoust J.C. (89324); Williams W. (300161, now officially reported prisoner of war, no rank given); Sigsworth T.C. (104722); Thompson F. (348146, now officially reported prisoner of war); Carnahan V. (300341); Powell C.H. (40473); Lauder W. (6508, now Reported to be at Can Base Depot); Brown G. (89258, unofficially reported Prisoner of war); Ridsdale R.H. (91825); Lepper B.R. (304275, officially wounded at CCAC); Bastien A.G. (96, officially wounded at CCAC); Mansfield P.F. (43153). The surnames Aminder, Ockenden, D'Aoust, Carnahan and Ridsdale are read as written but the handwriting is close; they are not independently verified.
- 4 June (continued): "B.4. Gun" is read as written; it may be a gun designation. "only 4 Gunners were able to report out of the crews of the whole 7 guns in action" — transcribed as written.
- 5 June: "Lieut. NOTMAN W.M. Killed by Shell Fire" — the only officer killed in the month. "working party 27 N.C.O's. + men" is read as written; the "27" is the size of the working party. "1 new 2" Howitzer" is a 2-inch howitzer, as written.
- 6 June: "Gr CLAIRMONT" (No. 300035) reported missing, "now officially reported wounded at CCAC".
- 10 June: "Received evacuation notices of following Gunners" — the word "evacuation" is read as written; the five gunners listed are Rowland E. (475562), Collier W. (305548), Bastien A.G. (7096), Southern E. (432406), Backus N.J. (301976).
- 11 June: "Gunners Edwards + Ridler admitted to Hospital (sick)" — no initials or numbers are given for Edwards and Ridler.
- 12 June: "S.P.13" is read as written; it is presumably a target reference, but the diary does not explain it.
- 15 June: "Gr. Compolo J." (No. 304037) — the surname is read as written.
- 20 June: "Sgt Norton S." (No. 348350) is admitted to hospital with Gunners Savident J. (476654) and Dignum J. (89153). This Sgt Norton is presumably the same Sgt Norton who returned from hospital on 2 June, but the diary does not say so.
- 25–27 June: "off Romers Line" is transcribed as written ("off" for "from"); "Sackville St" and "Pinch Gut" are trench names as written. "Warrington Ave." is spelled with a double "r" as written.
- 29 June: the 10 reinforcements are listed as: Gr Alexander B.G. (85264), Gr Peck L.A. (90202), Dr Phillips A. (10173), Dr Phillips G.W. (348264), Dr Wilson H.W. (5578), Dr Merritt A. (63), Dr Forster H.G. (303604), Gr Cunningham L. (41765), Gr Edwards L.W. (127), Gr Kett. F. (42154). "Dr" is read as Driver; "Kett." is read as written.
- 30 June: "Trench 65.R." is read as written.
- The stamp on the final sheet is dated "27/7/16" — 27 July 1916, after the month covered; it is the date the diary was stamped, not a diary entry date.
- The O.C.'s signature at the foot of the final sheet is not read with confidence; it appears to begin with the initials "St. J. B" but is recorded here as [uncertain] and not used as a name. "O.C." is written beneath it.
- No map coordinates (latitude/longitude) are given; places are named only: Field (the unit's location), Sanctuary Wood, Maple Copse, Vigo Street, Romers Line, Sackville St, Pinch Gut, Warrington Ave., Hooge frontage, Zillebeke Village, Trench 65.R.
- Casualties in June 1916: 1 officer killed (Lieut. Notman W.M., 5 June); 1 sergeant now officially reported killed (Sgt Aminder E., 2 June bombardment); 1 officer wounded (Lieut. Burnstead L.B., 2 June); Gunner Heaps T. wounded after serving his gun alone (2 June); 6 men now officially reported prisoners of war (Carter, Scott W., Williams W., Thompson F., plus Brown G. unofficially reported, and Scott W. also wounded); 4 men officially wounded at CCAC (Clairmont, Lepper B.R., Bastien A.G., plus Scott W. wounded and prisoner); 2 men reported in hospital / at Can Base Depot (Ockenden E., Lauder W.); 5 gunners evacuated (Rowland, Collier, Bastien, Southern, Backus, 10 June); Gunners Edwards and Ridler admitted to hospital sick (11 June); Sgt Norton S., Gunners Savident and Dignum admitted to hospital (20 June). No further deaths are recorded in the month.
