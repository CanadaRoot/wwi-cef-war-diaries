# War Diary — 3rd Canadian Divisional Trench Mortar Group — July 1916

- **Unit:** 3rd Canadian Divisional Trench Mortar Group, 3rd Canadian Division (Trench Mortar Brigade, 3rd Canadian Division)
- **Month:** July 1916 (Volume 3) — 1 to 31 July 1916
- **Source:** Library and Archives Canada, Collection Search Item ID 2004819 (RG 9 III-D-3, vol. 4980, file 592, reel T-10809)
- **Pages:** July 1916, p. 1 – p. 12 (image files page-0014 – page-0025)
- **July 1916 — COMPLETE** (pages 1–12): cover, contents, diary text 1–31 July, no appendices. Page 12 is the reverse of the last diary sheet, carrying an endorsement. Transcribed 2026-10-27.

## Transcription conventions
Original spelling, punctuation and abbreviations kept. Diary text is handwritten throughout (Army Form C. 2118, "WAR DIARY or INTELLIGENCE SUMMARY" — neither heading is struck through; the printed note reads "(Erase heading not required.)"). `[uncertain]` marks words I could not read with confidence — never guessed. The Place column reads "Field" throughout; the Hour column is blank throughout. The "Remarks and references to Appendices" column is mostly blank, with an "x" against most entries and a check mark against a few. The marginal initials "R.M.L." appear at the end of most entries and are transcribed each time they are legible. The printed footer on each diary sheet reads "2449 Wt. W14957/M90 750,000 1/16 J.B.C. & A. Forms/C.2118/12." "N.C.O." = Non-Commissioned Officer; "O.R." = Other Ranks; "Battn." = Battalion; "Lieut" = Lieutenant; "Sgt" = Sergeant; "H.E." = High Explosive; "D.R.S." = Divisional Rest Station; "O.C." = Officer Commanding; "H.Qs." = Headquarters; "St." = Street (trench/street names); "Av." = Avenue; "Saps" = as written. The batteries of the Group are V.3.C., W.3.C. (the Heavy Battery), X.3.C., Y.3.C. and Z.3.C. The place name is written "Zillebeck" throughout (not Zillebeke). "Tryed" = as written for "tried"; "handicaped" = as written; "Corrugates" = as written; "latters" = as written.

### Page 5 — 7 July (continued)–10 July 1916 (Sheet No. 3)

Place: Field.

> **7/7/16 (continued).** — LEINSTER Our Working Parties, went into two emplacements, no casualties. During a temporary lull both parties withdrew, unadvisable to take them back to work at present, as enemy is shelling vicinity. Emplacements are finished & piles in, also Saps & part of Magazines R.M.L.
>
> **8/7/16.** — Both Batteries in Trenches carried on with work on their respective Emplacements. R.M.L.
>
> **9/7/16.** — X.3.C. Battery proceeded to Trenches to relieve Y 3.C. Battery as parties were preparing to leave Zillebeck Bund. Lieuts Newton + Blackwood, + 4 Other Ranks were wounded by bursting of H.E Shrapnel Sgt McKenzie took charge of X.3.C. Battery & reports that after making best possible arrangements for wounded, he sent crews with 24 Hrs rations to relieve Y.3.C. Battery gun crews at Vigo St., Warrington Av + 65.R. R.M.L.
>
> **10/7/16.** — VIGO ST. Working on new position & got practically all digging of Emplacement & Trench completed. This morning the Dug out at old position received a direct hit, while the crew was out. R.M.L.
>
> DORMY HOUSE Working parties supplied by the Infantry are building Dug-Outs
>
> Z.3.C. Battery relieved V 3.C. Battery R.M.L.

## Open questions / uncertain items (July 1916)
- The cover reads "Volume 3", with a small pencilled mark below the "3" that may be a query mark; the contents page reads "Volume 16-V" — both as written. The contents page's "Diary Text 9 Pages" matches the 9 diary sheets (pp. 3–11).
- The marginal initials are read as "R.M.L."; the middle letter is a flourish and could be read otherwise. They appear at the end of most entries.
- 1 July: "X3C battery" and "Z3C Battery" are written without stops; "65R." / "65-R." and "S.3 Gun" are as written. "To-night" is written with a hyphen.
- 2 July: "67 Work practically finished with exception of bed & men's dug-out" — "bed" is the gun bed; "ready for bed" is used the same way in the next line.
- 5 July: "Work handicaped slightly, by Bombing attack of 8th Battn as shelling was quite severe at times" is transcribed as written; the sense is unclear — the 8th Battalion's bombing attack appears to be the reason the work was handicapped, but the diary does not explain the connection.
- 6 July: the LEINSTER / BOND ST entries are grouped with a brace in the diary; the brace is reproduced with "}" in the transcription.
- 7 July: the entry ends "(continues next page)" and runs on to p. 5.
- 9 July: "Lieuts Newton + Blackwood, + 4 Other Ranks were wounded by bursting of H.E Shrapnel" — "H.E Shrapnel" as written (no stop after "H.E"). "Sgt McKenzie" is the name as read.
- 11 July: "uncovering buried dead man in digging" — "buried" is read as written; "French Mats" is as written.
- 13 July: "Lieut Bennett H.O. assumes command of Trench Mortar Brigade, vice Captain Foster." The signature at the foot of the July sheets reads "H.O. Bennett Capt."
- 16 July: "In obedience to Telephone order from Brigade Major, 6th Divisional Artillery" — "6th" is as written. The marginal note beside this entry reads "(To 2nd ANZAC CORPS of 22nd July [initials])" — the word "of" and the initials under it are not read with confidence.
- 18–19 July and 27–28 July: the two dates are bracketed together in the Date column with a single entry for both days.
- 20 July: "the Heavy Battery (called W 3.C Battery)" — "called" is as written; this is the diary's own explanation that the Heavy Battery is W.3.C.
- 21 July: "Carried Corrugates Iron from Zillebeck to Pinch Gut" — "Corrugates" is as written.
- 22 July (continued): "Mining Sapper, figures on German Dug-Out between magazine & Gun." — "figures" is transcribed as written; its sense is unclear.
- 23 July: "Bed Cover put on [uncertain] Tunnel" — the word before "Tunnel" appears to read "Cover" but seems struck through; it is recorded as [uncertain] rather than guessed.
- 24 July: "2 Other Ranks returned from D.R.S. & 1 Other Rank taken on strength, transferred from (Canadian Corps Cyclists Battn.)" — the parenthesis is as written.
- 27–28 July: "good Roadway an Trench" — "an" is transcribed as written (for "and").
- 29 July: "Using 20 men of Infantry party to carry Ammunition" — the word before "carry" is written as a small cross-like mark, read as "to".
- 29 July (continued): "Trench Deepened for Grade" — "Grade" is read as written.
- The diary writes "Zillebeck" throughout (also "Zillebeck Bund"); "Tryed" for "tried"; "handicaped"; "Corrugates"; "latters" (15 July, "to the latters satisfaction").
- The 22 July entry says X.3.C. Battery "had 3 Casualties" while with 2nd ANZAC Corps; the diary does not say whether they were killed or wounded.
- Names read as: Lieut Bennett (H.O. Bennett, Capt.); Capt. Foster; Lieut Cameron; Lieut Newton; Lieut Blackwood; Lieut Davidson; Sgt McKenzie; Corporal Lake.
- Casualties in July 1916: Lieuts Newton and Blackwood and 4 Other Ranks wounded by H.E. shrapnel at Zillebeck Bund on 9 July; X.3.C. Battery had 3 casualties (nature not stated) during two bombardments with 2nd ANZAC Corps, reported 22 July. No deaths are recorded. Hospital admissions (sick): 2 Other Ranks on 4 July; 2 on 15 July; 1 evacuated and 1 admitted on 17 July; 2 on 18–19 July; 1 on 25 July; 1 on 31 July. Returns from hospital/D.R.S.: 1 on 23 July; 2 from D.R.S. on 24 July; 1 on 27–28 July; 1 on 29 July. One Other Rank was taken on strength from the Canadian Corps Cyclists Battalion on 24 July.
- No map coordinates (latitude/longitude) are given; places are trench and locality names only (65R, Dormy House, Halfway House, Vigo St, Warrington Ave, Leinster, Bond St, Pinch Gut, St Peters St, Davidson St, Hedge St, Windy Corner, Winnipeg St, Yeomanry Post, Zillebeck Bund).
