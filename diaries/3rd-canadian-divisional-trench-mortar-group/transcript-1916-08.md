# War Diary — 3rd Canadian Divisional Trench Mortar Group — August 1916

- **Unit:** 3rd Canadian Divisional Trench Mortar Group (3rd Canadian Division)
- **Month:** August 1916 (Volume 4) — 1 August to 31 August 1916
- **Source:** Library and Archives Canada, Collection Search Item ID 2004819 (RG 9 III-D-3, vol. 4980, file 592, reel T-10809)
- **Pages:** August 1916, p. 1 – p. 10 (image files page-0026 – page-0035)
- **August 1916 — COMPLETE** (pages 1–10): cover, contents, title sheet, diary text 1–31 August, no appendices. Transcribed 2026-10-27.

## Transcription conventions
Original spelling, punctuation and abbreviations kept. Diary text is handwritten throughout (Army Form C. 2118, "WAR DIARY or INTELLIGENCE SUMMARY" — neither heading is struck through on these sheets; the printed note reads "(Erase heading not required.)"). The printed footer on the diary sheets reads "2449 Wt. W14957/M90 750,000 1/16 J.B.C. & A. Forms/C.2118/12." `[uncertain]` marks words I could not read with confidence — never guessed. Gun positions are named after streets and places (Davidson St, St Peter St, Hedges St, Warrington Ave, Pinch Gut, Fir Lane, Dairy Maide, Metropolitan Left, Drive, Petticoat Lane, Marshall Walk, Benham Road) and are transcribed as written. Most entries are signed at the end "G. Hanson Davison, Lieut." (see open questions on the spelling of the surname) and are initialled "R.H.B." in large script in the right-hand margin, apparently the checking officer's initials; the "R.H.B." initials are not repeated after every entry below. "St." = Street; "Ave." = Avenue; "O.C." = Officer Commanding; "C.M.R." = Canadian Mounted Rifles; "D.A." = Direct Action (the diarist expands it himself on 14 August); "O.P." = Observation Post; "Arty." = Artillery; "Bdes." = Brigades (as written); "Minenwerfer" = German trench mortar (as written); "Imperials" / "Imperial Division" = as written. The "Remarks and references to Appendices" column is blank throughout. The Place column spells the same place differently on different sheets ("Mt. Sorrell" and "Mt. Sorrel"); both spellings are kept as written.

## Pages 4–10 — Diary text (1–31 August 1916)

### Page 4 — 1–9 August 1916

Place: Mt. Sorrell & Sanctuary Woods.

> **Aug. 1.** — Davidson St Gun — Emplacement completed — G. Hanson Davison, Lieut.
>
> St Peter St Gun — Work on emplacement continued — G. Hanson Davison, Lieut.
>
> Hedges St Guns — Work on emplacements continued. G. Hanson Davison, Lieut.
>
> Ammunition carried to magazines of each gun
>
> **Aug. 2.** — Hedges St. Digging Communication trench
>
> **Aug. 3.** — Hedges St — Work on emplacements carried on
>
> **Aug. 4.** — Pinch Gut Gun. Registered on enemy front line at 425 yards.
>
> St Peter St Gun. Registered on enemy front line at 400 yards.
>
> **Aug. 5.** — Hedges St Gun. Right hand emplacement completed.
>
> Warrington Ave Gun: Work on position here
>
> Officers' Dug-out at St Peter St [uncertain] — Work commenced on this dug-out
>
> **Aug. 6.** — Pinch Gut Gun. Emplacement under repair by small party
>
> St Peter St Gun. Emplacement badly damaged by heavy enemy shelling. Crews are repairing
>
> **Aug. 7.** — Pinch Gut Gun: Repair being carried on.
>
> St Peter St Gun. Repairs completed & gun again in action.
>
> **Aug. 8.** — Pinch Gut Gun: Minenwerfer blows in emplacement. Crews are repairing. Dugout for crews completed by Pioneers.
>
> St Peter St Gun. Four rounds fired from this gun on enemy front line.
>
> Davidson St Gun. Five rounds fired on enemy supports. Very effective.
>
> Warrington Ave Gun: Emplacement lowered & gun altered to lay on new target. Gun is out of action.
>
> **Aug. 9.** — Davidson St Gun. Two barrages on enemy front line. We used D.A. Fuzes & obtained very good effects.
>
> Hedges St Guns: Right gun emplacement completed & gun mounted for action

### Page 5 — 10–13 August 1916

Place: MT. SORREL & SANCTUARY WOODS (10–11 August); MT. SORRELL (12–13 August).

> **Aug. 10.** — Hedges St Guns: Left emplacement completed & gun mounted for action.
>
> Dugouts: Work carried on at DORMY HOUSE dug-outs — Officers' dugout on St Peter St + DAVIDSON ST GUN Crews dugout.
>
> **Aug. 11.** — Pinch Gut Gun: Fired five rounds on enemy front line. Good bursts & effects obtained. Emplacement requires repairs. Crews are carrying on.
>
> Davidson St Gun: Fired five rounds. Enemy retaliated with a few bombs & rifle grenades. [signature — [uncertain], Lieut.]
>
> Y.3.C and Z.3.C Batteries took gun positions of 1st Divisional Trench Mortar Brigade in Hill 60 District.
>
> **Aug. 12, 11 a.m.** — At request of infantry we opened up in retaliation to hostile shelling and fired 36 rounds. Infantry Commanders were very well pleased with effects.
>
> Davidson St Gun was put out of action — a 5.9" shell exploding just over parapet & burying gun. Crews are repairing.
>
> Officers' Dug-out (St Peter St) This dugout is nearly completed but a sapper has condemned it claiming that it is unsafe owing to weak supports & faulty construction. This will be converted into a reserve Bomb Store.
>
> **Aug. 13.** — Davidson St Gun: Emplacement is being repaired by crews with assistance of working party.

### Page 6 — 13 (continued)–14 August 1916

Place: MT. SORRELL (13 August, Cont.); HILL 60 DISTRICT (13–14 August); MT. SORRELL (14 August); METROPOLITAN LEFT (14 August).

> **Aug. 13 (Cont.).** — We salvaged two gun beds and refilled magazines with ammunition.
>
> Fired six rounds in retaliation.
>
> Fir Lane Gun
>
> Dairy Maide Gun } These two guns put out of action with direct hits. [G. Hanson Davison, Lieut. — written in the margin against the bracket]
>
> **Aug. 14.** — Fir Lane Gun has been repaired and is in action again. We fired ten rounds in retaliation to hostile Trench Mortar shelling.
>
> Pinch Gut Gun: New bed put in.
>
> Davidson St Gun: Work of repairing continues
>
> Hedges St Guns (2): Both of these guns were registered on German front line to-day.
>
> **2.30 p.m.** — We opened up with all guns in this district and sent over 75 rounds. Good results were obtained. All crews are now using D.A. (Direct Action) fuses which have been found more satisfactory than the time fuses which are not dependable.
>
> Hedges St Guns: Left gun again buried with Minenwerfer shell and out of action. Crews are repairing.
>
> We fired 29 rounds in retaliation. Enemy were shelling very heavily. G. Hanson Davison, Lieut.

### Page 7 — 15–18 August 1916

Place: MT. SORRELL (15–16 August); HILL 60 DISTRICT (15 August); MT. SORRELL (16–17 August); METROPOLITAN LEFT (17 August); MT. SORRELL (18 August); METROPOLITAN LEFT (18 August).

> **Aug. 15.** — Hedges St Guns: Left gun has been cleared & repaired and is now in action
>
> Officers' Dug-out (St Peter St) has been converted into a reserve Bomb Store.
>
> Davidson St Gun: Emplacement fully repaired and gun in action.
>
> We fired 9 rounds in retaliation to-day.
>
> **Aug. 16.** — Work done on all gun emplacements strengthening the walls. Magazines were re-stocked. G. Hanson Davison, Lieut.
>
> **Aug. 17.** — We continued re-stocking magazines & improved dug-outs, communication trenches, and magazines at all gun emplacements. G. Hanson Davison, Lt.
>
> Nine rounds fired in retaliation. Crews are filling up magazines and clearing out emplacements which were in bad shape after heavy enemy shelling in the morning. G. Hanson Davison, Lt.
>
> **Aug. 18.** — We fired 24 rounds at enemy supports with good effect
>
> Hedges St Guns: Left hand gun again put out of action by minenwerfer which went right into emplacement & smashed gun and all.
>
> Fir Lane Gun fired 9 rounds & was put out of action by a 4.2" shell.
>
> "Drive" Gun fired 26 rounds but did not obtain any direct hit on one of his O.P.'s [uncertain] which was the main target. It blew up his front line pretty much, however. G. Hanson Davison, Lieut.

### Page 8 — 18 (continued)–19 August 1916

Place: METROPOLITAN LEFT. Continued (18 August); MT. SORRELL (19 August, 2 p.m.); HILL 60 DISTRICT (19 August); DRIVE (19 August).

> **Aug. 18 (Continued).** — Petticoat Lane Gun fires 10 rounds in retaliation. G. Hanson Davison, Lt.
>
> **Aug. 19, 2 p.m.** — We opened up with all guns in this district and fired 86 rounds which were all very effective. We shelled his front & support lines & did considerable damage to his emplacements.
>
> St Peter St Gun needs repairing having been put out of action after firing its 75 rounds by a 5.9"
>
> 29 rounds fired in retaliation. G. Hanson Davison, Lt.
>
> Marshall Walk Emplacement & magazine wrecked by a direct hit. G. Hanson Davison, Lt.
>
> Benham Road Emplacement must have been hit several times as we could not locate the original position. G. Hanson Davison, Lt.
>
> We opened up a "Strafe" and fired 83 rounds in all. One gun fired 50 rounds inside of an hour and a Captain of the 2nd C.M.R's, who was observing [word struck through — [uncertain]] this gun reported a direct hit on a German Minenwerfer machine. G. Hanson Davison, Lt.

### Page 9 — 20–22 August 1916

Place: MT. SORRELL (20–22 August).

> **Aug. 20.** — All gun beds require to be straightened & replaced after heavy firing & rain of yesterday. This is being done.
>
> **Aug. 21.** — Pinch Gut Gun fired nine rounds
>
> Nine beds salvaged and placed at dump.
>
> St Peter St Gun registered on enemy support lines and obtained two direct hits.
>
> **Aug. 22.** — We opened up with all guns later and fired 42 rounds
>
> Pinch Gut Gun fired 9 rounds in retaliation during night and 11 rounds during Trench Mortar Strafe to-day.
>

> Davidson St Gun fired 12 rounds doing considerable damage. Observed by Arty. O.P.

>

## Open questions / uncertain items (August 1916)
- The cover reads "Volume 4" (with "7." written below the "4", apparently a later addition), but the typed contents page reads "Volume 16-8" — both as written; the contents page appears to be a later typed summary and disagrees with the cover on the volume number. The contents page's "Diary Text 7 Pages" matches the 7 diary sheets (pages 4–10), the title sheet on page 3 being extra.
- The title sheet on page 3 is itself an Army Form C. 2118 sheet used as a title page ("To Officer i/c A. G's Office at the Base"), and bears a handwritten "4" at the top right corner.
- The Place column spells the same place "Mt. Sorrell" (page 4) and "Mt. Sorrel" / "MT. SORREL" (pages 5, 10); both spellings are kept as written.
- 5 August: "Officers' Dug-out at St Peter St [uncertain]" — a short word follows "St Peter St" in the original and may read "Gun"; it is not read with confidence.
- 8 August: "Dugout for crews completed by Pioneers." is transcribed as written; the sense is that the dugout for the crews was completed by the Pioneers.
- 9 August: "Two barrages on enemy front line." — "barrages" is the best reading of a heavily written word.
- 11 August: the Davidson St Gun entry ends with a full signature in the right margin that is not read with confidence (it appears to begin "R. M." and end "Lieut."); it is recorded as [uncertain] and not used as a name.
- 13 August: the entry runs from the foot of page 5 onto the head of page 6 ("Aug. 13 Cont."); the bracketed "Fir Lane Gun / Dairy Maide Gun" entry is signed in the margin by G. Hanson Davison. "Dairy Maide" is read as written; it could also read "Dairy Maids" or "Dairy Maid".
- 14 August: the hour "2.30 p.m." is written in the Hour column against the "We opened up with all guns in this district" entry.
- 18 August: the "Drive" Gun entry reads "one of his O.P.'s [uncertain] which was the main target" — "O.P.'s" is the best reading; the entry runs from the foot of page 7 onto the head of page 8 ("Aug. 18 Continued", Petticoat Lane Gun).
- 19 August (Drive): a word after "who was observing" is struck through and is not read with confidence (it appears to read "for", with a small word above the line that may read "hit"); the entry is transcribed with the struck word marked [uncertain].
- 19 August: "St Peter St Gun needs repairing having been put out of action after firing its 75 rounds by a 5.9"" — the sentence ends at "5.9"" as written, with no word such as "shell" following it.
- 21 August: "Nine beds salvaged and placed at dump." — "Nine" is read as written; it could also read "New beds".
- 24 August: "Z.3.C. and X.3.C. Batteries were relieved and all ammunition turned over to 4th Imperial Division" — the word between "relieved" and "all ammunition" is read as "and" but is not fully clear.
- 24 August: "Y.3.C. Battery was relieved by Y. Battery of the 4th Imperial Division." — "Y. Battery" is read as written.
- The signature at the end of most entries is read as "G. Hanson Davison, Lieut." (sometimes "Lt."); the first surname could also read "Hansen". The diary is signed at the foot of the month by "J. O. Bennett, Capt., O.C. 3rd Can. Div. Trench Mortar Bdes." The large initials "R.H.B." in the right-hand margin throughout are read as written; the person they belong to is not identified in the diary.
- The diarist writes "D.A. Fuzes" on 9 August and "D.A. (Direct Action) fuses" on 14 August — both spellings kept as written.
- Names read as: G. Hanson Davison (Lieut.); J. O. Bennett (Capt., O.C.); a Captain of the 2nd C.M.R's (unnamed) who observed the "Drive" gun on 19 August.
- No human casualties are recorded in this month: no killed or wounded are named or numbered. The month is a record of gun positions, emplacement building and repair, registration and retaliatory firing, and enemy shelling that repeatedly put guns out of action (notably Davidson St Gun on 12 August, Fir Lane and Dairy Maide Guns on 13 August, Hedges St left gun on 14 and 18 August, and the Marshall Walk emplacement on 19 August). No map coordinates (latitude/longitude) are given; gun positions are given only by street/place names (Davidson St, St Peter St, Hedges St, Warrington Ave, Pinch Gut, Fir Lane, Dairy Maide, Metropolitan Left, Drive, Petticoat Lane, Marshall Walk, Benham Road).
