# War Diary — 3rd Canadian Divisional Trench Mortar Group — May 1918

- **Unit:** 3rd Canadian Divisional Trench Mortar Group, 3rd Canadian Division (cover: "3rd Canadian Trench Mortar Group. 3rd Can. Division.")
- **Month:** May 1918
- **Source:** Library and Archives Canada, Collection Search Item ID 2004819 (RG 9 III-D-3, vol. 4980, file 592, reel T-10809)
- **Pages:** May 1918, p. 1 – p. 9 (image files page-0296 – page-0304)
- **May 1918 — COMPLETE** (pages 1–9): cover, contents, title sheet, diary text 1–31 May on 6 sheets, no appendices. Transcribed 2026-10-27.

## Transcription conventions
Original spelling, punctuation and abbreviations kept. Diary text is handwritten throughout on Army Form C. 2118, "WAR DIARY or INTELLIGENCE SUMMARY" (neither heading is erased; the printed sub-heading reads "(Erase heading not required.)"). The printed instruction at the head of each diary sheet reads "Instructions regarding War Diaries and Intelligence Summaries are contained in F. S. Regs., Part II. and the Staff Manual respectively. Title pages will be prepared in manuscript." The printed footer on the diary sheets reads "A5834 Wt. W4973 M687 750,000 8/16 D. D. & L. Ltd. Forms/C.2118/13." `[uncertain]` marks words I could not read with confidence — never guessed. The diarist's initials "H.T.B." appear in small writing at the right-hand end of each entry and are transcribed at the end of each entry. On days where the entry is a ditto, the diarist writes "do" twice across the entry line ("do do"), followed by the initials. The "Hour" column is blank throughout, and the "Remarks and references to Appendices" column is blank throughout. "T.M." = Trench Mortar; "C.D.A." = Canadian Divisional Artillery; "D.T.M.O." = Divisional Trench Mortar Officer; "C.R.A." = Commander Royal Artillery; "C.R.E." = Commander Royal Engineers; "R.E." = Royal Engineers; "D.A.A.G." = Deputy Assistant Adjutant General; "G.H.Q." = General Headquarters; "N.C.O." = Non-Commissioned Officer; "M.M." = Military Medal; "Y.M.C.A." = Young Men's Christian Association; "Cdn." = Canadian; "Divl." / "Divn." = Divisional / Division; "do" = ditto; "sub. bed" = as written; battery designations are written without stops as "V3C", "X3C", "Y3C".

## Pages 4–9 — Diary text (1–31 May 1918)

### Page 4 — 1–7 May 1918

Place: 1 May — CITE ST PIERRE, M10d.4.5.35; 2 May — WAGON LINES; 3 May — BULLY GRENAY; 4 May onward — BELLERY.

> **1 May 1918.** — Harassing and neutralization fire carried out against enemy T.M's and strong points H.T.B.
>
> **2.** — Medium Trench Mortars again actively engaged neutralizing enemy T.M's one direct hit obtained causing small explosion H.T.B.
>
> **3.** — Relief with 2nd Divl Trench Mortar Batteries completed H.T.B.
>
> **4.** — Commenced march to First Army Reserve Area at 10 a.m. arrived at billets in BELLERY (85a.90.90 Sheet 44B) Headquarters established at Billet No 74 BELLERY H.T.B.
>
> **5.** — Thorough inspection of kit carried out by all Battery Commanders H.T.B.
>
> **6.** — Intensive training commenced by all batteries, in Physical Drill Gun Drill. Foot Drill. Gas Drill and route marching. H.T.B.
>
> **7.** — do do H.T.B.

### Page 5 — 8–11 May 1918

Place column blank (unit at BELLERY).

> **8.** — Orders received from Headquarters 3rd C.D.A to be ready to march at 3 hours notice. Enemy attack expected on front of XI or XII Corps. The artillery of the 3rd Canadian Division expecting to be called upon to support the artillery of either of these two Corps. H.T.B.
>
> **9.** — Discipline and training of unit carried out Lieut M. B. Hastings of X3C Battery made up plans for a sub. bed for the 6" Newton Trench Mortar which were submitted to the D.T.M.O. 3rd Can Divn. for approval. Approval was given and plans were sent to the C.R.A. 3rd Can Divn. H.T.B.
>
> **10.** — Plans of new sub. bed for 6" Newton Trench Mortar approved by C.R.A. 3rd Can Divn. and sent to C.R.E. 3rd Can Divn. asking that a bed be constructed H.T.B.
>
> **11.** — Physical training Route marching Gun Drill musketry and Gas Drill carried out by all Batteries H.T.B.

### Page 6 — 12–16 May 1918

Place column blank (unit at BELLERY).

> **12.** — Physical training. Route marching. Gun Drill musketry and Gas Drill carried out by all Batteries H.T.B.
>
> **13.** — Demonstration of 6" NEWTON Trench Mortars in open warfare given at LACOMTE. 2 officers from each Battery attended H.T.B.
>
> **14.** — Foot Drill. Gun Drill. musketry and Physical Drill carried out during the day. Arrangements were made with 7th Canadian Infantry Brigade for the use of the Rifle Ranges at BELLERY. V3C Battery proceeded to the ranges for shooting practice and fire discipline. 1 other rank reported from Cdn Corps Reinforcement Camp. H.T.B.
>
> **15.** — Work commenced on new 6" NEWTON Portable Sub. bed at Canadian Corps R.E. Park DIEVAL. Lieut Hastings proceeded to DIEVAL to go over plans and supervise work with the C.R.E. 3rd Canadian Division H.T.B.
>
> **16.** — Physical Training Gas Drill Foot Drill and Gun Drill carried out by all Batteries. V3C Battery at the Ranges shooting. Recreational training carried out during the day H.T.B.

### Page 7 — 17–21 May 1918

Place column blank (unit at BELLERY).

> **17.** — Training carried out as usual during the day One of our most reliable N.C.O's Sergeant W. Chrish [uncertain] M.M. of X3C. Battery admitted to Hospital and operated on at night for appendicitis. H.T.B.
>
> **18.** — Training carried out by all Batteries as usual Concert at the Y.M.C.A. Hut, LIERES arranged for the unit by Capt. Manning. Concert held in the afternoon and very much appreciated by all ranks. H.T.B.
>
> **19.** — Church Parade and Service held at AMES 1 other Rank reported from Cdn Corps Reinforcement Camp H.T.B.
>
> **20.** — Usual training carried out by all Batteries. V3C Battery at the Ranges in the afternoon shooting. Recreational training carried out at intervals during the day. H.T.B.
>
> **21.** — Training carried out as usual. X3C and Y3C Batteries shooting. H.T.B.

### Page 8 — 22–27 May 1918

Place column blank (unit at BELLERY).

>
> **23.** — Physical training, Route marching and Gun Drill carried out by the Batteries. 4 other Ranks reported from Canadian Corps Reinforcement Camp. H.T.B.
>
> **24.** — Training carried out by all Batteries as usual H.T.B.
>
> **25.** — do do H.T.B.
>
> **26.** — Brigade Inspection and Church Service again held at AMES H.T.B.
>
> **27.** — Regulations governing the "Dumbell" Competition for the unit made out. Events for the competing batteries to be judged in General Inspection, musketry, Rifle Drill, Foot Drill, Gun Drill and Gun Laying. Competition to be held at BELLERY on 1st June 1918. H.T.B.

## Open questions / uncertain items (May 1918)
- The cover reads "VOLUME 5" with "6." written in pencil below it, while the contents page reads "Volume 16-5" — both as written; the two pages disagree on the volume number.
- The contents page says "Diary Text 6 Pages" — the 6 diary sheets are pp. 4–9 (1–31 May); p. 3 is a title/forwarding sheet, so the count matches the diary sheets rather than the total pages in the month's file.
- The parenthetical line under the unit name on the cover is struck through in ink; only the word "Batteries" in it is legible, the preceding words are not read with confidence.
- 1 May, Place column: the map reference is read as "M10d.4.5.35" as written; the individual digits are not verified.
- 4 May: the Bellery reference is read as "(85a.90.90 Sheet 44B)" as written; the digits are not verified. "BELLERY" is spelled as written throughout the month.
- 8 May: "XI or XII Corps" — the Roman numerals are written with a bar over them, as transcribed.
- 13 May: "LACOMTE" is spelled as written.
- 17 May: the sergeant's surname is read as "Chrish" but is not read with confidence — recorded as "Sergeant W. Chrish [uncertain] M.M." and not used as a name. He was admitted to hospital and operated on at night for appendicitis; the diary gives no later update on his condition.
- 22 May: the word after "Observers" is not read with confidence — it appears to read "mak" (possibly "making") — recorded as "Observers [uncertain] Reading and lectures on observation".
- 27 May: "Dumbell" is spelled as written (one "b" after "Dum" is how it reads; the competition is to be held at BELLERY on 1st June 1918).
- Page 3: "G.H.Q." is read as written; the "Q" is formed like a "2" in the handwriting.
- The diarist's initials in the right margin of each entry are read as "H.T.B."; the signature at the foot of p. 9 is not legible as a name and is recorded as [uncertain] — it is signed "Capt., D.T.M.O., 3rd Canadian Division".
- The final line of the 16 May entry ("during the day") sits at the very foot of p. 6 and is partly cut off by the page edge, but reads as transcribed.
