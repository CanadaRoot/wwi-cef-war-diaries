# War Diary — 3rd Canadian Divisional Trench Mortar Group — January 1917

- **Unit:** 3rd Canadian Divisional Trench Mortar Group (3rd Canadian Divisional Trench Mortar Batteries), 3rd Canadian Division
- **Month:** January 1917
- **Source:** Library and Archives Canada, Collection Search Item ID 2004819 (RG 9 III-D-3, vol. 4980, file 592, reel T-10809)
- **Pages:** January 1917, p. 1 – p. 11 (image files page-0073 – page-0083)
- **January 1917 — COMPLETE** (pages 1–11): cover, contents, title sheet, diary text 1–31 January, no appendices. Transcribed 2026-10-27.

## Transcription conventions
Original spelling, punctuation and abbreviations kept. Diary text is handwritten throughout (Army Form C. 2118, "WAR DIARY or INTELLIGENCE SUMMARY" — neither heading is struck through on these sheets). `[uncertain]` marks words I could not read with confidence — never guessed. Each day's entry is initialled by an officer at its end, and the initials are transcribed each time: H.R., S.N.T., H.O.B., J.H., M.W.N., P.S., J.S.W., P.R., J.B.W., P.P.C., J.W., A.M.C. and H.N. all appear; several entries carry two sets of initials. The Place column is blank except for 1 January ("MAROEUIL"); the Hour column is used only on 15 January (2.45 to 3.10 p.m.). The "Remarks and references to Appendices" column is blank throughout. "T.M." = Trench Mortar; "M.T.M." = Medium Trench Mortar; "C.D.A." = Canadian Divisional Artillery; "L.E." = Lee-Enfield; "R.C.R." = Royal Canadian Regiment; "M.G." = Machine Gun; "O.C." = Officer Commanding; "A.A.G." = Assistant Adjutant General; "Divl" = Divisional; "rds" = rounds; "Stokes" = Stokes mortar. The year is written "1916" in the Date column on pages 6–10 (9–29 January) and "1917" on pages 5 and 11; the cover and contents give 1917, and all entries are treated as January 1917.

## Pages 4–11 — Diary text (1–31 January 1917)

Army Form C. 2118, "WAR DIARY or INTELLIGENCE SUMMARY". The printed footer reads "1875 Wt. W593/826 1,000,000 4/15 J.B.C. & A. A.D.S.S./Forms/C. 2118."

### Page 4 — 1 January 1917

Place: Maroeuil.

> ECURIE SECTOR covered by Z3C Battery
> THELUS SECTOR covered by X3C [ditto]
> LA FOLIE SECTOR covered by Y3C [ditto]
>
> **1 Jan 1917.** — V3C Heavy Trench Mortar Battery did no firing during the month of January 1917. Period was occupied in strengthening emplacements + putting in concrete Beds. J.H.P. Davis [uncertain]
>
> X3C. Y3C. Z3C. Batteries not so active as in previous months owing to weather conditions which rendered the emplacements unfit to fire from and continuous trouble with L.E. Rifle Mechanisms. Z3C Battery was loaned to 2nd C.D.A. 7-1-17 until 18-1-17 to cut wire on 2nd Canadian Division front for minor operation by that Division. Work has been done during the month on about 45 new emplacements for Medium Trench Mortars H.O.B.
>
> **1 Jan 1917.** — 10 rounds fired in LA FOLIE SECTION + 12 in ECURIE SECTION in retaliation to hostile Trench Mortars H.R. S.N.T.

### Page 5 — 2–8 January 1917

> **2.** — Y3C fired 20 rounds on enemy front line + supports with good effect H.R.
>
> Z3C fired 26 rounds retaliation + silenced enemy Trench mortars S.N.T.
>
> **3.** — 29 rounds on whole front fired to-day. Emplacements are caving in + will not stand much firing. H.O.B.
>
> **4.** — X3C fired 10 rounds on emplacements in vicinity of Sheba's Breast (A4 d 4 3) J.H.
>
> Y3C fired 15 rounds retaliation M.W.N.
>
> X3C fired 19 rounds retaliation. P.S.
>
> **5.** — 12 rounds fired in LA FOLIE SECTION — Retaliation M.W.N.
>
> 20 rounds fired in ECURIE SECTION — Retaliation P.S.
>
> **6.** — [M.T.M. — struck through] Medium Trench Mortars fairly active on entire front firing 50 rounds retaliation H.O.B.
>
> **7.** — 25 rounds fired in Ecurie Section — retaliation P.S.
>
> **8.** — Two rounds retaliation fired by M.T.M's on entire front H.O.B.

### Page 6 — 9–12 January 1917

> **9.** — In the LA FOLIE SECTION M.T.M.'s Co-operated with Stokes Guns in concentrated shoot on enemy front line and works. Much damage was done. M.W.N.
>
> In the ECURIE + THELUS section we retaliated to hostile T.M's effectively silencing same. P.S. J.H.
>
> **10.** — M.T.M's bombarded enemy front + support lines with 70 rounds H.O.B.
>
> **11.** — 70 rounds fired on enemy T.M. + M.G. emplacements One T.M. emplacement believed destroyed in THELUS SECTION H.O.B.
>
> **12.** — In the THELUS SECTION fired on + dispersed two enemy working parties in front line J.S.W.
>
> In the ECURIE SECTION fired 28 rounds retaliation. Much damage done to their front lines. S.N.T.
>
> LA FOLIE SECTION — Fired 12 rounds on suspected enemy dump. No direct hits were obtained but [Considerable — written above the line] damage was done. P.R.

### Page 7 — 13–15 January 1917

> **13.** — Moved billets from MAROEUIL to ECOIVRES. H.O.B.
>
> Very little firing — work done on emplacements + magazines + ammunition carried from Dumps to Guns.
>
> **14.** — Enemy T.M.'s very active on ECURIE + THELUS SECTIONS and quiet on LA FOLIE SECTION.
>
> In the THELUS SECTION in conjunction with Stokes Guns we retaliated vigorously to enemy T.M. fire. Enemy was silenced + much damage done to his front + support lines. J.B.W.
>
> In the ECURIE SECTION 25 rounds were fired in retaliation S.N.T.
>
> **15.** — [Hour: 2.45 to 3.10 p.m.] ECURIE SECTION — In conjunction with Stokes + Divisional Artillery a very successful bombardment was carried out on enemy front + support lines S.N.T.
>
> THELUS SECTION — 25 rounds fired in retaliation & J.B.W.

## Open questions / uncertain items (January 1917)
- The cover reads "Volume 9" (with "8." written below it) but the contents page reads "Volume 17-1" — both as written; the contents page appears to be a later typed summary and disagrees with the cover on the volume number. The contents page's "Diary Text 8 Pages" matches the 8 diary sheets (pages 4–11).
- The year is written "1916" in the Date column on pages 6–10 (9–29 January) and "1917" on pages 5 and 11 — a new-year slip; the cover, contents and title sheet all give 1917, and all entries are treated as January 1917.
- 1 January: the Date column carries "Jany 1st 1917" twice. The first entry is a retrospective monthly summary (it covers the whole month of January, including Z3C's loan 7–18 January), and the "10 rounds fired in LA FOLIE SECTION + 12 in ECURIE SECTION" line is a second entry dated the same day. The summary's round counts (10 + 12) do not match the daily entries' totals and are transcribed as written.
- 1 January: the initials after "concrete Beds." read "J.H.P. Davis" — the surname is underlined — but the hand is cramped and the reading is not fully certain; recorded here as [uncertain].
- 1 January: the sector table uses ditto marks under "Battery" for the X3C and Y3C lines; transcribed as [ditto].
- 3 January: "29 rounds on whole front fired to-day" — no battery is named.
- 4 January: "Sheba's Breast (A4 d 4 3)" — the map reference is read as written; its sheet/coordinates are not verified.
- 6 January: "M.T.M." appears struck through immediately before "Medium Trench Mortars"; transcribed as a struck-through word.
- 7 January: a small word after "fired" in "25 rounds fired in Ecurie Section" appears overwritten and is not readable; the sentence is transcribed as written.
- 9 January: the entry carries two sets of initials, "P.S. J.H."; 1 January likewise carries "H.R. S.N.T." at the end of the 10/12 rounds line.
- 12 January: "Considerable" in "but Considerable damage was done" is written above the line over "damage"; transcribed as an interlineation.
- 14 January: the figure "25" in "In the ECURIE SECTION 25 rounds were fired in retaliation" appears written over another figure; it reads as 25 but the overwrite is noted.
- 15 January: the THELUS SECTION line ends "25 rounds fired in retaliation & J.B.W." — the "&" before the initials is as written; the Hour column gives "2.45 to 3.10 p.m." for this date.
- 17 January: "A small lane was cut in wire" — "small" is read as written; the Ecurie Section line ("Z3C fired 15 rounds retaliation") carries no initials.
- 24 January: the "THELUS SECTION — 21 rounds retaliation" line carries no initials; the Y3C + Z3C line is initialled "J.B.W."
- 27 January: "S.N.T." appears mid-sentence after "bombarded enemy front line", and "J.W." at the end of the entry; both as written.
- 30 January: no section is named for the Stokes-gun bombardment; transcribed as written.
- The diary is signed at the foot of 31 January "J.R. [uncertain] Lt. for O.C. 3rd Canadian Divl Trench Mortar Batteries". The surname appears to read "Fawcett" but is not read with confidence and is recorded here as [uncertain]; the "Lt." is likewise read as written.
- Initials read as: H.R.; S.N.T.; H.O.B.; J.H.; M.W.N.; P.S.; J.S.W.; P.R.; J.B.W.; P.P.C.; J.W.; A.M.C.; H.N.; J.H.P. Davis [uncertain]. These are officers' initials only — no full names are given in the diary. The "J.W." and "J.S.W." readings are close and could be the same hand; the "J.B.W." initials are distinct.
- Places read as: Maroeuil (spelled "MAROEUIL"); Ecoivres (spelled "ECOIVRES"); Ecurie, Thelus and La Folie Sections; Sheba's Breast; Letter Box; Durand; the Snout. The map reference on 4 January is the only grid reference given: "(A4 d 4 3)".
- No human casualties are recorded anywhere in this month: no killed, wounded or missing are mentioned, and no personnel are named by full name. The only unit named is the R.C.R. (Royal Canadian Regiment), whose raiding party the La Folie Section cut a wire lane for on 21 January.
- A faint "No" (with a figure) appears at the top right of the cover; a handwritten "9" appears at the top right of the title sheet (page 3). Neither is read with confidence and neither is used as a page or volume number.
