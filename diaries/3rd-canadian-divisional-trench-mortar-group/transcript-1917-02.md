# War Diary — 3rd Canadian Divisional Trench Mortar Group — February 1917

- **Unit:** 3rd Canadian Divisional Trench Mortar Group (X 3 C, Y 3 C, Z 3 C Batteries and V 3 C Heavy Trench Mortar Battery)
- **Month:** February 1917
- **Source:** Library and Archives Canada, Collection Search Item ID 2004819 (RG 9 III-D-3, vol. 4980, file 592, reel T-10809)
- **Pages:** February 1917, p. 1 – p. 10 (image files page-0084 – page-0093)
- **February 1917 — COMPLETE** (pages 1–10): cover, contents, title page, diary text 1–28 February, no appendices. Transcribed 2026-10-27.

## Transcription conventions
Original spelling, punctuation and abbreviations kept. Diary text is typewritten throughout (Army Form C. 2118, "WAR DIARY or INTELLIGENCE SUMMARY"). `[uncertain]` marks words I could not read with confidence — never guessed. Handwritten initials in the right margin after many entries are the initials of the officer reporting that battery's activity; they are rendered in the transcript as "[marginal initials — [uncertain]]" because the hand is a rapid flourish and no reading is certain — the apparent recurring forms are listed in the Open questions section. The Place, Hour and "Remarks and references to Appendices" columns are blank on every sheet. The year in the Date column is written "1916" with the final figure corrected in ink to "7" on pp. 4–9; p. 10 reads "1917". "Feby." = February, as written; "Con." = continued; "T.M." = trench mortar; "H.T.M." = heavy trench mortar; "M.T.M." = medium trench mortar; "O.P." = observation post; "C.D.A." = Canadian Divisional Artillery; "G.H.Q." = General Headquarters; "A.A.G." = Assistant Adjutant General; "4.5 Hows." = 4.5-inch howitzers; "77mm's" = German 77 mm guns; "18 pounders" = 18-pounder field guns; "Stokes Mortars" as written. Map references such as "S 28 c 70.00", "A 16 b 7.6" and "A 4 a 7.95" are trench-map references as written; no latitude/longitude coordinates are given. "Releif" is "Relief" as typed; "Hus" is as typed (probably "his"); "12 pounds" on 7 February is as typed (probably a typist's error for "rounds").

## Open questions / uncertain items (February 1917)
- The cover reads "Volume 10" while the contents page reads "Volume 17-2" — both as written. The contents page's "Diary Text 7 Pages" matches the 7 diary sheets (pp. 4–10); the title page (p. 3) is additional.
- A handwritten "2" appears below the "VOLUME 10" line on the cover, and a partial "No" is visible at the top right corner, cut off by the edge of the image.
- The year in the Date column is written "1916" with the final figure corrected in ink to "7" on pp. 4–9; p. 10 reads "1917". The diary covers 1–28 February 1917 throughout.
- Marginal initials: the handwritten initials in the right margin appear to read H.R. (Y 3 C entries), J.W. (X 3 C entries), P.S. (Z 3 C entries) and G.W. (H.T.M. entries), but no reading is certain — the hand is a rapid flourish. Two sets of initials appear on the 5 February (continued) and 6 February Centre Sector entries, and again on the 10 February No. 2 H.T.M. entry. The 1 February Y 3 C entry carries what appears to be a fuller signature, possibly "H. Reade" — [uncertain].
- 4 February: the target is given as "A.d 3 2½" — the small figures are not fully clear.
- 6 February: "B4 & Grange" — "Grange" is as written; it may be a locality name.
- 7 February: "fired 12 pounds" is transcribed as written; it is almost certainly a typist's error for "rounds". The word before "H.T.M. No 2" in the same sentence is struck through with x's and cannot be read.
- 8 February: "Dugout mix near same place" — "mix" is as typed; its meaning is unclear. "A 16 b 0.8.." carries a double full stop as written.
- 10 February: "Hus support" is as typed, probably "his".
- 13 February: the word "vigorously" in "retaliated vigorously to hostile T.M. fire" is overtyped/corrected in the typescript.
- 14 February: "Grabnitz Weg" and "PATRICIA crater" are as written.
- 15 February: "29 rounds" is typed with a space as "2 9"; "Snipers post" has no apostrophe as typed.
- 16 and 17 February: "Releif" is as typed (twice) for "Relief".
- 21 February: "BULLY - GRENAY" was confirmed on an enlarged read (Bully-Grenay).
- The date figures "18th" and "22nd" in the Date column on p. 10 show overwriting.
- The closing signature at the foot of p. 10 appears to read "J. W. Lawson", Lt., for O.C., 3rd Can. D T M B's — [uncertain]; the surname's first letter is not clear.
- No human casualties are recorded anywhere in this month, and no person is named in the diary text apart from the closing signature. No map coordinates (latitude/longitude) are given; places are given only as sectors (Thélus, La Folie, Écurie), trench-map references, Amettes and Bully-Grenay.
- Names read as: none named in the diary text; marginal initials as above, all [uncertain].
