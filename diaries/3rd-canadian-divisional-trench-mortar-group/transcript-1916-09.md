# War Diary — 3rd Canadian Divisional Trench Mortar Group — September 1916

- **Unit:** 3rd Canadian Divisional Trench Mortar Group, 3rd Canadian Division
- **Month:** September 1916 (Volume 5) — 1 to 30 September 1916
- **Source:** Library and Archives Canada, Collection Search Item ID 2004819 (RG 9 III-D-3, vol. 4980, file 592, reel T-10809)
- **Pages:** September 1916, p. 1 – p. 16 (image files page-0036 – page-0051)
- **September 1916 — COMPLETE** (pages 1–16): cover, contents, title sheet, diary text 1–30 September, no appendices. Transcribed 2026-10-27.

## Transcription conventions
Original spelling, punctuation and abbreviations kept. Diary text is handwritten throughout (Army Form C. 2118, "WAR DIARY or INTELLIGENCE SUMMARY"). `[uncertain]` marks words I could not read with confidence — never guessed. The Place column is blank on every diary sheet; the Hour column is blank except where noted. The "Remarks and references to Appendices" column on each diary sheet carries initials against most entries, most often reading "Matt."; other initials could not be read with confidence and are not transcribed per entry. The printed footer on the diary sheets reads "2449 Wt. W14957/M90 750,000 1/16 J.B.C. & A. Forms/C.2118/12." "No." = Number; "rds." = rounds; "T.M." = Trench Mortar; "O.P." = Observation Post; "Boche", "Bosche" and "Hun" = the enemy, as written; "Rum Jar" = the German heavy trench-mortar bomb, as written; "Whiz-Bang" = a shell, as written; "Divl." = Divisional; "Div:" = Division. The group is organised in three sections — Right, Centre and Left — each with its own guns: the Left/Centre guns are numbered No. 1, No. 2, No. 3, No. 4 (and No. 6 once, 17 September), and the Right Section guns are designated Y.1, Y.2 and Y.3.

## Pages 4–16 — Diary text (1–30 September 1916)

### Page 4 — 1–11 September 1916 (Sheet No. 3)

> **Sep. 1. to Sep. 4.** — Rest Billets at STEENVOORDE
>
> **Sep. 5, 2.30 am.** — Orders received to relieve Lahore Divisional Trench Mortars who were supporting 1st Canadian Division. Arrived at new billets 2.30 P.M.
>
> **6.** — Took over all guns in line. Positions needed a great deal of repairing
>
> **7.** — Stood to to support Infantry raiding party until 4.45 am when we were advised infantry had returned having found German front line unoccupied.
>
> **8.** — Relieved by 4th Australian Div: Trench Mortars.
>
> **9.** — Moved Camp & relieved 19th Div: Trench Mortars (Imperials) & took over guns in line.
>
> **10.** — Right Section
>
> No 4 Gun retaliates to hostile shelling with 7 very effective rounds on enemy front line at N 15 b. e 2 (Sheet 28 S.W.)
>
> **11.** — Left Section
>
> No 2 Gun retaliates for Minenwerfers with 12 rounds. Nos 1 & 3 guns are being relaid on their targets. Work commenced on two new positions.
>
> Right Section
>
> O.P. selected in front line. No 2 gun registered on enemy wire, front line & supports

### Page 5 — 12–13 September 1916 (Sheet No. 4)

> **Sep. 12.** — Centre Section
>
> No 4 gun fired 5 rounds retaliation which were very effective. Defences being strengthened & magazines refilled.
>
> Left Section
>
> Nos 1 & 3 guns registered to-day on enemy front line & subsequently fired 34 rounds in retaliation on same.
>
> Right Section
>
> No Y.1. gun fired 12 rounds in retaliation which silenced enemy Trench Mortars. No Y.2 Gun registered on enemy wire & supports
>
> **13.** — Centre Section
>
> Registered all guns in this sector on enemy wire.
>
> Right Section
>
> Y.2 gun fired 10 rounds on Hun front line in retaliation for Minenwerfers. Y.3 gun fired 13 rounds on Hun wire opposite Z.4. Wire was very effectively cut.

### Page 6 — 14 September 1916 (Sheet No. 5)

> **Sep. 14.** — Left Section
>
> Work on new positions completed. No 1 Gun fired 12 rds in retaliation effectively silencing Boche T.M.'s.
>
> Right Section
>
> No Y.2. Gun fired 8 rounds on wire opposite Trench 7.4. All rounds were very effective avenues 20" wire being plainly discernible on a frontage of 50 yards. No Y.3 gun fired 15 rounds retaliation and registered on PECKHAM HOUSE securing a direct hit. Damaged his front line pretty badly.
>
> Centre Section, 3 p.m.
>
> No 3 gun fired 10 rounds on enemy wire & front line opposite J.3 & J.3R. Very little retaliation by Bosche. No 1 gun fired 10 rounds on enemy wire opposite H.2 & H.3. No Y.1 Gun fired 18 rounds on enemy front line & supports in retaliation. Very effective & enemy silenced.

### Page 7 — 15–16 September 1916 (Sheet No. 6)

> **Sep. 15.** — Left Section
>
> Men's dugouts being improved. No 1 Gun fired 24 rounds on enemy wire with good effects.
>
> Right Section
>
> Nos Y.1 & Y.3 guns effectively tore up enemy wire & blew up a sniper's post. Pall Mall Guns did good work on enemy wire at 410 yards. Fired 39 rounds.
>
> **16.** — Left Section
>
> No 1 gun retaliated to hostile shelling with 10 rds. on Hun supports. No 3 gun fired 10 rounds on wire doing a lot of damage. Also 3 rounds demonstration for General Lipsett who was very pleased with effects.
>
> Right Section
>
> Y.1 gun fired 9 rounds on enemy wire opposite PICCADILLY. Effects good. Y.3 gun fired 10 rounds on wire & enemy parapet opposite G.7. Cut his wire & damaged parapet very badly & blew up an O.P.

## Open questions / uncertain items (September 1916)
- The cover reads "Volume 5" (with "15." written below it), but the contents page reads "Volume 16-9" — both as written; the contents page appears to be a later typed summary and disagrees with the cover on the volume number. The contents page's "Diary Text 13 Pages" matches the 13 diary sheets (Sheets 3–15).
- The diary sheets are numbered 2–15 at the top right: the title sheet is Sheet No. 2 and the diary text runs on Sheets 3–15.
- The Place column is blank on every diary sheet. The only place named in the month is STEENVOORDE (rest billets, 1–4 September); no other place is given. The "1st Canadian Division", "4th Australian Div", "19th Div" and "Lahore Divisional Trench Mortars" are units, not places.
- 5 September: "Orders received to relieve Lahore Divisional Trench Mortars who were supporting 1st Canadian Division" — "Lahore" is read as written.
- 7 September: "Stood to to support Infantry raiding party" is transcribed with the double "to" as written.
- 10 September: the map reference reads "N 15 b. e 2 (Sheet 28 S.W.)" as written; the exact letters of the reference are not certain.
- 12 September: the "Centre Section" heading appears overwritten/corrected in the same hand; transcribed as "Centre Section".
- 14 September: "avenues 20" wire being plainly discernible" is transcribed as written; the word "avenues" (lanes cut through the wire) is as read. The trench designator is read as "Trench 7.4." but the hand could be "Z.4." (cf. "Z.4." on 13 September).
- 15 September: "Pall Mall Guns" is read as written — a named gun or gun group, not further explained in the diary.
- 16 September: "opposite G.7." is read as written; cf. "G.2. Trench" (27 September) and "our Trench G.1." (29 September), which are the same style of trench designator.
- 17 September: the map reference reads "N 24 a. 8.6. Sheet 28 S.W. @ 470 yards" as written; the exact letters/figures are not certain. "Operation Order fired 74 rounds" is transcribed as written. The diary's "Total Rounds fired to-day 327" does not add up from the figures given for the day (57 + 14 + 77 + 48 + 74 + 52 + 10 = 332); the discrepancy is the diary's own and has not been corrected.
- 24 September (continued): the section heading "Right Section" is struck through and "Left Section" written above it in the same hand. The last word of "Guns cleaning & carrying [uncertain]" is not read with confidence; it appears to read "bombs" but the letters are unclear.
- 25 September, 5.30 p.m.: the last word of "All rounds were effective & enemy quiet [uncertain]" is not read with confidence; it appears to read "succeeding" but the sense is unclear.
- 25 September: "which are rather loose owing to heavy enemy Trench Mortar shelling" — "rather loose" is read as written.
- 27 September: "'Strafed' the enemy severely this morning at request of O.C. Royal Inniskilling Fusiliers" — "O.C." is read as written; the Royal Inniskilling Fusiliers are named as the requesting unit.
- The Remarks column initials (most often reading "Matt.") are not transcribed per entry; they could not be read with confidence beyond that recurring reading.
- The O.C.'s signature at the foot of Sheet 15 appears to begin "J. F. B—" but is not read with confidence; it is recorded as [uncertain] and not used as a name.
- Names read as: General Lipsett; O.C. Royal Inniskilling Fusiliers (unit only, no personal name). No other personal names appear in the month.
- No human casualties are recorded in this month: on 22 September the diary states "Our positions were heavily shelled but had no casualties." Guns/emplacements put out of action: No. 4 Gun by a Rum Jar on 17 September (later repaired, 20 September); Y.3 gun on 25 September; No. 4 Gun emplacement hit by a "Whiz-Bang" on 27 September. A dugout was smashed on 21 September. No map coordinates (latitude/longitude) are given; map references are given only as trench-map sheet references ("Sheet 28 S.W.") and trench designators (N 15, N 24, Z.4, J.3, J.3R, H.2, H.3, G.7, G.2, G.1, 7.4.).
- Places named: STEENVOORDE; PECKHAM HOUSE; PICCADILLY (trench/locality name, as written).
