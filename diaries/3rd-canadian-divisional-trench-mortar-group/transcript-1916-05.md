## Transcription conventions
Original spelling, punctuation and abbreviations kept. Diary text is handwritten throughout (Army Form C. 2118, "WAR DIARY or INTELLIGENCE SUMMARY" — "INTELLIGENCE SUMMARY" struck through on the printed heading). `[uncertain]` marks words I could not read with confidence — never guessed. Place column reads "Field" throughout. "Batt." = Battery; "Battn." = Battalion; "Lieut." = Lieutenant; "Gnr." = Gunner; "N.C.O." = Non-Commissioned Officer; "O.R." = other ranks; "P.P.C.L.I." = Princess Patricia's Canadian Light Infantry; "C.I.B." = Canadian Infantry Brigade; "O.P." = Observation Post; "H.Q." = Headquarters; "M.G.E." = as written (not expanded); "2/Lieut." = Second Lieutenant; "Sgt." = Sergeant. Battery designations are written "X3C", "Y3C" (also "X 3C", "Y 3C"). The unit stamp on the final sheet reads "TRENCH HOWITZER BRIGADE, 3rd CANADIAN DIV." The "Remarks and references to Appendices" column is blank throughout.

## Pages 3–7 — Diary text (1–31 May 1916)

Army Form C. 2118, "WAR DIARY or INTELLIGENCE SUMMARY". The printed footer reads "2449 Wt. W14957/M90 750,000 1/16 J.B.C. & A. Forms/C.2118/12."

### Page 3 — 1–5 May 1916 (Sheet No. 1)

Place: Field.

> **May 1st.** — Two new drafts of reinforcements have arrived from the Canadian Base Depot recently, their training was carried on in camp and one N.C.O and twelve men were sent to the trenches for instruction. It was at this stage that the Canadian Field Artillery commenced to take over the work of the Trench Mortars of this Division from the infantry (P.P.C.L.I and 49th Battn) who at that time composed the unit. This transfer of course could not be made in a day, but as soon as it was deemed advisable, the infantry men of the unit were given the option of returning to their units or remaining with us.
>
> **May 2nd.** — Five rounds were fired at enemy's front line, and supports behind Sanctuary Wood, and what being observed to have good effects. Y3C Battery under Lieut. W. M. Norman, relieved X3C Battery under Lieut. C. H. Davidson. On this date, three gunners [uncertain] reported to this unit from 1st Canadian Heavy Artillery.
>
> **May 3rd.** — Four rounds fired at enemy trench behind Sanctuary Wood in retaliation for enemy shelling. In accordance with letter from [uncertain] Corps, all T. Friction Tubes were returned to [uncertain] Divisional Ammunition Column in exchange for .303 blank cartridges to be used with Lee-Enfield mechanism, the T. tubes having proved very unsatisfactory.
>
> **May 4th.** — Fired three rounds in retaliation for enemy artillery activity. Lieut. H. O. Bennett reported for duty from the Base.
>
> **May 5th.** — Five rounds fired at Birdcage or Noah's Ark (J.19 A.8-9) very effective. Y3C Battery under Lieut. Norman relieved X3C Battery under Lieut. Davidson. Eleven men of the new drafts under an N.C.O. were sent to the trenches for instruction

### Page 4 — 5–10 May 1916 (Sheet No. 2)

Place: Field.

> **May 5th (continued).** — in the place of men sent up on 1st May. This party as well as the first party were engaged during their tour of instruction in building new gun emplacements, magazines and dug-outs, and carrying up ammunition. On this day a few enemy shells fell in our camp without causing any damage. There were three [uncertain] casualties, one man being admitted to Hospital.
>
> **May 6th.** — Fired five rounds at enemy front line at Sanctuary Wood. One officer and thirteen other ranks were sent to 2nd Army School at Berthen to undergo a course of instruction in working Heavy Trench Mortars (240 m.m.).
>
> **May 7th.** — Fired four rounds retaliation for enemy artillery. X3C Battery relieved Y3C Battery.
>
> **May 8th.** — Fired five rounds at M.G.E at J.19 C 4.4 at request of infantry. One other rank reported from Base.
>
> **May 9th.** — Fired four rounds retaliation for enemy artillery. A working party of 15 other ranks sent to trenches to assist in building new gun emplacements, dug-outs, and magazines, and carrying ammunition.
>
> **May 10th.** — Fired six rounds at enemy front lines and supports at Sanctuary Wood. A working party of 20 other ranks sent to trenches, building emplacements, dug-outs, and magazines, and carrying ammunition from our "dump" at Zillebeke Village to our store in Maple Copse and to the guns. Work on new emplacements is being rushed in order that we may have two batteries operating in the trenches at a time.

### Page 5 — 11–20 May 1916 (Sheet No. 3)

Place: Field.

> **May 11th.** — Twentyfive rounds at Birdcage (119 A 8.9) [uncertain] 801940 Gnr. Backus N.J., 86044 Gnr. Mackensie admitted to Hospital. Two [uncertain] + [uncertain] being carried to new positions
>
> **May 12th.** — Two rounds at enemy front line. One N.C.O sent to Base School for instruction.
>
> **May 13th.** — Fired four rounds retaliation. New gun emplacements, magazines + dug outs for gun crews are now complete on St. Peter St, Vigo St. and Hedges St. and new 2" Howitzers have been sent up from H.Q. and placed in position in these emplacements. During the last few days enemy trench mortars have been fairly active especially in the vicinity of the Birdcage and the Appendix. Also hostile artillery fire did considerable damage to our positions necessitating several working parties 91687 Gnr Wilson J. [uncertain] Y3C. Battery relieved X 3C. Battery.
>
> **May 14th.** — Fired 6 rounds at Birdcage. Working party 16 all ranks sent to trenches. 1 officer + 13 other ranks returned from Berthen School. Reinforcements 22 other ranks reported from Base.
>
> **May 15th 16th.** — Ten other ranks attached to this unit were returned to other units (P.P.C.L.I + 49th C.I.B. Fired ten rounds at enemy's front lines at request of infantry.
>
> **May 17th.** — Fired 14 rounds at request of infantry. Today Y3C. Batt. took over Right Position from Hedge St. to Vigo St. and half of X3C. Batt. taking over Left Position, Bydand Ave. Gordon Rd. + 134. [uncertain] Just behind trench 64-4. Right position guns are at Hedge St. Crab Crawl and behind Romers Line half way between Sackville St. and Pinch Gut
>
> **May 18th.** — Fired few rounds at enemy's front line and supports at Sanctuary Woods. Gun positions are being connected with H.Q. at Maple Copse, by telephone.
>
> **May 19th & 20th.** — During the last few days the enemy's mortars have not been so active, possibly due to retaliation from our own mortars

### Page 7 — 31 May 1916 (summary) (Sheet No. 5)

Place: Field.

> **May 31st.** — Wire has now been laid connecting all the guns in the Right position with H.Q.s at Maple Copse + O.P. (Clk) Telephone was [uncertain] in at St Peter St. gun. In the left position Bydand Ave gun was connected with O.P on Sackville St. The enemy's artillery has been very active on our front, during the last week, all our guns being heavily shelled altho' no direct hits were obtained. Our only casualties were three Gunners suffering from shell shock. Enemy's minenwerfers were not so active, but our mortars on the contrary fired some 913 rounds at enemy's front line system opposite Sanctuary Wood, Birdcage etc. in retaliation and at request of infantry. Observing officers reported some 200 effective rounds, a great deal of damage being done to dug-outs etc.
>
> [Signed, bottom right:] *[signature — appears to read "H. O. Bennett", Lieut.,] O.C.*
>

## Open questions / uncertain items (May 1916)
- The cover reads "Volume 1" but the contents page reads "Volume 1—5" — both as written; the contents page is a later typed summary. The contents page's "Diary Text 5 Pages" matches the 5 diary sheets (Sheets 1–5).
- The diary is headed "ORIGINAL CONFIDENTIAL" in handwriting at the top of Sheet 1.
- 2 May: a word after "three gunners" (before "reported to this unit") could not be read.
- 3 May: the Corps named in "letter from [uncertain] Corps" and the Divisional Ammunition Column named in "returned to [uncertain] Divisional Ammunition Column" could not be read.
- 5 May (continued): the word before "casualties" in "There were three [uncertain] casualties" could not be read with confidence; it appears to read "draft".
- 11 May: the entry is crowded and partly unclear — the figure before "801940 Gnr. Backus N.J." and the words in "Two [uncertain] + [uncertain] being carried to new positions" could not be read.
- 13 May: the words after "91687 Gnr Wilson J." (before "Y3C. Battery relieved X 3C. Battery") could not be read.
- 15–16 May: the two dates share a single entry as written; the entry is unpunctuated and the closing parenthesis after "49th C.I.B." is missing in the original.
- 17 May: a word or abbreviation after "+ 134." (before "Just behind trench 64-4") could not be read.
- 26 May: "300101 Gnr. Williams W." appears with no verb or explanation in the original.
- 31 May (summary): the word after "Telephone was" (before "in at St Peter St. gun") could not be read; "(Clk)" is read as written and its meaning is unclear.
- The O.C.'s signature at the foot of the May sheets appears to read "H. O. Bennett" — Lieut. H. O. Bennett reported for duty from the Base on 4 May — but the signature is recorded here with that reservation and not used as a certain name.
- The unit stamp on the final sheet is dated 27/7/16, although the diary covers May 1916 — the diary was evidently compiled or stamped later.
- Names read as: Lieut. W. M. Norman; Lieut. C. H. Davidson; Lieut. H. O. Bennett; Lieut. Newton; Lieut. R. B. Cameron; 2/Lieut. Bumstead; 2/Lieut. Notman; Sgt. Morton; Gnr. Backus N.J. (801940); Gnr. Mackensie (86044); Gnr. Wilson J. (91687); Gnr. Riddler H. (43182); Gnr. Williams W. (300101); Gnr. Rice J.E. (96259).
- Casualties in May 1916: three gunners suffering from shell shock (31 May summary, the month's only casualties); several men admitted to hospital sick — Gnr. Backus and Gnr. Mackensie (11 May), Gnr. Riddler (21 May), one unnamed gunner (23 May), Sgt. Morton (27 May), Gnr. Rice evacuated sick (31 May); one man admitted to hospital on 5 May (unnamed). No deaths recorded. No map coordinates (latitude/longitude) are given; targets are given only as trench-map references (e.g. J.19 A.8-9, J.19 C 4.4).
- Total rounds fired in May 1916 as summarised on 31 May: some 913 rounds; about 200 reported effective.
