# War Diary — 3rd Canadian Divisional Trench Mortar Group — December 1917

- **Unit:** 3rd Canadian Divisional Trench Mortar Group (3rd C.D.T.M.B's. / Trench Mortar Batteries, 3rd Canadian Division)
- **Month:** December 1917 (Volume 20)
- **Source:** Library and Archives Canada, Collection Search Item ID 2004819 (RG 9 III-D-3, vol. 4980, file 592, reel T-10809, finding aid 9-52)
- **Pages:** December 1917, p. 1 – p. 14 (image files page-0171 – page-0184)
- **December 1917 — COMPLETE** (pages 1–14): cover, contents, diary text 1–31 December, Appendix A (firing reports 24, 25, 27, 28, 29, 30 and 31 December 1917) and Appendix B (extract from 3rd Canadian Divisional Artillery Operation Order No. 162, 20 December 1917). Transcribed 2026-10-27.

## Transcription conventions
Original spelling, punctuation and abbreviations kept. The diary text is handwritten throughout on Army Form C. 2118, "WAR DIARY or INTELLIGENCE SUMMARY" — "INTELLIGENCE SUMMARY" struck through on the printed heading. `[uncertain]` marks words I could not read with confidence — never guessed. "do" is the diarist's ditto for the preceding entry. "O.R." / "O.R's" = Other Ranks; "C.C.R.C." = Canadian Corps Reinforcement Camp (as written); "C.D.A.C." = Canadian Divisional Ammunition Column (as written); "D.T.M.O." = Divisional Trench Mortar Officer; "C.F.A." = Canadian Field Artillery; "O.O." = Operation Order; "T.M." = Trench Mortar; "H.T.M." = Heavy Trench Mortar. The margin initials on the diary sheets are not read with confidence and are recorded as [initials — uncertain]. The diary is signed at the foot of the last sheet by Capt. J. T. Bennett, O.C. 3rd Cdn Div. T.M. B's. The contents page lists "Volume 17—12" while the cover reads "VOLUME 20" — both as written.

## Open questions / uncertain items (December 1917)
- The cover reads "VOLUME 20" but the contents page reads "Volume 17—12" — both as written; the contents pages for September–December ("Volume 17—9" to "Volume 17—12") form a consistent later typed sequence that disagrees with the covers (17, 18, 19, 20).
- The contents page describes Appendix B as "Extract from 3rd Cdn. Division O.O. No. 168 dated 20-12-17 re move of 3rd C.D. T.M. Bde. on 27-12-17" — but the appendix itself (p. 14) is headed "Operation Order No 162 d 20th Decr 1917" and orders a move on 22 December, not 27 December. Both are transcribed as written; the contents entry appears to be wrong.
- The diary text (22 December) says the Group left Bellery by motor lorry and arrived in Bully Grenay at noon, while Appendix B orders a move from Ames to billets in Les Brebis on 22 December. The two destinations (Bully Grenay vs Les Brebis) and starting points (Bellery vs Ames) disagree as written.
- 1 December: the place after "BELLERY" is not read with confidence — transcribed as "BELLERY [uncertain] 6."
- 11 December: "Duty employed men left with [uncertain]" — the final word(s) are not read with confidence.
- 9 December appears on both diary sheets (p. 4 and p. 5): p. 4 ends with the 9 December entry about the School of Mortars course and 35 men attached to Field Artillery Brigades; p. 5 begins with a further 9 December entry, "5 other ranks from C.C.R.C." Both are transcribed as written.
- The diary entries for 24–31 December all refer to the firing report "of even date", but Appendix A contains no firing report for 26 December — the reports present are for 24, 25, 27, 28, 29, 30 and 31 December. The 26 December report appears to be missing from the file.
- The Place column for 23–31 December gives the divisional boundaries in dense handwriting, partly illegible: "Northern Div Boundary N25d 3.8 — A25c 5.2 — [uncertain] alley; Southern Div Boundary SOUCHEZ RIVER [uncertain] to 3rd Can. Div. as far west as M33a 7.70".
- The firing reports for 24 and 25 December are near-identical (same targets and round counts: 54 + 7 rounds each day); the 25 December sheet adds "Satisfactory" effects and "Several direct hits obtained" where the 24 December sheet has blanks and "3 direct hits obtained". Both are transcribed as written.
- Round totals in Appendix A: 24 Dec 61 rounds (54 + 7); 25 Dec 61 rounds; 27 Dec 114 rounds (12+38+25+5+5+15+14; no total given); 28 Dec 160 rounds; 29 Dec 220 rounds; 30 Dec 124 rounds; 31 Dec 133 rounds (110 + 23).
- No human casualties are recorded in December 1917. No map coordinates (latitude/longitude) are given; locations are trench-map references only.
- Names read as: Capt. J. T. Bennett (O.C., D.T.M.O.); Capt. J. M. Macdonnell (Brigade Major, R.A., 3rd Canadian Division — signatory of Appendix B).
