# War Diary — 3rd Canadian Divisional Trench Mortar Group — October 1916

- **Unit:** 3rd Canadian Divisional Trench Mortar Group, 3rd Canadian Division
- **Month:** October 1916 (Volume 6) — this volume covers 1 to 31 October 1916
- **Source:** Library and Archives Canada, Collection Search Item ID 2004819 (RG 9 III-D-3, vol. 4980, file 592, reel T-10809)
- **Pages:** October 1916, p. 1 – p. 7 (image files page-0052 – page-0058)
- **October 1916 — COMPLETE** (pages 1–7): cover, contents, title sheet, diary text 1–31 October, no appendices among these pages. Transcribed 2026-10-27.

## Transcription conventions
Original spelling, punctuation and abbreviations kept. Diary text is handwritten throughout (Army Form C. 2118, "WAR DIARY or INTELLIGENCE SUMMARY" — "INTELLIGENCE SUMMARY" struck through on the printed heading of the diary sheets, pp. 4–7; not struck through on the title sheet, p. 3). The printed footer on each diary sheet reads "1875 Wt. W593/826 1,000,000 4/15 J.B.C. & A. A.D.S.S./Forms/C. 2118." `[uncertain]` marks words I could not read with confidence — never guessed. Dates bracketed together in the Date column share a single entry. The Place column is entered only for 1 October ("LACLYTTE") and is blank thereafter; the Hour column is blank throughout. "Divl." = Divisional; "O.C." = Officer Commanding; "i/c" = in charge; "A.G." = Adjutant-General; "X.3.C", "Y.3.C", "Z.3.C" = the Group's three batteries (written by the diarist sometimes with and sometimes without stops). "H.R." and "J.P." are the initials appended to each battery's firing line. The Remarks column on each sheet carries the O.C.'s initialling ("J.P. o/c") and, on p. 4, a signature.

## Pages 4–7 — Diary text (1–31 October 1916)

Army Form C. 2118, "WAR DIARY or INTELLIGENCE SUMMARY". The printed heading "INTELLIGENCE SUMMARY" is struck through on each sheet.

### Page 4 — 1–7 October 1916 (Sheet No. 3)

Place: LACLYTTE.

> **Oct 1.** — Received orders to come out of action and report to 3rd Corps at the SOMME.
>
> **2–3.** — on train
>
> **4.** — Arrived at ACHEUX
>
> **5.** — Billeted in ENGLEBELMER
>
> **6.** — Work commenced on six gun emplacements in front of BEAUMONT HAMEL from PICCADILLY to CARLISLE trenches
>
> **7.** — Emplacements completed. Attached to 39th Division. Objective — wire cutting on 700 yard front. Wire is very thick and will take a great many rounds to make a number of good gaps. Y.3.C Battery fired 290 rounds. Hubert Reads
> X.3.C. Battery fired 150 rounds. [uncertain]
> Enemy artillery retaliated very heavily. Put all guns out of action.

Remarks column: [signature — [uncertain]]

### Page 5 — 8–22 October 1916 (Sheet No. 4)

> **Oct 8, 16, 18.** — Building new more advanced positions to try & reach enemy support line wire. Ammunition carried to magazines.
>
> **19.** — 51st Division relieved 39th Division and took over our Trench Mortars. On left flank Division front 31st Division Trench Mortars. On Right 63rd Division. 39th Division Trench Mortars relieved our X.3.C battery. Z.3.C Battery was brought into action on left of Y.3.C Battery and X.3.C Battery was brought in Reserve. Unable to reach enemy support line wire. Carrying on with our objective — Wire cutting. Enemy front line wire—
>
> Y.3.C Battery fired 38 rounds H.R.
> Z.3.C Battery fired 85 rounds J.P.
>
> **20.** — Y.3.C Battery fired 212 rounds H.R.
> Z.3.C Battery fired 190 rounds J.P.
> Y.3.C Battery fired 74 rounds H.R.
>
> **21.** — Z.3.C Battery fired 157 rounds J.P.
> Y.3.C Battery fired 200 rounds H.R.
>
> **22.** — Z.3.C Battery fired 258 rounds J.P.

Remarks column: initials written vertically, apparently "J.P. o/c" [uncertain].

### Page 6 — 23–29 October 1916 (Sheet No. 5)

>
> **25.** — Y.3.C Battery fired 190 rounds H.R.
> Z.3.C Battery fired 130 rounds J.P.
>
> **26.** — Y.3.C Battery fired 157 rounds H.R.
> Z.3.C Battery fired 128 rounds J.P.
>
> **27.** — Y.3.C Battery fired 90 rounds H.R.
> Z.3.C Battery fired 110 rounds J.P.
>
> **28.** — Y.3.C Battery fired 200 rounds H.R.
> Z.3.C Battery fired 160 rounds J.P.

Remarks column: "J.P. o/c" at the top of the sheet; beside the 28 October entry, "See Appendix" (preceded by a small mark, possibly a cross).

### Page 7 — 30–31 October 1916 (Sheet No. 6)

> **Oct 30–31.** — No firing — Cleaning up emplacements — guns and barrels.
>
> RESULTS: —
>
> MAP SHEET 57D
>
> A good continuous gap 25 yards wide made in "Y" Ravine — location Q11.C 5.4.
>
> Numerous good & continuous gaps made South of the "Y" Ravine from Q11.C.5.4 to Q17.C 8½.5
> Wire between these locations has been hammered & broken.
>
> On North side of Ravine we made a 30 yards continuous gap (location Q10.D 8½.4) & smashed the wire on both sides. Gap has since been repaired by the enemy.
>
> Rounds fired 3399.
>
> [Signed:] [signature — [uncertain]] Capt, O.C. 3rd Canadian Divl. Trench Mortars, 1/11/16.

Remarks column: "J.P. o/c".

## Open questions / uncertain items (October 1916)
- The cover reads "Volume 6." but the contents page reads "Volume 16-10" — both as written; the contents page appears to be a later typed summary and disagrees with the cover on the volume number. The contents page's "Diary Text 4 Pages" matches the 4 diary sheets (Sheets 3–6).
- The cover has "No." and "1." at the top right, and a small mark below "Volume 6." that is not read with confidence.
- No entries are recorded for 9–15 or 17 October. The 8, 16 and 18 October dates are bracketed together under a single entry, and 23–24 and 30–31 October are likewise bracketed.
- 7 October: "Y.3.C Battery fired 290 rounds. Hubert Reads" — "Hubert Reads" is read as written; the final "s" may be a flourish, so the name may be "Hubert Read". The "X.3.C." in the next line is preceded by a struck-out/corrected word that is not read with confidence, and the initials after "X.3.C. Battery fired 150 rounds." are not read with confidence (they appear to read "Jalmy") — both are recorded here as [uncertain] and not used as a name.
- The initials appended to the firing lines are read as "H.R." (Y.3.C Battery) and "J.P." (Z.3.C Battery). The first letter of the second monogram is read as "J" but in monogram form could be "S"; the Remarks-column initialling "J.P. o/c" on pp. 6–7 and the O.C.'s signature on p. 7 support "J.P."
- The date numbers on pp. 5–6 sit roughly alongside their day's firing lines rather than exactly on the first line, so the day-boundaries for the firing lines (notably the 74-round line on 20/21 October and the 200/160-round lines on 27/28 October) are inferred from alignment, not certain. The grouping used here is: 19 Oct — 38, 85; 20 Oct — 212, 190, 74; 21 Oct — 157, 200; 22 Oct — 258; 25 Oct — 190, 130; 26 Oct — 157, 128; 27 Oct — 90, 110; 28 Oct — 200, 160, 170, 160; 29 Oct — 200, 170.
- 31 October (Results): "Rounds fired 3399." is read as written, but the daily firing figures as read in this transcript sum to 3,519, not 3,399 — a difference of 120. Either the diarist's total is wrong or one of the daily figures is misread; no daily figure has been altered to fit the total.
- 31 October (Results): the map references read "Q11.C 5.4", "Q17.C 8½.5" and "Q10.D 8½.4" — the "8½" is written as a fraction in each case. "MAP SHEET 57D" has the "D" written as a superscript.
- The 28 October entry is marked "See Appendix" in the Remarks column, but no appendix is listed on the contents page and none is present among these 7 pages.
- The signature in the Remarks column on p. 4 is not read with confidence; it appears to begin with "S." followed by a long word and a flourish.
- The O.C.'s signature at the foot of p. 7 is not read with confidence; it appears to read "J. P. B......" followed by what may be "Lt.", with "Capt" below it and "O.C. 3rd Canadian Divl. Trench Mortars" and the date "1/11/16". It is recorded here as [uncertain] and not used as a name.
- Page 3: "Officer i/c A.G.'s office at Base." — "Base" is read as written; the final letter could be read as "Bass".
- Names read as: Hubert Reads (H.R.); J.P. (O.C., Capt.). No other personal names are given.
- Places read as: LACLYTTE; ACHEUX; ENGLEBELMER; BEAUMONT HAMEL; PICCADILLY and CARLISLE trenches; the SOMME; "Y" Ravine; Map Sheet 57D, locations Q11.C 5.4, Q17.C 8½.5, Q10.D 8½.4.
- No human casualties are recorded in this month: the only damage noted is to the guns themselves — on 7 October enemy artillery retaliation "Put all guns out of action". No map coordinates (latitude/longitude) are given; locations are given only as trench/map references.
