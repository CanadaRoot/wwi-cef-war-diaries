# War Diary — 3rd Canadian Divisional Trench Mortar Group — October 1917

- **Unit:** 3rd Canadian Divisional Trench Mortar Group (3rd C.D.T.M.B's. / 3rd Canadian Divl. T.M. Batteries)
- **Month:** October 1917 (Volume 18)
- **Source:** Library and Archives Canada, Collection Search Item ID 2004819 (RG 9 III-D-3, vol. 4980, file 592, reel T-10809, finding aid 9-52)
- **Pages:** October 1917, p. 1 – p. 6 (image files page-0159 – page-0164)
- **October 1917 — COMPLETE** (pages 1–6): cover, contents, diary text 1–31 October, Appendix A (progress of work report, 6 October 1917). Transcribed 2026-10-27.

## Transcription conventions
Original spelling, punctuation and abbreviations kept. The diary text is handwritten throughout on Army Form C. 2118, "WAR DIARY or INTELLIGENCE SUMMARY" — "INTELLIGENCE SUMMARY" struck through on the printed heading. `[uncertain]` marks words I could not read with confidence — never guessed. "C.D.A." = Canadian Divisional Artillery; "D.A.C." = Divisional Ammunition Column; "O.C." = Officer Commanding; "D.T.M.O." = Divisional Trench Mortar Officer; "T.M." = Trench Mortar; "H.T.M." = Heavy Trench Mortar; "Pte." = Private. Each diary entry is initialled "J.T.B." in the margin in ink. The diary is signed at the foot of the last sheet by Capt. J. T. Bennett, O.C. 3rd C.D.T.M.B's. The contents page lists "Volume 17—10" while the cover reads "VOLUME 18" — both as written.

## Pages 4–5 — Diary text (1–31 October 1917)

### Page 4 — 1–23 October 1917

Place: LA TARGETTE CORNER (1–12 October; place column blank thereafter).

> **1917 Oct 1. to 12** — Work on emplacements continuing — Copy of report on progress made appended (See APPENDIX "A") [initials: J.T.B.]
>
> **13.** — Moved from LA TARGETTE. Entrained at MONT ST. ELOY and detrained at TINCQUE where billets were found for the night. Attached to 52ND BATTALION for the move [initials: J.T.B.]
>
> **14** — [No entry.]
>
> **15 4.30am** — Proceeded to CAESTRE by train. Arrived 11.30 am. at GODEWAERSVELDE and marched to CAESTRE where billets were found with 52ND BN. [initials: J.T.B.]
>
> **16. 6am** — Rejoined 3RD C.D.A. and marched to WATOU where billets were found for the night. [initials: J.T.B.]
>
> **17** — Marched to ST LAWRENCE CAMP in the POPERINGHE area and encamped for the night [initials: J.T.B.]
>
> **18** — Marched to WARRINGTON CAMP (H2.C.2.5 Sheet 28 BELGIUM & FRANCE where permanent quarters were secured [initials: J.T.B.]
>
> **19-22** — [No entry.]
>
> **23.** — Officers [uncertain] attached to Batteries & D.A.C. for duty [initials: J.T.B.]

### Page 5 — 25–31 October 1917

> **1917 Oct 25 11.45 P.M.** — Pte. Walpole killed by bomb dropped from an enemy aeroplane and Gunner Turner wounded. [initials: J.T.B.]
>
> **26 to 31.** — Officers and men still away on fatigues [initials: J.T.B.]
>
> [Signed:] J. T. Bennett. / Capt. / O.C. 3rd C.D.T.M.B's.

## Open questions / uncertain items (October 1917)
- The cover reads "VOLUME 18" but the contents page reads "Volume 17—10" — both as written; the September contents page likewise read "Volume 17—9" against a cover of "VOLUME. 17", so the contents pages appear to be a later typed summary using a different numbering.
- 14 October and 19–22 October and 24 October have no diary entry (blank dates).
- 23 October: one word before "attached" is not read with confidence — transcribed as "Officers [uncertain] attached to Batteries & D.A.C. for duty". It may be a surname.
- 25 October: "Pte. Walpole killed by bomb dropped from an enemy aeroplane and Gunner Turner wounded." No initials or service numbers are given; Gunner Turner's first name is not given.
- The diary text for October is only 2 pages as the contents page states, and the month's entries are brief: most of the month (1–12 October) is covered by a single combined entry, and 26–31 October by another.
- The move of 13–18 October took the Group from La Targette Corner (near Arras) via Mont St. Eloy, Tincque, Caestre, Watou and St Lawrence Camp to Warrington Camp in the Ypres area, rejoining 3rd C.D.A. on 16 October.
- No map coordinates (latitude/longitude) are given; the Warrington Camp location is given only as "H2.C.2.5 Sheet 28 BELGIUM & FRANCE".
- Names read as: Capt. J. T. Bennett (O.C., D.T.M.O.); Pte. Walpole (killed 25 October); Gunner Turner (wounded 25 October).
- Casualties in October 1917: 1 killed (Pte. Walpole, 25 October, by enemy aeroplane bomb), 1 wounded (Gunner Turner, 25 October).
