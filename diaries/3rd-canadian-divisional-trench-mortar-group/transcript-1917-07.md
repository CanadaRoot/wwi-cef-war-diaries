# War Diary — 3rd Canadian Divisional Trench Mortar Group — July 1917

- **Unit:** 3rd Canadian Divisional Trench Mortar Group (titled "3rd Canadian Divl Trench Mortars Batteries" on the title sheet, p. 3)
- **Month:** July 1917
- **Source:** Library and Archives Canada, Collection Search Item ID 2004819 (RG 9 III-D-3, vol. 4980, file 592, reel T-10809)
- **Pages:** July 1917, p. 1 – p. 9 (image files page-0122 – page-0130)
- **July 1917 — COMPLETE** (pages 1–9): cover, contents, title sheet, diary text 1–31 July, no appendices. Transcribed 2026-10-27.

## Transcription conventions
Original spelling, punctuation and abbreviations kept. Diary text is handwritten throughout on Army Form C. 2118, "WAR DIARY or INTELLIGENCE SUMMARY" ("(Erase heading not required.)" — neither heading is struck through). The printed footer on each sheet reads "2353 Wt. W2544/1454 700,000 5/15 D. D. & L. A.D.S.S. Forms/C 2118." `[uncertain]` marks words I could not read with confidence — never guessed. The diarist's initials appear at the end of most entries and read as "W.B."; they are transcribed each time. The Place column gives "Avion" only on Sheet 3 (1–3 July); it is blank on Sheet 4, "Cont." on Sheet 5, and the dates "July 21st", "July 22" and "July 24" are written diagonally in the Place column on Sheets 6–8. The "1917" in the Date column heading is struck through on Sheets 6–8. The "Remarks and references to Appendices" column is blank throughout. "T.M." = Trench Mortar; "H.T.M." = Heavy Trench Mortar; "M.T.M." = Medium Trench Mortar; "C.R.A." = Commander Royal Artillery; "O.R." = other ranks; "Amm" = Ammunition; "R.R." = Railway; "D.T.M.G." = Divisional Trench Mortar Group; "Divn." = Division; "Bn." = Battalion; "Divl" = Divisional; "A.A.G." = Assistant Adjutant General; "G.H.Q." = General Headquarters. Map references are transcribed as written (sheet letter, square, sub-square letter, figures). Firing reports are transcribed as tables, preserving the diarist's column headings and ditto marks (").

## Page 4 — 1–3 July 1917 (Sheet No. 3)

Place: Avion.

> **Avion, July 1.** — Enemy appeared to be very nervous on our front during last 24 hours putting frequent bursts of fire in the vicinity of Avion during the night and early morning. Hostile Trench Mortars were also more active than usual. W.B.
>
> **2.** — Our front in Avion was reconnoitred for the purpose of selecting sites for Heavy & Medium T.M Emplacements. Map locations of selected positions:- Heavy T2 a 9½. 9. Medium N39 c. 5. 2 - Sheet BREWERY scale 1-10.000. Ground in this vicinity is not suitable for permanent emplacements until line is more settled. W.B.
>
> **3.** — Work commenced on new emplacements but owing to hostile shelling hard progress was made. W.B.

## Page 5 — 4–14 July 1917 (Sheet No. 4)

Place column blank.

> **July 4.5.6th** — Work continued on emplacements. W.B.
>
> **7.** — Reserve Amm magazine in BOIS DE LA CHAUDIERE blown up all ammunition destroyed. One Sergeant seriously wounded. Relief of working parties. W.B.
>
> **8.** — Work on emplacements continued. W.B.
>
> **9.** — Medium Trench Mortar Emplacement destroyed by hostile fire. Guns buried. No casualties but crews badly shaken up. Visited by General Staff. W.B.
>
> **10/11.12th 13th+14th** — Work continued on emplacements. Account of hostile shelling and enemy planes continually flying low over the positions the work was considerably hindered. Medium T.M. [continued on next sheet]

## Page 6 — 10–14 July (continued), 15–20 July 1917 (Sheet No. 5)

Place: Cont.

> **10/11.12th 13th+14th (continued).** — registered its Zero line with five rounds. W.B.
>
> **July 15.** — Work in line continued. Detailed 7 O.R's. to T.M. Course at First Army T.M. School. W.B.
>
> **16th * 17th * 18th + 19th** — Work on Medium & Heavy T.M's. Continued. Ammunition being carried forward and lines of communication improved. W.B.
>
> **20.** — Operation Order received from C.R.A. to fire in the preparatory bombardment in support of Minor operation by the 116th Bn. 9th Brigade. [struck through: Copy of firing report Heavy T.M.] Unable to fire. Mortars out of action by hostile fire. W.B.

## Open questions / uncertain items (July 1917)
- The cover reads "VOLUME 15" but the contents page reads "Volume 17-7" — both as written; the contents page appears to be a later typed summary and disagrees with the cover on the volume number. The contents page's "Diary Text 6 Pages" matches the 6 diary sheets (Sheets 3–8); the title sheet (Sheet 2) is additional.
- The cover has "No" written at the top right corner, "1" at the right margin, and "6." written below "VOLUME 15".
- The title sheet (p. 3) calls the unit "3rd Canadian Divl Trench Mortars Batteries", while the cover and contents call it the "3rd Canadian Divisional Trench Mortar Group". "ORIGINAL" is stamped diagonally across the title sheet.
- 2 July: the Heavy mortar map reference reads "T2 a 9½. 9." as written; it may be T.2.a.9.5.9. The Medium reference reads "N39 c. 5. 2 - Sheet BREWERY scale 1-10.000."
- 3 July: "hard progress was made" is transcribed as written; the phrasing is odd and may be a slip for "little progress".
- 10–14 July: "Account of hostile shelling" is transcribed as written; it may be a slip for "On account of hostile shelling". The entry runs from the foot of Sheet 4 onto the head of Sheet 5 ("Medium T.M. registered its Zero line with five rounds").
- 16th–19th July: the dates are written "16th * 17th * 18th + 19th" — the * and + marks are as written, possibly grouping or ditto marks.
- 20 July: the line "Copy of firing report Heavy T.M." is struck through in the entry. The Operation Order was to fire in the preparatory bombardment for the Minor operation by the 116th Bn., 9th Brigade, but the mortars were out of action by hostile fire and unable to fire.
- 21 July Heavy firing report: target references "N33. a. 65. 55.", "R.R. from N33 a. 65. 55 - N27 c. 8.0" and "R.R. N33 a. 90. 75." are transcribed as written.
- 22 July M.T.M. report (Sheet 6): the time reads "1 p.m. to 3 p.m." — the end time could also be read as 2 p.m.
- 22 July Heavy firing report (Sheet 7): the second target reads "TRENCH N33 c. [uncertain]" — the word after "N33 c." could not be read with confidence. "2 hits here. Somewhat effective." — "Somewhat" is not fully certain.
- Two separate M.T.M. firing reports are dated 22 July (one on Sheet 6, one on Sheet 7) with different times, round counts and results — as written; the diary gives no explanation.
- 24 July: "bomb store at N32 c 4. 5." — the figures are as written and could read "4.5".
- 25th–29th July: "3rd Canadian D.T.M.G. temporarily attached to 4th Canadian Divn. for operations" — the unit abbreviation reads "D.T.M.G." as written.
- 30 July: the word "commenced" is struck through in "New Heavy T.M. Emplacement [commenced] located at N32 a 1.8."
- The diarist's initials at the end of entries read as "W.B." The signature at the foot of Sheet 8 is not read with confidence; it appears to read "W.O.B. … H, Capt., O.C. 3rd C.D.T.M.G" but is recorded here as [uncertain] and not used as a name.
- No person is named anywhere in this month: the only casualty is one unnamed Sergeant seriously wounded on 7 July; the General Staff visit on 9 July names no one; the 116th Bn. and 9th Brigade are units, not people.
- No human casualties other than the one seriously wounded Sergeant on 7 July: a Medium Trench Mortar emplacement was destroyed on 9 July with no casualties, crews badly shaken up. No map coordinates (latitude/longitude) are given; positions and targets are given only as trench-map references on the BREWERY sheet (1:10,000).
- Every day of July 1917 has an entry (some dates grouped into single entries: 4–6, 10–14, 16–19, 25–29 July). No dates are missing.
- Names/units read as: Avion; Bois de la Chaudiere; 116th Bn.; 9th Brigade; 4th Canadian Division; First Army T.M. School; General Staff; C.R.A.
