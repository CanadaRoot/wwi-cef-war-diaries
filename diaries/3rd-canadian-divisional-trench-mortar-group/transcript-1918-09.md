# War Diary — 3rd Canadian Divisional Trench Mortar Group — September 1918

- **Unit:** 3rd Canadian Divisional Trench Mortar Group (Trench Mortar Batteries, 3rd Canadian Division)
- **Month:** September 1918
- **Source:** Library and Archives Canada, Collection Search Item ID 2004819 (RG 9 III-D-3, vol. 4980, file 592, reel T-10809)
- **Pages:** September 1918, p. 1 – p. 13 (image files page-0334 – page-0346)
- **September 1918 — COMPLETE** (pages 1–13): cover, contents, title sheet, diary text 1–30 September, no appendices. Transcribed 2026-10-27.

## Transcription conventions
Original spelling, punctuation and abbreviations kept. Diary text is handwritten throughout on Army Form C. 2118, "WAR DIARY or INTELLIGENCE SUMMARY" (the printed heading is not struck through on these sheets). `[uncertain]` marks words I could not read with confidence — never guessed. Map references are transcribed as written (e.g. "Q.34b 20.80", "O.23d 10.00"). "D.T.M.O." = Divisional Trench Mortar Officer; "C.F.A." = Canadian Field Artillery; "C.D.A." = Canadian Divisional Artillery; "C.D.A.C." = Canadian Divisional Ammunition Column; "C.C.R.C." = Canadian Corps Reinforcement Camp; "O.R." = other ranks; "N.C.O." = non-commissioned officer; "rds" = rounds; "W.L." = Wagon Lines; "M.G." = machine gun; "C.M.R." = Canadian Mounted Rifles; "E.T.M." = as written (not expanded); "do" = ditto. An initialled mark appears in the "Remarks and references to Appendices" column on most sheets; it is not read with confidence and is not transcribed per entry. The "Hour" column is blank throughout. The printed footer on each diary sheet reads "A5834 Wt. W4973/M687 750,000 8/16 D. D. & L. Ltd. Forms/C.2118/13."

## Open questions / uncertain items (September 1918)
- The cover reads "VOLUME 29" (29 handwritten after the printed "VOLUME"), but the contents page reads "Volume 18-9" — both as written; the two volume numbers disagree.
- The contents page says "Diary Text 10 Pages", but 11 diary sheets follow (pp. 3–13, including the title sheet on p. 3).
- The contents page types the unit as "3rd Canadi n Divl. Trench Mortar Group." — the "a" of "Canadian" appears dropped by the typewriter; transcribed as written.
- Page 3 (title sheet): the addressee line is read as "D.A.A.G. Canadian Section, G.H.2. 3rd Echelon" — the "G.H.2." may be "G.H.Q." written with a Q that looks like a 2; marked [uncertain].
- Page 3: "CONFIDENTIAL" is written diagonally in ink across the Place column.
- Page 4, Place column: the street is written "RUE PIGNON BIGERRE" — transcribed as written; the second word of the street name is not confirmed.
- 7 September: "Lieut L. S. Fraser Y Battery" — the "Y" carries a small raised flourish that may be a separate mark; read as Y Battery.
- 12 September: the name of the driver attached from 5th C.D.A.C. who was wounded while transporting ammunition forward could not be read — marked [uncertain].
- 12 September: "suspected E.T.M. in Q.32d 15.25 and W.4b 5.6" — "E.T.M." is as written; its expansion is not given in the diary.
- 14 September: "Sgt Wright G.J. No. 1 of Y Battery accidentally wounded" — the initials after "Wright" are read as "G.J." but are not fully clear; marked [uncertain].
- 15 September: "Y Battery fired 10 rds on Q.35a 20.70 and 23 [uncertain] 50 on Q.34b 20.80" — the word between "23" and "50" could not be read; it looks like a fraction-like squiggle ("1/50"). The day's firing figures as read (25 + 10 + 23) are used in the arithmetic note below.
- 19 and 26 September: "1 N.C.O. Casual reported from C.C.R.C." — "Casual" is spelled as written in both entries; it presumably means a casualty, but the diary does not say whether the N.C.O. was wounded, sick, or otherwise.
- 26 September: the newly posted lieutenant is read as "Lieut L. J. Bettison" — the surname could also be "Bettson"; marked [uncertain]. He is "posted to Y Battery" — the "Y" again carries a small raised flourish.
- 27 September: the wire-cutting targets are read as "E.8b and d E.9c E.15a" — the "and d" between the first two references is as written.
- 30 September: the first line reads "O.Rs. temp attached to Field Batteries of 9th and 10th Brigades C.F.A." with no number of O.Rs given, unlike 29 September ("61 O.Rs").
- 30 September: the signing officer's signature is not read with confidence; it appears to begin "J. T. B." followed by an illegible flourish, and is recorded here as [uncertain]. He signs as "Capt. D.T.M.O. 3rd Cdn Divn."
- Ammunition arithmetic: the daily firing figures in the diary add up to 532 rounds (30 + 20 + 35 + 34 + 45 + 58 + 45 + 25 + 50 + 30 + 160), but the month's total given on 30 September is 542 rds — a difference of 10 rounds. The uncertain reading on 15 September may account for part of the difference; the diary gives no further breakdown.
- Names read as: Lieut L. S. Fraser (Y Battery); Sgt Wright G.J. [uncertain]; Lieut L. J. Bettison [uncertain]; Driver [uncertain] (5th C.D.A.C.).
- Casualties in this month (no deaths recorded): 1 O.R. wounded attached to Field Batteries (1 September); Lieut L. S. Fraser slightly wounded (7 September, later evacuated wounded to England 15 September); 1 O.R. wounded by enemy shelling near Wagon Lines (9 September); a driver from 5th C.D.A.C. wounded carrying ammunition forward (12 September); Sgt Wright accidentally wounded (14 September). Several O.Rs evacuated sick and invalided wounded to England, but no names are given. No map coordinates (latitude/longitude) are given; positions are given only as trench-map references (Sheet 51B, O.23d 10.00, Q.34b etc.).
