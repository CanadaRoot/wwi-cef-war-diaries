# War Diary — 3rd Canadian Divisional Trench Mortar Group — June 1918

- **Unit:** 3rd Canadian Divisional Trench Mortar Group (3rd C.D.T.M.B.), 3rd Canadian Division
- **Month:** June 1918 (Volume 26; contents page reads "Volume 18-6")
- **Source:** Library and Archives Canada, Collection Search Item ID 2004819 (RG 9 III-D-3, vol. 4980, file 592, reel T-10809, finding aid 9-52)
- **Pages:** June 1918, p. 1 – p. 7 (image files page-0305 – page-0311)
- **June 1918 — COMPLETE** (pages 1–7): cover, contents, title page, diary text 1–30 June (4 pages), no appendices. Transcribed 2026-10-27.

## Transcription conventions
Original spelling, punctuation and abbreviations kept. Diary text is handwritten throughout (Army Form C. 2118, "WAR DIARY or INTELLIGENCE SUMMARY"). `[uncertain]` marks words I could not read with confidence — never guessed. The Remarks column on the diary sheets carries a repeated short entry on every line that I could not read with confidence; it is recorded as [uncertain] here rather than in the text. "T.M." = Trench Mortar; "H.T.M." = Heavy Trench Mortar; "C.D.T.M.B." = Canadian Divisional Trench Mortar Batteries; "D.T.M.O." = Divisional Trench Mortar Officer; "O.R." = other ranks; "N.C.O." = non-commissioned officer; "Cdn." = Canadian; "Div." / "Divn." = Division; "Arty." = Artillery; "do" = ditto. Map references (e.g. B5a 90.90, R22C 8.4, Sheet 51C) are transcribed as written; they are trench-map sheet/square references, not latitude/longitude. The signature at the foot of the diary sheets reads as initials resembling "J. O. B." followed by a surname initial resembling "H."; it is recorded as [signature — [uncertain]] and not used as a name. The diarist's rank is given as "Capt." and office as "D.T.M.O., 3rd Canadian Division". The Hour column is blank throughout.

## Open questions / uncertain items (June 1918)
- Cover reads "VOLUME 26" with "4." written below it; the contents page reads "Volume 18-6". Both as written. The "4." matches the contents page's "Diary Text 4 Pages" (pages 4–7). The volume number follows the sequence in this unit's earlier transcripts (January 1918: cover "Volume 21", contents "Volume 18-1").
- 1 June: the Brigade competition name is read as "Dunwell" [uncertain] — the fourth letter could be "v" ("Dunvell"). The battery is written "V3c H.T.M. Battery" as shown; the January 1918 transcript renders the same battery designation as "V.3.C. H.T.M. Battery", so "V3c" here is likely that battery with the stops omitted.
- Page 4, top right: a handwritten mark reading "26" — possibly a sheet or page number; not read with full confidence.
- Page 3 (title page): the addressee is read as "D.A.A.G. Canadian Sect. G.H.Q. 3rd Echelon". The January 1918 title page reads the same line as "To A A G. Canadian Section G.H.2. 3rd Echelon", so the first word may be "A.A.G." rather than "D.A.A.G." and "G.H.Q." may be "G.H.2."; the June hand is not clear enough to settle either.
- 24 June: "proceed" [uncertain] — the verb's final letter is unclear; it may read "proceeds". The superscript after "25th" is read as "th".
- 30 June: the final map reference is read as "Sheet FRANCE 51C S.E." — the "S" of "S.E." resembles a "5" in this hand, so it could be read "51C5E"; "S.E." (south-east sheet quadrant) is the likelier reading. The reference "R22C 8.4" is as written in both places.
- The Place column is blank on pages 5–7; the unit is at BELLERY (page 4) until the march on 26 June, then HOUVIN-HOUVIGNEUL 26–30 June, then WAILLY on 30 June.
- Ditto ("do") rows: 4 June repeats the 3 June entry; 6–8 June repeat the 5 June entry; 11–13 June repeat the 10 June entry; 17 June repeats the 16 June entry; 21–22 June repeat the 20 June entry; 28 June repeats the 27 June entry. The number of "do" marks varies with the phrasing of the entry repeated.
- The Remarks column carries the same short entry on every diary line (pages 4–7); it could not be read with confidence and is recorded as [uncertain] rather than guessed.
- No human casualties are recorded in June 1918: no killed, wounded or sick are named, and no names of officers or other ranks appear anywhere in the month except in the signature (not read). No firing reports, appendices, orders or maps are included in these seven pages; the only firing is the 25 test rounds from the 6" NEWTON at WESTREHEM Range on 18 June.
- Names/places read as: BELLERY; AMES; WESTREHEM; BAILLEUL-LES-PERNES; LINGHEM; HOUVIN-HOUVIGNEUL; WAILLY. No commanding officer is named in the diary text; the diarist signs only as Capt., D.T.M.O., 3rd Cdn. Divn.
