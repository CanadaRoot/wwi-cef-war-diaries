# War Diary — 3rd Canadian Divisional Trench Mortar Group — August 1917

- **Unit:** 3rd Canadian Divisional Trench Mortar Group (3rd Canadian Division Trench Mortar Batteries)
- **Month:** August 1917 (Volume 16)
- **Source:** Library and Archives Canada, Collection Search Item ID 2004819 (RG 9 III-D-3, vol. 4980, file 592, reel T-10809)
- **Pages:** August 1917, p. 1 – p. 11 (image files page-0131 – page-0141)
- **August 1917 — COMPLETE** (pages 1–11): cover, contents, title sheet, diary text 1–31 August, plus a month-end summary. No appendices, maps or orders are included. Transcribed 2026-10-27.

## Transcription conventions
Original spelling, punctuation and abbreviations kept. Diary text is handwritten throughout (Army Form C. 2118, "WAR DIARY or INTELLIGENCE SUMMARY" — neither heading is struck through on these sheets). `[uncertain]` marks words I could not read with confidence — never guessed. Map references are transcribed exactly as written (e.g. "N33 a 30.15", "N27 C 35.15", "T2 B 9.9"); the diary gives sheet names (AVION, BREWERY, LENS CANAL) and scales but no latitude/longitude. "T.M." = trench mortar; "H.T.M." = heavy trench mortar; "O.P." = observation post; "C.I.B." = Canadian Infantry Brigade; "C.D.T.M.B." = Canadian Divisional Trench Mortar Battery (as written); "Rly." = railway; "A.A.G" = Assistant Adjutant General. The Place column reads "Avion" throughout; the Hour column is blank throughout. The "Remarks and references to Appendices" column is blank except for initials beside most entries, apparently "J.P." with a flourish, which are not read with confidence and are not transcribed per entry. The Date column on the diary sheets is headed "1917". Entries for 4, 5 and 6 August are grouped under one brace in the Date column.

### Page 4 — 1–7 August 1917

Place: Avion.

> **Avion, Aug 1.** — Work carried on improving and building new emplacements and bomb stores. No firing
>
> **2.** — Right Infantry Brigade (12th C.I.B.) report strong obstacle running along sheet (AVION) from N33 a. 30 15, to N33 a 10.85 consisting of debris tied up into wire which is about six feet high — also strong wire fence from N27 C 35.15 to N33a 30.70 (Scale 1/10.000 BREWERY sheet). They are very anxious to have this taken on by T.M.'s as most of the ground is dead for field guns

### Page 6 — 11 (continued)–15 August 1917

Place: Avion.

> **Avion, Aug 11 (continued).** — bad condition of the roads due to heavy rainfall. Medium T.M. at N33 C 5.4. put out of action by hostile shelling, also trench leading to H.T.M emplacement at T2 B 9.9 receiving direct hits. No firing

### Page 10 — 31 August (continued) and month-end summary

Place: Avion.

> **Avion, Aug 31 (continued).** — to N33 b 3.6. Rear of Railway Embankment. Good bursts were obtained.
>
> Forward Headquarters 3rd C.D.T.M.B.'s Bois de la Chaudiere.
>
> The tasks carried out by the medium & Heavy Batteries for the month of August were very satisfactory. Considering the heavy rain-fall which lasted practically all through the month and the emplacements from which they fired were all in the outpost line. This did not enable us to keep up a continual fire all day, as it would have disclosed our positions. Our tasks were [confined — written above a struck-through word, [uncertain]] to cutting wire for raids, destroying machine gun emplacements, and enemy trench mortars.
>
> As to casualties we were very fortunate, only losing one sergeant wounded who will probably lose the use of his right hand.
>
> Carrying parties for ammunition were arranged

## Open questions / uncertain items (August 1917)
- The cover reads "VOLUME 16" but the contents page reads "Volume 17-8" — both as written. The contents page's "Diary Text 8 Pages" matches the 8 diary sheets (pages 4–11).
- The title sheet (p. 3) calls the unit "3rd Canadian Division Trench Mortar Batteries" while the cover and contents call it the "3rd Canadian Divisional Trench Mortar Group" — both as written.
- 1 August: the first word of the entry reads "Work" but is not entirely clear in the image.
- 2 August: "Right Infantry Brigade (12th C.I.B.)" — the "th" in "12th" is a small superscript and is read as written; "N33 a. 30 15" is read as written (the spacing/punctuation of the map references varies throughout the diary and is kept as written).
- 7 August: the entry runs from the foot of page 4 onto the head of page 5 ("New medium T.M. emplacement (temporary) at N32 b 8.6 to engage enemy T.M behind railway embankment at N33 b which has been firing gas bombs."); both parts are transcribed as written.
- 11 August: the reserve store is at "[uncertain] Dump S17.d" — the word before "Dump" is not read with confidence; it appears to read "Botty". The entry runs from the foot of page 5 onto the head of page 6.
- 11 August (continued): "Medium T.M. at N33 C 5.4. put out of action by hostile shelling" — the sub-square letter reads "C"; "T2 B 9.9" is read as written.
- 12 August: "Red Chateau N33.a 3.7" — the sub-square letter "a" appears corrected/overwritten in the original.
- 21 August: the entry runs from the foot of page 7 onto the head of page 8; the observation point is read as "T3 B. 0.5".
- 22 August: the word "with" in "with satisfactory results" appears written over another word.
- 24 August: the No. 2 medium T.M. lines use ditto marks (") for "medium T.M. fired" and "rounds"; "operation order 3/9336 12th Infantry Brigade" is read as written.
- 27 August: "60% [uncertain] hits were obtained on the wire" — a figure or word after "60%" is struck through and cannot be read.
- 31 August: the entry runs from the foot of page 9 onto page 10, and the month-end summary then runs from page 10 onto page 11.
- Month-end summary (p. 10): "Our tasks were [confined — written above a struck-through word, [uncertain]] to cutting wire for raids" — one word is struck through and another word, apparently "confined", is written above it; neither is read with full confidence.
- Month-end summary (p. 11): "Officers seven from the 4th C.D.T.M.B.'s" — the number is read as "seven" but is not entirely clear.
- The signature at the foot of page 11 is not read with confidence; it appears to read "J. P. B…" followed by "H." and is recorded here as [uncertain] and not used as a name. The initials in the Remarks column beside most entries likewise appear to read "J.P." but are not read with confidence and are not transcribed per entry.
- No battery designations of the form V3C/X3C/Y3C/Z3C appear in this month; the batteries are referred to only as "medium T.M." (No. 1 and No. 2) and "Heavy T.M." / "H.T.M.".
- No personal names are given in this month: the only casualty is "one sergeant wounded who will probably lose the use of his right hand" (month-end summary), with no name, date or battery stated. No other casualties are recorded.
- The diary does not name Hill 70 or any battle: the 15–25 August entries record wire-cutting shoots, the two raids of 19 August, and the shoots under operation order 3/9336 on 24 August, with no battle name given.
- Names/units read as: 12th Canadian Infantry Brigade (12th C.I.B. / 12th Infantry Bde.); 4th C.D.T.M.B.'s; 3rd C.D.T.M.B.'s (Forward Headquarters at Bois de la Chaudiere).
- Places read as: Avion (place column throughout); Bois de la Chaudiere (Forward Headquarters, 31 August summary); map sheets AVION, BREWERY and LENS CANAL; Red Chateau; railway embankment, slag heap, metal trench (all at map references around N33/N27, not named places).
