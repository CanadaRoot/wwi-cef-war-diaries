# War Diary — 3rd Canadian Divisional Trench Mortar Group — November 1916

- **Unit:** 3rd Canadian Divisional Trench Mortar Group (3rd Canadian Division)
- **Month:** November 1916 (Volume 7) — 1st to 30th November 1916
- **Source:** Library and Archives Canada, Collection Search Item ID 2004819 (RG 9 III-D-3, vol. 4980, file 592, reel T-10809)
- **Pages:** November 1916, p. 1 – p. 5 (image files page-0059 – page-0063)
- **November 1916 — COMPLETE** (pages 1–5): cover, contents, title sheet, diary text 1–30 November, no appendices. Transcribed 2026-10-27.

## Transcription conventions
Original spelling, punctuation and abbreviations kept. Diary text is handwritten throughout (Army Form C. 2118, "WAR DIARY or INTELLIGENCE SUMMARY"). `[uncertain]` marks words I could not read with confidence — never guessed. The initials "J.T.B." appear at the end of most entries on the second diary sheet and are transcribed each time. "Y3.C" and "Z3.C" are the two batteries of the Group, written as shown ("Y3.C.", "Z3.C."). "H.R." and "S.N.T." / "S.W.T." are target abbreviations as written (see open questions). "O.P." = Observation Post; "Divl." = Divisional; "O.C." = Officer Commanding; "N.E." = north-east; "Billets" as written. The "Remarks and references to Appendices" column is blank on both diary sheets. The printed footer on the diary sheets reads "1875 Wt. W593/826 1,000,000 4/15 J.B.C. & A. A.D.S.S./Forms/C. 2118."

## Pages 4–5 — Diary text (1–30 November 1916)

Army Form C. 2118, "WAR DIARY or INTELLIGENCE SUMMARY".

### Page 4 — 1–11 November 1916

Place: BEAUMONT HAMEL.

> OBJECTIVE
>
> Work continued cutting Front line wire
>
> **Nov. 1.** — Y3.C. Battery fired 30 rounds Aubert [uncertain]
>
> Z3.C Battery fired 40 rounds. S.W. [uncertain]
>
> **2** — Y3.C Battery fired 60 rounds H.R.
>
> Z3.C. Battery fired 50 rounds S.N.T.
>
> **3** — Y3.C Battery (1 gun) fired 100 rounds H.R.
>
> Z3.C " (1 gun) fired 25 rounds. S.N.T.
>
> Y3.C + Z3.C (2 guns) fired 130 rounds on wire at Q11.c.v.3
>
> **4** — Y3.C. Battery fired 145 rounds H.R.
>
> Z3.C. Battery fired 110 rounds S.N.T.
>
> **5 to 10** — No firing — Cleaning & repairing Guns & emplacements. Magazines rebuilt & filled. J.T.B.
>
> **11** — Y3.C Battery fired 75 rounds H.R.
>
> Z3.C Battery fired 55 rounds S.N.T.

### Page 5 — 12–30 November 1916

Place column blank (Beaumont Hamel carried over from Page 4).

> **Nov 12** — Y3.C Battery fired 102. rounds H.R.
>
> Z3.C Battery fired 105 rounds S.W.T.
>
> Every round fired was observed and line of Communication between Guns & O.P. was splendid J.T.B.
>
> 11 p.m. Guns taken out of action — Enemy Artillery very active J.T.B.
>
> **13** 5.45 A.M. — Infantry advanced and wire presented no obstacle. Took Station road and N.E. part of BEAUMONT-HAMEL then had to consolidate on account of flanks J.T.B.
>
> **14** — Advance Continued. A Party of our men volunteered & carried out wounded. We had one Casualty who died of wounds following day J.T.B.
>
> **15 to 18** — Awaiting orders. J.T.B.
>
> **19** 10 p.m. — Moved to SENLIS. J.T.B.
>
> **20 to 25** — Rest Billets at SENLIS J.T.B.
>
> **26** — Left SENLIS at 2 p.m. J.T.B.
>
> **27** — Arrived at MAROEUIL J.T.B.
>
> **28, 29, 30** — Took over guns in line from 56th Divl. Trench Mortars
>
> [Signed, bottom right:] *[signature — [uncertain]] Capt. / O.C. 3rd Canadian Divl. Trench Mortars*

## Open questions / uncertain items (November 1916)
- The cover reads "Volume 7." with a small "2." written beneath the line; the contents page reads "Volume 16-11" — both as written. The contents page's "Diary Text 2 Pages" matches the two diary sheets (Pages 4–5).
- The title sheet on Page 3 calls the unit the "3rd Canadian Divisional Trench Mortar Brigade", while the cover and contents page call it the "3rd Canadian Divisional Trench Mortar Group" — both as written.
- 1 November: after "Y3.C. Battery fired 30 rounds" the target reads "Aubert" followed by a second word written above the line that appears to read "Heath"; the second word is recorded here as [uncertain] and not used as a place name.
- 1 November: after "Z3.C Battery fired 40 rounds." the target reads "S.W." followed by a word written over a struck-through word that appears to read "Trench"; recorded here as [uncertain].
- The target abbreviation after "rounds" is written "S.N.T." on Page 4 (2, 3, 4 and 11 November) but "S.W.T." on Page 5 (12 November); both readings are transcribed as written. They may be the same target, but the hand differs between the two sheets.
- 3 November: "Y3.C + Z3.C (2 guns) fired 130 rounds on wire at Q11.c.v.3" — "Q11.c.v.3" is read as written; it is a map reference, not expanded.
- 12 November: "Y3.C Battery fired 102. rounds" — the full stop after "102" is as written. "H.R." is written above the line at the end of the line.
- 13 November: the hour is written "5.45 A.M."; the attack it records is the opening of the Battle of the Ancre, but the diary does not name the battle.
- 14 November: "We had one Casualty who died of wounds following day" — one man of the Group died of wounds on 15 November; the diary gives no name, rank or number.
- 19 November: "Moved to SENLIS" is written as shown (Senlis, Somme); 27 November: "Arrived at MAROEUIL" is written as shown (Maroeuil).
- The signature at the foot of Page 5 is not read with confidence; it appears to begin with the initials "J.T.B." followed by a surname that may read "Bennett", but it is recorded here as [uncertain] and not used as a name. The initials "J.T.B." at the end of most entries on Page 5 are the same hand.
- The Place column is blank on Page 5; Beaumont Hamel (entered on Page 4) is the place for 12–14 November, and the Group was at Senlis 19–26 November and Maroeuil from 27 November.
- No map coordinates (latitude/longitude) are given; the only map reference is "Q11.c.v.3" on 3 November.
- Names read as: none by name — no officer or other rank is named in this month's diary; the only personal identification is the initials "J.T.B." and the [uncertain] signature of the Captain commanding, O.C. 3rd Canadian Divisional Trench Mortars.
- One human casualty is recorded in this month: one man (unnamed) died of wounds on 15 November, after the Group's men volunteered to carry out wounded on 14 November.
