# War Diary — 3rd Canadian Divisional Trench Mortar Group — August 1918

- **Unit:** 3rd Canadian Divisional Trench Mortar Group (Trench Mortar Batteries, 3rd Canadian Division)
- **Month:** August 1918 (Volume 28) — this volume covers 1 to 31 August 1918
- **Source:** Library and Archives Canada, Collection Search Item ID 2004819 (RG 9 III-D-3, vol. 4980, file 592, reel T-10809)
- **Pages:** August 1918, p. 1 – p. 12 (image files page-0322 – page-0333)
- **August 1918 — COMPLETE** (pages 1–12): cover, contents, title page, diary text 1–31 August, no appendices. Transcribed 2026-10-27.

## Transcription conventions
Original spelling, punctuation and abbreviations kept. Diary text is handwritten throughout on Army Form C. 2118, "WAR DIARY or INTELLIGENCE SUMMARY" (neither heading is erased or struck through; the printed note reads "(Erase heading not required.)"). `[uncertain]` marks words I could not read with confidence — never guessed. The Hour column is blank throughout. The Place column gives a place and a map sheet for most entries; where the Place column is blank the place is taken from the diary text. "D.T.M.O." = Divisional Trench Mortar Officer; "M.T.M." = Medium Trench Mortar; "X3c" and "Y3c" are the two medium trench mortar batteries, written as shown; "C.D.A.C." = Canadian Divisional Ammunition Column; "C.F.A." = Canadian Field Artillery; "C.R.A." = Commander Royal Artillery; "O.R." = other ranks; "C.M.R." = Canadian Mounted Rifles; "D.A.A.G." = Deputy Assistant Adjutant General; "G.H.Q." = General Headquarters; "Cdn Infy. Bde" = Canadian Infantry Brigade; "Bde." = Brigade; "Battn." = Battalion; "O.C." = Officer Commanding; "Bde C.F.A." = Brigade, Canadian Field Artillery; "Capt." = Captain; "Do" = ditto. The Remarks column carries an initialled entry on each diary row, read as "R.A.6" [uncertain] (see Open questions). The printed footer on each diary sheet reads "A5834 Wt. W4973/M687 750,000 8/16 D. D. & L. Ltd. Forms/C.2118/13."

## Pages 4–12 — Diary text (1–31 August 1918)

### Page 4 — 1–6 August 1918

> **1.** — On the march to Fourth Army area. At HUMBERCOURT. Left HUMBERCOURT at 3.30 P.M. arrived at bivouac in PONT DE METZ 11 P.M.
>
> **2.** — at PONT DE METZ under orders to move at one half hour's notice
>
> **3.** — at PONT DE METZ under orders to move. Officers commanding X3c and Y3c M.T.M. Batteries reconnoitred forward area in view to future operations 12th Div Arty. supporting this line at time. Very little movement allowed in forward area
>
> **4.** — at PONT DE METZ under orders to move
>
> **5.** — at PONT DE METZ under orders to move. 1 officer from X3c Battery and 1 from Y3c Battery reconnoitred the roads from GENTELLE WOODS to DOMART. Orders for 2 more mortars to proceed to forward area
>
> **6.** — at PONT DE METZ under orders to move. 50 O.Rs. despatched to 3rd C.D.A.C. to work on ammunition dumps 10 O.Rs despatched to 9th Bde C.F.A. and 10 O.Rs to 10th

Place: 1 August — HUMBERCOURT, SHEET LENS 11; 2–6 August — PONT DE METZ, SHEET AMIENS 17. The Date column is headed "1918 Aug".

### Page 5 — 6 (continued)–8 August 1918

Place: 6 August — PONT DE METZ, SHEET AMIENS 17; 7 August — M.7.C.50.85. [uncertain] Sheet 62D.

> **6 (continued).** — Bde C.F.A for purpose of working captured enemy guns after the attack
>
> The D.T.M.O. Battery Commanders of X3c and Y3c M.T.M. Batteries with 2 gun crews, 2 mobile 6" Newton mortars proceeded to the BOIS DE BOVES for preliminary training in open warfare
>
> Orders received to move on morning of 7th to billets in BOVES
>
> **7.** — Commenced march to BOVES 9 a.m. arrived at billets 4.30 P.M. Headquarters established in the village at T.Y.C 50.85 [uncertain] Sheet 62D
>
> **8.** — D.T.M.O. and Officers commanding X3c and Y3c M.T.M. Batteries proceeded forward to the assembly position (in front of GENTELLE WOODS) in the evening, with gun crews and mobile mortars
>
> 6 O.Rs attached to Signals. 3rd Cdn Div Arty.
>
> at 4.20 a.m. the mobile mortars left assembly position in front of GENTELLE WOODS and proceeded to C.4.d Central (Sheet 66E) arriving at 5.30 a.m.

### Page 6 — 8 August 1918 (continued)

Place: M.7.C.50.85. [uncertain] Sheet 62D.

> **8 (continued).** — 2 mules being killed and 3 O.R's wounded. Officers went forward to reconnoitre positions on our front and found that the infantry were moving forward without any resistance from the enemy. They used part of the personnel of the gun crews (leaving enough behind in case mortars were wanted) and assisted in carrying out the wounded
>
> 8 a.m. moved mobile mortars forward to C.12.a 20.70 Sheet 66E and at 11 a.m. a further advance was made to D.7.a 70.50 Sheet 66E where the night was spent
>
> During afternoon constant communication was kept up with 7th Cdn Infy. Bde. Officers went forward to reconnoitre roads etc to RED LINE in direction of BEACOURT
>
> Casualties 3 O.Rs wounded

### Page 7 — 9–13 August 1918

Place: 9 August — M.7.C.50.85. [uncertain] Sheet 62D; 10–13 August — C.4.d. Central Sheet 66E.

> **9.** — 11 a.m. Took up positions of readiness at D.22.c 70.20 Sheet 66E in close liaison with 7th Cdn Infy. Bde
>
> at 2.00 P.M. orders received from C.R.A. for the two mobile mortars to rejoin their own formation at C.4.d Central Sheet 66E where the 3rd C.D.A.C. headquarters were located and help them with taking ammunition forward
>
> **10.** — Remaining stores and personnel brought up from BOVES to new Headquarters at C.4.d Central Sheet 66E
>
> **11.** — Headquarters and Wagon Lines established at C.4.d Central Sheet 66E
>
> **12.** — 50 O.R's despatched to 3rd C.D.A.C. to assist in burying dead horses at No 3 Section 3rd C.D.A.C.
>
> **13.** — Orders received to put 2 active mobile 6" Newton mortars in action in preparation for attack on the 14th, to be attached to 9th Cdn Infy. Bde.
>
> 1 Gun crew and mortar from X3c M.T.M. Battery

### Page 8 — 13 (continued)–16 August 1918

Place: 13–15 August — C.4.d. Central Sheet 66E; 16 August — Sheet 62D.

> **13 (continued).** — attached to 52nd Canadian Battn and 1 Gun crew and mortar from Y3c M.T.M. Battery attached to 116th Canadian Battn, both these mortars went forward and took up a position which we believed from information received to be in range of the enemy
>
> **14.** — The 9th Cdn Infy. Bde attacked on its front between PARVILLERS and DAMERY (Sheet 66E) and advanced so quickly that the mortars became out of range in a very short time, being unable to keep pace with the infantry
>
> **15.** — The enemy made a counter attack, and crews stood to with rifles, this being chiefly X3c Battery. Mortars came under orders of D.T.M.O. Guns and crews were withdrawn to position in rear of LE QUESNOY in L.25.d 90.20 Sheet 66E
>
> Casualties 2 O.Rs wounded
>
> **16.** — Working party of 50 O.R's returned from 3rd C.D.A.C.
>
> 2.00 P.M. Limbers with mortars withdrawn to Wagon Lines at DOMART. Personnel under direction of 52nd Can. Battn Chaplain buried 25 of this battalion's dead at R.3.a 80.80 Sheet 66E. On completion of this work they returned to Wagon Lines late at night.

## Open questions / uncertain items (August 1918)
- The cover reads "VOLUME 28" but the contents page reads "Volume 18-8" — both as written; the contents page appears to be a later typed summary and disagrees with the cover on the volume number. The cover also carries a typed line ":- 07 -:" between "WAR DIARY" and the unit name, and a handwritten "10." at the foot; what either denotes is not clear.
- The contents page's "Diary Text 10 Pages" matches the 10 diary sheets (the title page on p. 3 plus diary sheets pp. 4–12).
- The title page (p. 3) reads "Original to D.A.A.G. Canadian Section, G.H.Q. 3rd Echelon"; the final word "Echelon" is partly smudged at the right edge but is read as written.
- The Remarks column on every diary sheet carries an initialled entry on each row, read here as "R.A.6" [uncertain] — the characters are a flourish and could read "R.A.C." or similar; it is probably a reviewing officer's initials, not a reference to an appendix.
- 3 August: "12th Div Arty." — the "th" is written as a superscript after "12"; read as "12th".
- 6 August: "bivouac" in "arrived at bivouac in PONT DE METZ" is read as written.
- 7 and 9 August, Place column: the Boves headquarters map reference is written in a way that reads "M.7.C.50.85." — the first letter may be "T". The diary text for 7 August gives the same reference as "T.Y.C 50.85", the middle letter possibly "7". Both are taken to be the same map reference (Sheet 62D, Boves) but neither reading is certain.
- 8 August: "C.4.d Central" — the middle character of "C.4.d" could be read as "H"; the same reference is used for the headquarters place 10–16 August and on p. 9.
- 8 August: "BEACOURT" is transcribed as written; it is presumably Beaucourt. "RED LINE" is written in capitals as shown.
- 8 August: the casualty figures may be stated twice — "2 mules being killed and 3 O.R's wounded" at the head of the continuation, and "Casualties 3 O.Rs wounded" at the foot of the same day's entry. They are probably the same 3 wounded men, not 6; do not add them together.
- 15 August: "crews stood to with rifles, this being chiefly X3c Battery" is transcribed as written; the sentence is awkward as written.
- 16 August: "buried 25 of this battalion's dead" — the 25 dead are of the 52nd Canadian Battalion, buried by the Group's personnel under the battalion chaplain's direction at R.3.a 80.80, Sheet 66E.
- 25 and 26 August: "(CAMBRAI ROAD LOG MAP)" is transcribed as written; "LOG" may be a misreading of a scale or map title (e.g. "1:20,000"), but the characters as written read "LOG MAP".
- 26 August (continued, p. 11): "Orders were received from 3rd C.D.A. to despatch" (p. 10) — "3rd C.D.A." is written as shown and may be an abbreviation of "3rd C.D.A.C." (Canadian Divisional Ammunition Column), which is written in full elsewhere.
- 28 August: "RUE PIGNON BIGERRE" is transcribed as written; the street name may be misspelt in the diary.
- The signature at the foot of p. 12 reads "J.O.B." followed by a flourish; the surname is not read with confidence and is recorded here as [uncertain]. The signatory is a Captain signing "for D.T.M.O., 3rd Canadian Division". No commanding officer is named anywhere else in the month; the D.T.M.O. is referred to by appointment only.
- Names/units read as: X3c and Y3c M.T.M. Batteries; 52nd Canadian Battalion; 116th Canadian Battalion; 9th Canadian Infantry Brigade; 7th Canadian Infantry Brigade; 8th Canadian Infantry Brigade; 5th C.M.R. Battalion; 9th and 10th Brigades, Canadian Field Artillery; 3rd Canadian Divisional Ammunition Column; 12th Divisional Artillery.
- Casualties in August 1918: 8 August — 2 mules killed, 3 O.Rs wounded; 15 August — 2 O.Rs wounded. No officers are named and no deaths among the Group's own personnel are recorded.
- No map coordinates (latitude/longitude) are given; positions are given only as map references (sheet and square), e.g. C.4.d Central (Sheet 66E), D.22.c 70.20 (Sheet 66E), L.25.d 90.20 (Sheet 66E), K.15.b 50.10 (Sheet 51C).
