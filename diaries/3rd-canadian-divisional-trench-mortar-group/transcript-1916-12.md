# War Diary — 3rd Canadian Divisional Trench Mortar Group — December 1916

- **Unit:** 3rd Canadian Divisional Trench Mortar Group, 3rd Canadian Division
- **Month:** December 1916 (Volume 8)
- **Source:** Library and Archives Canada, Collection Search Item ID 2004819 (RG 9 III-D-3, vol. 4980, file 592, reel T-10809)
- **Pages:** December 1916, p. 1 – p. 9 (image files page-0064 – page-0072)
- **December 1916 — COMPLETE** (pages 1–9): cover, contents, title sheet, diary text 1–31 December, no appendices. Transcribed 2026-10-27.

## Transcription conventions
Original spelling, punctuation and abbreviations kept. Diary text is handwritten throughout (Army Form C. 2118, "WAR DIARY or INTELLIGENCE SUMMARY" — "INTELLIGENCE SUMMARY" struck through on the printed heading). `[uncertain]` marks words I could not read with confidence — never guessed. The batteries are written as "V3C", "X3C", "Y3C" and "Z3C" (V, X, Y and Z Batteries of the 3rd Canadian Divisional Trench Mortar Group); "X1C" is X Battery of the 1st Canadian Division, loaned to the Group (see 10 December). "fired" is written "fines" / "fired" in the diarist's hand and is transcribed as "fired" throughout; "rounds" is written "rounds" throughout. "No." = Number (of gun); "Bn." = Battalion; "C.M.R." = Canadian Mounted Rifles; "T.M.'s" = trench mortars; "Boche" = as written (German). Each battery entry ends with a small initialled signature in the right margin, apparently that of the battery officer reporting; most are written in a hurried hand and are transcribed only where legible, otherwise marked [uncertain] — the recurring sets appear to be "M.H.N." (Y Battery), "G.S.M." / "J.W." (X Battery), "S.M.T." / "P.S." (Z Battery) and "W.H.A." / "G.W.T." (V Battery), but individual readings are not all certain. The "Remarks and references to Appendices" column is blank throughout, except where an entry runs over into it. The printed footer on the diary sheets reads "1875 Wt. W593/826 1,000,000 4/15 J.B.C. & A. A.D.S.S./Forms/C. 2118."

## Open questions / uncertain items (December 1916)
- The cover reads "Volume 8." (with a "6." written below it), but the contents page reads "Volume 16-12" — both as written; the contents page appears to be a later typed summary and disagrees with the cover on the volume number. The contents page's "Diary Text 6 Pages" matches the 6 diary sheets (pages 4–9).
- Page 3 (title sheet): the line below "Original" reads "[uncertain] office at the Base" — the word before "office" could not be read with confidence (it appears to be an abbreviation, possibly "O.C."). It is not expanded here.
- Page 4, 1 December: the first battery letter is read as "X3C"; the letter is written in a looped hand and could be misread. The battery designations V, X, Y and Z are used consistently through the month, and "X1C" is the 1st Division battery loaned on 10 December.
- Page 4, 3 December: the date "3" is preceded by a small mark that could not be read; the map reference is read as "(ROCLINCOURT A4 B2. 2½)" — "ROCLINCOURT" is the place as written, and "2½" is the fractional part of the reference as written.
- Page 4, 5 December: the map reference is read as "A22 b2.3" — the final figure is not fully clear; the same reference is written "A22 b2.7" on 16 December, "A22 b.27" on 17 December and "A22 b27" on 18 December, so the 5 December figure may be "b2.3" or "b2.7".
- Page 4, 6 December: the only hours in the month are entered here — "5 a.m." for X3C and "2 p.m." for Z3C/Y3C. The Y3C line reads "Work continued on emplacements" — the word "emplacements" is spelled as written.
- Page 5, 10 December: two words could not be read with confidence — the word after "to assist" (apparently "our") and the adjective before "operation" (apparently describing the 20 December raid). Neither is guessed here.
- Page 5, 11 December: the word before "Infantry raiding party" could not be read with confidence (it appears to be "small"); it is marked [uncertain] and not asserted.
- Page 5, 12 December: the V3C guns are written "No. 1 fired 1 round" and "No. 3 fired 3 rounds" for the ECURIE SECTION, "Effect Good."
- Page 6, 16 December: "177 on wire A22 b2.7 to the SNOUT" — "SNOUT" is written in capitals as shown; it is a named feature, not expanded.
- Page 7, 17 December: the date "17" is overwritten on the sheet; the V3C line reads "Some good hits on enemy's trenches" — the word "enemies" appears struck through before "trenches".
- Page 7, 18 December: "CRATER 19" is as written; the Y3C line reads "to Boche's [uncertain] Rifle Grenades" — the word between "Boche's" and "Rifle Grenades" could not be read with confidence (it appears to read "GAP").
- Page 7, 20 December: the entry runs from the foot of Page 7 onto the head of Page 8 ("Wire presented no obstacle to the Infantry" / "X3C Battery fired 96 rounds…"); both parts are transcribed as written.
- Page 8, 22 December: the three retaliation entries are bracketed together; the Z3C signature at the end of the bracket appears to read a name ("Sandhurst") but is not read with confidence and is marked [uncertain].
- Page 8, 24 December: the V3C entry (No. 1 gun 3 rounds, No. 2 gun 6 rounds, "obtaining good hits on picked targets") follows the 24 December retaliation entries and precedes the 25 December "No firing" line, so it is assigned to 24 December; the diary does not date it separately.
- Page 8, 25 December: "No firing" is the whole entry for Christmas Day.
- Page 9, 28–31 December: the date column for 29–30 December is overwritten/corrected (a "29" appears struck through and overwritten), so the grouping of the 28–31 December entries follows the order of the lines on the sheet: 28 December — Y3C 21 rounds; 29 December — X3C 15, Z3C 36; 30 December — Y3C 6, X3C 5, Z3C 21, Y3C 8; 31 December — Y3C 10, Z3C 12. All nine lines are bracketed together as retaliation "to trench Mortars, Rifle Grenades, Whizz Bangs, and some Heavy Shells".
- Page 9: the signature at the foot of the month reads "Bennett" with preceding initials that could not be read with confidence (they appear to be "J. T. O."); the rank is read as "Capt." and the title as "Officer Commanding". The signature is recorded here as [uncertain] for the initials and is not used as a name elsewhere.
- Trailing marginal initials: nearly every battery entry ends with a small initialled signature in the right margin. The recurring sets are noted in the conventions above, but most individual readings are hurried and are marked [uncertain] in the text rather than guessed.
- Names/units read as: 43rd Bn. (15 December raid); 1st C.M.R. Bn. (20 December raid); X1C Battery, 1st Canadian Division (loaned 10 December); THELUS, ECURIE and LA FOLIE Sections; LILLE ROAD; DUMP AVE; SUPPORT LINE; ROCLINCOURT; CRATER 19; the SNOUT.
- No human casualties are recorded in this month: the diary records only rounds fired, wire-cutting, registration and retaliation, raids supported, and emplacements in bad condition from wet weather and rain. No map coordinates (latitude/longitude) are given; locations are given only as section names and map references (e.g. "A4 B2. 2½", "A22 b2.7").
