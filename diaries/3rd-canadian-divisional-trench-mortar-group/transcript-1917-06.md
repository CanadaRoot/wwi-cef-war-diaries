# War Diary — 3rd Canadian Divisional Trench Mortar Group — June 1917

- **Unit:** 3rd Canadian Divisional Trench Mortar Group, 3rd Canadian Division (the title sheet calls the unit "3rd Canadian Division Trench Mortor Brigade" — "Mortor" as written; the signature block reads "3rd Cdn Division Trench Mortar B's")
- **Month:** June 1917 (1st to 30th June 1917)
- **Source:** Library and Archives Canada, Collection Search Item ID 2004819 (RG 9 III-D-3, vol. 4980, file 592, reel T-10809)
- **Pages:** June 1917, p. 1 – p. 6 (image files page-0116 – page-0121)
- **June 1917 — COMPLETE** (pages 1–6): cover, contents, title sheet, diary text 1–30 June, no appendices. Transcribed 2026-10-27.

## Transcription conventions
Original spelling, punctuation and abbreviations kept. Diary text is handwritten throughout (Army Form C. 2118, "WAR DIARY or INTELLIGENCE SUMMARY" — "INTELLIGENCE SUMMARY" struck through with a heavy black line on the diary sheets, pp. 4–6). `[uncertain]` marks words I could not read with confidence — never guessed. The diarist's initials at the end of most entries are a flourish that appears to read "J.T.O."; they are transcribed as J.T.O. throughout but are not read with full confidence (see open questions). The Place column reads "VILLERS AU BOIS" on every diary sheet, with "Lens Sheet" written beneath it on p. 4. The Hour column and the "Remarks and references to Appendices" column are blank throughout. "F.A." = Field Artillery; "M.T.M." = Medium Trench Mortar; "H.T.M." = Heavy Trench Mortar; "T.M." = Trench Mortar; "Cdn." = Canadian; "BOSCHE" is spelled as written; "O.C." = Officer Commanding; "Bde." = Brigade. The printed footer on the diary sheets reads "2449 Wt. W14957/M90 750,000 1/16 J.B.C. & A. Forms/C.2118/12."

## Pages 4–6 — Diary text (1–30 June 1917)

### Page 4 — 1–8 June 1917 (Sheet No. 3)

Place: VILLERS AU BOIS, Lens Sheet.

> **1/6/17.** — Officers and men still attached to F.A. batteries for instruction in gunnery J.T.O.
>
> **2/6/17.** — Reconnaissance made from T.8.a.7.6. to T.2 c.11. (sheet LA COULETTE 1/10,000) for purpose of putting in Trench Mortars for cutting enemy front line wire. Wire has been well cut by our Heavy guns but we are to take on any fresh wire which may be put out during the night. Second scheme has been arranged by us to bombed at different intervals during the night. Emplacements located T.8.a.9.9. [uncertain] Trenches at this point along the railroad are very close together and care has to be taken in firing our mortars so as not to give the positions away J.T.O.
>
> **3/6/17.** — Two officers and 30 men recalled from the F.A. batteries to carry on with the work of building these emplacements so as to be ready for a push which is to be carried out by the 7th Cdn. Infantry Brigade J.T.O.
>
> **4/6/17 to 8/6/17.** — Work continued on emplacements and carrying up ammunition J.T.O.
>
> **8/6/17.** — So called push was called off and a raid was made by the 7th Cdn. Infantry Brigade. We fired 12 rounds in time given us for the bombardment, — 12 minutes — J.T.O.

### Page 5 — 10–24 June 1917 (Sheet No. 4)

Place: VILLERS AU BOIS.

> **10/6/17 to 13/6/17.** — We found that the M.T.M. emplacements located as per 2/6/17 were good ones and work has been started to make permanent ones out of them and the carrying of more ammunition. We found it very difficult to work in these places on account of heavy shelling. Working parties had to take cover many times during the day and the night. Work progressed very slowly J.T.O.
>
> **14/6/17.** — Sent some of our men to a T.M. course which is [struck-through word — [uncertain]] an essential thing in this work J.T.O.
>
> **15/6/17.** — Two officers made a reconnaissance of the forward area for purpose of locating a position for a H.T.M. Located a position at the head of the BOIS de CHAUDIERE J.T.O.
>
> **16/6/17 to 21/6/17.** — All officers and men recalled from the F.A. batteries and sent to the line to work on the H.T.M emplacement. This work is being carried out day and night so as it will be ready for the 9th Cdn. Infantry Brigade show which is coming off on the 28th of June J.T.O.
>
> **22/6/17.** — M.T.M's fired 15 rounds at different intervals through the night on Machine Gun and Light Trench Mortar emplacements. J.T.O.
>
> **23/6/17 + 24/6/17.** — Unable to fire on account of night working parties digging jumping off trench and not advisable to fire in daytime on account [continued on next sheet]

### Page 6 — 23–30 June 1917 (Sheet No. 5)

Place: VILLERS AU BOIS.

> **23/6/17 + 24/6/17 (continued).** — of giving away position J.T.O.
>
> **25/6/17.** — Work on H.T.M emplacement completed and gun in action ready to fire with 50 rounds ammunition J.T.O.
>
> **26/6/17.** — Without any bombardment the BOSCHE retired to-day to AVION trench so we were unable to fire on account of being out of range. The 9th Cdn. Infantry Brigade took up their objective in AVION J.T.O.
>
> **27/6/17.** — BOSCHE still retiring back into AVION and going too fast for us to keep up with him in our work J.T.O.
>
> **28/6/17.** — Our Reserve ammunition store hit by a BOSCHE shell and detonated about 100 rounds. Nothing remaining but a large crater. No casualties, two men buried J.T.O.
>
> **29/6/17 and 30/6/17.** — Standing by awaiting orders J.T.O.
>
> [Signed, bottom right:] *[signature — [uncertain]] Lt. Capt, / Officer Commandg / 3rd Cdn Division Trench Mortar B's*

## Open questions / uncertain items (June 1917)
- The cover reads "VOLUME 14" (with a "3" written beneath it) but the contents page reads "Volume 17-6" — both as written; the contents page's "Diary Text 3 Pages" matches the three diary sheets (Sheets 3–5).
- The title sheet (p. 3) calls the unit "3rd CANADIAN DIVISION TRENCH MORTOR BRIGADE" — "MORTOR" is spelled as written, not "Mortar", and "Brigade" where the cover and the rest of the diary say "Group". The signature block reads "3rd Cdn Division Trench Mortar B's".
- The Place column on p. 4 reads "VILLERS AU BOIS" with "Lens Sheet" written beneath it; "Lens Sheet" is read as written (a map-sheet reference) and is not repeated on pp. 5–6.
- 2 June: the grid references are read as written — "from T.8.a.7.6. to T.2 c.11." and "Emplacements located T.8.a.9.9." — the final digits of the emplacement reference are not read with confidence and are marked [uncertain]. The map sheet is "LA COULETTE 1/10,000".
- 2 June: "Second scheme has been arranged by us to bombed at different intervals during the night." is transcribed with "to bombed" as written.
- 4–8 June: the "Work continued on emplacements" entry is dated "4/6/17 to 8/6/17", and the next entry (push called off, raid by the 7th Cdn. Infantry Brigade) is also dated 8/6/17 — both as written.
- 9 June: there is no entry dated 9 June 1917; the diary jumps from the 8 June entry to the 10–13 June entry.
- 14 June: the entry contains a struck-through word after "which is" and a struck-through word written above the line before "work" ("an essential thing in this work"); neither struck word is read with confidence and both are marked [uncertain].
- The diarist's initials at the end of most entries are a flourish that appears to read "J.T.O."; they are transcribed as J.T.O. throughout but are not read with full confidence, and the signature at the foot of the June sheets is not read with confidence — it is recorded here as [uncertain] and not used as a name. The rank in the signature block reads "Lt. Capt".
- 23–24 June: the entry runs from the foot of Sheet 4 onto the head of Sheet 5 ("not advisable to fire in daytime on account / of giving away position"); both parts are transcribed as written.
- 28 June: "No casualties, two men buried" — the diary states there were no casualties in the same sentence as "two men buried" (by the detonation of the reserve ammunition store, about 100 rounds); whether the two men were dug out unhurt is not stated.
- No personal names appear anywhere in this month's diary: no officer or man is named, only the 7th and 9th Cdn. Infantry Brigades. No battery designations (e.g. V3C/X3C/Y3C/Z3C) are given; the mortars are referred to only as M.T.M. and H.T.M.
- No human casualties are recorded in this month other than the 28 June incident above: the diary expressly states "No casualties" for that incident, and no casualty names are given at any point.
- No map coordinates (latitude/longitude) are given; locations are given only as VILLERS AU BOIS, the LA COULETTE sheet (1/10,000), the grid references above, BOIS de CHAUDIERE, and AVION trench.
