# War Diary — 3rd Canadian Divisional Trench Mortar Group — October 1918

- **Unit:** 3rd Canadian Divisional Trench Mortar Group (3rd Canadian Division)
- **Month:** October 1918
- **Source:** Library and Archives Canada, Collection Search Item ID 2004819 (RG 9 III-D-3, vol. 4980, file 592, reel T-10809)
- **Pages:** October 1918, p. 1 – p. 11 (image files page-0347 – page-0357)
- **October 1918 — COMPLETE** (pages 1–11): cover, contents, diary text 1–31 October, no appendices. Transcribed 2026-10-27.

## Transcription conventions
Original spelling, punctuation and abbreviations kept. Diary text is handwritten throughout (Army Form C. 2118, "WAR DIARY or INTELLIGENCE SUMMARY" — neither heading erased). `[uncertain]` marks words I could not read with confidence — never guessed. The Place column gives the Headquarters location and a map reference for each move. The Hour column is blank throughout. The "Remarks and references to Appendices" column reads "Nil" (faint pencil) against every entry. "D.T.M.O." = Divisional Trench Mortar Officer; "C.D.A." = Canadian Divisional Artillery; "C.F.A." = Canadian Field Artillery; "Bde." = Brigade; "Bdes." = Brigades; "C.C.R.C." = Canadian Corps Reinforcement Camp; "O.R." = other ranks; "O.Rs." = other ranks (plural); "N.C.O." = non-commissioned officer; "O.C." = Officer Commanding; "H.T.M." / "M.T.M." = as written (battery designations, see open questions). The printed footer on each diary sheet reads "A5834 Wt. W4973/M687 750,000 8/16 D. D. & L. Ltd. Forms/C.2118/13." The diary sheets are numbered in pencil at the top right 2261–2269 (pages 3–11).

## Pages 3–11 — Diary text (1–31 October 1918)

Army Form C. 2118, "WAR DIARY or INTELLIGENCE SUMMARY".

### Page 3 — 1–3 October 1918

Place: Headquarters at BOURLON, E12a 10.60, SHEET 57c NE.

> **Oct 1.** — Headquarters of D.T.M.O at BOURLON Sheet 57c NE
>
> 1 O.R evacuated to Field Ambulance.
>
> All available personnel still attached to Field Batteries of the 9th and 10th Bdes C.F.A
>
> Total Strength 10 Officers 164 O.Rs.
>
> Strength of attached personnel 1 Officers 6 O.Rs
>
> **2.** — 4 O.Rs. returned from 1st Army Rest Camp
>
> 3 O.Rs. despatched to 1st Army Rest Camp.
>
> 2 O.Rs reinforcements from C.C.R.C. reported to our Wagon Lines.
>
> **3.** — 1 O.R wounded while attached to Field Battery
>
> 1 O.R evacuated sick to Field Ambulance
>
> All available personnel still attached to Field Batteries.

### Page 7 — 17–19 October 1918

Place: Headquarters at NEUVILLE ST. REMY, A9b 15.70, SHEET 57B NW.

> **Oct 17.** — Orders were received from 3rd C.D.A to put two Trench Mortars in action.
>
> 2 Officers and 22 O.Rs. returned from Field Batteries of the 9th and 10th Bdes. C.F.A
>
> D.T.M.O and O.C Y3c Battery made reconnaissance of line with view to putting in mortars.
>
> 1 O.R evacuated sick to Field Ambulance
>
> **18.** — 2 O.Rs evacuated sick to Field Ambulance
>
> All available personnel still attached to Batteries
>
> **19.** — O.C. Y Battery and one Officer went forward with Mortars and personnel to put gun in action. Enemy retired during the night and orders were received from 3rd C.D.A. that mortars were not required now and Mortars and personnel returned to Wagon Lines.

## Open questions / uncertain items (October 1918)
- The cover reads "VOLUME 30" but the contents page reads "Volume 18-10" — both as written; the contents page appears to be a later typed summary and disagrees with the cover on the volume number. The contents page's "Diary Text 9 Pages" matches the 9 diary sheets (pages 3–11).
- Map references: the Bourlon reference is read as "E12a 10.60, SHEET 57c NE" and the Neuville St. Remy reference as "A9b 15.70, SHEET 57B NW" — the letter after the sheet number ("c" / "B") and the spacing of the grid reference are read as written; the "c" in "57c" may be a degree mark.
- 1 October: "Strength of attached personnel 1 Officers 6 O.Rs" is transcribed as written, including the plural "Officers" after "1".
- 10 October: the entry reads "Do Do" — two "Do" (ditto) marks, transcribed as written; it repeats the 9 October entry.
- 15 October: "2 O.Rs. attached from C.C.R.C." is read as "attached"; the word could be "detached" in this hand.
- 17 October: "O.C Y3c Battery" is read as written; the battery designation "Y3c" is faint.
- 25 October: "Moved Headquarters and Wagon Lines at WALLERS" is transcribed as written ("at", where "to" would be expected).
- 26 October (continued, page 10): the two officers' battery designations are hard to read — "V3c H.T.M Battery" and "X3c M.T.M Battery" are best readings; the middle letters ("H.T.M" / "M.T.M") and the "3c" suffixes are marked [uncertain] in the transcription. The 36th and 35th Batteries C.F.A. are read as written.
- 29 October: "1 O.R. reported back from Field Ambulance having been evacuated from Canadian Corps Gas School" is transcribed as written; the route (Gas School to Field Ambulance) is the diary's own wording.
- 31 October: "Strength of attached personnel 1 Officer 4 O.Rs." — the "4" appears inserted above the line; transcribed as read.
- 31 October: the closing signature and the line below it are not read with confidence — the signature appears to be a single name followed by "Capt", and the line below reads "for 3rd C.D.T.M. B's."; both are recorded as [uncertain] and no name is used. The only officer named in the month is Capt Loy (26 October).
- The "Remarks and references to Appendices" column reads "Nil" in faint pencil against every entry; the reading is consistent across all nine diary sheets but the pencil is faint.
- Names read as: Capt Loy; O.C. Y Battery; O.C. Y3c Battery; D.T.M.O. (unnamed); Area Commandant at Hasnon (unnamed); 4 officers ordered to Hasnon as Town Majors (unnamed). No other personal names appear.
- Casualties/sickness in the month: 1 O.R. evacuated to Field Ambulance (1 October); 1 O.R. wounded while attached to a Field Battery (3 October); 1 O.R. evacuated sick to Field Ambulance (3 October); 1 O.R. wounded while attached to a Field Battery (7 October); 1 N.C.O. wounded while attached to a Field Battery (8 October); 1 O.R. evacuated sick (17 October); 2 O.Rs. evacuated sick (18 October). No deaths are recorded. No map coordinates (latitude/longitude) are given; places are given as sheet and grid references only.
