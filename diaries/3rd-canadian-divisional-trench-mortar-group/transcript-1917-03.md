# War Diary — 3rd Canadian Divisional Trench Mortar Group — March 1917

- **Unit:** 3rd Canadian Divisional Trench Mortar Group, 3rd Canadian Division
- **Month:** March 1917 (Volume 11)
- **Source:** Library and Archives Canada, Collection Search Item ID 2004819 (RG 9 III-D-3, vol. 4980, file 592, reel T-10809)
- **Pages:** March 1917, p. 1 – p. 7 (image files page-0094 – page-0100)
- **March 1917 — COMPLETE** (pages 1–7): cover, contents, diary title sheet, diary text 1–31 March, no appendices. Transcribed 2026-10-27.

## Transcription conventions
Original spelling, punctuation and abbreviations kept. Diary text is handwritten throughout (Army Form C. 2118, "WAR DIARY or INTELLIGENCE SUMMARY" — "INTELLIGENCE SUMMARY" struck through on the printed heading). `[uncertain]` marks words I could not read with confidence — never guessed. "T.M." = Trench Mortar; "H.T.M." = Heavy Trench Mortar; "Divl." = Divisional; "Divn" = Division; "Mch." = March; "Bosche" as written; "M.G." = machine gun; "C.T." as written (not expanded); "O.O." as written (not expanded); "4.1's" and "77 m.m's" as written (enemy weapons); "O.R." = other ranks; "Arty." = Artillery. The printed Date column header on each diary sheet carries "1916" although the diary is for March 1917 (old form stock). The Hour column and the "Remarks and references to Appendices" column are blank throughout. Pencil initials in a second hand appear at the end of many entries — read, where legible, as G.H.B., B.B. and H.O.P., all [uncertain]; longer diagonal marginal notes are illegible and are marked [uncertain]. "Coy." is not used in this month; batteries are V3C, X3C, Y3C and Z3C.

## Open questions / uncertain items (March 1917)
- The cover reads "VOLUME 11" and the diary title sheet (p. 3) also carries "11" at the top right, but the contents page (p. 2) reads "Volume 17-3" — both as written; the contents page appears to be a later typed summary and disagrees with the cover on the volume number. The contents page's "Diary Text 4 Pages" matches the four diary text sheets (p. 4–7); the diary title sheet (p. 3) is additional.
- The handwritten mark at the top right of the cover is read as "No" but is not clear — recorded as [uncertain].
- The printed Date column header on every diary sheet carries "1916" although the diary is for March 1917; the diarist's own dates are "Mch" 1917 throughout, so the year is a form-stock artefact, not a diary error.
- 1–8 March: the dates are braced together in the Date column as "Mch 1st to 8th" against a single entry; the Place column reads "AMETTES" (underlined).
- 9 March: "relieving" is written with "pre" inserted above the line before "lieving" (read as "relieving"); the checker's initials after "4th Canadian Divn" are not read with confidence.
- 12 March: the target of the 185 rounds is written "on [uncertain] & saps" — the word before "& saps" (read as "pat") is not read with confidence.
- 13 March: three long diagonal marginal notes — after "Heavy T.M.", after "77 m.m's." and after "H.T.M." — are illegible and marked [uncertain]; the diagonal word after "to this sap" is likewise illegible. The marginal notes may be names, but they are not read as names here.
- 14 March: a word after "50 rounds" is overwritten/struck through and is marked [uncertain]; "V3C. No 2 Heavy T.M." is read as written.
- Checker's initials: pencil initials in a second hand close most entries. Best readings are G.H.B., B.B. and H.O.P., but all are [uncertain] and no person is identified from them.
- 15 March: the Place column reads "Souchez Billets in Petit Servins"; the Y3C and Z3C lines use ditto marks (" " for "battery fired", " for "rounds"). "Sniper's post destroyed" is read as written.
- 17 March: "Destruction of C.T. & dugouts" — "C.T." is as written and is not expanded here.
- 18 March: the Place column contains the word "MARCH" and the Date column "18" — transcribed as written.
- 21 March: "393 rounds" and "M.G. emplacements" are read as written.
- 22 March: "Dugout blown in causing casualties. (ours)" is the month's only casualty mention; no number of casualties is given.
- 23 March: the marginal note after "retaliation" is illegible and marked [uncertain].
- 24 March: the Place column contains "Mch." and the Date column "24" — transcribed as written.
- 30 March: "in connection with O.O. # 72." is as written.
- 31 March: a line of writing above the signature is heavily struck through and illegible. The signing officer's surname is read as "Bennett" but is [uncertain]; the initials are "J.T.O." and the rank "Capt", Officer Commanding, 3rd Can Divl T.M. Group.
- Names read as: Capt. J.T.O. Bennett [uncertain] (Officer Commanding, signature 31 March). No other personal names appear in this month.
- No map coordinates (latitude/longitude) are given; places are Amettes, Petit Servins, Souchez and Neuville St Vaast only. No human casualty figures are given anywhere in the month — only "casualties (ours)" on 22 March, with no number.
