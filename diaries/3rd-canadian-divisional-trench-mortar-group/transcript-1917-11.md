- **Unit:** 3rd Canadian Divisional Trench Mortar Group (Trench Mortar Batteries, 3rd Canadian Division)
- **Month:** November 1917 (Volume 19)
- **Source:** Library and Archives Canada, Collection Search Item ID 2004819 (RG 9 III-D-3, vol. 4980, file 592, reel T-10809, finding aid 9-52)
- **Pages:** November 1917, p. 1 – p. 6 (image files page-0165 – page-0170)
- **November 1917 — COMPLETE** (pages 1–6): cover, contents, diary text 1–30 November, no appendices. Transcribed 2026-10-27.

## Transcription conventions
Original spelling, punctuation and abbreviations kept. The diary text is handwritten throughout on Army Form C. 2118, "WAR DIARY or INTELLIGENCE SUMMARY" — "INTELLIGENCE SUMMARY" struck through on the printed heading. `[uncertain]` marks words I could not read with confidence — never guessed. "do" is the diarist's ditto for the preceding entry. "N.C.O." = Non-Commissioned Officer; "D.A.C." = Divisional Ammunition Column; "C.D.A." = Canadian Divisional Artillery; "D.T.M.O." = Divisional Trench Mortar Officer; "O.O." = Operation Order; "a/cg." = acting (as written). The contents page lists "Volume 17—11" while the cover reads "VOLUME 19" — both as written.

## Open questions / uncertain items (November 1917)
- The cover reads "VOLUME 19" but the contents page reads "Volume 17—11" — both as written; the contents pages for September ("Volume 17—9"), October ("Volume 17—10") and November ("Volume 17—11") form a consistent later typed sequence that disagrees with the covers (17, 18, 19).
- 22 November has two dated lines (the overnight camp at Merris, then the 9.00 AM resumption of the march) and 23 November likewise (9.00 AM resumption, 1.00 PM arrival at Ames) — transcribed as written.
- 22 November, 9.00 AM: the operation order reference after "3rd C.D.A. O.O." is not read with confidence — transcribed as [uncertain]. It appears to read "172/601" but is not clear.
- The November diary is signed by a Lieutenant acting as D.T.M.O., not by Capt. Bennett; the signature is in faint pencil and is not read with confidence — recorded as [uncertain]. No other names are given in November.
- No human casualties are recorded in November 1917. No map coordinates (latitude/longitude) are given; Warrington Camp is given only as "H2c 2.5 Sheet 28 N.W. Belgium and France".
- The Group spent 1–20 November with its N.C.O.s and men detached to Field Artillery Brigades and the D.A.C., then marched 21–23 November from the Ypres area (Vlamertinghe/Warrington Camp) south to rest billets at Ames in the First Army area.
