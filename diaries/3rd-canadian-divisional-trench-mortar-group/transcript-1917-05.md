# War Diary — 3rd Canadian Divisional Trench Mortar Group — May 1917

- **Unit:** 3rd Canadian Divisional Trench Mortar Group (3rd Canadian Divisional Trench Mortar Batteries)
- **Month:** May 1917
- **Source:** Library and Archives Canada, Collection Search Item ID 2004819 (RG 9 III-D-3, vol. 4980, file 592, reel T-10809)
- **Pages:** May 1917, p. 1 – p. 5 (image files page-0111 – page-0115)
- **May 1917 — COMPLETE** (pages 1–5): cover, contents, title sheet, diary text 1–31 May, no appendices. Transcribed 2026-10-27.

## Transcription conventions
Original spelling, punctuation and abbreviations kept. Diary text is handwritten throughout (Army Form C. 2118, "WAR DIARY or INTELLIGENCE SUMMARY"). `[uncertain]` marks words I could not read with confidence — never guessed. The Place column is blank throughout the diary sheets; the place "VILLERS AU BOIS" appears in the text of the 1 May entry. "D.A.C." = Divisional Ammunition Column; "C.F.A." = Canadian Field Artillery; "D.A.A.G." = Deputy Assistant Adjutant General; "T.M.B's" = Trench Mortar Batteries; "Bty's" = Batteries; "Div:" = Division. The "Remarks and references to Appendices" column contains a repeated handwritten mark/initials beside each entry, read as [uncertain] and not transcribed as a word. The Hour column is blank throughout.

## Pages 4–5 — Diary text (1–31 May 1917)

Army Form C. 2118, "WAR DIARY or INTELLIGENCE SUMMARY". The printed footer on both sheets reads "1875 Wt. W593/826 1,000,000 4/15 J.B.C. & A. A.D.S.S./Forms/C. 2118."

### Page 4 — 1–30 May 1917 (Sheet No. 3)

> **May 1st.** — In camp at VILLERS AU BOIS. No mortars in action. 50 men proceeded to 3rd D.A.C. to assist in getting forward gun ammunition & withdraw captured Guns & Howitzers.
>
> **4 to 6.** — [No diary text opposite these dates.]
>
> **7th — 8th.** — Men attached 3rd D.A.C. recalled. All available men attached to C.F.A. batteries to relieve crews & receive instruction in gunnery
>
> **[uncertain — date entry between 8th and 28th, overwritten/illegible; no diary text opposite it]**
>
> **28th — 29th.** — Reconnaissance made of front line to select positions for 2" Trench Mortars. Emplacements secured at J8a.89 Sheet. 36c S.W.3. and orders were given to carry on with the construction. 2 Officers & 30 men withdrawn from batteries to carry on with above.
>
> **30th.** — Work started on emplacements and magazines. Hostile batteries very active on our front. Fatigue had

>
> **May 31.** — Work on new emplacements continued.

## Open questions / uncertain items (May 1917)
- The cover reads "VOLUME 13" (with a small "2." written underneath it), but the contents page reads "Volume 17-5" — both as written; the two volume numbers disagree. The contents page's "Diary Text 2 Pages" matches the two diary-text sheets (Sheets 3–4).
- Page 3 is a title sheet on an Army Form C. 2118, numbered sheet 2; it is not a diary-text sheet. The annotation at the foot of page 3 is read as "Original / D.A.A.G. Base"; the middle line ("D.A.A.G.") is not read with full confidence.
- 1 May: "In camp at VILLERS AU BOIS" — the first word is read as "In"; the place is written "VILLERS AU BOIS" in capitals.
- 4 to 6 May: the dates are grouped by a brace in the Date column, with no diary text opposite them.
- 7–8 May: "All available men attached to C.F.A. batteries" — the middle initial is read as "F" (C.F.A.); the entry has no full stop at the end as written.
- Between the 8th and the 28th in the Date column there is an overwritten, illegible date entry (it appears to be two lines, possibly a range, with a "9th" and a further date overwritten) with no diary text opposite it. It is recorded here only as [uncertain]; no date has been assigned to it, and no entry has been invented for the intervening days.
- 28–29 May: the map reference is read as "J8a.89 Sheet. 36c S.W.3." as written; the reading of "J8a.89" and "36c" is not fully certain. The reconnaissance entry is taken to cover 28–29 May (the two dates are stacked together before the paragraph), and the 30th is taken to begin at "Work started on emplacements and magazines" (the "30th" date entry sits beside that line).
- 30 May: the entry runs from the foot of Sheet 3 onto the head of Sheet 4 ("Fatigue had / to take cover a great many times..."); both parts are transcribed as written.
- The "Remarks and references to Appendices" column on Sheets 3–4 contains a repeated handwritten mark beside each entry; it looks like initials/signature, but it is not read with confidence and is not transcribed as a word.
- The Officer Commanding's signature at the foot of Sheet 4 is not read with confidence; it appears to begin "J. F. B." followed by further illegible characters, but it is recorded here as [uncertain] and not used as a name. He signs as "Capt / Officer Commanding / 3rd Canadian Div: T.M.B's".
- Names read as: none — no personal names are given in this month's diary text; only the illegible signature of the Officer Commanding.
- No human casualties are recorded in this month: no killed, wounded or sick are mentioned. No map coordinates (latitude/longitude) are given; the only location reference is the map sheet reference "J8a.89 Sheet. 36c S.W.3." and the place "VILLERS AU BOIS".
- No dates are given for 2–3 May, and no diary text is given for 4–6 May or for 9–27 May (apart from the illegible overwritten date entry noted above).
