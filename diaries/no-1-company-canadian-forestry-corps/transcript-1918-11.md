# War Diary Transcript — November 1918

- **Unit:** No. 1 Company, Canadian Forestry Corps (C.F.C.)
- **Month:** November 1918
- **Source:** Library and Archives Canada, CEF war diaries, RG 9 III-D-3, LAC Item 2004999 (second record)
- **Pages in this file:** page 69 (cover), page 70 (entries 1–24 November) and page 71 (entries 27–30 November)
- **Place:** Field
- **Commanding officer / signatory:** W. Hutchison, Capt., O.C. No. 1 Company C.F.C.

## Cover page — page 69

> WAR DIARY
>
> NO. 1. COMPANY C. F. C.
>
> NOVEMBER 1918.

## 1 November 1918 — page 70

> List of O. Rs for promotion to complete establishment, sent to 9th Dist. H. Qrs. for approval.

## 3 November 1918 — page 70

> French sportsman shot the Camp pet (wild pig)

## 5 November 1918 — page 70

> Instructions received to cease cutting road plank and cut S. G. sleepers.

## 6 November 1918 — page 70

> Promotions submitted on 1st approved, names appearing in D.R.O.

## 7 November 1918 — page 70

> Senior Chaplain of Corps arrived in Camp and stayed over-night. He addressed the men in the evening.

## 9 November 1918 — page 70

> Col. McDougall [uncertain] O. C. 9th Dist. paid a visit. A Concert was held (local talent) in Y. M. C. A. hut in the evening.

## 11 November 1918 — page 70

> News received of signing of Armistice.

## 12 November 1918 — page 70

> Received instructions from 9th Dist. to cease felling trees and to convert logs on hand into 2" 3" and 4" edged stock.

## 13 November 1918 — page 70

> Turnouts put in on Main line (M. 29) for siding on Coupe 28.

## 14 November 1918 — page 70

> Smoking Concert held in Dining Hall in evening.

## 14 November 1918 (second entry) — page 70

> Instructions received as to getting all sawn timber taken to dumps and piled open.

Note: the date column for this entry is not clearly entered; it immediately follows the 14 November entry, so it is treated as 14 November with the date [uncertain].

## 18 November 1918 — page 70

> Instructions received as to return of number of Officers and O. Rs. willing [uncertain] discharge at each Military District in Canada.

## 21 November 1918 — page 70

> Lieut. Fraser [uncertain] proceeded on leave to England.

## 23 November 1918 — page 70

> Civilian labour paid off. [uncertain trailing mark]

## 24 November 1918 — page 70

> Original No. 1 Forest Party held a dinner to commemorate 3 yrs. of work in France.

## 27 November 1918 — page 71

> Orders received from 9th Dist. as to holding Parades on Saturday forenoons.

## 30 November 1918 — page 71

> Attached labour and supervisors only working in forenoon. The Company was paraded at 9 a.m inspected and given 2 hrs. drill as per instructions.

Signed: W. Hutchison, Capt., O.C. No. 1 Company C.F.C.

### Notes on this month

- Headings on the diary sheets, in manuscript: page 70, "No. 1 Company C.F.C. for month of November 1918"; page 71, "November 1918." A stamp on both sheets reads "No. 1 COMPANY, CANADIAN FORESTRY CORPS."
- People named: Col. McDougall [uncertain], O.C. 9th District (visit, 9 November); Senior Chaplain of Corps (visit, 7 November); Lieut. Fraser [uncertain] (leave to England, 21 November).
- Armistice: news of the signing of the Armistice received 11 November 1918.
- Forestry work: cutting of road plank ceased 5 November (cut S.G. sleepers instead); felling of trees ceased 12 November, with logs on hand to be converted into 2", 3" and 4" edged stock; turnouts put in on the main line (M. 29) for a siding on Coupe 28 (13 November); sawn timber to be taken to dumps and piled open (14 November); civilian labour paid off 23 November.
- Camp life: camp pet (a wild pig) shot by a French sportsman (3 November); concert by local talent in the Y.M.C.A. hut (9 November); smoking concert in the Dining Hall (14 November); dinner held by the original No. 1 Forest Party to commemorate 3 years of work in France (24 November); Saturday forenoon parades ordered (27 November); company paraded, inspected and given 2 hours' drill (30 November).
- No casualties are recorded. No weather is recorded.
- Remarks column: appendix references entered against each entry, appearing as "W.X." [uncertain].

## Coverage notes

- Dates with no entry in November 1918: 2, 4, 8, 10, 15, 16, 17, 19, 20, 22, 25, 26, 28 and 29 November. Entries exist for 1, 3, 5, 6, 7, 9, 11, 12, 13, 14, 18, 21, 23, 24, 27 and 30 November.
- Page 69 is the typed monthly cover page ("WAR DIARY / NO. 1. COMPANY C. F. C. / NOVEMBER 1918."), not diary text.
- Pages 70 and 71 are the diary content for the month (Army Form C. 2118, manuscript daily entries).
- Number of [uncertain] flags in this file: 6 (the surname "McDougall" on 9 November; the word "willing" on 18 November; the surname "Fraser" on 21 November; a trailing mark after "Civilian labour paid off." on 23 November; the date of the second 14 November entry; the appendix-reference marks in the Remarks column, appearing as "W.X.").
