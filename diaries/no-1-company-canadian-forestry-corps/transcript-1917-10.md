## Coverage notes

- **Dates with entries:** all 31 days of October 1917. Sundays 14, 21 and 28 October are entered as "No Work" — no date has no entry.
- **Dates with no entry:** none.
- **Pages that are covers/contents rather than diary text:** LAC p. 26 (cover), p. 27 (contents — "Diary Text 5 Pages"), p. 28 (title page, "ORIGINAL"). Diary text is on LAC pp. 29–33 (5 pages, Army Form C. 2118).
- **Casualties:** none recorded in October 1917.
- **Weather:** none recorded in October 1917.
- **Movements:** 1 October — 14 N.C.Os. and Other Ranks of the 369th Forest Coy. left for Lyons Forest to rejoin their Company. No other movement is recorded.
- **Other notes:** The 1 October "Shipped 33.6" entry gives no unit in the original. The place column reads "CRECY" on diary pp. 1–3 and "CRECY FOREST." from diary p. 4 (19 October). The marginal initials in the "Remarks and references to Appendices" column are faint handwriting, read here as "M.C.H." (see the [uncertain] flag on 1 October). The mill worked two 9-hour shifts early in the month, dropping to one shift from 13 October; 20 October the mill ran 4 hours only for bench repairs and overhauling.
- **Count of [uncertain] flags in this file:** 12.
