## October 1918 — page 68

War Diary or Intelligence Summary, Army Form C. 2118. Heading, in manuscript: "No. 1 Company C. F. C." A stamp at the top right reads "No. 1 COMPANY, CANADIAN FORESTRY CORPS." Place: "In the Field". Date: "Oct. 1–31".

### Personnel — 2–4 October 1918 — page 68

> On the evening of Oct. 2nd a phone message was received from District H. Qrs. to the effect that a draft of 100 men had been called for from the District as Infantry reinforcements and that our proportion would be 15, also that the men from the other Companies would report here on the 3rd, the complete draft to proceed to C. E. R. C. Aubin St. Vaast on Oct. 4th. On Oct 3rd Capt. Hutchison arrived from Dist. H. Qrs. and he and Lieut Scott, of this Company took charge of all arrangements and personally conducted the draft to their destination, arriving there 12 noon Oct 4th. Owing to men from other Companies being unable to pass the local Medical examination the Company's quota was raised to 18.

### Work — 14 October 1918 — page 68

> The loss of the men drafted into the Infantry and the sickness amongst the P. O. W. attached labour has considerably handicapped operations. During the wk 19th however we despatched 4 trainloads of Road Slabs (27,286 pcs.) by rail. The sidings and dump at Madicourt [uncertain] are now in daily use, shipments being made over them. Eq. to the capacity of available rolling stock.

### Appointments — 21 October 1918 — page 68

> Maj A. J. Bell struck off strength on transfer to Dist. H. Qrs. and appointed 2nd in Command No. 9 Dist. C. F. C.
>
> Capt. W. Hutchison taken on strength on transfer from H. Qrs.

### Attached Labour — 22 October 1918 — page 68

> Influenza broke out early in Oct. in the camp of the 10th P. O. W. Coy. and by the end of the month 80% of the Company were either evacuated to hospital or sick in lines. Luckily, so far this epidemic has been confined to that Company.

### Camp — 31 October 1918 — page 68

> During the month a new Bath House and Y. M. C. A. Hut were completed for the use of the men.

Signed: W. Hutchison, Capt.

### Notes on this month

- Personnel: 18 men of the Company drafted as infantry reinforcements (quota raised from 15 after men from other companies failed the medical examination); the draft proceeded to C.E.R.C., Aubin St. Vaast, on 4 October, conducted by Capt. Hutchison and Lieut. Scott.
- Appointments: Maj. A. J. Bell struck off strength on transfer to District Headquarters and appointed 2nd in Command, No. 9 District C.F.C.; Capt. W. Hutchison taken on strength on transfer from Headquarters.
- Sickness: influenza broke out early in October in the camp of the 10th P.O.W. Company; by the end of the month 80% of that company were evacuated to hospital or sick in lines. The epidemic is noted as confined to that company.
- Forestry work: 4 trainloads of road slabs (27,286 pieces) despatched by rail during the week of the 19th; sidings and dump at Madicourt [uncertain] in daily use; operations handicapped by the loss of the drafted men and sickness among the P.O.W. attached labour.
- Camp: a new Bath House and Y.M.C.A. Hut completed during the month.
- No weather is recorded. No casualties among the Company's own men are recorded.
- Remarks column: appendix references entered against each section, appearing as "10.X." [uncertain].
