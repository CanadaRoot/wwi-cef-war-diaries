## Coverage notes

- **Dates with entries:** all 31 days of December 1917. Sundays 2, 9, 16 and 23 December are entered as "No Work" (23 December also records reinforcements arriving).
- **Dates with no entry:** none.
- **Pages that are covers/contents rather than diary text:** LAC p. 42 (cover), p. 43 (contents — "Diary Text 5 Pages"), p. 44 (title page, "CONFIDENTIAL. (ORIGINAL.)"). Diary text is on LAC pp. 45–49 (5 pages, Army Form C. 2118, numbered (1)–(5)).
- **Casualties:** none recorded in December 1917. 22 December: 10 Other Ranks despatched to the Canadian Labour Pool as unfit for Forestry.
- **Weather:** 15 December — "Heavy Snowfall" (in the Remarks column). No other weather is recorded.
- **Movements / personnel:** 3–4 December — work begins on a barbed-wire-fenced Chinese Compound (to accommodate approximately 600 Chinese) at Arret de la Foret and Machy; 8 December — the 5th Pontoon Park R.E. is not employed on forestry, making preparation to leave for Italy; 22 December — 10 Other Ranks despatched to the Canadian Labour Pool as unfit for Forestry; 23 December — 15 Other Ranks arrived as reinforcements from the Canadian Labour Pool; 25 December (Xmas Day) — a Chinese Company arrived at noon and 40 tents were erected for them, all other work suspended; 27 December — 50 Scottish Rifles attached for charcoal work in the forest, with 2 Sergeants of the L. of C. Charcoal Section R.E.; 28 December — Capt. H. Hellmuth appointed F.D.C.O. Crecy Forest from 21.8.17 and to assume the rank of Major without pay (Auth. CinC. List of Appointments No. 162 dated 19.11.17); 2nd Lieut J.D. Maitland-Kirwin appointed A.F.D.C.O. Crecy Forest.
- **Other notes:** The diary's horse figure drops from 216 (1 December weekly summary) to 44 (15, 22 and 29 December summaries), transcribed as written. The 29 December weekly total of 1,260 men includes a Chinese company (5 officers, 160 other ranks and 502 coolies) plus 3 officers, 7 other ranks and 89 coolies listed as convalescents. The mill was closed down for repairs and rearranging plant from 29 to 31 December. The marginal initials in the "Remarks and references to Appendices" column are faint handwriting, read here as "M.C.H." (see the [uncertain] flag on 1 December). The 28 December entry is split across LAC pp. 48 and 49.
