## Coverage notes

- **Dates with no entry:** none. The diary is kept as weekly summaries (Army Form C. 2118), not daily entries; the weekly blocks cover the whole month. Note the overlapping periods as written: 1st–8th and 3rd–9th February overlap.
- **Pages that are covers/contents rather than diary text:** p. 1 (cover), p. 2 (contents page — states "Diary Text 2 Pages"), p. 3 (title page). Diary text is on pp. 4–5 only.
- **Weather:** none recorded this month.
- **Movements / work:** excavation begun for the foundation of a new Canadian mill to be erected at Arret; 27 hours lost rearranging Marshall Benches (10th–16th); 51 hours lost on 3 benches rearranging and repairing (17th–23rd).
- **Casualties:** none recorded this month.
- **Commanding officer / signatory:** Lieut. M.C. Haight, signing for the O.C., 1st Canadian Forest Company.
- **Cross-reference:** the production figures for Sawlogs, Poles, Pickets etc. for 24th–28th February were not entered in this month's diary; the entry states they "will be entered on Diary for the month of March" — they appear in the March diary entry for 1st–2nd March (week ending 2nd March 1918).
- **Count of uncertain flags in the transcription above:** 1 (the "Logs produced No. 260" figure in the 3rd–9th February entry appears corrected/overwritten).
