## Coverage notes

- **Dates with entries:** 29 of 30 days. Sundays 4, 11, 18 and 25 November are entered as "No Work".
- **Dates with no entry:** 8 November 1917 — the diary jumps from 7 November (LAC p. 37) to 9 November (LAC p. 38) with no entry for 8 November. No other date is missing.
- **Pages that are covers/contents rather than diary text:** LAC p. 34 (cover), p. 35 (contents — "Diary Text 5 Pages"), p. 36 (title page, "CONFIDENTIAL (ORIGINAL)"). Diary text is on LAC pp. 37–41 (5 pages, Army Form C. 2118).
- **Casualties:** none recorded in November 1917.
- **Weather:** none recorded in November 1917.
- **Movements / reinforcements:** 20 November — 34 reinforcements arrived from C.I.B. Depot, Etaples; 22 November — 6 reinforcements arrived from C.I.B. Depot, Etaples. Mill manning rises from 40 to 44 men per shift after 20 November.
- **Other notes:** The entries for 15 and 16 November give identical production figures (Road Slabs 4391 F.B.M., Forest Planking 2112 F.B.M.), transcribed as written. The 23 November entry's forest planking figure (9822 F.B.M.) is identical to the 22 November figure — see the [uncertain] flag on that entry. The marginal initials in the "Remarks and references to Appendices" column are faint handwriting, read here as "M.C.H." (see the [uncertain] flag on 1 November). "All Defence Timber reclassified in Forest during the week" is noted on 17 November.
