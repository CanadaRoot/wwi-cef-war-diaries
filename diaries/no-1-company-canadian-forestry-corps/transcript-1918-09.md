## 1–30 September 1918 — page 66

War Diary or Intelligence Summary, Army Form C. 2118. Heading, in manuscript: "No. 1 Company Canadian Forestry Corps". Place: "Crecy Forest". Date: "Sept 1st to 30th 18."

> During the month of September there is very little of importance to comment upon. The continued success of the Allies kept all busy shipping Road Planks and Fascines for road-making etc. at the Front.
>
> Total Road Planks despatched 184,343 F. B. M.
>
> " Fascines " 7,716 No.
>
> 46 Other ranks were granted leave in France and the U. K. during the month.

Signed: [J.?] Flynn [uncertain], Lieut., O/C No. 1 Coy. C.F.C.

### Notes on this month

- No daily entries: the September diary is a single monthly summary, not day-by-day entries.
- No casualties, movements, or detachments are recorded for September 1918.
- Forestry work: shipping road planks and fascines for road-making at the Front; 184,343 F.B.M. of road planks and 7,716 fascines despatched during the month.
- Leave: 46 other ranks granted leave in France and the United Kingdom during the month.
- No weather is recorded.

## Coverage notes

- Dates with no entry: none individually — the diary gives one summary covering 1–30 September 1918 as a whole.
- Page 65 is the typed monthly cover page ("WAR DIARY / NO. 1 COMPANY C. F. C. / SEPTEMBER 1918."), not diary text.
- Page 66 is the full diary content for the month (Army Form C. 2118, manuscript summary).
