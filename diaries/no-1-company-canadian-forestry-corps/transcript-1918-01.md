## Coverage notes

- **Dates with no entry:** none. The diary is kept as weekly summaries (Army Form C. 2118), not daily entries; the five weekly blocks (1st–5th, 6th–12th, 13th–19th, 20th–26th, 27th–31st January) cover the whole month.
- **Pages that are covers/contents rather than diary text:** p. 1 (cover), p. 2 (contents page — states "Diary Text 2 Pages"), p. 3 (title page). Diary text is on pp. 4–5 only.
- **Weather:** snow hampering work and transport (1st–12th); thaw and bad roads (13th–19th).
- **Movements:** 117th Chinese Labour Company moved in on 7th January.
- **Casualties:** none recorded this month.
- **Commanding officer / signatory:** a Major, O.C. 1st Canadian Forest Company — signature illegible. The remarks column initials throughout the month read "M.C.H." (handwriting could also read "M.C.N."); they match Lieut. M.C. Haight, who signs the February diary for the O.C.
- **Count of uncertain flags in the transcription above:** 7 (remarks initials ×5, signatory signature ×1, handwritten mark on p. 3 ×1).
