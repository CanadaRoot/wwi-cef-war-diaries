## Coverage notes

- **Dates with no entry:** none. The diary is kept as weekly summaries (Army Form C. 2118), not daily entries; the weekly blocks (1st–2nd, 3rd–9th, 10th–16th, 17th–23rd, 24th–30th March, plus 31st March) cover the whole month.
- **Pages that are covers/contents rather than diary text:** p. 1 (cover), p. 2 (contents page — states "Diary Text 2 Pages"), p. 3 (title page). Diary text is on pp. 4–5 only.
- **Weather:** none recorded this month.
- **Movements:** 117th Chinese Labour Company replaced by the 118th P.O.W. Company on 31st March.
- **Work:** concentration on picket production in the last week (154,932 pickets produced 24th–30th March); mill reduced to 2 shifts of 10 hours only, owing to concentration on pickets.
- **Casualties:** none recorded this month.
- **Commanding officer / signatory:** a Major, O.C. 1st Canadian Forest Company — signature illegible; the same signature as in the January diary.
- **Cross-reference:** the 1st–2nd March entry gives the production figures for the week ending 2nd March 1918, i.e. the Sawlogs, Poles, Pickets etc. figures for 24th–28th February that the February diary deferred to March.
- **Count of uncertain flags in the transcription above:** 6 (remarks initials "H.H." ×5, signatory signature ×1).
