Transcription conventions: original spelling, dates, places and wording kept. Anything illegible or uncertain is marked [uncertain]. ## Notes on this volume

- **Missing dates:** None — every date 1–29 February 1916 has an entry (1916 was a leap year).
- **No blank or duplicate pages** in this volume. **No monthly summary chart is present in February 1916.**
- **Named people:** Capt. Colbourn (escorted 28 horses to the Convalescent Depot, Gournay-en-Bray, 2 February); Col. Hemming (inspected the "Dip", 4 February); Major Gowan, Financial Adviser (inspected books and Q.M. stores, 9 February); D.D.V.S. (Southern) (visited 13 and 19 February).
- **Places:** Havre (hospital); Gournay-en-Bray (Convalescent Depot); No. 22 Veterinary Hospital (125 horses and 15 mules admitted 6 February; 128 horses and 84 mules admitted 17 February); No. 4 Company, Divisional Train, 3rd Contingent C.E.F. (3 horses admitted 22 February).
- **Casualties (animals):** 1 horse destroyed (1 February); 1 horse died and 1 horse destroyed (7 February); 15 horses and 4 mules cast and sold by public auction (7 February); 1 horse destroyed (8 February); 1 horse destroyed (10 February); 1 horse destroyed (14 February); 1 horse destroyed (17 February); 2 horses died and 1 horse destroyed (19 February); 1 horse destroyed (22 February); 1 horse died, 1 horse shot, 1 horse destroyed (23 February); 2 horses shot and 2 horses destroyed (butcher) (26 February); 1 horse shot (27 February).
- **Notable — 12 February:** "Zeppelin alarm 9 p.m." — a Zeppelin air-raid alarm at 9 p.m. (the diary records no raid or damage).
- **Notable:** Large admissions from No. 22 Veterinary Hospital (140 animals on 6 February; 212 on 17 February). A separate "shot" category of horse casualties appears in the daily entries from 23 February (previously the diary used "destroyed" and "died").
- **Opening balance:** January closed with 507 horses and mules in hospital on 1 January 1916 (see transcript-1916-01.md); no February monthly summary is present to close the month.
