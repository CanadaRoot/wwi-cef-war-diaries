Form heading: WAR DIARY or INTELLIGENCE SUMMARY (the words "INTELLIGENCE SUMMARY" struck through).

## Notes

- All 30 days of April 1916 are covered on the single diary-text page (p. 4). No dates are missing and there are no duplicate pages.
- The cover page (p. 1) is headed "CONFIDENTIAL" and gives the volume as "Volume 12"; the typed inventory sheet (p. 2) gives it as "Volume 16-4". Both are transcribed as written.
- The diary is kept by Captain C. G. Saunders. Each daily entry ends with his initials, here rendered "C.G.S." (the manuscript initials are written in a flourish and are not always fully legible).
- Place is given only as "Havre" (ditto marks thereafter).
- Uncertain readings: the name after Capt. Watson on 2 April ([uncertain: Henmore]); the number of other ranks on 11 April ([uncertain: 202]); the visiting doctor's name on 12 April ([uncertain: Marshall]); the place name on 14 April ([uncertain: Gournay en Bray]); and a word before "admitted" on 24 April. The word "cast" on 3 April (animals cast and sold) is the best reading of the handwriting.
- Animal losses recorded in April: horses died on 2, 16 and 20 April (1 each); 1 horse shot on 21 April; 1 mule shot on 25 April. Large movements: 100 horses admitted from No. 22 on 10 April and 100 horses discharged to C.H. Depot on 14 April.
