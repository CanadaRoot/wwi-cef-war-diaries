## Notes

- **Page count and coverage:** All four pages for November 1916 (pp. 1–4) are present and transcribed. Page 1 is the cover, page 2 is a typed title page, page 3 is the Army Form C. 2118 title sheet, and page 4 is the single diary sheet carrying all 30 daily entries. No pages are missing or duplicated.
- **Volume numbers:** The cover gives "Volume 19" while the typed title page (p. 2) gives "Volume 16-11". Both are recorded as written (see the October notes for the "16-10/11/12" serial scheme).
- **Day count:** 30 entries for 30 days; the 30 November entry spans two lines. Unlike October, there is no missing or ambiguous line — the date column and the content lines align throughout.
- **"Cfs.":** This abbreviation trails most entries and is left unexpanded; it is probably the diarist's initials or a signature mark.
- **"D.D.V.S.":** The 1 November entry clearly reads "D.D.V.S. inspection" (Deputy Director of Veterinary Services). The same abbreviation on 2 and 20 November is written with an initial letter resembling "3" or "F"; it is read as "D.D.V.S." on the strength of the clear 1 November and 7 December renderings, with the initial letter marked [uncertain] where the hand is unclear.
- **Capt. Sprouston (3 November):** The captain's surname is read as "Sprouston" but the spelling is uncertain — the hand is cramped and the name could be read as "Sproston" or similar. It is marked [uncertain]. He went on leave on 3 November.
- **Uncertain numbers:** 187 horses (3 Nov), 184 horses (12 Nov), 177 horses (17 Nov), 172 horses (25 Nov). Several other large intake figures (57, 68, 83, 48) are legible but should be treated with the usual caution for this hand.
- **28 November:** No "Cfs" is visible at the end of this line; recorded as written.
- **Named people:** Major C. G. Saunders (Officer Commanding); Capt. Baker, Quartermaster (arrived 5 November, having departed 24 October 1916); Capt. Roy (reported 16 November); Capt. Sprouston [uncertain] (on leave from 3 November).
- **Notable events:** The D.D.V.S. inspected on 1 November (and again, in the diarist's shorthand, on 2 and 20 November). On 30 November a Fire Committee and Fire Parade were held, and the diary records that 200 animals fit for work were still in the hospital because the Remounts depot was full up — a sign of congestion in the veterinary evacuation chain. Large intakes from No. 7 and No. 22 continued through the month, with animals discharged to the Remounts when fit.
