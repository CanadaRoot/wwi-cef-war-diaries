## Notes

- **No missing dates:** entries run 1–30 September 1916 with no gaps (September has 30 days). No duplicate pages.
- **Blank/cover pages:** p. 1 is the manuscript cover; p. 2 is the typed index/header sheet ("Diary Text — 1 Page"); p. 3 is the typed title page on Army Form C. 2118. Only p. 4 carries diary text.
- **Volume and naming discrepancies:** the cover reads "Volume 17" while p. 2 reads "Volume 16-9". Unusually, this month's cover, header sheet and title page all drop "No. 1" and read simply "Canadian Veterinary Hospital".
- **Uncertain readings:** 2 September — "66 mules" is as written (could be "6 mules"); 6 September — the surnames read as "Baker" and "Bishop" but the handwriting is not fully clear; 15 September — "155 horses" is as written (the digits are close and could be read another way); 16 September — "194 horses" is as written; 23 September — no initials follow this entry, as written; 26 September — the opening words before "158 horses" are not legible (apparently an officer's initials and a verb such as "reported" or "inspected"). The handwriting is small and compressed; day-to-entry alignment was checked against the date column in overlapping enlarged sections.
- **Abbreviations as written (likely expansions, not in the original):** C.G.S. = Major C. G. Saunders' initials; D.V.S. = Director of Veterinary Services; A.D.V.S. = Assistant Director of Veterinary Services; C.H.D. = Canadian Horse Depot (not expanded in the original); remts = remounts; Hosp. = Hospital; V.H. = Veterinary Hospital; C.A.V.C. = Canadian Army Veterinary Corps.
- **Notable content:** the only named people in the three months transcribed are Capt. Baker and Lieut. Bishop, who "reported" on 6 September (the nature of their report is not stated). An A.D.V.S. inspection is recorded on 4 September and a D.V.S. inspection on 23 September (and again, in passing, on 30 September). Large admissions again came from No. 7 Veterinary Hospital and No. 22 (200 horses and 12 mules admitted on 8 September; 194 horses and 6 mules sent to C.H.D. on 16 September). The hospital's routine — admissions, discharges to remounts, animals sold to the butcher, shot, or dying — continues as in July and August.
