# War Diary — May 1915: No Separate Volume

**Unit:** Canadian Veterinary Hospital
**Month:** May 1915
**Source:** Library and Archives Canada, Item 2005175, RG 9 III-D-3, vol. 5044, file 894, reel T-10936

There is **no separate May 1915 volume** in this diary's manifest.

All May 1915 entries (1–31 May 1915) are contained in **Volume 1**, which is filed under April 1915 (manifest pages 1–13) and covers 1 April to 10 June 1915. They are transcribed in [transcript-1915-04.md](transcript-1915-04.md) (pages 10–13 of that volume).

This note file exists only so the absence of a May volume is recorded explicitly and May is not mistaken for missing or untranscribed.
