**Source:** Library and Archives Canada, Item 2005175, RG 9 III-D-3, vol. 5044, file 894, reel T-10936.

**Transcription conventions:** Original spelling, wording and abbreviations are kept. Ditto marks (") in the diary are rendered as [ditto]. Anything illegible or uncertain is marked [uncertain]. ## Notes on this month

- No dates are missing: entries run 1–31 December 1917, with the 18th entered twice (see table above).
- No pages are blank or covers beyond pages 178–180, which are the cover, diary-text cover sheet, and title page respectively; the only diary-text page is page 181.
- No duplicate pages.
- Discrepancy in volume numbering: the cover (page 178) gives "VOLUME 32", while the diary-text cover sheet (page 179) gives "Volume 17-12".
- The year on the first diary line (page 181) appears to read 1918, although the cover dates the month December 1917; treated as a slip and marked [uncertain] above.
- No place, camp, or location is named in the December diary. No human casualties are recorded; all entries concern animals admitted, cured to remounts, transferred to other veterinary hospitals (principally No. 2 Vet Hospital), shot, died, destroyed, or sent to the butcher.
