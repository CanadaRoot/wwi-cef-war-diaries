## Notes on this volume

- **Missing dates:** None — every date 1–31 July 1915 has an entry.
- **No blank or duplicate pages** in this volume; pages 26–27 are cover/title pages, pages 28–31 are the diary sheets, page 32 is the monthly return.
- **Named people:** Capt. Burnet (escorted 100 horses to No. 8 Veterinary Hospital, Forges-les-Eaux, 26 July); Major C. G. Saunders (Court of Enquiry, 12 July; returned 20 July; took over during the O.C.'s leave, 27 July); Corporal W. Scott (promoted provisional Corporal for Police duty, 21 July); Sergt. Cameron (reverted to Corporal at his own request, 27 July); Capt. Gundry (charges received from C.D.A.C., 29 July); Prince Arthur of Connaught (inspected the hospital, 26 July, 2.30 pm); Trooper No. 21204 [surname uncertain — Stoddart?] (entry at foot of page 28 partly cut off, [uncertain]); Capt. [Dunham] and [Balmain] (reported 3 July, departed 17 July — spellings [uncertain]).
- **Places:** Havre (hospital); Forges-les-Eaux (No. 8 Veterinary Hospital, A.V.C.).
- **Casualties (animals):** Horses destroyed or died of pneumonia on 7, 11, 16, 18, 22 and 29 July; monthly totals: 4 destroyed unfit for food, 3 died unfit for food, 2 destroyed fit for food.
- **Monthly totals (July):** 671 under treatment at 1 July; 378 admitted; 1,049 total cases; 396 cured to Remounts; 100 transferred to No. 8 Veterinary Hospital; 544 remaining under treatment at 31 July.
