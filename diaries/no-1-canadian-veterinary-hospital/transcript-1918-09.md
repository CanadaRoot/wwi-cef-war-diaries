## Page 218 — Diary entries, 2–30 September 1918

[Place column: Havre]

**Sept 2nd 1918 — Havre**
Capt J. D. Macdonald & Capt W. W. Batty reported from England. S.C.R.

**" 3rd —**
4 O.R. reported from Can. Gen. Base Depot. S.C.R.

**" 4th —**
Lt. W. [uncertain] left for 2nd Can. Div. — 1 man invalided to England. S.C.R.

**" 5th —**
1 Sgt sent to C.C.V.E.S. — 1 man sent to "A" C.M.V.S. — 1 man sent to No. 1 C.M.V.S. — 1 man transferred to Labour pool. S.C.R.

**" 7th —**
Capt C. French reported from 2nd Can. Div. — 6 O.R. reported on probation.
1 man invalided to England. S.C.R.

**" 8th —**
1 Sgt [uncertain], 1 Sgt [uncertain] left to C.C.V.E.S. S.C.R.

**" 10th —**
1 Vet Sgt sent 9th Bde C.F.A. — 1 O.R. left for No. 1 C.M.V.S. — 4 O.R. taken on strength as suitable. S.C.R.

**" 11th —**
20 O.R. reported on probation — 1 man invalided to England. S.C.R.

**" 12th —**
1 O.R. left to report O.C. Reinforcements Etaples. S.C.R.

**" 16th —**
1 O.R. reported from C.C.V.E.S. — 1 Sgt sent to C.C.V.E.S. S.C.R.

**" 17th —**
Lt. [uncertain] Blenkinsop [uncertain], Maj. [uncertain] Moore [uncertain] — Col Newsom visited the hospital. S.C.R.

**" 18th —**
2 men left for C.C.V.E.S. — 2 Staff Majors from Canadian Section, 1st Echelon G.H.Q. visited Camp — 1 Vet Sgt sent to 2nd [uncertain]. S.C.R.

**" 20th —**
12 O.R. sent to Etaples for transfer to England — Capt A. H. Hunter struck off strength — 5 O.R. reported from C.B.D. S.C.R.

**" 23rd —**
1 Vet Sgt sent to 4th Dist C.F.C. S.C.R.

**" 26th —**
1 [uncertain] — 10th Bde C.F.A. — 1 Vet Sgt sent to 3rd Bde C.F.A. — 10 O.R. reported from C.B.D. S.C.R.
1 O.R. reported from Hq 3rd Div. — 1 Vet Sgt from 3rd Bde C.F.A. — 1 Vet Sgt from C.B.D. — 1 O.R. from C.B.D. — 1 Cpl [uncertain] from C.C.V.E.S. S.C.R.

**" 25th —**
Capt J. W. Morgan & Lt. D. Halbert reported from England — 1 Sgt from 4th Dist C.F.C. S.C.R.
Capt W. W. Batty left for 5th Div. — Capt J. D. Macdonald to 5th Dist C.F.C.

**" 27th —**
3 O.R. reported on probation. S.C.R.

**" 30th —**
Col Newsom visited hospital. S.C.R.

**Monthly summary (undated, end of September):**
1 man classified unfit & posted to Gen. Depot St. Cliff [uncertain — place name].
1 man invalided to England.

[Signed:]
S. C. Richards Major Cavc.
Cmdg Canadian Vet'y Hospital

## Notes on this month

- **Missing dates (no entry):** 1, 6, 9, 13, 14, 15, 19, 21, 22, 24, 28 and 29 September 1918.
- **Blank / cover pages:** Page 215 is the cover page; page 216 is a contents/title page (no diary entries); page 217 is a diary title page (no diary entries, and the typed text on it is faded). The actual diary text is on page 218 only, as stated on page 216 ("Diary Text 1 Page").
- **No duplicate pages.**
- **Uncertain readings:** This month's diary page is closely written and several words are hard to read. Lt. W. —'s surname on 4 September, the two sergeants' designations on 8 September, the visitors' names on 17 September, the destination on 18 September, and parts of the 26 September entries are not certain. The depot name in the monthly summary is read as "St. Cliff" but is not certain. The marginal initials throughout appear to be "S.C.R." (Major S. C. Richards).
- **Place:** The place column throughout reads "Havre" (Le Havre, France).
- **Named people:** Capt J. D. Macdonald; Capt W. W. Batty; Capt C. French; Lt. [uncertain] Blenkinsop; Maj. [uncertain] Moore; Col Newsom; Capt A. H. Hunter; Capt J. W. Morgan; Lt. D. Halbert; Major S. C. Richards (Commanding Officer).
- **Movements:** 12 other ranks sent to Etaples for transfer to England (20 September); Capt A. H. Hunter struck off strength (20 September); officers arriving from England and posting out to the 5th Division and Forestry Corps districts.
