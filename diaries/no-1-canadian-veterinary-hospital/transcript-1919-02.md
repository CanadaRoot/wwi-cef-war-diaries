## Notes on this month

- **Date discrepancy:** The cover page (page 234) reads "FROM 1st Feby TO 28th Feby 1919", but the "1st" is circled with "3rd" written above it, and the contents page (page 235) reads "From 3rd to 28th February, 1919". The first diary entry is dated 3 February.
- **Page-count discrepancy:** The contents page states "Diary Text 2 Pages", but only one diary text page (page 237) is present in the manifest for this month. The entries on that page run 3–28 February and the page is signed, so no text appears to be missing from the diary itself.
- **Missing dates (no entry):** 1, 2, 4, 5, 6, 9, 10, 11, 12, 13, 14, 16, 17, 20, 24, 26 and 27 February 1919. (1–2 February are outside the corrected period, which begins 3 February.)
- **Blank / cover pages:** Page 234 is the cover page; page 235 is a contents/title page (no diary entries); page 236 is a diary title page (no diary entries). The actual diary text is on page 237 only.
- **No duplicate pages.**
- **Uncertain readings:** The third captain's surname and the remainder of the 3 February entry are not certain. The 19 February entry is partly illegible. The wording on 21 February is read as "4 N.C.O.s retained in England" but is not certain.
- **Place:** The place column throughout reads "Havre" (Le Havre, France).
- **Named people:** Capt A. F. Cameron; Capt A. W. Bunnell; J. H. [uncertain]; Lt. J. N. Gaffrey; Lt. W. Gunning; Lt. W. H. Foreman; Capt H. S. Clapp; Major S. C. Richards (Commanding Officer).
- **Movements — demobilisation:** Large drafts sent for evacuation to England — 144 other ranks to the Canadian Base Depot (8 February), 26 more (15 February), 7 to the Canadian Embarkation Camp (18 February), 39 to the Canadian Embarkation Camp, Havre (22 February), and 28 to the Canadian Embarkation Camp (28 February). Lt. Gunning left for the Fort Garry Horse (15 February).
