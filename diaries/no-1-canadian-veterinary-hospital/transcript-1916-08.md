## Notes

- **No missing dates:** entries run 1–31 August 1916 with no gaps. No duplicate pages.
- **Blank/cover pages:** p. 1 is the manuscript cover; p. 2 is the typed index/header sheet ("Diary Text — 1 Page"); p. 3 is the typed title page on Army Form C. 2118. Only p. 4 carries diary text.
- **Volume discrepancy:** the cover reads "Volume 16" while p. 2 reads "Volume 16-8".
- **31 August:** the entry wraps to a second line ("18 horses 14 mules discharged C.G.S."), which is treated here as a continuation of the 31 August entry.
- **Uncertain readings:** 11 August — "328 horses" is as written (the figure is large and the digits are close); 20 August — "168 horses" as written; 22 August — "cast for sold" is as written (possibly "cast, for sale"); 25 August — "144 horses" is as written (the digits read "14 4"); 27 August — "128 horses" as written. The handwriting is small and compressed in the middle of the page; day-to-entry alignment was checked against the date column in overlapping enlarged sections.
- **Abbreviations as written (likely expansions, not in the original):** C.G.S. = Major C. G. Saunders' initials; C.H.D. = Canadian Horse Depot (not expanded in the original); V.H. = Veterinary Hospital; C.A.V.C. = Canadian Army Veterinary Corps; O.C. = Officer Commanding.
- **Notable content:** no D.V.S. inspection is recorded this month (contrast July). The month is dominated by routine admissions from No. 7 Veterinary Hospital (328 horses and 52 mules on 11 August; 168 horses and 33 mules on 20 August; 128 horses and 22 mules on 27 August) and steady discharges, with animals sold to the butcher, shot, or dying on most days. 16 and 17 August both record a single "1 horse shot". No individual animals or other ranks are named; the only person named is Major C. G. Saunders.
