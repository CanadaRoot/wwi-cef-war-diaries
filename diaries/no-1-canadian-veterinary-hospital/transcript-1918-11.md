## Page 226 — Diary entries, 1–30 November 1918

[Place column: Havre]

**November 1st 1918 — Havre**
13 men reported on probation — Lt. J. A. Sheppard left for temp duty with No. 12 Dist C.F.C. S.C.R.

**" 4th —**
Col Newsom visited hospital. S.C.R.

**" 5th —**
1 Cpl reported from C.A.S.C. pool. S.C.R.

**" 6th —**
30 men taken on strength from Can. Lab. pool. Capt Hopkins reported from hospital. S.C.R.

**" 7th —**
3 Vet Sergts reported from C.B.D. (Casuals). S.C.R.

**" 9th —**
2 men left for No. 2 C.M.V.S. S.C.R.

**" 10th —**
1 Vet Sgt left for No. 5 Dist C.F.C. S.C.R.

**" 11th —**
Capt C. French struck off strength posted to [uncertain] Depôt. S.C.R.

**" 12th —**
Col Newsom visited hospital. S.C.R.

**" 15th —**
1 man adm. to hospital in England on leave. S.C.R.

**" 18th —**
Lt. K. W. McKinnon reported from England. S.C.R.

**" 21st —**
1 man reported from C.B.D. S.C.R.

**" 23rd —**
1 N.C.O. & 7 men sent to C.B.D. for Medical Board — 15 men left for temp duty with C.C.V.E.S. — Col Newsom visited Camp. S.C.R.

**" 27th —**
1 man reported from leave. S.C.R.

**" 29th —**
1 man invalided — 1 man reported from C.A.S.C. pool. S.C.R.

**" 30th —**
Lt. A. Chambers left for 14th Bde C.F.A. S.C.R.

[Signed:]
S. C. Richards Major Cavc.
Cmdg Canadian Veterinary Hospital

## Notes on this month

- **Missing dates (no entry):** 2, 3, 8, 13, 14, 16, 17, 19, 20, 22, 24, 25, 26 and 28 November 1918.
- **Blank / cover pages:** Page 223 is the cover page; page 224 is a contents/title page (no diary entries); page 225 is a diary title page (no diary entries, and no month/year is given on it). The actual diary text is on page 226 only, as stated on page 224 ("Diary Text 1 Page").
- **No duplicate pages.**
- **Uncertain readings:** The posting on 11 November is partly illegible ("posted to [uncertain] Depôt"). The marginal initials throughout appear to be "S.C.R." (Major S. C. Richards).
- **Place:** The place column throughout reads "Havre" (Le Havre, France).
- **Named people:** Lt. J. A. Sheppard; Col Newsom; Capt Hopkins; Capt C. French; Lt. K. W. McKinnon; Lt. A. Chambers; Major S. C. Richards (Commanding Officer).
- **Notable finding:** The diary makes no mention of the Armistice of 11 November 1918. The 11 November entry records only that Capt C. French was struck off strength.
- **Movements:** 30 men taken on strength from the Canadian Labour pool (6 November); 15 men left for temporary duty with the Canadian Corps Veterinary Evacuation Station (23 November); 1 N.C.O. and 7 men sent to the Canadian Base Depot for a Medical Board (23 November).
