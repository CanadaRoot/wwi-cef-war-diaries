## Notes on this month

- **Missing dates (no entry):** 3, 5, 8, 11, 14, 16, 17, 18, 19, 21, 22, 25 and 30 August 1918.
- **Blank / cover pages:** Page 211 is the cover page; page 212 is a contents/title page (no diary entries); page 213 is a diary title page (no diary entries). The actual diary text is on page 214 only, as stated on page 212 ("Diary Text 1 Page").
- **No duplicate pages.**
- **Uncertain readings:** This month's diary page is closely written and several words are hard to read. The rank on 4 August and the rank/word on 20 August are not certain. The destinations on 9 August are partly illegible. Capt A. A. —'s surname on 12 August is not certain. The "3rd A.F.A. Bde" on 29 August is as written but the abbreviation is not certain. The marginal initials throughout appear to be "S.C.R." (Major S. C. Richards).
- **Place:** The place column throughout reads "Havre" (Le Havre, France).
- **Named people:** Col Newsom; Capt J. F. Boswell; Capt A. A. [uncertain]; Major-General Moore; Major S. C. Richards (Commanding Officer).
- **Notable finding — casualty:** On 13 August, one man (unnamed in the diary) was killed in action by enemy aircraft. This is the only death recorded in the portion transcribed so far (April–August 1918).
- **Movements:** Several veterinary sergeants posted out to artillery brigades, the Canadian Machine Gun Corps and the Canadian Corps Veterinary Evacuation Station; several men invalided to England through the month.
