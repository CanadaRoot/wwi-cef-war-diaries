# War Diary Transcript — Canadian Veterinary Hospital, April 1915

**Unit:** Canadian Veterinary Hospital (later styled No. 1 Canadian Veterinary Hospital)
**Month (manifest):** April 1915
**Manifest pages:** 1–13 (image files page-0001 to page-0013)
**Source:** Library and Archives Canada, Item 2005175, RG 9 III-D-3, vol. 5044, file 894, reel T-10936
**Volume:** Volume 1 — "From 1st April to 10th June, 1915", Volume 15-4 to 15-6, Diary Text 11 Pages
**Author / O.C.:** Capt. Thomas Charles Evans, C.A.V.C. (signed "Chas Evans Capt C.A.V.C.")

> **IMPORTANT — date range of this file:** Although filed under "April 1915", Volume 1 covers **1 April to 10 June 1915**. It contains the whole of May 1915 and the first ten days of June 1915. There is **no separate May 1915 volume** in the manifest, and no separate May 1915 transcript file has been created — the May entries are transcribed here. A second June volume (Volume 2, 1–30 June 1915, by Capt. V. C. Leckie) overlaps 1–10 June; see transcript-1915-06.md.

Transcription conventions: original spelling, dates, places and wording kept. Anything illegible or uncertain is marked [uncertain]. The diary is handwritten on Army Form C. 2118 ("War Diary or Intelligence Summary"); the printed "Intelligence Summary" heading is struck out on most sheets. Remarks-column initials are mostly the diarist's initials ("C.E." / "Chas Evans").

## Notes on this volume

- **No May volume exists.** The manifest's "April 1915" pages 1–13 are Volume 1, 1 April–10 June 1915, and contain all May 1915 entries. See also transcript-1915-05.md (pointer note).
- **Overlap with Volume 2:** 1–10 June 1915 is covered again in Volume 2 (transcript-1915-06.md), written by Capt. V. C. Leckie after he assumed command on 2 June. The two accounts differ in detail (e.g. Volume 1 says the hospital took over 539 horses/mules on 10 April; Volume 2 says 223 sick horses on 2 June).
- **Missing dates in this volume:** No entries for 16 April, 3 May (other than the fire/burst entry dated "May 1st" continuation), 4 May is not separately dated; 30 May has an entry, 31 May has an entry. Dates 12–13 April are grouped; 21–26 April grouped; 27–29 April grouped. No entry for 30 April as a separate date (grouped with 27th–29th).
- **No monthly summary table** is included in this volume.
- **Blank/cover pages:** Pages 1–2 are cover/title pages only (no diary text).
- **Handwriting:** Volume 1 is in Capt. Evans's hand, which is frequently difficult; many words are marked [uncertain] rather than guessed. Names confirmed elsewhere in the diary: Turnbull, Edwards, Liddle, Burnet, Richards, Stedman, Leckie, Saunders, Gundry, Butler, Davis.
- **Casualties (animals):** Two horses drowned at Southampton Docks (2 April). No human casualties recorded in this volume.
- **Key movements:** Netheravon (Wiltshire) → Amesbury → Southampton (2 April) → Havre, France (4 April); hospital established at No. 7 Camp, Havre, taking over the "Mange Hospital" fields on 10 April; Evans leaves for No. 5 Veterinary Hospital, Abbeville (10 June).
