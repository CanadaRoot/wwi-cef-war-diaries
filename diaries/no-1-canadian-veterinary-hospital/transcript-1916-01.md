# War Diary Transcript — No. 1 Canadian Veterinary Hospital, January 1916

**Unit:** No. 1 Canadian Veterinary Hospital, Havre
**Month:** January 1916
**Manifest pages:** 77–82 (image files page-0077 to page-0082)
**Source:** Library and Archives Canada, Item 2005175, RG 9 III-D-3, vol. 5044, file 894, reel T-10936
**Volume:** Volume 9 — "From 1st January to 31st January 1916", Volume 16-1, Diary Text 4 Pages
**Author / O.C.:** Capt. C. G. Saunders, C.A.V.C.

> **Unit styling:** From January 1916 the unit is styled "No. 1 Canadian Veterinary Hospital" (previously "Canadian Veterinary Hospital").

Transcription conventions: original spelling, dates, places and wording kept. Anything illegible or uncertain is marked [uncertain]. The diary is handwritten on Army Form C. 2118; the printed "Intelligence Summary" heading is struck out. The diarist initials entries in the remarks column ("C.G.S.").

## Page 79 — Sheet 1

**Havre, Jan 1st**

> Total horses & mules in hospital — 507.

**" " 2nd**

> 2 horses destroyed.

**" " 3rd**

> 12 horses & 1 mule sold by Public Auction as unfit for Army purposes (by authority of D.D.V.S.). 1 horse died.

**" " 4th**

> 73 horses & 17 mules transferred sick from No 2 Vet Hospital. 1 horse destroyed. 30 horses discharged cured.

**" " 5th**

> 1 horse destroyed.

**" " 6th**

> D.V.S. & R. Canadians inspected hospital. 1 horse destroyed. 32 horses discharged cured.

**" " 7th**

> 3 horses admitted.

**" " 8th**

> 2 horses & 5 mules admitted. 8 horses & 2 mules discharged cured.

**" " 9th**

> Daily routine as usual.

## Page 80

**Havre, Jan 10th**

> 2 horses & 3 mules admitted. 1 horse destroyed.

**" " 11th**

> Daily routine as usual.

**" " 12th**

> 3 horses & 3 mules admitted. 2 horses destroyed.

**" " 13th**

> D.D.V.S. inspected hospital & cast 14 horses. Looked over site of prospective extension of stables. 3 horses & 2 mules admitted. 19 horses & 5 mules discharged cured.

**" " 14th**

> 1 horse & 3 mules admitted. 2 animals died & 2 destroyed.

**" " 15th**

> I.G.C. inspected hospital. 1 horse died. 16 horses discharged cured.

**" " 16th**

> 6 horses & 5 mules admitted.

**" " 17th**

> Daily routine as usual.

**" " 18th**

> 14 horses admitted. 33 horses & 8 mules discharged cured.

**" " 19th**

> About 11.45 a.m. the Orderly Officer reported to me that an Officer was prowling around the Horse lines acting in a suspicious manner. I sent the Orderly Officer to enquire who he was, but he evaded the Orderly Officer and slipped away through the back entrance. The Officer was afterwards identified as Capt T.C. Evans (late O.C. of this Hospital). Capt Evans did not report at the Orderly Room or Officers Mess or make his presence known in any way. In my opinion his actions are not those becoming an Officer or Gentleman. 92 horses [(8 mules) — uncertain] admitted from No 22 Vet Hospital, 6 mules from Remounts.

**" " 20th**

> D.D.V.S. and Major Wadly A.V.C. visited hospital. 7 horses & 3 mules discharged cured.

## Page 81

> Daily routine as usual.

> 21 horses & 10 mules discharged cured.

**" " 24th**

## Notes on this volume

- **Missing dates:** None — every date 1–31 January 1916 has an entry.
- **No blank or duplicate pages** in this volume. **No monthly summary chart is present in January 1916.**
- **Named people:** Capt. C. G. Saunders (O.C.); Capt. T. C. Evans (late O.C. — see 19 January entry); Major Wadly, A.V.C. (visited with D.D.V.S., 20 January); the D.V.S. and "R. Canadians" (inspected 6 January); the D.D.V.S. (inspected 13, 26 and 27 January; cast 14 horses on 13 January and 4 horses and 1 mule on 27 January); the I.G.C. (inspected 15 January).
- **Places:** Havre (hospital); No. 2 Veterinary Hospital; No. 22 Veterinary Hospital (92 horses admitted 19 January).
- **Casualties (animals):** 2 horses destroyed (2 January); 1 horse died (3 January); 12 horses and 1 mule sold by public auction as unfit for Army purposes (3 January); 1 horse destroyed (4 January); 1 horse destroyed (5 January); 1 horse destroyed (6 January); 1 horse destroyed (10 January); 2 horses destroyed (12 January); 2 animals died and 2 destroyed (14 January); 1 horse died (15 January); 1 horse died (23 January); 1 horse destroyed and 1 mule died (26 January); 1 horse destroyed (30 January).
- **Notable — the 19 January incident:** Saunders records that at about 11.45 a.m. the Orderly Officer reported an officer "prowling around the Horse lines acting in a suspicious manner"; the officer evaded the Orderly Officer and slipped away through the back entrance; he was afterwards identified as **Capt. T. C. Evans, the late O.C.** (who had departed for England on 13 December). Saunders writes: "Capt Evans did not report at the Orderly Room or Officers Mess or make his presence known in any way. In my opinion his actions are not those becoming an Officer or Gentleman."
- **Notable — 31 January:** Y.M.C.A. Concert Party gave a concert in the Canteen at 7 p.m.; by order of Base Headquarters all lights out at 9 p.m. — air raid expected.
- **Opening balance:** 507 horses and mules in hospital on 1 January 1916.
