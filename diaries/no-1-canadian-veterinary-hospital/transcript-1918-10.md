## Notes on this month

- **Missing dates (no entry):** 1, 3, 4, 7, 11, 14, 15, 17, 20, 21, 22, 26 and 31 October 1918. The diary period ends 30 October, so 31 October is outside the stated period.
- **Blank / cover pages:** Page 219 is the cover page; page 220 is a contents/title page (no diary entries); page 221 is a diary title page (no diary entries). The actual diary text is on page 222 only, as stated on page 220 ("Diary Text 1 Page").
- **No duplicate pages.**
- **Uncertain readings:** This month's diary page is closely written. The colonel's name on 2 October is not certain. Lt. Foreman's entry on 5 October includes a struck-through line that cannot be read. The entry on 16 October is partly illegible. The destination on 28 October is read as "8th & 6. Bde C.F.A." but is not certain. The entry on 30 October gives the date "(30-9-18)" as written — it appears to refer to an invaliding dated 30 September 1918.
- **Place:** The place column throughout reads "Havre" (Le Havre, France).
- **Named people:** Major Palmer; Col [uncertain]; Lt. W. W. Foreman [uncertain]; Lt. N. C. Coleman; Capt H. S. Clapp; Lt. S. F. T. Halbert; Lt. N. J. Coleman; Lt. A. Chambers; J. A. Sheppard; Capt W. J. Morgan; Lt. Col. C. E. Edgett, D.S.O., A.D.V.S. Can.; Capt W. F. A. Stubbs; Col Newsom; Major S. C. Richards (Commanding Officer).
- **Notable finding — death:** On 27 October, one man (unnamed in the diary) died. No cause is given. This is the second death recorded in the portion transcribed so far (April–October 1918); the other was the man killed in action by enemy aircraft on 13 August 1918.
- **Movements:** Men beginning to return to Canada via Etaples (6 and 10 October); 20 other ranks reported from Shorncliffe (18 October).
