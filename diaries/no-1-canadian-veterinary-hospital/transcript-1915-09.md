# War Diary Transcript — Canadian Veterinary Hospital, September 1915

**Unit:** Canadian Veterinary Hospital, Havre
**Month:** September 1915
**Manifest pages:** 38–47 (image files page-0038 to page-0047)
**Source:** Library and Archives Canada, Item 2005175, RG 9 III-D-3, vol. 5044, file 894, reel T-10936
**Volume:** Volume 5 — "From 1st September to 30th September 1915", Volume 15-9, Diary Text 5 Pages

Transcription conventions: original spelling, dates, places and wording kept. Anything illegible or uncertain is marked [uncertain]. The diary is handwritten on Army Form C. 2118; the printed "Intelligence Summary" heading is struck out. The diarist initials most entries in the remarks column ("C.E.").

## Page 41 — Sheet 1

**1-9-15**

> 4 horses admitted into hospital from Remounts. Daily Routine as usual. Col Butler visited hospital this day.

**2-9-15**

> 1 Officer [Chaplain — uncertain] and 12 horses admitted into hospital for treatment. Daily Routine as usual.

**3-9-15**

> 29 horses and mules admitted into hospital from Remounts. [Lieut — uncertain] [Gundry — uncertain] visited hospital this day.

**4-9-15**

> 19 horses and mules admitted into hospital. Daily Routine as usual.

**5-9-15**

> Captain Gundry left for seven days leave to England. 16 horses and mules admitted to hospital. 107 horses & mules for [Rations — uncertain].

**6-9-15**

> Captain Chas Evans left for 7 days leave to England. Thirteen horses & mules admitted to hospital.

**7-9-15**

> Daily horses returned Cured to Remounts and 21 admitted into hospital.

**8-9-15**

> 14 horses and mules admitted into hospital.

**9-9-15**

> [Casualties — uncertain] in hospital 18 horses & mules and discharged 14 cured to Remounts.

## Page 42

**Havre, 10-9-15**

> Admitted into hospital 13 horses and mules. One destroyed incurable disease.

**" 11-9-15**

> Admitted into hospital 9 horses & mules. Discharged to remounts Cured — eight.

**" 12-9-15**

> Captain Gundry reported back from leave this day. Eleven horses admitted to hospital from Remounts.

**" 13-9-15**

> Horses and mules admitted into hospital — [total — uncertain] — one Capt Dixon & Colbourne [three — uncertain] reported for duty from A.D.V.S. 1st Can Div B.E.F.

**" 14-9-15**

> Captain C. Evans returned from leave of absence in England. Capts Burnett & Richards departed for 1st Canadian Division for Duty — to report to A.D.V.S. Twenty nine horses admitted into hospital from Remounts. One horse died.

**Havre, 27-9-15**

**[28]-9-15** [date in left column partly obscured by a paper tab; read as 28th]

> Sgt L.S. Gale departed to Cadet School at [Blandecques — uncertain]. One horse admitted to Hospital from C.A.S.C. 19 horses and mules discharged Cured to remounts and one died in hospital.

**29-9-15**

> One horse admitted to Hospital.

**30-9-15**

## Notes on this volume

- **Missing dates:** None — every date 1–30 September 1915 has an entry (the 19–20 September entries run together on a crowded sheet; the split between them is [uncertain]).
- **Duplicate page:** Page 46 (image page-0046) is a duplicate photograph of the same sheet as page 45. Transcribed once only.
- **Blank/cover pages:** Pages 38–40 are cover/title pages only (no diary text).
- **Named people:** Col. Butler (visited 1 September); Capt. Gundry (leave to England 5 September, returned 12 September); Capt. Chas Evans (leave to England 6–14 September); Capt. Dixon and Capt. Colbourne (reported for duty from A.D.V.S., 1st Canadian Division, 13 September; their officers' chargers attached 17 September); Capts. Burnett and Richards (departed for 1st Canadian Division, 14 September); Sgt. L. S. Gale (departed to Cadet School, 28 September); Col. [Deeley] R.E. (visited 23 September — spelling [uncertain]).
- **Places:** Havre (hospital).
- **Casualties (animals):** One horse destroyed (incurable disease) 10 September; one horse died 14 September; one horse destroyed 17 September; one horse destroyed (incurable, suffering) 19/20 September; one horse died 25 September; one horse died 26 September; one died in hospital 28 September; three horses and one mule sold to butcher 27 September. Monthly totals: 3 died unfit for food, 9 destroyed fit for food, none destroyed unfit for food.
- **Conditions:** Heavy rains left the hospital "very wet under foot" (30 September); 103 horses and mules admitted in a single day (26 September).
- **Monthly totals (September):** 295 under treatment at 1 September; 398 admitted; 693 total cases; 169 cured to Remounts; 512 remaining under treatment at 30 September.
