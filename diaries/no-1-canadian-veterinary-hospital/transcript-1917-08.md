## Notes on this month

- **Coverage:** All 31 days of August 1917 have an entry. No dates are missing.
- **Volume-number discrepancy:** The cover (p. 162) gives "VOLUME 28"; the diary-text title page (p. 163) gives "Volume 17-8". Both are transcribed as written.
- **Extra line:** After the 31 August entry there is one further line, with no date in the date column, reading "admitted 32 — Died 2. To No 2 V.H. 7". It is transcribed above as written; whether it is a second entry for the 31st, a summary line, or a misdated entry is not clear from the page.
- **The initials "[uncertain: S.C.R.]":** From 15 August onward most entries end with initials read here as "S.C.R." — consistent with the signature of Capt S. C. Richards — but the handwriting is cramped and could also be read "S.L.R.". The reading is therefore flagged [uncertain] throughout.
- **Command change:** The remarks record that Capt Saunders relinquished command and Capt S. C. Richards took over the Canadian Veterinary Hospital, apparently about 12–14 August (the note sits against those entries; no exact date is given in the remark itself).
- **No duplicate pages; no blank diary pages.** Page 163 is a title page only (the rest of the sheet is blank), as in the other months.
- **Uncertain items:** admitted number for 5 August ([uncertain: 28]); the "56" transferred on 2 August; a trailing mark after "Cured to Remounts 11" on 4 August; the "S.C.R." initials (see above); the colonel's name and the wording of the two inspection/visit remarks against 24–27 August; the small printed form code on p. 164.
