## Notes

- **Page count and coverage:** All four pages for December 1916 (pp. 1–4) are present and transcribed. Page 1 is the cover, page 2 is a typed title page, page 3 is the Army Form C. 2118 title sheet, and page 4 is the single diary sheet carrying all 31 daily entries. No pages are missing or duplicated.
- **Volume numbers:** The cover gives "Volume 20" while the typed title page (p. 2) gives "Volume 16-12". Both are recorded as written (see the October notes for the "16-10/11/12" serial scheme).
- **Day count:** 31 entries for 31 days; the date column and the content lines align throughout, verified by cropping the diary sheet into bands and by a grid overlay. Unlike October, there is no missing or ambiguous line.
- **"Cfs.":** This abbreviation trails most entries and is left unexpanded; it is probably the diarist's initials or a signature mark.
- **"D.D.V.S.":** The 7 December entry clearly reads "D.D.V.S. inspection" (Deputy Director of Veterinary Services). This clear rendering, together with 1 November, confirms the reading of the same abbreviation in the October and November diaries, where the initial letter is written in a way that resembles "3" or "F".
- **Uncertain numbers:** The large intake/discharge figures are hard to read in places and are marked [uncertain] where they are most doubtful: 76 horses (2 Dec), 239 horses and 41 mules (5 Dec), 117 horses (9 Dec), and 129 horses and 86 mules (28 Dec). Other large figures (103, 256, 85, 98) are legible but should be treated with the usual caution for this hand.
- **24 and 25 December:** Both days read identically, "1 horse died Cfs". Recorded as written; this is not a duplication error in the transcription.
- **Named people:** Major C. G. Saunders (Officer Commanding) is the only person named in December. No other officers are mentioned this month.
- **Notable events:** The D.D.V.S. inspected on 7 December. The month is otherwise a steady routine of admissions from No. 7 and No. 22, discharges (fit for work) to the Remounts, and animals sent to the butcher or shot. Christmas Day (25 December) passes without any special remark in the diary — the entry is simply "1 horse died Cfs".
