## Notes on this month

- **Coverage:** All 30 days of September 1917 have an entry. No dates are missing.
- **Volume-number discrepancy:** The cover (p. 166) gives "VOLUME. 29"; the diary-text title page (p. 167) gives "Volume 17-9". Both are transcribed as written.
- **7 September:** No admission number is given for this date — the entry records only "Transferred to No 2 Vet Hospital 3." It is transcribed as written.
- **30 September:** A faint further line appears at the very foot of the page below the 30th entry, partly cut off by the printed form footer and overlapping the signature. It cannot be read and is flagged [uncertain] rather than guessed.
- **No duplicate pages; no blank diary pages.** Page 167 is a title page only (the rest of the sheet is blank), as in the other months. The Remarks column on p. 169 is blank throughout the month.
- **Uncertain items:** the "Cured to Remounts" figure on 25 September ([uncertain: 53]); the ditto-mark expansions for the "Transferred to No 2 Vet Hospital 1" figures on 5 and 6 September; the illegible line below the 30 September entry.
