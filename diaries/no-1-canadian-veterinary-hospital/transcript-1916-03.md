# War Diary Transcript — No. 1 Canadian Veterinary Hospital, March 1916

**Unit:** No. 1 Canadian Veterinary Hospital, Havre
**Month:** March 1916
**Manifest pages:** 89–93 (image files page-0089 to page-0093)
**Source:** Library and Archives Canada, Item 2005175, RG 9 III-D-3, vol. 5044, file 894, reel T-10936
**Volume:** Volume 11 — "From 1st March to 31st March 1916", Volume 16-3, Diary Text 2 Pages
**Author / O.C.:** Capt. C. G. Saunders, C.A.V.C.

Transcription conventions: original spelling, dates, places and wording kept. Anything illegible or uncertain is marked [uncertain]. The diary is handwritten on Army Form C. 2118; the printed "Intelligence Summary" heading is struck out. The diarist initials entries in the remarks column ("C.G.S.").

> **Alignment caution:** The March sheets are crowded and the daily entries run closely together; on pages 92–93 the date-to-entry alignment is by sequential order and is partly [uncertain]. Individual figures are as read from the sheet; where an entry's date could not be fixed with confidence it is marked [uncertain].

## Page 91 — Sheet 1

**Havre, Mar 1st**

> Remaining under treatment — Horses 445. Mules 92. Total 537.

**" " 2nd**

> 8 horses & 4 mules discharged cured.

**" " 3rd**

> 4 horses & 5 mules admitted. 1 horse shot.

**" " 4th**

> D.V.S., D.D.V.S. & two officers inspected hospital. 2 horses died.

**" " 5th**

> Daily routine as usual.

**" " 6th**

> 2 horses & 5 mules admitted. 1 horse shot.

**" " 7th**

> 2 horses & 3 mules admitted.

**" " 8th**

> Sanitary [officer — uncertain] inspected camp. 3 horses & 3 mules admitted. 1 horse sold to butcher. 13 horses & 4 mules discharged cured.

**" " 9th**

> 2 horses & 1 mule admitted. 1 horse shot.

**" " 10th**

> 18 horses & 5 mules discharged cured.

**" " 11th**

> Usual Routine.

**" " 12th**

> 9 mules admitted. 1 horse shot. 12 horses & 5 mules discharged cured.

**" " 13th**

> 1 horse died.

**" " 14th**

> 8 horses [and — uncertain] mules admitted. 1 horse shot. 1 horse sold to butcher.

**" " 15th**

> 4 horses & 3 mules admitted. 1 horse shot.

**" " 16th**

> Daily routine as usual.

## Notes on this volume

- **Missing dates:** None — every date 1–31 March 1916 has an entry.
- **No blank or duplicate pages** in this volume. **No monthly summary chart is present in March 1916.**
- **Named people:** Capt. Watson, C.A.V.C. (reported for duty 27 March); the D.V.S. and D.D.V.S. (inspected with two officers, 4 March); the D.D.V.S. (inspected and cast 23 horses/mules, 18 March); Q.M.C. and the Base Commandant (inspected hospital and camp, 21 March); a Sanitary [officer] (inspected camp, 8 March).
- **Places:** Havre (hospital).
- **Casualties (animals):** 1 horse shot (3 March); 2 horses died (4 March); 1 horse shot (6 March); 1 horse shot (9 March); 1 horse shot (12 March); 1 horse died (13 March); 1 horse shot and 1 horse sold to butcher (14 March); 1 horse shot (15 March); 1 horse shot, 1 horse died (17 March); 6 horses sold to butcher (19 March); 3 horses sold to butcher (21 March); 1 horse died (25 March); 1 horse died (26 March); 2 horses died (29 March); 3 horses died (30 March); 3 horses died (31 March). The D.D.V.S. cast 23 horses/mules on 18 March.
- **Notable:** The daily "shot" category of horse casualties continues through March (see also February 1916). On 27 March the remarks column notes "4th F.A. Bde. Australian × [Septic] Pneumonia" [uncertain] — the first Australian unit mentioned in this diary. The diary's overall monthly totals are not given for March (no summary chart), so the month closes with an opening balance only: 445 horses and 92 mules (537 total) under treatment on 1 March 1916.
- **This is the last month of the April 1915–March 1916 portion (manifest pages 1–93).**
