## Notes

- **No missing dates:** all 31 days of October 1917 are entered, 1st–31st. No duplicate pages.
- **No blank pages:** pages 170–172 are cover/inventory/title sheets; page 173 is the sole diary-text page, as stated on page 171 ("Diary Text 1 Page").
- **Place name [uncertain]:** the place column on page 173 reads as "House." but is ambiguous handwriting; the equivalent entry in the November diary (page 177) reads clearly "Havre". The hospital's location is therefore recorded as written, with this uncertainty flagged.
- **4 October [uncertain]:** the entry begins with a ditto mark followed by "8", read here as a repeat of "Destroyed to Butcher" from the 3 October line ("Destroyed to Butcher 8 - Cured to Remounts 118").
- **22 October [uncertain]:** "Transferred to No 1 C.H.D 160" — the abbreviation "C.H.D" and the figure "160" are as read but unclear; "160" may be a number of animals transferred.
- **Visitor's name [uncertain]:** the remarks name reads "Col Newman" in this month's entries, while the November diary spells it "Col Newsome". It may be the same officer; the spelling is transcribed as it appears on each page.
- **"Duke of Connaught" visit:** the remark is written across the 19th–20th October rows; it is listed against 19 October here.
- **Animal figures:** the diary records daily admissions (5–23 animals), animals destroyed "to Butcher", animals that died (1 each on 1, 5, 15 and 20 October), animals "Cured to Remounts" (notably 118 on 4 October, 72 on 6 October, 64 on 9 October, 69 on 11 October), transfers to No. 2 Vet Hospital, and 2 animals "Cast & Sold" on 8 October. No human casualties are recorded.
