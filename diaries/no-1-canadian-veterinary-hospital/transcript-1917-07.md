# No. 1 Canadian Veterinary Hospital — War Diary Transcript, July 1917

**Unit:** No. 1 Canadian Veterinary Hospital (Canadian Veterinary Hospital)

**Pages transcribed:** image files page-0158 through page-0161 (4 images)
**Source:** Library and Archives Canada, Item 2005175, RG 9 III-D-3, vol. 5044, file 894, microfilm reel T-10936
**Volume:** Volume 27 (as written on the cover; the contents page on p. 2 reads "Volume 17-7" — see Notes)

> Transcriber's note: This is a faithful transcription of the page images. Original spelling, punctuation and abbreviations are kept. Anything illegible or uncertain is marked [uncertain] — no name, date or number has been guessed. The one handwritten daily-summary page (p. 4) is written in very cramped, heavily overwritten shorthand; its figures are best-effort readings and are individually marked [uncertain] where they could not be read clearly. Check the original image before using any figure from that page for statistics.

## Notes — July 1917

- **No duplicate pages** in the July set; no blank pages (p. 2 is a short contents page, otherwise blank below its heading).
- **No dates missing** from the daily summary: 1–31 July 1917 are all listed. One additional un-dated line appears below the 31 July entry ("28. 1 shot. 56 discharged off. D.V.S. + D.O.V.S. [uncertain]") — it is transcribed above as written; whether it is a continuation of the 31 July entry or a monthly note could not be determined from the image.
- **Place:** the daily summary is headed "July 1st 1917 France" (no more precise place is given).
- **Volume-number discrepancy in the original:** the cover (p. 1) reads "VOLUME 27", while the contents page (p. 2) reads "Volume 17-7". Both are transcribed as written.
- **No appendices** are included in the July volume — unlike June, which carried the laboratory report and the "Notes on Mange". The contents page states "Diary Text 1 Page."
- **Named people:** Major C. G. Saunders (Officer Commanding) only.
- **Notable entries (all [uncertain] as to figures — see above):** transfers of animals to No. 2 Veterinary Hospital on 17 and 20 July; D.O.V.S. inspections on 13 and 19 July; a committee inspection noted on 15 July; deaths and animals shot recorded on most days.
