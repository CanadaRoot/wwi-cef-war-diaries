# War Diary Transcript — Canadian Veterinary Hospital, November 1915

**Unit:** Canadian Veterinary Hospital, 1st Canadian Division, Havre
**Month:** November 1915
**Manifest pages:** 58–68 (image files page-0058 to page-0068)
**Source:** Library and Archives Canada, Item 2005175, RG 9 III-D-3, vol. 5044, file 894, reel T-10936
**Volume:** Volume 7 — "From 1st November 1915 to 30th November, 1915", Volume 15-11, Diary Text 8 Pages + Appendix I (chart)
**Author / O.C.:** Capt. Thomas Charles Evans, C.A.V.C. ("War Diary of Thomas Charles Evans, Capt C.A.V.C. / From: November 1st to November 30th, 1915." — typed title page, stamped "Canadian Section No. C, 14 Dec 1915, H.Q. 3rd Echelon")

Transcription conventions: original spelling, dates, places and wording kept. Anything illegible or uncertain is marked [uncertain]. The diary is handwritten on Army Form C. 2118; the printed "Intelligence Summary" heading is struck out. The diarist initials most entries in the remarks column ("C.E.").

## Page 61

**Havre, Nov 1st**

> Two Dining Huts (100 men each) in process of construction by R.E., authorised by D.D.V.S. [Coke — uncertain] authorised to be issued at Base Supply Office. 488 Horses & mules under treatment.

**" " 2nd**

> 8 Horses & mules admitted. Capt Colbourne departed on leave to England for 7 days. Barrack Inspection. Winter clothing (woollen) issued to men.

**" " 3rd**

> Weekly pay decided on as expedient for N.C.O's & men. 54 Horses & mules sold by order of D.D.V.S. (attended sale personally).

**" " 4th**

> Daily routine as usual. 442 Remaining under treatment.

## Page 62

**Havre, Nov 5th**

> 1 Horse & 11 mules admitted. 1 Horse destroyed.

**" " 6th**

> 40 Horses & 25 mules admitted. 36 Horses & 14 mules discharged cured. 468 remaining. Palisade fence as wind shield [of — uncertain] Camp authorised.

**" " 7th**

> Daily routine as usual.

**" " 8th**

> 11 Horses & 9 mules admitted. 2 horses & 1 mule sold to butcher £200 / horse £140 / mule. 485 Remaining. Capt Stedman departed for 25th Division, 2nd Army, with groom & charger. [uncertain]

**" " 9th**

> 4 Horses & 9 mules admitted. 15 Horses & 6 mules discharged cured.

## Page 63

**Havre, Nov 10th**

> Daily routine as usual.

**" " 11th**

> 13 Horses & 14 mules returned cured to Remounts.

**" " 12th**

> Hauling stone from beach & cinders from factories for roads & paths. Gale from south-west & rain. Strengthened men's dining huts & Reading Room.

**" " 13th**

> Gale continues. All huts in men's compound, men's Mess & Reading Tent & Canteen Tent blown down & damaged during night. Camp flooded. Many tents ripped & damaged beyond repair. Fresh supply from Ordnance. Discharged 14 Horses & 11 mules.

**" " 14th**

> One horse died. 423 under treatment. D.V.S. [?] (Canadians) England reports that he cannot authorise recommendation for temporary promotion of [O.C. — uncertain].

**" " 15th**

> 80 Horses admitted from No 1 Vet Hospital Abbeville at 1 a.m. 503 remaining.

## Page 64

**Havre, Nov 16th**

> 5 Horses & 2 mules admitted. 25 Horses & 18 mules discharged cured. Letter from Capt Colbourne, he is at No 2 General Military Hospital, Leeds, England, suffering from Nephritis & Cystitis.

**" " 17th**

> Daily routine as usual.

**" " 18th**

> Camp visited by Base Commandant. Men without artificial warmth. General advises issue of Rum, also coke braziers for heating dining huts.

**" " 19th**

> Daily routine as usual.

**" " 20th**

> Authority from D.A.A.G. K.R. 11-8 for promotion of 3 Corporals to Lance-Sergeants, 2 Lance-Corporals to Corporals, 3 Private Corporals to Lance-Corporals. Returned cured 11 mules & 10 horses. Work commenced on new Dipping Vat authorised by D.V.S. I.G.C. in September.

## Page 65

**Havre, Nov 21st**

> 12 Horses admitted to Hospital. 1 mule & 1 horse returned cured.

**" " 22nd**

> Selection of cast animals for decision by D.D.V.S. D.D.V.S. (Southern) visited hospital 9 a.m. to 3 p.m. Garrison Quartermaster inspected stores 3 to 5 p.m. 12 horses & 3 mules admitted.

**" " 23rd**

> D.V.S. I.G.C. visited & inspected hospital. Horses are being kicked in shelters because they stand in couples with no [bails — uncertain] between. Engineers say sheds are not strong enough for bails. General suggests [larch — uncertain] poles & uprights to prevent kicking. Col. Neill objected to Capt Stedman (attached temporarily, surplus to establishment) being sent to 2nd Army, Imperial. General Moore states Stedman may be sent to England if Col. Neill so desires. 29 horses admitted to hospital.

**" " 24th**

> 8 Horses & 6 mules admitted. 28 Horses & 10 mules discharged cured.

**" " 25th**

> 1 horse destroyed.

## Page 66

> Daily routine as usual.

## Notes on this volume

- **Missing dates:** None — every date 1–30 November 1915 has an entry.
- **No blank or duplicate pages** in this volume; pages 58–60 are cover/title pages, pages 61–67 are the diary sheets, page 68 is Appendix I (the monthly chart).
- **Named people:** Capt. Colbourne (leave to England 2 November; letter 16 November reports him at No. 2 General Military Hospital, Leeds, England, suffering from Nephritis and Cystitis); Capt. Stedman (departed for 25th Division, 2nd Army, with groom and charger, 8 November); Col. Neill (objected to Stedman's posting, 23 November); General Moore, D.V.S. (23 November); D.D.V.S. (Southern) (visited 22 November); D.V.S. I.G.C. (visited 23 November); the Base Commandant (visited 18 November; advised issue of Rum and coke braziers for heating the dining huts); the Garrison Quartermaster (inspected stores 22 November); the D.A.A.G. (authority, 20 November, under K.R. 11-8, for promotion of 3 Corporals to Lance-Sergeants, 2 Lance-Corporals to Corporals, 3 Private Corporals to Lance-Corporals).
- **Places:** Havre (hospital); Abbeville (No. 1 Veterinary Hospital — 80 horses admitted from there, 15 November); Leeds, England (No. 2 General Military Hospital — Colbourne).
- **Weather / storm:** 12–13 November — gale from the south-west with rain; all huts in the men's compound, the men's Mess and Reading Tent and the Canteen Tent blown down and damaged during the night; camp flooded; many tents ripped and damaged beyond repair; fresh supply from Ordnance.
- **Casualties (animals):** 1 horse destroyed 5 November; 1 horse died 14 November; 1 horse destroyed 25 November; 1 horse sold to butcher with fractured leg 29 November; 2 horses and 1 mule sold to butcher 8 November (£200 per horse, £140 per mule); 54 horses and mules sold by order of the D.D.V.S. 3 November. Monthly totals: 1 died unfit for food, 7 destroyed fit for food, 54 cast and sold.
- **Health:** Capt. Colbourne suffering from Nephritis and Cystitis at No. 2 General Military Hospital, Leeds (16 November).
- **Notable:** Evans records (14 November) that the D.V.S. (Canadians) in England "cannot authorise recommendation for temporary promotion" of the O.C.; work commenced on a new Dipping Vat authorised by D.V.S. I.G.C. in September (20 November); horses being kicked in the shelters because they stand in couples with no bails between — Engineers say the sheds are not strong enough for bails; General suggests larch poles and uprights (23 November).
- **Monthly totals (November):** 488 under treatment at 1 November; 320 admitted; 808 total cases; 300 cured to Remounts; 54 cast and sold; 446 remaining under treatment at 30 November.
