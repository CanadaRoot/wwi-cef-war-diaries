## Notes on this month

- **Missing dates (no entry):** 1, 3, 4, 6, 7, 11, 14, 15, 20, 22, 23, 26, 27, 28 and 29 December 1918.
- **Blank / cover pages:** Page 227 is the cover page; page 228 is a contents/title page (no diary entries). Unlike the other months in this portion, December has no separate diary title page — page 229 is the diary form itself, as stated on page 228 ("Diary Text 1 Page").
- **No duplicate pages.**
- **Uncertain readings:** "Cauc. B.D." is as written; its expansion is not given in the diary. Lt. Gaffrey's surname on 19 December is not certain. The letters in brackets after Brig. Gen. Nicholson on 21 December are read as "(B.C.)" but are not certain.
- **Place:** The place column throughout reads "Havre" (Le Havre, France).
- **Named people:** Capt W. F. A. Stubbs; Capt W. W. Batty; Col Newsom; Lt. J. N. Gaffrey [uncertain]; Brig. Gen. Nicholson; Lt. J. A. Sheppard; Major S. C. Richards (Commanding Officer).
- **Notable findings:** One man was struck off as a deserter (9 December) — the only desertion recorded in the portion transcribed so far (April–December 1918). Several men were posted to "Cauc. B.D." in England (13, 18, 19 and 25 December). Men continued to leave for Etaples for return to Canada (17 December).
- **Movements:** 45 other ranks taken on strength from the Canadian Labour pool (8 December).
