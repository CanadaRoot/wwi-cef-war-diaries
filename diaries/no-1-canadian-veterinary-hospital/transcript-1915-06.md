## Page 23 — Sheet No 7

**30.6.15, HAVRE**

> During the month, a great deal of work has been put into the [paving — uncertain] flooring of the stables — these had been very badly broken up by the heavy horses [formerly — uncertain] in the hospital — a new quality of [brick — uncertain] has been obtained which is tougher than the [brick — uncertain] used formerly. Dangerous areas have been railed in — Three small [exercise — uncertain] rings have been built in the "dells" of the [picket — uncertain] field — these appear to be particularly useful in Catarrh Cases. The above work is being carried out by the NCOs & men of this hospital.
>
> Grazing grounds have been acquired and are in the process of being fenced in. Sub divisional forage sheds have been put up — the present average strength of sick horses and mules appears to be about 700. The strength in personnel as it now stands is 196 NCOs & men — For dealing with this number of sick — I consider that at least 25 more men are required, especially as this hospital is in the process of construction and so much fatigue work has yet to be done. An Expeditionary Force Canteen has been established in a Marquee for the NCOs & men.
>
> Owing to the number of horses now at the hospital the arrangements for water have become difficult — Some 150 horses are on lines in the neighbourhood of the Exercise Ring on the North Side.

## Notes on this volume

- **Missing dates:** No entries for **16 June** or **18 June 1915** (the diary jumps 15 → 17 June and 17 → 19 June). All other dates 1–30 June are covered.
- **No blank or duplicate pages** in this volume; pages 14–16 are cover/title pages, pages 17–25 are the diary sheets and monthly return.
- **Named people:** Capt. T. C. Evans (outgoing O.C.), Capt. V. C. Leckie (O.C. from 2 June), Capt. Richards (escorted 194 horses to Gournay), M. Corbelet (interpreter), Trooper J. Mickle No. 48579 (28 days Field Punishment No. 1, 3 June), Trooper J. D. Clark No. 16786 (remanded for F.G.C.M.; 56 days Field Punishment No. 1, sentence promulgated 17 June), Col. Macrae (Canadian Remount Commission, visited 27 June), the Quartermaster Master General B.E.F. and G.O.C. No. 1 Base, Havre (inspected 5 June).
- **Places:** Havre (hospital, No. 1 Base); Gournay-en-Bras (Convalescent Farm); Abbeville (No. 5 Veterinary Hospital — Evans's posting).
- **Casualties (animals):** 10 horses destroyed on 2 June (D.D.V.S. orders); 12 died & destroyed in the month; 12 sold to butcher for slaughter; 2 cast and sold by auction. Pneumonia is the named cause of death on 17, 19 and 22 June. No human casualties.
- **Discipline:** Trooper Mickle — 28 days Field Punishment No. 1 for loitering on stable picquet; Trooper Clark — 56 days Field Punishment No. 1 by Field General Court Martial.
- **Monthly totals (June):** 991 horses and mules dealt with; 100 cured to Remounts; 194 to Convalescent Farm, Gournay; 12 died/destroyed; 12 sold to butcher; 2 cast/sold by auction; 671 remaining under treatment at 30 June.
