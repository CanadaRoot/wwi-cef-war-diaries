## Notes

- **Page count and coverage:** All four pages for October 1916 (pp. 1–4) are present and transcribed. Page 1 is the cover, page 2 is a typed title page, page 3 is the Army Form C. 2118 title sheet, and page 4 is the single diary sheet carrying all 31 daily entries. No pages are missing or duplicated.
- **Volume numbers:** The cover gives "Volume 18" while the typed title page (p. 2) gives "Volume 16-10". Both are recorded as written. The November and December diaries give "Volume 16-11" and "Volume 16-12" respectively, so "16-10" appears to be a serial scheme (16 = year, 10 = month).
- **10 October — unidentified line:** The diary sheet has 31 numbered rows but only 30 distinctly readable content lines. Cross-checking the date column against the content lines (including a red grid overlay) fixes the anchors unambiguously: the last line, "44 horses 6 mules discharged", sits beside the "31", and the "A.D.V. + R.C." line sits beside the "22". Working back from those anchors leaves one line unaccounted for, falling on 10 October, between "Usual routine" (9 Oct) and the D.V.S. entry (11 Oct). The line may be blank or a repeated "Usual routine". It has NOT been silently assigned; it is recorded here as [uncertain].
- **"Cfs.":** This abbreviation trails most entries. It is left unexpanded; it is probably the diarist's initials or a signature mark. It is NOT expanded to a name in this transcription.
- **"D.V.S." / "D.D.V.S.":** The abbreviation on 11, 12 and 21 October is written with an initial letter that resembles "3" or "F". The November diary (1 November) clearly reads "D.D.V.S. inspection" (Deputy Director of Veterinary Services), and the same phrase appears on 7 December, so the October entries are read as "D.D.V.S." here, with the initial letter marked [uncertain] where the hand is unclear. The word after "D.V.S." on 11 October may be "inspection" (12 October repeats it with a ditto mark).
- **Uncertain numbers:** The large intake/discharge figures are hard to read in places and are marked [uncertain]: 185 horses (1 Oct), 116 horses (4 Oct), 10 horses (14 Oct), 41 horses (15 Oct), 272 horses (16 Oct), 27 mules (17 Oct), 41 horses (18 Oct), 20 mules (26 Oct).
- **Uncertain words:** "C.H.D." (4 Oct), the place from which the fatigue party arrived, "C.B.D." (20 Oct), the word after "officers" on 22 Oct (possibly "inspected"), the word after "Canadian Auditor." on 24 Oct (possibly "Garrison"), and the word after "44" on 27 Oct (possibly "O.R.s", i.e. other ranks).
- **"cast & sold"** (2 Oct) is written as such in the original.
- **Named people:** Major C. G. Saunders (Officer Commanding); Capt. Baker, Quartermaster (departed 24 October — he reappears, arriving, on 5 November 1916).
- **Notable events:** A fatigue party of 40 N.C.O.s and men arrived from [uncertain] C.B.D. on 20 October; A.D.V. and R.C. officers with a D.D.M.S. inspection on 22 October; a Canadian Auditor visited on 24 October; on 27 October the Remounts refused to accept 44 [uncertain] and riders. Horses and mules were admitted from the Remounts, No. 7 and No. 22, and discharged (fit for work) to the Remounts throughout the month.
