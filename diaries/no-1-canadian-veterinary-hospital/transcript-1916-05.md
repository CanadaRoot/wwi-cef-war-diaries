## Page 101 — May 1916, p. 4 (diary text, 1–27 May, Army Form C. 2118)

Form heading: WAR DIARY or INTELLIGENCE SUMMARY (the words "INTELLIGENCE SUMMARY" struck through).

- **1.5.16 — Havre:** Remaining 361 horses 136 mules. 4 horses 3 mules admitted. 1 mule died. C.G.S.
- **2.5.16:** D.V.S. inspected Hospital. 120 horses 138 mules admitted. 5 horses and 1 mule sold [uncertain]. C.G.S.
- **3.5.16:** 6 to butcher. 99 horses 1 mule sent to C.H.D. C.G.S.
- **4.5.16:** 3 mules admitted. 1 horse died. 3 horses sold to butcher. 1 horse shot. C.G.S.
- **5.5.16:** 6 horses 10 mules discharged to remounts. 1 horse to Can Base Depot. (Officers charger). C.G.S.
- **6.5.16:** Usual routine. C.G.S.
- **7.5.16:** Capt. [uncertain] came [uncertain] 33 other ranks report from Shorncliffe. 2 horses 2 mules admitted.
- **8.5.16:** 11 horses 11 mules discharged to remounts. C.G.S.
- **9.5.16:** Usual routine. C.G.S.
- **10.5.16:** 117 horses 29 mules admitted. 1 horse died. 1 horse sold to butcher. 87 horses 13 mules sent to C.H.D. C.G.S.
- **11.5.16:** 1 horse died. 1 horse 1 mule sold to butcher. 10 horses 5 mules discharged to remounts. C.G.S.
- **12.5.16:** 2 horses 6 mules admitted. Opened the horse Dip for first time. Dip leaking badly. 4 mules discharged. C.G.S.
- **13.5.16:** Usual routine. C.G.S. 30 horses 10 mules discharged. C.G.S.
- **14.5.16:** 6 horses 6 mules admitted. C.G.S.
- **15.5.16:** D.D.V.S. and [uncertain] visited Hospital re new construction. 5 horses 1 mule admitted. 2 horses 1 mule sold to butcher. 4 horses cast & sold. C.G.S.
- **16.5.16:** 4 horses 2 mules admitted. 1 mule sold to butcher. C.G.S.
- **17.5.16:** Y.M.C.A. gave a concert in Canteen. 1 horse sold to butcher. C.G.S.
- **18.5.16:** 8 horses admitted. 1 horse died. 18 horses 14 mules discharged. C.G.S.
- **19.5.16:** Capt. E. H. Dixon, 2 chargers & batman, left to report to A.D.V.S., 1st Can Div. C.G.S.
- **20.5.16:** [uncertain: Any] horse in Hospital dipped. 9 horses 2 mules admitted. 6 horses 7 mules discharged. C.G.S.
- **21.5.16:** 36 horses admitted & dipped. C.G.S.
- **22.5.16:** Usual routine. C.G.S.
- **23.5.16:** 13 horses 5 mules discharged. C.G.S.
- **24.5.16:** 10 horses admitted. 1 horse died. C.G.S. Sports held for men. C.G.S.
- **25.5.16:** Capt. Richards reported for duty. 5 horses 3 mules admitted. 8 horses 10 mules discharged. C.G.S.
- **26.5.16:** Usual routine. C.G.S.
- **27.5.16:** 6 horses 3 mules admitted. 9 horses 6 mules discharged. C.G.S.

## Notes

- All 31 days of May 1916 are covered: 1–27 May on p. 4 and 28–31 May on p. 5. No dates are missing and there are no duplicate pages.
- The cover page (p. 1) gives the volume as "Volume 13"; the typed inventory sheet (p. 2) gives it as "Volume 16-5". Both are transcribed as written. The diarist is now Major (not Captain) C. G. Saunders.
- Each daily entry ends with the diarist's initials, here rendered "C.G.S." (the manuscript initials are written in a flourish and are not always fully legible).
- The date column for 24 and 25 May appears to be written "6-16" rather than "5-16" — evidently a slip by the diarist; the entries are transcribed in sequence as 24 and 25 May.
- Uncertain readings: the last word of the 2 May entry ("sold" is the best reading); the officer's name and a word in the 7 May entry (33 other ranks reporting from Shorncliffe); the visitors' names on 15 May; the first word of the 20 May entry ([uncertain: Any] horse in Hospital dipped); and "Dip" on 12 May (the horse dip, opened for the first time, leaking badly).
- Animal losses recorded in May: mule died 1 May; horses died on 4, 10, 11, 18 and 24 May (1 each); 1 horse shot on 4 May and 1 horse shot on 29 May. Horses and mules were regularly sold to the butcher (e.g. 6 to butcher on 3 May) and large numbers were discharged to remounts or sent to C.H.D.
- Named people: Capt. E. H. Dixon (left 19 May with 2 chargers and a batman to report to A.D.V.S., 1st Canadian Division); Capt. Richards (reported for duty 25 May). Other events: Y.M.C.A. concert in the Canteen on 17 May; sports held for the men on 24 May; a visit re new construction on 15 May.
