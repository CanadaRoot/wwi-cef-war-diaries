# War Diary Transcript — Canadian Veterinary Hospital, December 1915

**Unit:** Canadian Veterinary Hospital, Havre
**Month:** December 1915
**Manifest pages:** 69–76 (image files page-0069 to page-0076)
**Source:** Library and Archives Canada, Item 2005175, RG 9 III-D-3, vol. 5044, file 894, reel T-10936
**Volume:** Volume 8 — "From 1st December 1915 to 31st December 1915", Volume 15-12, Diary Text 6 Pages
**Author / O.C.:** Capt. Thomas Charles Evans, C.A.V.C. (1–11 December), then Capt. C. G. Saunders, C.A.V.C. (from 12 December). Title page: "War Diary of Thomas Charles Evans, Capt C.A.V.C. / From: December 1st to December 31st, 1915." (stamped "Canadian Section No. C, 19 Jan 1916, H.Q. 3rd Echelon")

> **Change of command:** Evans received a wire on 10 December ordering his return to Shorncliffe, England; he handed over to Capt. C. G. Saunders on 11 December and departed for England on 13 December. Saunders takes over on 12 December.

Transcription conventions: original spelling, dates, places and wording kept. Anything illegible or uncertain is marked [uncertain]. The diary is handwritten on Army Form C. 2118; the printed "Intelligence Summary" heading is struck out. The diarist initials most entries in the remarks column ("C.E." to 11 December; "C.G.S." thereafter).

## Notes on this volume

- **Missing dates:** None — every date 1–31 December 1915 has an entry.
- **No blank or duplicate pages** in this volume. **No monthly summary chart is present in December** (unlike June–November, which each had a typed monthly return).
- **Named people:** Capt. C. G. Saunders (takes command 11–12 December); Col. Butler / D.D.V.S. (visited 3 December; visited 12 December at 2 p.m.; inspected 28 December); D.M.S. L of C (visited 16 December); D.O.W. (inspected buildings and roads, 29 December).
- **Places:** Havre (hospital); Shorncliffe, England (Evans's new posting — 5th Artillery Brigade); Montreal, Canada (where Evans says he recruited and organised the hospital); Abbeville (80 sick horses received 8 December; 100 horses admitted 13 December); Gournay-en-Bray (veterinary Convalescent Depot — 80 debilitated sick horses sent 7 December); No. 14 Veterinary Hospital (40 horses and 10 mules admitted 20 December).
- **Casualties (animals):** 4 horses sent to be destroyed to the butcher (4 December); 1 horse died (5 December); 6 horses destroyed for incurable diseases (15 December); 1 horse destroyed (16 December); 1 horse destroyed (18 December); 2 horses destroyed (29 December); 1 horse destroyed (30 December). Seven horses cast by the D.D.V.S. on 3 December.
- **Notable:** Evans's farewell entry (10 December) — "I am most disappointed as I have recruited and organised this Hospital since its inception at Montreal Canada. My orders are to report to 5th Artillery Bde Shorncliffe for duty." Christmas Day, 25 December: "Sunday Routine. Special dinner for the men." A senior visitor on 9 December reported "NO COMPLAINTS. Not a single case of [venereal disease] in the Canadian Vet Hospital since being in France." All horses in the hospital were subjected to the Mallein Test on 21 December.
- **Change of hand:** The handwriting changes on 12 December (Saunders's hand is less cramped than Evans's); the remarks-column initials change from "C.E." to "C.G.S."
- **Opening balance:** November's Appendix I (transcript-1915-11.md) gave 446 remaining under treatment at 30 November 1915; no December monthly summary is present to close the month.
