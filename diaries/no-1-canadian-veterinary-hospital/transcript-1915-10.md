## Page 52

**Havre, October 1**

> Capt W. T. Stedman departed for England on leave the boat leaving 1.00 a.m. 512 Horses under treatment in Hospital. Erected a tent in men's lines & provided it with [Condy's — uncertain] Fluid, [Calomel — uncertain] Ointment (Mercury) for use as ablution for men returning from town, Medical Officer gave men a lecture Venereal Diseases.

**" " 2**

> Sold 3 horses & 1 mule to butcher for meat. Cured to Remounts 14 Horses.

**" " 3**

> Sent 3 horses & 1 mule down to Abattoir, sold previous day. £200.00 per horse & £140.00 per mule. Capt Colbourne went in to Hospital this date.

## Page 53

**Havre, October 4**

> Capt Dixon left for England on leave (7 days). Daily routine as usual.

**" " 5**

> Commenced to draw shingle from beach to make roads & paths around stables. 42 Horses & mules were returned to Remounts (cured). By order of D.V.S. 99 Horses & mules admitted to Hospital from No 2 A.V.C. fully 20% of these will never be fit for military services and are in reality Cast horses.

**" " 6**

> Engineers handed over Forage Shed complete. Have not fixed machinery.

**" " 7**

> Daily routine as usual.

## Page 54

**" 9**

This causes considerable inconvenience because it necessitates a pioneer party being often in attendance to disinfect. Daily routine as usual.

> Daily routine as usual.

**" " 11**

## Notes on this volume

- **Missing dates:** None — every date 1–31 October 1915 has an entry.
- **No blank or duplicate pages** in this volume. Note the filing order: the typed monthly return (page 51) is filed **before** the daily diary sheets (pages 52–57).
- **Named people:** Capt. W. T. Stedman (leave to England 1 October, returned 8 October); Capt. Colbourne (went into hospital 3 October); Capt. Dixon (leave to England 4 October); Col. Butler, D.D.V.S. (inspected the hospital 14 and 15 October; authorised sale of 54 unfit horses; his commendation — "He is well satisfied with the progress and improvement made in this Hospital and great credit is due to all concerned" — was read on parade).
- **Places:** Havre (hospital, No. 7 Camp); Granville (city using ground east of camp for garbage disposal).
- **Casualties (animals):** One horse died 21 October; one died 23 October; one died 25 October; one died 28 October. Horses sold to the butcher for meat: 3 horses and 1 mule (2 October), 1 horse (9 October), 2 horses (12 October), 2 horses and 1 mule (20 October), 1 horse (21 October), 1 horse (16 October). Monthly totals: 3 died unfit for food, 13 destroyed fit for food.
- **Prices:** Butcher paid £200 per horse and £140 per mule (3 October entry).
- **Notable:** R.S.P.C.A. gift of machinery — 9 H.P. gas engine, chaff cutter and oat crusher — installed and working (12 October); a tent erected in the men's lines with disinfectant fluid and mercury ointment for men returning from town, with a Medical Officer lecture on venereal diseases (1 October); 99 horses and mules admitted from No. 2 A.V.C. on 5 October, "fully 20% of these will never be fit for military services and are in reality Cast horses"; the Engineers handed over the Forage Shed complete but had not fixed the machinery (6 October).
- **Monthly totals (October):** 512 under treatment at 1 October; 259 admitted; 771 total cases; 267 cured to Remounts; 488 remaining under treatment at 31 October.
