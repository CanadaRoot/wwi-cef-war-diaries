## Notes

- All 30 days of June 1916 are covered: 1–22 June on p. 4 and 23–30 June on p. 5. No dates are missing and there are no duplicate pages.
- The cover page (p. 1) gives the volume as "Volume 14"; the typed inventory sheet (p. 2) gives it as "Volume 16-6". Both are transcribed as written.
- Each daily entry ends with the diarist's initials, here rendered "C.G.S." (the manuscript initials are written in a flourish and are not always fully legible). The 1 June entry carries no legible initials.
- Uncertain readings: the unit abbreviation on 16 June ("13th Bde. Am. F.A." is the best reading); the colonel's name on 18 June; the officer's surname on 21 June ([uncertain: Kennedy]); and part of the 20 June entry — a word after "18 horses 1 mule" is illegible, and "detected" (in "Case of cutaneous Glanders detected, isolated & Tested") is the best reading of a partly legible word. The 17 June entry ends mid-phrase with "The D.V.S." as written.
- Animal losses recorded in June: 1 horse died on 16, 22 and 24 June (1 each); 1 horse and 1 mule destroyed on 13 June; 1 horse destroyed on 14 June; 7 horses and 1 mule shot on 22 June. Large movements: 202 horses and 34 mules admitted on 5 June; 70 horses and 10 mules admitted from No. 22 on 9 June; 70 horses and 10 mules sent to C.H.D. on 4 June.
- Notable findings: a case of cutaneous Glanders (20 June) — detected, isolated and tested; the D.D.V.S. inspected the hospital on 18 and 22 June; all animals in the hospital were inspected on 23 June; Capt. H. P. [uncertain: Kennedy] reported for duty on 21 June.
