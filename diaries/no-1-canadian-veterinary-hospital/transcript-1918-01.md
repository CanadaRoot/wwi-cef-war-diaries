# No. 1 Canadian Veterinary Hospital — War Diary, January 1918

**Source:** Library and Archives Canada, Item 2005175, RG 9 III-D-3, vol. 5044, file 894, reel T-10936.
**Month:** 1–31 January 1918
**Pages transcribed:** 182–185 (4 pages: page-0182-January_1918_p._1.jpg to page-0185-January_1918_p._4.jpg)

**Transcription conventions:** Original spelling, wording and abbreviations are kept. Ditto marks (") in the diary are rendered as [ditto]. Anything illegible or uncertain is marked [uncertain]. "Transf'd / Trans" = Transferred; "Hospl / Hos / H.S" = Hospital; "Remounts" = animals cured and returned to remounts; "Cast & Sold (Butcher)" is written as in the original.

## Notes on this month

- No dates are missing: entries run 1–31 January 1918. No duplicate pages.
- No pages are blank or covers beyond pages 182–184, which are the cover, diary-text cover sheet, and title page respectively; the only diary-text page is page 185.
- Discrepancy in volume numbering: the cover (page 182) gives "VOLUME 33", while the diary-text cover sheet (page 183) gives "Volume 18-1".
- No place or camp is named in the diary, apart from "Camp" in the remarks and "Rouen" as Brig Gen Neil's destination. No human casualties are recorded; all daily entries concern animals admitted, cured to remounts, transferred to other veterinary hospitals (principally No. 2 Vet Hospital), shot, died, destroyed, or cast and sold (butcher).
- Named people in the remarks: Col Newson [uncertain spelling] (visited Camp, twice — about 17th and 31st January); Brig Gen W J. Neil [uncertain surname] and two Staff Officers (arrived on inspecting duty from England, about 26th January; Brig Gen Neil left for Rouen about 28th January).
