Col Newsom visited the hospital.

**" 11th —**
1 man invalided England.

**" 19th —**

## Notes on this month

- **Missing dates (no entry):** 3, 5, 7, 9, 10, 15, 16, 18, 23, 24, 26 and 29 January 1919. The diary period ends 30 January, so 31 January is outside the stated period.
- **Blank / cover pages:** Page 230 is the cover page; page 231 is a contents/title page (no diary entries); page 232 is a diary title page (no diary entries, and no month/year is given on it). The actual diary text is on page 233 only, as stated on page 231 ("Diary Text 1 Page").
- **No duplicate pages.**
- **Uncertain readings:** The 2 January entry is a run-on list of destinations and is transcribed as written. Capt A. W. —'s surname on 4 January, Capt A. —'s surname on 19 January, and J. A. —'s surname on 21 January are not certain. The abbreviation on 25 January is as written ("D.D.S."). The 12 January entry uses ditto marks under the 11 January entry.
- **Place:** The place column throughout reads "Havre" (Le Havre, France).
- **Named people:** Capt W. W. Batty; Capt A. W. [uncertain]; Col Newsom; Capt A. [uncertain]; Capt W. J. Morgan; Capt J. F. Boswell; J. A. [uncertain]; Lt. Col. Edgett, D.S.O.; Lt. K. W. McKinnon; Major S. C. Richards, O.B.E. (Commanding Officer).
- **Notable finding:** Major S. C. Richards is now styled "O.B.E." on the diary title page — the first time in this portion (April 1918–March 1919) that the O.B.E. appears after his name.
- **Movements:** The hospital continued to disperse veterinary sergeants to artillery brigades and engineer battalions and to send men to the Canadian Base Depot and the Canadian Corps Veterinary Evacuation Station as demobilisation proceeded; several men left for Etaples on leave to Canada.
