## Page 193 — Diary text (Army Form C. 2118)

Place throughout: Havre. This month the diary is on a different form layout, with separate columns for Place, Date and Hour, and the entries are in a smaller hand than February's. The diary is a single page of daily animal returns: the number of animals admitted each day, with dispositions (cured to Remounts, destroyed to the Butcher or to the Carcase Economiser Depot, died, shot, transferred to No. 2 Veterinary Hospital). ## Transcriber's notes

- No dates are missing: all 31 days of March 1918 are listed. No duplicate pages; no blank pages.
- **Blank / number-only entries:** 21st March has no entry at all (date only, line blank). 6th, 10th, 13th, 19th, 24th and 25th record only the number admitted, with no dispositions. 27th records a transfer to No. 2 Vet. Hospital but no admitted number.
- **29th March:** the entry reads "7 - To Carcase Economiser Depot 4 - Destroyed (to Butcher) 10 - Cured to …" and the tail of the line is too faint to read; it is marked [uncertain] rather than completed by guesswork. Note the 28th and 29th entries carry the same figures (4 to the Carcase Economiser Depot, 10 destroyed to Butcher) — the 29th's tail may repeat the 28th's "Rmts 60 - Trans to No 2 Vet Hosp 1", but this cannot be confirmed from the image.
- **Spelling:** the writer spells the rendering depot "Carcase Economiser Depot" (also "Carcase Economiser plant" on the 2nd); "Rmts" is his abbreviation for Remounts; "Vet H.S" / "Vet Hosp" = Veterinary Hospital; "C.A.V.C." = Canadian Army Veterinary Corps. The "Economiser" spelling (s vs. z) is not always clear in the hand and is given as "Economiser" throughout.
- The March diary text is on a different printed form from February's (separate Place / Date / Hour columns, Forms/C.2118/14 imprint) and in a different, smaller hand.
