## Transcriber's notes

- No dates are missing: all 28 days of February 1918 have entries. No duplicate entries; no blank pages.
- **Date-label anomaly:** in the date column the 22nd appears twice — once as a corrected label at the end of the first block (a "22nd" overwritten on a crossed-out "21st", with no separate entry attached) and once at the head of the second block (22nd–28th). The 28 daily entries have been transcribed in sequence (1st–28th); the corrected duplicate label carries no entry of its own. A blank line separates the 21st and 22nd entries.
- The word "Animals" is written at the head of the summary column above the dispositions.
- "Remounts" is the writer's spelling throughout; "Vety. Hosp." = Veterinary Hospital; "C.A.V.C." = Canadian Army Veterinary Corps.
- Uncertain readings are marked [uncertain] in the table above: the 4th (transferred figure "61"), the 9th ("10 Shot"), the 11th (admitted number, written "313"), and the 18th (transferred figure, raised "7").
