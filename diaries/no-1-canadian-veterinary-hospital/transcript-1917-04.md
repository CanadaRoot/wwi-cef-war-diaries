## Transcriber's notes

- All 30 days of April 1917 are present, one entry per day, 1–30 April. No missing dates, no duplicate pages. Page 144 is an index sheet only (no diary text); no pages are blank.
- Place throughout is given as "Havre" (Le Havre).
- The diary is a daily return of animal admissions, deaths ("died"), animals destroyed ("shot"), and discharges, kept by Major C. G. Saunders, Canadian Army Veterinary Corps, Officer Commanding, Canadian Veterinary Hospital.
- The token "Cfs" closes almost every daily entry and is transcribed as written; its expansion is not certain.
- Many entries use the recurring formula "[number] to [uncertain]" before a discharge figure; the short word following "to" is illegible in the handwriting and is marked [uncertain] throughout.
- Uncertain items in this month: the character between "died" and "shot" on 1 April (reads as "&" and may be a hastily written numeral); the number of horses admitted on 2 April (cut at the page edge); the "36 men sent to … for front line service" wording on 7 April (two or three words illegible); "1 mule" on 8 April (at the page edge); the "308" intake figure on 27 April; and various small words/figures marked [uncertain] in the entries above.
- No human casualties are named. No other persons are named in the April entries.
