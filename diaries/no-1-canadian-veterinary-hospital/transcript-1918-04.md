## Page 197 — Diary entries, 1–19 April 1918

[Place column: Havre]

**1-4-18 — Havre**
Col Newsom D.D.V.S. [?] visited Camp and made arrangements for receiving a great number of horses at this unit over our establishment. S.C.R.

**2, 3, 4 Apl.**
Preparations were made for receiving horses by road from Rouen. 196 arrived this p.m. from Rouen. Also No 2 Vet Hospital, all of which were in fair condition. The weather was wet & cold making our outside standings very muddy. S.C.R.

**6th Apl.**
201 animals were admitted by road, two lame cases brought in an ambulance — all horses in good condition, considering the journey. Corrals in bad condition — it seems impossible to get sufficient material for repairs. S.C.R.

**7th Apl.**
199 horses & 7 mules were admitted — 1 man reported. S.C.R.

**9th Apl.**
200 horses admitted by road from Rouen — they were in poor condition — a great number of eye cases, some of which were totally blind. 15 of which have been cast for butcher by D.D.V.S. [?]. Others held as doubtful for further inspection. S.C.R.
2 N.C.O.s & 3 men reported on probation from Canadian Labour pool.

**10th Apl.**
10 men returned to Canadian Labour pool. S.C.R.

**11th Apl.**
Patrols in charge of officers were posted around Camp according to Base orders. S.C.R.
1 Veterinary Sergeant left.

**12th Apl.**
1 Veterinary Sergeant left. S.C.R.

**13th Apl.**
1 Veterinary Sergeant returned — 3 Veterinary Sergeants left. S.C.R.

**14th Apl.**
1 man returned to Canadian Labour pool. S.C.R.

**16th Apl.**
Col Newsom D.D.V.S. [?] visited Camp and inspected all horses and gave orders to deliver all serviceable horses to Remounts. S.C.R.

**19th Apl.**
160 horses sent to Remounts. 69 were accepted. S.C.R.

## Page 198 — Diary entries, 20–29 April 1918, and monthly summary

**20th Apl 1918 — Havre**
342 animals in good condition [sent to Remounts — interlineated above the line] 165 rejected mostly for being too poor. S.C.R.
1 man left. 18 [uncertain] reported.

**23rd Apl**
D.D.V.S. [?] visited and inspected horses refused at Remounts. About 140 of these were selected and kept for discharge at future date. — 1 man left. S.C.R.

**24th Apl**
1 Sgt, 2 Cpls, 1 Cpl [uncertain] and 1 [uncertain] left for Canadian Veterinary Evacuation Station. S.C.R.

**25th Apl**
10 men reported on probation from Canadian Labour pool. S.C.R.

**26th Apl**
Horses discharged to Remounts — 25 horses and 18 mules accepted. S.C.R.

**27th Apl**
Of the 140 animals previously selected by D.D.V.S. [?], 4 had in the meantime developed Catarrh [uncertain — word appears to be "Catarrh"]. These were replaced by other horses. Only 104 of these were accepted. S.C.R.

**28th Apl**
1 man returned to Canadian Labour pool. S.C.R.

**29th Apl**
7 men reported on probation from Canadian Labour pool. S.C.R.

**Monthly summary (undated, end of April):**
During the month 1172 horses and 108 mules were received.
125 horses and 8 mules were sent to butcher, an unusually large number.
698 horses and 125 mules were discharged to Remounts.
Daily average of N.C.O.'s and men extended duty 20. S.C.R.

[Signed:]
S. C. Richards Major Cavc.
Cmdg Canadian Veterinary Hospital

## Notes on this month

- **Missing dates (no entry):** 5, 8, 15, 17, 18, 21, 22 and 30 April 1918.
- **Blank / cover pages:** Page 194 is the cover page; page 195 is a contents/title page (no diary entries); page 196 is a diary title page (no diary entries). The actual diary text is on pages 197–198 only, as stated on page 195 ("Diary Text 2 Pages").
- **No duplicate pages.**
- **Uncertain readings:** The marginal initials throughout appear to be "S.C.R." (Major S. C. Richards); the abbreviation after "Col Newsom" and "D.D.V.S." is not fully legible and is shown as [?]. On 20 April the number/word before "reported" is illegible. On 24 April two ranks/words in the party leaving for the Canadian Veterinary Evacuation Station are illegible. On 27 April the ailment is read as "Catarrh" but is not certain.
- **Place:** The place column throughout reads "Havre" (Le Havre, France).
- **Named people:** Col Newsom, D.D.V.S.; Major S. C. Richards (Commanding Officer).
- **Movements / animals:** Large intakes of horses by road from Rouen (196 on 2–4 April, 200 on 9 April, plus 201 on 6 April and 199 horses & 7 mules on 7 April). Discharges to Remounts through the month; 125 horses and 8 mules sent to butcher.
