## Notes

- **No missing dates:** all 30 days of November 1917 are entered, 1st–30th. No duplicate pages.
- **No blank pages:** pages 174–176 are cover/inventory/title sheets; page 177 is the sole diary-text page, as stated on page 175 ("Diary Text 1 Page").
- **7 November [uncertain]:** the number of animals that died is unclear — it appears to read "11" but the usual entry is "Died 1"; recorded as "Died [uncertain: 1 or 11]".
- **Large admissions:** 290 animals admitted on 13 November and 192 on 24 November — far above the usual 8–23 a day. No explanation is given in the diary.
- **Visitor's name:** the remarks name reads "Col Newsome" in this month (1st and 15th November). The October diary spells the same name "Col Newman"; the spelling is transcribed as it appears on each page.
- **Animal figures:** the diary records daily admissions, animals destroyed "to Butcher" (3 on 1st, 6 on 6th, 6 on 19th, 9 on 30th), animals "Destroyed (Shot)" (1 on 6th) and "Shot" (1 each on 15th, 20th, 27th and 29th), animals that died (1 each on 15th, 20th, 21st and 23rd, plus the uncertain 7th), animals "Cured to Remounts" (103 on 3rd, 107 on 8th, 109 on 10th, 89 on 17th, 101 on 20th, 60 each on 24th and 27th), and frequent transfers to No. 2 Vet Hospital. No human casualties are recorded.
