## Page 150 — Diary text (Army Form C. 2118)

WAR DIARY or INTELLIGENCE SUMMARY.
(Erase heading not required.)

Hour, Date, Place: May 1st 1917 Havre

- **1 May:** 7 horses admitted 1 died 1 shot Cfs
- **2 May:** 12 admitted 1 died 3 shot 16 transferred to No. 2 V.H. Cfs
- **3 May:** Hostile air craft alarms at 2.45 and 3 o'clock 12 horses admitted Cfs
- **4 May:** 12 horses admitted 4 shot Cfs
- **5 May:** 9 horses admitted 1 died 1 shot Cfs
- **6 May:** 9 horses admitted 2 shot Cfs
- **7 May:** 9 horses admitted 1 shot Cfs
- **8 May:** 6 horses admitted 3 horses 1 mule died 69 horses 3 mules discharged Cfs
- **9 May:** 3 horses 1 mule admitted 3 shot 1 to [uncertain] Cfs
- **10 May:** 4 horses admitted 2 shot 1 horse 1 mule to [uncertain] 48 horses 11 mules discharged Cfs
- **11 May:** 4 horses admitted 1 died 8 to [uncertain] Cfs
- **12 May:** 3 horses admitted 1 shot 35 discharged Cfs
- **13 May:** D.V.S. inspection. R.S.M. [uncertain] decorated with M.S.M. & [uncertain] D.V.S. 5 horses admitted 1 died Cfs
- **14 May:** 7 horses admitted 1 died 2 shot Cfs
- **15 May:** 3 horses admitted 1 died 2 shot 2 to [uncertain] 27 horses 3 mules discharged Cfs — Remark: 102 animals [uncertain] sent out to Remounts fit for service Cfs
- **16 May:** 9 horses admitted 1 to [uncertain] Cfs
- **17 May:** 14 horses admitted 1 died 1 shot 1 mule to [uncertain] Cfs
- **18 May:** 11 horses admitted 2 shot 3 to [uncertain] Cfs
- **19 May:** 13 horses admitted 35 horses 1 mule discharged Cfs — Remark: 200 animals fit for service sent out Cfs
- **20 May:** 3 horses admitted Cfs
- **21 May:** 3 horses admitted Cfs
- **22 May:** 3 horses admitted 1 died 4 shot 1 to [uncertain] 26 horses 14 mules discharged Cfs — Remark: 80 animals fit for service sent out Cfs
- **23 May:** 6 horses admitted 2 to [uncertain] Cfs
- **24 May:** 7 horses admitted 1 shot. Remounts phoned up to say they could take 110 animals Cfs
- **25 May:** 4 horses admitted Cfs
- **26 May:** 44 [uncertain] horses admitted 1 died Cfs
- **27 May:** 28 horses 2 mules admitted 1 died 1 shot Cfs — Remark: 105 animals fit for service sent out Cfs
- **28 May:** 21 horses 4 mules admitted Cfs
- **29 May:** D.V.S. inspection 21 horses 6 mules admitted 54 horses 11 mules discharged Cfs
- **30 May:** Base Commandant inspection 258 [uncertain] horses 14 mules admitted from No. 2 Vet Hosp 1 shot Cfs
- **31 May:** 19 horses 4 mules admitted 1 shot 5 to [uncertain] 39 horses 2 mules discharged Cfs — Remark: 85 animals fit for service sent out Cfs

Signed at foot of page:

C. G. Saunders
Major C.A.V.C.
O.C. C.V.H.

## Transcriber's notes

- All 31 days of May 1917 are present, one entry per day, 1–31 May. No missing dates, no duplicate pages. Page 148 is an index sheet only (no diary text); no pages are blank.
- Place throughout is given as "Havre" (Le Havre).
- The diary is a daily return of animal admissions, deaths ("died"), animals destroyed ("shot"), and discharges, kept by Major C. G. Saunders, Canadian Army Veterinary Corps, Officer Commanding, Canadian Veterinary Hospital.
- The token "Cfs" closes almost every daily entry and is transcribed as written; its expansion is not certain.
- Many entries use the recurring formula "[number] to [uncertain]" before a discharge figure; the short word following "to" is illegible in the handwriting and is marked [uncertain] throughout.
- Notable entries: 3 May — "Hostile air craft alarms at 2.45 and 3 o'clock"; 13 May — D.V.S. inspection, and an R.S.M. [uncertain] decorated with the M.S.M.; 24 May — "Remounts phoned up to say they could take 110 animals"; 30 May — Base Commandant inspection, with a large intake of 258 [uncertain] horses and 14 mules from No. 2 Vet Hosp. Animals fit for service were sent out in batches noted in the remarks column: 102 on 15 May (to Remounts), 200 on 19 May, 80 on 22 May, 105 on 27 May, and 85 on 31 May.
- Uncertain items in this month: the R.S.M. abbreviation and the word joining "M.S.M." to "D.V.S." on 13 May; the word between "animals" and "sent out" in the 15 May remark; the "44" intake figure on 26 May; the "258" intake figure on 30 May; and the recurring "to [uncertain]" words marked in the entries above.
- No human casualties are named. The only person named is Major C. G. Saunders (the diarist); an unnamed R.S.M. is recorded as decorated with the M.S.M. on 13 May.
