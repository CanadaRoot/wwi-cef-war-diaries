## Notes on this month

- **Missing dates (no entry):** 3, 13, 15, 17, 22, 24, 26 and 29 June 1918.
- **Blank / cover pages:** Page 203 is the cover page; page 204 is a contents/title page (no diary entries); page 205 is a diary title page (no diary entries). The actual diary text is on page 206 only, as stated on page 204 ("Diary Text 1 Page").
- **No duplicate pages.**
- **Uncertain readings:** This month's diary page is closely written and several words are hard to read. The destination on 1 June is read as "No. 1 C.M.V.S." but the abbreviation is not certain. The place on 7 June is read as "Alençon, Orne". Lt Gunning's surname on 14 June is not certain. The second officer's name on 20 June and Lt Col Rayns's surname are not certain. The depot name on 11 June is partly illegible. The place on 25 June is read as "Gerardmer, Vosges". The marginal initials throughout appear to be "S.C.R." (Major S. C. Richards).
- **Place:** The place column throughout reads "Havre" (Le Havre, France).
- **Named people:** Capt J. F. Boswell; Col Newsom; Capt H. Colebourn; Lt W. Gunning [uncertain]; Lt Col A. H. Rayns, Scots Guards; Major S. C. Richards (Commanding Officer).
- **Movements / animals:** Capt Boswell escorted 200 horses by road to No. 1 C.M.V.S. and returned with 60 other ranks (1–6 June). 29 "A" men sent to the Canadian Base Depot for transfer to combatant units (12 June); 5 men sent to O.C. Reinforcements, Etaples, for transfer to the Home Establishment (27 June). Two veterinary sergeants on probation were confirmed in their rank (27 June).
