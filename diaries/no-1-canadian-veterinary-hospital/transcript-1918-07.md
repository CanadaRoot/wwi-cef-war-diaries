## Page 210 — Diary entries, 2–31 July 1918

[Place column: Havre]

**July 2nd 1918 — Havre**
1 Vet Sgt left for 4th Bde C.F.A. — 1 Vet Sergt left for 14th Bde 5th Can. Div. artillery. S.C.R.

**" 3rd —**
Capt H. W. Craig reported from Rouen. S.C.R.

**" 5th —**
1 man reported from No. 6 C.M.V.S. — 1 Vet Sgt left for 9th C.R.T. and returned 13-7-18 (no vacancy). S.C.R.

**" 6th —**
25 "A" men sent to C.B.D. — General Moore and Col Newsom visited the hospital. S.C.R.
16 O.R. returned to Can. Lab. pool as unsuitable.

**" 8th —**
Lt.-Col C. E. Edgett, D.S.O., A.D.V.S. Canadians, England, arrived — 11 O.R. reported from Can. Lab. pool on probation. S.C.R.

**" 9th —**
Capt H. W. Craig left for Can. Cav. Brigade. S.C.R.

**" 10th —**
Lt A. McIntosh reported from England — Lt.-Col Edgett left. S.C.R.

**" 12th —**
2 Vet Sgts left for 13th Bde C.F.A. 5th Can. Div. artillery. S.C.R.

**" 15th —**
1 man invalided to England. S.C.R.

**" 16th —**
1 man sent to "A" C.M.V.S. — 1 O.R. reported from Can. Lab. pool on probation. S.C.R.

**" 17th —**
1 Cpl (Veterinary Student) sent to Etaples for transfer to England — 6 O.R. reported from Can. Lab. pool on probation. S.C.R.

**" 18th —**
1 Vet Sgt invalided from Canadian Forestry Corps. S.C.R.

**" 21st —**
4 O.R. reported from Can. Lab. pool on probation. S.C.R.

**" 22nd —**
1 Cook sent to No. 2 C.M.V.S. — 1 man over 50 years of age sent to Etaples for transfer to England. S.C.R.

**" 23rd —**
Col Newsom visited the Hospital. S.C.R.

**" 25th —**
Lt. W. Gunning left for Hq No. 6 Dist C.F.C. S.C.R.

**" 27th —**
1 man reported from No. 6 C.M.V.S. S.C.R.

**" 28th —**
1 Vet "A" man returned from Can. Lab. pool Category B1. S.C.R.

**" 31st —**
Col Newsom visited the Hospital. S.C.R.

**Monthly summary (undated, end of July):**
44 Men attached on probation taken on strength during the month as suitable.
1 Vet Sgt struck off strength after 2 months attachment on probation to 76th Coy C.F.C. S.C.R.

[Signed:]
S. C. Richards Major Cavc.
Cmdg Canadian Veterinary Hospital

## Notes on this month

- **Date discrepancy:** The cover page (page 207) reads "FROM 2nd July TO 31st July 1918", while the contents page (page 208) reads "From 1st to 31st July, 1918". The first diary entry is dated 2 July.
- **Missing dates (no entry):** 1, 4, 7, 11, 13, 14, 19, 20, 24, 26, 29 and 30 July 1918.
- **Blank / cover pages:** Page 207 is the cover page; page 208 is a contents/title page (no diary entries); page 209 is a diary title page (no diary entries). The actual diary text is on page 210 only, as stated on page 208 ("Diary Text 1 Page").
- **No duplicate pages.**
- **Uncertain readings:** The marginal initials throughout appear to be "S.C.R." (Major S. C. Richards). A few words are closely written but legible; no names, dates or numbers have been guessed.
- **Place:** The place column throughout reads "Havre" (Le Havre, France).
- **Named people:** Capt H. W. Craig; General Moore; Col Newsom; Lt.-Col C. E. Edgett, D.S.O., A.D.V.S. Canadians, England; Lt A. McIntosh; Lt. W. Gunning; Major S. C. Richards (Commanding Officer).
- **Movements:** 25 "A" men sent to the Canadian Base Depot (6 July); 16 other ranks returned to the Canadian Labour pool as unsuitable (6 July); a veterinary sergeant returned from the 9th C.R.T. for want of a vacancy (13 July); one man over 50 sent to Etaples for transfer to England (22 July).
