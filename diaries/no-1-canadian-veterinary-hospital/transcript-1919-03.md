## Notes on this month

- **Missing dates (no entry):** 1, 2, 4, 5, 6, 7, 9, 11, 13, 16, 17, 18, 20, 21, 23, 24, 26, 27, 28, 29 and 30 March 1919. (1–2 March are outside the stated period, which begins 3 March.)
- **Blank / cover pages:** Page 238 is the cover page; page 239 is a contents/title page (no diary entries); page 240 is a diary title page (no diary entries). The actual diary text is on page 241 only, as stated on page 239 ("Diary Text 1 Page").
- **No duplicate pages.**
- **Uncertain readings:** Capt. Hanagan's surname on 19 March is not certain. The struck-through line on the title page (page 240) cannot be read.
- **Place:** The place column throughout reads "Havre" (Le Havre, France).
- **Named people:** Capt. R. Hanagan [uncertain]; Col. Newsom; Major S. C. Richards, O.B.E. (Commanding Officer).
- **Notable findings:** The diary is in a different, neater hand this month. The final entry, dated 4 April 1919 and written below the signature, reads "Embarked for England" — the hospital's departure from Le Havre. The 31 March entry notes 15 other ranks posted to C.A.V.C. General "on Various Dates", and 24 other ranks were sent to the Canadian Embarkation Camp on 15 March.
- **Movements:** Veterinary sergeants continued to report in from Forestry Corps districts (2nd, 31st Coy, 1st and 5th Districts) while other ranks were sent to the Canadian Embarkation Camp for return to England/Canada.
