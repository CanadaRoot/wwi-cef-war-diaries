# War Diary Transcript — Canadian Veterinary Hospital, August 1915

**Unit:** Canadian Veterinary Hospital, Havre
**Month:** August 1915
**Manifest pages:** 33–37 (image files page-0033 to page-0037)
**Source:** Library and Archives Canada, Item 2005175, RG 9 III-D-3, vol. 5044, file 894, reel T-10936
**Volume:** Volume 4 — "From 15th August to 31st August 1915", Volume 15-8, Diary Text 3 Pages
**Author / O.C.:** Capt. Thomas Charles Evans, C.A.V.C. (signed "Chas Evans Capt C.A.V.C.")

> **Coverage gap:** This volume covers **15–31 August 1915 only**. No diary text survives in the manifest for **1–14 August 1915** — the volume's own cover states it runs "From 15th August". The monthly return on page 37 nevertheless gives whole-month figures (under treatment 1 August: 539).

Transcription conventions: original spelling, dates, places and wording kept. Anything illegible or uncertain is marked [uncertain]. The diary is handwritten on Army Form C. 2118; diary sheets are numbered "Sheet 1" and "Sheet 2".

## Page 35 — Sheet 1

**Havre, 15-8-15**

> Reported at Canadian Veterinary Hospital to take over command of Hospital from Capt V.C. Leckie who has orders to proceed to Rouen to take over command of Indian Veterinary Hospital.

**" 17-8-15**

> Take over command of Canadian Veterinary Hospital this day from Capt V.C. Leckie. Received 206 horses, mules and 18 [sick — uncertain] horses. Returned 16 cured to Remounts. General Moore D.V.S. visited hospital.

**" 18-8-15**

> Daily routine as usual. Capt Stedman reported for duty.

**" 19-8-15**

> Returned 33 horses and mules Cured to remounts. Captain Leckie departed with his groom & horse for Rouen this day to take Command of Indian Veterinary Hospital. He takes over [this — uncertain] [day — uncertain].

**20-8-15**

> Daily Routine as usual.

**21-8-15**

> 10 Horses & 10 mules returned Cured to Remounts. 3 French Veterinary Officers visited Hospital also D.O. Camps.

**22-8-15**

> Commence a discharge stable in Charge of Capt Stedman. All horses convalescent will be turned into this stable for finish. 24 horses admitted into Hospital.

## Notes on this volume

- **Missing dates:** No diary text for **1–14 August 1915** (the volume itself begins 15 August). Within 15–31 August there are no entries for **16 August** or **27 August**.
- **No blank or duplicate pages** in this volume; pages 33–34 are cover/title pages, pages 35–36 are the diary sheets, page 37 is the monthly return.
- **Discrepancy to note:** The August return gives "Under treatment Aug. 1st 1915: 539", but the July return (transcript-1915-07.md) gave "Remaining under treatment July 31st 1915: 544" — a difference of 5 animals, unexplained in the diary.
- **Named people:** Capt. V. C. Leckie (hands over command 15–17 August; leaves for Rouen to command the Indian Veterinary Hospital); General Moore, D.V.S. (visited 17 August); Capt. Stedman (reported for duty 18 August; in charge of the new discharge stable); Major [Dawn — uncertain] (leave to England, 25 August); the A.D.M.S. (visited 25 August); three French Veterinary Officers (visited 21 August).
- **Places:** Havre (hospital); Rouen (Indian Veterinary Hospital — Leckie's new posting).
- **Casualties (animals):** Monthly totals: 2 destroyed unfit for food, 1 died unfit for food, none destroyed fit for food. No individual deaths are named in the daily entries for 15–31 August.
- **Health:** Outbreak of severe diarrhoea in camp on 29 August — not serious, all cases recovered in a few days; no report made by the medical authorities (dysentery [uncertain]).
- **Monthly totals (August):** 539 under treatment at 1 August; 265 admitted; 804 total cases; 356 cured to Remounts; 150 transferred to No. 2 Veterinary Hospital; 295 remaining under treatment at 31 August.
