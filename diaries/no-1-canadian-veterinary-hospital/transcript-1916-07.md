## Notes

- **No missing dates:** entries run 1–31 July 1916 with no gaps. No duplicate pages.
- **Blank/cover pages:** p. 1 is the manuscript cover; p. 2 is the typed index/header sheet ("Diary Text — 1 Page"); p. 3 is the typed title page on Army Form C. 2118. Only p. 4 carries diary text.
- **Volume discrepancy:** the cover reads "Volume 15" while p. 2 reads "Volume 16-7".
- **"18 horses sent to C.H.D." (31 July):** this line sits between the 30 and 31 July entries with no date of its own; it is assigned here to 31 July, but it may be a continuation of the 30 July entry.
- **Uncertain readings:** 17 July — the words after "admitted" before "11th Div. Amm. Col." are not clearly legible (possibly "3rd Sect."); 18 July — "cast for sold" is as written (possibly "cast, for sale"); 22 July — "163 horses" could be read as "16 horses 3 [?]"; 23 July — the inspecting officer is written "D.V.S. & D.V.S." (the second may be "A.D.V.S."); 24 July — one word after "admitted" is illegible; 26 July — "admitted to 7 Hosp." is as written ("to" may be "from"); 28 July — the tail of the entry ("6 mule[s] shot") is partly interlineated and uncertain; 31 July — the inspecting body is written "D.V.S. [?] Canadian Inspection" (possibly "D.V.S. & A.D.V.S. Canadian Inspection"); the signature's corps abbreviation reads as "C.A.V.C." but is written in a flourish.
- **Abbreviations as written (likely expansions, not in the original):** C.G.S. = Major C. G. Saunders' initials; D.V.S. = Director of Veterinary Services; A.D.V.S. = Assistant Director of Veterinary Services; C.H.D. = Canadian Horse Depot (not expanded in the original); remts = remounts; Hosp. = Hospital; V.H. = Veterinary Hospital; O.C. = Officer Commanding; C.A.V.C. = Canadian Army Veterinary Corps.
- **Notable content:** the hospital at Havre held 462 horses and 111 mules on 1 July. Large admissions came from No. 7 (Veterinary) Hospital and No. 22 Hospital throughout the month; animals were discharged to remounts, sent to C.H.D., sold to the butcher, shot, or died. D.V.S. inspections are recorded on 5, 17, 23 and 31 July. No individual animals or other ranks are named; the only person named is Major C. G. Saunders.
