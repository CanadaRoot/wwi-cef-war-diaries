## Notes

- The month's diary is a single diary page (p. 4) of daily horse and mule tallies, plus a single dated entry in the Remarks column recording a D.D.V.S. inspection on 3 March. The typed title sheet on p. 3 names the diarist as Major C. G. Saunders, Canadian Veterinary Hospital (in mixed case, and without the month line that the February title sheet carries).
- The abbreviation "Cfs" appears at the end of most daily entries, exactly as written; its expansion is not given in the diary. Four entries have no trailing "Cfs": 13 March ("23 horses 6 mules discharged"), 17 March (ending "work much disorganised"), 18 March ("7 to No 2") and 23 March ("3 to No 2").
- The cover page (p. 1) gives the volume as "Volume 23", while the inner title page (p. 2) gives "Volume 17-3". Both are transcribed as written. The March cover is a typed form with the unit name, dates and volume number filled in by hand.
- The handwritten "No" (with a raised "o", i.e. the abbreviation for "Number") in the top right corner of the cover page is transcribed as seen; its meaning is not stated on the page. The same mark appears on the January and February covers; enlarged inspection shows it is "No", not "40" as it appears at low resolution.
- Day-by-day attribution: the diary page carries one tally line for each day, 1–31 March, presented here in the order written. As in January and February, the day numerals in the left-hand ("Hour, Date, Place") column are unevenly spaced and in places do not sit exactly level with the tally line for the same day, so the day attribution follows the order of the entries rather than the precise vertical position of each numeral. The first entry sits on the "March 1st 1917. Havre" line and the last entry sits immediately above the signature. The numerals "5", "15" and "25" in the date column are followed by a dash ("5-", "15-", "25-"), as written.
- "No 2" in the March entries means No. 2 Veterinary Hospital (compare January's "100 horses transferred to No 2 Vet Hosp."). March is dominated by very large movements between this hospital and No. 2: large drafts admitted "from No 2" (233 horses on 4 March; 161 horses on 8 March) and large numbers sent "to No 2" (200 on 9 March; 178 horses and 12 mules on 10 March; 72 horses and 116 mules on 16 March; 66 on 29 March).
- The large figures are transcribed exactly as written, even where they are unusually large: 233 horses admitted on 4 March, 282 horses admitted on 18 March, 292 horses admitted on 26 March, 191 horses admitted on 10 March, 182 horses admitted on 9 March, and 116 mules sent to No. 2 on 16 March. The "116 mules" and the "51 mules" of 24 March were checked by enlarged inspection and read as written.
- The 17 March entry is transcribed exactly as written, including the plus sign and the compressed phrase "28 work horses turned in to Remounts + work much disorganised" — the sense appears to be that 28 work horses were turned in to the Remounts and the hospital's work was much disorganised as a result. The entry runs past the diary column into the Remarks column.
- The 24 March entry reads, as written, "11 horses admitted 51 mules 1 shot 46 discharged Cfs" — "51 mules" is as written.
- The signature block reads "C. G. Saunders / Major O.C. / O.C. C.V.H." — the third line abbreviates the unit as "C.V.H." rather than "Can Vet Hosp." as in January and February.
- No dates are missing: entries are present for every day 1–31 March 1917. No duplicate pages and no blank pages in this month's set.
- Notable entries: very large intakes on 4 March (233 horses, 5 mules, from No. 2), 8 March (161 horses, 11 mules, from No. 2), 10 March (191 horses, 5 mules), 18 March (282 horses, 12 mules) and 26 March (292 horses, 2 mules); very large transfers to No. 2 Veterinary Hospital, including 200 horses on 9 March, 178 horses and 12 mules on 10 March, and 72 horses and 116 mules on 16 March; a D.D.V.S. inspection on 3 March (Remarks column); on 17 March, 28 work horses were turned in to the Remounts and the work was much disorganised.
