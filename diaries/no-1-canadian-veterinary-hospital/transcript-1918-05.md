## Page 202 — Diary entries, 2–28 May 1918

[Place column: Havre]

**May 2nd 1918 — Havre**
Col Newsom visited hospital.

**" 5th —**
1 Vet Sergt left for 76th Coy Canadian Forestry Corps on two months probation. S.C.R.
3 men left for Canadian Veterinary Evacuation Station. S.C.R.

**" 7th —**
1 Vet Sergt left for 75th Coy Canadian Forestry Corps. S.C.R.

**" 9th —**
Capt A. H. Hunter left for Canadian Forestry Corps Le [uncertain — place name, appears to read "Jura"]. S.C.R.

**" 10th —**
200 animals sent by road to No 6 Vet Hosp Rouen. S.C.R.

**" 12th —**
Capt A. Savage left for Rouen with 200 animals. S.C.R.

**" 13th —**
2 Vet Sergts left for 51st Coy and 77 Coy Canadian Forestry Corps on 2 months probation. S.C.R.

**" 14th —**
Capt J. A. Boswell reported from 2nd Canadian Division.
1 Vet Sergt left for 191st Coy Canadian Forestry Corps on 2 months probation. [Coy number uncertain — appears to read "191st".]

**" 16th —**
Major W. G. Stedman reported from England. S.C.R.
Capt A. Savage returned from Rouen.
Col Newsom visited the hospital. S.C.R.

**" 17th —**
1 Vet Sgt left for 6th Battalion Canadian Railway Troops. S.C.R.

**" 18th —**
8 "A" men sent to C.B.D. for transfer to Combatant units. S.C.R.

**" 21st —**
Capt A. J. McLean left for 2nd Canadian Division. S.C.R.

**" 22nd —**
2 men reported from 3rd Canadian Mobile Vet Section.
Col Newsom visited the hospital. Capt A. H. Hunter returned from C.F.C. Le [uncertain — place name, appears to read "Jura"]. S.C.R.

**" 23rd —**
Col Hamilton & two Staff officers from 3rd Echelon, G.H.Q. Rouen for the purpose of consultation. S.C.R.

**" 24th —**
1 Vet Sergt left for 14th [uncertain] C.F. [uncertain].

**" 25th —**
1 man transferred from C.A.S.C. Pool. S.C.R.

**" 27th —**
Major W. G. Stedman left for 4th Canadian Division as D.A.D.V.S. S.C.R.
Capt H. H. Sharp left for 1st Canadian Division. Capt H. S. Clapp reported from Boulogne. S.C.R.

**" 28th —**
Col Newsom visited the hospital. S.C.R.
Major Gibbs and Captain Bell of the American Veterinary Service visited Camp — Major Gibbs is an [uncertain] and Capt Bell a Remount Officer. Purpose was to get information & ideas — every facility given them. S.C.R.

**Monthly summary (undated, end of May):**
Major Stedman here for 13 days — average no. of men in hospital daily, 21 — 3 officers reported — 3 officers departed — 23 O.R. departed — 3 men reported.

[Signed:]
S. C. Richards Major Cavc.
Cmdg Canadian Veterinary Hospital

## Notes on this month

- **Date discrepancy:** The cover page (page 199) reads "FROM 2nd May TO 28th May 1918", while the contents page (page 200) reads "From 1st to 28th May, 1918". The first diary entry is dated 2 May.
- **Missing dates (no entry):** 1, 3, 4, 6, 8, 11, 15, 19, 20, 26, 29, 30 and 31 May 1918.
- **Blank / cover pages:** Page 199 is the cover page; page 200 is a contents/title page (no diary entries); page 201 is a diary title page (no diary entries). The actual diary text is on page 202 only, as stated on page 200 ("Diary Text 1 Page").
- **No duplicate pages.**
- **Uncertain readings:** The place name after "Canadian Forestry Corps Le …" on 9 and 22 May is illegible (appears to read "Jura"). The Forestry Corps company number on 14 May appears to read "191st" but is not certain. The destination on 24 May is partly illegible. Major Gibbs's role/description on 28 May is illegible. The marginal initials throughout appear to be "S.C.R." (Major S. C. Richards).
- **Place:** The place column throughout reads "Havre" (Le Havre, France).
- **Named people:** Col Newsom; Capt A. H. Hunter; Capt A. Savage; Capt J. A. Boswell; Major W. G. Stedman; Capt A. J. McLean; Col Hamilton; Capt H. H. Sharp; Capt H. S. Clapp; Major Gibbs and Captain Bell (American Veterinary Service); Major S. C. Richards (Commanding Officer).
- **Movements / animals:** 200 animals sent by road to No. 6 Veterinary Hospital, Rouen (10 May); Capt Savage escorted 200 animals to Rouen (12 May). Several veterinary sergeants posted out to Canadian Forestry Corps companies on two months' probation; officers coming and going between the hospital and the 1st, 2nd and 4th Canadian Divisions.
