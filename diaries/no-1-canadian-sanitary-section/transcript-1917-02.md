## Notes — February 1917
- Every day from 1 to 28 February is covered; no dates are missing.
- 2 February: C.F.A. billets burned down during the night.
- 7–8 February: water pipes to the front line were frozen and water was brought up in tins; a strong smell of coal gas in dugouts led to warnings about open braziers.
- 15 February: Pte. Judge went to the 1st 6th Heavy Battery as water detail, relieving Cpl. Beasley, who joined the Sanitary Section and reverted to private.
- 17 February: three cases of mumps in the 7th Battalion; a platoon was isolated and billets disinfected.
- 18 February: one man returned from leave with venereal disease.
- 23 February: a case of diphtheria was investigated at Bully Grenay (2nd [uncertain] Hospital).
- 25 February: the section was told it would be located at Camblain l'Abbé; the move was arranged with the 2nd Division's Sanitary Officer on 28 February.
- Named people: Capt. W. C. Lawson (O.C.); Capt. Duguid (Town Major, Bully Grenay); Lt. Col. Ford; Lt. Col. Templeton; Major Rankin; Cpl. Beasley; Pte. Judge; Q/Sgt. Ferris.
- No casualties (killed/wounded) are recorded in February 1917.
- No appendices are listed or included for February.
