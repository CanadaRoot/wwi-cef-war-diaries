## Transcription conventions
- Faithful to the original: spelling, punctuation, abbreviations and capitalisation as written (the diary is handwritten throughout).
- `[uncertain]` marks a word or name that could not be read with confidence — never guessed.
- Text struck through in the original is shown as `[struck through in original: "..."]`.
- The diary-text pages are Army Form C. 2118, "WAR DIARY or INTELLIGENCE SUMMARY", handwritten in columns: Place | Date | Hour | Summary of Events and Information | Remarks and references to Appendices. The July diary-text pages are headed "1st Can Sanitary Section" and "Medical Original" in manuscript.
- Place names are as written in the diary ("Dickeybusch", "Poperinghe", "Connaught", "Patricia", "Scottish Lines", "Dominion Lines", "Devonshire Lines", "Swan Chateau", "Belgian Chateau", "Cassel", "Abele"). Map references such as L 17.8 are trench-map squares as written; no latitude/longitude coordinates are given.
- "W.C.L." in the Remarks column is the officer's initials (W. C. Lawson).

## Notes — July 1916
- No entries are recorded for 2, 9, 16, 23 or 30 July; the 24 July entry is obscured by a stamp and no summary text is legible for that date.
- 3 July: a case of (cerebro-spinal) meningitis at 1st Battery, R.G.A.; contacts were sent to No. 10 C.C.S. and the room was sprayed.
- 12 July: Poperinghe was being shelled.
- 21 July: considerable aeroplane activity and bombing in the vicinity early in the morning; men of the 3rd Tunnelling Company were released from quarantine for measles.
- 22 July: Lt. Keen, 1st Battalion, took over the Convalescent Company from Capt. Lawson.
- 29 July: the Divisional Baths were now in operation, though the disinfecting chamber was not yet finished; the 3rd D.A.C. moved into the area.
- Named people: Capt. W. C. Lawson (O.C.); Capt. Evans (loaned as sanitary inspector at the dugouts); Lt. Keen (1st Battalion).
- No casualties (killed/wounded) are recorded in July 1916.
- No appendices are listed or included for July.
