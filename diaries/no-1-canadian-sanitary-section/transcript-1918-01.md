## Transcription conventions
- Faithful to the original: spelling, punctuation, abbreviations and capitalisation as written. The diary is handwritten throughout on Army Form C. 2118, "WAR DIARY or INTELLIGENCE SUMMARY" (the words "INTELLIGENCE SUMMARY" are struck through in the printed heading), in columns: Place | Date | Hour | Summary of Events and Information | Remarks and references to Appendices.
- `[uncertain]` marks a word or name that could not be read with confidence — never guessed.
- Text struck through in the original is shown as `[struck through in original: "..."]`.
- Each diary-text page is headed "Original" (handwritten) and bears the oval stamp "1st CANADIAN DIVISION / SANITARY SECTION".
- No map coordinates (latitude/longitude) are given in the diary.
- Abbreviations used as written: A.D.M.S. = Assistant Director Medical Services; D.D.M.S. = Deputy Director Medical Services; Fld Amb = Field Ambulance; Mob. Lab. = Mobile Laboratory; C.A.M.C. = Canadian Army Medical Corps.

## Notes — January 1918
- Command changed on 20 January: Captain A. J. Lomas left for Base and Captain A. B. Chapman took over command of the Section. (Chapman had relieved Lomas once before, 30 September–11 October 1917.)
- The Section remained based at Ablain St Nazaire for the whole month, working the same Arras–Lens area villages (Souchez, Carency, Aix Noulette, Sains-en-Gohelle, Bouvigny, Boyeffles, Angres, Liévin).
- The Sains-en-Gohelle incinerator was built during the month: under construction on 21–23 January, nearly finished on 25 January, working well by 27 January, fitted with a solder-collecting device described as "fairly successful".
- 26 January: shelling at Liévin was "rather heavy".
- 28 January: a water sample was taken to the Mobile Laboratory (No. 1 Canadian Mobile Laboratory).
- 29 January: a case of cerebro-spinal meningitis was investigated (place name illegible) and the contacts were quarantined; a sanitary inspector was left at Bully Grenay.
- 31 January: the Section intended to move to Les Brebis the next day.
- Named people: Captain A. J. Lomas, C.A.M.C. (outgoing O.C.); Captain A. B. Chapman, C.A.M.C. (incoming O.C.); the Town Majors of the Ablain-area villages (names not given in the diary).
- No appendices are listed or included for January. No casualties of the Section itself are recorded.
