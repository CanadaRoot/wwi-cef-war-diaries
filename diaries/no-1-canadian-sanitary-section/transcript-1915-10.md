## Transcription conventions
- Faithful to the original: spelling, punctuation, abbreviations and capitalisation as written (the diary is handwritten throughout, on Army Form C. 2118).
- `[uncertain]` marks a word or name that could not be read with confidence — never guessed.
- Text struck through in the original is shown as `[struck through in original: "..."]`.
- The diary-text pages are Army Form C. 2118, "WAR DIARY or INTELLIGENCE SUMMARY", handwritten in columns: Place | Date | Hour | Summary of Events and Information | Remarks and references to Appendices.
- No map coordinates (latitude/longitude) are given in the diary; the unit remained at Nieppe throughout the month.
- "Convalescents" figures are given daily in the form Remaining / Admitted / To Duty / To Hosp. (or To Fld Amb.) / Remaining; they do not always reconcile arithmetically as written, and are transcribed as read.

## Notes — October 1915
- No entry for 25 October 1915 — the diary jumps from 24 October (p. 14) to 26 October (p. 15) with no explanation.
- No casualties or deaths are recorded in the month; it is a sanitary/inspection record. The unit stayed at Nieppe all month, with daily work in the divisional area: Bedford/Bulford Camp wells and water supply, Dranoutre and Aldershot Camps, baths and wash houses at Neuve Eglise, trench inspections (trenches 128–142), and the Convalescent Camp figures tracked daily.
- Main threads: the louse/vermin problem in clothing (2% Cresol soak, boiling, and plans for steam disinfectors — a third disinfector installed at Bulford Camp doing 60–80 blankets an hour at 220°F); water supply (new wells at Bedford/Bulford Camp, chlorination insisted on); paratyphoid carriers investigated (a 19-year-old girl, 6–8 October); measles and influenza cases in 3rd Field Co. C.E.
- Discipline: Sgt-Bartholomew reduced to Corporal by Field General Court Martial for drunkenness (sentence promulgated 15 October); another man given 7 days No. 1 Field Punishment for drunkenness and absence from billets (29 October).
- The cover says "Volume 7" while the contents page says "Volume 15-10"; the contents page's "Diary Text 15 Pages" matches the 15 diary-text pages (pp. 3–17).
- The convalescent figures do not always reconcile arithmetically as written (e.g. 23 October and the 26–28 October figures); transcribed as read, with notes in the text. The 31 October entry has the convalescent header but no figures entered.
- The O.C.'s signature is illegible on most pages; the name J. A. Amyot is legible on pp. 7–8. Named people include Lt. McKee (Field Laboratory, Bailleul), Col. Ross, Col. Hammill, Majors Jarvis, Anderson and Chisholm, Major Stanley, Gen. Mercer, Sgt-Bartholomew, and Capt. [uncertain] (O.C. baths, 17 October).
- No appendices are listed or included for October.
