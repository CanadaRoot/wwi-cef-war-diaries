## Notes — January 1919
- The march to the Rhine continued: Wasseiges to Bomal (5 January), Dion-le-Val (6 January), Huldenburg (7 January), about 19 kilometres south-east of Brussels, where the section stopped until further notice and remained for the rest of the month.
- The unit was still broken up, with most of the men attached to the various brigades as inspectors (11 and 23 January).
- Disease: cases of mumps reported in the 44th Battalion headquarters at Overijse (20 January), with billets disinfected; two further cases investigated (22 January).
- Major Chapman went on leave on 27 December and returned on 16 January.
- The Ford car was sent to the workshop for repairs on 17 January (it had been a casualty on 5 December).
- A quarter of the unit was on 48-hour passes to Brussels (12 January), with trips to Waterloo also arranged.
- No signature appears at the foot of the 1–13 or 14–26 January pages; only the final page (27–31 January) is signed by Major Chapman.
- No military casualties are recorded in January 1919. No appendices are listed or included.
- No dates are missing: 1–31 January are all covered.
