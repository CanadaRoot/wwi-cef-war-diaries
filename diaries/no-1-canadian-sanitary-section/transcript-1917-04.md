## Notes — April 1917
- Every day from 1 to 30 April is covered; no dates are missing.
- 2 April: cases of measles, diphtheria and cerebro-spinal meningitis were investigated, all in Imperial units.
- 4 April: Lt. Col. R. M. Simpson, C.A.M.C., was attached to the A.D.M.S.
- 7 April: Lawson met the D.A.D.M.S. (Sanitation), 1st Army, at Villiers-au-Bois, with all the Canadian Corps' sanitary officers, to discuss sanitary matters.
- 9 April (Vimy Ridge): Lawson spent the day at Ambulance Corner and Aux Rutz assisting the walking wounded; a Staff Sergeant and 12 men were sent to assist the 2nd 7th Ambulance. The diary does not name the battle.
- 14 April: Major Rankin informed Lawson that the Sanitary Sections were Corps Troops and would remain in the same area until the Canadian Corps moved.
- 15 April: part of the section moved to Ariene (tents). Locations: 1 officer and 17 other ranks at Ariene; 14 O.R. at Ecoivres; 1 O.R. each at Cambligneul, Estrée Cauchie and Camblain l'Abbé; the lorry at Ecoivres. By 25 April, 1 officer and 22 O.R. were at Ariene.
- 28 April: Lawson watched the shelling of Arleux from Farbus Wood (R.A.P. of the 3rd Battalion) and assisted at the main dressing station during the day.
- Named people: Capt. W. C. Lawson (O.C.); Sergt. Ferris; Lt. Col. R. M. Simpson (C.A.M.C.); Lt. Col. Templeton; Major Rankin; Sergt. Williams; Col. Templeton.
- No casualties (killed/wounded) of the section itself are recorded in April 1917.
- No appendices are listed or included for April.
