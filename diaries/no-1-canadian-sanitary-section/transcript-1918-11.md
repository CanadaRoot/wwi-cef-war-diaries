## Notes — November 1918
- The section was based at Auberchicourt until 8 November, then moved to Valenciennes on 9 November, taking the part of the Canadian Corps area east of the Canal d'Escaut.
- 11 November 1918 — the Armistice: "Peace day — news received very calmly." President Poincaré had visited Valenciennes the day before (10 November).
- On 14 November the section was attached to the 4th Canadian Division for the march to the Rhine, travelling with the 13th Canadian Field Ambulance: Hensies (15 November), La Bouverie (16 November), Ville sur Haine (21 November), where it remained parked for the rest of the month.
- No signature appears at the foot of the 1–9 or 10–19 November pages; only the final page (20–30 November) is signed by Major Chapman.
- No military casualties are recorded in November 1918. No appendices are listed or included.
- No dates are missing: 1–30 November are all covered.
