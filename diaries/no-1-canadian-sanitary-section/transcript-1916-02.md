## Notes — February 1916
- All 29 days of February 1916 (a leap year) are covered, one entry per day, all timed 7 P.M. No dates are missing. No appendices are listed or included for February.
- The cover gives "VOLUME.. 11" while the contents page gives "Volume 16-2" — the two volume numbers disagree in the original.
- The commanding officer's signature at the foot of each diary-text page is difficult to decipher; it appears to read "J. A. Amy" and the Remarks column is initialled "J.A.A." throughout. The name is marked [uncertain] in the transcript.
- Named people: Sgt. Labelle (Convalescent Company, 7 Feb); Major Simpson (18 Feb); Cpl Nasmith (22 Feb — separated from the Sanitary Section); a Major whose surname could not be read (21 Feb — his request for the transfer of a Sergeant Major). No casualties are recorded in the month.
- Recurring work: daily inspection reports from the section's inspectors; lectures on sanitation to the men of the unit and to officers in training at the Training School for Officers; inspections of billets, latrines, water supplies and the Convalescent Camp at Bailleul; repeated visits to the A.D.M.S. and the Mobile Laboratory.
- Gas helmets: on 13 Feb the section received instruction on gas helmets and the H.P. (hypo?) helmet supply was not yet complete; on 19 Feb the O.C. indented for "P.H." gas helmets, noting those supplied by Ordnance were "P" helmets; on 27 Feb the "H.P." helmets were inspected and found O.K.
- Sanitation themes: coal-oil spraying of dug-outs against lice (13 Feb — with iodine added to the oil and the oil called "White" so men would not burn it); dirty billets of the 4th and 2nd C.M.R. (6 Feb) and of the 8th C.M.R. (18 Feb), with the C.M.R. units objecting to being told to clean up; water-supply worries at Hyde Park Corner and Bailleul Camp; a heavy snowstorm with 2 inches of snow and freezing temperatures on the night of 22 Feb, leaving roads too slippery for horse travel on 23–25 Feb.
- On 26 Feb the O.C. presented six sanitary appliances for exhibition at Hazebrouck: a disinfector and clothes drier, a covered well with the pump set at a distance, a paper destructor for camp refuse and faeces, a "Thermos Box" for carrying hot food to the trenches, a field stove made from petrol cans, and an odourless latrine.
- The section's car broke down repeatedly (15, 22, 25, 27, 29 Feb) and was still unfinished in the workshop at the end of the month.
- The 22 Feb entry records an establishment dispute: the section had 27 men plus 2 attached, short of its full establishment, and the D.D.M.S. questioned whether two extra drivers should be added.
- Place names as written: St Jans Cappel (the section's station), Bailleul Camp, Hyde Park Corner, Red Lodge, Dranoutre, Neuve Eglise, Hazebrouck, Mereville district, Convalescent Camp.
- The diary-text pages are printed "T2134. Wt. W708—776. 500000. 4/15. Sir J. C. & S." at the foot.
