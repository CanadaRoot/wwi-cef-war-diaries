## Transcription conventions
- Faithful to the original: spelling, punctuation, abbreviations and capitalisation as written (the diary text is typed; the cover is handwritten, the contents page typed).
- `[uncertain]` marks a word or name that could not be read with confidence — never guessed.
- Text struck through in the original is shown as `[struck through in original: "..."]`.
- The diary-text pages are Army Form C. 2118, "WAR DIARY or INTELLIGENCE SUMMARY", typed in columns: Place | Date | Hour | Summary of Events and Information | Remarks and references to Appendices.
- Place names are given as typed (e.g. "DRANOUTRE", "FLETRE", "LOCRE"); no map coordinates (latitude/longitude) are given in the diary.
- The unit was in the field in France/Belgium (Ypres salient area) throughout the period covered.

## Notes — March 1916
- The diary for this month begins on 12 March, not 1 March: the cover originally read "From 1st March", corrected in handwriting to "12th March", and the contents page also says "From 12th to 31st March, 1916." No entries exist for 1–11 March in this month's diary. The change of command is the reason: Capt. Lawton was instructed on 12 March to take over the Section and reported to the A.D.M.S., 1st Canadian Division, on 14 March, taking over from Major Amyot.
- **No entry for 13 March** — the diary jumps from 12 March to 14 March with no explanation.
- The date "23" on p. 3 is partly obscured by the oval "1st CANADIAN DIVISION / SANITARY SECTION" stamp, but the sequence 22 → 24 makes the reading certain.
- The cover gives "Volume 12" (with "2." written below the "12"); the contents page gives "Volume 16-3". Both are transcribed as written.
- "BULFORD CAMP" (15 March) is typed as written; the unit was in the Ypres salient area (Dranoutre, Locre, Hill 63, Hyde Park Corner, Flêtre), so the name is reproduced exactly as typed.
- Sergt. Major Rees: placed under arrest on 19 March, a summary of evidence was taken on 23 March, and he was reprimanded on 24 March ("Paid men - Sergt. Major reprimanded"); by 28 March he was back on duty, sent with a lorry to the 50th Division with surplus stores.
- Court martial at Locre: postponed on 27 March, held on 28 March (no outcome recorded).
- The Section moved on 30 March: men marched out at 8.30 a.m., lorry at 9 a.m., move completed by 10.30 a.m.; the 41st Sanitary Section took over the old area that afternoon. L/Cpl. Williams and Pte. Harris were left behind to assist the incoming section.
- No casualties are recorded in this portion of the month. No appendices are listed or included for March.
