## Notes — October 1916
- No entry is recorded for 8 October.
- 3 October: Lawson inspected a German prison camp; from 12 October German prisoners worked on sanitary fatigue around Warloy-Baillon under the Sanitary Section's supervision.
- 6 October: 1st Division Headquarters moved to Albert.
- 7 October: temporary baths were put up at Senlis for brigades passing through, using the Foden machine and sprays.
- 17–26 October: the section moved in stages — Rubempré (17th), Canaples (18th), Bernaville (20th), Fromen-le-Grand (21st), Le Couroy (22nd), Rollecourt (23rd), then to the new headquarters at Camblain l'Abbé (26th), taking over the area from the 3rd Division.
- 30 October: H.R.H. the Duke of Connaught visited Headquarters; Major Ware inspected with Lawson.
- Named people: Capt. W. C. Lawson (O.C.); Col. Foster; Major Rankin; Major Ware; Col. Ross; Capt. Gibson; H.R.H. the Duke of Connaught.
- No casualties (killed/wounded) are recorded in October 1916.
- No appendices are listed or included for October.
