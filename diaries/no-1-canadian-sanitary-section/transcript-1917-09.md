## Notes — September 1917
- The Section remained based at Bruay throughout September, working the mining villages around it (Marles-les-Mines, Allouagne, Lozinghem, Burbure, Auchel, Ferfay, Calonne, Divion, La Pugnoy, Amettes, Ames, Nédon, Lieres).
- Pte McCoy: placed under arrest on 16 September for being overdue from leave; tried on 18 September and remanded for Field General Court Martial; on 21 September he was sent from D.F.P.S. to a Field Ambulance.
- 28 September: the Section reported unsatisfactory conditions because it could not secure material for constructing sanitary appliances.
- 29 September: cases of infectious disease were investigated at Lieres.
- 30 September: Captain Lomas went on leave to England; Captain A. B. Chapman, C.A.M.C., 5th Canadian Field Ambulance, relieved him in command.
- Named people: Captain A. J. Lomas, C.A.M.C. (O.C.); Captain A. B. Chapman, C.A.M.C., 5th Cdn Field Ambulance; Lt Humphries; Pte McCoy; the Town Majors of the Bruay-area villages (names not given in the diary).
- No appendices are listed or included for September. No casualties of the Section itself are recorded.
