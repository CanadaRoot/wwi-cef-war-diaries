- `[uncertain]` marks a word or name that could not be read with confidence — never guessed.

## Notes — February 1918
- The section moved to Les Brebis on 1 February 1918 and was based there for the whole month, working across the 1st Canadian Division area: Bully Grenay, Bouvigny, Bracquemont, Aix Noulette, Sains (Gohelle), Boyeffles, Angres, Calonne, Lievin, St Pierre and "Maroe".
- Main work: inspecting billets, bath houses and incinerators; building and repairing incinerators (a "Solder" plant/incinerator recurs through the month — started at Bully 11 Feb, completed at Bouvigny 12 Feb, a second started 19 Feb, "working well" 22 Feb); arranging refuse collection; water points and forward-area water tanks.
- Disease: a measles case investigated at Bracquemont (3 Feb) with quarantine of measle contacts (4 Feb); quarantine raised on 2nd Battalion diphtheria suspects (7 Feb); a mumps case in the 14th Battalion at Lievin (14 Feb); mumps at the 10th Battalion with all contacts isolated (8 Feb).
- The word after "Fine" on 1 Feb appears to read "huidly" and is marked [uncertain]; the abbreviation "D.O.M.S." (2 and 4 Feb) is as read but uncertain; "15 the first day" and "To Cdn Corps" (11 Feb) are as written and their sense is unclear; "presquets" (21 Feb) is as written, possibly the writer's spelling of "piquets" (water details); "Loose 2" (24, 25, 27 Feb) and "Maroe" (16, 20, 22 Feb) are as written.
- No casualties are recorded in February 1918. No appendices are listed or included.
- No dates are missing: 1–28 February are all covered.
