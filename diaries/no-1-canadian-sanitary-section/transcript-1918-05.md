## Transcription conventions
- Faithful to the original: spelling, punctuation, abbreviations and capitalisation as written (the diary is handwritten on Army Form C. 2118 throughout).
- `[uncertain]` marks a word or name that could not be read with confidence — never guessed.
- Text struck through in the original is shown as `[struck through in original: "..."]`.
- The diary-text pages are Army Form C. 2118, "WAR DIARY or INTELLIGENCE SUMMARY" (the "INTELLIGENCE SUMMARY" heading is struck through on every page), handwritten in columns: Place | Date | Hour | Summary of Events and Information | Remarks and references to Appendices. Each page is headed "Original" in handwriting, bears the rectangular stamp "1st CANADIAN / SANITARY SECTION" (No. and Date fields left blank), and is marked "MEDICAL" at top right.
- Each day's entry begins with a check mark (✓) in the original, shown here at the start of the Summary.
- No map coordinates (latitude/longitude) are given in the diary; the unit was based at Divion throughout May 1918.
- Place names are as written; the same places recur with variant spellings (the Place column spells the base "DIVION", except on the 15–20 May page where it is written "DNION").

## Notes — May 1918
- The section was based at Divion for the whole month, its area now extended to take in La Comte, Bajus, Burbure and Houdain (taken over from the 3rd Sanitary Section 3 May), with men posted in the outlying villages.
- Main work: inspecting camps, billets and villages; building brick incinerators (Camblain Chatelin 4 May); supplying fly-proof latrines, incinerators, ablution benches, grease traps and meat safes as new camps sprang up (16 May); water supply and baths; estimating sanitary fixtures for Divion with the C.R.E. (27 May).
- Disease: a diphtheria case at Camblain Chatelin (21 May); typhoid cases at Hurionville (20 May) and Burbure (28–29 May), the latter investigated and reported to Corps.
- The unit was inoculated on 5 May; the Ford car broke down ("a casualty") the same day and was in the workshop until 15 May.
- "Burton" (9 and 27 May) is the same place read as "Burlon" in April; "Hurionville" (18 and 20 May) is as written; the Place column reads "DNION" on the 15–20 May page.
- No military casualties are recorded in May 1918. No appendices are listed or included.
- No dates are missing: 1–31 May are all covered.
