## Notes — January 1917
- Every day from 1 to 31 January is covered; no dates are missing.
- 4 January: Acting Sergeant-Major Rees proceeded to Havre for transfer to England, granted three months' leave to Canada.
- 6 January: a case of measles was found in the civil population at Bruay (8th Brigade billets inspected).
- 10–11 January: the O.C. of the 2nd Canadian Sanitary Section visited Bruay and drove Lawson around the new area to arrange the move.
- 20 January: the Sanitary Section moved from Bruay to Boyeffles [as read], the move completed at 2 p.m.
- 22–24 January: hard frost — roads frozen, water-supply pipe lines to the support and front trenches frozen up, and the lorry was very difficult to start.
- Named people: Capt. W. C. Lawson (O.C.); Act. S.M. Rees; Major Sutcliffe; Capt. Parkinson (D.A.D.M.S. Sanitation, 1st Army); Capt. Byrne (detailed as Sanitary Inspector, Barlin); Capt. Jeffries; Major Rankin; Sergt. Williams; Town Major Diguis [as read].
- No casualties (killed/wounded) are recorded in January 1917.
- No appendices are listed or included for January.
