## Transcription conventions
- Faithful to the original: spelling, punctuation, abbreviations and capitalisation as written (the diary is handwritten on Army Form C. 2118 throughout).
- `[uncertain]` marks a word or name that could not be read with confidence — never guessed.
- Text struck through in the original is shown as `[struck through in original: "..."]`.
- The diary-text pages are Army Form C. 2118, "WAR DIARY or INTELLIGENCE SUMMARY" (the "INTELLIGENCE SUMMARY" heading is struck through on every page), handwritten in columns: Place | Date | Hour | Summary of Events and Information | Remarks and references to Appendices. Each page is headed "Original" in handwriting, bears the rectangular stamp "1st CANADIAN / SANITARY SECTION" (No. and Date fields left blank), and is marked "MEDICAL" at top right.
- Each day's entry begins with a check mark (✓) in the original, shown here at the start of the Summary.
- No map coordinates (latitude/longitude) are given in the diary; the unit was based at Divion throughout June 1918.
- Place names are as written; the same places recur with variant spellings.

## Notes — June 1918
- The section was based at Divion for the whole month, working across the same area: Burbure, Burton, Beuvry, Bruay, Houdain, La Comte, Bajus, Marles, Auchel, Calonne, Camblain Chatelin, Lozinghem, Raimbert, Beugin and the Bois des Dames.
- Main work: inspecting camps, billets, horse standings and horse lines; water points and water supply; a ration dump at Calonne recommended to be moved owing to dust (6 June).
- Disease: measles and diphtheria cases at Marles and Auchel (6 June), diphtheria contacts isolated with throats swabbed by a specialist and the unit paraded for throat inspection (12–14 June), a typhoid case at Burbure (13 June). On 29 June "most of Unit sick with influenza" — the 1918 influenza reaching the section.
- The Remarks initials are "A.B.C." throughout, except that the 15 June initials are struck through in the original and the 16 June entry shows only "A"; the 1–8 June page also shows a single "A" for one entry (the rest of the initials there may be cropped by the page edge).
- The 1–8 June page bears an oval stamp, partly over the heading, dated 3 July 1918 (partly legible, "… COMMANDANT / 131 / 3 JUL 1918 / 19-11").
- "Hurionville" (6, 18, 23 June) and "Helfort" (17 June) are as written; "Helfort" may be Helfaut.
- No military casualties are recorded in June 1918. No appendices are listed or included.
- No dates are missing: 1–30 June are all covered.
