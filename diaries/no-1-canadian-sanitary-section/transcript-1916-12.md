## Notes — December 1916
- Every day from 1 to 31 December is covered; no dates are missing.
- 8 December: sanitary conditions at Chateau de la Haie were reported bad to the A.D.M.S.
- 12–20 December: the section handed the Camblain l'Abbé area over to the 4th Canadian Division Sanitary Section and moved to Bruay, arriving 20 December and taking over from the 4th Division's Sanitary Officer.
- 21 December: Bruay's population is given as 56,000; the Town Major and 49 P.B. men (Imperial) did police and sanitary duties; water came from deep wells pumped to water towers and distributed through mains; garbage and refuse were thrown on the street; some cases of typhoid were reported among civilians, and Lawson suggested appointing a permanent Sanitary Officer for the town.
- 25 December: the men had Christmas dinner at 4.15 p.m. in an establishment, by permission of the A.P.M.
- 29 December: Lawson discussed with the D.A.D.M.S. and D.D.M.S. the appointment of a permanent Sanitary Officer for Bruay and a progress course in sanitation.
- Named people: Capt. W. C. Lawson (O.C.); Capt. Jeffries (2nd 7th Ambulance); Major Ware; Major Rankin; Col. Ross; Sergt. Killenugham [as read — cf. "Cpl. Killen" in June 1916].
- No casualties (killed/wounded) are recorded in December 1916.
- No appendices are listed or included for December.
