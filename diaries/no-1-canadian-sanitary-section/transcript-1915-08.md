## Transcription conventions
- Faithful to the original: spelling, punctuation, abbreviations and capitalisation as written (the diary is handwritten throughout).
- `[uncertain]` marks a word or name that could not be read with confidence — never guessed.
- Text struck through in the original is shown as `[struck through in original: "..."]`.
- The diary-text pages are Army Form C. 2118, "WAR DIARY or INTELLIGENCE SUMMARY", handwritten in columns: Place | Date | Hour | Summary of Events and Information | Remarks and references to Appendices. The "INTELLIGENCE SUMMARY" heading is struck through on the printed form.

## Notes — August 1915
- **The sanitary exhibit:** the month's main work was building a Divisional Sanitary Exhibit at Aldershot Camp (near Neuve Église), ordered by Divisional Headquarters on 25 August and reported ready for 1 September. It displayed destructors/incinerators, latrine types (trench, deep trench, biscuit-box fly-proof, oil-can), grease traps, sump holes, urine disposal pits, urinals and fly-proof meat and food boxes — a ground plan with a numbered key (I–XVII) is drawn on pp. 7–8, and two appendix drawings (field incinerator, fly-proof latrine) follow on pp. 9–10.
- **Incinerators:** the Horsfall incinerator was discussed at a II Corps D.A.D.M.S. meeting on 7 August (considered slow; the brick one preferred); a new brick incinerator built at Aldershot was "burned out" on 19 August and found "excellent, much greater capacity than anticipated". A third destructor was completed at the 2nd hut area on 27 August and a fourth was under way.
- **Named people:** Gen. Currie (visited 30 August about the interruption of the sanitary police); J. M. Osborn, Acting Q.M. Sergeant (suggested the field incinerator design); Major Woodhouse throughout.
- **Disease:** measles contacts in the 10th Battalion — four huts disinfected (10 August). No other disease is recorded this month.
- **Casualties:** none of the section's own men are recorded killed or wounded.
- **Friction with other units:** two members of the section were interrupted in their duties at 2nd Brigade Headquarters (26 August); the R.E.H. (Royal Engineers? — [uncertain] expansion) repeatedly fouled ground around camp and failed to decant faeces before bringing them to the incinerator, and its men were caught fouling ground on 28 August.
- **Movements:** the section remained at Nieppe all month.
- The contents page states "Diary Text 6 Pages" but the volume in fact contains 8 diary-text pages (pp. 3–8) plus the two appendix drawings (pp. 9–10).
