## Transcription conventions
- Faithful to the original: spelling, punctuation, abbreviations and capitalisation as written. The diary is handwritten throughout on Army Form C. 2118, "WAR DIARY or INTELLIGENCE SUMMARY" (the words "INTELLIGENCE SUMMARY" are struck through in the printed heading), in columns: Place | Date | Hour | Summary of Events and Information | Remarks and references to Appendices.
- `[uncertain]` marks a word or name that could not be read with confidence — never guessed.
- Text struck through in the original is shown as `[struck through in original: "..."]`.
- Each diary-text page is headed "Original" (handwritten) and bears the oval stamp "1st CANADIAN DIVISION / SANITARY SECTION".
- No map coordinates (latitude/longitude) are given in the diary. The Section was based at Ariane throughout May 1917, working across the 1st Canadian Division area around Arras (Thelus, Bois Carré, Nine Elms, Fond de Vase, Bunechaut Farm, Ecoivres, Camblain l'Abbé, Mont St. Eloi).
- Abbreviations used as written: A.D.M.S. = Assistant Director Medical Services; D.D.M.S. = Deputy Director Medical Services; A.A. & Q.M.G. = Assistant Adjutant & Quartermaster General; D.A.D.M.S. = Deputy Assistant Director Medical Services; D.A.A. & Q.M.G. = Deputy Assistant Adjutant & Quartermaster General; D.A.C. = Divisional Ammunition Column; Bgde = Brigade; Divn = Division; M.O. = Medical Officer; O.C. = Officer Commanding; S.O.S. = struck off strength; C.A.M.C. = Canadian Army Medical Corps.

| Place | Date | Hour | Summary of Events and Information | Remarks |

This matter was reported to A.A. & Q.M.G. | W. L. |

| Place | Date | Hour | Summary of Events and Information | Remarks |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| ARIANE | May 1917 2 (Contd.) | | The bodies of two Germans were found and the Camp Commandant of area notified. The Artillery position on the slope of the hill is in fairly good shape. The 13th Machine Gun Co. lines very dirty. THELUS and BOIS CARRÉ heavily shelled. | W. L. |
| | | p.m. | Rode through Bunechaut Camp Area. Some improvement has been made in consequence of adverse report sent in yesterday. Sent report to A.D.M.S. regarding Condition of latrines in 1st Cdn Divnl area. | W. L. |
| | 3rd | | Fine - Bright. | |
| | | a.m. | With Col R. [uncertain] Simpson C.A.M.C. and Col. Templeton D.A.D.M.S. left at 5.30 a.m. for the advanced dressing stations and Regimental aid posts. Wounded began to come in fairly early, met many walking cases on our way up. Owing to the haze very little could be seen from the Ridge. Returned about 9 a.m. and reported to Col Ford. A.D.M.S. that wounded were being brought out from the front line satisfactorily. Assisted at main dressing Station. Col Ross and Captain Gibson, Cdn Corps visited office. The wounded practically all cleared before 1 p.m. | W. L. |

## Notes — May 1917
- The 1st Canadian Division was relieved in the Arras sector during the month: the 1st Canadian Brigade was relieved by troops of the 5th Division on the night of 4/5 May, and the Section's work shifted to inspecting the areas the Division was handing over and the new 5th Division sub-areas.
- 3 May: the Section assisted at the main dressing station as wounded came in from the front (the aftermath of the Arras fighting); the wounded were "practically all cleared before 1 p.m." A dead German was found unburied on 1 May and two more on 2 May; burial was arranged through the Camp Commandant.
- 17 May: King Albert of Belgium and Field Marshal Sir Douglas Haig visited the area.
- Personnel: Pte Beckerson returned from hospital (5 May); Pte Elliot transferred to 1st Cdn Heavy Battery in exchange for Pte Judge (28 May), Judge being taken before Col. Ross, D.D.M.S. Canadian Corps.
- Named people: Sgt Williams; Staff Sgt. Kitteringham; Col. R. [uncertain] Simpson C.A.M.C.; Col. Templeton, D.A.D.M.S.; Col. Ford, A.D.M.S. (Col. F. S. L. Ford); Lt-Col. C. P. Templeton, D.A.D.M.S.; Col. Ross, D.D.M.S. Canadian Corps; Captain Gibson, Canadian Corps; Major Rankin, C.A.M.C., Canadian Corps; Captain McDonald, O.C. 3rd Cdn Sanitary Section; Captain Tennant, Camp Commandant; Captain Finlay, 5th Battalion; Captain Duguid, 15th Battalion, Town Major of Mont St. Eloi; Lt-Col. Scott, 14th Field Ambulance; Major Wake, D.A.A.G.; Captain O'Hagan, M.O. 1st Cdn Divisional Train; Corporal Blondel; Captain Johnson, 5th Divisional Train; Captain Richardson, O.C. 2nd Canadian Sanitary Section; Pte Beckerson; Pte Elliot; Pte Judge.
- The Section's Ford ambulance car was in the workshop for most of the month (reported still unrepaired 13, 19 and 24 May; returned 28 May).
- No appendices are listed or included for May. No casualties of the Section itself are recorded.
