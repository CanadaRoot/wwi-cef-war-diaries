## Notes — October 1917
- Captain A. B. Chapman kept the diary 1–10 October while Captain Lomas was on leave; Lomas resumed duties on 11 October and Chapman returned to the 3rd Canadian Field Ambulance.
- Disease: a case of diphtheria at Lieres was investigated on 7 October (followed up 10 October) and a case of typhoid at Auchel on 18 October.
- The Section left the Bruay area for the Ypres salient: on 21 October it moved with the 3rd Canadian Field Ambulance to the Busnes area, then via Steenbecque to Oxelaere and Eecke (22–23 October). It was to occupy the Watou area, but the New Zealand Sanitary Section was found still in occupation there (24 October), so the Section was ordered to remain at Eecke (27 October).
- On 29 October the Section was ordered to be divided into three squads, attached to brigade sub-staffs to instruct battalion sanitary squads; the squads were arranged by 31 October and the men drilled and route-marched while awaiting orders.
- Named people: Captain A. B. Chapman, C.A.M.C. (relieving O.C., 1–10 October); Captain A. J. Lomas, C.A.M.C. (O.C.); Captain W. C. Laidlaw (Corps Sanitary Officer, Canadian Corps); the Town Majors of the Bruay-area villages (names not given in the diary).
- No appendices are listed or included for October. No casualties of the Section itself are recorded.
