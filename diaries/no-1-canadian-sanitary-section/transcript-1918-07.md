## Notes — July 1918
- The section was based at Divion until 19 July, then moved to Arras on 20 July, relieving No. 5a Sanitary Section. The move followed a 10 p.m. order on 19 July.
- Area changes around Divion: No. 66 Sanitary Section took over part of the area (7–8 July); Barlin, Ruitz and Maisnil Ruitz came into the section's area (11 July); sub-sections were brought in from Ferfay, Burbure and Auchel (9 July).
- Arras work: the town's bucket-latrine system and many cellars full of old refuse made sanitation very labour-intensive; the section had six wagons for daily refuse collection but at first no fatigue parties, and had to rely on units in the town. Colonel Ross, D.O.M.S., called on the fatigue question on 23 July; at least 40 fatigue men were needed to keep the town sanitary. A 20-man fatigue party was drawn from a battalion on 28 July.
- Disease: further typhoid cases amongst civilians at La Comte (15 July), reported to First Army.
- 1 July is noted as "Dominion Day". The unit was paraded to the Gas depot for inspection of gas helmets on 28 July.
- "Helfort" (12 July, "Bois Helfort") is as written; it is the same word as the "Helfort" of 17 June and may be Helfaut. "Cupp…" (30 July) is as written and unreadable.
- No military casualties are recorded in July 1918. No appendices are listed or included.
- No dates are missing: 1–31 July are all covered.
