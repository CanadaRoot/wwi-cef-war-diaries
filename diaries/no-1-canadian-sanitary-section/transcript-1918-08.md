## Notes — August 1918
- The section left Arras on 11 August (the Battle of Amiens began 8 August) and moved with the Canadian Corps to the Amiens front: Boves (12 August), Beaucourt (18 August), Aubigny (25 August), then back to St Sauveur near Arras (29 August) to take over the area along the Cambrai Road.
- Arras work (1–10 August): two large brick incinerators built to replace corrugated-iron structures; a permanent 20-man fatigue party supplied; refuse collected by working parties, teams and wagons, the incinerator operated only after dark (permission later obtained to run it on dull days); excreta taken in special carts to dust pits on the outskirts.
- Boves (12–18 August): the town was filthy after evacuation, houses dirty and littered with civilian and military refuse; German prisoners and Chinese labourers were promised and reported for duty to clean houses and yards under the section's inspectors; over 150 houses were cleaned and disinfected and latrine accommodation provided; flies very prevalent and latrine accommodation practically nil at first (15 August).
- Beaucourt (19–23 August): the forward area was full of dead horses and dead Germans requiring burial; a working party buried some and the nearest units buried others; units cleaned their own lines but working parties were needed for outlying places; the section was to move with No. 3 Canadian Field Ambulance.
- Transport was a constant difficulty: no lorries were provided for the moves on 11, 12, 23 and 24 August, and the section borrowed lorries from nearby units, the British Siege Park and the relieving French Corps.
- "Longuez" (8 August) is as written and may be Longueau; "Agnez Dure" (11 August) is as written and probably Agnez-Duisans; "Dury" (9 August) is as written. "Mezieres" (22 August) is Mézières-en-Santerre; "Tillers" is as written.
- No military casualties of the section itself are recorded in August 1918, but dead British and German bodies and horses were found and buried in the forward area (19–22 and 30–31 August). No appendices are listed or included.
- No dates are missing: 1–31 August are all covered.
