## Notes — November 1917
- The Section moved repeatedly in November: Eecke → Watou (3 Nov, office at 33 Winnezeele Road), Watou → Brandhoek → Merville (12 Nov) → Oblinghem (13 Nov) → Hersin (14 Nov, with the 3rd Canadian Field Ambulance at the Convent) → Grand Servins (25 Nov) → Ablain St Nazaire (25–26 Nov). Subsections were detached to Winnezeele (4 Nov) and to the 9th Canadian Field Ambulance at Lunar Farm (5 Nov).
- 23 November: the Section's billets at Hersin were struck by shell during a day of hostile shelling — 8 men killed and 30 wounded. The diary does not name the dead or wounded. The survivors were moved by ambulance to No. 4 Canadian Field Ambulance at Grand Servins; equipment was recovered from Hersin the next day.
- Pte McCoy returned from hospital on 24 November, and a summary of evidence concerning him was taken on 26 November (he had been tried and remanded for Field General Court Martial in September).
- Named people: Captain A. J. Lomas, C.A.M.C. (O.C.); the O.C. of the New Zealand Sanitary Section (name not given); Sgt Byrne; Robertson; Pte McCoy.
- No appendices are listed or included for November.
