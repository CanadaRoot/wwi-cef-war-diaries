## Notes — March 1917
- No entries are recorded for 3, 4, 6, 7, 9, 10, 11, 12 or 13 March. Lawson handed over to Capt. McAlpine (3rd 7th Ambulance) on 2 March and went on leave; he returned and took over command again on 14 March. The 5 and 8 March entries (takeovers from the 4th Sanitary Section, 2nd Division) appear to have been entered after his return.
- 22 March: the section moved to Ecoivres. Unit locations are given as: Ecoivres — [uncertain] officer, 23 other ranks including attached men, workshop etc.; Camblain l'Abbé 6; Estrée Cauchie 3; Cambligneul 1.
- 27 March: considerable enemy artillery activity; many dead horses were noticed on the road. An automatic chlorinating system was in operation at the Ecoivres pumping station, with a special man in charge; water in the tanks at Ecoivres and Bois de Bray was repeatedly tested and showed free chlorine at each test.
- 29 March: at a meeting of Divisional Sanitary Officers, Major Rankin showed a map of the proposed Canadian Corps Sanitary area — much larger than the present one. Two sanitary sections would have charge of the front line and two of the back area: the 1st and 3rd Sanitary Sections to look after the front, the 2nd and 4th the rear.
- Named people: Capt. W. C. Lawson (O.C.); Capt. McAlpine (3rd 7th Ambulance); Lt. Col. Wright; Lt. Col. Fraser; Major Rankin; Q/Sergt. Ferris.
- No casualties (killed/wounded) are recorded in March 1917.
- No appendices are listed or included for March.
