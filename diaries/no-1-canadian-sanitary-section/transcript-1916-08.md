## Notes — August 1916
- No entries are recorded for 6, 20 or 27 August; the date of the final entry on page 5 is overwritten and [uncertain] (reads as 26).
- 8 August: a gas alarm at 10.30 p.m.; all men fell in with gas helmets. Capt. Lawson took over the Convalescent Company from Lt. Keen.
- 12–13 August: the unit moved camp to Nadarque [as read], the lorry party arriving 1.30 p.m. on the 12th and the remainder at 1 p.m. on the 13th.
- 15 August: drinking water plentiful except in the area occupied by 1st Brigade, where it was obtained from Watten.
- 16 August: some shelling during the day; Capt. Richardson (D.A.C.) took over as M.O. from Major Rantin [as read].
- 26 August [uncertain date]: the lorry and Foden left in charge of Capt. Leonard (2nd 7th Ambulance); the area was handed over to the 2nd Division Sanitary Section (Capt. Romer).
- 28–29 August: the unit marched out at 3.15 a.m. on the 28th, entrained at Arduick [as read], detrained at Auxi-le-Château and marched to Longvillers; on the 29th it moved on to Canaples. Total strength of the Sanitary Section and attached men: 1 officer, 40 other ranks.
- 31 August: Lawson rode over to the new area the section was taking over from the 2nd Australian Division.
- Named people: Capt. W. C. Lawson (O.C.); Lt. Keen; Capt. Richardson; Major Rantin [as read]; Capt. Leonard; Capt. Romer (2nd Div Sanitary Section); Sergt. Logan; S. L. Beckerson [as read].
- No casualties (killed/wounded) are recorded in August 1916; one man fell out on the 28 August march and was absent at roll call, and a missing man was picked up on the 29th.
- No appendices are listed or included for August.
