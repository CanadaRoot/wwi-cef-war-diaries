## Notes — February 1919
- The unit remained at Huldenburg, Belgium, for the whole month, still attached to the 4th Canadian Division (4 February).
- Disease: cases of measles investigated on 1 and 21 February; a case of diphtheria investigated on 2 February. The A.O.M.S. was called on 24 February about infectious disease and the erection of disinfectors.
- No signature appears at the foot of the 1–17 February page; only the final page (18–28 February) is signed by Major Chapman — the last entry of the diary, which ends on 28 February 1919.
- No military casualties are recorded in February 1919. No appendices are listed or included.
- No dates are missing: 1–28 February are all covered.
