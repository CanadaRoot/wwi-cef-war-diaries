## Notes — September 1916
- Every day from 1 to 30 September is covered; no dates are missing.
- 1 September: the unit marched from Canaples and took over billets at Rubempré from the 1st Australian Division; the lorry broke down (short circuit) en route.
- 4 September: the section moved to Albert, taking over billets from the 4th Australian Division; camp grounds and billets were very dirty, with many flies.
- 7 September: heavy shelling at night at Albert.
- 9 September: a case of dysentery was reported in West Indian troops; they were moved into isolated billets and special latrines were built.
- 11 September: the section moved to Val de Maison; the 2nd Division Sanitary Section took over at Albert.
- 18 September: the 1st Canadian Sanitary Section was ordered to look after the reserve area while the 2nd Division was away.
- 30 September: the bath shower attachment on the Foden lorry went into operation — "Very successful — Bathed 50 men of Div Train P.M." — and the section moved to Warloy, relieving the 2nd Division Sanitary Section and taking over from the 3rd Division Sanitary Section.
- Named people: Capt. W. C. Lawson (O.C.); Major Hodgins (C.E.).
- No casualties (killed/wounded) are recorded in September 1916.
- No appendices are listed or included for September.
