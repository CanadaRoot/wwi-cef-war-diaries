- Faithful to the original: spelling, punctuation, abbreviations and capitalisation as written. The diary is handwritten throughout on Army Form C. 2118, "WAR DIARY or INTELLIGENCE SUMMARY" (the words "INTELLIGENCE SUMMARY" are struck through in the printed heading), in columns: Place | Date | Hour | Summary of Events and Information | Remarks and references to Appendices.
- `[uncertain]` marks a word or name that could not be read with confidence — never guessed.
- Text struck through in the original is shown as `[struck through in original: "..."]`.
- Each diary-text page is headed "Original" (handwritten) and bears the oval stamp "1st CANADIAN DIVISION / SANITARY SECTION".
- No map coordinates (latitude/longitude) are given in the diary.
- Abbreviations used as written: A.D.M.S. = Assistant Director Medical Services; D.D.M.S. = Deputy Director Medical Services; A.P.M. = Assistant Provost Marshal; F.G.C.M. = Field General Court Martial; D.F.P.S. = [uncertain — as written]; Fld Ambulance = Field Ambulance; C.A.M.C. = Canadian Army Medical Corps.

## Notes — December 1917
- The Section remained based at Ablain St Nazaire throughout December, working the Arras–Lens area villages (Souchez, Carency, Aix Noulette, Angres, Liévin, Sains-en-Gohelle, Bouvigny, Boyeffles, Givenchy).
- Pte McCoy: his Field General Court Martial was held on 14 December and the sentence was promulgated on 19 December. (He had been arrested in September for being overdue from leave and remanded for court martial.)
- 12 December: diphtheria contacts at Mont St Eloy were swabbed.
- 25 December: Christmas Day — fine, with snow; no work is recorded.
- 30 December: the diary notes an appendix to the diary was completed, but no appendix pages are among the December page images in the LAC set.
- Named people: Captain A. J. Lomas, C.A.M.C. (O.C.); Pte McCoy; the Town Majors of the Ablain-area villages (names not given in the diary).
- No casualties of the Section itself are recorded in December.
