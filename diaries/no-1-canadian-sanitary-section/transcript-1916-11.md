## Notes — November 1916
- No entries are recorded for 15–25 November: Lawson left for Boulogne on leave on 14 November (with Col. Ross) and Capt. Foster (1st 7th Ambulance) acted in command; Lawson took over again on 26 November. The diary contains no entries written by Foster during the leave.
- 5 November: at Houdain, the Divisional laundry's Thresh disinfectors were overworked and there was a complaint that not all lice were killed.
- 11 November: a trench warfare and gas school expected 600 men; Lawson suggested sanitary arrangements.
- 27 November: temporary [uncertain] men were placed on train water points to examine carts and see that all water is chlorinated — two men each at Mount St Eloy, Villiers au Bois, Gouy Servins, Camblain l'Abbé and Maisnil Bouche [as read], and one at Gauchin Legal.
- Named people: Capt. W. C. Lawson (O.C.); Capt. Foster (1st 7th Ambulance); Col. Ross.
- No casualties (killed/wounded) are recorded in November 1916.
- No appendices are listed or included for November.
