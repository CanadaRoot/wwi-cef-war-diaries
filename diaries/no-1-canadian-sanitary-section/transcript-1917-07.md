- Faithful to the original: spelling, punctuation, abbreviations and capitalisation as written. The diary is handwritten throughout on Army Form C. 2118, "WAR DIARY or INTELLIGENCE SUMMARY" (the words "INTELLIGENCE SUMMARY" are struck through in the printed heading), in columns: Place | Date | Hour | Summary of Events and Information | Remarks and references to Appendices.
- `[uncertain]` marks a word or name that could not be read with confidence — never guessed.
- Text struck through in the original is shown as `[struck through in original: "..."]`.
- Each diary-text page is headed "Original" (handwritten) and bears the oval stamp "1st CANADIAN DIVISION / SANITARY SECTION".

## Notes — July 1917
- The Section moved base on 17 July: from Ecurie Station to Bracquemont, opening its office there at 3 p.m. The new area covered Bracquemont, Hallecourt, Barlin, Houchin, Maroc, Les Brebis, Grenay, Ruitz and Maisnil Ruitz.
- A/Cpl Bond: under arrest from 2 July for a civil offence; tried by Field General Court Martial at Camblain l'Abbé on 20 July (adjourned to Tuesday 24 July); convicted on 24 July of an offence against a civilian and sentenced to six months' imprisonment with hard labour; the sentence was promulgated on parade on 27 July. Major Langley, 1st Cdn Divisional Signal Company, defended him.
- 11 July: H.M. King George inspected a battalion near the Section's lines.
- 14 July: a meeting at Bethune of the D.D.M.S. Corps, A.D.M.S. of Divisions and O.C.s of Sanitary Sections discussed sanitation and administration of Sanitary Sections; recommendations were to be incorporated in Army Orders.
- Personnel: Q.M.S. Osborn left for England on 1 July to take a commission in the Canadian Engineers; A/Cpl Calvert went on leave on 12 July (Pte [uncertain] Martin replacing him); Captain A. J. Lomas, C.A.M.C., reported from Base on 26 July on Col. Ross's instructions; the Ford ambulance car was exchanged for an Austin car on 25 July on A.D.M.S. instructions.
- Named people: Q.M.S. Osborn; A/Cpl Bond; Pte Calvert; Lt-Col. Pinhale [uncertain], 1st Cdn D.A.C.; Major Vanderslyp [uncertain], D.A.A.G. 1st Cdn Division; Lt-Col. Ross, D.D.M.S. Canadian Corps; Captain Duguid, Town Major of Bracquemont; Major McMillan, Camp Commandant, Canadian Corps; Lt-Col. Betty [uncertain]; Lt Carson [uncertain], Town Major; Captain A. J.
