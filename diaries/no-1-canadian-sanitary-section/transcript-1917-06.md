- Faithful to the original: spelling, punctuation, abbreviations and capitalisation as written. The diary is handwritten throughout on Army Form C. 2118, "WAR DIARY or INTELLIGENCE SUMMARY" (the words "INTELLIGENCE SUMMARY" are struck through in the printed heading), in columns: Place | Date | Hour | Summary of Events and Information | Remarks and references to Appendices.

## Notes — June 1917
- The Section's base moved during the month: the Place column changes from ARIANE to Ecurie Station in the course of 15 June.
- 4 June: Colonel F. S. L. Ford, A.D.M.S. 1st Canadian Division, was "badly wounded last night". By 12 June he was "much improved"; Colonel Wright was acting A.D.M.S. by 25 June.
- 5 June: the area was bombed by aeroplanes at 10 p.m.
- 10 and 17 June: a platoon of a Labour Company, then a labour party of 40 men, was allotted to the Section for cleaning the Ecoivres area; the party was still at work on 26 June and the sanitary condition of the village had "much improved".
- The Ford ambulance car broke down on 5 June (lack of oil and a loose bearing) and was sent to Cambligneul for repairs on 19 June.
- Personnel: Cpl Blondel and two men went on leave to Paris (30 June); Pte Powell reported to 1st Cdn D.S. Column and was replaced by Pte Fleming (30 June); Pte Calvert was placed in charge of a water point (26 June).
- Named people: Staff Sgt. Kitteringham; Sgt Williams; Major Gibson; Lt-Col. Templeton, D.A.D.M.S.; Col. F. S. L. Ford, A.D.M.S.; Major Rankin, C.A.M.C., Canadian Corps; Col. Ross, D.D.M.S. Canadian Corps; Major Wollard, C.A.M.C., M.O. 1st Cdn D.A.C.; Major Ryan (1st Cdn Divisional Baths); Major Bennett; Major Spence, Town Major of Cambligneul; Cpl Blondel; Sgt Orr... [uncertain]; A/Cpl Byrne; Captain Richardson, O.C. 2nd Canadian Sanitary Section; Colonel Wright, A.D.M.S.; Pte Calvert; Pte Powell; Pte Fleming; the M.O. of 179 Bde R.F.A. (name not given).
