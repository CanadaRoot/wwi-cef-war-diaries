## Transcription conventions
- Faithful to the original: spelling, punctuation, abbreviations and capitalisation as written. The diary is handwritten throughout on Army Form C. 2118, "WAR DIARY or INTELLIGENCE SUMMARY" (the words "INTELLIGENCE SUMMARY" are struck through in the printed heading on most pages), in columns: Place | Date | Hour | Summary of Events and Information | Remarks and references to Appendices.
- `[uncertain]` marks a word or name that could not be read with confidence — never guessed.
- Text struck through in the original is shown as `[struck through in original: "..."]`.
- Each diary-text page is headed "Original" (handwritten) and bears the oval stamp "1st CANADIAN DIVISION / SANITARY SECTION".
- No map coordinates (latitude/longitude) are given in the diary.
- Abbreviations used as written: A.D.M.S. = Assistant Director Medical Services; D.D.M.S. = Deputy Director Medical Services; Bgde = Brigade; Divn = Division; M.O. = Medical Officer; O.C. = Officer Commanding; R.C.H.A. = Royal Canadian Horse Artillery; F.G.C.M. = Field General Court Martial; C.A.M.C. = Canadian Army Medical Corps.

## Notes — August 1917
- Command changed on 1 August: Captain W. C. Laidlaw left to become Corps Sanitary Officer, Canadian Corps; Captain A. J. Lomas took command of the Section. The handwriting changes with the new commanding officer, and the Remarks initials change from "W. L." to "A.J.L."
- A/Cpl Bond: the Field General Court Martial proceedings against him (he had been convicted on 24 July and sentenced to six months' imprisonment with hard labour) were quashed, and he returned to the Unit on 12 August.
- The Section moved base on 26 August: from Bracquemont to Bruay, covering a new area including Amettes, Ames, Ferfay, Calonne, Allouagne, La Pugnoy, Auchel, Raimbert and Marles. A proposed move on 21 August had its movement order cancelled on 22 August.
- The lorry was exchanged for a 10 cwt Ford box car on 11–12 August.
- Two men temporarily attached from the 102nd Canadian Battalion were returned to their unit on 23 August.
- Named people: Captain W. C. Laidlaw, C.A.M.C. (outgoing O.C., now Corps Sanitary Officer); Captain A. J. Lomas, C.A.M.C. (incoming O.C.); A/Cpl Bond; Sgt Williams; the Town Majors of Bracquemont, Les Brebis, Ruitz, Maisnil Ruitz, Houchin, Hallecourt, Allouagne and La Pugnoy (names not given in the diary).
- No appendices are listed or included for August. No casualties of the Section itself are recorded.
