- Faithful to the original: spelling, punctuation, abbreviations and capitalisation as written (the diary is handwritten on Army Form C. 2118 throughout).
- `[uncertain]` marks a word or name that could not be read with confidence — never guessed.
- Text struck through in the original is shown as `[struck through in original: "..."]`.
- The diary-text pages are Army Form C. 2118, "WAR DIARY or INTELLIGENCE SUMMARY" (the "INTELLIGENCE SUMMARY" heading is struck through on every page), handwritten in columns: Place | Date | Hour | Summary of Events and Information | Remarks and references to Appendices. ## Notes — December 1918
- The march to the Rhine began on 12 December: Ville sur Haine to Fayt (12 December), Gorsselies (13 December), Auvelais (14–15 December), St Germain (16–17 December), Wasseiges (18 December), where the section parked for the rest of the month.
- The unit was attached to the 13th Canadian Field Ambulance for the march to the Rhine (12 December); it had earlier been attached to the 4th Canadian Division for the same march (14 November).
- The Ford car was again a casualty on 5 December (it had been a casualty in May 1918 as well).
- The 1–11 December page bears an oval stamp "CAMP COMMANDANT / 201 / 10 JAN 1919 / CANADIAN CORPS".
- The entries for 28–31 December record only the weather, with no other summary.
- No military casualties are recorded in December 1918. No appendices are listed or included.
- No dates are missing: 1–31 December are all covered.
