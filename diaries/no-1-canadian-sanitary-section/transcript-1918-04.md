- Faithful to the original: spelling, punctuation, abbreviations and capitalisation as written (the diary is handwritten on Army Form C. 2118 throughout).
- `[uncertain]` marks a word or name that could not be read with confidence — never guessed.
- Text struck through in the original is shown as `[struck through in original: "..."]`.
- The diary-text pages are Army Form C. 2118, "WAR DIARY or INTELLIGENCE SUMMARY" (the "INTELLIGENCE SUMMARY" heading is struck through on every page), handwritten in columns: Place | Date | Hour | Summary of Events and Information | Remarks and references to Appendices. - Each day's entry begins with a check mark (✓) in the original, shown here at the start of the Summary.
- No map coordinates (latitude/longitude) are given in the diary; the unit was based at Bruay until 22 April, then at Divion.
- Place names are as written; the same places recur with variant spellings.

## Notes — April 1918
- The section was based at Bruay until 22 April, when it moved to Divion after Bruay was shelled.
- Shelling: Bruay was shelled on 20 April and again on 21 April, with "several civilian casualties near billet" on the 21st — the first casualties recorded in this diary. The move to Divion followed on 22 April.
- Main work: inspecting camps and billets; completing incinerators (Calonne, Auchel); a Solder recovery plant completed at Lozinghem (8 April); 16 latrines erected at the evacuation camp by the workshop staff (14 April); six men placed at the camp at Marles to construct latrines (8 April); water and sanitary arrangements discussed with the A.D.M.S. (26–27 April).
- The place "Burton" (3, 5, 15, 25, 27, 29 April) was first read as "Burlon" but the same place reads clearly as "Burton" in May (9 and 27 May), so "Burton" is used here, still flagged [uncertain]; "Le Brun" (17 April) is as written and unclear — it follows a visit to the 4th Sanitary Section.
- No military casualties are recorded in April 1918. No appendices are listed or included.
- No dates are missing: 1–30 April are all covered.
