## Transcription conventions
- Faithful to the original: spelling, punctuation, abbreviations and capitalisation as written.
- `[uncertain]` marks a word or name that could not be read with confidence — never guessed.
- The diary-text pages are Army Form C. 2118, "WAR DIARY or INTELLIGENCE SUMMARY", typed in columns: Place | Date | Hour | Summary of Events and Information | (Weather report) | Remarks and references to Appendices. The "Hour" column is struck through on the form and a "(Weather report)" column is used instead; no hour is recorded for individual entries.
- The Remarks column carries repeated handwritten initials on each row (illegible); they are not transcribed.
- No map coordinates (latitude/longitude) are given in the diary; places are recorded as written.
- "do." in the Place column means "ditto" (same place as the entry above).

## Page 86 — Diary text, 1–13 January 1918 (January 1918, p. 2)

Army Form C. 2118. Heading: "WAR DIARY or INTELLIGENCE SUMMARY of the 185th Canadian Infantry Battalion (Cape Breton Highlanders) Volume XI". Handwritten "Original" at top left; Orderly Room stamp dated FEB 1918 (partly legible).

### 1 January 1918 — WITLEY

> Holiday Holiday.

Weather: Cold – Fair.

### 2 January 1918 — WITLEY

> BRIGADE Route March. Battalion passed starting point at 8.28 a.m. Strength: Officers – 23; Other Ranks – 611. Half holiday for sports.

Weather: Foggy.

### 3 January 1918 — WITLEY

> Six hours individual training.

Weather: Fine – Cold.

### 4 January 1918 — WITLEY

> Six hours individual training.

Weather: Fine – Cold.

### 5 January 1918 — WITLEY

> Four hours individual training. Half holiday.

Weather: Fair.

### 6 January 1918 — WITLEY

> Church Parade [uncertain] at 9.00 [uncertain] Day of Prayer as per King's Proclamation.

Weather: Fine.

### 7 January 1918 — WITLEY

> Platoon Training for Efficiency Competition. Commanding Officer's Inspection of Battalion at 10.30 a.m. Full Battalion Parade in Heavy Marching Order.

Weather: Fair.

### 8 January 1918 — WITLEY

> Six hours individual training.

Weather: Fine – Very Cold.

### 9 January 1918 — WITLEY

> BRIGADE Route March. Battalion passed starting point at 8.27 a.m. Strength: Officers – 25; Other Ranks – 624. Half holiday for sports.

Weather: Fine – Very Cold.

### 10 January 1918 — WITLEY

> Six hours Platoon Training for Efficiency Competition. Battalion Pay Day.

Weather: Fine – Warmer.

### 11 January 1918 — WITLEY

> Six hours Platoon Training for Efficiency Competition.

Weather: Fine – Warmer.

### 12 January 1918 — WITLEY

> Muster Parade 8.30 a.m. Three hours individual Training. 3 Officers and 74 Other Ranks proceed to Mytchett.

Weather: Fine.

### 13 January 1918 — WITLEY

> Church Parade at 9.00 a.m. in Men's Mess Room. Inspection by Commanding Officer at 10.15 am. 21 Other Ranks proceed to Mytchett: 3 Officers and 74 Other Ranks return from Mytchett.

Weather: Fine.

## Page 87 — Diary text, 14–23 January 1918 (January 1918, p. 3)

Heading: "WAR DIARY or INTELLIGENCE SUMMARY of the 185th Canadian Infantry Battalion (Cape Breton Highlanders) Volume XI".

### 14 January 1918 — WITLEY

> Two hundred Other Ranks on fatigue filling in Brigade excavations. Remainder of Battalion on Brigade duties.

Weather: Fine – Three inches snow.

### 15 January 1918 — WITLEY

> Battalion on Brigade duties and filling excavations.

Weather: Rained during night, snow gone – Rained all day.

### 16 January 1918 — WITLEY

> BRIGADE Route March. Battalion passed starting point at 8.25 a.m. Strength: Officers – 13; Other Ranks – 399. 10 Officers and 7 Other Ranks proceed to Mytchett. Half holiday.

Weather: Snowy.

### 17 January 1918 — WITLEY

> Battalion on Brigade duties and filling in excavations. Inspection by Major-General Howard postponed to 18-1-18.

Weather: Fine.

### 18 January 1918 — WITLEY

> Battalion inspected at work by Major-General Howard. Commanding Officer proceeds to France on command for instruction, and Major LeGallais assumes command of Battalion.

Weather: Fine.

### 19 January 1918 — WITLEY

> Battalion on Brigade duties and filling excavations. Half holiday.

Weather: Rainy.

### 20 January 1918 — WITLEY

> Church Parade in Men's Mess Room at 9.00 a.m. Inspection by Commanding Officer at 10.15 a.m.

Weather: Rainy.

### 21 January 1918 — WITLEY

> Six hours individual training.

Weather: Fine.

### 22 January 1918 — WITLEY

> Six hours individual training.

Weather: Fine.

### 23 January 1918 — WITLEY

> BRIGADE Route March. Battalion passed starting point at 8.20 a.m. Strength: Officers – 13; Other Ranks – 544. 10 Officers and 7 Other Ranks return from Mytchett. Half Holiday.

Weather: Fine.
