## Transcription conventions
- Faithful to the original: spelling, punctuation, abbreviations and capitalisation as written.
- `[uncertain]` marks a word or name that could not be read with confidence — never guessed.
- The diary-text pages are Army Form C. 2118, "WAR DIARY or INTELLIGENCE SUMMARY", typed in columns: Place | Date | Hour | Summary of Events and Information | (Weather report) | Remarks and references to Appendices. The "Hour" column is struck through on the form and a "(Weather report)" column is used instead; no hour is recorded for individual entries.
- The Remarks column carries repeated handwritten initials on each row (illegible); they are not transcribed.
- No map coordinates (latitude/longitude) are given in the diary; places are recorded as written.
- "do." in the Place column means "ditto" (same place as the entry above).

## Page 82 — Diary text, 1–13 December 1917 (December 1917, p. 2)

Army Form C. 2118. Heading: "WAR DIARY or INTELLIGENCE SUMMARY of the 185th Canadian Infantry Battalion. (Cape Breton Highlanders) Volume X". Orderly Room stamp dated JAN 1 1918.

### 1 December 1917 — WITLEY

> Inspection by Lord Derby. Battalion training on 104th Battalion Parade Ground.

Weather: Misty.

### 2 December 1917 — WITLEY

> Church Parade in Men's Mess Rooms. Inspection of lines by C.O.

Weather: Fine.

### 3 December 1917 — WITLEY

> Six hours individual training. Voting in Dominion Election commenced.

Weather: Fine.

### 4 December 1917 — WITLEY

> Training carried out in accordance with special syllabus of individual training in lieu of route March. Voting continued.

Weather: Fine.

### 5 December 1917 — WITLEY

> Four hours in Musketry. Judging distance, Fire Orders, Fire Discipline and Field Practice. Half Holiday for sports.

Weather: Fine.

### 6 December 1917 — WITLEY

> Six hours individual training.

Weather: Fine.

### 7 December 1917 — WITLEY

> Six hours individual training.

Weather: Misty.

### 8 December 1917 — WITLEY

> Four hours individual training. Half Holiday.

Weather: Fine.

### 9 December 1917 — WITLEY

> Church Parade in Men's Mess Room. Inspection by C.O. at 10.15 a.m.

Weather: Raining.

### 10 December 1917 — WITLEY

> Six hours individual training. 14 officers proceeded to Longmoor Range to see a demonstration of Machine gun Barrage Fire. Concert by London Concert Party in Men's Mess at 7 p.m.

Weather: Raining.

### 11 December 1917 — WITLEY

> BRIGADE Route March. Battalion passed starting point at 9.23 a.m. Strength Officers: 22 Other ranks 670 men.

Weather: Fine.

### 12 December 1917 — WITLEY

> Four hours individual training. Half Holiday for Sports.

Weather: Fine.

### 13 December 1917 — WITLEY

> Six hours individual training.

Weather: Misty.

(continued)

### 14 December 1917 — WITLEY

> Six hours individual training.

Weather: Misty.

> Muster Parade at 7.45 a.m. Four hours individual training. Half Holiday.

### 16 December 1917 — WITLEY

> Church Parade in Men's Mess. C.O.'s Inspection.

Weather: Fair.

> 120 Other ranks passing through Rifle Grenade tests. Six hours individual training.

### 18 December 1917 — WITLEY

> Four hours individual training. Proposed route march cancelled. Training in Afternoon. 2 hours individual training.

## Page 84 — Diary text, 28–31 December 1917 (December 1917, p. 4)

### 28 December 1917 — WITLEY (continued)

> (continued) Witley village; thence to camp.

Weather: Snowy.

### 29 December 1917 — WITLEY

> Eight Platoons proceed to Mytchett to fire Field Practice. Half Holiday.

Weather: Fine.

### 30 December 1917 — WITLEY

> Church Parade at 9 a.m. C.O.'s Inspection. Firing Party return from Mytchett.

Weather: Misty.

### 31 December 1917 — WITLEY

> The eight remaining platoons proceed to Mytchett. Party carrying out Rifle Grenade tests.

Weather: Misty.

Signed at foot of page: F. P. Day, Lieut. Col., Commanding 185th Canadian Infantry Bn. (Cape Breton Highlanders).
