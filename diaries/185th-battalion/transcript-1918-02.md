## Transcription conventions
- Faithful to the original: spelling, punctuation, abbreviations and capitalisation as written.
- `[uncertain]` marks a word or name that could not be read with confidence — never guessed.
- The diary-text pages are Army Form C. 2118, "WAR DIARY or INTELLIGENCE SUMMARY", typed in columns: Place | Date | Hour | Summary of Events and Information | (Weather report) | Remarks and references to Appendices. The "Hour" column is struck through on the form and a "(Weather report)" column is used instead; no hour is recorded for individual entries.
- The Remarks column carries repeated handwritten initials on each row (illegible); they are not transcribed.
- No map coordinates (latitude/longitude) are given in the diary; places are recorded as written.
- "do." in the Place column means "ditto" (same place as the entry above).

## Page 90 — Diary text, 1–12 February 1918 (February 1918, p. 2)

Army Form C. 2118. Heading: "WAR DIARY or INTELLIGENCE SUMMARY of the 185th Canadian Infantry Battalion (Cape Breton Highlanders) Volume XII/12". Orderly Room stamp dated FEB 28 1918.

### 1 February 1918 — WITLEY

> Six hours individual training. Lt. Col. F.P. Day returned from France.

Weather: Foggy.

### 2 February 1918 — WITLEY

> Four hours individual training. Half Holiday.

Weather: Misty.

### 3 February 1918 — WITLEY

> Church Parades at [uncertain] a.m. Men's Mess. Inspection of lines by C.O.

Weather: Misty.

### 4 February 1918 — WITLEY

> Six hours individual training. Training in huts at 10.00 a.m.

Weather: Raining.

### 5 February 1918 — WITLEY

> Six hours individual training.

Weather: Fair.

### 6 February 1918 — WITLEY

> Brigade Route March. Battalion passed starting point at 9.18 a.m. Half holiday.

Weather: Fine.

### 7 February 1918 — WITLEY

> Six hours individual training.

Weather: Fair.

### 8 February 1918 — WITLEY

> Battalion Route March and Scheme cancelled. Six hours individual training, in huts in the morning, as usual in afternoon.

Weather: Raining.

### 9 February 1918 — WITLEY

> Four hours individual training. Half holiday.

Weather: Fine.

### 10 February 1918 — WITLEY

> Church Parades at 9.00 a.m. in Men's Mess.

Weather: Fine.

### 11 February 1918 — WITLEY

> Duty Battalion. All available men on working parties filling excavations and repairing Marehill Ranges. Five Officers on Revolver Course.

Weather: Fine.

### 12 February 1918 — WITLEY

> All available men on working parties. Quartermaster ordered to turn in all Mobilization Stores by following Monday.

Weather: Fine.

## Page 91 — Diary text, 13–22 February 1918 (February 1918, p. 3)

Heading: "WAR DIARY or INTELLIGENCE SUMMARY of the 185th Cape Breton Highlanders. Volume II." — the volume number is misprinted; this is Volume XII.

### 13 February 1918 — WITLEY

> Order received to furnish reinforcements of 100 men each to the following Units in France: 25th Bn., 85th Bn., R.C.R. The remainder of the Battalion to be absorbed by the 17th Reserve, Bramshott.

Weather: Fine.

### 14 February 1918 — WITLEY

> Orders received authorizing despatch of four supernumerary captains to each of the following Units in France: 25th, 59th, 85th, R.C.R. Orders received re draft of six Officers to proceed to France as Lieutenants, two each to the 25th, 85th, and R.C.R.

Weather: Fine.

### 15 February 1918 — WITLEY

> Individual training carried on, also work at preparing the draft for Overseas.

Weather: Fine.

### 16 February 1918 — WITLEY

> Individual training carried on, also work at preparing the draft for Overseas.

Weather: Fine.

### 17 February 1918 — WITLEY

> Church Parades at 9.00 a.m. in Men's Mess. Farewell Sermon preached by Capt. A.J. McDonald. Order instructing six Officers to proceed Overseas cancelled.

Weather: Fine.

### 18 February 1918 — WITLEY

> Six hours individual training and fitting out draft. Orders received for the draft of six Officers to hold themselves in readiness to proceed overseas at any time after reveille 29-2-18.

Weather: Fine.

### 19 February 1918 — WITLEY

> Six hours individual training and fitting out draft.

Weather: Fine.

### 20 February 1918 — WITLEY

> Battalion Route March in morning. Battalion passed starting point at 8.30 a.m. Half holiday.

Weather: Rain.

### 21 February 1918 — WITLEY

> Six hours individual training and fitting out draft.

Weather: Fine.

### 22 February 1918 — WITLEY

> Six hours individual training. Orders received about 11 p.m. for Unit except personnel warned for draft and a rear party, to proceed to Bramshott tomorrow at 1 p.m., as the 185th Battalion was to be absorbed by the 17th Reserve Battalion.

Weather: Fine.

## Page 92 — Diary text, 23 February 1918 (February 1918, p. 4)

Heading: "WAR DIARY or INTELLIGENCE SUMMARY of the 185th Canadian Infantry Battalion (Cape Breton Highlanders) (Volume II)" — the volume number is misprinted; this is Volume XII.

### 23 February 1918 — WITLEY

> At 1 p.m. the Battalion moved out of camp and marched to Bramshott. The Commanding Officer and Quartermaster remain behind to hand over the lines. Major LeGallais remains to close up all accounts. Major Macdonald remains in charge of the various drafts for overseas.

Weather: [not recorded]

Signed at foot of page: F. P. Day, Lieut. Col., Commanding 185th Canadian Infantry Bn. (Cape Breton Highlanders).

---

**End of the diary.** This is the last page of the LAC record (page 92 of 92). The battalion was absorbed by the 17th Reserve Battalion at Bramshott on 23 February 1918.
