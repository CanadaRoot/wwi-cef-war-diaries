## Transcription conventions
- Faithful to the original: spelling, punctuation, abbreviations and capitalisation as written.
- `[uncertain]` marks a word or name that could not be read with confidence — never guessed.
- The diary-text pages are Army Form C. 2118, "WAR DIARY or INTELLIGENCE SUMMARY", typed in columns: Place | Date | Hour | Summary of Events and Information | (Weather report) | Remarks and references to Appendices. The "Hour" column is struck through on the form and a "(Weather report)" column is used instead; no hour is recorded for individual entries.
- The Remarks column carries repeated handwritten initials on each row (illegible); they are not transcribed.
- No map coordinates (latitude/longitude) are given in the diary; places are recorded as written.
- "do." in the Place column means "ditto" (same place as the entry above).

## Page 68 — Diary text, 1–10 October 1917 (October 1917, p. 2)

Army Form C. 2118. Heading: "WAR DIARY or INTELLIGENCE SUMMARY of the 185th Canadian Infantry Battalion (Cape Breton Highlanders) Volume VIII". Orderly Room stamp dated NOV 1 1917.

### 1 October 1917 — WITLEY

> Battalion proceeded to THURSLEY COMMON to carry out manoeuvres for Motion Picture purposes.

Weather: Fine.

### 2 October 1917 — WITLEY

> Battalion Route March. Starting point—Battalion Parade Ground. The Battalion marched off at 8 a.m. via the following route: road junction at Camp Post Office – cross-roads D 28 c 4.4 – Pt 235 – WITLEY VILLAGE – WORMLEY HILL – NORTH BRIDGE – CHIDDINGFOLD – STONEHURST FARM – Pt. 210 – Pt. 238 – HAMBLEDON – HYDESTILE – Pt 317 & Pt 281 – Pt 128 – Pt. 146 – Pt 266 – EASHING – Pt.219 – MILFORD STATION – Pt. 303 – Pt. 285 – Battalion Parade ground.

Weather: Fine.

### 3 October 1917 — WITLEY

> 4 hours individual training. Half-holiday.

Weather: Fine.

### 4 October 1917 — WITLEY

> Battalion Scheme for the day cancelled on account of Rain. (See Appendix "A"). Eight hours individual training carried on in huts.

Weather: Rain.

Remarks: See Apdx A

### 5 October 1917 — WITLEY

> Battalion Scheme (See Appendix "B") carried out.

Weather: Fine.

Remarks: See Apdx B

### 6 October 1917 — WITLEY

> Individual training for four hours. Lecture by Brigadier to Officers and Non-commissioned Officers.

Weather: Fine.

### 7 October 1917 — WITLEY

> Church Parade held in Men's Mess Room.

Weather: Rain.

### 8 October 1917 — WITLEY

> Training cut down to six hours per diem this date. Refresher training commenced in all subjects. Training is under Company system – each Company being self-supporting in regard to instruction. Rain in the afternoon caused the training to be done in huts.

Weather: Showery.

### 9 October 1917 — WITLEY

> A Battalion Route March was performed. The Battalion marched off at 8 a.m. from the Battalion Parade Ground via GODALMING (via PORTSMOUTH RD) – FARNCOMBE – then N.W. to Pt. 191 – Pt 333 – Pt 271 – Cross-roads at SHACKLEFORD Church – thence South to EASHING – MILFORD – MOUSEHILL – Battalion Parade Ground.

Weather: Fine.

### 10 October 1917 — WITLEY

> The row of huts in C 2 Lines next BURROUGH FARM road were evacuated for occupation be The 19th Canadian Machine Gun Company. Divisional Half Holiday – granted in order that Battalions might attend a deciding base-ball game.

Weather: Fine.

## Page 70 — Diary text, 24–31 October 1917 (October 1917, p. 4)

Heading: "WAR DIARY or INTELLIGENCE SUMMARY of the 185th Canadian Infantry Battalion (Cape Breton Highlanders) Volume VIII Page 3".

### 24 October 1917 — WITLEY

> Four hours individual training. Half-holiday. Rain.

Weather: Rain.

### 25 October 1917 — WITLEY

> The Battalion took part in the following Brigade Route March: Passing the starting point (Junction of PORTSMOUTH & BRIGADES ROADS "D" 28 a 4.2½) at 8.17 a.m. the following route was taken.—MILFORD – road through B of AMBERLEY FM – SOMERSET FARM – road junction immediately South of [uncertain] of GATWICK – SHACKLEFORD – Road running North-east immediately west of P of PRIOR'S WOOD – COMPTON – Road running through P of NEW POND to junction with PORTSMOUTH ROAD – thence South-west via that road through GODALMING to junction with HASLE-MERE road 550 yards North of [uncertain] of HIGHCROFT – WEBB ROAD.

Weather: dark & cloudy.

### 26 October 1917 — WITLEY

> Six hours individual training. Non-commissioned officers of the rank of Sergeants and upwards adopt the Hair Sporran and diced red and white hose-tops as the completion of their Highland Uniform for Church Parade and walking out.

Weather: Fine.

### 27 October 1917 — WITLEY

> Four hours individual training. Half-holiday.

Weather: Fine.

### 28 October 1917 — WITLEY

> Church Parade at WITLEY PARK.

Weather: Fine.

### 29 October 1917 — WITLEY

> Six hours individual training.

Weather: Fine.

### 30 October 1917 — WITLEY

> Five hours individual training. This training was partly carried on in huts on account of rain. At 11 a.m. the presentation of prizes won in the summer was carried out. This unit received four out of six prizes: namely for Best Platoon (Platoon No 14), Regimental Band, Transport Section and P.T. Class.

Weather: Fine.

### 31 October 1917 — WITLEY

> Four hours individual training. Half-Holiday.

Weather: Fine.

Signed at foot of page: [uncertain — signature], Lieut.-Col., Commanding 185th Canadian Infantry Bn. (Cape Breton Highlanders).
