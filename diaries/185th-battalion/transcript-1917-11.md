## Transcription conventions
- Faithful to the original: spelling, punctuation, abbreviations and capitalisation as written.
- `[uncertain]` marks a word or name that could not be read with confidence — never guessed.
- The diary-text pages are Army Form C. 2118, "WAR DIARY or INTELLIGENCE SUMMARY", typed in columns: Place | Date | Hour | Summary of Events and Information | (Weather report) | Remarks and references to Appendices. The "Hour" column is struck through on the form and a "(Weather report)" column is used instead; no hour is recorded for individual entries.
- The Remarks column carries repeated handwritten initials on each row (illegible); they are not transcribed.
- No map coordinates (latitude/longitude) are given in the diary; places are recorded as written.
- "do." in the Place column means "ditto" (same place as the entry above).

## Page 74 — Diary text, 1–13 November 1917 (November 1917, p. 2)

Army Form C. 2118. Heading: "WAR DIARY or INTELLIGENCE SUMMARY of the 185th Canadian Infantry Battalion. (Cape Breton Highlanders) Volume IX". Orderly Room stamp dated DEC 1 1917.

### 1 November 1917 — WITLEY

> Brigade Route March ordered for today postponed until Friday the 2nd Instant on account of wet weather. Individual training in huts.

Weather: Showery.

### 2 November 1917 — WITLEY

> Brigade Route March postponed from yesterday carried out. Battalion passed starting point at 9.20 a.m. For route and order of march, see Appendix "A".

Weather: Cloudy.

Remarks: See Appendix A

### 3 November 1917 — WITLEY

> All leave cancelled at 9 a.m. Men on courses not recalled. Bde Signal Message BM 520 & 527. Half Holiday.

Weather: Misty.

### 4 November 1917 — WITLEY

> Church Parade in morning. Inspection by C.O.

Weather: Fine.

### 5 November 1917 — WITLEY

> Six hours individual training. Brigadier General Ashton leaves for Canada to accept position of Adjutant General.

Weather: Fine.

### 6 November 1917 — WITLEY

> Lt. Col A.E. Ings assumes command of 15th Brigade temporarily. Battalion at work inspected by Lt Col J.L.R. Parsons, G.S.O.1 Restrictions placed on leave on 3rd instant are withdrawn and normal procedure adopted 10 a.m. Bde. Signal Message B.M. 545.

Weather: Fine.

### 7 November 1917 — WITLEY

> Four hours individual training. Half holiday for sports. +

Weather: Fine.

### 8 November 1917 — WITLEY

> Brigade Route March as per attached order. 185th Battalion passed starting point 9.14 a.m. Strength on parade: 34 officers and 707 other ranks. Battalion returned at 3 p.m.

Weather: Fine.

Remarks: See Appendix "B"

### 9 November 1917 — WITLEY

> Six hours individual training.

Weather: Fine.

### 10 November 1917 — WITLEY

> Four hours individual training. Half Holiday.

Weather: Misty.

### 11 November 1917 — WITLEY

> Church Parade in Battalion Mess Room. Inspection by [uncertain] O.C.

Weather: Fine.

### 12 November 1917 — WITLEY

> Six hours individual training.

Weather: Fine.

### 13 November 1917 — WITLEY

> Six Hours individual training. All supernumerary Lieutenants ordered back to Reserve Units. Establishment of Officers: 36 including P.M. and M.O.

Weather: Fine.

(continued)

## Page 75 — Diary text, 14–26 November 1917 (November 1917, p. 3)

Heading: "WAR DIARY or INTELLIGENCE SUMMARY of the 185th Canadian Infantry Battalion. (Cape Breton Highlanders) Volume IX".

### 14 November 1917 — WITLEY

> Four hours individual training. Half-holiday for sports. 50 Other ranks commenced a 24 hours course in Lewis Gun Training.

Weather: Fair.

### 15 November 1917 — WITLEY

> Battalion on Brigade Route March. 185th Battalion passed starting point at 9.10 a.m.

Weather: Foggy.

Remarks: See Appendix C

### 16 November 1917 — WITLEY

> Battalion placed at disposal of Senior Officers School, Aldershot. On parade: 800 (150 from 119th Bn.) Battalion reported at Hankley Common at 9.30 a.m.

Weather: Foggy.

### 17 November 1917 — WITLEY

> Four hours individual training. 9 sub-Lieutenants ordered to hold themselves in readiness to proceed to 17th Battalion.

Weather: Foggy.

### 18 November 1917 — WITLEY

> Church Parade in Men's Mess Room. C.O.'s inspection.

Weather: Fine.

### 19 November 1917 — WITLEY

> Six hours individual training. Six officers transferred to 17th Reserve Battalion, today.

Weather: Fair.

### 20 November 1917 — WITLEY

> Six hours individual training.

Weather: Fair.

### 21 November 1917 — WITLEY

> Four hours individual training. Half-holiday for sports.

Weather: Fair.

### 22 November 1917 — WITLEY

> Brigade Route March. Battalion passed starting point at 9.23 a.m. and returned at 4.30 p.m. Strength: 27 officers, 545 Other Ranks.

Weather: Fair.

Remarks: See Appendix D

### 23 November 1917 — WITLEY

> Six hours individual training. 80 other ranks commenced rifle grenade firing on Thursley Common. Two officers and three other ranks proceeded to London on election duty.

Weather: Fair.

### 24 November 1917 — WITLEY

> Four hours individual training. Half Holiday. Two officers proceed to Reserve Battalion. (Lieut.s Wiswell and McPhie.)

Weather: Fine.

### 25 November 1917 — WITLEY

> Church Parade in Men's Mess. Company Commanders Inspection.

Weather: Fair.

### 26 November 1917 — WITLEY

> Six hours individual training. Dental examination carried on. Lt. Murray proceeded to Shorncliffe for Musketry. Lieut McIvor and four other ranks to Bexhill for Anti-gas. 50 other ranks for rifle grenade practice at 8 a.m. and 50 at 1.15 p.m.

Weather: Fine.
