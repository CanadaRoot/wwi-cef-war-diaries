## Transcription conventions
- Faithful to the original: spelling, punctuation, abbreviations and capitalisation as written.
- `[uncertain]` marks a word or name that could not be read with confidence — never guessed.
- The diary-text pages are Army Form C. 2118, "WAR DIARY or INTELLIGENCE SUMMARY", typed in columns: Place | Date | Hour | Summary of Events and Information | Remarks and references to Appendices. The "INTELLIGENCE SUMMARY" line is struck through on each page, with "(Erase heading not required.)" beneath it. "VOL 2." is handwritten across the top of the Summary column on page 8.
- The Hour column heading is crossed out on both diary-text pages, and no hours are recorded.
- No map coordinates (latitude/longitude) are given in the diary; places are recorded as written.
- The weather (Fine, Rain, Snow storms, etc.) is given at the end of most April entries, as written.

## Page 8 — Diary text, 1–14 April 1917 (April 1917, p. 2)

Army Form C. 2118. Heading: "WAR DIARY or INTELLIGENCE SUMMARY." Handwritten "ORIGINAL" at top right. An oval Orderly Room stamp at top right reads "185th OVERSEAS BATTALION C.E.F. ORDERLY ROOM … CAPE BRETON HIGHLANDERS", dated "MAY 1" in the centre, No. left blank. The Remarks column carries a repeated handwritten endorsement on each row, appearing to read "Seen" [uncertain].

### 1 April 1917 — Witley

> No church Parade in lines. Battalion marched to All Saints Church Witley for service at 10 a.m. at which time and service the regimental colours were deposited.

Note: this entry also closes Volume 1 (January 1917, p. 6) — see `transcript-1917-01.md`.

### 2 April 1917 — Witley

> Colonel E.C. Ashton assumes command of the 15th Canadian Infantry Brigade. Two inches of snow in morning.

### 3 April 1917 — Witley

> Battalion on Frensham Common advance guard work. Snow storm in afternoon.

### 4 April 1917 — Witley

> Major-General G.B. Hughes accompanied by Brigadier (Col Ashton) inspected Battalion. On Parade Officers 25 Other Ranks 716. Battalion in Heavy Marching Order. Fine.

### 5 April 1917 — Witley

> Battalion on Outpost Scheme at Frensham Pond. Number under quarantine 117. Fine.

### 6 April 1917 — Witley

> Good Friday. Occasional rain and snow storms.

### 7 April 1917 — Witley

> Inspection of Battalion lines by Brigadier.

### 8 April 1917 — Witley

> Easter Sunday. Kilts authorized for Battalion. Fine.

### 9 April 1917 — Witley

> Inspection of Battalion by Sir Robert Laird Borden, accompanied by Generals Turner and Hughes and Brig-General Ashton. The Premier addressed the battalion (the only Nova Scotian Battalion in the 5th Division) intimating that it would soon be in France with its sister Battalion the 85th. Fine.

### 10 April 1917 — Witley

> Routine. Fine.

### 11 April 1917 — Witley

> Routine. Snow storms.

### 12 April 1917 — Witley

> Advance Guard Scheme at Kettlebury Hill. Inspector of Infantry present. Snow.

### 13 April 1917 — Witley

> Routine. Fine.

### 14 April 1917 — Witley

> Inspection by His Royal Highness the Duke of Connaught. March Past Rain.
