## Note on August 1917, p. 15 — MISSING PAGE
The LAC manifest for this record jumps from "August 1917, p. 14" to "August 1917, p. 16", and the downloaded page images confirm it: there is no file for August p. 15 (page-0046 is p. 14; page-0047 is p. 16). The missing page is **genuinely absent from the digitised set, not mislabelled**: page 14 ends "(P.T.O.)" in the middle of a march/sports order for the 20–22 August brigade scheme (Appendix "D"), and page 16 begins a different, complete order (185th Battalion Order No. 20, relief of 16/17 August). Page 15 would have been the continuation sheet of the page-14 order. No diary-text dates are lost — the diary text (pages 2–4) runs continuously from 1 to 31 August — but the tail of the 20 August march order is missing. Nothing has been invented to fill it.

## Transcription conventions
- Faithful to the original: spelling, punctuation, abbreviations and capitalisation as written.
- `[uncertain]` marks a word or name that could not be read with confidence — never guessed.
- The diary-text pages are Army Form C. 2118, "WAR DIARY or INTELLIGENCE SUMMARY", typed in columns: Place | Date | Hour | Summary of Events and Information | Remarks and references to Appendices.
- The "Hour" column is struck through on these forms and carries no entries; no hour is recorded for the daily entries. The weather is recorded at the end of each day's summary ("Fine.", "Showers.", etc.), under the heading "Weather Report".
- The Remarks column on each diary-text page carries the same handwritten initials against every entry; the initials could not be read with confidence and are not transcribed per entry (see Uncertain items).
- No map coordinates (latitude/longitude) are given in the diary; places are recorded as written. The appendices use Aldershot map sheet references (e.g. D 36 c 2.4) as written.
- "do." in the Place column means "ditto" (same place as the entry above).

Army Form C. 2118. Heading: "WAR DIARY or INTELLIGENCE SUMMARY of the 185th Canadian Infantry Battalion. (Cape Breton Highlanders). Volume VI." "INTELLIGENCE SUMMARY" struck through. A circular stamp (15th Canadian Infantry Brigade Orderly Room, "SEP 2 1917") appears at the top of the page.

> Individual Training in Huts 8 a.m. to 5 p.m. Heavy rainfall.

### 2 August 1917 — Witley

> Individual Training from 8 a.m. to 5 p.m. Showers.

### 3 August 1917 — Witley

> The Battalion, less the Transport Section, moved from "G" Lines to "C" 2 Lines. Cloudy.

### 4 August 1917 — Witley

> Individual training in huts 2 hours. Remainder of forenoon employed in improving conditions in new lines. These lines were not up to the standard of the battalion in so far as cleanliness and repairs were concerned. Bn. reached semi-finals in both events of Div.
