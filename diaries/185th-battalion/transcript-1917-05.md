## Transcription conventions
- Faithful to the original: spelling, punctuation, abbreviations and capitalisation as written.
- `[uncertain]` marks a word or name that could not be read with confidence — never guessed.
- The diary-text pages are Army Form C. 2118, "WAR DIARY or INTELLIGENCE SUMMARY", typed in columns: Place | Date | Hour | Summary of Events and Information | Remarks and references to Appendices.
- The "Hour" column is struck through on these forms and carries no entries; no hour is recorded for the daily entries. The weather is recorded at the end of each day's summary ("Fine.", "Rainy.", etc.).
- The Remarks column on each diary-text page carries the same handwritten initials against every entry; the initials could not be read with confidence and are not transcribed per entry (see Uncertain items).
- No map coordinates (latitude/longitude) are given in the diary; places are recorded as written.
- "do." in the Place column means "ditto" (same place as the entry above).

## Page 2 — Diary text, 1–16 May 1917 (May 1917, p. 2)

Army Form C. 2118. Heading: "WAR DIARY or INTELLIGENCE SUMMARY. 185th Can. Inf Battalion. Volume 3." "INTELLIGENCE SUMMARY" struck through. A circular date stamp (partially legible, "JUN 2 1917") appears at the top of the page.

### 1 May 1917 — Witley

> Routine. Fine.

### 2 May 1917 — Witley

> Routine. Fine.

### 3 May 1917 — Witley

> Battalion ordered to mobilize. This was carried out. Battalion ordered to stand to until next morning.

### 4 May 1917 — Witley

> Right half battalion marched to Godalming 1.30 p.m. Left half at 3.30 p.m. At 7.50 p.m. orders were received to resume normal conditions. The order was only a test. Fine.

### 5 May 1917 — Witley

> Routine Fine

### 6 May 1917 — Witley

> Routine Fine

### 7 May 1917 — Witley

> Routine Fine

### 8 May 1917 — Witley

> Routine Fine

### 9 May 1917 — Witley

> Brush fire south of officers' quarters in afternoon. Fine.

### 10 May 1917 — Witley

> Brush fire again causes alarm to be sounded. Fine.

### 11 May 1917 — Witley

> Alarm sounded for brush fire in evening. Fine.

### 12 May 1917 — Witley

> Routine Fine

### 13 May 1917 — Witley
