# War Diary — 5th Canadian Divisional Trench Mortar Group — September 1917

- **Unit:** 5th Canadian Divisional Trench Mortar Group, 5th Canadian Division
- **Month:** September 1917
- **Source:** Library and Archives Canada, Collection Search Item ID 2004821 (RG 9 III-D-3, vol. 4980, file 594, reel T-10809)
- **Pages:** September 1917, p. 1 – p. 4 (image files page-0062 – page-0065)
- **September 1917 — COMPLETE as filed** (pages 1–4): cover, contents, diary text 1–30 September, no appendices. **Diary entries exist only for 1–10, 15, 21, 29 and 30 September; 11–14, 16–20 and 22–28 September have no entries.** Transcribed 2026-10-28.

## Transcription conventions
Original spelling, punctuation and abbreviations kept. Diary text is handwritten throughout (Army Form C. 2118, "WAR DIARY or INTELLIGENCE SUMMARY." with "INTELLIGENCE SUMMARY" struck through), in a new hand from the August sheets. `[uncertain]` marks words I could not read with confidence — never guessed. Entries are initialled in the right margin: on the first diary sheet the initials read "H. W." followed by a flourish, transcribed as "H. W. [uncertain]"; on the second sheet they read "C. M. J. P.", transcribed as written. The Place column gives NEDONCHELLE for 1–5 September and BARLIN for 6–10, 15, 21, 29 and 30 September, with ditto marks (") for repeated places. The "Remarks and references to Appendices" column is blank throughout. The first diary sheet is stamped "TRENCH-MORTAR-BATTERIES 5TH CAN. DIV. No. 1 1st Sep. 17" and is headed in manuscript "War Diary for Month of September 1917."; the second sheet is stamped "No. 2 1 OCT. 1917". The printed footer reads "A5834 Wt. W4973/M687 750,000 8/16 D. D. & L. Ltd. Forms/C.2118/13." "T.M." = Trench Mortar; "O.R." = Other Ranks; "C.D.A." = Canadian Divisional Artillery; "C.D.A.C." = Canadian Divisional Ammunition Column; "C.F.A." = Canadian Field Artillery; "Bde." = Brigade; "Bty" = Battery; "T.O.S." = Taken On Strength; "H.Q." = Headquarters; "D.T.M.O." = Divisional Trench Mortar Officer; "T.M.G." = Trench Mortar Group; "Pt." = Private (as written).

## Open questions / uncertain items (September 1917)
- **Missing dates: no entries for 11–14, 16–20 or 22–28 September.** The diary jumps from 10 September to 15 September, then to 21 September, then to 29 September. Nothing in the month explains the gaps; the unit was at the 1st Army Trench Mortar School at Clarques from 10 September until its return on 29 September, which may account for the missing entries.
- The cover reads "VOLUME." followed by a struck character with a "9" written above it; the contents page reads "Volume 9".
- 4 September: "Pt. B.F. Image transferred to 14th Bde. C.F.A." — the surname reads "Image" and is repeated on 9 September ("Pt. Image left to join 14th Bde."), which supports the reading; it is transcribed as written.
- 5 September: "Arrived Billets, Fosse 9, Barlin, 3.15 p.m." — "Fosse 9" is as written; the hour column gives 3.15 for the arrival and the text repeats "3.15 p.m.".
- 10 September: "On Parade. 6 Officers, 68 O.R's. attached from 5th C.D.A.C. 1 Officer + 2 O.R's." — the words are arranged across two lines in the original; the sense is that 1 Officer and 2 O.R's are attached from 5th C.D.A.C. The reading is as written.
- The margin initials on the first diary sheet read "H. W." followed by a flourish that is not readable; the initials on the second sheet read "C. M. J. P." The month-end signature reads "C. M. J. Puchas" — the surname is flourished and is not read with full confidence, so it is marked [uncertain]; the margin initials "C. M. J. P." support a surname beginning with P.
- 30 September: "Strength:— 7 Officers. 135 Other Ranks." — this follows the 21 September increase of 66 Other Ranks from 5th C.D.A.C. and the 29 September return from Clarques; the arithmetic with the 29 September parade state (6 Officers, 69 O.R's, plus 66 attached) is not reconciled in the diary and is transcribed as written.
- Places read as: Nedonchelle; Barlin (Fosse 9); Clarques (1st Army Trench Mortar School). All in France.
- No human casualties are recorded anywhere in this month: no killed, wounded or missing are mentioned. Hospital admissions are recorded (1 O.R. in hospital 1 September; 1 Other Rank admitted to hospital 29 September), and one Other Rank in hospital was struck off strength on 21 September. Named personnel: Pt. B.F. Image (transferred to 14th Bde. C.F.A., 4 September; left to join 14th Bde., 9 September) and Lieut G. V. Sherlock (taken on strength from 31st Bty C.F.A., 15 September).
