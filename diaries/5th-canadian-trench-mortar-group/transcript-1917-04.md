# War Diary — 5th Canadian Divisional Trench Mortar Group — April 1917

- **Unit:** 5th Canadian Divisional Trench Mortar Group (Trench Mortar Batteries, 5th C.D.A.), 5th Canadian Division
- **Month:** April 1917
- **Source:** Library and Archives Canada, Collection Search Item ID 2004821 (RG 9 III-D-3, vol. 4980, file 594, reel T-10809)
- **Pages:** April 1917, p. 1 – p. 8 (image files page-0026 – page-0033)
- **April 1917 — COMPLETE** (pages 1–8): cover, contents, diary text 1–30 April, no appendices. Transcribed 2026-10-28.

## Transcription conventions
Original spelling, punctuation and abbreviations kept. Diary text is handwritten throughout (Army Form C. 2118). On sheets 1–3 the heading reads "WAR DIARY or INTELLIGENCE SUMMARY" with neither heading struck through; on sheets 4–6 it reads "WAR DIARY or" with "INTELLIGENCE SUMMARY" struck through. `[uncertain]` marks words I could not read with confidence — never guessed. Each day's entry is initialled "W.H.A." (Capt. W. H. Abbott, D.T.M.O.); the diary is signed by him at the foot of sheet 6. The Place column reads "Witley Camp" throughout (spelled "Witley Camp" on sheet 1 and "WITLEY CAMP" on sheets 2–6); the Hour column is not used; the "Remarks and references to Appendices" column is blank throughout. A circular stamp reading "TRENCH MORTAR BATTERIES - APR. 1917 5TH CAN. DIV." (Vol. IV, Sheet 1–6; sheets 5–6 marked "P.5" and "P.6") is struck on each diary sheet — the stamp now reads 5th Canadian Division, not 4th. The printed footer on sheets 1–5 reads "2353 Wt. W2544/1454 700,000 5/15 D. D. & L. A.D.S.S./Forms/C. 2118."; on sheet 6 it reads "A5834 Wt. W4973/M687 750,000 8/16 D. D. & L. Ltd. Forms/C.2118/13." "T.M." = Trench Mortar; "C.D.A." = Canadian Divisional Artillery; "D.T.M.O." = Divisional Trench Mortar Officer; "C.F.A." = Canadian Field Artillery; "O.R." = Other Ranks; "Bde." = Brigade; "a.m." / "p.m." as written.

## Open questions / uncertain items (April 1917)
- 1 April: "4th Canadian Div'n Artillery changed to 5th Canadian Div'n Artillery. A. G. Canadians. 21-2-4." — the authority reference is transcribed as written. From this month the unit is the 5th Canadian Divisional Artillery, and the circular stamp on the diary sheets reads "5TH CAN. DIV." instead of "4TH CAN. DIV."
- 6 April: Good Friday church parades.
- 9 April: inspection by Sir Robert Borden, who visited the T.M. work; the medium batteries' mumps quarantine (imposed 13 March) was lifted.
- 10 April: difficulty with water at 10 feet depth in the deep gun emplacement; 17 April records 7 feet of water in one entrance and 18 April both entrances filled with water, and work on that emplacement was stopped (21 April).
- 12 April: 32 O.R. taken on strength from various brigades in 5th C.D.A.; total strength 7 officers, 147 O.R., 10 of them in hospital. This is the first strength total stated since 31 March (7 officers, 92 O.R.); the increase of 55 O.R. is not accounted for day by day in the diary.
- 14 April: visit of the Duke of Connaught.
- 16 April: Lts. W. M. Waddington and S. L. McLenaghan left for Reading to join the Flying Corps. Waddington's initials are given as "M." on 25 January and "W.M." here; McLenaghan's surname is spelled "McClanaghan" on 25 January, "McCLENAGHAN" on 1 February and "McLENAGHAN" here — each transcribed as written.
- 17 April: "LT. P.A. WOODX [uncertain] from 14th Bde." — the surname appears written "WOODX", the final letter possibly a stray mark; recorded as [uncertain].
- 20 April: all available personnel at Mytchet Ranges for musketry; average score obtained 36.8%.
- 21 April: the first word of the entry ("New") appears corrected or overwritten; transcribed as written. A new location for the T.M. emplacement was chosen and digging commenced; timber was removed from the waterlogged emplacement.
- 28 April: purple patches (the 5th Canadian Division's distinguishing patch) issued to all ranks.
- 30 April: a detail of 2 officers and 14 O.R. on the Divisional Artillery tactical scheme; bombing instruction for telephonists. The month's signature reads "D.T.M.O. 5th Canadian Divn."
- No killed, wounded or missing are recorded anywhere in this month.
- Places read as: Witley Camp; Reading; Mytchet Ranges. No map references are given.
