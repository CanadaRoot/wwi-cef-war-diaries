# War Diary — 5th Canadian Divisional Trench Mortar Group — January 1918

- **Unit:** 5th Canadian Divisional Trench Mortar Group (5th Canadian Divisional Trench Mortar Batteries), 5th Canadian Division
- **Month:** January 1918
- **Source:** Library and Archives Canada, Collection Search Item ID 2004821 (RG 9 III-D-3, vol. 4980, file 594, reel T-10809)
- **Pages:** January 1918, p. 1 – p. 5 (image files page-0092 – page-0096)
- **January 1918 — COMPLETE** (pages 1–5): cover, contents, diary text 1–31 January (no entry for 7 January), appendix (casualties and courses). Transcribed 2026-10-28.

## Transcription conventions
Original spelling, punctuation and abbreviations kept. Diary text is handwritten throughout (Army Form C. 2118, "WAR DIARY or INTELLIGENCE SUMMARY" — "INTELLIGENCE SUMMARY" is struck through on the diary sheets; the appendix sheet is headed "INTELLIGENCE SUMMARY"). `[uncertain]` marks words I could not read with confidence — never guessed. The Place column reads "Les Brebis" on 1 January and ditto marks thereafter; the Hour column is not used. The "Remarks and references to Appendices" column carries a short initialled mark against each day's entry; it is the same mark throughout and is not read with confidence, so it is not transcribed entry by entry. The diary sheets are signed at the foot "J. McEachern Lieut & Adjt 5th C.D. T.M. Group." — the surname is read as "McEachern" (consistent with the November appendix entry "Lieut. J.N. McEachern") but is not read with full confidence; see open questions. The printed footer on the diary sheets reads "9353 Wt. W2541/1454 700,000 5/15 D. D. & L. A.D.S.S./Forms/C. 2118." "T.M." = Trench Mortar; "C.D.A." = Canadian Divisional Artillery; "D.T.M.O." = Divisional Trench Mortar Officer; "S.O.S." = as written; "H.Q." = Headquarters. "Confirmed" and "Suspected" Enemy T.M. are the diary's own terms for targets. Map references are read as written and are not verified.

## Pages 3–4 — Diary text (1–31 January 1918)

### Page 3 — 1–19 January 1918 (Sheet 1)

Place: Les Brebis.

> **1/1/18.** — Operations: Fired 40 Rounds on Hostile active Trench Mortars.
>
> **2/1/18.** — Operations: Fired 120 Rounds on Enemy Communication Trenches and Active Trench Mortars.
>
> **3/1/18.** — Operations: Fired 90 Rounds on Enemy Trench Mortars.
>
> Great inconvenience caused by Elevating and Traversing keys continually breaking
>
> **4/1/18.** — Operations: Fired 60 Rounds on Suspected Enemy Trench Mortar Emplacements.
>
> **5/1/18.** — Operations: Fired 50 Rounds on Confirmed Enemy Trench Mortar with Aeroplane Observation. Satisfactory Results. 1 direct hit on Emplacement.
> Fired 20 Rounds on suspected Enemy Trench Mortar Emplacements.
>
> **6/1/18.** — Operations: Commenced shoot with Aeroplane Observation, but owing to wireless breakdown had to be discontinued. Unsuccessful.
>
> **8/1/18.** — Operations: Fired 10 Rounds on S.O.S. Points.
>
> **9/1/18.** — Operations: Fired 30 Rounds on Enemy T.M. Emplacement with Aeroplane Observation. Successful.
>
> **10/1/18.** — Operations: Fired 44 Rounds on Enemy T.M. Emplacement with Aeroplane Observation. Successful.
>
> **11/1/18.** — Operations: Commenced shoot with Aeroplane Observation but owing to elevating guy breaking it was unsuccessful.
>
> **12/1/18.** — Operations: Fired 165 Rounds on Suspected Enemy Trench Mortars.
>
> **13/1/18.** — Operations: Fired 27 Rounds on Enemy Trench Mortar with Aeroplane Observation. Successful.
> Fired 75 Rounds on suspected T.M's. with ground observation.
>
> **14/1/18.** — Operations: Fired 109 Rounds on suspected Trench Mortars.
>
> **15/1/18.** — Operations: Fired 139 Rounds on Enemy T.M. Emplacements and Battalion Headquarters.
>
> **16/1/18.** — Operations: Fired 100 Rounds on Suspected Enemy Trench Mortars.
>
> **17/1/18.** — Strength: 8 Officers & 134 Other Ranks.
>
> Operations: Fired 110 Rounds on Enemy Trench Mortars.
>
> **18/1/18.** — Operations: Fired 57 Rounds on Suspected Enemy T.M. Emplacements.
>
> **19/1/18.** — Operations: Fired 63 Rounds on Confirmed Enemy Trench Mortars. Successful.
>
> [Signed, bottom right:] *J. McEachern Lieut & Adjt 5th C.D. T.M. Group.*

## Open questions / uncertain items (January 1918)
- Cover reads "VOLUME 13" with the "13" written above a struck-through character; contents page agrees on Volume 13.
- Contents page calls the appendix "Appendix I" (not "Appendix A"), and lists "Casualties 13-1-18 to 21-1-18" although the appendix's casualty dates run 13-1-18 to 31-1-18, and "Courses 12-1-18 to 1-2-18" although the courses listed run 13-1-18 to 1-2-18; all as written.
- No entry for 7 January 1918: the diary jumps from 6 to 8 January.
- 3 January: "Elevating and Traversing keys continually breaking" — "keys" as written.
- 6 and 11 January: aeroplane-observation shoots were begun but failed (wireless breakdown on 6 January; elevating guy breaking on 11 January).
- 21 January: "Fired 42 " " Suspected " ground "" — the ditto marks are as written: 42 rounds on suspected enemy T.M. with ground observation.
- Appendix casualties: four men wounded on 13-1-18 (Cox, Maitland, Dumont, Saunders); the daily entries for 13 January record no casualties. Bates (gassed 29-1-18) and Rankin (shell shock, hysteria, 29-1-18) likewise do not appear in the daily entries. Regimental numbers are read as written and not verified.
- Appendix courses: "Gnr. Newer E.J." — the surname is read as "Newer" but is not fully certain; flagged [uncertain]. "S/Sgt." and "Bdr." (Bombardier) are as written. Gnr. Greig J.W. (336905) appears on this tunnelling course and is listed as killed on 6-2-18 in the February appendix.
- The signature "J. McEachern" is read as written; the November appendix names "Lieut. J.N. McEachern", which supports the surname reading, but the signature itself is not read with full confidence.
- Places read as: Les Brebis only. No entry breaks off mid-sentence; the only missing day is 7 January.
