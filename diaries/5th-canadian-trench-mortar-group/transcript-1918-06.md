# War Diary — 5th Canadian Divisional Trench Mortar Group — June 1918

- **Unit:** 5th Canadian Divisional Trench Mortar Group (5th Canadian Divisional Trench Mortar Brigade), 5th Canadian Division
- **Month:** June 1918
- **Source:** Library and Archives Canada, Collection Search Item ID 2004821 (RG 9 III-D-3, vol. 4980, file 594, reel T-10809)
- **Pages:** June 1918, p. 1 – p. 8 (image files page-0120 – page-0127)
- **June 1918 — COMPLETE** (pages 1–8): cover, contents, diary text 1–30 June, Appendices A (casualties 8–21 June), B (officers taken on or struck off strength) and C (copy of a message from 44th Infantry Brigade). Transcribed 2026-10-28.

## Transcription conventions
Original spelling, punctuation and abbreviations kept. Diary text is handwritten throughout (Army Form C. 2118, "WAR DIARY or INTELLIGENCE SUMMARY" — "INTELLIGENCE SUMMARY" is struck through on these sheets). `[uncertain]` marks words I could not read with confidence — never guessed. Every entry ends with the initials "C.A.B." except 6 June (no initials), 22 June (no initials) and 30 June (no initials and no closing stop); Sheets I is signed by Capt. C.A.B. Purchas and Sheets II–V by Lieut. C. Lloyd, for D.T.M.O. 5 C.D.A. The Place column reads "Waverly Camp" throughout June (spelled "Waverley Camp" in May); the Date column on Sheets III–V gives the day number only ("June 12", "June 20", "June 26" etc.) with "1918" at the head of the column. The Hour column is blank throughout. "T.M." = Trench Mortar; "E.T.M." = Enemy Trench Mortar; "E.A." = Enemy Aircraft; "C.D.T.M." = Canadian Divisional Trench Mortar; "C.D.A." = Canadian Divisional Artillery; "C.D.A.C." = Canadian Divisional Ammunition Column; "C.F.A." = Canadian Field Artillery; "D.T.M.O." = Divisional Trench Mortar Officer; "D.A.C." = Divisional Ammunition Column; "G.R.O." = General Routine Order; "G.O.C. R.A." = General Officer Commanding Royal Artillery; "S.O.S." = Save Our Souls (emergency barrage signal); "O.C." = Officer Commanding; "O.R." = Other Rank; "Bty" = Battery; "C.C.S." = Casualty Clearing Station; "Bdr." = Bombardier; "Gnr." = Gunner; "Cpl." = Corporal; "Lieut" = Lieutenant. "Archie" is anti-aircraft fire. Place spellings are kept as written: "Roclincourt", "Arras", "Gavrelle" (see May), "Tinques".

## Pages 3–7 — Diary text (1–30 June 1918)

Army Form C. 2118, "WAR DIARY or INTELLIGENCE SUMMARY". The printed footer on Sheets I–II reads "2353 Wt. W2544/1454 700,000 5/15 D.D.&L. A.D.S.S./Forms/C. 2118."; on Sheets III–V it reads "A2990. Wt. W... 750,000. 1/17. D. D. & L., Ltd. Forms/C.2118/14."

### Page 3 — 1–5 June 1918 (Sheet I)

Heading: "Trench Mortar Brigade 5th Canadian Division Artillery / June 1918."

> **Waverly Camp, 1918 June 1st.** — Weather — warm, fair and bright: good visibility. Enemy rather quiet on our immediate front. Operations — Aeroplane shoot on hostile T.M. undertaken but with very little success as gun was erratic. 49 rounds expended. X Battery registered its second active mortar. Waggon lines — Parades held as usual. C.A.B.
>
> **,, 2nd.** — Weather — warm and bright. Visibility good. Operations — nil. V Bty. commenced work on a new position. C.A.B.
>
> **,, 3rd.** — Weather — fair but cooler. Visibility good. Operations — Aeroplane shoot carried out by X2 on hostile T.M. 50 rounds fired but [words struck through] 20 rounds fired from Y2 on hostile T.M. with ground observation — good results. Y2 put in action and registered. Mobile sections turned out for practice. Time taken 38 minutes. C.A.B. [Remarks column: "App."]
>
> **,, 4th.** — Weather fair and cool and visibility still good. Operations — 50 rounds fired on hostile trench mortar with aeroplane observation. Successful. 40 rounds harassing fire on hostile T.M's & enemy trenches. Good results. C.A.B.
>
> **,, 5th.** — Weather still fair. Operations — 50 rounds fired on new enemy trench. Pay parade at waggon lines. Mobile section turned out for practice and went into action near Roclincourt. C.A.B.
>
> [Signed, bottom right:] *[signature — C.A.B. Purchas] Capt C.F.A. / D.T.M.O. 5 C.D.A.*

### Page 4 — 6–11 June 1918 (Sheet 2)

Heading: "Trench Mortar Brigade 5th Canadian Division Artillery / June 1918."

> **Waverly Camp, 1918 June 6.** — Weather fair. Visibility good. Operations 10 rounds fired on Hostile T.M. silencing it. V3 put into action. Destructive shoots attempted on both X and Y Batteries but no damage sustained by either personell or materiel.
>
> **,, 7.** — Weather remains fine. Operations. Two shoots on E.T.M's. undertaken to aid aeroplane observation. Day shoot successful 50 rounds. In second shoot plane had to go in on account of engine trouble after a few rounds. 18 rounds fired in retaliation. C.A.B.
>
> **,, 8.** — Weather fair. Good Visibility. Operations. Aeroplane shoot — successful. C.A.B. An enemy bomb dropped [uncertain] 'A' south of Arras. C.A.B.
>
> **,, 9.** — At 2.35 a.m. Enemy put down a barrage and sent across raiders south of our immediate sector. S.O.S. on our front but raiders penetrated to south of us. X Bty fired 8 rounds when one gun went out of action and front row of [uncertain] then was put in fire. Several of the men showed great coolness in extinguishing it. Y Battery fired 20 rounds. Lieut Magann and Gnr. McLeish of V Bty wounded. C.A.B. [Remarks column: "App. 'A'"]
>
> **,, 10.** — Weather slightly dull. Front quiet on the whole. Enemy put on a destructive shoot against X Bty and damaged gun pit. Our own operations consisted of 42 rounds on enemy trenches. C.A.B. Lieut R. Hay reported for duty C.A.B. [Remarks column: "App. 'B'"]
>
> **,, 11.** — Weather slightly dull with rather low visibility. Enemy rather active. Operations — 50 rounds in destructive shoot on enemy trenches and hostile T.M's. C.A.B.
>
> [Signed, bottom right:] *C. Lloyd Lieut / for D.T.M.O. / 5 C.D.A.*

### Page 5 — 12–19 June 1918 (Sheet III)

Heading: "5th Canadian Division Artillery / Trench Mortar Brigade / June 1918."

> **Waverly Camp, June 12.** — Weather warm and cloudy. Visibility slightly obscured. Enemy quiet on our sector. Operations — 99 rounds expended in harassing fire on hostile T.M's and in retaliation. C.A.B.
>
> **,, 13.** — Weather fair. Visibility good. Enemy T.M's. more than usually active. 47 rounds expended in harassing fire and retaliation on E.T.M's. C.A.B.
>
> **,, 14.** — Weather fair and warm. 35 rounds expended in harassing fire. C.A.B. [Remarks column: "App. 'B'"]
>
> **,, 15.** — Weather dull and slight rain during night. Two E.A. paid a flying visit between frightened away by Archie. No damage done to either of us. Operations — 34 rounds in Camouflaged wire cutting for a raid. C.A.B.
>
> **,, 16.** — Weather still fair. 73 rounds fired wire cutting, retaliation and harassing fire. C.A.B.
>
> **,, 17.** — Weather cloudy. Operations nil owing to difficulty of ammunition supply. C.A.B. [Remarks column: "App. 'A'"]
>
> **,, 18.** — Rain during night. Operations 15 rounds fired. Received hurry order to replenish supply of ammunition at active guns. Two waggon with 60 rounds and carrying party sent forward. All available taken to position. C.A.B.
>
> **,, 19.** — Mr Wood and [Blondell — struck through] Mr Stairs relieved Capt. McCadam [uncertain] and Mr Blondell. Important Communication regarding manoeuvres received. Minute directions given for performing all simple manoeuvres. 50 rounds expended registering targets for raid C.A.B.
>
> [Signed, bottom right:] *C. Lloyd Lieut C.F.A. / for D.T.M.O. 5 C.D.A.*

### Page 6 — 20–25 June 1918 (Sheet 4)

Heading: "Trench Mortar Brigade 5th Canadian Division Artillery / June 1918."

> **Waverly Camp, 1918 June 20.** — Weather dull and rainy. Orders received from Divisional Headquarters to take bicycles to Ordnance to be scaled. Ordnance informed us that we had no right to any bicycles and referred us to G.R.O. no. 2785. On looking up this G.R.O. we find it refers to Venereal disease an inefficiency but makes no specific reference to bicycles. We are now at a loss to know what to do with our bicycle. Raid made on enemy trenches. We fired 64 rounds in support. Lieut de Sherwin and one O.R. wounded and evacuated. Raid was reported successful. C.A.B. [Remarks column: "Appen 'B'" and "Appen 'A'"]
>
> **,, June 21.** — Weather fair. Front quiet. Operations a few rounds fired in retaliation. C.A.B. Lieut A.R. Montgomery reported for duty C.A.B.
>
> **,, June 22.** — Mr Magann returned after a brief but successful stay at No. 7 C.C.S. He looks quite fit again. He has not fully decided whether to submit a claim for a new pair of breeches as his were slightly worse before being hit and can be still worn.
>
> **,, June 23.** — V4 completed and put into action with bed at slope 20°. Found that gun will not elevate above 56° and as a result will not shoot at all ranges. C.A.B.
>
> **,, June 24.** — Had two elevating gears shortened for V4 but find that the gun will not yet shoot at all ranges. Necessary for D.T.M.O. to take trip to army to prove that bed at slope of 20° is not practicable. 20 rounds expended in harassing fire C.A.B.
>
> **,, June 25.** — Weather still fine. Front quiet. Expenditure 10 rounds retaliation.
>
> [Signed, bottom right:] *C. Lloyd Lieut for / D.T.M.O. 5 C.D.A.*

### Page 7 — 26–30 June 1918 (Sheet 5)

Heading: "Trench Mortar Brigade 5th Canadian Division Artillery / June 1918."

> **Waverly Camp, 1918 June 26.** — Weather fair. Front quiet. Operations nil except for retaliation. C.A.B. Lieut T. Sheard reported for duty. C.A.B. [Remarks column: "App. 'B'"]
>
> **,, June 27.** — Both sides very quiet and operations confined to harassing fire, and auction bridge at [waggon — struck through] waggon lines. Mobile section transferred to D.A.C. in order to have better facilities for training for competitive demonstration expected in the near future. C.A.B. Lieut Wood appointed O.C. mobility C.A.B.
>
> **,, June 28.** — Secured use of rifle ranges for personnell on rest at waggon lines. Some fair scores were made average being 18 points out of 35. Great interest shown. C.A.B.
>
> **,, June 29.** — Whole day very quiet and taken up with reliefs. C.A.B.
>
> **,, June 30.** — Secured use of rifle for whole day. Best 20 were given permission to attend Canadian Corps sports to be held at Tinques July 1st. Competition very keen
>
> [Signed, bottom right:] *C. Lloyd Lieut / C.F.A. / for D.T.M.O. 5 C.D.A.*

## Open questions / uncertain items (June 1918)
- The cover reads "VOLUME 17" struck through with "18." written above it; the contents page reads "Volume 18". Transcribed as written.
- 1 June: a struck-through character precedes "49 rounds expended"; transcribed without it.
- 3 June: several words after "50 rounds fired but" are struck through and not read; transcribed as [words struck through]. "Time taken 3 8 minutes" — the figures are spaced as "3 8" and read as 38 minutes. The Remarks column for this date reads "App." — the appendix that applies is Appendix C, the 44th Infantry Brigade message about this shoot.
- 6 June: this entry carries no initials, unlike the other June entries.
- 7 June: the word "retaliation" in "18 rounds fired in retaliation" is struck through and rewritten above the line; transcribed as written.
- 8 June: "An enemy bomb dropped [uncertain] 'A' south of Arras" — the word between "dropped" and "'A'" is not read with confidence; it appears to read "west of" or "near". Appendix A lists Gnr. Slate J wounded on 8-6-18, but the diary entry names no casualty.
- 9 June: "front row of [uncertain] then was put in fire" — the word after "of" is not read with confidence; it appears to be a short word beginning with "t". The raiders "penetrated to south of us" — i.e. south of the brigade's own front. Lieut Magann's initials are given in Appendix A as "A. C." and the surname is spelled "Magaan" there, against "Magann" in the diary entries of 9 and 22 June; both spellings are transcribed as written.
- 10 June: "Lieut R. Hay reported for duty" — Appendix B gives his full name as Lieut Robert Hay, taken on strength 10-6-18 and struck off strength 14-6-18.
- 14 June: the contents page gives Appendix B's range as 10-6-18 to 28-6-18, but no officer change is recorded on 14 June in the diary; Lieut Robert Hay's striking-off on 14-6-18 appears only in Appendix B.
- 15 June: "Two E.A. paid a flying visit between frightened away by Archie" — the word "between" is read as written; it may be a hasty "but were".
- 17 June: the diary records "Operations nil", but Appendix A lists Gnr. Anderson R.B. wounded 17-6-18; transcribed as written.
- 19 June: the struck-through word before "Mr Stairs" appears to read "Blondell", written in error and struck out. "Capt. McCadam [uncertain]" — the surname is read as "McCadam" but could be "McAdam".
- 20 June: "G.R.O. no. 2785" — read as "G.R.O." (General Routine Order); the diary says it "refers to Venereal disease an inefficiency" — "an" is read as written and may be "and". The diary says Lieut de Sherwin was wounded on 20 June, but Appendix A dates his wounding 21-6-18; transcribed as written. The second casualty that day ("one O.R.") is not named in the diary; Appendix A lists Cpl Scott P.J. wounded 21-6-18, which may be the same man, but the dates do not match.
- 21 June: "Lieut A.R. Montgomery reported for duty" — Appendix B gives "Lieut A. R. Montgomery Taken on strength 21-6-18".
- 22 June: "Mr Magann returned after a brief but successful stay at No. 7 C.C.S." — Magann was wounded on 9 June; the diary does not say when he left for the C.C.S. This entry carries no initials.
- 25 June: this entry carries no initials.
- 27 June: "auction bridge at [waggon — struck through] waggon lines" — the word "waggon" appears struck through and then written again as part of "waggon lines". "Lieut Wood appointed O.C. mobility" — read as written; Wood is later "Capt. P.A. Wood" (July).
- 30 June: the entry ends "Competition very keen" with no initials and no closing stop; the month ends there. The Canadian Corps sports at Tinques are the subject of the 1 July entry.
- Appendix C: the map reference in the 44th Infantry Brigade message reads "H 11 d 9 5 9 5" — transcribed as written; the sheet and coordinates are not verified.
- The contents page gives the casualty appendix range as "9-6-18 to 21-6-18", but Appendix A itself lists a casualty dated 8-6-18 (Gnr. Slate J); transcribed as written.
