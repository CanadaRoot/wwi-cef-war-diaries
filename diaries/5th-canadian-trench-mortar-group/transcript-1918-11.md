# War Diary — 5th Canadian Divisional Trench Mortar Group — November 1918

- **Unit:** 5th Canadian Divisional Trench Mortar Group (5th Canadian Divisional Trench Mortar Brigade), 5th Canadian Division
- **Month:** November 1918
- **Source:** Library and Archives Canada, Collection Search Item ID 2004821 (RG 9 III-D-3, vol. 4980, file 594, reel T-10809)
- **Pages:** November 1918, p. 1 – p. 5 (image files page-0164 – page-0168)
- **November 1918 — COMPLETE** (pages 1–5): cover, contents, diary text 1–16 November (2 sheets), appendix (officers taken/struck off strength and casualties). The diary ends 16 November 1918 with the brigade broken up and its batteries distributed to the 13th and 14th Brigades C.F.A. Transcribed 2026-10-28.

## Transcription conventions
Original spelling, punctuation and abbreviations kept. Diary text is handwritten throughout (Army Form C. 2118, "WAR DIARY or INTELLIGENCE SUMMARY" — neither heading is struck through on these sheets, and the heading is handwritten "Trench Mortar Brigade / 5th Can. Div. Art."). `[uncertain]` marks words I could not read with confidence — never guessed. The hand is the same small cramped hand as September–October. The Remarks column carries "Mess." after most entries (reading uncertain — see September open questions); 3 November instead cites "APP. "A"" and 14 November "APP. B". The Place column gives "Denain" (1 November), "Valenciennes" (4 November) and "Hensies Belgium" (10 November); other days carry ditto marks. The Hour column is blank throughout. "T.M." = Trench Mortar; "C.D.A.C." = Canadian Divisional Ammunition Column; "D.A.C." = Divisional Ammunition Column; "H.V's." = high-velocity (shells); "C.I.B." = Canadian Infantry Brigade; "C.F.A." = Canadian Field Artillery; "C.M.M.G." = Canadian Motor Machine Gun; "C.A.R.D." = Canadian Artillery Regimental Depot; "D.T.M.O." = Divisional Trench Mortar Officer; "Adjt" = Adjutant; "S.O.S." = struck off strength; "D.S.O." = Distinguished Service Order; "Sig." = Signaller; "Gnr." = Gunner. The printed footer on the diary sheets reads "2353 Wt. W2544/1454 700,000 5/15 D.D.&L. A.D.S.S./Forms/C. 2118."

## Pages 3–4 — Diary text (1–16 November 1918)

Army Form C. 2118, "WAR DIARY or INTELLIGENCE SUMMARY". The printed footer reads "2353 Wt. W2544/1454 700,000 5/15 D.D.&L. A.D.S.S./Forms/C. 2118."

### Page 3 — Sheet I (1–9 November 1918)

Place: Denain.

> **1/11/18.** — The 4 guns in position at Saultain [uncertain] fired 220 rounds this morning. Attack a success and, troops going through Valenciennes. One of "Y" Batteries guns knocked out and [uncertain] one wounded. Shelling Denain with H.V.'s.
>
> **2/11/18.** — Day quiet. Bath parade at wagon lines.
>
> **3/11/18.** — Orders to place two mortars at disposal of 12th C.I.B. Lt Williams in charge makes H.Q. at [uncertain] and wagon lines are established near that place. One casualty occurred today.

Place: Valenciennes.

> **4/11/18.** — Moved to Valenciennes and H.Q. established at E 10 a 00.30.
>
> **5/11/18.** — Lt Williams returns [uncertain] Lt Stairs. No action yet. Enemy's shellfire heavy on towns along Mons road.
>
> **6/11/18.** — Our mobile section now at Quarouble [uncertain]. Bath parade at wagon lines.
>
> **7/11/18.** — Mobile section relieved and returns to wagon lines.
>
> **8/11/18.** — General cleaning up. Route march 1 hour.
>
> **9/11/18.** — Route march, laying practice and instruction in preparation of Ammunition. Orders to move to Hensies area tomorrow.

[Remarks column: "Mess." against the entries; "APP. "A"" against 3 November.]

### Page 4 — Sheet II (10–16 November 1918)

Place: Hensies, Belgium.

> **10/11/18.** — Moved to Hensies today starting at 5 A.M. and arriving here at 9 A.M. Good billets obtained and remainder of the day spent in fixing them up.
>
> **11/11/18.** — Route march, laying practice etc. Armistice signed by [uncertain]. Day quiet. Lt Stairs has been awarded D.S.O. for his work on 2 Sept.
>
> **12/11/18.** — Route march. [uncertain] cleaning up of guns and wagons.
>
> **13/11/18.** — [No entry — see open questions.]
>
> **14/11/18.** — Orders received that the Brigade is to be broken up and distributed between 13th and 14th Brigades C.F.A. as follows — 13th Brigade — "V" and "Y" Batteries. 14th " — "X" Battery. D.T.M.O., Adjt, and small office staff to be attached to 3rd Section D.A.C. for the meantime.
>
> **15/11/18.** — "V" and "Y" Batteries report to 13th Bde at Crespin. "X" Battery then returns from C.M.M.G. Corps. Stores turned in to Ordnance.
>
> **16/11/18.** — "X" Battery send to 14th Bde at Thulin [uncertain]. Remainder of Brigade reports to 5th C.D.A.C.

[Remarks column: "Mess." against the entries; "APP. B" against 14 November. No closing signature appears on this sheet; the diary ends here.]

## Open questions / uncertain items (November 1918)
- The contents page says "Diary Text 3 Pages", but only two diary sheets are present (p. 3–p. 4, covering 1–16 November); p. 5 is the appendix. Both as written.
- The contents page dates Appendix A "Casualties 2-11-18", but the appendix's only casualty is dated 3-11-18. Both as written.
- 1 November: "at Saultain [uncertain]" — the place name is read as written and is not verified. "One of "Y" Batteries guns knocked out and [uncertain] one wounded" — the word before "one wounded" could not be read. No 1 November casualty appears in the appendix; the appendix's only November casualty is dated 3-11-18.
- 3 November: "makes H.Q. at [uncertain]" — the place name could not be read. "One casualty occurred today" matches the appendix: Sig. M. Macken T.F. wounded 3-11-18.
- 5 November: "Lt Williams returns [uncertain] Lt Stairs" — the word between the names (possibly "relieves") could not be read.
- 6 November: "Our mobile section now at Quarouble [uncertain]" — the place name is read as written and is not verified.
- 11 November: "Armistice signed by [uncertain]" — the word after "by" could not be read.
- 12 November: the word before "cleaning up of guns and wagons" could not be read.
- 13/14 November: 13 November has no entry of its own. The "Orders received that the Brigade is to be broken up…" entry's first line sits level with the 13/11/18 date row, so it may belong to 13 November rather than 14 November; it is placed under 14 November here because the entry's continuation and the "APP. B" remark sit against the 14/11/18 row. The ambiguity is flagged.
- 16 November: "at Thulin [uncertain]" — the place name is read as written and is not verified. The entry ends the diary; no closing signature appears on the sheet, and the sentence "Remainder of Brigade reports to 5th C.D.A.C." is the diary's last line.
- Appendix: Lieut Montgomery's initials read "A. R." but are not fully certain. "S.O.S. to C. A. R. D. Witley [uncertain]" — the last word is read as written and is not certain. The casualty's surname reads "Macken" but is not fully certain.
- The appendix confirms the break-up of the brigade: officers struck off strength to the 13th Bde C.F.A. (Boyd, Montgomery, Williamson, Williams) and 5th C.D.A.C. (Blundell, McEachern, Wood) on 15-11-18, and to the 14th Bde C.F.A. (McKenzie, Sheard, Boland) on 16-11-18; Lieut Lomas to C.A.R.D. Witley.
- Places read as: Denain; Saultain [uncertain]; Valenciennes; Quarouble [uncertain]; Hensies, Belgium; Crespin; Thulin [uncertain]. The only map reference given is E 10 a 00.30 (4 November), as written and not verified.
- Casualties this month (appendix): 3 November — Sig. M. Macken T.F. wounded. The diary also notes one man wounded on 1 November, not named in the appendix.
