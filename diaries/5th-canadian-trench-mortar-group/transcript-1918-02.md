# War Diary — 5th Canadian Divisional Trench Mortar Group — February 1918

- **Unit:** 5th Canadian Divisional Trench Mortar Group (5th Canadian Divisional Trench Mortar Batteries), 5th Canadian Division
- **Month:** February 1918
- **Source:** Library and Archives Canada, Collection Search Item ID 2004821 (RG 9 III-D-3, vol. 4980, file 594, reel T-10809)
- **Pages:** February 1918, p. 1 – p. 6 (image files page-0097 – page-0102)
- **February 1918 — COMPLETE** (pages 1–6): cover, title sheet, contents, diary text 1–28 February, appendix (casualties and courses). Transcribed 2026-10-28.

## Transcription conventions
Original spelling, punctuation and abbreviations kept. Diary text is handwritten throughout (Army Form C. 2118, "WAR DIARY or INTELLIGENCE SUMMARY" — "INTELLIGENCE SUMMARY" is struck through on the diary sheets; the appendix sheet is headed "WAR DIARY or INTELLIGENCE SUMMARY" with the second heading struck through). `[uncertain]` marks words I could not read with confidence — never guessed. The Place column reads "Les Brebis" on every diary sheet; the Hour column is not used. The "Remarks and references to Appendices" column carries a short initialled mark against each day's entry; it is the same mark throughout and is not read with confidence, so it is not transcribed entry by entry. The diary sheets are signed at the foot "J. McEachern Lieut & Adjt 5th C.D. T.M. Group." (sheet 1) and "J. McEachern Lieut. & Adjt for O.C. 5th C.D. T.M. Bde." (sheet 2) — the surname is read as "McEachern" (consistent with the November appendix entry "Lieut. J.N. McEachern") but is not read with full confidence; see open questions. The printed footer on the diary sheets reads "9353 Wt. W2541/1454 700,000 5/15 D. D. & L. A.D.S.S./Forms/C. 2118." "T.M." = Trench Mortar; "C.D.A." = Canadian Divisional Artillery; "D.T.M.O." = Divisional Trench Mortar Officer; "S.O.S." = as written; "H.Q." = Headquarters; "M.G." = Machine Gun; "O.C." = Officer Commanding. Map references are read as written and are not verified.

## Pages 4–5 — Diary text (1–28 February 1918)

### Page 4 — 1–16 February 1918 (Sheet 1)

Place: Les Brebis.

> **1/2/18.** — Visibility: Very poor. Operations: Usual daily shelling.
>
> **2/2/18.** — Operations: Usual daily shelling, also an unsuccessful shoot with Aeroplane Observation on account of guys breaking
>
> **3/2/18.** — Operations: Usual daily operations and retaliation on active Hostile Trench Mortars at request of Infantry.
>
> **4/2/18.** — Strength: 10 Officers & 139 Other Ranks.
>
> Operations: Usual daily shelling. We also cooperated in Barrage during raid by 3rd Canadian Infantry Battalion.
>
> **5/2/18.** — Operations: Daily firing and night Harassing fire on suspected Machine Guns and movement.
>
> **6/2/18.** — Visibility: Poor.
>
> Operations: Retaliation at request of Infantry and Harassing fire on Enemy Communication Trenches.
>
> Casualties: 2 men killed. See appendix.
>
> **7/2/18.** — Operations: Retaliation, dispersing movement and night Harassing fire.
>
> **8/2/18.** — Operations: Usual daily shelling on Trench Junctions, etc.
>
> **9/2/18.** — Operations: Usual shelling. Retaliation and Harassing Fire.
>
> **10/2/18.** — Strength: Officers. 10. Other Ranks 139.
>
> Operations: Concentrated shoots of 3 Hostile Trench Mortars with good results.
>
> **11/2/18.** — Operations: Fired on enemy movement and active Trench Mortars. One Gun blown out of Trench.
>
> **12/2/18.** — Operations: Usual daily firing, also cooperated in Barrage during raid by 3rd Canadian Infantry Brigade, Total expenditure 133 Rounds.
>
> **13/2/18.** — Operations: Very little shelling done. 41 Rounds in all expended.
>
> Visibility: Poor.
>
> **14/2/18.** — Operations: Retaliation at request of Infantry and Night Harassing Fire.
>
> **15/2/18.** — Strength Increase: 3 Other Ranks Taken on strength.
>
> Operations: S.O.S. and retaliation for Enemy Gas Projection. 126 Rounds expended.
>
> **16/2/18.** — Operations: Successful shoot on Enemy Trench Mortar Emplacement with Aeroplane Observations.
>
> [Signed, bottom right:] *J. McEachern Lieut & Adjt 5th C.D. T.M. Group.*

### Page 5 — 17–28 February 1918 (Sheet 2)

Place: Les Brebis.

> **17/2/18.** — Strength: 10 Officers 136 Other Ranks.
>
> Operations: Retaliation and Harassing Fire. Also successful shoot on Enemy Trench Mortar with Aeroplane Observation. Battery well on Target.
>
> **18/2/18.** — Operations: Suspected Battalion H.Q. engaged with satisfactory results. One direct hit.
> also successful shoot on Enemy Trench Mortar with Aeroplane Observation. Battery shot excellent and Emplacement damaged.
>
> **19/2/18.** — Operations: Retaliation, also successful shoot on Enemy Trench Mortar with Aeroplane Observation. Battery shot well and very rapid. 70 Rounds fired in an Hour.
>
> **20/2/18.** — Visibility: Very poor. Operations: Limited to Harassing fire on Trench Junctions.
>
> **21/2/18.** — Operations: Retaliation at request of Infantry.
>
> **22/2/18.** — Operations: Harassing fire on enemy movement.
>
> **23/2/18.** — Strength: 10 Officers 134 Other Ranks.
>
> Operations: Successful shoot with Aeroplane Observation on Hostile Trench Mortar, also usual Harassing fire.
>
> **24/2/18.** — Operations: Harassing fire on roads and Communication Trench, also retaliation at request of Infantry.
>
> **25/2/18.** — Visibility: Good.
>
> Operations: Usual daily firing.
>
> **26/2/18.** — Visibility: Fair with good intervals.
>
> Operations: Successful shoot with Aeroplane Observation on Enemy Trench Mortar, also daily Harassing fire. etc.
>
> **27/2/18.** — Operations: Usual daily firing.
>
> **28/2/18.** — Casualties: 3 Other Ranks wounded.
>
> Operations: Successful shoot on Enemy Trench Mortar with Aeroplane Observation.
>
> Casualties: 4 Other Ranks Wounded.
>
> [Signed, bottom right:] *J. McEachern Lieut. & Adjt for O.C. 5th C.D. T.M. Bde.*

## Open questions / uncertain items (February 1918)
- Cover reads "VOLUME 14" with the "14" written above a circled "7"; contents page agrees on Volume 14. There is no typed contents page before the title sheet: the title sheet ("On His Majesty's Service") is page 2 and the typed contents is page 3.
- 4 February: the raid is by the "3rd Canadian Infantry Battalion"; 12 February's raid is by the "3rd Canadian Infantry Brigade" — both as written.
- 6 February: the daily entry records "2 men killed. See appendix." The appendix names Gnr. Greig J.W. (336905) and Gnr. Walker A.C. (319928), both killed 6-2-18. Greig had attended the January tunnelling course.
- 15 February: "S.O.S. and retaliation for Enemy Gas Projection. 126 Rounds expended."
- 28 February: the daily entry carries two casualty lines — "Casualties: 3 Other Ranks wounded." before the operations line and "Casualties: 4 Other Ranks Wounded." after it. The appendix names four men wounded on 28-2-18 (Sgt. Henry J., Gnr. Armstrong J.C., Gnr. Singleton T., Gnr. Garney B.F.A.); the "3 Other Ranks wounded" line is not explained and the two lines are transcribed as written.
- Appendix casualties: "Gnr. Garney B.F.A" — the surname is read as "Garney" but is not fully certain; flagged [uncertain]. Regimental numbers are read as written and not verified.
- Appendix courses: "Sgt. Osgoode H.W." — the surname is read as "Osgoode" but is not fully certain; "Pte Linn B.M." — the surname is read as "Linn" but is not fully certain; both flagged [uncertain]. "Clarques" (1st Army School of Mortars) as written, consistent with the November appendix.
- The signature "J. McEachern" is read as written; the November appendix names "Lieut. J.N. McEachern", which supports the surname reading, but the signature itself is not read with full confidence.
- Places read as: Les Brebis only. No entry breaks off mid-sentence; every day 1–28 February has an entry.
