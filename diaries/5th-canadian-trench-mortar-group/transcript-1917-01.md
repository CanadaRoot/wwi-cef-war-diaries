# War Diary — 5th Canadian Divisional Trench Mortar Group — January 1917

- **Unit:** 5th Canadian Divisional Trench Mortar Group (Trench Mortar Batteries, 5th C.D.A.), 5th Canadian Division
- **Month:** January 1917
- **Source:** Library and Archives Canada, Collection Search Item ID 2004821 (RG 9 III-D-3, vol. 4980, file 594, reel T-10809)
- **Pages:** January 1917, p. 1 – p. 6 (image files page-0001 – page-0006)
- **January 1917 — COMPLETE** (pages 1–6): cover, contents, diary text 18–31 January, Appendix II. Appendix I (listed in the contents) is not among the images supplied. Transcribed 2026-10-28.

## Transcription conventions
Original spelling, punctuation and abbreviations kept. Diary text is handwritten throughout (Army Form C. 2118, "WAR DIARY or INTELLIGENCE SUMMARY" — the unused heading is heavily struck through on these sheets). `[uncertain]` marks words I could not read with confidence — never guessed. Each day's entry is initialled "W.H.A." (Capt. W. H. Abbott, D.T.M.O.); the diary is signed by him at the foot of sheets I and III. The Place column reads "Witley Camp" throughout; the Hour column is not used; the "Remarks and references to Appendices" column is blank except for 18 January (Appendix I and " II). A circular stamp reading "TRENCH MORTAR BATTERIES 17 FEB. 1917 4th CAN. DIV." is struck across the top of each diary sheet. The printed footer reads "A5834 Wt. W4973/M687 750,000 8/16 D. D. & L. Ltd. Forms/C.2118/13." "T.M.B." = Trench Mortar Battery; "C.D.A." = Canadian Divisional Artillery; "D.A.C." = Divisional Ammunition Column; "D.T.M.O." = Divisional Trench Mortar Officer; "C.R.A." = Commander Royal Artillery; "C.F.A." = Canadian Field Artillery; "C.I." = Canadian Infantry; "O.R." = Other Ranks; "W.O." = War Office; "A.G." = Adjutant General; "D.R.O." = Divisional Routine Order; "Res. Bde." = Reserve Brigade; "q.m." = quartermaster; "Btn." = Battalion; "Bde." = Brigade.

## Open questions / uncertain items (January 1917)
- Cover: "from Jan 23rd 1917" — "23rd" is circled and "18th" written above it; the diary itself begins 18 January. Transcribed as a correction.
- Contents: "Diary Text 3½ Pages" — the diary text is in fact 3 sheets (I–III, pages 3–5).
- Contents lists "Appendix I & 2" but only Appendix II is among the images supplied; Appendix I is absent from this set.
- The diary begins 18 January 1917; there are no entries for 1–17 January. The 18 January entry records men and stores taken on 12 January, before the diary opens.
- Page 3: a handwritten addition over the circular "17 FEB. 1917" stamp is not read with confidence; recorded as [uncertain].
- 25 January: the surname of the sixth officer taken on is read as "McClanaghan. S.L." as written; it is spelled "McCLENAGHAN" on 1 February and "McLENAGHAN" on 16 April — three spellings across the diary, each transcribed as written.
- 31 January: "transferred from surplus 1 ist. [uncertain] 4 C.D.A." — the word after "surplus" is read as "1 ist." but may be "list"; recorded as [uncertain].
- Appendix II: the Brigade Major's signature (Major, R.F.A.) is not read with confidence; recorded as [uncertain]. The "AG 21-2-20" file reference and the struck-through word in the letterhead are as written.
- The officer signing the diary is read as "W.H. Abbott" (initials "W.H.A." at the end of each day's entry); he signs as Lt. on sheets I–II and as Capt. on sheet III. No other officers are named by full name in January beyond the lists of 18, 25 and 31 January.
- No human casualties are recorded anywhere in this month: no killed, wounded or missing are mentioned. No firing took place — the batteries had no guns and no authority for equipment (18, 21, 26 and 29 January).
- Places read as: Witley Camp (spelled "Witley Camp"). No map references are given.
