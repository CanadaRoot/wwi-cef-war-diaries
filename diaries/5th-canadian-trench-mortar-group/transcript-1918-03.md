# War Diary — 5th Canadian Divisional Trench Mortar Group — March 1918

- **Unit:** 5th Canadian Divisional Trench Mortar Group (5th Canadian Divisional Trench Mortar Brigade), 5th Canadian Division
- **Month:** March 1918
- **Source:** Library and Archives Canada, Collection Search Item ID 2004821 (RG 9 III-D-3, vol. 4980, file 594, reel T-10809)
- **Pages:** March 1918, p. 1 – p. 4 (image files page-0103 – page-0106)
- **March 1918 — COMPLETE** (pages 1–4): cover, contents, diary text 1–31 March, no appendix. Transcribed 2026-10-28.

## Transcription conventions
Original spelling, punctuation and abbreviations kept. Diary text is handwritten throughout (Army Form C. 2118, "WAR DIARY or INTELLIGENCE SUMMARY" — "INTELLIGENCE SUMMARY" is struck through on both diary sheets). `[uncertain]` marks words I could not read with confidence — never guessed. The Place column reads "Les Brebis" 1–14 March, "Bruay" 15–22 March, "Les Brebis" 23–30 March and "Maroeuil" 31 March; the Hour column is used for the Bruay rest-period parades and drills. The "Remarks and references to Appendices" column carries a short initialled mark against each day's entry; it is the same mark throughout and is not read with confidence, so it is not transcribed entry by entry. The diary sheets are headed "Trench Mortar Brigade, 5th Canadian Divl. Artillery" — the unit is called a Brigade, not a Group, on the March sheets — and are signed at the foot by the adjutant; the signature appears to read "Frank McEachern" and is flagged [uncertain] (see open questions). The printed footer on the diary sheets reads "9353 Wt. W2541/1454 700,000 5/15 D. D. & L. A.D.S.S./Forms/C. 2118." "T.M." = Trench Mortar; "C.D.A." = Canadian Divisional Artillery; "D.T.M.O." = Divisional Trench Mortar Officer; "S.O.S." = as written; "H.Q." = Headquarters; "M.G." = Machine Gun. Map references are read as written and are not verified.

## Pages 3–4 — Diary text (1–31 March 1918)

>

> **4/3/18.** — Operations: Fired 140 Rounds Retaliation and S.O.S.
>

> **6/3/18.** — Operations: Fired 75 Rounds in Retaliation for Hostile T.M. activity.

## Open questions / uncertain items (March 1918)
- Cover reads "VOLUME 15" with the "15" written above a struck-through character; contents page agrees on Volume 15. The cover carries no "With appendix 'A'" note and the contents page lists no appendix; there is no appendix in the March file. A "2" appears at the bottom centre of the cover; it is not used as a page number.
- The March sheets head the unit "Trench Mortar Brigade, 5th Canadian Divl. Artillery" and the adjutant signs "5th C.D. T.M. Bde." / "5th C.D.A T.M. Brigade" — the unit is called a Brigade in March, a Group in November–February.
- 11 March: 5 men admitted to hospital gassed; the men are not named and no appendix lists them.
- 14 March: orders to proceed on rest received; the group moved to the Bruay area for rest and training. The Place column reads "Bruay" 15–22 March.
- 15 March: a word in "Parade: [uncertain] 120 men present." is not read. 16 March: the 10:00 A.M. activity is not read; transcribed as [uncertain]. 20 March: the word before "Parade" in the 9:00 A.M. line is not read; it may be "General".
- 22 March: the route back to Les Brebis is not read; transcribed as [uncertain].
- 23 March: the group took over the Hill 70 Section at Lens from the 1st Canadian Division's T.M. Brigade.
- 28 March: the group turned the Hill 70 Section over to the 46th Division's Trench Mortar Group and stood to awaiting orders to move.
- 30 March: the route from Les Brebis to Maroeuil reads "via Bully Grenay, Loos, [uncertain] Mount St Eloy to Maroeuil"; the word before "Mount St Eloy" is not read. "Billets: Other Ranks billeted in a large Brick Stable."
- 31 March: "attached to 5th C.D.A.B" — "C.D.A.B" is read as written and flagged [uncertain]; it may be an abbreviation for the 5th C.D.A. Brigade.
- The March signatures appear to read "Frank McEachern" — flagged [uncertain]. The December–February sheets appear to read "J. McEachern", and the November appendix names "Lieut. J.N. McEachern"; the first name on the March sheets is not read with confidence and may be a flourish of the same signature.
- Places read as: Les Brebis; Bruay; Maroeuil; Bully Grenay; Loos; Mount St Eloy; Hill 70; Lens. No entry breaks off mid-sentence; every day 1–31 March has an entry.
