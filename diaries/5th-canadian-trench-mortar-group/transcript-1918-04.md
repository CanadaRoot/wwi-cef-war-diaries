# War Diary — 5th Canadian Divisional Trench Mortar Group — April 1918

- **Unit:** 5th Canadian Divisional Trench Mortar Group (5th Canadian Divisional Trench Mortar Brigade), 5th Canadian Division
- **Month:** April 1918
- **Source:** Library and Archives Canada, Collection Search Item ID 2004821 (RG 9 III-D-3, vol. 4980, file 594, reel T-10809)
- **Pages:** April 1918, p. 1 – p. 5 (image files page-0107 – page-0111)
- **April 1918 — COMPLETE** (pages 1–5): cover, contents, diary text 1–30 April, Appendix I (casualties 10–28 April). Transcribed 2026-10-28.

## Transcription conventions
Original spelling, punctuation and abbreviations kept. Diary text is handwritten throughout (Army Form C. 2118, "WAR DIARY or INTELLIGENCE SUMMARY" — "INTELLIGENCE SUMMARY" is struck through on these sheets). `[uncertain]` marks words I could not read with confidence — never guessed. Unlike the May–July sheets, the April entries carry no initials at the end of each entry; both diary sheets are signed at the foot by the Adjutant. Each row carries a large flourish in the "Remarks and references to Appendices" column, not transcribed. The Place column gives MAROEUIL 1–17 April and GIVENCHY 18–30 April; the Hour column is blank throughout. "T.M." = Trench Mortar; "C.D.T.M." = Canadian Divisional Trench Mortar; "C.D.A.C." = Canadian Divisional Ammunition Column; "C.F.A." = Canadian Field Artillery; "D.T.M.O." = Divisional Trench Mortar Officer; "O.C." = Officer Commanding; "O.R." = Other Rank; "Bty" = Battery; "C.C.S." = Casualty Clearing Station; "Bdr." = Bombardier; "Gnr." = Gunner; "rds" = rounds. "Oppy" and "Avion-Lens" are sectors of the front; "Mericourt" is spelled "MERICOURT".

## Pages 3–4 — Diary text (1–30 April 1918)

Army Form C. 2118, "WAR DIARY or INTELLIGENCE SUMMARY". The printed footer reads "2353 Wt. W2544/1454 700,000 5/15 D.D.&L. A.D.S.S./Forms/C. 2118." The heading on both sheets reads "Trench Mortar Brigade 5th Canadian Divl. Artillery / Month of APRIL 1918."

### Page 3 — 1–19 April 1918 (Sheet I)

Place: Maroeuil 1–17 April; Givenchy 18–19 April.

> **1/4/18.** — Orders cancelled re attachment to 3rd C.D.A.C. 6 Other Ranks attached to 13th Brigade C.F.A.
>
> **2/4/18.** — 5th C.D.T.M. Brigade attached to 4th Canadian Division, and we took over the OPPY Section of that front.
>
> **3/4/18.** — D.T.M.O. Locating Emplacements for Trench Mortars in Oppy Section.
>
> **3/4/18.** — Putting Guns in action in Oppy Section.
>
> **4/4/18.** — Same as for 3.4.18.
>
> **5/4/18.** — Two 6" T.M's. put in action and registered, and usual daily shelling
>
> **6/4/18.** — Strength:- 10 Officers and 182 Other Ranks. Usual daily shelling
>
> **7/4/18.** — 4-6" T.M's placed in action and registered. Usual daily shelling
>
> **8/4/18.** — Only usual daily shelling.
>
> **9/4/18.** — Same as for 8.4.18.
>
> **10/4/18.** — Casualties: 1 Other Rank Wounded. Usual daily shelling
>
> **11/4/18.** — Usual daily shelling.
>
> **12/4/18.** — Same as for 11/4/18.
>
> **13/4/18.** — Strength:- 10 Officers. 179 Other Ranks. Usual Daily shelling.
>
> **14/4/18.** — Usual Daily shelling
>
> **15/4/18.** — Usual Daily shelling. Orders received to move to GIVENCHY and take over AVION-LENS Section of 2nd Canadian Division Y. Battery to remain in MERICOURT Section.
>
> **16/4/18.** — Proceeded from MAROEUIL to Wagon Lines at Fort George.
>
> **17/4/18.** — Took over LENS-AVION Section of 2nd Canadian Division front, and commenced digging emplacements for Trench Mortars.
>
> **18/4/18.** — Placed 2-6" T.M's. in action and registered them.
>
> **19/4/18.** — Usual Daily Shelling and preparing emplacements for further Trench Mortars.
>
> [Signed, bottom right:] *[signature — J. Meakins [uncertain]] Lieut. & Adjt. / 5th C.D.T.M. Brigade*

### Page 4 — 20–30 April 1918 (Sheet II)

Place: Givenchy throughout.

> **20/4/18.** — Building Emplacements for Trench Mortars. Carried out Harassing Fire on Enemy Trench Mortars.
>
> **21/4/18.** — Strength:- 9 Officers 182 Other Ranks. Usual Daily Harassing Fire
>
> **22/4/18.** — Operations:- Successful shoot on Hostile Trench Mortar with Aeroplane observation, also usual daily Harassing Fire.
>
> **23/4/18.** — Strength Increase:- 1 Officer Taken on strength from 5th C.D.A.C. Operations:- Usual Daily shelling.
>
> **24/4/18.** — Operations:- Usual Daily shelling.
>
> **25/4/18.** — Operations:- Usual Harassing and Retaliation fire. Two - 6" Trench Mortars placed in action.
>
> **26/4/18.** — Operations:- Very little firing today owing to ammunition supply being very short.
>
> **27/4/18.** — Strength Increase:- 1 Officer reported for duty. Operations:- Usual daily shelling
>
> **28/4/18.** — Casualties:- 1 Other Rank Killed 4 Other Ranks wounded Operations:- Usual daily shelling
>
> **29/4/18.** — Operations:- Counter Mortar and Harassing fire carried out.
>
> **30/4/18.** — Operations:- Very little firing owing to poor visibility
>
> [Signed, bottom right:] *[signature — J. Meakins [uncertain]] Lieut. & Adjt. / 5th C.D.T.M. Brigade*

## Open questions / uncertain items (April 1918)
- The Adjutant's signature at the foot of both diary sheets and the appendix reads "J. Meakins" — the surname is read as "Meakins" but the hand is a flourish and the reading is not fully certain; recorded as [uncertain].
- 1 April: "6 Other Ranks attached to 13th Brigade C.F.A." — the figure "6" is read as written.
- 3 April: two entries carry the same date — the D.T.M.O. locating emplacements, and guns being put in action in the Oppy Section; transcribed as two entries.
- 15 April: "Y. Battery to remain in MERICOURT Section" — the battery letter is "Y" as written; the diary does not say where the rest of the brigade's batteries went.
- 16 April: "Wagon Lines at Fort George" — read as "Fort George"; the same place appears as "Ft. George" in May.
- 23 April: the officer taken on strength from 5th C.D.A.C. is named in Appendix I as Lieut. Basil Roy Laffer; the surname is read as "Laffer".
- 27 April: the officer who reported for duty is named in Appendix I as Lieut. Richard Charles Blundell.
- 28 April: the diary records 1 killed and 4 wounded; Appendix I names them — Bdr. Bland S.V. killed, and Gnr. Foster T., Gnr. McNab J., Gnr. Saunders S.S. and Gnr. Slinn A.H. wounded. The killed man's surname is read as "Bland".
- Appendix I gives Bdr. Bland's burial place (Plot I, Row K, Grave 10) and a map reference, "Sheet. 51 B. N.W. A.8 c 5.9." — transcribed as written; the sheet and coordinates are not verified.
- No other officers are named in April; no place is given for the 10 April casualty beyond the Place column (Maroeuil).
- Strength falls from 10 officers / 182 other ranks (6 April) to 10 officers / 179 other ranks (13 April), then 9 officers / 182 other ranks (21 April); the two strength increases (23 and 27 April) bring the officer count back up. The figures are transcribed as written and not reconciled.
