# War Diary — 5th Canadian Divisional Trench Mortar Group — May 1918

- **Unit:** 5th Canadian Divisional Trench Mortar Group (5th Canadian Divisional Trench Mortar Brigade), 5th Canadian Division
- **Month:** May 1918
- **Source:** Library and Archives Canada, Collection Search Item ID 2004821 (RG 9 III-D-3, vol. 4980, file 594, reel T-10809)
- **Pages:** May 1918, p. 1 – p. 8 (image files page-0112 – page-0119)
- **May 1918 — COMPLETE** (pages 1–8): cover, contents, diary text 1–31 May, Appendices A (casualties 23–28 May) and B (officers taken on or struck off strength) on a single sheet. Transcribed 2026-10-28.

## Transcription conventions
Original spelling, punctuation and abbreviations kept. Diary text is handwritten throughout (Army Form C. 2118, "WAR DIARY or INTELLIGENCE SUMMARY" — "INTELLIGENCE SUMMARY" is struck through on these sheets). `[uncertain]` marks words I could not read with confidence — never guessed. Every entry in May ends with the initials "C.A.B.", and each sheet is signed at the foot by the same officer, Capt. C.A.B. Purchas, C.F.A., D.T.M.O. 5 C.D.A. — the initials could also be read "A.W.P." (see open questions). The Place column uses ditto marks (",,") for repeated places; the Hour column is used only on 5, 7, 12, 13 and 16 May. "T.M." = Trench Mortar; "C.D.T.M." = Canadian Divisional Trench Mortar; "C.D.A." = Canadian Divisional Artillery; "C.D.A.C." = Canadian Divisional Ammunition Column; "C.F.A." = Canadian Field Artillery; "C.I.B." = Canadian Infantry Brigade; "D.T.M.O." = Divisional Trench Mortar Officer; "D.A.C." = Divisional Ammunition Column; "O.C." = Officer Commanding; "O.R." = Other Rank; "Bty" = Battery; "C.C.S." = Casualty Clearing Station; "C.C.R.C." = Canadian Corps Reinforcement Camp; "C.A.P.D." = Canadian Artillery Pool Depot; "R.A.F." = Royal Air Force; "K.R. & O" = King's Regulations and Orders; "P.T." = Physical Training; "Bdr." = Bombardier; "Gnr." = Gunner; "rds" = rounds; "L.G.S." = Limbered General Service (waggons). "H.V." = High Velocity (gun). Place spellings are kept as written: "Houdain" is also written "HOUDAIN"; "La Comte" / "LACOMTE" / "La Compté" all appear; "Amettes", "Ranchicourt", "Auchel", "Barlin", "Neuville St. Vaast", "Savy", "Agnières", "Anzin", "Waverley Camp", "Gavrelle", "Monchy-Breton", "Bruay".

## Pages 3–7 — Diary text (1–31 May 1918)

Army Form C. 2118, "WAR DIARY or INTELLIGENCE SUMMARY". The printed footer reads "2353 Wt. W2544/1454 700,000 5/15 D.D.&L. A.D.S.S./Forms/C. 2118."

### Page 3 — 1–10 May 1918 (Sheet I)

Heading: "5th CANADIAN DIVISION TRENCH MORTAR BRIGADE / Month of MAY 1918."

> **Givenchy, 1/5/18.** — Operations — Usual daily destructive Shoots. Whole front very quiet. C.A.B.
>
> **,, 2/5/18.** — Orders received to effect that relief of unit will be complete by May 6th and unit will move to Amettes area. C.A.B.
>
> **,, 3/5/18.** — Operations — Fired 35 rounds 6" and 13 rds. 9.45-" on destructive shoots during day X. & V. Btys. relieved. C.A.B.
>
> **,, 4/5/18.** — X & V Btys. marched to Ft. George. C.A.B. Y Bty relieved night 4th–5th C.A.B.
>
> **Ft. George, 5/5/18. [Hour: 9:00 AM]** — Bath parade. General parade 2:00 p.m. Afternoon devoted to Sports. C.A.B.
>
> **,, 6/5/18.** — Brigade moved to Houdain. Dismounted personnel by light railway. Entrained NEUVILLE ST. VAAST 9:00 a.m. detrained BARLIN 10:00 a.m. marched to HOUDAIN arrived 11:15 a.m. Stores moved by motor lorry. Move complete 4:00 p.m. C.A.B.
>
> **HOUDAIN, 7/5/18. [Hour: 6:15 AM]** — Physical drill. 3 mobile medium trench mortar batteries organized. Unit placed in Quarantine. C.A.B.
>
> **,, 8/5/18.** — Physical drill 6:15 a.m. Mobile Batteries held manouvres near RANCHICOURT. C.A.B.
>
> **,, 9/5/18.** — Same program as for 8/5/18. C.A.B.
>
> **,, 10/5/18.** — Program carried out as for 8/5/18. manouvres held near LA COMTE. C.A.B.
>
> [Signed, bottom right:] *[signature — C.A.B. Purchas] Capt C.F.A. / D.T.M.O. 5 C.D.A.*

### Page 4 — 11–16 May 1918 (Sheet II)

Heading: "5th Canadian Division WAR DIARY / Trench Mortar Brigade / May 1918."

> **HOUDAIN, 11/5/18.** — Parades as for 8/5/18. Quarantine lifted. During manouvres near LACOMTE a scheme was carried out by which mobile mortars were brought into action against strong points holding up advance of infantry. 16 rounds fired. C.A.B.
>
> **,, 12/5/18. [Hour: 9:00 AM]** — Church parade. 1:15 P.M. manouvres to La Comte. C.A.B.
>
> **,, 13/5/18. [Hour: 6:15 AM]** — P.T. parade. 8:15 a.m. Batt parade. 1:15 P.M. Mobile batteries proceeded to La Compté and gave a demonstration of the feasibility of bringing mortars into action against strong points holding up advance of infantry. Demonstration was witnessed by 1st Army Commander, Canadian Corps Commander and G.O.C. R.A. Canadian Corps. Demonstration was successful in spite of rain and gusty wind. 41 rounds fired. C.A.B.
>
> **,, 14/5/18.** — Morning spent cleaning guns and vehicles. In afternoon a fatigue party was sent to range to fill shell holes made 13/5/18. C.A.B. Lieut R.J.E. Graham returned to 14th Bde C.F.A. C.A.B.
>
> **,, 15/5/18.** — D.T.M.O. 9 Officers and 97 O.R. leave for rehearsal of a scheme with 12th C.I.B. in vicinity of Monchy-Breton. Remainder devoted afternoon to baseball. — C.A.B.
>
> **,, 16/5/18. [Hour: 6:30 AM]** — P.T. parade. 9:00 A.M. Gun laying and practice knocking down and assembling sub-td [uncertain] for mobile mortars. Afternoon route march and games. C.A.B.
>
> [Signed, bottom right:] *[signature — C.A.B. Purchas] Capt C.F.A. / D.T.M.O. 5 C.D.A.*

### Page 5 — 17–24 May 1918 (Sheet III)

Heading: "5th Canadian Division / WAR DIARY / Trench Mortar Brigade. / May 1918."

> **HOUDAIN, 17/5/18.** — Program carried out as for 16/5/18. Mobile sections returned from manouvres which were very successful. 43 rounds expended. C.A.B.
>
> **,, 18/5/18.** — 6:15 A.M. P.T. parade 9:00 A.M. Batt parade. 2:00 P.M. Route march to Auchel. Majority of men went in swimming. C.A.B.
>
> **,, 19/5/18.** — 9:00 A.M. Church Parade. Whole afternoon devoted to sports and games. C.A.B.
>
> **,, 20/5/18.** — 3 mobile sections proceeded to Auchel area [for — struck through] to take part in scheme with 3rd C.D.A. C.A.B. Details were given foot drill, rifle drill and gas drill during forenoon and played ball during afternoon. C.A.B.
>
> **,, 21/5/18.** — 6:15: Physical Training parade. 9:00 A.M. Muster parade and reading of K.R. & O and Army Act. In the afternoon a base ball game was played with U.S. mechanics attached to a R.A.F. squadron near Bruay, score 17-10 favour of U.S.A. C.A.B. Orders received to move to Agnières. C.A.B. Several bombs dropped in Houdain 11:15 P.M. C.A.B.
>
> **,, 22/5/18.** — Move carried out. Personnel marched to Barlin arriving 9:15, entrained Barlin travelled via light railway to Savy marched to Agnières. Stores moved by motor transport. Move complete 3:45 P.M. C.A.B.
>
> **Agnières, 23/5/18.** — Usual 6:15 parade. Gun parks laid out. Sports in afternoon. Another bombing raid. 7 our men wounded by one bomb. 1 died of wounds at C.C.S. C.A.B. [Remarks column: "See App. A."]
>
> **,, 24/5/18.** — Usual parades cancelled on account of rain C.A.B. 2:00 P.M. Inspection of gas respirators held in billets. Orders received to move on 25/5/18 to Anzin area C.A.B.
>
> [Signed, bottom right:] *[signature — C.A.B. Purchas] Capt C.F.A. / D.T.M.O. 5 C.D.A.*

### Page 6 — 25–30 May 1918 (Sheet IV)

Heading: "5th Canadian Division Artillery / WAR DIARY / Trench Mortar Brigade / May 1918."

> **Agnières, 25/5/18.** — Moved to Waverley Camp in Anzin area. Personnel moved by light railway from Savy to Anzin. Stores moved by motor transport. Move complete 2:36 P.M. Gun parks laid out. Camp shelled by H.V. gun but no damage done. C.A.B.
>
> **Waverley Camp, 26/5/18.** — Parades held as for 23/5/18. D.T.M.O. and battery commanders went forward to see positions to be taken over. Lieut B.R. Laffer transferred to 55th Bty [uncertain] C.A.B. [Remarks column: "App B"]
>
> **,, 27/5/18.** — 5 Officers 43 O.R. went into line 8:45 a.m. took over three 6" Newton T.M. two active one silent in Gavrelle sector. Guns taken over from 2nd Imperial Division T.Ms. One man wounded. C.A.B. 10 O.Rs. [uncertain] from C.C.R.C. C.A.B. [Remarks column: "App. A."]
>
> **,, 28/5/18.** — Operations — 30 rounds fired in harassing fire on enemy trenches. 9 additional O.Rs went forward. 6 L.G.S. waggons attached from 5 C.D.A.C. Parades at waggon lines as on 23/5/18 C.A.B.
>
> **,, 29/5/18.** — Operations — 10 rounds harassing fire on enemy trenches. 20 O.Rs attached from 5-C.D.A.C. as carrying party. 2 mobile Trench mortar sections placed in readiness and personell detailed. Weather warm and bright. C.A.B.
>
> **,, 30/5/18.** — Weather — Bright and warm. Operations — 30 rounds fired on hostile Trench mortars and harassing fire on enemy's trenches. X Battery took over one more 6" mortar from X2 T.M.B. Waggon line parades as usual. C.A.B.
>
> [Signed, bottom right:] *[signature — C.A.B. Purchas] Capt C.F.A. / D.T.M.O. 5 C.D.A.*

## Open questions / uncertain items (May 1918)
- The initials at the end of every May entry read "C.A.B.", and the signature at the foot of each sheet reads "C.A.B. Purchas". The signature's Christian-name initials could also be read "A.W.", which would make the initials "A.W.P."; recorded as "C.A.B." as written. The surname is read as "Purchas".
- 3 May: "13 rds. 9.45-"" — the calibre is read as 9.45-inch; the diary does not name the gun.
- 13 May: the word "witnessed" in "Demonstration was witnessed by 1st Army Commander" appears struck through and rewritten; transcribed as written. The demonstration was watched by the 1st Army Commander, the Canadian Corps Commander and the G.O.C. R.A. Canadian Corps — none is named.
- 16 May: "assembling sub-td [uncertain]" — the word after "assembling" appears to read "sub-td" and is not expanded; it may be an abbreviation for a mortar part.
- 20 May: the word "for" before "to take part in scheme" is struck through; transcribed as a struck-through word.
- 21 May: "score 17-10 favour of U.S.A." — the baseball game was against U.S. mechanics attached to a R.A.F. squadron near Bruay; transcribed as written.
- 23 May: the diary says 7 men wounded by one bomb and 1 died of wounds at a C.C.S., but Appendix A names only 4 men wounded on 23-5-18 (Talbot, Smith J.N., Tarte, Stark) — Talbot is the man who died of wounds on 24-5-18. The other three wounded men are not named in the appendix; the figures are transcribed as written and not reconciled.
- 26 May: "Lieut B.R. Laffer transferred to 55th Bty [uncertain]" — the destination appears to read "55th Bty"; Appendix B instead says Laffer was struck off strength to 13th Brigade, C.F.A., on the same date. The two are transcribed as written and not reconciled.
- 27 May: "10 O.Rs. [uncertain] from C.C.R.C." — the word between "O.Rs." and "from" appears to read "repost" and is not read with confidence; it may be "reported" or "reposted". The diary records one man wounded this day; Appendix A names Bdr. Smith A.B. wounded 28-5-18, not 27-5-18 — transcribed as written.
- 28 May: "6 L.G.S. waggons attached from 5 C.D.A.C." — "L.G.S." is read as written and not expanded with confidence.
- 31 May: "XII put into action" — read as "XII"; it may mean X Battery's second mortar, or a battery designated XII. The raid supported was by the "4th or 5th Black Watch" — the "or" is written as a fraction-like "or" between the two numbers.
- The contents page gives the casualty appendix range as "25-5-18 to 28-5-18", but Appendix A itself lists casualties dated 23-5-18 and 28-5-18; transcribed as written.
- The 6 May move: the Place column gives "Ft. George" for 5–6 May and "HOUDAIN" from 7 May; the brigade entrained at Neuville St. Vaast, detrained at Barlin and marched to Houdain. The 22 May move went by light railway from Barlin via Savy to Agnières; the 25 May move went by light railway from Savy to Anzin (Waverley Camp).
