# War Diary — 5th Canadian Divisional Trench Mortar Group — December 1917

- **Unit:** 5th Canadian Divisional Trench Mortar Group (5th Canadian Divisional Trench Mortar Batteries), 5th Canadian Division
- **Month:** December 1917
- **Source:** Library and Archives Canada, Collection Search Item ID 2004821 (RG 9 III-D-3, vol. 4980, file 594, reel T-10809)
- **Pages:** December 1917, p. 1 – p. 7 (image files page-0085 – page-0091)
- **December 1917 — COMPLETE** (pages 1–7): cover, contents, title sheet, diary text 1–31 December, appendix (casualties). Transcribed 2026-10-28.

## Transcription conventions
Original spelling, punctuation and abbreviations kept. Diary text is handwritten throughout (Army Form C. 2118, "WAR DIARY or INTELLIGENCE SUMMARY" — "INTELLIGENCE SUMMARY" is struck through on the diary sheets; the appendix sheet is headed "INTELLIGENCE SUMMARY"). `[uncertain]` marks words I could not read with confidence — never guessed. The Place column reads "Les Brebis" on every diary sheet; the Hour column is not used. The "Remarks and references to Appendices" column carries a short initialled mark against each day's entry; it is the same mark throughout and is not read with confidence, so it is not transcribed entry by entry. The diary sheets are signed at the foot "J. McEachern Lieut for Capt D.T.M.O. 5 C.T.M.G." — the surname is read as "McEachern" (consistent with the November appendix entry "Lieut. J.N. McEachern") but is not read with full confidence; see open questions. The printed footer on the diary sheets reads "9353 Wt. W2541/1454 700,000 5/15 D. D. & L. A.D.S.S./Forms/C. 2118." — a different form printing from November. "T.M." = Trench Mortar; "T.M.G." = Trench Mortar Group; "C.D.A." = Canadian Divisional Artillery; "C.F.A." = Canadian Field Artillery; "D.T.M.O." = Divisional Trench Mortar Officer; "D.A." = Divisional Artillery; "N.C.O." = Non-Commissioned Officer; "C.C.S." = Casualty Clearing Station; "H.Q." = Headquarters; "M.G." = Machine Gun; "S.O.S." = as written. Map references are read as written and are not verified.

## Pages 4–6 — Diary text (1–31 December 1917)

### Page 4 — 1–11 December 1917 (Sheet 1)

Place: Les Brebis.

> **1/12/17.** — Strength: 7 Officers and 180 Other Ranks.
>
> Operations: Fired 26 Rounds on Oval Brickstacks and enemy wire.
>
> Work: Commenced work on No. 3. Emplacement for 9.45" T.M.
>
> **2/12/17.** — Weather: Fine.
>
> Operations: Fired 47 Rounds on Enemy T.M. Emplacements and Communications Trenches.
>
> **3/12/17.** — Operations: Fired 15 Rounds by Aeroplane Observation with good results.
>
> Weather: Very clear.
>
> **4/12/17.** — Operations: Fired 60 Rounds on Enemy T.M. Emplacements.
>
> Strength Decrease: 37 Other Ranks transferred to 13th & 14th Brigades C.F.A.
>
> Work: Nearing completion of No. 3. Emplacement.
>
> **5/12/17.** — Operations: Fired 70 Rounds on Enemy T.M. Emplacements.
>
> Works: Telephone Communication to all Guns on Hill 70 completed with Central Exchange at [uncertain] 5000 connected with Artillery Groups and Headquarters in Les Brebis. Same works very satisfactory.
>
> **6/12/17.** — Operations: Fired 60 Rounds on Enemy Communication Trenches.
>
> Weather: Frosty and Visibility good.
>
> **7/12/17.** — Operations: Heavy T.M. Battery fired 20 Rounds on Enemy T.M. at request of Infantry and silenced same.
> Medium Batteries fired 60 Rounds on Enemy wire.
>
> **8/12/17.** — Operations: Fired 50 Rounds on Enemy T.M's. & Support Trenches.
>
> Weather: Fine.
>
> **9/12/17.** — Operations: Medium Batteries fired 89 Rounds on Enemy T.M. Emplacements and Front Line.
> Heavy Battery fired 20 Rounds on Enemy Communication Trenches.
>
> **10/12/17.** — Operations: Medium Batteries fired 40 Rounds on Enemy T.M. Emplacements.
> Heavy Battery fired 30 Rounds on Enemy Front Line and Oval Stacks.
>
> Courses: Two N.C.O's. proceeded on Tunnelling Course at Hersin [uncertain].
>
> **11/12/17.** — Operations: Heavy Battery fired 10 Rounds on Enemy Communication Trenches.
>
> Works: No. 3. Gun Pit finished and Gun put into position.
>
> [Signed, bottom right:] *J. McEachern Lieut for Capt D.T.M.O. 5 C.T.M.G.*

### Page 5 — 12–23 December 1917 (Sheet 2)

Place: Les Brebis.

> **12/12/17.** — [Hour: 2 P.M.] Parades: Parade of Heavy Battery for re-organization into Groups.
>
> Operations: Nil. Orders received limiting ammunition expenditure to 60 Rounds per day.
>
> Visibility: Poor. Heavy mist.
>
> **13/12/17.** — Operations: 20 Rounds fired on Enemy T.M. Emplacements.
>
> Works: T.M.G. 5th C.D.A. & T.M.G. 11th D.A. organised into 3 Groups ie Left. Centre and Right Groups. Each Group Commanded by an Officer and all Administrative Work done by D.T.M.O. 11th D.A. Each Group Commander lives at Artillery Group Headquarters.
>
> **14/12/17.** — Visibility: Poor all Day.
>
> Operations: 3rd Heavy Trench Mortar registered and fired 15 Rounds on [uncertain] des Mines.
> Medium Batteries fired 26 Rounds on Enemy front line trench.
>
> Attachments: 3 Officers attached for Duty.
>
> **15/12/17.** — Operations: Fired 60 Rounds on Enemy Wire.
>
> **16/12/17.** — Weather: Slight snow flurries.
>
> Operations: Fired 50 Rounds on Enemy Trench Mortars and Wire.
>
> **17/12/17.** — Weather: Very frosty and considerable snow on the ground.
>
> Operations: 65 Rounds fired on Enemy Wire.
>
> Miscellaneous: 30 Germans came over to our lines and surrendered.
>
> **18/12/17.** — Operations: Fired 75 Rounds on Enemy T.M. Emplacements.
>
> Works: Concrete bed put in for 6" Trench Mortar.
>
> **19/12/17.** — Weather: Very Cold.
>
> Operations: Fired 20 Rounds on Enemy Wire.
>
> **20/12/17.** — Visibility: Very poor.
>
> Operations: Nil.
>
> **21/12/17.** — Visibility: Very poor.
>
> Operations: Nil.
>
> **22/12/17.** — Operations: Fired 40 Rounds on Enemy Machine Gun Emplacement.
> Fired 15 Rounds on Time [uncertain]. Des. Mines
>
> **23/12/17.** — Operations: Fired 35 Rounds on Enemy Trench Mortars, 12 Rounds on Enemy movement and 30 Rounds on Enemy Wire.
>
> [Signed, bottom right:] *J. McEachern Lieut for Capt D.T.M.O. 5 C.T.M.G.*

### Page 6 — 24–31 December 1917 (Sheet 3)

Place: Les Brebis.

> **24/12/17.** — Visibility: Very poor.
>
> Operations: Fired 5 Rounds on active Hostile T.M. and obtained a direct hit.
>
> **25/12/17.** — Weather: Heavy fall of snow during night and snow flurries all day.
>
> Operations: Fired one Round on Enemy Working Party inflicting several casualties.
> Fired two Rounds at request of Infantry on Enemy Machine Gun.
> Fired 14 Rounds on Enemy T.M's.
>
> **26/12/17.** — Visibility: Good.
>
> Operations: Fired 19 Rounds on Enemy Trench Mortars.
>
> **27/12/17.** — Operations: Fired 66 Rounds at request of Infantry on Enemy Machine Gun and Trench Mortar Emplacements.
>
> **28/12/17.** — Operations: 4 Rounds fired on Enemy T.M. Emplacement.
>
> **29/12/17.** — Visibility: Fair.
>
> Operations: Nil.
>
> **30/12/17.** — Miscellaneous: Handed over Positions to Trench Mortar Group. 3rd Can. Divt arty — and took over from Trench Mortar Group. 2nd Can. Divt arty
>
> **31/12/17.** — Operations: Fired 60 Rounds on Enemy T.M. Emplacements and Enemy Wire.
>
> — " — " — "
>
> [Signed, bottom right:] *J. McEachern Lieut for Capt D.T.M.O. 5 C.T.M.G.*

## Open questions / uncertain items (December 1917)
- Cover reads "VOLUME 12" with the "12" written above a struck-through "5"; contents page agrees on Volume 12. A "5" appears at the top right of the title sheet (page 3); it is not used as a page number.
- Contents page says "Diary Text 3 1/2 Pages" but the diary text is 3 sheets (pages 4–6); the appendix sheet (page 7) is marked "Sheet 4." The "3 1/2" is as written.
- 5 December: the word before "5000" in the telephone-exchange line is not read; transcribed as [uncertain].
- 10 December: "Hersin" is read as written for the tunnelling course place; flagged [uncertain].
- 13 December: the 5th C.D.A. and 11th D.A. trench mortar groups were organised into Left, Centre and Right Groups; the D.T.M.O. 11th D.A. did all administrative work.
- 14 December: the target of the 15-round shoot reads "[uncertain] des Mines" — the word before "des Mines" is not read.
- 17 December: 30 Germans surrendered to the group's lines; no further detail is given.
- 19 December: the daily entry records no casualty, but the appendix records Gnr. Hill M.R. wounded seriously on 19-12-17; he died of wounds on 20-12-17 in No. 7 C.C.S.
- 22 December: "Fired 15 Rounds on Time. Des. Mines" — "Time" is read as written and flagged [uncertain]; it may be a fuse setting (time fuse) rather than a place.
- 25 December: the "several casualties" inflicted on the enemy working party are not numbered.
- 30 December: the group handed its positions to the 3rd Canadian Division's trench mortar group and took over positions from the 2nd Canadian Division's trench mortar group; the Place column still reads "Les Brebis" for 30 and 31 December, and no new place is named.
- The signature "J. McEachern" is read as written; the November appendix names "Lieut. J.N. McEachern", which supports the surname reading, but the signature itself is not read with full confidence.
- Places read as: Les Brebis; Oval Brickstacks / Oval Stacks; Hill 70; Hersin [uncertain]. No entry breaks off mid-sentence; every day 1–31 December has an entry.
