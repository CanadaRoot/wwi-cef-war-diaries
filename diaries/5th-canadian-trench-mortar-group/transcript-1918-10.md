# War Diary — 5th Canadian Divisional Trench Mortar Group — October 1918

- **Unit:** 5th Canadian Divisional Trench Mortar Group (5th Canadian Divisional Trench Mortar Brigade), 5th Canadian Division
- **Month:** October 1918
- **Source:** Library and Archives Canada, Collection Search Item ID 2004821 (RG 9 III-D-3, vol. 4980, file 594, reel T-10809)
- **Pages:** October 1918, p. 1 – p. 9 (image files page-0155 – page-0163)
- **October 1918 — COMPLETE** (pages 1–9): cover, contents, diary text 1–31 October (6 sheets), appendix (officers taken/struck off strength and casualties). Transcribed 2026-10-28.

## Transcription conventions
Original spelling, punctuation and abbreviations kept. Diary text is handwritten throughout (Army Form C. 2118, "WAR DIARY or INTELLIGENCE SUMMARY" — neither heading is struck through on these sheets, and the heading is handwritten "Trench Mortar Brigade / 5th Can. Div. Artillery"). `[uncertain]` marks words I could not read with confidence — never guessed. The hand is the same small cramped hand as September. The Remarks column carries "Mess." after most entries (reading uncertain — see September open questions); a few entries instead cite "APP. 'B'" or "APPEN. 'A'". The Place column is filled for 1 October ("Bourlon"), 10 October ("Marquion"), 14 October ("Escaudouvres"), 22 October ("Mastaing") and 29 October ("Denain"); other days carry ditto marks. The Hour column is blank throughout. "T.M." = Trench Mortar; "C.D.A.C." = Canadian Divisional Ammunition Column; "D.A.C." = Divisional Ammunition Column; "O.R's." = Other Ranks; "H.V's." = high-velocity (shells); "H.E." = high explosive; "C.I.B." = Canadian Infantry Brigade; "C.M.Rs." = Canadian Mounted Rifles; "D.T.M.O." = Divisional Trench Mortar Officer; "S.O.S." = struck off strength; "Gnr." = Gunner; "Bdr." = Bombardier; "Sgt." = Sergeant; "H.Q." = Headquarters; "O.P." = observation post. The printed footer on the diary sheets reads "2353 Wt. W2544/1454 700,000 5/15 D.D.&L. A.D.S.S./Forms/C. 2118."

## Pages 3–8 — Diary text (1–31 October 1918)

Army Form C. 2118, "WAR DIARY or INTELLIGENCE SUMMARY". The printed footer reads "2353 Wt. W2544/1454 700,000 5/15 D.D.&L. A.D.S.S./Forms/C. 2118."

### Page 3 — Sheet I (1–3 October 1918)

Place: Bourlon.

> **1/10/18 [date column appears to read "20/10/18" — uncertain].** — Salvaged 250 rounds 18 pdr ammunition from Quarry [uncertain] district and hauled them to 2nd Sec. D.A.C. N.W. of Bourlon. There are a number of Hun guns near our lines, calibre ranging from 3" to 8". The 8" guns — two batteries of them are in Bourlon Wood, the remainder being in the open. Lt. Williams established a H.Q. at A 8 a 10.90 and an O.P. at A 9 a 36.30.
>
> **2/10/18.** — Work of salvaging ammunition carried on with good results. Lieut K. Williamson reported for duty today. The mortar near Cambrai fired 20 rounds with excellent results at strong points in A 9 d. C.M.R. snipers co-operated and picked off Huns as they scouted for more substantial cover.
>
> **3/10/18.** — 25 rounds fired at strong points on canal bank at Cambrai. Infantry we are working with very pleased with results — Direct hits obtained. Enemy retaliation fairly heavy and consisted of H.E. and Gas. 650 rounds of 18 pr. ammunition in all have been salvaged during the last three days and drawn to D.A.C.

[Remarks column: "Mess." against 1 and 3 October; "APP. 'B'" against 2 October.]

### Page 4 — Sheet II (4–8 October 1918)

Place: Bourlon.

> **4/10/18.** — One more mortar put in today at Noyelles [uncertain] St Remy, on request of 8th C.I.B. Eleven rounds fired at machine gun posts and dugouts a number of hits being obtained. Enemy planes very active in vicinity of wagon lines. Capt McPherson [uncertain spelling — MacPherson in the appendix], D.T.M.O. wounded severely by a piece of shrapnel from a bomb.
>
> **5/10/18.** — Three mortars now in action before Cambrai. 9 rounds fired on target requested by 5 C.M.Rs, two direct hits being obtained. Mules put into a trench, making them as safe as possible from bombing.
>
> **6/10/18.** — Mortars in the lines taken over by 51st Division. Day spent in getting shell through, baths at Bourlon and getting ready for a move.
>
> **7/10/18.** — Day quiet. Usual parades and rifle inspection. Weather considerably colder.
>
> **8/10/18.** — Weather cold with light rain. No word of a move yet though rest of Division have moved.

[Remarks column: "APPEN. 'A'" and "Mess." against 4 October; "Mess." against the other entries.]

### Page 5 — Sheet III (9–15 October 1918)

Place: Bourlon.

> **9/10/18.** — Orders received tonight to move over to vicinity of New H.Q. tomorrow. All necessary arrangements made. Weather better today.

Place: Marquion.

> **10/10/18.** — Moved today to vicinity of Marquion. Fairly good billets obtained in a railway cutting and in dugouts. We are right beside Bois de Cocret [uncertain], which was apparently a German stronghold. We are not occupying the German billets in the wood as a few shells drop into it at intervals. Several enemy heavies and much ammunition left behind here by owners.
>
> **11/10/18.** — Work fixing up camp. Large quantity of good lumber and coal to be obtained from Bois de Cocret [uncertain].
>
> **12/10/18.** — Day quiet. Weather rainy again. Usual parades and inspections.
>
> **13/10/18.** — Orders received to move tomorrow to Escaudouvres [uncertain spelling] area. Preparations made. Still rainy.

Place: Escaudouvres [uncertain spelling].

> **14/10/18.** — Moved to Escaudouvres, going through Cambrai en route. Good billets obtained for both men and mules.
>
> **15/10/18.** — Work cleaning out billets and fixing stoves. Weather cool with some rain.

[Remarks column: "Mess." against the entries.]

### Page 6 — Sheet IV (16–21 October 1918)

Place: Escaudouvres [uncertain spelling].

> **16/10/18.** — Actg. D.T.M.O. Lt Boyd left to attend a demonstration of French mortars at First Army School of Mortars. Day quiet. Usual parades.
>
> **17/10/18.** — Orders received to make a reconnaissance of front held by 6th C.I.B. with object of putting in mortars to take on Wassigny-au-Bac [uncertain]. No satisfactory position could be found and as Wassigny is apparently not occupied by enemy putting in of mortars deferred for tonight.
>
> **18/10/18.** — Front again visited today. Infantry have no targets for us and do not need us at present so orders are that we stand fast for the time being. Lt. Boyd returns.
>
> **19/10/18.** — Bath parade to [uncertain]. No word yet of work in line.
>
> **20/10/18.** — Capt. McEachern returned from leave and takes over D.T.M.O. work. Orders received regarding a move tomorrow and preparations made. Billets selected in Paillencourt.
>
> **21/10/18.** — Moved off at 10.00 and reached Paillencourt at 12.00. Orders received to move further forward, so moved off again at 14.00 and arrived in Mastaing at 16.30. Billets obtained

[Remarks column: "Mess." against the entries. The 21 October entry ends without a full stop; it continues on Page 7.]

### Page 7 — Sheet V (22–26 October 1918)

Place: Mastaing.

> **22/10/18.** — There are 27 civilians in town who have no food. Endeavouring to get into touch with whoever has been appointed to look after the district and in the meantime feeding them ourselves. The people are very glad to be released from German rule and we are doing everything in our power to help them out. Lts Williams and Montgomery go forward with 2 mortars and 40 rounds of ammunition. Guns are put in near Valenciennes. We are working with 12th C.I.B.
>
> **23/10/18.** — Fired 21 rounds on targets selected by Infantry. Good results.
>
> **24/10/18.** — No firing today — some difficulty in getting ammunition. We have sent 2 G.S. wagons out in hopes of salvaging some.
>
> **25/10/18.** — Fired 43 rounds today on M.G. nests. G.S. wagons return having managed to collect some 60 bombs from Vis-en-Artois area.
>
> **26/10/18.** — Fired 40 rounds fired today. Bdr. Orr killed and Sergt. Macpherson, Bdr. McKenzie and Gnr Baxter wounded by a bomb.

[Remarks column: "Mess." against the entries; "APPEN. "B" [uncertain]" against 26 October.]

### Page 8 — Sheet VI (27–31 October 1918)

Place: Mastaing.

> **27/10/18.** — Fired 9 rounds on Minnenwerfer [uncertain] with good results. Bdr. Orr buried today at D 15 d 85.50.
>
> **28/10/18.** — Fired 30 rounds, 15 on Minnenwerfer [uncertain] and 15 to cover bridging operations.

Place: Denain.

> **29/10/18.** — Headquarters moved to Denain today and good billets obtained. 15 rounds fired — area shoots.
>
> **30/10/18.** — 15 rounds registration. Took in 170 rounds tonight.
>
> **31/10/18.** — 15 rounds registration. Everything ready for the attack. Four guns in action with 100 rounds at each.

[Remarks column: "Mess." against the entries.]

## Open questions / uncertain items (October 1918)
- The contents page lists Appendix B as "Order issued by 5th Cdn. Divl. T.M. Group", but the appendix page (p. 9) contains only the officers taken/struck off strength list (headed APPENDIX "B") and the casualty list (APPENDIX A); no order is included. Both as written.
- 1 October: the Date column appears to read "20/10/18". It is treated as 1 October 1918 — the following entries are 2 and 3 October, and the sheet covers 1–3 October — but the reading is flagged.
- 1 October: "from Quarry [uncertain] district" — the word after "Quarry" could not be read.
- 2 October: "Lieut K. Williamson" is confirmed by the appendix, which gives his full name as "Lieut Kenneth Williamson. Taken on Strength 2-10-18."
- 4 October: "at Noyelles [uncertain] St Remy" — the first part of the place name is not fully certain. "Capt McPherson" in the diary is spelled "MacPherson" in the appendix, which gives his full name as "Capt Stanley MacPherson".
- 10–11 October: "Bois de Cocret [uncertain]" — the wood's name is read as written and is not verified.
- 13–14 October: the place is written "Escaudouvres" (Place column and text); the spelling is flagged as uncertain.
- 17 October: "Wassigny-au-Bac [uncertain]" — the place name is read as written and is not verified.
- 19 October: "Bath parade to [uncertain]" — the destination could not be read.
- 21 October: the entry ends without a full stop ("Billets obtained") and the 22 October entry begins a new subject on the next sheet; no text appears to be missing. The destination "Mastaing" is confirmed by the Place column on 22 October.
- 26 October: the diary writes "Fired 40 rounds fired today" — the repeated "fired" is as written. The appendix gives the wounded as Sgt J. A. McPherson, Bdr McKenzie and Gnr. Baxter R. H., and the killed man as Bdr. Orr C. A., matching the diary.
- 27–28 October: "Minnenwerfer [uncertain]" — the target is read as written (German Minenwerfer); the spelling is not certain.
- 26 October remarks: the appendix reference reads "APPEN. "B" [uncertain]" — it may be "A", since the casualties are in Appendix A.
- Appendix: "Capt. John Neil McEachern Taken on Strength 15-4-18 [uncertain date]" — the date reads "15-4-18" but is odd in context (McEachern returned from leave on 20 October and took over D.T.M.O. work); it may read "15-10-18". Recorded as written with the flag.
- Appendix: Bdr McKenzie's initials could not be read.
- Places read as: Bourlon; Bourlon Wood; Cambrai; Noyelles [uncertain] St Remy; Marquion; Bois de Cocret [uncertain]; Escaudouvres [uncertain spelling]; Wassigny-au-Bac [uncertain]; Paillencourt; Mastaing; Valenciennes; Vis-en-Artois; Denain. Map references are given as written (A 8 a 10.90; A 9 a 36.30; A 9 d; D 15 d 85.50) and are not verified.
- Casualties this month (appendix): 4 October — Capt. S. MacPherson wounded (severely, by shrapnel from a bomb, per the diary). 26 October — Bdr. Orr C. A. killed in action; Sgt J. A. McPherson, Bdr McKenzie and Gnr. Baxter R. H. wounded. Bdr. Orr was buried 27 October at D 15 d 85.50.
