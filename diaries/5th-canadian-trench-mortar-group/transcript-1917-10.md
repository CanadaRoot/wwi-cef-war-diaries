# War Diary — 5th Canadian Divisional Trench Mortar Group — October 1917

- **Unit:** 5th Canadian Divisional Trench Mortar Group (styled "Trench Mortar Group, 5th Canadian Divisional Artillery"), 5th Canadian Division
- **Month:** October 1917
- **Source:** Library and Archives Canada, Collection Search Item ID 2004821 (RG 9 III-D-3, vol. 4980, file 594, reel T-10809)
- **Pages:** October 1917, p. 1 – p. 7 (image files page-0066 – page-0072)
- **October 1917 — COMPLETE** (pages 1–7): cover, contents, diary text 1–31 October, Appendices 1 and 2. **1–14 October is a single grouped entry** (all personnel attached to the 2nd and 4th C.D.A. trench mortar groups); there are no separate daily entries for 2–13 October. Transcribed 2026-10-28.

## Transcription conventions
Original spelling, punctuation and abbreviations kept. Diary text is handwritten throughout, on ruled sheets (printed footer "T.124. Wt. W708—776. 500000. 4/15. Sir J. C. & S.") rather than the Army Form C. 2118 used in June–September; the heading reads "WAR DIARY or [second heading struck through]" with "Trench Mortar Group. 5th Canadian Divisional Artillery. Month of October 1917." written in at the top. `[uncertain]` marks words I could not read with confidence — never guessed. Each entry is initialled in the "Remarks and references to Appendices" column with the monogram "C. M. J. P.", transcribed as written. The Place column gives Sains-En-Gohelle for 1–16 October and Les Brebis for 17–31 October, with ditto marks (") for repeated places. The diary sheets are stamped "TRENCH-MORTAR-BATTERIES 5TH CAN. DIV. — 1 NOV. 1917 Sheet 1", "Sheet 2" and "Sheet 3". "T.M." = Trench Mortar; "O.R." = Other Ranks; "C.D.A." = Canadian Divisional Artillery; "C.D.A.C." = Canadian Divisional Ammunition Column; "T.M.G." = Trench Mortar Group; "S.O.S." = Struck Off Strength; "T.O.S." = Taken On Strength; "O.P." = Observation Post; "B.M." = Brigade Major; "D.T.M.O." = Divisional Trench Mortar Officer; "C.T.M.B." = Canadian Trench Mortar Battery; "B.M.B." is as typed in Appendix 2 (presumably Trench Mortar Battery).

## Pages 3–5 — Diary text (1–31 October 1917)

Ruled sheets, printed footer "T.124. Wt. W708—776. 500000. 4/15. Sir J. C. & S."

### Page 3 — 1–21 October 1917 (Sheet 1)

> **1/10/17. to 14/10/17.** — Place: Sains-En-Gohelle. All personnel of Trench Mortar Group, 5th C.D.A. were attached to T.M.G. 2nd C.D.A. and Trench Mortar Group, 4th C.D.A. for duty. C. M. J. P.
>
> **15-10-17.** — Place: " [ditto — Sains-En-Gohelle] 2 A.M. Weather:— Fine. Strength:— 6 Officers. 130 Other Ranks. 3 Officers and 38 Other Ranks proceeded to Clarques on T.M. Course. 18 Other Ranks attached to 5th Divisional Train for Duty. 3 Officers + 71 O.R. moved from Barlin to Les Brebis. Strength Decrease:— 3 Other Ranks S.O.S. C. M. J. P.
>
> **16-10-17.** — Place: " [ditto — Sains-En-Gohelle] Weather:— Fine. Strength:— 6 Officers. 127 Other Ranks. Trench Mortar Group, 5th C.D.A took over Left Group & rest of 6th Divisional Artillery. C. M. J. P.
>
> **17-10-17.** — Place: Les Brebis. Weather:— Fine. Strength:— 6 Officers + 127 Other Ranks. Personnel of "X" + "Z" Batteries started building Gun Emplacements and completing emplacements in NIZAM TRENCH. Personnel of "V" Battery on T.M. Course at Clarques. C. M. J. P.
>
> **18-10-17.** — Place: " [ditto — Les Brebis] Weather:— Fine. Strength:— 6 Officers + 127 Other Ranks. Duty:— Same as detailed on 17-10-17. C. M. J. P.
>
> **19-10-17.** — Place: " [ditto — Les Brebis] Weather:— Fine. Strength:— 6 Officers + 127 Other Ranks. Duty:— Same as detailed on 17-10-17. C. M. J. P.
>
> **20-10-17.** — Place: " [ditto — Les Brebis] Weather:— Fine. Strength:— 6 Officers + 127 Other Ranks. Duty:— Same as detailed on 17-10-17. C. M. J. P.
>
> **21-10-17.** — Place: " [ditto — Les Brebis] Weather:— Fair. Visibility:— Good. Strength Increase:— 1 Other Rank. Strength:— 6 Officers + 128 Other Ranks. "X" Battery completed No. 2. Gun pit and fired three Rounds to test same, with very satisfactory results. C. M. J. P.

### Page 4 — 22–26 October 1917 (Sheet 2)

> **22-10-17.** — Place: Les Brebis. Weather:— Fair. Strength:— 6 Officers + 128 Other Ranks. Completed beds for two guns and fired seven Rounds to register same. Enemy aircraft active. C. M. J. P.
>
> **23-10-17.** — Place: " [ditto — Les Brebis] Weather:— Cold and raining during morning. Fair in afternoon. Strength increase. 3 Officers + 66 Other Ranks. Strength:— 9 Officers. 194 Other Ranks. Fired 10 Rounds on Enemy Wire. C. M. J. P.
>
> **24-10-17.** — Place: Les Brebis. Weather:— Fair. Visibility:— Good all day. Strength:— 9 Officers + 194 Other Ranks. "X" Battery fired 22 Rounds. 5 on new registration point and 17 on wire. with very satisfactory Results. Enemy retaliated on Front Line and Communication with Battery was destroyed. C. M. J. P.
>
> **25-10-17.** — Place: Les Brebis. Weather:— Fair during morning but dull and cold in afternoon. Visibility:— Good but failed [uncertain] about 4:00 P.M. Strength:— 9 Officers + 194 Other Ranks. Attachments:— 1 Officer + 3 Other Ranks attached from H.Q. 5th C.D.A for all purposes. 4 Other Ranks attached from 5th C.D.A.C. for all purposes. 2 S.B. Horses from H.Q. 5th C.D.A + 8 mules from 5th C.D.A.C. "X" Battery fired 28 Rounds on enemy Front Line wire with satisfactory results. Slight retaliation by minenwerfers on our Front Line. C. M. J. P.
>
> **26-10-17.** — Place: Les Brebis. Weather:— Dull and cold. Visibility:— very poor. Strength:— 9 Officers + 194 Other Ranks. "X" Battery fired 68 Rounds on enemy Front Line wire with good results. Obtained direct hit on enemy post and destroyed same Trench Mortar. Line to O.P. was cut twice but was repaired by # 1251617. Pte. Craig a J.M. [uncertain] although cut well above the parapet. C. M. J. P.

### Page 5 — 27–31 October 1917 (Sheet 3)

> **27-10-17.** — Place: Les Brebis. Weather:— Fair. Strength:— 9 Officers and 194 Other Ranks. 2 Officers + 38 Other Ranks returned from T.M. Course at Clarques. 1 Officer was admitted to Hospital while on Course. Fired 18 Rounds on Enemy front line wire with satisfactory Results. C. M. J. P.
>
> **28-10-17.** — Place: Les Brebis. Weather:— Dull in morning. Fair in afternoon. Visibility:— Poor. Strength:— 9 Officers + 194 Other Ranks. Fired 3 rounds for Registration on enemy Brickstacks and scattered a German working Party. Fired 28 Rounds on enemy front line wire and 12 Rounds on Hostile T.M's. with satisfactory results. Enemy retaliated without doing any material damage. C. M. J. P.
>
> **29-10-17.** — Place: Les Brebis. Weather:— Fine. Visibility:— Good all day. Strength:— 9 Officers + 194 Other Ranks. Hospital:— 1 Other Rank admitted to Hospital. "X" Battery:— Fired 47 Rounds on Hostile T.M's. with good results. C. M. J. P.
>
> **30-10-17.** — Place: Les Brebis. Weather:— Fine. Visibility:— Good. Strength:— 9 Officers. 194 Other Ranks. 2 Officers + 26 Other Ranks proceeded on T.M. Course at Clarques. 1 Officer + 30 Other Ranks went up line to build emplacements for Heavy T.M. Battery. "X" Battery fired 27 Rounds on Enemy wire and T.M's. with satisfactory results. C. M. J. P.
>
> **31-10-17.** — Place: Les Brebis. Weather:— Fine. Visibility:— Good. Hospital:— 1 Other Rank admitted Hospital. Strength:— 9 Officers. 194 Other Ranks. "X" Battery fired 10 Rounds on Enemy T.M's. with good results. "V" Battery completed emplacement and fired 15 Rounds for Registration. "Z" Battery completed emplacement and fired 15 Rounds for Registration. Casualties during month:— Nil. C. M. J. P.
>
> [Signed, bottom right:] *C. M. J. Puchas [uncertain] Capt., D.T.M.O. 5th Canadian Div'n.*

### Page 7 — Appendix 2: Instructions to Lieut. C. L. Boyd, 21 October 1917

> Appendix 2
>
> SECRET.
>
> Trench Mortar Group. 5th C.D.A.
>
> 26A/10/49.
>
> Lieut. C.L. Boyd.
>
> X B.M.B. 5th C.D.A.
>
> 21st October 1917.
>
> The following for your guidance while in command of 2 Mortars in Nizam Trench.
>
> 1. You will at request of Infantry retaliate up to 25 Rounds per day. It is best not to retaliate while enemy Mortars are firing unless it is a severe bombardment as enemy observers are usually active while their own Mortars are firing, and very often they fire in order to cause retaliation on our part and thus locate our Mortars.
>
> 2. There will be special tasks detailed by B.M. each day in addition to these should you observe a Mortar in action you should attempt to silence it. Always taking care to see that you have at least 75 Rounds in your pit.
>
> 3. Mortars reported very active during the past 2 Days. in houses in CITE ST AUGUSTE. A. N 3 a 30.60 B. N 3 a 60.20. and others in near vicinity.
>
> 4. Every effort must be made to conceal pits as much as possible and no chances should be taken to give positions away by firing when Planes are up, etc.
>
> 5. You will fire on enemy front line in N 2 b in case of S.O.S, keeping up a steady rate of fire of 2 Rounds per gun per minute, but not endangering your supply of ammunition running short. S.O.S. Signal should be found out by consulting Infantry you are covering.
>
> 6. Task for tomorrow, 22nd instant. You will fire 25 Rounds Instantaneous Fuze into wire at N 2 b 60.47 informing Infantry before starting. This ammunition should be fired at different intervals during the day so as to give enemy no chance of locating the gun. Report will be sent in on this work by wire to reach here by 4:30 P.M. using Trench Mortar Battery Code. Full report to follow by ration party at night.
>
> [Signed:] *C. M. J. Puchas [uncertain] Capt. C.F.A. D.T.M.O. 5th Canadian Divisional Artillery.*
>
> [Handwritten, bottom left:] War Diary

## Open questions / uncertain items (October 1917)
- **Grouped entry: 1–14 October.** The first entry is dated "1/10/17. to 14/10/17." and covers the whole fortnight in one line; there are no separate daily entries for 2–13 October. The unit spent this period attached to the 2nd and 4th C.D.A. trench mortar groups for duty, at Sains-En-Gohelle.
- The cover reads "VOLUME" followed by a struck character with a "10" written above it; the contents page reads "Volume 10". The cover also carries the handwritten note "With appendices. 1-2" at the bottom left.
- 15 October: the hour column reads "2 A.M." for the move from Barlin to Les Brebis; transcribed as written.
- 16 October: "Trench Mortar Group, 5th C.D.A took over Left Group & rest of 6th Divisional Artillery." — "rest" is read as written; the phrase is terse and may be short for "Left Group and rest of [the front of] 6th Divisional Artillery".
- 24 October: ""X" Battery fired 22 Rounds. 5 on new registration point and 17 on wire." — the total (22) matches the split (5 + 17); transcribed as written.
- 25 October: "Visibility:— Good but failed about 4:00 P.M." — "failed" is read as written but the sense (visibility failing/deteriorating) is not fully certain; marked [uncertain].
- 25 October: "2 S.B. Horses from H.Q. 5th C.D.A" — "S.B." is as written and is not expanded.
- 26 October: "Obtained direct hit on enemy post and destroyed same Trench Mortar." — "same" is as written; the sentence is awkward and may mean the enemy post's trench mortar, but it is transcribed as it stands.
- 26 October: "repaired by # 1251617. Pte. Craig a J.M." — the regimental number and name read as written; "a J.M." after the name is not read with confidence and is marked [uncertain].
- Appendix 1: the reference "26A/10/3." is read as written but the last figure is faint; Appendix 2 carries the fuller reference "26A/10/49.", and the two may be the same file reference.
- Appendix 1: the copies list reads "1 o/c 5 C.T.M.B." followed by three ditto lines, then "1 B.M. 5th C.D.A.", "1 File Copy." and "—3 War Diary." — transcribed as written.
- Appendix 2: the addressee line reads "X B.M.B. 5th C.D.A." — transcribed as written, though "B.M.B." is presumably a typing error for "T.M.B." (Trench Mortar Battery). Point 1: "retaliate up to 25 Rounds per day" — the figure "25" appears written over another figure; transcribed as 25.
- Appendix 2 gives the only map references in the month: enemy mortars in houses in CITE ST AUGUSTE at N 3 a 30.60 and N 3 a 60.20, and wire at N 2 b 60.47; the S.O.S. task is to fire on the enemy front line in N 2 b.
- The month-end signature reads "C. M. J. Puchas" — the surname is flourished and is not read with full confidence, so it is marked [uncertain]; the margin monogram "C. M. J. P." supports a surname beginning with P.
- Places read as: Sains-En-Gohelle; Barlin (Fosse 9); Les Brebis; Nizam Trench; Clarques; Cité St Auguste. All in France. This is the unit's first month in action: it takes over the Left Group front from 6th Divisional Artillery on 16 October and "X" Battery opens fire on 21 October.
- Casualties: the diary states "Casualties during month:— Nil." (31 October). No killed or wounded are recorded anywhere in the month; hospital admissions are recorded (1 Officer admitted while on course, 27 October; 1 Other Rank admitted 29 October; 1 Other Rank admitted 31 October). Named personnel: Pte. Craig (#1251617), who repaired the cut line to the O.P. on 26 October; Lieut. C. L. Boyd (Appendix 2, in command of 2 mortars in Nizam Trench); Capt. C. M. J. Puchas [uncertain] (D.T.M.O., signatory).
