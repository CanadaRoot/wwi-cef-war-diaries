# War Diary — 5th Canadian Divisional Trench Mortar Group — June 1917

- **Unit:** 5th Canadian Divisional Trench Mortar Group (styled on this month's cover "Trench Mortar Batteries 5-C-D-A"), 5th Canadian Division
- **Month:** June 1917
- **Source:** Library and Archives Canada, Collection Search Item ID 2004821 (RG 9 III-D-3, vol. 4980, file 594, reel T-10809)
- **Pages:** June 1917, p. 1 – p. 9 (image files page-0041 – page-0049)
- **June 1917 — COMPLETE** (pages 1–9): cover, contents, diary text 1–30 June, no appendices. Transcribed 2026-10-28.

## Transcription conventions
Original spelling, punctuation and abbreviations kept. Diary text is handwritten throughout (Army Form C. 2118, "WAR DIARY or [second heading struck through]"). `[uncertain]` marks words I could not read with confidence — never guessed. Each day's entry is initialled at its end, always with the same initials, transcribed as "W.H.A." — the first initial is read as W but the flourish makes M possible. The Place column gives CUTMILL COMMON for 1–6 June, MILFORD CAMP. WITLEY. for 7 June, and WITLEY CAMP for 12–30 June. The "Remarks and references to Appendices" column is blank throughout. Each diary sheet is stamped "TRENCH-MORTAR-BATTERIES 5TH CAN. DIV. Vol VI — JUN. 1917" with a sheet number (1–7). The printed footer reads "A5834 Wt. W4973/M687 750,000 8/16 D. D. & L. Ltd. Forms/C.2118/13." "T.M." = Trench Mortar; "O.R." = Other Ranks; "G.S." = General Service; "P.H." = the P.H. gas helmet; "N.C.O." = Non-Commissioned Officer; "Pdr" = pounder; "How." = howitzer; "D.T.M.O." = Divisional Trench Mortar Officer; "Rt" = Right.

## Open questions / uncertain items (June 1917)
- The cover reads "(Volume VI)" but the contents page reads "Volume 7" — both as written; the diary-sheet stamps read "Vol VI".
- The left edge of the cover is cut off in the image: only "om. June 1st 1917" is visible where "From June 1st 1917" is expected; transcribed as "[From]".
- 1 June: "Anti-Gas " " — the ditto mark stands for "drill"; transcribed as [ditto].
- 8 June: "Squadering drill" — read as written; the word may be a misspelling of a drill name, but it is transcribed as it appears.
- 8 June: a word after "transferred to T.Ms from" is struck through and is not readable; the sentence is transcribed with the struck-through word marked illegible.
- 11 June: the 2 p.m. line reads "Brigade T.M. emplacement" but "Brigade" is partly obscured by print showing through from the other side of the sheet; the reading is not fully certain.
- 15 June: the middle of the 2 p.m. line is obscured by print showing through; only "Section Gun drill" and "Gas Helmet (P.H.) drill." are readable.
- 18 June: the figure "18" in "18 O.R.s" in the guards paragraph appears written over another figure; it reads as 18 but the overwrite is noted.
- 26 June: the entry refers to the detail of the previous day; the figure reads as "25th" but the hand is cramped and "26th" cannot be ruled out — a self-reference to the 26th would make no sense, since the 27th–29th entries all refer back to the 26th.
- The recurring initials are read as "W.H.A."; the first initial is a flourish that could also be read as M.
- The month-end signature (30 June) is a long flourished signature that is not readable; only "Capt., D.T.M.O. 5th Canadian Div'n." is legible.
- Places read as: Cutmill Common; Milford Camp, Witley; Witley Camp. All in England; the unit is still training in England and has not yet gone to France.
- No human casualties are recorded anywhere in this month: no killed, wounded or missing are mentioned, and no personnel are named by full name. The only battery named is "V" Battery (Right and Left Sections, kit inspections 20–21 June).
