# War Diary — 5th Canadian Divisional Trench Mortar Group — July 1917

- **Unit:** 5th Canadian Divisional Trench Mortar Group (styled on this month's cover "Trench Mortar Batteries 5-C-D-A"), 5th Canadian Division
- **Month:** July 1917
- **Source:** Library and Archives Canada, Collection Search Item ID 2004821 (RG 9 III-D-3, vol. 4980, file 594, reel T-10809)
- **Pages:** July 1917, p. 1 – p. 8 (image files page-0050 – page-0057)
- **July 1917 — COMPLETE** (pages 1–8): cover, contents, diary text 1–31 July, no appendices. Transcribed 2026-10-28.

## Transcription conventions
Original spelling, punctuation and abbreviations kept. Diary text is handwritten throughout (Army Form C. 2118, "WAR DIARY or [second heading struck through]" — except on the last sheet, page 8, where the heading reads "WAR DIARY or INTELLIGENCE SUMMARY." with neither heading struck through). `[uncertain]` marks words I could not read with confidence — never guessed. Each day's entry is initialled at its end, always with the same initials as in June, transcribed as "W.H.A." — the first initial is read as W but the flourish makes M possible. The Place column reads MILFORD CAMP. throughout. The "Remarks and references to Appendices" column is blank throughout. Each diary sheet is stamped "TRENCH-MORTAR-BATTERIES 5TH CAN. DIV. Vol VII — JUL. 1917" with a sheet number (1–6). The printed footer reads "A5834 Wt. W4973/M687 750,000 8/16 D. D. & L. Ltd. Forms/C.2118/13." "T.M." = Trench Mortar; "T.M.B." = Trench Mortar Battery; "O.R." = Other Ranks; "Gnr" = Gunner; "P.H." = the P.H. gas helmet; "Pdr" = pounder; "D.T.M.O." = Divisional Trench Mortar Officer; "A.M." = morning; "P.M." = afternoon/evening.

## Open questions / uncertain items (July 1917)
- The cover reads "VOL. VII." and the contents page reads "Volume 7" — consistent; the contents page styles the unit in the singular ("5th Cdn. Trench Mortar Battery") where the cover uses the plural ("Batteries").
- 5 July: "Results of telephony course show a high standard attained by men of T.Ms." — the only evaluative remark in the month; no course dates or names are given.
- 6 July: "Court Martial of Gnr Hutton TMB adjourned for production of witness" — Gnr Hutton is the only person named in the month; no first name, initial or service number is given, and no outcome is recorded later in the month.
- 10 July: "4.00 Defaulters." — no hour marker (a.m./p.m.) is given for the defaulters parade; transcribed as written.
- 20 July: "Officer's Gymkhana P.M." — no further detail is given.
- 22 July: the hour "9.00" is followed by a ditto mark (") for "am"; transcribed as written.
- The recurring initials are read as "W.H.A."; the first initial is a flourish that could also be read as M.
- The month-end signature (31 July) is a long flourished signature that is not readable; only "Capt., D.T.M.O. 5th Canadian Div'n." is legible.
- Places read as: Milford Camp (Witley). All in England; the unit is still training in England and has not yet gone to France.
- No human casualties are recorded anywhere in this month: no killed, wounded or missing are mentioned. The only person named is Gnr Hutton, in connection with a court martial.
