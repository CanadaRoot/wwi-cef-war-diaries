# War Diary — 5th Canadian Divisional Trench Mortar Group — August 1917

- **Unit:** 5th Canadian Divisional Trench Mortar Group, 5th Canadian Division
- **Month:** August 1917
- **Source:** Library and Archives Canada, Collection Search Item ID 2004821 (RG 9 III-D-3, vol. 4980, file 594, reel T-10809)
- **Pages:** August 1917, p. 1 – p. 4 (image files page-0058 – page-0061)
- **August 1917 — PARTIAL** (pages 1–4): cover, contents, diary text 22–31 August only, no appendices. **No diary pages for 1–21 August exist in this set** — the diary resumes on 22 August with the unit disembarking at Le Havre. Transcribed 2026-10-28.

## Transcription conventions
Original spelling, punctuation and abbreviations kept. Diary text is handwritten throughout (Army Form C. 2118, "WAR DIARY or INTELLIGENCE SUMMARY." with "INTELLIGENCE SUMMARY" struck through), in a new hand from the June–July sheets, and the unit is now styled "5th Canadian Divisional Trench Mortar Group" rather than "Trench Mortar Batteries 5-C-D-A". `[uncertain]` marks words I could not read with confidence — never guessed. Each day's entry ends with an initialled flourish that is not readable; it is transcribed as "[initials — illegible]" each time. The "Remarks and references to Appendices" column is blank throughout. The diary sheets are stamped "TRENCH-MORTAR-BATTERIES 5TH CAN. DIV. — 31 AUG. 1917 Sheet 1" and "Sheet 2". The printed footer reads "A5834 Wt. W4973/M687 750,000 8/16 D. D. & L. Ltd. Forms/C.2118/13." "T.M." = Trench Mortar; "T.M.B." = Trench Mortar Battery; "O.R." = Other Ranks; "D.A.C." = Divisional Ammunition Column; "D.T.M.O." = Divisional Trench Mortar Officer; "M.O." = Medical Officer; "C.D.A." = Canadian Divisional Artillery; "P.T." = Physical Training; "R.A." = Royal Artillery.

## Open questions / uncertain items (August 1917)
- **Gap: 1–21 August 1917 are not covered.** The cover and contents both give the month as 22nd to 31st August only, and no diary sheets for 1–21 August are in this set. The unit's movements between the end of July (Milford Camp, England) and 22 August (disembarking at Le Havre) are not recorded here.
- The cover reads "VOLUME. X" with an "8" written above the X; the contents page reads "Volume 8".
- 22 August: the ship's name reads "Anticraft." — the reading is not fully certain, and the phrase that follows reads "Cleanest ship." — transcribed as written, though its sense is unclear. "Left for No. 1. Camp, S. & A." — the "S. & A." is read as written but is not expanded; it may be a camp designation.
- 22 August: the entry-ending initials appear to begin with J but are a flourish and are not read with confidence; they are transcribed as "[initials — illegible]" throughout this month.
- 24 August: the Place column carries "Buchy" beside the lower part of the entry (the 9.00 p.m. arrival); the hour column reads "11.30 to 3.15 p.m." for the loading and entraining, and "9.00 p.m." for the arrival at Buchy, with "Left at 9.30" in the text.
- 29 August: "Inspection by Brig-Gen. Morrison, C.D.A. R.A. Can. Corps." — transcribed as written; the "R.A. Can. Corps" that follows "C.D.A." is unusual and is noted, not corrected.
- The month-end signature (31 August) is a long flourished signature that is not readable; it is followed by "Lieut. Capt., D.T.M.O. 5th Canadian Div'n." — "Lieut." and "Capt." both appear in the signature block as written.
- Strength changes are not explained: 5 Officers, 69 O.R. disembark 22 August; 6 Officers, 69 O.R. at Buchy 24 August; parade strength 5 Officers, 59 O.R. on 29 August. No reinforcement or detachment is recorded for these changes, apart from Lieut. R.A. Wood joining from England on 28 August.
- Places read as: Le Havre; Buchy; Lillers; Nedonchelle; Amettes; Clarques. All in France. This is the unit's arrival in France: it disembarks at Le Havre, entrains for Lillers, and goes into billets at Nedonchelle.
- No human casualties are recorded anywhere in this month: no killed, wounded or missing are mentioned. The only officers named are Lieut. R.A. Wood (joins from England, 28 August) and Brig-Gen. Morrison (inspects the unit, 29 August).
