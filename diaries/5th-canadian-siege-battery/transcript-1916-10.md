## Page 3 (October 1916, p. 3) — [diary text]

WAR DIARY or INTELLIGENCE SUMMARY. (Erase heading not required.)

5th CANADIAN SIEGE BATTERY.

### POIZIERS — Oct. 8th

Opened fire on Roads and lines of approach at M 14 b 7.9 and the sides searched 50 yards each way. Firing was kept up nearly all day at slow rate and stopped at 7.35 p.m. 86 rounds being expended.

### POIZIERS — Oct. 9th

Commenced registering with aeroplane observation at 8.15 A.m. Target being at M 14 b 7.8 24 Rounds were fired and we finished this task by 8.45 a.m. Lieut Palfreyman R.F.C. was the observing officer and stated the shoot was very satisfactory. At 2.25 p.m. we opened fire on Regina Trench at M 14.b 7.8. at the rate of 3 Rounds per hour at irregular intervals.

### POIZIERS — Oct. 10th

Commenced firing at 5.25.a.m at the slow rate of 3 rounds per hour and continued to do so until we received orders from a plane to stand by for a shoot on target No 432. We went into action on this target but after firing one round the plane gave C I as it was too cloudy to observe. During the afternoon plane again called up and shoot was commenced on target in M.14.6.7 7 Rounds were expended on registration and 30 Rounds were then fired at the target with very good results. A slow bombardment was kept up during the evening, three rounds per minute at irregular intervals.

### POIZIERS — Oct. 11th

4.16 A.m. commenced firing at steady rate of one round every two minutes which was kept up until 5.50.a.m. We had arranged to do a shoot to-day on German Battery situated at L23 [uncertain] 58.96 but plane did not turn up so shoot had to be abandoned. During afternoon and evening we were firing at slow rate until 6.15.p.m.

### POIZIERS — Oct. 12th

From 2.30.A.M. to 6.45 A.M. we were firing slow bombardment At 8.a.m. we registered by aeroplane observation on Trench junction at M 14 b 5.8. after registering 200 Rounds were fired on the trench running south east of this point keeping well within the safety limits of our own troops at M 15 A 4.5. We also registered visually on portions of Regina Trench and then fired 60 Rounds for effect with very good results, several shells dropping into the trench. From 8.p.m. to Midnight a slow bombardment was kept up on enemies trenches and approaches.

## Page 4 (October 1916, p. 4) — [diary text]

### POIZIERS — Oct. 12th (second entry)

At 6.32 A.M. we were to start registering by aeroplane observation but owing to low clouds and poor visibility this had to be abandoned. At 10.35 A.M. we started a shoot on the trench south of Pys from M 8 a 11 eastwards and the REGINA TRENCH from M 14 b 7.8 to M 14 b 5.3. only 4 Rounds were fired before Plane called and we continued the shoot by aeroplane observation after firing 10 Rounds for registration we fired a total of 150 Rounds with very good results. During the afternoon we fired at intervals on BELOW TRENCH front and support lines and also on GALOWITZ TRENCH front and support lines.

### POIZIERS — Oct. 13th

At 10 A.M. we registered by visual observation on BELOW TRENCH and M 8 c 4.7. expending 13 Rds During the morning we were firing on Junction of BELOW TRENCH and PRACTICE ROADS. At 2.5.P.M. the 3rd Corp in conjunction with the 18th and 14th Corp attacked on a point adjoining the Canadian Corp Area and from Zero to 30 minutes we bombarded at steady rate the Southern and Eastern half of M 14 b 6.8 to 7.8. we then increased our rate of fire until we had completed 100 Rounds and then turned on to REGINA TRENCH down to M 14 b 2.3. and continued firing at steady rate. At 5.20 P.M. we put in 20 minutes rapid on same target.

### POIZIERS — Oct. 14th

During the morning we were again firing on REGINA TRENCH at slow rate. Capt. L.C. ORD the F.O.O. observing and keeping check on our firing. At 1.40 P.M. we fired 100 Rounds into REGINA support trenches at M 14 b 1.4 to 4.8. During the afternoon we fired 73 Rounds at slow rate into these trenches and then increased to steady rate until 4.30.P.M. firing 235 Rounds. Observation was very difficult owing to poor visibility but despite this very good work was done.

### POIZIERS — Oct. 15th

The day was a very stormy one and very little work could be done. A very high wind increasing at times to a gale was accompanied by violent squalls of rain changing later to snow which made observation impossible. There was no firing today.

### POIZIERS — Oct. 16th

NIL.

## Page 5 (October 1916, p. 5) — [diary text]

### POIZIERS — Oct. 17th

120 Rounds were fired today as intermittant and harrassing fire, mostly on REGINA front line and GALOWITZ TRENCH. Hostile shelling was particularly heavy during the early part of the day and it was noticed that the enemy was using a new type of shell. A green light was observed to become detached from these shells about 150 yards before reaching the point of contact. These would have been plainly visible from the German trenches. It was thought that this was a new experiment for observing fire.

### POIZIERS — Oct. 18th

The day was again taken up by intermittant and harrassing fire on BELOW, GALOWITZ trenches and supports. and there was nothing unusual to report.

### POIZIERS — Oct. 19th

Intermittant and Harrassing Fire was kept up during the morning on Crest Trench and Below front. And during the afternoon 75 Rounds were fired on BELOW TRENCH.

### POIZIERS — Oct. 20th

NIL

### POIZIERS — Oct. 21st

396 Rounds were fired today, mostly by visual observation, BELOW TRENCH, BELOW SUPPORT LINES and Crest Trench, Twice during the day we attempted shoots with aeroplane observation but both times had to discontinue. At 3.P.M. German re-inforcements were seen emerging from PYS near Summer House we immediately opened up on them and after firing 10 Rounds they dispersed. During the night we fired 100 Rounds at slow rate on CREST TRENCH and westerly.

### POIZIERS — Oct. 22nd

At 8.30. a.m. we fired 25 rounds with aeroplane observation on Target 420 (Hostile Battery) the results were very good. 2 O.K.s being registered. After getting good registration the plane gave C.I. and we fired a further 75 Rounds on the Battery without observation. 436 Rounds were fired during the day on PYS. IRLES and MIRAMONT.

### POIZIERS — Oct. 23rd

NIL

### POIZIERS — Oct. 24th

Raining heavily and very heavy mist hung about all day, it was impossible to see any targets and consequently there was no firing.

## Page 6 (October 1916, p. 6) — [diary text]

### POIZIERS — Oct. 25th

We were engaged all day on Counter Battery Work, taking on three Hostile Batteries numbered F2, F3 and F14. During the afternoon we put 8 Rounds at intervals into each of these batteries.

### POIZIERS — Oct. 26th

NIL

### POIZIERS — Oct. 27th

NIL.

### POIZIERS — Oct. 28th

Fired 200 Rounds on REGINA TRENCH from Practice Road to its junction with BELOW at fairly regular intervals throughout the day. This was done by visual observation.

### POIZIERS — Oct. 29th

Yesterdays program was repeated and opening rounds were observed by aeroplane observation.

### POIZIERS — Oct. 30th

Fired at intervals throughout the day on REGINA trench but as visibility was very poor the shooting was unsatisfactory. A southernly gale blowing all the afternoon and heavy squalls made observation impossible.

### POIZIERS — Oct. 31st

Fired 12 Rounds on concentrated shoot on orders from the group, Nothing unusual occurred today.

## Transcriber's notes — October 1916

- **Date gaps (days with no entry):** None — every day from 8 to 31 October 1916 has an entry. (The volume covers 8 – 31 October only; 1 – 7 October belong to the preceding period and are not in this volume.)
- **Entries spanning pages:** No entry runs across a page break. However, the 12th October is entered twice: one entry closes p. 3 and a second, different entry dated 12th October opens p. 4. Both are transcribed as written, in page order; this appears to be a dating error in the original (or two parts of the day's work entered separately), not a continuation.
- **Duplicate date:** See above — two separate entries are both dated 12 October 1916 (pp. 3 and 4).
- **"C I" / "C.I.":** The 10 October entry has the plane giving "C I" (no stops) after one round on target No 432; the 22 October entry has the plane giving "C.I." after registration on Target 420. Both are reproduced as written.
- **11 October entry:** The German battery's map reference is written "L23 [uncertain] 58.96" — a faint, overtyped letter (apparently "d") stands between "L23" and "58.96" and is flagged [uncertain]. The 10 October target reference "M.14.6.7" is reproduced as written; it is in an unusual form with no square letter.
- **10 October entry:** "three rounds per minute" is reproduced as written, although the same entry (and the 9 October entry) otherwise speaks of 3 rounds per hour; this may be a typing error in the original.
- **Show-through at the foot of p. 4:** Faint typewritten words "During the day" and "17th" show through at the very foot of p. 4, below the form's imprint line. They are not part of p. 4's diary text (the 17 October entry is the first entry on p. 5) and are not transcribed as an entry.
- **"POIZIERS":** The place name is spelled "POIZIERS" throughout the diary (Pozières). "Corp" is written for "Corps" throughout (e.g. "the 3rd Corp", "the Canadian Corp Area"). "intermittant", "harrassing", "southernly" and "Yesterdays" (no apostrophe) are reproduced as written.
- **31 October entry:** "occurred" and "today" run together in the original ("occurredtoday"); they are separated here.
- **Casualties:** None recorded in October 1916. No battery casualties are mentioned; the 17 October entry records heavy hostile shelling but no casualties, and the 21 October entry records German reinforcements dispersed by the battery's fire (10 rounds).
- **People named:** Lieut PALFREYMAN, R.F.C. (observing officer, 9 October); Capt. L. C. ORD, the F.O.O. (14 October). No commanding officer is named.
- **Places:** Poiziers (as written "POIZIERS"), Regina Trench, Below Trench, Galowitz Trench, Crest Trench, Practice Roads / Practice Road, Pys, Irles, Miramont, Summer House (near Pys).
- **No appendices** are listed or included with this volume; the contents page lists only "Diary Text 4 Pages", and the Remarks column is blank throughout.
- Form imprint at the foot of the diary pages: "2149 Wt. W14957/M90 750,000 1/16 J.B.C. & A. Forms/C.2118/12."
