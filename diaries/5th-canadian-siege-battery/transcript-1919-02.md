### Jemappes — Feby. 1919

During the month of February the Battery remained billeted at Jemappes, attached to 1st Brigade C.G.A. Nothing of unusual interest occured during the month. Parades were held each morning and educational classes in various subjects were in full swing. The 1st Brigade Concert party put on their first show during the month and gave an excellent entertainment which was appreciated by all ranks. Sports and outdoor games were also indulged in as weather permitted.

[Signed:] W. A. Buscroft Lt, for O/C 5th Canadian Siege Battery.
