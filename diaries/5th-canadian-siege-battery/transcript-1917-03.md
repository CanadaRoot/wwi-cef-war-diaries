## Page 3 (March 1917, p. 3) — [diary text]

WAR DIARY or INTELLIGENCE SUMMARY. (Erase heading not required.)

### MT ST ELOY — Mar 1st

At 3.15 a.m. opened fire on Hostile Batteries No 628, 615, and 562 in S18b 05.13, S18a 10.20 and S22d 5.15. Thirty Rounds being fired on each target. From 5.a.m. to 8.a.m. 229 Rounds were fired on the above targets, and 80 Rounds were expended in cutting cable in S29a 95.05. During the afternoon we registered by aeroplane on Trench Mortars in S28b 23.25 and S28b28.28 shoot was very successful, plane reporting several A's and Z's and three O.K's

### MT ST ELOY — Mar 2nd

Nil.

### MT ST ELOY — Mar 3rd

Nil.

### MT ST ELOY — Mar 4th

Started at noon on shoot by aeroplane observation on H.B. No 635 in S24 b 14.92, the target was bracketed but plane gave C I after 8 Rounds. Started again on same target at 2.15.p.m. by aeroplane observation, shoot very successful, plane reporting 3 O.K's several Y's and Z's. Plane was attacked by hostile aeroplane and during the fight that followed our plane had its wireless aerial shot away so could do no more registering for us we therefore fired the remaining 38 Rounds without observation. 105 Rounds in all were fired on this target.

### MT ST ELOY — Mar 5th

Nil.

### MT ST ELOY — Mar 6th

From 8.30a.m. to 9.45 a.m. conducted a very satisfactory shoot by aeroplane observation on Trench Mortars in S28b 28.28 and S28b 23.25, Plane reporting 5 O.K's and several A's, Y's and Z's. one round at C3 caused the explosion of a quantity of ammunition. The airman later reported that the target was completely destroyed.

### MT ST ELOY — Mar 7th

Nil.

### MT ST ELOY — Mar 8th

Nil.

### MT ST ELOY — Mar 9th

During the day we fired 44 Rounds on Thelus Mill which was observed by F.O.O. from Hilltop O.P. Capt L.C.Ord left Battery on special leave to England.

### MT ST ELOY — Mar 10th

Nil.

### MT ST ELOY — Mar 11th

11.20a.m. to 1.40.p.m. we fired by aerial observation 87 Rounds on Hostile Battery No 553 at S6c 75.70. plane reported 2 O.K's and several Y's, Z's and A's. Shoot was very satisfactory.

## Page 4 (March 1917, p. 4) — [diary text]

### MT ST ELOY — Mar 11th (continued)

At 11 a.m. we fired 16 Rounds on a crater D62 in S15d 6.2 for registration. From 2.p.m. to 2.15 p.m. at the request of Major Keith of the 11th Infantry Brigade we fired 40 Rounds on Railhead, Trench Mortar Ammunition Dump and Dugouts in S16d 70.15, This was done without observation but to the satisfaction of the infantry. During the afternoon a plane attempted to register us on Hostile Battery No 555 in S6b 00.08 but without success, plane gave C I after 4 Rounds all of which were unobserved.

### MT ST ELOY — Mar 12th

During the day we fired 22 Rounds on Crater D62 at S15d 6.2 and on Trench Mortars in rear of crater to check error of the day, this was done by ground observation.

### MT ST ELOY — Mar 13th

Nil.

### MT ST ELOY — Mar 14th

Nil

### MT ST ELOY — Mar 15th

An attempt to conduct a shoot by aeroplane observation during the afternoon had to be abandoned on account of bad visibility, our target being Trench Junction at S15b 16.45. Only 6 Rounds were fired. From 3.50.p.m. to 4.15.p.m. we fired 50 Rounds for effect without observation on S16c 11.

### MT ST ELOY — Mar 16th

In compliance with Counter Battery Order we fired during the early hours of the morning, 45 Rounds on three Hostile Batteries in [uncertain] S6c 15.85, S6a 99.05 and S11b 35.41. From 3.40.p.m. to 4.20.p.m. fired 20 Rounds on Ecole Commune in S23c 03.30 to calibrate new line.

### MT ST ELOY — Mar 17th

5.55 P.m. to [uncertain] 5.12.8.M. fired 62 Rounds by aerial observation on Hostile Battery at S6b 00.05. Shoot was unsatisfactory.

### MT ST ELOY — Mar 18th

2.15 p.m. to 3.45 p.m. we fired 80 Rounds on Trench Mortar in S5a 17.64, This was observed by F.B.O. and was very satisfactory, 4 direct hits being obtained and 19 Rounds in Trench destroying it for 50 yards on each side on the trench mortar.

### MT ST ELOY — Mar 19th

NIL.

### MT ST ELOY — Mar 20th

NIL.

### MT ST ELOY — Mar 21st

During the afternoon we fired 44 Rounds on trenches in S17c 8.4 without observation.

## Page 5 (March 1917, p. 5) — [diary text]

### MT ST ELOY — Mar 22nd

At Noon we fired 30 Rounds on Crater D62 in S16d 6.2 double line calibration shoot, we intended afterwards to switch to a point behind Vimy Ridge, but shoot was unsatisfactory on account of poor visibility. 2.30.p.m. to 3.p.m. fired 16 Rounds on Ecole Commune in S23c 03.30 This was for calibrating 3rd Charge in preparation for shoot on Ammunition dump, a very good registration being obtained. At 4.p.m. we fired 30 Rounds on an Ammunition dump at S30a 6.3 without observation.

### MT ST ELOY — Mar 23rd

During the evening we fired 50 Rounds at slow rate on Hostile Battery at B1d 37.50. no observation.

### MT ST ELOY — Mar 24th

At 4.30.a.m. in compliance with counter battery orders we fired 30 Rounds on Hostile Batteries in S24b 10.90, A6b 85.70, and B1d 05.60. Ten Rounds being fired on each battery. At 9.55.a.m. we started shoot on Hostile Battery No 826 by aeroplane observation. This was not successful and after firing 87 Rounds 27 of which were unobserved, the shoot was abandoned and plane gave C I. During the afternoon we again tried by aeroplane observation to conduct a shoot on Hostile Battery No 821 at B2c 15.75. but was again unsuccessful, after 14 Rounds had been fired, plane gave C I on account of heavy mist.

### MT ST ELOY — Mar 25th

During the morning we fired 100 Rounds on Ecole Commune in S23c 25.30. for calibration of 2nd, 3rd, and 4th Charges, results were very satisfactory. In the afternoon we conducted a shoot on Hostile Battery S18 in S6a 99.05 using both aeroplane and balloon for observing the shoot was successful, plane reporting a good many corrections but balloon only observed 4 Rounds out of 12 Rounds fired. A total of 49 Rounds were fired on this target. Later in the afternoon we fired 101 Rounds without observation on this target for effect. From 5.30.p.m. to 6.30.p.m. we fired 56 Rounds on a machine gun emplacement in S23b 85.65 3 direct hits were obtained and a quantity of Elephant Iron and other material were thrown up.

### MT ST ELOY — Mar 26th

During the afternoon we fired 144 Rounds on a machine gun emplacement in S23b 85.65 which was very satisfactory, 8 O.K's were obtained and a number of rounds fell in Zwischen Stellung 7.30.p.m. to 8.10.p.m. fired 100 Rounds on Hostile Battery T56 at T18c 20.75 no observation.

## Page 6 (March 1917, p. 6) — [diary text]

### MT ST ELOY — Mar 27th

During the afternoon we fired 40 Rounds on Trench Junction in S22b 22.80, S22b 18.90 and S3c 65.85. During the evening 30 Rounds were fired on hostile battery E24 at E2c 18.74. Both these shoot were carried out without observation.

### MT ST ELOY — Mar 28th

An aeroplane shoot on trench junctions in S15a 83.72 and S9c 91.05 during the morning was unsuccessful, 8 Rounds were fired all of which were unobserved.. At Noon we fired 10 Rounds on Thelus Mill to check error of day. During the afternoon we registered on Crater D62 at S16d 6.2, fired 22 Rounds and obtained very good registration and then switched to Trench Junction S15a 83.72 to S9c 97.95 and fired 100 Rounds. shoot very satisfactory.

### MT ST ELOY — Mar 29th

11.30 a.m. to 1.12.p.m. after registering on crater D62, switched and fired 100 Rounds on S22a 10.50 and S22a 20.32. Very good shooting and much light material was seen to be thrown up. During the afternoon we fired 100 Rounds on machine gun emplacement in S15d 90.10 by ground observation, obtained one direct hit on the emplacement but it was not demolished. 6.50.p.m. to 7.p.m. we fired 80 Rounds on Hostile Battery S53 in S18d 15.85 without observation.

### MT ST ELOY — Mar 30th

5.55.p.m. to 6.15.p.m. fired without observation 100 Rounds on S16c 3.8 tp S16c 5.5 and during the evening 100 Rounds without observation on Hostile Battery T60. at T15d 15.95.

### MT ST ELOY — Mar 31st

Fired continuously throughout the day on various targets in support of a raid made by our infantry our first targets being trenches in S15a 85.75 to S15d 30.85 which was observed by our F.O.O. several rounds were seen to fall in the trench, we then switched to S15a 85.75 and S15d 30.85 Firing 70 Rounds and then turned back to the first target. At 10.30 a.m. we fired 82 Rounds on Hostile Battery at S16c 15.85 and H.B. at S6a 30.15.

## Transcriber's notes — March 1917

- **Date gaps (days with no entry):** none — every day 1–31 March has an entry, though 2, 3, 5, 7, 8, 10, 13, 14, 19 and 20 March are entered as "Nil".
- **Entries spanning pages:** the 11 March entry runs from p. 3 onto p. 4.
- **Initials in the Place column:** handwritten initials appear beside most entries in the Place column margin; they are illegible and have not been transcribed.
- **16 March entry:** a word (apparently a map reference) before "S6c 15.85" is struck through in the typescript and is illegible, flagged [uncertain].
- **17 March entry:** the end time of the shoot is typed as "5.12.8.M." and is reproduced as written, flagged [uncertain]; the start time reads "5.55 P.m.".
- **30 March entry:** "tp" in "S16c 3.8 tp S16c 5.5" is reproduced as typed (presumably "to").
- **"C I":** the aeroplane signal given when a shoot ends or the plane breaks off, as typed. "M.Q." and "O.K's" are aeroplane observation codes as typed. "F.O.O." = Forward Observation Officer; "F.B.O." (18 March) is reproduced as typed.
- **"Elephant Iron"** (25 March) is corrugated iron sheeting used for dugouts; "Zwischen Stellung" (26 March) is German for "intermediate position".
- **Casualties:** none recorded in March.
- **People named:** Capt L.C. Ord (leaves on special leave to England, 9 March); Major Keith, 11th Infantry Brigade (11 March).
- **Places:** Mt St Eloy (battery position throughout the month), Thelus Mill, Ecole Commune, Vimy Ridge, Crater D62.
