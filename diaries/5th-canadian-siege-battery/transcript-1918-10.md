## Page 3 (October 1918, p. 3) — [diary text]

WAR DIARY or INTELLIGENCE SUMMARY. (Erase heading not required.)

### Cagnicourt — Oct. 1st

All Guns being out of action, several parties were sent forward to assist other Batteries in the Brigade. 1 party each of 1 N.C.O. and 20 Men were sent to 1st and 2nd Heavy Batteries and 4th and 6th Siege Batteries. Wallet [uncertain] attached to 2nd C.H.B. No 830269 Gnr Scott I.J. was severely wounded and died later in Hospital.

### Cagnicourt — Oct. 2nd

Preparations were made today to move 2 Guns forward to a new position at Haynecourt in X.16.a. How No 602 received from shops.

### Cagnicourt — Oct. 3rd

2 Guns and crews left for Haynecourt. Village was being shelled when party arrived but guns were put into action with only two casualties. No 51112 Gnr Duperow W.E. and No 2327549 Gnr Odell J.A. being wounded. Gnr Duperow died later at Dressing station.

### Cagnicourt — Oct. 4th

Hows No 579 and 585 were in action at Haynecourt at approximately X.16.a.70.00. 1 N.F. Call was received during the evening in response to which we fired 6 Rounds on M.9.d.6.9.

### Cagnicourt — Oct. 5th

A shoot with aeroplane observation on Bridge at N.22.a.7.7. was unsuccessful owing to bad visibility. Shoot was observed by Captain Stevenson R.A.F. and after 12 ranging rounds sent C.F. [uncertain] mist. Battery position was heavily shelled during the afternoon and Gnr Moore A and Gnr MacLeod D.J. being wounded. Cross Roads near guns and approaches to village were subjected to heavy harassing fire during the night.

### Cagnicourt — Oct. 6th

From 00.01 to 09.00 we fired 30 Rounds harassing fire on Cross roads at Marquette and Wavrechain.

### Cagnicourt — Oct. 7th

Arrangements were to be made for exchange of Guns in situ with R.G.A. Battery who were in position east of Monchy. Captain N.P. MacLeod and Lieut D.A. Guildford went to look over the position and complete arrangements for transfer. This transfer however did not take place. A total of 124 Rounds were expended during the day. 30 Rounds of which were harassing fire on roads and approaches to Marquette and Wavrechain, the remainder being fired in support of special operations.

### Cagnicourt — Oct. 8th

Battery came into action at 09.00 in support of special operations and guns were kept busy all day answering N.F. calls, Harassing fire and special operations. Our targets being Cross roads

## Page 4 (October 1918, p. 4) — [diary text]

### Haynecourt — Oct. 8th (continued)

Cross roads at Marquette and Monchencourt. Courvillers, Bantigny, Paillencourt, Estrun and the Bantigny Valley. A total of 275 rounds being expended.

### Haynecourt — Oct. 9th

2 New Hows were drawn today to replace those already condemned. During the day we fired 275 rounds on Monchencourt, Marquette and Wavrechain and all roads and approaches to these villages were kept under harassing fire.

### Haynecourt — Oct. 10th

How No 585 condemned with cracked chamber was removed to No 20 M.O.W.

### Haynecourt — Oct. 11th

All Headquarter personnel moved from Cagnicourt to Haynecourt today leaving only Q.M. stores and guards on gun stores on old billets. 90 Rounds were fired during the day and cross roads and approaches to Marquette, Marq and Wavrechain kept under harassing fire.

### Haynecourt — Oct. 12th

Piece No 502 brought up from Cagnicourt and placed in cradle of 585, this gun was in action by 4.a.m. 13th. Harassing fire on approaches to Marq. and Marquette was kept up intermittently all day. A total of 145 rounds being expended.

### Haynecourt — Oct. 13th

B. sub's gun went out of action for change of grid, new bed being dug a few yards ahead of old pit and gun shifted. Piece No 434 received from shops. During the day we fired 133 rounds harassing fire on Marq, Marquette and roads in G.28.a. and 44 rounds on S.O.S. in R.12.a.5.0.

### Haynecourt — Oct. 14th

138 Rounds harassing fire was put over on approaches to Marq and Marquette.

### Haynecourt — Oct. 15th

170 rounds fired on Marq, Marquette and valley between and on Monchencourt and roads to the north.

### Haynecourt — Oct. 16th

170 rounds harassing fire was put over on Cross Roads at Monchencourt during the day.

### Haynecourt — Oct. 17th

Nil

## Page 5 (October 1918, p. 5) — [diary text]

### Haynecourt — Oct. 18th

Nil

### Haynecourt — Oct. 19th

Nil

### Haynecourt — Oct. 20th

All 6" Gun and 9.2" How Batteries were left behind when Brigade advanced today and came under the tactical control of Major Dobbie now O.C. of the new 9.2" How Brigade, all batteries still remained in 2nd Brigade CGA for administration.

### Haynecourt — Oct. 21st to 31st

Guns were pulled out and hauled into the road ready for an immediate advance forward. In the meantime while waiting for orders parties of Gunners were sent forward to work at dumps etc and and the remainder of the gun crews left in old position were employed in cleaning up shells and component parts left behind by various batteries. New guns were received from shops and Battery complete with 6 Guns were standing by awaiting developments.

[Signed:] W. A. Buscroft Lieut, f.o.C. 5th C.S.B

## Transcriber's notes — October 1918

- **Date gaps (days with no entry):** none as such — 1–20 October have daily entries (17, 18 and 19 October entered as "Nil"), and 21–31 October are covered by a single grouped entry.
- **Entries spanning pages:** the 8 October entry runs from p. 3 onto p. 4.
- **Handwritten marks:** each entry bears a small handwritten mark in the Date/Hour columns, apparently the initials "W. A. B." (Lieut W. A. Buscroft, who signs the volume). The Place column gives the place as "Cagnicourt." (1–7 October) and "Haynecourt." (8–31 October).
- **1 October entry:** the word rendered "Wallet" [uncertain] appears to be a mistyping of "Whilst" — "Wallet attached to 2nd C.H.B." is reproduced as written.
- **5 October entry:** the phrase after "after 12 ranging rounds" reads "sent C.F. mist" and is unclear; "C.F." is flagged [uncertain]. The sentence about Gnr Moore and Gnr MacLeod is reproduced as written ("Gnr Moore A and Gnr MacLeod D.J. being wounded").
- **8 October entry:** "Courvillers" is reproduced as written; the place name is unclear in the original.
- **Spelling as written:** "Marq" is used throughout as a shortening of Marquette; "etc and and" (21st–31st entry) is reproduced as written.
