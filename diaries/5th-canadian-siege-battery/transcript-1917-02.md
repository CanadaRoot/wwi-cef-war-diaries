## Page 3 (February 1917, p. 3) — [diary text]

WAR DIARY or INTELLIGENCE SUMMARY. (Erase heading not required.)

### Mt St Eloy — Feb 1st

Nil.

### Mt St Eloy — Feb 2nd

NIL

### Mt St Eloy — Feb 3rd

Nil.

### Mt St Eloy — Feb 4th

At 1.10.p.m. commenced shoot by aeroplane observation on Hostile Battery No 636 at T19c 20.75. 80 Rounds were fired on this target with very good results, plane reporting several Y's, Z's and O.K's After the 89th round our plane was attacked by an enemy plane and a hot fight ensued in which the observer in our plane was severely wounded and brought down. The shoot was consequently abandoned.

### Mt St Eloy — Feb 5th

During the afternoon we started a shoot by aeroplane observation on Hostile Battery No 644 in B1a 41.07. but as plane was driven off by hostile planes, shoot again had to be stopped.

### Mt St Eloy — Feb 6th

Nil

### Mt St Eloy — Feb 7th

During the morning we carried on with a shoot by aeroplane observation on Hostile Battery No 644 in B1a 41.07. We fired 34 Rounds for registration and effect, with very satisfactory results. From 1.15 p.m. to 3.30 p.m. fired 67 Rounds on Trench Mortars in S28d 40.30. Observed by F.O.O. At 3.p.m. we started a shoot on Hostile Battery No 670 in B1a 35.52. Had only fired 3 Rounds when we received urgent message from H.F.C. to send plane in as it was required for other work,

### Mt St Eloy — Feb 8th

A shoot this morning by aeroplane observation was very unsatisfactory owing to our wireless gear being out of order, 34 Rounds were fired on target which was a Hostile Battery in B1d 65.52.

### Mt St Eloy — Feb 9th

An aeroplane shoot carried out at Midday was very satisfactory, the target being Hostile Battery No 644 in B1a 41.07. Plane reported several O.K's Y's, and Z's. During the afternoon we fired 32 Rounds for calibration of bands and error of day on THELUS MILL, using double line observation.

## Page 4 (February 1917, p. 4) — [diary text]

### Mt St Eloy — Feb 10th

Nil

### Mt St Eloy — Feb 11th

Nil.

### Mt St Eloy — Feb 12th

Fired 21 Rounds on Trench Junction at S28d 65.70 by ground observation, results were very satisfactory, several rounds being seen to drop into the trench, others were very near.

### Mt St Eloy — Feb 13th

From 4.a.m. to 5 a.m. we fired on three Hostile Batteries Numbered respectively 634, 604, and 615. in S18c 9.3. S18d 15.90 and S18e 1.2. 115 Rounds being fired on these targets. During the morning we received a request from the 7th Infantry Brigade to open fire on Trench Junction, Switches and Artillery Ways in S29d 45.55. 40 Rounds being fired.

### Mt St Eloy — Feb 14th

At noon we started an aeroplane shoot on Trench Mortars at S28b 23.25, shoot very unsatisfactory as we again had considerable trouble with the wireless installation and only received one correction for 8 Rounds fired.

### Mt St Eloy — Feb 15th

Nil.

### Mt St Eloy — Feb 16th

At 3.a.m. we went into action against Hostile Batteries No's 615, 618, and 628. fired 5 Rounds on each target. At 4.a.m. we received orders from Adjutant to fire 5 more rounds on H.B. No 615 which was done, firing at 3 minute intervals. During the day 10 Rounds were fired on FARBUS and VIMY at request of Group.

### Mt St Eloy — Feb 17th

Nil.

### Mt St Eloy — Feb 18th

Officers quarters moved up to Battery position. Major Maxwell died at 7.20.p.m. this evening.

### Mt St Eloy — Feb 19th

Major Maxwell was buried at 3.p.m. today, all officers except Lieut R.A.Fraser attended, also 75 O.R's.

## Page 5 (February 1917, p. 5) — [diary text]

### Mt St Eloy — Feb 20th

Nil.

### Mt St Eloy — Feb 21st

Nil.

### Mt St Eloy — Feb 22nd

Engaged in shifting Guns for new arc of fire. During the evening 5 Rounds each were fired on Hostile Batteries No's 615 and 671 in S18a 10.20 and S23c 65.75.

### Mt St Eloy — Feb 23rd

NIL.

### Mt St Eloy — Feb 24th

One Gun was put back into new position during the evening, but line was not laid out.

### Mt St Eloy — Feb 25th

Gun ready for action at 10.30.a.m. and started to take down other guns.

### Mt St Eloy — Feb 26th

Lieut L.H.Gass reported to H.Q. 54th Batt. as liason officer in connection with C.B.

### Mt St Eloy — Feb 27th

At Noon commenced shoot by aeroplane observation on Trench Mortars in S23b 23.25, fired 2 Rounds which were unobserved, plane then gave C.I. on account of mist and clouds.

### Mt St Eloy — Feb 28th

Nil.

## Transcriber's notes — February 1917

- **Date gaps (days with no entry):** None. Every day 1–28 February has an entry. "Nil" days: 1, 2, 3, 6, 10, 11, 15, 17, 20, 21, 23 and 28 February.
- **Entries spanning pages:** None. No entry runs across a page break in this volume.
- **Hour-column mark:** every entry bears the same repeated handwritten mark in the Hour column, which is illegible — it appears to be an initialled signature, but it cannot be read with confidence. The Remarks column is blank throughout.
- **4 February entry:** the diary states "80 Rounds were fired" and then "After the 89th round our plane was attacked" — the two figures disagree as written; both are reproduced as typed.
- **7 February entry:** the urgent message is said to come from "H.F.C.", reproduced as written; the abbreviation is not explained in the diary. The entry ends mid-sentence with a comma.
- **22 February entry:** the word "NIL" at the start of the entry is struck through in the diary; the entry itself describes shifting the guns.
- **26 February entry:** "liason" is spelled as written in the diary. "C.B." (counter-battery) is not spelled out in the diary.
- **28 February entry:** a further line below the 28th entry repeats "28th" with a mark in the Hour column and no text — it appears to be a stray or duplicate line.
- **"Y's and Z's" and "O.K's":** aeroplane observers' reports of fall of shot, as written (4 and 9 February).
- **Casualties:** 4 February — the observer in the battery's aeroplane was severely wounded when the plane was attacked by an enemy plane and brought down (unnamed). 18 February — Major Maxwell died at 7.20 p.m.; 19 February — he was buried at 3 p.m., attended by all officers except Lieut R.A. Fraser and 75 other ranks. No cause of death is given in the diary. No other battery casualties are recorded in February.
- **People named:** Major Maxwell; Lieut R.A. Fraser; Lieut L.H.Gass. The Adjutant (16 February), the 7th Infantry Brigade, and the unnamed aeroplane observer are named only by role.
- **Places:** Mt St Eloy (Mount St Eloy), Thelus Mill, Farbus, Vimy.
- **No appendices** are listed or included with this volume; the contents page lists only "Diary Text 3 Pages".
- Form imprint at the foot of the diary pages: "A6945 Wt. W11423/M1160 350,000 12/16 D. D. & L. Forms/C.2118/14."
