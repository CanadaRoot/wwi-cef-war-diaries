## Page 3 (August 1917, p. 3) — [diary text]

WAR DIARY or INTELLIGENCE SUMMARY. (Erase heading not required.)

5th CANADIAN SIEGE BATTERY

### ANGRES — AUGUST

During the month of August the Battery took part in the capture of Hill 70, also the taking of the Green Crassier and all the fighting on the Lens front. During this time the Battery was twice heavily shelled by 8" and on several occasions we were bombarded with Gas Shells.

### ANGRES — 6th

Lieut. R.L. Smith was sent to No 8 Canadian Siege Battery.

### ANGRES — 12th

Lieut. F.R. May proceeded on Leave to England.

During a heavy bombardment by the enemy a large number of stores and ammunition were blown up and a direct hit on the earth box of No 1 Gun. Two men No 1250744 Gunner Beales J.D. and No 203 Gunner Sloan O.S. were buried in a cellar which collapsed as the result of a direct hit. The men were extricated and sent to Field Ambulance. No 91881 Sgt Ware J.A. was also knocked unconscious by the concussion of a shell

### ANGRES — 25th

Lieut W.A. Beecroft joined Battery and Lieut F.R.May returned from leave to England.

### ANGRES — 26th

Lieut. D.J. Maxwell proceeded on leave to England. and Lieut. D.A. Guildford proceeded to Canadian Corp Gas School.

[Signed, bottom right:] W. A. Beecroft Lt.
for O.C 5.C.S.B

## Transcriber's notes — August 1917

- **Date gaps (days with no entry):** this volume covers only 6–26 August 1917, and only five dated entries survive (6, 12, 25 and 26 August, plus an undated monthly summary). There is no day-by-day record for 1–5 or 27–31 August, and no daily entries for 7–11 or 13–24 August.
- **Lost records:** the covering letter dated 3 November 1917 (p. 4) states that the diaries for August and September were delayed because "the records were lost during moving operations". The August diary is therefore a retrospective summary written from memory, not a contemporary daily log.
- **Place column:** the word "was" is written in the Date column beside the "ANGRES" place entries (appearing as "ANGRES was" / "was"); it appears to be a stray or repeated word and is not part of the place name.
- **12 August entry:** the heavy bombardment described in the second paragraph of the 12 August entry is not separately dated in the diary; it is transcribed under 12 August as written.
- **Regimental numbers:** Gunner Sloan's number is written as "No 203" — unusually short, but reproduced as written. Gunner Beales (1250744) is the same man listed among the July reinforcements.
- **Casualties:** 12 August — Gunner J. D. Beales (1250744) and Gunner O. S. Sloan (No 203) buried in a cellar that collapsed after a direct hit; both were extricated and sent to Field Ambulance. Sgt J. A. Ware (91881) knocked unconscious by the concussion of a shell. No deaths are recorded in August.
- **People named:** Lieut. R. L. Smith (sent to No. 8 Canadian Siege Battery, 6 August); Lieut. F. R. May (leave to England, 12 August; returned 25 August); Gunner J. D. Beales (1250744); Gunner O. S. Sloan (No 203); Sgt J. A. Ware (91881); Lieut. W. A. Beecroft (joined the battery, 25 August; signed the diary for the O.C.); Lieut. D. J. Maxwell (leave to England, 26 August; Acting O.C., signed the covering letter); Lieut. D. A. Guildford (Canadian Corps Gas School, 26 August).
- **Places:** Angres, Hill 70, Green Crassier, Lens front.
- **Units mentioned:** No. 8 Canadian Siege Battery; Canadian Corps Gas School; A.A.G., Canadian Section, 3rd Echelon, G.H.Q. (addressee of the covering letter).
- **Appendices:** the covering letter dated 3 November 1917 (p. 4) is filed with this volume; it covers the August, September and October diaries and is transcribed in full above. The contents page lists only "Diary Text 1 Page".
