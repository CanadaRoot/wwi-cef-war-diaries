## Page 3 (November 1918, p. 3) — [diary text]

WAR DIARY or INTELLIGENCE SUMMARY. (Erase heading not required.)

### Haynecourt — November 1st to 9th

Battery standing by for orders, all guns limbered ready for an immediate move were on roadside with cats' parked nearby. The men were engaged in salving ammunition and thousands of rounds of all calibres were gathered in from the neighbourhood and piled in small dumps near the roadside where they could be easily loaded in lorries etc. Those men not engaged on fatigues indulged in various games and took full advantage of this quiet period.

### Haynecourt — 9th to 22nd

Battery pulled out early this morning and left Haynecourt for Lourches. The guns moved away immediately after breakfast, personnel and stores leaving later. One of the caterpillars had the misfortune to break a tread whilst crossing a light line railway and was consequently delayed two days in reaching Lorches. Guns were parked in Railway Yard and armed guards mounted, the men being billeted in Houses adjoining railway. Parades were held each morning and both men and guns held in readiness for immediate move. The news of the signing of the armistice was received at 11.30.a.m. November 12th great enthusiasm being displayed by all ranks. The 9.2" Howitzer Brigade ceased to exist as from the 21st inst; this Unit being transferred to 91st Brigade. R.G.A. for administration.

### Haynecourt — 23rd to 26th

Battery moved forward to Quievrechain where guns were parked in Town square. One gun was left behind at Lorches on account of broken drawbar and was brought up some days later after the necessary repairs had been made. Excellent billeting accomodation was found for the men and a large room in the Chateau which contained a piano in good condition was used as a recreation room. The usual parades were held in the morning and the men, with the exception of small fatigue parties were allowed to indulge in various sports during the afternoon.

### Haynecourt — 27th

## Page 4 (November 1918, p. 4) — [diary text]

### Quievrechain — 27th (continued)

desirous of proceeding to the Rhine were transferred to various batteries in 2nd Brigade CGA for this purpose.

### Quievrechain — 28th

We were today reinforced by 4 Officers viz:— Lieuts E.A. Leslie, W.E.Hare, O.R.Crowell and J.E. Mooney

### Quievrechain — 30th

Battery left Quievrechain and proceeded to Jemappes. 1st Brigade CGA and all batteries administered by it also moved to same town. On account of the shortage of lorries Batteries were moved one per day, all moves being completed by 30th. Guns were parked in square and good billets obtained for personnel.

[Signed:] N. P. MacLeod Major, O.C. 5th Canadian Siege Battery.
