# War Diary Transcript — 5th Canadian Siege Battery, January 1917

- **LAC Item ID:** 2004798, RG 9 III-D-3, vol. 4976, file 572, reel T-10804, finding aid 9-52
- **Month / Volume:** January 1917, Volume 5 (covers 1 – 31 January 1917)
- **Pages in this file:** LAC images pp. 1–6 (6 pages: cover, contents, 4 diary text pages)
- **Diary text date range:** 1 – 31 January 1917
- **Commanding officer / signatory:** No signature block closes this volume and no commanding officer is named in the text. Capt L.C. Ord and Lieut Fraser are named as observing officers.
- **Transcription note:** The diary is typewritten on Army Form C. 2118 ("War Diary or Intelligence Summary"). The printed heading "INTELLIGENCE SUMMARY" is not struck through on the diary pages. Every entry bears the same repeated handwritten mark in the Hour column, which is illegible (see Notes). The Remarks column is blank throughout. The place is "Mt St Eloy" for every entry.

WAR DIARY or INTELLIGENCE SUMMARY. (Erase heading not required.)

### Mt St Eloy — Jan 1st

Nil

### Mt St Eloy — Jan 2nd

"A" Gun was put into new position at SOX.

### Mt St Eloy — Jan 3rd

Nil

### Mt St Eloy — Jan 4th

"D" Gun was put into new position at SOX.

### Mt St Eloy — Jan 5th

At 11.30a.m. started a double line shoot on THELUS MILL at A6c 55.95 for registration of No 1 Gun, the result being very satisfactory. At Noon we fired 15 Rounds on the same target to test the difference in range between Gas Check and flat band type of shell. During the afternoon a registration was carried out on La Folie Farm in S29a 4.4. Excellent registration was obtained and four direct hits observed.

### Mt St Eloy — Jan 6th

During the morning we fired 100 Rounds Bombardment on trenches A5a 40.25, In the early morning observation was impossible owing to poor visibility but it cleared up later and the general effect was observed to be very satisfactory.

### Mt St Eloy — Jan 7th

Nil.

### Mt St Eloy — Jan 8th

Fired 6 Rounds on THELUS MILL for Line and in the afternoon fired 10 Rounds on La Folie Farm for registration.

### Mt St Eloy — Jan 9th

Fired 16 Rounds on La Folie in the morning. Observed by Lieut Fraser and very satisfactory several shells being observed to fall in the building. In the afternoon we fired 30 Rounds on Trench Junction at S28b 75.80. Shoot very satisfactory.

### Mt St Eloy — Jan 10th

Fired at THELUS MILL to check error of day, During the after noon fired 41 Rounds on Trench Junction A17b 5.7 several O.K's and shoot very satisfactory.

## Page 4 (January 1917, p. 4) — [diary text]

### Mt St Eloy — Jan 11th

Heavy Snow and Mist prevented any shooting by observation to-day.

### Mt St Eloy — Jan 12th

Fired on A5a 5.5. to A5b 1.5. Visibility very poor, had to cease fire after 8 Rounds.

### Mt St Eloy — Jan 13th

Nil

### Mt St Eloy — Jan 14th

The day opened up very misty with occasional snow flurries making observation very difficult a shoot was attempted on A11b 2.6 but had to be abandoned after only 4 Rounds had been fired as it was found impossible to observe.

### Mt St Eloy — Jan 15th

During the morning we fired 11 Rounds on Trench junction at A11d 64.27 to correct error of day. 11 Rounds were fired during the afternoon, bombardment on A23a 48.85.

### Mt St Eloy — Jan 16th

On account of the heavy fall of snow extra precautions had to be taken to conceal our positions New camouflage was rigged made of calico fastened on wire. No shooting was done to-day.

### Mt St Eloy — Jan 17th

NIL

### Mt St Eloy — Jan 18th

NIL

### Mt St Eloy — Jan 19th

During the morning we fired salvos from two guns on a Hostile Battalion Headquarters situate S29a 65.00. Capt L.C. Ord observed these from a front line O.P. and very good results were obtained. 15 Rounds were also put over on Trench Junction in S28b 6.7. after which salvos were fired at intervals during the evening.

### Mt St Eloy — Jan 20th

Capt L.C. ORD again observed a shoot on Enemies Battalion H.Q. 14 Rounds were fired with satisfactory results. During the afternoon 22 Rounds were fired on trench junction at S28b 65.70.

### Mt St Eloy — Jan 22nd

Fired 38 Rounds by ground observation on target in A5c 41.52. Shoot observed by Lieut Fraser and was very satisfactory, after this shoot we switched to A5a 4.2 and fired 10 Rounds [uncertain] which started a fire amongst some material in the trench.

### Mt St Eloy — Jan 23rd

Early in the morning we fired 4 Rounds on VIMY STATION for effect ( T20c 13.60 ) Plane went up to observe for us on target in T13d 39.95, this was the first time a plane had observed for us since coming on this front, and was not successful. No correction was given for the first shoot and only two corrections followed for the next four rounds, these being most erratic, the plane was therefore given C I and the shoot postponed. During the afternoon we fired 35 Rounds by ground observation on A11a 7.3. This was very satisfactory, three rounds were seen to fall in the trench and three direct hits were observed on dug-outs in the trench junction.

### Mt St Eloy — Jan 24th

At 11.10 A.M. Plane went up to observe for us on Hostile Battery No 618 in T15b 44.05. 40 Rounds were fired with very good results, plane reporting several O.K's. During the afternoon we fired several rounds on this target for effect.

### Mt St Eloy — Jan 25th

Started shoot on S24c 72.32 by aeroplane observation but again had considerable trouble, 17 Rounds were fired, only 4 of which were observed. The Guns were carefully checked and fuses changed but plane could not get our shots. At 11.30 A.M. started shoot by ground observation on trenches from S29c 2.6 to S29c 4.6. This was a very effective shoot, a considerable number of rounds being seen to fall into the trench. From 1.0 P.M. to 3.0 P.M. we fired 150 Rounds without observation on a triangle of trenches at A16d 4.5 to 4.6. We were complimented on the effectiveness of this shoot by the infantry. During the afternoon we fired 40 Rounds without observation on DUMP AVENUE TRENCH in S29d 7.0 to S29d 2.0. and 50 Rounds on trenches in A5b 0.6.

### Mt St Eloy — Jan 26th (continued)

Contd. after we had registered 100 Rounds were put over with very good results, Plane gave mostly Y's and Z's and several O.K's

### Mt St Eloy — Jan 27th

49 Rounds were fired on Artillery Commanders Post in S18d 39.73 and track Junction in S18d 4.6. At 10.30. a.m. aeroplane attempted to register us on Trench Junction in A11d 63.26 but was not successful and shoot had to be abandoned after firing 9 Rounds. 11.30.a.m. to 11.50 a.m. opened up bombardment at slow rate on S29a 05.00 and S28b 6.7. Fired for effect without observation. At 2.45 p.m. commenced shoot by aeroplane observation on Hostile Battery No 634 in S18c 9.3. Fired 141 Rounds with very satisfactory results Plane reported a number of Y's, Z's and O.K's

### Mt St Eloy — Jan 28th

Enemy shelled Mont St Eloy during the morning and orders were received from the adjutant to retaliate at the rate of two shells to each one of the enemy's. We fired 20 Rounds on Farmus [uncertain] and 20 Rounds on the town of VIMY, cease fire was given at 10.55 a.m.

### Mt St Eloy — Jan 29th

11.05 a.m. started shoot by aeroplane observation on Hostile Battery No 648 in B2d 4.2. Shoot was again unsatisfactory, only one correction given for 11 Rounds, Plane was then given C I. From 11.15 a.m. to 12.10.p.m. fired 25 Rounds on Trench Junction in S29c 2.6. This was observed by Lieut F.R.May and was very satisfactory.

### Mt St Eloy — Jan 30th

Fired 47 Rounds registration shoot on S29c 2.6. Registration satisfactory.

### Mt St Eloy — Jan 31st

At 2.50 P.M. started shoot on Hostile Battery in B1a 15.45 Fired 23 Rounds, plane reported one pit hit and badly damaged, in the meantime Hostile Battery No 657 in B14b 74.10 had become very active plane switched us on to this target. One of our shells fell just behind centre Gun Pit and exploded some of the enemy's ammunition, As this gun was working at the time it is probably that some of his gunners were hit, The battery at once ceased fire and plane shortly after gave CI on account of clouds.

## Transcriber's notes — January 1917

- **Date gaps (days with no entry):** None. Every day 1–31 January has an entry. "Nil" days: 1, 3, 7, 13, 17 and 18 January.
- **Entries spanning pages:** the 21 January entry runs from p. 4 onto p. 5; the 26 January entry runs from p. 5 onto p. 6.
- **Hour-column mark:** every entry bears the same repeated handwritten mark in the Hour column, which is illegible — it appears to be an initialled signature, but it cannot be read with confidence. The Remarks column is blank throughout.
- **"SOX.":** the new gun positions of 2 and 4 January are given as "SOX." as written; the reference is not explained in the diary.
- **5 January entry:** the La Folie Farm reference is typed with wide spacing ("S 2.9 a 4.4") and is read here as "S29a 4.4".
- **22 January entry:** a word after "10 Rounds" is struck through in the diary and is illegible, flagged [uncertain].
- **25 January entry:** the last target is typed without a space ("A5b0.6") and is read here as "A5b 0.6".
- **27 January entry:** "track Junction" is reproduced as written.
- **28 January entry:** the place name reads "Farmus" as typed, flagged [uncertain]. It is very likely Farbus, the village near Vimy — the February diary spells the same place "FARBUS" (16 February).
- **"C I" / "CI":** the aeroplane-observation code given when a shoot is abandoned (23 and 29 January) and at the end of the 31 January entry; its meaning is not explained in the diary.
- **"Y's and Z's" and "O.K's":** aeroplane observers' reports of fall of shot, as written (26 and 27 January).
- **Casualties:** No battery casualties are recorded in January. On 31 January the diary notes it is "probably that some of his gunners were hit" when a shell exploded enemy ammunition behind a German gun pit — enemy casualties, not the battery's.
- **People named:** Capt L.C. Ord; Lieut Fraser; Lieut F.R.May. The adjutant (28 January) and the infantry who complimented the 25 January shoot are unnamed.
- **Places:** Mt St Eloy (Mount St Eloy), Thelus Mill, La Folie Farm, Vimy Station, Vimy, Farbus [uncertain], Dump Avenue Trench.
- **No appendices** are listed or included with this volume; the contents page lists only "Diary Text 4 Pages".
- Form imprint at the foot of the diary pages: "T2134. Wt. W708—776. 500000. 4/15. Sir J. C. & S."
- The diary pages are numbered 13–16 in the top right corner (LAC image numbers 3–6).
