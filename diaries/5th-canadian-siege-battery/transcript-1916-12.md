## Page 3 (December 1916, p. 3) — [diary text]

WAR DIARY or INTELLIGENCE SUMMARY. (Erase heading not required.)

### POZIERS — Dec 1st

NIL.

### POZIERS — Dec 2nd

Only 10 Rounds on concentrated shoot were fired to-day, Nothing unusual occured.

### POZIERS — Dec 3rd

For the purpose of counter Battery work one more Hostile Battery Numbered 151 was added to our program. There was no firing to-day.

### POZIERS — Dec 4th

A machine of the 7ta Squadron R.F.C. went up to observe for us at 7.20a.m. on hostile Battery No 416 but gave C I Clouds before any rounds were fired. Plane went up again at 1.45.P.M. but again gave C I Clouds after 13 Rounds had been fired., and shoot had to be discontinued.

### POZIERS — Dec 5th

We took on Four Hostile Batteries for counter Battery work to-day Six Rounds from each gun being fired on each target, these were numbered 524, 144, 145. 146., Received orders to-day that we are to pull out at 8.00 A.M. on 7th.

### POZIERS — Dec 6th

Battery went out of action today preparing to move.

### POZIERS — Dec 7th

Battery left Poziers at 8. A.M. and proceeded by road to TOUTENCOURT which was reached at 2.p.m. The Men went into a rest camp here and guns arrived later in the evening

### POZIERS — Dec 8th

Battery resting.

### TOUTENCOURT — Dec 9th

Battery left TOUTENCOURT at 7.a.m. and proceeded to SAVY BERLETTE, which was reached at 7.p.m. Men went into billets here.

### SAVY BERLETTE — Dec 10th

Resting and awaiting the guns.

### SAVY BERLETTE — Dec 11th

Two of our guns arrived to-day.

### SAVY BERLETTE — Dec 12th

Men were transferred to Huts in village of ECROIVES.

## Page 4 (December 1916, p. 4) — [diary text]

WAR DIARY or INTELLIGENCE SUMMARY. (Erase heading not required.)

### Mt St Eloy — Dec 13th

Remaining two guns arrived to-day. Officers quarters were taken in Mt. St Eloy and a room fitted up for orderly room. Lieut. D.A.Guildford joined Battery.

### Mt St Eloy — Dec 14th to 16th

Preparing positions for guns. On night of 16th Two guns were put in and got ready for firing in the morning.

### Mt St Eloy — Dec 17th

The day opened up very foggy and later in the day increased, it was too foggy to observe and guns could not be registered.

### Mt St Eloy — Dec 18th

Started firing about 10.30.a.m. on A16d 8.6 and up trench 200 yards, on account of very poor visibility the registration was not satisfactory.

### Mt St Eloy — Dec 19th

Visibility was better to-day and we were able to get good registration with two guns in action.

### Mt St Eloy — Dec 20th

A broken cam in breech mechanism of "D" Gun put that gun out of action for the day, a cam was taken from one of the other guns and "D" Gun put back into action.

### Mt St Eloy — Dec 21st

Working on new gun positions at "SOX" which is to be our permanent position. New dugouts were also being built and Lieut's May and Maxwell scouted for an Observation post. A good O.P. was found in [uncertain] a 3.4 in an Estaminet but we were advised by the Adjutant to find a new one as this was already overcrowded. "B" & "C" Guns are now out of the shops and parked at [uncertain].

### Mt St Eloy — Dec 22nd

No firing but working on new positions.

### Mt St Eloy — Dec 23rd

We were all ready to open fire to-day but weather was too bad to observe, Heavy rains and high winds increasing at times to gale force prevented any work being done. "B" Gun was however put into position during the night.

### Mt St Eloy — Dec 24th

Fired to-day on THELUS MILL for registration from new O.P. which was named the "Coalbox" Observation was good and satisfactory registration was obtained. Remaining gun was put into position during the night.

[Form imprint at the foot of the page: "T1134. Wt. W708—776. 500000. 4/16. Sir J. C. & S."]

## Page 5 (December 1916, p. 5) — [diary text]

WAR DIARY or INTELLIGENCE SUMMARY. (Erase heading not required.)

### Mt St Eloy — Dec 25th

Received message stating that no action will be taken to-day bu C.C.H.A. except in retaliation which would then be very severe. All Guns were kept laid on night lines in readiness.

### Mt St Eloy — Dec 26th to 28th

NIL.

### Mt St Eloy — Dec 29th

Officers moved to new quarters in old Cuateau.

### Mt St Eloy — Dec 30th

Prepared to remove two guns from position at Carbolic to new position at SOX.

### Mt St Eloy — Dec 31st

Preparing positions at Sox for the two guns removed from Carbolic.
