# War Diary Transcript — 5th Canadian Siege Battery, July 1917

- **Unit:** 5th Canadian Siege Battery (originally 165th Canadian Siege Battery), Canadian Garrison Artillery
- **LAC Item ID:** 2004798, RG 9 III-D-3, vol. 4976, file 572, reel T-10804, finding aid 9-52
- **Month / Volume:** July 1917, Volume 11 (covers 1 – 31 July 1917)
- **Pages in this file:** LAC images pp. 56–62 (7 pages: cover, contents, 5 diary text pages)
- **Diary text date range:** 1 – 31 July 1917
- **Commanding officer / signatory:** Each entry in the Remarks column is initialled or signed by a Major whose signature reads "[uncertain] Macdonald, Major". No other commanding officer is named in the text.
- **Transcription note:** The diary is typewritten on Army Form C. 2118/12 ("War Diary or Intelligence Summary"); the printed heading "INTELLIGENCE SUMMARY" is struck through. The "Hour" column is blank throughout. The Place column reads "Field." for 1 July and 20–31 July and is otherwise blank. Target references are given in the diary's own abbreviated form (e.g. "M20d40.75", "M33a60.25"). "O.K." is used for a direct hit; "K" is also used for a direct hit (e.g. "10K", "20Ks"). "H.E." is high explosive; "5.9" and "4.2" are shell calibres in inches. "R.F.C." is Royal Flying Corps; "C.H.A.G." is Canadian Heavy Artillery Group.

## Page 3 (July 1917, p. 3) — [diary text]

WAR DIARY or INTELLIGENCE SUMMARY. (Erase heading not required.)

JULY 1917.

### Field. — 1.7.17

Between 11am and 12 noon fired 40 rounds on Concentrated Troops in Quarry at M20d40.75.

At Noon all Batteries in Canadian Corps fired 3 salvos for Dominion Day Celebration.

In the afternoon we carried out successful shoots on Trenches in M20c and and on Bridge at M35a and Railway at M27d.

### [place not stated] — 2.7.17

During the afternoon we fired 100 rounds on Trench at M33a60.25 to 85.00 with ground observation. This shoot was very satisfactory, Trench and Houses in vicinity being demolished and a fire caused.

In the evening we carried out a successful shoot on Hostile Battery MY70 at M21d99.03 with balloon observation. After we had completed this shoot the enemy shelled the position with 5.9.H.E. and Gas Shells. We had only one casualty, 43982 2/AM Carpenter, W.H. (16th. Squadron R.F.C.) being slightly wounded in the hand with a splinter.

### [place not stated] — 3.7.17

From 6.30pm to 11pm we engaged Hostile Battery MZ28 at M28b40.05 obtaining 2 oks. The shoot was very satisfactory.

### [place not stated] — 4.7.17

To-day was very quiet. Only fired 50 rounds on Trenches in M33b and M27d.

### [place not stated] — 5.7.17

During the day we fired a few rounds for registration. In the evening we carried out a very successful shoot on Hostile Battery MX41 at M16d84.30. We obtained 6 OKs.

### [place not stated] — 6.7.17

During the morning we fired 64 rounds on Hostile Battery MX49 at [uncertain] M5o52.44. We obtained 10K but the shoot had to be discontinued owing to hostile shelling.

During the evening we fired 70 rounds on Trench at M34c00.85, with aeroplane observation. Shoot was successful.

During the evening the position was straffed with 5.9 HE and 4.2 gas Shells. The undermentioned were gassed,

## Page 5 (July 1917, p. 5) — [diary text]

### [place not stated] — 13.7.17

In the forenoon we engaged Hostile Battery MX34 at M11a26.33. The registration was not very satisfactory in the morning and later in the day we went back to this target expending a total of 185 rounds.

### [place not stated] — 14.7.17

To-day fired 200 rounds on Hostile Battery MX40 at M16d88.47, with aeroplane observation. Fired 26 rounds during ranging and 174 rounds for effect.

### [place not stated] — 15.7.17

During the afternoon fired 150 rounds on Hostile Battery MW15 at M15d 00.55. Shoot was satisfactory. In the evening we fired several rounds on Trenches and Houses at M15a, M14b and M34a.

### [place not stated] — 16.7.17

During the after noon we fired several rounds on Trench Junction at M33d98.90, Railway in M34c and T.Ms. at M19b70.65 to M19b77.45.

In the evening we fired 120 rounds on Hostile Battery at M11c00.32. Shoot had to be discontinued owing to 2 guns going out of Action with broken elevating gear. This is the first time the Battery has been completely out of Action since arrival in France.

### [place not stated] — 17.7.17

Nothing to report, all guns out of Action.

### [place not stated] — 18.7.17

No firing, guns still out of Action.

### [place not stated] — 19.8.17 [sic — written "19.8.17" in the diary; the entry falls between 18 and 20 July]

During the evening fired 25 rounds neutralization on Hostile Battery TX30 at T6a50.65.

## Page 6 (July 1917, p. 6) — [diary text]

### Field. — 20.7.17

Fired 170 rounds on Trenches in T4a6.9 to M34c8.0 to M33c7.3, also 10 rounds concentration on SALLAUMINES.

### [place not stated] — 21.7.17

Fired 150 rounds on Trench at M33d95.85 with aeroplane observation but shooting appeared erratic.

### [place not stated] — 22.7.17

Day quiet. Only fired 39 [a word is struck through here] rounds on Concentration Shoots on Houses at M20a and M25a.

### [place not stated] — 23.7.17

During the forenoon fired several rounds on Trenches in M19d, M35c and d, and M34b. In the evening we fired 140 rounds on Hostile Battery MZ90 at M29b4.9. Results were satisfactory.

During the [evening] position was again shelled with 5.9HE and 4.2 gas Shells. We had no casualties.

### [place not stated] — 24.7.17

From 12noon to 5pm fired 250 rounds on Hostile Battery MX34 at M11a20.35 with aeroplane observation. Obtained satisfactory results.

8.15pm to 9.40pm fired 90 rounds on Hostile Battery MZ99 at M22a9.0. Orders from Counter Battery Group.

At midnight fired 18 rounds rapid on NOYELLES.

### [place not stated] — 25.7.17

Day quiet, only fired 35 rounds calibration on Fosse 4 de COURIERES.

### [place not stated] — 26.7.17

Fired 30 rounds rapid on CITE ST EMILE at 11.35am.

11.55am to 12.45pm fired 75 rounds concentration on Hostile Battery MX28 at M10b 4.5.

### [place not stated] — 28.7.17

[One line of the entry is struck through and is illegible — flagged [uncertain].] The Battery Position was shelled to-day with H.E. Lieut. J.Kay was seriously wounded and died on way to Dressing Station. 346828 Cpl. E. Sheldon was also wounded.

## Transcriber's notes — July 1917

- **Date gaps (days with no entry):** none — every day 1–31 July is covered.
- **Entries spanning pages:** the 6 July entry runs from p. 3 onto p. 4 (the list of men gassed); the 26 July entry runs from p. 6 onto p. 7.
- **Signatures:** every entry in the Remarks column is signed or initialled by a Major whose signature reads "[uncertain] Macdonald, Major" — the surname appears to be Macdonald but the initials are not legible enough to read with confidence. The entries of 8, 9, 10, 12, 17 and 18 July bear a shorter initialled mark in the same hand.
- **1 July entry:** at noon all batteries in the Canadian Corps fired 3 salvos for the Dominion Day celebration.
- **2 July entry:** the only casualty is 43982 2/AM Carpenter, W.H. (16th Squadron R.F.C.), slightly wounded in the hand by a splinter — he is not a member of the battery.
- **6 July entry:** the target reference for Hostile Battery MX49 is written "M5o52.44" — the middle character may be a letter "o" or a figure; the reading is [uncertain]. The same reference recurs on 7 July. The list of men gassed continues onto p. 4; the second name in the list (302914 Gnr. [uncertain], W.J.) is not legible, and a handwritten annotation reading "Smith, E.C." appears beside it. The other gassed men are: 1204 Amt. S. Sgt. Smith, E.C.; 303476 Gnr. Gillen, W.J.; 303425 Gnr. Davis, P.H.; 314723 Gnr. Lajoie, W.P.
- **11 July entry:** "We obtained 20Ks and 16Ys" — reproduced as written; the meaning of "Ys" is unclear.
- **16 July entry:** two guns went out of action with broken elevating gear; the diary notes this is the first time the battery has been completely out of action since arrival in France. The battery remained out of action 17–18 July.
- **19 July entry:** the date is written "19.8.17" in the diary — evidently a slip for 19 July, as the entry falls between 18 and 20 July.
- **22 July entry:** a word before "rounds" is struck through in the typescript; the entry reads "Only fired 39 rounds".
