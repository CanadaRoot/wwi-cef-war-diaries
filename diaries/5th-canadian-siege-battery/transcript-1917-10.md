# War Diary Transcript — 5th Canadian Siege Battery, October 1917

- **Unit:** 5th Canadian Siege Battery (originally 165th Canadian Siege Battery), Canadian Garrison Artillery
- **LAC Item ID:** 2004798, RG 9 III-D-3, vol. 4976, file 572, reel T-10804, finding aid 9-52
- **Month / Volume:** October 1917, Volume 14 (covers 1 – 31 October 1917)
- **Pages in this file:** LAC images pp. 70–74 (5 pages: cover, contents, 3 diary text pages)
- **Diary text date range:** 1 – 31 October 1917
- **Commanding officer / signatory:** Each diary page is signed "W. A. Beecroft, Lt., for O.C. 5.C.S.B." Major T. W. F. MacDonald commanded the battery until he was admitted to hospital on 28 October, leaving Lieut. D. J. Maxwell in command.
- **Transcription note:** The diary is typewritten on Army Form C. 2118/12 ("War Diary or Intelligence Summary"); the printed heading "INTELLIGENCE SUMMARY" is struck through. Daily entries resume this month after the retrospective August and September volumes. The "Hour" column is blank throughout; the Remarks column is blank throughout. The Place column reads "ANGRES" for 1–19 October and "YPRES." for 26–31 October, with the word "was" written in the Date column beside the place entries (see Notes). Target references are given in the diary's own abbreviated form (e.g. "M 21 c 90.18", "T 5 a 10.85"). "S.O.S." is the distress call for defensive fire; "C.I." is the aeroplane's "cease fire" / "cannot identify" signal as written.

## Page 4 (October 1917, p. 4) — [diary text]

### ANGRES — Oct 14th

Fired 9 Rounds on S.O.S. Lines Lens Sector at M 21 a 25.51. and 15 Rounds S.O.S. Lines Mericourt Sector at T.4 b 9.8.

### ANGRES — 15th

Nil

### ANGRES — 16th

Nil

### ANGRES — 17th

We received orders to be prepared to move North leaving our guns and equipment.

### ANGRES — 18th

We received orders to move and the Battery took the road in Motor Lorries leaving one Officer Lieut. Beecroft and 10 Men as Rear guard to hand over to relieving Battery. The remainder of the Battery proceeded to Bethune where we billeted for the night.

### ANGRES — 19th

Left Bethune and proceeded North via Hazebrouck and reached Steenvoorde about dusk. The lorries were parked in the Town Square where we stayed for the night.

### [place not stated] — Oct 20th

Reached YPRES about Noon and the men proceeded to their billets, The Officers quarters being in the CLOTH HALL.

### [place not stated] — Oct 21st

We took over from the 55th Australian Battery. They had two guns in action and one to be mounted.

### [place not stated] — 22nd

No 2001258 Gunner Marshall wounded in arm and side. No Firing today but work was carried on with mounting guns in preparation for the advance.

### [place not stated] — 23rd

Nil

### [place not stated] — 24th

No 1257815 Gunner MacFarlane W.P. seriously wounded and No 1257836 Gunner Gorham E.R. Slightly wounded. Fired 143 Rounds on strong points by visual observation and 48 Rounds Bursts of Fire and Harrassing.

### [place not stated] — 25th

[The word "Fired" is struck through here.] Fired 22 Rounds Barrage and 154 Rounds on strong points and Harrassing Fire on Graf Farm

[Signed, bottom right:] W. A. Beecroft Lt
for O.C. 5.C.S.B

## Page 5 (October 1917, p. 5) — [diary text]

### YPRES. — Oct 26th

Got gun into action for the advance of today but gun went out of action before the end of the day and had to be pulled out and put back on new line. Fired 298 Rounds attack barrage and 31 Rounds protective barrage on Battery lanes. During the evening we fired 12 Rounds, Bursts of Fire and during the dark hours fired 50 Rounds on Valour Farm.

### YPRES. — 27th

Fired 22 Rounds Barrage on Battery Lanes and 54 Rounds on a Pill Box at V 29 d 80.60 by Aeroplane observation. During the afternoon fired 12 Rounds as Bursts of Fire on Labour Farm and D.5 Central. At 3.15 P.M. we fired 20 Rounds barrage on Battery Lanes. During the night Bursts of fire were kept up and 38 Rounds expended.

### YPRES. — 28th

No 92919 Gunner Guerrin G.E. wounded in leg. Major T.W.F. MacDonald was admitted to Hospital leaving Lieut D.J. Maxwell in command of Battery. Fired Barrage of 8 Rounds on Battery Lanes. Battery heavily shelled by 5.9"s and No 1 Gun received direct hit putting it permanently out of action. The loading derrick of No 2 Gun was blown off and the gun was out of action for 24 hours. This put the Battery completely out of action until following evening when No 2 Gun was put in action again and No 3 Gun went into action the following morning in time for the advance on October 30th 1917.

### YPRES. — 29th

Lieut Fraser R.A. who had been sent to England to raise new section for two extra guns rejoined Unit bringing Lieut E. Scott and 70 Other ranks. Fired 33 Rounds Barrage in Battery Lanes, and during the night 30 Rounds on Valour and Vegetable Farms.

### YPRES. — 30th

Lieut J.M. Stairs arrived at Unit for duty with Battery. Fired 45 Rounds by visual observation on Vanity House and Vine Cottage, 50 Rounds were then fired by Map on Vapour Farm and 45 Rounds on Vocation Farm. During the afternoon 287 Rounds were fired on Barrage and 46 on S.O.S.

### YPRES. — 31st

At 5.A.M. 25 Rounds were fired on barrage and 10 on S.O.S. lines. At 1.45 P.M. an shoot was commenced on Valour Farm by Aeroplane observation but Plane gave C.I. after 2 Rounds had been fired. Bursts of fire were kept up during the afternoon and 20 Rounds fired on S.O.S. lines.

[Signed, bottom right:] W. A. Beecroft Lt
for O.C. 5.C.S.B

## Transcriber's notes — October 1917

- **Date gaps (days with no entry):** none — every day 1–31 October is covered.
- **Entries spanning pages:** none — each page ends at a day boundary (13, 25 and 31 October).
- **Place column:** the word "was" is written in the Date column beside the "ANGRES" and "YPRES." place entries, as in the August and September volumes; it appears to be a stray or repeated word and is not part of the place name.
- **9 October entry:** "No 91880 Gunner Burgess A. was accidentally killed by a shell explosion from a 105 Fuse" — reproduced as written.
- **12 October entry:** the first target reference is written "T 4 25" and appears incomplete (no sheet letter or second figure); it is flagged [uncertain].
- **17–21 October — the move north:** on 17 October the battery was ordered to prepare to move north, leaving its guns and equipment; on 18 October it travelled by motor lorry to Bethune (Lieut. Beecroft and 10 men remaining as rear guard to hand over to the relieving battery); on 19 October it travelled via Hazebrouck to Steenvoorde; on 20 October it reached Ypres, the officers quartered in the Cloth Hall; on 21 October it took over from the 55th Australian Battery, which had two guns in action and one to be mounted. This is the move to the Passchendaele front.
- **22 October entry:** Gunner Marshall's regimental number is written "2001258" — reproduced as written.
- **24 October entry:** the two wounded gunners' numbers are written "1257815" (MacFarlane) and "1257836" (Gorham) — reproduced as written.
- **25 October entry:** the word "Fired" at the start of the entry is struck through in the typescript, and the entry then begins again with "Fired 22 Rounds Barrage…".
- **28 October entry:** No. 1 Gun received a direct hit and was put permanently out of action; No. 2 Gun's loading derrick was blown off and the gun was out of action for 24 hours; the battery was completely out of action until the following evening, when No. 2 Gun was put in action again and No. 3 Gun went into action on the morning of 30 October, in time for the advance that day. Major T. W. F. MacDonald was admitted to hospital, leaving Lieut. D. J. Maxwell in command.
- **29 October entry:** Lieut. R. A. Fraser rejoined the unit from England, where he had been sent to raise a new section for two extra guns, bringing Lieut. E. Scott and 70 other ranks — the battery was being expanded from four to six guns.
