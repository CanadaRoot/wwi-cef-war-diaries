### Jemappes — December

The month of December was passed very quietly at Jemappes. Parades were held each morning at 9.a.m. and a sylabus of training which included Foot drill, Gun drill, and physical training was carried out. The afternoons being devoted to outdoor games and the evenings to concerts and entertainments. Sightseeing trips to Brussels were arranged but on account of the difficulties in transportation these trips were necessarily greatly curtailed and could not be indulged in as often as desired. A very enjoyable time was spent at Christmas and an excellent dinner, consisting of Plum pudding, Turkey etc with an good supply of Fruit, nuts and beer, was thoroughly appreciated by all ranks. An entertainment given after dinner by various members of the Battery was assisted by a blind professor of music. Dances were held in the evening and every endeavour was made to make Christmas as enjoyable as possible met with great success. The usual parades etc were resumed on 28th inst. Nothing of unusual interest occurred during the latter part of the month.

[Signed:] N. P. MacLeod Major, O.C. 5th Canadian Siege Battery.
