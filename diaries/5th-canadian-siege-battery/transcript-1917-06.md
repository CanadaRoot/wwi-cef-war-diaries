# War Diary Transcript — 5th Canadian Siege Battery, June 1917

- **Unit:** 5th Canadian Siege Battery (originally 165th Canadian Siege Battery), Canadian Garrison Artillery
- **LAC Item ID:** 2004798, RG 9 III-D-3, vol. 4976, file 572, reel T-10804, finding aid 9-52
- **Month / Volume:** June 1917, Volume 10 (covers 1 – 30 June 1917)
- **Pages in this file:** LAC images pp. 50–55 (6 pages: cover, contents, 4 diary text pages)
- **Diary text date range:** 1 – 30 June 1917
- **Commanding officer / signatory:** The volume is signed at the foot of the last diary page by a Lieutenant whose signature reads "[uncertain] Maxwell, Lt.", signed "for O/C 5th Canadian Siege Battery". No other commanding officer is named in the text; the O.C. is referred to without a name on 23 June.
- **Transcription note:** The diary is typewritten on Army Form C. 2118/12 ("War Diary or Intelligence Summary"); the printed heading "INTELLIGENCE SUMMARY" is struck through. The "Hour" column is blank throughout; the Remarks column is blank throughout. Unlike May, the Place column in June gives map references for the battery position (e.g. "S1946.2", "M33e5.9") rather than a place name; they are transcribed as written. Target references are given in the diary's own abbreviated form (e.g. "T1b9.6", "M31d4.2"). "O.K." is used for a direct hit. "M.G.E." is Machine Gun Emplacement. "F.O.O." is Forward Observation Officer; "S.O.S." is the distress call for defensive fire; "H.E.T." is the shell type as written.

## Page 3 (June 1917, p. 3) — [diary text]

WAR DIARY or INTELLIGENCE SUMMARY. (Erase heading not required.)

June, 1917.

### S1946.2 — June 1/17

Nothing to report.

### S1946.2 — June 2/17

Fired a few rounds on Junction Railway Embankment and Road at [uncertain] M31e95.90 also on Brewery at M31a95.15. Ground observation. F.O.O. reported 19 O.Ks. on Brewery and one round caused small fire.

### S1946.2 — June 3/17

Fired a few rounds on Houses in M31b1.2. Opening rounds were observed by F.O.O. Concentration shoot. During afternoon fired a few rounds on Fosse 3 at M30b., also on houses in M31b1.2, and Trenches and Cross Roads in ELEU DIT LEAUVETTE.

### [place not stated] — June 4th. and June 5th.

Nothing to report.

### [place not stated] — June 6/17

This evening fired one hundred rounds on Trench Junction T1b9.6 and Trench T1b9.6 to M31d4.2. F.O.O. reported several rounds falling in trench.

### [place not stated] — June 7/17

Fired 150 rounds on Trench T2a1.5 to 4.3. Shoot was very effective.

### [place not stated] — June 8/17

To-day we engaged three M.G.Es. Fired 120 rounds on M.G.E. at T1a90.35. F.O.O. Shoot was observed from Group O.P. and results were reported as satisfactory. F.O.O. sent to [uncertain] F.O.O. could not get communication through to Battery. Fired 15 rounds on M.G.E. at T1a2.6. This point was very difficult to identify and shoot had to be abandoned. Fired 80 rounds on M.G.E. at T2b3.9. Shoot was successful. Obtained 1 direct hit on Concrete House which tore a large hole in building. Many rounds fell close and building was very badly damaged.

8.57 pm to 9.10 pm. fired 53 rounds on Houses in ELEU DIT LEAUVETTE and Trench System immediately S.E. in response to S.O.S. Call.

11.45 pm to 1.15 am. June 9/17 fired 251 rounds on Houses in ELEU DIT LEAUVETTE and Trench System immediately S.E. Infantry Operation.

### [place not stated] — June 9/17

Responded to S.O.S. Call at 4.09 am. Fired 7 rounds on ELEU DIT LEAUVETTE and Trench System

## Page 4 (June 1917, p. 4) — [diary text]

### S1946.2 — June 9/17 (continued)

to South East.

### [place not stated] — June 10th. and 11th.

Nothing to report. Shooting Nil.

### [place not stated] — June 12/17

Early this morning we fired 39 rounds on our S.O.S. lines. German attack did not materialise. During the afternoon fired 150 rounds on Railway Embankment at M30d15.38, with ground observation. Shoot was very satisfactory, 1 explosion and 1 fire being caused. 10.05 pm to [uncertain] 10.55 pm fired 91 rounds on M25a90.05, M25a85.10, M25a65.25, M25a50.40. Response to S.O.S.Call.

### [place not stated] — June 13/17

Shooting Nil. Howitzer No. 163 was sent to No. 3 Ordnance Workshop.

### [place not stated] — June 14/17

Fired 6 rounds on M29a3.5. Concentrated shoot ordered by Group.

### [place not stated] — June 15th and 16th. 1917.

Nothing to report.

### [place not stated] — June 17/17

Fired several rounds on Trench Junctions at T1b6.8 and at T1b85.60. Rounds observed by Kite Balloon and shoot reported as satisfactory.

### [place not stated] — June 18/17

Fired 10 rounds registration on M31b27.90 and 40 rounds for effect on Trench Junction at M25a75.22 and 50 yards South. Ground observation, double line.

## Page 5 (June 1917, p. 5) — [diary text]

### S1946.2 — June 19/17

Fired 30 rounds on Trench Junction M31a87.60. Ground observation. Shoot rather unsatisfactory as we were obliged to use different lots of H.E.T. and one round fell in our own lines.

Responded to S.O.S. Calls at 7.15 pm, 9.20 pm, 10.29 pm. In each instance we fired on Trench at M25a65.20 to [uncertain] M19a45.99.

### [place not stated] — June 20/17

To-day fired several rounds on Trench Junction at M31d40.15 obtaining fairly satisfactory results.

### [place not stated] — June 21st. and 22nd, 1917.

Nothing to report.

### [place not stated] — June 23/17

Fired 40 rounds on Strong Point at T2b9.8 with double line observation. Many rounds fell close but had no direct hits. Shoot considered unsuccessful.

Fired several rounds on House at M33e2.8. Double line observation. This target was obscured by other houses and difficult to identify. We obtained 2 hits.

Fired 90 rounds on Bridge at M25a8.7. Balloon observation and part of time double balloon observation. Observers reported 3 hits on bridge and buttresses.

To-day O.C. located new forward position at ANGRES and party was sent up this evening and laid platform for one gun.

### [place not stated] — June 24/17

Fired 50 rounds on Bridge at M25a9.9. Obtained 3 o.Ks. but bridge was not demolished. Fired several rounds on Houses in ELEU DIT LEAUVETTE.

### [place not stated] — June 25/17

Forward Section at ANGRES, M33e5.9, fired several rounds on Strong Point at M31e5.1. 4.O.Ks. reported. Other section fired several rounds on Trench at M31d95.65.

## Page 6 (June 1917, p. 6) — [diary text]

### S1946.2 and M33e5.9 — June 26/17

(Advanced Section) Fired a few rounds on Hostile Batteries MZ4 at M29a50.05 and MZ2 at M29a00.45. Aeroplane called at 4.45 pm and Section engaged Hostile Battery MZ30 at [uncertain]. Were only able to fire 72 rounds owing to hostile 5.9 in shelling of Position by 4.2 and 5.9. One shell dropped in D gun pit Killing 346361 Gunner R.F.Turner. A quantity of ammunition was also destroyed.

### [place not stated] — June 27/17

Fired 36 rounds on Hostile battery at M 30 d 2.9. Shoot abandoned owing to poor visibility.

To-day we were taken over by MACGEE's GROUP (Counter Battery).

### M33e5.9 — June 28/17

To-day fired several rounds neutralisation fire on Hostile batteries MY 70 at M21d95.05, MZ 15 at M25a50.55, MY70 at M21a95.05.

This afternoon engaged Hostile Battery MY 74 at M 33b 20.50 with Balloon observation. Shoot abandoned owing to call for neutralisation fire.

7.10 pm to 8.45 pm fired 296 rounds on Hostile batteries MZ 17 at M36a8.6 and MZ7 at M23d1.1 in support of Infantry Operation.

### [place not stated] — June 29/17

Expended 45 rounds on Hostile batteries in reply to call for neutralisation fire.

### [place not stated] — June 30/17

To-day we returned to COLE'S GROUP but C.C.H.A. will have a call on us for 2 Hostile Battery Shoot per day.

Expended 40 rounds on Fosse 5 at M25a5.7.

[Signed, bottom right:] [uncertain] Maxwell, Lt., for O/C 5th Canadian Siege Battery.

## Transcriber's notes — June 1917

- **Date gaps (days with no entry):** none — every day 1–30 June is covered, though 4–5, 10–11, 15–16 and 21–22 June are each covered by a single bracketed "Nothing to report" entry.
- **Entries spanning pages:** the 9 June entry runs from p. 3 onto p. 4 ("to South East.").
- **Place column:** in June the Place column gives map references rather than place names — "S1946.2" for 1–3, 9, 19 and 26 June, and "M33e5.9" (the forward position at Angres) for 25, 26 and 28 June. They are transcribed as written; the full map-sheet references are not given in the diary.
- **2 June entry:** the Junction Railway Embankment target reference is partly struck through and overwritten; the reading "M31e95.90" is [uncertain].
- **8 June entry:** the phrase after "F.O.O. sent to" is illegible and is flagged [uncertain]; the sense appears to be that the F.O.O. could not get communication through to the battery.
- **12 June entry:** the start time of the night shoot is overwritten in the typescript; the reading "10.05 pm to 10.55 pm" is [uncertain] for the first time.
- **19 June entry:** the second trench reference in the S.O.S. response is read as "M19a45.99" — this reading is [uncertain], as the sheet letter/number does not match the pattern of the surrounding references.
- **26 June entry:** the map reference for Hostile Battery MZ30 is illegible and is flagged [uncertain].
- **30 June signature:** the signing officer's surname appears to read "Maxwell" but the signature is not clear enough to read with confidence; it is flagged [uncertain]. The signature is "for O/C 5th Canadian Siege Battery".
- **Casualties:** 26 June — Gunner R. F. Turner (regimental number 346361) killed by a shell dropping in D gun pit during hostile 4.2" and 5.9" shelling of the position; a quantity of ammunition was also destroyed. No other casualties are recorded in June.
- **People named:** Gunner R. F. Turner, 346361 (killed, 26 June). No officers are named in June; the O.C. is referred to without a name on 23 June.
- **Places:** Eleu-dit-Leauvette, Angres, Fosse 3, Fosse 5, the Brewery (near the railway embankment), Railway Embankment.
- **Units/groups mentioned:** MACGEE's GROUP (Counter Battery) — the battery was taken over by this group on 27 June; COLE'S GROUP — the battery returned to it on 30 June; C.C.H.A.; No. 3 Ordnance Workshop (Howitzer No. 163 sent there 13 June).
- **Equipment:** Howitzer No. 163 sent to No. 3 Ordnance Workshop on 13 June.
