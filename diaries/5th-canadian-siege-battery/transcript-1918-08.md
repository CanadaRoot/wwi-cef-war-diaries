## Page 3 (August 1918, p. 3) — [diary text]

WAR DIARY or INTELLIGENCE SUMMARY. (Erase heading not required.)

### Field — Aug. 1st

Nil

### Field — Aug. 2nd

Day was exceptionally quiet and nothing of unusual interest occured during the day. Harassing fire was carried out throughout the day during which 50 Rounds were expended on various targets. During the night 30 Rounds were expended on trench work, part of which were in support of an Infantry Raid. 12 Rounds were also put over on H.Q's at C13a.

### Field — Aug. 3rd

A shoot of 50 rounds was carried out during the afternoon on H.Q. in T 30.c. The shoot was conducted with ground observation very satisfactory results being obtained. In Counter Battery work we engaged five Hostile Batteries, two rounds being fired on each in conjunction with Corps and Brigade Salvos. Harassing fire was kept up during the night on selected targets. Nothing of special interest occurred.

### Field — Aug. 4th

A special service of remembrance and Intercession was held today at First Army Headquarters being the fifth anniversary of the Declaration of War by the British Empire. One O.R. was despatched to this service to represent the battery. 137 Rounds were expended on Counter Battery work during the day. One destructive shoot of 100 Rds on C.B.58 in C 11c was successfully carried out during the evening with aeroplane observation.

### Field — Aug. 5th

Very little shooting was done today, only 4 rounds being fired in Counter Battery work. Hostile Battery CB 58 was again harassed during the night. 25 rounds being put over during the dark hours.

### Field — Aug. 6th

Ecurie, tracks and railways in vicinity were considerably harassed during the night by H.V. Guns but no damage was done to either material or personnel. Most of the shells falling in the open fields adjoining the Railway tracks. Shooting during the day was entirely devoted to Counter Battery work, mostly Corps and Brigade salvos. 5 salvos were participated in and 5 rounds rapid were placed on HB Ie 41 [uncertain].

### Field — Aug. 7th

Day was very quiet and nothing of exceptional interest occurred during the day. 5 Corps salvos were participated in during the day on active hostile batteries and a special operation during the night in support of a moonlight raid by the infantry.

## Page 4 (August 1918, p. 4) — [diary text]

### Field — Aug. 8th

Very little shooting was done today and nothing of interest occurred. 10 Rounds were fired during the day on Counter Battery work. During the afternoon both guns at Ecurie section were registered using Neuville Church as a target. 7 Rounds were fired obtaining a satisfactory registration

### Field — Aug. 9th

Lieut H.M. Stairs carried out a destructive shoot with aeroplane observation on OA 72. 100 Rounds being fired obtaining 7 Z's, 10 Y's and 2 O.K's. Airman reported shooting excellent. 2 explosions were caused and position badly damaged. Counter Battery work at Thelus and Ecurie positions was confined mostly to Corps salvos and N.F. Calls. Five Hostile Batteries and an H.Q. was engaged. a total of 62 Rounds being expended.

### Field — Aug. 10th

Two destructive shoots were carried out during the day, one of which was observed by aeroplane Lieut W.A. Buscroft conducted this shoot on IA 65. obtaining 10 A's, 11 Z's, 4 Y's and 1 O.K. Z 6 was an O.K. on No 3 pit. Visibility was very poor during this shoot. The second shoot was of 50 rounds observed by Lieut E.S. Bushell and was very successful. a large explosion being caused and a good M.P.I. on target. A total of 195 Rounds were expended during the day on C.B. work. 40 Rounds in Harassing fire and 39 on destructive work, these latter being fired on a dump at Neuville Farm in S 16 d and was very successful. Dump being destroyed.

### Field — Aug. 11th

From 3.25 p.m. to 4 a.m. we fired 60 rounds in reply to an S.O.S. call for Right and centre brigades. A destructive shoot on H.B. CB 58 was successfully carried out by Lieut Payne. 100 Rounds being fired and 4 O.K's, 8 Y's and 7 Z's were obtained. Shooting was well around the target, the pits were blown in and target very badly pitted with shell holes. This shoot was observed by 52nd squadron R.A.F. 10 Rounds were also fired in C.B. work on targets in reply to N.F. calls. 50 Rounds harassing fire on I.A. 63 and CB 58 were put over during the night.

### Field — Aug. 12th

Nil

## Page 5 (August 1918, p. 5) — [diary text]

### Field — Aug. 13th

Day was very quiet and nothing of special interest occurred during the day. 28 Rounds were fired in the early morning on selected targets in support of special operations by the Infantry, and 8 rounds expended in Counter Battery Work.

### Field — Aug. 14th

Nil

### Field — Aug. 15th

Nil

### Field — Aug. 16th

On Counter Battery work during the day we expended 16 rounds spread over 7 selected targets firing salvos on each. No 1 Gun was successfully registered during the evening. Orders were received during the day to commence work on new positions at G.10.a.4.7. (halfway between our present position at Ecurie and our right section at St Nicholas).

### Field — Aug. 17th

Early this morning the off duty crews of Ecurie position were sent under the Sgt Major to get two new positions prepared at G.10.a. This work was got under way at once and good time made. Two pits were completed by evening and 200 Rounds of ammunition received and stored. On Counter Battery work we expended 54 Rounds.

### Field — Aug. 18th

Guns at Ecurie position went out of action during evening and were at once dismantled in readiness for removal to new position. Guns went out of action at 5.pm and at 7.30 pm were pulled out on the road awaiting orders to move. About this time the original orders were cancelled as it was thought the enemy was withdrawing on this front and further orders were received to put guns in position at a more advanced position at E.13.a. This was done and two guns and 200 rounds of ammunition were removed during the evening to new position. Work was carried on all night and guns were ready for action by 6.am next morning.

### Field — Aug. 19th

Work was carried on all day at improving the new positions and arranging the transfer of remaining personnel and stores from Ecurie

### Field — Aug. 20th

Remainder of personnel and stores were removed from Ecurie during the day. Battery Headquarters were established with Right Section at St Nicholas. Our positions now are as follows:—

## Page 6 (August 1918, p. 6) — [diary text]

### Field — Aug. 20th (continued)

2 Guns under Lieut F.R. May at Thelus (A.17.a.) 2 Guns under Lieut H.M. Stairs at St Nicholas (G.16.b.) and 2 Guns under Lieut D.A. Guildford at Blangy E.13.a. The section at St Nicholas came under 19th Brigade for tactics but remained with 2nd Brigade CGA for administration. Work was also begun on advanced Gun Positions at "Spider Corner" H.29.d. in preparation for a further advance.

### Field — Aug. 21st

Work was continued on advanced positions and all work completed and pits got ready to receive guns.

### Field — Aug. 22nd

130 Rounds were fired during the day on Counter Battery work including one destructive shoot of 120 rounds on H.B. at G.27.d. A special operation was also ordered in which we fired 20 rounds on targets in H.6.d.

### Field — Aug. 23rd

Nil

### Field — Aug. 24th

Operations during the day were confined to salvos and bursts of fire on Hostile Batteries 5 Active batteries being engaged for neutralisation and effectively silenced.

### Field — Aug. 25th

36 Rounds were fired during the day on Counter Battery work mostly bursts of fire on active Hostile Batteries.

### Field — Aug. 26th

In support of Infantry attack this morning we fired 750 rounds on various targets until 11.30 am when two guns in rear position was supposed to be out of range. During the afternoon however these guns again came into action, target being Jigsaw wood which was holding up the advance of the infantry. Howitzer No 574 went out of action during the afternoon on account of flaws in the chamber causing it to split for a length of about 20 inches. The gun was at once hauled out and removed to No 20 M.O.Workshops where it was condemned.

### Field — Aug. 27th

Battery was again in action in support of Infantry, a total of 380 rounds being fired. Amongst the targets engaged were, Jigsaw Wood, Boiry, Long Valley, Trench Systems from Boiry to Victoria Copse and Pelves. Rear Section was now out of range and Howitzer No 475 was pulled out and removed to advanced position at Spider Corner (H.20.d.)

## Page 7 (August 1918, p. 7) — [diary text]

### Field — Aug. 28th

Howitzer No 468 was removed during the morning and placed in position at Spider Corner H.20.d. Rear position was now vacated with the exception of Quarter Masters Stores which on account of the rapid advance and frequent moves of the Battery it was decided to leave in the old position for the time being.

### Field — Aug. 29th

Firing was kept up continually during the day targets including H.B's, Biache St Vaast, Hamblain, Vitry-en-artois and the roads in O6 central. A total of 476 Rounds being fired during the day How No 468 was put in during the day and came into action in the evening. We had now three guns in action at advanced position at H.29.d. the remaining gun from Thelus position having been brought up, and two guns in action at Blangy H.13.a.

### Field — Aug. 30th

6 N.F. calls were received during the day and answered and 5 Hostile batteries engaged for neutralization. a total of 70 rounds being expended.

### Field — Aug. 31st

At 5.30.pm orders were received to remove the two guns from Blangy to a more advanced position in the vicinity of Pampoux in order to reach the whole of Brebieres. These guns were to be in action by 11.pm 1st Sept. The two guns went out of action at 8.30.pm and were pulled out in readiness for removal. This was done during the night. In shooting we fired 11 Rounds on Vitry Church, 50 Rounds on Vitry Station and 35 Rounds in Counter Battery work. During the latter half of this month work has been exceptionally heavy. With the exception of three weeks spent in intensive training in February, this Battery has now been constantly in action mostly in very advanced positions for 16 months without a break. The last rest of 10 days being in May 1917.

[Signed:] N. P. MacLeod Capt, 5th C. S. Bty

## Transcriber's notes — August 1918

- **Date gaps (days with no entry):** none — every day 1–31 August has an entry, though 1, 12, 14, 15 and 23 August are entered as "Nil".
- **Entries spanning pages:** the 20 August entry runs from p. 5 onto p. 6.
- **Place column:** the Place column on each entry bears the same handwritten mark, the diarist's signature (N. P. MacLeod — matching the signature closing the 31 August entry), rather than a place name. The place is given as "FIELD" in the September volume, so entries here are headed "Field". The battery's positions during the month are named in the text: Ecurie, St Nicholas, Thelus, Blangy, and the advanced position "Spider Corner".
- **6 August entry:** the target reference written "HB Ie 41" is unclear and is flagged [uncertain].
- **Spelling as written:** "occured" (2 August), "despatched" (4 August), "Intercession" (4 August), "M.O.Workshops" (26 August). "H.V. Guns" (6 August) is reproduced as written; the abbreviation is not expanded in the diary.
- **Casualties:** none reported in August. Howitzer No. 574 was condemned after its chamber split (26 August); Howitzer No. 475 was moved forward to Spider Corner (27 August).
- **People named:** Captain N. P. MacLeod (O.C., signatory); Lieut H. M. Stairs; Lieut W. A. Buscroft; Lieut E. S. Bushell; Lieut Payne; Lieut F. R. May; Lieut D. A. Guildford; the Sgt Major (unnamed). 52nd Squadron R.A.F. observed the 11 August shoot.
- **Places:** Ecurie, St Nicholas, Thelus, Blangy, Neuville Church, Neuville Farm, Spider Corner (H.29.d. / H.20.d.), Pampoux, Brebieres, Jigsaw Wood, Boiry, Long Valley, Victoria Copse, Pelves, Biache St Vaast, Hamblain, Vitry-en-Artois (Vitry Church and Vitry Station), First Army Headquarters.
