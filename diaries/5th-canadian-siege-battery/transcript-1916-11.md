# War Diary Transcript — 5th Canadian Siege Battery, November 1916

- **Unit:** 5th Canadian Siege Battery (originally 165th Canadian Siege Battery), Canadian Garrison Artillery
- **LAC Item ID:** 2004798, RG 9 III-D-3, vol. 4976, file 572, reel T-10804, finding aid 9-52
- **Month / Volume:** November 1916, Volume 3 (covers 1 – 30 November 1916)
- **Pages in this file:** LAC images pp. 1–6 (6 pages: cover, contents, 4 diary text pages)
- **Diary text date range:** 1 – 30 November 1916
- **Commanding officer / signatory:** Major Maxwell commanded the battery until 24 November, when he was seriously burned in an explosion of N.C.T. and taken to hospital; Lieut D.J. Maxwell took command of the battery the same day. No signature block closes this volume; an initialled mark appears beside the 30 November entry (see Notes).
- **Transcription note:** The diary is typewritten on Army Form C. 2118 ("War Diary or Intelligence Summary"). The printed heading "INTELLIGENCE SUMMARY" is not struck through. The Hour column is blank throughout and the Remarks column is blank throughout. The Date column on every diary page is heavily over-written with what appear to be initials or a scribbled signature, but the dates remain legible. The diary pages are numbered 6, 7, 8 and 9 at the top right, but they do not appear in that order in the file: the file order is p. 3 (= 6), p. 4 (= 8), p. 5 (= 7), p. 6 (= 9), so the chronological order of the diary pages is p. 3, p. 5, p. 4, p. 6. The pages are transcribed below in file order (see Notes).

## Page 3 (November 1916, p. 3) — [diary text]

WAR DIARY or INTELLIGENCE SUMMARY. (Erase heading not required.)

### POIZIERS — Nov 1st

During the day we fired 100 Rounds by visual observation on REGINA TRENCH at M 14 a 0.3 to Practice Roads. The shoot was observed by Major Maxwell and was very satisfactory.

### POIZIERS — Nov 2nd

To-days task was a repetition of yesterday and we again shot up portions of Regina Trench by visual observation.

### POIZIERS — Nov 3rd

There was no task for us today. but stood by all day to answer Zone calls. During the night we kept up a slow rate fire on GRANDCOURT TRENCH, COULEC and [uncertain] TRENCH.

### POIZIERS — Nov 4th

NIL

### POIZIERS — Nov 5th

No Firing during the day but during the hours of darkness slow rate fire was kept up on Line 100 North of GRANDCOURT TRENCH and COULEE TRENCH.

### POIZIERS — Nov 6th

NIL

### POIZIERS — Nov 7th

NIL

### POIZIERS — Nov 8th

NIL

### POIZIERS — Nov 9th

From 2 P.M. to 4 P.M. we conducted a shoot by aeroplane observation on Target No 464 (Hostile Battery) This shoot was unsatisfactory on account of very poor visibility and heavy clouds.

### POIZIERS — Nov 10th

Aeroplane went up again today to finish shoot on Target No 464 which was completed satisfactorily

### POIZIERS — Nov 11th

At 5.30 A.M. we fired 24 Rounds on BELOW TRENCH at M 8 d P.E to M 8 c 5.7 and then switched to M 8 c 5.7 to M 8 a 0.1 to M 8 a 3.2. 54 Rounds being fired on these targets. At 7.50.A.M. plane went up to observe shoot on Target No 416 (Hostile Battery) but after 6 Rounds had been fired Plane gave C I on account of clouds and shoot had to be abandoned. During the morning we put over 6 Rounds on this target for effect without observation.

## Page 4 (November 1916, p. 4) — [diary text]

WAR DIARY or INTELLIGENCE SUMMARY. (Erase heading not required.)

### POIZIERS — Nov 20th

Six Hostile Batteries were taken on today, neutralization and harrassing fire being kept up on them all morning, the Batteries were numbered 496, 523, 493, 498, 494. A registration shoot of 16 Rounds was fired on Target No 540 by aerial observation. Shoot was satisfactory and very good registration was obtained.

### POIZIERS — Nov 21st

Very little counter battery work was done today, good observation being almost impossible owing to very bad weather and poor visibility. During the afternoon 200 Rounds were fired on trenches and supports.

### POIZIERS — Nov 22nd

NIL.

### POIZIERS — Nov 23rd

Very little work was done today, 18 Rounds were put into Hostile Battery No 149, and later in the day 6 Rounds were fired into Hostile Battery No 150.

### POIZIERS — Nov 24th

From 8.a.m.to 8.40.a.m. 36 Rounds were fired on Hostile Batteries. Major Maxwell was seriously burned on face and hands as the result of an explosion of N.C.T. He was taken to Hospital and Lieut D.J. Maxwell took command of the Battery.

### POIZIERS — Nov 25th

NIL.

### POIZIERS — Nov 26th

During the afternoon we fired 60 Rounds on Communication Trench R12c 1.9. to GRANDCOURT TRENCH and then up and down SUNKEN ROAD in R12a between R12c 1.9 and CREST TRENCH. This was done with ground observation.

### POIZIERS — Nov 27th

Only 4 Rounds were fired to-day on concentrated shoot on Target No 150.

### POIZIERS — Nov 28th

NIL

### POIZIERS — Nov 29th

At 4.40.P.M. we opened up a bombardment on MIRAMONT in L6.5c and continued for half an hour at steady rate. Harrassing fire was kept up during the night on selected targets.

[Form imprint at the foot of the page: "T1134. Wt. W708—776. 500000. 4/16. Sir J. C. & S."]

## Page 5 (November 1916, p. 5) — [diary text]

WAR DIARY or INTELLIGENCE SUMMARY. (Erase heading not required.)

### POIZIERS — Nov 11th (continued)

During the afternoon we fired 75 Rounds on BELOW TRENCH from M 8d [uncertain] to M 8a [uncertain].

### POIZIERS — Nov 12th

From 5.30. A.M. we fired 85 Rounds into BELOW TRENCH and BELOW SUPPORT LINES and harrassing Fire was kept up all morning. During the afternoon two concentrated shoots were carried out on Hostile Batteries No. 465 and 496. There was no firing during the night.

### POIZIERS — Nov 13th

Starting at 5.30 A.M. we had a [uncertain] days firing, no less than 10 Hostile Batteries being engaged by us. These were numbered 500, 525, 514, 451, 146, 145, 475, 414, 418, 465, A total of 326 Rounds being fired into these batteries. 24 Rounds were fired between 2.P.M. and 5.P.M. on MIRAMONT and MIRAMONT QUARRIE. During the evening in answer to an S.O.S. call we fired on [uncertain] 8. to Mec2.6 First 20 Rounds rapid and then dropping to 30 Rounds at slow rate. Hostile shelling was very heavy at times eight men being badly wounded and several slightly. Two men were killed.

### POIZIERS — Nov 14th

NIL

### POIZIERS — Nov 15th

During the day four Hostile Batteries were taken on for neutralization and firing was kept up continuously all day. Hostile shelling was again very heavy, the enemy coming back on our position with heavy bursts of shrapnel and H.E. We lost Four men wounded, one of which died on the way to Hospital. There was no firing during the night.

### POIZIERS — Nov 16th

11.33 A.M. Plane went up and registered on target No 416 (Hostile Battery) This shoot was very satisfactory several direct hits being made whilst registering, Plane gave C I after registration and we then fired a further 28 Rounds for effect. During the afternoon 12 Rounds Neutralization Fire was put over on target No 519.

### POIZIERS — Nov 17th

During the day a continuous bombardment was kept up on several Hostile Batteries, a total of 249 Rounds being fired.

### POIZIERS — Nov 18th

At 6.50 A.M. we put up a protective barrage and at 8.30. a.m. we went back to our Counter Battery work which consisted of the same targets as yesterday. At 4.15 P.M. we received orders for all Batteries to cease fire on counter batteries but this was again resumed at 9.30.P.M. and a slow rate of fire was kept up all night on selected targets.

### POIZIERS — Nov 19th

A plane shoot was arranged for today but had to be abandoned owing to poor visibility.

[Form imprint at the foot of the page: "T1134. Wt. W708—776. 500000. 4/15. Sir J. C. & S."]

## Page 6 (November 1916, p. 6) — [diary text]

WAR DIARY or INTELLIGENCE SUMMARY. (Erase heading not required.)

### POZIERS — Nov 30th

Very little firing was done to-day, 12 Rounds were fired on targets No'd 150 & 149 (Hostile Batteries) [uncertain] Gun was taken out and new firing beams laid and Gun re-mounted. Six men of the 175th Tunnelling Company were attached to us to-day for the purpose of building Dug-outs.

## Transcriber's notes — November 1916

- **Date gaps (days with no entry):** None — every day from 1 to 30 November is covered. Days recorded as NIL: 4, 6, 7, 8, 14, 22, 25 and 28 November.
- **Entries spanning pages:** the 11 November entry runs from p. 3 onto p. 5.
- **Page order:** the diary text pages are numbered 6, 7, 8 and 9 at the top right, but they appear in the file in the order 6 (p. 3), 8 (p. 4), 7 (p. 5), 9 (p. 6). The chronological order of the pages is therefore p. 3 (1–11 Nov), p. 5 (11–19 Nov), p. 4 (20–29 Nov), p. 6 (30 Nov). The transcript above follows the file order.
- **Date column:** on every diary page the dates are heavily over-written with what appear to be initials or a scribbled signature; the dates themselves remain legible.
- **"C I":** the entries of 11 and 16 November say the plane "gave C I" (11 November: "on account of clouds"; 16 November: "after registration"). The phrase is reproduced as written; the diary does not explain it.
- **3 November entry:** the trench name after "COULEC" is unclear and is flagged [uncertain]. "COULEC" is reproduced as written; the 5 November entry spells the trench "COULEE TRENCH".
- **11 November entry (continued, p. 5):** the map references after "M 8d" and "M 8a" are overtyped and unclear, both flagged [uncertain].
- **13 November entry:** the word describing the day's firing ("we had a [uncertain] days firing") is unclear, and the target of the S.O.S. shoot ("we fired on [uncertain] 8. to Mec2.6") is partly struck through and unclear; both are flagged [uncertain]. "MIRAMONT QUARRIE" is reproduced as written.
- **20 November entry:** the text says six hostile batteries were taken on, but only five battery numbers are listed (496, 523, 493, 498, 494).
- **24 November entry:** "N.C.T." is reproduced as written; the diary does not explain the abbreviation.
- **30 November entry:** the gun number before "Gun was taken out" is unclear and is flagged [uncertain]. An initialled mark appears in the Date column beside this entry.
- **Casualties:** 13 November — two men killed (unnamed) and eight men badly wounded, several more slightly wounded, under heavy hostile shelling. 15 November — four men wounded, one of whom died on the way to hospital. 24 November — Major Maxwell seriously burned on face and hands in an explosion of N.C.T., taken to hospital; Lieut D.J. Maxwell took command of the battery. No casualty is named.
- **People named:** Major Maxwell (commanding officer until 24 November); Lieut D.J. Maxwell (took command 24 November). Six men of the 175th Tunnelling Company were attached on 30 November (unnamed).
- **Places:** Poziers (spelled "POIZIERS" on pp. 3–5 and "POZIERS" on p. 6), Regina Trench, Grandcourt Trench, Coulee Trench, Below Trench, Below Support Lines, Miramont, Miramont Quarrie, Sunken Road, Crest Trench, Communication Trench R12c 1.9.
- **No appendices** are listed or included with this volume; the contents page lists only "Diary Text — 4 Pages".
- Spelling and punctuation are reproduced as written, including "harrassing", "to-day" and "To-days".
