# War Diary Transcript — 5th Canadian Siege Battery, September 1917

- **Unit:** 5th Canadian Siege Battery (originally 165th Canadian Siege Battery), Canadian Garrison Artillery
- **LAC Item ID:** 2004798, RG 9 III-D-3, vol. 4976, file 572, reel T-10804, finding aid 9-52
- **Month / Volume:** September 1917, Volume 13 (covers 2 – 30 September 1917 only)
- **Pages in this file:** LAC images pp. 67–69 (3 pages: cover, contents, 1 diary text page)
- **Diary text date range:** 2 – 30 September 1917
- **Commanding officer / signatory:** The diary page is signed "W. A. Beecroft, Lt., for O.C. 5.C.S.B." Major T. W. F. MacDonald is named in the text (leave to England, 15 September) — he is the Major who signed the July entries.
- **Transcription note:** The diary is typewritten on Army Form C. 2118/12 ("War Diary or Intelligence Summary"); the printed heading "INTELLIGENCE SUMMARY" is struck through. Like August, this volume is not a day-by-day diary: the single diary text page is a retrospective summary, and the covering letter filed with the August volume explains that the August and September diaries were delayed because "the records were lost during moving operations". The "Hour" column is blank throughout; the Remarks column is blank throughout. "Liason" is spelled as written in the diary. "Bdr" is Bombardier; "A/Bdr" is Acting Bombardier; "C.C.H.A." is Canadian Corps Heavy Artillery.

## Page 3 (September 1917, p. 3) — [diary text]

WAR DIARY or INTELLIGENCE SUMMARY. (Erase heading not required.)

5th CANADIAN SIEGE BATTERY

### ANGRES. — SEPT.

During the month we were bombarded with Gas Shells on several occasions also shelled with 8" and 5.9" losing a quantity of ammunition. We took part in a raid by the 11th Brigade Canadians on the 6th and while in action the enemy came back on our position with Gas Shells compelling the crews to work with their Gas Masks on. Four of our Men were badly gassed and had to be taken to Field Ambulance, they were No 346826 Bdr Trotter D.D. 346850 A/Bdr Russell W.G. 346891 Sgt Cornell E.G. and No 303392 Gunner Floyd P.J.

### ANGRES. — 2nd

Lieut. F.R. May proceeded to 11th Brigade as Liason Officer.

### ANGRES. — 7th

Lieut D.J. Maxwell returned from leave to England. Lieut. D.A. Guildford appointed Gas Officer C.C.H.A.

### ANGRES. — 8th

Lieut. F.R.May returned from 11th Brigade.

### ANGRES. — 15th

Major. T.W.F. MacDonald proceeded on leave to England.

### ANGRES. — 21st

Lieut. R.L. Smith proceeded to 11th Brigade as Liason Officer.

### ANGRES. — 30th

Capt L.C. Ord proceeded on leave to England.

[Signed, bottom right:] W. A. Beecroft Lt.
for O.C. 5.C.S.B.

## Transcriber's notes — September 1917

- **Date gaps (days with no entry):** this volume covers only 2–30 September 1917, and only six dated entries survive (2, 7, 8, 15, 21 and 30 September, plus an undated monthly summary). There is no day-by-day record for 1 September or for 3–6, 9–14, 16–20 or 22–29 September.
- **Lost records:** the covering letter dated 3 November 1917, filed with the August volume, states that the diaries for August and September were delayed because "the records were lost during moving operations". The September diary is therefore a retrospective summary written from memory, not a contemporary daily log.
- **Place column:** the word "was" is written in the Date column beside the "ANGRES." place entries, as in the August volume; it appears to be a stray or repeated word and is not part of the place name.
- **6 September raid:** the monthly summary records that the battery took part in a raid by the 11th Brigade Canadians on 6 September; the enemy shelled the battery position with gas shells while the guns were in action, forcing the crews to work in gas masks.
- **Sgt Cornell's initials:** the gassed men are listed as "346891 Sgt Cornell E.G." — in the July volume the same man is listed among the reinforcements as "Sgt. Cornell, E.C." The July reading of the regimental number (346891) is confirmed here. Both initial readings are reproduced as written.
- **Major MacDonald:** the July entries are signed by a Major whose signature could not be read with confidence; the September entry names him as Major T. W. F. MacDonald, on leave to England 15 September.
- **Casualties:** September (raid of 6 September) — four men badly gassed and taken to Field Ambulance: No. 346826 Bdr D. D. Trotter, No. 346850 A/Bdr W. G. Russell, No. 346891 Sgt E. G. Cornell, No. 303392 Gunner P. J. Floyd. No deaths are recorded in September.
- **People named:** Bdr D. D. Trotter (346826); A/Bdr W. G. Russell (346850); Sgt E. G. Cornell (346891); Gunner P. J. Floyd (303392); Lieut. F. R. May (Liaison Officer to 11th Brigade, 2–8 September); Lieut. D. J. Maxwell (returned from leave, 7 September); Lieut. D. A. Guildford (appointed Gas Officer, C.C.H.A., 7 September); Major T. W. F. MacDonald (leave to England, 15 September); Lieut. R. L. Smith (Liaison Officer to 11th Brigade, 21 September); Capt L. C. Ord (leave to England, 30 September); Lieut. W. A. Beecroft (signed the diary for the O.C.).
- **Places:** Angres.
- **Units mentioned:** 11th Brigade (Canadian); C.C.H.A.
- **No appendices** are listed or included with this volume; the contents page lists only "Diary Text 1 Page". The covering letter for the August, September and October diaries is filed with the August volume.
