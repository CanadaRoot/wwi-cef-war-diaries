### Jemappes — January 1919

During the month of January the Battery remained billeted in Jemappes with the 1st Bde.C.G.A. On January the fourth the Brigade was inspected by the G.O.C.R.A. The Brigade was drawn up in close column in the square at Jemappes and after the inspection the guns, cookhouse, Guard and billets were visited by the G.O.C.R.A. January 16th the XXII Corps H.A. were inspected at Mons by the Corps Commander. Three lorries were detailed to convey battery personnel to Mons for the parade. The 1st and 3rd Bdes.C.G.A. 6th 48th and 61st Bdes.R.G.A. and 56th (S.A.) Bde. were present and medals and decorations were presented to 107 Officers and men. A letter of congratulation for the smart appearance and steadiness of the men was received from the G.O.C.R.A. the following day. Great strides were made in the Educational scheme in the Brigade, and classes in Gas Engines, Mathematics, and French were started, great enthusiasm was shown by all ranks in these classes. In addition a Brigade Cinema was inaugurated run by the members of the Brigade, and the pictures were greatly appreciated by the troops. Nothing of especial interest occured during the month.

[Signed:] N. P. MacLeod Major, O.C. 5th Canadian Siege Battery.
