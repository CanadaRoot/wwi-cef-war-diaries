# War Diary — 5th Canadian Divisional Signal Company (Artillery Detachment) — December 1918

- **Unit:** 5th Canadian Divisional Signal Company (Artillery Detachment)
- **Month:** December 1918 (Volume 16)
- **Source:** Library and Archives Canada, Collection Search Item ID 2004909 (RG 9 III-D-3, vol. 5006; microfilm reel T-10854, files 694–695; finding aid 9-52)
- **Pages:** December 1918, p. 1 – p. 6 (image files page-0106 – page-0111 of the 132-page diary)
- **December 1918 — COMPLETE:** cover, contents, diary text 1–31 December 1918. Transcribed 2026-10-26.

## Transcription conventions
Original spelling, punctuation and abbreviations kept. Handwritten diary text. `[uncertain]` marks words I could not read with confidence — never guessed. The Hour column on each diary page carries a repeated check-like mark rather than times; no hours are recorded except where times appear in the text itself. No map coordinates are given in the diary; places are recorded as written. This month is the march from Belgium into Germany and across the Rhine to Bonn.

## Pages 3–6 — Diary text (December 1918, pp. 3–6)

Army Form C. 2118, "WAR DIARY or INTELLIGENCE SUMMARY". Header on each page: "5th Cdn. Div Signal Coy Arty Det."

### Page 3

> [Place: December 1918. Jambes, then Laroche, Cherain]
>
> **1** — Clear and cool, H.Q. section put in morning at cleaning wagons and equipment, "A" sub section at Havelange moves to Grand Han. "B" Sub-section resting at Andenne.
>
> **2** — Mild and Cloudy, H.Q. moves to Laroche. "A" Sub-section resting in Grand Han owing to shortage of Rations "B" Sub-section marches to Bois-St-B [uncertain].
>
> **3** — Cloudy with showers, H.Q. resting in Laroche. A Sub-section resting in Grand Han awaiting arrival of Rations "B" Sub-section marched to Verlaine.
>
> **4** — Showery, H.Q. moves to Cherain, A. Sub-section to Vaux Chavanne and "B" Sub Section to Chevron passing through very hilly country.
>
> **5** — Cloudy and mild. H.Q. resting at Cherain, A Sub-section marches to Salm, Château, "B" Sub-section resting at Chevron on account of short rations, Wagons and Equipment cleaned.
>
> **6** — Cool and Cloudy, H.Q. still at Cherain, A sub section marches to Hunderhausen [uncertain], crossing the German [entry continues on next page]

### Page 4

> [Place: Beho, Wallerode, Cosheim, Schmidtheim]
>
> **6** (continued) — border. Just west of Beho at 15.00 hours. B sub section marches to Arbrefontaine.
>
> **7** — Cold and Cloudy. H.Q. marches to Beho, A sub section marches to Herresbach. "B" sub section marched to Recht crossing the border at 11.00 hours.
>
> **8** — Cold and Cloudy, H.Q. marches to Wallerode, A sub-section resting at Herresbach. "B" sub section marches to Wirtzfeld.
>
> **9** — Weather fine, H.Q. resting at Wallerode. Wagons cleaned, A sub section marches to Stadtkyll, B sub-section marches to Kreuzberg.
>
> **10** — Cloudy with a little rain, H.Q. marches to Cosheim [uncertain]. A sub-section marches to Sch [uncertain]. B sub section marches Kommern.
>
> **11** — Cold and Clear. H.Q. resting at Schmidtheim, A sub-section marches to Odendorf, B sub section marches to Weilerswist.
>
> **12** — Rain, All sections making preparations for Crossing the Rhine.
