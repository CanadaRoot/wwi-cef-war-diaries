# War Diary — 5th Canadian Divisional Signal Company (Artillery Detachment) — August 1917

- **Unit:** 5th Canadian Divisional Signal Company (Artillery Detachment)
- **Month:** August 1917 (Volume 1) — this volume covers 20 August to 30 September 1917
- **Source:** Library and Archives Canada, Collection Search Item ID 2004909 (RG 9 III-D-3, vol. 5006, microfilm reel T-10854, files 694–695; finding aid 9-52)
- **Pages:** August 1917, p. 1 – p. 8 (image files page-0001 – page-0008 of the 132-page diary)
- **August–September 1917 — COMPLETE** (pages 1–8 of 132): cover, contents, diary text 20 August – 30 September, no appendices. Transcribed 2026-10-26.
- **Note on the September gap:** there is NO separate September 1917 section in the LAC manifest — the diary jumps from August to October 1917. September 1917 is not missing: the September entries (1–30 September) are contained in this volume (pages 4–7) and are transcribed here. The next section in the manifest is October 1917 (pages 9–13).

## Transcription conventions
Original spelling, punctuation and abbreviations kept. Handwritten diary text throughout (Army Form C. 2118, "WAR DIARY or INTELLIGENCE SUMMARY"). `[uncertain]` marks words I could not read with confidence — never guessed. No map coordinates are given in the diary; places are recorded as written. "H.Q." = Headquarters; "Bdes." = Brigades (the 13th and 14th Brigades, C.F.A.); "C.F.A." = Canadian Field Artillery; "Divarty" = Divisional Artillery; "D.R." = Despatch Rider; "D.R.L.S." = Despatch Rider Letter Service; "C.R.A." = Commander Royal Artillery; "R.F.A." = Royal Field Artillery; "R.A." = Royal Artillery; "D.A.C." = Divisional Ammunition Column; "D.T." = Divisional Train; "Cyps." = Cypher office. A repeated handwritten mark, read as "M.L.M." [uncertain] — apparently the diarist's initials (cf. the signature "M L Maitland" at 30 September) — appears against most entries, in the Hour column on pages 4–7 and in the Remarks column on page 3; it is noted here rather than repeated against every entry. The diary's own heading on the diary sheets reads "5th Divarty — 13th & 14th Bdes. C.F.A. Signal Sub-Sections."

## Pages 3–7 — Diary text (20 August – 30 September 1917)

Army Form C. 2118, "WAR DIARY or INTELLIGENCE SUMMARY". Heading on each sheet: "5th Divarty — 13th & 14th Bdes. C.F.A. Signal Sub-Sections."

### Page 3 — 20–27 August 1917

> **Witley, 20/8/17** — Weather fine H.Q. entrained at 2.00 P.M. arrived Southampton 4.30 P.M. Embarked and sailed at 9.30 P.M.
>
> **Le Havre, 21** — To disembarked at 1 P.M. wagon slightly damaged disembarking - marched to rest camp. Bdes after a night march on the night of the 20-21 entrained for Southampton
>
> **22** — Showery & very windy - Cleaned up equipment - had wagon repaired - men fitted with box respirators. Bdes disembarked - marched to rest Camp arriving late in the afternoon.
>
> **23** — Showery with a high wind. H.Q. medical Parade & gas drill - Bdes. received balance of equipment - 13th. changed two riding horses.
>
> **24** — Showery & windy. Bdes left Camp at 3 P.M. and entrained. H.Q. stood fast - had gas drill.
>
> **25** — Showery H.Q. left Camp & entrained at 5 P.M. Bdes. detrained at Lillers 13th. marched to Camp at Amettes - 14th. marched to Camp at Ames.
>
> **26** — Fair Bdes. remained in their Camps. H.Q. detrained at Lillers and marched to Camp at Fontaine les Hermans 10 P.M. in a heavy rain
>
> **27** — Weather fair - Div Office opened at Fontaine les Hermans. Bde Offices opened at Amettes & Ames - fine Communication to all units including Div. Corps. [uncertain — "Div."?] Indent for Technical Stores put in. [uncertain — "Indent"?]

### Page 4 — 28 August – 4 September 1917

> **Fontaine les Hermans, 28** — Heavy rain - H.Q. & Bdes. remained in Camps & Carried on Signal Service. D.S.R.C. [uncertain] reported to H.Q. from Cyps. D.R.L.S. started - 3 runs daily to all units including Corps.
>
> **29** — Still Raining - speaking circuits poor owing to wet weather - Captain M. L. Maitland reported & took Charge of H.Q. & Bdes.
>
> **30** — Weather still wet - trouble on Corps. line beyond our sector - All signals put through gas chamber - bathing parade for all men.
>
> **31** — Showery H.Q. & Bdes. Still in Camp - Signal service better owing to break in weather.
>
> **1 [Sept]** — Weather fair - Carried on Signal service.
>
> **2** — Weather fair - carried on - received orders and dates of move.
>
> **3** — Weather fine - everything made ready for move - Capt. M. L. Maitland went in advance to arrange for wires etc.
>
> **4** — Weather fine - H.Q. Remained in Camp - carried on Service with D.A.C. D.T. & Corps. - Bdes Offices closed at 7 A.M. Lines picked up by H.Q. 13th. Bde. marched to wagon lines in Bully Grenay area. 14th. Bde marched to wagon lines in Carency area.

### Page 5 — 5–12 September 1917

> **Sains en Gohelle, Sept. 5** — H.Q. marched to new Quarters nearer the line - Bdes remained in lines.
>
> **6** — Weather fine and warm - Communication opened up with all units of 5th Divarty. including Corps.
>
> **7** — Weather fine but misty - some technical stores received - Bdes received their allotment.
>
> **8** — service poor on account of having to depend on other Units. 13th & 14th took over from 14th & 17th R.F.A. Bdes respectively. Weather fine - Service slightly improved but not yet satisfactory - 14th Bde. congratulated by Lt.-Col. A. T. Ogilvie on their fine work.
>
> **9.** — Weather still fine - service somewhat improved - another D.R. run put on.
>
> **10** — Continued fine weather - more technical stores received for H.Q. & Bdes.
>
> **11** — Fine - 1st. C.R.A. took over from 2nd C.R.A. - 5 C.R.A took over 8th Bde. C.F.A. & 13th Bde. C.F.A. at 10 A.M. 5th C.R.A. working Signal Office in Conjunction with 1st. C.R.A. & supplying men for each shift also a Supt. - Service greatly improved.
>
> **12** — Cloudy - nothing unusual happened.

### Page 6 — 13–22 September 1917

> **Sains en Gohelle, Sept. 13.** — Fair - started to open up our own Office.
>
> **14** — Showery - opened our own Office at H.Q. satisfactorily.
>
> **15.** — Fair - nothing unusual to report.
>
> **16** — Weather fine - Two linemen sent to each of the Bdes. to look after H.Q. lines.
>
> **17** — Continued fine weather - nothing unusual to report.
>
> **18** — Weather fine - forward lines shelled heavily all day - airline route changed at far end.
>
> **19** — Fine - lines still heavily shelled but all units were kept in Communication.
>
> **20** — Continued fine weather - situation normal.
>
> **21.** — Weather fine - 13th Bde. came under 6th Div. R.A. for tactical purposes at 6 A.M. this morning. One line to [uncertain — "next"?] handed over to 6th Div. R.A.
>
> **22** — Weather continued fine - Enemy activity around 14th Bde. H.Q. continually cut line to [uncertain] & lateral to [uncertain] Service [uncertain] fair.

### Page 7 — 23–30 September 1917

> **Sains-en-Gohelle, Sept. 23** — Fine - situation normal.
>
> **24** — Fine - 14th Bde. came under 6th Div. R.A. for tactical purposes at 6 A.M. this morning - One line [uncertain — "bound"?] to 14th handed over to Signals 6th Div. R.A. leaving one direct line to 13th & a lateral to 14th which handled the Bdes. nicely for Administration.
>
> **25** — Fine & warm - situation normal.
>
> **26** — Fine & very warm - situation normal. Received orders & date of intended move.
>
> **27** — Showery - advance party moved forward to Souchez to open up office. Communication established 5.00 P.M.
>
> **Souchez, 28** — Cloudy - Office at Sains-en-Gohelle closed at 8 A.M. and remaining party moved off at 9 A.M. New Office opened at Souchez 8 A.M. New Camp opened and all in order [uncertain — "order"?] & working nicely by 1 P.M.
>
> **29** — Cloudy - situation normal.
>
> **30** — Fine & very warm - Situation Normal
>
> [Signed, bottom right:] *M L Maitland Lieut C.E. / O/C 5th Div Sig Coy / artillery Detach[t]*
