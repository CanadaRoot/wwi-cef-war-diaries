# War Diary — 5th Canadian Divisional Signal Company — May 1919

- **Unit:** 5th Canadian Divisional Signal Company (Artillery Detachments)
- **Month:** May 1919 (Volume 21)
- **Source:** Library and Archives Canada, Collection Search Item ID 2004909 (RG 9 III-D-3, vol. 5006; microfilm reel T-10854, files 694–695; finding aid 9-52)
- **Pages:** May 1919, p. 1 – p. 3 (image files page-0130 – page-0132 of the 132-page diary)
- **May 1919 — COMPLETE:** cover, contents, diary text 1–31 May 1919. This is the final month of the diary; the diary ends 31 May 1919. Transcribed 2026-10-26.

## Transcription conventions
Original spelling, punctuation and abbreviations kept. Typed diary text. `[uncertain]` marks words I could not read with confidence — never guessed. Struck-through original text is shown as `[struck through in original: "..."]`. The Hour column on the diary page carries a repeated check-like mark rather than times. No map coordinates are given in the diary; places are recorded as written. May entries are grouped into date ranges rather than written day by day. "C.D.A." = Canadian Divisional Artillery; "H.Q." = Headquarters; "Bde" = Brigade; "L P C's" as written in the original.

## Page 3 — Diary text (May 1919, p. 3)

Army Form C. 2118, "WAR DIARY or INTELLIGENCE SUMMARY" — the "INTELLIGENCE SUMMARY" heading is struck through. Header: "5th Canadian Divisional Signal Co — Artillery Detachments".

> [Place: Orp, Belgium; then Havre; then Witley]
>
> **May 1919**
>
> **1st** — Showery and overcast. Routine work carried out. This consists, as during last month in maintaining communication between 5th Canadian Div Artillery Units, though the sports program of last month has been discontinued.
>
> **2nd to 5th** — Weather bad for sports as it rained. The only item outside of regular routine work was an inspection on the 2nd of headquarters section by Brig-Gen Ralston, commanding officer of the 5th C.D.A.
>
> **6th** — Fine and warm. Headquarters section left Orp-le-Petit about 9 hours and picking up the 13th and 14th Bde sections at Jauche proceeded to Jodoigne. Entrained at latter place, leaving about noon.
>
> **7th** — Fine and warm. On the train most of the day, arrived at Embarkation camp Havre 20.30.
>
> **8th 9th** — Fine and warm. At Havre Embarkation Camp, Going through the regular routine of that camp.
>
> **10th 11th** — Fine and warm. Embarked for England 22.30.
>
> **12th** — Rainy. Arrived at Southampton this morning at 7.45 and at Camp at noon.
>
> **13th to 20th** — Weather fine. Between these dates this unit was put through the Medical and Dental Boards Clothing parades and had the various papers made out. The medical showing made by this unit was excellent, and with the exception of one man going sick with diptheria [sic] all men passed the examination.
>
> **20th to 31st** — Weather fine. With the exception of two men in hospital, all men in the unit were on leave from the 20th to the 29th and on returning signed L P C's and again went on leave.
>
> [Signed] *J.S. McPherson Captain C E O i/c 5th C.D. Signal Co*

## Open questions / uncertain items (May)
- The May cover and contents page name the whole "5th Canadian Divisional Signal Company" (no "Artillery Detachment"), while the diary-text header names the "Artillery Detachments" — transcribed as written on each page.
- The Place column for the final entry (20th–31st) appears struck through; the camp is Witley, as for the 12th–20th entries.
- "diptheria" is spelled as in the original (sic).
- 13–20 May: one man went sick with diphtheria; two men were in hospital at 20–31 May. No names are given.
- No battle casualties are reported in May 1919. The diary ends 31 May 1919, with the unit at Witley camp in England on leave pending demobilization.
