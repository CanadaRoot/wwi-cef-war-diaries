- **Unit:** 5th Canadian Divisional Signal Company (Artillery Detachment)

- **Source:** Library and Archives Canada, Collection Search Item ID 2004909 (RG 9 III-D-3, vol. 5006, microfilm reel T-10854, files 694–695; finding aid 9-52)

- **August 1918 — COMPLETE** (pages 74–81 of 132): cover, contents, diary text 1–31 August, month's traffic note. Transcribed 2026-10-26.
- August 1918 onwards — not yet transcribed by this run. Next month is September 1918 (page 82).

## Transcription conventions
Original spelling, punctuation and abbreviations kept. Handwritten diary text on Army Form C. 2118. `[uncertain]` marks words I could not read with confidence — never guessed. The Hour column is filled with the word "All" (as written) for every entry. "D.R." = despatch rider; "C.D.A." = Canadian Divisional Artillery; "C.F.A." = Canadian Field Artillery; "C.I.B." = Canadian Infantry Brigade; "R.H.A." = Royal Horse Artillery; "C.R.A." = Commander, Royal Artillery; "F.O.O." = Forward Observation Officer; "D.R.L.S." = Despatch Rider Letter Service (as written); "H.Q." = headquarters; "R.C." = Report Centre; "O.P." = observation post; "Bde" = Brigade; "S.M.T.O." = Senior Mechanical Transport Officer [uncertain expansion]; "M.C." = Military Cross; "D.W" = "D.W." cable (as written); "o.k." = all correct. No map coordinates are given except the map references "L 13 b. 50" (10 August) and "O.7 B.30.70" (29 August); places are otherwise recorded as written. The diary covers the Artillery Detachment of the company only. The unit took part in the Amiens offensive (8 August) and the advance toward Arras later in the month.

## Pages 3–8 — Diary text (August 1918, pp. 3–8)

Army Form C. 2118, "WAR DIARY or INTELLIGENCE SUMMARY". Place: Cagny 1–6 August (as "E Cagny" [uncertain] on page 3); Longueau 7–10 August; Beaufort 10 August; Le Quesnel 11–13 August; no place entered 14–27 August; Arras 28 August (Rear H.Q. moved to Arras from Habarcq).

### Page 3

> [Handwritten heading: "Arty Detachment 5th Can. Div. Signal Co"]
>
> **1-8-18** — Fair and warm, Unit complete, detrained Saleux at 5.00 A.M. and marched to camp in Cagny area, communication established among our units and line run into 4th Australian Div. exchange. 5th C.D.A. now came under 4 Div. Australians
>
> **2-8-18** — Fair and very warm, received orders of intended show and the part we were to play in it. Indents for necessary supplies.
>
> **3-8-18** — Dull and showery. reconnaissance of new area made by Signal Officer and routes for lines mapped out. received Cable for work
>
> **4-8-18** — Fine and warm, work commenced on lines from Cagny to Thonville wood [uncertain — place name] (intended Report Centre). Signal office established in Chateau and lines run into our units
>
> **5-8-18** — Dull with heavy showers in afternoon, two lines to new report centre completed. Lines run from R.C. (Thonville wood) to 13th and 14th Bde C.F.A. H.Q. behind Cachy.
>
> **6-8-18** — Dull and showery. Div. H.Q. moved from Cagny to Longueau. R.C. at Thonville wood washed out on account of change in Div. area's. New R.C. established in Dugout behind Gentille wood [uncertain — written as "Deville" on this page; "Gentille Wood" is given as the new H.Q. location on 7 August].

### Page 4

> **7-8-18** — Fine and warm, Lines run from New H.Q. (Gentille Wood) to 13th and 14th Bdes C.F.A. behind Cachy. 13th and 14th Bdes C.F.A laid lines to their Batteries, Infantry Brigade and O.P's. Two mounted Orderlies and 8 runners reported from Brigades for duty at this H.Q. and shown their routes also two mounted Orderlies reported from Bdes to "R.A." Cdn. Corps. Visual Communication established between Division and Brigades.
>
> **8-8-18** — Fine and warm, Zero hour four a.m. all lines o.k. during the show. never out of telephonic communication, 10.00 A.M. 5th C.D.A. out of action and held in reserve.
>
> **9-8-18** — Fine. Brigades moved to Domart Wood, order received at 10.00 p.m to move forward under 32nd Division
>
> **10-8-18** — Fine and warm, 5th C.D.A. moved off at 4 a.m. and joined 32nd Division as their Artillery at Beaucourt [uncertain spelling]. Battle H.Q. established at L 13 b. 50, Visual and Telephonic Communication with Bdes, and Batteries [Entry continued on page 5.]

### Page 5

> **10-8-18 (continued)** — also lines with Infantry established by 4 p.m. rear H.Q. at Beaufort. [uncertain — word read as "Remaining"] Douglas M.C. became a casualty.
>
> **11-8-18** — Fine, Rear H.Q. moved to Le. Quesnel. Line run into 3rd Can. Div exchange thus giving us communication with Advanced H.Q. as well as Adm. Corps [uncertain].
>
> **12-8-18** — Fine and very warm, situation quiet, unable to obtain new Motorcycle from either 3rd or 4th Cdn. Div. or S.M.T.O. thus leaving us without a Motorcycle During the night of 11th-12th Sgt A.R. Mawdsley [uncertain surname] and Cpl E. Nash were recommended for decoration for their splendid work which enabled their Bde Commander to keep in close touch with the Infantry and the General situation which at the time was so [struck through in original] so critical
>
> **13-8-18** — Fine and warm, situation unchanged, Rear H.Q. shelled out of Le. Quesnel village. Sapper W. Pace and Sapper M. G. Henderson were recommended for decoration. These men with absolute disregard for danger maintained Flag Visual Station and Lines in the face of the enemy and heavy shelling enabling their Bde Commander to keep in touch with the situation [Entry continued on page 6.]

### Page 6

> **13-8-18 (continued)** — and transmit very valuable information to the rear as a heavy Counter attack by the enemy was imminent.
>
> **14-8-18** — Fine, Rear H.Q. established in Le. Quesnel woods. Line run into 3rd Cdn. Div. exchange, one M.C. and rider reported for duty from 3rd Cdn. Div. one rider from this H.Q. reported to 3rd Cdn. Div for duty.
>
> **15-8-18** — Dull, more wire and phones sent forward situation unchanged
>
> **16-8-18** — Dull, Brigade advanced one thousand yards, otherwise situation unchanged.
>
> **17-8-18** — Fair, 15 miles D.W cable drawn from Cable dump at Beaufort and sent to Forward H.Q.
>
> **18-8-18** — Cloudy, situation Normal. one remount drawn for 13th Bde C.F.A. Spr Morton D.R (14 Bde) evacuated to Hospital.
>
> **19-8-18** — Fair and Cloudy. received order to be prepared to move on short notice
>
> **20-8-18** — Fair and Cloudy situation quiet.

### Page 7

> **21-8-18** — Fair H.Q. moved to rear H.Q. (Le. Quesnel woods) Bdes. moved to Domart Wood
>
> **22-8-18** — Fair and very warm, 5th C.D.A. marched to Saleux [uncertain — place name] area
>
> **23-8-18** — Fair and very warm, H.Q. and 13th Bde C.F.A marched to Saleux and entrained, detrained at Aubigny at 11 p.m. H.Q. marched to Berles [uncertain — place name]. 13th Bde C.F.A. camped in Aubigny area
>
> **24-8-18** — Fair with light showers, 14th Bde C.F.A. entrained at Saleux, detrained at Aubigny and Camped 13th Bde C.F.A. marched to wagon lines in Anzin area, H.Q. moved to Habarcq.
>
> **25-8-18** — Fair with heavy showers during evening and night. H.Q. advanced, moved to St. Sauveur, 14th Bde moved to wagon lines in R18 B8.0 (abt 51 C) [uncertain — map reference as read]
>
> **26-8-18** — Showery, Zero hour 4 A.M. 5th C.D.A moves forward [struck through in original] with 7th C.I.B. and took up position on Orange Hill, Communication to Bdes. immediately established
>
> **27-8-18** — Fair, 9 hours [uncertain] 10th Bde. C.F.A. 19th and 2nd Bdes. H.Q. came under 6th C.D.A also 126 and 56 R.H.A. Bde

### Page 8

> **28-8-18** — Fair and Cool, Rear H.Q. moved to Arras from Habarcq. Heavy fighting ensued on the front but advance progressed most favourably. during the show Communication with Bdes, Batteries, F.O.O.'s and Liaison was exceptionally good. C.R.A. was never out of touch with any of his units
>
> **29-8-18** — Fair, Forward H.Q. established at O.7 B.30.70, lines run in to units in their new positions, Report centre maintained at old H.Q. (Orange Hill)
>
> **30-8-18** — Fair, situation quiet, line advances 5000 yds.
>
> **31-8-18** — Fair and cloudy, situation unchanged, line still further advances. New Liaison lines run into Infantry Bde. in their new positions.
>
> Traffic: message traffic light. D.R.L.S. heavy. phone calls exceptionally heavy. unable to keep detailed account.
>
> [Signed] *A. H. Allop* [uncertain surname] *Lieut C.E for O.C. Arty Det 5th Can Div Signal Co.*
