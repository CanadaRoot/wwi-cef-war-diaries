- **March 1918 — COMPLETE** (pages 38–46 of 132): cover, contents, diary text 1–31 March, monthly business summary. Transcribed 2026-10-26.

## Transcription conventions
Original spelling, punctuation and abbreviations kept. Handwritten diary text throughout. `[uncertain]` marks words I could not read with confidence — never guessed. The "Hour" column is struck through on every diary page (the diarist wrote "Nil" or similar across it). No map coordinates are given in the diary; places are recorded as written. "C.D.A." = Canadian Divisional Artillery; "C.F.A." = Canadian Field Artillery; "Bde" = Brigade; "D.R." = despatch rider; "O.P." = observation post; "H.Q." = headquarters; "D.A." = Divisional Artillery; "C.D.T.M." = Canadian Divisional Trench Mortars; "L.D." = limber driver (as written); "D.R.L.S." = despatch rider letter service (as written in the monthly business table).

## Pages 3–9 — Diary text (March 1918, pp. 3–9)

Army Form C. 2118, "WAR DIARY or INTELLIGENCE SUMMARY". Place: Sains-en-Gohelle unless otherwise noted.

### Page 3

> **1** — Cold and cloudy, snowfall in afternoon, Lt. H.K. Cox left for England on 14 days leave.
>
> **2** — Cold and cloudy, heavy snowfall in afternoon. Second course 5th C.D.A. Signal School closed today.
>
> **3** — Cold and foggy, situation quiet.
>
> **4** — Cold and foggy, enemy raided our front shortly before 6.00 A.M. all lines held up until 6.15 A.M. when both lines to 14th Brigade C.F.A. dis, Magneto line to 13th Brigade C.F.A. dis at 6.31 A.M. Fullerphone line to 13th Brigade C.F.A. dis, at 6.40 A.M. [uncertain — one word illegible] 14th Brigade C.F.A. Magneto line in at 7.18 A.M. could get 13th Brigade C.F.A. through their latteral to 14th Brigade C.F.A. 13th Brigade magneto line in at 7.55 A.M. 14th Brigade Fuller line in at 9.00 A.M. The line trouble in the case of each Brigade was all on about 200 yards of overland from Brigade H.Q. to the Buried cable test box. The lines being necessarily close together [struck through in original] and the shelling heavy.

### Page 4

> **4** (continued) — Communication from Brigades to Batteries, O.Ps. and Infantry Liaison was maintained by means of latteral lines throughout.
>
> **5** — Cold and cloudy, clearing in afternoon, 13th Brigade C.F.A. started to bury from their headquarters to buried cable test-box a distance of 200 yards.
>
> **6** — Clear and cool, [uncertain — "Linear"] line 3rd C.I.B. was shot out at 10.00 A.M. repaired at 11.13 A.M., 13th Bde C.F.A. Fullerphone line out at 2.40 P.M. repaired at 3.15 P.M.
>
> **7** — Clear and cool, situation quiet.
>
> **8** — Clear and cool, situation quiet.
>
> **9** — Clear and mild. D.R's now make the run to Brigade in 39 minutes on account of good roads. Fullerphone line to 13th Bde C.F.A. dis. for 30 minutes, fault found in their office.
>
> **10** — Fair and mild, situation quiet. 13th Bde finished Bury from their H.Q. to [uncertain] exchange test box.

### Page 5

> **10** (continued) — Bury from their H.Q. to [uncertain] exchange test box.
>
> **11** — Fair and mild, 14th Bde C.F.A. finishes their Bury from end of Sewer at M23 A25.45 to K-R 3 box at M23 A75.80. K-R 3 box being opened up and racks put in.
>
> **12** — Fair and mild, new line to 4th C.D.A. put on Switchboard.
>
> **13** — Fair and mild, morning D.R. to Brigades delayed by air blowout. Magneto line to 14th Bde. C.F.A. dis 11.30 A.M. ok at 11.45 A.M. Fullerphone line, very busy from 11.00 A.M. to 1.00 P.M. and from 6.00 P.M. to 8.00 P.M.
>
> **14** — Dull and showery. Switchboard and Instruments of 2nd C.D.A. installed in preparation of our relief by them on the 16th instant. Fullerphone superimposed on magneto line to 4th C.D.A. and taken off line to 1st C.D.A.
>
> **15** — Dull and cool, situation quiet.
>
> **16** — Clear and cool, 2nd C.D.A. signals took over at 10.00 A.M., our section marched to [uncertain] opened up office with advance party at 10.00 A.M.

### Page 6

> **16** (continued) — section marched to [uncertain], opened up office with advance party at 10.00 A.M. Lines to 2nd Division and Brigade Units, our O.P. loaned us by 2nd Div Signals. Lt. F.J. Featherstonhaugh left for C.W. Wireless Course (three weeks) at First Army Signal School.
>
> **17** — Fair and mild, Lt H.K. Cox returned [uncertain] from leave.
>
> **18** — Fair and mild.
>
> **19** — Fair and mild.
>
> **20** — Fair and mild, 10 Candidates for 5th C.D.A. Signal School reported [uncertain] School to start morning of 21st. Orders received to move on account of German attack.
>
> **21** — Fair and mild, Candidates for Signal School returned to their section account of move. Lt H.K. Cox posted to 12th Brigade Signal section.
>
> **22** — Fair and mild.
>
> **23** — Fair and mild, Capt M.L. Maitland and advance party proceeded to Sains-en-Gohelle to arrange to take over from 2nd C.D.A.

### Page 7

> **23** (continued) — 2nd C.D.A.
>
> **24** — Fair and mild, relieved 2nd C.D.A. Signals at 10.00 A.M. Brigade Sections relieved first and second sections 4th C.D.A. Came under 4th Div Signals, Bracquemont, at same hour.
>
> **25** — Fair and mild, very poor line to 14th Brigade C.D.A. all messages to 14th Brigade C.D.A. transmitted through 13th Brigade C.F.A.
>
> **26** — Place: Bracquemont. Dull and cool, Headquarters moves to Bracquemont at 10.00 A.M. all lines good to units, communication much easier on account of being behind our Brigades. Exchange kept open at Sains-en-Gohelle as a Joint report centre for ourselves and 4th C.D.A.
>
> **27** — Cold and cloudy, lines improved to [uncertain].
>
> **28** — Cold and showery, advance party from 16th D.A. Signals arrives Bracquemont to arrange for relief of 8th and 5th C.D.A. Signals. Report centre at Sains-en-Gohelle taken over by 16th D.A. Signals.

### Page 8

> **29** — Place: Sains-en-Gohelle. Dull and showery, closes office at Bracquemont at 12.00 noon and opened office at Sains-en-Gohelle at same time. Lt. F.J. Featherstonhaugh returned from wireless course. Lt H.K. Cox returned to H.Q. from 14th Bde C.F.A.
>
> **30** — Cold and showery. 13th and 14th Bdes. C.F.A. moved from wagon lines to battle position in Roelincourt area and came under 4th C.D.A. for tactical control at 6.00 P.M. Div. H.Q. closed office at Sains-en-Gohelle at 2.00 P.M. and moved to Chateau-de-la-Haie.
>
> **31** — Place: Chateau de la Haie. Fair with high wind, opened office at 9.00 A.M., closed office at 6.00 P.M. Opened office at Victory Redoubt at 6.00 P.M. at which time we took over our own Bdes [sentence ends here in the original].
