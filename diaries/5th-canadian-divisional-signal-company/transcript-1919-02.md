- **February 1919 — COMPLETE:** cover, contents, diary text 1–28 February 1919. Transcribed 2026-10-26.

## Transcription conventions
Original spelling, punctuation and abbreviations kept. Typed diary text (February 1919 onwards). `[uncertain]` marks words I could not read with confidence — never guessed. Struck-through original text is shown as `[struck through in original: "..."]`. The Hour column on each diary page carries a repeated check-like mark rather than times. No map coordinates are given in the diary; places are recorded as written. "C.F.A." = Canadian Field Artillery; "C.D.A." = Canadian Divisional Artillery; "H.Q." = Headquarters; "Bde" = Brigade; "Y.M.C.A." = Young Men's Christian Association; "A/Sgt" = Acting Sergeant; "2/Cpl" = Second Corporal; "L/Cpl" = Lance Corporal; "S.O.S." = struck off strength (used in the March entries).

## Open questions / uncertain items (February)
- 1 Feb: two words in the typed text are struck through (before "communication" and before "Canadian Corps") and cannot be read — shown as struck-through gaps.
- 17 Feb: Sgt Love (Hq section) sent to hospital — no cause given.
- 24 Feb: Sgt Love struck off strength; "J.C." follows in the original and is unexplained.
- 25 Feb: promotions — 2/Cpl Foote to A/Sgt (Hqrs), L/Cpl Paisley to A/2/Cpl, Sapper Morton E.J. to A/L/Cpl. The surname "Paisley" may carry a trailing initial that could not be read — marked [uncertain].
- A horse and saddlery were stolen from "B" section on the night of 27/28 February; the loss is not recorded in the February diary text itself but is the subject of the court of inquiry on 4 March 1919.
- No battle casualties are reported in February 1919.
