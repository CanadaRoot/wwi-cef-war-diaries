# War Diary — 5th Canadian Divisional Signal Company (Artillery Detachment) — January 1919

- **Unit:** 5th Canadian Divisional Signal Company (Artillery Detachment)
- **Month:** January 1919 (Volume 17)
- **Source:** Library and Archives Canada, Collection Search Item ID 2004909 (RG 9 III-D-3, vol. 5006; microfilm reel T-10854, files 694–695; finding aid 9-52)
- **Pages:** January 1919, p. 1 – p. 6 (image files page-0112 – page-0117 of the 132-page diary)
- **January 1919 — COMPLETE:** cover, contents, diary text 1–31 January 1919. Transcribed 2026-10-26.

## Transcription conventions
Original spelling, punctuation and abbreviations kept. Handwritten diary text. `[uncertain]` marks words I could not read with confidence — never guessed. The Hour column on each diary page carries a repeated check-like mark rather than times; no hours are recorded except where times appear in the text itself. No map coordinates are given in the diary; places are recorded as written. "C.D.A." = Canadian Divisional Artillery; "C.D." = Canadian Division; "H.Q." = Headquarters; "Bde" = Brigade.

## Open questions / uncertain items (January)
- 2 Jan: Headquarters sub was quarantined for mumps — no names given; no casualties.
- 14–15 Jan: "Mr Detherstonhaugh" and "Lt Detherstonhaugh" as read; "Lt Cox" relieved at B Sub.
- 16 Jan: the place B Sub left reads "Walrus" but that cannot be confirmed as a place name — marked [uncertain].
- 23 Jan: 3 kilos of line stolen from B Sub lines on the night of 22–23 January; thief unknown. (A horse and saddlery were later reported stolen from B Sub on the night of 27/28 February — see March 1919, 4 March entry.)
- No casualties are reported in January 1919.
