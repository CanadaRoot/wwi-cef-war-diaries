# War Diary — 5th Canadian Divisional Signal Company (Artillery Detachment) — November 1917

- **Unit:** 5th Canadian Divisional Signal Company (Artillery Detachment)
- **Month:** November 1917 (Volume 3)
- **Source:** Library and Archives Canada, Collection Search Item ID 2004909 (RG 9 III-D-3, vol. 5006, microfilm reel T-10854, files 694–695; finding aid 9-52)
- **Pages:** November 1917, p. 1 – p. 6 (image files page-0014 – page-0019 of the 132-page diary)
- **November 1917 — COMPLETE** (pages 14–19 of 132): cover, contents, diary text 1–30 November, including a monthly signal-traffic table, no appendices. Transcribed 2026-10-26.

## Transcription conventions
Original spelling, punctuation and abbreviations kept. Handwritten diary text throughout (Army Form C. 2118, "WAR DIARY or INTELLIGENCE SUMMARY"). `[uncertain]` marks words I could not read with confidence — never guessed. No map coordinates are given in the diary except the map references noted as written (e.g. "in 28 to 30.15"). "Mackerel", "Newt", "Cream", "Cartridge" are code names used in the diary for units/lines — transcribed as written. "H.Q." = Headquarters; "Bdes." = Brigades (the 13th and 14th Brigades, C.F.A.); "C.F.A." = Canadian Field Artillery; "Divarty" = Divisional Artillery; "D.A." = Divisional Artillery; "D.A.C." = Divisional Ammunition Column; "R.A." = Royal Artillery; "R.F.A." = Royal Field Artillery; "Spr." = Sapper; "disc." = disconnected; "S.O.S." = call for artillery support; "D.R.L.S." = Despatch Rider Letter Service. The repeated handwritten mark in the Hour column in this month reads "A.K.C." [uncertain] — different from the "M.L.M." of August–October — and is noted here rather than repeated against every entry.

## Pages 3–6 — Diary text (1–30 November 1917)

Army Form C. 2118, "WAR DIARY or INTELLIGENCE SUMMARY".

### Page 3 — 1–10 November 1917

> [Sheet heading: "5th Cdn. Divnl Sig [uncertain] Coy. Artillery Detachments."]
>
> **Sains-en-Gohelle, Nov. 1** — Cloudy - took over Cream at 6 P.M. Mr. Fetherstonhaugh [uncertain spelling] will supervise the Signal section. Line run into Garden Dump.
>
> **2.** — Dull - situation normal.
>
> **3.** — Cloudy with misty rain - at 12 midnight we came directly under 11th Div. Magneto line direct to them.
>
> **4.** — Cloudy & very dull - situation normal.
>
> **5.** — Dull light south wind - situation normal - 507508 Spr. Walker S. E. P. operator taken on strength from Canadian Corps. to complete personnel.
>
> **6** — Dull, light winds with occasional showers - situation normal.
>
> **7** — Showery with light winds - at 10 A.M. Mackerel H.Q. handed over to Cartridge at in 28 to 30.15. At 2 P.M. Mackerel opened up their new office Ebarston Castle [uncertain]. all communication for Mackerel through L.A.
>
> **8** — Fine in the morning but showery towards evening - situation normal.
>
> **9** — Dull with occasional rain - Newt S.O.S. to Batteries 20 seconds.
>
> **10** — Rain with light wind - took over from 11th R.A. closed office at Sains-en-Gohelle at 4 P.M. opened office at Bracquemont at same hour. Communication uninterrupted.

### Page 4 — 11–21 November 1917

> [Sheet heading: "5th Cdn. Divnl [uncertain] Artillery Detachments."]
>
> **Bracquemont, Nov. 11** — Cloudy but no rain - situation normal.
>
> **12** — Fair and fine - Signal office inadequate so built an addition.
>
> **13** — Fair - Signal office moved into new quarters. Adequate room now.
>
> **14** — Dull no rain during the day - situation normal.
>
> **15** — Fine - situation unchanged.
>
> **16** — Dull but no rain - front very quiet.
>
> **17** — Cloudy - Cartridge line disc. for a few minutes but communication uninterrupted.
>
> **18** — Fair and cloudy - situation normal with a quiet front.
>
> **19** — Dull with occasional light shower - induction on Allan Dump line cleared.
>
> **20** — Dull with misty rain - line to 58 R.A. Bde. disc. 8 30 P.M. but quickly repaired. also trouble with indicator on board cleared.
>
> **21** — Steady but light rain - Infantry covered by 5 C. D.A. put on a raid at 6 15 A.M. B.M. plugged through direct to both groups thus enabling reports to be received at maximum speed - existing Bde. lines handled the work nicely and all messages handled expeditiously.

### Page 5 — 22–29 November 1917

> [Sheet heading: "5th Cdn. Divnl [uncertain] Artillery Detachments."]
>
> **Bracquemont, Nov. 22** — Dull with variable winds & thick mist or drizzle - line to Newt cleared of induction.
>
> **23** — Fine - line to 11th D.A.C. shut down this was immediately reported to Corps for their information and action.
>
> **24** — Dull with very high winds accompanied by heavy mist or drizzle - lines stood up O.K. trouble on bury to 58th Bde. cleared.
>
> **25** — Cold - high winds with a flurry of snow or sleet - 58th Bde. Fullerphone line cut through someone stealing 100 yds. of cable out of it. Damaged quickly repaired but culprit not caught.
>
> **26** — Clear and cold with light winds - situation normal.
>
> **27** — Dull with drizzle or heavy mist - at 6 A.M. 13th Bde. became the centre group and 14th Bde. became the left group. Line to 14th taken over giving us a direct communication also 2 laterals run from 14th to 13th.
>
> **28** — Fair - 14th Bde. - left group was relieved by 59th Bde. R.F.A. night of Nov. 28/29. 14th Bde. came out to their wagon lines.
>
> **29** — Dull with heavy mist in the morning but clearing towards evening situation normal.

## Open questions / uncertain items (November 1917)
- 1 November: "Mr. Fetherstonhaugh will supervise the Signal section." — surname spelling [uncertain].
- 5 November: Spr. S. E. P. Walker, #507508, operator, taken on strength from Canadian Corps — initials and number as read.
- 7 November: "Mackerel H.Q. handed over to Cartridge at in 28 to 30.15." — "at in" as written; "Ebarston Castle" [uncertain]; "all communication for Mackerel through L.A." — "L.A." as written.
- 9 November: "Newt S.O.S. to Batteries 20 seconds." — the 20 seconds is the time taken to get the S.O.S. through, cf. 18 October (58 seconds).
- 21 November: "B.M. plugged through direct to both groups" — "B.M." as written.
- 25 November: 100 yards of cable stolen from the 58th Bde. Fullerphone line; "culprit not caught".
- The Hour-column mark in this month reads "A.K.C." [uncertain], not the "M.L.M." of August–October.
- No casualties are recorded in November 1917.
