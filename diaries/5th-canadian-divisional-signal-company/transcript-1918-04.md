# War Diary — 5th Canadian Divisional Signal Company (Artillery Detachment) — April 1918

- **Unit:** 5th Canadian Divisional Signal Company (Artillery Detachment)
- **Month:** April 1918 (Volume 8)
- **Source:** Library and Archives Canada, Collection Search Item ID 2004909 (RG 9 III-D-3, vol. 5006, microfilm reel T-10854, files 694–695; finding aid 9-52)
- **Pages:** April 1918, p. 1 – p. 6 (image files page-0047 – page-0052 of the 132-page diary)
- **April 1918 — COMPLETE** (pages 47–52 of 132): cover, contents, diary text 1–30 April, monthly business summary. Transcribed 2026-10-26.

## Transcription conventions
Original spelling, punctuation and abbreviations kept. Handwritten diary text throughout. `[uncertain]` marks words I could not read with confidence — never guessed. The "Hour" column is struck through on every diary page. The heading "INTELLIGENCE SUMMARY" is struck through on the diary pages; a handwritten heading "5th Can Div Signal Coy. Artillery Detachment" is written in above the table on p. 3. No map coordinates are given in the diary; places are recorded as written. "C.D.A." = Canadian Divisional Artillery; "C.F.A." = Canadian Field Artillery; "R.F.A." = Royal Field Artillery; "D.R." = despatch rider; "O.P." = observation post; "D.T.M." = Divisional Trench Mortars; "D.A.C." = Divisional Ammunition Column; "C.D.T.M.B." = Canadian Divisional Trench Mortar Battery (as written).

## Pages 3–6 — Diary text (April 1918, pp. 3–6)

Army Form C. 2118, "WAR DIARY or INTELLIGENCE SUMMARY". Place: Aux Rietz unless otherwise noted.

### Page 3

> **1** — Rainy, established office at Aux-Rietz with forward exchange at Victory Redoubt in conjunction with 4 C.D.A. Signals under whom we are working. 13th and 14th Bdes. C.F.A. established H.Q. at A29 a 30.40 and A29 a 10.40 and came into action at 6.00 P.M.
>
> **2** — Fair and cool, Capt M.L. Maitland became lines officer for the 4th and 5th C.D.A's and the 6 Brigades under them.
>
> **3** — Cool with high wind, lines cut through to 4th, 5th C.D.A. C and 56th D.A.C. and 5th C.D.T.M.B. near H.Q.
>
> **4** — Dull and showery, communications established with and for 5th C.D.T.M. Hdqrs and batteries H.Q. (advanced) at B15 a 20.60.
>
> **5** — Showery, records of all lines completed.
>
> **6** — Fair with light wind, situation normal.
>
> **7** — Bright with occasional showers, 280th and 281st R.F.A. Brigades relieved by the 12th C.F.A. Brigade.
>
> **8** — Dull and showery. C.F.A. Brigade relieved by 36th Brigade R.F.A.
>
> **9** — Dull and showery, several pairs buried cable cut through for 36th Brigade R.F.A.

### Page 4

> **10** — Dull and showery, situation quiet.
>
> **11** — Clear and cool. Three men loaned to 4 C.D.A. to assist in racking new buried.
>
> **12** — Fair and mild, three men loaned to 4th C.D.A. to assist in racking buried. 13th and 14th Bdes. C.F.A. moved from F.Y. [uncertain — "(Red...?)"] to machine gun post. relief completed by 3.00 P.M.
>
> **13** — Fair and mild, nine men at Victory Redoubt withdrawn to Aux-Rietz. Installed office in Aux-Rietz cave, now running separate office from 4th C.D.A.
>
> **14** — Cold with high wind, Signal Office moved to another part of the Aux Rietz cave on account of hole being [uncertain] through our office. All Units 5 C.D.A. now direct onto 5 C.D.A. exchange.
>
> **15** — Dull and cold, Lt H.K. Cox attached to D.T.M.O. 5th C.D.A. to install communication to T.M. batteries which are going into action from Souchez river to Avion.

### Page 5

> **16** — Dull and cold, situation quiet.
>
> **17** — Dull and foggy, report centre for 5th C.D.T.M's established at S6 d 7.5.75. direct lines to all batteries, O.P's, and H.Q. cut through. Lt H.K. Cox rejoined unit on [sentence ends here in the original].
>
> **18** — Cold and foggy with high wind, signal office in cave again moved.
>
> **19** — Clear and cool, Signal Office, less switchboard moves up out of cave.
>
> **20** — Fine, situation quiet.
>
> **21** — Fine. The eleven men attached to us from batteries on 9/9/17 returned to Units.
>
> **22** — Fine with high winds, visual communication used between Div H.Q. and Brigades quite successfully.
>
> **23** — Fine and mild, situation quiet.
>
> **24** — Fair with showers in afternoon, Line to Div, Train, dis.
>
> **25** — Dull with showers, Div, Train, line dis, at 10. A.M. repaired by our linemen at 10.20 A.M.

## Open questions / uncertain items (April)
- 12 April: the place the 13th and 14th Bdes. C.F.A. moved from is written "F.Y." followed by a word in brackets that could not be read.
- 14 April: one word in "hole being [uncertain] through our office" could not be read.
- 17 April: the entry ends mid-sentence ("Lt H.K. Cox rejoined unit on"); the continuation is not on the page.
- 30 April: the day's entry is only "Cool with light rain," followed by the monthly business table.
- No casualties are recorded in April 1918.
- No map coordinates are given in the diary (map references such as A29 a 30.40 are recorded as written).
