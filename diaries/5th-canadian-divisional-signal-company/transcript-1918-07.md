- July 1918 onwards — not yet transcribed by this run. Next month is August 1918 (page 74).

## Transcription conventions
Original spelling, punctuation and abbreviations kept. Handwritten diary text on Army Form C. 2118. `[uncertain]` marks words I could not read with confidence — never guessed. The Hour column is filled with the word "All" (as written) for every entry. "D.R." = despatch rider; "C.D.A." = Canadian Divisional Artillery; "C.F.A." = Canadian Field Artillery; "C.D.T.M." = Canadian Divisional Trench Mortar [uncertain expansion]; "D.R.L.S." = Despatch Rider Letter Service (as written); "H.Q." = headquarters; "O.R." = other ranks; "O.P." = observation post; "Bde" = Brigade; "o.k." = all correct. No map coordinates are given in the diary; places are recorded as written. The diary covers the Artillery Detachment of the company only; the unit was at Etrun throughout July.
