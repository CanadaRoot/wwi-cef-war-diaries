# War Diary — 5th Canadian Divisional Signal Company (Artillery Detachment) — December 1917

- **Unit:** 5th Canadian Divisional Signal Company (Artillery Detachment)
- **Month:** December 1917 (Volume 4)
- **Source:** Library and Archives Canada, Collection Search Item ID 2004909 (RG 9 III-D-3, vol. 5006, microfilm reel T-10854, files 694–695; finding aid 9-52)
- **Pages:** December 1917, p. 1 – p. 6 (image files page-0020 – page-0025 of the 132-page diary)
- **December 1917 — COMPLETE** (pages 20–25 of 132): cover, contents, diary text 1–31 December, including a monthly signal-traffic table, no appendices. Transcribed 2026-10-26.

## Transcription conventions
Original spelling, punctuation and abbreviations kept. Handwritten diary text throughout (Army Form C. 2118, "WAR DIARY or INTELLIGENCE SUMMARY"). `[uncertain]` marks words I could not read with confidence — never guessed. No map coordinates are given in the diary except the map references noted as written (e.g. "L 23 d. 10.70"). "H.Q." = Headquarters; "Bdes." = Brigades (the 13th and 14th Brigades, C.F.A.); "C.F.A." = Canadian Field Artillery; "C.D.A." = Canadian Divisional Artillery; "D.A." = Divisional Artillery; "D.A.C." = Divisional Ammunition Column; "D.T.M.O." = Divisional Train [uncertain — as written]; "T.M." = Trench Mortar; "T.M.O." = Trench Mortar Officer; "O.P." = Observation Post; "R.A." = Royal Artillery; "S.O.S." = call for artillery support; "D.R.L.S." = Despatch Rider Letter Service; "W.L." = Wagon Lines. The repeated handwritten mark in the Hour column reads "A.K.C." [uncertain] on 1–3 December and "M.L.M." [uncertain] for the rest of the month — apparently the diarist's initials (cf. the signature "M L Maitland") — and is noted here rather than repeated against every entry.

## Pages 3–6 — Diary text (1–31 December 1917)

Army Form C. 2118, "WAR DIARY or INTELLIGENCE SUMMARY".

### Page 3 — 1–9 December 1917

> [Sheet heading: "5th Cdn. Divisional Sig. [uncertain] Arty. Detachments."]
>
> **Bracquemont, Dec. 1** — Fair and cool - 14th Bde. moving to new Position - 13th Bde. sent S.O.S. to its batteries & had R.D. [uncertain] in 11 seconds.
>
> **2** — Fair and cold - 14th Bde. went into action under 46 D.A. at L 23 d. 10.70 [uncertain] night of 2nd/3rd.
>
> **3** — Fair & cold - line to D.T.M.O. disc. through Imperial lineman crossing jumpers at terminal pole. Line to Allan Dump blown out.
>
> **4** — Cloudy & cold - situation normal - Capt. M. L. Maitland O.C. left for 14 days leave.
>
> **5** — Clear & cool - situation normal.
>
> **Sains-en-Gohelle, 6.** — Fine - 11th D.A. H.Q. relieved 5th C.D.A. H.Q. at 12 noon. This H.Q. closed Bracquemont 12 noon & opened Sains-en-Gohelle at the same time. 5th C.D.A. H.Q. out of action.
>
> **7** — Dull & cold - cleared the Bde. lines.
>
> **8** — Clear - repaired Allan Dump and fixed their phone.
>
> **9** — Rain - Linemen cleared trouble on 5th T.M's. lines.

### Page 4 — 10–18 December 1917

> [Sheet heading: "5th Cdn. Divisional Sig. [uncertain] Arty. Detachments."]
>
> **Sains-en-Gohelle, Dec. 10** — Dull - line to C.7.A.C. [uncertain — possibly "C.D.A.C."] shelled down: out of communication for an hour.
>
> **11** — Clear and cool - trouble with T.M. lines cleared.
>
> **12** — Dull with a heavy mist - line to 5th C.7.A.C. [uncertain — possibly "C.D.A.C."] shelled down - out of communication for an hour.
>
> **13** — Drizzly rain - Allan Dump closed & phone brought in.
>
> **14** — Dull and misty with high wind - situation normal.
>
> **15** — Clear and cloudy - D.A.C. line shot down at 11 30 A.M. in communication again at 2. P.M.
>
> **16** — Cloudy with high wind and occasional snow fluries - situation normal.
>
> **17** — Clear & cold - lines to 11th D.A. & 5th C.7.A.C. [uncertain — possibly "C.D.A.C."] shot down and repaired three different times.
>
> **18** — Cold - 11th D.A. lines by N.C. route [uncertain] shot down but shortly repaired by Army linemen.

### Page 5 — 19–26 December 1917

> [Sheet heading: "5th Cdn. Divisional Sig. [uncertain] Arty. Detachments." — the printed "(Erase heading not required.)" is left in place on this sheet]
>
> **Sains-en-Gohelle, Dec. 19** — Clear and cold - one of our 11th D.A. lines handed over to Can. Corps. line to Coupigny [uncertain spelling] Can. Corps. R.C. cut onto our board.
>
> **20** — Dull & cold - situation normal.
>
> **21** — Fine & cold - B.H.S. [uncertain] & N.C. routes shot down and communication intermittent all day.
>
> **22** — Cold - Situation normal.
>
> **23** — Dull - 5th C.D.A. H.Q. & 13th & 14th Bdes. came under 3rd Div. at 10 A.M. 14th came out to W.L. at Beuvry at 1 P.M. and then came back to Sains-en-Gohelle.
>
> **24** — Misty with rain - situation normal.
>
> **25** — Cool with heavy snow fall towards evening - 14th Bde. took over from 2nd Bde. C.F.A. at 9 A.M.
>
> **26** — Bright & cool - situation normal.

## Open questions / uncertain items (December 1917)
- 1 December: "13th Bde. sent S.O.S. to its batteries & had R.D. in 11 seconds." — "R.D." [uncertain].
- 2 December: map reference "L 23 d. 10.70" [uncertain].
- 10, 12 and 17 December: "C.7.A.C." (10 Dec: "line to C.7.A.C."; 12 and 17 Dec: "5th C.7.A.C.") — written consistently as "C.7.A.C." [uncertain — possibly "C.D.A.C.", Canadian Divisional Ammunition Column].
- 18 December: "11th D.A. lines by N.C. route" — "N.C." [uncertain].
- 19 December: "line to Coupigny Can. Corps. R.C. cut onto our board." — "Coupigny" [uncertain spelling]; "R.C." as written.
- 21 December: "B.H.S. & N.C. routes shot down" — "B.H.S." [uncertain].
- 31 December: "move being cancelled for 3 days" — as written.
- December totals: the H.Q. row is headed "H.Q. R.S." [uncertain]; the 13th Bde. phone-call figure (26,338) is far above H.Q. (8,451) and 14th Bde. (5,487) — transcribed as read.
- Capt. M. L. Maitland, O.C., left for 14 days' leave on 4 December.
- No casualties are recorded in December 1917.
