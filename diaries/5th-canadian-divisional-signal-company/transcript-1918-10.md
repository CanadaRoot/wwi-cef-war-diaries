- October 1918 onwards — not yet transcribed by this run. Next month is November 1918 (page 99).

## Transcription conventions
Original spelling, punctuation and abbreviations kept. Handwritten diary text on Army Form C. 2118. `[uncertain]` marks words I could not read with confidence — never guessed. The Hour column is filled with the word "All" (as written) for every entry. The Date column is headed "1918 October" and numbered 1–31. "D.A." = Divisional Artillery; "C.D.A." = Canadian Divisional Artillery; "C.F.A." = Canadian Field Artillery; "C.I.B." = Canadian Infantry Brigade; "R.F.A." = Royal Field Artillery; "H.A." = Horse Artillery; "C.R.A." = Commander, Royal Artillery; "O.C." = Officer Commanding; "H.Q." = headquarters; "R.C." = Report Centre; "Bde" = Brigade; "C.W." = continuous wave (wireless); "D.R." = despatch rider; "O.R." = other ranks; "o.k." = all correct. Map references are given as written. No map coordinates are given beyond the map references noted. The diary covers the Artillery Detachment of the company only. The month covers the relief of the 5th C.D.A. by the 39th Division, the 51st Division coming under 5 C.D.A., and the advance through Bourlon, Blecourt, Beauvois, Denain and the Valenciennes area.

## Pages 3–7 — Diary text (October 1918, pp. 3–7)

Army Form C. 2118, "WAR DIARY or INTELLIGENCE SUMMARY". Headed "Arty Det 5th Cdn. Div Signal Co". Place: White Chateau, Bourlon 1–8 October; Leek Wood 9 October; Blecourt 10 October; Beauvois 14 October; Hem-Lenglet 18–20 October; Denain 25–31 October. No place entered 11–13, 15–17 and 21–24 October.

### Page 3

> **1-10-18** — H.Q. in Bourlon with 9th C.I.B. 14th Bde in Bourlon, 13th Bde one mile in front of Bourlon. Rear H.Q. one mile behind Bourlon. Lines run into these units including 3rd C.D.A. R.C. in Bourlon Wood
>
> **2-10-18** — Lines frequently cut by enemy activity
>
> **3-10-18** — Day was generally quiet.
>
> **4-10-18** — At night warning order was received that Divarty would be relieved by Bg [uncertain] H.Q. preliminary operations were carried out. During the night lines to Bdes, and batteries were cut by heavy shelling
>
> **5-10-18** — Relief of 5th C.D.A by 39th Div. was commenced
>
> **6-10-18** — Relief practically completed. Orders received that 51st Div. would come under 5 C.D.A during the night
>
> **7-10-18** — Relief completed 5 C.D.A Signals taking over Communications of 39th D.A. Liaison line run to 1st Inf. Bde. which we are now covering. Rear H.Q. moved to V.24.C.40.60
>
> **8-10-18** — Day fairly quiet.

### Page 4

> **9-10-18** — Hdqrs, in front of Leek Wood [uncertain spelling], Bdes, 400 yds in front of Hdqrs. Lines to 1st Inf. Bde, and 2nd Inf. Bde. Visual was also worked Line to Rear H.Q. Signal Office in V24 C40.60, Received 3 C.W. Wireless sets and personnel of 8 Bdes from [uncertain] Capt.
>
> **10-10-18** — H.Q. moved to Blecourt. Lines run to 13 Bde which moved into Blecourt also to 4 Bde in Ramillies and 3rd Inf. Bde in Auvillers [uncertain spelling]. Wireless Station set up and working with [Entry continued on page 5.]
>
> **11-10-18** — Bdes. 26 R.F.A. and 58th H.A. came under 5 C.D.A. and lines run to them.
>
> **12-10-18** — O.C. left for 14 days leave to U.K. Lieut Featherstonhaugh [uncertain spelling] in Command whilst attending a course in C.W. Wireless at Rue. 13th Bde moved to Thun-Leveque. Report centre established in Beauvois [uncertain] and lines run to Bde 36th R.F.A. 58th H.Q. [uncertain] in Beauvois, to 2nd Inf. Bde. Lines to 2nd C.D.A. in [uncertain — place name] and 3rd C.D.A. General [uncertain — read as "Hodds"] returned and functioned from H.Q. in Blecourt. Considerable difficulty was experienced in maintaining so many lines under severe enemy shellfire and limited personnel.
>
> **13-10-18** — Situation quieter, maintenance called for continuous work of linemen

### Page 5

> **14-10-18** — H.Q. moved to Beauvois at 10.00 a.m. Office in Blecourt closed 1.00 a.m. 14 Bde. moved from Ramillies to Beauvois Rear H.Q. moved to Escaudeuvres. lines run to above also to Inf. Bde. in Beauvois.
>
> **15-10-18** — Rear H.Q. moved to Ramillies
>
> **16-10-18** — Situation unchanged. H.Q. and Bde. devoted their time to salvaging wire
>
> **17-10-18** — Situation quiet. an exchange made from a German Switchboard.
>
> **18-10-18** — Received warning order of intended move, and reconnaissance carried out. 13 Bde moved to Paillencourt. and line run to them in their new position, 14 Bde moved to Estrun, lines cut through 5 C.D.A. H.Q. closed in Beauvois 12. noon and opened in Hem-Lenglet at 1 p.m. [uncertain] Lines run to Bdes in Paillencourt and Estrun. 13 & 14 Bdes moved to Marquion at 5.00 p.m. lines run from Hem-Lenglet to Report centre in Marquion also Bdes. Line run from Marquion to 4 C.D.A in Fressin [uncertain — place name] [Entry continued on page 6.]
