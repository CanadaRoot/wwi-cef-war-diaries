- September 1918 onwards — not yet transcribed by this run. Next month is October 1918 (page 92).

## Transcription conventions
Original spelling, punctuation and abbreviations kept. Handwritten diary text on Army Form C. 2118. `[uncertain]` marks words I could not read with confidence — never guessed. The Hour column is filled with the word "All" (as written) for every entry. The Date column is written in the form "1/9/18", "2/9/18", etc. "D.A." = Divisional Artillery; "C.D.A." = Canadian Divisional Artillery; "C.F.A." = Canadian Field Artillery; "C.I.B." = Canadian Infantry Brigade; "R.F.A." = Royal Field Artillery; "C.R.A." = Commander, Royal Artillery; "O.C." = Officer Commanding; "H.Q." = headquarters; "R.C." = Report Centre; "Bde" = Brigade; "HFA" = as written [uncertain]; "o.k." = all correct. Map references are given as written (sheet numbers in brackets). No map coordinates are given beyond the sheet/map references noted. The diary covers the Artillery Detachment of the company only. The month covers the advance from Arras/Monchy through Dury and Saudemont to the Canal du Nord and the approach to Cambrai (Bourlon, Pallencourt, St. Olle).

## Pages 3–10 — Diary text (September 1918, pp. 3–10)

Army Form C. 2118, "WAR DIARY or INTELLIGENCE SUMMARY". Place: Monchy 1 September; no place entered for the rest of the month (the Place column is blank or marked "(Cont.)" / "(Contin.)" where entries continue across pages). Headed "5th Can. Div. Signal Coy (Artillery Det.)".

### Page 3

> **1-9-18** — Fair and warm Rear Hq at Arras. Forward Hq at Monchy also intermediate exchange at Orange Hill with lines to 14 Bde C.F.A and 32nd Inf. Bde and rear and forward. This exchange closed later in the day when 14th Bde moved to forward positions behind Beiry [uncertain — place name] lines in and working for tomorrows attack
>
> **2-9-18** — All lines remained in from zero hour at 5 am until the cessation of the barrage at 8 am. when the brigades moved forward in close support of the 10th C.I.B. Communication was established to both Brigades and infantry bde Hq behind Dury by the time 5th C.D.A Hq arrived at the new location in Ocean Work in O29 B25.35 (Sheet 51B) Bde to battery Communications were established [Entry continued on page 4.]

### Page 4

> **2-9-18 (continued)** — shortly after batteries got into action.
>
> **3-9-18** — Brigade Hqs still in the same positions but batteries moved forward immediately west of Dury with forward sections in Saudemont [uncertain spelling] covering the further advance of the infantry to the canal Brigade to batteries lines were extended and maintained throughout. The lines between D.A Hq and bdes repeatedly cut by harassing fire but were never out over half an hour.
>
> **4-9-18** — 13th Bde Hq moved forward to Mont Dury [uncertain] line extended to there. 13th Bde batteries now moved forward to the vicinity of their forward section 13th Bde report center in Saudemont [uncertain spelling] lines continue being cut by shell fire frequently
>
> **5-9-18** — Rear Hq moved up to O19D 40.60 (Sheet 51B) and telephone communication established with Forward Hq [Entry continued on page 5.]

### Page 5

> **5-9-18 (continued)** — Received one TRIUMPH motor Cycle. at 8 p.m. came under 3rd C.D.A. Our rear at [uncertain — place name]
>
> **6-9-18** — 14th Bde will be relieved tomorrow by 293rd Bde [uncertain] Expected to move to Dury with 9th C.I.B. but
>
> **7-9-18** — on account of lack of accomodation decided to remain at Ocean Work 14th Bde will now go to their old position west of Dury to Cover defensive lines They will use the old lines that were left when in these positions before DA Hqrs heavily shelled in afternoon and lines were all out These were repaired as soon as shelling ceased at a bit
>
> **8-9-18** — Hqrs again shelled and lines all out but only for a short time
>
> **9-9-18** — Nothing unusual happened Lines all OK during [Entry continued on page 6.]

### Page 8

> **24-9-18 (continued)** — work commenced on night of the 24/25th on division lines
>
> **25-9-18** — Night of 25th/26th division lines finished DA Hqrs wired up Switchboard and local phones installed Advance parties from Bde Sections moved up and laid their lines to their batteries
>
> **26-9-18** — D.A. and Bde Hqs moved up to their new positions and remained silent 5 C.D.A came under C.R.A 1st Cdn for Tactical Control
>
> **27-9-18** — Zero at 5.20 lines all OK and remained in throughout the barrage at Zero plus 6 hours and 51 minutes brigades limbered up and moved just over the canal E15 (sheet 57C) O.C went forward to locate forward Hqrs with 9th C.I.B. in front of Quarry Wood no dugouts available So Hdqrs established at E9 C1.9 and lines rear to both Bdes and 9th [Entry continued on page 9.]

### Page 9

> **27-9-18 (continued)** — C.I.B where C.R.A and Bm. remained for the night
>
> **28-9-18** — Hdqrs party moved up to 9th C.I.B in readiness to move further forward. Hdqrs officers moved forward with 9th C.I.B Hqrs to F8D Central (sheet 57C) But on arrival found that line was not so far advanced as expected and that no accommodations for a Headquarters was available Lines were however laid from here to Bdes and communications established in time to get barrage orders out for an attack of 9th C.I.B at 7.00 pm Hqrs then moved back to Chateau at Bourlon and lines established to Brigades from there immediately on arrival.
>
> **29-9-18** — 8th army Bde placed under 5th C.D.A line put in by 3 am Line to 9th C.I.B report centre extended to 8th C.I.B as we are now covering them All other lines OK for attack at 8 am on Cambrai Batteries of both brigades moved forward to [Entry continued on page 10.]

### Page 10

> **29-9-18 (continued)** — vicinity of PALLENCOURT also 13th Bde Hdqrs moved forward to NE of Bourlon Lines were established immediately The 251st Bde RFA placed under 5th CDA and are moving into area NE of Bourlon Wood
>
> **30-9-18** — 251 Bde RFA got into positions and lines laid and working by 1.35 am Attack under preparation for the morning which if successful will require everyone to move forward. Attack only partially successful 13th Bde Hqrs moved forward in afternoon west of St OILE [uncertain — probably St. Olle] which is on the outskirts of Cambrai Batteries moved further forward to Vicinity of Pallencourt and St OILE Lines established by time of arrival of units 251st Army Bde RFA leave us and go to 4th CDA Report Centre established in front of Bourlon Preparations for attack in morning in progress
