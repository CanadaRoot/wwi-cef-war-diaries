# War Diary — 5th Canadian Divisional Signal Company (Artillery Detachment) — November 1918

- **Unit:** 5th Canadian Divisional Signal Company (Artillery Detachment)
- **Month:** November 1918 (Volume 15)
- **Source:** Library and Archives Canada, Collection Search Item ID 2004909 (RG 9 III-D-3, vol. 5006; microfilm reel T-10854, files 694–695; finding aid 9-52)
- **Pages:** November 1918, p. 1 – p. 7 (image files page-0099 – page-0105 of the 132-page diary)
- **November 1918 — COMPLETE:** cover, contents, diary text 1–30 November 1918. Transcribed 2026-10-26.

## Transcription conventions
Original spelling, punctuation and abbreviations kept. Handwritten diary text. `[uncertain]` marks words I could not read with confidence — never guessed. Struck-through original text is shown as `[struck through in original: "..."]`. The Hour column on each diary page carries a repeated check-like mark rather than times; no hours are recorded except where times appear in the text itself. No map coordinates are given in the diary; places are recorded as written. Abbreviations as written: "C.D.A." = Canadian Divisional Artillery; "C.I.B." = Canadian Infantry Brigade; "Bde" = Brigade; "F.O.O." = Forward Observation Officer; "L.O." = Liaison Officer; "D.A." = Divisional Artillery; "D.A.C." = Divisional Ammunition Column; "R.F.A." = Royal Field Artillery; "T.M." = Trench Mortar; "O.C." = Officer Commanding; "H.Q." = Headquarters.

## Pages 3–7 — Diary text (November 1918, pp. 3–7)

Army Form C. 2118, "WAR DIARY or INTELLIGENCE SUMMARY". Header on each page: "5th Cdn. Div. Signal Co. Arty. Det."

### Page 3

> [Place: Denain]
>
> **1** — Capt M.L. Maitland M.C. turned over the Command of the Det. to Lieut J.S. McPherson, of the 3rd Can. Div. Sig. Co. on his transfer to 2nd Can. Div. Sig. Co. 13th Bde moved to Le Sentinelle, their line installed to them also lateral run from 13th to 14th Bde.
>
> **2** — Report Centre established in Herin, Two lines from D.A. H.Q. run in, one to 13th Bde, one to 12th C.I.B. in Anzin, one to 14th Bde in Herin, 13th Bde in close support of the 12th C.I.B., maintained an F.O.O. line also a Battalion L.O. line, 14th Bde maintained Left Bn. L.O.
>
> **3** — Received Order of intended move. 14th Bde moved to Anzin, Line run from 14th to 13th Bde. D.A. H.Q. closed office in Denain at 17.00 hrs and opened in Valenciennes at same hour, 13th Bde and 12th C.I.B. also moved to Valenciennes. But [uncertain] lines run with them, 14th Bde remained in Anzin Rear H.Q. moved to Denain and lines out through to them

### Page 4

> [Place: Valenciennes]
>
> **3** (continued) — Bdes. in touch with their Batteries and Infantry in front of Valenciennes.
>
> **4** — Line run to new 14th H.Q. in St. Saulve, and to new Rear H.Q. in Le Sentinelle.
>
> **5** — 13th Bde and 12th C.I.B. and R.H.Q. [uncertain] moved Onnaing, established a Report Centre in Denain and took on 13th Bde and 12th C.I.B. at 18.00 hours. 14th Bde and Batteries moved from St. Saulve to Onnaing and established Communication. D.A. in Communication with all units and Infantry.
>
> **6** — All H.Q.'s remained the same but Batteries and Battalions moved forward.
>
> **7** — At 06.00 hrs. 5th C.A.S. ceased to function and came under Canadian Corps. [uncertain] with the Valenciennes Area, Lines run to all units.
>
> **8** — Artificers Shop established at H.Q. and any Instrument requiring repair was brought in from Units, at 23 hrs Warning Order of intended move received.

### Page 5

> [Place: Valenciennes, then Thulin]
>
> **8** (continued) — Bdes. and Batteries notified to collect Instruments and supply of wire.
>
> **9** — Fifty miles of wire collected from Cdn. Corps Cable Dump and Issued to Bdes. Bdes. moved to Crespin Area. [struck through in original: "H.Q."] H.Q. held in readiness for move.
>
> **10** — H.Q. moved to Thulin, closed office in Valenciennes at 10.00 a.m. and opened in Thulin at same hour. Order to go into action cancelled. Line run into 14th Bde. also 2nd C.D.A. for Corps. Communication to 13th Bde., by wire.
>
> **11** — Received order that the D.A. would be in rest.
>
> **12** — All three sections brought up to establishment in personnel and equipment.
>
> **13** — All Instrument requiring repair brought into Division H.Q. for same
>
> **14** — Order received as to disposition of units of H.Q. in the march to Germany. 13th Bde., to come under 2nd C.D.A.,

### Page 6

> [Place: Thulin, then Jemappes]
>
> **14** (continued) — 13th Bde. to come under 1st C.D.A. with their respective sections. D.A.C. remained at H.Q. to march with Can. Corps. Troops.
>
> **15** — All wire and stores surplus to establishment turned in. T.M. Bde broken down, demobilized [uncertain] and Signal Equipment on loan, to them returned.
>
> **16** — 13th Bde. coming under 2nd C.D.A. moved to Mons area at 18.00 hours. 311 Bde R.F.A. came under 5th C.D.A. at same hour.
>
> **17** — Received order for D.A. to move to Mons, arrangements completed but order cancelled with instructions to stand fast. 14th Bde came under 1st C.D.A. and started to move off at 18.00 hours.
>
> **18** — Received order to move, with Cdn. Corps troops.
>
> **19** — Preparations made for coming move, H.Q. section held muster parade and equipment completed.
>
> **20** — Marches to Jemappes. Signal Office installed and line run to 2nd C.G.A. giving us Communication with Canadian Corps.
>
> **21** — Muster Parade and Route march
>
> **22** — Muster Parade at 9. a.m., Cleaning Wagon from 2 p.m.

>

>
> **25** — Received Warning order of intended move.
>
> **26** — Preparations completed for coming move
>
> **27** — Moves to Marlanwelz [uncertain — possibly Morlanwelz]
>
> **28** — Marched to Velaine (near Namur)
>
> **29** — Marches to Jambes
>
> **30** — Day spent in cleaning wagons and equipment.
