# War Diary — 5th Canadian Divisional Signal Company (Artillery Detachment) — January 1918

- **Unit:** 5th Canadian Divisional Signal Company (Artillery Detachment)
- **Month:** January 1918 (Volume 5)
- **Source:** Library and Archives Canada, Collection Search Item ID 2004909 (RG 9 III-D-3, vol. 5006, microfilm reel T-10854, files 694–695; finding aid 9-52)
- **Pages:** January 1918, p. 1 – p. 6 (image files page-0026 – page-0031 of the 132-page diary)
- **January 1918 — COMPLETE** (pages 26–31 of 132): cover, contents, diary text 1–31 January, including a monthly signal-traffic table, no appendices. Transcribed 2026-10-26.

## Transcription conventions
Original spelling, punctuation and abbreviations kept. Handwritten diary text throughout (Army Form C. 2118, "WAR DIARY or INTELLIGENCE SUMMARY"), in a different hand from August–December 1917. `[uncertain]` marks words I could not read with confidence — never guessed. No map coordinates are given in the diary except the map references noted as written (e.g. "M.11 C 05.45", "M.28 to 30.15"). "Mackerel" is a code name used in the diary for a unit/line — transcribed as written. "H.Q." = Headquarters; "Bdes." = Brigades (the 13th and 14th Brigades, C.F.A.); "C.F.A." = Canadian Field Artillery; "C.D.A." = Canadian Divisional Artillery; "Divarty" = Divisional Artillery; "D.A." = Divisional Artillery; "D.T.M.O." = Divisional Train [uncertain — as written]; "T.M." = Trench Mortar; "T.M.O." = Trench Mortar Officer; "O.P." = Observation Post; "Bty." = Battery; "D.R.L.S." = Despatch Rider Letter Service. The repeated handwritten mark in the Hour column, read as "M.L.M." [uncertain] — apparently the diarist's initials — appears against most entries and is noted here rather than repeated against every entry.

## Pages 3–6 — Diary text (1–31 January 1918)

Army Form C. 2118, "WAR DIARY or INTELLIGENCE SUMMARY".

### Page 3 — 1–9 January 1918

> [Sheet heading: "5th Cdn. Divnl Sig [uncertain] (Artillery Detachments)"]
>
> **Sains-en-Gohelle, Jan. 1** — Bright & cold - situation normal.
>
> **2** — Dull & mild - cutting only 2 pr. [uncertain] on 5th Bde. from T.A. to Sains-en-Gohelle for Bdes. - Cut over D.T.M.O. lines for their guns & exchange.
>
> **3** — Fine - 5th C.D.A. H.Q. came in to action at 12 noon and took over our own Brigades.
>
> **4** — Clear and fine - H.Q.'s section working on a system of Communication for 5th C.D.A. T.M's. - line put through to King's Dump.
>
> **5** — Fine and mild - T.M. Communication completed - exchange put in at M.11 C 05.45 [uncertain] with lines to all guns and O.P. - System working well.
>
> **6** — Clear & cold with a light, cold wind - situation normal.
>
> **7** — Fine - two more lines run in for 5th T.M. thus giving them Communication with every gun from their Control station.
>
> **8** — Bright with snow fluries - situation normal.
>
> **9** — Bright with light wind accompanied by snow fluries in the after noon - unable to carry out scheme of Visual Communications from Div. H.Q. to 13th Bde. to 13th [entry ends here — the last word is cut off at the foot of the page]

### Page 4 — 10–18 January 1918

> [Sheet heading: none written; the printed "(Erase heading not required.)" is left in place]
>
> **Sains-en-Gohelle, Jan. 10** — Mild - considerable trouble on (Mackerel) 14th Bde fullerphone lines.
>
> **Jan. 11** — Mild with occasional showers trouble on 14th Bde fullerphone line cleared up.
>
> **12** — Mild and cloudy - a very successful scheme of visual signalling from the batteries of the 14th Bde. from Bde. to Div. was carried out. the men of the 14th Bde. C.F.A. being used. Lt. A. H. Cox [a word before "Cox" is struck through in the original] & 3 other ranks reported to 7th C. T. B. for burying Cable on Hill 65.
>
> **13.** — Dull & mild - situation normal.
>
> **14** — Snow - Fullerphone line to 3rd C.D.A.
>
> **15.** — Heavy mist with rain & wind at night - visual scheme from batteries of 13th Bde. from Bde. to Div. H.Q. carried out with success. This terminates the 1st and last of the 3 day refresher course in Lucas Lamp. One man from each Bty. & Bde. attending thus each unit has seven men with a good knowledge of Lamp Signalling
>
> **16** — Drizzly rain - party of 16 men from Batteries reported to Div. H.Q. to Bury Cable & anti-bombing activity.
>
> **17** — Dull and rainy - situation quiet.
>
> **18** — Cloudy with some rain - Lt. A. H. Cox & 3 other ranks reported back from Cable work - Bury not finished owing to the impossibility of getting working parties

### Page 5 — 19–28 January 1918

> [Sheet heading: "5th Cdn. Divnl. Sig. [uncertain] Detachments."]
>
> **Sains-en-Gohelle, Jan. 19** — Fine - Cable around Div. H.Q. to the pole routes buried & men returned to their Batteries. The cable is still to be tested & connected through.
>
> **20** — Fine & mild - situation quiet.
>
> **21** — Cloudy with occasional rain - at 10 A.M. 14th Bde. came under tactical control of 4th C.D.A. one Div. line turned over to 4th C.D.A. Signals. 13th Bde. hands over H.Q. at M.28 to 30.15 to 4th C.D.A. and establishes new H.Q. at M.27 to 40.35.
>
> **22** — Fair - at 12 noon 14th Bde. came under 5th C.D.A. & Div. line is taken over.
>
> **23** — Mild and fair - situation quiet.
>
> **24** — Fine and mild - 5th Divarty came under 1st C.D.A.
>
> **25** — Mild - situation quiet.
>
> **26** — Fair and mild - all work for anti-bombing preparation was finished up.
>
> **27** — Fine and mild - visual scheme from O.P.s to Batteries to Bdes. to Div. H.Q. worked out.
>
> **28** — Bright & mild - situation quiet.

## Open questions / uncertain items (January 1918)

- 5 January: exchange put in at "M.11 C 05.45" — map reference as read, [uncertain].
- 9 January: the entry is cut off at the foot of the page ("...from Div. H.Q. to 13th Bde. to 13th") — the sentence does not continue on the next page.
- 12 January: "Lt. A. H. Cox & 3 other ranks reported to 7th C. T. B. for burying Cable on Hill 65." — a word before "Cox" is struck through in the original; "7th C. T. B." as written.
- 14 January: the entry reads only "Snow - Fullerphone line to 3rd C.D.A." — short as written; it may be incomplete in the original.
- 15 January: "This terminates the 1st and last of the 3 day refresher course in Lucas Lamp." — as written.
- 16 January: "party of 16 men from Batteries reported to Div. H.Q. to Bury Cable & anti-bombing activity." — "anti-bombing activity" as read.
- 18 January: the entry ends "Bury not finished owing to the impossibility of getting working parties" — as written.
- January totals: the 13th and 14th Bde. phone-call figures are both 6,940 — transcribed as read.
- Names read as: Lt. A. H. Cox; Capt. M. L. Maitland (O.C., signatory).
- No casualties are recorded in January 1918.
