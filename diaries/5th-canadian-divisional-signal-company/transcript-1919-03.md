## Transcription conventions
Original spelling, punctuation and abbreviations kept. Typed diary text. `[uncertain]` marks words I could not read with confidence — never guessed. Struck-through original text is shown as `[struck through in original: "..."]`. The Hour column on each diary page carries a repeated check-like mark rather than times. No map coordinates are given in the diary; places are recorded as written. Unlike earlier months, March entries are grouped into date ranges rather than written day by day. "C.F.A." = Canadian Field Artillery; "C.D.A." = Canadian Divisional Artillery; "H.Q." = Headquarters; "Bde" = Brigade; "S.O.S." = struck off strength; "C.D.3" = Dispersal Certificate form.

## Pages 3–4 — Diary text (March 1919, pp. 3–4)

Army Form C. 2118, "WAR DIARY or INTELLIGENCE SUMMARY" — the "INTELLIGENCE SUMMARY" heading is struck through. Header: "5th Canadian Divisional Signal Co — Artillery Detachments".

### Page 3

> [Place: Orp-le-Petit, Belgium. March]
>
> **1st to 4th** — Routine work, carried out. Routine work consists, as in February, of care of horses including exercising same, maintaining of communication between units of the 5 Canadian Divisional Artillery and Canadian Corps Hq, and a sports program and educational work.
>
> **4** — Court of inquiry held at Jauche to-day for the purpose of inquiring into and reporting on the loss of a horse and saddlery on the night of Feb 27/28 from "B" section (attached Hq 14th Bde C.F.A. FINDING:—"Stolen by person or persons unknown and that owing to the scattered disposition of stables and the shortage of men that it was impossible to picquet each stable, and that the horse and saddlery should be replaced at the public expense".
>
> **5 to 15** — Routine work carried out. Matters have remained more or less at a standstill and no further steps towards demobilization were taken. Educational books were obtainable at 5th Canadian Divisional Artillery Headqrs and a great number of the personnel of this unit have taken advantage of this opportunity. The weather has been very favorable for the Sports Program and a number of inter-section meets have taken place, [struck through in original: "mostly"] Baths Have been established at Jauche,
>
> **15** — In accordance with the program of turning in horses four ID were turned in and S.O.S. by A section (attached to 13th Bde C.F.A.) with horses from 13th Bde C.F.A.
>
> **15 to 19** — Routine work carried out.
>
> **19** — Three riding 1 Light Draft horse and two mules from B section (attached 14th Bde C.F.A. were turned over with horses from that bde to Belgian Government and S.O.S.
>
> **20** — Routine work carried out.
>
> **21** — Routine work carried out.

### Page 4

> [Place: Orp-le-Petit Belgium. March. Header: "5th Canadian Divisional, Signals Signal Co — Artillery Detachments" (as typed in the original)]
>
> **22** — The Canadian Corps Athletic Championships took place to-day at Brussels, a special train was run for the benefit of the personnel of the 5th C.D.A. and a number of the men in this unit took advantage of it.
>
> **23 to 27** — Routine work carried out, owing to the weather sports program was a little delayed as several meets had to be cancelled on account of rain. Progress was made in connection with preparation for turning in equipment, harness etc and reports rendered as to the condition of clothing.
>
> **28** — Received a supply of Dispersal Certificates (Form C.D.3) from Hq 5th C.D.A. and these forms for H.Q. section completed.
>
> **29 and 30** — Routine work carried out.
>
> **31** — Forms C.D.3 for A and B sections filled out.
>
> [Signed] *J.S. McPherson Captain C.E. O i/c 5th Canadian Divisional Signal Co Artillery Detachments.*

## Open questions / uncertain items (March)
- The date column on page 3 groups entries by range; the "15" entry's date appears overwritten in the original (a struck entry above it). Entries are transcribed with the dates as they appear.
- 4 Mar: court of inquiry at Jauche into the loss of a horse and saddlery from "B" section on the night of 27/28 February; finding: stolen by person or persons unknown, horse and saddlery to be replaced at public expense. No personnel casualties.
- 15 Mar: "four ID" as typed in the original — the meaning of "ID" here is not explained in the diary.
- 19 Mar: "Three riding 1 Light Draft horse and two mules" as typed (sic).
- No casualties are reported in March 1919.
