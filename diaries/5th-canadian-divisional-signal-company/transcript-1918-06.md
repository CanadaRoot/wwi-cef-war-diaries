# War Diary — 5th Canadian Divisional Signal Company (Artillery Detachment) — June 1918

- **Unit:** 5th Canadian Divisional Signal Company (Artillery Detachment)
- **Month:** June 1918 (Volume 10)
- **Source:** Library and Archives Canada, Collection Search Item ID 2004909 (RG 9 III-D-3, vol. 5006, microfilm reel T-10854, files 694–695; finding aid 9-52)
- **Pages:** June 1918, p. 1 – p. 7 (image files page-0060 – page-0066 of the 132-page diary)
- **June 1918 — COMPLETE** (pages 60–66 of 132): cover, contents, diary text 1–30 June, monthly business summary. Transcribed 2026-10-26.

## Transcription conventions
Original spelling, punctuation and abbreviations kept. Handwritten diary text throughout. `[uncertain]` marks words I could not read with confidence — never guessed. The "Hour" column is struck through on every diary page. The heading "INTELLIGENCE SUMMARY" is struck through on the diary pages. No map coordinates are given in the diary; places are recorded as written. "C.D.A." = Canadian Divisional Artillery; "C.F.A." = Canadian Field Artillery; "D.A." = Divisional Artillery; "D.T.M." = Divisional Trench Mortars; "D.R." = despatch rider; "O.P." = observation post; "O.K." = all correct; "dis" = disconnected; "Spr" = Sapper; "N.C.O." = non-commissioned officer; "O.R." = other ranks.

## Pages 3–7 — Diary text (June 1918, pp. 3–7)

Army Form C. 2118, "WAR DIARY or INTELLIGENCE SUMMARY". Place: Maroeuil unless otherwise noted.

### Page 3

> **1** — Fine and warm, Line to Div, Train out at 1.00 P.M., trouble found at Ecoivres, repaired at 2.00 P.M.
>
> **2** — Fine and warm, Divisional Train line out again, repaired shortly after.
>
> **3** — Fine and warm, Line to 13th Brigade out at 7.40 A.M. ok at 8.00 P.M.
>
> **4** — Cloudy, clearing in afternoon, H.Q. moving to new location, Junction of Arras, St Paul road and Scarpe river. Parties wiring up new H.Q. Line to Div, Train out at 2.45 P.M. ok at 4.10 P.M.
>
> **5** — Fine and warm, advanced party completed wiring of new position.
>
> **6** — Place: Etrun. Fine and warm. Closed office at Maroeuil at 3.00 P.M. and opened office at new location at same time. From 8 A.M. [struck through in original: "to"] till 8 A.M. 7th inst all Telephone and Telegraph communication superceded by Visual communication.

### Page 4

> **6** (continued) — as far as the Tactical situation would allow. Lieut Featherstonhaugh returned from leave.
>
> **7** — Fair and warm, Lieut Cox H.K. reported back to H.Q. from 14th Bde C.F.A. Both Fullerphone [inserted above the line: "lines"] to Bde section dis. at 8.00 A.M., linemen worked on them until 1.30 P.M. when same were reported O.K.
>
> **8** — Fair and warm, Line to 14th Bde C.F.A. dis at 11.10 A.M. linemen found cable cut and 400 yds taken out. ok at 3.30 P.M.
>
> **9** — Fair and warm, An O.P. exchange established at T.C. test box (H.2 A 90.20) Personnel, 1 N.C.O. and 1 O.R. supplied by 13th Bde, and 2 O.R.'s from 14th Bde. The equipment from the H.Q. [sentence ends here in the original].
>
> **10** — Cloudy, clearing in afternoon, Line to 5 C.D.A.C. dis, at 6. P.M. reported to 2nd C.D.A. signals, not repaired till 8.40 A.M. the [uncertain] Orderly was despatched at 6. A.M. returned at 7.25 A.M.
>
> **11** — Fine and warm with light wind, situation quiet.

### Page 5

> **12** — Fair and mild, situation quiet.
>
> **13** — Fine and warm, situation quiet. No 66029 Spr Weir R.E. taken on strength from C.C. Bde.
>
> **14** — Fine and warm, Div, Train line out at 9.35 A.M. linemen out, repaired line ok at 10.20 A.M. Special D.R. to 45th Infantry Bde, at 2.20 P.M.
>
> **15** — Cloudy but fair, Line to report centre dis at 9.35 A.M. ok at 10.40 A.M. 11.45 P.M. Special D.R. to 13th and 14th Bde C.F.A., 5th C.D.T.M. Bde and 45th Infantry Bde,
>
> **16** — Fine and warm, with light showers, Line to Div, Train dis at 4.29 P.M. ok again at 5.45 P.M. Line to report centre out at 11.10 P.M. ok at 2.12 P.M.
>
> **17** — Fine and warm, situation quiet.
>
> **18** — [struck through in original: "Fine"] warm, light showers, clearing in afternoon.
>
> **19** — Fair and warm, an air charge put in at Infantry Brigade, for Brigade H.Q. direct line to 13th and 14th Bde C.F.A. is [uncertain] H.Q., to Infantry brigade, to this H.Q. cut through on this exchange for an intended raid on the [uncertain] 19th [uncertain] 20th inst.

### Page 6

> **20** — Fine at first followed by heavy mist and rain in afternoon. Raid of the night of 19/20 postponed and successfully [uncertain] on the night of 20/21. communications Splendid.
>
> **21** — Fine and warm, situation quiet.
>
> **22** — Cloudy with high wind, work commenced on small air route between this H.Q. and 15th D.A. H.Q.
>
> **23** — Cloudy and cool, situation quiet.
>
> **24** — Clear and cool, work on small air route between this H.Q. and 15th D.A. H.Q. completed.
>
> **25** — Clear and cool, four new pairs picked up for Brigade and report Centre from M.E. test point to L.R.7. at F28 D60.20.
>
> **26** — Fine and warm, Break in Fullerphone to 15 D.A. reported at 11.45 A.M. cleared at 12 noon. Line to 13th Bde, C.F.A.
>
> **27** — Fine and warm, Line to 14 Bde C.F.A. cut. Brigade linemen repaired cable and line working at 6. A.M. River route abandoned, all lines cut over on new Buried cable. Div Train Line reported out at 5.15 P.M. ok again at 5.30 P.M.
>
> **28** — Fine and warm, Line to Report centre open at 10 A.M. repairs at 11.15 A.M. 10.05 P.M. time synchronised.
