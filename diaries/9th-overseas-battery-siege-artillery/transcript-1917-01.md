## Page 27 (January 1917, p. 27) — other: Special Remarks letter — week ending 6-1-17


[M.F.B. 239. stamp, top right]
[Small printed box, top centre: "In any further correspondence on this subject please quote Number and Date of this Communication."]
No. ....................

Partridge Island, 6th January 1917

From The C. O. #9 O/S Siege Battery,
St. John, N. B.

To The G. O. C., N. B. Troops,
St. John, N. B.

[Stamp, right: HEADQUARTERS OFFICE / NEW BRUNSWICK TROOPS / ST. JOHN, N. B. / JAN 7 1917 / No N.B. 5-3-2]

Training
Special Remarks
Weekly Diary, 6-1-17.

Sir:-

With reference to the marginally noted subject, I have the honour to report as follows:

Particular attention has been paid during the past week to outside work. This is necessary owing to the number of men now in the Battery, and the fact that lectures have taken up the larger part of the time since November 1st.

A route march was taken on Friday, 5th January, and this will be done each week, or more often if possible.

It is expected that some way will be found to give some time to physical training, as suggested by the Inspecting Officer.

I have the honour to be,
Sir,
Your obedient servant,
[signature]
Major.,
C.O. #9 O/S Siege Battery C.E.F.

SPECIAL REMARKS
Weekly Diary

General. The mild weather during the first part of the week permitted the carrying on of outside work such as Recruits Drill and a Route March. With these instructions improvement is already noticed in the marching and discipline of the men,

Physical Training.
Efforts have been made to get Physical Training Manuals, but without success. If these are obtainable we would be able to carry on Physical Training each day as suggested by the Inspecting Officer.

[signature]
Major
C.O. No. 9 Overseas Siege Battery

Special remarks
WEEKLY DIARY

GENERAL,-
During the past week new Signalling and Layers classes have been started to develop more men for these Branches.

Squad drill and Recruits Drill have been carried on when possible and the men are showing up better in this work.

No classes have yet been carried on in Physical Training.

SPECIAL REMARKS
Weekly Diary

Layers and Signallers.-
Classes for Layers and Signallers have been carried on continuously all week. Lack of space has hindered squad and Company Drill but a short Route March was held on Wednesday in the City.

Physical Training.
The return of our Physical Training Class from Halifax, N.S. soon will enable work in this branch to be carried on.

[signature]
Major
C.O. No. 9 Overseas Siege Battery.
