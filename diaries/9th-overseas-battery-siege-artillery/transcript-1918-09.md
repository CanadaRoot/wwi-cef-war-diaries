# 9th Overseas Battery Siege Artillery — Transcript, September 1918

**Unit:** 9th Overseas Battery, Siege Artillery (MD No. 7: 9th Siege Battery)
**LAC Item ID:** 2005388
**Reference:** RG 9 III-D-3, vol. 5064, file 985, microfilm reel T-11127
**Month:** September 1918
**Manifest pages covered:** 352–365 (diary pp. 136–149)
**Diary date range:** 2–28 September 1918 (weeks ending 7, 14, 21 and 28 September 1918)
**Location:** Battery Point Camp, then Partridge Island (from 11 September), then the Armouries, St. John, N.B. (from 20 September)
**Commanding officer / signatory:** Major, O.C. #9 o/s Siege Battery, C.E.F. through the week ending 21 September; a Captain, then a Lieutenant, signs "for Major" from the week ending 28 September (names [uncertain]; no reason for the change is stated)

**Notes:** The typed year on the syllabus for the week ending 7 September reads "1916" [uncertain]; the day-dates fit September 1918. A special Coast Defence Course on the 4.7" Q.F. gun ran 16–21 September at Partridge Island.

**Filenames note:** the image filenames label every page "January_1918" — this is wrong. Dates below are those printed on the pages themselves. Each weekly sheet is filed in the month of its week-ending date; covering letters are filed with the week they forward. Illegible or uncertain readings are marked [uncertain]; nothing has been added from outside the pages.

## Page 365 (September 1918, p. 149) — covering letter

**Type:** Covering letter with Special Remarks. **Date:** Armouries, St. John, N.B., September 28th, 1918. **To:** The G.S.O. M.D. No. 7, St. John, N.B. **Subject:** Weekly Diary & Proposed Syllabus — Special Remarks — 9th o/s Siege Battery, C.E.F. **Signatory:** [uncertain — same Lieutenant], Lieut for Major, C.O. 9th o/s Siege Battery, C.E.F. **Stamp:** Military District No. 7, St. John, N.B., OCT 1 1918, M.D 7 S-3-2.

> Beg to forward herewith in triplicate, please, Diary of Training for the past week also the proposed syllabus for the coming week.
>
> During the past week, a working party consisting of forty-four men has been sent to Partridge Island where they have been engaged in constructing ammunition pits and gun emplacement. The guard which No. 9 Siege Battery has been furnishing at West St. John was relieved at 12 o'clock Friday noon. During the past week, eleven recruits have been taken on the strength and they are being trained in squad and arm drill.
