# 9th Overseas Battery Siege Artillery — Transcript, May 1918

**Unit:** 9th Overseas Battery, Siege Artillery (MD No. 7: 9th Siege Battery)
**LAC Item ID:** 2005388
**Reference:** RG 9 III-D-3, vol. 5064, file 985, microfilm reel T-11127
**Month:** May 1918
**Manifest pages covered:** 283–297 (diary pp. 67–81)
**Diary date range:** 29 April–27 May 1918 (weeks ending 4, 11, 18 and 25 May 1918)
**Location:** West St. John, N.B.; Camp Red Head from 16 May 1918
**Commanding officer / signatory:** Major, O.C. #9 o/s Siege Battery, C.E.F. (script signature; name [uncertain])

**Notes:** The week ending 4 May straddles April/May and is filed here by its week-ending date. Includes an undated HQ file cover sheet for the War Diaries (Weekly) found between the 25 May and 1 June letters, and a 27 May letter on machine-gun instruction. On Thursday 16 May the battery moved into camp at Red Head Fort.

**Filenames note:** the image filenames label every page "January_1918" — this is wrong. Dates below are those printed on the pages themselves. Each weekly sheet is filed in the month of its week-ending date; covering letters are filed with the week they forward. Illegible or uncertain readings are marked [uncertain]; nothing has been added from outside the pages.
