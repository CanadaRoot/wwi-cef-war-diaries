**Reference:** RG 9 III-D-3, vol. 5064, file 985; microfilm reel T-11127
**Month:** November 1917
**Manifest pages covered:** 191–205
**Diary page numbers covered:** 82, 86, 88, 89, 91, 92, 93, 99, 100, 101, 102, 108, 109; diary p. numbers on pp. 197 and 203 partly obscured [uncertain]

**Notes:** Manifest filenames label these pages "November 1916"; the dates on the pages themselves are November 1917. Page 206 (week ending 1 December 1917) straddles November/December and is filed under December 1917.

Transcribed faithfully from the LAC page images. Original spelling and abbreviations are preserved. Anything illegible or uncertain is marked [uncertain]; nothing has been guessed. No information from outside the pages has been added.

Partridge Island, 17th November 1917.

To:—The A.A.G., M.D.No. 7,
St. John, N.B.

Special Remarks
Weekly Diary of Training.

[Stamps: M.D. NO. 7 / NOV 19 1917 / 5-3-2; and No. 9 O.S. Siege Battery / C.E.F. / NOV 17 1917 / ORDERLY ROOM]

Reference the marginally noted, I beg to report that No. 9 Siege Battery was paraded for instructional purposes during the calibration of the two 4.7" Guns on Partridge Island and the tests of mountings of fixed guns, for both discipline and instruction. ## Page 205 (diary p. 109) — Special remarks (cover letter) — Date: 1917-11-24 — Month: 1917-11

Partridge Island, 24th November '17.

To:—The G.S.O., M.D. No. 7,
St. John, N.B.

Special Remarks
Weekly Diary of Training.

[Stamps: M.D. NO. 7 / NOV 26 1917 / 5-3-2; and No. 9 O.S. Siege Battery / C.E.F. / NOV 24 1917 / ORDERLY ROOM]

Reference the marginally noted, I beg to report that fifteen minutes were spent daily in saluting instruction, along with other subjects, and good improvement was made in all subjects. During the coming week it is intended, when quarantine is lifted and instructors mentioned in Headquarters' letter arrive, to devote attention to Physical Training and Guard Mounting.

[Handwritten at lower left: "Unit remarks." — reading uncertain] [uncertain]

[Signature illegible], Major,
C.O. #9 o/s Siege Battery.
