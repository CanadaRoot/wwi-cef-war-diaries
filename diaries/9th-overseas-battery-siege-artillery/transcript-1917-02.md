## Page 39 (February 1917, p. 39) — other: Special Remarks letter — week ending February 3rd 1917


[Stamp, top right: circular "No. 9 O.S. Siege Battery C.E.F. / FEB 3 1917 / ORDERLY ROOM" with handwritten "S.B. 2-1-2" across it]

Partridge Island February 3rd 1917

To,- The G.O.C. N.B. Troops,
St. John, N.B.

[Stamp, right: HEADQUARTERS OFFICE / NEW BRUNSWICK TROOPS / ST. JOHN, N. B. / FEB 5 1917 / No N.B. 5-3-2]

SPECIAL REMARKS
Weekly Diary.

Signalling and Laying.-
Classes in Signalling and Laying have been continued during the past week for specialists. The new men are making progress and the qualified men are carrying on with the work they have had previously.

Physical Training.-
Classes in Physical Training started Wednesday and have been carried on daily since. The men are interested in the work and the several instructors show a thorough grasp of the work in hand.

[signature]
Major
C.O. No. 9 Overseas Siege Batt'y

## Page 41 (February 1917, p. 41) — other: Special Remarks letter — week ending 10-2-17


[M.F.B. 239. stamp, top right: "M.F.B. 239. / 500m.—12-16. / H.Q. 1772-39-194."]
[Small printed box, top centre: "In any further correspondence on this subject please quote Number and Date of this Communication."]
No. ....................

Partridge Island, 10th February 1917

From The C.O. #9 O/S Siege Battery,
St. John, N.B.

To The G.O.C., N.B. Troops,
St. John, N.B.

Special Remarks
Weekly Diary, 10-2-17

Sir:-

Regarding the marginally noted, I have the honour to state:-

The course of physical training is being carried on with a marked improvement in the bearing and smartness of the men.

Men absent on [last — handwritten insertion above "leave"] leave has hindered the progress of the several specialist classes but general practice has been carried on.

I have the honour to be,
Sir,
Your obedient servant,
[signature]
Major.,
C.O. #9 O/S Siege Battery.

## Page 42 (February 1917, p. 42) — other: Special Remarks letter — week ending February 17th 1917


[Diary's own page number circled at foot: 1 — as written; appears to be a filing number rather than the diary's sequence]

Partridge Island February 17th 1917

To,-
The G.O.C. N.B. Troops,
St. John, N.B.

[Stamp, right: HEADQUARTERS OFFICE / NEW BRUNSWICK TROOPS / ST. JOHN, N. B. / FEB 20 1917 / No N.B. 5-9-2]
[Stamp, right: circular "No. 9 O.S. Siege Battery C.E.F. / FEB 17 1917 / ORDERLY ROOM" with handwritten "S.B. 2-1-2" across it]

SPECIAL REMARKS
Weekly Diary

Physical training, Squad Drill and Company drill have occupied most of the time during the past week. The men are showing a marked improvement in their Physical Training work.

Owing to the lack of Field Telephones and wire indented for on January 31st 1917 and not yet received, practical signalling work is not progressing as would be desired.

[signature]
Major
C.O. No. 9 Overseas Siege Batt'y

SPECIAL REMARKS
Weekly Diary

Medical Examinations took up considerable time during the past week, confining the drill to Physical Training, which is carried on daily, and a few classes for the signallers and layers.

Work during the next week will be related to the working of the 4.7 Field Guns and necessary lectures in Connection [handwritten insertion above the struck-through word "Ammunition"] with the same.

[Signature — reads "Harry S. Bennett", Capt. [uncertain]] Major
C.O. No. 9 Overseas Siege Batt'y
