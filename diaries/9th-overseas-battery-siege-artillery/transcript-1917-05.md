# War Diary Transcript — May 1917

**Unit:** No. 9 Overseas Battery Siege Artillery (MD No. 7: 9th Siege Battery), C.E.F.
**LAC Item ID:** 2005388
**Reference:** RG 9 III-D-3, vol. 5064, file 985, microfilm reel T-11127
**Month:** May 1917
**Pages covered:** manifest pages 71–97 (diary pp. 71–97); pp. 93–97 transcribed in the batch covering pages 93–184
**Diary date range:** 30 April – 26 May 1917
**Commanding officer / signatory:** Maj. P. W. Wetmore, C.O. (one programme sheet, p. 80, is signed "D.O." instead of "C.O.")
**Location:** Partridge Island, St. John, N.B., throughout (no move appears in these pages)

Transcribed faithfully from the LAC page images; original spelling, abbreviations and punctuation preserved. Anything illegible or uncertain is marked [uncertain] — nothing has been guessed. Note: the image filenames label these pages "November 1916", but the diary's own dates run from November 1916 to May 1917; pages are filed here by the diary's own dates, each weekly sheet under the month of its week-ending date.

**Month straddle:** The week ending 5 May 1917 begins Monday 30 April 1917, so its first day falls in April; the week is filed here under its week-ending date.

Special Remarks
Weekly Diary of Training.

Sir:—

Regarding the marginally noted subject I have the honour to state that training during the past week has practically followed the proposed syllabus. Route march planned for Wednesday was carried out on Friday on account of weather conditions.

I have the honour to be,
Sir,
Your obedient servant,
