# 9th Overseas Battery Siege Artillery — Transcript, July 1918

**Unit:** 9th Overseas Battery, Siege Artillery (MD No. 7: 9th Siege Battery)
**LAC Item ID:** 2005388
**Reference:** RG 9 III-D-3, vol. 5064, file 985, microfilm reel T-11127
**Month:** July 1918
**Manifest pages covered:** 313–330 (diary pp. 97–114)
**Diary date range:** 1–29 July 1918 (weeks ending 6, 13, 20 and 27 July 1918)
**Location:** Battery Point Camp, St. John, N.B.
**Commanding officer / signatory:** Major, O.C. #9 o/s Siege Battery, C.E.F.; a Lieutenant signs for the Major on some sheets (name [uncertain])

**Notes:** Monday 1 July was "Nil" on both syllabus and diary. The covering letter for the week ending 6 July also refers to a weekly training diary for the week ending 10 July, but no sheet headed week ending 10 July appears in these pages. From the week ending 13 July the training shifts to "Training 1st Class" (Elementary Gunnery, Section Gun drill) with a separate Signalling stream.

**Filenames note:** the image filenames label every page "January_1918" — this is wrong. Dates below are those printed on the pages themselves. Each weekly sheet is filed in the month of its week-ending date; covering letters are filed with the week they forward. Illegible or uncertain readings are marked [uncertain]; nothing has been added from outside the pages.

A signalling Inspection was begun last Friday, but not completed owing to the exceedingly foggy weather on Saturday. However this inspection will be completed this week.

The two forty pounder Armstrong Guns were brought here on Thursday last and are being thoroughly overhauled. As soon as this work is completed, gun classes will be formed for instruction.

The men warned for the draft have all returned to camp and all details for their departure is arranged. Numerous applications for enlistment are being received and recruits are being taken on steadily.
