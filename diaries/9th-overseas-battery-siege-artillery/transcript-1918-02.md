## Page 232 (diary p. not visible) — Special remarks — Date: 1918-02-02 — Month: 1918-02

[Handwritten "#5" at top right. Stamp, top right: MILITARY DISTRICT NO. 7, ST. JOHN, N. B., FEB 2 1918, M. D. 5-3-2. No handwritten circled p. number visible on this page.]

West St. John, N.B. 2nd February 1918.

To:—The G.S.O., M.D. No. 7,
St. John, N. B.

Special Remarks
Weekly Diary of Training.

Reference the marginally noted subject, I beg to state that a guard of 48 N.C.O.'s and Men and one Officer was employed at West St. John continuously during the past week. The remainder of the Battery showed great improvement from the instruction given them, outlined in diary. As we have no physical training instructor detailed for duty here, it will be impossible to carrye [uncertain] on this branch of training during the coming week. The men will be given elementary drill and lectures, as up to the present practically their whole training consisted of guard duty.

[Signed] F. C. Walker
Lieut.
C.O. #9 o/s Siege Battery C.E.F.

West St. John, N.B. 9th February 1918.

To:—The G.S.O., M.D. No. 7,
St. John, N.B.

[Stamp, top right: MILITARY DISTRICT No. 7 / ST. JOHN, N.B. / FEB 11 1918 / M. D 7 5-3-2]

Special Remarks
Weekly Training.

Herewith a diary of the training followed by No. 9 Siege Battery during the past week, and a diary of the training proposed for the week following. It will be noted that the Battery at present consists almost entirely of recruits and is instructed as such.
During the past week the classes made very satisfactory progress, both in the drills — under the instruction of Q.M.S. Devinney (3rd Regt. C.G.A.) and in lectures given by Lieut. Walker.

F. C. Walker
Lieut.
O.C. #9 o/s Siege Battery C.E.F.

## Page 243 (diary p. 27; circled number not visible) — Special remarks / cover letter (Weekly Diary of Training, Syllabus proposed) — Date: 1918-02-24 — Month: 1918-02

[Top right: R-8. Stamp, top centre: MILITARY DISTRICT No. 7 / ST. JOHN, N.B. / FEB 25 1918 / M.D. 7 5-3-2]

West St John, NB., 24th. Feb. '18

To—The G.S.O., M.D. #7
St John, NB

Special remarks
Weekly Diary of Training. (Syllabus proposed)

Herewith copies of Diary of Training for week ending March 2nd, 1918.
It will be noted that thirty minutes is allowed for inspection by Officers. This includes the time taken to march to armoury and prepare for class.
Anent the period of one hour allowed for lecture: This includes the time taken to return from armouries to lecture room in Barracks, and vice versa.
No. 1 squad is the more advanced and is the largest.
No. 2 includes all recruits. Syllabus for this squad will remain practically as it is week after week, until unit is up to strength, men being advanced to No. [uncertain] squad as they progress.

[Stamp, lower left: No. 9 O. S. Siege Battery / C.E.F. / FEB 24 1918 / ORDERLY ROOM]

[Signature] Wetmore Major
OC #9 o/s Siege Battery, CEF.
