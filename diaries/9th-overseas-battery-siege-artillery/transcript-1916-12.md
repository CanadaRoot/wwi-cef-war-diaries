## Page 14 (December 1916, p. 14) — other (special remarks, weekly diary) — week ending December 2nd 1916


Partridge Island December 4th 1916

To,— The G O C Troops, N B
St. John, N.B.

[Top right stamp: HEADQUARTERS OFFICE NEW BRUNSWICK TROOPS ST. JOHN, N.B. — DEC 6 1916 — No N B. 5-3-3]

Special remarks
WEEKLY DIARY

Signalling, Signalling classes under Q M S Dean were completed on Thursday, November 30th 1916 The final test being held on that date in the Morse Code on the Buzzer, Disc and Flag. The report of the instructor contains the following sentence:
"The Officers, N C O's and men are very much interested in signalling and have made very satisfactory progress during the short period they have been under my instructions".

Siege Artillery, 15 men are attending Plotters and B.C.'s Assistants class under Lieut Anglin. He reports very good progress.
The remainder of the Battery about 60 men have taken 6" Howitzer Gun drill, Laying under C S M Whitfield, I.G. He is being assisted by the qualified Layers of the Battery and good progress is being made.
A Class for N C O's is being started under C S M Whitfield.

[Signature:] [uncertain] Major
C.O. No.. 9 Overseas Siege Battery.

SPECIAL REMARKS
Weekly Diary

Plotters and B.C.'s Assistants. This class has been taking advanced work and the Instructor reports very good progress. Weekly tests show considerable ability among the members and by the end of the month the class will have covered all the work prescribed for B.C.'s Assistants.

Gun Layers. Battery tactics and drill have been taken up this week and with the two directors recently received the work has progressed rapidly.

Signallers. Telephone work has occupied the time of this class during most of the week.

N.C.O.'s Class. One hour per day is taken up with the N.C.O.'s in the duties devolving upon them.

Training
Special Remarks
Week ending Dec. 30th '16.

Sir:-

Regarding the marginally noted subject, I have the honour to report as follows:

A general Christmas leave prevented special classes from being carried on early in the week. Infantry and gun drill was carried on during that time. This was needed especially after the two months of special lectures by the several instructors.

The men of the Draft began work constructing dugouts, which will be carried on as the weather permits.

I have the honour to be,
Sir,
Your obedient servant,
[signature]
Major.,
C.O. #9 O/S Siege Battery C.E.F.
