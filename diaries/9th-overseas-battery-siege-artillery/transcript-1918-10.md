## Page 366 (October 1918, p. 150) — covering letter

**Type:** Covering letter with Special Remarks. **Date:** Armouries, St. John, N.B., October 5th, 1918. **To:** The G.S.O. M.D. No. 7, St. John, N.B. **Subject:** Weekly Diary & Proposed Syllabus — Special Remarks — 9th o/s Siege Battery, C.E.F. **Signatory:** [uncertain — same Lieutenant], Lieut for Major [printed "Major" overwritten by signature], C.O. 9th o/s Siege Battery, C.E.F. **Stamp:** Military District No. 7, St. John, N.B., OCT 7 1918, M.D 7 S-3-2 (stamp heading partly faint [uncertain]).

> Beg to forward herewith in triplicate, please, Diary of Training for the past week also the Proposed Syllabus of Training for the coming week.
>
> During the past week a working party consisting of forty-five men has been sent to Partridge Island where they have been engaged in the construction of gun emplacements and ammunition pits. The recruits have been given squad drill and have made very satisfactory progress. Last Saturday morning, this unit was inspected by Brig-General MacDougall.
