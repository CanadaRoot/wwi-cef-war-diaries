# 9th Overseas Battery Siege Artillery — December 1917

**Unit:** 9th Overseas Battery Siege Artillery ("MD No. 7: 9th Siege Battery"), C.E.F., Military District No. 7
**LAC Item ID:** 2005388
**Reference:** RG 9 III-D-3, vol. 5064, file 985; microfilm reel T-11127
**Month:** December 1917
**Manifest pages covered:** 162, 206–216
**Diary page numbers covered:** 104, 110, 111, 117, 118, 119, 121, 123, 126, 127 [uncertain], 128, 129
**Diary date range:** 26 November–29 December 1917 (weeks ending 1, 8, 15, 22 and 29 December 1917)
**Commanding officer / signatory:** Major A. Wetmore to 22 December 1917; Capt. Harry S. Bennett from 22 December 1917 (Lt. E. S. Bennett signs "for Major" on the 8 December letter)

**Notes:** Manifest filenames label these pages "November 1916"; the dates on the pages themselves are December 1917. The week ending 1 December 1917 straddles November/December and is filed here by its week-ending date. On 10 December 1917 the whole battery moved from Partridge Island into the Exhibition Buildings, St. John; on 18 December 1917 a draft of 153 all ranks embarked. Manifest page 162 (diary p. 129, the 29 December 1917 guard-work letter) is filed out of chronological order in the LAC manifest, between the August and October 1917 sheets; it is transcribed here in its December 1917 place. It was restored by the parent run after a concurrent write by another transcription agent overwrote this file's earlier version.

Transcribed faithfully from the LAC page images. Original spelling and abbreviations are preserved. Anything illegible or uncertain is marked [uncertain]; nothing has been guessed. No information from outside the pages has been added.

## Page 207 (diary p. 111) — Special remarks (cover letter) — Date: 1917-12-01 — Month: 1917-12

Partridge Island, 1st December 1917.

To:—The G.S.O., M.D. No. 7,
St. John, N.B.

Special Remarks
Weekly Diary of Training.

[Stamps: M.D. NO. 7 / DEC 1 1917 / 5-3-2; and No. 9 O.S. Siege Battery / C.E.F. / DEC 1 1917 / ORDERLY ROOM]

Reference the marginally noted subject, I beg to report that the five instructors detailed for duty here by Headquarters M.D. No. 7 arrived on Monday and have conducted classes in Physical Training, Guard Mounting, Rifle Drill, Squad Drill, Musketry and Saluting. The results from this instruction are apparent.

[Signature illegible], Major,
C.O. #9 o/s Siege Battery.

## Page 213 (diary p. 123) — Special Remarks letter — Date: 1917-12-22 — Month: 1917-12

St. John, N.B. — 22nd December 1917.

To:—The G.S.O., M.D. No. 7,
St. John, N.B.

Special Remarks
Weekly Diary of Training.

[Stamp: "MILITARY DISTRICT NO. 7, ST. JOHN, N.B. — DEC 22 1917 — M.D. NO. 7 5-3-2."]

Reference the marginally noted subject, I beg to report that the work during the past week consisted of guard duty at West St. John and on the quarters here in the Exhibition Buildings. The draft of 153 all ranks embarked on the 18th, leaving barely sufficient men to carry on the work mentioned above. Seven recruits were secured during the latter part of the week and these have been given squad and rifle drill.

[Signature: Harry S. Bennett [uncertain]], Captain,
C.O. #9 o/s Siege Battery.
