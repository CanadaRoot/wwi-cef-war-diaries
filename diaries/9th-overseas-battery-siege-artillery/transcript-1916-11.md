## Page 4 (November 1916, p. 4) — other (weekly progress report letter) — week ending November 11th 1916


Partridge Island, Nov. 11th 1916

To,— The G.O.C. Troops, N.B.
St. John, N.B.

[Top right stamp: HEADQUARTERS OFFICE NEW BRUNSWICK TROOPS ST. JOHN, N.B. — NOV [uncertain] 1916 — No. N.B. 5-3-3]

Weekly progress report.

Signalling. The beginners and advanced signalling classes have been carried on each morning during the past week without interruption. The alphabet being given on the flags, buzzers and Disc. The Battery is in need of 50 flags to train the class in sending.

Infantry drill — Infantry drill was carried out under the section Officers on three days of the week. This is necessary owing to the number of new men in the Battery.

Specialists and Gun Drill.
Any additional time during the week was taken up in Gun drill including one afternoon of aiming tube practice.

[Signature:] [uncertain] Major
C.O. No. 9 Overseas Siege Battery

## Page 7 (November 1916, p. 7) — other (special remarks, weekly diary) — week ending November 18th 1916


[Top left stamp: HEADQUARTERS OFFICE NEW BRUNSWICK TROOPS ST. JOHN, N.B. — NOV [uncertain] 1916 — No. N.B. 5-3-3]

Partridge Island
November 18th 1916

The G.O.C. Troops, N.B.
St. John, N.B.

Special remarks
Weekly diary

Gunnery. Gun drill was not carried on extensively this week, owing to most of the time being taken up in signalling.

Signalling
Signalling was carried on through the week by S.M. Dean, C S C. [uncertain] to which most of the time was given by the Officers, N.C.O.'s and men of this Battery

Firing Practice
Owing to bad weather and other unfavorable conditions, no firing was undertaken during the week.

[Signature:] [uncertain] Major
C.O. No. 9 Overseas Siege Batt'y.

[Handwritten, bottom left:] Received
25/11/16

## Page 10 (November 1916, p. 10) — other (special remarks, weekly diary) — week ending November 25th 1916


Partridge Island November 25th 1916

To,— The G.O.C. Troops, N.B.
St. John, N.B.

[Top right stamp: HEADQUARTERS OFFICE NEW BRUNSWICK TROOPS ST. JOHN, N.B. — NOV 28 1916 — No. N.B. 5-3-3]

Special Remarks
Weekly diary.

Signalling — Signalling instruction under Q.M.S. Dean has been continued. The weekly test showing about 50 men who will be carried along as Battery signallers.

Infantry Drill — The short time devoted to foot Drill was taken up with the Revruits drill and a Route March of 7 miles on Wednesday Nov. 22nd 1916

Artillery Work.— On Friday Nov. 24th Siege Artillery classes were organized by Lieut W. A. I. Anglin R S A Halifax

[Signature:] [uncertain] Major
C.O. No. 9 Overseas Siege Battery.
