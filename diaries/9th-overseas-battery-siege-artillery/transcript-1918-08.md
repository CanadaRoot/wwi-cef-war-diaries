# 9th Overseas Battery Siege Artillery — Transcript, August 1918

**Unit:** 9th Overseas Battery, Siege Artillery (MD No. 7: 9th Siege Battery)
**LAC Item ID:** 2005388
**Reference:** RG 9 III-D-3, vol. 5064, file 985, microfilm reel T-11127
**Month:** August 1918
**Manifest pages covered:** 331–351 (diary pp. 115–135)
**Diary date range:** 29 July–5 September 1918 (weeks ending 3, 10, 17, 24 and 31 August 1918)
**Location:** Battery Point Camp, St. John, N.B.
**Commanding officer / signatory:** Major, C.O./O.C. #9 o/s Siege Battery, C.E.F.; a Lieutenant signs for the Major on some sheets (name [uncertain])

**Notes:** The week ending 3 August straddles July/August and is filed here by its week-ending date. The covering letter dated 5 September 1918 is filed here because it forwards the diaries for the week ending 31 August. The week ending 24 August is devoted almost entirely to Royal Guard of Honour training for the visit of H.R.H. Prince Arthur of Connaught.

**Filenames note:** the image filenames label every page "January_1918" — this is wrong. Dates below are those printed on the pages themselves. Each weekly sheet is filed in the month of its week-ending date; covering letters are filed with the week they forward. Illegible or uncertain readings are marked [uncertain]; nothing has been added from outside the pages.
