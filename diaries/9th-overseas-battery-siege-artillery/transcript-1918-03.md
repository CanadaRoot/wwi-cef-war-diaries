# 9th Overseas Battery Siege Artillery — March 1918

**Unit:** 9th Overseas Battery Siege Artillery ("MD No. 7: 9th Siege Battery"), C.E.F., Military District No. 7
**LAC Item ID:** 2005388
**Reference:** RG 9 III-D-3, vol. 5064, file 985; microfilm reel T-11127
**Month:** March 1918
**Manifest pages covered:** 244–262
**Diary page numbers covered:** 28–47 (part 2 numbering)
**Diary date range:** 25 February–30 March 1918 (weeks ending 2, 9, 16, 23 and 30 March 1918)
**Commanding officer / signatory:** F. C. Walker signs as Major on the 2 March 1918 letter; the Major's signature is illegible on the other sheets in this range

**Notes:** The week ending 2 March 1918 straddles February/March and is filed here by its week-ending date. Mumps cases in the battery led to quarantine of contacts (16 March letter); Classes 1 and 2 did practically no work on Thursday and Friday of the week ending 23 March because those men comprised a draft that sailed about 22 March 1918. The diary sheets for the week ending 23 March 1918 are referred to in the 23 March letter but are not in this range — the week-ending-23-March sheets (pp. 257–259) are the first pages of the next chunk and are filed here under March 1918.

Transcribed faithfully from the LAC page images. Original spelling and abbreviations are preserved. Anything illegible or uncertain is marked [uncertain]; nothing has been guessed. No information from outside the pages has been added.

## Page 244 (diary p. 28; circled number not visible) — Special remarks / cover letter (Weekly Diary of Training and Proposed Syllabus) — Date: 1918-03-02 — Month: 1918-03

[Top centre: R-8. Stamp, top right: MILITARY DISTRICT No. 7 / ST. JOHN, N.B. / [date partly obscured] 1918 / M. D. 5-3-2]

West St John, NB March 2, 1918.

To—The G. S. O.
MD#7, St John, NB

Special Remarks: Weekly Diary of Training, and Proposed Syllabus.

Herewith, please, copies of Diary of Training for week ending March 9th. Owing to the increase of our "Parade" strength three squads will be formed, one to be composed entirely of recruits, another of advanced men, and the third of still less advanced men.
In the event of the draft men going on leave, it may later be advisable to revert to the two squads again. This may become a necessity providing one of the instructors still continues on the sick list.
The work carried on during the past week has been badly broken up by the removal to new quarters, but was producing very favourable results.

F. C. Walker [uncertain] Major
OC #9 o/s Siege Battery, CEF.

Special Remarks: Weekly training.
Proposed Syllabus, Week ending 16-3-18

Attached herewith, please find weekly diary of training for past week, also proposed syllabus for week ending March 16th.

The first class, consisting of the men of #5 draft, being on leave until Thursday morning, no work was carried on under that syllabus for Monday, Tuesday and Wednesday.

New Recruits coming in during the week were started out as per Monday's syllabus.

Length for lectures, it will be noted, was reduced to half an hour after Monday.

The week's work was satisfactory, a marked improvement being noticeable on the general standing.
