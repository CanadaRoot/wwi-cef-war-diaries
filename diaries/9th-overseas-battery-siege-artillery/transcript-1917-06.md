- **LAC Item ID:** 2005388
- **RG reference:** RG 9 III-D-3, vol. 5064, file 985, microfilm reel T-11127
- **Month:** June 1917
- **Pages covered (this file):** manifest pages 98–132 (diary pp. 98–132)
- **Diary date range in this file:** 28 May–30 June 1917 (weeks ending 2, 9, 16, 23 and 30 June 1917; the week ending 2 June straddles May/June — assigned to June, the month of the week-ending date)
- **Location:** Partridge Island, St. John, N.B.
- **Commanding officer / signatory:** Major, C.O. #9 o/s Siege Battery C.E.F. (signature illegible — [uncertain])
- **Note on LAC labels:** the LAC manifest labels these pages "November 1916", but the sheets themselves are dated May–June 1917. Dates below follow the diary sheets themselves.
- **Form:** typed weekly diaries on M.F.B. 316 forms, plus a covering letter on M.F.B. 440. Spelling, abbreviations and punctuation preserved as in the original. Anything illegible or uncertain is marked [uncertain].

## Page 98 (June 1917, p. 98) — covering letter, Weekly Diary of Training

[Top left: M. F. B. 440.]
[Stamp, centre right: "HEADQUARTERS OFFICE / NEW BRUNSWICK TROOPS / ST. JOHN, N.B. / JUN 4 1917 / No. N.B. 5-3-21"]
[Stamp, right: "No. 9 O.S. Siege Battery C.E.F. / JUN 2 1917 / ORDERLY ROOM"]

**MILITIA AND DEFENCE.** In reply please quote No. [blank]

Partridge Island, 2nd June 1917

From:— The Commanding Officer, No. 9 o/s Siege Battery.
To:— The G.O.C. N.B. Troops, St. John, N.B.

Special Remarks
Weekly Diary of Training.

Sir:—

Regarding the marginally noted subject, I have the honour to report that regular classes were carried on until Tuesday afternoon, when the Signalling Classes were organized. Thirty-six men of the Battery are taking signalling, divided into two classes. The two classes alternate between signalling and working parties. The recruits' class was carried on according to schedule during the entire week,

I have the honour to be, Sir,
Your obedient servant,

[Signature: [uncertain — signature illegible]] Major.
C.O. #9 o/s Siege Battery.
