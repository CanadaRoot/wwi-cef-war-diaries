**LAC Item ID:** 2005388
**Reference:** RG 9 III-D-3, vol. 5064, file 985; microfilm reel T-11127
**Month:** January 1918
**Manifest pages covered:** 217–231
**Diary page numbers covered:** cover sheet (no diary p. number); 130, 131, 132, 132, 134, 135, 136, 138, 139; several sheets with no diary p. number visible

Transcribed faithfully from the LAC page images. Original spelling and abbreviations are preserved. Anything illegible or uncertain is marked [uncertain]; nothing has been guessed. No information from outside the pages has been added.

## Page 219 (diary p. 132) — Special Remarks letter — Date: 1918-01-05 — Month: 1918-01

St. John, NB.,
January 5, 1918.

To:— The G.S.O., MD #7,
St. John, NB.

Special Remarks
Weekly Diary of Training

[Stamp: "MILITARY DISTRICT NO. 7, ST. JOHN, N.B. — JAN 5 1918 — M.D. NO. 7 5-3-2."]

Reference to above noted subject, beg to report that the work during past week consisted of guard duty on the west side. On account of several men who were warned for overseas service having been given leave, there were barely enough men remaining to fill in all guards. Twenty-three recruits were taken on during the week covered by the report, some of whom were sufficiently advanced in rifle drill to carry on with the guard duties relinquished by men on leave. The remaining recruits have been given squad and rifle drill.

[Signature: Harry S. Bennett [uncertain]], Captain,
C.O., #9 o/s Siege Battery.

## Page 229 (diary p. not visible) — Special remarks — Date: 1918-01-26 — Month: 1918-01

[Stamp, top centre: MILITARY DISTRICT NO. 7, ST. JOHN, N. B., JAN 28 1918, M. D. 5-3-2. Circular stamp, top right: No. 9 O. S. Siege Battery, C.E.F., JAN 26 1918, ORDERLY ROOM. No handwritten circled p. number visible on this page.]

West St. John, N.B. 26th January 1918.

To:—The G.S.O., M.D. No. 7,
St. John, N. B.

Special Remarks
Weekly Diary of Training.

Reference the marginally noted subject, I beg to report that a guard of forty-two N.C.O.'s and Men have been continuously employed at West St. John during the past week. The remainder of the Battery, practically all members of the draft warned for overseas, have been given Physical Training, Squad Drill, Rifle Drill etc. by C.S.M. Instr. Johnson and Sgt. Instr. Cooney. Some of the time was also spent in preparation of members of draft for overseas. Very good results are apparent from instruction given.

[Signed] Harry S. Bennett
Captain,
C.O. #9 o/s Siege Battery C.E.F.
