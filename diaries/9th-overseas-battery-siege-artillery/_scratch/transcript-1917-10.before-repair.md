## Page 185 (diary p. 74) — cover letter — Date: 1917-10-20 — Month: 1917-10

M. F. B. 449.

MILITIA AND DEFENCE. In reply please quote No....................

Partridge Island, 20th October 1917

To:—The C.S.O., N.B. Troops,
St. John, N. B.

Special Remarks
Weekly Diary of Training.

[Stamp: HEADQUARTERS OFFICE / NEW BRUNSWICK TROOPS / ST. JOHN, N. B. / OCT 20 1917 / No N.B. 5-3-2]

Reference the marginally noted, I beg to state that different subjects were dealt with by the respective classes during the entire week, with the exception of Thursday, when the Battery went on a route march, attending divine service in the City on this day also.

A great many recruits were taken on this week and these have been given the elementary drills, lecture etc. necessary. Good progress in their work was shown by this as well as the other classes.

During the coming week it is proposed to carry out squad and company drill as the Battery is today moving into quarters at the Martello Hotel at West St. John. About sixty men have been selected to remain at this Station to carry on with the work on the new Battery here and also to paint the Barracks.

[Signature — illegible: [uncertain]]
C.O. #9 o/s Siege Battery.

## Page 187 (diary p. 78) — cover letter — Date: 1917-10-27 — Month: 1917-10

West St. John, N.B. 27th October 1917.

To:—The C.S.O., N.B. Troops,
St. John, N.B.

Special Remarks
Weekly Diary of Training.

[Stamp: HEADQUARTERS OFFICE / NEW BRUNSWICK TROOPS / ST. JOHN, N. B. / OCT 29 1917 / No N.B. 5-3-2]
[Stamp: No. 9 O. S. Siege Battery / C.E.F. / OCT 27 1917 / ORDERLY ROOM.]

I beg to report on the marginally noted subject, and to state that the Recruits' Class has been given the usual elementary drill and lectures during the past week and have made good progress in their work. Instruction to the plotters' and layers' classes has been carried out as far as possible, but owing to the fact that laying and other work has to be done on the guns, this has not been carried out as fully as usual. A working party of sixty N.C.O.'s and Men proceeded to the Island each morning for work on new gun positions and painting of the Barracks there; this party returned to Barracks here each night.

[Signature — illegible: [uncertain]] Major,
C.O. #9 o/s Siege Battery.
