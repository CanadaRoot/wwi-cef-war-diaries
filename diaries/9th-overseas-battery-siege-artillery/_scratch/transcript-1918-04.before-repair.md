## Page 263 (diary p. 47) — Cover letter / Special Remarks — Date: 1918-04-06 — Month: 1918-04

St. John, NB., April 6 1918

[Stamp: MILITARY DISTRICT No. 7, ST. JOHN, N.B., APR 8 1918, M.D.7 5-3-2]

To—The GSO.
MD #7, St. John

Special Remarks on Diary and proposed Syllabus of training

I beg to attach hereto, please, proposed syllabus for coming week (four classes) also diary of training as carried on during week past.

During past week the training was carried on according to syllabus, except Wednesday afternoon, when all ranks proceeded to Partridge Island for instruction in gun mountings and firing; also the MO, on account of being otherwise employed, was unable to give lectures on subjects connected with his department, other lectures being substituted.

The progress was very good, all ranks exhibiting great interest in their work.

Signature [uncertain] Lieut. for Major, OC #9 o/s Siege Battery CEF

Special Remarks; Weekly Diary
and proposed Syllabus training

[Stamp, partly legible: MILITARY DISTRICT No. 7 ST. JOHN, N. B. APR 22 1918 M.D.7 5-3-2]

I beg to attach hereto, please, weekly diary of training as followed last week; also proposed syllabus for coming week, squads one, two and three. On Monday, Tuesday and Wednesday of last week all officers, NCO's and men were on Route March and instructional work to Red Head Fort.

The progress made was fair, some men showing more aptitude for the work than others, the better men being put in advance squads.
