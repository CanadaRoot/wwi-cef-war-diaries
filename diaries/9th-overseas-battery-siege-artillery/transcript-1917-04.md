St John, N.B.
[Stamp: HEADQUARTERS OFFICE NEW BRUNSWICK TROOPS ST. JOHN, N. B., APR 9 1917, No. [uncertain] 5-3-2]

Training
Special Remarks Weekly Diary

Sir:—

With reference to the marginally noted subject, I have the honour to report as follows for the week ending 7th April 1917:—

Regular training of No. 9 Siege Battery began on April 2nd. The work consisted of squad drill and lectures in elementary subjects. It is the intention to grade the men in classes in order that the progress of the men in all subjects may be noted.

I have the honour to be,
Sir,
Your obedient servant,
[Signature: P. W. Wetmore — [uncertain]] Major.,
C.O. #9 o/s Siege Battery.

St. John, N.B.
[Stamp: No. 9 O. S. Siege Battery, C.E.F., APR 28 1917, ORDERLY ROOM.]
[Stamp: HEADQUARTERS OFFICE NEW BRUNSWICK TROOPS ST. JOHN, N. B., APR 30 1917, No. N.B. 5-3-2]

Special Remarks
Weekly Diary of Training.

Sir:—

With reference to the marginally noted subject, I have the honour to report:—
Men who have completed recruits' drill last week were placed in a 2nd class. These two classes are now carried on distinctly, the 2nd class receiving the elementary artillery work.
The work was inspected by Lieut.-Col. C. J. Mersereau during the week and the future work will include six hours per day drill and lectures. Shorter periods will be given for each subject. Gun Drill will necessitate longer periods than 45 minutes as it will include the time occupied in the preparation for drill and the collecting of stores after the work is completed.

I have the honour to be,
Sir,
Your obedient servant,
[Signature: P. W. Wetmore — [uncertain]] Major.,
C.O. #9 o/s Siege Battery.
