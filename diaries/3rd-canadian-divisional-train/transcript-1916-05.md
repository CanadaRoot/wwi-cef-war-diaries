# War Diary — 3rd Canadian Divisional Train — May 1916

- **Unit:** 3rd Canadian Divisional Train, 3rd Canadian Division (styled "Headquarters 3rd Canadian Divisional Train" on the cover)
- **Month:** May 1916 (Volume 4) — this volume covers 1 to 31 May 1916
- **Source:** Library and Archives Canada, Collection Search Item ID 2005004 (RG 9 III-D-3, vol. 5020, microfilm reel T-10904, file 769; finding aid 9-52)
- **Pages:** May 1916, p. 1 – p. 8 (image files page-0049 – page-0056 of the 370-page diary)
- **May 1916 — COMPLETE** (pages 1–8): cover, contents, diary text 1–31 May, no appendices. Transcribed 2026-10-27.

## Transcription conventions
Original spelling, punctuation and abbreviations kept. Diary text is typewritten throughout (Army Form C. 2118, "WAR DIARY or INTELLIGENCE SUMMARY" — "INTELLIGENCE SUMMARY" struck through on the printed heading). `[uncertain]` marks words I could not read with confidence — never guessed. The Place column is left blank on every May diary sheet; no place is stated in May, and Busseboom (the place throughout April) is carried forward in the index only because no move is recorded in either month. The diarist is Lt-Col J. A. Findlay, O.C. 3rd Canadian Divisional Train; his initials "J.A.F." appear at the foot of every sheet. "Hd. Qrs." = Headquarters; "S.S.O." = Senior Supply Officer; "O.C." = Officer Commanding; "F.G.C.M." = Field General Court Martial; "A.D.V.S." = Assistant Director of Veterinary Services; "V.O." = Veterinary Officer; "D.D.R." and "R.S.O." are used as written and are not expanded in the diary. The heading at the top of the first diary sheet reads "O.C., 3rd Canadian Divl. Train." Diary sheets are numbered 43–48, continuing from April. The printed footer on the diary sheets reads "1875 Wt. W593/826 1,000,000 4/15 J.B.C. & A. A.D.S.S./Forms/C. 2118."; the last sheet reads "2449 Wt. W14957/M90 750,000 1/16 J.B.C. & A. Forms/C.2118/12."

## Pages 3–8 — Diary text (1–31 May 1916)

Army Form C. 2118, "WAR DIARY or INTELLIGENCE SUMMARY". The Place column is blank on all six diary sheets.

### Page 3 — 1–7 May 1916 (Sheet No. 43)

> **1/5/16.** — Capt. Roy very much better. Met A.D.V.S. at Roy's Hut, and arranged for A.D.V.S. to write to D.D.R. to come on Saturday to evacuate 5 horses. Visited Divisional Headquarters. Major Brown, O.C. Gas School, came to day and will join our Mess. Capt Nealy came to see me and stayed to Dinner. Remanded a N.C.O. of No. 3 Company for G.C.M. Fine all day.
>
> **2/5/16.** — Visited Headquarters Company Lines and plowing almost complete, and ground round camp much improved thereby. Watched Farriers at work. Visited Headquarters showed A.A.Q.M.G. letter from Capt. Hargreaves. Heavy thunder and lightning afternoon. Generally fine.
>
> **3/5/16.** — Visited General Headquarters with S.S.O. Met Colonel Panet Camp Comdt., and Capt. Murray at Railhead, and discussed on the spot position for Postal shed. Headquarters Company sports afternoon, tea in Officers Mess. Gave lecture on "Discipline" at 5 o'clock to Officers & N.C.O's. Dull in morning, later fine and sunny.
>
> **4/5/16.** — Visited Headquarters with S.S.O. he went on later to D.S.&T. re charcoal, coke & clover, and got satisfaction. Wrote about promotions of Stirrett and Webb, cannot see why this has not been through weeks and weeks ago. Someone has blundered as per usual. Inspected Nos. 2 & 4 Companies. Best news for a long time is the trial of the Irish leaders by Court Martial and shot, and Birrel resigned, now things are moving. Fair all day, and good deal of sun.
>
> **5/5/16.** — Visited Headquarters Company. Divisional Headquarters spoke about casting unfit horses D.A.Q.M.G. sent memo to A.D.V.S. re same. Old enemy back and feeling cheap. Things generally quiet. Colonel Lamb called. Fine all day. Received Pay Book Driver Fairie and letter from D.A.Q.M.G. making charge out against him.
>
> **6/5/16.** — Headquarters, 2. & 3 Convoys inspected as they passed, and they were certainly in good shape. F.G.C.M. in Office one train man and one from another Unit. Major Fry returns to England.
>
> **7/5/16.** — Adjutant and S.S.O. visited Salvage Company and Divisional Headquarters. Promulgation of S.S. Stewart says reduced to Corporal, got off light. Letter from Mrs Hargreaves, forwarded same to A.A. & Q.M.G. for his information. Fine all morning. Later had to cancel Church Parade because of rain. Rv. Wood called here.

### Page 4 — 8–14 May 1916 (Sheet No. 44)

> **8/5/16.** — D.D.R. – A.D.V.S. – V.O. – acting O.C. Headquarters Company, and self held inspection of certain horses A.D.V.S. would not cast as they were not veterinary cases. D.D.R. made him cast them and backed me up in saying I did not care about his percentages, what I wanted was a class of horse capable of doing its work when called upon. S.S.O. & Adjutant to Head Quarters. Wrote Mrs Hargreaves re her husband. Wet and fine alternating all day.
>
> **9/5/16.** — Office routine all day. Wet most of the day. Adjutant and O.C. Headquarters Coy. returned from leave.
>
> **10/5/16.** — V.O. gone to Hospital, it seems his back is hurt in the same way Blakes was. A.D.V.S. called in on his way to see him, and I told him V.O. did not want to leave, and I do not want to loose him. Dined No. 2. Fine all day.
>
> **11/5/16.** — Walked round Companies. Visited Headquarters. Called on O.C. 1st Train re Hogarth and ascertained certain facts I wanted re Courtney. Belgian gang to start to morrow on road close to here. Headquarters with Adjutant. S.S.O. suffering from Boils.
>
> **12/5/16.** — Visited Headquarters with Adjutant. Gang started road making but my they are a lazy lot, beat those at Salisbury Plane, still they are making a good job. A.D.V.S. called up to see if anything doing in his line. No further word from Hargreaves and will report to Head Quarters. McCalum back. Tried Salvage case. Fine all day.
>
> **13/5/16.** — Reported Hargreaves non return. S.S.O.'s meeting at Corps Headquarters. McCalum reported, and goes on leave, this will set him up. Particularly pleased with the treatment in Hospital. Beastly cold and wet all day. Great progress being made on road. Correspondence and general snapping up of Office work.
>
> **14/5/16.** — 1st Line Transport reports in, show improvement, hope I will find it so when I go round next week. Oliphant and McCalum on leave. Church Parade, Rv. Wood took service. Wet all morning and evening.

### Page 5 — 15–19 May 1916 (Sheet No. 45)

> **15/5/16.** — It looks as if it had rained alright. Inspected 1st Line Transport of 8th Field Ambulance, pointed out things I wanted them to attend to, and they expressed anxiety to do their best. Rained three parts of the day, sun actually came out at 5 p.m. Divisional Cavalry and Cyclists fed last time to day by us.
>
> **16/5/16.** — A.D.V.S. called, he is sending a V.O. to take Capt. Roy's place during his absence, which should not be more than ten days, so he informs the M.O. by letter to day. Adjutant got a touch of what looks like influenza, and is feeling very cheap. Major Goldie called. Inspected 1st Line Transport of 9th Field Ambulance.
>
> **17/5/16.** — Terrible congestion of traffic here, no one seems to regulate it, one horse had hoof cut off by steam lorrie. Colonel Simson and Capt. MacDougal looked in when passing. Major Ware passed in car and complimented me on state of convoy. Saw Capt. Fortin and Lieut. Macdonald re late reporting back from leave. Orderly Room Case, out of bounds after hours, reported by A.P.M. Wrote Headquarters re late notification of arrival of drafts, consequent difficulty in getting their food to them, and it is not fair on S.S.O. Inspected 10th Field Ambulance first line, just moved into new quarters, and hope to find improvements next inspection. Very fine all day. New V.O. reported, Capt. Hanmore.
>
> **18/5/16.** — Appointment with A.A. & Q.M.G., spoke to him about the delay in the promotions of Captains Webb and Stirrett, he is going to give certain particulars furnished by me to Colonel Anderson who is to interview Colonel Gibbs this week re this matter and I hope sincerely the delay in their promotions will be ended, and justice rendered to two good Officers. I also spoke to him and to the D.A.Q.M.G. about horses and wagons turned over to Corps Train for Cyclists and Cavalry. I have given in on this point, but they admit I am technically correct. Heard two cases, one I had to dismiss, what evidence there was was insufficient and complicated. Inspected No 4 Company, and had them put through 15 minutes drill. Fine and sunny all day.
>
> **19/5/16.** — Inspected No. 2 Company and gave them a turn at drill. Visited Headquarters. Fine all day.

### Page 6 — 20–25 May 1916 (Sheet No. 46)

> **20/5/16.** — Tried case from Salvage and remanded for F.G.C.M. Inspected 1st Line Transport, 9th Infantry Brigade, Headquarters, Machine Gun Section and 52nd Battalion. Inspected Nos 3 & 4 Companies and drilled same. S.S.O. visited Capts. Headquarters, Colonel Brown presided. Fine all day.
>
> **21/5/16.** — Paymaster gone on leave. Capt. Craig taking his place as acting Paymaster. Lieut. Sheard also goes on leave. Colonels Anderson and Simson visited Colonel Gibbs re promotions. D.A.Q.M.G. called meeting of all Supply Officers, Quartermasters, and Transport Officers, and I addressed them on their duties, so did S.S.O. and D.A.Q.M.G. I called on Colonel Simson and ascertained from him result of his and Colonel Andersons meeting with Colonel Gibb re promotion of Captains Webb and Stirrett, and cannot understand his attitude. If there are two surplus Majors in 2nd Division these promotions cannot go through, this is not correct. These two Majors came over from Canada as O.C. of Companies when they were not entitled to such, further why was my own promotion sanctioned the 2nd had a Lieut, Colonel as S.S.O. when he should be a Major. Capt. Corrigan, S.S.O. to 1st Train was promoted Major, Capt. Lawson, O.C. Divisional Supply Colm. was promoted, why should they when these two Majors were in existence. Hogarth and Lawhead came out with the 3rd Train and could not be absorbed, so like men reverted so as to stay out here. Furthermore Corps Orders came out distinctly stating that these ranks should be taken down. Fine and very hot.
>
> **22/5/16.** — Inspected 1st Line Transport of 60th, 58th, 43rd, and Pioneer Battalions, and found quite an improvement. Very hot, shower at mid-day and in evening.
>
> **23/5/16.** — Inspected horse lines No 3 Company, and Adjutant inspected Books of Company. Remanded case for further evidence, and will try it to morrow. Capt Oliphant reported back from leave. Fine all day.
>
> **24/5/16.** — General case off. Train Sports, General and Staff attended. All went off without a hitch. Simson, Corregan and Le Croix dined with us. Dull but fine, shower in evening.
>
> **25/5/16.** — I with Adjutant and Capt. Oliphant inspected 1st Line Transport of R.C.R., P.P.C.L.I.

### Page 7 — 25–29 May 1916 (Sheet No. 47)

> **25/5/16 (continued).** — and 42nd Battalion, and in all cases saw some improvements. Harness of P.P.C.L.I. not so good as at last inspection. Stirrett, Adjutant and self saw Baseball match at Headquarters Company between that Company and No. 2 Company. Score 13 love. Dined at Headquarters Company. Fine with slight showers.
>
> **26/5/16.** — Inspected 1st Line Transport of 49th Battn., and 7th Bde. Headquarters. Visited Corps Headquarters re promotion of Captains Stirrett and Webb, there seems no chance of these Officers getting promotion because of the 2nd Divisional Train being two Majors instead of two Captains to Command Companies, and they were not made to revert. They have Majors doing Captains work, and I have Captains doing Majors work, and doing it extremely well too, anyone inspecting the Train must admit the fact. Can they say the same work is being done better or as well by 2nd Train ?. Visited Divisional Headquarters with S.S.O. Adjutant at drilling of Headquarters Company. Fine but dull, sun occasionally.
>
> **27/5/16.** — Visited 1st Train. Drill at No 3 Company and Inspection. Inspected 8 men for 10th Field Ambulance, and addressed them on their duties. Inspected 5 returned from same. S.S.O. meeting at Corps Headquarters. Fine all day.
>
> **28/5/16.** — Adjutant and Self went to No 3 Company, and explained why we wanted any wagons dismantled during the day assembled before evening. Witnessed Base Ball match and later football match both matches Headquarters Company won. Sgt. G.J.M. Pegg killed at Coal Dump by shell fire. A good N.C.O. gone. Wrote to his wife. Pte. Shields was also wounded and evacuated. 39 shells came into this spot. Cap. Turnbull and Lieut. Jack Alexander came to lunch, later stayed to tea. Fine and sunny all day.
>
> **29/5/16.** — Inspected 8th Infantry Brigade 1st Line Transport with Lieut. Grant. V.O. and S. Captain Stainer accompanied me also. Found a decided improvement all round. Funeral of Sgt Pegg Nearly the whole of No.4 Company turned out, and representatives of all the other Companies were present. His Company produced a Union Jack and white flowers. A most impressive ceremony. He evidently was a great favourite and held in high esteem by all. Baseball match in evening. Headquarters Company again successful. Dined at Headquarters Company Mess. ## Open questions / uncertain items (May 1916)
- The Place column is blank on all six May diary sheets; no place is stated anywhere in May. Busseboom is used as the place in the index only because the Train was at Busseboom throughout April and no move is recorded in April or May.
- The cover reads "Volume 4" but the contents page reads "Volume 16 — 5." — both as written; the contents page appears to be a later typed summary and disagrees with the cover on the volume number (the same pattern as April: cover "Volume 3", contents "Volume 16 — 4.").
- Names with variant spellings as written: McCallum (April) is typed "McCalum" throughout May (12, 13, 14 May); "Colonel Gibbs" (18 May, 21 May) is also written "Colonel Gibb" (21 May); "Corrigan" (21 May) is written "Corregan" (24 May); "La Croie"/"La Croix"/"Lt. Croix" in April is written "Le Croix" (24 May). All recorded as written.
- "Salisbury Plane" (12 May) is as typed; it reads as Salisbury Plain.
- "S.S. Stewart" (7 May, reduced to Corporal) is as typed; the rank abbreviation is not expanded in the diary.
- "Birrel resigned" (4 May) is as typed.
- "General case off." (24 May) is as typed; its sense is unclear.
- The signature at the foot of the May sheets reads "J. A. Findlay" with the middle name not read with confidence; it may read "Andrew" but is recorded here as [uncertain] and the middle name is not used as a name.
- Capt. Roy is the V.O. (Veterinary Officer): 12 April records which hospital Roy was taken to; 1 May says "Capt. Roy very much better"; 16 May says a V.O. is being sent "to take Capt. Roy's place during his absence", and 17 May records the new V.O., Capt. Hanmore, reporting.
- Capt. Hargreaves (O.C. No. 3 Coy) was given leave on 21 April for treatment of a rupture; 13 May records him as a non-return from leave ("Reported Hargreaves non return"), and Findlay wrote to Mrs. Hargreaves about her husband on 8 May.
- The diarist calls Capt. Webb "Major" in April, but the May entries (18, 21, 26 May) show Webb and Stirrett were still Captains whose promotion to Major was blocked — Findlay argues the block is because 2nd Divisional Train carries two surplus Majors who were never made to revert.
- No unit move is recorded in May; the Train stays in place all month. The only fatal casualty in May is Sgt. G.J.M. Pegg, killed by shell fire at the Coal Dump on 28 May; Pte. Shields was wounded and evacuated the same day after 39 shells fell on the spot. Other injuries: the V.O. (Capt. Roy) went to hospital with a hurt back (10 May); the Adjutant showed signs of influenza (16 May); the S.S.O. suffered from boils (11 May). A horse had a hoof cut off by a steam lorry (17 May).
- Names read as: Lt-Col J. A. Findlay (O.C.); Capt. Roy (V.O.); Capt. Nealy; Maj. Brown (O.C. Gas School); Col. Panet; Capt. Murray; Stirrett; Webb; Col. Lamb; Driver Fairie; Maj. Fry (also written "Frye" in April); S.S. Stewart; Mrs. Hargreaves; Rv. Wood; Blakes; Hogarth; Courtney; McCalum; Col. Simson; Capt. MacDougal; Maj. Ware; Capt. Fortin; Lieut. Macdonald; Capt. Hanmore; Col. Anderson; Col. Gibbs; Capt. Craig; Lieut. Sheard; Capt. Corrigan; Capt. Lawson; Lawhead; Lieut. Grant; Capt. Stainer; Sgt. G.J.M. Pegg; Pte. Shields; Cap. Turnbull; Lieut. Jack Alexander; Le Croix; Corregan; Capt. Oliphant.
