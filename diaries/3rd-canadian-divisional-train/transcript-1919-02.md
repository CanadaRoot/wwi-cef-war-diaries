# War Diary — 3rd Canadian Divisional Train — February 1919

- **Unit:** H.Q. 3rd Canadian Divisional Train, 3rd Canadian Division (Canadian Army Service Corps)
- **Month:** February 1919 (Volume 37) — this volume covers 1 to 28 February 1919
- **Source:** Library and Archives Canada, Collection Search Item ID 2005004 (RG 9 III-D-3, vol. 5020, finding aid 9-52)
- **Pages:** February 1919, p. 1 – p. 6 (image files page-0362 – page-0367 of the 370-page diary)
- **February 1919 — COMPLETE** (pages 1–6 of 370): cover, contents, title page, diary text 1–28 February, no appendices. Transcribed 2026-10-27.

## Transcription conventions
Original spelling, punctuation and abbreviations kept. Diary text is typewritten throughout (Army Form C. 2118, "WAR DIARY or INTELLIGENCE SUMMARY", with the printed note "(Erase heading not required.)"; neither heading is struck through). `[uncertain]` marks words I could not read with confidence — never guessed. The Place column reads "Field" on 1 February with ditto marks below it for the rest of the France entries; on Sheet 2 the date "13/2/19." is written in the Place column, an alignment slip in the original. A repeated handwritten initial mark appears at the end of most entries; it reads "de G" (Major W. A. de Graves' initials) and is not repeated against every entry here. The printed form imprint at the foot of the diary sheets reads "D. D. & L., London, E.C. (20340) Wt W5300/P713 750,000 3/18 & 3000 Forms/C2118/16." Abbreviations as written: C.I.B. = Canadian Infantry Brigade; Bde. C.E. = Brigade, Canadian Engineers; S. & T. = Supply & Transport; D. of S. & T. = Director of Supplies & Transport; R.T.O. = Railway Transport Officer; G.O.C. = General Officer Commanding; M.O. = Medical Officer; S.S.O. as written; i/c = in charge; H.Q. = Headquarters; O.C. = Officer Commanding. "Halte repas" and "Brakesman" are as written. Place names are as written: Bassieu, Renaix, Tournai, Froyennes, Le Havre, Weymouth, Witley, Bramshott.

## Pages 4–6 — Diary text (1–28 February 1919)

Army Form C. 2118, "WAR DIARY or INTELLIGENCE SUMMARY". The Place column reads "Field" with ditto marks; the Hour column is blank throughout.

### Page 4 — 1–13 February 1919 (Sheet 1)

> **1/2/19.** — Weather very cold with Ice & Snow, and Roads consequently very slippery for Horse Transport. The Division commencing to proceed to England for Demobilization. 7th C.I.B. First. Supplies and Transport working smoothly.
>
> **2/2/19.** — Nothing to report.
>
> **3/2/19.** — S.S.O., arranged for rationing of troops, proceeding to Le Havre by demobilization trains from Bassieu. Also rations for horses at Horse Demobilization Depot: at Renaix
>
> **4/2/19.** — Rations 7th C.I.B. for last time from railhead to-day as all the personnel have proceeded and will be clear on the 5th. Established a ration dump at Bassieu Station for issue of train rations fuel and straw for demobilization. Sgt. MacDonald. A.G. in charge.
>
> **5/2/19.** — No. 2. Coy. turning in vehicles and equipment to equipment depot to-day. Supply Details 7th C.I.B. Lieut. G. W. Underwood. despatched to Renaix to ration the Divisional Groups at Horse Demobilization Depot.
>
> **6/2/19.** — No. 2. Coy. turning in Horses to-day to Horse Demobilization Depot,
>
> **7/2/19.** — Everything running smoothly. Ration Strength decreasing daily.
>
> **8/2/19.** — No. 4. Coy. Rationed 9th C.I.B. for the last time to-day to railhead.
>
> **9/2/19.** [uncertain] — No. 3. Coy. turn in equipment and vehicles to-day.
>
> **10/2/19.** — No. 3. Coy. turn in Horses to Depot at Renaix to-day. 8th C.I.B. 3rd. Bde. C.E. 8th & 10th Ambulances rationed for the last time to-day. Lieut. W. S. Day to S.O. 9th C.I.B. and Supply Details move to Tournai to-day to ration Div. Troops in and near Tournai. No. 3. Coy. turned in equipment to-day to Depot. No. 3. Coy. move to Froyennes to-day.
>
> **11/2/19.** — Nothing to report.
>
> **12/2/19.** — All of 2. & 4. Coys. Personnel proceed to England to-day on leave to rejoin this unit at Bramshott on expiration of 8 Days leave. No. 3. Coy. turned in equipment to depot to-day. All Brakesman from 7th C.I.B. units and 9th C.I.B. units despatched on 8 Days leave to England with instructions to rejoin their units in England on expiration.
>
> **13/2/19.** — Last of equipment and horses turned in at Bassieu and Renaix. respectively. All H. Q. Details and Company H. Qs of various Companies left Bassieu for Le Havre en route for England entraining at Bassieu at 11.00 Hrs. Train 5.00 Hrs late. Major. W. A. DeGraves took over command of Train

### Page 5 — 13–22 February 1919 (Sheet 2)

> **13/2/19.** (continued) — Proceeding with Adjutant and Demobilization Party to Le Havre. Lieut-Col. Findlay and Capt. Keay. M.O. Proceeding by later train later. Capt. R. E. Fisher & S. S. O. s Staff remaining in Tournai to feed troops at demobilization centre at Renaix and close up accounts with S. & T. 5th Army. Capt. G. UNDERWOOD and supply details 7th C.I.B. remaining at Renaix to act as supply details for all troops 3rd. Can. Division left in area.
>
> **14/2/19.** — On train all day. Feeding and seating arrangements working well and all troops on train comfortable Train halted at 3 Halte repas during journey and hot meals available at each place.
>
> **15/2/19.** — Arrived at Le Havre at 7.00 A.M. Baggage taken to No. 2. Can. Demobilization Camp and troops marched there. Good arrangements in camp for Officers and men. Opened Orderly Room and Train H. Q. s in Div. Artillery H. Q. s Orderly Room.
>
> **16/2/19.** — In camp at Le Havre awaiting orders for embarkation.
>
> **17/2/19.** — Orders received to stand tow [uncertain] for embarkation to-morrow 18th inst.,
>
> **18/2/19.** — Left Le Havre at 23.00 Hrs. on H.M. Transport DIEPPE for Weymouth travelling with 3rd. Can. Div. Artillery Group. Arrived Weymouth 09.00 Hrs after a rough passage. all men provided with Breakfast at canteen on shore, and then marched to station for entraining informed by R.T.O. that orders were changed and 3rd. Can. Div. Train will now go to Witley Camp. instead of Bramshott. arrived Witley 07.00 Hrs.
>
> **19/2/19.** — Installed in "H" Wing., Witley Camp. opened offices and documentation for demobilization commenced. Men who left France on leave beginning to report back. Capt Keay. M.O. reported back.
>
> **20/2/19.** — All available ranks employed on Documentation. Request from D. of S. & T. Canadians London that volunteers be asked for to remain for further service in England and France.
>
> **21/2/19.** — Men arriving daily from leave. Capt. Robinson reported back.
>
> **22/2/19.** — Capt. Hessay & Lieut. Day reported from leave, former takes over as Officer i/c Records

> **24/2/19.** — All available help at work on documentation and demobilization which is proceeding satisfactorily

## Open questions / uncertain items (February 1919)
- The cover reads "VOLUME 37" but the contents page reads "Volume 19 - 2." — both as written; the contents page appears to be a later typed summary and disagrees with the cover on the volume number, as in other volumes of this diary.
- 9/2/19 and 10/2/19: the date column shows "9/2/19." overtyped with "10/2/19." on a single line, with two entries following ("No. 3. Coy. turn in equipment and vehicles to-day." and the longer entry beginning "No. 3. Coy. turn in Horses to Depot at Renaix to-day."). The division of the two entries between 9 and 10 February follows the line layout and is not certain.
- 13 February appears on both Sheet 1 (page 4) and Sheet 2 (page 5): the entry is continued across the two sheets, not a second entry for the same date. The same applies to 22 February, which is continued from Sheet 2 (page 5) onto Sheet 3 (page 6).
- 17 February: "stand tow [uncertain]" — the word reads "tow" as typed, which is presumably a typographical error for "stand to".
- 13 February (Sheet 2): "Proceeding by later train later." is as written; the phrasing is odd but the words are legible.
- 4 February: "Sgt. MacDonald. A.G. in charge." — "A.G." is as written; whether it is part of the sergeant's name or a separate note is not clear from the text.
- 12 February: "All Brakesman" is as written (singular form used for the group).
- 22 February: the captain's name reads "Hessay" here, but "Hessey" in the final entry of 18 March 1919 — the two spellings are as written and appear to be the same officer.
- 18 February: the entry ends "arrived Witley 07.00 Hrs." although the unit left Le Havre at 23.00 Hrs. the same day and only reached Weymouth at 09.00 Hrs.; the Witley arrival at 07.00 Hrs. presumably belongs to the morning of 19 February but is recorded in the 18 February entry as written.
- The date in the date column for 18 February is partly obscured ("18/2/19" with the final figure faint) but is read as 18/2/19.
- The Place column on Sheet 2 carries the date "13/2/19." in the Place column at the head of the sheet, an alignment slip; the Place column is otherwise "Field" with ditto marks.
- Faint show-through text is visible on the left of the cover (page 1) but is not legible and is not transcribed.
- Names read as: Major W. A. DeGraves (O.C., signed "W. A. de Graves"); Sgt. MacDonald; Lieut. G. W. Underwood; Lieut. W. S. Day; Lieut-Col. Findlay; Capt. Keay (M.O.); Capt. R. E. Fisher; Capt. Robinson; Capt. Hessay; Lieut. Boyd.
- No casualties are recorded in this volume. No map coordinates (latitude/longitude) are given.
