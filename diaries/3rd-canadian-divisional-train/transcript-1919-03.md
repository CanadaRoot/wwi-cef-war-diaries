## Transcription conventions
Original spelling, punctuation and abbreviations kept. Diary text is typewritten throughout (Army Form C. 2118, "WAR DIARY or INTELLIGENCE SUMMARY", with the printed note "(Erase heading not required.)"; neither heading is struck through). `[uncertain]` marks words I could not read with confidence — never guessed. The Place column is blank throughout this sheet; the Hour column is blank except for the times given in the text of the 8 and 18 March entries. A repeated handwritten initial mark appears at the end of most entries; it reads "de G" (Major W. A. de Graves' initials) and is not repeated against every entry here. The printed form imprint at the foot of the diary sheet reads "D. D. & L., London, E.C. (20340) Wt W5300/P713 750,000 3/18 & 3000 Forms/C2118/16." Abbreviations as written: C.R.A. = Commander, Royal Artillery; G.O.C. = General Officer Commanding; N.C.O. = Non-Commissioned Officer; S.S. = steamship; i/c = in charge; H.Q. = Headquarters; O.C. = Officer Commanding; Reg'tl = Regimental. Place names are as written: Witley, Bramshott, Liphook, Southampton, Liverpool, Halifax, Calgary, Toronto, Winnipeg, Ottawa, Kingston.

## How the diary ends

The diary ends with the entry of **18 March 1919**, the last date in the volume; there is no entry after 18 March and no closing statement or formal "diary closed" note. The final entry records a farewell inspection by the G.O.C. 3rd Canadian Division, who "spoke very nicely to the men on parade and bade everybody good-bye and personally shook hands with everybody on Parade"; the grouping of drafts for Nos. 2 & 3 Companies completed; No. 3 Company to entrain at Liphook at 02.45 the next morning and No. 2 Company at 07.30 for Liverpool; and the unit's end stated in terms of demobilization: "All Officers. N.C.O.s and Men of the unit struck off the Strength in England on departure for Canada, and Reg'tl Depots in England." Capt. Higgins, Capt. Ferg, Lieut. Boyd and Capt. Underwood proceeded to Canada, while Major W. A. DeGraves, Capt. R. V. Robinson and Capt. Hessey remained in England for duty. The sheet is signed by Major W. A. de Graves and bears the unit's round H.Q. stamp. In short, the diary ends not with a battle or a formal disbandment order but with the unit being struck off strength as its companies sailed for Canada for demobilization — Nos. 1 & 4 Companies having already sailed on S.S. Olympic on 17 March, and Nos. 2 & 3 Companies warned to sail on S.S. Cedric on 19 March.

## Open questions / uncertain items (March 1919)
- The cover reads "VOLUME 38" but the contents page reads "Volume 19 - 3." — both as written; the contents page appears to be a later typed summary and disagrees with the cover on the volume number, as in the February volume (cover "VOLUME 37", contents "Volume 19 - 2.").
- 9 March: the officer for No. 3 (Winnipeg) Dispersal Company is given as "Capt. F. M. Ferg." — the surname is abbreviated as written; it is not expanded here.
- 9 March: No. 1 (Halifax) Dispersal Company has no officer named in this entry, although the 11 March entry shows No. 1 Company moving to Halifax.
- 13 March: "Lieut. L. B. Boyd despatched to London to Auditor General." — "to Auditor General." is as written; the phrase reads as "to the Auditor General" with the article omitted.
- 15 March: the entry ends with the word "Train [uncertain]" after "for Kingston and Ottawa." — the word appears to read "Train" but its sense is unclear; it may be a stray typed word or the start of an unfinished sentence, and the handwritten initials follow it.
- 18 March: the captain's name reads "Hessey" here, but "Hessay" in the 22 February entry — the two spellings are as written and appear to be the same officer.
- 18 March: "All Officers. N.C.O.s and Men of the unit struck off the Strength in England on departure for Canada, and Reg'tl Depots in England." is as written; the second clause is a fragment as typed.
- The inner text of the round rubber stamp below the signature is not read with confidence and is recorded as [uncertain]; the outer ring reads "H.Q., 3rd CANADIAN DIVISIONAL TRAIN".
- The Place column is blank for every entry on this sheet; locations are taken from the entry text.
- Faint show-through text is visible on the left of the cover (page 1) but is not legible and is not transcribed.
- Names read as: Major W. A. DeGraves (O.C., signed "W. A. de Graves"); Lieut. Boyd (also "Lieut. L. B. Boyd" on 13 March); Capt. C. T. Higgins; Capt. F. M. Ferg; Lieut. W. S. Day; Capt. Underwood; Capt. R. V. Robinson; Capt. Hessey; the G.O.C. 3rd Canadian Division is not named.
