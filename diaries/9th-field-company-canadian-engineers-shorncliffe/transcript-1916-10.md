### France, W23 Central, Sh 57D S.E. — 1-10-16

Weather.— Fine and clear. Light wind from N.E.

Lieut. Dunsmore with No 2 Section and infantry parties from the 49th and 60th Battalions working on construction of six dug-outs for H.Q. of 7th and 9th Brigades at R36 central. Sh 57D S.E. working in three shifts of eight hours each (See work report) [uncertain]

Lieut. Thornely with No 1 Section working with the 6th Field Coy from 1 a.m. to daybreak on construction of Pozieres Courcelette tramway

O.C. attended C.R.E 2nd Canadian Division and remained with him and O.C. 3rd Canadian Division during attack, which commenced at 3:15 p.m. whereby the 2nd Division and part of 3rd Canadian Division captured, Regina Trench, west of Courcelette and were later driven back to original line.

Lieut. Robertson with N.C.Os and Sappers from Nos 1 & 3 Sections working on tram line at R29 B2.5.15 Sh 57D S.E. (See report)

Balance of 6th F.C.C.E moved out from dug-out at 2 p.m. and the Officers and men of the 9th F.C.C.E moved into the vacated quarters. Same being located at W23 central Sh 57D S.E.

### France, W23 Central (Sh 57D S.E.) — 3-10-16

Weather: Dull and cloudy in forenoon with light wind from S.E., clearing in afternoon

Lieuts Young and Robinson after receiving instructions last night, from 4th and 6th Brigade H.Q., left with No 1 Section, to dig front line trench for above named brigades from which to make attack.

After great difficulty, owing to the darkness and rain and the doubt of the infantry as to the exact location of the advanced infantry, Lieut. Young located position and taped out trenches, and set infantry party to work in locality, approximately from M14.C.9.3 westerly to R18.C.6.1. (Le Sars Edition 2)

## Transcriber's notes — October 1916

- **Contents vs. pages present:** the contents page (p. 2) lists "Diary Text — 59 Pages" and only "Appendix E. — Work report 1-10-16 to 30-10-16." In fact the diary text is 40 pages (pp. 4–43, 1–31 October) and the appendix is 49 pages (pp. 44–92). The appendix contains the Officers Work Report (Appendix E) plus Daily Work Reports numbered from Appendix D No. 1 (1 Oct) through the night of 9–10 Oct and later reports covering 10–11 Oct, 25, 27, 28, 28–29, 30 and 31 Oct. The diary text cites work reports #2–#33 in the Remarks column throughout; the reports cited as #31 (28 Oct), #32 (29 Oct) and #33 (30–31 Oct) are not among the appendix pages in this volume as transcribed.
- **Date gaps (days with no diary entry):** none — every day 1–31 October 1916 has a diary entry. Appendix gap: no work report for 8 October — No. 2 Section's reports jump from 7 October (p. 64, #22) to 9 October (p. 65, #23), though the appendix numbering runs continuously, so no appendix is missing from the sequence.
- **Page numbering:** the printed form page number at the head of each diary page runs one behind the LAC image page number used here (e.g. LAC p. 24 bears printed "23"; LAC p. 43 bears printed "42"). The appendix cover (LAC p. 44) bears printed "43", and the two work reports (LAC pp. 45–46) bear printed "44" and "45". The same one-behind offset applies to the appendix pages.
- **Form imprints:** diary pages pp. 24–29: "1875 Wt. W393/1166 1,000,000 4/15 J.B.C. & A. A.D.S.S./Forms/C.2118."; pp. 31–43: "2409 Wt. W14957/M290 750,000 1/16 J.B.C. & A. Forms/C.2118/12." The 31 October diary page (p. 43) also bears the imprint "2409 Wt. W14957/M290 750,000 1/16 J.B.C. & A. Forms/C.2118/12."
- **Commanding officer's signature:** the O.C.'s signature on the inner title page (p. 3) and on the 31 October diary entry (p. 43) reads "H. P. - **Movements:** based at W23 Central, Sh 57D S.E., France, 1–11 Oct; Nos. 2 & 4 Sections moved to Senlis 11 Oct, Nos. 1 & 3 Sections and HQ marched to Senlis 12 Oct (via Albert and Bouzincourt); the whole company moved Senlis → Harponville 13 Oct (via Warloy and Vadencourt).
