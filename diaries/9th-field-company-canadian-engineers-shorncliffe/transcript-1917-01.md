### Maroeuil, France — 1-1-17

Weather: Dull and cloudy

Upon receiving orders for a special report on condition of trenches O.C. attended at Advanced Billets

Two Sappers superintending water works installation at Ecoivres

Sappers at Rear Billets making revetting and concertina wire and carrying on with usual routine of work

Lieut. Walley with No 2 Section and parties from the 43rd and 58th Battalions cleaning and repairing trenches, dugouts, working on drying room, Building Cook House (See report)

No 3 Section in charge of Senior N.C.O. and parties from the 60th and 43rd Battalions pumping water out of front line trenches. Party of 3 Sappers working on Engineer Shelters. (See report)

Lieut. Robinson with three Sappers and parties from the 58th and 60th Battalions working on Vistula and Bessan pumping water and clearing out trenches. (See Report)

## Notes — January 1917

- **Contents vs. pages present:** the contents page (p. 2) lists "Diary Text — 32 Pages" and "Appendix H, work report 1-1-17 to 30-1-17." The diary text is exactly pp. 3–34 (32 pages, 1–31 January 1917) and the appendix is pp. 35–161 (127 pages: Appendix H title page on p. 35, then work reports pp. 36–161). The appendix reports run to 30 January, as the contents page states; the diary text runs to 31 January.
- **Date gaps (days with no diary entry):** none — every day 1–31 January 1917 has a diary entry.
- **Page numbering:** the appendix sheet/report numbers do not run cleanly. Appendix sheet numbers run 1 (p. 36), 2 (p. 37), 3 (p. 38), then 5 (p. 40) — sheet 4 is not among pp. 36–40. Appendix numbers run #6 to #38 with lettered continuations (20a, 20b, 24a, 28a, 32a, 32b, 36a, 36b) in pp. 41–80; report numbers skip No. 46 and No. 49 in pp. 81–120. In pp. 121–161 the internal printed page numbers (top right) run 66–104 but skip: p. 138 carries no internal number and p. 141 carries the date "24-1-17" instead of a number, so internal numbers 81 and 84 are unaccounted for in the printed sequence.
- **Year errors in the original:** several January 1917 work reports are headed "1916" as written — p. 38 (Light Railway, "January 1st 1916"), pp. 43, 46 and 65, and pp. 82 ("January 11-1916"), 98 ("January 14-1916"), 104 ("January 15th 1916") and 110 ("January 17-1916"). Each is flagged [sic] in its section; the surrounding sequence confirms January 1917.
- **Date-order anomalies in the appendix:** p. 76 is headed "January 9th 1917" but is filed after the 10 January report (p. 75); pp. 76–78 (appendix #36, 36a, 36b) are a second, separate 9 January No. 1 Section report, distinct from the 9 January No. 1 Section report on pp. 70–72 (#32, 32a, 32b). In pp. 121–161 the Thelus Section reports consistently run a day behind the section daily reports (e.g. p. 143 is dated 24 Jan while p. 142 is 25 Jan; p. 148 is headed 27 Jan but p. 149 is headed 26 Jan). The day figures on pp. 148, 154 and 157 appear overwritten. Time errors are preserved as written (e.g. p. 68 "9.00 AM – 4.00 AM", p. 69 "5.30 p.m. to 4 p.m.", p. 74 "8.30–8.00", p. 75 "12 p.m. to 8 a.m.").
- **Commanding officer's signature:** Major H. P. Robertson (H.P.R.) signs most diary pages as O.C. Capt. J. W. Harrison signs the closing diary page (p. 34) in full as "J. W. Harrison, Capt., O.C. 9th Field Coy. Canadian Engineers" (with the 9th Field Company stamp dated 31 Jan. 1917) and the Appendix H title page; the recurring scribbled "J. W. H., Capt." signature on pp. 7–12, 20, 23 and 27–34 is read as his and flagged [uncertain] each time. Robertson signs with rank "Capt" on p. 5 though "Major" everywhere else.

- **Movements:** based at Maroeuil, France, 1–17 January; the company marched Maroeuil → Ecoivres via Bray on 18 January under 3rd Canadian Divisional Engineers Operation Order No. 18 (which cleared Anzin, Maroeuil, Louez, St. Aubin, Etrun and Bray of 3rd Division troops by noon 18 January); based at Ecoivres 18–31 January.
- **Operations:** trench-warden parties and trench cleaning/repair in the front and support lines, the Pozieres–Courcelette tramway and Thelus Section light railway, dug-out and shelter work, water supply and hutting, and a gas alarm stand-to 11:30 a.m.–5:00 p.m. on 10 January (p. 79). The Thelus Section reports repeatedly note Party B (8 men) from the Right Sub-sector reporting short (8 instead of 15 scheduled) and quitting early.
