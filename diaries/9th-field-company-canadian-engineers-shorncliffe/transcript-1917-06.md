# War Diary Transcript — 9th Field Company, Canadian Engineers (Shorncliffe), June 1917

- **Unit:** 9th Field Company, Canadian Engineers, 3rd Canadian Division
- **LAC Item ID:** 2005998, "War diaries - 9th Battalion, Canadian Engineers", RG 9 III-D-3, vol. 4999, file 669, microfilm reels T-10841–T-10843
- **Month / Volume:** June 1917, Volume 14
- **Pages in this file:** LAC images pp. 1–113 (113 pages: cover, inner title page, contents, 30 diary text pages, appendix cover + 79 work-report sheets)
- **Diary text date range:** 1–30 June 1917
- **Commanding officer / signatory:** Major W. R. Robertson, O.C. 9th Field Company, Canadian Engineers (Capt. B. Ross in command 5–17 June 1917 while the O.C. and Lieut. H.D. Robertson were on leave in England)
- **Transcription note:** The diary is handwritten on Army Form C. 2118 ("War Diary or Intelligence Summary"). The appendix work reports are handwritten on squared paper, in several different layouts, and are faint or crowded in places — the June sheets are much harder handwriting than March. Page numbers in this transcript are the LAC image page numbers. The diary-text Place column throughout June is a map reference, not a village name as in March; it is read "S.26.c.1.8, Sheet 36 C. S.W." on most pages (see Notes).

## Notes

- **Structure:** 113 LAC images. Cover (p. 1), inner title page (p. 2), contents (p. 3), diary text pp. 4–33 (30 pages, matching the contents page's "Diary Text — 30 Pages"), Appendix "M" cover (p. 34), and 79 daily work-report sheets (pp. 35–113).
- **Contents-page note:** The contents page describes Appendix M as "Work report issued by 9th Field Co. C.E. 1-6-17 to 29-6-17", which matches the appendix as filed in date range (sheets dated 1–29 June 1917), but the sheets are filed out of order and their sheet numbers are irregular (see below).
- **Date gaps:** None in the diary text — all dates 1–30 June 1917 are present, in sequence.
- **Split entries:** The 1 June entry is split across pp. 4–5; 14 June across pp. 14–15; 21 June across pp. 22–23; 28 June across pp. 29–30; 29 June across pp. 30–31; and the 30 June entry spans pp. 31–33, each marked "(Contd)".
- **Place column:** Unlike March (village names), the June diary text gives a map reference in the Place column, read "S.26.c.1.8, Sht 36 S.W." on most pages, but "S.26.c.1.9, Sheet 36 C. S.W." on p. 4 and "S.26c.1.8 and Sht.36c.1.10" in the readings for pp. 5–19 — the reference is the same repeated entry and the readings vary; transcribed as written per page, not reconciled. Subheadings in this transcript carry the map reference as read on each page.
- **Appendix sheet numbers irregular:** Sheets are numbered 1, then 19–78, but with duplicates and gaps — sheet 25 appears twice (pp. 57 and 60, the second overwritten and possibly 26), sheets 35, 36 and 37 each appear twice (pp. 36–38 and pp. 70–72), and sheets 57 and 58 each appear twice (pp. 86–87 and pp. 92–93). Sheet numbers 2–18 and 38 are missing from the appendix as filed, although the diary text cites Reports #2–#18 and #38. Sheet 40 is also unaccounted for in this run: p. 40 is sheet 39 and p. 42 is sheet 41. P. 41 reads sheet "7" [uncertain] with a crossed-out figure before it. P. 96 has no sheet number visible (sequence suggests 61). Several sheets have stray top-right figures that do not match their sheet numbers (e.g. pp. 35, 38, 39, 44, 46–53).
- **Appendix filing order:** Sheets are not in chronological order. P. 36 (sheet 35, dated 11–12 June) is filed second, ahead of the 1–2 June sheets; pp. 86–87 (sheets 57–58, 22–23 June) are filed before pp. 88–91 (sheets 53–56, 23–24 June); p. 56 (night of 10–11 June, sheet 21) follows p. 55 (morning of 11 June, sheet 20); p. 53 (morning of 10 June) follows p. 52 (10–11 June); p. 47 (4–5 June) follows the undated p. 46. Overlapping coverage: pp. 35 and 37 both cover 1–2 June; pp. 40 and 41 both cover 2–3 June.
- **Appendix date miswritings:** P. 92 is dated "Sunday 23/7/17" with a "24" written above the "23" (Sunday was 24 June 1917). P. 95 is dated "28/7/16" as written — almost certainly a miswriting of 25/6/17 given its position. P. 78 gives the month/year as "7/16", evidently a slip for 6/17. P. 63 gives the second date as "16.7.17", evidently for 16.6.17. P. 90 and p. 105 carry no date (p. 105 presumably 28 June from sequence). P. 38 is undated ("6/12" at top right). P. 71's header date "17/18" is written over a crossed-out date. All transcribed as written.
- **Stamped page numbers:** The appendix sheets bear a stamped or ink number at the right edge exactly one less than the LAC image page number (34 on p. 35 … 112 on p. 113). The appendix cover (p. 34) bears a handwritten "33" at top right.
- **Casualty discrepancies:**
  - Spr Campbell, W.: p. 9 gives his number as #524429 (first aid, 5 June); p. 22 gives #524439 in the D.C.M. recommendation (21 June). One number is miswritten in the original.
  - Spr Hill, F.L.: p. 22 gives his number as #76617 [uncertain] — only five digits against six-digit numbers elsewhere.
  - The D.C.M. recommendation dates (p. 22) read "June 4th-5th and 4th-5th and 5th-6th respectively" [uncertain] — the first two dates are duplicated as written.
  - Spr Crowley, E.E. (#445695) is listed sick on 11 June (p. 12) and again on 30 June (p. 33), transcribed as written.
  - The appendix's "Uncertain, probably 30" killed or wounded (p. 47, night of 4–5 June) is the diary's heaviest single casualty figure and does not appear in the diary text; the diary text for 5 June records only "a number of casualties in killed and wounded" without a figure.
  - Appendix casualty lines that give a bare figure with no names: p. 36 (3 wounded — 1 sapper and 2 infantry, carrying party, 11–12 June), p. 38 (3 in the Cas. column, undated sheet), p. 43 (3, 3–4 June), p. 49 (3 of 1st C.M.R. wounded slightly and gassed, 2 C.E. gassed, night of 5–6 June).
- **Tournament discrepancy:** The 7 June entry says the Divisional Tournament is "to be held on the 12th Inst.", but the 14 June entry records it as held on 14 June near Berthonval.

R. Robertson (title page, p. 2); Capt. B. Ross was in command 5–17 June. The No. 1 Section officer is read "Wm. Robertson" on the appendix sheets but "H. H. Robertson" in March; the diary text names Lieut. H.D. Robertson. The officer signing "C. Mc[uncertain]" on several sheets could not be identified beyond "Mc". The night-report officer is read "Wm Robinson" on pp. 74–87 but "C. M. Robinson" [uncertain] on pp. 97–113, and the diary text names Lieut. C.M. Robinson; a "C. M. Stevens" [uncertain] signs the No. 1 Section day sheets. "C. A. Lyon" [uncertain] signs p. 88 only. The officer signing "W. C. - **Form heading flip:** Pp. 4–19 are headed "WAR DIARY or INTELLIGENCE SUMMARY" with neither heading struck through in the transcription as given; pp. 20–33 are headed "WAR DIARY" with "INTELLIGENCE SUMMARY" struck through.
- **Other notes from the pages:** P. 42 is the hardest appendix sheet (very small, crowded handwriting). The June sheets use at least four different layouts (ruled C.E./Inf./Bn. tables; Inf.+C.E. sub-column tables; Party tables with a Cas. column; continuous-line prose), and several contain small drain/trench cross-section sketches in the Amount Done column (pp. 65, 66, 69 and the Walley sheets in the final chunk). The diary writes "C.M.R." and "C.A.M.C." / "A.M.C." as written; "M.T.M." / "H.T.M." do not occur in June. Times on work reports sometimes carry a "th" suffix (e.g. "12th") — transcribed literally.
- **Uncertain items:** See the [uncertain] flags throughout the transcript — 326 exact "[uncertain]" occurrences in the page transcriptions, plus 7 extended "[uncertain — ...]" annotations (333 uncertain markings in total; 339 exact "[uncertain]" in the file including the 13 mentions in this Notes section). The heaviest uncertainty is on the early-June appendix sheets, especially pp. 36–49 (209 in chunk C alone, pp. 35–53); p. 42 is the worst single sheet.
