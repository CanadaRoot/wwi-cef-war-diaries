WAR DIARY for month of JULY 1916, Original. 9th Field Coy Canadian Engineers, 3rd Canadian Division. Army Form C. 2118.

### H.13.d.2.7, Ref Map Belgium Sht 28 N.W. — 1.7.16

Weather: Fine warm and clear

O.C. & Mr DUNSMORE with party of Sappers attended funeral of #502624 Sapper Fox R.E. to BRANDHOEK Cemetery. Buried at line "G" 3rd grave from south end. Ref Map Belgium, Sht 28 N.W. — G 12 b.6.9.

Word received from Mr YOUNG that he had commenced his course of instruction to 8th I.B. on June 26th. Instruction in organization of wiring parties, organization of working parties, trench digging, Machine gun emplacements, etc.

Party of 1 N.C.O. and 10 Sappers working at Hutting Dump. Lieut WEST with No 4 Section working on NEW OXFORD St. Lieut ROBERTSON working on drains Areas 18 C. See work reports
