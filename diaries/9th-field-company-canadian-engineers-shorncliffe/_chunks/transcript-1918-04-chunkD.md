- **Pages in this file (chunk D):** LAC images pp. 91–118 (28 pages: all appendix Officers' Work Reports — nightly work reports dated 21–29 April 1918)

O.R. / Unit / Time / Remarks layout, and some are narrative "Party / Time / Work" reports. Each sheet's table is transcribed with that sheet's own column headings as read. Map references are transcribed as written and marked [uncertain] where the figures are hard to read. Page numbers in this transcript are the LAC image page numbers.

## Notes

- **Structure observed:** All 28 pages (pp. 91–118) are appendix Officers' Work Reports; there is no diary text, cover, contents page or appendix cover in this chunk. The sheets fall into three formats: (a) ruled tables headed Party / Unit / Strength (O and OR) / C.E. (O and S) / Time (From and To) / Work Done — the No. 1 Section sheets (pp. 92, 93, 96, 99, 102, 104, 109, 113) and the No. 2 Section sheets pp. 115 and 118; (b) ruled tables headed Party / C.E. / Inf. O.R. (or O.R. and Inf. as separate columns) / Unit / Time / Remarks — the No. 3 and No. 4 Section sheets (pp. 91, 95, 98, 101, 105, 108, 112, 116, 117); (c) narrative "Party / Time / Work" reports — the No. 2 Section sheets pp. 94, 97, 100, 103, 114, the No. 3 Section sheet p. 111, and the M.G. Emplacement report p. 107. One sheet (p. 106) is a typed-style summary in block capitals by another unit (see below). One sheet (p. 100) includes a small hand-drawn sketch of wire/gap layouts with a legend ("old work" / "proposed work for tomorrow"). P. 110 is the reverse (verso) of the p. 109 sheet, carrying its "P.T.O." continuation.
- **Date gaps:** The reports in this chunk are dated 21 to 29 April 1918 (night work 21–22 April on pp. 92–93; the M.G. summary on p. 106 covers 20–24 April). There are no work reports dated 30 April 1918 in this chunk, although April has 30 days and the diary text (per the contents page) runs to 30 April — the appendix as filed ends with the 29-4-18 reports on p. 118. No reports dated before 21 April appear in this chunk (those are in earlier chunks).
- **Split entries:** No diary-text entries split across pages in this chunk (there is no diary text). Within the appendix, the p. 109 report continues overleaf to p. 110 ("P.T.O."). The p. 113 sheet carries two blocks on one page: night work (8.00 PM–4.00 AM) and a separate block headed "For Saturday 27-4-18" (day work). The p. 92 sheet's final row runs to the foot of the page and its last line is cut off in the image.
- **Duplicate photographs:** None observed — each of the 28 pages is a distinct sheet. P. 110 is the reverse side of the p. 109 sheet, not a duplicate.
