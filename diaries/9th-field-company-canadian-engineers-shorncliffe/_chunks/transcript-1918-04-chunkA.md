### R.11.c.2.7. [uncertain] Sht 36a, France — 12-4-18

Weather: Clear and cool. North Wind.
Capt Miller, Lieut Baldock and Lieut Serson proceeded to Rear Billets of 466 Field Coy R.E. No 3 Section and 6 O.R. of No 2 Sec proceeded on bicycles to Rear Billets of the 466th Field Coy R.E.
Balance of Coy with transport, under Major Robertson, moved off at 7 P.M. and proceeded via Souchez and Ablain-St Nazaire to a field just outside Bully-Grenay (R.11.c.2.7. Sheet 36a) where the Coy bivouacked for the night as billets of 466th Field Coy R.E. will not be vacated until tomorrow.
This night move made as Souchez-St. Nazaire road cannot be used in daylight, being in view of the enemy lines.
Relief of 466th Coy R.E. by this Coy. complete by at 6 P.M. today. [The word "by" appears struck through.]
No 3 Section, and No. 2 Section except 6 O.R. proceeded to Advanced Billets in Cite St Pierre (M.11.d.25.50. Sheet 36c SW)

### Bully-Grenay — 30-4-18

## Notes

- **Structure observed:** Cover (p. 1), inner title page (p. 2), contents (p. 3), diary text pp. 4–24 (21 pages, matching the contents page's "Diary Text — 21 Pages"), appendix cover (p. 25) and work-report sheets pp. 26–30. Diary text covers 1–30 April 1918 with no date gaps; every day has an entry. The appendix in this chunk contains only sheets numbered 1–4 (work reports dated 1–3 April 1918); the contents page describes Appendix W as running 1-4-18 to 29-4-18, so the remaining reports (diary references run to Report #77, dated 29 April) lie in later pages of the month, outside this chunk.
- **Date gaps:** None in the diary text. Diary entries split across page breaks: 3 April (pp. 5–6), 17 April (pp. 14–15), 27 April (pp. 20–21). Page 21 is mostly blank, holding only the continuation of the 27 April entry (4 lines).
- **Contents-vs-pages discrepancies:** The contents page (p. 3) gives the unit as "9th Field Company C.E. 2nd Cdn. Divn."; the cover (p. 1) and inner title page (p. 2) both give the 3rd Canadian Division, and the diary text is headed 3rd Canadian Division throughout — transcribed as written. The place column for 1–11 April gives only a map reference ("F.5.d.4.3. Sheet 51c"), which on p. 5 is written "F.5.d.4.9." [uncertain] against "F.5.d.4.3." on pp. 4 and 6–10. The place column for 12 April gives the map reference "R.11.c.2.7. Sht 36a".
- **Diary text ends 30 April (p. 24)** with the unit stamp and the O.C.'s signature; no diary entry continues onto p. 31. The last appendix sheet in this chunk (p. 30, No. 4 Section report 2–3 April) is signed and complete, so no appendix sheet continues onto p. 31 either. The appendix itself continues beyond this chunk (reports #5–#77).
- **Appendix format change vs March:** The April work reports are not in the March tabular "Nature of Work / Location / C.E. / Inf. / Bn. / From / To / Amount Done" form. The No. 3 Section sheets (pp. 26–27) are narrative, in lettered items (A, P, O, F, K, C, B) with parties, strengths and times written into the prose. The No. 1 Section sheets (pp. 28–29) and No. 4 Section sheet (p. 30) are hand-ruled tables, but with different column sets from each other (p. 28: Party / Unit / O. / O.R. / C.E. / Time / Work Done; p. 29: Party / Unit / Strength / C.E. / Time / Work Done; p. 30: Party / C.E. / O. / O.R. / Unit / Time / Remarks). The column headings are faint or abbreviated and are transcribed as read.
- **Appendix sheet-number irregularities:** p. 26 is numbered "1" (top left); p. 27 is numbered "2" (top left and top right, the latter ticked); p. 28 bears only a tick-like mark at top right (uncertain whether a number); p. 29 is numbered "3" (top right); p. 30 is numbered "4" (top right). The No. 3 Section report (pp. 26–27) is headed "April, 1–2. 1918" but its first dugout item includes a shift dated "31st" (31 March). The report-number mapping against the diary text is consistent — No. 3 Section sheet = Report #1 (1 April), Serson's No. 1 Section sheet = Report #2, Steeves's No. 1 Section sheet = Report #3, Sproule's No. 4 Section sheet (2–3 April) = Report #4 — but the sheets themselves carry no report numbers, so this is an inference from the diary's Remarks column, not a fact stated on the sheets.
- **Duplicate photographs:** None observed in pp. 1–30.
- **Other units' material:** The appendix sheets in this chunk are all 9th Field Company work reports. The 25 April diary entry says "See Summary attached" for the M.G. Emplacements in Canteen Tunnel; no such summary appears in pp. 1–30 — it may be filed later in Appendix W.
- **Internal inconsistencies in the original (transcribed as written):** 12 April: "Relief of 466th Coy R.E. by this Coy. complete by at 6 P.M. today" although the company only bivouacked outside Bully-Grenay that night because the 466th's billets "will not be vacated until tomorrow". 24 April: Lieut Serson is given as "No 2 Section" although he is otherwise with No 1 Section, and Lieut Murdie is also given as No 2 Section in the next line. 11 April: "Major Robertson and Nos 2 and 4 Sections returned to Rear Billets in trenches at Fort George". 12 April: "3rd Cdn Div Engineer's Operation Order No 53 today date received" and the phrase "whereby the 3rd Cdn Div." is repeated. The diary writes "466 Field Coy R.E." (no "th") once on 12 April and "466th" elsewhere.
- **Form heading:** "INTELLIGENCE SUMMARY" is struck through on diary pp. 4–21; on pp. 22–24 it appears un-struck. The printed footer "A5834 Wt. W4973 M687 750,000 8/16 D. D. & L. Ltd. Forms/C.2118/13." appears at the foot of pp. 7–24.
- **Name-reading notes:** The officer written "Lieut Serson" throughout the diary text signs the No. 1 Section report (p. 28) as "D. V. Serson" [uncertain]. The No. 1 Section report of 1 April (p. 29) is signed "Cecil Steeves" [uncertain]. The No. 3 Section report (p. 27) is signed "J. S. Jones" [uncertain]; the No. 4 Section report (p. 30) is signed "J. C. Sproule" [uncertain]. The O.C.'s signature on the title page (p. 2) and at the close of the month (p. 24) is "W. R. Robertson, Major"; the daily signatures are the cursive initials "W.R.R."
- **Casualty summary (diary text, this chunk):** Lieut Steeves slightly injured by a piece of shrapnel striking him on the foot, 20 April — remained at duty. One sapper, name inserted above the line and read as "Bomb" [uncertain], slightly wounded, 28 April — at duty. No killed and no regimental numbers are given in the diary text for this chunk. Appendix sheets pp. 28–29 record "Casualties Nil"; pp. 26–27 and p. 30 carry no casualties line. Three unnamed reinforcements arrived from the Base Depot, 17 April, taken on the strength from that day.
