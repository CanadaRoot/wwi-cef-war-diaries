# War Diary Transcript — 9th Field Company, Canadian Engineers (Shorncliffe), April 1918 (Chunk C: pp. 61–90)

- **Unit:** 9th Field Company, Canadian Engineers, 3rd Canadian Division
- **LAC Item ID:** 2005998, "War diaries - 9th Battalion, Canadian Engineers", RG 9 III-D-3, vol. 4999, file 669, microfilm reels T-10841–T-10843
- **Month:** April 1918
- **Pages in this file:** LAC images pp. 61–90 (30 pages: appendix work-report sheets only; the diary text, pp. 4–24, is in earlier chunks)
- **Work-report date range:** 10–21 April 1918 (one sheet undated; no sheet dated 12 April 1918 in this chunk)
- **Transcription note:** The appendix sheets are handwritten Officers' Work Reports on squared paper, in ink and pencil, in several different hands and in two forms: narrative reports (No. 3 Section, and the #2 Section Bois de la Chaudière sheets) and tabular reports (No. 1, No. 2 and No. 4 Sections) whose column headings vary from sheet to sheet; the headings are transcribed as written on each sheet. Page numbers in this transcript are the LAC image page numbers. Sheet numbers are those written on the sheets themselves (top right unless noted).

## Notes

- **Structure observed:** All 30 pages in this chunk are appendix Officers' Work Reports — nightly (and some daytime) work reports from the four sections of the 9th Field Company. No diary-text pages, cover, or contents pages fall in pp. 61–90. Two report forms are used: narrative reports (No. 3 Section throughout; the #2 Section Bois de la Chaudière sheets, pp. 61 and 68; the #4 Section Bully Switch sheet, p. 87) and tabular reports on squared paper (No. 1, No. 2 and No. 4 Sections), whose column headings vary from sheet to sheet and are transcribed as written. The work is night work in the Lens–Avion sector: wiring (double-apron fences, wire blocks), trench cleaning and repair by trench-warden parties, dugout excavation and timbering, gas-curtain work, water-pipe (2" main) patrols, push-truck/tramway maintenance, and carrying/pushing material between dumps (Eddy, Durrand, Betty, Catapult, Osler, Brewery, Cycle, Victoria Hump, Avion).
- **Sheet numbers:** Sheets in this chunk are numbered 30 to 51 (with "a" continuations 30a, 33a, 33b, 39a, 47a [inferred], 50a), in rough chronological order: 30/30a (10 Apr), 31 (10 Apr), 32 (11 Apr), 33/33a/33b (11 Apr), 34 (13 Apr), 35 (13–14 Apr), 36 (14 Apr), 37 (14–15 Apr), 38 (15 Apr), 39/39a (15–16 Apr), 40 (16 Apr), 41 (16–17 Apr), 42 (16 Apr), 43 (17–18 Apr), 44 (17 Apr), 45 (18 Apr, P.T.O. to p. 82), 46 (18–19 Apr), 47 (19–20 Apr, P.T.O. to p. 85), 48 (19–20 Apr), 49 (19 Apr), 50/50a (20–21 Apr), 51 (20 Apr). P. 61 carries "294" written vertically at the top left instead of a sheet number in the 30–51 sequence, and carries no date. The No. 2 Section sheets use the same sheet-number sequence as the other sections (not a separate per-section sequence).
- **Date gaps:** No work-report sheet dated 12 April 1918 appears in this chunk (the sequence jumps from 11 April, sheets 32–33b, to 13 April, sheet 34). Sheets dated 1–9 April 1918 are not in this chunk (they fall in earlier chunks, if present). Within dated sheets, several reports are headed with a two-day range (e.g. "Apr. 13-14", "Apr. 15-16"), i.e. night work spanning midnight.
- **Split entries:** Reports split across page breaks: sheet 30/30a (pp. 62–63), sheet 33/33a/33b (pp. 66–68), sheet 39/39a (pp. 74–75), sheet 45 (pp. 81–82, marked P.T.O.), sheet 47 (pp. 84–85, marked P.T.O.), sheet 50/50a (pp. 88–89). Several sheets also carry trench-warden rows dated a day earlier than the report date (I/K/L rows headed 16.4.18 in the 17.4.18 report, p. 80; 18.4.18 in the 19–20.4.18 report, p. 84; 19.4.18 in the 20.4.18 report, p. 90, which also carries a row headed 21.4.18).
- **Out-of-order sheets:** Sheet 42 (p. 78, dated 16/4/18) follows sheet 41 (p. 77, dated 16–17 Apr); sheet 49 (p. 87, dated 19/4/18) follows sheet 48 (pp. 86, dated 19–20 Apr); sheet 44 (p. 80, dated 17.4.18) follows sheet 43 (p. 79, dated 17–18 Apr). The filing order is broadly chronological but not exact.
