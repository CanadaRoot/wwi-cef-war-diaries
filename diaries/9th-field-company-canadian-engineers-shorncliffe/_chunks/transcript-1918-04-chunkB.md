## Notes

- **Structure observed:** All 30 pages in this chunk (pp. 31–60) are appendix Officers' Work Reports; no diary-text pages fall in this chunk. The sheets cover work of 2–10 April 1918 and come from all four sections in rotation: No. 1 Section (pp. 31–32, 35, 38, 40, 42–44, 46), No. 2 Section (pp. 33–34, 39, 48–49, 51–52, 56–57, 59–60), No. 3 Section (pp. 48, 53, 58), and No. 4 Section (pp. 36–37, 41, 45, 47, 50, 54–55). Three different sheet layouts are used: (a) the No. 1 Section tabular form with columns Party / Unit / Strength (O / O.R.) / C.E. (O / S or O / O.R.) / Time (From / To) / Work Done; (b) the No. 4 Section tabular form with columns Party / C.E. / Inf. / O.R. / Unit / Time / Remarks; (c) narrative (non-tabular) reports from No. 2 and No. 3 Sections, written as "Party X … Time … Work" paragraphs. Several sheets are continuations of the previous sheet (p. 33 = sheet 6a continuing p. 32; p. 37 = sheet 9a continuing p. 36; p. 60 = sheet 29a continuing p. 59), and those continuation sheets repeat the column headings but carry no separate date heading.
- **Date gaps:** The sheets in this chunk are dated 2–10 April 1918. Within that span, every date 2–10 April has at least one work report in this chunk. No diary-text dates fall in this chunk, so no diary-text date range or diary-text date gaps can be reported from these pages. Note that the No. 1 Section sheets dated 2-4-18 (pp. 31–32) record work done on the night of 1–2 April (times given as 1.4.18 evening to 2.4.18 early morning), and the No. 2 Section sheet dated 8-4-18 (p. 52) records a party dated 7/4/18 working the night of 7–8 April.
- **Split entries:** Several reports run across two pages: sheet 6 (pp. 32–33), sheet 9 (pp. 36–37), sheet 29 (pp. 59–60). Individual party rows also split across the page break on pp. 35 (Party N, bathmatting SASKATOON trench), 41 (Party P), 45 (Party M / R rows), 51 (Party H dug-out rows), 57 (Party H dug-out rows) and 59 (Party H dug-out rows).
- **Duplicate photographs:** None observed in this chunk.
- **Appendix sheet-number irregularities:** Sheet numbers in this chunk run 5, 6, 6a, 7, 8, 9, 9a, 10, 11, 12, 13, 14, 15, 16, 17, 16, 19, 20, 21, 22, 23, 23a, 24, 25, 26, 27, 27a, 28, 29, 29a.
