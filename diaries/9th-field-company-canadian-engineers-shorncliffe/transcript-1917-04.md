### France, Villers-au-Bois — 9-4-17

Weather: Cold with rain in forenoon. Clearing in afternoon. High wind from Southwest.
Slow continued bombardment all last night by our guns of enemy trenches and rear defences. At 5:30 am heavy rolling barrage fire commenced by our guns on enemy lines. Bombardment could be seen reaching from Souchez on the north to south of Arras.
Our infantry attacked within three minutes of commencement of artillery barrage.
Contact aeroplane of 3rd Canadian Division was brought down soon after zero hour apparently from our own shell.
Enemy retaliation to our barrage on rear area appeared to be slight. A few shells hit near F 10 central along Road La Targette, Mont St. Eloy.

(Contd)

Signed: W.R.R., Major.

### France, Villers-au-Bois — 9-4-17 (continued)

Our artillery which had lessened its fire opened up and dispersed enemy formations.
Royal Canadian Regiment reported today to have repelled three local counter attacks in the neighbourhood of La Follie Wood, S 23 c (Vimy Map). 7th Canadian Infantry Brigade (42nd Infantry Battalion) report that right of 4th Canadian Division (11th Brigade) appeared to be meeting considerable enemy opposition and had not gained all of Hill 145. Consequently 42nd Battalion left flank was open and was compelled to swing back part of Company facing north to protect left flank, from Blunt trench S 22 a 95.10 S. Westerly to Staubwasser Weg S 22 a 55.70 which trench was dug only about 4 ft deep. The 42nd Battalion in this trench and the R.C.R. in Swischen Stellung were exposed to enfilade rifle and machine gun fire and suffered a considerable number of casualties.

(Contd)

Signed: W.R.R., Major.
