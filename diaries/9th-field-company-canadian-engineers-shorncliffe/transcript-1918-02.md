### France, Lambes — 1-2-18

Weather. Dull and cold S.W. wind.
Lieut Jones with No 3 Section completed erection of 15 Nissen huts at ANNEZIN.
Nos 1 & 4 Sections working on Signal School buildings at Liettres.
Lieut C.M.W. Steeves proceeded on leave to England today.

[Remarks column: work list. [uncertain]]

### France, Lambes — 2-2-18

Weather. Dull and warmer. West Wind.
Nos 2 and 3 Sections were given a holiday today by reason of their having completed their task (erection of 15 Nissen huts) one day ahead of allotted time.
Nos 1 and 4 Sections carried on with usual work at Liettres.

[Remarks column: work list. [uncertain]]

### France, Lambes — 3-2-18

Weather. Dull and foggy. S.E. wind.
No work for Nos 1 and 4 Sections.
Nos 2 and 3 Sections commenced work on new Park at new R.E. workshops.

[Remarks column: work list. [uncertain]]

### France, Lambes — 4-2-18

Weather. Clear and warm in forenoon. Cloudy in afternoon.
Sections at ANNEZIN and Liettres carried on with work of erecting huts.

[Remarks column: work list. [uncertain]]

## Notes

- **Structure:** 35 LAC images. Cover (p. 1), contents (p. 2), inner title page (p. 3), diary text pp. 4–18 (15 pages), Appendix "U" cover (p. 19), and 16 daily work-report sheets (pp. 20–35, numbered 1–13 at top right).
- **Contents-page discrepancies:** The contents page says "Diary Text — 16 Pages", but the diary text as filed is 15 pages (pp. 4–18). It also describes Appendix U as "Work reports issued by 9th Field Co. C.E. 21-2-18 to 28-2-18", but the appendix as filed contains work reports dated 21–26 February only (reports 1–13); no work reports for 27 or 28 February are present, although the 28 February diary entry's remarks column carries a partly illegible note reading "(Work reports with diary to [uncertain])".
- **Date gaps:** None in the diary text — all dates 1–28 February 1918 are present, in sequence.
- **Split entries:** The 16 February entry is split across pp. 8–9; 17 February across pp. 9–10; 19 February across pp. 11–12; 23 February across pp. 13–14; 26 February across pp. 16–17; and 27 February across pp. 17–18, each marked "(Continued)" or "(Cont'd)".
- **Appendix report numbering:** Thirteen reports, numbered 1–13 at top right. Three reports run to two sheets: report 6 (pp. 25–26, second sheet marked "2 over 6"), report 9 (pp. 29–30, second sheet marked "9a") and report 11 (pp. 32–33, second sheet marked "11a"). Small extra digits appear beneath the main number on pp. 21 ("2" over "3"), 22 ("3" over "8") and 24 ("5" over "0"). Report 13 (p. 35) is not a table but a narrative report by Lieut Jones on work at Cellars Camp, Neuville St Vaast, covering 23, 25 and 26 February (no 24 February entry).
- **Casualties:** The month's only named casualty is #505999 Spr White J., accidentally injured 5 February and sent to hospital (p. 5). No one is recorded killed or wounded in action. Every work report that carries a casualty line records "Casualties Nil" (reports 1–10 and 12); reports 11 (pp. 32–33) carry no casualty line.
- **Name-reading discrepancies (not reconciled):** The O.C. is written "Major N.R. Robertson" in the 10 February entry (p. 6), but the title page (p. 3) and the 28 February signature give "W. R. Robertson, Major". The place column spells the base "Lambes" throughout (pp. 4–11), while the text uses "Lambres" (pp. 7, 11, 12). The lorry rendezvous is written "Berquette" on 17 February (p. 9) and "Berguette" on 19 February (p. 11). The dump is "Ziry Dump" on 26 February (p. 16) and "Zivy Dump" on 28 February (p. 18). "Cellar camp", "Cellar Camp", "Cellar Dump" and "Cellars Camp" are all used for the same place. The hut type is written "Dechet" throughout pp. 5–6. In the wiring reports the word reads "felling" in most entries (pp. 20, 23, 24, 28, 31) and "filled" in others; transcribed as written. The work-report signatures are read "C.M.W. Steeves", "J. E. Sproule" and "J. S. Jones" [uncertain] — the diary text names Lieuts Steeves, Sproule and Jones without initials except for "Lieut C.M.W. Steeves" (1 February) and "Lieut H.V. Serson" (24 February).
- **Remarks column:** On pp. 4–6 the remarks column beside each early entry reads "work list." [uncertain]. From 21 February the remarks column cites the appendix report numbers (Reports #1–#13). Each day's diary entry from 10 February onward is initialled in the remarks column "W.R.R., Major" (spelled "Majr." on some pages).
- **Other-unit material in the appendix:** None filed as separate other-unit diary material. Report 13 notes that the Cellars Camp work was "Started by No. 4 F. C., C.E. so far as it was turned over to this unit". Infantry working parties from the 43rd, 52nd, 58th and 116th Battalions and the P.P.C.L.I. appear throughout the work reports as parties working under 9th Field Company sappers; they are not presented here as 9th Field Company activity.
- **Other notes from the pages:** p. 24 and p. 31 are cut off at the foot (the last row and part of the signature are incomplete). The abbreviation "R. Suff." (reports 8 and 10) and "Rl. Suff." (report 12) in the last row of the No. 1 Section reports could not be read with confidence. Map references in the work reports (N.32.d..., T.3.a..., T.2.d..., N.32 C..., N.31 d...) are transcribed as written; individual digits are [uncertain] where the handwriting is unclear. The work reports use party letters A–M and Q, with "Push party", "Carrying party" and "Trench Working Parties" as party descriptions.
- **Uncertain items:** See the [uncertain] flags throughout the transcript. The heaviest uncertainty is on the faint, crowded squared-paper work reports, especially pp. 20, 24, 25, 30 and 31.
