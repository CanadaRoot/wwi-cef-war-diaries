### France — 6-5-17

[Place column: France — S26c. 1.8 — Sht 36c S.W.]

Weather: Bright and warm. S.E. wind.
At 8 p.m. heavy bombardment commenced in the neighbourhood of Lens.
Enemy aeroplane flew over Company lines at dusk and dropped bombs in the immediate neighbourhood. No casualties.
Lieut Robertson with 46 N.C.Os and Sappers continued work on Vimy Ridge tramline. (See report) [Remarks column: Report #7]
1 N.C.O. and 5 Sappers working on bunks in dugouts near Pont St. 2 Sappers at Bois-des-Alleux loading fascines. 1 N.C.O. and 5 Sappers erecting Nissen Hut at Divisional Hqrs.
It is noticed that the enemy counter battery work on our batteries in the neighbourhood of the Coy Billets has increased considerably of late. The counter battery work being carried on by heavy guns (apparently naval guns)

## Notes

- **Structure:** 127 LAC images. Cover (p. 1), inner title page (p. 2), contents (p. 3), diary text pp. 4–36 (33 pages), Appendix "L" cover (p. 37), work-report sheets pp. 38–76 and pp. 83–127 (84 sheets), and a 6-sheet Operation Order (pp. 77–82) filed between work-report sheets 34 and 36.
- **Contents-page discrepancies:** The contents page (p. 3) says "Diary Text — 35 Pages", but the diary text is 33 pages (pp. 4–36). It describes Appendix L as "Work reports 1-5-17 to 30-5-17", but the appendix as filed contains work reports dated 1–31 May 1917 (including reports for 30–31 May on pp. 125–126 and a 30 May report on p. 127). The Operation Order (pp. 77–82) is not listed separately on the contents page.
- **Date gaps:** None in the diary text — all dates 1–31 May 1917 are present, in sequence.
- **Split entries:** The 6 May entry is split across pp. 8–9; 19 May across pp. 21–22; 20 May across pp. 22–23; 22 May across pp. 25–26; 23 May across pp. 26–27–28; 24 May across pp. 28–29, each marked "(Contd)" or "(continued)". The 25 May entry breaks off mid-sentence at the foot of p. 30 ("There were 9 casualties in this party but…") with only a partly legible line below the printed footer ("and from the 9—" [uncertain]); it is not continued on p. 31, which starts the 26 May entry fresh.
- **Appendix sheet numbers irregular:** Sheets are numbered 1–34 (pp. 38–76), then the Operation Order (pp. 77–82, its own sheets (1)–(6)), then sheets 36–71 (pp. 83–127) with gaps and duplicates — no sheet numbered 35 appears anywhere; sheet 62 is missing (the 27/28 May report, pp. 115–116, bears no sheet number); sheet 66 appears twice (pp. 120 and 121); sheet 68a (p. 123) precedes sheet 68 (p. 124); sheets 27 and 26 are out of order (p. 69 is sheet 27, p. 70 is sheet 26); pp. 59 and 60 show no sheet number (possibly lost to the file-punch hole at top right); the number on p. 63 is partly cut off at the sheet edge; p. 68 is written on a printed Army Form C.212b "Messages and Signals" form and carries the number 67, outside the work-report sequence. Supplementary sheets 41a (p. 89), 54a/54b (pp. 104–105), 56a, 60a (p. 113) and 68a (p. 123) are interleaved. The stamped appendix foliation at the foot of the sheets runs one behind the LAC image page number through much of the appendix and repeats (pp. 113–127 are stamped 111, 113, 113, 115, 114, 115, 116, 117, 118, 119, 120, 121, 122, 123, 124). The diary's 18 May entry cites Report #35 against the Operation Order, which may be what #35 refers to.
- **Appendix cover note wrong:** P. 37 carries the handwritten note "reports 7 + 17 missing", but both reports are present — Report #7 (6-5-17) at p. 46 and Report #17 (11-5-17, No. 4 Section) at p. 56.
- **Casualty discrepancies:**
  - Regimental number #715541 is given for two different men: #715541 Dvr Spence A. (sick, 17 May, p. 19) and #715541 Spr Lewis, W. (sick, 22 May, p. 26). Both verified by enlarged crops; transcribed as written.
  - Spr Robinson: the diary (27 May, p. 32) records #506916 Spr Robinson, P wounded slightly by shellfire and returned to duty; the appendix (night of 27/28 May, p. 113) records "Casualties: — One / Spr. Robinson" with no regimental number and no killed/wounded stated. Possibly the same man; not merged.
  - 23 May: the diary records a Sapper wounded by enemy aeroplane machine-gun fire with his name cut off at the foot of p. 26 ("wounding Spr [uncertain]") and, on p. 28, #500400 Spr. Smith T wounded by machine gun fire and evacuated. Likely the same man; not merged.
  - 25 May: the diary entry breaks off before naming the 9 casualties in Lieut. Walley's party; appendix Report #54 (p. 103) gives "9 Casualties" for the same jumping-off trench party (116th Battalion, Victoria Road) but also gives no names.
  - Appendix casualty lines are otherwise unnamed: "4. Infantry" (night of 21/22 May, p. 90), "One Rfl." [uncertain] (night of 22/23 May, p. 91), "Two Infantry" (24/25 May, p. 99). All other appendix sheets in May record "Nil".
  - #541966 Spr Tierney, W.J. is recorded only as buried on 1 May (p. 4); no date or cause of death is stated in the diary.
- **Name-reading discrepancies across the month (same hands read differently):** The trench officer is written "Mudie" (diary text, pp. 4, 5, 11, 13, 14), "Mundie" (15–16 May and on leave, pp. 17, 31), "Murrie" [uncertain] (14 May, p. 16) and "Murdie" (31 May diary text, p. 36, and the W. C. Murdie signatures on the work reports). The officer signing the work reports is read "H. H. Robertson", "H. D. Robertson" and "H. W. Robertson" on different sheets; the diary text consistently names him Lieut. H.D. Robertson. Jones" on pp. 122 and 125 [uncertain]. The #3 Section sheets (pp. 98, 102) are signed "H. D. L." [uncertain]. The Operation Order (p. 78, item 7) writes "Lt Robinson" without the "t" (Robertson elsewhere); transcribed as written.
