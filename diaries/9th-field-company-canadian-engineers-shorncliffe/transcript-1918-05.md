### France, Bully-Grenay — 1-5-18

Weather:— Dull and warmer. N.E. wind.
Information received from C.R.E. that this Company would move with the 9th Brigade billeting group and move under orders of the 9th Brigade.
O.C. attended Brigade Major 9th C.I. Bde and arranged certain details re. embussing of this Coy.
Advance party of the 103rd Field Coy R.E. arrived at Rear Billets of this Coy.
Lieut Murdie with No 4 Section and party from 7th M.G. Coy and 43rd Battn, repairing tramline, patrolling pipe line and working on M.G. Emplacement (N.1.c.75.40.)
No 2 Section with parties from the 43rd and 58th Battalions making and placing trench blocks, cleaning out Counter, Cosy + Nash trenches, placing gas curtains, (Left Sector) Completed No 12 D.O. on Nestor [uncertain].
Sappers at Rear Billets cleaning, repairing and loading wagons.

[Remarks column: Appendix, with a struck-through word beneath it — illegible [uncertain].]

Signed: N.R.R., Major.

### Bully Grenay — 2-5-18

Weather: Fine and clear. West wind.
Relief of 9th Field Coy by 103rd Field Coy R.E. completed by 3 P.M. today.
Major Robertson, Officers and other ranks returned to Rear Billets.
Lieut Joyes with party of cyclists from all Sections proceeded as advance billeting party to Marquay.
9th C.I. Bde O.O. 160 received whereby this Coy with 9th C.I. Bde billeting group will move to Valhuon area. Dismounted portion of the Company to embus at 1:30 a.m. tomorrow. Transport to march independently 6 a.m. tomorrow.

Signed: N.R.R., Major.

### Marquay — 3-5-18

Weather: Clear and warmer. West wind.
Company embussed at 2 a.m. and proceeded to Marquay arriving at 6 a.m. Billeted in houses and barns in the village. Transport left Bully-Grenay at 6 a.m. and arrived at Marquay at 4 P.M.

Signed: N.R.R., Major.

### Marquay — 4-5-18

Weather: Dull. North wind.
Company engaged in Gas drill, Squad drill, Rifle Exercise.
9th C.I. Bde O.O. No 161 received with march table attached whereby 9th Field Coy C.E. to move first on Brigade march from Marquay to Burbure. Head of column to pass from Marquay 10 A.M. and not to cross Pernes-Divion Road before 2 P.M.
O.C. attended Brigade Major at la Thieuloye re certain points re. move.

(Continued)

### 4-5-18 (continued)

C.R.E. 19-20 referring to 3rd Cdn Div. O.O. 158 received whereby 9th Field Coy C.E. to move from Marquay to Burbure under orders of 9th C.I. Bde and advising C.R.E. office to open in Norrent Fontes.

Five reinforcements arrived from the Base Depot and are taken on the strength of the Coy as from this date.

Signed: N.R.R., Major.

### Burbure — 5-5-18

Weather: Clear in forenoon. Rain in afternoon. South wind.
Lieut Baldock with two Pontoon and one trestle wagons, teams and drivers left Marquay at 6 am and proceeded to Rebreuve, pursuant to C.R.E. orders received and loaded on Company pontoon and trestle equipment.
Company with transport fell in and marched off from Marquay under the O.C. and proceeded via Bours, Valhuon, Pernes and Rainbeet to Burbure, leaving at 10 a.m. and arriving at Burbure at 4:15 P.M.
Lieut. Baldock with pontoons, etc, joined the Coy on the line of march at Rainbeet.
Company billeted in Burbure in houses and barns. Horses under corrugated iron shelters.

Signed: N.R.R., Major.

### Auchy-au-Bois — 25-5-18

Weather:— Dull and cloudy. S.E. wind.
C.R.E. 11-154 of 25-5-18 received giving ammended list of Officers selected for temporary appointments to Battalions and Bridging Unit as approved by C.E. Canadian Corps, whereby inter. alia, 2nd Section of "B" Coy 9th Cdn Engrs Battn is to be commanded by Lieut H.L. Chittenden C.E. (from C.E.R.D.) vice Lieut J.E. Sproule, who has not completed his 9 months in France. Also advising Lieut Walley and Lieut Chittenden to report to 9th C.E. Battalion.
Reinforcements from C.E.R.D. including 5th Canadian Division Engineers to be expected at the rate of 100 O.R. per battalion in a few days.
9th Field Coy C.E. fell in on parade with transport at 7:30 a.m., moved off at 8 a.m. under command of Major Robertson and proceeded via Hurionville, Amettes to Auchy-au-Bois where the Company billeted in very congested billets in the west end of the village.
Groups 7, 8 and 9 of the 123rd Pioneer Battalion also marched in and billeted further west in the hamlet of Tatineloux [uncertain].
The 8th Field Coy with their groups of Pioneers also moved in and billeted in Auchy-au-Bois temporarily.

Signed: N.R.R., Major.

### Auchy-au-Bois — 26-5-18

Weather. Cloudy. South wind.
Major Bogne, Major Brown, Capt Macdonald and 9 Subalterns of the 123rd Pioneer Battalion reported to Major Robertson for duty. Details of the organisation of the 9th C.E. Battalion proceeded with being worked out by Major Robertson and Major Boone.
The N.C.Os and promotions of N.C.Os being decided upon as far as possible, together with their allotment to Coys.
The breaking up of groups and forming into Coys is interfered with by the lack of accommodation in billets.
C.R.E. 11/185 received (for information) advising that C.R.E. had indented for transport and horses to complete establishment of Engineer Battalions.
C.R.E. 8/212 of this date received advising that groups 7, 8 & 9 of 123rd Battalion and other details who have arrived are automatically absorbed into the 9th Canadian Engineer Battalion, and come under Major Robertson C.O. for all purposes.

Signed: N.R.R., Major.

The Battalion paraded today under Major Robertson for the first time as a Battalion, Hqrs, A B & C Coys being on parade. Battalion engaged in Battalion drill. Inspection by C.O.
12 O.R. arrived as reinforcements from the C.E.R.D. today.

Signed: N.R.R., Major.

### Auchy-au-Bois — 31-5-18

Weather:— Clear and warm. S.W. wind.
C.R.E. with his Adjutant inspected the 9th C.E. Battalion 10 a.m. today and after inspection, Battalion marched past in column of fours.
The 3rd Canadian Engineer Brigade Band was on parade.
The inspection appeared to be quite satisfactory. The men were astonishingly smart and the marching exceedingly good, considering the short time they have been together.

(Contd.)

Signed: N.R.R., Major.
