### Maroeuil, France — 1-12-16

Weather.— Dull and cool.

Sappers at Rear Billets making bath mats, bath mat rests and slats, and revetting screens. Party of three men working on 9th Brigade hut at Bray. Sappers put in new trough to conduct water from above water falls at old cotton mill to horses watering trough.

Lieut. WALLEY with sappers of No. 3 Section worked on Right Sub-section, cleaning out and revetting front line, maintaining in good condition VASE, CLAUDOT, DOUAI + BESSAN trenches, and building Y.M.C.A., FIRST AID POST + Bn. H.Q. dugout on ELBE.

Lieut. WEST with No. 2 Section worked on Left Sub-Section building GUILHERMOT trench mortar emplacement, cleaning out and revetting front line and improving TERRITORIAL + ASHTON

Lieut. MURDIE with sappers from No. 2 Section worked on Ambulance Siding, lowering water main and maintenance of Tram line. See Work Reports as indicated by numbers in margin.

### Maroeuil, France — 2-12-16

Weather.— Cloudy and cool.

Sappers at Rear Billets making bath mat supports and slats, revetting screens and barbed wire concertinas. Fixing stove pipes in drying shed at Divisional Baths. Finished Officers quarters and hut at BRAY.

## Notes

- **Contents page vs actual pages:** The contents page (p. 3) states "Diary Text — 34 Pages" — this matches: diary text runs pp. 4–37 (34 pages), 1–31 December 1916, all at Maroeuil, France. Appendix G is listed as "Work reports issued by 9th Field Co. 29-11-16 to 30-11-16 and 1-12-16 [uncertain] to 30-12-16" — the appendix (pp. 38–178, 141 pages) does include late-November reports filed in the December volume, and one report dated 12 November 1916 (p. 95).
- **Date gaps:** No date gaps in the diary text — every day 1–31 December 1916 is covered. Page 14 is a 2-line continuation of the 10 December entry with the rest of the page blank.
- **Appendix dating anomalies:** The appendix work reports (pp. 38–60) actually cover 29/30 November – 4/5 December 1916, i.e. November reports filed in the December volume; p. 39 is explicitly headed "(First 3 Reports:— (To accompany War Diary for November)". Appendix numbering is inconsistent: the diary-text remarks cite reports #76–#90 (26–30 Dec), while the appendix pages carry their own #1–#10 / section-based numbering and report dates a month earlier. In the later appendix ranges, report #56 does not appear (pp. 121–150, #51–#67), and #84 and #87 are missing (pp. 151–178, #69–#89); several pages carry no appendix number at all.
- **Weekday errors in the original:** Lieut. Walley's reports head p. 62 "Tuesday 4th Dec 16" and p. 67 "Wed. 5th Dec/16", but 4 December 1916 was a Monday and 5 December a Tuesday — the weekday labels are one day ahead of the calendar dates (later sheets agree with the calendar). Page 39's header reads "Thursday 29/30 Nov/16", but 29 November 1916 was a Wednesday (30 November was Thursday). Transcribed as written.
- **"INTELLIGENCE SUMMARY" heading:** Struck through on most diary pages, but left un-struck on pp. 8, 11, 12, 14, 16, 17.
- **Spelling variants in the original:** Frevin-Capelle / FREVENT-CAPELLE; VISTULA / VISSEC (p. 7).
- **Physical condition:** P. 39's first two table columns are obscured by a stain (plus an "ORIGINAL COPIES" stamp); p. 91's left edge is physically damaged, cutting off the start of several lines; p. 98 contains only a single entry with the rest of the sheet blank; p. 150's header is torn at the top, so its exact date and appendix number are flagged [uncertain]; p. 172 shows heavy show-through (a second, fainter signature) from the reverse; p. 71 has one location entry blacked out.
- **Casualties:** No killed or wounded company men are recorded in the December diary text or appendix. Every appendix work report that records casualties says "Nil", with two exceptions: p. 80 (8/9 Dec) records "Infantry one blown to pieces" (no name given), and p. 114 (16 Dec) records that work was interrupted by shelling and "[uncertain] men of working party killed" — the number is illegible and is flagged, not guessed. One O.R. was evacuated (29 Dec, p. 34). One disciplinary case: Dvr. Flood J.C. (No. 45373 [uncertain]) heard by the C.R.E. on 7 Dec for disobedience of orders and remanded for F.G.C.M.
- **Uncertain items:** 1,660 `[uncertain]` flags in this file, concentrated in the appendix work reports (dense, small cursive handwriting on squared paper, especially the tabular sheets). Diary-text pages needed very few flags. Flagged items include illegible signatures (the 1–2 Dec diary signatures are an illegible flourish, not the usual "H.P.R."), place names, trench references, and figures — all flagged rather than guessed.
