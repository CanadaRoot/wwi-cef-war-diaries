- **Pages in this file:** LAC images pp. 1–71 (71 pages: cover, contents, 16 diary text pages, 53 appendix pages)

### Ecoivres, France — 1-2-17

Weather: Dull and cold.

Sappers at Rear Billets working in Coy yard making wire concertinas, dugout frames, bath mat rests and revetting screens.

C.S.M. with 5 Sappers and party from 60th Battn and Brigade party cleaning out well and tramline, unloading Engineering material and clearing ration dumps. See report.

# 521829 [uncertain] L/Cpl FELTHAM, R. Sick. Sent to Field Ambulance.

Two reinforcements reported and are taken on the strength of the Coy from today.

Lieut. JONES with No 1 Section proceeded to Adv. Billets. Lieut. ROBERTSON with No 3 Sec returned to Rear Billets.

### Auchel, France — 15-2-17

Weather: Clear and cold.

Company fell in under the O.C. and moved off from Maisnil-les-Ruitz at 8 a.m. and proceeded to Auchel via Ruitz, Bruay and Marles-les-Mines without incident.

Slight difficulty was met by transport at the commencement of the march on account of the icy condition of the roads.

Lieut. Robinson with two other ranks who had been attending the Canadian Corps School of Instruction at Pernes returned to the unit reporting at Auchel.

[Remarks: W.R.R. Major — signature, [uncertain]]

## Notes

- **Appendix naming discrepancy:** The cover (p. 1) says "With appendix I"; the contents page (p. 2) lists the appendix as "Appendix A. Work report issued by 9th Field Co. [one word struck through, illegible] 1-2-17 to 12-2-17."; the appendix title page (p. 19) reads "OFFICERS-WORK-REPORTS. Appendix "I" To Accompany War Diary. 9th Field Company Canadian Engineers. 3rd Can. Div. FEBRUARY — 1917", signed W. R. Robertson Major, O.C. 9th Field Coy. Canadian Engineers, with a 28 FEB. 1917 stamp.
- **Contents vs actual pages:** The contents page says "Diary Text 16 Pages" — this matches pp. 3–18 (16 pages). The appendix, however, is far larger than the contents page implies: pp. 19–71 (53 pages) of daily work reports, reports #1–#36, covering 1–12 February 1917 only. The appendix ends mid-series at Work Report 36 (12 February); there is no closing page or end-of-month statement.
- **Duplicate page:** P. 52 is a duplicate / second copy of p. 50 — the same Daily Work Report 22a, No. 2 Section, 8/2/17, same handwriting and entries. It is transcribed in full and must not be double-counted in any index.
- **Report-order anomalies in the appendix:** P. 61 is headed "29b" and p. 62 "29a" (both 10/2/17, No. 1 Section) — the a/b order is reversed in file order. P. 65 is headed "33" [uncertain] and p. 66 "32b" (both 11/2/17, No. 1 Section) — the numbering is out of sequence in file order; p. 65's number may be "32a" misread, or a sheet is misfiled. P. 69 is headed "35" (possible "a") and p. 70 "35b".
- **Undated appendix sheets:** P. 27 (Report 7a), p. 30 (Report 8b) and p. 34 (Report 11b) carry no date on the sheet; their dates (3, 3 and 4 February) are taken from their paired "a" sheets (pp. 28, 29, 33).
- **Date discrepancies as written:** P. 35 (Work Report 12, headed 4.2.17) has one entry reading "5.2.17". P. 49 (Work Report 21, headed 7.2.17) has dated entries spanning 6.2.17, 7.2.17 and 8.2.17 — a running log. P. 55 (Work Report 24, headed 8.2.17) has a last entry reading "8.2.16" as written, almost certainly "8.2.17", transcribed as written and not silently corrected.
- **Torn page:** The top of p. 20 is torn; the heading reads "DAILY — [torn] — REPORT".
- **Signatory changes:** Pp. 3–10 signed "G.W. Capt."; pp. 11–12 signed "H.H.P. Major" [uncertain — initials not fully clear, could be W.H.P.]; pp. 13–17 signed "W.R.R. Major" [uncertain]; p. 18 signed in full "W. R. Robertson Major. O.C. 9th Field Coy. Canadian Engineers." with the 9th Field Company Canadian Engineers 28 FEB. 1917 stamp.
- **Scribal repetition:** P. 6, 4-2-17 (Contd.): "received Copy No. 3, dated 4-2-17, received with reference to..." — the word "received" appears twice as written, transcribed as written.
- **Relief and handover:** 3rd Canadian Divisional Engineers Operation Order No. 19 (4-2-17) covers the relief of the 3rd Canadian Division by the 2nd Canadian Division and of the 9th Field Coy by the 4th Field Coy C.E. Handover of works, maps, documents, stores and billets was completed at midnight 12/13 February; the company then marched Ecoivres to Maisnil-les-Ruitz (14 February) and to Auchel (15 February), remaining at Auchel on training until 28 February.
- **Personnel:** Sgt C.A.H. Carson granted a commission in the Canadian Engineers and posted temporarily to the company from 11 February (formal instructions received 13 February; the name/number are in a small inserted hand on p. 11 and are partly legible). #482971 2/Cpl Delakey left 21 February for Officers Cadet School R.E., Chatham. Capt. E. W. Harrison and Lieut. R. L. Dunsmore proceeded to England 25 February; Lieut. B. Ross of the 2nd Field Coy, 1st Canadian Division, reported for duty 26 February.
- **Casualties:** No killed or wounded are recorded in the diary text for February 1917. Diary-text sick cases sent to Field Ambulance: #521829 [uncertain] L/Cpl Feltham, R. (1 Feb); #541944 Spr Neal, T.G. (2 Feb); #541749 Spr McNally (10 Feb); #692820 Spr Fraser, E. (11 Feb); #502776 A/L/Cpl Pemberton, A.W. (13 Feb); No 541924 Dvr Kennedy, J.C. and 541842 Spr McKay, S. (21 Feb); #192900 Spr Clark, J.S. (27 Feb). Appendix work reports that state casualties read "Casualties — Nil." (pp. 25, 30, 34, 38, 43, 48, 53, 58, 61, 66, 70). P. 62 mentions a casualty in the Strong Point entry, but the wording is illegible and is marked [uncertain], not counted. Two Sappers were sent to the Canadian Base, Havre, as medically unfit for front line work (17 February).
- **Appendix legibility:** The appendix work reports are faint, small handwriting on squared paper. The narrow intermediate columns (party letters, strength figures, From/To times) are largely illegible and are not transcribed as numbers where they could not be read; "Amount Done" phrases are frequently partly illegible and are marked [uncertain] inline. Figures given in the tables are best readings only and must not be treated as verified.
- **Place-name spellings** are as written in the originals and are not modernised (e.g. Ecoivres, Maisnil-les-Ruitz, Auchel, Thelus, Guillermot, Claudot, Vistula, Vase, Elbe, Douai, Rocade, Mowcop, Reitz, Zivy, Maitland, Territorial, Glasgow, Lower Glasgow, Fort B, Fort Redoubt). Abbreviations are as written (D.O., T.M., H.T.M., S.A.A., B. mats, C.E., C.F.A., C.S.M., O.C., C.R.E.).
- **No date gaps** in the diary text: 1–28 February 1917 is continuous, one entry per day, with entries routinely running across page boundaries.
