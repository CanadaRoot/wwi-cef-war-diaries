# War Diary Transcript — 9th Field Company, Canadian Engineers (Shorncliffe), June 1916

- **Unit:** 9th Field Company, Canadian Engineers, 3rd Canadian Division
- **LAC Item ID:** 2004882 ("War diaries - 9th Battalion, Canadian Engineers"), RG 9 III-D-3, vol. 4998, file 668, microfilm reels T-10840–T-10841
- **Month / Volume:** June 1916, Volume 2 (covers 1 June – 30 June 1916)
- **Pages in this file:** LAC images pp. 1–139 (139 pages: cover, title page, blank reverse, contents, 34 diary text pages, Appendix "B" cover and Officers' Work Reports Sheets No. 1–79, and a 7-page copy of a commendation letter dated 19 June 1916)
- **Diary text date range:** 1 June – 30 June 1916
- **Commanding officer / signatory:** Captain H. R. Robertson, O.C. 9th Field Coy., Canadian Engineers (signed on the title page; diary pages initialled "H.R.R. Capt" / "W.R.R. Capt"; 30 June entry signed in full "W. R. Robertson, Capt Major")
- **Transcription note:** The diary is handwritten on Army Form C. 2118 ("War Diary or Intelligence Summary"). The printed heading "INTELLIGENCE SUMMARY" is struck through on the diary pages, except on p. 27 where it is left unstruck. The "Hour" column is blank throughout, except for a note on p. 28. The Place column on the diary pages gives map references (e.g. "H.13 d.2.7, Ref Map Belgium, Sh 28 N.W.") rather than a place name. Appendix "B" is a set of handwritten Officers' Work Reports on graph paper, each headed "WORK REPORT" with a sheet number; several cells are cramped or faint and are marked [uncertain] where they could not be read with confidence.
- **Contents-page note:** The contents page (p. 4) lists Diary Text 34 pages and Appendix B — work report 1-6-16 to 30-6-16. The diary text is indeed 34 pages (pp. 5–38). The appendix sheets run No. 1–79, covering the nights of 31 May/1 June – 29/30 June 1916; the diary text cites sheets #41–#75, but the appendix sheets bound at pp. 40–56 are numbered 1–12 and cover the first week of June, not 18–30 June — they appear to be a separate, earlier sequence bound with the June diary.
- **Gaps:** No work report for the night of 19–20 June — the sheets jump from No. 42 (night 18–19 June) to No. 43 (night 20–21 June). P. 52 is the blank reverse of a work report sheet; p. 127 is a blank page (presumably the reverse of Sheet 74).

Weather Fine bright & warm.

## Transcriber's notes — June 1916, Part 1 (pp. 1–28)

- **Date gaps (days with no entry in this part):** none — every day 1–17 June 1916 has an entry. The 18–30 June entries are on pp. 29 onwards, outside this part.
- **Contents vs. pages present:** the contents page (p. 4) lists "Diary Text — 34 Pages" and "Appendix B. — Work report issued by 9th Field Co. 1-6-16 to 30-6-16". This part contains diary text pp. 5–28 only (24 diary pages); the remaining diary text (to p. 38, if the 34-page count runs from p. 5) and Appendix B itself are not in this part. The Remarks column throughout refers to Appendix B sheets numbered 1–40, but no appendix sheets are included in pp. 1–28.
- **Place column:** every diary page gives the same map reference in the Place column, read as "H.13 d.2.7, Ref Map Belgium, Sh 28 N.W." The first token is faint on some pages; it is read consistently as H.13 d.2.7.
- **"F.C.C.E.":** the unit is written throughout as "9th F.C.C.E." (9th Field Company, Canadian Engineers), and the 8th and 7th Field Companies as "8th F.C.C.E." and "7th F.C.C.E."
- **Robertson vs. Robinson:** both spellings occur in the diary as written — "Lieut Robertson" (most entries) and "Lieut Robinson" (4 June wagon escort, 7 June wagon escort, 16 June). They are transcribed exactly as written on each page; whether they are one man or two is not stated in the diary.
- **"65 S" / "65 R":** trench designations as written; on 5 June the diary first writes "65 S" with a word struck through after it, then "65 R" for the rest of that entry.
- **Page 10 casualties:** the casualty list is cramped and the first line is hard to parse — "Spr McDonald G.A. S.W.L [uncertain] Temple #502922" is transcribed as written; it may conflate two men (McDonald and Temple, #502922). The numbers #502482 (Murdock A, slightly wounded) and #541921 (Gillis J., abdominal pains) are legible.
- **Page 24:** the paragraph about Lieut Robertson is heavily corrected in the original (words struck through and inserted above the line, including "Robertson" written over "Robinson" and a struck phrase about "completing wiring on Hill St"), so the wording there is uncertain in places.
- **Page 26 marginal note:** "June 17/16" is written in the Hour column, bracketed against the 16 June entry; its meaning is unclear (it may be the date the entry was written up).
- **Page 27:** the printed "INTELLIGENCE SUMMARY" heading is not struck through on this page, unlike all the other diary pages in this part.
- **Page 28 Hour column:** the note reads "See Page for June 16th" [uncertain], overlapping the O.C.'s signature; its meaning is unclear.
- **Casualties in this part:** 6 June — Spr Grove F.J. (#502825) killed by H.E. shell; Spr Campbell J (#502770) wounded, died of wounds (death confirmed 8 June); 2nd Cpl. Ennis (#500033) wounded. 4 June — Spr McDonald G.A. [uncertain, see above], Spr Murdock A (#502482) slightly wounded, Spr Gillis J. (#541921) abdominal pains, all sent to hospital. 5 June — Lieut Young slightly wounded in the head by H.E. shell fragments and one Sapper slightly wounded; both returned to duty. 7 June — Spr Ward A. (#502599) shrapnel wound in leg, Spr Foley H. (#502563) shrapnel wound, Spr Jones E. (#502541) shrapnel wound in arm. 9 June — Spr Brealey R. ## Transcriber's notes — June 1916, Part 2 (pp. 29–56)

- **Date gaps (days with no diary entry in this part):** none — diary entries run daily from 18 June to 30 June 1916. (Two dates share a page: 20–21 June on p. 31; 23–24 June on p. 33; 27–28 June on p. 36.)
- **Diary text vs. appendix sheets:** the diary pages cite appendix sheets #41–#47, #51–#53 and #57–#75; the appendix sheets in this part are numbered 1–12 (with 2a, 2b and 9a) and cover work reports for the nights of 31 May/1 June – 7/8 June 1916, i.e. the first week of the month, not the 18–30 June dates of the diary text. The diary's cited sheet numbers (#41 onwards) do not match the Appendix "B" sheet numbers in this part; the appendix here appears to be a separate, earlier sequence of work reports bound with the June diary.
- **"W.R.R. Capt.":** the diary pages are initialled "W.R.R. Capt."; the 30 June entry is signed in full "W. R. Robertson, Capt Major", and the Appendix "B" cover is signed "W. R. Robertson, Capt."

## Transcriber's notes — June 1916 (Part 3, pp. 57–84)

- **What this part contains:** No cover, contents page or diary text — all 28 pages are appendices to the June 1916 diary: work reports (Sheets No. 13–36, consecutive, no sheet missing) and three notes written on the backs of sheets (pp. 72, 77, 80 are the backs of Sheets No. 26, 30 and 32). Sheet No. 13 (pp. 57–58) is a two-page narrative report; the rest are single-page tabular work reports, except Sheets No. 34–36 (pp. 82–84), which are short narrative reports.
- **Date coverage:** The reports cover night work from the night of 7/8 June to the night of 15/16 June 1916. There is no diary text in this part, so no day-by-day diary gap list applies; the work reports themselves are dated by night.
- **Section officers:** Lieut. A. M. West signs for No. 1 and No. 4 Sections; Lieut. E. G. Young for No. 1 Section; Lieut. P. H. Dunsmore for No. 2 Section; Lieut. H. O. Robertson for No. 3 Section. Young's signature is also initialled "E.G.Y." on the three back-of-sheet notes.
- **Times written "p.m." where "a.m." seems meant:** p. 62 gives "Dismissed 12.30 p.m." and p. 79 gives "Dismissed 2.30 p.m." — both are dismissal times after midnight work, so "a.m." is likely intended; both are transcribed as written.
- **Signing dates vs. work dates:** Several sheets are signed a day or two after the night worked (e.g. Sheet No. 27, night 11-12 June, signed 12/6/16; Sheet No. 31, night 13-14 June, signed 13/6/16; Sheet No. 33, night 14-15 June, signed 15/6/16).
- **"P.O.":** The rendezvous "P.O." appears on many No. 2 Section sheets; its meaning is not stated on the pages.
- **"RS71":** A trench reference written "RS71" on p. 66 (170 yds and 140 yds measured from the "RS71 line").
- **Sketches:** p. 67 carries a small "Plan of Work" sketch of the Hellfire Corner barricade at the Menin Road / Zillebeke Road junction; p. 79 carries a "Sketch of Fence" showing four strands of barbed wire with a French wire apron.
- **Casualties:** Every report in this part records "Nil" / "none" / "—" / "no casualties" except p. 84, whose casualties line is largely illegible (it appears to describe someone hurt, but the wording cannot be read with confidence). No named casualties appear in this part.
- **Weather and incidents:** Rain all night 14/15 June (p. 80); a report of a German advance on the night of 14/15 June turned out to be a false alarm (p. 80); heavy shelling delayed and twice interrupted the Hill St wiring on the night of 13/14 June (pp. 76–77); an inspection tour of trenches 47–52 on the night of 14/15 June was cut short when the party was surrounded by shell fire (p. 82); three "T" rifle pits on Regent St were badly damaged by shells (p. 72).
- **Places named:** Regent St (Upper and Lower), Reserve Line, Culvert, Menin Road, Gordon House, China Wall, Hellfire Corner, Yeomanry Post, Maple Lodge, Hill St, Vigo St, John Border Lane, New Bond St, Oxford St, Zillebeke Road, Asylum.
- **No pages missing:** LAC images pp. 57–84 are all present and legible, though the handwriting on pp. 57–58, 64, 65, 70, 82 and 84 is faint in places and several words could not be read.

## Transcriber's notes — June 1916, Part 4 (pp. 85–112)

- **Sheets:** Work Report Sheets No. 37–61 run consecutively with no gaps. Sheet No. 41a is an additional sheet between Nos. 41 and 42. The "Notes" on p. 99 are the continuation of Sheet No. 48 and carry no sheet number. Sheet No. 38 spans pp. 86–87.
- **Nights covered:** 16–17 June through 26–27 June 1916. No work report for the night of 19–20 June appears in this part (Sheets jump from No. 42, night 18–19 June, to No. 43, night 20–21 June).
- **Casualties:** one shell-shock casualty (p. 88, night 17 June); two men of a carrying party wounded (p. 92, night 18–19 June). All other reports in this part record nil / no casualties.
- **Weather:** Fine on 18 and 22 June; Fair with Light Shower on 23 June; Fair with Light Showers on 24 June.
- **Places:** Mount Sorrel / Sorrel trenches (trenches 46–52), New Oxford Street, Old Oxford Street, Union Street, Upper Union Street, Dormy House Lane, Zillebeke, Zillebeke Road, Zouave Wood, Y Wood, Gordon House, Half Way House, Lille Gate, Hellfire Corner, China Wall, Regent Street, the Culvert, Valley Cottages.

- **Officers named:** Lieut. H. D. Robertson; Lieut. C. M. West; Lieut. C. M. Robertson; Lieut. P. H. [uncertain surname]; Lieut. G. W. Harrison; Mr. Harrison; Mr. Reynolds (P.P.C.L.I.); an unnamed infantry officer criticised in the remarks on pp. 86–87; an illegible signature on Sheet No. 48 (p. 98).
- **Notable content:** the remarks on pp. 86–87 complain that the infantry officer in charge of a working party took no responsibility for getting the work done and hampered it; heavy shelling at Mount Sorrel on the night of 17 June, with the party standing to for 1 hour 15 minutes expecting an attack; a carrying party that did not reach Valley Cottages until 11.45 p.m. (p. 92); and a reconnaissance for a deep trench and drainage on the night of 21–22 June (p. 97).
