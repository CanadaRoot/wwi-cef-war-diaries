## Page 13 (August 1916, p. 13) — [diary text]

### H.13.d.3.7 — 8-8-16 (continued)

Received operation order No. 11, Copy No 3 at 5.45 P.M. instructing the 9th F.C.C.E. to take over work in trenches 29 to 33 inclusive from 1st F.C.C.E. after work on the night of 9th/10th August; also to take over work in trenches 34 to 38 inclusive from the 2nd F.C.C.E. after work on the night of 9th/10th August; also that the 9th F.C.C.E. will arrange with 1st F.C.C.E. for taking over 1st F.C.C.E. Advanced Billets in Bluff Tunnels.

The O.C. returned from Advanced Billets on receipt of order and with Mr. Harrison attended 1st and 2nd Field Companies to discuss matters in connection with taking over the work.

A siren gas alarm was sounded at 11.15 P.M. from camp to south and was passed rapidly from camp to camp by bugle. The 9th F.C.C.E. fell in and stood to, the drivers standing to their horses. As symptoms of gas became quite pronounced from N.E., helmets were put on at 11.45 P.M. and kept on until 12.25 A.M. No serious results were noticed although two [continued on p. 14]

## Transcriber's notes — August 1916

### Completeness

- All 167 pages of the August 1916 section were transcribed: cover (p. 1), title page (p. 2), contents (p. 3), inner diary title page (p. 4), diary text pp. 5–45 (41 pages, 1–31 August 1916, every date has an entry; no date gaps), Appendix D title page (p. 46), and Appendix D work reports #1–#83 (pp. 47–167). No pages are missing from the sequence; no non-report appendix item (order, map, sketch) appears in this month — the entire appendix is the numbered work reports.
- The contents page (p. 3) states "Diary Text — 41 Pages", which matches pp. 5–45 exactly. It dates Appendix D "1-8-16 to 26-8-16", but the reports run from 31 July–1 August (Report #1) through 24–25 August (Reports #81d, #82d, #83), so the contents-page date range for Appendix D is incomplete.

### Place references (diary text)

- The Place column changes within the diary text: p. 5 reads "H.18.D.2.7"; pp. 8–37 read "H.13.d.3.7" (Ref. Map Belgium Sht. 28 N.W.); pp. 38–45 read "L.25.c.2.4" (Ref. Map Belgium and part of France, Sheet 27). The change to L.25.c.2.4 coincides with the company's move to new billets on 26 August.

### Signatures

- Diary pages are signed "N.R.R. Capt." on early pages, then "Scott Capt." from p. 14 onward, then "N.R.R. Maj." from p. 31/32 onward. The identity behind "Scott Capt." was not verified; the signature is transcribed as read.
- West's signature reads "A. D. West" in most places but "A. M. West" on several pages (e.g. pp. 87–89, 96, 108, 109, 113, 114, 120, 126); transcribed as read on each page.

### Date inference

- p. 33: the date column looks like "20th", but the sequence (p. 32 is the 20th continued, p. 34 is the 22nd) places it on 21 August 1916; transcribed as the 21st.
