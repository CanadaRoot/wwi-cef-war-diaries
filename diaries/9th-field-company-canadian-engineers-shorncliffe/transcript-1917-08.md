### France, Marles-les-Mines — 1-8-17

Weather. Rain N.E. wind
C.R.E. inspected sections at work training in Billets
Lectures and instruction in tunneling, Use of Compasses, and Field Geometry. Pay Parade in afternoon.

Signed: W.R.R. Major.

### France, Marles-les-Mines — 2-8-17

Weather. Rain. West wind.
Company marched to rifle ranges under O.C. and Officers at J.20.C.9.4 near Bruay. France Sheet 36 B

Signed: W.R.R. Major.

### France, Marles-les-Mines — 3-8-17

Weather Dull. Rain S.W Wind.
Communication C.R.E. 30-8 referring to G.282 received giving particulars of Proposed Practice Manoeuvres of the 9th C.I.B. to take place on Aug 11th & 12th 1917. Under said instructions the 9th F.C.C.E is placed under orders of G.O.C. of 9th C.I.B. for these manoeuvres during said dates

Signed: W.R.R. Major.

[Handwritten, top:] 9th Field Company Canadian Engineers — 3rd Canadian Division

### France, Marles-les-Mines — 4-8-17

Weather. Rain S.W. Wind
Company employed for first hour in the morning with Section drill, Musketry, Fire Control, and Saluting. Remainder of the morning the 4 sections carried on respectively with, (1) Wiring (2) Taping of Trenches, instruction in deep Dugouts and Strong Points. (3) Demolitions including destruction of Wire Entanglements (4) Pontooning.
In the afternoon Physical Drill and Sports.

Signed: W.R.R. Major.

### France, Marles-les-Mines — 5-8-17

Weather. Cloudy W. Wind
Special Church Parade ordered for 16 O.Rs who attended Church Service at Houdain to commemorate the 4th anniversary of the Declaration of War. Remainder of the Coy under the O.C. attended Church Service on Training Grounds. The 7th & 8th F.C.C.E. & 123 Pioneer Bn being also present. B.M. 282 received reciting certain particulars of the Proposed Manoeuvres of the 9th C.I.B. V.O. attended at Horse lines of this Coy and specially marked 4 mares to be selected for breeding purposes according to General Orders.

Signed: W.R.R. Major.

### [No place given] — 11-8-17

Weather Rain with bright intervals
3rd Cdn Division Wire G.352 received confirming the C.R.E's instructions for this Company to march to Camblain Abbe for manoeuvres.
As per orders O.C. detailed two sections with transport etc to report to and come under the orders of O.C Vanguard (Lt/Col. Sharpe O.C 116th Bn)
Lieuts West and Walley with Nos 4 and 2 sections with section transport moved off from camp and passed starting point of Vanguard (Fork Road at W.16.c.3.8) 9.10 am. The Vanguard consisted of 116th Infantry Bn 43rd Inf. Bn 2 sections of 9th F.C.C.E and one bearer section 8th Field Ambulance. Remainder of the Coy under command of O.C marched past above mentioned starting Point with Main Guard at 10.20 am the order of march of the Main guard being 58th Inf Bn 9th C.I.B. H.Qs, so called Major Sykes's Bde C.F.A. and 9th F.C.C.E (less two sections) and two bearer subdivisions 8th Field Ambulance.
The enemy were represented by the 52nd Bn Infantry. Manoeuvres were held in the Chateau de la Haie Area extending to the east to Carency.

Signed: W.R.R. Major.

### France, Marles-les-Mines — 11-8-17 (continued)

Late in the afternoon manoeuvres were cancelled by reason of the continuous rainfall. G.O.C. 3rd Cdn Division addressed O.C.s units Adjutants and Company Commanders reference manoeuvres

Verbal orders received from Bde Major 9th C.I.B. for the 9th F.C.C.E. to return to Marles-les-Mines on the 12th August, this was confirmed by wire B.M. 265 of the 9th C.I.B.

Signed: W.R.R. Major.

### France, Marles-les-Mines — 12-8-17

Weather Clear S.W Wind
Company moved off from Billets in Maronil Bouche at 8 am and marched to and occupied same billets as before in Marles-les-Mines. Company proceeded by same route on the down march and arrived about 3 pm and again came under orders of C.R.E 3rd Cdn Div from this time. No casualties and no men fell out during the marches on these manoeuvres

Signed: W.R.R. Major.

Diary text pp. 4–27 (24 pages, matching the contents page's "Diary Text — 24 Pages"). - **No-entry dates:** None.
- **Contents-vs-pages discrepancies:** None of substance. The contents page (p. 3) states "Diary Text — 24 Pages" and "Appendix O. Work report issued by 9th Field Co. 23-8-17 to 31-8-17", both of which match the pages present. The contents page gives the division as "3rd Cdn. Division".
- **Diary entries split across page breaks:** 10, 11, 15, 16, 19, 20, 22, 28 and 30 August.
- **Place-name spellings as written:** The camp of 10–12 August is written "Maronil Bouche" (pp. 8, 10); the march route on 10 August is written "Haillicourt, Barlin, Verdel [uncertain] and Grand Servins"; "Camblain Abbe" is written so throughout (pp. 7–9); "Hersin-Coupigny" and "Bully-Grenay" are written so throughout; the trench is written "Cowdray" in the diary text (26 August) and "Cowdry" in the appendix reports, and "Cowdray" again in reports #15 and #18 — transcribed as written in each place.
- **Casualty summary:** Killed in action by shell fire, 26 August: #502767 Corp. Douglas, R.C.; buried 28 August in the Military Cemetery at Fosse 10, Row C, Grave 43 (map reference as written: "R.a.d. v.4 [uncertain] (Sheet France 36 B)").
