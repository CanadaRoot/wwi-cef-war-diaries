### Maroeuil, France — 1-11-16

Weather: Clear and fine in forenoon, cloudy in afternoon with showers in the evening.

According to Divisional Orders dated 30-10-16, honours and awards were granted to N.C.Os and men of the 9th Field Coy C.E. as follows: #529 Sgt. Emrey D.J., #541919 Corp. Fletcher J.F., #502694 L/Cpl Brown, K.N.; #541821 Spr. Feltham R.F., #502802 " Coyle, J.J., 541767 " Sinker H. The above N.C.Os and men were awarded the Military Medal

According to Divisional Orders 658/1, 30-10-16 one Coy of the 3rd Canadian Pioneer Battalion is allotted to work under this company in the forward area.

Lieut. Robertson, with Sappers of Nos 3 & 4 Sections continued work with trench wardens, also cleaning out trenches preparing dug-outs etc, with parties from the 52nd and 43rd Battalions (See reports)

Parties from Nos 1 & 2 Sections continue work on Divisional Hut, Aubigny, Gas School, Frévin-Chapelle, Y.M.C.A. 9th C.I.B and C.R.E. Headquarter

## Transcriber's notes — November 1916

- **Contents vs. pages present:** the contents page (p. 3) lists "Diary Text — 34 Pages" and "Appendix F. — Work report issued by 9th Field Company 31-10-16 and 1-11-16 to 29-11-16." Both check out: the diary text is 34 pages (pp. 4–37, 1–30 November) and the appendix is 118 pages (pp. 38–155), the first work report being dated 31 October 1916 and the last 29 November 1916.
- **Date gaps (days with no diary entry):** none — every day 1–30 November 1916 has a diary entry. Appendix gaps: no work report for 9 November (reports jump from 8 November to 10 November), and no No. 4 Section report for 22 November. Work reports otherwise run 31 October–29 November, with several sections reporting the same dates.
- **Duplicate page:** p. 46 is an apparent duplicate photograph of p. 44 — identical date (1 November, No. 3 Section), identical entries and identical signature.
- **Page numbering:** the appendix sheet numbers do not run in LAC page order and repeat (e.g. #5 on both pp. 43 and 47, #10 on both pp. 55 and 56, #58 on pp. 116 and 120, #65 on pp. 130, 133 and 138, sheet 70 on both pp. 143 and 144, sheet 73 on both pp. 147 and 148). Report numbers also run out of sequence against dates and page order. The daily work report headings on pp. 85, 88, 91, 94 and 97 still read "October", crossed out and corrected to "November" — reused October forms.
- **Out-of-order pages:** p. 65 is a 6 November No. 3 Section report filed after the 7 November report on p. 64. Pages 75–79 are in a different hand and columnar layout, headed "6.45 p.m. Nov 10/16" and circled (2), (3), (4), (6) — out of sequence, with (1) and (5) not in this volume.
- **Weekday/date mismatches:** the officers' night reports pair the weekday with the second date — "Wednesday 21/22nd Nov" (p. 121), "Thursday 22/23 Nov" (p. 129), "Friday 23/24 Nov" (p. 133), "Sat. 24/25 Nov" (p. 138); likewise "Sunday 25/26 Nov." (p. 141), "Monday 26/27 Nov." (p. 145) and "Tuesday 27/28 Nov." (p. 149). The No. 1 Section night reports (pp. 101–120) pair the weekday with the first date and check out (Fri 17th, Sat 18th, Sun 18/19th, Mon 19/20th, Tue 20/21st).
- **Commanding officer's signature:** daily entries are signed "H.P.R. Major" for 1–9 November (pp. 4–11) and "G.W. Capt." from the 9 November continuation onward (pp. 12–20), matching the October pattern where G.W. Capt. signed from 25 October. Pages 21–25 bear a different, illegible signature, flagged [uncertain]. The month is closed by Lieut E. J. Young (p. 37): "The O.C. and Capt E. W. Harrison being absent on leave the diary for the month is being signed by Lieut E. J. Young, the senior Officer present." The O.C. left for England for a ten days' furlough, and Capt. Harrison and Lieut H. D. Robertson proceeded on leave to England.
- **Military Medals:** Divisional Orders dated 30-10-16 awarded the Military Medal to six men of the company for the Somme — #529 Sgt. Emrey D.J., #541919 Corp. Fletcher J.F., #502694 L/Cpl Brown, K.N., #541821 Spr. Feltham R.F., #502802 A/L/Cpl. Coyle, J.J., and #541767 Spr. Sinker H. (Sgt. Emrey's number is given as "#529" only — three digits, unlike the six-digit numbers for the others.) General Plummer, Canadian Corps Commander, presented the medals at Bray; four of the six were on parade (Brown, Coyle, Feltham, Sinker) — Sgt. Emrey was on leave and Corp. Fletcher was wounded and in hospital.
- **Casualties (diary text, November 1916):** no men killed in the diary text this month. One man accidentally wounded — #500790 Corp. Mann, E.W. Sick and sent to hospital / Field Ambulance — #504025 Spr. Coles J.C., Lieut. Robinson, #502766 Spr. McFarlane J.N., #541808 Spr. Benoit F.E., #445695 Spr. Crowley E.E., #541951 Dvr. Taylor E.M., #502782 Dvr. Amy P., #443519 Spr. Spencer A.G., #502859 Dvr. Dempsey P., #541927 L/Cpl. Huntley A., #2164 Richardson F., #641934 Kennedy J.C., and #2282 Spr. de la Penotiere. Appendix casualty columns read "Nil" throughout, except for the No. 2 Section reports noting Lieut. Robinson sick.
- **Arrivals and departures:** Lieut. C. S. Walley arrived from C.E.T.D. Shorncliffe and was taken on the strength of the company; one O.R. reinforcement arrived from the Canadian Base Depot. No. 5326 Q.M.S. Downie R.W. left for England, transferred to C.E.T.D. Lieut. West had left in October to take a course at the First Army School.
- **Movements:** the company remained at Maroeuil, France, for the whole month. Sections rotated between rear billets and advanced billets in the forward area; Nos. 1 and 2 Sections worked at the Divisional Hut (Aubigny), the Gas School (Frévin-Capelle), the Y.M.C.A. 9th C.I.B. hut and C.R.E. Headquarters, while Nos. 3 and 4 Sections worked the forward trenches. On 28 November the company stood to in attack quarters at Fork Redoubt (reveille 3:30 a.m., sections marched off 4:50 a.m., stood to 5:30 a.m. — times as written).
- **Operations:** the trench-warden system set up in late October continued through November (10 parties, each under a Sapper, with French wardens and infantry parties). Main work: cleaning, draining, revetting and laying bath-mats in the front and support trenches (Claudot, Douai, Elbe, Guillemont, Mercier, Rocade, Territorial, Vase, Victoire and other named trenches), running levels for drainage, deep dug-outs at the advanced billets, and the tramway — ballasting and cleaning the Glasgow Dump line and the Bessew/Vistula branches, excavating for sidings and switches, and hauling material. One company of the 3rd Canadian Pioneer Battalion was allotted to work under this company in the forward area (Divisional Orders 658/1, 30-10-16). The appendix (p. 144) notes working parties "are very small and becoming smaller every day. Very little can be accomplished unless more men are supplied."
- **Place-name spellings:** transcribed as written; recurring spellings include "Quitz", "Zivy", "Vissec"/"Visseck", "Victorie", "Claudot", "Dentata", "Rocade", "Reitz", "Guillemont"/"Guillermot"/"Guillemot", "Bessan"/"Bessew", "Bentata". - **"Dunsmore"/"Murdie"/"Mundie":** the No. 4 Section signatory is read as "W. C. Murdie, Lieut." on most pages, but as "W. C. Mundie" on pp. 119–120; both spellings are transcribed as they appear.
