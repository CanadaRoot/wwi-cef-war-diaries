### F.5.d.4.3. Sheet 51c, France — 1-4-18

Weather, Cloudy. South wind.
Nos 2 and 4 Sections attended bath parade.
Lieut Jones with No 3 Section and parties from the P.P.C.L.I. carrying material and wiring.
Lieut Serson with party of Sappers from No 1 Section and parties from the 49th Battalion cleaning out trench, widening and deepening trench and carrying material.
Lieut Steeves with No 1 Section and parties from the 42nd and 49th Battalions salvaging and carrying material and cleaning out trenches.
Lieut Sproule with No 4 Section proceeded to Advanced Billets and Lieut Jones with No 3 Section returned to Rear Billets.
Information received from C.R.E. Office (C.R.E 14/30) that a Composite Brigade of all unbrigaded units and parties, under the command of Lt Col Kingsmill, 123rd Pioneer Battalion is being formed.
Under G.276. (Extract from) the dismounted men at Rear Billets of 7th, 8th and 9th Field Coys C.E. will form one unit of Composite Brigade. This unit for tactical purposes being under the orders of Maj. K Stuart, 7th Field Coy C.E.

[Remarks column: Appendix W — Report #1; #2; #3.]

Signed: W.R.R., Major.

### R.11.c.2.7. [uncertain] Sht 36a, France — 12-4-18

Weather: Clear and cool. North Wind.
Capt Miller, Lieut Baldock and Lieut Serson proceeded to Rear Billets of 466 Field Coy R.E. No 3 Section and 6 O.R. of No 2 Sec proceeded on bicycles to Rear Billets of the 466th Field Coy R.E.
Balance of Coy with transport, under Major Robertson, moved off at 7 P.M. and proceeded via Souchez and Ablain-St Nazaire to a field just outside Bully-Grenay (R.11.c.2.7. Sheet 36a) where the Coy bivouacked for the night as billets of 466th Field Coy R.E. will not be vacated until tomorrow.
This night move made as Souchez-St. Nazaire road cannot be used in daylight, being in view of the enemy lines.
Relief of 466th Coy R.E. by this Coy. complete by at 6 P.M. today. [The word "by" appears struck through.]
No 3 Section, and No. 2 Section except 6 O.R. proceeded to Advanced Billets in Cite St Pierre (M.11.d.25.50. Sheet 36c SW)
3rd Cdn Div Engineer's Operation Order No 53 today date received; it being issued in conjunction with 3rd Cdn Div G 676 and O.O. 155 whereby the 3rd Cdn Div. whereby the 3rd Cdn Div will relieve the 46th Division by 6 am 13th April 1918.
By said Operation Order 9th Field Coy C.E. to hand over work in the Avion Sector, new boundary to 7th Field Coy C.E. and the work between the new boundary to the present Southern boundary to the 10th Field Coy C.E.

Signed: W.R.R., Major.

## Notes

- **Structure:** 118 LAC images. Cover (p. 1), inner title page (p. 2), contents (p. 3), diary text pp. 4–24 (21 pages, matching the contents page's "Diary Text — 21 Pages"), appendix cover (p. 25) and 93 work-report sheets (pp. 26–118).
- **Date gaps:** None in the diary text — all dates 1–30 April 1918 are present, in sequence. In the appendix, no work-report sheet dated 12 April 1918 is filed (the sequence jumps from 11 April to 13 April), and no work report dated 30 April 1918 is filed — the appendix ends with the 29 April reports on p. 118.
- **Split entries:** Diary text: 3 April (pp. 5–6), 17 April (pp. 14–15), 27 April (pp. 20–21; p. 21 is otherwise blank). Appendix: sheets 6 (pp. 32–33), 9 (pp. 36–37), 29 (pp. 59–60), 30/30a (pp. 62–63), 33/33a/33b (pp. 66–68), 39/39a (pp. 74–75), 45 (pp. 81–82, P.T.O.), 47 (pp. 84–85, P.T.O.), 50/50a (pp. 88–89); p. 109 continues overleaf to p. 110 (its verso); p. 113 carries two blocks (night work plus a block headed "For Saturday 27-4-18").
- **Contents-page discrepancy:** The contents page (p. 3) describes the unit as "9th Field Company C.E. 2nd Cdn. Divn."; the cover, inner title page and diary text all give the 3rd Canadian Division. Transcribed as written.
- **Appendix sheet numbers irregular:** Sheets are numbered in more than one sequence and are not in strict chronological order. Sheets 1–4 (pp. 26–30); then 5, 6, 6a, 7, 8, 9, 9a, 10–17, 16 (used twice, pp. 44 and 46), 19–29, 29a — no sheet numbered 18; then 30–51 (with "a" continuations 30a, 33a, 33b, 39a, 45a, 47a, 50a); then a roughly sequential run 55, 58, 59, 61–65, 67–77 (skipping 56, 57, 60, 66), plus separate per-section sequences (Sheets #1, #2; "5"; marginal 24, 27, 3, 63, 73) and a "0" on pp. 96, 104, 109, 110. P. 61 is undated and carries "294" written vertically at top left. The date on p. 102 reads "24.3.18" although the surrounding sheets are dated 24-4-18.
- **Duplicate photographs:** None in April. P. 110 is the reverse (verso) of the p. 109 sheet, not a duplicate.
- **Photograph problems:** Pp. 82 and 85 (continuations of sheets 45 and 47) were photographed upside-down; both are very faint pencil with heavy show-through from pp. 81 and 84, and most of their rows are illegible.
- **Other-unit material:** P. 106 is a "Summary of Work Done on M.G. Emplacements in Canteen Tunnel" (20–24 April 1918), signed H. C. Banks, Lieut, 123rd Bn Cdn Pioneers — a 123rd Battalion, Canadian Pioneers document filed in the 9th Field Company appendix. It is transcribed here but is not indexed as 9th Field Company activity. It is the "Summary attached" referred to in the 25 April diary entry.
- **Casualty summary (whole month):** Killed: 1 (infantry, unnamed, 25 April, No. 4 Section report, p. 105). Wounded: 14 in total, none named and no regimental numbers given except Lieut. Steeves — Lieut. Steeves slightly injured by a piece of shrapnel striking him on the foot, 20 April, remained at duty; one sapper, name read as "Bomb" [uncertain], slightly wounded, 28 April, at duty; 1 private slightly wounded, 10 April (sheet 30a); 7 infantrymen slightly wounded by shell fire or wire, 15 April (sheet 38); 1 man of the 116th Battalion slightly wounded, night of 15–16 April (sheet 39a); 3 infantry wounded, 25 April (No. 4 Section report). Reinforcements: three unnamed from the Base Depot, taken on strength 17 April.
