### A.2.c.1.7, Sheet 51B, France — 15-3-18

Weather, Clear and cold, North wind.
Major Robertson attended demonstration held by the First Army Officer in charge of mines as to the ability of our tanks to cross a series of craters.
Craters were blown by 50 lbs of ammonal inserted through a bore hole to a depth of 6 feet. a second charge was then laid in the bottom of the first crater to increase its size. The result was to make the crater approximately 26 ft across the top and 8½ ft deep with steep sides.
The tank went in and out of the series of craters without difficulty. It was suggested that had the mud been deep at the bottom of the crater the thread of the tanks would probably have sunk in so deeply as to mire the tank.
Lieut Sproule with No 4 Section and parties from the 42nd Battalion deepening and repairing trenches and working on dugouts for command posts.

## Notes

- **Coverage:** 31 diary-text entries for 1–31 March 1918 (no date gaps; every day has a diary-text entry) plus appendix work reports #1–100. Diary text pp. 4–26 (23 pages, matching the contents page's "Diary Text — 23 Pages"). Appendix V pp. 27–166 (cover + 139 work-report sheets, reports #1–100, sheets numbered 1–100 at top right with "a"-suffixed continuations), covering work 1–31 March 1918.
- **No-entry dates:** None.
- **Contents-vs-pages discrepancies:** None of substance. The contents page (p. 3) describes Appendix V as "Work report issued by 9th Field Company dated 1-3-18 to 31-3-18", and the appendix as filed does contain reports #1–100 covering 1–31 March 1918. Appendix sheet numbers are irregular in filing order: p. 68 is headed sheet 37 while p. 69 is sheet 33; p. 72 sheet 39, p. 73 sheet 37, p. 74 sheet 38; p. 108 is headed 61a and p. 109 headed 61. Diary entries split across page breaks: 10, 14, 15, 18, 20 and 28 March.
- **Casualty summary:** Wounded in action by shell fire, all evacuated except where noted: #505287 Spr Gibson E. (12 Mar, evacuated); #502461 Spr Beice J.H. [uncertain] (12 Mar, returned to duty); #528904 Spr E.J. Wilson (16 Mar, evacuated); #502759 2/Cpl Hopkins W.B. (28 Mar, evacuated); #2114953 Spr Storey G.C. (29 Mar, evacuated). Sick, evacuated: #409063 [uncertain — number overwritten in the original] Spr Hall H.R. and #502935 Spr Blackwell W.E. (17 Mar). Appendix-only casualties (not in the diary text): one unnamed wounded man, No. 1 Section, 10 Mar (report p. 63); 1 O.R. wounded, R.C.R., night of 27–28 Mar (report p. 151); and "One Sapper wounded" in the No. 1 Section report for 29 Mar (p. 161), which is probably Spr Storey, already recorded in the diary text for 29 Mar — see below. Spr Wilson is also entered as wounded in the No. 1 Section work reports for both 15 Mar (p. 87) and 16 Mar (p. 95); the diary text records him wounded 16 Mar, so this may be one casualty reported twice — do not double-count. No one is recorded killed. Three unnamed reinforcements arrived from the C.E.R.D. on 24 Mar.
- **Name-reading notes:** The lieutenant spelled "Serson" (pp. 11, 13–16) is spelled "Seeson" (pp. 12, 26); both readings are flagged [uncertain] and left as written. The diary is signed "W.R.R., Major" 1–9 Mar; then a lieutenant's cursive signature read "W.O.M." [uncertain] 10–19 Mar; then "J.W.B." [uncertain] 20–25 Mar; W.R.R., Major resumes 26–31 Mar — this coincides with Maj. Robertson acting as C.R.E. 8–25 Mar. The officer signing the No. 2 Section reports is read "W. C. Murdie"; the No. 4 Section officer is read "J. C. Sproule" / "J. E. Sproule" [uncertain] on different sheets; No. 1 Section signatures are largely illegible ([uncertain]) and one No. 4 Section sheet (p. 107) is signed "J. W. B[uncertain], Lieut."
- **Place/sheet discrepancies:** The place column reads "A.2.c.1.7, Sheet 51B" for 1–27 Mar, except 13 Mar, which reads "Sheet 36B" (36B is the sheet used for the Fort George area from 29 Mar); 28 Mar reads "F.5.d.4.3, Sheet 51C"; 29–31 Mar read "F.5.d.4.3, Sheet 36B". All transcribed as written.
- **Appendix notes:** p. 99 is dated "17/3/17" — the year is written as 17, presumably 18 (all surrounding sheets are March 1918). p. 130 repeats p. 126 almost verbatim (same parties, strengths and work), dated 23-3-18 against 22/3/18 — transcribed as written; possibly a duplicated sheet. p. 160 gives an Avion dugout party as "from 11.00 P.M. 29th to 5.00 A.M. 29th" — the 5.00 A.M. is presumably the 30th. p. 114 (No. 2 Section, 20–21 Mar) is partly illegible in the middle entries. Report #6 (p. 34) carries no date. The Avion dugout exit was completed 24 Mar; the Avion dugout entrance fell in and was cleared 25 Mar and again 28–30 Mar. No parties on the night of 25–26 Mar "on account of operations", and no party from 5.00 P.M. 28th to 11.00 P.M. 29th "on account of operations". The diary writes "M.T.M." / "T.M." and "thread of the tanks" (15 Mar, presumably "tread") as written. Report #3 (p. 30) notes "1 Casualty Inf. [uncertain]" in a material line.
- **Other-unit material in the appendix:** None filed as separate other-unit diary material. Infantry working parties from the 43rd, 52nd, 58th, 116th, 42nd and 49th Battalions, the P.P.C.L.I., the R.C.R. and the 7th M.G. Company appear throughout the work reports as parties working under 9th Field Company sappers; they are not presented here as 9th Field Company activity.
