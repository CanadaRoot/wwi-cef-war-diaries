## Page 4 (May 1916, p. 4) — [diary text]

WAR DIARY or INTELLIGENCE SUMMARY. (Erase heading not required.)

### SHORNCLIFFE — 20th May 1916

The Weather Clear & bright.

The Company entrained at SHORNCLIFFE. Nº 3 & 4 sections & H.Qs. in the first train in charge of Lieut HARRISON left at 6.15 a.m. The remainder of the company on the second train in charge of the O.C. left at 8.15 a.m.

The first train arrived at SOUTHAMPTON at 1.45 p.m. the second train at 3.20 p.m.

Three horses were exchanged at the Remount Depot. One horse injured loading. [uncertain] at 5.25 p.m. the company embarked on the S.S. ANGLO-CANADIAN.

Capt. N.R. ROBERTSON. O.C.

Lieut. E.W. HARRISON 2nd in C.

Lieut. E.T. YOUNG. #1 section

" R.L. DUNSMORE #2 section

" H.O. ROBERTSON #3 section

" A.M. WEST. #4 section

" C.S. WALLEY } Supernumary & 224 N.C.O's & men.

" C. ROBINSON } & 83 horses.

Weather bright & warm.

[Remarks: C.S.W.]

### HAVRE — 21st May 1916

The Weather bright & warm.

The S.S. ANGLO-CANADIAN arrived at HAVRE 5.30 a.m. & the company disembarked at 11.30 a.m. & had dinner, then marched to DOCKS REST CAMP #5 & arrived there 2.30 p.m.

[Remarks: C.S.W.]

## Page 5 (May 1916, p. 5) — [diary text]

### HAVRE — 22nd May 1916

The Company in charge of Lieut DUNSMORE went for a route march through HARFLEUR.

[Remarks: C.S.W.]

### HAVRE — 23rd May 1916

Nºs 1 & Nº 2 sections on Camp Fatigue until 12 a.m. Nºs 3 & 4 sections relieving them & carrying on from 2 to 5 p.m. 1 man removed to Hospital — #341784 Pte. T.C. Wilmot — [uncertain]

[Remarks: C.S.W.]

### HAVRE — 24th May 1916

The company entrained at HAVRE 10.0 p.m. & left at 11.39 p.m. Five horses were exchanged at the HAVRE Remount Depot. One man removed to Hospital — 2nd/Cpl. Pte C.F. #341916.

Weather bright & warm — last two days. Vaccination.

[Remarks: C.S.W.]

### POPERINGHE — 25th May 1916

The company arrived at POPERINGHE (Siding) & detrained 9 p.m. & marched to H.13.d. 3.7. (Ref. Map.) Belgium. Sht. 28. N.W.) & went under canvas. Camp C.

Weather:— Clear, warm day, towards evening sky clouded & rain from 10 p.m. to 2 p.m.

[Remarks: C.S.W.]

### H.13.d. 3.7. (Ref. Map. Belgium, Sht. 28. N.W.) — 26th May 1916

The Company spent the day fixing up the camp. Lieut WEST in charge of two pontoon wagons went to [uncertain] 3rd Divisional [uncertain] Dump. & drew beds & bath mats.

In the evening the O.C. accompanied by Major Vince proceeded to advance C.F. Billets YPRES.

Weather:— Warm, & Clear.

[Remarks: C.S.W.]

## Page 6 (May 1916, p. 6) — [diary text]

### H.13.d. 3.7. — 27th May 1916

The Company paraded at 8.30 a.m. & moved camp in the morning to the next field. The O.C. Lieuts. YOUNG, DUNSMORE & ROBERTSON accompanied by #1, 2, 3 section Sergeants proceeded to Advanced C.F. billets YPRES in the afternoon on bicycles.

At 6 p.m. #1 & #2 sections, & two forage carts with water cart proceeded to Advanced C.F. billets YPRES.

The O.C. & Lieut DUNSMORE toured the left section of the front lines & Hooge Sector area in the rear with 8th F.C.C.F. section officer. Lieut YOUNG & ROBERTSON toured the right sector of the front line with the 8th F.C.C.F. section officer. Nº 2 section settled in their billets.

Two men removed to Hospital. #541701 Actg. Farr. Sgt. Alexander J. — injured leg — #2282 Sppr. de la [uncertain] [uncertain]

Weather:— Fine, warm & Clear.

[Remarks: C.S.W.]

## Page 7 (May 1916, p. 7) — [diary text]

### H.13.d. 3.7. — 28th May 1916

Lieut WEST & #4 section re-vetting shell trenches situated in camp C. & #3 section erecting bomb-proof shelter of steel bee-hive frames & sand-bags.

Lieut WALLEY & one pontoon wagon went to R.E. Dump ST. [uncertain] to draw lumber for camp improvement.

Working party in charge of Lieut HARRISON started to-day to construct new horse lines.

The O.C. toured Right Sector trenches with Section officer of 8th F.C.C.E.

Lt. YOUNG & part of #1 section working on drain near gap left of Right Sector, the balance of #1 section accompanied 8th Coy guides.

Lt DUNSMORE & part of #2 section working — reclaiming French left Sector, the balance of #2 section on different works in Left Sector with 8th Coy Sappers. Lieut ROBERTSON & his Sergeant accompanied 8th Coy. Section officers inspecting different works in Reserve trenches & supporting posts.

Weather: Fine, Warm, & Clear. R. Ballantyne church at 9 a.m. Major Gordon visited the camp and held services in the afternoon. (Ralph Connor)

[Remarks: C.S.W.]

## Page 8 (May 1916, p. 8) — [diary text]

### H.13.d. 3.7. — 29th May 1916

Nºs 3 & 4 Sections working on Camp improvement during the day. Drivers working on new horse lines & harness shelter. Tool carts & forage wagons were shifted to new park.

Nº 3 section in charge of C.S.M. [uncertain] marched to advanced Billets YPRES at 8.30 p.m. arriving at 10.30 p.m.

Six drivers went over different routes to Advanced R.E. dumps guided by Drivers from Nº 8 Coy.

During night 29/30 Lieut. YOUNG's section worked on improving trenches, excavating new communication trench and carrying supplies on right sector in conjunction with 8th F.C.C.E.

Lieut. DUNSMORE's section repairing trenches & carrying supplies on left sector in conjunction with 8th F.C.C.E.

Casualty #802941 Sppr. Tilley H. sent to hospital from Advanced billets with [uncertain]

Weather: Fine, Warm, Clear.

[Remarks: C.S.W.]

### H.13.d. 3.7. (do.) — 30th May 1916

No. 4 section working on camp improvement to-day. Drivers working on horse lines. Six drivers went over different routes guided by Drivers from 8th F.C.C.F.

One driver trampled by horse. He sustained a fracture of one of the small bones [uncertain] leg [uncertain] was [uncertain] to Hospital by Doctor, #302335 Pte. [uncertain] J.W.

## Page 9 (May 1916, p. 9) — [diary text]

### H.13.d. 3.7. — 30th May 1916 (Contd.)

Nº1 Section worked on placing "A" Frames, revetting, making & improving trench. Building dug-out. Fixing tank. Raising parapet screen at dug-out R.E. dump.

Nº2 Section working on H.Q. [uncertain] Dug-out. Also part of the section made a tour of the ASYLUM, meeting place for guides, & working parties.

## Transcriber's notes — May 1916

- **Date gaps (days with no entry):** none — entries cover every day from 20 to 31 May 1916.
- **Contents vs. pages present:** the contents page lists only "Diary Text — 7 Pages". The 7 diary text pages (pp. 4–10) are all present, so the contents and the file agree. No appendices are listed or included.
- **Cover vs. contents:** the cover reads "From 20th May to 31st May 1916"; the contents page and the diary text agree.
- **"C.S.W.":** the Remarks column on nearly every entry reads "C.S.W." Its meaning is unclear — it may be the diarist's initials or a file reference. It is not the commanding officer's initials (N. R. Robertson).
- **"8th F.C.C.E." / "8th F.C.C.F.":** the unit the company relieved and worked alongside at Ypres is written both ways in the diary (8th Field Company, Canadian Engineers); the spelling is transcribed as written each time.
- **Page 4, 20 May:** the first weather line ("The Weather Clear & bright.") appears partly struck through. The inserted line after "One horse injured loading." is largely illegible — only "One horse injured loading." can be read with confidence.
- **Page 5, 23 May:** the word after "Pte. T.C. Wilmot" is illegible; it appears to begin with a capital "A" and may read "Asthma".
- **Page 5, 24 May:** "2nd/Cpl. Pte C.F. #341916" is transcribed as written; the rank and initials are cramped and the reading is not fully certain.
- **Page 5, 26 May:** the place Lieut West's pontoon wagons went to is struck through and illegible, as is the word before "Dump" in "3rd Divisional [uncertain] Dump".
- **Page 6, 27 May:** "Actg. Farr. Sgt." is read as Acting Farrier Sergeant. The second man's surname ("de la [uncertain] [uncertain]") is illegible.
- **Page 7, 28 May:** the place after "ST." is struck through and illegible. The final lines are interlineations: a struck-through line (partly reading "Capt. Tompkins") precedes "Major Gordon visited the camp and held services in the afternoon. (Ralph Connor)" — the surname is written unclearly, but the "(Ralph Connor)" annotation identifies the visitor as Major Gordon (Ralph Connor was the pen name of chaplain Major Charles W. Gordon).
- **Page 8, 29 May:** the C.S.M.'s name is illegible. The casualty line is an interlineation; the illness word after "with" is illegible but appears to begin "dys—" (possibly dysentery).
- **Page 8, 30 May:** the driver's injury sentence has interlineations ("of one of the small bones") and a struck-through word before "to Hospital"; the driver's surname is illegible.
- **Page 9, 30 May:** "Major WALSH A.V.O.S." is transcribed as written; it may be "A.V.C." (Army Veterinary Corps — he inspected the horses).
- **Page 10, 31 May:** the destination of the new communication trench ("from Reserve Trench to [uncertain]") is struck through and illegible.
- **Weather note, 25 May:** "rain from 10 p.m. to 2 p.m." is transcribed as written (the second time is presumably 2 a.m.).
- **Casualties:** no deaths and no combat casualties. Six men were removed to hospital: Pte. T. C. Wilmot #341784 (23 May); #341916 (24 May); Actg. Farr. Sgt. Alexander J. #541701, injured leg, and Sppr. de la [uncertain] #2282 (27 May); Sppr. Tilley H. #802941, illness (29 May); Pte. [uncertain] J.W. #302335, trampled by a horse, fractured a small bone (30 May).
- **Movements:** Shorncliffe → Southampton (20 May) → S.S. Anglo-Canadian → Havre (21 May) → Poperinghe, Belgium (25 May) → camp at map reference H.13.d.3.7. (Sheet 28 N.W.), with sections moving up to advanced billets at Ypres from 27 May.
- **Form imprint** at the foot of the diary pages: "2449 Wt. W14957/8269 750,000 1/16 J.B.C. & A. Forms/C.2118/12."
