### France, W.5.d.7.0, Sht 36B — 15-7-17 (continued)

9th C.I.Bde Operation Order No 105 Copy No 10 dated today received whereby the 9th C.I.Bde will relieve the 8th C.I.Bde in the forward area on nights of July 17-18 and 18-19th/1917
[One line struck through — illegible]
The projection of gas on the enemy lines took place at 1 a.m. and appeared to be quite successful although enemy gave no indication of its effects by flares, shell fire or otherwise. The absence of the ordinary enemy trench flares in the locality of the projected gas was noticeable.
3rd Canadian Divisional Engineers Operation Order No 38 dated today received, whereby the 7th Field Coy C.E will relieve the 9th Field Coy C.E in the forward area on the night of July 18, 1917. the 9th Field Coy C.E. to be responsible for the directions of the Engineers work in the forward area until 12 noon July 19, 1917. ## Notes — July 1917

- **Coverage:** Diary text pp. 4–31 (28 pages, matching the contents page's "Diary Text — 28 Pages"), covering every calendar date 1–31 July 1917 with no date gaps. Appendix N pp. 32–112 (cover + 80 work-report sheets), containing work reports numbered 1–59, dated 3–23 July 1917, plus the move orders of 24 July 1917 filed as #59.
- **Contents-vs-pages discrepancies:** The contents page (p. 3) describes the appendix as "Work reports issued by 9th Field Co. C.E. [first date overwritten, uncertain] 6-7-17 to 24-7-17", but the appendix as filed contains work reports dated 3 July to 23 July 1917, plus the move orders of 24 July 1917. The first date on the contents page appears to have been overwritten; the earliest report in the appendix is dated 3 July 1917.
- **Appendix filing order:** The appendix sheets are not filed in report-number order. In the order filed: Reports #47–#58 (pp. 34–46), the move orders #59 (pp. 47–49), Reports #38a, #39–#46 (pp. 50–59), Reports #31–#37 (pp. 60–71), Reports #28–#30 (pp. 72–77), Reports #16–#27 (pp. 78–93), and Reports #1–#15 (pp. 94–112). All report numbers #1–#59 are present; no report number is missing. Several reports run to two or three sheets, marked with letter suffixes (5a, 7a, 12a, 13a, 20a, 22a, 25a, 26a, 29a, 29b, 30a, 31a, 31b, 34a, 38a, 43a, 56a). Report #1 is spread over three sheets (pp. 33, 56 and 94); Report #31 over three sheets (pp. 60–62); Report #29 over three sheets (pp. 73–75).
- **Appendix sheet numbers:** The second number at the top right of each report sheet (the internal appendix pagination) does not run in order as filed: e.g. pp. 50–59 are numbered 85–94, pp. 60–71 are numbered 73–84, pp. 72–77 are numbered 67–72, p. 78 is numbered 16, pp. 79–93 are numbered 52–66, and pp. 94–112 are numbered 32–50. The move-orders sheets are numbered 110 (page 2), 109 (page 1) and 111 (page 3). The continuation sheet of Report #1 on p. 56 is marked "43a" although it follows Report #43 in the file; the letter-suffix markings appear to follow the filing order rather than the report numbers.
- **Missing appended report:** Report #8 (p. 103) states of the inspection party on the 42nd Battalion front line "(Report appended)", but no such inspection report was found among the appendix sheets as filed.
- **Casualties recorded in the appendix but not in the diary text:**
  - Night of 5–6 July (Report #7, p. 101): Cas column reads "1 man R.C.R."; killed or wounded is not stated. The diary entry for 5 July records no casualties.
  - Night of 7–8 July (Report #12, p. 107): Cas column reads "1 R.C.R."; killed or wounded is not stated. The diary entry for 7 July records no casualties.
  - Night of 8–9 July (Report #20, p. 82): 2 infantrymen killed and 1 officer and 5 other ranks wounded (R.C.R. party on the Resistance line). The diary entry for 8 July records no casualties.
  - Night of 8–9 July (Report #19, p. 81): "Casualties — 2 [uncertain]" for West's fire-trench party; killed or wounded is not stated.
  - Night of 10–11 July (Report #25, p. 89): 1 wounded, before [uncertain] was over. The diary entry for 10 July records no casualties.
  - Night of 11–12 July (Report #29b, p. 75): "Casualties — 1."; killed or wounded is not stated. The diary entry for 11 July records no casualties.
  - Night of 14–15 July (Report #40, p. 52): 2 infantrymen slightly wounded (Murdie's 58th Battalion party). The diary entry for 14 July records 5 infantrymen wounded under Robinson's Report #39, which appears to be a different party.
  - Night of 17–18 July (Report #52, p. 39): 3 O.R. C.M.R. slightly wounded. The diary entry for 17 July records no casualties.
  - Night of 4–5 July (Report #5, p. 98): the Cas entry for the first party (G, R.C.R.) is blacked out in the original, with a "2" visible beneath the ink; it could not be read.
- **Spr Leslie J.A. #503991:** Seriously wounded on 15 July at Dead Man's Corner (Report #42, p. 54, records "1 Sapper fatally" in the waggon party); the diary entry for 16 July reports word from the O.C. #7 C.C.S. (Beaquesne [uncertain]) that he had died of wounds "during night of 16–17th", although the entry itself is dated 16 July. He was buried on 17 July at Villers-au-Bois Station Cemetery, Plot 8, Row F, Grave No 8 (W.18.a.2.9).
- **Explosives find, 14 July:** The diary entry states West's party "discovered 250 boxes of [uncertain] [inserted: Enemy] and a large quantity of fuse and detonators (S.18.d.8.8)"; Report #38 (p. 71) records the same find as "250 boxes Perdite [uncertain]. 70 tin [uncertain]. Large quantity fuse & detonators" at "approx S18 d 8.8".
- **Move orders discrepancies (pp. 47–49, filed as #59):** Paragraph 2 states the Chateau-de-la-Haie billets are to be handed over to the 11th F.C.C.E., whereas the diary entries for 23 and 24 July state the billets are to be handed over to the 10th Field Coy C.E. Paragraph 5 gives "fall in ... at 6:45 a.m. ... and move off 6:30 a.m."; the diary entry for 25 July records the company fell in at 6:30 a.m. and marched off at 7:06 a.m.

- **Other discrepancies and oddities:** Report #23 (p. 87) gives the working time as "10.15 pm 9.7.17 to 1.45 pm 10.7.17" [sic — presumably 1.45 a.m.]. Report #3 (p. 96) is headed "No 4 Sect." but is signed by Steeves, who signs other sheets as No 3 Section, and is dated "July 2-4/17". Report #13 (p. 110) has a second, duplicate entry struck through. Report #15 (p. 112) has "Men — 100 men" struck through for the Resistance line party. The R.C.R. working party in Report #20 (p. 83) "Consisted of only 91 [uncertain] instead of 175". Report #8 (p. 103) notes that heavy shelling prevented work starting until 12.30 a.m. and that one half of the party withdrew with the Captain in charge. Report #10 (p. 105) notes a carrying party "waited for limber till 12.00 pm no wagon showed up". Report #19 (p. 81) and Report #24 (p. 88) are both headed "July 8-9/17" but are different reports (West's No 4 Section, fire trench and dugouts); Report #19 and Report #24 are both numbered and dated the same night, transcribed as written.
