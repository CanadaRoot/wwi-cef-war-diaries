## What the diary is

The battery was formed at Locre on 21 March 1916 from detachments of the 24th, 25th and 26th Battalions plus the 2nd Division Trench Mortar Group (5 officers, 16 men) under Lt R. M. Fair, reporting to Lt Leather, O.C. 2nd Division Trench Mortar Group. It served on the St Eloi front (April–July 1916), at Hill 60 and Zillebeke (June 1916), and on the Vierstraete front (late July–August 1916), then marched to the 2nd Canadian Division training area near Eperlecques. The July 1917 section shows it in the Lens sector (Gouy-Servins, Aix-Noulette, Les Brebis, Bully) under Lt H. D. Anderson.

## Casualties

- 8 June 1916: Lieut R. W. Morrison killed by shell in Square Wood (Zillebeke) — the battery's first officer fatality.
- 1 July 1916: Cpl J. Innis (No. 65481) killed; Pte G. Trembly (No. 61530) and Sgt G. E. Roche (No. 67454) wounded, supporting a raid at St Eloi (321 + 89 rounds fired).
- 2 August 1916: 1 killed and 1 wounded (unnamed).
- July 1917: Lieut J. A. Parke shell shocked (4 July, to hospital); Lieut Calkin wounded (night of 9/10 July); 1 O.R. wounded 6 July, 1 O.R. wounded 7 July, 1 O.R. wounded night of 9/10 July, 1 O.R. injured 10 July.

## Notable items

- 1 May 1916: bomb store at Noormegule [uncertain] (Voormezele) hit by a shell — 1,059 Stokes shells exploded, two Stokes guns slightly damaged.
- 15 June 1916: battery renamed "6th Trench Mortar Battery" in place of the 5th and 6th batteries (wording [uncertain]); Lieut R. M. Fair appointed O.C.; the diary heading changes to "6th Trench Mortar Battery" on the last June page. The July/August volumes revert to the 5th title.
- 17 July 1916: Fair given temporary rank of Captain while O.C., dated 9 June.
- 13 July 1916: a Stokes gun and 100 rounds destroyed in Shelly Lane C.T. by a German rifle grenade detonating the Stokes bombs.
- The battery's sections in July 1917 are numbered after the battalions they support — 22nd, 24th, 25th, 26th — rotating between the front line and support at Leavin (Liévin).
- Secondary sources (Brooker, canadiansoldiers.com) say the battery's diary entries cease 31 August 1916; this record also contains a July 1917 month, so the diary does not end in August 1916.
