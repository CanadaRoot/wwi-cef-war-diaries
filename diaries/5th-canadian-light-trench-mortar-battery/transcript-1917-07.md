## Page 3 — Diary text, 1–9 July 1917 (page-0037-July_1917_p._3.jpg)

Army Form C. 2118 — WAR DIARY or INTELLIGENCE SUMMARY. (Form heading: "Instructions regarding War Diaries and Intelligence Summaries are contained in F. S. Regs., Part II. and the Staff Manual respectively. Title Pages will be prepared in manuscript." Form footer: "2449 Wt. W14957/M90 750,000 1/16 J.B.C. & A. Forms/C.2118/12." The same printed form is used on pages 4–6.) Signed in the Remarks column, vertically: H D Anderson.

**1 July 1917 — Gouy Servins**
5th Cdn. T.M. Battery moved from Gouy SERVINS to AIX NOULETTE HUTS.

**2 July 1917 — AIX NOULETTE**
Battery moved from Noulette huts to LES BREBIS. Battery relieved 137th and 135th T.M. Battery. 2 sections in support at LEAVIN Two sections in front line

**3 July 1917 — LES BREBIS**
22nd 25th sections in front line 24th and 26th in support at LEAVIN.

**4 July 1917 — LENS SECTOR**
Lieut J.A. Parke to hospital shell shocked. Lieut Calkin joined Battery from 26th.

**5 July 1917**
2 sections of Battery in line Two in support no firing.

**6 July 1917**
24th and 26th sections relieved 22nd and 25th in front line latter moved to support at LEAVIN. 1 O.R. wounded.

**7 July 1917**
24th and 26th sections in line 22nd and 25th in support. 1 O.R. wounded

**8 July 1917**
during the night of 8/9th we fired 16 rounds in retaliation to [uncertain].

**9 July 1917**
On night of 9/10th L.T.Ms. fired 130 rds. in retaliation to intermittent [uncertain] for all night. Lt. Calkin wounded 1 O.R. wounded.

## Notes

- **Coverage:** All 31 days of July 1917 have an entry; there are no missing dates. The diary text is only 4 pages (pages 3–6), as stated on the contents page. No appendices are listed; the Remarks column is blank apart from the signatures.
- **Why July 1917 is at the end of a March–August 1916 record:** The pages themselves give no explanation. There is no filing note or covering minute about the placement. The cover reads "VOLUME 1" with a "7." written below it, and the contents page gives the volume as "Volume 17-7". The July 1917 month is simply bound/filed at the end of this part of the file (part 5) as received.
- **Officers named:** Lieut J. A. Parke — sent to hospital, shell shocked, 4 July. Lieut Calkin — joined the battery from the 26th (Battalion), 4 July; wounded, night of 9/10 July. The diary is signed throughout by H. D. Anderson, Lieut., O.C. 5th T.M.B.
- **Casualties:** No men killed. Wounded: 1 other rank on 6 July; 1 other rank on 7 July; Lieut Calkin and 1 other rank on the night of 9/10 July. Injured: 1 other rank on 10 July. Shell shock: Lieut Parke, 4 July.
- **Movements:** 1 July, Gouy-Servins to Aix-Noulette huts; 2 July, Aix-Noulette to Les Brebis, taking over from the 137th and 135th T.M. Batteries in the Lens sector (sections in the front line and in support at Leavin); 10 July, relieved by the 4th Cdn. T.M. Battery and moved to the reserve area at Bully; 17 July, relieved the 4th Cdn. T.M. Battery, H.Q. near Cité St. Pierre; 23 July, relieved by the 6th Cdn. T.M. Battery and back to the reserve area at Bully; 31 July, relieved the 6th Cdn. T.M. Battery in the front line.
- **Battery sections:** The sections are numbered after the battalions they support — 22nd, 24th, 25th and 26th (the battalions of 5th Canadian Infantry Brigade) — and rotate between the front line and support at Leavin.
- **Firing:** 555 rounds recorded in total — 16 on the night of 8/9 July, 130 on the night of 9/10 July, 196 on 10 July, 100 on 18 July, 45 in wire-cutting on 19 July, and 46 + 22 on 22 July (the last against map references N 7. A. 7. H. and N. 7. A. 5. [uncertain], and suspected German trench-mortar emplacements). No firing on 5 July or 20–21 July.
- **Routine in reserve at Bully:** inspections, cleaning of guns and transport, church parades (15 and 29 July), bathing parade (26 July), muster parade (24 July), and a route march wearing gas helmets (27 July).
- **Place spellings as written:** "LEAVIN" is the diarist's spelling (Liévin); "Gouy Servins" (Gouy-Servins); "AIX NOULETTE" (Aix-Noulette); "Cite ST. PIERRE" (Cité St-Pierre); "Bully" (Bully, in the Lens sector).
- **Uncertain readings (4 [uncertain] flags in total):**
  1. 8 July — the word after "in retaliation to" is not clearly legible; it appears to read "Fishtail" but the sense is retaliation fire, so it is left as [uncertain] rather than guessed.
  2. 9 July — the same word after "intermittent", again left as [uncertain].
  3. 22 July — the final character of the second map reference ("N. 7. A. 5. …") is unclear; it looks like "6" or "b".
  4. 27 July — the word between "Battery went out," and "with" appears to read "tapes" but is not clear, and the entry breaks off at the foot of the page after "with".
