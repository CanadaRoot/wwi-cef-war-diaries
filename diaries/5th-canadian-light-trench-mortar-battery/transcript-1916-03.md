> Men taken to Divisional Baths. Sgt Moran 26th Bn placed under arrest by the camp Sgt Major, for insolence.

## Notes

- **Coverage:** Entries run 21–31 March 1916 with no date gaps; this is the battery's first diary month (it was formed on 21 March 1916).
- **Formation:** The battery was formed at Locre on 21 March 1916 from detachments of the 24th, 25th and 26th Battalions plus the 2nd Division Trench Mortar Group — 5 officers and 16 men in total — and ordered to report to Lt Leather, O.C. 2nd Division Trench Mortar Group.
- **Officers named:** Lt Leather (O.C. 2nd Division Trench Mortar Group); Lt R. M. Fair (O.C. 5th Trench Mortar Battery, who signs the diary); Lt Watson (O.C. 5th Bde M.G. Co).
- **Other ranks named:** Sgt Moran (26th Bn); an unnamed senior corporal made acting sergeant; an unnamed private sent in Moran's place.
- **Equipment:** Four 4-inch R.M.L. (rifled muzzle-loading) trench mortars handed over on 24 March, with "very little training" for the men.
- **Movements:** Locre → Dickebusch (advance party 26 March, as part of a Trench Mortar Group move); half the group moved on towards St Eloi on 27 March; the battery was attached to the 5th Brigade Machine Gun Company for rations, billets and discipline, and moved into new quarters in a barn near Locre on 30 March — although the Place column still reads Dickebusch for 30–31 March.
- **Discipline:** Sgt Moran arrested 22 March for insolence, returned to duty 23 March; several men arrested for drunkenness 30 March and remanded 31 March.
- **Casualties:** None recorded in March 1916.
- **Anomalies:** (1) The 24 March entry reads as if a word or punctuation is missing ("handed over by Lt Leather very little training for the men"); transcribed as written. (2) A stray "1" appears below the totals line of the 21 March composition table; it is not transcribed as content. (3) The initial letter of "Locre" in the Place column on page 3 appears struck through, but the place is clear from page 4.
