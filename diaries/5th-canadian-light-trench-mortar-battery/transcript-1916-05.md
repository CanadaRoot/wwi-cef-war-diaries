## Page 3 — Diary text (Army Form C.2118)

### 1 May 1916 — Noormegule [uncertain]

Bomb store in Noormegule [uncertain] hit by shell, and 1059 Stokes shells exploded. Two Stokes guns slightly damaged. During this tour the Stokes guns were being kept 1000 yds back from the front line.

### 2 May 1916 — ditto

Nil.

### 3 May 1916 — ditto

Relieved by the 6th Bde T.M. Batteries.

### 4 May 1916 — Reninghelst

Men taken to Baths at Reninghelst and to Quartermaster of 24th Bn for clothing and deficiencies of kit.

### 5 May 1916 — ditto

One officer going on leave.

### 6 May 1916 — ditto

Lt Lefebre [uncertain] 22nd Bn Canadians reports to replace Lt Morrison on leave.

### 7 May 1916 — ditto

Details for second Battery, report to Lt Fair for training. They were taken from the Battalions as follows.

| Unit | N.C.Os. | Men |
|---|---|---|
| 24th Bn | 2 | 4 |
| 25th Bn | 1 | 6 |
| 26th Bn | 1 | 5 |
| 22nd Bn | [uncertain — cell appears blank] | 1 |
| 5- C/L Battery | 1 act N.C.O. | 1 |
| **Total** | **5 N.C.Os.** | **17 men** |

## Page 4 — Diary text (Army Form C.2118)

### 8 May 1916 — Reninghelst

Training commenced for the 5-½ T.M. Battery.

### 9 May 1916 — ditto

Leave allotment 1 other Rank per week.

### 10 May 1916 — ditto

Men sent to baths. N.C.Os. given lecture and drills in accordance with the new drill phamlet [sic].

### 11 May 1916 — ditto

Slits were dug near camp as a bombardment of our billeting area was expected.

### 12 May 1916 — ditto

Men issued with two new type of helmets (the same as those issued to the artillery personnel.)

### 13 May 1916 — ditto

Several indents for equipment returned by the ordnance officer with instructions to submit them through Brigade H.Q. in future instead of direct.

### 14 May 1916 — ditto

Chaplain from 25th Bn holds service for the 5th Bde M.G. Coy and Trench Mortar Batteries.

### 15 May 1916 — ditto

Advance party sent ahead to arrange for the relief of the 4½ T.M. Battery on the St Eloi Front

## Page 5 — Diary text (Army Form C.2118)

### 15 May 1916 — St Eloi

Very little had been done in the way of making emplacements or bomb stores. Relief over early. Reconnaissance made that night of the right sector.

### 16 May 1916 — ditto

Bomb store built in trenches R5. and filled with 48 Stokes bombs.

### 17 May 1916 — ditto

Bomb store built in Estaminet Lane C.T.

### 18 May 1916 — ditto

Bomb store built in Communication Trench behind Triangular wood [uncertain].

### 19 May 1916 — ditto

Nil.

### 20 May 1916 — ditto

Due to the persistent shelling of Noormegule [uncertain], men were moved to partly finished dugouts in a bank in rear.

### 21 May 1916 — ditto

Until 22/5/16 Nil.

### 23 May 1916 — ditto

Relieved by 6th Bde T.M. Battery.

### 24 May 1916 — Reninghelst

Nil.

### 25 May 1916 — ditto

Arrangements made with the 5th Bde Quarter Master to draw deficiencies etc. in clothing for the men.

## Notes

- **Date coverage:** Every day 1–31 May 1916 is accounted for; 21–22 May are covered by a single combined "Nil" entry ("Until 22/5/16 Nil"). No dates are missing.
- **No casualties** are recorded in May 1916. The only incident of note is 1 May: a bomb store at Noormegule [uncertain] was hit by a shell and 1,059 Stokes shells exploded; two Stokes guns were slightly damaged.
- **Officers named:** Lt Morrison (on leave 6 May; the officer replaced), Lt Lefebre [uncertain] (22nd Bn, reported 6 May to replace Lt Morrison), Lt Fair (training the second battery, 7 May), Lt L. H. Johnston (reported for duty 27 May). The diary is signed by Lt R. M. Fair, O.C. 5th T.M. Battery.
- **Other ranks named:** Pte Thompson (arrested for drunkenness, 26 May).
- **Movements:** In the line near Noormegule [uncertain] until 3 May; relieved by the 6th Bde T.M. Batteries and back at Reninghelst 4–15 May; relieved the 4½ T.M. Battery on the St Eloi Front 15 May; in the St Eloi trenches 15–23 May; relieved 23 May and back at Reninghelst 24–31 May; relieved the 6th Bde T.M. Batteries on the St Eloi Front again on 31 May.
- **Other notable items:** A second (5½-inch) battery was formed from details drawn from the 24th, 25th, 26th and 22nd Battalions (5 N.C.Os., 17 men) plus 1 acting N.C.O. and 1 man from the 5th C/L Battery; training for it began 8 May. New-type steel helmets (same as artillery issue) were issued 12 May. The first 4-inch trench mortar bombs were obtained 29 May, and the 4-inch mortars were fired at the Divisional grenade school 30–31 May.
- **Place name:** The place written "Noormegule" throughout May is spelled "VOORMEZELE" in the June diary entries, which appears to be the same place; the May spelling is transcribed as written and flagged.
- **No appendices** are listed for May; the Remarks column carries only the officer's initials on each line.
