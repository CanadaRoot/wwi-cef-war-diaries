## Page 3 — Diary, 1–7 July (page-0024-July_1916_p._3.jpg)

Army Form C. 2118. WAR DIARY or INTELLIGENCE SUMMARY — 5th Cdn T.M. Battery.

### 1 July 1916 — Place: ST. ELOI.

On the Left Sector 321 rds were fired in support of the raid and on the night 89 rds. Casualties No 61530 Pte G. Trembly wounded. No. 67454. Sgt. G. E Roche. wounded 65481 Cpl. J Innis killed.

### 2 July 1916

Nil

### 3 July 1916

Stokes Gun successful in stopping hostile bombing

### 4 July 1916

Nil.

### 5 July 1916

Guns kept busy retaliating to hostile bombing. Guns mounted in front line & crews stood to support 22nd raiding party. No rounds were fired. Two prisoners captured.

### 6 July 1916

Meeting of Bn and Unit Commanders at Bde H.Q. to discuss future offensive operations on this front. Bde frontage roughly outlined so that preparations might be made

### 7 July 1916

As many men as possible from the Battery were [uncertain — word struck through in the original], helped by a Cpl & 2 Sappers from the engineers were used to build bomb stores and emplacements in view of the offensive. It was assumed that 200 Rds per mortar should be stored in dugouts on the front line and saps.

## Page 4 — Diary, 8–13 July (page-0025-July_1916_p._4.jpg)

### 8 July 1916 — Place: ST. ELOI

120 rounds fired in retaliation to hostile Bombing. Emplacements for 4 guns to support raid on the Centre Section by the 25th Bn were started. 300 rds delivered to Voormezele

### 9 July 1916

Nil

### 10 July 1916

Engineers working on Shelly Lane C.T. object very strongly to Stokes Guns being placed in or near communication trenches but Lt. Col. [uncertain] Helliam O.C. the Centre Section, gives permission for the guns to be placed wherever the Trench Mortar Officer thinks best.

### 11 July 1916

Raid planned for the 25th Bn cancelled and preparations made for Raid on Piccadilly Farm by the 22nd Bn.

### 12 July 1916

Retaliation by the Stokes Guns taking effect. Practically all the firing was done on the Craters as these seemed to be the only points within reach that the Germans kept a day garrison.

### 13 July 1916

One Stokes Gun and 100 rds bombs destroyed in Shelly Lane C.T. by a German rifle grenade hitting and detonating the Stokes bombs. New Gun sent for. Four Guns set up to cut wire at four points in front of PICCADILLY Fm. for the Raid. Fifty rounds were prepared for each gun. Operations called off on account of wind being unfavourable for gas.

## Page 5 — Diary, 14–22 July (page-0026-July_1916_p._5.jpg)

### 14 July 1916 — Place: ST. ELOI

Raid again called off on account of the wind. New gun arrives.

### 15 July 1916

5th Cdn T.M. Battery relieved by the 4th Cdn T.M Battery. An advanced party of 2 Officers an 2. N.C.Os sent in advance. Total number of bombs used during tour = 1500 approx. German trench Mortars much less Active than during early part of the tour.

### 16 July 1916 — Place: RENNINGHELST

Battery in permanent camp at Renninghelst

### 17 July 1916

Arrangements made to train and organize a second Battery Stokes Gun. Lieut R. M. Fair given Temporary rank of Captain while O.C. of the Battery, dated from June 9th.

### 18 July 1916

Detail for new Battery report. Officers detailed Lieut C. Fontaine 22nd Battn and Lieut Holmes 26th Bn. The 5th Cdn T.M. Battery is separated from the 5th Inf Bde for rations and discipline.

### 19 July 1916

Training of the new N.C.Os and men started. These were nearly all volunteers from the Battalions but with the exception of a few had not taken the course at the Divisional School or Army School
("course" is inserted above the line in the original.)

## Page 6 — Diary, 23–28 July (page-0027-July_1916_p._6.jpg)

### 23 July 1916 — Place: Vierstraete Front

5th Cdn T.M Battery relieved 6th Cdn T.M. Battery in accordance with 5th Cdn Inf Bgde. O.O. No. 41. The same scheme of operation with the infantry used by 6th T.M. Battery was adopted until more satisfactory plans were made. Lieut Gale joins the Battery to replace Lieut Johnson admitted to Hospital

### 24 July 1916

Four Stokes Guns placed in front line ready to retaliate for Hostile bombing. Between 2.30 P.M. & 3.00 P.M. 195 rds fired effectively on Hostile M.G. Emplacements in BOIS QUARANTE. Defensive emplacement in rear of No 6 Started and also one in rear of M. Dugout in BOIS CARRIE for Trench Mortar H.Q. Started.

### 25 July 1916

450 rds Stokes ammunition delivered to the BRASSERIE

### 26 July 1916

Nil

### 27 July 1916

129 Rds expended in retaliation for hostile bombing which is being reduced considerably.

### 28 July 1916

Hostile bombing Nil. Two guns trained to cover flanks of a raid on trenches near HOLLANDSCHESCHUUR Fm, Raid called off

## Page 7 — Diary, 29–31 July (page-0028-July_1916_p._7.jpg)

### 29 July 1916 — Place: VIERSTRAETE FRONT

Nil

### 30 July 1916

Three guns set up to cover raid by 25th Batt on BOIS QUARANTE the task allotted to Stokes Guns being to bombard the trench to be raided for five minutes and then lift the fire to form a barrage in rear. Raid called off. Hostile Trench Mortars very inactive all day until 9.30 P.M. When 2 fishtails and 20 Rum Jars were fired on the Right Sector. Stokes Guns retaliated with 56 rounds.

### 31 July 1916

German Trench Mortars still very inactive which is so unusual for this front. Reconnoissance made to find an emplacement for Stokes Gun in Support Trench at the head of POPPY LANE. Good positions were found but communications in daytime were bad.

Signed: R. M. Fair Capt, O.C. 5th Cdn T.M.B.

## Notes

- **Casualties (1 July 1916):** Pte G. Trembly, No. 61530, wounded; Sgt. G. E. Roche, No. 67454, wounded; Cpl. J. Innis, No. 65481, killed. No other casualties are recorded in July.
- **Officers named:** Lt. Col. Helliam, O.C. the Centre Section (10 July); Lieut R. M. Fair, given temporary rank of Captain while O.C. of the Battery, dated 9 June (17 July); Lieut C. Fontaine (22nd Bn) and Lieut Holmes (26th Bn) detailed to the new battery (18 July); Lieut Gale joins the Battery, replacing Lieut Johnson, admitted to hospital (23 July). The diary is signed by Capt. R. M. Fair, O.C. 5th Cdn T.M.B.
- **Movements:** St. Eloi front, 1–15 July; relieved by the 4th Cdn T.M. Battery on 15 July and in permanent camp at Renninghelst, 16–22 July; relieved the 6th Cdn T.M. Battery on the Vierstraete front on 23 July and remained there to 31 July.
- **Notable items:** a Stokes gun and 100 rounds destroyed by a German rifle grenade in Shelly Lane communication trench (13 July); raids by the 25th Bn and 22nd Bn repeatedly planned and called off, the Piccadilly Farm raid cancelled because the wind was unfavourable for gas; about 1,500 bombs used during the 1–15 July tour; a second Stokes gun battery being organised and trained from battalion volunteers; the Battery separated from the 5th Infantry Brigade for rations and discipline (18 July); two prisoners captured on 5 July.
- **Gaps:** none — every day from 1 to 31 July 1916 has an entry. No pages are missing or duplicated in this month's set.
