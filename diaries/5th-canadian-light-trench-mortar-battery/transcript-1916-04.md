## Notes

- **Coverage:** Entries run 1–30 April 1916 with no date gaps. 18–21 April are recorded as "No Record" (18th) with ditto marks for the 19th–21st.
- **Officers named:** Lt R. M. Fair (O.C. 5th Trench Mortar Battery); Lieut R. W. Morrison (26th Bn Canadians, took temporary command 11–22 April while Fair was in hospital); Sir Douglas Haig (named as cancelling the bombing attack on the St Eloi craters, 16 April).
- **Other ranks named:** Pte Drew (made acting corporal, employed as clerk and storeman, 1 April); two unnamed corporals returned from a Stokes gun course (2 April).
- **Strength:** Parade state given 9 April — 1 officer, 4 NCOs, 1 acting NCO, 15 gunners, 1 batman; total 22. (The battery had been formed with 5 officers and 16 men on 21 March; by 9 April only one officer was on parade.)
- **Health:** Lt Fair sent to hospital with measles on 10 April; returned 22 April. No other casualties recorded in April 1916.
- **Movements:** Locre → Reninghelst (3 April, with 5th Bde M.G. Co) → Dickebusch (12 April) → St Eloi trenches (15 April, reconnaissance) → back to Dickebusch (16 April) → Reninghelst camp (17 April) → St Eloi Front (27 April, relieving the 4th Trench Mortar Battery).
- **Training and equipment:** Stokes gun short course (two corporals returned 2 April; Lt Fair sent on the course 3 April, returned 9 April); a Stokes gun fetched from Locre on 12 April; gun drill and a few rounds fired for training; the battery's N.C.O. instructed men of the 4th Brigade Trench Mortar Battery on the Stokes gun (14 April); grenade-throwing training (25 April); Stokes gun training for 30 men and 2 N.C.Os under Lt Morrison (25 April). Map reference given for the Reninghelst camp: "h. 34. b. 1. 9."
- **Action:** On 16 April the battery was ordered to put a gun in an emplacement at T.4 to cover a bombing attack on the craters at St Eloi; the attack was cancelled by Sir Douglas Haig and the guns were taken back to Dickebusch. On 27 April the battery relieved the 4th Trench Mortar Battery on the St Eloi Front, and spent 28–30 April making emplacements and bomb stores, carrying bombs into the trenches, and cleaning ammunition.
- **Anomalies:** (1) The 1 April entry ends mid-sentence ("supplies for 4" R.M.L. Trench mortars indented for"); transcribed as written. (2) The 9 April entry begins with "Lt. Fair" written and struck through before the entry proper starts. (3) The 15 April entry has a word struck through before "Trench T.7."; the struck word cannot be read.
- **[uncertain] flags used:** 3 — the place name on 2 April and 3 April (appears to read "Lerdigham" both times; the Stokes gun course location), and the struck-through word in the 15 April entry.
