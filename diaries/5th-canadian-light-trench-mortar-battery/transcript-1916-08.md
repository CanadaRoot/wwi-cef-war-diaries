## Page 3 — Diary, 1–7 August (page-0031-August_1916_p._3.jpg)

Army Form C. 2118. WAR DIARY or INTELLIGENCE SUMMARY — ORIGINAL — 5th CAN. T.M. BATTERY.

### 1 August 1916 — Place: VEERSTRATT FRONT

German trench mortars still inactive.

### 2 August 1916

120 Stokes Bombs fired in retaliation for 14 Rum Jars, 75 Fish tails and 2 Minenwerfers. Bombardments started by 60th T.M Battery registering. Casualties. 1 killed and 1 wounded.

### 3 August 1916

Only one round fired; it was in retaliation for 1 rifle grenade. Lieut Fontaine & Lieut Holmes return to their battalions, not being suitable for Trench Mortar Work. Lieut [uncertain] Da'Cosli from the 22nd Bn joins instead.

### 4 August 1916

Ammunition expenditure nil.

### 5 August 1916

Gun in No 11 (Support Trench) fired one round at O.7.b.1.1. to register. Germans retaliated very heavily. Ammunition expended = 126 rounds. The disposition of the 6 Stokes Guns in the line is shown on the attached Sketch. For retaliation purposes the majority of the guns are moved forward into the front line.

### 6 August 1916

Nil

### 7 August 1916

Nil

## Page 4 — Diary, 8–18 August (page-0032-August_1916_p._4.jpg)

### 8 August 1916 — Place: VEERSTRATT FRONT

Relieved by the 4th Cdn T.M Battery. Battery moves to permanent Camp at RENNINGHELST.

### 9 August 1916 — Place: RENNINGHELST

Sixteen men 4 N.C.Os 3 instructors and 4 guns sent to the Divisional grenade school for instruction with 4 guns.

### 10 August 1916

Baths and deficiency parades held.

### 11 August 1916

Training started Lieut Rogers from 24th Bn joins the battery

### 12 August 1916

Nil

### 13 August 1916

Battery taken on an 8 mile route march.

### 14 August 1916

16 men & 4 N.C.Os from the battalions detailed for a Stokes Gun course at Divisional school

### 15 August 1916

Bde route march 12 miles. Stokes gun transport consisted of six hand carts fastened to the rear of Bde M.G. Transport.

### 16 August 1916

6th Bde T.M Battery relieved by 5th Bde T.M Battery on the ST. ELOI. Front. Relief complete by 11.30 P.M.
(The "6th" appears corrected over another figure in the original.)

### 17 August 1916 — Place: ST. ELOI

Lieut Anderson A.C. from the 26th Bn is attached to the 5th Cdn T.M. Battery for instruction. Ammunition expenditure nil.

### 18 August 1916

11th Bde T.M Battery is attached to the 5th Cdn T.M. Battery for instruction. Ammunition expenditure nil.

## Page 5 — Diary, 19–29 August (page-0033-August_1916_p._5.jpg)

### 19 August 1916 — Place: ST. ELOI FRONT.

NIL

### 20 August 1916

Germans trench mortars active during the afternoon. Stokes Guns retaliated with 284 rounds.

### 21 August 1916

Ammunition expended nil

### 22 August 1916

Stokes Guns retaliated heavily to the german trench mortars activity during the evening. 11th Trench Mortar Battery take over from the 5th Cdn T.M. Battery but the crews remain in till the following day to instruct

### 23 August 1916

[No separate entry for this date in the original. The 22 August entry states the crews remained in until the following day to instruct the 11th Trench Mortar Battery.]

### 24 August 1916 — Place: RENNINGHELST

Battery leaves VOORMEZEELE at 2.30 P.M for RENNINGHELST.

### 25 August 1916

Preparations made to leave area. Guns, equipment & officers kit packed on 12 hand carts which were fitted with a tongue to fasten to rear of M.G. [uncertain] limber

### 26 August 1916 — Place: STEENVOORTE

Battery moves off from camp at 4.30.A.M. and marched in rear of the 26th Bn. Billets reached at 1.00 P.M. Billets near STEENVOORTE.

### 27 August 1916 — Place: NOORDPEENE

March is continued to billets near NOORDPEENE

### 28 August 1916

March is continued to 2nd Cdn Div Training area near EPERLECQUES. 5th Cdn T.M. Battery Billets situated near HELLEBROUCQ.

### 29 August 1916 — Place: HELLEBROUCQ

Training started in the training area which is 2½ hrs march from the Billets. Area allotted to this Unit is in the vicinity of CORMETTE. Guns are taken to area in carts and arrangements made to leave them there with 1 detachment to look after them.

## Page 6 — Diary, 30–31 August (page-0034-August_1916_p._6.jpg)

### 30 August 1916 — Place: HELLEBROUCQ

Training for the day cancelled on account of the weather.

### 31 August 1916

Nil.

Signed: R. M. Fair, Capt, O.C. 5th Cdn T.M.B.

## Notes

- **Casualties (2 August 1916):** 1 killed and 1 wounded; no names are given in the diary. No other casualties are recorded in August.
- **Officers named:** Lieut Fontaine and Lieut Holmes returned to their battalions on 3 August as not suitable for trench mortar work; Lieut Da'Cosli [uncertain spelling] from the 22nd Bn joined in their place; Lieut Rogers (24th Bn) joined on 11 August; Lieut Anderson A.C. (26th Bn) was attached for instruction on 17 August. The diary is signed by Capt. R. M. Fair, O.C. 5th Cdn T.M.B.
- **Movements:** Vierstraete front, 1–8 August; relieved by the 4th Cdn T.M. Battery on 8 August and in camp at Renninghelst, 9–15 August; relieved the 6th Bde T.M. Battery on the St. Eloi front on 16 August; relieved by the 11th Trench Mortar Battery on 22 August, the crews staying a further day to instruct; left Voormezele at 2.30 p.m. on 24 August for Renninghelst; marched 26–28 August via Steenvoorte and Noordpeene to the 2nd Canadian Division training area near Eperlecques, with billets near Hellebroucq; training started 29 August near Cormette.
- **Notable items:** 284 rounds fired in retaliation on 20 August; the sketch showing the disposition of the 6 Stokes guns, referred to on 5 August, is not among the page images supplied; Stokes gun transport was six hand carts fastened to the rear of Brigade machine-gun transport (15 August); the 11th Bde T.M. Battery was attached to the 5th for instruction on 18 August.
- **Gaps:** 23 August 1916 has no separate entry (see the 22 August entry). All other days from 1 to 31 August 1916 have an entry. No pages are missing or duplicated in this month's set.
- **[uncertain] flags used: 2** — (1) the surname of Lieut Da'Cosli in the 3 August entry; (2) one word in the 25 August entry ("M.G. [uncertain] limber").
