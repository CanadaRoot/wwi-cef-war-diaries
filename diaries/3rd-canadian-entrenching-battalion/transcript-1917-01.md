# War Diary — 3rd Canadian Entrenching Battalion — January 1917

- **Unit:** 3rd Canadian Entrenching Battalion – 3rd Canadian Division
- **Month:** January 1917 — 1 January to 31 January 1917
- **Source:** Library and Archives Canada, Collection Search Item ID 2004945 (RG 9 III-D-3, vol. 5009, file 718, reel T-10858, finding aid 9-52)
- **Pages:** January 1917, p. 1 – p. 4 (image files page-0018 – page-0021)
- **January 1917 — COMPLETE** (pages 1–4): title page, contents page, diary text 1–31 January on 2 sheets, no appendices. Transcribed 2026-10-28.

## Transcription conventions
Original spelling, punctuation and abbreviations kept. Diary text is typewritten throughout (Army Form C. 2118, "WAR DIARY or INTELLIGENCE SUMMARY"). `[uncertain]` marks words I could not read with confidence — never guessed. Ditto marks (`"`) are as written in the original, repeating the word above; they are preserved rather than expanded. Several place names are heavily overtyped in the original (a contemporary censoring) but remain legible and are transcribed as read; where a place name could not be read at all it is marked `[uncertain]`. The Place column reads "FIELD" (partly blacked out) throughout. The "Hour" column is blank throughout. The "Remarks and references to Appendices" column is blank except for handwritten initials beside each entry. "W.P." = Working Party; "O.R." = Other Ranks; "Coy." = Company; "Offr." = Officer; "Can." = Canadian; "inst." = instant (of this month); "A. T. Coy" = as written (not expanded); "O.C." = Officer Commanding. The printed footer reads "2449 Wt. W14957/M90 750,000 1/16 J.B.C. & A. Forms/C.2118/12."

## Open questions / uncertain items (January 1917)
- 9th and 14th–18th, 27th, 30th–31st: the entries consist only of ditto marks in the original; the bracketed notes in this transcript explain which entry they repeat — the ditto marks themselves are the faithful reading.
- 4th: "1 Casualty" is as written; the casualty is not described or named.
- 11th: "1 Coy. [uncertain] pipe-laying" — the place name is blacked out in the original and could not be read.
- 22nd: "Moved from Hutsments at [uncertain] to tents at BOIS-de-ALIAN[uncertain]" — the camp the battalion moved from is blacked out, and the destination name is partly obscured by the censor's initials; it appears to be the same place given in the March diary as "BOIS DES ALLIEUX", but that reading could not be confirmed here and is not asserted.
- 24th: "3 Offr. arrived from Can. Base [uncertain]" — the line runs to the edge of the sheet and the final word (presumably "Depot") is cut off.
- 25th: "1 Coy. at [uncertain] on W.P." — the place name is blacked out in the original and could not be read.
- 28th: "Hostile Heavy Howitzers shelled vicinity of Camp this date. No Casualties." is as written.
- The signature at the foot of the January sheets is illegible; the signatory is given only as "Lieut-Col., O.C. 3rd Can. Entrenching Battn." and is not identified by name.
- Names read as: no individual officers or men are named in January 1917; the only unit named is the 43rd Can. Bn. (20th).
- Casualties in January 1917: 1 casualty on 4 January (not described or named); no other casualties are recorded — every other dated entry records "No Casualties".
- No map coordinates (latitude/longitude) are given; the only places named are Bois-de-Bray, La Targette, Chaussee-Brunehaut, Maroeuil, Neuville-St-Vaast, Savy, and the [uncertain] places noted above.
