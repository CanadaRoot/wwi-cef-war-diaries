# War Diary — 3rd Canadian Entrenching Battalion — July 1917

- **Unit:** 3rd Canadian Entrenching Battalion (3rd Canadian Division)
- **Month:** July 1917 — 1 July to 31 July 1917
- **Source:** Library and Archives Canada, Collection Search Item ID 2004945 (RG 9 III-D-3, vol. 5009, file 718, reel T-10858, finding aid 9-52)
- **Pages:** July 1917, p. 1 – p. 5 (image files page-0039 – page-0043)
- **July 1917 — COMPLETE** (pages 1–5): title page, index page, cover sheet, diary text 1–31 July, no appendices. Transcribed 2026-10-28.

## Transcription conventions
Original spelling, punctuation and abbreviations kept. Diary text is typewritten throughout (Army Form C. 2118, "WAR DIARY or INTELLIGENCE SUMMARY"). `[uncertain]` marks words I could not read with confidence — never guessed. `[signature — illegible]` marks a signature I could not read with confidence. The "Hour" column is blank throughout. The "Remarks and references to Appendices" column is blank except for a handwritten initial beside most entries. Place column reads "BOIS DES ALLEUX / MONT ST ELOY" throughout the month. "O.R." = Other Ranks; "C.B.D." = Canadian Base Depot; "R.E." = Royal Engineers; "A.F.", "C.C.T.D." = as written (not expanded). Ditto marks (") in the original are written out as "Working parties as on previous day" (etc.) in the transcription below, following the wording of the entry they repeat.

## Open questions / uncertain items (July 1917)
- 1 July: a word before "Pipe line" (after "AUX RITZ & NEUVILLE ST VAAST —") is illegible and is recorded as [uncertain]; it may read "laying" but that could not be confirmed.
- 1 July: "LA MOTTE R RESERVOIR" is as written (the "R" stands as typed).
- 6 July: "25 building Reservoir near SOUCHEZ" is as written — no "O.R." is legible after the figure "25".
- 6 July: the figure of O.R. working on Corps Light Railway is smudged in the original; read as "90 O.R." but a further digit may be present.
- 16 July: "56 O.R. operating Light Railway from NEUVILLE ST VAAST — SOUCHEZ — MAROC & BULLY-GRENAY" is as written; the "79 O.R. and 1 Officer from Canadian Base Depot" at the end of the 16th entry is on the last line of that entry's block, before the 17th entry begins.
- 24 July: a word before "5 O.R. wounded" (after "8 Officers arrived from Canadian Base Depot.") appears overtyped and illegible; recorded as [uncertain].
- 24 July: "CITE DE ABBOTTOIR" is as written.
- The signature at the foot of the July sheets is illegible; the signatory is given only as "Captain, Comdg 3rd Canadian Entrenching Battalion". The signature appears to read "A. B. Cusson" but that reading is not confident enough to record as a name.
- No officer or other rank is named anywhere in the July entries; only the commanding officer's signature appears.
- Casualties in July 1917: 8 O.R. killed in action (2 on 1 July, 2 on 25 July, 1 on 26–27 July, 3 on 27 July) and 25 O.R. wounded (2 on 8 July, 5 on 24 July, 4 on 25 July, 5 on 26–27 July, 6 on 27 July, 2 on 28 July, 1 on 29 July). No casualties on 30–31 July ("Casualties nil"). No individual casualties are named.
- No map coordinates (latitude/longitude) are given; the places named are Bois des Alleux and Mont St Eloy (Place column), and in the text: La Motte, Villers au Bois, Souchez, Aux Ritz, Neuville St Vaast, Vimy, Acq, Gouy Servins, Cité de Gaumont, Lens, Bully Grenay, La Targette, Château de la Haie, Gouy, Liéven (as written "LIEVEN"), Maroc, Hulluch, La Chaudière, and Cité de Abbottoir.
- Units named in the text: 172nd Tunnelling Company (172 Tunnelling Co.), 215th A.F. Camp, R.E. Park Gouy Servins, Canadian Base Depot.
