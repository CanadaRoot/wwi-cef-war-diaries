# War Diary — 3rd Canadian Entrenching Battalion — April 1917

- **Unit:** 3rd Canadian Entrenching Battalion — 3rd Canadian Division
- **Month:** April 1917 — 1 April to 30 April 1917
- **Source:** Library and Archives Canada, Collection Search Item ID 2004945 (RG 9 III-D-3, vol. 5009, file 718, reel T-10858, finding aid 9-52)
- **Pages:** April 1917, p. 1 – p. 3 (image files page-0028 – page-0030)
- **April 1917 — COMPLETE** (pages 1–3): cover, title page, diary text 1–30 April on a single Army Form C. 2118 sheet, no appendices. Transcribed 2026-10-28.

## Transcription conventions
Original spelling, punctuation and abbreviations kept. Diary text is typewritten throughout (Army Form C. 2118, "WAR DIARY or INTELLIGENCE SUMMARY"). `[blacked out]` marks words obliterated by the censor's black ink — much of this month's sheet, including most place names and the whole Place column, was blacked out before the diary was filed, and those words cannot be recovered from the image. `[uncertain]` marks words I could not read with confidence — never guessed. `[ditto marks]` marks rows where the original uses ditto marks (") repeating the previous row's entry. Each entry ends with handwritten initials (illegible) in the Summary column. The "Hour" column is blank throughout. The "Remarks and references to Appendices" column is blank throughout. The sheet heading ("THIRD CANADIAN ENTRENCHING BATTALION. MONTH ENDING APRIL 30th 1917.") is partly blacked out. "O.R." = Other Ranks; "Cdn." = Canadian; "Coy." = Company; "Div." = Division; "R.F.C." = Royal Flying Corps; "Rly." = Railway; "A.M." / "P.M." as written. "VAAST" = Vaast (Neuville-St Vaast); "VIMY RIDGE" as written.

## Open questions / uncertain items (April 1917)
- This month's diary sheet was heavily censored before filing: the whole Place column is blacked out, the sheet heading is partly blacked out, and most place names inside the entries (roads, pipe-line and sleeper-road locations, the reservoir and camp names) are obliterated. The legible places are VIMY RIDGE, the VIMY sleeper road and cable line, MONT ST ELOY Road, and the "VAAST Road" (the word before VAAST is blacked out in each case). The censored place after "LA" (pipe line, sleeper road) is a long word in each case but is not legible and is not guessed here.
- 1 Apr: the word after "mining" is blacked out; the road the two Coys were on is blacked out.
- 2 Apr: the destination of the despatched officer reads "R.F.C." (Royal Flying Corps).
- 4 Apr: camp shelled by enemy heavy howitzers 9–10 A.M. and 8–9 P.M.; no casualties stated for this shelling.
- 5 Apr: the second line of the entry is damaged — "pipe line. One platoon [blacked out] and [blacked out] platoon on MONT ST ELOY Road. 100 O.R. despatched to Units." — the number of O.R. despatched reads as 100.
- 7 Apr: both platoon tasks are blacked out; only "One platoon [blacked out] on [blacked out] Road. One platoon [blacked out]." survives.
- 8th–9th: the entry records the camp shelled at 9-00 P.M. followed by ditto marks and the words "three casualties" — whether the three casualties were caused by that shelling is not stated.
- 23rd–25th: the date column for the combined row after the 22nd reads as "23rd, 24th, 25th" (the figures are blurred); the 26th is a separate row ("5 Officers arrived from Base and 14 O.R. despatched to Unit."). All 30 days of April are covered with no gaps.
- The closing sentence's middle clause is blacked out: "Long hours and arduous work was performed by the troops during this month owing to the large programme of work [blacked out] by the capture of VIMY RIDGE."
- The signature at the foot of the sheet is illegible; the signatory is given only as "Lieut.-Col., Comdg 3rd CANADIAN ENTRENCHING BATTALION." and is not identified by name.
- Names read as: none — no officer or man is named in the April entries; only "2nd. Tunnelling Coy." is named as a unit. The despatched officer's destination (R.F.C.) is given but not his name.
- Casualties in April 1917: "three casualties" recorded 8th–9th (camp shelled at 9-00 P.M.); no names, and no killed/wounded split, are given. No other casualties are recorded in April.
- No map coordinates (latitude/longitude) are given; the only places legible are VIMY RIDGE, the VIMY sleeper road, the VIMY RIDGE cable line, MONT ST ELOY Road, and the VAAST Road.
