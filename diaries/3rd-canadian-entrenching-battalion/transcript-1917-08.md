# War Diary — 3rd Canadian Entrenching Battalion — August 1917

- **Unit:** 3rd Canadian Entrenching Battalion (3rd Canadian Division)
- **Month:** August 1917 — 1 August to 31 August 1917
- **Source:** Library and Archives Canada, Collection Search Item ID 2004945 (RG 9 III-D-3, vol. 5009, file 718, reel T-10858, finding aid 9-52)
- **Pages:** August 1917, p. 1 – p. 5 (image files page-0044 – page-0048)
- **August 1917 — COMPLETE** (pages 1–5): title page, index page, cover sheet, diary text 1–31 August, no appendices. Transcribed 2026-10-28.

## Transcription conventions
Original spelling, punctuation and abbreviations kept. Diary text is typewritten throughout (Army Form C. 2118, "WAR DIARY or INTELLIGENCE SUMMARY"). `[uncertain]` marks words I could not read with confidence — never guessed. `[signature — illegible]` marks a signature I could not read with confidence. The "Hour" column is blank throughout. The "Remarks and references to Appendices" column is blank except for a handwritten initial beside most entries. The Place column reads "BOIS DES ALLEUX / MONT ST ELOY" for 1–18 August and "VILLERS AU BOIS" from 19 August. "O.R." = Other Ranks; "Off." = Officer(s); "R.E." = Royal Engineers; "A.F.", "C.C.T.D." = as written (not expanded). Ditto marks (") in the original are written out as "Working Parties as on previous day" (etc.) in the transcription below, following the wording of the entry they repeat.

## Open questions / uncertain items (August 1917)
- 1 August: "1 Officer and 75 working at AUX RITZ" is as written — no "O.R." follows the figure "75".
- 20 August: "from NEUVILLE ST VAAST THELUS to PETIT VIMY" is as written (no separator between "NEUVILLE ST VAAST" and "THELUS" is legible).
- 20 August: "2 Officer and 54 operating Light Railway" is as written ("Officer" singular).
- 20 August: "AIX NOULETTE" and "LEIVEN" are as written.
- 23 August: the number of officers transferred to units is struck through and illegible; recorded as "[uncertain] Officers and 20 OR transferred to Units".
- 27 August: a word before "15 Off. and 505 O.R." is struck through and illegible; recorded as "[uncertain] 15 Off. and 505 O.R. ...". The struck word may read "Strength" but that could not be confirmed.
- 31 August: the word "the" in "dispatched to the 3rd Canadian Divisional Training Centre" is overtyped in the original but reads as "the".
- The signature at the foot of the August sheets is illegible; the signatory is given only as "Captain, Comdg 3rd Cdn Entrenching Battalion". The signature appears to read "A. B. Cusson" — the same signature as on the July sheets — but that reading is not confident enough to record as a name.
- No officer or other rank is named anywhere in the August entries; only the commanding officer's signature appears.
- Casualties in August 1917: 4 O.R. killed in action (2 on 20 August, 2 on 23 August) and 17 O.R. wounded (5 on 20 August, 12 on 23 August), plus 50 casualties reported only as a number with no killed/wounded split (4 on 4 August, 2 on 9 August, 24 on 13 August, 14 on 14 August, 4 on 19 August, 2 on 27 August). No individual casualties are named.
- No map coordinates (latitude/longitude) are given; the places named are Bois des Alleux and Mont St Eloy (Place column to 18 August), Villers au Bois (Place column from 19 August), Ferfay and Houdain (the move of 28–30 August), and in the text: La Motte, Aux Ritz, Gouy Servins, La Chaudière, Neuville St Vaast, Souchez, Cité de Gaumont, Maroc, Bully Grenay, Lens, Thélus, Petit Vimy, Liéven (as written "LEIVEN"), Angres, Aix Noulette, Fosse 11, Loos, St Pierre, Calonne, Château d'Acq, and Le Pendu.
- Units named in the text: 215th A.F. Co., 172nd Tunnelling Co., No 2 Forestry Company, Canadian Corps Signals, Canadian Corps Light Railway, Canadian Corps Tramway Company, Canadian Base Depot, 3rd Canadian Divisional Training Centre.
- Notable non-combat item: on 14 August, "Voting took place for the Alberta Provincial Parliament".
