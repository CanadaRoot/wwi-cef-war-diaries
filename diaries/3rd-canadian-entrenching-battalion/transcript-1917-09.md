# War Diary — 3rd Canadian Entrenching Battalion — September 1917

- **Unit:** 3rd Canadian Entrenching Battalion (3rd Canadian Division)
- **Month:** September 1917 — 1 September to 30 September 1917
- **Source:** Library and Archives Canada, Collection Search Item ID 2004945 (RG 9 III-D-3, vol. 5009, file 718, reel T-10858, finding aid 9-52)
- **Pages:** September 1917, p. 1 – p. 3 (image files page-0049 – page-0051)
- **September 1917 — COMPLETE** (pages 1–3): title page, index page, diary text 1–30 September, no appendices. Transcribed 2026-10-28.

## Transcription conventions
Original spelling, punctuation and abbreviations kept. Diary text is typewritten throughout (Army Form C. 2118, "WAR DIARY or INTELLIGENCE SUMMARY"). `[uncertain]` marks words I could not read with confidence — never guessed. `[signature — illegible]` marks a signature I could not read with confidence. The "Hour" column is blank throughout. The "Remarks and references to Appendices" column is blank except for a handwritten initial beside most entries. Place column reads "FERFAY" throughout the month. "O.R." = Other Ranks. Ditto marks (") in the original are written out in full in the transcription below, following the wording of the entry they repeat.

## How the diary closes
September 1917 is the final month of this diary — the last of 14 volumes (Volume 14). There is no separate ORIGINAL/CONFIDENTIAL cover sheet for September, and the diary text is a single page. At midnight on 15 September all personnel on the strength of the battalion were absorbed on the strength of the Canadian Corps Reinforcement Camp; over 27–29 September all reinforcements for the 8th, 9th and 7th Canadian Infantry Brigades were transferred to the 3rd Canadian Divisional Training Depot; and on 30 September all transport, stores and equipment were transferred to the 3rd Canadian Divisional Training Depot. The sheet is signed by a Lieutenant-Colonel commanding the battalion. No further diary pages follow in this file.

## Open questions / uncertain items (September 1917)
- The 15 September entry is written inside the 1st–27th block, out of date order; it is transcribed in the position it appears on the page.
- The 28th and 29th entries are ditto marks in the original, with only the brigade number changed ("9th" on the 28th, "7th" on the 29th); they are written out in full above.
- The signature at the foot of the September sheet is illegible; the signatory is given only as "Lieut Col., Comdg 3rd Canadian Entrenching Battalion". It is a different hand from the Captain's signature on the July and August sheets. The signature appears to read "A. B. Hopkins" but that reading is not confident enough to record as a name.
- No officer or other rank is named anywhere in the September entries; only the commanding officer's signature appears.
- Casualties in September 1917: none recorded. No deaths, no wounded, and no casualty figures of any kind are mentioned in the September entries.
- No map coordinates (latitude/longitude) are given; the only place named is Ferfay (Place column). The other bodies named are the Canadian Corps Tramway Company, the 3rd Canadian Divisional Training Depot, the Canadian Corps Reinforcement Camp, and the 8th, 9th and 7th Canadian Infantry Brigades.
- No appendices accompany the September diary text, and no further pages follow in this file — the diary ends with the 30 September entry.
