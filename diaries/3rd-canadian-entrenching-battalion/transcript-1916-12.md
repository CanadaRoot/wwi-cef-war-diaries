# War Diary — 3rd Canadian Entrenching Battalion — December 1916

- **Unit:** 3rd Canadian Entrenching Battalion – 3rd Canadian Division
- **Month:** December 1916 — 1 December to 31 December 1916
- **Source:** Library and Archives Canada, Collection Search Item ID 2004945 (RG 9 III-D-3, vol. 5009, file 718, reel T-10858, finding aid 9-52)
- **Pages:** December 1916, p. 1 – p. 4 (image files page-0014 – page-0017)
- **December 1916 — COMPLETE** (pages 1–4): title page, contents page, diary text 1–31 December on 2 sheets, no appendices. Transcribed 2026-10-28.

## Transcription conventions
Original spelling, punctuation and abbreviations kept. Diary text is typewritten throughout (Army Form C. 2118, "WAR DIARY or INTELLIGENCE SUMMARY"). `[uncertain]` marks words I could not read with confidence — never guessed. Ditto marks (`"`) are as written in the original, repeating the word above; they are preserved rather than expanded. Several place names are heavily overtyped in the original (a contemporary censoring) but remain legible and are transcribed as read; where a place name could not be read at all it is marked `[uncertain]`. The Place column reads "FIELD" (partly blacked out) throughout. The "Hour" column is blank throughout. The "Remarks and references to Appendices" column is blank except for handwritten initials beside each entry. "W.P." = Working Party; "O.R." = Other Ranks; "Coy." = Company; "Offr." = Officer; "Can." = Canadian; "Lt. Ry." = Light Railway; "C.B. Depot" and "Can. Base Depot" = as written (Canadian Base Depot); "Inf. Bn." = Infantry Battalion; "D.S.O." = Distinguished Service Order; "O.C." = Officer Commanding. The printed footer reads "2449 Wt. W14957/M90 750,000 1/16 J.B.C. & A. Forms/C.2118/12."

## Open questions / uncertain items (December 1916)
- 3rd: the two place names in "W.P. for [uncertain], MAROEUIL & [uncertain], St. ELOY" are blacked out in the original and could not be read; the same blacked-out place before "St. ELOY" recurs on 16th–24th and is marked [uncertain] each time.
- 6th: "6 Offr. Arrived from C.B. Depot" is as written; 7th: the "4 Offr." at the end of the row is as written — the ditto marks make it unclear whether the 4 officers arrived or were despatched.
- 12th: "W.P. furnished for Town Major at [uncertain]" — the place after "at" is blacked out and could not be read.
- 19th: "laying pipe at CHAUSSEE-BRUNEHAUT" is as written (elsewhere "laying pipe line").
- 29th: "Casualties... 1 O.R. Killed in Action" is as written; the one O.R. killed in action is not named.
- The place names MAROEUIL, CHAUSSEE-BRUNEHAUT, BOIS-de-BRAY and LA TARGETTE are read through heavy overtyping in the original; the readings are consistent across the month's entries.
- The signature at the foot of the December sheets is illegible; the signatory is given only as "Lt-Col. D.S.O., O.C. 3rd Can. Entrenching Battn." and is not identified by name.
- Names read as: no individual officers or men are named in December 1916; the only unit named is the 43rd Can. Inf. Bn. (1st).
- Casualties in December 1916: 1 O.R. killed in action on 29 December (not named); no other casualties are recorded — every other dated entry records "No Casualties".
- No map coordinates (latitude/longitude) are given; the only places named are Maroeuil, Chaussee-Brunehaut, Bois-de-Bray, St. Eloy, La Targette, and the [uncertain] places noted above.
