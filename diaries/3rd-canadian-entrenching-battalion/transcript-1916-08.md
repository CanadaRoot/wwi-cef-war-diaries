# War Diary — 3rd Canadian Entrenching Battalion — August 1916

- **Unit:** 3rd Canadian Entrenching Battalion — 3rd Canadian Division
- **Month:** August 1916 — 6 August to 31 August 1916
- **Source:** Library and Archives Canada, Collection Search Item ID 2004945 (RG 9 III-D-3, vol. 5009, file 718, reel T-10858, finding aid 9-52)
- **Pages:** August 1916, p. 1 – p. 4 (image files page-0001 – page-0004)
- **August 1916 — COMPLETE** (pages 1–4): title page, diary-text header page, diary text 6–31 August, no appendices. Transcribed 2026-10-28.

## Transcription conventions
Original spelling, punctuation and abbreviations kept. Diary text is typewritten throughout on Army Form C. 2118, "WAR DIARY or INTELLIGENCE SUMMARY". `[blacked out]` marks words deliberately blacked out (censored) in the original — several place names are blacked out throughout, including the whole Place column. `[uncertain]` marks words I could not read with confidence — never guessed. The ditto mark `"` in the original is reproduced as written, repeating the word above it. A handwritten initial appears beside each entry. The "Hour" column is blank throughout. The "Remarks and references to Appendices" column is blank throughout. "O.C." = Officer Commanding; "O.R." = other ranks; "Battn." = Battalion; "Can." = Canadian; "Div." = Division. The battalion was mobilized on 6 August 1916, so there are no entries for 1–5 August. The Place column is blacked out on both diary sheets, so no camp location is recorded for August except the camp names given in the text (Winnipeg Camp, Vancouver Camp, P Camp).

## Open questions / uncertain items (August 1916)
- Title page: "From 1st August" has "1st" struck through with "6th" written below in pencil; the diary itself begins 6 August, the day the battalion was mobilized.
- The Place column is blacked out on both diary sheets, and several place names in the text are blacked out: the arrival point on 7 Aug (two words blacked out), the place north-west of which the enemy gas attack fell on 8 Aug, the wood on 13 Aug (one word blacked out before "WOODS"), the direction of the gas alarm on 21 Aug, and a word in the 29 Aug entry ("Working Party suspended [blacked out] operations"). None of these has been guessed.
- 7 Aug: "Arrived at [blacked out] at 10.00 P.M." — the blacked-out place name appears to be two words.
- 8 Aug: "Arrived at P CAMP ( Winnipeg) 2.30 A.M." is as written ("P CAMP" as written; "Winnipeg" is the camp name, not the Canadian city). "(Stood To " is as written, with an opening parenthesis and a closing quotation mark after "To".
- 10 Aug: "Battalion inspected by O.C. Second Army Group of Entrenching Battalions" is as written; no inspecting officer is named.
- 12 Aug: the ditto line ends with a word or mark I could not read, rendered "[uncertain] ( 1st in Battn.)".
- Dates on the second diary sheet (p. 4) are not in sequence as typed: 21, 23, 25, 26, 28, 30, then 27, 28, 29, 30, 31 — the dates 28 and 30 each appear twice. No entries are dated 20, 22 or 24 August. The dates are transcribed exactly as typed on the sheet.
- 23 Aug: "14 Officer and 650 Other Rank" is as written (singular "Officer" and "Rank").
- 29–30 Aug: the word after "suspended" on 29 Aug is blacked out; the 30 Aug entry is a ditto line ending in "operations."
- The signature at the foot of the August sheets is illegible; the signatory is given only as "Lt.-Col. D.S.O., Comdg. 3rd Canadian Entrenching Battalion" and is not identified by name.
- Names read as: no individual officers or other ranks are named in the August entries. Units named: Second Army Group of Entrenching Battalions; 3rd Divisional Units / 3rd Division Units / 3rd Canadian Div. Units; 52nd Battn.; 3rd Can. Pioneer Battn.; CANADIAN BASE DEPOT / CAN. BASE DEPOT.
- Casualties in August 1916: 1 casualty on 23 Aug and 2 casualties on 25 Aug (no names, no detail of killed or wounded given); no casualties on all other working-party days. No deaths are named in August.
- No map coordinates (latitude/longitude) are given. Places named are Havre (marched to, 6 Aug), P Camp (Winnipeg Camp), Vancouver Camp, E. & Q. Trenches (working-party location, daily), and [blacked out] WOODS (13 Aug). No other places are named.
