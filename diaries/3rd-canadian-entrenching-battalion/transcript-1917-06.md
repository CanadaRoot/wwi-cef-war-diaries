# War Diary — 3rd Canadian Entrenching Battalion — June 1917

- **Unit:** 3rd Canadian Entrenching Battalion — 3rd Canadian Division
- **Month:** June 1917 — 1 June to 30 June 1917
- **Source:** Library and Archives Canada, Collection Search Item ID 2004945 (RG 9 III-D-3, vol. 5009, file 718, reel T-10858, finding aid 9-52)
- **Pages:** June 1917, p. 1 – p. 5 (image files page-0034 – page-0038)
- **June 1917 — COMPLETE** (pages 1–5): cover, title page, handwritten "ORIGINAL / CONFIDENTIAL" cover sheet, diary text 1–21 June on the first Army Form C. 2118 sheet and 22–30 June on the second ("continued") sheet, no appendices. Transcribed 2026-10-28.

## Transcription conventions
Original spelling, punctuation and abbreviations kept. Diary text is typewritten throughout (Army Form C. 2118, "WAR DIARY or INTELLIGENCE SUMMARY" — on the first sheet the "INTELLIGENCE SUMMARY" line is struck through). `[blacked out]` marks words obliterated by the censor's black ink — a few words on the June sheets were blacked out and cannot be recovered from the image. `[uncertain]` marks words I could not read with confidence — never guessed. `[ditto marks]` marks rows where the original uses ditto marks (") repeating the previous row's entry. Each entry ends with handwritten initials (illegible) in the Summary column. The "Hour" column is blank throughout. The "Remarks and references to Appendices" column is blank throughout. The sheet headings ("THIRD CANADIAN ENTRENCHING BATTALION. MONTH ENDING JUNE 30TH 1917." / "... (continued)") are partly blacked out. "O.R." = Other Ranks; "Cdn." = Canadian; "Coy." = Company; "Div." = Division; "Off." = Officer; "R.E." = Royal Engineers; "Rly." = Railway; "A.F. Coy" / "A.F. Camp" = as written (not expanded); "A.Q." = as written (not expanded). "VAAST" = Vaast (Neuville-St Vaast); "MAISON BLANCHE" as written.

## Open questions / uncertain items (June 1917)
- The Place column on both diary sheets reads "BOIS-DES-ALLEAUX" over "NEAR ST. ELOY", partly blacked out. "ST. ELOY" here is read as written; the battalion's work sites throughout the month are around MONT ST ELOY, so the second line may have originally read "NEAR MONT ST. ELOY" with "MONT" blacked out — this is not assumed in the transcript.
- "MERLIN": the railway/road name after "NEUVILLE ST VAAST -" (7th, 12th, 22nd, 28th, 30th) appears to read "MERLIN" but is not fully legible in any instance; it is flagged [uncertain] each time and is not treated as certain.
- "RUGBY DUMP": the reservoir name near SOUCHEZ (15th, 19th, 22nd, 25th, 30th) appears to read "RUGBY DUMP" but is not fully legible; flagged [uncertain] each time.
- "216th A.F. Coy" / "216th A.F. Camp": the number reads as "216th" in all four mentions (22nd, 25th, 28th, 30th) but the figures are blurred; the reading is kept as written with this reservation. On the 22nd, a word between "216th A.F. Coy" and "MONT ST ELOY Station" is illegible and is flagged [uncertain].
- 11 June: the ditto row ends with what appears to read "& 3 Off" and the rest is illegible; the entry is otherwise lost.
- 12 June: "1 Officer & 42 working on BOIS DE BRAY Reservoir" — "O.R." after 42 is not clearly legible in the original. "21 O.R. water supply A.Q. [uncertain — appears to read Aerodrome]" — the word after "A.Q." is not certain.
- 18 June: "23 O.R. loading rails [uncertain] MONT ST ELOY" — the word(s) between "rails" and "MONT ST ELOY" are not legible (the 19th entry gives "R.E. Park" in the same position, but that reading is not assumed here).
- 19 June: "1 Officer & 64 O.R. Light Railway [blacked out] grading track" — a word is blacked out.
- 22 June: the number of O.R. at the Railway Yard, MONT ST ELOY Station, reads as 97 but could be 87.
- 28 June: the second place in "LA MOTTE - LA [uncertain — appears to read TARGETTE] Pipe Line" is not fully legible.
- 29 June: "7 Officers and [uncertain] 25 O.R. from Base" — there is an unclear mark before "25" which may be a stray ink mark.
- The signature at the foot of the second sheet is illegible; the signatory is given only as "Lieut. Col., Comdg 3rd Canadian Entrenching Battalion." and is not identified by name. The first sheet (1–21 June) is unsigned.
- Names read as: none — no officer or man is named in the June entries.
- Casualties in June 1917: none recorded. No deaths, no wounded, no men admitted to hospital are mentioned in the June entries.
- No map coordinates (latitude/longitude) are given; the places named are BOIS DES ALLEAUX (camp and saw mill), MONT ST ELOY (water stores, reservoirs, station, R.E. Park), LA MOTTE (reservoirs), BOIS DE BRAY (reservoirs), AUX RITZ and MAISON BLANCHE (pipe line), ECOIVRES (pumping station), NEUVILLE ST VAAST (light railway), SOUCHEZ, and the 216th A.F. Coy/Camp.
