# War Diary — 3rd Canadian Entrenching Battalion — November 1916

- **Unit:** 3rd Canadian Entrenching Battalion — 3rd Canadian Division
- **Month:** November 1916 — 1 November to 30 November 1916
- **Source:** Library and Archives Canada, Collection Search Item ID 2004945 (RG 9 III-D-3, vol. 5009, file 718, reel T-10858, finding aid 9-52)
- **Pages:** November 1916, p. 1 – p. 3 (image files page-0011 – page-0013)
- **November 1916 — COMPLETE** (pages 1–3): title page, diary-text header page, diary text 1–30 November on a single sheet, no appendices. Transcribed 2026-10-28.

## Transcription conventions
Original spelling, punctuation and abbreviations kept. Diary text is typewritten throughout on Army Form C. 2118, "WAR DIARY or INTELLIGENCE SUMMARY". `[blacked out]` marks words deliberately blacked out (censored) in the original. `[uncertain]` marks words I could not read with confidence — never guessed. The ditto mark `"` in the original is reproduced as written, repeating the word above it. A handwritten initial appears beside each entry. The "Hour" column is blank throughout. The "Remarks and references to Appendices" column is blank throughout. "O.R." = other ranks; "Bn." = Battalion; "O.C." = Officer Commanding; "C.B. Depot" = Canadian Base Depot; "Fd. Coy. C.E." = Field Company, Canadian Engineers; "Div." = Division. The Place column reads "ACQ." for the month. Dates are grouped where the original groups them (to 14th, to 21st, 23rd & 24th, & 30th).

## Open questions / uncertain items (November 1916)
- 26th: a word after "arrived" in "10 Other Ranks arrived [uncertain] for Engineer Pool." is struck through and illegible; it is not guessed. The sentence continues on the next line ("Engineer Pool.").
- 28th: the ditto line ends "& RECEIVED. No Casualties." The word "RECEIVED" is transcribed as it appears, but its sense in the sentence is unclear — it may be a mistyping. It is rendered as read, not interpreted.
- The sheet heading's unit name is partly blacked out; the heading is given as "( MONTH ENDING NOVEMBER 30th, 1916 )." and the unit name is not reproduced from the heading.
- The signature at the foot of the November sheet appears to read "Robinson", Captain, O.C. 3rd Canadian Entrenching Battn. — a change from the "Lt.-Col. D.S.O." who signed the August, September and October sheets. The signature is a flourish and is read with that reservation.
- Names read as: no individual officers or other ranks are named in the November entries, apart from the signature (Robinson). Units named: Can. Base Depot / C. B. Depot; 3rd Div. Gas School; 9th Fd. Coy. C.E.; Engineer Pool.
- Casualties in November 1916: none recorded. Every working-party entry states "No Casualties", and no deaths, wounded or hospital admissions are mentioned in November.
- No map coordinates (latitude/longitude) are given. Places named are ACQ (the battalion's camp, Place column), MAROEUIL, and MONT ST. ELOY (working parties, 15–30 Nov). The battalion spent the month at ACQ preparing the camp for winter occupation, drilling and field training, and furnishing working parties at MAROEUIL and MONT ST. ELOY from 15 Nov.
