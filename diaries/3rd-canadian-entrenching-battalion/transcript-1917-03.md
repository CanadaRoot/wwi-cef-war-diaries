# War Diary — 3rd Canadian Entrenching Battalion — March 1917

- **Unit:** 3rd Canadian Entrenching Battalion – 3rd Canadian Division
- **Month:** March 1917 — 1 March to 31 March 1917
- **Source:** Library and Archives Canada, Collection Search Item ID 2004945 (RG 9 III-D-3, vol. 5009, file 718, reel T-10858, finding aid 9-52)
- **Pages:** March 1917, p. 1 – p. 3 (image files page-0025 – page-0027)
- **March 1917 — COMPLETE** (pages 1–3): title page, contents page, diary text 1–31 March on 1 sheet, no appendices. Transcribed 2026-10-28.

## Transcription conventions
Original spelling, punctuation and abbreviations kept. Diary text is typewritten throughout (Army Form C. 2118, "WAR DIARY or INTELLIGENCE SUMMARY"). `[uncertain]` marks words I could not read with confidence — never guessed. Ditto marks (`"`) are as written in the original, repeating the word above; they are preserved rather than expanded. Several place names are heavily overtyped in the original (a contemporary censoring) but remain legible and are transcribed as read. The Place column reads "BOIS DES ALLIEUX." for the 1st–24th entries and "MONT ST ELOY." from the 25th. The "Hour" column is blank throughout. The "Remarks and references to Appendices" column is blank except for handwritten initials beside each entry. "W.P." = Working Party; "O.R." = Other Ranks; "Coy." = Company; "Offr." = Officer; "Cdn." = Canadian; "Battn." = Battalion; "Comdg" = Commanding; "O.C." = Officer Commanding. The printed footer reads "2449 Wt. W14957/M90 750,000 1/16 J.B.C. & A. Forms/C.2118/12."

## Open questions / uncertain items (March 1917)
- 3rd: "BOIS DE VAAST" and "AU RITZ" are transcribed as read; neither name is corroborated elsewhere in these four months and both should be treated with reservation.
- 6th: the entry is only ditto marks plus "425 O.R." — the original does not say what the 425 O.R. did (the 5th entry, also ditto marks plus a number, says "176 O.R. transferred to Units of 3rd Cdn Division").
- 12th: the entry is ditto marks plus "One [uncertain] 2 [uncertain]" — the words around the figures could not be read; it is not clear whether officers or men are meant.
- 18th: "4: Officers despatched to Units" is as written (the colon after "4" is in the original).
- 24th: "camp shelled by enemy 21cm Howitzer. One casualty" is as written; the casualty is not described or named.
- 25th: "58, 8" shells fell in camp area" is as written — read as 58 eight-inch shells, but the original punctuation is preserved and the reading is not expanded.
- 26th is entered twice in the original: once as its own dated row ("Work parties as on previous date. 4 Officers arrived from Cdn Base Depot.") and once as the first date of the grouped row "26, 27, 28, 29." — both are transcribed as they appear.
- 9th, 10th and 11th are grouped under a single entry ("9, 10, 11."), as are 14th, 15th and 16th, 20th–23rd, and 26th–29th.
- The signature at the foot of the March sheet is illegible; the signatory is given only as "Lieut-Col., Comdg 3rd CANADIAN ENTRENCHING BATTALION." and is not identified by name.
- Names read as: no individual officers or men are named in March 1917; the only units named are the 4th Can Entrenching Battn. (2nd), the 172nd Tunnelling Coy (18th), and the 42nd Battn. (19th).
- Casualties in March 1917: 1 casualty on 24 March (camp shelled by a 21cm howitzer; not described or named). No other casualties are recorded in the March entries.
- No map coordinates (latitude/longitude) are given; the only places named are Bois des Allieux, Mont St Eloy, Savy, Neuville-St-Vaast, Gouy-Servins, Bois de Vaast, La Targette, and Au Ritz.
