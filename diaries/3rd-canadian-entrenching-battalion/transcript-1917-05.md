# War Diary — 3rd Canadian Entrenching Battalion — May 1917

- **Unit:** 3rd Canadian Entrenching Battalion — 3rd Canadian Division
- **Month:** May 1917 — 1 May to 31 May 1917
- **Source:** Library and Archives Canada, Collection Search Item ID 2004945 (RG 9 III-D-3, vol. 5009, file 718, reel T-10858, finding aid 9-52)
- **Pages:** May 1917, p. 1 – p. 3 (image files page-0031 – page-0033)
- **May 1917 — COMPLETE** (pages 1–3): cover, title page, diary text 1–31 May on a single Army Form C. 2118 sheet, no appendices. Transcribed 2026-10-28.

## Transcription conventions
Original spelling, punctuation and abbreviations kept. Diary text is typewritten throughout (Army Form C. 2118, "WAR DIARY or INTELLIGENCE SUMMARY"). `[blacked out]` marks words obliterated by the censor's black ink — several place names in this month's sheet were blacked out and cannot be recovered from the image. `[uncertain]` marks words I could not read with confidence — never guessed. `[ditto marks]` marks rows where the original uses ditto marks (") repeating the previous row's entry. Each entry ends with handwritten initials (illegible) in the Summary column. The "Hour" column is blank throughout. The "Remarks and references to Appendices" column is blank throughout. The sheet heading ("THIRD CANADIAN ENTRENCHING BATTALION. MONTH ENDING MAY 31st 1917.") is partly blacked out. A rubber stamp at the bottom left of the diary sheet reads "3rd CANADIAN ENTRENCHING BATTALION. No.......... Date.........." (blank). "O.R." = Other Ranks; "Cdn." = Canadian; "Coy." / "Coys." = Company / Companies; "Div." = Division; "Rly." = Railway; "A.M." as written. "VAAST" = Vaast (Neuville-St Vaast); "MAISON BLANCHE" as written.

## Open questions / uncertain items (May 1917)
- The Place column reads, as far as it can be made out through the censor's blacking, "BOIS-DES-ALLEAUX" over "NEAR ST. ELOY" — the same camp location given plainly in the June sheet. The reading is recorded with that reservation.
- 1 May: the place after "LA" in "Loading lumber for LA [blacked out] Sleeper roads" and in "Pipe line at [blacked out] & MAISON BLANCHE" is blacked out in both cases; the censored word is long in each case but is not legible and is not guessed here.
- 4 May: the place after "LA" in "95 O.R. LA [blacked out] & MAISON BLANCHE pipe line" is blacked out.
- 5 May: the camp moved "from huts [blacked out] MONT ST ELOY to new location BOIS DES ALLEAUX" — a word between "huts" and "MONT ST ELOY" is blacked out.
- 14th–16th: the date column for the combined ditto row reads as "14th, 15th, 16th" (the figures are blurred where the entry spans the Place/Date column rule). All 31 days of May are covered with no gaps.
- 19 May: "60 O.R. LA MOTTE & [blacked out], pipe line" — the second place in the pipe-line pair is blacked out; "115 O.R. Digging Reservoir at [blacked out]" — the reservoir site is blacked out.
- 20 May: three reservoir/pipe-line places are blacked out ("40 Men digging Reservoir [blacked out]", "relaying piping at [blacked out]", "Working on [blacked out] pipe line").
- 21 May: administration of the battalion changed — "This battalion ceased to be administered by 3rd. Cdn. Division, Now administered by Cdn. Corps Entrenching Group."
- 27 May: "Camp shelled with enemy long ranged high velocity [blacked out] guns from 9.30 A.M. to 11. A.M. No. Casualties." — the type of gun is blacked out (the word reads as a short word before "guns" but is not legible).
- The signature at the foot of the sheet is illegible; the signatory is given only as "Lieut. Col., Comdg. 3rd. Cdn. Entrenching Battn." and is not identified by name.
- Names read as: none — no officer or man is named in the May entries. The "Town Major, MONT ST ELOY" is mentioned (18th and 20th) but not named.
- Casualties in May 1917: none on 1 May ("Vicinity of camp shelled with Enemy Howitzers, no casualties") and none on 27 May ("No. Casualties"); 5 O.R. wounded on working parties on 29 May. No names, and no deaths, are recorded in May.
- No map coordinates (latitude/longitude) are given; the places named are BOIS DES ALLEAUX (camp from 5 May), MONT ST ELOY, LA MOTTE, MAISON BLANCHE, and the Cdn. Corps Light Railway.
