# War Diary — 3rd Canadian Entrenching Battalion — October 1916

- **Unit:** 3rd Canadian Entrenching Battalion — 3rd Canadian Division
- **Month:** October 1916 — 1 October to 31 October 1916
- **Source:** Library and Archives Canada, Collection Search Item ID 2004945 (RG 9 III-D-3, vol. 5009, file 718, reel T-10858, finding aid 9-52)
- **Pages:** October 1916, p. 1 – p. 3 (image files page-0008 – page-0010)
- **October 1916 — COMPLETE** (pages 1–3): title page, diary-text header page, diary text 1–31 October on a single sheet, no appendices. Transcribed 2026-10-28.

## Transcription conventions
Original spelling, punctuation and abbreviations kept. Diary text is typewritten throughout on Army Form C. 2118, "WAR DIARY or INTELLIGENCE SUMMARY". `[blacked out]` marks words deliberately blacked out (censored) in the original — none in this month. `[uncertain]` marks words I could not read with confidence — never guessed. The ditto mark `"` in the original is reproduced as written, repeating the word above it. A handwritten initial appears beside each entry. The "Hour" column is blank throughout. The "Remarks and references to Appendices" column is blank throughout. "O.R." = other ranks; "Bn." = Battalion; "O.C." = Officer Commanding; "nr." = near; "RD." = Road. The Place column reads "1916. In The Field." for the whole month. Dates are grouped where the original groups them (11–13, 17–18–19, 23 & 24, 27–31).

## Open questions / uncertain items (October 1916)
- 8 and 9 Oct: the ditto lines each contain a "2" in the second position, rendered as written (" " 2 " " ...). What the "2" stands for is not clear from the sheet — it may be a second working party, or a ditto mark mis-struck as "2". It is transcribed exactly as it appears and not interpreted.
- 15 Oct: the word between "8.00 a.m." and "LA-VICOGNE" is a ditto mark, read as repeating "arrived" from the 14 Oct entry ("Left CONTAY 8.00 a.m. [arrived] LA-VICOGNE at 1.00 p.m.").
- 21 Oct: the arrival time at HEBLINGHURST reads "12.30 p.m."; the "12" is partly over-struck by the handwritten initial beside the entry, so the hour is read with that reservation.
- Place names are spelled as written: ALBERT, CONTAY, LA-VICOGNE, CANDAS, BAMLY, HEBLINGHURST, LE TIRLETT, ACQ, Ancre River, ALBERT-BOUZINCOURT RD. "BAMLY" and "HEBLINGHURST" are as written (they may be mistypings of nearby villages, but they have not been corrected or guessed).
- The signature at the foot of the October sheet is illegible; the signatory is given only as "Lt.-Col. D.S.O., O.C. 3rd Canadian Entrenching Bn." and is not identified by name.
- Names read as: no individual officers or other ranks are named in the October entries. Units named: Units of 3rd Can. Div. (implied by "to Units"); Can. Base Depot.
- Casualties in October 1916: none recorded. Every working-party entry states "No Casualties", and no deaths, wounded or hospital admissions are mentioned in October.
- No map coordinates (latitude/longitude) are given. Places named are the Ancre River near ALBERT, ALBERT-BOUZINCOURT RD., and the march route ALBERT – CONTAY – LA-VICOGNE – CANDAS – BAMLY – HEBLINGHURST – LE TIRLETT – ACQ (14–25 Oct), ending in camp at ACQ, where the battalion prepared for winter occupation 27–31 Oct.
