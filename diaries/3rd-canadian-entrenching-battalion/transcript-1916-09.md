# War Diary — 3rd Canadian Entrenching Battalion — September 1916

- **Unit:** 3rd Canadian Entrenching Battalion — 3rd Canadian Division
- **Month:** September 1916 — 1 September to 30 September 1916
- **Source:** Library and Archives Canada, Collection Search Item ID 2004945 (RG 9 III-D-3, vol. 5009, file 718, reel T-10858, finding aid 9-52)
- **Pages:** September 1916, p. 1 – p. 3 (image files page-0005 – page-0007)
- **September 1916 — COMPLETE** (pages 1–3): title page, diary-text header page, diary text 1–30 September on a single sheet, no appendices. Transcribed 2026-10-28.

## Transcription conventions
Original spelling, punctuation and abbreviations kept. Diary text is typewritten throughout on Army Form C. 2118, "WAR DIARY or INTELLIGENCE SUMMARY". `[blacked out]` marks words deliberately blacked out (censored) in the original — many place names are blacked out throughout this month, including the whole Place column. `[uncertain]` marks words I could not read with confidence — never guessed. The ditto mark `"` in the original is reproduced as written, repeating the word above it; where a ditto line changes a number, the changed number is shown in place. A handwritten initial appears beside each entry. The "Hour" column is blank throughout. The "Remarks and references to Appendices" column is blank throughout. "O.R." = other ranks; "Battn." = Battalion; "Can." = Canadian; "Div." = Division; "C.B. Depot" = Canadian Base Depot. No entries are dated 11, 14 or 26 September.

## Open questions / uncertain items (September 1916)
- The Place column is blacked out throughout, and many place names in the text are blacked out: the advanced party's destination (6 Sep), the destination and the two places passed through on the move from VANCOUVER CAMP (7 Sep), the entrainment and detrainment points and the road destination (8 Sep), the billets (9 Sep), the camp the battalion moved to (10 Sep), and two words of the road name worked on (13 and 19 Sep, "[blacked out] ROAD"). None of these has been guessed.
- The sheet heading is partly blacked out; what can be read is "SEPT., 30th, 1916."
- The 13th entry's road name is blacked out before "ROAD" (two words, hyphenated, blacked out); the same road is worked on 19 Sep.
- 17th: the ditto line ends "5 O.R." followed by a word I could not read (possibly "wounded", obscured by the handwritten initial beside the entry) — rendered "5 O.R. [uncertain]".
- 18th: the second line is a ditto line reading " " 3 " " 65 O.R. — i.e. a further despatch, with "3" and "65" in place of "1 Officer and" and "226" in the line above; the rest of the sentence is dittoed ("reinforcements to Units of 3rd Can. Div.").
- 20th and 21st: each ditto line ends "2 O.R." followed by a word I could not read (possibly "wounded", obscured by the handwritten initials) — rendered "2 O.R. [uncertain]" in both cases. The date labels 20th and 21st are faint on the sheet; the assignment of the two "2 O.R." lines to 20 and 21 September follows the row order, and the 21st label is read with that reservation.
- No entries are dated 11, 14 or 26 September. The 14th label is faint and may be absent; no entry stands against it. The 11th and 26th do not appear at all.
- The signature at the foot of the September sheet is illegible; the signatory is given only as "Lt.-Col. D.S.O., Commanding 3rd Can. Entrenching Bn." and is not identified by name.
- Names read as: no individual officers or other ranks are named in the September entries. Units named: Units of 3rd Can. Div.; Can. Base Depot / C.B. Depot.
- Casualties in September 1916: 1 killed and 1 wounded (O.R.) on 15 Sep; 5 O.R. wounded on 16 Sep; 5 O.R. on 17 Sep (word after the number not legible, likely wounded); 1 O.R. wounded on 19 Sep; 2 O.R. on 20 Sep and 2 O.R. on 21 Sep (words after the numbers not legible, likely wounded). No casualties on the other working-party days (1, 13, 28, 29, 30 Sep). No deaths are named in September.
- No map coordinates (latitude/longitude) are given. Places named are VANCOUVER CAMP, ALBERT (vicinity, 13 Sep), Camp South of [blacked out] (10 Sep), and [blacked out] ROAD (13 and 19 Sep). The battalion moved from VANCOUVER CAMP on 7 Sep, by road and motor bus to entrain on 8 Sep, detrained 9.30 p.m. 8 Sep, reached billets 4.00 a.m. 9 Sep, moved to camp south of [blacked out] on 10 Sep, and marched to the vicinity of ALBERT on 13 Sep.
