# War Diary — 3rd Canadian Entrenching Battalion — February 1917

- **Unit:** 3rd Canadian Entrenching Battalion – 3rd Canadian Division
- **Month:** February 1917 — 1 February to 28 February 1917
- **Source:** Library and Archives Canada, Collection Search Item ID 2004945 (RG 9 III-D-3, vol. 5009, file 718, reel T-10858, finding aid 9-52)
- **Pages:** February 1917, p. 1 – p. 3 (image files page-0022 – page-0024)
- **February 1917 — COMPLETE** (pages 1–3): title page, contents page, diary text 1–28 February on 1 sheet, no appendices. Transcribed 2026-10-28.

## Transcription conventions
Original spelling, punctuation and abbreviations kept. Diary text is typewritten throughout (Army Form C. 2118, "WAR DIARY or INTELLIGENCE SUMMARY"). `[uncertain]` marks words I could not read with confidence — never guessed. Ditto marks (`"`) are as written in the original, repeating the word above; they are preserved rather than expanded. Several place names are heavily overtyped in the original (a contemporary censoring) but remain legible and are transcribed as read. The Place column reads "FIELD" (partly blacked out) throughout. The "Hour" column is blank throughout. The "Remarks and references to Appendices" column is blank except for handwritten initials beside each entry. "W.P." = Working Party; "O.R." = Other Ranks; "Coy." = Company; "Offr." = Officer; "Can." = Canadian; "Feby" = February; "Amm: Park" = Ammunition Park; "A. T. Coy" = as written (not expanded); "O.C." = Officer Commanding. The printed footer reads "2449 Wt. W14957/M90 750,000 1/16 J.B.C. & A. Forms/C.2118/12."

## Open questions / uncertain items (February 1917)
- 3rd–5th: the entries are built from ditto marks; the 4th entry reads "One Officer taken on strength" only by the ditto marks repeating "One Officer" from the 3rd — the original does not spell it out. The 4th records "One Casualty" and the 5th "Three Casualties", neither described nor named.
- 7th: "loading raval at MONT ST ELOY" is as written — "raval" is presumably a mis-typing of "gravel", but the original word is transcribed as it appears.
- 8th, 9th and 10th are grouped under a single entry ("8, 9, 10th"); 13th, 14th and 15th are likewise grouped ("13, 14, 15th"), as are 21st and 22nd and 26th, 27th and 28th. The single casualty recorded for 8th–10th and the single casualty on 11th are not described or named.
- 24th: the entry is only ditto marks plus "104 O. R." — the original does not say what the 104 O.R. did (the 25th entry, also ditto marks plus a number, says "107 O.R. despatched to UNITS").
- The signature at the foot of the February sheet is illegible; the signatory is given only as "Lieut-Col., O. C. 3RD CANADIAN ENTRENCHING BATTALION." and is not identified by name.
- Names read as: no individual officers or men are named in February 1917; the only units named are the 3rd A. T. Coy, 216th A. T. Coy, and 197th Sand Drainage Coy.
- Casualties in February 1917: 1 casualty on 4 February; 3 casualties on 5 February; 1 casualty across 8–10 February; 1 casualty on 11 February — none described or named. 13th–15th and 23rd record "Casualties Nil"; 6th records "No Casualties".
- No map coordinates (latitude/longitude) are given; the only places named are Savy, Gouy-Servins, Neuville-St-Vaast, La Targette, Bois-de-Bray, Mont St Eloy, and Maroeuil.
