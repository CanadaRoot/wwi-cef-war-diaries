# War Diary — 9th Canadian Reserve Battalion (Alberta)

## Source
- **Unit:** 9th Canadian Reserve Battalion (Alberta) — organized at Bramshott 4 January 1917 under Lieut.-Colonel W. C. G. Armstrong, absorbing the 9th, 194th and 209th Battalions; reinforcing the 10th and 49th Battalions; absorbed by the 21st Canadian Reserve Battalion 15 October 1917
- **Archival reference:** RG 9 III-D-3, vol. 4950, file 476, part 1; microfilm reel T-10761
- **LAC Collection Search:** Item ID 2004677 — "War diaries - 9th Reserve Battalion"
  - Record: https://recherche-collection-search.bac-lac.gc.ca/eng/Home/Record?app=fonandcol&IdNumber=2004677
- **Page images:** pages 67–72 of this file (`page-0067-01-08-1917_cover.jpg` … `page-0072-27-08-1917.jpg`)
- **Place:** Bramshott throughout
- **Note on the record:** this LAC record also contains an unrelated 1914–15 diary of the original 9th Battalion on pages 1–25 and 83–90; those pages are NOT transcribed here. This chunk covers only the Reserve Battalion diary, July–August 1917 (pages 61–72).

## Transcription conventions
- Faithful to the original: spelling, punctuation, abbreviations and capitalisation as written.
- `[uncertain]` marks a word or name that could not be read with confidence — never guessed. The August sheets are handwritten in a small cursive hand, and many surnames are illegible; they are flagged, not guessed.
- The diary-text pages are Army Form C. 2118, "WAR DIARY or INTELLIGENCE SUMMARY", handwritten in columns: Place | Date | Hour | Summary of Events and Information | Remarks and references to Appendices.
- The "Hour" column is blank throughout this volume — no hours are recorded.
- The "Remarks" column is blank throughout this volume.
- "S.O.S." = struck off strength; "Alta. Reg. Depot" / "A.R.D." = Alberta Regimental Depot; "O.T.C." = Officers' Training Corps; "recd." = received; "attd." = attached; "Temp." = temporary; "D.S.O." = Distinguished Service Order; "M.C." = Military Cross.
- Dates are given as written in the Date column (e.g. "1/8/17"). Some entries have no date written; they are placed under the date they fall between, and this is noted.
- No map coordinates (latitude/longitude) are given in the diary; places are recorded as written.

## Notes and gaps
- The August sheets are handwritten throughout (not typed), in a small cursive hand; many surnames are illegible and are flagged [uncertain] rather than guessed.
- 5 August: no date is written for the Capt. McGill entry (reverting to temporary rank of Lieutenant with effect 10/8/17); it falls between the 4 August and 6 August entries. The initials before "McGill" could not be read with confidence — the officer is likely the same Captain G. A. McGill taken on strength 27 July, but this is not stated on the page and is not assumed in the transcription.
- 13, 26 and 30 August have no entries at all.
- 19 August: no date is written for the Lieut. J. W. [uncertain] entry; it falls between the 18 August and 20 August entries.
- 25 August is the month's largest reinforcement draft: 99 Other Ranks struck off strength on proceeding overseas to the 10th Battalion (net decrease 91 that day). A further 50 Other Ranks and four lieutenants proceeded overseas to the 10th Battalion on 27 August.
- 27–29 August: the sheets overlap — page 71 runs 23–29 August and page 72 runs 27–31 August. The 27 and 28 August entries appear only on page 72; 29 August appears on both sheets (Major Weaver taken on strength on page 71; "Net Decrease 2 Other Ranks" on page 72).
- People named (readable): Lieut. [uncertain] Richards; Cadet R. W. [uncertain] (from O.T.C. No. 4 Cadet Battalion, Oxford); Sir Francis Howard and staff; Lieut. W. J. White and Lieut. A. M. White; Lieut. J. W. [uncertain] (supervised the Lewis Gun team); Capt. C. J. Corrigan (D.S.O., M.C.); Capt. R. N. Taylor; Major C. J. Weaver (D.S.O.); Lieut. J. A. Clark; Lieut. L. Parker; Lieut. W. J. Harris. The two brigadier-generals who visited with Sir Francis Howard on 15 August and the lieutenant-general who visited on 16 August could not be identified from the handwriting — flagged [uncertain].
- Inspections and events: 15 August — Sir Francis Howard and staff, with two brigadier-generals and their staffs, visited the battalion and inspected training in the various branches. 16 August — a lieutenant-general and staff visited the area for the Divisional Efficiency Competitions; the battalion's Lewis Gun team won first place in its competition.
- Movements: no movement of the battalion — Bramshott throughout. Overseas drafts: two lieutenants to the 10th Battalion on 23 August, 99 Other Ranks to the 10th Battalion on 25 August, four lieutenants and 50 Other Ranks to the 10th Battalion on 27 August.
- Casualties: none recorded in August 1917 — the diary records only strength changes (officer postings to/from the Alberta Regimental Depot and the 1st Central Ontario Regimental Depot, cadets taken on strength, and daily net increases/decreases of Other Ranks), plus the month-end strength return: 61 Officers, 1,350 Other Ranks on 31 August.
- The month-end return (31 August) is signed by the commanding officer, but the signature is illegible and is not attributed to any named officer in the transcription.
- No map coordinates are given.
