# War Diary — 9th Canadian Reserve Battalion (Alberta)

## Source
- **Unit:** 9th Canadian Reserve Battalion (Alberta) — organized at Bramshott 4 January 1917 under Lieut.-Colonel W. C. G. Armstrong, absorbing the 9th, 194th and 209th Battalions; reinforcing the 10th and 49th Battalions; absorbed by the 21st Canadian Reserve Battalion 15 October 1917
- **Archival reference:** RG 9 III-D-3, vol. 4950, file 476, part 1; microfilm reel T-10761
- **LAC Collection Search:** Item ID 2004677 — "War diaries - 9th Reserve Battalion"
  - Record: https://recherche-collection-search.bac-lac.gc.ca/eng/Home/Record?app=fonandcol&IdNumber=2004677
- **Page images:** pages 61–66 of this file (`page-0061-01-07-1917_cover.jpg` … `page-0066-27-07-1917.jpg`)
- **Place:** Bramshott throughout
- **Note on the record:** this LAC record also contains an unrelated 1914–15 diary of the original 9th Battalion on pages 1–25 and 83–90; those pages are NOT transcribed here. This chunk covers only the Reserve Battalion diary, July–August 1917 (pages 61–72).

## Transcription conventions
- Faithful to the original: spelling, punctuation, abbreviations and capitalisation as written.
- `[uncertain]` marks a word or name that could not be read with confidence — never guessed. The July sheets are handwritten in a small cursive hand, and many surnames are illegible; they are flagged, not guessed.
- The diary-text pages are Army Form C. 2118, "WAR DIARY or INTELLIGENCE SUMMARY", handwritten in columns: Place | Date | Hour | Summary of Events and Information | Remarks and references to Appendices.
- The "Hour" column is blank throughout this volume — no hours are recorded.
- The "Remarks" column is blank throughout this volume.
- "S.O.S." = struck off strength; "Alta. Reg. Depot" / "A.R.D." = Alberta Regimental Depot; "O.T.C." = Officers' Training Corps; "O.C.C." as written (see Notes); "recd." = received; "attd." = attached; "Mily." as written.
- Dates are given as written in the Date column (e.g. "1/7"). Some entries have no date written; they are placed under the date they fall between, and this is noted.
- No map coordinates (latitude/longitude) are given in the diary; places are recorded as written.

## Notes and gaps
- The July sheets are handwritten throughout (not typed), in two hands: a small cursive hand on pages 62–65 and a clearer hand on page 66. Many surnames on pages 62–65 are illegible and are flagged [uncertain] rather than guessed.
- "Milty." (6 July, Capt. S. B. [uncertain]) is written as shown — the abbreviation for Military as written on the page.
- 6 July: the cadets taken on strength "on completion of O.T.C. Course (5/7)" are dated to the previous day in the entry itself, as written.
- 9 July: the course is written "O.C.C. Course" (not O.T.C. as on 6 July), followed by a further word/date that could not be read — flagged [uncertain]. It is not corrected.
- 12 July: the line naming "C. P. Miller" is largely illegible — two names before it and the words after it could not be read. The final line of the page (an officer proceeding O/S for service) is also largely illegible, and its last word is cut at the foot of the page.
- 13 July: two "Net Increase" lines (1 and 4 Other Ranks) are written on the same date, as shown.
- 15 July: no date is written for the Capt. P. [uncertain] entry; it falls between the 14 July and 16 July entries.
- 22 July: no date is written for the Capt. J. A. Carter entry; it falls between the 21 July and 23 July entries.
- 24 July: no date is written for the "Net decrease 2 Other Ranks" line; it falls between the 23 July and 25 July entries.
- 29 and 30 July have no entries at all — the sheet jumps from 28 July to 31 July.
- People named (readable): Major J. C. A. Young; Lieut. J. R. Cunningham; Cadet J. Cropper; Capt. N. B. Pearson (struck off 12 July on admission to Bramshott Military Hospital, re-taken on strength 26 July); Capt. J. A. Carter; Captain G. A. McGill; Lieuts. A. C. Grigg and J. C. Knott (in Cherryhinton Hospital); Lieuts. J. Anderson and V. C. Shannon (proceeded overseas to the 49th Battalion, 27 July); Lieut. V. E. Ballard (transferred to 1st Central Ontario Regiment, 28 July).
- Movements: no movement of the battalion — Bramshott throughout. The only overseas movement recorded is the four lieutenants proceeding to the 49th Battalion on 27 July; a Lt. Col. and a Major were struck off strength "upon return to Canada" on 23 July.
- Casualties: none recorded in July 1917 — the diary records only strength changes (officer postings to/from the Alberta Regimental Depot, cadets taken on strength, and daily net increases/decreases of Other Ranks).
- No map coordinates are given.
