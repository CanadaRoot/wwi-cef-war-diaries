# 9th Canadian Reserve Battalion (Alberta) — diary notes

- **LAC record:** Item ID 2004677, "War diaries - 9th Reserve Battalion", RG 9 III-D-3, vol. 4950, file 476, part 1, microfilm reel T-10761. 90 page images.
- **This diary (transcribed):** pages 26–82, 1 March – 15 October 1917, 57 pages, 8 monthly transcripts (`transcript-1917-03.md` … `transcript-1917-10.md`), 213 dated index entries in `INDEX-F.md`. The battalion spent the whole diary at Bramshott, England; it was absorbed by the 21st Reserve Battalion on 15 October 1917 (54 officers, 757 Other Ranks).
- **NOT transcribed here:** pages 1–25 (Oct 1914 – Jan 1915) and pages 83–90 (Jul–Aug 1915) are the diary of the original 9th Battalion, CEF — a different unit. They are in this LAC record but belong to another eighth's queue (Infantry Battalions). Flagged for the daily digest, as with the 17th Reserve Battalion record (Item 2004685).
- **Command unresolved:** the 9 March entry appears to record Armstrong ceasing command and Forbes succeeding, but the wording is illegible; the May and June month-end sheets are signed "W. C. G. Armstrong, Lieut.-Colonel, Comdg." — no clean handover is asserted in the transcripts or index.
- **Notable items:** 24–26 Mar 1917 Emergency call-out (3rd Emergency Battalion under Col. Rattray); 5 May 1917 practice mobilization (Composite Battalion, march to Bordon Station); 29 May 1917 visit by H.R.H. the Duke of Connaught; 202nd Battalion absorbed (officers and 14 O.R. on strength 29 May 1917); large reinforcement drafts to the 10th and 49th Battalions. No battle casualties; no map coordinates given. 527 [uncertain] flag occurrences across the 8 transcripts.
