## Page 3 (October 1918, p. 3) — [diary text, Army Form C. 2118]

WAR DIARY or INTELLIGENCE SUMMARY. (Erase heading not required.)

| Place | Date | Hour | Summary of Events and Information |
|---|---|---|---|
| SAUCHY-LESTREE (Q35d 88) | 1-10-18 | | At 10.10 am 5 min rapid fire was placed on CHATEAU WOOD (R.16.central), 20 rounds being fired. From 3.20 pm to 5.20 pm, 300 rounds were fired in barrage put down in support of operations carried out by CANADIAN CORPS. Many rounds of harassing fire on enemy roads, bridges etc during the day and night. At 4.30 am, a counter-preparation shoot was carried out, 500 yds in advance of our S.O.S. line, 198 rounds being fired. Enemy artillery harassed approaches to SAUCHY-LESTREE during the night. |
| —"— | 2-10-18 | | Registration of guns carried out at 4 pm on ARLEUX CHURCH, successfully, 26 rounds being fired. A wire-cutting task on wire from R.23.c.5.0 to R.30.a.0.7 was carried out with good results, in which we fired 115 rounds. At 12 mn we opened up with the attack barrage in accordance with C.B. order No.9, firing 168 rounds. During the night 150 rounds of harassing fire on various enemy positions in the vicinity of AUBIGNY-AU-BAC. Enemy artillery shelled vicinity of our position with a 2 gun, two casualties being caused. GUNNERS MOFFATT and DOHERTY both being wounded. |
| —"— | 3-10-18 | | Two sharp bursts of intense fire placed on enemy positions in PECHAIN and HAM LENGLET, 40 rounds being fired. An area shoot of 30 rounds carried out on R.11.d.9.3, R.11.d.12., & R.11.d.F.0. Heavy harassing fire maintained on various enemy positions throughout the day and night, in which we fired 150 rounds. Enemy artillery again shelled vicinity of battery position, and GUNNER T. ROSE was wounded. No material damage caused. |

| SAUCHY-LESTREE (Q35.d.8.8) | 8-10-18 | | At 4.30 am we opened up with the ATTACK BARRAGE, in connection with Operation order No.10 and in support of Canadian Corps Offensive Operations, and continued till 6.30 am, firing 228 rounds. | —"— / BLECOURT (S.4.C.3.7) | 10-10-18 | | The battery was put out of action at 7 am, in order to move forward to new positions located at BLECOURT. All 6 howitzers and personnel were moved. Location of position S.4.c.3.7. All guns in action by 18.30. Village of WAVRECHAIN subjected to heavy harassing fire, also the village of WASNES, a total of 250 rounds being fired, during the night and early morning of the 11th. |
| —"— | 11-10-18 | | Bombardment of enemy positions in WAVRECHAIN and WASNES commenced at 9.00 in connection with Bde. Order No.146. Shoot completed at noon, a total of 357 rounds being fired. Harassing fire on these villages continued throughout the night, in which 200 rounds were fired. Enemy artillery of all calibres shelled S.9a, 3, 2, + 10 with H.E. shrapnel and gas during the night. No material damage caused and there were no casualties. |

## Notes

- **Date coverage:** Entries exist for every day, 1–31 October 1918. **No date gaps.** The battery was silent on 27 and 30 October; 31 October carries the date line and the commanding officer's signature but no diary entry.
- **Casualties:** 2 October — Gunners MOFFATT and DOHERTY both wounded when enemy artillery shelled the vicinity of the battery position at Sauchy-Lestree. 3 October — Gunner T. ROSE wounded by enemy shelling of the battery position. No battery deaths recorded this month. 11 October — enemy shelling of S.9a, 3, 2, + 10 with H.E., shrapnel and gas caused no material damage and no casualties.
- **Decorations:** None recorded this month.
- **Change of command:** The month is signed by Major W. J. Stewart, C.G.A., and the Remarks column carries his initials "W.J.S." throughout — a change from September 1917, when the diary was signed by Major J. A. Stewart, R.C.A. (initials "JAS").
- **The advance:** The battery moved forward four times this month — Sauchy-Lestree to Blecourt (10 October, all 6 howitzers moved, guns back in action by 18.30), to Bantigny (17 October), then a rendezvous march with 1st Bde C.G.A. to Marquette (19–20 October), delayed overnight at Pont-Rade because the plank road to the pontoon over the Sensée Canal had not been laid, the guns bivouacking on the roadside with the 7th, 2nd and 3rd Canadian Siege Batteries also waiting at the crossing; ammunition lorries were turned back and crossed instead at Fressies. Then to Escaudain (22 October), into position at Wavrechain-sous-Denain (23 October), and finally forward to Herin (29 October).
- **Operations:** Heavy firing in support of the Canadian Corps offensive in the first half of the month — attack barrages on 1, 2 and 8 October (Operation order No. 10), wire-cutting tasks (2 and 5 October, 519 rounds on 5 October alone), gas and H.E. bombardments (4 and 7 October), and the 24–26 October shoots under C.C.H.A. Operation orders No. 151 and No. 152 in conjunction with the Canadian Corps attack (436 rounds on 24 October, 300 rounds on 26 October, plus a response to an "N.F. call").
- **Places and map references:** SAUCHY-LESTREE (Q35.d.8.8); CHATEAU WOOD (R.16.central); ARLEUX CHURCH; AUBIGNY-AU-BAC; PECHAIN; HAM LENGLET; FECHAIN; FRESSIES; BLECOURT (S.4.c.3.7); WAVRECHAIN; WASNES; BANTIGNY (M.34.c.7.2); MARQUETTE; PONT-RADE and the SENSEE CANAL; ESCAUDAIN; WAVRECHAIN-SOUS-DENAIN (J.1.b.4.9); HERIN (O.22.a.9.9.); and numerous trench-map references (R.23.c.5.0, R.30.a.0.7, R.11.d.9.3, R.11.d.12., R.11.d.F.0., R.10, R.16, M.7.h.80.20, M.8.c.30.30, R.18a, R.23.a.0.1, R.23.d.8.7, R.2.a.70.5.5, M.15c., M.8c., R.12c, R.10a.3.5, M.9d, M.7d, R.11c, M.9b, R.10d, S.9.a.3.8, N.8.d.70, M.6.b.6.3, N.1a., M.12b., M.6b., N.8.b.8.0, N.3.a.2.0, H.25a.d., N.6, H.25a.u.3., H.25c.0.9, N.1.c.5.2, N.2d.4.3, S.4.c.1, H.27 central, H.19.d., H.21.d.0.9, H.28a.0.9, K.3.b.0.6, K.5.c.4.0, K.5.c.2.9, E.27.d.28, K.17.a.6.5, E.21.b., E.21.d.40.80, A.31 central, L.35a).
