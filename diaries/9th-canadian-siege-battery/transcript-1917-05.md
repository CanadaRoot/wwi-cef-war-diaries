## Page 3 (May 1917, p. 3) — [diary text]

WAR DIARY or INTELLIGENCE SUMMARY. (Erase heading not required.)

### Cité de Gaumont — May 1st '17

Our task for to-day was wire-cutting in Avion. Fired 300 rounds with good results. In the afternoon the village in which we are situated, was heavily shelled. Several batteries nearby had many casualties in "material".

[Remarks: here]

### Cité de Gaumont — May 2nd '17

The Battery is now doing Counter Battery work. We are under the 1st. C.H.A.G. for Administration and under the 2nd C.H.A.G. for tactical purposes. Had our first shoot with aeroplane observation. Was unsuccessful owing to ground mist.

[Remarks: here]

## Page 4 (May 1917, p. 4) — [diary text]

### Cité de Gaumont — May 3rd/17

Took part in heavy bombardment at 3.45 A.M. this morning. Our task was to neutralize two batteries situated in MERICOURT. Had two aeroplane shoots one being very successful.

[Remarks: here]

### Cité de Gaumont — 4/5/17

Battery detailed for two Aerial Shoots. One commencing at 4.10 pm a failure owing to airman being short of petrol. He observed only three rounds then returned. The 2nd shoot did not materialize. The battery however fired 250 rounds on special targets, and retaliation during the night. The battery was shelled intermittently during the night. No material damage was done and there were no casualties.

[Remarks: here]

### Cité de Gaumont — 5/5/17

The battery engaged several hostile batteries during the day. One Aeroplane shoot was carried out, 150 rounds being fired, the result being satisfactory. At 3 am 2 batteries were engaged in answer to S.O.S. call from Infantry. Light shelling throughout the 24 hours by 5.9 enemy batteries. There were no casualties.

[Remarks: here]

[The letters "NR NC." appear written at the end of the line "by 5.9 enemy" in the 5 May entry; see Notes.]

## Page 5 (May 1917, p. 5) — [diary text]

### Cité de Gaumont — May 6th '17

Two aeroplane shoots were attempted, but both were unsuccessful. Counter battery work was carried out during the day and night. No. 4 gun out of action and sent to No. 3. Medium Workshops AULAIN.

[Remarks: here]

### Cité de Gaumont — 7-5-17

Two shoots were carried out on hostile batteries with aeroplane observation. Both were successful. In one case an enemy gun pit was seen to be hit and badly damaged. In the other case, the hostile battery was well registered, but darkness set in before the airman could observe for effect. Splinter proof gun pits commenced and ammunition isolated in small quantities. Very little shelling, but many Gas Shell fell around Battery position in the evening. The B.C. post was hit for the 3rd time. No material damage.

[Remarks: here]

### Cité de Gaumont — 8-5-17

Aeroplane shoots were cancelled on account of bad weather. Counter-battery work without observation was carried out 320 rounds being fired. Moderately quiet at battery position. H. R. C.

[Remarks: here]

## Page 6 (May 1917, p. 6) — [diary text]

### Cité de Gaumont — 9-5-17

Two Aeroplane shoots were carried out during the day. One was unsuccessful, through rounds being unobserved, the other presumably successful. Several night tasks were undertaken in accordance with instruction received from Group Commander. 2nd H.A.G.

[Remarks: here]

### Cité de Gaumont — 10-5-17

Two shoots with balloon observation were postponed owing to poor visibility. During the day much Counter battery work was carried out. The battery position was heavily shelled during the 24 hours. No material damage and no casualties.

[Remarks: here]

### Cité de Gaumont — 11-5-17

One shoot with balloon observation on hostile battery N.7.b. 250 rounds were fired. The balloon observer reports about twenty % of rounds were hits, and some of the gun pits were seen to be struck. A few other tasks were given, including zone calls. Reports from observer not yet in. Very quiet all day.

[Remarks: here]

## Page 7 (May 1917, p. 7) — [diary text]

### Cité de Gaumont — 12-5-17

A shoot with balloon observation was carried out, and reported as very successful. The battery fired 250 rounds during the day. Generally very quiet.

[Remarks: here]

### Cité de Gaumont — 13-5-17

A very quiet day in the battery. 100 rounds fired. Nos 1, 3 & 4 guns out of action owing to defective recuperators. Enemy shelling at intervals directly in front of battery. Shortly after noon a shell burst in battery cook house. 6 NCOs & men being killed, and seven wounded.

[Remarks: here]

### Cité de Gaumont — 14-5-17

The battery was heavily shelled with Gas and H.E. with no effect. 160 rounds were fired on the work of neutralization of hostile batteries with good results. Nos. 2, 3 and 4 guns out of action.

[Remarks: here]

### Cité de Gaumont — 15-5-17

Neutralization of hostile batteries throughout the day was carried on by the battery. The battery position was shelled intermittently and one direct hit was made on one of our dugouts. One man slightly wounded.

[Remarks: here]

## Page 8 (May 1917, p. 8) — [diary text]

### Cité de Gaumont — 16-5-17

The battery carried out several tasks on hostile batteries throughout the day and night, with good results. The day was quiet in the vicinity of the battery position. In the evening the battery position was heavily shelled with Gas shell. No damage. H. R. C.

[Remarks: here]

### Cité de Gaumont — 17-5-17

Counter Battery work without observation was carried out, during the greater part of the day. One special target was engaged, 100 rounds being fired. O.K. was received on the 4th round. Nos 1 & 4 guns out of action. The battery was shelled intermittently without result.

[Remarks: here]

### Cité de Gaumont — 18-5-17

A successful shoot on a hostile battery was carried out. The observer reporting a large explosion. Harassing fire was kept up throughout the day, 450 rounds being fired. No. 2 gun out of action.

[Remarks: here]

### Cité de Gaumont — 19-5-17

Two shoots with balloon observation were carried out at one and the same time. Both were successful. Counter Battery work was undertaken with satisfactory results. About 460 rounds were fired. Enemy artillery quiet.

[Remarks: here]

### Cité de Gaumont — 20-5-17

Two aeroplane shoots were undertaken, one being observed as successful, the other being unsuccessful owing to defective aerial at battery. H. R. C.

[Remarks: here]

## Page 9 (May 1917, p. 9) — [diary text]

### Cité de Gaumont — 21-5-17

At 10.15 am the enemy opened fire with 5.9" and 4.1" guns on the battery, using High Explosive and Shrapnel. He continued until 2.10 pm, firing about 300 shells. No damage was done but one N.C.O. was badly wounded. No shooting was carried out by the battery. During the night, the battery position was subjected to heavy bursts of fire from enemy 8" guns, with no effect.

[Remarks: here]

### Cité de Gaumont — 22-5-17

The day was occupied in the changing of the Right Section to a new position. This was successfully carried out in between bursts of enemy shell fire.

[Remarks: here]

### Cité de Gaumont — 23-5-17

The Right Section registered guns from new position with success. Harassing fire was maintained throughout the day on enemy trenches, roads, and railway.

[Remarks: here]

### Cité de Gaumont — 24-5-17

The shooting of the battery today was of a general nature, much harassing fire being maintained both day and night. A successful shoot without observation was also carried out. H. R. C.

[Remarks: here]

## Page 10 (May 1917, p. 10) — [diary text]

### Cité de Gaumont — 25-5-17 to 26-5-17 (bracketed together)

No Task was set on these days — Much harassing fire was however kept up on the enemy's approaches.

[Remarks: here]

### Cité de Gaumont — 27-5-17

A very successful shoot was undertaken on a "dangerous" target. Results being very good. About 140 rounds were fired. Battery position and vicinity very quiet. H. R. C.

[Remarks: here]

### Cité de Gaumont — 28-5-17

[No entry — the date is written but the text space is blank.]

[Remarks: here]

### Cité de Gaumont — 29-5-17

A very successful shoot on hostile battery with ground observation was carried out. 5 O.Ks. being observed. We also successfully shelled enemy front line trench, direct hits being observed. H. R. C.

[Remarks: here]

## Page 11 (May 1917, p. 11) — [diary text]

### Cité de Gaumont — 30-5-17 to 31-5-17 (bracketed together)

Nothing of note these 2 days. Harassing fire was carried out by the battery however. Enemy artillery active both days.

[Remarks: here]

## Transcriber's notes — May 1917

- **Date gaps:** none — every day 1–31 May has a dated entry. However, the 28 May entry is blank (date written, no text).
- **"here":** the Remarks column on the May pages bears the word "here" on most entries. Its meaning is unclear — it may be a filing mark or a reference to the entry itself.
- **"NR NC.":** in the 5 May entry the letters "NR NC." appear written at the end of the line "by 5.9 enemy batteries" (possibly the diarist's initials or a marginal note). They are not part of the sentence and are recorded here rather than in the text.
- **10 May entry:** the word "work" in "much Counter battery work was carried out" is inserted above the line in the original.
- **"H. R. C.":** the initials at the end of the entries of 8, 16, 20, 24, 27 and 29 May are those of the signing officer, Capt. H. R. Cobbett, R.C.A. (see the signature block on p. 11).
- **"Regt.":** the last line of the signature block on p. 11 appears to read "Regt." but is unclear, so it is flagged [uncertain].
- **Casualties:** 13 May — 6 NCOs and men killed and 7 wounded when a shell burst in the battery cook house (none named); 15 May — one man slightly wounded (unnamed); 21 May — one N.C.O. badly wounded (unnamed). No casualty in May is named.
- **People named:** none by surname in the May entries, other than the signing officer H. R. Cobbett.
- **Places:** Cité de Gaumont, Avion (wire-cutting), Mericourt, Aulain (No. 3 Medium Workshops).
