| Place | Date | Hour | Summary of Events and Information | Remarks and references to Appendices |
|---|---|---|---|---|

YPRES. | 1-12-17 | | At 7.00 am we replied to S.O.S call and fired 20 rounds. Two enemy batteries W.C.11 and W.D.16 were engaged during the morning, expenditure 36 rounds. Harassing fire was maintained on enemy roads, throughout the day, 60 rounds being fired. During the night fire was kept up on our Night lines. Enemy shelled battery position and vicinity intermittently with H.V. Guns. | H.R.N.C

" | 2-12-17 | | At 2.10 am we opened up with Attack Barrage, which was maintained until 5.am, a total of 197 rounds being fired. Several "crashes" were fired on enemy batteries Ex 45. and position at E 5.C.15.90, in which 103 rounds were expended. Enemy artillery put down heavy barrage shortly after our attack barrage opened, and our battery position was subjected to heavy shelling. An S.O.S was responded to, however, and 143 rounds were fired. | H.R.N.C

" | 3-12-17 | | Enemy batteries E.W.23 and E.W.51. were engaged during the day, and 168 rounds were expended. Two 'crashes' of 10 rounds each on E210 + E260 were fired. Harassing fire on target, reference No YR11/44 was maintained throughout the night, 60 rounds being expended. Enemy activity Nil. Routine Order No.1550 (Honours & Awards) by Lieut.-General SIR. A.W. CURRIE K.C.M.G. C.B. dated 30th November, publishes the name of No.1257506. Sgt. W. RAWLING as having been Awarded the MILITARY MEDAL for gallant conduct during the operations of the 25th November. |

YPRES. | 4-12-17 | | Two concentrations on areas E 4.C.90.45 and E 10.d 82.23 were fired during the morning, and calibration was carried out, target being EDDY FARM E13.C.10.95. Harassing fire on target reference No. YR11/44 was maintained throughout the afternoon, 177 rounds being expended in all. Enemy shelling of battery position and vicinity was fairly heavy, without damage to us, and no casualties. | H.R.N.C

— " — | 5-12-17 | | Enemy battery position Ex 50 was engaged with Harassing fire, 30 rounds being expended. At 5.26 pm we responded to S.O.S call with 30 rounds rapid. During the night target reference No. YR11/44 was subjected to harassing fire, 60 rounds being expended. One 'crash' fired on enemy battery E260 at 7.35 pm. Enemy artillery quiet, but a squadron of GOTHA planes attempted to bomb various battery positions. |

— " — | 6-12-17 | | At 2.03 am we promptly responded to S.O.S call, and fired 118 rounds on our S.O.S lines. At 4.20 pm a concentration was placed on Hostile Battery Ex 46, in which 120 rounds were expended. During the night target, reference No YR11/44 was again subjected to harassing fire, in which we fired 60 rounds. | H.R.N.C

— " — | 7-12-17 | | No targets today. Preparations are being made to hand battery position over to 510th SIEGE BATTERY. (IMPERIALS). The transfer is carried out, and we take over the position of this battery. | H.R.N.C

— " — | 8-12-17 } 12-12-17 } | | No guns in action during this period, and only a guard on ammunition and stores was kept at the position. Battery receives orders to move out of the line. |

— " — | 13-12-17 | | Battery leaves YPRES sector at about 9.30 am. The whole column moves to MORBECQUE. Column held outside HAZEBROOK for one hour, on account of enemy shelling with H.V. Guns. Arrive MORBECQUE about 2.30 pm, and billet there for the night. | H.R.N.C

## Notes on this transcript

- **Date coverage:** 1–31 December 1917, complete. Individual daily entries run 1–7 December and 13–15 December. Three periods are covered by single grouped entries rather than daily entries: 8–12 December ("No guns in action during this period…"), 16–25 December ("Battery at Rest"), and 27–31 December ("Battery in Training"). There are no missing days, but the grouped entries give no day-by-day detail for 8–12, 16–25 (except the 20th and 23rd, named within the group entry), and 27–31 December.
- **Casualties:** None named. The entry for 4 December 1917 states expressly "no casualties." No killed or wounded are named anywhere in this month.
- **Persons named (other than casualties):** Sgt. W. Rawling, No. 1257506 — awarded the Military Medal (3 December entry, Routine Order No. 1550, for gallant conduct 25 November); Bomb. H. W. Horwill, No. 1257518 — received the Military Medal (23 December parade); Lieut.-General Sir A. W. Currie, G.O.C. Canadian Corps; Brig.-General Massie, G.O.C. C.C.H.A.
- **Places:** Ypres, Morbecque, Hazebrook, Bethune (Montmorency Caserne), Lillers, Ham-en-Artois, Villers-au-Bois; target/position references include Eddy Farm and target reference No. YR11/44.
- **Oddities:** The contents page (p. 2) states "Diary Text 3 Pages," matching the three diary-text sheets (pp. 3–5 of this chunk). The cover is marked "CONFIDENTIAL" and "VOLUME 10," with a small handwritten "5" below the volume line. Only the first diary-text page carries a visible page number ("10", top right). The Remarks column on the diary pages is initialled "H.R.N.C" beside most entries. The battery handed its Ypres position to the 510th Siege Battery (Imperials) on 7 December and took over that battery's position, then moved out of the line on 13 December.
