## Page 3 (April 1918, p. 3) — [War Diary / Intelligence Summary, Army Form C. 2118]

WAR DIARY
or
INTELLIGENCE SUMMARY.
(Erase heading not required.)

Army Form C. 2118.

Instructions regarding War Diaries and Intelligence Summaries are contained in F. S. Regs., Part II. and the Staff Manual respectively. Title pages will be prepared in manuscript.

Place: PETIT-VIMY.

### 1-4-18

Harassing fire on BOIS BERNARD and HENIN LETARD till 9am. Four short bursts of fire on hostile batteries at U.14 b and a, 36 rounds fired. An aeroplane shoot was commenced at 9.05am on hostile battery at U.14 d 27.44, 300 rounds were fired and shoot completed at 12.45 pm. The rear section carried out several bursts of fire on FRESNOY and ARLEUX, firing 45 rounds. The usual programme of harassing fire was carried out from both positions. At about 5.45pm, an enemy plane crossed our lines and succeeded in destroying four of our observation balloons. Enemy artillery lightly shelled vicinity of No.1 GUN. (T.25a). No damage caused.

[Remarks: E. Scott, Lt.]

### 2-4-18

Many short bursts of fire placed on hostile batteries, and on S.O.S lines ACHEVILLE. Concentrations on hostile batteries at U.14 b+a at 5.40am, 60 rounds being fired. Many rounds of harassing fire put on enemy positions in ROUVROY. At 3.10 pm an aeroplane shoot was commenced on hostile battery at U.1 d 8.0, a total of 200 rounds was fired, and shoot reported as successful. Concentrations on hostile batteries were continued from 10.30 pm to 12.30 am. The rear position engaged several hostile batteries during the day with short bursts of fire. Night firing carried out from both positions. The enemy shelled the battery position, T.25a and S.24d with H.E, shrapnel, and gas. No casualties caused and no damage.

[Remarks: E. Scott, Lt.]

### 3-4-18

At 5.0am we opened up on S.O.S lines ACHEVILLE, firing 20 rounds. Harassing and neutralizing fire on various hostile batteries was maintained throughout the day, about 80 rounds being fired. Rear position shelled HENIN LETARD Road. Programme of night firing carried out from both positions. During the morning the enemy carried out what appeared to be an observed shoot on our position S.24d. No.4 GUN PIT was damaged, and gun was temporarily put out of action. No casualties.

[Remarks: E. Scott, Lt.]

### 4-4-18

Many hostile batteries were subjected to harassing fire during the day, and neutralizing fire on H.B's at U.14 a 9.6, 45 rounds being fired. No other targets. The enemy carried out a heavy area shoot in vicinity of battery position, with 24 C.M. HOW. No damage caused and no casualties.

[Remarks: E. Scott, Lt.]

## Page 4 (April 1918, p. 4) — [War Diary / Intelligence Summary, Army Form C. 2118]

Place: PETIT-VIMY.

### 5-4-18

Hostile batteries at U.14 a + b. were again subjected to short bursts of fire, 36 rounds being fired. Harassing fire was placed on L'HOMME MORT at U.2.a. and roads at U.13. 14 + 19, about 36 rounds being expended. At 11.30pm we commenced a heavy gas bombardment with H.E. and gas shell. This bombardment was maintained throughout the night. We fired 600 gas shell and 128 H.E shell. Our rear position maintained harassing fire on enemy roads, light railways etc during the day and night, and fired 158 of gas shell into CHEZ BON-TEMPS, in conjunction with the gas bombardment order.

[Remarks: E. Scott, Lt.]

### 6-4-18

Gas bombardment continued by both positions until 11.30am. Remainder of the day occupied in harassing fire on hostile batteries U.14 a + b, a total of 116 rounds being fired. Rear Section kept up harassing fire through the day and night on ACHEVILLE and enemy roads and trenches, expending 100 rounds. No hostile shelling, attitude of enemy very quiet.

[Remarks: E. Scott, Lt.]

### 7-4-18

No targets of importance today. Harassing fire on enemy movement and positions, also roads at BOIS BERNARD, 52 rounds being expended. Rear position carried out registration on bridge at T.5a.18, which was satisfactory. They also completed their usual programme of night firing. Rounds fired 48. Enemy fired 10 rounds shrapnel into vicinity of S.24.d.

[Remarks: E. Scott, Lt.]

### 8-4-18

Neutralization fire was placed on hostile batteries U.1.a + U.9.d. and harassing fire placed on many enemy roads and positions throughout the day and night, 46 rounds being fired. Rear position fired 45 rounds on to roads, and enemy trenches. Enemy artillery fired into VIMY and vicinity, intermittently during the day. No damage and no casualties.

[Remarks: E. Scott, Lt.]

### 9-4-18

At 8.25am a concentration was placed on hostile batteries located in U.14 a + b, in which 45 rounds were fired. A further concentration was carried out at 11.30 am on HUDSON POST. T.24c, 50 rounds expended. Harassing fire on hostile batteries was maintained during the afternoon + night. Rear position carried out a series of harassing fire shoots on to enemy trenches, FRESNOY PARK and ACHEVILLE. Enemy fired about 60 rounds 4.2" and 5.9" H.E. into S.24c. and S.24d.

[Remarks: E. Scott, Lt.]

## Page 5 (April 1918, p. 5) — [War Diary / Intelligence Summary, Army Form C. 2118]

Place: PETIT-VIMY.

### 10-4-18

Several batteries were engaged with neutralizing fire, principally those in U.14 and 15, about 90 rounds being fired. Harassing fire was maintained on enemy positions in BOIS BERNARD, ROUVROY and CHEZ-BONTEMPS, during the night. Our rear position fired 40 rounds of harassing and neutralizing fire during the afternoon on enemy battery at U.7.b. 8.5 and FRESNOY PARK. Enemy shelled C.T. in S.24.c + 29.b, with shrapnel + H.E. [uncertain] 4.1" + 5.9".

[Remarks: E. Scott, Lt.]

### 11-4-18

Hostile battery at U.31 d 50.45 was neutralized at 1.15pm, 10 rounds fired. Harassing fire during the day on enemy positions in BETRINCOURT FARM and ROUVROY. During the night bursts of fire were put on to hostile batteries in U.8.c + U.15.c, and night firing programme carried out on L'HOMME MORT, and enemy roads. Rear position fired only few rounds harassing fire. Enemy shelled light railway, A.4 d with 5.9s at 8.30 am. LIEUT. J. S. MILLER joined unit for duty.

[Remarks: E. Scott, Lt.]

### 12-4-18

At 9.07am a successful shoot was carried out on hostile battery located at U.1.d.79.01, with aeroplane observation. Calibration completed satisfactorily on RAILWAY COTTAGE at 12.30p.m. Opportunity targets of enemy movement were engaged during the afternoon, and parties dispersed. Rear Section fired 60 rounds on hostile batteries U.14.1, U.14.2 + U.14.7 in 10 minute bursts of fire. Enemy shelling - NIL.

[Remarks: E. Scott, Lt.]

### 13-4-18

At 5.25am, concentrations were fired on H.Bs, U.9.a U.8.a. O.32a + O.26c, 80 rounds being fired. Harassing fire maintained throughout the day and night on various targets. Rear Section inactive. Enemy shelled vicinity of T.19 + T.25, with gas, H.E and shrapnel. Few rounds very close to No.1 Gun. No damage.

[Remarks: E. Scott, Lt.]

### 14-4-18

Enemy roads, railways and tracks were subjected to many rounds of harassing fire. At 3.50pm hostile battery at U.9a.1.9 was neutralized; and a party of infantry on NOYELLES Road. N.2a were engaged with good results. Over 200 rounds were expended on many H.Bs, in carrying out of night firing programme. Rear Section on S.O.S lines (LENS) fired 10 minutes rapid. No other targets. Enemy fired 25 4.2" H.E into vicinity of T.25a. C.C.H.A. ROUTINE Order No.28/d/14th April publishes awards of MILITARY MEDAL to 1257571 GR. C. M. DICKEY and 1257622 Gr C. A. WEST for conspicuous bravery and devotion to duty.

[Remarks: E. Scott, Lt.]

## Page 6 (April 1918, p. 6) — [War Diary / Intelligence Summary, Army Form C. 2118]

Place: PETIT-VIMY.

### 15-4-18

Thirty rounds of harassing fire were expended on various hostile battery positions between 6 am and 7 am. Two shoots of neutralizing fire were carried out on hostile battery located at U.8.b.11.9, fifty-five rounds being fired. During the night 60 rounds were expended in harassing fire on hostile batteries at O.25.c and O.35.c. At about 10 am the enemy commenced shelling our position S.24.d. firing about 20, time H.E, apparently for ranging. At 2.30 pm he commenced a heavy bombardment on same location, and fired about 200 rounds. One shell burst in No.1 GUN PIT, in which LIEUT. E. A. MACKENZIE, and the gun crew were working. The undernoted casualties occurred. LIEUT. E. A MACKENZIE (S.W.), Bombr J. T. WENTZELL, (KILLED), Gr. E. C. PARSLOW (DIED of W) Gr. C. G. LEROUX (DIED of W), Gr. L. L. LAWRENCE (S.W.). Four other casualties also occurred during the bombardment, GR J. BARCLAY (DIED of W), GR. CROFT. (W), GR. BATEMAN (S.W.) and Gr A. N. MATHESON (W), NO MATERIAL was damaged.

[Remarks: E. Scott, Lt.]

### 16-4-18

Four shoots of neutralizing fire were carried out on hostile battery located at U.8.b.11.9, fifty rounds being fired. Harassing fire on BETRINCOURT FARM. U.2.c was maintained from 11.30am to 3.30pm. Hostile battery at N36a.5.6 9.5 was also neutralized. Rear position inactive. Enemy artillery shelled S.24.c + d. during the night. Twenty rounds of 4.1" H.E. being reported. No damage and no casualties.

[Remarks: E. Scott, Lt.]

### 17-4-18

Several hostile batteries were subjected to bursts of rapid fire, during the day, in which over 100 rounds were fired. Two shoots of neutralizing fire were carried out on hostile batteries U.8.b. 10.29, + U.8.b. 20.68. Harassing fire on enemy roads and light railways during the night. Rear position fired 68 rounds on hostile batteries in connection with a pre-arranged counter battery scheme. Enemy artillery shelled S.24 + S.30 T.25 and T.19. with shrapnel and gas. THELUS was subjected to harassing fire, in which we had one casualty A/CORPL. H. W. HORWILL (M.M) being wounded. No damage caused and no other casualties.

[Remarks: E. Scott, Lt.]

### 18-4-18

Hostile batteries located at U.3.c.95.02 and U.6.11.9. were neutralized, 25 rounds being fired. Thirty rounds of harassing fire was put on to road junctions at O.31.a and O.32.a, also enemy light railway. Two further neutralizations were carried out on H.B's located at U.8.d and U.9.b. Enemy lightly shelled vicinity of T.25.a with 5.9" H.E, at [uncertain] 3.30pm. One or two rounds fell near No.1. Gun. No damage nor casualties.

[Remarks: E. Scott, Lt.]

## Page 7 (April 1918, p. 7) — [War Diary / Intelligence Summary, Army Form C. 2118]

Place: PETIT-VIMY.

### 19-4-18

Neutralization of hostile batteries was carried out both morning and afternoon, as many as five separate shoots being carried out, of 10 rounds each. Calibration was completed successfully in 14 rounds on RAILWAY COTTAGE. U.1.C.1. Harassing fire was maintained on enemy roads during the night, and a large night programme was carried out on H.B's at U.8.b.11.8. and U.9.b.51.33, in accordance with Counter-Battery order No.64, 300 rounds were fired. Rear Section carried out calibration, firing 40 rounds, target at T.11.a.55.95. Enemy apparently carried out registration on Cross Roads in S.30.a, firing several rounds of [uncertain] 10.45.

[Remarks: E. Scott, Lt.]

### 20-4-18

Hostile batteries at U.8.b.11.8 and U.9.b.51.33, were engaged from 6.00am to 7.20 A.M. under C.B.O. No.64, and 135 rounds were fired. An aeroplane shoot was commenced at 5.10pm on H.B at U.8.b.52.29. but was discontinued as unsuccessful, due to very poor visibility. During the night, sharp bursts of fire were put on to H.Bs, and harassing fire was maintained, a total of 70 rounds being fired. Enemy artillery very active, over 300 rounds were reported on S.24d + S.30. No damage nor casualties.

[Remarks: E. Scott, Lt.]

### 21-4-18

At 10.30 am a shoot with aeroplane observation was carried out on hostile battery at U.8.b.52.20. Shoot completed successfully, 300 rounds fired. Hostile batteries at U.8.b.11.8 + U.9.a.73.35, were engaged in accordance with C.B.O. No.65. During the night harassing fire was put on to enemy roads + tramways. Rear position also carried out C.B.O. No.65 programme on H.Bs. U.7.b.0.31.c. + U.1.a. during the night.

[Remarks: E. Scott, Lt.]

### 22-4-18

Neutralizing fire was placed on hostile battery at U.2.b.90.70 at 9.30 am, 10 rounds being fired. At 2.55pm. twenty rounds were fired on RAILWAY COTTAGE, U.1.C.61. for purposes of calibration. Hostile battery at N.30.c.48.98, was neutralized at 4.55pm. Over 100 rounds were expended during the night in harassing and rapid fire on enemy troops, positions and roads. Rear position fired 70 rounds on H.B. at U.14 a 45.65 in connection with C.B.O. No.65. Enemy artillery shelled T.25a and Cross Roads at S.30.a with 15-C.M. and 10.5 C.M. A direct hit was made on No.1 Gun pit, but did no damage.

[Remarks: E. Scott, Lt.]

## Page 8 (April 1918, p. 8) — [War Diary / Intelligence Summary, Army Form C. 2118]

Place: PETIT-VIMY.

### 23-4-18

Calibration carried out on RAILWAY COTTAGE at 4.15pm. At 5.10pm, an aeroplane shoot was commenced on hostile battery at U.8.b.84.b, and brought to successful conclusion, 200 rounds being fired. Harassing fire on H.B's was maintained during the night. Rear Position fired 20 rounds on H.B at N.35.b.50.81. Enemy artillery shelled S.30.a + b. + S.24d, with shrapnel, gas and H.E, firing about 100 rounds.

[Remarks: E. Scott, Lt.]

### 24-4-18

A sound ranging shoot was attempted at 11am, but was abandoned owing to weather conditions. Twenty fours harassing fire was placed on H.B's at O.25c, O.31.a, + O.32c. during the afternoon. Neutralizing and harassing fire was maintained throughout the night on various targets, in which we expended 100 rounds. Rear Section neutralized H.B. at N.36.c.16.18 at 1.30pm. Enemy shelled vicinity of T.25a with about 20 rounds 4.2" H.E, and also vicinity of H.50, with 30 rounds gas + H.E.

[Remarks: E. Scott, Lt.]

### 25-4-18

An attempt was made to calibrate with Y SECTION, Sound Ranging, at 12.15pm, unsuccessful owing to poor weather conditions. Hostile batteries were engaged in connection with Operation Order No.66 in which 214 rounds were fired. Harassing fire during the night on enemy roads, cuttings + tramways. Rear position fired 376 rounds on H.B. at N.6a. 70.45 in connection with C.B.O. No.66. Enemy shelled S.24c and T.25a with H.E. intermittently during the day.

[Remarks: E. Scott, Lt.]

### 26-4-18

Hostile battery at U.8.b.05.50 was neutralized at 7.30 am. No other targets during the day. Harassing fire placed on enemy road junction + tramway during the evening. Neutralizing fire was carried out on several H.B's during the night, and C.B.O. No.70 was completed, by both front and rear sections. At 5.30 pm the enemy artillery fired 200 rounds on to vicinity of T.25a with 15.C.M. No damage was caused, and there were no casualties. Lieut. W. A. F. FAIRCHILD posted for duty.

[Remarks: E. Scott, Lt.]

## Page 9 (April 1918, p. 9) — [War Diary / Intelligence Summary, Army Form C. 2118]

Place: PETIT-VIMY.

### 27-4-18

No operations during the day. During the night harassing fire was maintained on enemy roads + junctions. H.B at N.30.c.75.72 was neutralized, and Operation Order No.69 was carried out, about a total of 90 rounds being fired. Enemy shelled S.24d. with 4.2" and 5.9" H.E, about 40 rounds being reported.

[Remarks: E. Scott, Lt.]

### 28-4-18

At 10.55 am, a successful calibration was carried out with Y Section, Sound Ranging O.K. being reported. Hostile batteries U.8.b, N.39c, + N.30.b. were neutralized, 45 rounds being expended. At 11.10pm a concentration was placed on H.Bs at U.9.a.28.66 + U.9.a.30.55, in which 75 rounds were fired. Enemy roads + tramways were harassed during the night, 120 rounds being fired. S.24d again received attention from the enemy, about 30 rounds gas + H.E shell being reported. No damage and no casualties.

[Remarks: E. Scott, Lieut.]

### 29-4-18

Neutralization of H.B's at U.8.b. + U.9.a was carried out during the morning, in which 75 rounds were fired. Registration carried out on RAILWAY COTTAGE was successful. Seventy five rounds of harassing fire was placed on enemy roads and tramways during the night. Enemy shelled vicinity of T.25a. with H.E and shrapnel, about 35 rounds being reported. No damage nor casualties.

[Remarks: E. Scott, Lt.]

### 30-4-18

Harassing fire during the day on road junctions and tramways, 20 rounds. Hostile batteries at O.25.c.00.80 and U.2.c.20.49 were neutralized at 2pm and 3.45pm, 50 rounds being fired. A shoot was attempted at 6.5pm with Y Section Sound Ranging but was unsuccessful. During the night the usual programme of harassing fire was carried out, and hostile batteries in U.9.a were neutralized. Enemy artillery shelled S.30.b, firing 38 rounds H.E. 7.7 M.M. MAJOR S. A. HEWARD, OFFICER COMMANDING to SICK LIST. LIEUT. J. E. LEAN takes over duties of O.C.

[Remarks: W. E. B. Scott, Lt.]
