## Page 3 (July 1917, p. 3) — [diary text, Army Form C. 2118]

WAR DIARY or INTELLIGENCE SUMMARY. (Erase heading not required.)

| Place | Date | Hour | Summary of Events and Information |
|---|---|---|---|
| Cité de Caumont | 1-7-17 | | Harassing fire was maintained throughout the day. About 400 rounds being fired. At noon the CANADIAN CORPS ARTILLERY fired 3 salvos at a given signal from observation balloon, to commemorate DOMINION DAY. |
| | 2-7-17 | | About 300 rounds were fired on enemy trenches. One shot being reported as very satisfactory. A further 100 rounds were fired into a communication trench, but observation very poor. Enemy artillery very quiet. |
| | 3-7-17 | | An S.O.S. call at 12.45 pm was given. We opened up immediately and fired 60 rounds into enemy trench. Harassing fire was maintained throughout the night on Roads, Bridges etc. |
| | 4-7-17 | | Registration and calibration of guns was carried out, 40 rounds being fired. A further 60 rounds were fired on to enemy railway. No results were obtained, the observation being very poor. |
| | 5-7-17 | | A very quiet day. Practically no firing all day. In the night a programme of harassing fire was kept up enemy buildings, Roads Railways etc. Enemy artillery still very quiet. |

## Page 4 (July 1917, p. 4) — [diary text, Army Form C. 2118]

| Place | Date | Hour | Summary of Events and Information |
|---|---|---|---|
| Cité de Caumont | 6-7-17 | | A concentration shoot on LENS was carried out with good effect. Also an opportunity shoot on enemy working party, the result being the party was dispersed. About 150 rounds being fired during the day. The enemy opened up on the battery position with very heavy gun fire. He did no damage however, but Gunners ARCHIBALD and BISHOP were wounded. |
| | 7-7-17 | | 50 rounds were put into enemy trench with good results. The usual programme of harassing fire was maintained throughout the night. |
| | 8-7-17 | | Numerous targets were engaged, about 150 rounds being fired into enemy strong points, houses, cross Roads etc. Enemy heavily fired into battery position with gas shell — No Casualties. |
| | 9-7-17 | | At 6 am a barrage fire was placed on enemy trenches, 24 rounds being fired. We bombarded special target (trench) with good effect, many rounds seem to fall into the trench, a total of 152 rounds were fired at this target. |

## Page 5 (July 1917, p. 5) — [diary text, Army Form C. 2118]

| Place | Date | Hour | Summary of Events and Information |
|---|---|---|---|
| Cité de Caumont | 10-7-17 | | Many targets were taken on, a total of 180 rounds being expended on Cross Roads, Railway Embankment etc. Enemy artillery quiet. |
| | 11-7-17 | | Registration completed. Enemy trench was taken on with good results, 100 rounds being expended. Enemy artillery active but not on battery position. |
| | 12-7-17 | | One shoot on enemy wire carried out with good results, also fired 80 rounds into trench with good effect. Many rounds were observed to fall into this trench, and it appeared to be badly damaged. |
| | 13-7-17 | | One shoot of 60 rounds on two enemy trenches, results reported fairly satisfactory, but observation difficult. Night firing on enemy roads and bridges. |
| | 14-7-17 | | A good result was obtained from a shoot on enemy trench, 50 rounds were fired, and many were seen to fall into the trench. 100 rounds were expended during night on usual programme of harassing fire. |
| | 15-7-17 | | Two enemy trenches were bombarded with satisfactory results, 150 rounds being fired. 6 minutes rapid fire was also put on with good effect. |

## Page 6 (July 1917, p. 6) — [diary text, Army Form C. 2118]

| Place | Date | Hour | Summary of Events and Information |
|---|---|---|---|
| Cité de Caumont | 16-7-17 | | We fired 200 rounds on enemy trench with good effect. In addition we carried out the usual programme of night firing. 100 rounds fired. |
| | 17-7-17 | | Our tasks for today were of a very varied nature, no fewer than 9 different targets being engaged, one of which was a balloon shoot. 200 rounds were fired and reports were satisfactory. |
| | 18-7-17 | | An aeroplane shoot on enemy wire was carried out, and aeroplane report results as fairly good — 300 rounds were fired. The enemy artillery sent a few 5.9s into ANGRES but without doing much damage — |
| | 19-7-17 | | A good shoot was carried out on enemy trenches, during this shoot, the observer reported large dump of flares were set on fire. 198 rounds were expended on this task. 40 rounds expended on night firing programme. |
| | 20-7-17 | | A concentration shoot on a hostile battery was fired, apparently with good results, enemy trench also engaged. 78 rounds being fired. |
| | 21-7-17 | | Enemy wire was well cut in a shoot of 400 rounds, parts being very badly damaged — Five minutes rapid fire on SALLUMINES was also put on. The usual programme of night firing was undertaken. |

## Page 7 (July 1917, p. 7) — [diary text, Army Form C. 2118]

| Place | Date | Hour | Summary of Events and Information |
|---|---|---|---|
| Cité de Caumont | 22-7-17 | | Another very heavy shoot on enemy wire was carried out, with results very satisfactory. 450 rounds were fired. The observer reports that during shoot an explosion was caused — His further report states that the wire was totally destroyed. Enemy Artillery very quiet. |
| | 23-7-17 | | Enemy wire again subjected to heavy fire — One shoot with aeroplane observation, was reported as satisfactory. 200 rounds were expended. |
| | 24-7-17 | | Five minutes rapid fire was put on to Railway embankment at 8.55 am. Enemy trench also engaged with good effect. 140 rounds fired. |
| | 25-7-17 | | Many targets taken on, Roads, Bridges, Strong points etc. [one word struck out] A shoot of 300 rounds on enemy trench and wire was successful — Many rounds observed to fall in trench, and on wire. |
| | 26-7-17 | | COMMOTION TRENCH heavily engaged by us day and night. Over 500 rounds were fired into it during the 24 hours. Results reported satisfactory. |
| | 27-7-17 | | We again fired very heavily on COMMOTION TRENCH, practically all day and night. A total of 560 rounds being fired — The observer reported observation difficult, but claims satisfactory results were obtained. |

## Page 8 (July 1917, p. 8) — [diary text, Army Form C. 2118]

| Place | Date | Hour | Summary of Events and Information |
|---|---|---|---|
| Cité de Caumont | 28-7-17 | | A further shoot of 450 rounds on COMMOTION TRENCH were carried out. Portion of trench visible to observer reported destroyed. A barrage of 36 rounds was put on to Railway embankment in the morning. |
| | 29-7-17 | | Same target today — Another 400 rounds into this trench. Many rounds observed to fall into trench + much wire was destroyed. Three other shoots carried out, results of which were uncertain — Visibility poor. |
| | 30-7-17 | | We again engaged COMMOTION TRENCH with very good results — 870 rounds being fired — We were given several other tasks, 2 of which were rapid fire shoots, and concentrated on enemy trench. |
| | 31-7-17 | | An S.O.S call was made at 10.05 am — 39 rounds were fired in response on enemy's trench and wire — A shoot of 400 rounds was carried out with apparently good results, visibility very poor. 2 rapid fire shoots were put on at 5.00 pm + 7.00 pm. 62 rounds being fired. |

## Notes

- **Date coverage:** Every day 1–31 July 1917 has an entry; no date gaps.
- **Casualties:** 6 July — Gunners ARCHIBALD and BISHOP wounded (enemy shelling of the battery position). 8 July — enemy gas shelling of the battery position, no casualties.
- **Places named:** Cité de Caumont (battery position), LENS, ANGRES, SALLUMINES, COMMOTION TRENCH.
- **Oddities:** Cover is stamped CONFIDENTIAL and marked "VOLUME 5" with a pencilled "6" beneath. Contents page states "Diary Text 6 Pages" — the diary text is indeed 6 pages (pp. 3–8 of this month's file). On 25 July one word in the entry is struck out (illegible under the deletion).
