## Notes

- **Date coverage:** The whole month, 1–31 January 1919, is covered — but in five grouped blocks (1–6, 7, 8–19, 20, 21–31 January) rather than daily entries. No days are missing, but there is no day-by-day record.
- **Casualties:** None recorded.
- **Decorations:** 20 January — No. 1257580 Sergeant W. KEMP was presented with the Distinguished Conduct Medal by the G.O.C. XXII Corps at Mons, at a presentation parade attended by a detachment of selected N.C.O.s and men of the battery.
- **Places:** Jemappes, Lez and Mons — the battery is in the Mons area of Belgium in the months after the Armistice. No map references are given this month.
- **Oddities:** The whole month's diary is a single page — the first month in this diary to be so brief, and there is no firing, movement or operational entry at all: the battery's routine is battery training, educational classes and care of equipment. The cover is entirely handwritten (including "Confidential"), where the 1917 covers were printed. The diary-text page is headed "1st Page" in handwriting, and "23" (the volume number) is written at the top right. The Remarks and references to Appendices column carries only a single dot, against the first entry. The month is signed "for MAJOR" by a lieutenant whose surname is illegible, rather than by the O.C. in person — the first such change of signatory in this diary.
