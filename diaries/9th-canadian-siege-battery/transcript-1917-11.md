## Page 3 (November 1917, p. 3) — [diary text, Army Form C. 2118]

WAR DIARY or INTELLIGENCE SUMMARY. (Erase heading not required.)

| Place | Date | Hour | Summary of Events and Information |
|---|---|---|---|
| YPRES | 1-11-17 | | The map location of the battery position since the 21st Oct has been C.30 c 11.18. It is not by any means a good one, being subjected to heavy hostile shell fire. We receive word today, that 1257531 Bdr (A/Cpl) SMITH. J, has died of wounds. Date of death 28.10.17. A barrage was placed on enemy positions at 5.45 a.m. 25 rounds being fired. Protective barrage also fired. Targets being Track N. of [uncertain] Copse + Roads from D 6.b.50.35 and WRATH FARM - 120 Rounds fired. The battery position was bombed by enemy airmen, also shelled at intervals with enemy H.V. Gun. No Casualties. |
| —"— | 2-11-17 | | At 3 p.m. we put a Concentration on enemy wire at D.66.65.40, and at 4 p.m. opened up barrage fire (Varb.) [uncertain] On these shoots 84 rounds were expended. Bursts of fire were kept up on a line 500° east of our S.O.S line. A further Concentration was placed on W.25.c.28 [uncertain] a total of 60 rounds being fired. The battery position was heavily shelled, and a percentage of gas shell was observed. One man No 2163349 Gunner J. W. Brown was reported slightly gassed, and admitted to Hospital otherwise no harm was done. |
| —"— | 3-11-17 | | The battery position was again heavily shelled, large numbers of H.E. and Shrapnel falling near the guns. We put two bursts of fire on to a line from 100 to 300 Yards. EAST of S.O.S lines, 36 rounds being expended. We also participated in 3 large Concentration shoots on Areas N25.c.40.90-70.90+40.50. and W25a 60-20.75-35-60.20, in which we expended 220 rounds. |
| —"— | 4-11-17 | | At 4.40 a.m. we opened up a barrage x(a) firing 36 rounds. On completion, a protective barrage of 66 rounds was fired on D.6.d [uncertain] One gun goes out of action. From 4 p.m. to 6 p.m. the battery position was again shelled heavily with 5.9's and 4.1's. No damage resulted. The battery [uncertain] was again bombed by enemy airmen, and also shelled at intervals with enemy H.V. Gun. No Casualties. |

## Page 4 (November 1917, p. 4) — [diary text, Army Form C. 2118]

| Place | Date | Hour | Summary of Events and Information |
|---|---|---|---|
| YPRES | 5-11-17 | | At 10.35 a.m. a concentration was placed on E9c 15.30, and a similar shoot on the same target was carried out at 2.55 p.m. A total of 42 rounds being fired. A barrage Y(a)(b) was put up at 4.45 p.m. In the evening further concentrations were placed on E7a 91.51 firing 42 rounds. Harassing fire on roads leading to WRATH FARM was maintained throughout the night, in which 120 rounds were fired. Intermittent enemy shelling on battery position from 1.00 to 3.00 p.m. |
| —"— | 6-11-17 | | In conjunction with the attack by the Canadian Divisions this morning at 6 a.m. we opened up with an attack barrage. This was immediately followed by the protective barrage, which was maintained up to 3 p.m. The S.O.S call was promptly responded to at 5.40 p.m. and we continued until the situation was again quiet. The enemy shelled the battery position and vicinity heavily for the greater part of the day, but did no damage. |
| —"— | 7-11-17 | | Two barrages (A) and (B) were fired 400 yards in advance of F.A. S.O.S lines, and at 4.21 p.m. an S.O.S Call was responded to, and 35 rounds were fired. Enemy shelling was mild, and was really the only day, we have not received the enemy's attentions, since taking over the position. |
| —"— | 8-11-17 | | At 9 a.m. we relieved 35-2 Siege Battery, and take up Counter-battery work. Our position is now in D.25.b 88.23 on the ZONNEBEKE Road. The position is an extremely poor one, and has very many disadvantages. Our tasks were of a minor nature, consisting of several bursts of fire, and harassing fire on roads, and enemy positions in MOORSLEDE. We were subjected to very heavy enemy shelling, approximately 1200 rounds of H.E. + Shrapnel, but little damage resulted. We had only one gun in action, the others being out for temporary repairs. |

## Page 5 (November 1917, p. 5) — [diary text, Army Form C. 2118]

| Place | Date | Hour | Summary of Events and Information |
|---|---|---|---|
| YPRES | 9-11-17 | | A shoot on hostile battery at E.W.39 [uncertain] was carried out at 5 a.m., in which we fired 30 rounds. At 6 a.m. barrage C(a) was commenced and continued till 6.32 a.m. These were our only shoots this day. The enemy artillery secured practically a direct hit on one of our guns and No 1257506 Sergt W. RAWLING was wounded, one gun is kept in action, repairs having been effected. |
| —"— | 10-11-17 | | Our one gun opened up in response to S.O.S. call at 2.50 p.m., 10 rounds being fired. Several bursts of fire were placed in W.26.d, about 45 rounds being expended. This was eventually stopped by enemy neutralising fire, which was unusually heavy. No damage was done, and our gun went into action again later. During the night we maintained fire on enemy roads, gun positions etc. |
| —"— | 11-11-17 | | MOORSLEDE was subjected to 16 rounds rapid fire at 6.45 a.m. No other targets during the day. From 9 p.m. onwards through the night harassing fire was kept up on enemy positions at E3a 20.5 and E26.5-6. The battery position was again subjected to heavy enemy fire, and secured direct hits on "Pillboxes" in which the detachments had taken cover. As a result we had 2 casualties (shell shock) A/Sgt R.J. TAYLOR and Gnr H. SPINNEY. Our billets were also subjected to hostile shelling and one shell smashed in a cellar, used as a telephone cabinet. As a result we had one casualty, Gunner M.R. JONES, and the communication to group and battery temporarily broken. |

## Page 6 (November 1917, p. 6) — [diary text, Army Form C. 2118]

| Place | Date | Hour | Summary of Events and Information |
|---|---|---|---|
| Ypres | 12-11-17 | | From 5 a.m. today throughout a 24 hour period, we only fired 17 rounds, these being on our usual night lines. The battery position is again subjected to heavy shelling, approximately 600 rounds, consisting of H.E. Shrapnel, and Gas being fired. Very little damage was done and there were no Casualties. No guns now in action. |
| —"— | 13-11-17 | | All guns go to workshop, working parties are therefore put on to improve position. Routine Order No 1530 dated 13th Novr gives the names of 1257531 Bdr (A/Cpl) J. SMITH and 1257597 Gnr L.C. ACTON as having been awarded the MILITARY MEDAL. |
| —"— | 14th. to 19th. | | No guns arrived from workshop yet. Opportunity is taken to improve Battery position and to commence a new one. The work is frequently interrupted by hostile shell fire, and during this period two small cartridge dumps are blown up. No other damage done and no casualties caused. — The following has been received from G.O.C.R.A. "It affords the G.O.C.R.A. CANADIAN CORPS the greatest satisfaction to communicate to the Artillery who have served in the CORPS during the PASSCHENDAELE operations, the Army Commander's appreciation of the splendid work they have performed. The G.O.C.R.A desires... to add his congratulations and express his admiration at the manner in which all ranks had done their work under most trying conditions. The following has been received from the [uncertain] General Commanding 2nd Army. [continued on next page] |

## Page 7 (November 1917, p. 7) — [diary text, Army Form C. 2118]

| Place | Date | Hour | Summary of Events and Information |
|---|---|---|---|
| Ypres | 14th. to 19th. | | (Continued) "I should be glad if you would express to all Brigades, Groups and Batteries of Artillery who have served in the 2nd Army throughout the offensive operations this summer + autumn, my appreciation of the splendid work they have done - I fully realise how arduous this work has been. I know that they have suffered heavy losses, and have had to remain in action under the most trying circumstances for very long periods - They have never flinched or abated their efforts for a moment but have steadily continued to overcome all difficulties, and as a result they have contributed very materially to the success achieved." (sd) H. Plumer, General |
| —"— | 20-11-17 | | One gun reported from workshop but not yet in action. Will probably be ready for action tomorrow. The battery position was shelled intermittently throughout the day, but without damage or casualties. Gun put into action position, and made ready for action. |
| —"— | 21-11-17 | | No gun yet in action. The vicinity of battery position was heavily shelled throughout the day and night, and 150 cartridges were destroyed. No other damage and no casualties. |
| —"— | 22-11-17 | | The gun in position and ready for action. Supply of ammunition for same delayed through light Railway not being available. Battery position and vicinity shelled with 5.9s during morning. |
| —"— | 23-11-17 | | The gun in position is located at D.25.d 62.68. A second now in transit to battery and a third at billets awaiting stores. Unable to register the gun owing to climatic conditions. |
| —"— | 24-11-17 | | Weather conditions still unfavourable for registration. Much work carried out preparing new gun positions. Another gun put into position and ready for action. Enemy activity again below normal. |

## Page 8 (November 1917, p. 8) — [diary text, Army Form C. 2118]

| Place | Date | Hour | Summary of Events and Information |
|---|---|---|---|
| YPRES | 25-11-17 | | One gun now in action, our first shoot was at 7.30 p.m. on S.O.S lines in response to S.O.S. call. During the night harassing fire on enemy roads was maintained. Total rounds fired 60. The Officer Commanding desires to place on record the excellent work of the gun detachments on the 8th and 9th, and especially to the good examples set by the No. 1 Sergeants PALMER, MOSLEY + RAWLING in their untiring efforts not only to put, but to keep the only available gun in action throughout the night, under very adverse circumstances. Sgt. RAWLING who was No. 1 on the morning of the 9th inst, during the barrage, continued with great coolness and gallantry to fire his gun under heavy hostile shelling until finally put out of action by a direct hit on the gun, which wounded him. |
| —"— | 26-11-17 | | Many minor tasks were carried out on hostile batteries, the principal ones being two concentrations of 40 rounds each on positions E4.28 and E2.15. We had 3 calls to open fire on night lines, and after completion, harassing fire was maintained on enemy road E2a + b. Enemy shelling was below normal, a few rounds being fired in the vicinity of battery position. |
| —"— | 27-11-17 | | Three N.F. calls were answered during the morning, and 60 rounds were fired at Hostile Batteries E.W.51 and E.H.52. Harassing fire on H.B. W.A.73. from 11 a.m. to 1 p.m. Calibration carried out in the afternoon, after which 163 rounds rapid were fired on night lines from 5.50 p.m. to 10.15 p.m. Two guns in action the whole period. Enemy fired about 150 rounds gas shell into vicinity of battery position. No casualties. |
| —"— | 28-11-17 | | One concentration at 5 a.m. on H.B. W.C.84. Ten other H.B's were engaged during the day, 170 rounds being fired. One S.O.S. call was responded to at 6.40 a.m. Harassing fire was maintained on enemy roads, 150 rounds being fired. The battery position was again shelled intermittently throughout the day. There were no casualties + no damage. |

## Page 9 (November 1917, p. 9) — [diary text, Army Form C. 2118]

| Place | Date | Hour | Summary of Events and Information |
|---|---|---|---|
| YPRES | 29-11-17 | | Four minor tasks were carried out on Hostile Batteries, 35 rounds being expended. At 2 p.m. a concentration was placed on Hostile Battery W.D.16. Registration was carried out during the morning. Three S.O.S calls were responded to between 11.25 p.m. + 6.0 p.m. Harassing fire was maintained on enemy positions throughout the night. In YPRES, and in vicinity of billets the enemy fired many rounds from H.V. Guns, with the apparent intention of harassing transport etc. |
| —"— | 30-11-17 | | At 6.28 a.m. S.O.S. call was given, and we immediately responded. Fire on S.O.S lines was maintained from 6.28 a.m. to 9.00 a.m., a total of 180 rounds being fired. From 9 a.m. many targets were engaged with harassing fire and concentrations, over 200 rounds being fired. YPRES was again shelled with enemy H.V. Guns, the firing being intermittent and the shooting scattered. Three of our guns are now in action. The map locations now being D.24.62.68 [uncertain], 56.73, 23.61 and 19.71. |
