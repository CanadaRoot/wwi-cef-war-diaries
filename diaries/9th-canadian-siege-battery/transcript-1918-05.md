## Page 3 (May 1918, p. 3) — [diary text, Army Form C. 2118]

WAR DIARY or INTELLIGENCE SUMMARY. (Erase heading not required.)

| Place | Date | Hour | Summary of Events and Information |
|---|---|---|---|
| PETIT-VIMY. | 1-5-18. | | An attempt at calibration with 'Y' Section Sound Ranging, at 4 pm was unsuccessful. Hostile battery at U.2.C.40.15 was subjected to neutralizing fire at 6 pm, 25 rounds being fired. During the night harassing fire was maintained on enemy roads and road junctions, total expenditure, 75 rounds. Rear Section inactive throughout the 24 hours. Enemy artillery activity - NIL. |
| " | 2-5-18. | | At 2.35 pm registration was carried out with 'Y' Section Sound Ranging on hostile battery at U.8.b.11.19, shoot was successful, O.K's being reported. An N.F. call was responded to at 5.35 pm, and hostile battery at U.9.b.30.20 was neutralized, 25 rounds being fired. During the night enemy roads and approaches were harassed, expenditure 90 rounds. Rear Section inactive. Twice during the evening enemy artillery shelled vicinity of T.25a with gas and H.E. shell. No damage and no casualties. |
| " | 3-5-18 | | A shoot with aeroplane observation was commenced at 2.05 pm on hostile battery at U.3.c.25.75, but had to be abandoned owing to low visibility. Neutralizing fire was placed on hostile battery at U.8.b.52.29, at 3.25 pm, 25 rounds being fired. The aeroplane shoot was again attempted on H.B. U.3.c.25.75, this time successfully, a total of 250 rounds being fired. Rear Section carried out registration and fired salvos at intervals during the day. A hostile working party was shelled and dispersed at U.19.d.45.60. Enemy shelled S.24.c and vicinity intermittently during the night. |
| " | 4-5-18. | | Hostile batteries at U.14.a.72.66 + U.14.a.55.45 were each subjected to 10 rounds rapid fire at 12.55 pm. Calibration successfully carried out at 6.10 pm. An aeroplane shoot was carried out on hostile battery at U.12.b.64.46, which was successfully completed, 250 rounds being fired. H.B. at U.8.b.11.19 was neutralized at 10.25 pm. Rear Section carried out a programme of harassing fire during the night on enemy roads, firing 80 rounds. Enemy artillery shelled S.24.d + T.25.d with H.E. and gas shell. Lieut. P. C. Harding posted to unit for duty. |

## Notes

- **Date coverage:** Entries exist for every day, 1–31 May 1918. **No date gaps.**
- **Casualties:** 27 May — Gnr. F. HOLLAND, Gnr. J. J. LYNCH and Gnr. J. MORELAND, all gassed (none dangerous) in the enemy gas bombardment of S.24.d. and T.25.a that began on 26 May and continued until 5.45 a.m. on 27 May (estimated 1,100 rounds fired into the area). 29 May — a shell burst in the mouth of No. 1 gun pit at T.25.a: Gnr. MacGregor (severe burns), Gnr. MacAloney (burns), G. Mead (burns) and Penney (shell shock). No deaths are recorded in May. Enemy shelling repeatedly reported "No damage and no casualties" on 2, 13, 15, 17, 22 May and 31 May.
- **Command change:** 30 May — Captain A. T. SEAMAN takes over command vice A/Capt J. E. Lean, who goes to 1st Can. Siege Bty. Seaman signs the month's diary as a/O.C. (acting Officer Commanding).
- **Other personnel:** 4 May — Lieut. P. C. Harding posted to the unit for duty.
- **Places and map references:** Petit-Vimy (battery position throughout); S.24.d. and T.25.a. (the battery's own area, shelled repeatedly); BOIS BERNARD; ROUVROY; BETRICOURT FARM; L'HOMME MORT; TRIANGLE CUTTING; MAUD VILLAS; MERICOURT; RAILWAY COTTAGE; RAILWAY EMBANKMENT; SALLUMINES TRENCH; ACHEVILLE CHURCH; LENS (S.O.S. calls); and numerous trench-map references (U.2.C.40.15, U.8.b.11.19, U.9.b.30.20, U.3.c.25.75, U.8.b.52.29, U.19.d.45.60, U.14.a.72.66, U.14.a.55.45, U.12.b.64.46, N.36.b.30.30, U.9.c.90.85, N.29.c.65.00, N.36.b.35.20, U.15.c.26.50, U.14.c.23.49, U.14.d.30.30, N.16.d.50, U.2.d., N.36.b., N.30.c., U.26.d.00.65, O.31.a.40.70, N.36.c., U.2.b., U.1.d.79.01, N.27.c., U.1.b., N.36.b., U.14.a.49.52, U.2.b.26.90, T.6.a., N.30.a., U.8.b.61.55, O.31.b.60.95, O.32.c, O.25.d.60.00, U.9.b.50.20, U.2.b.96.23, O.25.c, T.6.d.30.28, N.36.c., N.36.b., U.15.c.40.30, T.11.a, T.6.a.20.60, N.30.b.50.50, U.8.a.20.35, U.8.c.95.60, N.34.d.90.15, U.9.b.45.35, U.9.c.00.45, N.30.d.60.80 to 55.90, U.15.c.61.21, U.15.c.30.20, N.29.b.39.28, U.26.a.12.23, N.30.c., N.29.d., N.36.a., U.8.b.84.68, N.34.d.58.90 to N.34.b.70.10, U.8.b.40.35, U.15.d.92.22, T.6.b.20.60, U.15.d.65.11, N.36.d.90 + N.36.c.95.10, U.15.a.7.26, N.30.b, U.15.b.12.30, N.34.d.55.58 to 60.75, U.8.d.45.28, N.30.a, N.20.a).
