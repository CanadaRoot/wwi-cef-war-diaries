## Page 3 (August 1917, p. 3) — [diary text, Army Form C. 2118]

WAR DIARY or INTELLIGENCE SUMMARY. (Erase heading not required.)

["Original" is written diagonally across the top left corner of this page.]

| Place | Date | Hour | Summary of Events and Information |
|---|---|---|---|
| Cité de Caumont | 1-8-17 | | One special shoot on CITE STE AUGUSTE. Effect good. 450 rounds fired on enemy trench, observation very difficult but reports of shoot show fairly good results. Two barrages were placed on enemy lines during the early hours of the morning. |
| " | 2-8-17 | | A shoot of 350 rounds on COMMOTION TRENCH, results satisfactory. A further 150 rounds fired on miscellaneous targets throughout the day and night. |
| | 3-8-17 | | COMMOTION TRENCH again subjected to heavy fire. 275 rounds being expended. Effect apparently good. During the night a further 50 rounds were expended on this trench, this being the night firing programme. Enemy quiet. |
| " | 4-8-17 | | The principal target today was again COMMOTION TRENCH. 250 rounds being fired with satisfactory results. 3 Rapid fire shoots were carried out. 75 rounds being expended. Enemy artillery still very quiet. |
| | 5-8-17 | | An aeroplane shoot was commenced on COMMOTION TRENCH, but had to be given up on account of unsatisfactory results. This shoot was completed from Map references. A total of 400 rounds were fired. |
| " | 6-8-17 | | Several targets were engaged the chief being again COMMOTION. A total of 400 rounds were expended. Observation very difficult, the visibility being poor. |

## Page 4 (August 1917, p. 4) — [diary text, Army Form C. 2118]

| Place | Date | Hour | Summary of Events and Information |
|---|---|---|---|
| Cité de Caumont | 7-8-17 | | Various targets have been engaged, principally enemy trenches. 345 rounds being expended. A barrage of 24 rounds was placed on enemy lines in the evening. 1 NCO and 6 men have been transferred to No. 8. Can. S. Bty. as re-inforcements, also LIEUT. GUNTER sent for temporary duty. |
| " | 8-8-17 | | Many shoots were undertaken today, enemy trenches, houses, railways being shelled with good effect. A few enemy shell of the "new" Mustard gas type were fired into the vicinity of the battery position. NO Casualties. Information comes to hand that COMMOTION TRENCH is in a very bad state. This would indicate that our heavy shelling of this trench during the past few days has been most successful. |
| | 9-8-17 | | Targets today were of a varied nature, railway cutting, embankment and houses being fired on. About 300 rounds being fired. Barrages were placed on enemy lines at 5 am and 4.15 am. One gun out of action. |
| | 10-8-17 | | Enemy trenches were taken on with good effect. One shoot of 250 rounds was fired into CINNIBAR TRENCH with satisfactory results. The usual programme of night firing was carried out. |

## Page 5 (August 1917, p. 5) — [diary text, Army Form C. 2118]

| Place | Date | Hour | Summary of Events and Information |
|---|---|---|---|
| Cité de Caumont | 11-8-17 | | CINNIBAR TRENCH again heavily engaged. We fired 250 rounds, many of which were observed to fall into trench and on wire. 100 rounds were also expended on other minor tasks. |
| " | 12-8-17 | | Wire in front of CINNIBAR TRENCH well cut in a shoot of 200 rounds. A concentration shoot was carried out at 7.15 am, and 10 minutes rapid fire was put on CONDUCTOR TRENCH. |
| " | 13-8-17 | | A concentration shoot on LOISON of 28 rounds was carried out at 8.15 am. CINNIBAR TRENCH again received greatest attention — About 250 rounds being fired with satisfactory results, wire in front of trench reported well cut. |
| | 14-8-17 | | Many tasks were carried out — 132 rounds were put onto enemy machine gun emplacement, also 250 rounds into trenches and wire — Results satisfactory. |
| | 15-8-17 | | Barrage fire on enemy positions was maintained from 5 am to 10 am, about 460 rounds being fired. At 8 am the enemy heavily shelled our rear position, No. 1 gun being put out of action, and we had 4 Casualties. Gr. FAIRFOWL being Killed, Bdr. NORWILL [uncertain], Gnr ALTHAM and MITCHELL. W.H. being severely wounded — The COMMANDING OFFICER desires to place on record, the coolness and devotion to duty shewn by the Officer, [continued on next page] |

[Note on the 15 August casualties: the Bombardier's surname is written "NORWILL" here, but the continuation of this entry on the next page gives the name as "Bdr. Horwill". The two spellings almost certainly refer to the same man; "Horwill" is used in the continuation.]

## Notes

- **Date coverage:** Every day 1–31 August 1917 has an entry; no date gaps. The 15 August entry runs over two pages.
- **Casualties:** 15 August — Gnr FAIRFOWL killed; Bdr. HORWILL [written "NORWILL" on the casualty page — see note above], Gnr ALTHAM and Gnr MITCHELL W.H. severely wounded. No. 1 gun put out of action by enemy shelling of the rear position. 8 August — enemy mustard gas shells fired near the battery position, no casualties.
- **Personnel:** 7 August — 1 NCO and 6 men transferred to No. 8 Canadian Siege Battery as reinforcements; Lieut. GUNTER sent for temporary duty. 15 August — Lieut. HOARE, Cpl TROOP and Bdr. Horwill submitted to H.Q. for consideration of an award for their conduct under fire. 24 August — letter of appreciation received from O.C. 3rd Canadian Division for the Canadian Corps Heavy Artillery's work in the raid of the night of 22nd/23rd August; the quoted passage breaks off mid-sentence and is not continued on the following page.
- **Places named:** Cité de Caumont (battery position), CITÉ STE AUGUSTE, COMMOTION TRENCH, CINNIBAR TRENCH, CONDUCTOR TRENCH, ALPACA TRENCH, ANTELOPE TRENCH, GREEN CRASSIER, LOISON, LOISON STATION, LENS, CANAL ROAD, NABOB'S ALLEY, CITÉ DU NORD, NOYELLES.
- **Oddities:** Cover is stamped CONFIDENTIAL and marked "VOLUME 6" with a pencilled "6" beneath. Contents page states "Diary Text 6 Pages" — the diary text is indeed 6 pages (pp. 3–8 of this month's file). "Original" is written diagonally across the top left of the first diary page (p. 3). The 24 August quotation from the 3rd Canadian Division's letter ends mid-sentence with a dash and is not resumed. The 9 August entry gives barrage times as "5 am and 4.15 am" in that order, as written.
