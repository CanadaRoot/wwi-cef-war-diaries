| Place | Date | Hour | Summary of Events and Information |
|---|---|---|---|
| Near SOUCHEZ (S.9.d.04.59) | 1-8-18 | | An enemy OBSERVATION POST located at N21d.15.50 was heavily bombarded from 2.45 pm to 5.10 pm. A total of 150 rounds were fired, shoot successful, OKs being reported. Enemy road at N29c.55.15 and Trench Junction at T.5a.81.48, were both subjected to harassing fire during the night, 75 rounds being expended. |
| —"— | 2-8-18 | | A few rounds of harassing fire on enemy roads is the only task for the day, Target same as yesterday. During the night 35 rounds harassing fire on to enemy position at N29.c.65.57. |
| —"— | 3-8-18 | | At 11 am, enemy trench was subjected to heavy bombardment at N21d.70.50. Observation carried out from COLE and HAWK O.Ps. Shoot reported very successful, many rounds observed to burst in or near trench. A total of 200 rounds were expended. A further shoot on enemy trench at N34b.50.70 was carried out between 5.15 pm and 7.55 pm, in which 125 rounds were fired. Enemy roads at N35b.78.98 and N22d.50.00 were subjected to 65 rounds harassing fire during the night. |
| —"— | 4-8-18 | | A bombardment of SALLUMINES TRENCH at N34b.65.10 to N34d.55.80 was carried out with ground observation. Excellent results were obtained, many OKs being reported and a fire started in trench; a total of 200 rounds were expended. A further shoot of 140 rounds was carried out on enemy trench at N27c.85.20 to 65.35, with balloon observation. Observer reports trench badly damaged. No night firing. |
| —"— | 5-8-18 | | No tasks during the day. Night firing and harassing fire on enemy roads, railway Jct. at N29c.02.58, N34b.76.98, and N35a.35.40, 95 rounds being expended. |

## Notes

- **Date coverage:** Entries exist for every day, 1–31 August 1918. **No date gaps.**
- **Casualties:** 15 August — No. 1257628 Gnr. McLean, L.W. (Despatch Rider), wounded when enemy bombing planes bombed SOUCHEZ CORNER during the night. 29 August — enemy shelled billets in LIEVIN heavily with gas and H.E. during the night of 28 August; "No damage or casualties to our personnel." 27 August — enemy movement "seen and dispersed twice during the day" (enemy casualties, number not given). No battery fatalities are recorded this month.
- **Move:** 18 August — the battery (personnel of 4 guns, headquarters, etc.) moved forward to LIEVIN, relieving No. 162 Siege Battery R.G.A.; the section remaining near SOUCHEZ became the rear position. From 19 August the diary is headed LIEVIN, with the rear section's shoots reported separately in most entries.
- **26 August:** Both sections carried out heavy counter-preparation (3–6 am, 511 rounds) in support of the 17th Corps offensive, under operation order C.P/6 — the heaviest single day's firing in the month.
- **Places and map references:** Near SOUCHEZ (S.9.d.04.59); LIEVIN (M29c.06.62); SALLUMINES TRENCH; LOISON STATION; Fosse 21; Fosse ST LOUIS; Railway Cutting at N28c.50.70; SOUCHEZ CORNER; COLE and HAWK O.Ps; No. 10 Balloon; and numerous trench-map references (N, O and U sheets).
- **Oddities:** Contents page states "Diary Text 8 Pages" — the diary text is indeed 8 pages (pp. 3–10 of this month's file). The 25 August entry runs over two pages. The 28 August entry says "Five hostile batteries" but gives only two map references (N.23a.10. and N.11d.54.33). The 31 August entry's first map references are unclear as written. The 21 August entry's final line is partly obscured by the printed form footer. The 7 August entry's second house reference (N20a.3.0) appears incomplete. The Place heading on p. 6 is partly obscured by an ink blot. The commanding officer's initials change from the previous month's J. A. Stewart to W. J. Stewart (initials "W.J.S." in the Remarks column throughout).
