- **Pages transcribed:** 9 (diary pages 1–9; LAC images 124–132)

Transcription conventions: original spelling and punctuation kept as far as readable. Anything illegible or uncertain is marked [uncertain]. No casualties are named anywhere in this month's diary.

## Page 3 (July 1918, p. 3) — [diary text, Army Form C. 2118]

WAR DIARY or INTELLIGENCE SUMMARY. (Erase heading not required.) Unit heading at top of page: "9th Can. Siege Battery".

| Place | Date | Hour | Summary of Events and Information |
|---|---|---|---|
| PETIT-VIMY | 1-7-18 | | Hostile battery located at U15c 65.18 was engaged during the day, with aeroplane observation. Shoot reported as successful. A total of 235 rounds were fired. Harassing fire programme carried out during the night on enemy roads at U15c, U8a, and N30c and H.B. at U15c.65.18, 70 rounds being fired. Rear Section inactive throughout the 24 hours. Hostile artillery activity — NIL. |
| —"— | 2-7-18 | | At 11am hostile battery at O.25c 23.60 [uncertain] was subjected to 20 rounds neutralizing fire. Battery reported silenced. During the night harassing fire was maintained on enemy positions at O.25c 30.25, and U16 70.95, in which 60 rounds were fired. Rear Section carried out calibration on ACHEVILLE CHURCH, and fired 50 rounds at enemy dump at N29b 13.53. An enemy 5.9 battery attempted to range on No.4 gun (S. 2nd) [uncertain] with air burst, which was apparently given up as unsuccessful. |
| —"— | 3-7-18 | | No targets throughout the day. Harassing fire maintained during the night on various enemy positions, roads etc. Rear Section inactive. Hostile artillery activity — NIL. |
| —"— | 4-7-18 | | An aeroplane shoot was attempted, but had to be abandoned owing to the plane failing to pick up battery ground strip. A hostile battery was engaged for neutralization during the afternoon. Harassing fire during the night, enemy roads and positions being subjected to short bursts. Rear Section inactive. Hostile artillery shelled T.25a with [uncertain] without effect. S. 2nd (Nos 2 + 3) guns also received considerable attention. |

## Notes and oddities

- No appendices are included with this month's diary; the contents page lists only "Diary Text 7 Pages" (diary pages 3–9).
- The 13 July entry is split across pages 5–6, and the 22 July entry is split across pages 7–8.
- The 22 July entry ends mid-sentence on page 8 with a trailing comma ("…on enemy positions at N21a, N15c, N16c, + N27b,") and does not continue; the next entry is 23 July.
- The 24 July entry reads, as written, "a hostile battery was at N30c, was engaged" — the wording is awkward in the original and has been kept as written.
- The battery moved from Petit-Vimy to near Souchez on the night of 5–6 July, relieving the 7th Canadian Siege Battery; a section was temporarily withdrawn to S.19 D near the Lens–Arras Road for instructional work on 12 July and reported back in action on 13 July.
- No casualties are named in this month. No personnel are named other than Major W. G. Scully (13 July) and the 7th Canadian Siege Battery (5 July).
