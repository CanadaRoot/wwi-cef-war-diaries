## Page 3 (January 1918, p. 3) — [diary text, Army Form C. 2118]

WAR DIARY or INTELLIGENCE SUMMARY. (Erase heading not required.)

| Place | Date | Hour | Summary of Events and Information |
|---|---|---|---|
| VILLERS-AU-BOIS. | 1-1-18 } 4-1-18 } | | Battery, in training |
| " | 5-1-18 | | The battery parades for inspection by General Sir H. S. HORNE, K.C.B. K.C.M.G., Commanding 1st Army. All Siege Batteries in training at VILLERS-AU-BOIS were present at this inspection. |
| " | 6-1-18 } 10-1-18 } | | Training period completed. |
| . | 11-1-18 | | Battery moves into position, relieving No. 6. Can. Siege Battery. 2 guns in ANGRES. 4 guns in PETIT. VIMY. Billets occupied at THELUS. |
| THELUS. (Billets). | 12-1-18 | | Gun positions are now located as under:- Angre - 2 guns. Map location M 33. b. central. PETIT VIMY. 4 guns. " " No.1. T25.C.25.40. No.2 S 24. d 70.25. No.3. S.24.d.20.10. No.4. S.24 d 45.45. Work of [one word heavily blacked out — illegible] battery now wholly Counter-Battery. An aeroplane shoot was attempted, but after 10 rounds, given up through engine trouble of plane. Four N.F. calls were responded to on targets OY36. OY28. OY29, NX63. a total of 100 rounds being fired. The Section at ANGRES fired 25 rounds on target H8 NX63. Neutralizing fire. Enemy artillery fairly quiet. |

## Page 4 (January 1918, p. 4) — [diary text, Army Form C. 2118]

| Place | Date | Hour | Summary of Events and Information |
|---|---|---|---|
| THELUS (BILLETS). | 13-1-18 | | One S.O.S. call was responded to, only 4 rounds being fired. Several shoots of neutralizing fire were carried out on Hostile Batteries, about 130 rounds being expended. Harassing fire on N29C 90.70 to 30.90 was maintained from 2 p.m. to 5 p.m. 20 rounds fired. Battery position and vicinity was shelled from 12 to 4 p.m. No damage caused and no casualties. |
| —"— | 14-1-18 | | Neutralizing fire was carried out on area O25 d.82.16, & N27 b 39 28. a total of 75 rounds being fired. Enemy activity below normal. Section at Angres, guns now silent. |
| —"— | 15-1-18 | | Harassing fire on triangle cutting N35. C 4.d. and T.5.a.b. 20 rounds only. No other tasks. Hostile artillery very quiet. |
| —"— | 16-1-18 | | One hostile Battery, NZ 86 located at N22 b.70 65 was engaged with neutralizing fire, 50 rounds. Enemy artillery maintained harassing from 10 a.m. to 5 p.m. on S.30.a.b. position of 1st. Can. Hvy. Bty. |
| —"— | 17-1-18 | | We maintained harassing fire on Rly embankment N 22.d.50. to N.16 b.90. between the hours of 2 to 6 p.m. Visibility has been very poor during the past few days. |
| —"— | 18-1-18 | | Hostile battery at N.16 d.85.15 subjected to neutralizing fire, a total of 20 rounds being fired. No other tasks. Enemy artillery quiet. |
| —"— | 19-1-18 | | An aeroplane shoot was attempted at 10.35 a.m. on H.B. OY.30. location O.26 c 66.64. After 9 rounds were fired, shoot given up (defective signals). At 2.56 p.m. Aeroplane shoot resumed successfully. Battery fired 200 rounds. 2 Hostile Batteries were neutralized during the day, 25 rounds being fired on each. Visibility fairly good. Enemy artillery quiet. |

## Notes

- **Date coverage:** Entries exist for every day 1–31 January 1918. The 1–4 January and 6–10 January entries are bracketed together ("Battery, in training" / "Training period completed"). No date gaps in the month.
- **Casualties:** None named or reported this month. 13 January — the battery position and vicinity were shelled 12 to 4 p.m., "No damage caused and no casualties."
- **Honours:** 25 January — Daily Order Part II No. 3 (25 January) publishes, under "Honours and awards", that No. 1257525 B.S.M. Stevenson was "Mentioned in Despatches 7.11.17. London Gazette No 30448. Supplement 28-12-17."
- **Places and map references:** VILLERS-AU-BOIS (training); THELUS (billets); ANGRES (2 guns, M 33. b. central); PETIT VIMY (4 guns, No. 1 T25.C.25.40, No. 2 S 24. d 70.25, No. 3 S.24.d.20.10, No. 4 S.24 d 45.45); NOYELLES ROAD; MAUP. VILLAS. T.6.a; Rly embankment N 22.d.50. to N.16 b.90.; triangle cutting N35. C 4.d. and T.5.a.b.; and numerous hostile-battery and trench-map references (OY36, OY28, OY29, NX63, H8 NX63, N29C 90.70 to 30.90, O25 d.82.16, N27 b 39 28, NZ 86 at N22 b.70 65 and N 22 b.76 63, S.30.a.b. (1st Can. Hvy. Bty. position), N.16 d.85.15, H.B. OY.30 at O.26 c 66.64, OY 37 at O 32. a. 08 22, NZ16 at N22. b.69.03, NZ21 at N30 b.77.66, OY.11 at O.20.c.07.14, NZ.35 at N22 b.90.28, H.B. OY.18 at O.25.d.63.06, T.M. at T.R.a 93.31).
- **Oddities:** Cover is stamped CONFIDENTIAL and marked "VOLUME 11" with a pencilled "4" beneath. Contents page states "Diary Text 4 Pages" — the diary text is indeed 4 pages (pp. 3–6 of this month's file). The 12 January entry has one word heavily blacked out before "battery" (apparently a later censoring of a battery designation). The battery was out of action for 24 hours on 21 January while cracked concrete gun-pit roofs were repaired. The 30 January entry records the battery's first "Sound Ranging" shoot, reported as very successful. The 19 January aeroplane shoot was first abandoned after 9 rounds (defective signals) and then resumed successfully the same afternoon. The printed form footer on the diary pages reads "A3934 Wt. W4973/M687 750,000 8/16 D. D. & L. Ltd. Forms/C.2118/13."
