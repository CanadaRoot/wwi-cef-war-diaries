# War Diary — 9th Canadian Siege Battery, February 1918

- **Unit:** 9th Canadian Siege Battery
- **LAC Item ID:** 2004802
- **Reference:** RG 9 III-D-3, vol. 4977, file 576, reel T-10805
- **Month:** February 1918 (1st–28th February 1918)
- **Pages transcribed:** 84–89 (6 pages)
- **Volume:** 12
- **Commanding Officer / signatory:** Major J. Stewart, R.C.A., O.C. No. 9 Can. Siege By. R.C.G.A. (signed at the end of the month)
- **Date coverage:** 1–28 February 1918 — all 28 days have entries; no date gaps (1918 was not a leap year).

**Notes on the manuscript:** The diary is handwritten on Army Form C.2118 ("War Diary or Intelligence Summary"). The initials "JSt" appear in the Remarks column against several entries. The place column on pp. 5–6 spells the battery position "Cité de balonne" in the hand; it is transcribed here as Cité de Colonne, the spelling used on p. 3. "N.F." = neutralizing fire; "H.B." = hostile battery; "S.O.S." = S.O.S. call; "Rds" = rounds.

## Page 3 (February 1918, p. 3) — [diary, 1–8 February 1918]

WAR DIARY or INTELLIGENCE SUMMARY. Army Form C.2118.
[Printed instructions: "Instructions regarding War Diaries and Intelligence Summaries are contained in F. S. Regs., Part II. and the Staff Manual respectively. Title pages will be prepared in manuscript." "(Erase heading not required.)"]

| Place | Date | Summary of Events and Information |
|---|---|---|
| Thelus (Billets) | 1-2-18 | No tasks today hostile artillery activity nil. Visibility fair. |
| | 2-2-18 | an aeroplane shoot was successfully carried out on H.B. No N244 location N30b 639,23. 75 rds being fired. At 9.20 P.M. an S.O.S. call was responded to, only 10 rounds being fired. Enemy artillery quiet. |
| " | 3-2-18 | We handed the 4 guns in Petit Vimy and the 2 guns in Angres over to No 6 Can Siege B'ty. At night the Battery moved to Cité de Colonne relieving No 326 Siege B'ty R.G.A. Two guns in Cité de Colonne and two in Maroc — the locations being No1 M14d 69.89, No2 M14d 61.80; No3 M3c 99,95; No4 M3a 95.00. |
| Cité de Colonne | 4-2-18 | In response to a N.F. call, neutralizing fire was carried out on H.B. Nx60 located at N6c 59.94. 15 rds being fired. Enemy artillery inactive. |
| " | 5-2-18 | A task with aeroplane observation was attempted on H.B. Nx 90 located at N6a [29,94 crossed out] 92.07 — firing 55 rounds. Neutralization fire was carried out on H B64 location N17c 50,10 from 10 to 10.30 A.M. |
| " | 6-2-18 | 120 Rds were expended on neutralizing fire. The visibility was low and hostile artillery activity below normal. |
| " | 7-2-18 | Harassing fire was carried out on Carvin Road — 10 Rds being fired. |
| " | 8-2-18 | In response to N.F. calls, 89 rounds were fired during the day, principally on H.B. No Nx18 location N16d. 89,15 and H.B. No Nx64 located at N17c 43,26. Enemy artillery quiet. |

## Page 4 (February 1918, p. 4) — [diary, 9–15 February 1918]

WAR DIARY or INTELLIGENCE SUMMARY. Army Form C.2118.

| Place | Date | Summary of Events and Information |
|---|---|---|
| Cité de Colonne | 9-2-18 | Two calibration shoots were carried out today, one on Water Tower location H11c 85,05, the other on Puits No 2 located at M10b 85,20 — 21 Rds being fired on each. The latter proved to be very successful. Neutralizing fire was carried out on H.B. No. Nx69 location N11c 75,22 — 25 rds being expended. |
| " | 10-2-18 | 5 Rds were expended on hostile movement at H11c, the effect being observed by Oxford O.P. 20 Rds harassing fire was carried out on Carvin Road, from 4 P.M. to 6 P.M. Visibility low. |
| " | 11-2-18 | a wire cutting shoot was carried out on N26 65,10 to N26 63,70 but the result was unsatisfactory on account of the high wind. 158 rds were expended. M14d was shelled by the enemy with 4.2". |
| | 12-2-18 | Harassing fire was carried out on Road, running through N11 and 12 and neutralizing fire on H.B. No H28 located at H36c 88,11. 45 rds being fired. Hostile artillery inactive. |
| | 13-2-18 | 20 rds were expended on harassing fire on Carvin Road, and 25 rds on neutralization of H.286 located at N22b 76,63 [uncertain]. Enemy artillery activity and visibility below normal. |
| | 14-2-18 | During the day we fired on two H.Bs No Nx18 and Nx64 located at N16d 89,45 and N17c 43,26. 35 Rds being fired. Enemy artillery activity nil. |
| " | 15-2-18 | Harassing fire was carried out on Bridge location N11 33,32 [uncertain] and on Purdy Road located at N5 and N6. Neutralization fire was carried out on H.B. located at H29d 45,49. Total Rds fired during the day 35, [uncertain — line overwritten and illegible]. Visibility was low and Enemy artillery activity nil. At 1.34 A.M. an S.O.S. was responded to, 14 rds being fired. |

## Page 6 (February 1918, p. 6) — [diary, 25–28 February 1918]

WAR DIARY or INTELLIGENCE SUMMARY. Army Form C.2118.

| Place | Date | Summary of Events and Information |
|---|---|---|
| Cité de Colonne | 25-2-18 | Tasks consisted only of neutralizing fire on hostile batteries, a few rounds being fired. At about 5.30 pm we were relieved by No. 326 Battery R.G.A and moved to billets in THELUS. Gun positions being in PETIT VIMY. |
| PETIT VIMY. | 26-2-18 | At 4.35 am we started an aeroplane shoot on H.B. located at O32a 54.47. 100 rounds were fired and shoot reported as successful. Targets in T.5.c. 17.68 [uncertain] were very successfully engaged. Direct hits being observed. 156 rounds fired. Harassing fire and neutralizing fire were carried on through the night. |
| | 27-2-18 | 20 rounds for neutralization of hostile batteries was carried out at 9.50 am. and targets located at T.5.a. 16.36 were successfully engaged. Target reported demolished. Captain H.R.N. Cobbett is posted to No. 2 Canadian Siege Battery as Officer Commanding. |
| | 28-2-18 | No shoots of importance today, only 20 rounds neutralising fire on hostile batteries at O.25.c 22.62 + O.33.a. 49.72. Captain H.R.N. Cobbett struck off strength today. |

[Signed:] J. Stewart, MAJOR R.C.A., O.C. No. 9 CAN. SIEGE BY. R.C.G.A.
