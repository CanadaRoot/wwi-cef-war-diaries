# War Diary Transcript — 9th Canadian Siege Battery, April 1917

- **Unit:** No. 9 Canadian Siege Battery
- **LAC Item ID:** 2004802, RG 9 III-D-3, vol. 4977, file 576, reel T-10805
- **Month / Volume:** April 1917, Volume 2 (covers 1 – 30 April 1917)
- **Pages in this file:** LAC images pp. 1–9 (9 pages: cover, contents, 7 diary text pages)
- **Diary text date range:** 1 – 30 April 1917
- **Commanding officer / signatory:** No signature block closes this volume. Captain COBBETT is named in the text as commanding the Advance Party (31 March, carried over) and the battery's guns are handed over to No. 3 Canadian Siege Battery on 17–18 April. The entries of 19, 20 and 22 April are initialled "[uncertain] Am." (see Notes).
- **Transcription note:** The diary is handwritten on Army Form C. 2118 ("War Diary or Intelligence Summary"). The printed heading "INTELLIGENCE SUMMARY" is struck through on the diary pages. The handwriting changes during the month: the entries of 1–18 April are in a flowing cursive hand; the entries of 19–23 April are in a neater hand (in pencil on pp. 7–8); the entries of 24–30 April are in a similar neat hand. The "Hour" column is blank throughout. The Remarks column bears the same repeated mark on every entry, read here as "J101" (see Notes).

## Page 3 (April 1917, p. 3) — [diary text]

WAR DIARY or INTELLIGENCE SUMMARY. (Erase heading not required.)

### HAVRE — Ap. 1st

Got orders at midday to move at 9 p.m. — entraining for HOUDAIN.

[Remarks: J101]

### HOUDAIN — Ap. 3rd

Detrain at 9 a.m. in blizzard — R.T.O. could not tell us where to go & Advance Party only arrived a few hours before we did — Send Officer for instructions & at 5 p.m. Got billets at HOUDAIN. All stores dismounted from M.T. by order so that they can carry ammⁿ.

[Remarks: J101]

### HOUDAIN — Ap. 4th

See Group Commander & then position pointed out — Guns sent to workshop & got stuck on road — [uncertain] we can [uncertain] take in position & ready to open fire by day after tomorrow — Battery moves to St. ELOY at midnight by M.T. taking all night to get there — Start carrying stores for dugouts — Erect camouflage over position of guns & place platforms ready for moving guns in at night.

[Remarks: J101]

### MOUNT ST ELOY — Ap. 5th

Stores & billets & gun positions widely detached making much work & taking much time — Guns & stores arrive night of 5/6 [uncertain] men lost all night.

[Remarks: J101]

### MOUNT ST ELOY — Ap. 6th

men dead beat as one relief could not manage Guns last night. [uncertain] did not [uncertain] in consequence till noon for Guns & ammⁿ.

[Remarks: J101]

## Page 4 (April 1917, p. 4) — [diary text]

### MOUNT ST ELOY — Ap. 7th

Register in aftn — daylight poor & results not satisfactory, with low Observation from Major [uncertain] O.P. with his Officers. Continue registering. 2.45 p.m. to 5.45 p.m. on THELUS MILL 23 rounds — unsatisfactory — 31 rounds on trench junction Nos 1 & 2 — Guns registered, No. 3 partly registered — Battery very exposed — no time yet for constructing proper dugouts.

[Remarks: J101]

### MOUNT ST ELOY — Ap. 8th

Bombardment of BATTER TRENCH at 1.50 a.m. to 5.25 a.m. — S15d 2030 to 30.10, 200 rounds — Satisfactory result. No. 4 Gun registered. The following has been received from O.C. 1st Can. Heavy Arty. Group: — 'I wish to add my hearty congratulations on the results of the strenuous work of your Officers & men during the past few days and nights. It is a satisfaction to know that your efforts have put the Battery in Action in time to take part in the forthcoming operations.'

[Remarks: J101]

### MOUNT ST ELOY — Ap. 9th

The attack on VIMY begins — According to instruction the Battery opened fire at 5.30 a.m. & engaged S22b 05.60. with Left Section and S22b 45.25. After first round No. 4 Gun went out of action owing to lack of pressure in recuperator — All recuperators were pumped up late last night. No. 3 Gun very shortly afterwards also went out of action temporarily for same reason — At plus 17 Battery switched to S23a 25.80. with Left Sec: S16d 70.25 with Right Sec: No. 3 Gun was soon in action again. At plus 75 Battery switched to S17c 70.70. with Left Sec: and 18a 1.2. with Right Sec:

[Remarks: J101]

[This entry continues on the next page.]

## Page 5 (April 1917, p. 5) — [diary text]

### MOUNT ST ELOY — Ap. 9th (continued)

The trails were digging in quickly. At 3 hrs 19 min the Guns were ordered to switch to A6c 4.4 — A2a 3.9. At 301 & plus 416 [uncertain] then Guns of Battery engaged B1d 4½ to 2.4 and carried on until ordered at about 12.15 p.m. to engage [uncertain] Trench — All Guns now in action — About 2.30 p.m. the Guns were switched on to Banff Trench at S16d 00.20 and kept on until 8.50 p.m. — During this last action 2 Guns were always in action — sometimes four. The Guns were carefully examined & the washers in the recuperator found to be too thin causing a leak in the valve. Prisoners in batches of 50 & 100 began coming in past the Battery at 9.30 a.m. — The battle appears to be almost a complete success, only on the right the 4th Divⁿ is held at by Hill 145.

[Remarks: J101]

### MOUNT ST ELOY — Ap. 10th

All Guns out of action — Armament Artificer from workshops sent for — All objectives of Canadian Corps have been gained & Counter Attack repulsed.

[Remarks: J101]

### MOUNT ST ELOY — Ap. 11th

I.O.M. says the fault in the Guns is one of manufacture (Cranks.) & all Guns must go to the workshops

[Remarks: J101]

[This entry continues on the next page.]

## Page 6 (April 1917, p. 6) — [diary text]

### MOUNT ST ELOY — Ap. 11th (continued)

Guns come back from workshops & were in action again at 3 p.m. Fired 14 rounds at [uncertain] between 3.12 P.M. and 3.35 P.M. Result unsatisfactory as guns were out of range.

[Remarks: J101]

### MOUNT ST ELOY — Ap. 15th & Ap. 16th (bracketed together)

Guns out of range.

[Remarks: J101]

### MOUNT ST ELOY — Ap. 17th

An order has been received to hand over guns to No. 3 CANADIAN SIEGE BATTERY at once. Three have gone.

[Remarks: J101]

### MOUNT ST ELOY — Ap. 18th

Remaining gun has gone to No. 3 CAN. Siege Battery.

[Remarks: J101]

### MOUNT ST ELOY — Ap. 19th

Our guns having now all been handed over we are in the reserve. Spent day in putting up better shelters for the men, improving the telephonists' dug-out etc. T. Am. [uncertain]

[Remarks: J101]

### MOUNT ST ELOY — Ap. 20th

One officer & 20 men on ammunition fatigue at TERRITORIAL DUMP. Remainder of battery spent day on improving the living quarters. T. Am. [uncertain]

[Remarks: J101]

## Page 7 (April 1917, p. 7) — [diary text]

### Mount St Eloy — 21st

Battery on ammunition fatigues to-day in accordance with C.C.H.A. instructions. Party of one N.C.O. and 20 men at 4.15 P.M. to help 5th C.S.B. to new position.

[Remarks: J101]

### MOUNT ST. ELOY — 22-4-17

Party of 20 men & 1 officer spent day shifting ammunition at Territorial Dump, on the BETHUNE-ARRAS road, about two miles from our position. Another party doing fatigues for 5th Canadian Siege Battery close by. Our two artificers have gone to No 3 medium workshop at OHLAIN to do work on our new guns. T. Am. [uncertain]

[Remarks: J101]

### MOUNT ST. ELOY — 23-4-17

Party of 12 men went to No 3 Workshop OHLAIN to pump up recuperators of our new guns.

[Remarks: J101]

## Page 8 (April 1917, p. 8) — [diary text]

### Mont St Eloy — 24/4/17

Battery still in reserve. Lt. THACKRAY sent to No. 3 Can S.B. for duty. Lt. STARR sent to 72 S.A.H.A. Battery for duty.

[Remarks: J101]

### Mont St Eloy — 25/4/17

Rt Section moved up and took over guns & stores from No. 3 C.S.B. We now have our own equipment back again. Our position is now on ARRAS-BETHUNE Rd.

[Remarks: J101]

### ARRAS BETHUNE ROAD — 26/4/17

Lt. Section moved up to New position on ARRAS-BETHUNE Rd. at 8 P.M. Left Section moved forward again to a position in Cité de Gaumont.

[Remarks: J101]

### ARRAS BETHUNE ROAD — 27/4/17 & 28/4/17 (bracketed together)

Battery is now divided into two sections. Left section are in Cité de Gaumont under Lt. MACKENZIE. Rt. section on the ARRAS-BETHUNE RD.

[Remarks: J101]

### ARRAS BETHUNE ROAD — 29/4/17

Rt section moved up to Cité de GAUMONT.

[Remarks: J101]

## Page 9 (April 1917, p. 9) — [diary text]

### Cité de Gaumont. M33a33 Sheet 36c SW — Ap 30th.

Right Section completed placing guns in position. From 7.30 AM to 8.45 AM the battery was heavily shelled by enemy's 5.9" Hows. The battery retired to the excellent German dugouts which afforded good cover. Only one man wounded. Right Section registered. In the evening the battery was again heavily shelled, one gun being temporarily put out of action.

[Remarks: J101]

## Transcriber's notes — April 1917

- **Date gaps (days with no entry):** 2 April; 12, 13 and 14 April. (15 and 16 April are covered by a single bracketed entry, "Guns out of range.")
- **Entries spanning pages:** the 9 April entry runs from p. 4 onto p. 5; the 11 April entry runs from p. 5 onto p. 6.
- **"J101":** the Remarks column on every entry bears the same repeated mark, read here as "J101". Its meaning is unclear — it may be an initial, a file reference, or an appendix code.
- **"T. Am.":** the entries of 19, 20 and 22 April each end with an initialled signature that appears to read "T. Am." — the initial before "Am." is unclear, so the signature is flagged [uncertain]. It is a different hand from the "H. R. C." (H. R. Cobbett) signatures that appear in the May volume.
- **7 April entry:** the word "Observation" is crossed out in the first line, with "daylight" written above it: "Register in aftn — daylight poor & results not satisfactory". The name of the Major whose O.P. is mentioned is unclear and is flagged [uncertain].
- **9 April entry:** the target references are given in the diary's own abbreviated form (e.g. "S22b 05.60.", "S23a 25.80.", "S16d 70.25", "18a 1.2."). The reference written as "18a 1.2." may be incomplete (a letter prefix may be missing). The times written as "301" and "416" in "At 301 & plus 416" are reproduced as written; they may be clock times written without punctuation. The sentence "only on the right the 4th Divⁿ is held at by Hill 145" is reproduced as written.
- **11 April entry:** no target is given for the 14 rounds fired between 3.12 P.M. and 3.35 P.M. — a blank is left in the diary where the target would be, flagged [uncertain].
- **"ammⁿ":** abbreviation of "ammunition", written with a superscript "n". "Divⁿ" is written with a superscript "n" (Division).
- **Casualties:** 30 April — one man wounded (unnamed) when the battery was shelled by enemy 5.9" howitzers; one gun temporarily put out of action. No one is named as a casualty in April.
- **People named:** Captain COBBETT; Lieut MACKEEN; O.C. 1st Can. Heavy Arty. Group (unnamed, quoted); Lt. THACKRAY; Lt. STARR; Lt. MACKENZIE.
- **Places:** Havre, Houdain, Mount St Eloy, Thelus Mill, Vimy, Batter Trench, Banff Trench, Territorial Dump (Bethune–Arras road), Ohlain, Arras–Bethune Road, Cité de Gaumont (map reference M33a33, Sheet 36c SW).
