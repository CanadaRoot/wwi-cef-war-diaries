## Page 3 (March 1918, p. 3) — [diary text]

[Army Form C. 2118. WAR DIARY or INTELLIGENCE SUMMARY. (Erase heading not required.) Place: PETIT-VIMY.

**1-3-18.** An enemy position in House located at T.5c 95.60 was successfully engaged. A total of 45 rounds were fired, and the house was reported demolished by F.O.O. Calibration was attempted but was unsuccessful. Neutralizing fire was placed on hostile batteries located at O.25.d.70.43 and O.32.a 54.07. Twenty rounds being fired. LIEUT. A.T. SEAMAN posted to unit vice CAPT. H.R.N. COBBETT. to. No. 2. C.S.B. Much work carried out in building of alternative defensive positions, in preparation of an anticipated enemy offensive.

**2-3-18.** Harassing fire on enemy roads N.23b and d. and N.29a.6.5. No other targets. Rear position completed. Location A.5a.43.11. (ARRAS-VIMY ROAD). Work continued on defensive positions — ARRAS-BETHUNE ROAD and CARENCY. Attitude of enemy quiet.

**3-3-18.** Ten rounds neutralizing fire on hostile battery, location U.2.c 30.30, and twenty rounds harassing fire on enemy dugouts, at N.35.c.20.6 [uncertain] . Work on defensive positions making good progress.

**4-3-18.** Neutralizing fire on hostile batteries carried out in the early morning, 30 rounds being fired. At 6.06 am. S.O.S (AVION) was responded to, and at 6.23 am S.O.S (LENS) also. 59 rounds being fired in all. Neutralizing and harassing fire was maintained during the evening. Two howitzers arrive from [uncertain] O.O. CALAIS, and put into our Rear position. Battery now complete as 6 gun unit.

**5-3-18.** A successful shoot on enemy target at T.11a.90.50 of 50 rounds was completed. F.O.O. reports many rounds on target. An aeroplane shoot on H.B. located at N.16.b.98.05 was successful, 150 rounds being fired. 26 rounds were fired on H.B. in support of raid by 5th C.M.R. Work on alternative positions progressing satisfactorily.

## Page 4 (March 1918, p. 4) — [diary text]

[Army Form C. 2118. WAR DIARY or INTELLIGENCE SUMMARY. Same printed heading and instructions as p. 3.]

Place: PETIT-VIMY.

**6-3-18.** At 9.30 am an aeroplane shoot on hostile battery located at O.25.c.23.62 was commenced but had to discontinue after 16 rounds, due to fog. Shoot recommenced at 1.5 pm, and brought to successful conclusion, 10 rounds neutralizing fire was put on H.B. at O.20.c.17.11 at 5.00 pm. No other targets. Rear position inactive. Enemy artillery very quiet.

**7-3-18.** Neutralization of hostile batteries carried out during morning, twenty rounds expended. At 2.15 pm, a shoot with front line observation on enemy strong point located at T.19.c.65.65 was reported as successful, several direct hits being observed. Harassing fire on enemy roads, bridges etc during the night. Work on alternative positions proceeding satisfactorily.

**8-3-18.** A successful shoot with aeroplane observation was carried out on hostile battery located at N.17a.02.46, a total of 100 rounds being fired. Calibration of guns successful. Target, Railway Cottage located at U.1.c.66.07, 48 rounds expended. Our rear position registered today on the same target, firing 29 rounds.

**9-3-18.** Four N.F. calls were responded to during the morning. At 2.55 pm we carried out an aeroplane shoot of 100 rounds on hostile battery located at U.1.a 56.97, which was reported as successful. Work on alternative positions in THELUS completed, and progressing satisfactorily on ARRAS-BETHUNE Road. During the night, the enemy fired a few gas shells into and near the battery position.

**10-3-18.** Another successful aeroplane shoot of 100 rounds carried out on H.B at N.29.a.20.53. This shoot was favourably commented on by O.C. 1st Bde. C.G.A. Further shoots on enemy wire were carried out with ground observation, 70 rounds being fired. Usual programme of harassing fire maintained. No shooting from rear position.

## Page 5 (March 1918, p. 5) — [diary text]

[Army Form C. 2118. WAR DIARY or INTELLIGENCE SUMMARY. Same printed heading and instructions as p. 3.]

Place: PETIT-VIMY.

**11-3-18.** A shoot of 200 rounds with aeroplane observation was carried out successfully on hostile battery, located at O.26.c.66.57. Two successful shoots with ground observation on enemy position and wire, several direct hits being observed; 50 rounds were fired. The enemy scattered H.E + gas and shrapnel in the vicinity of battery position. No damage and no casualties.

**12-3-18.** Aeroplane shoot on hostile battery O.25 b.01.60, 200 rounds being fired. Neutralizing fire on other H.B.'s during the day. At 3.30 pm the enemy heavily shelled T.25a in vicinity of No.1 gun with 5.9's about 250 rounds being fired. Rear position completes registration, firing 26 rounds on Railway Cottage.

**13-3-18.** A sound ranging shoot was attempted, and after 22 rounds had been fired, hostile battery was quieted. An aeroplane shoot commenced at 2.45 pm, and 70 rounds were fired. This shoot was not successful, the ranging being very unsatisfactory. Calibration and registration of No.1 gun completed. Harassing fire on gas projectors located at N.3e.d.40.00 was maintained. 60 rounds fired.

**14-3-18.** Enemy machine gun position was engaged with good results, 35 rounds being fired. Neutralizing fire was carried out throughout the night. No other tasks. Enemy shelled T.13.19 [uncertain] with H.E + gas intermittently.

**15-3-18.** Hostile battery O.32.a.71.80 was engaged with aeroplane observation, shoot reported successful. 200 rounds fired. Neutralizing fire on H.B. located at U.3.c.2.7, 10 rounds. Work on alternative position at ARRAS-BETHUNE Road nearing completion. Rear position inactive.

**16-3-18.** Calibration with Cordite carried out successfully, 34 rounds being fired. Aeroplane shoot on hostile battery located at O.32.a 85.26, reported satisfactory, 200 rds fired. At 8.00 pm S.O.S. (LENS) was responded to, [uncertain — a line of the entry is overwritten by the printed form footer and is illegible; the visible words are "...only ... rds were fired."] Attitude of enemy quiet, generally.

## Page 6 (March 1918, p. 6) — [diary text]

[Army Form C. 2118. WAR DIARY or INTELLIGENCE SUMMARY. Same printed heading and instructions as p. 3.]

Place: PETIT-VIMY.

**17-3-18.** A further shoot of 200 rounds was carried out on hostile battery located at O.32.a 85.26. with aeroplane observation, reported as successful. Four batteries were neutralized in the evening, a total of 67 rounds being fired. Rear position inactive. ARRAS-BETHUNE road position completed.

**18-3-18.** Enemy O.P. and dugouts were bombarded and several hits were reported, 45 rounds being fired. An aeroplane shoot on hostile battery located at U.1.a.56.97, was completed, 200 rds fired. Reported successful. Enemy fired few rounds of 4.1 into area T.25.d. Work commenced on alternative position in CARENCY.

**19-3-18.** Neutralizing and harassing fire was placed on enemy dug-outs and T.M.'s, 27 rounds being fired. No other targets. Work on position in CARENCY, progressing satisfactory. Enemy artillery quiet.

**20-3-18.** Only 10 rounds were fired today, target being located at T.18.a 2.99 [uncertain]. Ten Rds neutralizing fire at night on H.B at U.3.c.25.70. Enemy fired few H.E. and gas shell into position and vicinity.

**21-3-18.** Registration carried out successfully, 14 rounds. An aeroplane shoot on hostile battery located at U.2.c.20.52, was completed, a total of 200 rounds being fired. CARENCY position completed.

**22-3-18.** Hostile battery located at N.18.c.03.28 was engaged with aeroplane observation, a total of 48 rounds being fired. Shoot reported as successful. Neutralizing and harassing fire on H.B's during the day. Enemy fired a few gas shells into battery position and vicinity.

**23-3-18.** A shoot with sound ranging was carried out on hostile battery N.17a.35.27, a total of 30 rounds being fired. An aeroplane shoot of 200 rounds on H.B. D.32.c.62.74 was completed. Reported as successful. Numerous N.F. calls were responded to during the night, and harassing fire maintained on enemy roads, bridges etc. Enemy activity—Nil.

## Page 7 (March 1918, p. 7) — [diary text]

[Army Form C. 2118. WAR DIARY or INTELLIGENCE SUMMARY. Same printed heading and instructions as p. 3.]

Place: PETIT-VIMY.

**24-3-18.** Two S.O.S calls were responded to. One at 5.25. MERICOURT. The other at 5.40 am. AVION. 34 rounds being expended. Neutralizing fire on enemy trench mortars during the early morning. At 12.15 pm we carried out aeroplane shoot on hostile battery U.8.t.84.68 [uncertain]. and fired 200 rounds. Harassing fire was kept up during the night. 50 rounds being fired. Enemy artillery quiet.

**25-3-18.** At 4.30 am, 30 rounds rapid fire was placed on enemy trench mortars T.19a.6. [uncertain — coordinate appears incomplete in the original]. Four N.F. calls were replied to, on hostile batteries, and T.Ms. Concentration on a wood U.29e. [uncertain — coordinate appears incomplete in the original]. Commenced at 8 pm. A further concentration commenced on BOIS BERNARD in which the rear section again became active.

**26-3-18.** All guns of both positions concentrated on ROUVROY at [uncertain — an earlier time is crossed out and illegible] 1.30 am, and at 4.00 am concentrations were made on ACHEVILLE by both positions, which were maintained until 5.30 am. In all about 300 rounds were fired. Harassing fire maintained during the night on enemy approaches etc. The battery positions were shelled intermittently with enemy 5.9's.

**27-3-18.** Rear position carried out registration on RAILWAY COTTAGE, firing 28 rounds. Neutralizing fire on H.B. U.9.b 50.31. by forward position. Night firing on enemy roads was carried out, 35 rounds being fired. Enemy shelled road [uncertain] with shrapnel during the evening, without effect.

**28-3-18.** An S.O.S (OPPY) was quickly responded to at 6 am, in which we fired 41 rounds. N.F. calls were very frequent throughout the day. About 150 rounds being fired. At 1.05 pm a further S.O.S (OPPY) was answered, and 53 rounds fired. During the night, 50 rounds expended harassing fire on enemy roads etc. Our Rear section became active and opened up on the same S.O.S. firing 51 rounds. They also responded to N.F. calls during the night. Thelus (billet) was very heavily shelled throughout the day, as was VIMY + PETIT VIMY, and roads [entry ends here at the foot of the page; the sentence is unfinished and does not continue on p. 8, which begins with 29-3-18].

## Page 8 (March 1918, p. 8) — [diary text]

[Army Form C. 2118. WAR DIARY or INTELLIGENCE SUMMARY. Same printed heading and instructions as p. 3.]

Place: PETIT-VIMY.

**29-3-18.** A few rounds harassing fire was put on to enemy roads between 6.00 + 8.00 am. Two N.F. calls were answered and H.B.'s at U.2.c.05.99 and U.2.a.26.05 were engaged, a total of 40 rounds being fired. At 2.05 pm. S.O.S call was responded to, and fire was maintained on enemy front line trenches and communications at T.22 d. and 30 b. until 4.15 pm, 108 rounds were fired. Much harassing and neutralizing fire was kept up during the evening. The rear section fired concentrations on S.O.S. lines, bursts of fire, and answered N.F. calls. A total of 120 rounds being fired. Enemy activity maintained, the area in vicinity of battery position, and roads being intermittently shelled all day.

**30-3-18.** Many tasks carried out, concentrations and short bursts of fire being carried on all day. S.O.S (ACHEVILLE) was responded to, and 30 rounds were fired. Harassing fire was maintained during the night. The rear section also kept up harassing fire and neutralizing fire, expending 100 rounds. VIMY was subjected to heavy enemy shelling, 4.2's and 5.9's. Shrapnel and H.E, also some rounds of gas. No damage or casualties.

**31-3-18.** Many bursts of fire on S.O.S lines ACHEVILLE, 40 rounds being fired. At 4.35 pm a shot was commenced with aeroplane observation on hostile battery at U.15.c.65.12 [uncertain]. Shot completed, 200 rounds fired. Harassing fire on BOIS BERNARD maintained through the night. Many minor tasks, consisting of rapid bursts of fire carried out by rear section. Enemy artillery shelled T.25.d. heavily, and many rounds fell near No.1 gun. No damage caused.

[Signed] J. A. Stewart
MAJOR R.C.A.
O.C. No. 9 CAN. SIEGE BTY. R.G.C.A.

## Transcriber's notes

- Every day 1–31 March 1918 has an entry; there are no date gaps in this month.
- The diary is handwritten throughout on Army Form C. 2118; the Hour column is blank on all diary pages.
- Abbreviations used as written: H.B. = hostile battery; F.O.O. = Forward Observation Officer; S.O.S = SOS call; N.F. = (night firing / "N.F." calls, as written); T.M. = trench mortar; O.P. = observation post; C.M.R. = Canadian Mounted Rifles; C.G.A. = Canadian Garrison Artillery; R.G.C.A. = Royal Canadian Garrison Artillery.
- The 28 March entry ends mid-sentence at the foot of p. 7; the sentence is not completed on p. 8.
- The 16 March entry has a line overwritten by the printed form footer at the foot of the page and is partly illegible.
- No casualties are named in this month. Enemy shelling is repeatedly reported, but each time the diary records no damage and/or no casualties.
