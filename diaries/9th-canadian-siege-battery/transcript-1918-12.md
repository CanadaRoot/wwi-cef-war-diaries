## Notes

- **Date coverage:** The entire month, 1–31 December 1918, is covered by a single summary entry. **No daily entries exist for December 1918.**
- **Casualties:** None recorded.
- **Decorations:** None recorded.
- **Places:** Jemappes and Mons — the place column reads "JEMAPPES 7E2 MONS" (the middle line "7E2" is not clearly legible [uncertain]).
- **Oddities:** Contents page states "Diary Text 1 Page" — the diary text is indeed a single page (p. 3 of this month's file), and most of that page is blank below the one entry. The cover is handwritten in ink on lined paper, as in November, and reads "Volume 22". The diary is signed by Lieut M. A. Wilson "for Major, O.C." rather than by the Major himself.
