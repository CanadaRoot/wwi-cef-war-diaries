## Notes

- **Date coverage:** The whole month, 1–28 February 1919, is covered by a single block entry. There are no daily entries and no days with individual records — the month is summarised as one "Period of instruction".
- **Casualties:** None recorded.
- **Decorations:** None recorded.
- **Places:** Jemappes, Lez and Mons — the battery remains in the Mons area of Belgium. No map references are given.
- **How the diary ends:** This is the final month of the diary. The last entry runs 1–28 February 1919 and closes with the underlined line "No events of importance." There is no closing statement, no demobilisation note and no "diary closed" entry — the diary simply ends with the Captain's signature at the foot of this single page. The diary runs 6 February 1917 – 28 February 1919, in 24 volumes.
- **Oddities:** As in January, the whole month is a single diary-text page with no firing, movement or operational entries — training, drill, signalling, civil and educational classes, lectures, entertainments and games only. The cover is entirely handwritten. The Remarks and references to Appendices column carries initials that appear to read "JAS" against the entry (as in the 1917 volumes). The change of signatory continues: January was signed "for MAJOR" by a lieutenant, and February is signed by a Captain, with the printed "MAJOR R.C.A." in the stamp crossed out — suggesting the O.C. (Maj. J. A. Stewart, who signed the diary through 1917) is no longer in command by early 1919.
