## Page 3 (June 1917, p. 3) — [diary text]

WAR DIARY or INTELLIGENCE SUMMARY. (Erase heading not required.)

### Cité de Gaumont — 1-6-17

The battery has been engaged in wire cutting tasks throughout the day 106 E fuse being used. Results reported very good. Very quiet in the vicinity of the battery position. In the night harassing fire on cross roads was maintained

[Remarks: J101; S. Am. 84. [uncertain]]

### Cité de Gaumont — 2-6-17

The principal task today has again been wire cutting. A minor task on an enemy trench was also undertaken with good results. 80 rounds were fired.

[Remarks: J101]

### Cité de Gaumont — 3-6-17

Wire cutting tasks principally. Many other tasks on battery positions, trenches & cross roads, were undertaken during the day and night.

[Remarks: J101]

### Cité de Gaumont — 4-6-17

Wire cutting tasks. 238 rounds fired with successful results. 14 rounds were fired on enemy position without observation. 1st party, NCOs & men to REST CAMP.

[Remarks: J101]

### Cité de Gaumont — 5-6-17

Wire cutting tasks. One shoot on wire very successful, about 200 rounds being fired. Cross roads and enemy positions fired on with good effect.

[Remarks: J101]

### Cité de Gaumont — 6-6-17

Wire cutting tasks continued. During the day salvos fire was put on to cross roads and bridge. Harassing fire on Overland Route was carried out.

[Remarks: J101]

### Cité de Gaumont — 7-6-17

Wire cutting tasks were again carried out with good results. 300 rounds were fired into enemy trench. Cross Roads were also kept under fire for short periods

[Remarks: J101]

## Page 4 (June 1917, p. 4) — [diary text]

### Cité de Gaumont — 8-6-17

200 rounds were fired at wire cutting task. Very good results. A further 200 rounds were fired on a trench which was levelled. During the day and night a total of 900 rounds were fired by the battery. GENERAL MASSIE G.O.C., C.C.H.A visited the battery position.

[Remarks: J101]

### Cité de Gaumont — 9-6-17

The day has been one of extreme quiet. BOMBR. GEORGE, SIG. 4 was wounded while on duty at the Observation Post. 6 different shoots on enemy trenches were carried out. One of them in response to S.O.S. call.

[Remarks: J101]

### Cité de Gaumont — 10-6-17

Harassing fire was maintained throughout the day and night on various enemy positions, cross roads, embankments and streets. Enemy artillery very quiet.

[Remarks: J101; S. Am. 84. [uncertain]]

### Cité de Gaumont — 11-6-17

An opportunity shoot on enemy working party was undertaken with the result the party was dispersed. Two shoots were undertaken on enemy trenches and one house (with wireless)

[Remarks: J101; S. Am. 84. [uncertain]]

### Cité de Gaumont — 12-6-17

No. 4 Gun out of action temporarily. 3 shoots were taken up in response to S.O.S. Calls, about 400 rounds being fired. 2 retaliatory shoots were carried out on enemy positions

[Remarks: J101]

### Cité de Gaumont — 13-6-17

Our artillery extremely quiet during day, but active at night. 123 rounds fired on enemy positions (Harassing fire)

[Remarks: J101]

## Page 5 (June 1917, p. 5) — [diary text]

### Cité de Gaumont — 14-6-17

The battery position was shelled throughout the day intermittently, without effect, by enemy artillery. Our battery carried out several tasks, mostly harassing and neutralizing fire. GENERAL MASSIE, G.O.C. C.C.H.A. visited the battery at noon and presented the MILITARY MEDAL to Number 1257620 Gunner T. MAKIN — He expressed how pleased he was with the battery, since they had arrived — That they had been placed in an exceptionally hot corner & that they had done very good work.

[Remarks: J101; S. Am. 84. [uncertain]]

### Cité de Gaumont — 15-6-17

Harassing fire was carried out by us day and night. During the day enemy artillery shelled the vicinity of the battery position heavily firing about 350 rounds, without causing any damage.

[Remarks: J101]

### Cité de Gaumont — 16-6-17

160 rounds fired on concentration shoots and harassing fire. Enemy artillery normal.

[Remarks: J101]

### Cité de Gaumont — 17-6-17

Only 27 rounds were fired throughout the day on a N.F. call. The enemy artillery bombarded billets and adjacent battery positions for two hours with very little effect.

[Remarks: S. Am. 81. [uncertain]]

## Page 6 (June 1917, p. 6) — [diary text]

### Cité de Gaumont — 19-6-17

We carried out an experimental shoot today with ground observation and aerial reports. The result was not very successful. Much harassing night firing was undertaken. The enemy artillery fired ONE round, nearly obtaining a direct hit on No. 1 Gun, otherwise there was no shelling.

[Remarks: J101; S. Am. 81. [uncertain]]

### Cité de Gaumont — 20-6-17

An S.O.S call was answered at 10.22, 48 rounds being fired. Other tasks being one N.F. call, and a shoot on AVION TRENCH. Enemy artillery quiet.

[Remarks: J101; S. Am. 81. [uncertain]; A.C.]

### Cité de Gaumont — 21-6-17

150 rounds were fired into KIRK TRENCH, many rounds were observed to fall into this trench. 50 rounds were fired during the night at various targets.

[Remarks: J101]

### Cité de Gaumont — 22-6-17

An effective shoot of 300 rounds were fired into trenches, and one hostile battery was taken on, observation was undertaken by the Group Observation Post. No results received.

[Remarks: J101]

### Cité de Gaumont — 23-6-17

Many targets were taken on today, including 2 shoots on enemy H.Q. 200 rounds were also fired at enemy trench, many shots observed to fall into trench.

[Remarks: J101; S. Am. 81. [uncertain]]

### Cité de Gaumont — 24-6-17

300 rounds were fired into enemy trench with good results. An observation shoot by balloon, was also carried out with fair results. Enemy artillery still very quiet

[Remarks: J101]

## Page 7 (June 1917, p. 7) — [diary text]

### Cité de Gaumont — 25-6-17

350 rounds were fired today. One shoot of 75 rounds being on enemy machine gun emplacement the results being good. 150 rounds were also fired into enemy trench, many shots were seen to fall into trench.

[Remarks: A.C.]

### Cité de Gaumont — 26-6-17

A shoot on enemy trench without observation was carried out, about 200 rounds being fired. Enemy artillery very quiet.

[Remarks: J101]

### Cité de Gaumont — 27-6-17

Our tasks today are of a very minor nature, only a few rounds being fired throughout the day. Nothing of note.

[Remarks: J101; S. Am. 81. [uncertain]]

### Cité de Gaumont — 28-6-17

About 450 rounds expended on various tasks, the principal one being 150 rounds Wire Cutting with good results.

[Remarks: J101]

### Cité de Gaumont — 29-6-17

The principal task for today was for Harassing fire during the night. Intermittent fire was carried on throughout the night, about 100 rounds being fired.

[Remarks: J101; S. Am. 81. [uncertain]]

### Cité de Gaumont — 30-6-17

Many targets have been engaged during the day. 100 rounds being expended on enemy position with good effect. Enemy artillery quiet.

[Remarks: J101]

[Signed at the foot of the page:]

J. A. Stewart [uncertain] Major R.C.A

O.C. No. 9. Can. S.B. R.C.G.A.

## Transcriber's notes — June 1917

- **Date gaps (days with no entry):** 18 June only. Every other day 1–30 June has a dated entry.
- **"J101":** the Remarks column on most entries bears the same repeated mark, read here as "J101". Its meaning is unclear — it may be an initial, a file reference, or an appendix code.
- **"S. Am. 84." / "S. Am. 81.":** a second mark appears in the Remarks column on the entries of 1, 10, 11, 14, 17, 19, 20, 23, 27 and 29 June, reading "S. Am. 84." (1, 10, 11, 14 June) or "S. Am. 81." (17, 19, 20, 23, 27, 29 June). The first letter and the final digit are unclear, so the mark is flagged [uncertain]. It may be an appendix reference.
- **"A.C.":** the initials "A.C." appear in the Remarks column on the entries of 20 and 25 June.
- **9 June casualty:** "BOMBR. GEORGE, SIG. 4" — Bombardier George, signaller, wounded while on duty at the Observation Post. The "4" after "SIG." is written as a superscript; its meaning is unclear. This is the only named casualty in this chunk of the diary (pages 1–31).
- **14 June:** General MASSIE, G.O.C. C.C.H.A., presented the Military Medal to No. 1257620 Gunner T. MAKIN. (The service number is read as 1257620; the digits are written close together and should be checked against the service record.)
- **June signature:** the signature closing the volume appears to read "J. A. Stewart" — the surname is written in a flowing hand and could also read "Steward" — so it is flagged [uncertain]. The officer signs as Major, R.C.A., O.C. No. 9 Can. S.B., R.C.G.A.
- **Casualties:** 9 June — Bombardier GEORGE (signaller) wounded at the Observation Post (named). No other casualties are recorded in June.
- **People named:** General MASSIE (G.O.C., C.C.H.A.); Gunner T. MAKIN (No. 1257620, Military Medal); Bombardier GEORGE (signaller, wounded).
- **Places:** Cité de Gaumont throughout; Avion Trench; Kirk Trench; Overland Route; Rest Camp (4 June — a party of NCOs and men sent to Rest Camp).
- **No appendices** are listed or included with this volume; the contents page lists only "Diary Text — 5 Pages".
- Form imprint at the foot of the diary pages: "A6945 Wt. W11422/M1160 350,000 12/16 D. D. & L. Forms/C.2118/14." The diary sheet numbers "2" to "6" are written at the head of the Summary column on pp. 3–7.
