## Page 3 (September 1917, p. 3) — [diary text, Army Form C. 2118]

WAR DIARY or INTELLIGENCE SUMMARY. (Erase heading not required.)

| Place | Date | Hour | Summary of Events and Information |
|---|---|---|---|
| Cité de Caumont. (M33.b.03.07) and (M33.a.66.08) | 1-9-17 | | No shooting throughout the day, and only 60 rounds night firing into LENS and approaches during night. The following letter has been received:— H.Q. 4th CAN. DIV. To. O.C. 1st C.H.A.G. — "I will take this early opportunity of congratulating yourself, staff and personnel of No. 1 Group. C.H.A. on the splendid Co-operation and work during the recent operations in the LENS area. From all sides I have heard the same opinion, that the support rendered by the artillery was most satisfactory in every way, and it further gives me very great pleasure in enclosing you a copy of the Corps Commander's remarks on the same subject" (Sd) Maj: Gen. Watson Commanding 4th CAN. DIV. |
| —"— | 2-9-17 | | A shoot of 390 rounds commenced today at 2 pm, object being to cut wire in front of CINNIBAR TRENCH. Shoot very successful, wire being reported as "destroyed". 3 small opportunity shoots on enemy working parties were carried out and parties dispersed. At 9.25 pm an S.O.S call was reported, and 29 rounds were fired into the LENS Sector. |
| —"— | 3-9-17 | | Registration on Water tower N.8.d.5.2 at 1.30 pm 29 rounds. Another heavy shoot on wire in front of CINNIBAR TRENCH, 371 Rounds. Results good, the wire being reported as destroyed. Enemy artillery very quiet, probably due to bad visibility. |
| —"— | 4-9-17 | | Harassing fire maintained on LOISON STATION from 3 pm to 6 pm. At 12.25 am [continued on next page] |

## Page 4 (September 1917, p. 4) — [diary text, Army Form C. 2118]

| Place | Date | Hour | Summary of Events and Information |
|---|---|---|---|
| Cité de Caumont | 4-9-17 (Contd.) | | A response was made to S.O.S call fire being put on to enemy road and railway N 34b.79, 33 rounds being fired. Night firing carried out on LOISON and roads in the vicinity. |
| —"— | 5-9-17 | | In the very early hours of this morning the enemy sent over many gas shells into the battery position and near vicinity. Gas masks were worn by the gun crews and fire maintained on tasks in hand. Minor tasks carried out throughout the day, observation extremely difficult. No Casualties resulted from the enemy's gas bombardment. |
| —"— | 6-9-17 | | At about 1 am the enemy carried out an unprecedented bombardment of this village. Gas shell, H.E. and shrapnel were fired into all parts of the village, and it was undoubtedly the heaviest bombardment yet experienced by this battery. It lasted till 3.45 am. It is considered that at least 5000 gas shell were fired, some of which were of a very large calibre. Owing to the very effective nature of the precautions taken, the Casualties were small, only 3 men being gassed. Grs. BRUNTON + THORNE, and 1st A.M. LEE of the R.F.C. At about 7 am an enemy shell burst on roadway near battery position, and Gr E.F. MARTIN was Killed — Two S.O.S. calls were responded to and fire put on to the LENS Area — at 7.35. and 8.20 pm. |

## Notes

- **Date coverage:** Entries exist for 1–8, 10–13, 15–22 and 24–30 September 1917. **No entries for 9, 14 or 23 September.**
- **Casualties:** 6 September — Gnr E. F. MARTIN killed by a shell bursting on the roadway near the battery position; Gunners BRUNTON and THORNE and 1st Air Mechanic LEE (R.F.C.) gassed during the enemy's heavy gas bombardment of the village (at least 5,000 gas shells estimated). 5 September — enemy gas shelling of the battery position, no casualties. 19 September — an enemy working party engaged, "Casualties seen to be caused" (enemy casualties, number not given).
- **Decorations:** 16 September — extract from Routine Order No. 46 (12 Sept 1917): Military Medal awarded to No. 1257633 A/Cpl J. B. TROOP and No. 1257518 Bdr H. W. HORWILL. This confirms the spelling "Horwill" for the Bombardier wounded on 15 August (written "Norwill" in that entry).
- **Letters:** 1 September — letter from Maj-Gen. Watson, commanding 4th Canadian Division, to O.C. 1st C.H.A.G., congratulating No. 1 Group, Canadian Heavy Artillery, on its work in the recent Lens operations, with the Corps Commander's remarks enclosed.
- **Places and map references:** Cité de Caumont (battery position, M33.b.03.07 and M33.a.66.08); LENS; LOISON STATION; CINNIBAR TRENCH; CITÉ DE NORD; NOYELLES; MERICOURT; AVION; Water Tower N.8.d.5.2; road/railway N 34b.79; and numerous trench-map references (N.27.b.10.85, N.21.b.5.10, N.23.a, N.4.C.9.6, N.34.b.53.73, N.35.a.85.00, N.8.d.+9.c., N.21a.60.85, N.23.d.55-10, N.36.c.20.5, N.10.d.15.60, T X 23, N 2 98).
- **Oddities:** Cover is stamped CONFIDENTIAL and marked "VOLUME 7" with a pencilled "6" beneath. Contents page states "Diary Text 6 Pages" — the diary text is indeed 6 pages (pp. 3–8 of this month's file). Three dates have no entry (9, 14, 23 September). The 30 September entry has words heavily crossed out in two places, apparently a later deletion (possible censoring); the inserted words "Hostile batteries" above the line suggest the entry was edited after writing. The 4 September entry runs over two pages, and the 15 September entry runs over two pages. The 19 September entry's continuation appears as an undated line at the top of p. 7.
