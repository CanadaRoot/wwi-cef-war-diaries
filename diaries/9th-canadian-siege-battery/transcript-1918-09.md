## Notes

- **Date coverage:** Entries exist for every day, 1–30 September 1918. **No dates are missing.**
- **Casualties:** 6 September — two signallers gassed while repairing communication to the O.P., as a result of hostile shelling the previous day: 1257638 Signaller H. E. Selco [uncertain] and 1257622 Signaller C. A. West. 28 September — two drivers of the A.S.C. Ammunition Column wounded by enemy shelling of the roads during the move to Sauchy-Lestree (names not given). No battery personnel killed this month.
- **Command:** A new signatory — W. H. Scully — replaces Major J. A. Stewart, who signed the September 1917 diary. His initials "W.H.S." appear against every entry in the Remarks column.
- **The move south:** 19 September — orders to rejoin Canadian Corps; relieved by 23 Siege Battery R.G.A. at 9 p.m. and marched to Bray Camp, Ecoivres. 20 September — Ecoivres to Wancourt by way of Maroeuil and Arras. 21 September — four guns into position at V.4.d (Villers-Cagnicourt); the left section (two guns) follows on 22 September. 27 September — three guns move up west of Baralle Wood during the barrage, the other two later, one gun remaining for repairs. 28 September — forward to Sauchy-Lestree (Q35.b.60.85) by way of Inchy (D bridge).
- **The 27 September barrage:** In connection with offensive operations, a barrage fired 5.20–11.08 a.m. expending 1,404 rounds, followed the same afternoon by a heavy bombardment of Oisy-le-Verger and Area K (336 rounds) under 11th Division order No. 134.
- **Gas warfare:** The first half of the month is dominated by gas shelling — N.C. and S.K. gas against enemy dugouts at N11a.3.7, N35.c.7.0, N5.c.50.80 and N29.a.60.50, with "P.N.C." gas also named on 3 September. Enemy gas shelling of the battery's billets at Lievin on 4 September caused no casualties.
- **Places and map references:** LIEVIN (M29c.06.62); ECOIVRES and BRAY CAMP; WANCOURT; MAROEUIL; ARRAS; VILLERS-CAGNICOURT (V.4.d); SOUCHICOURT FARM (W.12.a); SAUCHICOURT FARM + WOOD; DARTFORD WOOD; AREA E; AREA K; BARALLE WOOD (W.7.c); OISY-LE-VERGER (R.14.c.22); SAUCHY-LESTREE (Q35.b.60.85); INCHY (D bridge); WATER TOWER at O.25.d.57.64 (4 Sept) and O.25.d.42.60 (15 Sept); Church at O.24.b.98.50; and numerous trench-map references (N.10.d.7.6, N11a.3.7, N35.c.7.0, O.26.a.80.63, N22.a.8.5, N23.c.6.3, N26.b.8.8, N29.a.3.8, O.26.d.75.66, N.10.b.66.20, N5.c.50.80, N29.a.60.50, N11.d.70.85, T.6.a.9.3, N5.c.5.8, O.26.a.8.6, N.23.d, O.25.c, N.29.a.22.58, O.31.b, N36.d.00.15, N12.d.00.25, N36.d.0015, N18d.28.36, O.19.c, N30.b.28.62, N36.b.96.51, O.25c 30.20, N35.d.30.85, O.19c.00.30, O.25.b.70.45, N24.b.5.2, W.6.d.20.18, W.7c and d, E.9.c, R.29.c.70.52, R.23, Q35c, R.29.a.15, R35a.60, R.23.d, R.21.c, R.23.a, R.21.a).
- **Oddities:** The date column changes format mid-month — 1–8 September are written "1-9-18" etc., 9–12 September as "9th.", "10th.", "11th.", "12th.", then "13-9-18" onwards. On 6 September a word is crossed out in the text and "N.C.+S.K." inserted above the line. On 7 September the writer writes "96 pounds" where "rounds" is clearly meant. On 11 September hostile batteries are given as "N×45, N×63, + N250" — the "×" as written, apparently standing for an unknown digit. On 12 September "N18d" is listed twice among the four hostile batteries. On 5 September the dugout reference is written "N29.a.6.5." where the same target is "N29.a.60.50" in the rest of the entry. The writer's capital "N" in "N35" is sometimes written like an "H" (6 and 7 September); it is transcribed as N35 throughout for consistency with the rest of the diary. The 22 September entry runs over to p. 8 and the 27 September entry runs over to p. 9. The cover is entirely handwritten, unlike the printed cover of the September 1917 diary. The contents page states "Diary Text 7 Pages" — the diary text is indeed 7 pages (pp. 3–9).
- **Appendices:** None listed; the contents page lists only "Diary Text 7 Pages".
