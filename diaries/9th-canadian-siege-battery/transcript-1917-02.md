- **Unit:** No. 9 Canadian Siege Battery
- **LAC Item ID:** 2004802, RG 9 III-D-3, vol. 4977, file 576, reel T-10805
- **Month / Volume:** February 1917, Volume 1 (covers 6 February – 30 March 1917)
- **Pages in this file:** LAC images pp. 1–4 (4 pages: cover, contents, 2 diary text pages)
- **Diary text date range:** 6 February – 31 March 1917
- **Commanding officer / signatory:** No signature or commanding officer is named on these pages. Captain COBBETT is named in the text as commanding the Main Body (8 February) and the Advance Party (19 and 31 March).

The "Hour" column is blank throughout. The Remarks column bears the same repeated mark on every entry, read here as "J101" (see Notes).

## Page 3 (February 1917, p. 3) — [diary text]

WAR DIARY or INTELLIGENCE SUMMARY. (Erase heading not required.)

### Lydd — Feb. 6th 1917

Receive final order for mobilization on 8th inst at CODFORD - WILTS.

[Remarks: J101]

### Lydd — Feb. 7th

Advance Party leave for CODFORD.

[Remarks: J101]

### Lydd — Feb. 8th

Main Body leave under Captain COBBETT.

[Remarks: J101]

### CODFORD — Feb. 10th

Send four Officers & half NCOs & men on mobilization leave — four clear days -

[Remarks: J101]

### CODFORD — Mch. 4th

Very few stores have yet arrived — So [uncertain] [uncertain] & Sec Canadian H.Q. re. non delivery of stores — Our Indents were all sent in to CANⁿ ORDNANCE at ASHFORD according to instructions, although nearly all stores must come from WOOLWICH — much delay caused by this procedure -

[Remarks: J101]

### CODFORD — Mch. 17th

Mechanical Transport arrive — Load up & pack -

[Remarks: J101]

### CODFORD — Mch. 18th

Guns, Stores & Lorries move off at 7-a.m. for PORTSMOUTH - Lieut MACKEEN & 12 Other Ranks go with Guns -

[Remarks: J101]

## Page 4 (February 1917, p. 4) — [diary text]

### CODFORD — Mch. 19th

Advance Party of 12 Other Ranks under Captain COBBETT leave for SOUTHAMPTON

[Remarks: J101]

### CODFORD — Mch. 21st

Main Body leave for SOUTHAMPTON — Embark at 6 p.m.

[Remarks: J101]

### [No place or date given — headed "N.B. Organization"]

N.B. Organization — The normal Time for mobilization of 6" 26 cwt. Siege Battery is fourteen days — we were over one month owing to delay at CANADIAN ORDNANCE — ASHFORD.

[Remarks: J101]

### HAVRE — Mch. 22nd

Arrive at 4 a.m. — Disembark at 9 a.m. March to No 5 Dock REST CAMP

[Remarks: J101]

### HAVRE — Mch. 22nd to Mch. 29th

Dock REST CAMP

[Remarks: J101]

### HAVRE — Mch. 31st

Orders for Advance Party & M.T. — They move off at 5 [uncertain] m. taking 100 Inf. [uncertain] with them — CAPT COBBETT in Command. 15 cases of Mumps in M.T. & 1 in Battery with 8 Contacts — all Signallers — This necessitates leaving 9 Signallers at HAVRE and sending 75 men to M.T.

[Remarks: J101]

## Transcriber's notes — February 1917

- **Date gaps (days with no entry):** 9 February; 11 February – 3 March; 5–16 March; 20 March; 30 March.
- **Cover vs. last entry:** the cover states the volume runs "FROM 6th February TO 30th March 1917", but the final diary entry is dated 31 March 1917.
- **"J101":** the Remarks column on every entry in this volume (and in the April and June volumes) bears the same repeated mark, read here as "J101". Its meaning is unclear — it may be an initial, a file reference, or an appendix code.
- **"CANⁿ ORDNANCE":** written with a superscript "n" (abbreviation of "Canadian").
- **"6\" 26 cwt.":** the battery's armament — the 6-inch 26 cwt siege gun.
- **Casualties:** none in combat. 15 cases of mumps in the Mechanical Transport and 1 in the Battery are recorded on 31 March (16 cases of illness in total); 9 signallers were left at Havre as contacts.
- **No appendices** are listed or included with this volume; the contents page lists only "Diary Text — 2 Pages".
- Form imprint at the foot of the diary pages: "A5834 Wt. W4973/M687 750,000 8/16 D. D. & L. Ltd. Forms/C.2118/13."
