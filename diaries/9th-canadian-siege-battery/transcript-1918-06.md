WAR DIARY or INTELLIGENCE SUMMARY. (Erase heading not required.)

| Place | Date | Hour | Summary of Events and Information |
|---|---|---|---|
| PETIT-VIMY | 1-6-18 | | An aeroplane shoot of 100 rounds only was successfully completed, on hostile battery at U.3.c.25.75 at 11.22 am. During the afternoon, calibration carried out satisfactorily, targets being RAILWAY COTTAGE at U.1.c.50.10 and ACHEVILLE CHURCH at T.18.d.60.40. Three hostile batteries were neutralized during the day in which 50 rounds were expended. Harassing fire maintained during the night. Rear Section fired 26 rounds for calibration purposes on ACHEVILLE CHURCH, and 25 rounds harassing fire on to enemy roads etc. Enemy artillery shelled S.24.d and S.30.b. with 15 c.m. how. firing about 60 rounds H.E. No damage and no casualties. |
| — | 2-6-18 | | A shoot of 100 rounds was carried out on hostile battery at U.15.d.63.41, and completed successfully. At 5.15 pm, 30 rounds were fired into SALLUMINES TRENCH at N34.b.00.58, direct hits in trench being observed. Hostile battery at U.8.b.54.56 was neutralized at 10.45pm, 20 rounds being fired. Rear Section fired 20 rounds on enemy position at T.6.d.35.13 and carried out registration on ACHEVILLE CHURCH firing 32 rounds. Enemy artillery attempted to carry out aeroplane shoot on our positions at S.24.b. He fired about 40 rounds without result. |
| — | 3-6-18 | | A shoot with aeroplane observation was commenced at 2.40 pm. on hostile battery at U15.b.12.30. Successfully completed, 200 rounds being fired. Rear Section carried out harassing fire on enemy roads at N36.c. + N.30.c, firing 50 rounds. Enemy artillery carried out a destructive shoot on an empty gun pit, firing over 250 rounds of 5.9's. No damage whatsoever and no casualties. |
| — | 4-6-18 | | At 2.50 pm we commenced an aeroplane shoot on hostile battery at U.9.b.51.33 and completed it very successfully at 4.53pm, 200 rounds fired. Harassing fire during the night on Hostile battery at U.9.b.51.33. Rear Section carried out registration on targets in N34c. + N34d. and fired 102 rounds on to Railway Embankment N34c and enemy trench from N34d.50.00 to N.34.b.70.05 in connection with Artillery Order No.16 Enemy artillery shelled S.22.d. intermittently with 15 cm Gun H.E. No damage done and no casualties. |

## Notes

- **Date coverage:** Every day 1–30 June 1918 has an entry; no date gaps.
- **Casualties:** No 9th Battery personnel casualties are recorded this month, and no casualty is named. Both casualty incidents befell personnel of No. 3 Canadian Siege Battery, who were manning the 9th's No. 3 and No. 4 gun pits after the 7 June relief: 18 June — 4 men killed and 2 wounded when a heavy explosion was caused at No. 4 pit during heavy shelling of S.24.d.; 28 June — 1 man killed and 4 wounded when a shell burst in No. 4 gun pit, damaging the gun (No. 4 gun temporarily out of action). Total for the month: 5 killed, 6 wounded, all No. 3 C.S.B.
- **Decorations recorded:** 18 June — Military Medal awarded to 1257501 Sergt. G. E. M. Mosley, 1257512 Corpl. A. W. Sillett and 1257650 L/Bdr. J. A. O'Leary (Routine Orders No. 57, dated 16 June, by Brig.-Gen. L. J. Chapman, C.M.G., R.A.). 23 June — Meritorious Service Medal awarded to 1257533 Gnr. A. Bridle (now S/Sgt. A.O.C.) and 1257508 Sgt. J. H. Palmer (London Gazette Supplement, 17 June).
- **Places named:** PETIT-VIMY (battery position throughout), ACHEVILLE CHURCH, RAILWAY COTTAGE, SALLUMINES TRENCH, SALLUMINES ROAD, MAUD VILLAS, MERICOURT SECTOR, BETRICOURT.
- **Oddities:**
  - The cover is handwritten on lined paper and reads "Volume 16"; the contents page states "Diary Text 7 Pages" — the diary text is indeed 7 pages (pp. 3–9 of this month's file).
  - Two different form print runs are used in the month: pp. 3–4 are printed "A5834 Wt. W4973 M687 750,000 8/16 D. D. & L. Ltd. Forms/C.2118/13", pp. 5–9 are "A6945 Wt. W11422/M1160 350,000 12/16 D. D. & L. Forms/C.2118/14".
  - Page 3 is headed "Original" in ink at the top left, with a pencilled "4" and "9th Can. Siege Battery" at the top right.
  - 10 June: two words after "was commenced" are heavily struck out (illegible under the deletion). 30 June: one word before "carried out" in the month summary is struck out.
  - 3 June: "30 rounds being filed" is transcribed as written; it appears to be a slip for "fired". 24 June: "84 pounds being fired" is transcribed as written; it appears to be a slip for "rounds". 30 June: "Rear position inactive" is transcribed as written; it appears to be a slip for "Rear Section".
  - Several grid references are given without numbers in the original: 15 June, the third battery is listed only as "U.8.b."; 23 June, batteries are listed only as "U.3.c. and U.2.d."; 8 June, registration target "Railway Cottage at U.1.c." has no numbers.
  - 7 June: the personnel of the forward positions in PETIT-VIMY were relieved by the rear section and that of the 3rd C.S.B. for purposes of rest; the 3rd C.S.B. then manned No. 3 and No. 4 gun pits.
  - 30 June: the month closed with the note that the battery "carried out no fewer than 28 aeroplane shoots during the month, which beats all previous records of this unit."
