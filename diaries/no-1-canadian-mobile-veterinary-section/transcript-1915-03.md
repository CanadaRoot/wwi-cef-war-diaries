## Transcription conventions
- Faithful to the original: spelling, punctuation, abbreviations and capitalisation as written (the diary is handwritten throughout, except for the typed contents page).
- `[uncertain]` marks a word or name that could not be read with confidence — never guessed.
- Text struck through in the original is shown as `[struck through in original: "..."]`.
- The diary-text pages are Army Form C. 2118, "WAR DIARY or INTELLIGENCE SUMMARY", handwritten in columns: Hour, Date, Place | Summary of Events and Information | Remarks and references to Appendices.
- No map coordinates (latitude/longitude) are given in the diary.
- "Issued … cured" means a recovered horse was returned to its unit; "to replace" means issued in replacement of a horse received.

## Notes — March 1915
- The diarist's initials "M.G.O'G." (M. G. O'Gogarty) appear at the end of every daily entry; the month is signed "M. G. O'Gogarty Capt., O.C. Mobile Vet Section, 1st Canadian Contingent". June's typed title page gives the name as "Captain M. G. O' Gogarty", confirming the reading.
- Places: the Section was at Meteren (1–3 March), then moved to Croix du Bac (4 March) and stayed there for the rest of the month; it did not follow the Division's move to Estaires on 26 March, having received no orders to do so.
- Horses: 113 sick horses received during March (the 5 March entry is 5 from the 6th Division Mobile Veterinary Section plus 2 from the 2nd and 3rd Infantry Brigades); 7 died, 2 destroyed (1 March at Merris, 16 March), 1 found dead at Caestre on 2 March; 39 evacuated to the Base Veterinary Hospital (12 + 11 + 16); 29 issued cured.
- The 17 March entry records the congratulatory message from Field Marshal Sir John French and General Sir Douglas Haig on the Battle of Neuve Chapelle, issued with Divisional Routine Orders.
- Remarks raise four equipment/supply concerns: shoeing (shortage of shoes or neglected feet), no microscope for skin disease, no light float for evacuating lame horses, and difficulty obtaining bran for sick horses; pneumonia cases are attributed to bad water.
- The contents page's "Volume 15-3" is the LAC filing volume; the cover's "Volume 1" is the diary's own volume number.
- No appendices are listed or included for March.
