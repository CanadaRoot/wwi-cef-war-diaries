## Transcription conventions
- Faithful to the original: spelling, punctuation, abbreviations and capitalisation as written (the diary is handwritten throughout, except for the typed contents page).
- `[uncertain]` marks a word or name that could not be read with confidence — never guessed.
- Text struck through in the original is shown as `[struck through in original: "..."]`.
- The diary-text pages are Army Form C. 2118, "WAR DIARY or INTELLIGENCE SUMMARY", handwritten in columns: Hour, Date, Place | Summary of Events and Information | Remarks and references to Appendices. On the November diary-text pages the printed words "INTELLIGENCE SUMMARY" in the heading are struck through.
- No map coordinates (latitude/longitude) are given in the diary.
- "Issued … cured" means a recovered horse was returned to its unit.
- Ditto marks (") in the Place and Summary columns are expanded as the repeated place or words in the tables below, following the diary's own shorthand.

## Notes — November 1915
- No entries for 3 or 12 November: the diary jumps from 2 to 4 November, and from 11 to 13 November. The date "10.11.15" was written twice in the date column on p. 3, the first time struck through.
- Places: Neuve Eglise until 13 November; the Section moved to a farm 1½ kilometres NW of Nieppe on the Bailleul–Nieppe road on 14 November because the road to its old billet was flooded.
- Horses: 98 sick horses received during November, plus 2 surplus horses from the 8th Labour Battalion (25 November) and 2 reinforcements from the Canadian Veterinary Hospital, Havre (30 November); 66 evacuated to No. 10 Veterinary Hospital, Neufchatel; 24 issued/returned cured, and 2 surplus horses issued to the 118th Bde. R.F.A.; 1 died; none destroyed.
- On 23 November O'Gogarty went to Meteren to collect 3 sick horses left at farms by the Canadian Mounted Rifles; they were unfit to move, so he arranged to hire floats — the light float he had asked for in March (see March Remarks) still not having been supplied.
- The contents page's "Volume 15-11" is the LAC filing volume; the cover's "(Volume 9.)" is the diary's own volume number.
- No appendices are listed or included for November.
