## Transcription conventions
- Faithful to the original: spelling, punctuation, abbreviations and capitalisation as written (the diary is handwritten throughout, on Army Form C. 2118).
- `[uncertain]` marks a word or name that could not be read with confidence — never guessed.
- Text struck through in the original is shown as `[struck through in original: "..."]`.
- The diary-text pages are Army Form C. 2118, "WAR DIARY or INTELLIGENCE SUMMARY", handwritten in columns: Place | Date | Hour | Summary of Events and Information | Remarks and references to Appendices. The "INTELLIGENCE SUMMARY" heading is not struck through on these pages.
- The Place column is written once per page; it is repeated in the tables below for each entry on that page. Where the original leaves the Place column blank (the unit was on the move), the table shows "—".
- The remarks column is initialled throughout with what appears to be "A.E.C." — the hand is stylised and the reading is not certain; it matches the initials of Capt. A. E. Coulombe, the officer commanding.
- No map coordinates (latitude/longitude) are given in the diary.
- "Admitted … horses to M.V.S." is the section's daily intake of sick or wounded horses; "Evacuated … to St. Omer / Neufchatel" is transfer onward (by road to St. Omer, or by rail from Wippenhoek Siding to No. 10 Veterinary Hospital, Neufchâtel; from 18 August the receiving hospital is No. 23 Receiving Veterinary Hospital, St. Omer); "Returned to Unit" is horses returned fit for duty; "Destroyed" is horses destroyed (put down). "D.V.S.+R." is the Director of Veterinary Services and Remounts, Folkestone, England.

## Notes — August 1916
- Monthly totals (as written on p. 17): 10 horses remaining from July; 381 admitted during August (391 in total); 379 evacuated, 6 destroyed, 6 cured and returned to unit (391 accounted for); none remaining under treatment at month's end.
- The section moved twice. It left Poperinghe on 13 August, marching via Abele and Steenvoorde to billets near Le Menegat crossroads (14 August), then on 15 August via Watten and Merville to Tilques, where it stayed until 27 August. On the night of 27–28 August it travelled by rail to Auchonvillers station (Somme) and marched to a billet in Chateau Longueville; on 29 August it moved on to Billet B.10 (via Beaumetz and Bernaville), and was at Longueville on 31 August. The 1st Canadian Division was moving south to the Somme.
- His Majesty The King Emperor (King George V) inspected the troops on the march on 14 August and passed the unit at 9.15 A.M.
- Horses destroyed this month (6, per the monthly report): cases 2706, 2741 and 2745 (7 August), 2811 (9 August, tetanus) and 2903 (10 August) — five are named in the daily entries; case 2717 died of influenza and pneumonia on 7 August rather than being destroyed.
- On 11 August, 41 horses and 2 mules were transferred to No. 3 Canadian Mobile Veterinary Section, in addition to the 120 horses and 2 mules evacuated to Neufchâtel that day.
- From 18 August the section's evacuations go to No. 23 Receiving Veterinary Hospital, St. Omer, rather than to Neufchâtel.
- Personnel: Pte. W. Lessard (No. 48766) — read "Leonard" in the July transcript — was admitted to No. 39 General Hospital, Havre, on 26 July. Corpl. J. Salmon (No. 34730) was admitted to No. 39 General Hospital, Havre, on 3 August. Pte. U. Mead (No. 48779) was admitted to No. 1 Field Ambulance on 5 August. Pte. J. J. Murray (No. 18453) reported as a reinforcement from No. 1 Canadian Veterinary Hospital, Havre, on 15 August. Pte. H. Ellison (No. 5304) left on 21 August for Watten station to report to the Officer in Charge, Reinforcements, Havre, for transportation to the Canadian Training Depot, Shorncliffe. Pte. J. Ulland (No. 48731) was admitted to No. 2 Field Ambulance on 29 August. Corpl. H. McEwen (No. 49458) returned to No. 1 Canadian Veterinary Hospital for duty on 30 August. Sgt. P. W. Southern is now "Staff Sergeant Southern" (20 August onwards). Corpl. Hamilton remains in charge of most conducting parties.
- The pay-book entries name Pte. F. J. Gauthier (No. 48750), Pte. H. Fontaine/Fountain (No. 18343), Pte. R. Papineau (No. 48183), Pte. E. G. MacDonald (No. 48765), Pte. J. McRae (No. 1278) and Pte. W. Wenham — given as No. 48812 on 13 July but as No. 48112 on 10 August; the two numbers differ in the original.
- Col. Watson, D.D.V.S., Second Army, visited the section on 23 August.
- The "Muster Parade" entries of 24–25 August confirm that the July entry read as "Muscle Parade" is "Muster Parade" — see the July transcript note.
- The place name written "Abel station" in July is confirmed as Abele by the 13 August march route ("through Abele to Steenvoorde").
- The route words on 28 August (Chalais, Boulogne, Auxi-le-Chateau, Maizieres) and the billet place "Longueville" are uncertain readings; the place may be Longuevillette (near Doullens, Somme).
- No appendices are listed or included for August; the contents page claims "Diary Text 15 Pages" for the 15 diary-text pages (pp. 3–17).
