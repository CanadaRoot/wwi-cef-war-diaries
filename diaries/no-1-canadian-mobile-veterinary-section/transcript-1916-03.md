## Transcription conventions
- Faithful to the original: spelling, punctuation, abbreviations and capitalisation as written (the diary is handwritten throughout, on Army Form C. 2118).
- `[uncertain]` marks a word or name that could not be read with confidence — never guessed.
- Text struck through in the original is shown as `[struck through in original: "..."]`.
- The diary-text pages are Army Form C. 2118, "WAR DIARY or INTELLIGENCE SUMMARY", handwritten in columns: Hour, Date, Place | Summary of Events and Information | Remarks and references to Appendices. The "INTELLIGENCE SUMMARY" heading is struck through on the diary pages.
- The diary is written as continuous narrative prose; each day's entry is given in full, in the diarist's own words and order.
- No map coordinates (latitude/longitude) are given in the diary; the section remained at Nieppe throughout March 1916, and was preparing to move to a new location on 1 April 1916.
- "M.V.S." = Mobile Veterinary Section; "A.D.V.S." = Assistant Director of Veterinary Services; "V.O." = Veterinary Officer; "O.C." = Officer Commanding; "Bde." = Brigade; "Batty." = Battery; "Batt." = Battalion; "C.F.A." = Canadian Field Artillery; "A.S.C." = Army Service Corps; "A.P.M." = Assistant Provost Marshal; "D.A.A.G." = Deputy Assistant Adjutant General; "A.A. + Q.M.G." = Assistant Adjutant and Quartermaster General; "R.T.O." = Railway Transport Officer; "G.H.Q." = General Headquarters; "C.A.V.C." = Canadian Army Veterinary Corps; "S.S.O." = [uncertain — as written]; "B.R.O." = [uncertain — as written].
- "Received/admitted … sick horses" is the section's daily intake of sick or wounded horses; "Evacuated … to No. 13 Vet. Hospital, NEUFCHATEL" is transfer to the veterinary hospital at Neufchâtel; "cast" horses are horses condemned as unfit for further service; the Mallein test is the diagnostic test for glanders.

## Notes — March 1916
- The contents page's "Diary Text 13 Pages" counts the thirteen diary-text pages (pp. 4–16); p. 3 is the handwritten title page, making 16 pages in the month's volume.
- The O.C.'s monthly remark in the remarks column of pp. 13–14 is the diary's most substantial commentary so far: a complaint that admitted horses are dirty, in poor skin condition and with bad teeth for want of grooming, concluding that nearly every case of skin disease can be traced to the "Want of Proper Grooming", and claiming good grooming would cut animal sickness by 75%.
- The unit was preparing to leave Nieppe: on 27 March the O.C. notified the S.S.O. of the new location (effective 1 April 1916), on 29 March he inspected the new billet, and on 31 March the diary says the unit leaves the billet the next morning and is ready to move as per instructions.
- Monthly summary (p. 16, the diary's own figures): 11 horses under treatment at 1 March; 111 admitted during March (122 in total); 103 evacuated to Base Veterinary Hospital; 15 returned to their own units; 2 destroyed; 2 remaining under treatment at 31 March. The daily entries account for 103 evacuated and 2 destroyed, matching the summary; the summary's 111 admitted and 15 returned are higher than the 94 horses (plus 2 mules) admitted and 12 horses returned that can be counted from the daily entries — the difference is not explained in the diary.
- Horses destroyed in March: Case No. 1634 (19 March — "General Paralysis"; post-mortem: broken articular surfaces of the femoro-tibial articulation and a femoral thrombosis) and Case No. 1651 (26 March — a brown mule, 15.2 hands, "Aged L.D.", of 3rd Canadian Field Ambulance, gangrene dermatitis).
- Mallein tests (for glanders) were carried out on 19 horses on 8 March and 3 more on 9 March; no reaction in any case. The section also reported weekly mange states for itself and for the 8th Entrenching Battalion, and rendered "Animal Class" returns for both.
- "118 Bde." / "118th Brigade" recurs (13 March "461 Batty 118 Bde", "458 Batty. R.F.A."; 20 March "118th Brigade Amm Col.") and is transcribed as written; the 27 February entry had "46th Batty 118 Bde. R.F.A.".
- Uncertain readings: "Class Animals" (3 March — the same return is written "Animal Class" on 10 March); "25 Batty 7 Bde." (3 March — written "25- Batty"); "Aged L.D." (26 March — the mule's description); "S.S.O." (27 March).
- No appendices are listed or included for March.
