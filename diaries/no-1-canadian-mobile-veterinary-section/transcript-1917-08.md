## Transcription conventions
- Faithful to the original: spelling, punctuation, abbreviations and capitalisation as written (the diary is handwritten throughout, on Army Form C. 2118).
- `[uncertain]` marks a word or name that could not be read with confidence — never guessed.
- Text struck through in the original is shown as `[struck through in original: "..."]`.
- The diary-text pages are Army Form C. 2118, "WAR DIARY or INTELLIGENCE SUMMARY", handwritten in columns: Place | Date | Hour | Summary of Events and Information | Remarks and references to Appendices.
- The Place column gives "Mazingarbe, Fosse 2" on the first row of each diary-text page and ditto marks thereafter; ditto rows are shown as "—".
- No hours are recorded in the Hour column at any point in the month.
- The Remarks column is initialled with a large flourish on 1–24 August, read as "M.G.O.T." [uncertain]; from 25 August the remarks are initialled "P.S." (circled on pages 14–16) — the initials of Capt. Pierre Smaithard.
- The station name is written "Gouy Les Mines" throughout; it is transcribed as written.
- "M.V.S." = Mobile Veterinary Section; "V.A.C." = Veterinary Ambulance Corps (as written); "C.R.S." as written; "D.A.D.V.S." = Deputy Assistant Director of Veterinary Services; "A.A. & Q.M.G." = Assistant Adjutant and Quartermaster General.

## Notes — August 1917
- The unit remained at Mazingarbe, Fosse 2, for the whole month; the only movement was the daily evacuation of sick/wounded animals by rail from Gouy Les Mines Station and Bethune Station to No. 22 Veterinary Hospital, Abbeville.
- Horses admitted to the M.V.S. during the month total 345 (daily admissions: 3, 9, 28, 9, 13, 18, 29, 5, 10, 6, 5, 13, 12, 5, 8, 13, 29, 27, 2, 6, 21, 9, 10, 4, 12, 4, 15, 8, 2, 7, 3).
- Horses evacuated: 129 from Gouy Les Mines Station and 187 from Bethune Station (316 in all); mules evacuated: 7 from Gouy Les Mines and 43 from Bethune (50 in all). The 20 August Bethune evacuation included 6 cast horses received from D.D.R., 1st Army.
- Animals destroyed or died: Cases 6448 and 6449 destroyed 5 August (shrapnel wounds); Cases 6622 and 6623 destroyed 19 August (shrapnel wounds); Case 6683 died 21 August; Case 6672 died 27 August (debility, colic); Cases 6676 (shrapnel wounds) and 6689 (debility, sand colic) destroyed 28 August.
- Cases returned fit for duty: No. 6344 (1 August, to No. 2 Section 1 Can. V.A.C.); No. 6505 (10 August, to 15 Batty. 3 Inf. Bde.); No. 6361 (17 August, to 2 Batty 1 Bde. CFA). Eight other mules were returned to No. 1 Can. Vety. Hospital for duty on 18 August.
- Command changes: on 7 August Capt. Coulombe A.E. assumed duties as acting D.A.D.V.S., 1st Canadian Division, and Capt. Smaithard P. assumed duties as acting O.C., 1 Can. M.V.S. Capt. Coulombe was granted 10 days leave from 25 August to 4 September 1917.
- Capt. Vosburgh G. is the subject of a Statement of Evidence on 15 August, and of correspondence and a Summary of Evidence concerning Sergt. Ulyss J., No. 41147 (2 and 12 August). Vosburgh's regimental number is given as 48511 on 15 August and as 48711 on 22 August — both as written.
- Tpr. Gibbs J.L. appears with three different numbers in the original: 48954 (3 August, admitted to C.R.S., initials written "J. I."), 48754 (8 August, A.F.B. 117 forwarded to D.A.G. Base) and 48784 (9 August, rejoins from No. 1 Can. Field Ambulance). The differences are in the original, not in this transcription.
- Discipline: Pte. Lee R.J., No. 21131, was fined 3 days' pay on 27 August for riding on a G.S. Waggon contrary to A.R.O. 1344.
- Uncertain readings: the 12 August "Reply as to [uncertain] of Canadian News" (the word appears to read "issue"); the reinforcement on 15 August — the trooper's surname, the word before "General" (appears to read "Can.") and the word after "Base" (appears to read "Depot") could not be read with confidence; the staff-sergeant's surname on 26 August appears to read "Rouleau"; the spelling "Smaithard" is as written in the diary (7 August entry and both signatures); the remarks initials for 1–24 August are a flourish read as "M.G.O.T.".
- The 20 August conducting party is written "Corpl. Vosburgh G.", although Vosburgh is given as Captain everywhere else in the month (14, 22, 24, 27 and 30 August) — transcribed as written.
- A muster parade was held on 25 August; the muster parade state was rendered to D.A.A.G., Canadian Section, G.H.Q., 3rd Echelon, on 26 August.
- No appendices are listed or included for August; the contents page lists only "Diary Text 13 Pages."
