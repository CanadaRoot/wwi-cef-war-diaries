## Transcription conventions
- Faithful to the original: spelling, punctuation, abbreviations and capitalisation as written (the diary is handwritten throughout, on Army Form C. 2118).
- `[uncertain]` marks a word or name that could not be read with confidence — never guessed.
- Text struck through in the original is shown as `[struck through in original: "..."]`.
- The diary-text pages are Army Form C. 2118, "WAR DIARY or INTELLIGENCE SUMMARY", handwritten in columns: Place | Date | Hour | Summary of Events and Information | Remarks and references to Appendices. The "INTELLIGENCE SUMMARY" heading is not struck through on these pages.
- The Place column is written once per page (and repeated in the tables below for each entry on that page); ditto marks in the Place column are expanded to the place name.
- The diary-text sheets are numbered 2–13 at the top right of the Summary column (pp. 4–15).
- The remarks column is initialled throughout with what appears to be "A.E.C." — the hand is stylised and the reading is not certain; it matches the initials of Capt. A. E. Coulombe, the officer commanding.
- No map coordinates (latitude/longitude) are given in the diary; the diary gives map-sheet references for the two moves (Berthonval Farm, Sheet 51 S N.E.; Fosse 2 Mazingarbe, Sheet 36 B N.E.).
- "Admitted … horses to M.V.S." is the section's daily intake of sick or wounded horses; "Evacuated … to No. 13 Veterinary Hospital Neufchatel / No. 22 Vet Hospital Abbeville" is transfer onward by rail; "Returned to Unit" is horses returned fit for duty; "Destroyed" is horses destroyed (put down); "Died" is horses that died under treatment.

## Notes — July 1917
- Monthly totals (summed from the daily entries; no monthly statistical summary is given at the end of July 1917, unlike July 1916): 215 horses admitted (21 and 22 July have no admission entry); 116 horses and 39 mules evacuated in six rail shipments; 3 horses destroyed (cases 6177 and 6189 on 3 July, 6271 on 17 July); 2 horses died (case 6228, debility, 10 July; case 6311, shrapnel wounds to the abdomen, 24 July); 1 horse returned to its unit fit for duty (case 6232, 23 July). The admissions are far lower than July 1916 (484), and mange is no longer the dominant theme — the month's work is mostly routine administration, evacuations and the two moves.
- Movements: the section starts July at Ecurie (near Arras), moves on 4 July to Berthonval Farm (map reference "F 10- A. 8.8 Sheet 51 S N.E."), and moves again on 21 July at 9:30 a.m. to Fosse 2 Mazingarbe ("Sheet 36 B N.E. L 33 B 5.6"), where it remains to the end of the month. Evacuations are by rail from Ecurie Station (to No. 13 Veterinary Hospital, Neufchatel, and No. 22 Vet Hospital, Abbeville) and, on 28 July, from Bethune Station to No. 22 Vet Hospital, Abbeville.
- Personnel: Capt. A. E. Coulombe commands and signs the diary. Sergt. Lynch G.T. repeatedly leads the evacuation conducting parties and rejoins from Base (2, 11, 22 July; in charge 18 and 28 July). A second sergeant's name reads "Ulyo" [uncertain] with the initial "J." (3, 4, 6, 8, 12, 17, 21, 24 July) — it is not clear whether this is the same man written differently or a second sergeant; the initials differ (G.T. vs J.) and the name as written is not a recognisable surname (possibly "Lyon"). Corpl. Salmon J. (No. 34730) is on leave 6–16 July and rejoins 17 July. Dvr. Fountaine H. (No. 18343) is granted 10 days' leave 1 July and rejoins 12 July. Pte. Christensen G. (No. 19273) is on leave 8–18 July and rejoins 20 July. Pte. Baily [uncertain] J.R. (No. 484) is on leave 9–19 July and rejoins 20 July. Pte. Lee R.J. (No. 21631) is on leave 20–30 July and rejoins 31 July. Sergt. Hamilton G.C. (No. 11801) is granted 10 days' leave 30 July. Pte. Truchett [uncertain] J.J. (No. 426821) is granted 10 days' leave 29 July. Pte. Votough [uncertain] G. is granted leave 7–17 July as Pte. No. 48011 but rejoins 19 July as Corpl. No. 48511 — the number and rank differ between the two entries, so the two readings are as written and the discrepancy is flagged. Pte. Butts A.G. (No. 48538) is temporarily attached as Bandsman to 1 Can. Div. Trains on 4 July, vice Pte. Gauthier F.T. (No. 48750), who rejoins the unit. Completed Active Service Pay Books for Ptes. Gilb [uncertain] J.L. (No. 48784), Baily [uncertain] J.R. (No. 484) and Wood P.G. (No. 49766) are forwarded to the Chief Paymaster, London, on 3 July.
- The O.C. (Capt. Coulombe) assumes duties as temporary Veterinary Officer to 14th Army Bde. R.F.A. (22 July) and to 331st Road Construction Coy. (23 July).
- Horses received/collected: one horse hide from 2nd Can. M.V.S. (1 July); one stray horse from the M.M.P., case 6361 (29 July); a horse ambulance arrives at Ecurie Station (15 July). Remount halters are received from 1 Can. D.A.C. (53 on 10 July, 206 on 18 July) and from 1 Can. Divl Train (20 on 13 July); panels slings and unit chests are issued to D.A.D.V.S., 1 Can. D.A.C., the artillery and infantry brigades through the month.
- The section also renders the weekly A-2000 returns for 182 Tunnelling Coy R.E. and 318 Road Construction Coy R.E., as well as its own.
- No appendices are listed or included for July; the contents page claims "Diary Text 12 Pages" for the 12 diary-text pages (pp. 4–15).
