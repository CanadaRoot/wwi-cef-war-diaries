## Transcription conventions
- Faithful to the original: spelling, punctuation, abbreviations and capitalisation as written (the diary is handwritten throughout, except for the typed contents and title pages).
- `[uncertain]` marks a word or name that could not be read with confidence — never guessed.
- Text struck through in the original is shown as `[struck through in original: "..."]`.
- The diary-text pages are Army Form C. 2118, "WAR DIARY or INTELLIGENCE SUMMARY", handwritten in columns: Hour, Date, Place | Summary of Events and Information | Remarks and references to Appendices.
- No map coordinates (latitude/longitude) are given in the diary.
- "Issued … cured" means a recovered horse was returned to its unit.

## Notes — June 1915
- The 7 June entry is dated "7.7.15" in the original — evidently a slip for 7.6.15; transcribed as written with [sic].
- Places: Hinges until 7 June; Bethune 7–27 June; La Creche from 27 June. The 27 June move was troubled: the farm NE of Noote Boome was already occupied, so the Section fed and watered its horses on the road before taking over a farm near La Creche vacated by the 48th Division's Mobile Veterinary Section, which was in a very insanitary condition and was inspected the next day by the A.D.V.S., II Army, the A.D.O.S. and the Divisional Sanitary Officer.
- Horses: 177 sick horses received during June; 167 evacuated to No. 5 Veterinary Hospital, Abbeville; 5 destroyed; none recorded as died; 17 issued cured.
- The Remarks on p. 4 complain that remounts sent up from the Base are in poor condition — broken wind, blind, mares in foal, debilitated — and have to be sent on to hospital.
- On 5 June O'Gogarty was instructed by the A.D.V.S. to take over the duties of Veterinary Officer, 4th Corps Headquarters, until further notice.
- The contents page's "Volume 15-6" is the LAC filing volume; the cover's "Volume [struck through: 3] 4" is the diary's own volume number.
- No appendices are listed or included for June.
