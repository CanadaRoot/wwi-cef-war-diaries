## Transcription conventions
- Faithful to the original: spelling, punctuation, abbreviations and capitalisation as written (the diary is handwritten throughout, on Army Form C. 2118).
- `[uncertain]` marks a word or name that could not be read with confidence — never guessed.
- Text struck through in the original is shown as `[struck through in original: "..."]`.
- The diary-text pages are Army Form C. 2118, "WAR DIARY or INTELLIGENCE SUMMARY", handwritten in columns: Place | Date | Hour | Summary of Events and Information | Remarks and references to Appendices. The "INTELLIGENCE SUMMARY" heading is not struck through on these pages.
- The Place column is written once per page; it is repeated in the tables below for each entry on that page. A ditto mark (") in the Place column is expanded to the repeated place.
- The remarks column is initialled throughout with a circled two-letter monogram that appears to read "P.S." — the reading is not certain; it matches the initials of Capt. P. Smallland, who signs the 31 January entry.
- No map coordinates (latitude/longitude) are given in the diary; map-sheet references (e.g. "Sheet 2.L", "Sheet Liege 7") are transcribed as written.
- "Admitted … horses to M.V.S." is the section's daily intake of sick or wounded horses; "Transferred … to C.C.V.E.S." is transfer onward to the Canadian Corps Veterinary Evacuating Station (Bonn). "D.A.D.V.S." = Deputy Assistant Director of Veterinary Services. "D.A.A.G." = Deputy Assistant Adjutant General. "A.A.+ Q.M.G." = Assistant Adjutant and Quartermaster General. "C.F.A." = Canadian Field Artillery.

## Notes — January 1919
- The section spent 1–12 January in Cologne, at the Artillery Barracks, Karinburgs [uncertain] Strasse, transferring sick horses to the C.C.V.E.S. at Bonn: 31 horses on 3 January, 8 on 7 January, 18 horses and 1 mule on 9 January. On 12 January it entrained at Cologne Station at 4 p.m. and arrived at Huy Station, Belgium, at 0830 hours on 13 January, locating at No. 2 Rue Vieille Chaussee de Statte, Huy, where it remained to month's end.
- A new case-number series begins in January: the old series ended at case 999 (December); the January cases run from 9000 (4 January, a horse of 5th Battery, 2/13th C.F.A., destroyed as unfit for further service) through 9083 (11 January), with the completed case books up to 9083 forwarded to the D.A.D.V.S. A second, smaller series also appears: case 34 (5 January, from 7th Battery, 2/13th C.F.A.), case 57 (12 January), case 96 (16 January — died of pneumonia), and cases 121 and 122 (30 January, horses collected at Vinalmont [uncertain] and Burdinne).
- Demobilisation is under way: animals "classified D" (unfit) were destroyed for butchery in bulk — 9 on 22 January, 9 on 23 January, 5 on 27 January and 3 on 29 January. The diary also records a "Nominal Roll of Unit showing Demobilization etc." on 27 January. On 21 January 14 horses and 2 mules were transferred to IX Corps V.E.S. at Ciney.
- Personnel: Pte. J. A. Stains [uncertain] (No. 48598) rejoined from leave on 13 January; Pte. E. W. Roy (No. 406920) rejoined from leave on 17 January; Pte. S. J. Bougen [uncertain] (No. 49652) rejoined from the U.K. on 18 January; Sergt. J. H. Southern (No. 34703) rejoined from leave on 18 January. Pte. R.A. [uncertain] (No. 7228) was granted leave of absence to 1 February 1919 on 23 January. A.F.B. 122 for Dvr. L. Lee (No. 35462) was forwarded to H.Q. Divisional Train on 25 January.
- The cover's volume number reads "45" (circled) with "47." written above it. The contents page's "Diary Text 10 Pages" counts the 10 C. 2118 diary-text pages (pp. 4–13), not the cover, contents or title page.
- No monthly summary table of admissions/evacuations is given for January; the month closes with "Close of War Diary for Month Ending January 31st 1919" and the O.C.'s signature.
- No appendices are listed or included for January.
