## Transcription conventions
- Faithful to the original: spelling, punctuation, abbreviations and capitalisation as written (the diary is handwritten throughout, except for the typed contents and title pages).
- `[uncertain]` marks a word or name that could not be read with confidence — never guessed.
- Text struck through in the original is shown as `[struck through in original: "..."]`.
- The diary-text pages are Army Form C. 2118, "WAR DIARY or INTELLIGENCE SUMMARY", handwritten in columns: Hour, Date, Place | Summary of Events and Information | Remarks and references to Appendices. On the July diary-text pages the printed words "INTELLIGENCE SUMMARY" in the heading are struck through.
- No map coordinates (latitude/longitude) are given in the diary.
- "Issued … cured" means a recovered horse was returned to its unit.

## Notes — July 1915
- No entries for 2, 3, 4 or 25 July: the diary jumps from 1 to 5 July, and from 24 to 26 July.
- Places: La Creche until 16 July; Zwartemolenhoek from 17 July (the 16 July move was first to a billet on the Nieppe–Bailleul road, then, for want of water, to a farm off the Bailleul–Neuve Eglise road).
- Horses: 87 sick horses received during July; 72 evacuated to No. 10 Veterinary Hospital, Neufchatel; 6 destroyed, 2 died; 4 issued cured.
- The Remarks complain about remounts received as replacements: indented for "2 Chargers I and 4 Riding Horses R.I.", O'Gogarty received 1 Charger II, 1 Riding Horse R.I., 3 pack ponies and 1 unlabelled horse — the R.I. horse was unfit and the pack horses unsuitable for riding.
- On 29 July the A.D.V.S. instructed the Section to receive horses suspected of glanders, or unthrifty ones, for the mallein test; 5 cases were received for testing. On 30 July the D.D.V.S., 2nd Army, visited the billet and inspected the lines.
- The contents page's "Volume 15-7" is the LAC filing volume; the cover's "Volume 5." is the diary's own volume number.
- No appendices are listed or included for July.
