## Transcription conventions
- Faithful to the original: spelling, punctuation, abbreviations and capitalisation as written (the diary is handwritten throughout, on Army Form C. 2118).
- `[uncertain]` marks a word or name that could not be read with confidence — never guessed.
- Text struck through in the original is shown as `[struck through in original: "..."]`.
- The diary-text pages are Army Form C. 2118, "WAR DIARY or INTELLIGENCE SUMMARY", handwritten in columns: Hour, Date, Place | Summary of Events and Information | Remarks and references to Appendices. The "INTELLIGENCE SUMMARY" heading is struck through on the diary pages.
- From February the diary is written as continuous narrative prose rather than the terse daily tallies of January; each day's entry is given in full, in the diarist's own words and order.
- No map coordinates (latitude/longitude) are given in the diary; the section remained at Nieppe throughout February 1916.
- "M.V.S." = Mobile Veterinary Section; "A.D.V.S." = Assistant Director of Veterinary Services; "V.O." = Veterinary Officer; "O.C." = Officer Commanding; "Bde." = Brigade; "Batty." = Battery; "C.F.A." = Canadian Field Artillery; "A.S.C." = Army Service Corps; "A.P.M." = Assistant Provost Marshal; "B.R.O." = [uncertain — as written]; "D.A.A.G." = Deputy Assistant Adjutant General; "D.A.Q.M.G." = Deputy Assistant Quartermaster General; "R.T.O." = Railway Transport Officer; "G.H.Q." = General Headquarters; "L.S.H." = Lord Strathcona's Horse (Royal Canadians); "R.F.A." = Royal Field Artillery.
- "Received/admitted … sick horses" is the section's daily intake of sick or wounded horses; "Evacuated … to No. 13 Vet. Hospital, NEUFCHATEL" is transfer to the veterinary hospital at Neufchâtel; "cast" horses are horses condemned as unfit for further service.

## Notes — February 1916
- 1916 was a leap year, so 29 February 1916 exists; the diary covers 1–29 February in full.
- The contents page's "Diary Text 9 Pages" counts the nine diary-text pages (pp. 4–12); p. 3 is the handwritten title page, making 12 pages in the month's volume.
- Monthly horse figures (as written): 98 horses evacuated to No. 13 Veterinary Hospital, Neufchâtel (21 on 1 Feb, 24 on 5 Feb, 16 on 12 Feb, 18 on 18 Feb, 8 on 24 Feb, 11 on 29 Feb); 1 horse died (Horse No. 1478, 27 Feb — post-mortem showed a rupture); about 76 horses admitted/received, including horses collected from units; 6 horses returned cured and fit for duty (Case No. 1477 on 2 Feb; one to 8th Entrenching Battalion on 7 Feb; one to the V.O., 3rd Infantry Brigade, on 13 Feb; two to the 4th and 5th C.F.A. Brigades on 21 Feb; one iron grey gelding to 21st Battery, 2nd Division, on 25 Feb).
- The 14 February report to the A.D.V.S. gives the section's cumulative figures: 1,189 horses evacuated since 15 February 1915; 216 cases cured; 9 died or destroyed; 1,496 horses received in total up to 11 February 1916. (The three disposal figures sum to 1,414, not 1,496 — the balance is not explained in the diary.)
- "118 Batty." (17 Feb) and "118 Bde. R.F.A." (27 Feb) are written as "118" in both places; the reading may be "11th" (11th Battery / 11th Brigade, R.F.A.), but the diary is transcribed as written.
- Uncertain readings: the transfer destination for Pte Richards on 2 February reads "R.C.10." (possibly "R.C.V.D."); the unit sending four cast horses on 11 February reads "W.W.R."; the 19 February place names are written "Madame Sabage's", "Meteen" and "Fletre"; "Hayte Delahaye B.4 A-8-2" (25 February) is transcribed as written and may be a map reference.
- "Pte. Bilot" is written "Bilot" (16 and 26 February) — the same spelling both times.
- The 28 February entry ends with a trailing dash; the 29 February entry begins a new page and does not continue it.
- No appendices are listed or included for February.
