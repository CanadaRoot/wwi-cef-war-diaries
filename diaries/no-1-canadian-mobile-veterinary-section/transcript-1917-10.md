## Transcription conventions
- Faithful to the original: spelling, punctuation, abbreviations and capitalisation as written (the diary is handwritten throughout, on Army Form C. 2118).
- `[uncertain]` marks a word or name that could not be read with confidence — never guessed.
- Text struck through in the original is shown as `[struck through in original: "..."]`.
- The diary-text pages are Army Form C. 2118, "WAR DIARY or INTELLIGENCE SUMMARY", handwritten in columns: Place | Date | Hour | Summary of Events and Information | Remarks and references to Appendices. The "INTELLIGENCE SUMMARY" heading is not struck through on these pages. "Original" is written at the top left of each diary-text page. The printed form imprint at the foot of each diary-text page reads "2353 Wt. W2544/1454 700,000 5/15 D. D. & L. A.D.S.S./Forms/C. 2118."
- The Place column is written once per page (with ditto marks for following days); it is repeated in the tables below for each entry on that page. Where the Place column changes mid-page, the place given is the day's location.
- The remarks column is initialled throughout with a stylised monogram read here as "A.E.C."; it matches the initials of Capt. A. E. Coulombe, the officer commanding, who signs the title page and the 31 October entry.
- No map coordinates (latitude/longitude) are given in the diary, except for two map references: "Q 29 C.4.4" at Petit Servins (6 October) and "Sheet 28 G.11.A.4.6" at Brandhoek Area (30 October).
- "Admitted … horses to M.V.S." is the section's daily intake of sick or wounded horses; "Evacuated … to No. 7 Veterinary Hospital, Forges-les-Eaux" is transfer onward by rail; "Destroyed" is horses destroyed (put down). "D.A.D.V.S." is the Deputy Assistant Director of Veterinary Services; "D.V.S.+R." is the Director of Veterinary Services and Remounts; "A.A.+Q.M.G." is the Assistant Adjutant and Quartermaster General.

## Notes — October 1917
- No monthly horse totals are given this month (unlike October 1916, which closed with a full accounting). Summed from the daily entries: 162 horses admitted (plus 3 mules on 1 October); 132 horses and 25 mules evacuated to No. 7 Veterinary Hospital, Forges-les-Eaux; 2 horses destroyed (cases 7025 on 11 October and 7037 on 13 October); 5 horses and 1 mule transferred to 3rd Canadian M.V.S. on 31 October.
- The section moved north in stages during the month, following the 1st Canadian Division toward the Ypres salient: Bouvigny-Boyeffles (1–5 October) to Petit Servins (6–13 October, map reference Q 29 C.4.4, relieving 4th Canadian M.V.S.), to Barlin (14–19 October), to Haverskerque (20 October), to Eecke (21–29 October), and to the Brandhoek Area (30–31 October, Sheet 28 G.11.A.4.6). Two horses (cases 7113 and 7114) were left at Barlin with Headquarters, 5th Army Corps, when the section moved on 20 October.
- Evacuations went by rail from Barlin station to No. 7 Veterinary Hospital, Forges-les-Eaux, throughout the month.
- Personnel: Tpr. E. G. MacDonald (No. 48786) went on 10 days' leave 1 October and rejoined 12 October. Tpr. A. G. Cutts (No. 48538) went on leave 5 October and rejoined 17 October. Tpr. H. Davey (No. 48793) went on leave 15 October and rejoined 28 October. Tpr. F. A. Wright (No. 48541) went on leave 26 October, to 5 November. Tpr. J. J. Murray (No. 18453) was admitted to the British Red Cross Hospital, Forges-les-Eaux, on 1 October and rejoined from hospital on 5 October. Dvr. J. W. Francis (No. 35414, 1st Canadian Divisional Train) was admitted to hospital on 16 October (and is recorded as admitted to hospital again on 17 October). Tpr. W. Wenham (No. 48512) rejoined from the Canadian General Base Depot on 3 October. Pte. S. E. Post (No. 195648) rejoined from the Canadian Corps Veterinary Detachment on 14 October. Dvr. F. A. Hutchings (No. 30080) was temporarily attached as a driver, A.S.C., on 17 October. S/Sergt. B. A. Waddy (No. 12008) reported for duty from the Canadian Veterinary Hospital (Base) on 26 October. Three other ranks temporarily attached to the Canadian Corps Veterinary Detachment rejoined the section on 11 October. Corpl. G. Vosburgh, Corpl. J. Salmon and Sergt. G. C. Hamilton repeatedly ran the conducting parties escorting evacuated horses to Forges-les-Eaux.
- The monthly return of remounts evacuated for mange (month ending 30 September) was rendered on 1 October; the monthly returns of horses sold for butchery and of carcases and hides were rendered on 31 October.
- Stray and left-behind animals feature this month: case 6928, a stray collected by 48th Howitzer Battery and 18th C.F.A. (1 October); two stray mules received from the A.P.M., 1st Canadian Division (10 October); case 7107, left at Mingoval by No. 3 Coy., 3rd Canadian Divisional Train (15 October); a horse belonging to the Senior Chaplain, Canadian Corps, collected at Lillers (18 October); case 7117, a stray mule collected at Haverskerque (25 October); case 7120, left at Hazebrouck by 2 Battery, 1st Brigade C.F.A. (29 October).
- 23 and 24 October have no diary entries (dates only, with ditto marks in the Place column).
- The diary-text pages carry no printed page numbers; the title page on p. 3 is numbered "2". The contents page's "Diary Text 10 Pages" counts the 10 C. 2118 diary-text pages (pp. 4–13).
- The cover volume number is "32", with "10." written beneath it — the reading of the "10." is uncertain.
- No appendices are listed or included for October.
