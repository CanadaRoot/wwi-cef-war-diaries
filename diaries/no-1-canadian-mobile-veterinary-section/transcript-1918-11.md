## Transcription conventions
- Faithful to the original: spelling, punctuation, abbreviations and capitalisation as written (the diary is handwritten throughout, on Army Form C. 2118).
- `[uncertain]` marks a word or name that could not be read with confidence — never guessed.
- Text struck through in the original is shown as `[struck through in original: "..."]`.
- The diary-text pages are Army Form C. 2118, "WAR DIARY or INTELLIGENCE SUMMARY", handwritten in columns: Place | Date | Hour | Summary of Events and Information | Remarks and references to Appendices. The "INTELLIGENCE SUMMARY" heading is not struck through on these pages.
- The Place column is written once per page; it is repeated in the tables below for each entry on that page. A ditto mark (") in the Place column is expanded to the repeated place.
- The remarks column is initialled throughout with a circled two-letter monogram that appears to read "P.S." — the reading is not certain; it matches the initials of Capt. P. Smallland, who signs the 30 November entry.
- The diary-text pages are numbered in pencil at the top right, 2627–2635 (pp. 4–12).
- No map coordinates (latitude/longitude) are given in the diary; map-sheet references (e.g. "Sheet 51 A", "Sheet Brussels 6") are transcribed as written.
- "Admitted … horses to M.V.S." is the section's daily intake of sick or wounded horses; "Transferred … to C.C.V.E.S." is transfer onward to the Canadian Corps Veterinary Evacuating Station. "D.A.D.V.S." = Deputy Assistant Director of Veterinary Services. "D.A.A.G." = Deputy Assistant Adjutant General. "A.A.+ Q.M.G." = Assistant Adjutant and Quartermaster General.

## Notes — November 1918
- The Armistice of 11 November 1918 is not mentioned anywhere in the diary. The 11 November entry reads only: "Inspection of Unit today Billeting Papers Forwarded to B.R.O. Hdqtrs First Army. Admitted 2 Horses to M.V.S." The section was at Fenain (near Valenciennes) when the fighting stopped.
- After the Armistice the section marched east into Belgium with the 1st Canadian Division's advance to the Rhine: Fenain to Aubry (13 November), Elouges (14 November), Flenu (15 November), Neufvilles Station (18 November), Braine-le-Comte (21 November), Thines (23 November), Gembloux (25 November), Vezin (27 November) and Gives (30 November, near Liège). No battle detail is recorded — only veterinary work, movements and administration.
- Case numbers run from 923 (carried over from October) to 975 (19 November). No horses are recorded as destroyed or dying in November; the month's work was transfers to the C.C.V.E.S., admissions, and collecting remounts and stray animals — 11 remounts were collected at Neufvilles on 19 November, and 5 stray mules were collected from the Mairie at Hasnon on 10 November.
- Horses left behind on the line of march (with the names and addresses of the civilians holding them) are a new feature of this month: 6 horses with Mr. Raymond Demoulin, No. 19 Rue de. Garn... [uncertain], Neufville (20 November); 4 horses with Mgr. Arthur [uncertain], No. 98 Rue de la Gare, Neufvilles Station (21 November); 3 horses with Mr. Leopold Bousqueau [uncertain], Grand Farm, Thines (25 November).
- Personnel: Pte. E. J. MacDonald (No. 48785) rejoined from leave on 6 November. Pte. N. Gunnin [uncertain] (No. 49084) rejoined No. 4 Company, 1st Canadian Divisional Train, with a supply wagon on 8 November; Dvr. L. Lee (No. 35462) is named in the same entry. Pte. J. Chase (No. 34722) and Pte. H. Navey [uncertain] (No. 48793) rejoined from leave on 16 November; Pte. O. F. Reed (No. 12466) and Pte. J. King (No. 799552) likewise on 17 November. Pte. L. James [uncertain] (No. 49754) was admitted to a Field Ambulance sick on 17 November — the only human casualty recorded this month.
- The cover's volume number reads "43" (circled) with "45." written above it; the title page is numbered "43." top right. The contents page's "Diary Text 9 Pages" counts the 9 C. 2118 diary-text pages (pp. 4–12), not the cover, contents or title page.
- No monthly summary table of admissions/evacuations is given for November; the month closes with "Close of War Diary Month Ending November 30th 1918" and the O.C.'s signature.
- No appendices are listed or included for November.
