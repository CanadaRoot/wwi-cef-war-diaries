## Transcription conventions
- Faithful to the original: spelling, punctuation, abbreviations and capitalisation as written (the diary is handwritten throughout, on Army Form C. 2118).
- `[uncertain]` marks a word, name or number that could not be read with confidence — never guessed.

## Notes — March 1917
- The section moved from Barlin to Estree Cauchie (Estrée-Cauchie, Pas-de-Calais) on 8 March 1917, arriving 10.30 AM and taking Billet No. 33. Evacuations continued to be shipped from Barlin station (and once from Frevent Capelle station, 21 March).
- Monthly totals as written: 23 horses on hand at 28 February; 400 admitted in March — more than double February's 175; 252 evacuated to No. 13 Veterinary Hospital, Neufchatel; 30 died at the section; 34 destroyed; 4 returned to units; 103 remaining under treatment at 31 March. The diarist's arithmetic balances (23 + 400 = 423; 252 + 30 + 34 + 4 + 103 = 423).
- The month's heaviest days: 89 horses admitted on 30 March (7 destroyed and 8 died the same day), 53 admitted on 29 March, 44 on 22 March, 32 on 20 March. Debility was the overwhelming cause of death and destruction in March; mange is barely mentioned as a cause this month.
- Sergt. Southern P.W. (No. 34729) was struck off strength on 17 February (back-dated entry, 10 March) and transferred to the C.C.A.C., Shoreham-on-Sea.
- Case No. 4586 (No. 2 Field Company, Canadian Engineers) was destroyed en route to Barlin on 27 March.
- The contents page says "Diary Text 11 Pages" but the volume as scanned has 12 diary-text pages (pp. 3–14); the cover/contents/title pages are pp. 1–3, so the diary-text count should be 12, not 11.
- The cover's volume number is ambiguous: "25" is written on the printed "Volume" line, with "11." written below it. The contents page says "Volume 17-3".
- The March monthly summary is headed "Monthly Horse Statement" and, unlike January and February, has no "Horses Remaining under treatment" line — the closing figure is "Horses Remaining at [Section] Month Ending March 31 — 103".
- No appendices are listed or included for March.
- Uncertain readings in this month: the diarist's initials ("M.G.O.T."); the captain in charge of the 2 March conducting party; the captain rejoining on 6 March; the captain "P. A." rejoining on 26 March; the cause for Case No. 4352 (2 Mar) and Case No. 4508 (21 Mar); the place "Bruay [uncertain]" (14 Mar); the number of the unit for Case No. 4607 (27 Mar); the rank and surnames of the two reinforcements (10 Mar); the word(s) before "Temp attached P.B Men" (17 Mar).
