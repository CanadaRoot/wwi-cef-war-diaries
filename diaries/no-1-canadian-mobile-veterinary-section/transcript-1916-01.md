## Transcription conventions
- Faithful to the original: spelling, punctuation, abbreviations and capitalisation as written (the diary is handwritten throughout, on Army Form C. 2118).
- `[uncertain]` marks a word or name that could not be read with confidence — never guessed.
- Text struck through in the original is shown as `[struck through in original: "..."]`.
- The diary-text pages are Army Form C. 2118, "WAR DIARY or INTELLIGENCE SUMMARY", handwritten in columns: Hour, Date, Place | Summary of Events and Information | Remarks and references to Appendices. The "INTELLIGENCE SUMMARY" heading is struck through on the diary pages.
- Ditto marks (") in the original are expanded in the tables below to the repeated place ("NIEPPE") or the repeated phrase ("Received … sick horses").
- No map coordinates (latitude/longitude) are given in the diary; the section remained at Nieppe throughout January 1916.
- "Received … sick horses" is the section's daily intake of sick or wounded horses from units of the 1st Canadian Division; "Evacuated … to No. 13 Veterinary Hospital, NEUFCHATEL" is transfer to the veterinary hospital at Neufchâtel; "Issued … cured" is horses returned fit for duty; "Destroyed" is horses destroyed (put down).

## Notes — January 1916
- No entry is given for 28 January 1916 — the diary jumps from 27 to 29 January.
- Monthly totals (as written): 211 sick horses received; 199 evacuated to No. 13 Veterinary Hospital, Neufchâtel; 6 issued cured; 3 died; 3 destroyed.
- The remarks column on p. 3 is initialled throughout with what appears to be "A.E.C." — the hand is stylised and the reading is not certain; it matches the initials of Capt. A. E. Coulombe, who took command on 24 January, though the initials appear from 1 January. The remarks column on p. 4 (17–31 January) is blank.
- "NEUFCHATEL" is Neufchâtel (Neufchâtel-Hardelot area, France), site of No. 13 Veterinary Hospital.
- No appendices are listed or included for January.
