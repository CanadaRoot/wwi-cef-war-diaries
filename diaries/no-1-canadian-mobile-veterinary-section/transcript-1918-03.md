## Transcription conventions
- Faithful to the original: spelling, punctuation, abbreviations and capitalisation as written (the diary is handwritten throughout, except for the typed cover and contents pages).
- `[uncertain]` marks a word or name that could not be read with confidence — never guessed.
- Text struck through in the original is shown as `[struck through in original: "..."]`.
- The diary-text pages are Army Form C. 2118, "WAR DIARY or INTELLIGENCE SUMMARY", handwritten in columns: Place | Date | Hour | Summary of Events and Information | Remarks and references to Appendices.
- No map coordinates (latitude/longitude) are given in the diary.
- "Admitted ... to M.V.S." = animals received into the Section for treatment. "Evacuated ... to No. 14 Veterinary Hospital" = animals sent on to the veterinary hospital at Abbeville. "Treated at Horse Dip" = mange dipping.

## Notes — March 1918
- The Section was at Barlin until 27 March, when it received orders to move; it moved to Couturelle on 28 March and on to Fosseux on 30 March, following the 1st Canadian Division's move south during the German spring offensive (begun 21 March). The place is spelled "COUTURELLE" on 28–29 March and "Couturelle" on 30 March.
- Animal totals for the month (as recorded in the diary): admitted to the M.V.S. — 10+2+2+3+6+4+2+7+6+7+14+3+7+5+3+3+7+1+2+2+6+1+6+14+5+2 = 130 horses (no admissions recorded 17 or 28–31 March); evacuated to No. 14 Veterinary Hospital — 19 horses + 7 mules (5 Mar), 13 (8 Mar), 27 + 4 (12 Mar), 11 + 5 (15 Mar), 11 + 1 (19 Mar), 30 + 5 (26 Mar), 2 + 2 (28 Mar) = 113 horses and 24 mules; no animals are recorded as destroyed in March (Case 7723 was sent to Lillers for butchery, 5 Mar).
- Horse-dip throughput: 41 (15 Mar), 720 (17 Mar), 174 (18 Mar), 20 (20 Mar), 38 (21 Mar), 79 (22 Mar) — 1,072 horses dipped in the month.
- Officers: Capt. Vosburgh G. (No. 48511) was granted 14 days' leave 15–29 March; he had rejoined the unit from base on 1 March.
- Personnel: Sgt. Gauthier F. J. (No. 48750) rejoined from 1 Canadian D.F.P.S. on 3 March after his 28-day Field Punishment sentence (3 Feb). Sergt. Hamilton G. C. (No. 16501) was granted leave 9 March and rejoined 25 March. Sgt. Turner I. (No. 49754) reported as a reinforcement from the Canadian Veterinary Hospital Base (13 Mar). No. 7228 is given as "Pte. Muir T. Smith" (22 Feb) and as "Sgt. Muir R. A." (21 Mar). The 25 March entry names "No. 48538 Sgt. Cults [uncertain] A. G." with no action stated.
- The weekly return form is written "A-2000" / "A.2000" throughout March.
- "Canine Certificate" (with the B.158 Officers Monthly Nominal Roll) appears again on 31 March, as on 31 January and 28 February — transcribed as written, though the reading remains uncertain.
- The contents page says "Diary Text 12 Pages" — the diary text is in fact 12 pages (pp. 4–15).
- No appendices are listed or included for March.
