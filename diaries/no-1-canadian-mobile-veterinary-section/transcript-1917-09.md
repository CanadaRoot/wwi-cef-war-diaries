## Transcription conventions
- Faithful to the original: spelling, punctuation, abbreviations and capitalisation as written (the diary is handwritten throughout, on Army Form C. 2118).
- `[uncertain]` marks a word or name that could not be read with confidence — never guessed.
- Text struck through in the original is shown as `[struck through in original: "..."]`.
- The diary-text pages are Army Form C. 2118, "WAR DIARY or INTELLIGENCE SUMMARY", handwritten in columns: Place | Date | Hour | Summary of Events and Information | Remarks and references to Appendices. The "INTELLIGENCE SUMMARY" heading is not struck through on these pages.
- The Place column is written once per page (and once more on p. 8, where the place changes mid-page); it is repeated in the tables below for each entry on that page.
- The remarks column is initialled throughout with what appears to be "A.E.C." — the hand is stylised and the reading is not certain; it matches the initials of Capt. A. E. Coulombe, the officer commanding.
- The printed form footer on the diary-text pages reads: "2353 Wt. W2544/1454 700,000 5/15 D. D. & L. A.D.S.S./Forms/C. 2118."
- No map coordinates (latitude/longitude) are given in the diary, except the map reference "Sheet 36 B N.E. Q 24. d. 7.7." given for the new camp on 13 September.
- "Admitted … horses to M.V.S." is the section's daily intake of sick or wounded horses; "Evacuated … to No. 22 Veterinary Hospital" or "to No. 7 Veterinary Hospital, Forges-les-Eaux" is transfer onward by rail. "I/c" is in charge; "Re" is regarding; "d/-" is dated; "Dep." is the diarist's abbreviation, presumably for Depot.

## Notes — September 1917
- Monthly totals (added from the daily entries; no monthly summary table is given this month, unlike October 1916): 228 horses admitted to the section during September. 221 horses and 14 mules were evacuated: 32 horses on 2 September, 16 horses and 1 mule on 3 September, 31 horses and 1 mule on 5 September, 12 horses and 3 mules on 6 September, 19 horses and 2 mules on 9 September and 15 horses and 1 mule on 13 September, all from Vermy [uncertain] Les Mines Station to No. 22 Veterinary Hospital (Abbeville named on 9 September); and 48 horses and 4 mules on 21 September, 20 horses and 2 mules on 25 September and 28 horses on 28 September, all from Barlin Station to No. 7 Veterinary Hospital, Forges-les-Eaux. No horses are recorded as died or destroyed this month.
- The section was at Mazingarbe, Fosse 2, 1–12 September. On 12 September one N.C.O. and 5 other ranks went ahead to the new camp; the section moved on 13 September to the Chateau Grounds, Bouvigny-Boyeffles (map reference "Sheet 36 B N.E. Q 24. d. 7.7."), where it remained to month's end. Construction of the camp was still in progress on 23 September.
- Personnel: Capt. Coulombe rejoined from leave on 5 September. Sergt. Wynn (No. 41647) was acquitted at a Field General Court Martial on 2 September, Tpr. Wood (No. 49765) having attended as a witness from the Canadian Veterinary Hospital, Havre; both men then reported to that hospital for duty on 5 September. Tpr. Ohell (No. 183331) was granted 10 days' leave 2–12 September, failed to rejoin, was reported to the Officer i/c Remounts, London, and the D.A.A.G. Base on 22 September, and was struck off strength on 26 September on admission to hospital in England (Part II Order No. 45, 24-9-17). Three men — Tpr. Fournier (No. 48814), Tpr. Gauthier (No. 48750) and Tpr. Staines (No. 48198) — were detached for duty to the Canadian Corps Veterinary Detachment on 15 September; Pte. Post (No. 195648) reported to the Officer i/c Canadian Corps Veterinary Detachment on 14 September; Sergt. Hamilton (No. 16581) reported for duty at Fosse 10 Horse Depot on 16 September; Tpr. McRae (No. 1278) was temporarily attached to No. 1 Coy., 1 Canadian Divisional Train, on 12 September; Tpr. Winham (No. 48612) was admitted to No. 6 Casualty Clearing Station on 12 September; Tpr. Merritt (No. 48172) was on leave 18–28 September; S/Sergt Pouleau (No. 23730) rejoined from leave on 4 September and Tpr. Post rejoined from leave on 10 September.
- Horses and mules: stray mule Case No. 6318 was issued to the 7th Battalion on 7 September; stray mule Case No. 6902 was received from the 18th Canadian Infantry Battalion on 22 September and collected by 1 Canadian D.A.C. on 27 September. Horse hides were received from the 88th Battery on 6 September and forwarded to the Base Veterinary Hospital on 9 September. Ambulance cases collected: No. 6727 (8th Army Brigade Ammunition Column, CFA, 1 September), No. 6813 (35th Battery, 10th Brigade, CFA, 5 September), No. 6904 (3rd Field Company, Canadian Engineers, Bully Grenay, 22 September) and No. 6926 (10th Battalion, 2nd Infantry Brigade, at Barlin, 26 September).
- The recurring weekly returns are the A-2000 (Class Animals, Mange State, horses collected from inhabitants), the Weekly Ration Statement, the Present Strength Return, the Weekly Offence Report, and the AFB 213 Location Report. A muster parade was held on 25 September. A return of Saskatchewan personnel qualified to vote was rendered on 9 September.
