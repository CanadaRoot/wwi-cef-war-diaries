## Transcription conventions
- Faithful to the original: spelling, punctuation, abbreviations and capitalisation as written (the diary is handwritten throughout, except for the typed cover and contents pages).
- `[uncertain]` marks a word or name that could not be read with confidence — never guessed.
- Text struck through in the original is shown as `[struck through in original: "..."]`.
- The diary-text pages are Army Form C. 2118, "WAR DIARY or INTELLIGENCE SUMMARY", handwritten in columns: Place | Date | Hour | Summary of Events and Information | Remarks and references to Appendices.
- No map coordinates (latitude/longitude) are given in the diary.
- "Admitted ... to M.V.S." = animals received into the Section for treatment. "Evacuated" = animals sent on, in early April to No. 14 Veterinary Hospital, Abbeville, and from 16 April to the Canadian Corps Veterinary Evacuating Station (C.C.V.E.S.).

## Notes — April 1918
- The Section was at Fosseux 1–7 April, then moved to Eturn on 8 April (spelled "Eturn" in the place column, "ETURN" on 8 and 25 April) on orders received 7 April.
- Evacuation destinations changed mid-month: evacuations went from Agnes-les-Duisans to No. 14 Veterinary Hospital, Abbeville, up to 8 April; from 16 April animals were evacuated to the Canadian Corps Veterinary Evacuating Station (C.C.V.E.S.).
- Animal totals for the month (as recorded in the diary): admitted to the M.V.S. — 4+2+4+1+2+4+8+7+1+2+6+3+7+3+4+2 stray mules = 58 horses and 4 mules (no admissions recorded 1, 8–9, 13–14, 18–19, 21, 25–30 April); evacuated — to No. 14 Veterinary Hospital 6 (3 Apr), 6 (5 Apr), 6 horses + 1 mule (8 Apr); to C.C.V.E.S. 22 (16 Apr), 4 (17 Apr), 6 (18 Apr), 7 + 1 (19 Apr), 6 + 1 (20 Apr), 11 + 1 (24 Apr), 1 horse + 2 mules (27 Apr), 4 (28 Apr), 7 (30 Apr) = 86 horses and 6 mules in total; destroyed — Case 865 (10 Apr, shrapnel wound to head, jaw and neck, unfit for further service).
- The war's effect reaches the diary: Case 865 was destroyed for a shrapnel wound, and Sgt. Currie L. (No. 40062) was admitted to No. 8 Casualty Clearing Station (7 Apr). No. 40062 differs from the "No. 40012 Pte. Currie L." of 20 January — transcribed as written in each case.
- Personnel: Capt. Vosburgh G. (No. 48511) rejoined from leave 1 April and rejoined from base with a conducting party 5 April. Sergt. Hamilton G. C. (No. 16501) rejoined from base with a conducting party 1 April and conducted the 8 April evacuation. Sgt. Muir R. A. (No. 7228) rejoined from leave 6 April. Pte. Reed O. J. (No. 124696) reported for duty from Base Depot (11 Apr). Sgt. [uncertain] (No. 49084) was admitted to a Field Ambulance 3 April and rejoined from No. 46 R.A.M.C. 7 April. Six other ranks were detached to the Canadian Corps Veterinary Evacuating Station for duty (16 Apr).
- The place "Fosseux" is spelled "Fosseux" on 1–7 April (and "Fosseux" in the 8 April entry "Section moves From Fosseux"); "Agnes-les-Duisans" is spelled "AGNES-LES-DUISANS" (3 Apr) and "Agnez-Les-Duisans" (5 and 8 Apr).
- "Canine Certificate" (with B.158) appears again on 30 April, as on 31 January, 28 February and 31 March — transcribed as written, though the reading remains uncertain.
- The contents page says "Diary Text 10 Pages" — the diary text is in fact 10 pages (pp. 4–13).
- No appendices are listed or included for April.
