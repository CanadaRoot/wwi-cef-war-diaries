## Transcription conventions
- Faithful to the original: spelling, punctuation, abbreviations and capitalisation as written (the diary is handwritten throughout).
- `[uncertain]` marks a word or name that could not be read with confidence — never guessed.
- Text struck through in the original is shown as `[struck through in original: "..."]`. No struck-through text was observed in December 1917.
- The diary-text pages are Army Form C. 2118, "WAR DIARY or INTELLIGENCE SUMMARY", handwritten in columns: Place | Date | Hour | Summary of Events and Information | Remarks and references to Appendices. Each page is headed "1st Canadian Mobile Veterinary Section" (left) and "1st Canadian Division" (right), marked "Original" at top left, and carries the printed footer "2353 Wt. W2544/1454 700,000 5/15 D. D. & L. A.D.S.S./Forms/C. 2118."
- Place-column ditto marks are shown as " (ditto).
- Multi-line lists in the original (e.g. the Horse Clip tallies) are joined with semicolons.
- Abbreviations as written: M.V.S. = Mobile Veterinary Section; D.A.D.V.S. = Deputy Assistant Director of Veterinary Services; A.A. & Q.M.G. = Assistant Adjutant and Quartermaster General; D.A.C. = Divisional Ammunition Column; C.F.A. = Canadian Field Artillery; C.C.S. = Casualty Clearing Station; A/O.C. = Acting Officer Commanding; O.R. = Other Ranks; N.F. / O.F. / N.H. / O.H. = near fore / off fore / near hind / off hind (of a horse).

## Notes — December 1917
- **Move:** the unit was at Barlin 1–19 December and moved to Bruay on 20 December, locating there at 10 A.M. Evacuations after the move leave from Bruay Station (21 Dec) and Houdain Station (28 Dec).
- **Horse Clip:** the month's main work after routine admissions was horse clipping. The Clip was prepared 11 December, commenced operations 13 December, and 1,065 horses passed through it 13–19 December (13 Dec: 160; 14 Dec: 248; 16 Dec: 66; 17 Dec: 150; 18 Dec: 283; 19 Dec: 158). Two clipping machines were received from the D.A.D.V.S. on 23 December, and Pte. Post C.E. (No. 195648) reported to 3rd Can. M.V.S. on 28 December as engineer at the Horse Clip.
- **Admissions and evacuations (as stated in the diary):** 142 horses admitted to the M.V.S. on the days an admission figure is given. 123 horses and 18 mules evacuated to veterinary hospitals: 30 horses and 6 mules to No. 22 Veterinary Hospital (4 Dec); 34 horses and 1 mule to No. 22 (7 Dec); 19 horses and 5 mules to No. 7 Veterinary Hospital (14 Dec); 16 horses and 3 mules to No. 7 (18 Dec); 13 horses and 3 mules to No. 14 Veterinary Hospital (21 Dec); 11 horses to No. 14 (28 Dec).
- **Destroyed:** 3 horses destroyed in the month — 2 on 3 December (Cases No. 7170 and No. 7222, sold to the butcher) and 1 on 18 December (Case No. 7373, 5th Can. D.A.C., injury to the off hock).
- **Hides:** 1 mule hide received from No. 1 Section, 59 Division, on 1 December and forwarded to No. 22 Veterinary Hospital; 1 horse hide received from 3rd Can. Railway Troops on 16 December; 1 horse hide forwarded to No. 14 Veterinary Hospital on 18 December.
- **Officers:** the O.C., Capt. E.M. Mlejon [uncertain], was granted 14 days' leave of absence on 12 December and rejoined the unit on 29 December. Capt. N. Souillard [uncertain], 2 Can. Artillery Bde., assumed duties as A/O.C. on 12 December. Capt. Vosburgh G. was in charge of the evacuation party on 18 December.
- **Personnel:** reinforcements reporting 10 December — Tpr. Leonard R. (No. 400925), Tpr. Cregeen S.G. (No. 49652), Tpr. Downing F.H. (No. 513077) from Canadian Veterinary Hospital Base; Tpr. Leonard was admitted to a C.C.S. on 14 December. Corpl. Southern J.H. (No. 34705) and Tpr. Reidy J.P. (No. 484) rejoined the unit from Canadian General Base Depot on 5 December (the two names are bracketed together in the original); 5 other ranks of 1 Cdn. D.A.C. ceased to be attached on rejoining their unit the same day. Dvr. Hutchings F.A. (No. 30080) was admitted to No. 22 C.C.S. on 23 December. Pte. Currie L. (No. 40062), attached from 1 Can. Div. Headquarters, was granted leave to 29 December and rejoined on 31 December. Two different men named Faulkner appear: Bomb. Faulkner F.W. (No. 44089) and Tpr. Faulkner F.J. (No. 48750).
- **Christmas:** Muster Parade held 25 December, with Christmas Dinner at the Section the same day.
- **Uncertain readings:** the O.C.'s surname, read as "Mlejon", appears in the 12 December and 29 December entries and in the signature at the foot of p. 15, but the handwriting is stylised and the reading is not certain. "Souillard" (Capt. N., 12 Dec), "McRae" (Tpr. J., No. 1278, 18 Dec), "Morson" (Tpr. P., No. 49084, 21 Dec) and "Steuns" (Tpr. J.A., No. 48598, 23 Dec) are the best readings of their names but are not certain. "1 case things" (23 and 26 Dec) is as written, sense unclear. "Injury O.H. Past." (21 Dec) is as written — "Past." is presumably an abbreviation. "Paymaster Base Reg. 87." (15 Dec) and "Month Ending Dec 26" (28 Dec) are as read. "Muster Parade State rendered to D.A.A.G. Base." (27 Dec) is as written.
- **Initials:** the Remarks column is initialled "M.G.O.T." as read. Earlier months of this diary (1915) attribute the same flourish to Capt. M. G. O'Gogarty ("M.G.O'G."), confirmed by a typed title page in June 1915 — but the December 1917 flourish ends in a T-like stroke and the month is signed by Capt. E.M. Mlejon as O.C., so it is rendered here as read, "M.G.O.T.", rather than assumed to be O'Gogarty's.
- **Contents page:** "Diary Text 12 Pages" — the 12 diary-text pages are pp. 4–15, as transcribed. No appendices are listed or included for December.
- A tick mark appears in the original after the 22 December "Admitted 2 horses to M.V.S." line.
- No hour is entered in the Hour column on any day in December.
