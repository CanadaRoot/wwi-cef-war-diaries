## Transcription conventions
- Faithful to the original: spelling, punctuation, abbreviations and capitalisation as written (the diary is handwritten throughout, including the cover and title pages).
- `[uncertain]` marks a word or name that could not be read with confidence — never guessed.
- Text struck through in the original is shown as `[struck through in original: "..."]`. No struck-through text occurs in this month.
- The diary-text pages are Army Form C. 2118, "WAR DIARY or INTELLIGENCE SUMMARY", handwritten in columns: Place | Date | Hour | Summary of Events and Information | Remarks and references to Appendices. The "INTELLIGENCE SUMMARY" heading is not struck through on these pages. Each diary-text page is headed "Original / 1st Canadian Mobile Veterinary Section WAR DIARY / 1st Canadian Division" and carries the printed form footer "2353 Wt. W2544/1454 700,000 5/15 D. D. & L. A.D.S.S./Forms/C. 2118."
- The Remarks column on every diary-text page is initialled "M.G.O.T." [uncertain] — the diarist's initials as read.
- "Admitted … horses to M.V.S." is the section's daily intake of sick or wounded horses; "Evacuated … to No. 5 / No. 22 Veterinary Hospital" is transfer onward. No horses are recorded as died or destroyed in this month.

## Notes — November 1917
- **Command change (7 Nov):** Capt. Migon E.M. [uncertain] assumed command of the section, vice Capt. Coulombe, who was struck off strength on posting to 1st Canadian Division Headquarters for duty as D.V.S. (D.V.S. order No. 3/3263/17 dated 28-10-17; reported by G.O.C. 1 Can. Div., A.Q. 38-149 dated 11-11-17). Migon signs the close of the month's diary.
- **Movements:** the section was in the Brandhoek area until 12 Nov; moved to Merville on 12 Nov and on to Barlin on 13 Nov, remaining at Barlin for the rest of the month. An Advanced Collecting Post was established at Gouy-Servins on 28 Nov, Sergt. Hamilton G.C. in charge. The section took over Billet No. 9, Rue de Marquise [uncertain], Barlin, vacated by the 59th Division's M.V.S.
- **Horses (tally from the daily entries):** 96 horses admitted to the M.V.S. during the month; 81 horses and 13 mules evacuated (22 horses and 2 mules to No. 5 Veterinary Hospital on 20 Nov; 18 horses and 4 mules to No. 22 Veterinary Hospital on 23 Nov; 15 horses and 4 mules to No. 22 Veterinary Hospital, Abbeville, on 27 Nov; 26 horses and 3 mules to No. 22 Veterinary Hospital on 30 Nov). Sergt. Salmon took 80 sick horses to Base on 8 Nov with 10 men; Sergt. Hamilton took 189 horses from 3 Can. M.V.S. to Base Hospital on 2 Nov. Four horses and 1 mule were received from 33rd M.V.S., 21st Division, on 21 Nov; a H.D. bay mare and about 2 horse hides were received from the 59th Divisional M.V.S. on 17 Nov, and two more horse hides from the same source on 20 Nov (forwarded to Base). Sergt. Vorburgh collected 2 remounts on 29 Nov. No horses are recorded as died or destroyed this month, and no monthly horse summary is given.
- **Men — admissions and strength changes:** No. 48814 Spr. Frumis [uncertain] A.E. and No. 34703 Corpl. Southern J.H. admitted to a Casualty Clearing Station on 1 Nov; No. 21631 Pte. Lee W.J. admitted to 4th Canadian Field Ambulance on 2 Nov and struck off strength on 24 Nov on admission to Canadian General Base Depot; No. 484 Spr. Reidy J.P. admitted to No. 2 C.C.S. on 5 Nov. A statement on the injury to No. 48512 Spr. Wenham W. (17-12-16) was rendered to A.A.G. Base on 11 Nov. No. 40062 Pte. Carrier [uncertain] L. was attached from 1st Canadian Division Headquarters staff on 7 Nov; 1 NCO and 10 other ranks reported from 1 Can. D.A.C. for temporary duty on 30 Nov.
- **Leave:** 14 days' leave granted to Spr. Wenham W. (from 6 Nov), Spr. Chase G. and Spr. Henningan C. (from 7 Nov), and Spr. Murray J.J. (from 13 Nov). Chase and Henningan rejoined 23 Nov; Wenham and Murray rejoined 24 and 29 Nov respectively.
- **Other units named in the month's returns:** on 29 Nov the weekly A-2000 was rendered for the 4th Canadian Divisional School, the 22nd Bath Group and the 77th Labr Coy. as well as the section itself. A Butchery Report for the month ending 22 Nov was rendered on 29 Nov, and monthly returns of carcases and hides and of colonial animals evacuated on 30 Nov.
