## Transcription conventions
- Faithful to the original: spelling, punctuation, abbreviations and capitalisation as written (the diary is handwritten throughout, except for the typed cover and contents pages).
- `[uncertain]` marks a word or name that could not be read with confidence — never guessed.
- Text struck through in the original is shown as `[struck through in original: "..."]`.
- The diary-text pages are Army Form C. 2118, "WAR DIARY or INTELLIGENCE SUMMARY", handwritten in columns: Place | Date | Hour | Summary of Events and Information | Remarks and references to Appendices. From May the form is a new printing (footer: "1640... Wt. W... 750,000 1/17 D. D. & L., Ltd. Forms/C.2118/4").
- No map coordinates (latitude/longitude) are given in the diary.
- In June the diary stops saying "Evacuated" and says "Transferred to No. 17 V.E.S." for animals sent on to No. 17 Veterinary Evacuating Station.

## Notes — June 1918
- The Section stayed at Tincquette for the whole month — its longest unbroken stay since January (Bruay/Barlin).
- Animal totals for the month (as recorded in the diary): transferred to No. 17 V.E.S. — 2 (1 Jun), 2 (2 Jun), 2 (3 Jun), 2 (4 Jun), 7 horses + 2 mules (5 Jun), 2 (7 Jun), 1 (8 Jun), 3 (9 Jun), 5 + 1 (10 Jun), 2 + 1 (11 Jun), 5 (12 Jun), 1 (15 Jun), 2 (16 Jun), 6 (19 Jun), 3 (20 Jun), 1 + 1 (21 Jun), 2 (22 Jun), 5 (24 Jun), 2 (25 Jun), 8 (26 Jun), 1 (28 Jun) = at least 64 horses and 5 mules; the 30 June transfer figure is illegible, the number being obscured by damage to the bottom edge of page 13, so the true total is higher. Admitted to the M.V.S. — 1 stray mule from 3rd Canadian Infantry Battalion (10 Jun, Case No. 7), 1 mule for treatment from 1 Cdn. D.A.C. (17 Jun), 2 horses (23 Jun). Mules were also issued from the Section to units on 16–18 June (1st Cdn. M.G. Batt., 1 Bde. C.F.A., Hdqts 1 Cdn. Divl Train, 3rd Cdn. Inf. Bde., 2 Bde. C.F.A., 1 Cdn. Inf. Bde., 2nd Cdn. Inf. Bde.) — numbers not stated.
- Human casualties: Capt. Vosburgh G. (No. 48511), the Section's second officer, was admitted to No. 7 Casualty Clearing Station on 20 June. The 10 June entry records a "Case No. 11 Contused Wnd. Forearm" collected by the ambulance from 48 Howitzer Battery, 2 Bde. C.F.A. — whether man or horse is not stated; the wording suggests a man. The 21 June entry records Case No. 36, a shrapnel wound, collected from 1st Canadian Machine Gun Battalion.
- The cover is a re-used May cover with the May dates overwritten in handwriting ("June" over "May", "30th June" over "31st May"). The contents page misprints the year as "1916" and the volume as "16-6" (the series to date is 18-1 to 18-5) — transcribed as written.
- "Canine Certificate" (with B.158) appears again on 30 June, as on 31 January, 28 February, 31 March, 30 April and 31 May — transcribed as written, though the reading remains uncertain.
- The contents page says "Diary Text 10 Pages" — the diary text is in fact 10 pages (pp. 4–13).
- The bottom edge of page 13 is damaged/torn, obscuring the number of animals transferred on 30 June (see the 30 June entry above); the month's closing line and signature remain legible.
- No appendices are listed or included for June.
