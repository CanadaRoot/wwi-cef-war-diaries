## Transcription conventions
- Faithful to the original: spelling, punctuation, abbreviations and capitalisation as written (the diary is handwritten throughout, except for the typed contents page).
- `[uncertain]` marks a word or name that could not be read with confidence — never guessed.
- Text struck through in the original is shown as `[struck through in original: "..."]`.
- The diary-text pages are Army Form C. 2118, "WAR DIARY or INTELLIGENCE SUMMARY", handwritten in columns: Hour, Date, Place | Summary of Events and Information | Remarks and references to Appendices. On the December diary-text pages the printed words "INTELLIGENCE SUMMARY" in the heading are struck through.
- No map coordinates (latitude/longitude) are given in the diary.
- "Issued … cured" means a recovered horse was returned to its unit.
- Ditto marks (") in the Place and Summary columns are expanded as the repeated place or words in the tables below, following the diary's own shorthand.

## Notes — December 1915
- No entries for 11 or 25 December: the diary jumps from 10 to 12 December, and from 24 to 26 December.
- Places: the Section stayed at Nieppe all month, moving on 10 December to a new billet on the opposite side of the road, about 1 kilometre nearer Bailleul.
- Horses: 78 sick horses received during December; 72 evacuated to No. 13 Veterinary Hospital, Neufchatel — the evacuation hospital changes from No. 10 to No. 13 in this month (first on 3 December); 4 issued cured; 1 died; 2 of the Section's own horses destroyed on 28 December after reacting to the mallein test (glanders).
- The 26 December entry begins with ditto marks whose referent is unclear; it is transcribed as the evacuation entry only.
- The month is signed by a new officer, Capt. J. H. Burnet, Acting O.C., in place of Capt. M. G. O'Gogarty — although the daily entries are still initialled "M.G.O'G.".
- The contents page's "Volume 15-12" is the LAC filing volume; the cover's "Volume [struck through: 8] 10" is the diary's own volume number.
- No appendices are listed or included for December.
