## Transcription conventions
- Faithful to the original: spelling, punctuation, abbreviations and capitalisation as written (the diary is handwritten throughout, except for the typed contents page).
- `[uncertain]` marks a word or name that could not be read with confidence — never guessed.
- Text struck through in the original is shown as `[struck through in original: "..."]`.
- The diary-text pages are Army Form C. 2118, "WAR DIARY or INTELLIGENCE SUMMARY", handwritten in columns: Hour, Date, Place | Summary of Events and Information | Remarks and references to Appendices. On the April diary-text pages the printed words "INTELLIGENCE SUMMARY" in the heading are struck through.
- No map coordinates (latitude/longitude) are given in the diary.
- "Issued … cured" means a recovered horse was returned to its unit.

## Notes — April 1915
- No entry for 16 April: the diary jumps from 15 to 17 April.
- Places: Croix du Bac until 5 April; Steenvoorde 6–17 April; Elverdinghe 18–22 April; shelled out of the Elverdinghe billet on the evening of 22 April and billeted on the Vlamertinghe–Poperinghe road; Reninghelst 25–26 April; Poperinghe from 26 April. The moves of 22–26 April are the Section falling back during the Second Battle of Ypres (the diary does not name the battle).
- Horses: 147 sick or wounded horses received during April (including 4 received from the advanced portion on 26 April); 2 died, 3 destroyed; 119 evacuated to the Base Veterinary Hospital (18 + 19 + 14 + 8 + 8 + 5 + 12 + 8 + 27); 36 issued cured. The 29–30 April receipts (18 + 25 sick and wounded) are the heaviest of the month, reflecting the Ypres fighting.
- On 20 April the Section received horses from units other than the Canadian Division: 1 Canadian A.S.C., 1 Algerian [uncertain] and 1 Zouave.
- The diarist's initials "M.G.O'G." (M. G. O'Gogarty) appear at the end of most daily entries; the month is signed "M. G. O'Gogarty Capt., O.C. Canadian Mobile Veterinary Section". June's typed title page gives the name as "Captain M. G. O' Gogarty", confirming the reading.
- The contents page's "Volume 15-4" is the LAC filing volume; the cover's "Volume 2." is the diary's own volume number.
- No appendices are listed or included for April.
