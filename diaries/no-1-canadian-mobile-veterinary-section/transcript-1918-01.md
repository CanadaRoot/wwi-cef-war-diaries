## Transcription conventions
- Faithful to the original: spelling, punctuation, abbreviations and capitalisation as written (the diary is handwritten throughout, except for the typed cover and contents pages).
- `[uncertain]` marks a word or name that could not be read with confidence — never guessed.
- Text struck through in the original is shown as `[struck through in original: "..."]`.
- The diary-text pages are Army Form C. 2118, "WAR DIARY or INTELLIGENCE SUMMARY", handwritten in columns: Place | Date | Hour | Summary of Events and Information | Remarks and references to Appendices.
- No map coordinates (latitude/longitude) are given in the diary; the unit remained at Bruay throughout January 1918.
- "Admitted ... to M.V.S." = animals received into the Section for treatment. "Evacuated ... to No. 14 Veterinary Hospital Abbeville" = animals sent on to the veterinary hospital.

## Notes — January 1918
- Page 6 (6–9 January) was missing from the first transcription pass and was recovered and inserted 2026-10-26 (Eighth G, run 4) from page image `page-0426-January_1918_p._6.jpg` (zoom crops `zoom-jan1918-p6-top.jpg`, `zoom-jan1918-p6-mid.jpg`); the "Sergt. Hamilton" conducting-party line and the AFB. 122 line for Dvr. Beltley A. remain partly [uncertain].
- The Section was at Bruay 1–22 January, then moved to Barlin on 23 January (Billet No. 9, Rue des [uncertain]) on orders received 22 January. Evacuations continued from Houdain station (1–15 January) and then Barlin station (22–29 January) to No. 14 Veterinary Hospital, Abbeville.
- Animal totals for the month (as recorded in the diary): admitted to the M.V.S. — 6+4+3+2+3+2+6+1+4+2+17+1+2+5+2+5+14+6+9+8+8+7+2 = 119 horses (plus 31 January's figure, obscured by the form footer); evacuated to No. 14 Veterinary Hospital — 8 horses + 1 mule (1 Jan), 17 + 2 (8 Jan), 7 + 1 (11 Jan), 17 (15 Jan), 13 (22 Jan), 16 (25 Jan), 32 + 3 (29 Jan) = 110 horses and 7 mules; destroyed — Case 7458 (14 Jan, open joint), Case 7476 (15 Jan, enteritis), Cases 7497 and 7501 (25 Jan), Case 7540 (30 Jan, quittor / open joint pastern / mange / cellulitis).
- The horse dip (mange treatment) was in heavy use after the move: 56 horses (23 Jan), 105 horses + 25 mules (24 Jan), 110 (25 Jan), 77 (26 Jan), 21 (27 Jan), 78 + 2 (28 Jan), 65 (29 Jan), 347 + 4 (30 Jan), 8 (31 Jan).
- Officers named: Capt. Vosburgh G. (conducting evacuation parties 1, 15 and 29 January). The O.C. (initials "M.G.O.T." [uncertain]) took on veterinary-officer duties for the 5th and 6th Sections, 1st Pontoon Park (29 Jan) and for the 9th Chinese Labour Coy., 1st and 4th Canadian Army Troops Coys. and a Canadian [uncertain] School (30 Jan).
- Personnel: Sgt. Gauthier F. J. is given as No. 48753 on 2 January but as No. 48750 on 17 January; Pte. Leonard W. is given as No. 400925 on 9 and 14 January. Sgt. Gibbs J. L. (No. 48754) and Sgt. Cregeen S. G. (No. 49652) were granted 14 days' leave. Dvr. Beltley A. [uncertain] (No. 12969) ceased attachment to the M.V.S. on rejoining 1st Canadian Divisional Train (10 Jan) and his AFB 122 followed (9 and 14 Jan).
- The weekly return form number is written variously as "A-2800" (3, 10, 17 Jan), "A-2000" (24 Jan) and "AFA-2000" (31 Jan); "A-2111" is also used (17 Jan). All transcribed as read.
- The 26 January entry says the Weekly Ration Return went to "Q. Branch Hdqts 3 Can. Division" — transcribed as written, though the Section belongs to the 1st Canadian Division.
- The contents page says "Diary Text 13 Pages" — the diary text is in fact 13 pages (pp. 4–16).
- No appendices are listed or included for January.
