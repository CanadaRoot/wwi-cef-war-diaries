## Transcription conventions
- Faithful to the original: spelling, punctuation, abbreviations and capitalisation as written (the diary is handwritten throughout, on Army Form C. 2118).
- `[uncertain]` marks a word, name or number that could not be read with confidence — never guessed.
- Text struck through in the original is shown as `[struck through in original: "..."]`.
- The diary-text pages are Army Form C. 2118, "WAR DIARY or INTELLIGENCE SUMMARY", handwritten in columns: Place | Date | Hour | Summary of Events and Information | Remarks and references to Appendices. The form heading carries a handwritten "1st Canadian Mobile Veterinary Section / 1st Canadian Division" at the top.

"R.T.O." = Railway Transport Officer. "B.R.O." = Base Records Office. "A.A. + Q.M.G." = Adjutant-General and Quartermaster-General (branch). "C.F.A." = Canadian Field Artillery. "R.F.A." = Royal Field Artillery. "M.G.C." = Machine Gun Corps. "TPR" = Trooper.

## Notes — June 1917
- The section stayed at Ecurie (Écurie, Pas-de-Calais) for the whole month — no move.
- On 10 June Capt. Coulombe A.E. assumed the duties of A/ADVS, 1 Canadian Division, and Capt. Southam J.H. assumed the duties of A/O.C. of the section. Southam was granted leave of absence to 21 June and rejoined the unit from leave on 23 June. Capt. Vosburgh G. and Capt. Salmon J. also served with the section during the month.
- Like April and May, June has NO monthly horse statement/summary table: the month closes on p. 13 with the 30 June entries and Capt. Coulombe's signature only.
- Pte. Lessard W. (No. 48766) was struck off strength on 6 June, sick, posted to General Depot, Shoreham. Ptes. Fournier A.E. and Merritt A.J. were admitted to field ambulances (16 and 17 June) and rejoined the unit on 25 June. Pte. Gauthier F.J. rejoined the unit from the Divisional Train on 8 June and was temporarily attached back to it on 15 June; Pte. Henningan C. rejoined from the Divisional Train on 15 June — both are now written "TPR" (Trooper).
- The section was now also filing the weekly A-2000 for the 182nd Tunnelling Company, R.E. (29 June).
- The contents page says "Diary Text 10 Pages" and the volume as scanned has exactly 10 diary-text pages (pp. 4–13) — this month the count matches.
- The cover's volume number is ambiguous: "26" is written (and circled) on the printed "Volume" line, with "28" written above it and "10." below it. The contents page says "Volume 17-6".
- No appendices are listed or included for June.
- Uncertain readings in this month: the diarist's initials ("W.E.C."); Capt. Southam's initials on 10 June (read "J.H." from the 11 June entry); the surname of Pte. Cook (Pay Book No. 49128, 6 June); the surname and posting for Pte. Lessard W. (6 June); the sergeants' surnames (4, 12, 19, 22, 24, 27, 30 June); the statement number for Pay Book No. 49128 (6 June); "65- Batty" (9 June).
