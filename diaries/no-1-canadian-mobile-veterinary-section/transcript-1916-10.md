## Transcription conventions
- Faithful to the original: spelling, punctuation, abbreviations and capitalisation as written (the diary is handwritten throughout, on Army Form C. 2118).
- `[uncertain]` marks a word or name that could not be read with confidence — never guessed.
- Text struck through in the original is shown as `[struck through in original: "..."]`.
- The diary-text pages are Army Form C. 2118, "WAR DIARY or INTELLIGENCE SUMMARY", handwritten in columns: Place | Date | Hour | Summary of Events and Information | Remarks and references to Appendices. The "INTELLIGENCE SUMMARY" heading is not struck through on these pages.
- The Place column is written once per page; it is repeated in the tables below for each entry on that page. Where the Place column lists a route (21–23 October), the place given is the day's destination.
- The remarks column is initialled throughout with what appears to be "A.E.C." — the hand is stylised and the reading is not certain; it matches the initials of Capt. A. E. Coulombe, the officer commanding.
- No map coordinates (latitude/longitude) are given in the diary.
- "Admitted … horses to M.V.S." is the section's daily intake of sick or wounded horses; "Evacuated … to No. 7 Veterinary Hospital, Forges-les-Eaux" is transfer onward by rail; "Destroyed" is horses destroyed (put down). "V.O." is Veterinary Officer; "D.V.S.+R." is the Director of Veterinary Services and Remounts.

## Notes — October 1916
- Monthly totals (as written on p. 15): 4 horses remaining from September; 370 admitted during October (374 in total); 356 evacuated, 6 died, 3 destroyed, 9 remaining under treatment (374 accounted for).
- The section was on the Somme until 18 October, then moved north in stages: Albert to Canaples (18 October, via Val-de-Maison), to Bernaville (20 October), then 21–23 October via F...-le-Grand [uncertain] and Le Cauroy to St. Michel (billet No. 47 on the St. Pol–St. Michel road), and on 28 October via Noeux-les-Mines, Tincques and Aubigny to Frévent (billet No. 8, taken over from 36th M.V.S.), where it remained to month's end. The 1st Canadian Division was leaving the Somme for the Arras–St. Pol area.
- Winter Time was adopted at 1 A.M. on 1 October (the end of British Summer Time).
- Horses destroyed this month (3, per the monthly report): case 3073 (1 October, 3rd Canadian Division); case 3396 (27 October, suppurating wound of the off fore foot — a stray horse collected from a farm at St. Michel on 24 October). The third destroyed horse is not named in the daily entries. Six horses died at the unit, including case 3326 (12 October, 10th Field Ambulance, 3rd Canadian Division — shrapnel wounds of jaw and neck).
- Evacuations went to No. 7 Veterinary Hospital, Forges-les-Eaux (by rail from Albert or Héricourt), except for 4 horses sent from St. Pol station to No. 22 Veterinary Hospital, Abbeville, on 27 October, and 33 horses and 2 mules sent from Ber... station to Neufchatel on 31 October. The November entries name Barlin station repeatedly, confirming that the 31 October "Ber... station" is Barlin station.
- On 4 October the section moved back to its old billet on the Albert–Bouzincourt road (Brickfield Area) and relieved the 2nd Canadian Mobile Veterinary Section, receiving 3 horses from it. On 11 October the 4th Canadian Mobile Veterinary Section arrived in the area; on 18 October case 3371 (pneumonia) was transferred to it. On 28 October the section took over a billet from 36th M.V.S. and received 5 lame horses from it.
- Stray and left-behind horses feature this month: case 3213 from a French civilian at Warloy-Baillon (Delaunay Faucon, No. 6 Rue Jean Juteau); case 3286 left behind by the 21st Infantry Battalion, 2nd Canadian Division, at Bouzincourt; two stray horses from the 15th Battalion, 3rd Canadian Infantry Brigade (2 October); case 3396, a stray collected from Henry Simeon Valhuon's farm at St. Michel; case 3399, a stray from a French motor school.
- Personnel: Corpl. G. C. Hamilton returned on 3 October after instruction in clipping machines at No. 7 Veterinary Hospital. Corpl. Vorburgh (No. 48511) and Trooper J. A. Staines (No. 48098) reported for duty from 1st Canadian D.A.C. on 11 October. Driver G. Forsythe (No. 41248) and his supply waggon rejoined No. 4 Coy., 1st Canadian Divisional Train, on 11 October, reported back to the unit on 17 October, and went back to No. 4 Coy. on 30 October. Corpl. J. Salmon (No. 34730) and Pte. W. Lessard (No. 48766) reported back for duty on 30 October after their time in hospital. Pte. Fournier (No. 48814) and Pte. [uncertain] (No. 19273) were admitted to No. 3 Field Ambulance on 29 October. Trooper A. [uncertain] (No. 23674) reported to the unit on 31 October. Capt. Coulombe assumed duties as Veterinary Officer to 2nd Reserve Park while in the area.
