## Transcription conventions
- Faithful to the original: spelling, punctuation, abbreviations and capitalisation as written (the diary is handwritten throughout, except for the typed cover and contents pages).
- `[uncertain]` marks a word or name that could not be read with confidence — never guessed.
- Text struck through in the original is shown as `[struck through in original: "..."]`.
- The diary-text pages are Army Form C. 2118, "WAR DIARY or INTELLIGENCE SUMMARY", handwritten in columns: Place | Date | Hour | Summary of Events and Information | Remarks and references to Appendices.
- No map coordinates (latitude/longitude) are given in the diary; the unit remained at Barlin throughout February 1918.
- "Admitted ... to M.V.S." = animals received into the Section for treatment. "Evacuated ... to No. 14 Veterinary Hospital" = animals sent on to the veterinary hospital at Abbeville. "Passed thru/treated at Horse Dip" = mange dipping.

## Notes — February 1918
- The Section remained at Barlin all month; evacuations went from Barlin station to No. 14 Veterinary Hospital, Abbeville.
- Animal totals for the month (as recorded in the diary): admitted to the M.V.S. — 4+2+7+3+4+13+4+4+23+3+11+5+2+8+6+2+5+9+8+9+2+2+5+7+7+7+2 = 164 horses (no admissions recorded on 16 February); evacuated to No. 14 Veterinary Hospital — 14 horses + 1 mule (1 Feb), 13 + 1 (5 Feb), 17 + 4 (8 Feb), 32 + 6 (12 Feb), 31 + 6 (20 Feb), 28 + 6 (26 Feb) = 135 horses and 24 mules; destroyed — Case 7594 (10 Feb, 2nd Canadian Heavy Battery) only.
- Horse-dip (mange treatment) throughput was very high: 34 (11 Feb), 354 (12 Feb), 504 (13 Feb), 147 (14 Feb), 52 (15 Feb), 32 (16 Feb), 354 (17 Feb), 367 (18 Feb), 33 (19 Feb), 38 horses + 2 mules (20 Feb) — over 1,900 animals dipped in the month.
- Personnel: Sgt. Gauthier F. J. (No. 48750) was sentenced to 28 days' Field Punishment No. 1 for neglect of duty on active service and admitted to 1 Canadian D.F.P.S. (3 Feb). The number 48750 is used consistently in February; January gave 48753 on 2 January and 48750 on 17 January. Sgt. McRae appears as No. 1278, initials J. (4 Jan) and as No. 1277, W. P. (18 Feb) — possibly two men, or a diarist's error. No. 48572 is used for both Sgt. Merritt A. J. (17 Feb) and Sgt. Wenham W. (18 Feb). Sgt. Riely J. P.'s number is written as "484" on both 12 and 28 February.
- Capt. Vosburgh G. rejoined the unit from base on 1 February and conducted the 26 February evacuation; Sergt. Hamilton G. C. conducted the evacuations of 1, 5 and 20 February.
- The weekly return form is written as "A-2000" / "A.2000" throughout February (January also used "A-2800" and "AFA-2000").
- The "Weekly Ration Strength Return" is addressed to "Q Branch Hdqts 3 Can. Div." on 2, 9 and 16 February, while the "Weekly Ration Statement" goes to "Q. Branch Hdqts 1 Can Div." — transcribed as written.
- "Canine certificate" (with the B 158 Nominal Roll of Officers) appears again on 28 February, as on 31 January — transcribed as written, though the reading remains uncertain.
- The contents page says "Diary Text 11 Pages" — the diary text is in fact 11 pages (pp. 4–14).
- No appendices are listed or included for February.
