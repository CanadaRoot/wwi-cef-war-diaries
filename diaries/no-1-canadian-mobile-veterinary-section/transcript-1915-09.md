## Transcription conventions
- Faithful to the original: spelling, punctuation, abbreviations and capitalisation as written (the diary is handwritten throughout, except for the typed contents page).
- `[uncertain]` marks a word or name that could not be read with confidence — never guessed.
- Text struck through in the original is shown as `[struck through in original: "..."]`.
- The diary-text pages are Army Form C. 2118, "WAR DIARY or INTELLIGENCE SUMMARY", handwritten in columns: Hour, Date, Place | Summary of Events and Information | Remarks and references to Appendices. On the September diary-text pages the printed words "INTELLIGENCE SUMMARY" in the heading are struck through on p. 3.
- No map coordinates (latitude/longitude) are given in the diary.
- "Issued … cured" means a recovered horse was returned to its unit.
- Ditto marks (") in the Place and Summary columns are expanded as the repeated place or words in the tables below, following the diary's own shorthand.

## Notes — September 1915
- No entries for 5 or 12 September: the diary jumps from 4 to 6 September, and from 11 to 13 September.
- The 15 September entry is a late addition for 13 September ("on 13.9.15 Received 5 sick horses") — the 13 September entry on p. 3 records only the collection of 2 horses from Renescure.
- Places: the Section stayed at Neuve Eglise for the whole month; it collected horses from Renescure (13 September, left by the Royal Canadian Horse Artillery), Hazebrouck, Erquinghem and Nieppe (from the 50th Division's Mobile Veterinary Section).
- Horses: 89 sick horses received during September (including the 2 collected at Renescure on 13 September and the 5 recorded late for 13 September); 56 evacuated to No. 10 Veterinary Hospital, Neufchatel; 14 issued cured; none recorded as died or destroyed.
- The contents page's "Volume 15-9" is the LAC filing volume; the cover's "Volume 7." is the diary's own volume number.
- No appendices are listed or included for September.
