## Transcription conventions
- Faithful to the original: spelling, punctuation, abbreviations and capitalisation as written (the diary is handwritten throughout, except for the typed cover and contents pages).
- `[uncertain]` marks a word or name that could not be read with confidence — never guessed.
- Text struck through in the original is shown as `[struck through in original: "..."]`.
- The diary-text pages are Army Form C. 2118, "WAR DIARY or INTELLIGENCE SUMMARY", handwritten in columns: Place | Date | Hour | Summary of Events and Information | Remarks and references to Appendices. From May the form is a new printing (footer: "1640... Wt. W... 750,000 1/17 D. D. & L., Ltd. Forms/C.2118/4").
- No map coordinates (latitude/longitude) are given in the diary; map-sheet references are given for the moves on 6, 20 and 25 May.
- "Admitted ... to M.V.S." = animals received into the Section for treatment. "Evacuated" = animals sent on to a veterinary evacuating station — in May the Canadian Corps Veterinary Evacuating Station (C.C.V.E.S.), then X Corps V.E.S., then No. 17 V.E.S.

## Notes — May 1918
- The Section moved twice: from Eturn to Etrée Wamin on 6 May (Billets No. 46 and 49, map reference Sheet 51 S H 83. B. 9. 8. [uncertain]), then to Le Cauroy on 20 May (map reference N.6.A 3.9 Sheet 51 S [uncertain]), then to Tincquette on 25 May (map reference C 4. C 7.6 Sheet 51 S [uncertain]). The place is spelled "ETREE WAMIN" in the place column and "Etre Wamin" in the text; "LE CAUROY" in the place column and "Le Cauroy" in the text.
- Evacuation destinations changed as the Section moved: C.C.V.E.S. (1–5 May), X Corps V.E.S. (19–24 May), No. 17 V.E.S. (29–31 May).
- Animal totals for the month (as recorded in the diary): admitted to the M.V.S. — 1 horse + 1 mule (2 May), 2 horses + 1 mule from New Zealand Field Artillery (18 May), 2 horses (21 May) = 5 horses and 2 mules (2 remounts were also received on strength on 1 May); evacuated — to C.C.V.E.S. 2 (1 May), 7 horses + 3 mules (3 May), 3 + 2 (4 May), 3 + 2 (5 May); to X Corps V.E.S. 4 + 1 (19 May), 3 (22 May), 1 mule Case 972 (24 May); to No. 17 V.E.S. 3 (29 May), 4 (31 May) = 29 horses and 9 mules in total; died — Case 955 (4 May, pneumonia).
- Personnel: Sergt. Hamilton G. C. (No. 16501) joined a remount collecting party at Izel-lez-Hameau (15 May). Sgt. Henningham C. [uncertain] (No. 48556) was admitted to No. 2 Canadian Field Ambulance (14 May); his AFB 122 followed on 31 May. Sgt. Doughty J. A. (No. 48541) was transferred to 2nd Canadian M.V.S. (AFB 122, 18 May). Pte. Post C. E. (No. 198648) rejoined the unit from C.C.V.E.S. (15 May).
- The Section's records were forwarded to the Historical Section, Canadian War Records Office, London (29 May).
- "Canine Certificate" (with B.158) appears again on 31 May, as on 31 January, 28 February, 31 March and 30 April — transcribed as written, though the reading remains uncertain.
- The contents page says "Diary Text 9 Pages" — the diary text is in fact 9 pages (pp. 4–12).
- No appendices are listed or included for May.
