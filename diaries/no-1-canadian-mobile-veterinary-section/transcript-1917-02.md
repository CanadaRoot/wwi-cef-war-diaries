## Transcription conventions
- Faithful to the original: spelling, punctuation, abbreviations and capitalisation as written (the diary is handwritten throughout, on Army Form C. 2118).
- `[uncertain]` marks a word, name or number that could not be read with confidence — never guessed.
- Text struck through in the original is shown as `[struck through in original: "..."]`.
- The diary-text pages are Army Form C. 2118, "WAR DIARY or INTELLIGENCE SUMMARY", handwritten in columns: Place | Date | Hour | Summary of Events and Information | Remarks and references to Appendices.
- No map coordinates (latitude/longitude) are given in the diary. The section remained at Barlin throughout February 1917.
- "M.V.S." = Mobile Veterinary Section. "A.D.V.S." = Assistant Director of Veterinary Services. "D.F.P.S." = Divisional Field Punishment Station. "R.T.O." = Railway Transport Officer. "B.R.O." = Base Records Office. "A.A. + Q.M.G." = Adjutant-General and Quartermaster-General (branch). "C.F.A." = Canadian Field Artillery. "U.A.C." = [written as "U.A.C." throughout]. "C.A.V.C." = Canadian Army Veterinary Corps. "L.D." = Light Draught (horse). "R.G.A." = Royal Garrison Artillery.
- Ditto marks (") are transcribed as written.

## Notes — February 1917
- The section remained at Barlin all month; no move.
- Monthly totals as written: 98 horses on hand at 31 January; 175 admitted in February; 202 evacuated to No. 13 Veterinary Hospital, Neufchatel; 16 returned to units; 4 died at the section; 28 destroyed; 23 remaining under treatment at 28 February. The diarist's arithmetic balances (98 + 175 = 273; 202 + 16 + 4 + 28 + 23 = 273).
- The O.C., Capt. A. E. Coulombe, was on leave until 26 February (granted 10 days' leave on 16 February, authority Canadian Corps A 57-7-21); Capt. Vosburgh ran the section in his absence and conducted the evacuation parties.
