## Transcription conventions
- Faithful to the original: spelling, punctuation, abbreviations and capitalisation as written (the diary is handwritten throughout, on Army Form C. 2118).
- `[uncertain]` marks a word or name that could not be read with confidence — never guessed.
- Text struck through in the original is shown as `[struck through in original: "..."]`.
- The diary-text pages are Army Form C. 2118, "WAR DIARY or INTELLIGENCE SUMMARY", handwritten in columns: Place | Date | Hour | Summary of Events and Information | Remarks and references to Appendices. The "INTELLIGENCE SUMMARY" heading is not struck through on these pages.
- The Place column is written once per page; it is repeated in the tables below for each entry on that page. The section remained at Frévent for the whole month.
- This month's diary-text pages are on a different print run of the form ("2353 Wt. W2514/1454 700,000 5/15 D. D. & L. A.D.S.S./Forms/C. 2118."), and the Date column gives the day only, with "Nov. 1916" written at the top of each page.
- The remarks column is initialled throughout with what appears to be "A.E.C." — the hand is stylised and the reading is not certain; it matches the initials of Capt. A. E. Coulombe, the officer commanding.
- No map coordinates (latitude/longitude) are given in the diary.
- "Admitted … horses to M.V.S." is the section's daily intake of sick or wounded horses; "Evacuated … to No. 13 Veterinary Hospital, Neufchatel" is transfer onward by rail from Barlin station; "Destroyed" is horses destroyed (put down) as unfit for further service. "V.O." is Veterinary Officer; "D.V.S.+R." is the Director of Veterinary Services and Remounts; "D.R.L.S." is the Despatch Rider Letter Service.

## Notes — November 1916
- Monthly totals (as written on p. 15): 9 horses remaining under treatment from October; 262 admitted during November (271 in total); 245 evacuated, 9 destroyed, 1 died, 3 returned to unit, 13 remaining under treatment (271 accounted for).
- The section stayed at Frévent for the whole month, evacuating horses by rail from Barlin station to No. 13 Veterinary Hospital, Neufchatel. Barlin station is the station named in the October diary's 31 October entry as "Ber... station" — the November entries confirm the reading.
- Horses destroyed this month (9, per the monthly report, and all nine are named in the daily entries): cases 3453 (broken wind) and 3462 (broken knees), 4 November; cases 3537 (lame off hind, spavined hock) and 3544 (broken wind), 15 November; cases 3598 (debility) and 3601 (lacerated wound of the off hind fetlock), 20 November; case 3527 (lymphangitis), 23 November; case 3656 (necrosis of the coronet), 25 November; case 3675 (contused wound of the off hock), 28 November.
- One horse died at the unit: case 3657 (23 November, admitted as debility; post-mortem showed an abnormal liver with abscess).
- Three horses were returned to their units as fit: case 3399 (7 November, the stray horse collected on 27–28 October, claimed by the 15th Battalion, 3rd Infantry Brigade); case 3617 (27 November, to the 182nd Tunnelling Company, R.E.). The third returned horse is not named in the daily entries.
- Human casualty: Pte. A. S. Young (No. 13696) died of appendicitis at No. 23 Clearing Hospital — the section received the wire on 10 November, two days after he was admitted to No. 3 Field Ambulance on 8 November.
- Discipline: Pte. F. J. Gauthier (No. 48750) and Pte. A. E. Fournier (No. 48814) each pleaded guilty to absence from duty without permission and were sentenced to forfeit 15 days' pay (9 and 10 November). Pte. [uncertain] (No. 19273) pleaded guilty to the same offence and was sentenced to forfeit 20 days' pay (19 November). Pte. C. Henningham (No. 48552) was sentenced to forfeit 5 days' pay for insolence to an N.C.O. (25 November). Court-martial proceedings were held at Camblain-l'Abbé on 16 November in connection with Staff-Sgt. Southern (No. 84703, initials [uncertain]), and Court Martial Proceedings No. 990 were returned to the A.D.V.S. on 28 November.
- Personnel: Pte. A. E. Fournier (No. 48814) returned to duty from No. 3 Field Ambulance on 8 November. Trooper A. [uncertain] (No. 23674) was transferred to the unit (papers received 5 November, authority D.A.G., 3rd Echelon, C.R. No. 58711/CA/, 12-10-16). Pte. P. Papineau (No. 48583) was admitted to No. 3 Field Ambulance on 28 November — the number differs from the Pte. Papineau (No. 48183) whose pay book was dealt with on 10 August. Sergt. P. W. Southern (No. 34729) was granted 10 days' leave of absence on 20 November. Corpl. A. E. Coulombe and 11 other ranks went out collecting horses on 28 November and returned with 3 horses on 30 November. Majors Cutcliffe and Langley visited the section on 8 November.
- The 1st Canadian Division was now under First Army: billeting papers went to B.R.O., Headquarters, First Army (6 and 14 November), whereas in October they went to Second Army. The O.C. continued as Veterinary Officer to 2nd Reserve Park (now given as "2nd Imperial Reserve Park, attached to Canadian Corps").
- The contents page's "Diary Text 13 Pages" counts the 13 C. 2118 diary-text pages (pp. 3–15). The diary-text page p. 3 carries the printed number "19" in the top right corner.
- The initials after Corpl. McRae's name ("U.P.") appear again on 20 November; the reading is uncertain.
- The 30 November entry names "Corpl. Southern J.H." in charge of the conducting party, whereas the 20 November entry names "Sergt. Southern P.W." (No. 34729) — this may be a different man or a diarist's error; both readings are given as written.
- No appendices are listed or included for November.
