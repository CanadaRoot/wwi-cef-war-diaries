## Notes — May 1917
- The section moved on 2 May from the "A.C.Q. Road" station to Ecurie (Écurie, Pas-de-Calais), at map reference P 24 A. 9. 5, Map 51c N.E. — about a mile west of Vimy Ridge. It stayed at Ecurie for the rest of the month.
- Like April, May has NO monthly horse statement/summary table: the month closes on p. 14 with the 31 May entries and Capt. Coulombe's signature only.
- The Vimy aftermath is visible in the casualty causes: shrapnel wounds now appear regularly (Cases No. 5768, No. 5784, No. 5785, No. 5841 gunshot wounds, No. 5649), alongside the usual debility and mange. Case No. 5700 (1st Company, Divisional Train) was destroyed for wounds to both hind legs complicated by tetanus.
- The D.D.V.S. First Army visited on 7 May and the D.V.S. inspected the section on 24 May.
- On 30 May, 42 attached other ranks (21 from No. 12 and 21 from No. 13 Veterinary Hospitals) rejoined their units on the authority of a D.D.V.S. 1st Army letter of 28 May — the Vimy reinforcement detachments were being stood down.
- Horse hides were collected and forwarded in bulk: 33 hides received from No. 5 M.V.S. on 28 May alone; the section also forwarded hides to No. 13 Veterinary Hospital.
- The contents page says "Diary Text 11 Pages" but the volume as scanned has 12 diary-text pages (pp. 3–14); the cover/contents/title pages are pp. 1–3, so the diary-text count should be 12, not 11.
- The cover's volume number is ambiguous: "25" is written on the printed "Volume" line, with "27." written above it and "11." below it. The contents page says "Volume 17-5".
- No appendices are listed or included for May.
- Uncertain readings in this month: the diarist's initials ("W.E.C."); the place from 1–2 May ("A.C.Q. Road Station"); the surname of Pte. No. 49084 (6 May, also 10 Mar); the surnames of Ptes. No. 15836 and No. 22922 (6 May); the wound for Case No. 5701 (6 May); the surnames of Gnr. No. 248431 and Dvr. No. 85545 (8 May); the surname of Pte. Lymburner (9 May); the surname of Dvr. No. 40640 (10 and 15 May); the sergeants' surnames (11, 15, 25 May); "5- [uncertain] M.V.S." (19 May); the captain "E.A." (22 May); the surnames of Ptes. No. 876030 (23 May) and Reg. No. SE/9612 (24 May).
