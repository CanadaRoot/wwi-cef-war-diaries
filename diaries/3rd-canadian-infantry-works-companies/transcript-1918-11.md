# 3rd Canadian Infantry Works Company — War Diary, November 1918

- **Unit:** 3rd Canadian Infantry Works Company
- **LAC Item:** 2004932, RG 9 III-D-3, vol. 5008, reel T-10856, file 710 part 1
- **Month:** 1–30 November 1918 (Volume 3)
- **Pages transcribed:** 0012–0017 (cover p. 1, title page p. 2, diary sheets 1–4, pp. 3–6)
- **Form:** Army Form C.2118 (War Diary). Place column reads "In the Field" throughout. The "INTELLIGENCE SUMMARY" heading is blacked out on each sheet; the diary text is handwritten.
- **Transcribed from:** LAC page images in this folder, 2026-10-28. Spelling, abbreviations and capitalisation follow the original. "O.Rs" = Other Ranks; "Coy" = Company; "R.S.O." = Railway Supply Officer (as written); "Bde" = Brigade; "F.A." = Field Ambulance.

## Notes

- Every day 1–30 November has an entry. 13 November is written as ditto marks under 12 November.
- The 1 November entry records the Armistice-period routine: unloading ammunition trains at Somain railhead, road work, and a party of 50 O.Rs detailed to Somain.
- 11 November 1918 (Armistice Day) is an ordinary working day in the diary — road work, a change of Royal Engineers units supervising the work, and the Group moving — with no mention of the Armistice itself.
- From 14 November the Company stands by for orders, then marches north into Belgium 20–27 November: Rouvignies – St Saulve – Onnaing – Hornu – St Symphorien – Binche – Namur, ending the month clearing out the Cadet School at Namur/Jambes.
- Uncertainties: the abbreviation on 11 November is written "I.C.C. R.E." (319th taking over from 307th). The officer named on 4 November is written "Lt F. N. Buchan" and on 10 November "Lt J. N. Buchan" — the initials differ in the original. The place on 14 November is only partly legible but is confirmed as Rouvignies by the 19–20 November entries. On 28 November the camp is written "2nd Army [uncertain] Assembly Camp" — the word appears to read "P.O.W.". The Remarks column on each sheet carries a circled initial (appears to read "W.C." / "J.B.") — not transcribed as text.
- The month is signed at the foot of sheet 4: "G. E. Jack, Capt., O.C. 3rd Can Infantry Works Coy".

## 1 November 1918

**Place:** In the Field

Weather Fine Cool pt cloudy. Coy at work as usual except party of 50 O.Rs detailed to Somain railhead to offload train of ammunition 370 tons. Lt J. E. Taylor to [uncertain] to prosecute on Court Martial case. Pte E. E. Reynolds accidentally injured unloading train of ammunition. Detail of 100 O.Rs sent to unload train of ammunition at 7 p.m. Somain railhead. [uncertain] ammunition detachment relieved by 2nd [uncertain] Co today.

## 2 November 1918

Weather Cloudy showery cool. Coy to work as usual. Ammunition party of 100 O.Rs resting today. 12 O.Rs to U.K. on leave.

## 3 November 1918

Weather pt cldy warm calm. Party of 60 O.Rs to work at 4 a.m. to offload train of ammd 370 tons Somain railhead. Balance of Company to work as usual. Capt. Cooke, Group Padre, held Church Parade at 11 a.m. with 40 O.Rs in attendance. Conference of Coy Commanders + Group H.Q. held at Group H.Q. at 1500 hours re points of interest.

## 4 November 1918

Weather clear warm fine. Coy at work as usual. Party of 75 O.Rs sent to Somain station to offload train for R.S.O. Party of 75 O.Rs sent to offload train of ammunition at Somain. Both these parties on duty till 2100. Lt F. N. Buchan returned from Course at Boulogne.

## 5 November 1918

Weather Bad. Cool rain steady all day. Whole Company less [uncertain] detachment at work on roads. In evening 75 O.Rs under Lt Bell [uncertain] proceeded to Somain to offload pack trains, worked until 1 a.m.

## 6 November 1918

Weather Raining all day. Cool. Company at work on Roads as usual less Ammunition Detachment and 75 O.Rs resting from night work.

## 7 November 1918

Weather still Bad Raining all day. Whole Company at work on roads less Ammunition Detachment.

## 8 November 1918

Weather cloudy cool showery. Company at work same as yesterday. Capt G. E. Jack returned from 14 day — U.K. leave. 4 O.Rs proceeded to U.K. on leave.

## 9 November 1918

Weather Fine warm. Company at work as usual. All on roads less [uncertain] Dump Party and 10 O.Rs to R.O. & 1st Bde H.Q. Sgt Warren proceeded to [uncertain] Gas School on gas Course.

## 10 November 1918

Weather fine cool. Company at work same as yesterday. Lt J. N. Buchan proceeded on transfer to 5th Cdn. Inf. Wks. Co. 6 O.Rs proceeded on leave to U.K. + 2 on short leave Paris.

## 11 November 1918

Weather cloudy cool. Company at work as yesterday. All on roads less [uncertain] Dump Party. 319th I.C.C. R.E. took over charge of work from 307th I.C.C. R.E.s. Ammunition [uncertain] into effect today. Group moved to [uncertain].

## 12 November 1918

Weather clear cool fine. Company at work as yesterday.

## 13 November 1918

[Ditto marks in the original — same as 12 November.]

## 14 November 1918

5 O.Rs proceeded to U.K. on leave. Orders received at noon for Company to move by road to Rouvignies. Orders cancelled in evening.

## 15 November 1918

Weather clear fine cool. Company kept off work standing by for orders to move. Drill + fitting of equipment during day.

## 16 November 1918

Weather clear fine cold. Company standing by waiting for orders re proceeding North by road to Germany. Drill in Camp. Inspection by O.C. Company full marching order [uncertain] in P.M. 2.-3.-4 Platoons O.K. No 1 Platoon dirty.

## 17 November 1918

Weather cloudy cold. Company paraded to Abscon [uncertain] for Church Parade 11 a.m. Group Padre failed to show up then off for [uncertain] except No 1 Platoon who were re-inspected and found clean by O.C. 4 O.Rs proceeded on leave. Coy still standing by waiting orders to move.

## 18 November 1918

Weather cloudy cold. Some snow. Coy still waiting orders re move. 17 O.Rs proceeded to U.K. on leave. Drill in a.m. Short route march in P.M.

## 19 November 1918

Weather cloudy cold foggy. Company received orders to move to Rouvignies tomorrow etc St Saulve on 21st inst. Coy today training drill short route march etc. 4 O.Rs to England on leave.

## 20 November 1918

Weather foggy cold. Coy moved to Rouvignies by road, stayed overnight in Billets. Own transport.

## 21 November 1918

[Weather as previous day.] Rouvignies to St Saulve by road. In Billet overnight. 45 O.Rs evacuated to 66 F.A. unable to stand march. 10 O.Rs to U.K. on leave. Own transport.

## 22 November 1918

Weather clear frosty. Company moved by road St Saulve to Onnaing. In Billets overnight. Own transport + 2 lorries.

## 23 November 1918

Weather fine clear frosty. Coy moved Onnaing to Hornu by road. Own transport + 2 lorries. 10 O.Rs sent on leave to U.K. Detachment of 1 N.C.O. + 25 O.Rs sent to work for C.C.T.M.T. Co [uncertain].

## 24 November 1918

Weather fine clear frosty. Coy moved by road Hornu to ST SYMPHORIEN — 9 miles — own transport + 2 lorries.

## 25 November 1918

Weather Wet in a.m. fine in p.m. Moved to St Symphorien own transport + 2 lorries from C.C.T.M.T. Coy.

## 26 November 1918

Coy moved to Binche weather fine own transport and 2 lorries.

## 27 November 1918

Weather Misty. Moved to NAMUR by 12 lorries. Orders were received to send guard to localities as follows to guard German Rolling Stock SENEFFE, ARQUENNES + TILLY. These were sent from BINCHE.

## 28 November 1918

Weather Wet. Commenced work on clearing out Cadet School for 2nd Army [uncertain] Assembly Camp. 31 O.R. called out at 19.00 to offload pack train. Corpl Chandler left for back to Italy.

## 29 November 1918

Weather Fine. 31 O.R. stood by until 0100 when they were dismissed by R.S.O. Daily working on Cadet School. Party clearing up new Quarters, O.C. moved to JAMBES. 92 Ave. des Acacias being central to work. [uncertain] 3rd R.C.R.E. called to say that he would want us for work tomorrow.

## 30 November 1918

Weather fine. Work on Cadet School as usual. Party of 80 went to work on Liege Road from Railroad Bridge forward.

*[Signed at foot of sheet 4:] G. E. Jack, Capt., O.C. 3rd Can Infantry Works Coy.*
