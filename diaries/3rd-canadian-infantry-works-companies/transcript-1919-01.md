# War Diary Transcript — January 1919

**Unit:** 3rd Canadian Infantry Works Company
**Source:** Library and Archives Canada, CEF War Diaries, RG 9 III-D-3, vol. 5008, reel T-10856, file 710 part 1; LAC Item 2004932
**Month:** 1–31 January 1919
**Pages transcribed:** 0024–0028 (LAC image numbers)

- p. 0025 — Title page: "WAR DIARY. / 3rd Canadian Infantry Works Company. / From 1st January to 31st January, 1919. / Volume 5. / Diary Text 3 Pages."

- p. 0027 — Army Form C.2118, Sheet II (9–18 January 1919)

**Place throughout:** Aubin St Vaast (Aubin-St-Vaast, France)

## Notes

- The diary is handwritten in ink on Army Form C.2118 ("WAR DIARY or [heading blacked out]"), with columns: Place, Date, Hour, Summary of Events and Information, Remarks and references to Appendices. The Hour column is blank throughout. Each sheet is headed "3rd Canadian Inf. Works Coy", marked "only 1 copy" on Sheet I, and stamped "3RD CANADIAN INFANTRY WORKS COMPANY". The printed "or" heading is blacked out on all three sheets.
- The Remarks column on every entry contains the circled initials "WJC". The month's sheets are signed at the foot of Sheet III: "W. J. [uncertain], Capt., O.C. 3rd Can. Inf. Wks Coy".
- Spelling, grammar and abbreviations are as written (e.g. "has came in", "pt" for partly, "O.Rs" for Other Ranks, "N.C.O's", "F.G.C.M." for Field General Court Martial, "DCM" for Distinguished Conduct Medal, "DSO" for Distinguished Service Order, "C.E.R.C." [uncertain expansion]).
- The diarist's abbreviation "Weather" is written throughout as "Weather" (cursive); "Coy" = Company.
- 28–29 January are entered with ditto marks (") repeating the previous day's entry; they are transcribed as such below.
- 17 January has one line crossed out (illegible).
- The 20 January entry begins "Sunday weather cloudy dull" although 20 January 1919 was a Monday (19 January was the Sunday); transcribed as written.
- No days are missing: all 31 days of January have entries. No casualties are recorded in January. No map coordinates are given.
- Uncertain readings are marked [uncertain] and are listed in the notes below the entries where helpful. Count of [uncertain] flags in this file: 37.

## 1 January 1919

Place: Aubin St Vaast. Weather Cool Cloudy. Rain in evening. Coy still in Same Billets [uncertain] Base [uncertain] waiting demobilization. Muster Parade held. Escort returned from Lille with Pte Dalton but unable to get Pte Kitchener, who has been taken away from Somain. 7th [uncertain] Battn having moved. Orders received to send out all married N.C.O's who have [uncertain] service in [uncertain] who can be spared. Lt P. H. Buchan struck off strength. [uncertain] Taylor who was relieved by Lt Bell has came in to Coy H.Q. to give evidence for summary [uncertain] Cpl Otis H..

## 2 January 1919

Weather cold (part) + cool. Lt Lang E returned with SENEFFE detachment, all very fatigued. Coy Billets at Battn Aubin St Vaast. Orders received O.Rs for demobilization leave on 5th inst.

## 3 January 1919

Weather clear [uncertain]. Coy still around [uncertain] preparing for despatch to Base on 15th. Lt Bell [uncertain] was left to conduct men of No 4 Coy to Etaples.

## 4 January 1919

Weather same as yesterday. Company still standing by for Orders to move off. Orders received that account [uncertain] [uncertain] new Etaples party would not leave until 7th inst.

## 5 January 1919

Weather same as yesterday. Company still standing by for movement orders. Each men proceeding to Base for demobilization given 15 francs canteen money.

## 6 January 1919

Weather [uncertain] warm. Company still standing by. Capt [uncertain] & Otis tried at Aubin St Vaast by F.G.C.M.

## 7 January 1919

Weather same as yesterday. Company standing By waiting arrival of train. Coy Roll Call in p.m. All correct. No trains arrived. Lt Bell [uncertain] returned from conducting duty Etaples.

## 8 January 1919

Weather same as yesterday. 3 N.C.O's 275 O.Rs proceeded to Etaples for demobilization under Lt J.E. Taylor leaving [uncertain] 1530. Lt Lange left to take over temporary command of No 1 Co. Act Capt [uncertain] proceeding to Canada.

## 9 January 1919

Place: Aubin St Vaast. Weather pt cloudy cool. Muster Parade of personnel returned held in a.m. [uncertain] O.R. records etc cleared up & despatch of "Canadian Gazette" shows award of DCM to 46147 Sgt Bagley [uncertain].

## 10 January 1919

Weather pt cloudy cool. Draft of 50 O.Rs for new Coy strength arrived from 1st Divnl wing in a.m. and draft of 100 O.Rs for new Coy strength arrived in p.m. from 2nd Divnl wing.

## 11 January 1919

Weather pt cloudy cool. Draft of 72 O.Rs arrived in a.m. from 1st Divnl wing completing new Company personnel for Burial duties. 7 O.Rs returned from leave.

## 12 January 1919

Weather pt cloudy cool. Coy Parade held in a.m. at 1030. [uncertain] by O.C., huts inspected by orderly Officers.

## Movements and events in January 1919

- Company at Aubin St Vaast throughout the month, in billets, awaiting demobilization; standing by for movement orders 4–7 Jan.
- 8 Jan: 3 N.C.O's and 275 O.Rs proceeded to Etaples for demobilization under Lt J.E. Taylor.
- 10–11 Jan: drafts of 50 + 100 + 72 O.Rs arrived from 1st and 2nd Divisional wings to rebuild the Company for burial duties.
- 13 Jan: rifles handed in to C.E.R.C. Q.M. Stores at Aubin St Vaast.
- 15 Jan: whole Company marched to Aubin St Vaast for baths; 12 O.Rs and others left to be demobilized.
- 18 Jan: 25 O.Rs reinforcement draft arrived; Field General Court Martial held at Aubin St Vaast.
- 21 Jan: F.G.C.M. proceedings promulgated on parade.
- 30 Jan: 100 O.Rs received from No 4 Can. Inf. Wks Co as reinforcements.
- No casualties recorded in January 1919.
