# War Diary — April 1919

- **Unit:** 3rd Canadian Infantry Works Company
- **LAC Item:** 2004932, RG 9 III-D-3, vol. 5008, reel T-10856, file 710 part 1
- **Month:** 1–30 April 1919 (Volume 8)
- **Pages:** 0041–0047 (cover p. 1, title page p. 2, diary sheets I–V pp. 3–7)
- **Form:** Army Form C.2118 — War Diary or Intelligence Summary

## Notes

- Full month covered, 1–30 April 1919; no days missing, no pages missing.
- Place throughout: Andenne, Belgium (Company H.Q. at Andenne; detachments at Huy, Namur, Verviers, Auvelais and elsewhere).
- The diary is handwritten; a new, bolder hand takes over from the March writer. Uncertain readings are marked [uncertain] and never guessed.
- The Remarks column on each sheet carries circled initials (appear to read "W.C." on Sheet I and "P.L." — likely Lt P. Lange, Adjutant — on Sheets II–IV; blank on Sheet V).
- No casualties are recorded this month.
- No signature closes the April diary (unlike March).
- Abbreviations as written: O.C. = Officer Commanding; O.Rs = Other Ranks; N.C.O.s = Non-Commissioned Officers; D.A.D.O.S. = Deputy Assistant Director of Ordnance Services; D.A.Q.M.G. = Deputy Assistant Quartermaster General; D.A.A.G. = Deputy Assistant Adjutant General; Adjt = Adjutant; Q.M. = Quartermaster; H.Q. = Headquarters; Divn / Divl = Division / Divisional; i/c = in charge of; M.T. Co = Motor Transport Company; C.E. = Canadian Engineers.

## 1 April 1919

- **Place:** Andenne, Belgium
- **Summary:** Weather cloudy cool. All detachments carrying on as usual. 1 pte [uncertain] detailed from 66th M.T. Co to this Coy for [uncertain]. Parade at 10.00 Hours for all unemployed personnel ANDENNE. Lieut Patterson proceeded to VERVIERS to relieve Lt HAWES, who is to proceed on leave. All [uncertain] Coy H.Q. [uncertain] in am. Lt P. Lange appointed Adjt + Q.M. of unit.

## 2 April 1919

- **Summary:** Weather fine cool. 4 N.C.O.s + 69 O.Rs proceeded to AUVELAIS on duty for DADOS 2nd Can Divn i/c Sgt Davies. Balance of Coy carrying on as usual.

## 3 April 1919

- **Summary:** Weather fine clear warmer. All detachments carrying on normally. 30 O.Rs down from HUY for Baths clothing etc.

## 4 April 1919

- **Summary:** Weather fine. 38 O.Rs from HUY for Bath and Clothing inspection. Lieut. Hawes, G.E. reported in from VERVIERS, and proceeded on leave. Brig. Genl. Panet, D.A.Q.M.G. and Col. De [uncertain], Chief Ordnance Officer, Canadian Corps, visited unit re, administration. They also visited HUY detachment.

## 5 April 1919

- **Summary:** Weather fine. 30 O.Rs. from HUY detachment for bath and clothing inspection. O.C. away on tour of inspection of detachments in NAMUR area. Lieut. L. H. Watts reported for duty from Corps H.Q.

## 6 April 1919

- **Summary:** Weather fine. 32 O.Rs from Huy for Bath and clothing inspection.

## 7 April 1919

- **Summary:** Weather fine. 30 O.Rs from Huy for Bath & Clothing inspection. O.C. inspected Huy Detachment.

## 8 April 1919

- **Summary:** Weather fine. Usual parade for Bath & clothing from HUY. Lieut. Labelle took charge of 2nd Can. Div. Ordnance detachment at AUVELAIS. Capt Chandler inspected this detachment at same time.

## 9 April 1919

- **Summary:** Weather fine, but becoming colder with occasional rain. Detachments carrying on normally. Lieut. L. H. Watts with 7 O.Rs. took ordnance train to CALAIS.

## 10 April 1919

- **Summary:** Weather as yesterday. Capt. Chandler and Lieut Lange visited Corps H.Q. at JODOIGNE returning via Huy. Usual bath parade from Huy, and all men in camp here detailed for work at ordnance demobilization train for DADOS - ANDENNE.

## 11 April 1919

- **Summary:** Weather dull & cold. 14 O.Rs. from Huy for bath, etc., 7 O.Rs. departed on convoy duty for DADOS, 1st Can Div. to Calais. Capt. W. Carruthers, Lieut. M. J. Dougherty and Lieut. P. Peterson reported for duty. O.C. & Lieut. Bell on inspection trip to detachments around NAMUR.

## 12 April 1919

- **Summary:** Weather rainy and cool. Lieut L. Barker reported for duty and was detailed to No 5 Platoon at HUY. Lieut Dougherty and Peterson proceeded to HUY for duty with No. 3 Platoon.

## 13 April 1919

- **Summary:** 40 O.Rs. who returned from AUVELAIS on 12/4/19 paraded for inspection, and half detailed for work at DADOS. Detachments carrying on normally. O.C. to JODOIGNE for interview with Camp Commandant, and to bring back Paymaster tomorrow.

## 14 April 1919

- **Summary:** Bath and pay parades for men in camp. Huy detachment also paid. Weather cold & rainy.

## 15 April 1919

- **Summary:** Weather fair & windy. Clothing inspection for men in camp. 2 N.C.O.s & 12 O.Rs. proceeded on convoy duty for DADOS, 1st Can. Div.

## 16 April 1919

- **Summary:** Weather cold & rainy. 14 O.Rs. from Huy for bath & clothing inspection. O.C. & Adjt on inspectional trip to Jambes, Wepion [uncertain] & Auvelais detachments.

## 17 April 1919

- **Summary:** Weather fair. All detachments carrying on normally. Lieut. F. L. Mitchell & A. R. Ratcliff [uncertain] reported for duty from 102nd Bn.

## 18 April 1919

- **Summary:** Weather fair. Lieut. J. N. Ferguson reported for duty from No 1 Co. 4th Div. Train.

## 19 April 1919

- **Summary:** [Weather as 18 April.] All detachments carrying on normally. O.C. at Corps H.Q. on Company affairs.

## 20 April 1919

- **Summary:** Easter Sunday. Lieut. H. Warmington reported for duty from 4th Bridging Transport Section, C.E. Lieut. J. Bell, M.M. appointed Quartermaster. Lieuts Mitchell & Labelle take over No 2 Platoon.

## 21 April 1919

- **Summary:** Weather fine. All detachments carrying on normally. Bath & clothing parade for men of VERVIERS detachment who returned yesterday, and reorganization of them. Lieut [uncertain] No 6 Platoon. Capt Carruthers and Lieut Warmington take charge of this platoon.

## 22 April 1919

- **Summary:** Weather fine. No 8 section returns to No 2 platoon from HUY. Lieut. J. A. Patterson returns to 4th Co. S. G. Bn. [uncertain].

## 23 April 1919

- **Summary:** Weather fine. Lieut. L. Barker with No. 19 & 20 sections (49 O.Rs.) from HUY proceeded to COURT ST. ETIENNE and LIMAL on duty for DADOS 4th Can. Div. Lieut. [uncertain] reported for duty from 11th Bn. Can. Engrs.

## 24 April 1919

- **Summary:** Weather dull and cold. Q.M. drew sufficient underclothing to carry on [uncertain] Billet rearranged, adding bath after Corps Laundry departs, for a short time. [uncertain] comfort of No. 2 & 6 platoons. Lieut. F. J. Waite reported for duty from 102nd. Bn.

## 25 April 1919

- **Summary:** Weather fair. Pay parade for all men in camp at ANDENNE. Lieut. C. A. Payhorn and R. H. M. Gilbert reported for duty from 4th. Div. Train.

## 26 April 1919

- **Summary:** Weather cold and rainy. Detachments carrying on normally.

## 27 April 1919

- **Summary:** Weather damp [uncertain] and cool. 21 O.Rs. from No 5 platoon HUY left for WAVRE to take over guard at 4th Can. Div. Ordnance. Lieut. B. Saunderson [uncertain] reported for duty from 4th Can. D.A.C. Lieut. Warmington and [uncertain] standing by to proceed to [uncertain] Tomorrow.

## 28 April 1919

- **Summary:** Weather quite cool. Lieut. Warmington & Titus with 50 O.Rs. left for BONN at 11.30 for work under Corps Burial Officer; 7 O.Rs. on convoy duty for DADOS 1st Can Div — (omitted from yesterday). Lieut. A. Labelle with balance of detail for 2nd Can. Divl DADOS returned to this H.Q. Lieut. F. J. Waite & 8 O.Rs. on convoy duty to 1st Can Divl DADOS. Lieut. [uncertain] & party returned from convoy duty.

## 29 April 1919

- **Summary:** Pay parade for Huy detachment. Lieut. [uncertain] Wilson reported for duty from 4th Div. Train. Weather quite cool & rainy.

## 30 April 1919

- **Summary:** Weather cold & rainy. Parades to obtain additional volunteers for re-burial work yielded no additional volunteers. Major Miller, D.A.A.G. Can. Corps visited Co. H.Q. Lieut. B. Saunderson & 7 O.Rs. on convoy duty for DADOS, 1st Can. Div.
