## Notes

- The diary is handwritten in ink on Army Form C.2118 ("WAR DIARY or [heading blacked out]"), with columns: Place, Date, Hour, Summary of Events and Information, Remarks and references to Appendices. The Hour column is blank throughout. Each sheet is headed "3rd Canadian Inf. Works Coy" and stamped "3RD CANADIAN INFANTRY WORKS COMPANY". The printed "or" heading is blacked out on all four sheets.
- The Remarks column on every entry contains the circled initials "WJC". The month's sheets are signed at the foot of Sheet IV: "W. J. [uncertain], Capt., O.C. 3rd Can. Inf. Works Coy".
- Spelling, grammar and abbreviations are as written (e.g. "pt" for partly, "O.Rs" for Other Ranks, "N.C.O's", "CERC" for Canadian Engineer Reinforcement Centre/Camp, "C.S.M." for Company Sergeant Major, "S.M.O." for Senior Medical Officer, "DCM" for Distinguished Conduct Medal, "R.C." for Roman Catholic, "Kilos" for kilometres).
- Place names are as written by the diarist: Aubin St Vaast, Courcelette, Baisieux, Rennix (Belgium), Alost (Aalst), Ninove, Tournai, Huy, Ware L'Eveque. "Rennix" is the diarist's spelling (Renaix/Ronse). "BORDEAUX" (11 Feb) is transcribed as written, though the destination is surprising for a unit working with the 3rd Canadian Division in northern France/Belgium.
- Ink blots/stains obscure parts of the 14 February entry on Sheet II.
- No days are missing: all 28 days of February have entries. No casualties are recorded in February. No map coordinates are given.
- Uncertain readings are marked [uncertain] and are listed in the notes below the entries where helpful. Count of [uncertain] flags in this file: 44.

## Movements and events in February 1919

- 1–12 Feb: at Aubin St Vaast, standing by; Courcelette burial-duty move ordered 6 Feb, then cancelled 8 Feb — instead the Company was to be reinforced from Base to 500 strong to work for the 3rd Division.
- 12 Feb: draft of 57 O.Rs arrived from CERC as reinforcements; orders to move to Baisieux.
- 13 Feb: Company moved by train Aubin St Vaast to Baisieux (left 12.00, arrived 23.00); horses by train.
- 14 Feb: a detachment of 52 O.Rs left at Baisieux for Ordnance work with the 3rd Canadian Division; the rest moved by [motor lorries?] to Rennix, Belgium.
- 15–24 Feb: at Rennix (Ronse/Renaix), Belgium, working at the 3rd Canadian Horse Demobilization Depot — cleaning stables, moving horses (12 km for shipment), conducting horse/mule drafts to Alost (40 km), [Ghent?], and Ninove; 935 horses turned over to the Company's care (18 Feb).
- 15 Feb: 52 O.Rs quarantined by the S.M.O., 3rd Canadian Division, on account of one case of [mumps?] in billets; a further case of mumps sent to hospital 17 Feb; men in quarantine released on orders from No. 2 Coy (17 Feb).
- 20 Feb: the 52-O.R. Baisieux detachment returned to the Company.
- 23–24 Feb: orders to move to Huy on 25 Feb; Lt Lange went ahead to make arrangements.
- 25 Feb: Company left Rennix by lorry to Tournai, then by special train (left 18.00) toward Huy.
- 26 Feb: arrived Huy 15.30, billeted in Huy for the night.
- 27 Feb: moved Huy to Ware L'Eveque (15 km), marching in full marching order in rain; baggage by lorry.
- 28 Feb: resting in billets at Ware L'Eveque; C.R.P.C. details with the Company (160 strong); preparations to start work next day.
- No casualties recorded in February 1919 (two cases of mumps sent to hospital/quarantine only).
