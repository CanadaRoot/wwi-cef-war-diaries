# War Diary — March 1919

- **Unit:** 3rd Canadian Infantry Works Company
- **LAC Item:** 2004932, RG 9 III-D-3, vol. 5008, reel T-10856, file 710 part 1
- **Month:** 1–31 March 1919 (Volume 7)
- **Pages:** 0035–0040 (cover p. 1, title page p. 2, diary sheets I–IV pp. 3–6)
- **Form:** Army Form C.2118 — War Diary or Intelligence Summary

## Notes

- Full month covered, 1–31 March 1919; no days missing, no pages missing.
- Place: Waret l'Eveque (written "WARE L'EVEQUE"), Belgium, 1–18 March; Moha, Belgium, 19–26 March; Moha & Andenne, 27 March; Andenne, Belgium, 28–31 March.
- The diary is handwritten in faint cursive in places; uncertain readings are marked [uncertain] and never guessed.
- The Remarks column on each sheet carries circled initials (appear to read "W.C.").
- No casualties are recorded this month.
- The diary is signed at the end by the Officer Commanding (signature appears to read "W. J. Chandler", Capt.).
- Abbreviations as written: O.C. = Officer Commanding; O.Rs = Other Ranks; C.C.R.C. = Canadian Corps Reinforcement Camp; D.A.D.O.S. = Deputy Assistant Director of Ordnance Services; D.A.A.G. = Deputy Assistant Adjutant General; Hd Qrs = Headquarters; H.Q. = Headquarters; Plat = Platoon; Sect = Section.

## 1 March 1919

- **Place:** Ware l'Eveque, Belgium
- **Summary:** Weather fine warm. Company Parade at 9.00 for muster, reorganization & rebilleting. O.C. Coy into Huy to make Billets arrangements with [uncertain] to make [uncertain] arrangements. 2 Officers + 157 O.Rs C.C.R.C. Detail Nos 1 + 2 absorbed into Unit as from today. Officers Lt J. Irons and Lt Labelle A. 18th + 22nd Battalions respectively.

## 2 March 1919

- **Summary:** Weather cloudy cool. Company resting in village. No parade. Large number of men attended local church.

## 3 March 1919

- **Summary:** Weather cloudy cool. Whole Company marched to BAS-OHA near HUY for Bath change of clothing to other parade. Distance to BAS-OHA return 22 Kilos. Capt W.B. Wood of Can Y.M.C.A. called re [uncertain] Canteen to be erected in Unit.

## 4 March 1919

- **Summary:** Weather raining cool. New draft recently absorbed on parade at 10.00 for clothing inspection by O.C. Balance of Coy standing By for orders.

## 5 March 1919

- **Summary:** Weather showery warm. Parade of Coy at 10.00 for clothing inspection by platoon Officers. Balance of day Coy standing By in Billets. O.C. into Huy to make arrangements re relieving guards of 1st Can Divn in [uncertain].

## 6 March 1919

- **Summary:** Weather cool. Raining steadily. 300 O.Rs + 3 Officers Lieut. Lang E. Grant, & Irons, despatched to [uncertain] to Huy for guard duty. Balance of Coy standing By in Billets.

## 7 March 1919

- **Summary:** Weather [uncertain] cloudy cool. Lt J. Bell [uncertain] + 50 O.Rs sent to ANDENNE for guard duties under D.A.D.O.S. Can Corps. Party sent by lorry. Balance of Company in Hd Qrs Camp parade 10.00 hours for inspection followed by 1 1/2 hours drill. Balance of Coy in HUY & ANDENNES on guard duties & work at Halte Repas HUY.

## 8 March 1919

- **Summary:** Weather cloudy cool. Hd Qrs Camp parade 1000 for inspection & followed by 1 1/2 hours drill. Balance of Company at HUY & ANDENNES carrying on guard duty.

## 9 March 1919

- **Summary:** Weather cloudy cool. Every available man in Hd Qrs Camp despatched under Lt Labelle A to Huy to stay overnight in Huy & proceed by lorry to NAMUR. 10th [uncertain] for Horse Conducting duty with CANADIAN CAVALRY BRIGADE. Parties in Huy & ANDENNE carrying on as usual.

## 10 March 1919

- **Summary:** Weather cloudy cool. Horse Conducting Party under Lt Labelle proceeded by lorry Huy to NAMUR for duty. Detachment HUY & ANDENNES carrying on as usual. Lt Labelle A returned NAMUR to HUY to await return of horse conducting party.

## 11 March 1919

- **Summary:** Weather [uncertain] cloudy warmer. All detachments HUY - NAMUR & ANDENNES carrying on with their various duties.

## 12 March 1919

- **Summary:** Weather [uncertain] cloudy warm. Work & duties same as yesterday.

## 13 March 1919

- **Summary:** Weather [uncertain] cloudy warm. Party returned from NAMUR Horse conducting duties, after taking horses to different Parts of Belgium. HUY and ANDENNES guards carrying on as usual.

## 14 March 1919

- **Summary:** Weather fine clear warm. Company parade of men in camp (No 1 Plat & No 5 Sect) at 10.00 for roll call and inspection. Huy & Andenne detachments as usual.

## 15 March 1919

- **Summary:** Weather [uncertain] cloudy cool. Work & duties same as yesterday. Major Hilliard [uncertain] D.A.A.G. Can Corps and Colonel [uncertain] [uncertain] Commandant, Can Corps, visited H.Q. Coy at noon on Business.

## 16 March 1919

- **Summary:** Weather cloudy cool. No parade in Camp (Sunday). The Huy & Andenne detachments carrying on as usual. Draft of 1 Officer (Lt Hawes G.E.) + 84 O.Rs part of C.C.R.C. Details No 2 arrived 10.30 a.m.

## 17 March 1919

- **Summary:** Weather cloudy Raining cool. Muster parade of new draft & inspection of men in Camp held 10.00 Hours. Other detachments carrying on as usual.

## 18 March 1919

- **Summary:** Weather [uncertain] cloudy cool. New draft + No 5 Sect sent by lorry & train under Lt Hawes to VERVIERS for HALTE REPAS duty. Balance of Company carrying on as usual. Concert & dance for men in Camp held in Waret l'Eveque in evening [uncertain] Prof Ducksbury of [uncertain] assisted by local talent orchestra. Orders received to move to Moha tomorrow by lorry.

## 19 March 1919

- **Place:** Moha, Belgium
- **Summary:** Weather fine clear Cool. Coy H.Q. moved by lorry to MOHA. Move complete by noon. All detachments carrying on with their various duties. Lt J. Duquet arrived as officer reinforcement from C.C.R.C. [uncertain].

## 20 March 1919

- **Summary:** Weather cold sleeting. [uncertain] Wind. All detachments carrying on normally. Lt S. Irons guard on gun park HUY increased by 25 O.Rs sent from No 1 Plat H.Q. Lt Duquet proceeded to Huy to take over No 1 Platoon from Lt Lang E. latter coming to H.Q. as Adjutant & [uncertain].

## 21 March 1919

- **Summary:** Weather cloudy cool. All detachments carrying on normally.

## 22 March 1919

- **Summary:** Weather cloudy cool. [Ditto marks — same as 21 March.] All detachments carrying on normally.

## 23 March 1919

- **Summary:** Weather fine warmer. Lt Patterson + 40 O.Rs arrived as reinforcements [uncertain] today from [uncertain]. All detachments carrying on normally.

## 24 March 1919

- **Summary:** Weather cloudy cool. Raining. Lt Labelle with 70 O.Rs on Horse Conducting duty to Hasselt. All other detachments carrying on as usual.

## 25 March 1919

- **Summary:** Weather [uncertain] cloudy cool. All detachments & parties carrying on work as usual. Coy Parade for unemployed personnel at MOHA held at 10.00 for roll call & inspection.

## 26 March 1919

- **Summary:** Weather cloudy cool. Orders received to move to ANDENNE today, but no transport arrangements made. All detachments & parties carrying on. Parade for roll call & inspection of unemployed personnel held at 10.00 at MOHA.

## 27 March 1919

- **Place:** Moha & Andenne
- **Summary:** Weather cold sleeting. Company H.Q. & 100 unemployed personnel MOHA moved by lorry to ANDENNE. Move complete by 14.00. All other parties & detachments carrying on as usual. Lt Labelle & party returned from Horse Conducting duty 09.00.

## 28 March 1919

- **Place:** Andenne, Belgium
- **Summary:** Weather cloudy cool [uncertain] raining. All detachments carrying on normally. Coy Parade for unemployed personnel held at H.Q. at 10.00 for roll call and inspection. [uncertain] Col [uncertain] Labour Corps [uncertain] on visit of inspection.

## 29 March 1919

- **Summary:** Weather cloudy snowing nearly all day. All detachments carrying on normally. Unemployed personnel & Coy H.Q. Baths at ANDENNE during morning.

## 30 March 1919

- **Summary:** Weather cool [uncertain] cloudy. Sunday. No parades. All detachments carrying on normally. Lt Labelle left with guard for Calais on train [uncertain] D.A.D.O.S. 1st Cdn Divn material.

## 31 March 1919

- **Summary:** Weather cool [uncertain] cloudy. Coy parade at 10.00 of unemployed personnel ANDENNE for roll call and inspection. All other detachments & parties carrying on normally. Lt Patterson returned with Calais train guard.

[Signed] [signature — appears "W. J. Chandler"] Capt, O.C. 3rd Can Inf Works Coy.
