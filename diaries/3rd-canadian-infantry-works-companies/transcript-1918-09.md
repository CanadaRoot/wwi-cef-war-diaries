# 3rd Canadian Infantry Works Company — War Diary, September 1918

- **Unit:** 3rd Canadian Infantry Works Company (3rd Can. Inf. Works Coy)
- **LAC Item:** 2004932, RG 9 III-D-3, vol. 5008, reel T-10856, file 710, part 1
- **Month:** September 1918 (14–30 September 1918)
- **Pages transcribed:** 0001–0005 (cover p. 1, title page p. 2, diary Sheets I–III, pp. 3–5)
- **Form:** Army Form C.2118, War Diary (manuscript). Diary Text: 3 pages (per title page).
- **Place:** "In the Field" throughout
- **Signed:** W. J. Chandler, Capt., O.C. 3rd Can. Inf. Works Coy.

## Notes

- The diary for this month begins on 14 September 1918: the Company itself only came into existence at midnight 13/14 September, on the breaking up of the 1st Canadian Infantry Works Battalion.
- The handwriting is cursive and often cramped; words I could not read with confidence are marked [uncertain]. Place-names are transcribed as written in the diary; the writer spells Croisilles as "Crinville"/"Crinilles" and "Cagnicourt" is written "Cagnicourt". "R.C.C. R.E." is transcribed literally as written (probably a Road Construction Company, Royal Engineers).
- Ditto marks (") in the diary mean "same as yesterday"; they are expanded here as "[ditto — as yesterday]" where the repeated text is clear.
- The Remarks column on each sheet carries circled initials (apparently "WJC") against most entries; no appendix references are listed.
- No casualties are recorded anywhere in September; enemy shells fell close to the camp on 27 September but caused no casualties.
- Gaps: no entries exist before 14 September (the Company did not yet exist). 18–21 September are ditto entries only. No appendices accompany this month's diary in the file.

## 14 September 1918

**Place:** In the Field. **Hour:** —

> Upon the breaking up of the 1st Canadian Infantry Works Battn, which [latter?] was reformed into (2) Canadian Infantry Works Companies, known as the 1st Canadian Infy Works Co and the 3rd Canadian Infantry Works Co. The latter was made up of [Nos 3? uncertain] & 4 Companies of the old 1st Canadian Infy Works Battn. The change came into effect at midnight Sept 13th 14th. The officers of the Company were detailed as follows:— Capt W. J. Chandler (O.C.), Lieut G. N. Buckham [uncertain], Lieut J. D. W. Large [uncertain], Lieut J. E. S. Taylor with Capt G. B. Jack attached for duty. 907642 C.S.M. Austin (late C.S.M. No 3 Co 1st C.W.B.) as C.S.M. and 879522 Sgt [uncertain] was appointed C.Q.M.S. (Sgt Skinner being C.S.M. No 4 Co late 1st C.W.B.). The N.C.O's and men of Nos 3 & 4 Companies late 1st C.W.B. composed the other ranks of this Co which started with an initial strength of 5 Officers (1 attached) and 397 O.Rs, camp located between Fontaine and Crinville [uncertain — probably Croisilles]. Transport consisting of 4 G.S. waggons, 1 water cart, 1 Maltese cart and 1 Bicycle [uncertain] + 7 [uncertain] Horses being approximately 1/2 of [uncertain] Transport of late 1st C.W.B. The Company being employed by 317th [uncertain] R.E. on road work between Fontaine + Crinville [uncertain — probably Croisilles]. Billets [in?] shelters and huts. Lieut J. E. S. Taylor was appointed O.C. No 1 Plat, Lieut J. D. Large O.C. No 2 Plat, Lieut G. N. Buckham O.C. No 3 Plat, & Capt G. B. Jack O.C. No 4 Plat.
