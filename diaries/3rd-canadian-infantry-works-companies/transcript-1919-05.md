# War Diary — May 1919

- **Unit:** 3rd Canadian Infantry Works Company
- **LAC Item:** 2004932, RG 9 III-D-3, vol. 5008, reel T-10856, file 710 part 1
- **Month:** 1–15 May 1919 (Volume 9)
- **Pages:** 0048–0051 (cover p. 1, title page p. 2, diary sheets I–II pp. 3–4)
- **Form:** Army Form C.2118 — War Diary or Intelligence Summary

## Notes

- The diary ends 15 May 1919 with the company's demobilization; this is the final volume of the diary (Volume 9).
- The cover gives the range as 1st May to 15th May 1919 (the printed "30" is struck through and "15th" written in). The title page instead reads "From 1st May to 31st May, 1918" — the year 1918 and the 31st are errors in the original; the diary text itself is dated May 1919 and ends 15 May 1919. "Diary Text 2 Pages."
- Place: Andenne, Belgium, 1–8 May; Etaples, France, 9–15 May.
- The diary is handwritten in the same bold hand as April. Uncertain readings are marked [uncertain] and never guessed.
- The Remarks column on both sheets carries circled initials (appear to read "P.L.").
- No casualties are recorded this month.
- 14 May is entered as "Nil" — no events that day.
- The diary is signed at the end by Capt W. J. Chandler, O.C. 3rd Can Inf Works Coy.
- Abbreviations as written: O.C. = Officer Commanding; O.Rs = Other Ranks; D.A.D.O.S. = Deputy Assistant Director of Ordnance Services; C.F.A. = Canadian Field Artillery; C.E.B.D. = Canadian Expeditionary Base Depot (as written); Can. Genl Base Depot = Canadian General Base Depot.

## 1 May 1919

- **Place:** Andenne, Belgium
- **Summary:** Weather very bad. Lieut. J. Irons & balance of No 5 platoon left HUY and took over 4th Div. guards at COURT ST. ETIENNE. Lieut J. N. Ferguson joined No. 5 platoon for duty.

## 2 May 1919

- **Summary:** Weather still dull and rainy. Lieut. F. J. Waite returned from convoy duty, and Lieut C. A. Payhorn left on convoy duty to Calais for DADOS 1st Can Div.

## 3 May 1919

- **Summary:** Weather fair. Detachments carrying on normally.

## 4 May 1919

- **Summary:** Weather fair. Capt. V. H. Kitchen reported for duty from 5th Div. C.F.A.

## 5 May 1919

- **Summary:** Weather fine. Lieuts. J. Grant, L. Barker and L. H. Watts left for demobilization, the two former to England, & the latter to AUBIN-ST VAAST.

## 6 May 1919

- **Summary:** Weather fine. All outstanding detachments with exception of Guard at WAVRE, and Lieut. Warmington's detachment at BONN, Germany all reporting back to platoons today in preparation for move tomorrow.

## 7 May 1919

- **Summary:** Weather fine. Parade at 10.00 hrs. full marching order for O.C.'s inspection. Company loaded completely at 1330 hrs., and left ANDENNE at 15.45 hrs. for C. E. B. D. ETAPLES.

## 8 May 1919

- **Summary:** Weather fine. Journey going well, and arrived Calais at 16.00 hrs. Left about 22.00 hrs. for ETAPLES.

## 9 May 1919

- **Place:** Etaples, France
- **Summary:** Arrived ETAPLES at 1.45 hrs this morning, and after necessary preparation at station, company arrived at Can. Genl Base Depot at shortly after 4.00 hrs. in good order. Men put away in billets for a few hours rest.

## 10 May 1919

- **Summary:** 147 O.Rs. with Lieut. A. Labelle & J. D. Duquet proceeded to England for demobilization. [The number appears corrected in the original — "152" struck through and "147" written in.]

## 11 May 1919

- **Summary:** 58 O.Rs. proceeded to England for demobilization.

## 12 May 1919

- **Summary:** 150 O.Rs. proceeded to England for demobilization.

## 13 May 1919

- **Summary:** 150 O.Rs. proceeded to England for demobilization.

## 14 May 1919

- **Summary:** Nil.

## 15 May 1919

- **Summary:** 36 O.Rs. proceeded to England. Balance of Company transferred to Can. Genl. Base Depot, and now completely demobilized.

[Signed] W. J. Chandler, Capt, O.C. 3rd Can Inf Works Coy.
