# 3rd Canadian Infantry Works Company — War Diary, December 1918

- **Unit:** 3rd Canadian Infantry Works Company
- **LAC Item:** 2004932, RG 9 III-D-3, vol. 5008, reel T-10856, file 710 part 1
- **Month:** 1–31 December 1918 (Volume 4)
- **Pages transcribed:** 0018–0023 (cover p. 1, title page p. 2, diary sheets 1–4, pp. 3–6)
- **Form:** Army Form C.2118 (War Diary). Place column reads "In the Field" except 28 December, where the place is given as Aubin St Vaast. The "INTELLIGENCE SUMMARY" heading is blacked out on sheets 1, 3 and 4, and left visible on sheet 2; the diary text is handwritten.
- **Transcribed from:** LAC page images in this folder, 2026-10-28. Spelling, abbreviations and capitalisation follow the original. "O.Rs" = Other Ranks; "Coy" = Company; "R.S.O." = Railway Supply Officer (as written); "C.C.S." = Casualty Clearing Station; "Bde" = Brigade; "F.A." = Field Ambulance.
- **Handwriting:** December is in a different, fainter hand than November. Several entries (notably 3 December) are very faint and partly legible; those words are flagged [uncertain].

## Notes

- Every day 1–31 December has an entry. The 5 December entry runs on into 6 December ("Coy arrived at Stavelot detraining at 7 A.M. 6/12/18").
- Notable events: on 1–2 December Pte [uncertain] (No. 690957) was found dead on Rue Dave, Jambes, and a Court of Inquiry was held (Capt George Price, Capt G. E. Jack, Lt J. Bell). On 10 December the Company crossed into Germany on the march to Weywertz. On 22 December the diary records the death of the Commanding Officer, Capt G. E. Jack, in hospital at Spa (broncho-pneumonia); Lt Jas Bell had taken acting command on 9 December and Capt J. J. Chandler took over command on 28 December. Christmas Day 1918 was spent on a troop train to Arras/Aubin St Vaast, with the men complaining of the poor accommodation and administration. The month ends with demobilisation orders (31 December).
- The Company moved: Jambes/Namur – Stavelot (5–6 Dec, by train) – Weywertz, Germany (10 Dec) – Euskirchen (11 Dec) – Godesberg (16 Dec) – then by train back to France, Aubin St Vaast (24–26 Dec).
- The officer written "Lt F. N. Lang" on 16 December is written "Lt Lange" on 29 December; both readings are transcribed as written. The Group Commandant is written "Lt Col McKinney, D.S.O." (12 and 22 December); the same name on 1 December is only partly legible.
- The Remarks column on each sheet carries circled initials (appears to read "J.B.") — not transcribed as text.
- The month carries the unit stamp "3RD CANADIAN INFANTRY WORKS COMPANY" and is signed at the foot of sheet 4: "J. J. Chandler, Capt., O.C. 3rd Can Inf Works Coy."

## 1 December 1918

**Place:** In the Field

Weather fine. Party working on [uncertain] Cadet were employed in loading + offloading clothing for Prisoners of War Collecting Station. Road Party worked. A man 690957 Pte [uncertain] was found dead on Rue Dave, JAMBES. Lt Col [uncertain] D.S.O. Group Commandant called + ordered a Court of Inquiry.

## 2 December 1918

Weather cold + fine. Party at School Cadet and Road party working on LIEGE Rd as far as VIGNEROUX [uncertain]. Court of Inquiry held Capt George Price [uncertain], Capt G. E. Jack + Lt J. Bell members, re finding of body of Pte [uncertain]. Body taken away by the C.C.S. whom I asked to advise me the cause of death.

## 3 December 1918

[Entry very faint and partly legible.] Party at School Cadet employed loading & unloading lorries and stacking bales of blankets + clothing. Road party working on Liege Rd. cleaning Rd. [uncertain] employed by 302nd R.E. R.C.C.

## 4 December 1918

Weather fine. Guard at Arsenal inspected by Capt. Jack. Work party at Ecole Cadet cleaning out rooms + piling blankets. Road party on Liege Rd. cleaning + repairing road, employed by 302nd R.E.

## 5 December 1918

Weather cold + dull. Company preparing to move from Jambes to Stavelot by train. Men busy cleaning + packing up. Baggage taken to Namur Station by transport. Coy. entrained and left Namur by 6 P.M. everything passed off successfully. No 1 Coy travelled by same train. Horse transport by Rd. Coy arrived at Stavelot detraining at 7 A.M. 6/12/18.

## 6 December 1918

Weather fine. Company billeted in Stavelot. Men employed cleaning up new billets. Muster parade held.

## 7 December 1918

Weather dull. Coy standing by awaiting orders. Parade + inspection held.

## 8 December 1918

Weather dull. Coy standing by awaiting orders. Parade + inspections held. Lt Col [uncertain] D.S.O. visits Coy gives some details about move. Capt Jack not feeling well. Transport arrives and put away in billets.

## 9 December 1918

Weather dull. Coy standing by. Orders received to move to Weywertz on the 10th by route march. Capt G. E. Jack sick in bed, goes to Hospital at Spa leaving Stavelot by Train 1.00 P.M. Two lorries to report for move. Lt J. Bell takes over acting command of Company.

## 10 December 1918

Weather fine. Coy leaves Stavelot 7.30 A.M. by route march for Weywertz, under command of Lt J. Bell. Crossing the border into Germany at 3.10 A.M. [uncertain — the time as written is inconsistent with the 2.00 P.M. arrival] arriving at Weywertz 2.00 P.M. Hot meal ready for men on arrival. Horse transport by Rd. Men after some difficulty put away into billets.

## 11 December 1918

Weather showery. Company ordered to proceed to Blankenheim. Entraining at Weywertz. No 1, 3 & 4 Coys all travelling by same train. On entraining move to Blankenheim cancelled, and ordered to proceed to Euskirchen by Capt Harcourt Asst. Labor Commandant. Transport by road. Move successfully completed and men billeted in [uncertain: a word crossed out in the original] Gymnasium. Group H.Q. also in Euskirchen.

## 12 December 1918

Weather Wet. Moving men from Gymnasium to Tivoli Theatre. Parade & Inspection by Lt. Jas Bell. Meeting of Coy Commanders at Group H.Q. Lt. Col. McKinney [uncertain] D.S.O. calls for inspection on the 13th. Men preparing for inspection & cleaning out Billets.

## 13 December 1918

Weather very wet. Preliminary parade & inspection by Lt Jas Bell. Group Commanders parade & inspection called off owing to bad weather. Transport arrives and billeted.

## 14 December 1918

Weather dull & wet. Company parades & inspections. Men utilized in cleaning up area around billets. Meeting of Coy commanders at Group H.Q. Lt. Jas Bell attends. Church parade ordered for Sunday.

## 15 December 1918

Weather fair. Group Church parade of Nos 1.-3. and 4. Coys was held in a hall at Euskirchen. Capt McGillivray our new padre officiated at the service. Lt Col. [uncertain] McKinney D.S.O. accompanied the singing on the piano. Bathing parade during afternoon. Whole Coy bathed at Euskirchen Infantry Barracks. Good baths and abundance of water. Men enjoyed it very much. Orders to move.

## 16 December 1918

Weather fine. Coy entrained at 6.30 o'clock, Euskirchen Station for [uncertain] when they detrained thence by route march to Godesburg via Vilip [uncertain] and Muffendorf. Move successfully completed. Transport moved by road arriving at Godesburg same evening. H.Q. and orderly room in Jungbluth [uncertain] Hotel. Lt. F. N. Lang reports back from Paris leave.

## 17 December 1918

Weather fine. Company cleaning and fixing up billets, parades and inspections.

## 18 December 1918

Weather dull but fine. Coy carried out preliminary parade & inspection. Numbers one, three & four coys paraded and inspected by Col. Wilby, Labor Commandant. Inspection passed off well and men complimented on their turn out. Lt J. E. Taylor and detachment arrive from [uncertain].

## 19 December 1918

Weather fine. Company carried out usual parade & inspection. Pay parade at 09.30 clock. Men being paid in marks for first time.

## 20 December 1918

Weather dull & fine. Company parades and inspections. Men turned out clean in appearance & equipment.

## 21 December 1918

Weather dull. Company parade and inspection. Turn out of men good. Equipment clean.

## 22 December 1918

Weather clear & fine. Company attended Group Church parade. Coy marched past Group H.Q. at 10.00 & Group Commdr. taking the salute, proceeding thence to Kurpark Saal where church service was held. Capt McGillivray officiating. After the service Lt Col McKinney D.S.O. spoke to the men about their return to Canada and gave them some political advice. Information received of death of Capt G. E. Jack in hospital at Spa (Broncho-pneumonia).

## 23 December 1918

Weather clear & fine. Coy paraded [uncertain: words illegible]. About half the Coy were given passes to Cologne. Move order received 22.15 o'clock. To entrain at BONN for ARRAS at 11.00 o'clock 24-12-18. Horse transport of Nos 1, 3 & 4 Coys left GODESBERG for ARRAS at 13.00 o'clock under command of Lt. H. Nelson, No 1 Coy.

## 24 December 1918

Weather fine. Coy moved off at 08.45 for BONN entraining there at 11.00 for AUBIN ST VAAST. Men very disappointed at having to spend Xmas on the train. Accommodation on train poor. 30 men to a box car and no heating or cooking facilities.

## 25 December 1918

Weather cold & wet. Coy on train proceeding through Belgium. Xmas dinner Bread Bully Beef & Beans. Much complaint being made about administration and treatment. No time being allowed in which to prepare a hot meal for the men.

## 26 December 1918

Weather cold. Showing bad administration, train instead of stopping at AUBIN ST VAAST went on to Camiers [uncertain] where it was held up for four hours and then shipped back to AUBIN ST VAAST. On arrival there men were taken over by 3 DIV. WING and marched to Cdn Corps School for quarters & rations.

## 27 December 1918

Weather wet. Coy quartered at Cdn Corps School & 3 Div. Wing. Capt Chandler reports back from leave to ROME.

## 28 December 1918

**Place:** Aubin St Vaast

Weather cool, raining. Capt J. J. Chandler took over Command of Coy. Coy located in Nissen Huts at 7th Bde H.Q. and in Barracks at 9th Bde H.Q. Standing by awaiting instructions.

## 29 December 1918

Weather cool raining. Company in same Billets. Lt J. E. Taylor with 1 NCO + 3 men sent to Chateau [uncertain: appears to read "Vaufosse"] as guard. Escorts sent out to [uncertain: appears to read "Lille"] for Pte Dalton & [uncertain: appears to read "Somain"] for Pte Kitchen. Orders received Group cease to function, & submit nominal Roll of [uncertain] men, NCOs & H.Q. staff who are to be demobilized. Lt Lange left to bring its detachment from SENEFFE.

## 30 December 1918

Weather cool raining. Coy in same Billets. Escort sent to LIEGE for Pte [uncertain], absence of trains.

## 31 December 1918

Weather cool raining. Company standing by for instructions. Local celebrations held to greet the New Year at midnight. Orders received [uncertain] for demobilization leave on 9th inst, & that Group still administer to Companies as heretofore.

*[Unit stamp at foot of sheet 4:] 3RD CANADIAN INFANTRY WORKS COMPANY. [Signed:] J. J. Chandler, Capt., O.C. 3rd Can Inf Works Coy.*
