# 3rd Canadian Infantry Works Company — War Diary, October 1918

- **Unit:** 3rd Canadian Infantry Works Company (3rd Can. Inf. Works Coy)
- **LAC Item:** 2004932, RG 9 III-D-3, vol. 5008, reel T-10856, file 710, part 1
- **Month:** October 1918 (1–31 October 1918)
- **Pages transcribed:** 0006–0011 (cover p. 1, title page p. 2, diary Sheets I–IV, pp. 3–6)
- **Form:** Army Form C.2118, War Diary (manuscript). Diary Text: 4 pages (per title page).
- **Place:** "In the Field" throughout
- **Signed:** W. J. Chandler, Capt., O.C. 3rd Can. Inf. Works Coy.

## Notes

- The handwriting is cursive and often cramped; words I could not read with confidence are marked [uncertain]. Place-names are transcribed as written in the diary; the writer spells Croisilles as "Crinville", and Cagnicourt, Inchy, Bucquoy, Marquion, Dury, Arleux, Abscon, Aniche and Palluel appear throughout. "R.C.C. R.E." is transcribed literally as written (probably a Road Construction Company, Royal Engineers).
- Ditto marks (") in the diary mean "same as yesterday"; they are expanded here as "[ditto — as yesterday]" where the repeated text is clear.
- The Remarks column on each sheet carries circled initials (apparently "WJC") against most entries; no appendix references are listed.
- Movements this month: camp near Cagnicourt/Inchy until 17 October, when the Company moved to a new camp near Dury (map ref. 51B P.2.1 C.1.9); on 22 October Nos. 2 & 3 Platoons and H.Q. moved to billets at Arleux while Nos. 1 & 4 stayed at Dury; on 27 October the whole Company moved to Abscon.
- Casualties: none killed or wounded are recorded in October. One man was accidentally burned on 3 October and sent to a Field Ambulance. Parties working at the Marquion stone dump were shelled on 2, 7 and 8 October, with no casualties in the Company; Pte Dawson (stretcher bearer) and another private are commended on 7 October for carrying wounded men of other units.
- Gaps: 13 October entry is partly cut off at the foot of Sheet 2 / head of the following page in the scan; the 21 October entry's opening words are partly illegible. No appendices accompany this month's diary in the file.
