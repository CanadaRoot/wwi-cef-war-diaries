## Transcription conventions
- Faithful to the original: spelling, punctuation, abbreviations and capitalisation as written (the diary is handwritten throughout).
- `[uncertain]` marks a word or name that could not be read with confidence — never guessed.
- Text struck through in the original is shown as `[struck through in original: "..."]`.
- The diary-text pages are Army Form C. 2118, "WAR DIARY or INTELLIGENCE SUMMARY", handwritten in columns: Hour, Date, Place | Summary of Events and Information | Remarks and references to Appendices. The "INTELLIGENCE SUMMARY" heading is struck through on the printed form.
- The Remarks column throughout the 1914 pages carries a set of initials, read here as "A.M." — the reading is uncertain; it may be the diarist's initials.
- No map coordinates (latitude/longitude) are given in the diary.

# NOVEMBER 1914

## Page 4 — Diary text (September 1914, p. 4) — 12 November

| Place | Date | Hour | Summary of Events and Information | Remarks |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| London | Nov 12, 1914 | 10 A.M. | march from West Down North Camp to Ravington [uncertain] and thence by rail to Paddington Station London. | A.M. |
| Hampstead | Nov 12th 1914 | 2 P.M. | from Paddington Station thence by tube to Military Hospital Hampstead. | A.M. |

## Notes — November 1914
- **On 12 November 1914 the hospital moves from West Down North Camp to London**, marching to the railhead, travelling by rail to Paddington Station, and taking the tube to the Military Hospital, Hampstead, where it is based until February 1915.
- **"Ravington" [uncertain]:** the railhead name is not read with confidence; it may read "Lavington".
- **Named people:** none. No personal names are given in these entries.
- **Casualties:** none recorded.
- No appendices are listed or included for this volume.
