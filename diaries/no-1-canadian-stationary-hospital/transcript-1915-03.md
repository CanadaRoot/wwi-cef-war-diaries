## Transcription conventions
- Faithful to the original: spelling, punctuation, abbreviations and capitalisation as written (the diary is handwritten throughout).
- `[uncertain]` marks a word or name that could not be read with confidence — never guessed.
- Text struck through in the original is shown as `[struck through in original: "..."]`.
- The diary-text pages are Army Form C. 2118, "WAR DIARY or INTELLIGENCE SUMMARY", handwritten in columns: Hour, Date, Place | Summary of Events and Information | Remarks and references to Appendices. The "INTELLIGENCE SUMMARY" heading is struck through on the printed form.
- The Remarks column throughout the March pages carries a set of initials, read here as "A.M." — the reading is uncertain; it may be the diarist's initials.
- The camp name is written with variant spellings (Honeault / Honneault) — transcribed as written on each page; the reading is [uncertain] where flagged.
- No map coordinates (latitude/longitude) are given in the diary.

## Notes — March 1915
- **The hospital is established in tents at Honeault Camp in March 1915**: ground is staked on 13 March for a 300-bed tented hospital, and the rest of the month is spent establishing and equipping it. On 24 March the A.D.M.S. is notified that accommodation for 150 patients is complete.
- **The hospital's place changes from Honeault Camp to Wimereux by 31 March**; the move itself is not recorded in these pages (no entry is made between 24 and 31 March).
- **Named people:** none. No personal names are given in these entries.
- **Casualties:** none recorded. No patients had yet been received by 31 March.
- No appendices are listed or included for this volume.
