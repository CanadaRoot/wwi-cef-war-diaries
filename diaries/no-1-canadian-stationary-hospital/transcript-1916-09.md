## Notes — September–October 1916
- **October 1916 is present.** The volume covers 1 September–31 October 1916; the October entries (5–31 October) occupy the lower half of p. 64. There is no gap for October.
- **The hospital is at Salonica** throughout, and the diary is kept as weekly summaries rather than daily entries.
- **Admissions / discharges / deaths (weekly):** September — week ending 2 September: 326 admitted, 328 discharged, no deaths; week ending 9 September: 306 admitted, 289 discharged, 5 deaths; week ending 16 September: 291 admitted, 341 discharged, 2 deaths; week ending 23 September: 291 admitted, 294 discharged, 5 deaths; week ending 30 September: 343 admitted, 278 discharged, 2 deaths. October — week ending 7 October: 318 admitted, 360 discharged, 522 in hospital; week ending 14 October: 259 admitted, 255 discharged; week ending 21 October: 183 admitted, 212 discharged, 526 in hospital; week ending 28 October: 174 admitted, 227 discharged, 444 in hospital; 29–31 October: 140 admitted, 74 discharged, 530 in hospital.
- **Sickness in the unit, September:** 2 officers (both malaria relapse), 8 nursing sisters (3 malaria, 1 relapse) and 48 N.C.O.s and men (40 malaria, 28 relapse); 44 cases were due to malaria and 31 had two or more attacks. At midnight on 30 September one sister and fifteen N.C.O.s and men were in hospital.
- **Disease:** malaria dominates, mostly relapses and of a malignant tertian type, often in a very serious condition; malaria decreasing through October. I.C.T. (boils etc.) increasing in October. Weather getting cooler and more rainy.
- **Deaths:** hospital deaths (patients) are given only as weekly totals — 14 in September (5 + 2 + 5 + 2). No deaths among the unit's own personnel are recorded in these two months.
- **Named people:** Capt. Stammers (invalided to England with malaria, 11 September); Nursing Sisters Goodeve and Foulds (invalided to England, debility, 25 September); Matron Charleson and 19 nursing sisters — Alway, Dixon, Edgecombe, Everett, Fraser, Gamblin, Gray, Holden, Hudson, Loneragan, McClean, McKenzie, Reid, Robinson, Sampson, Smith, Townsend and Wake — reported for duty 5 October; Sisters Scobie, Moore and Brankin (invalided to England with malaria, 23 October); Capt. Frederick, R.A.M.C., attached (invalided to England with malaria, 28 October).
- **Uncertain readings:** none in the diary text on this page; the cover's diagonal handwriting and the signature are not legible.
- No appendices are listed or included for this volume.
