- Ward blocks A, B, C, D, E, F — block F is marked "CAPACITY 100 Beds" and the marquees "35x17"
- DINING TENT HOSP. (×2), COOK HOUSE (H.P. COOK HOUSE)

- MENS LATRINE, OFF LATRINE, NS LAT.
- Total capacity = 600 Beds. Compass points N/E/S/W.

"X R" is read as written; it may stand for X-Ray — the October diary records the X ray department moving into the Operating Room building.

## Notes — August 1915
- **The voyage to Lemnos:** 1 August — the unit entrains from Shorncliffe to Southampton and embarks on the hospital ship Asturias, accompanied by No. 3 and No. 5 Canadian Stationary Hospitals; sails 3 Pm. 6 August — passes Cape of Gibraltar; 8 August — arrives off Malta, is ordered to Alexandria and sails 11.15 Am; 11 August — arrives Alexandria 5 Am. Equipment is transferred to lighters and loaded on S.S. Afric; the personnel embark 13 August, the Matron and Nursing Sisters on the Delta. Afric sails 14 August; off Carpathos on 15 August a French torpedo boat warns of a hostile submarine and the course is changed through the archipelago via Santorini, Melos, Siphnos, Syros, Tenos and Skyros. Arrives Lemnos 16 August and disembarks at West Mudros.
- **"Hospine ship Delta":** the word before "ship Delta" (13 August) is written "Hospine" in the original — apparently for "Hospital ship". Transcribed as written.
- **Edge cut:** the 1 August line "accompanied by No 3 – No 5 Stat. Hosp. (Can.)" runs to the right edge of the scan; "(Can.)" is partly cut off.
- **Uncertain name:** the Colonel accompanying General Bapty on 22 August is read as "Malin" but is marked [uncertain].
- **Building the hospital:** the camp at West Mudros is built from eastern-pattern marquees (35' x 17'), erected in rows of six with ends joined to form wards of 100 beds. The hand-drawn plan on p. 12 shows a total capacity of 600 beds.
- **Admissions:** 125 patients admitted 22 August (mostly minor, many acute enteritis); 125 more on 23 August; 59 in the morning and 25 in the afternoon of 24 August; 75 on 29 August.
- **Supply difficulties:** the diary repeatedly notes difficulty getting ordnance and supplies, a short water supply (no water cart in the equipment), and difficulty with the sanitary situation for want of ordnance supplies and disinfectants.
- **Named people:** Capt. Foster (taken on strength 1 August); General Bapty, D.M.S. of the Mediterranean Expeditionary Force, and Colonel Malin [uncertain] (22 August).
- No appendices are listed or included for this volume.
