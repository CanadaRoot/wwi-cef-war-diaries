## Notes — September 1917
- **The unit returns to England.** On 3 September orders are received to embark next day at 9.30 a.m.; the ambulance is stored with the 245th M.T. Company. On 4 September the officers and men embark on the Italian transport Regina Elena at 9.30 a.m. — a fine ship, well equipped — with some French and Italian troops on board; she leaves the harbour at 4 p.m. in convoy with two other transports and three Italian torpedo boat destroyers.
- **The voyage home:** 5–6 September spent in the harbour of Skyros; the convoy leaves Skyros at 7 p.m. on 7 September, travels in darkness all night, and arrives at Milos harbour at 6 a.m.; 8 September spent in Milos, leaving at 5 p.m.; arrives at Navarino at 6 a.m. on 9 September, spends the day in harbour, leaves at 6 p.m.; arrives at Corfu at 8 a.m. on 10 September and spends the day in the harbour; arrives at Taranto at 7 a.m. on 11 September and disembarks at 5 p.m., going to the Rest Camp, which is very dusty and uncomfortable.
- **Taranto, 12 September:** Lieut-Col. Williams and Major Johnson leave by Rapide for London; the remainder of the party is to follow by troop train. Various groups of units arrived in England.
- **27 September:** officers and men are sent to Hastings; nursing sisters to Basingstoke.
- **Hastings, 28 September:** ordered to take over the Canadian Military Hospital Hastings from No. 8 Canadian Stationary Hospital. The name is to become changed to No. 13 Canadian General Hospital. This is the last entry of the diary.
- **Named people:** Lieut-Col. E. J. Williams and Major Johnson (left Taranto for London, 12 September).
