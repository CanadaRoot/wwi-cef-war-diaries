- `[uncertain]` marks a word or name that could not be read with confidence — never guessed.
- Text struck through in the original is shown as `[struck through in original: "..."]`.

## Notes — November 1916
- **The hospital is at Salonica** throughout, and the diary is kept as weekly summaries rather than daily entries.
- **Admissions / discharges / deaths (weekly):** week ending 4 November: 119 admitted, 150 discharged, no deaths, 476 remaining in hospital; week ending 11 November: 166 admitted, 272 discharged, 1 death, 369 remaining; week ending 18 November: 190 admitted, 175 discharged, 1 death, 383 remaining; week ending 25 November: 186 admitted, 140 discharged, 2 deaths, 427 remaining; past 5 days to 30 November: 180 admitted, 172 discharged, 2 deaths, 433 remaining.
- **Enemy air activity:** German Taubes flew over the camp on 4 and 6 November — the first air-raid mentions in this diary.
- **Disease:** a marked decrease in malaria and enteritis admissions. Weather rainy and getting cooler.
- **Deaths:** hospital deaths (patients) are given only as weekly totals — 6 in November. No deaths among the unit's own personnel are recorded.
- **Named people:** Nursing Sisters Edgecombe, Everett, Gamblin, Loneragan, McKenzie, Robinson, Smith, Townsend and Viets transferred to No. 4 Canadian General Hospital (8 November); Nursing Sisters Alway and Hudson invalided to England (13 November); Nursing Sisters Fraser, Gray and Wake transferred to No. 4 Canadian General Hospital (16 November). Major C. H. Morris signs as O.C.
- **Uncertain readings:** none in the diary text on this page; the wording of the oval date stamp is partly illegible.
- No appendices are listed or included for this volume.
