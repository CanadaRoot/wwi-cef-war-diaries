## Transcription conventions
- Faithful to the original: spelling, punctuation, abbreviations and capitalisation as written (the diary text in this volume is typewritten, not handwritten, apart from one handwritten interlineation noted below).
- `[uncertain]` marks a word or name that could not be read with confidence — never guessed.
- Text struck through in the original is shown as `[struck through in original: "..."]`.
- The diary-text pages are Army Form C. 2118, "WAR DIARY or INTELLIGENCE SUMMARY", typewritten in columns: Place | Date | Hour | Summary of Events and Information | Remarks and references to Appendices. The "INTELLIGENCE SUMMARY" heading is struck through on the printed form.
- Place names are transcribed as written. The diary gives no place column entry for December (the hospital is at Salonica, per the preceding volumes).
- No map coordinates (latitude/longitude) are given in the diary.

## Notes — December 1916
- **The hospital is at Salonica** (no place is entered in the Place column this month; the preceding volumes place it at Salonica).
- **Admissions / discharges:** 1 December: 3 officers and 65 other ranks admitted, 26 discharged; in hospital on 2 December 12 officers and 463 other ranks. 3–9 December: 2 officers and 170 other ranks admitted; 7 officers and 264 O.R. discharged; in hospital on 9th 7 officers and 369 O.R. 10–16 December: 8 officers and 163 O.R. admitted; 2 officers and 212 O.R. discharged; in hospital on 16th 13 officers and 320 other ranks. 17–23 December: 68 admitted; 5 officers and 160 O.R. discharged; in hospital on 23rd 8 officers and 228 O.R. 24–30 December: 6 officers and 215 O.R. admitted; 3 officers and 143 O.R. discharged; in hospital on 30th 11 officers and 300 other ranks. 31 December: 42 admitted, 11 discharged; in hospital on 31st 11 officers and 331 other ranks. 7 of the unit's personnel were in hospital on 31 December; during the month 10 of the personnel were admitted and 9 discharged.
- **Enemy air activity:** frequent visits from Taubes during the month; on one day in December (the day is not legible) an anti-aircraft shell fell in the Officers' ward.
- **Weather:** rainy at first, fine for the last ten days.
- **Deaths:** none recorded this month — no death figures are given in the December entries.
- **Named people:** Lt-Col. E. J. Williams, S/Sgt Gough, Cpls Hughes and Galloway (to England on leave, 18 December); Capt. Halsall (returned to duty from leave); Major C. H. Morris became O.C. during Lt-Col. Williams' absence.
- **Uncertain readings:** the day of the Taubes visit / anti-aircraft shell incident in December is not legible; the word after "December" in that line is also not legible.
- No appendices are listed or included for this volume.
