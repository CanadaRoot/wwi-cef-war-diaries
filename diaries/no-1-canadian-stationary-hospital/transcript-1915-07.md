## Notes — July 1915
- **Place name:** the camp in France is written "Honveault Camp" throughout these pages. The June transcript reads the same name as "Honeault" — the two readings differ and the spelling should be reconciled. On p. 2 the place line reads "Honveault France" with a horizontal stroke through "France".
- **Cover correction:** the cover says "From 1st July To 31st August 1915" with "31st" corrected above to "30th".
- **Struck through:** after the 20 July entry (Sir Robert Borden's inspection) the words "A.D.M.S" are struck through in the original.
- **Uncertain word:** in the 3 July entry, the word before "begun" in "Special work in Hospital & Company motion begun" is read as "motion" but is marked [uncertain].
- **The move to the Mediterranean:** on 22 July the hospital is ordered to stop receiving patients and pack for "Abbeville"; on 28 July a wire from the War Office orders the equipment stored at Boulogne and the unit embarked for the Mediterranean. On 30 July the personnel sail from Boulogne on the hospital ship St Patrick to Dover and entrain for Shorncliffe.
- **Named people:** General Woodhouse (2 July); Lieutenants Frederick, Groff, Marcey and Kerfoot, R.A.M.C. (Canadians) (3 July); Sir Robert Borden, Generals Sawyer and Col Evans (20 July); Capt. Gwynn (operation for a septic hand, 25 July; removed to No. 7 Stationary Hospital, Boulogne).
- **Casualties:** 13 July — 42 cases admitted, several serious cases requiring immediate amputation, one fatality due to cardiac failure. No other deaths are recorded in July.
- No appendices are listed or included for this volume.
