## Notes — January 1916
- **The evacuation of Helles:** on 1 January there are 200 patients in hospital and no wounded from Helles, which is now being evacuated; the hospital is very quiet, with many patients awaiting evacuation to Base.
- **Turkish prisoners:** 35 Turkish sick and wounded prisoners are admitted on 10 January, four very seriously; on 27 January the Turkish prisoners are evacuated to No. 3 Canadian Hospital.
- **The move to Egypt:** on 19 January the unit is ordered to be ready to move within 24 hours' notice; equipment is packed up and huts taken down, Egypt being the destination. On 28 January the nurses go on board the hospital ship Dover Castle, the officers following the next day; on 31 January all equipment is loaded on the Dover Castle and the unit leaves Mudros Harbour for Alexandria at 8 am.
- **Weather:** high winds for 3 days before 10 January; heavy rain and wind on 14 January — 75 soldiers who could not get away on transport are accommodated, and 1,200 troops are turned back to camp in the rain after their tents had been struck. Many developed coughs and colds the following day.
- **Uncertain word:** in the 19 January entry the word between "in" and "empty" in "Officers move out of huts in [uncertain] empty hospital huts" could not be read with confidence.
- **Line through "move":** in the 19 January entry the word "move" in "Nurses move into Ward huts" has a horizontal line through it in the original; it is transcribed as written.
- **Edge cuts:** the 1 January entry ("evacuation to Base") is cut at the right edge; the ship name "Dover Castle" is cut at the right edge on p. 6.
