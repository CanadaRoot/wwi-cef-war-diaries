## Notes — August 1917
- **The unit leaves Salonica.** On 2 August the D.M.S. orders a nominal roll of personnel for embarkation with a view to the unit's return to England. A board meets 6 August to take over the Canadian equipment (Colonel Binall, president; Captain Preistly for 68th General Hospital; Major Johnson for No. 1 Canadian Stationary Hospital; Lieut-Col. Longhurst and Lieut-Col. Williams, D.S.O., in attendance), inventories are checked and valued 7–9 August, and all inventories pass on 10 August.
- **Embarkation of the first party:** on 15 August orders are received for all nursing sisters, two officers and ninety-five other ranks to embark at 6.45 p.m. the next day, taking all equipment with them; Major Sutherland and Captain Ramsay are chosen to go with the party. On 16 August 17 nursing sisters, 2 officers and 95 other ranks embark on H.M. Transport Aragon, with parties from Nos. 4 and 5 General Hospitals; the men are paid in English silver. Colonel Longhurst with R.A.M.C. officers and nursing sisters takes over the hospital at 9 a.m. H.M. Transport Aragon sails at 5 p.m. on 17 August.
- **The Great Fire of Salonica:** the town catches fire on 18 August, beginning in the Turkish Quarters at 2 p.m.; by midnight half the city is burnt, the wind very high and constantly changing, over 100,000 people rendered homeless. On 19 August the fire is still burning in some areas; very little damage is done to British stores except No. 7 Base Medical Stores; headquarters is burnt out but all papers are saved. Streets are under martial law, policed by French troops. Fresh food is very scanty, the markets having been burnt. The remaining party awaits orders to embark.
- **Patients:** 178 in hospital on 1 August; 210 on 3 August; 230 patients and two nursing sisters in hospital on 11 August (24 of the unit's own personnel in hospital); 100 patients transferred to other hospitals on 12 August.
- **Weather:** hot and humid, 99° on 4 August, 97° on 6 August, still very hot, 92° on 12 August.
- **Tenth Division** embarked for another base on 31 August.
- **Named people:** Colonel Binall (president of the equipment board; spelled "Binell" in the 17 August entry), Captain Preistly (68th General Hospital), Major Johnson (No. 1 Canadian Stationary Hospital), Lieut-Col. Longhurst, Lieut-Col. E. J. Williams D.S.O., Major Sutherland and Captain Ramsay (chosen to go with the embarking party).
- **Uncertain readings:** none in the diary text on these pages; the initials in the Remarks column and the "Binall / Binell" spelling variation are as written.
- No appendices are listed or included for this volume.
- **Gaps:** no entries for 5, 13–14, 20–30 August; the diary resumes on 31 August.
