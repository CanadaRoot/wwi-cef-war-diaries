## Transcription conventions
- Faithful to the original: spelling, punctuation, abbreviations and capitalisation as written (the January diary-text sheet is typed; the February–May sheets are handwritten).
- `[uncertain]` marks a word or name that could not be read with confidence — never guessed.
- Text struck through in the original is shown as `[struck through in original: "..."]`.
- The diary-text pages are Army Form C. 2118, "WAR DIARY or INTELLIGENCE SUMMARY", in columns: Place | Date | Hour | Summary of Events and Information | Remarks and references to Appendices.
- No map coordinates (latitude/longitude) are given in the diary.
- Ditto marks (") in the original are reproduced as " in the tables.

## Notes — January 1916
- The diary opens mid-journey: the detachment marched from its billet at Folkestone on 17 January, entrained at Shorncliffe, embarked at Southampton on the steamer "Sou'wester Millar", landed at Le Havre on 18 January, and reached Abbeville by train on 21 January, where it worked in the Supply Depot as checkers (with one man as interpreter) for the rest of the month.
- No entry is dated before 17 January; the diary begins with the move to France.
- The "7.00 a.m. Detrained." row is dated " (ditto of 21-1-16) in the original, although the train only arrived at Abbeville at 5.30 p.m. on 21 January — the detraining was presumably on the morning of 22 January; transcribed as written.
- The parenthetical about Lieut. Hollister spans the 22 and 23 January rows and is not closed in the original: "( Lieut. Hollister departed for Boulogne" on 22 January, with "to attend Supply School of Instruction" following "Issued extra blankets to men. -(" on 23 January.
- The remarks column carries handwritten initials on every row (they appear to read "L.S.B.", with initials of the form "S.B.H.C." from 27 January, when T/2nd Lt. S.B.H. Coppard took temporary command). A large ink blot obscures the remarks for the Shorncliffe and Southampton rows. Individual initials could not be read with confidence, so they are marked [uncertain] in the table.
- The 19 January row has no place entered (the unit was still at Le Havre).
- No appendices are listed; the contents page lists "Diary Text 1 Page" only. The envelope (p. 3) is the despatch cover for this volume, stamped 7-FEB.1916.
