## Transcription conventions
- Faithful to the original: spelling, punctuation, abbreviations and capitalisation as written (the diary is handwritten throughout).
- `[uncertain]` marks a word or name that could not be read with confidence — never guessed.
- Text struck through in the original is shown as `[struck through in original: "..."]`.
- The diary-text pages are Army Form C. 2118, "WAR DIARY or INTELLIGENCE SUMMARY", handwritten in columns: Place | Date | Hour | Summary of Events and Information | Remarks and references to Appendices.
- The weather for each day is written at the right-hand end of the Summary column; it is shown here after an em dash in the Summary cell.
- Ditto marks (`"`) in the original are reproduced as `"`.
- The Remarks column contains the initialling officer's initials, written in a flowing hand — in November the whole column is scribbled over (see Notes).
- No map coordinates (latitude/longitude) are given in the diary; the unit remained at Reninghelst Siding throughout the month.

## Notes — November 1916
- The unit remained at Reninghelst Siding for the whole month; every day's entry is the same duty line, "Men employed on Railhead Supply Duties", with only the weather varying. No casualties, movements or other events are recorded.
- 14 November weather reads "Fair", but the word is written faintly, as if partly erased.
- The Remarks column has been heavily scribbled over in ink for every row on both diary pages, so the initialling officer's initials cannot be read; the scribble appears to cover repeated "J.H.S." initials (Capt. J. H. Sproule).
- The month is signed "J. H. Sproule Capt" at the foot of p. 4, with no unit line beneath the signature (unlike August and October).
- No appendices are listed or included for November.
