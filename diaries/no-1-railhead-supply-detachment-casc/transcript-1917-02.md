## Transcription conventions
- Faithful to the original: spelling, punctuation, abbreviations and capitalisation as written (the diary is handwritten throughout, except for the typed cover and contents pages).
- `[uncertain]` marks a word or name that could not be read with confidence — never guessed.
- Text struck through in the original is shown as `[struck through in original: "..."]`.
- Ditto marks in the original are rendered `〃`.
- The diary-text pages are Army Form C. 2118, "WAR DIARY or INTELLIGENCE SUMMARY", handwritten in columns: Place | Date | Hour | Summary of Events and Information | Remarks and references to Appendices. The weather for each day is written at the right-hand end of the Summary column and is shown here after an em dash in the Summary cell.
- No map coordinates (latitude/longitude) are given in the diary; the detachment remained at Reninghelst Siding throughout the month.
- The place name is spelled "Reninghelst Siding" as written in the original (modern spelling Reningelst, Belgium).

## Notes — February 1917
- Every day's entry is the same: "Men employed on Railhead duties." The only variation is the daily weather note. No casualties, movements, personnel changes or other events are recorded in February.
- No days are missing; all 28 days are entered (1–17 on p. 3, 18–28 on p. 4).
- The Remarks column carries initials on every row. On p. 3 rows 1–10 they are heavily scribbled and illegible; from 11 February onwards they appear to read "G.T.L." — shown as [uncertain] throughout, as the reading is not confident.
- A vertical handwritten note runs down the right-hand margin of p. 3, outside the table; it is illegible [uncertain].
- No appendices are listed or included for February.
- The cover's "Volume 14." (with "2." written below) and the contents page's "Volume 17-2" are the volume numbers as written; the discrepancy is in the original.
- A new officer signs this month: Lieut. G. A. [uncertain] as O.C., replacing the captain who signed January.
