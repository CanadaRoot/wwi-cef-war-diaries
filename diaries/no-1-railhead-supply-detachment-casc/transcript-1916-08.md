## Transcription conventions
- Faithful to the original: spelling, punctuation, abbreviations and capitalisation as written (the diary is handwritten throughout).
- `[uncertain]` marks a word or name that could not be read with confidence — never guessed.
- Text struck through in the original is shown as `[struck through in original: "..."]`.
- The diary-text pages are Army Form C. 2118, "WAR DIARY or INTELLIGENCE SUMMARY", handwritten in columns: Place | Date | Hour | Summary of Events and Information | Remarks and references to Appendices.
- The weather for each day is written at the right-hand end of the Summary column; it is shown here after an em dash in the Summary cell.
- Ditto marks (`"`) in the original are reproduced as `"`.
- The Remarks column contains the initialling officer's initials, written in a flowing hand (read as "G.H.C." and "J.H.S." — see Notes).
- No map coordinates (latitude/longitude) are given in the diary; the unit remained at Reninghelst Siding throughout the month.

## Notes — August 1916
- The unit remained at Reninghelst Siding for the whole month; every day's entry is the same duty line, "Men employed on Railhead Supply Duties", with only the weather varying, apart from the three events noted below.
- Gas attack, 9 August: "Gas attack. Alarm first given at 10.50 p.m. fumes reached camp ([uncertain], Sheet 28 N.W. B21a.) at 11.15 p.m. Weather – fine." The word or words in the parenthesis before "Sheet 28 N.W. B21a." could not be read. No casualties are recorded.
- Change of command, 12 August: "Capt Sproule assumes command of Unit" is written in the Summary column on the 12 August row. Capt. J. H. Sproule (C.A.S.C.) signs the month as O.C.
- 16 August: "G. Circuitt left today on instructions D.S.O. [uncertain] T. II Army." — written at the foot of p. 3 under the 16 August row. The abbreviation between "D.S.O." and "T." could not be read with confidence (it may be "&", i.e. "D.S. & T. II Army").
- The Remarks column on p. 4 (17–31 August) has been heavily scribbled over in ink for every row, so the initialling officer's initials cannot be read there. The same scribbling over the Remarks column recurs in the September–December pages. The 16 August row on p. 3 is initialled "J.H.S." (Capt. J. H. Sproule).
- The Remarks initials on p. 3 (1–15 August) read "G.H.C." (2/Lieut. G. H. Circuitt).
- No appendices are listed or included for August.
