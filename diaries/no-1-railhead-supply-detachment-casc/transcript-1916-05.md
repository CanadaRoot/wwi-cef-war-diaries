## Transcription conventions
- Faithful to the original: spelling, punctuation, abbreviations and capitalisation as written (the May diary-text sheets are handwritten).
- `[uncertain]` marks a word or name that could not be read with confidence — never guessed.
- Text struck through in the original is shown as `[struck through in original: "..."]`.
- The diary-text pages are Army Form C. 2118, "WAR DIARY or INTELLIGENCE SUMMARY", in columns: Place | Date | Hour | Summary of Events and Information | Remarks and references to Appendices.
- No map coordinates (latitude/longitude) are given in the diary.
- Ditto marks (") in the original are reproduced as " in the tables; long horizontal rules in the Summary column are shown as ——.

## Notes — May 1916
- The detachment remained at Reninghelst Siding all month, employed on railhead duties.
- Pte. Belleau: reported back from hospital 9 May, re-admitted to hospital 14 May, and on 30 May was evacuated to Base and struck off the strength of the unit from 23 May.
- Pte. Hay was transferred to the Canadian Casualty assembly Centre, Folkestone, from 4 April 1916 (recorded 12 May).
- Pte. Dolby reported from Base on 19 May and was taken on strength from 23 May; Pte. Scott reported at 8.45 p.m. on 27 May and was taken on strength from 21 May.
- A shell trench was dug on 25 May (p.m.). A gas alert at 5 a.m. on 28 May was followed by a heavy bombardment of Poperinghe that afternoon and all through the night; another gas alert came at 7.30 a.m. on 30 May.
- The remarks column carries "Cert." against every row on both sheets.
- No appendices are listed; the contents page lists "Diary Text 2 Pages" only.
