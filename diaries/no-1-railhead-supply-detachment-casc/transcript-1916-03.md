## Transcription conventions
- Faithful to the original: spelling, punctuation, abbreviations and capitalisation as written (the March diary-text sheets are handwritten).
- `[uncertain]` marks a word or name that could not be read with confidence — never guessed.
- Text struck through in the original is shown as `[struck through in original: "..."]`.
- The diary-text pages are Army Form C. 2118, "WAR DIARY or INTELLIGENCE SUMMARY", in columns: Place | Date | Hour | Summary of Events and Information | Remarks and references to Appendices.
- No map coordinates (latitude/longitude) are given in the diary.
- Ditto marks (") in the original are reproduced as " in the tables; long horizontal rules in the Summary column are shown as ——.

## Notes — March 1916
- The detachment remained at Bailleul all month, employed on railhead duties.
- Pte. McBride's eye wound dominates the month: discharged from hospital 8 March, his eye not so well on 9 March so he went and had it re-dressed, re-admitted to hospital 18 March for an operation on his eye, and discharged again 21 March.
- Pte. Hay sustained an injury to his knee on 15 March and was admitted to hospital that night; on 16 March he was evacuated to Base Hospital and was expected to be unfit for duty for at least three weeks.
- Major Longheed took over command of the unit on 27 March. Note: Longheed's signature already appears at the foot of the February sheet (p. 8) and of the 16–26 March sheet (p. 4) — the diary does not explain the change of command; transcribed as written. He signs as Captain on the 31 March sheet although the 27 March entry calls him Major.
- 3 March weather: the word before "at night" could not be read with confidence (it appears to read "August"); marked [uncertain].
- 10 March has no weather entry recorded.
- 26 March weather is partly obscured by the signature written across it; the middle word could not be read and is marked [uncertain].
- The remarks column on pp. 3–4 carries a single brace marked "C.A.S."; on p. 5 the brace is marked "Cert."
- No appendices are listed; the contents page lists "Diary Text 3 Pages" only.
