## Transcription conventions
- Faithful to the original: spelling, punctuation, abbreviations and capitalisation as written (the diary is handwritten throughout).
- `[uncertain]` marks a word or name that could not be read with confidence — never guessed.
- Text struck through in the original is shown as `[struck through in original: "..."]`.
- The diary-text pages are Army Form C. 2118, "WAR DIARY or INTELLIGENCE SUMMARY", handwritten in columns: Place | Date | Hour | Summary of Events and Information | Remarks and references to Appendices.
- The weather for each day is written at the right-hand end of the Summary column; it is shown here after an em dash in the Summary cell.
- Ditto marks (`"`) in the original are reproduced as `"`.
- The Remarks column contains the initialling officer's initials, written in a flowing hand (read as "C.F.T." where legible — see Notes).
- No map coordinates (latitude/longitude) are given in the diary; the unit remained at Reninghelst Siding throughout the month.

## Notes — October 1916
- The unit remained at Reninghelst Siding for the whole month; every day's entry is the same duty line, "Men employed on Railhead Supply Duties", with only the weather varying. No casualties, movements or other events are recorded.
- Duplicate date: 20 October is entered twice in succession on p. 3, both rows reading "Men employed on Railhead Supply Duties — Fine". The page therefore has 22 rows covering 1–21 October.
- The Remarks column is initialled "C.F.T." for 1–7 October, then heavily scribbled over in ink from 8 October to the end of the month, so the initials cannot be read there.
- No diary entry records a change of command, but the month is signed by Capt. J. H. Sproule as O.C., whereas September was signed by 2/Lieut. C. F. Tatham as acting O.C. (see `transcript-1916-09.md`).
- The contents page (p. 2) is scanned sideways in the LAC image set.
- No appendices are listed or included for October.
