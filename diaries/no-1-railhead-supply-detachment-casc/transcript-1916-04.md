- Faithful to the original: spelling, punctuation, abbreviations and capitalisation as written (the April diary-text sheets are handwritten).
- `[uncertain]` marks a word or name that could not be read with confidence — never guessed.
- Text struck through in the original is shown as `[struck through in original: "..."]`.
- The diary-text pages are Army Form C. 2118, "WAR DIARY or INTELLIGENCE SUMMARY", in columns: Place | Date | Hour | Summary of Events and Information | Remarks and references to Appendices.
- No map coordinates (latitude/longitude) are given in the diary.
- Ditto marks (") in the original are reproduced as " in the tables; long horizontal rules in the Summary column are shown as ——.

## Notes — April 1916
- The detachment moved from Bailleul to a new railhead at Reninghelst Siding, between Reninghelst and Poperinghe, on 7 April: left Bailleul by motor lorry at 12.30 p.m., arrived at 4.00 p.m., set up the office hut, and the men slept in a hut already erected. The place is written "Reninghelst Siding" in the Place column from 8 April.
- A move ordered on 2 April was afterwards cancelled; fresh orders on 6 April to move on 7 April.
- Issuing on the new railhead to the 3rd Canadian Division began 8 April; supplying Corps Troops began 12 April; supplying the 2nd Canadian Division began 15 April.
- Gas warnings were received on three nights running — 28 April (at night), 29 April (another gas attack, between midnight and 1.00 a.m.), and 30 April (another gas attack, about 10 p.m.). No gas attack on the detachment itself is recorded.
- Pte. Belleau was admitted to hospital on 26 April.
- 11 April has no weather entry recorded. The 20 April weather entry ("Showery.") is struck through in the original, leaving no weather for that day.
- The remarks column is blank on both sheets.
- No appendices are listed; the contents page lists "Diary Text 2 Pages" only.
