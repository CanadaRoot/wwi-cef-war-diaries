## Transcription conventions
- Faithful to the original: spelling, punctuation, abbreviations and capitalisation as written (the diary is handwritten throughout).
- `[uncertain]` marks a word or name that could not be read with confidence — never guessed.
- Text struck through in the original is shown as `[struck through in original: "..."]`.
- The diary-text pages are Army Form C. 2118, "WAR DIARY or INTELLIGENCE SUMMARY", handwritten in columns: Place | Date | Hour | Summary of Events and Information | Remarks and references to Appendices.
- The weather for each day is written at the right-hand end of the Summary column; it is shown here after an em dash in the Summary cell.
- Ditto marks (`"`) in the original are reproduced as `"`.
- The Remarks column contains the initialling officer's initials, written in a flowing hand — in December the column is largely scribbled over (see Notes).
- No map coordinates (latitude/longitude) are given in the diary; the unit remained at Reninghelst Siding throughout the month.

## Notes — December 1916
- The unit remained at Reninghelst Siding for the whole month. The duty line changes wording this month, from "Men employed on Railhead Supply Duties" (July–November) to "Men employed on Railhead duties." No casualties, movements or other events are recorded.
- The diary-text heading this month adds "C.A.S.C." after the unit name: "No. 1 Canadian Railhead Supply Detachment, C.A.S.C."
- The word "the" in the p. 3 and p. 4 headings ("Original for the month of December, 1916.") appears struck through and rewritten.
- The Remarks column has been heavily scribbled over in ink for 1–24 December, so the initialling officer's initials cannot be read there; from 25 December the initials read "J.H.S." (Capt. J. H. Sproule).
- Christmas Day, 25 December, is recorded as "Fair. Heavy wind" with no holiday note — the men were employed on railhead duties as usual.
- The month is signed "J. H. Sproule Capt" at the foot of p. 4, with no unit line beneath the signature (as in November).
- No appendices are listed or included for December.
