## Transcription conventions
- Faithful to the original: spelling, punctuation, abbreviations and capitalisation as written (the February diary-text sheets are handwritten).
- `[uncertain]` marks a word or name that could not be read with confidence — never guessed.
- Text struck through in the original is shown as `[struck through in original: "..."]`.
- The diary-text pages are Army Form C. 2118, "WAR DIARY or INTELLIGENCE SUMMARY", in columns: Place | Date | Hour | Summary of Events and Information | Remarks and references to Appendices.
- No map coordinates (latitude/longitude) are given in the diary.
- Ditto marks (") in the original are reproduced as " in the tables; long horizontal rules in the Summary column are shown as ——.

## Notes — February 1916
- The detachment moved from Abbeville to Bailleul on 11–12 February: orders to move were received on 11 February, the detachment left Abbeville by train at 4 p.m. and arrived at Bailleul at 8.00 p.m., securing billets for the men. The move text is transcribed under 11 February, where it begins, but it spans into the 12 February row, which is headed "En route" and has a ruled line in the Summary column.
- Pte. McBride was accidentally wounded in the left eye by gunshot on 17 February and admitted to hospital — the month's only casualty.
- Lt. Hollister left on 28 February for Bagneux [uncertain]; a Lt. [uncertain] Fry, R.S.O., took temporary charge of the detachment. Neither the place name nor the lieutenant's initials could be read with confidence.
- The 28 February row has no weather entry recorded.
- The remarks column on both diary-text sheets carries a single brace with the words "Cert. for [uncertain] N." — the final word could not be read with confidence.
- 1916 was a leap year; 29 February has an entry.
- No appendices are listed; the contents page lists "Diary Text 2 Pages" only.
