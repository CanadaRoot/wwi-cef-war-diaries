## Notes — June 1916
- No appendices are listed on the contents page; none are included.
- No casualties are recorded in June 1916.
- Notable items: 2 June — Capt. Lougheed proceeded on leave; 2nd Lieut. Circuitt temporarily in command. 8 June — 2nd Canadian Division (Army) started drawing [supplies] today. 15 June — Capt. Lougheed returned off leave. 16 June — 2nd Lieut. Circuitt proceeded to Hippenhoek [uncertain] (place name as read; not identified).
- The remarks-column initials are a flowing hand and are read as "C.S.L." (Lougheed) and "G.H.C." (Circuitt) — both flagged [uncertain]. The 6, 14, 16 and 18 June weather entries are ditto marks repeating the previous day's weather.
- No map coordinates are given in this diary.
