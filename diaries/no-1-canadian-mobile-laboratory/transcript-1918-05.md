## Transcription conventions
- Faithful to the original: spelling, punctuation, abbreviations and capitalisation as written (the diary is typed, except for the handwritten cover and title pages).
- `[uncertain]` marks a word or name that could not be read with confidence — never guessed.
- Text struck through in the original is shown as `[struck through in original: "..."]`.

## Notes — May 1918
- May is the busiest month of the diary for personnel movement: Capt. Little to London (2 May) and Portsmouth (31 May), both day trips; Pte. F. Earnshaw (No. 526785) reported from the C.A.M.C. Depot (8 May) and was posted to the Canadian Special Hospital eleven days later (19 May); Capt. C. A. Rae ceased to be attached on posting to the C.A.M.C. Depot (10 May); Capt. P. T. McIlroy to London on leave and back the same day (18 May); Capt. P. A. Williams attached for duty from the C.A.M.C. Depot (21 May).
- The remarks column is initialled "T.P.L." only on days with an entry of note (1–2, 8, 10, 18–19, 21, 31); the other days are blank.
- The title page was first headed "June 1918" and corrected to "May 1918".
- No appendices are listed or included for May.
