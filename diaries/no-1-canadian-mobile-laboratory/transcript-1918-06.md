## Transcription conventions
- Faithful to the original: spelling, punctuation, abbreviations and capitalisation as written (the diary is typed, except for the handwritten cover).
- `[uncertain]` marks a word or name that could not be read with confidence — never guessed.
- Text struck through in the original is shown as `[struck through in original: "..."]`.
- The diary-text page is Army Form C. 2118, "WAR DIARY or INTELLIGENCE SUMMARY", typed in columns: Place | Date | Hour | Summary of Events and Information | Remarks and references to Appendices.
- No map coordinates (latitude/longitude) are given in the diary; the unit remained at Witley Camp, Surrey, England throughout.

## Notes — June 1918
- No entry exists for 17 June: the date column runs 16, 18, 19 … — the typist skipped the 17th (the 18 June line is typed shifted left, starting in the Hour column). Verified by enlarged reading of the date column (`zoom-jun-dates-16-19.jpg`).
- The "present epidemic" of 25–30 June is the influenza epidemic: on 2 July Capt. Little read an "Original Article on Influenza Epidemic" before the Witley Area Medical Society (see July transcript).
- The two other ranks (10 June) and four other ranks (14 June) who reported from the C.A.M.C. Depot are not named.
- The 15 June visitors — Major General Hughes, Hon. Mr. Meighen, Hon. Mr. Calder and Hon. Mr. Rowell — are named as written; no first names or titles beyond those given are recorded in the diary.
- June has no separate handwritten title page (cover, contents, diary text only).
- No appendices are listed or included for June.
