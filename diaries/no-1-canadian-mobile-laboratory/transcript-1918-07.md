## Transcription conventions
- Faithful to the original: spelling, punctuation, abbreviations and capitalisation as written (the diary is typed, except for the handwritten cover).
- `[uncertain]` marks a word or name that could not be read with confidence — never guessed.
- Text struck through in the original is shown as `[struck through in original: "..."]`.
- The diary-text page is Army Form C. 2118, "WAR DIARY or INTELLIGENCE SUMMARY", typed in columns: Place | Date | Hour | Summary of Events and Information | Remarks and references to Appendices.
- No map coordinates (latitude/longitude) are given in the diary; the unit remained at Witley Camp, Surrey, England throughout.

## Notes — July 1918
- The O.C.'s initials are typed "T.R. Little" in the 24, 25 and 31 July entries, but "T.P." everywhere else in the diary (May 2 and 31; June 1 "Capt. T.P. Little, O.C.") and the January signature reads "Thos. P. Little". The July "T.R." is treated as a typist's error and transcribed as written, not corrected.
- Capt. McIlroy's initials are also inconsistent: "P.T." (18 May; 19 July) but "P.F." (12 July) — transcribed as written each time.
- "Capy P.T. McIlroy" (19 July) is typed as written — evidently a typo for "Capt.".
- "Jult 28" is typed as written — a typo for "July 28".
- The remarks column in July is a repeated cursive flourish, illegible — not the clear "T.P.L." of December–June (see `zoom-jul-remarks2.jpg`). The month is signed off in the 31 July remarks by Capt. P. McIlroy [uncertain], presumably acting while Little was on leave from 31 July.
- The 2 July entry ties the June epidemic investigation to influenza: Capt. Little read an original article on the influenza epidemic before the Witley Area Medical Society.
- Post-mortem work at Bramshott (6, 14 and 23 July), guinea pigs shipped to Eastbourne (12 July), Canadian Press representatives visiting the camp and animals moved to new quarters (15 July), and a visit by Sir Robert Borden (19 July) are the month's notable events. Sir Robert Borden is named as written; no further identification is given in the diary.
- July has no separate handwritten title page (cover, contents, diary text only).
- No appendices are listed or included for July.
- **End of diary:** the LAC record for this diary ends 31 July 1918. The successor diary (Canadian General Laboratory, Witley, 1 Aug. 1918 – 31 Mar. 1919, same volume 5038) is a separate LAC record.
