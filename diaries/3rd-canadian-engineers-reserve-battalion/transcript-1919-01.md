# War Diary — 3rd Canadian Engineers Reserve Battalion — January 1919

- **Unit:** 3rd Canadian Engineers Reserve Battalion (3rd C.E.R.B.)
- **Month:** January 1919 (Volume No. 6) — 1 January to 31 January 1919
- **Source:** Library and Archives Canada, Collection Search Item ID 2004925 (RG 9 III-D-3, vol. 5008, file 705, reel T-10856, finding aid 9-52)
- **Pages:** January 1919, p. 1 – p. 3 (image files page-0032 – page-0034)
- **January 1919 — COMPLETE** (pages 1–3): cover, diary text 1–31 January, no appendices. Transcribed 2026-10-27.

## Transcription conventions
Original spelling, punctuation and abbreviations kept. Diary text is typewritten throughout (Army Form C. 2118, "WAR DIARY or INTELLIGENCE SUMMARY" — neither heading is struck through; the printed note reads "(Erase heading not required.)"). `[uncertain]` marks words I could not read with confidence — never guessed. Each January entry is initialled in ink at the end of the Summary column; the initials are not legibly readable and are not transcribed. Place column reads "Seaford" throughout. Hour column is blank throughout. The "Remarks and references to Appendices" column is blank throughout. "O.C." = Officer Commanding; "G.O.C." = General Officer Commanding; "M.M." = Military Medal; "Battn" = Battalion; "Capt." = Captain; "Sgt." = Sergeant; "O.R." = other ranks; "C.E.R.B." = Canadian Engineers Reserve Battalion; "C.E.R.Bns" = Canadian Engineers Reserve Battalions; "Kinmel Park" is the camp at Rhyl, Wales (written "Kinmel Park Rhyll" on 13 January). "T.B.D's" (16 January) is as written and is not expanded. The printed footer reads "D. D. & L., London, E.C. (20960) Wt W5300/P713 750,000 3/18 & 2888 Forms/C2118/16."

## Pages 2–3 — Diary text (1–31 January 1919)

Army Form C. 2118, "WAR DIARY or INTELLIGENCE SUMMARY". The printed instructions at the top left read "Instructions regarding War Diaries and Intelligence Summaries are contained in F. S. Regs., Part II. and the Staff Manual respectively. Title pages will be prepared in manuscript."

### Page 2 — 1–15 January 1919

Place: Seaford.

> **1-1-19.** — Very wet and stormy.
>
> **2-1-19.** — Very heavy rains, all the day. Camp lines in bad condition.
>
> **3-1-19.** — Very heavy rains all day. Camp lines in bad condition.
>
> **4-1-19.** — Men paraded to visit submarine. Battn Parade addressed by O.C. Khaki College.
>
> **5-1-19.** — Blowing very hard today but dry. 13 Officers left, [for Canada — inserted in ink above the line] tonight under Capt. Hardy at 8.40.
>
> **6-1-19.** — Still very windy with heavy hail showers.
>
> **7-1-19.** — Khaki College opened new session. 112 Other Ranks attending.
>
> **8-1-19.** — Very wet. Half holiday.
>
> **9-1-19.** — Very Wet. — Showers. Party No 19 inspected by G.O.C.
>
> **10-1-19.** — Very wet. Parade for presentation of M.M. to Sgt. Lea called off owing to rain. Party No [struck through] 19 left for Canada 0600 a.m. 5 Officers despatched to Canada,
>
> **11-1-19.** — Very heavy showers. Documents for party No 21 of 35 Other Ranks forwarded to Regtl. Depots.
>
> **12-1-19.** — Fine [uncertain] weather at last. G.O.C. inspection of Party No 20 of 34 Other Ranks. Church Parade.
>
> **13-1-19.** — Heavy showers. Party No 20 of 34 Other Ranks despatched to Kinmel Park Rhyll.
>
> **14-1-19.** — Showery. Bleak.
>
> **15-1-19.** — Very wet. Half holiday. 15 Other Ranks returned from France posted to "B" Coy.

### Page 3 — 16–31 January 1919

Place: Seaford. The sheet is headed "Continued." at the top left.

> **16-1-19.** — Very heavy showers and heavy hail storms. T.B.D's left Newhaven.
>
> **17-1-19.** — Beautiful bright day. G.O.C's inspection of Draft No 22.
>
> **18-1-19.** — Fine sunny. Party No 22 of 55 Other Ranks dispatched to Kinmel Park Camp.
>
> **19-1-19.** — Fine sunny day. Church parades. O.C's inspection of parties 23 and 24.
>
> **20-1-19.** — Fine. Frosty. Bridge match between 2nd and 3rd C.E.R.Bns Officers' messes 2nd Bn won 112 points.
>
> **21-1-19.** — Wet. Cold. Party No 23, 29 Other Ranks left for Kinmel Park Camp 0615 o'clock.
>
> **22-1-19.** — Wet and cold. Party No 24 of 16 Other Ranks left for Kinmel Park.
>
> **23-1-19.** — Very wet and stormy all day.
>
> **24-1-19.** — Blowing hard and dry all day.
>
> **25-1-19.** — Cold bleak, slight rain.
>
> **26-1-19.** — Fine. frosty day.
>
> **27-1-19.** — Fine during day heavy rain in evening. Bridge tournament opens at Officers' Club.
>
> **28-1-19.** — Cloudy with occasional rain.
>
> **29-1-19.** — Cold frosty. snow. Dance at Officers' Club.
>
> **30-1-19.** — Cold frosty. Fall of snow.
>
> **31-1-19.** — Fine and frosty. A covering of snow all over the camp.
>
> [Signed, bottom right:] *[signature — appears to read "J. B. Dunbar", Col.,] O.C. 3rd C.E.R.B.*

## Open questions / uncertain items (January 1919)
- 5 January: "for Canada" is inserted in ink above the line between "left," and "tonight" — the insertion is legible and is transcribed as written.
- 10 January: a word after "Party No" is struck through before "19" and could not be read; it may have been a party number corrected to 19.
- 12 January: a word between "Fine" and "weather" could not be read — transcribed as "Fine [uncertain] weather at last."
- 13 January: "Kinmel Park Rhyll" is as written (double "l" in Rhyll).
- 16 January: "T.B.D's left Newhaven" is as written; the abbreviation is not expanded.
- 20 January: "2nd Bn won 112 points" is as written.
- The O.C.'s signature at the foot of the January sheets appears to read "J. B. Dunbar" — the same signature is legible on the February sheets as "J. B. Dunbar, Lieut. Col. C.E., Commanding 3rd C.E.R.B." — but the January signature is recorded here with that reservation.
- The per-entry ink initials in the Summary column are not legibly readable and are not transcribed; several days' initials appear to have been added at once (23–26 January carry a cluster of initials together).
- Names read as: Capt. Hardy; Sgt. Lea; the O.C. (signature, see above). No other officers are named in January.
- No casualties, deaths, or hospital admissions are recorded in January 1919. No map coordinates (latitude/longitude) are given; the only places named are Seaford, Newhaven, Kinmel Park (Rhyl), and Canada.
- The month is dominated by demobilisation: repatriation parties No. 19–24 leave Seaford for Canada via Kinmel Park, officers sail for Canada, and men return from France. Weather is recorded every day and is wet, stormy or snowy for most of the month.
