# War Diary — 3rd Canadian Engineers Reserve Battalion — February 1919

- **Unit:** 3rd Canadian Engineers Reserve Battalion (3rd C.E.R.B.)
- **Month:** February 1919 (Volume No. 7) — 1 February to 28 February 1919
- **Source:** Library and Archives Canada, Collection Search Item ID 2004925 (RG 9 III-D-3, vol. 5008, file 705, reel T-10856, finding aid 9-52)
- **Pages:** February 1919, p. 1 – p. 4 (image files page-0035 – page-0038)
- **February 1919 — COMPLETE** (pages 1–4): cover, diary text 1–28 February, no appendices. Transcribed 2026-10-27.

## Transcription conventions
Original spelling, punctuation and abbreviations kept. Diary text is typewritten throughout (Army Form C. 2118, "WAR DIARY or INTELLIGENCE SUMMARY" — neither heading is struck through; the printed note reads "(Erase heading not required.)"). `[uncertain]` marks words I could not read with confidence — never guessed. Place column reads "Seaford" throughout (written "Seaford." with a full stop on most February rows). Hour column is blank throughout. The "Remarks and references to Appendices" column carries a handwritten "Ok" against each entry. "O.C." = Officer Commanding; "G.O.C." = General Officer Commanding; "Lieut." = Lieutenant; "Capt." = Captain; "O.R." = other ranks; "C.E.R.B." = Canadian Engineers Reserve Battalion; "Res. Bn." = Reserve Battalion; "Kinmel" = Kinmel Park camp, Rhyl, Wales. The printed footer reads "D. D. & L., London, E.C. (20960) Wt W5300/P713 750,000 3/18 & 2888 Forms/C2118/16."

## Pages 2–4 — Diary text (1–28 February 1919)

Army Form C. 2118, "WAR DIARY or INTELLIGENCE SUMMARY". The printed instructions at the top left read "Instructions regarding War Diaries and Intelligence Summaries are contained in F. S. Regs., Part II. and the Staff Manual respectively. Title pages will be prepared in manuscript."

### Page 2 — 1–11 February 1919

Place: Seaford.

> **1-2-19.** — Cold and Damp.
>
> **2-2-19.** — Fine frosty day, snow on the ground. 250 Other Ranks came in from France.
>
> **3-2-19.** — Dull Day. G.O.C's inspection Party 25, Nil Other Ranks proceeding.
>
> **4-2-19.** — Fine, inclined to snow. Party No.25 despatched, Nil other Ranks proceeded.
>
> **5-2-19.** — Weather Fine and Cold.
>
> **6-2-19.** — Weather Fine, Cold. O.C's inspection of Other Ranks with dependents returning to Canada.
>
> **7-2-19.** — Weather, good, very cold. G.O.C's inspection of 37 Other Ranks of Party 27. 2 Other Ranks proceeded to Buxton.
>
> **8-2-19.** — Weather very cold. Party 27, 37 Other Ranks proceeded to Rhyl.
>
> **9-2-19.** — Weather, good, cold.
>
> **10-2-19.** — Weather, good, cold. 20 Other Ranks returned from On Command Bexhill.
>
> **11-2-19.** — Weather, good, cold.

## Open questions / uncertain items (February 1919)
- 3–4 February: "Nil Other Ranks proceeding" / "Nil other Ranks proceeded" is as written — Party No. 25 was inspected and despatched but no other ranks proceeded with it.
- 10 February: the number of Other Ranks returned from On Command Bexhill reads "20", but the second digit appears overtyped in the original; the reading "20" is given with that reservation.
- 12 February: "50 Other Ranks reported from 1st Res. Bn., having reported from France" is as written, including the repeated "reported". "Lieut. F. Daniels, reported from Matlock, Bath" is as written.
- 15 February: Party 30 "proceeded to Canada" is as written (other parties proceeded to Kinmel).
- 19 February: "Major Gunn proceeded to Port of Embarkation" is as written; "Port of Embarkation" is not named.
- 22 February: "Muster Parade" is appended at the end of the entry with no further detail, and the next page begins a new entry for 23 February.
- 27 February: "Capt." is inserted in ink above "Lieut." before J.C. Brown — the rank correction is legible and is transcribed as written.
- The handwritten "Ok" in the Remarks column against each entry is as written; it may be the O.C.'s initials or a check mark.
- Party numbering jumps from No. 27 (7–8 February) to No. 30 (14–15 February); no parties No. 28 or No. 29 are mentioned in February.
- Names read as: Lieut. F. Daniels; Adjutant Lieut. J.C. Brown (admitted to hospital with influenza 16 February, discharged 27 February); Major Gunn; Lieut. Riley; Lieut. McLeod; Lieut.-Col. J. B. Dunbar, Commanding.
- Casualties in February 1919: none recorded — no deaths, no wounded. The only hospital case is Adjutant Lieut. J.C. Brown, admitted with influenza on 16 February and discharged on 27 February. No map coordinates (latitude/longitude) are given; the only places named are Seaford, France, Rhyl/Kinmel Park, Buxton, Bexhill, Matlock Bath, and Canada.
- The month is dominated by demobilisation: 250 other ranks arrive from France on 2 February, and repatriation parties No. 25–33 leave Seaford for Canada via Kinmel Park, Rhyl, or Buxton, with G.O.C. inspections of each party before departure.
