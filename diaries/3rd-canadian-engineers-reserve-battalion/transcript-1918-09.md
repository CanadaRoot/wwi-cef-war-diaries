# War Diary — 3rd Canadian Engineers Reserve Battalion — September 1918

- **Unit:** 3rd Canadian Engineers Reserve Battalion (3rd C.E.R.B.)
- **Month:** September 1918 — 1 September to 30 September 1918
- **Source:** Library and Archives Canada, Collection Search Item ID 2004925 (RG 9 III-D-3, vol. 5008, file 705, reel T-10856, finding aid 9-52)
- **Pages:** September 1918, p. 1 – p. 8 (image files page-0007 – page-0014)
- **September 1918 — COMPLETE** (pages 1–8): title page, filing sheet, Form X 401 minute sheet, diary text 1–30 September, no appendices. Transcribed 2026-10-27.

## Transcription conventions
Original spelling, punctuation and abbreviations kept. Diary text is typewritten throughout (Army Form C. 2118, "WAR DIARY" — the second line of the printed heading, "or INTELLIGENCE SUMMARY", is blacked out on the September sheets, except on the 13–19 September sheet where it is left standing). `[uncertain]` marks words I could not read with confidence — never guessed. `[blacked out]` marks words deliberately blacked out in the original. Place column reads "Seaford" throughout. The "Hour" column is blank throughout. The "Remarks and references to Appendices" column is blank except for a handwritten initial beside each entry. "C.E." = Canadian Engineers; "C.E.R.B." = Canadian Engineers Reserve Battalion; "C.E.T.C." = Canadian Engineers Training Centre; "C.S.M.E." = Canadian School of Military Engineering; "O.R." = other ranks; "O.C." = Officer Commanding; "G.O.C." = General Officer Commanding; "D.S.O." = Distinguished Service Order; "M.C." = Military Cross; "C.M.G." = Companion of the Order of St Michael and St George; "K.C.B." = Knight Commander of the Order of the Bath; "K.C.M.G." = Knight Commander of the Order of St Michael and St George; "V.C." = Victoria Cross; "C.A.P.C." = Canadian Army Pay Corps; "C.A.M.C." = Canadian Army Medical Corps; "O.T.C." = Officers' Training Corps; "M.O." = Medical Officer; "A.D.C.S.", "C.E.R.D.", "C.T.S.", "T.W.S." = as written (not expanded); "S.O.S." = struck off strength. Every day 1–30 September has an entry; there are no gaps in the month.

### Page 4 — 1–6 September 1918

Place: Seaford.

> **1-9-18**
>
> 1. Weather:- Fine but blowing.
> 2. 3rd. C.E.R.B took over the Brigade duties for the week and mounted their first guard.
>
> **2-9-18**
>
> 1. Weather:- Fine.
> 2. Call for one Officer for Tramways. Lt. W.H. Stuart's name was submitted.
> 3. Capt. [blacked out] C.S. Tyrrell attached from C.A.P.C.
>
> **3-9-18**
>
> 1. Weather:- Warm.
> 2. Capt. H.M. Yelland attached from C.A.M.C. effect 29-8-18.
>
> **4-9-18**
>
> 1. Weather:- Very warm.
> 2. Afternoon spent on sports, Lacrosse game between 2nd. C.E.R.B. vs 3rd. C.E.R.B. resulting in a win for the 2nd. Score 1-0.
> 3. 633 Other Ranks went on 6 days Landing Leave.
> 4. Draft of 279 Other Ranks arrived from Frensham Pond.
>
> **5-9-18.**
>
> 1. Weather:- Very warm.
> 2. Lieut. J.A. Rankin reported from Canada and is taken on strength.
> 3. [item numbered but blank in the original]
>
> **6-9-18**
>
> 1. Weather:- Warm.
> 2. Captain P.H. Lazenby reported back from Frensham Pond Off Command.
> 3. Lieut. W.H.A. Hamilton 4th Battalion Engineers reported from Hospital.
> 4. 19 Officers reported from O.T.C. wing of C.S.M.E., who have just received their commissions.

### Page 5 — 7–12 September 1918

Place: Seaford.

> **7-9-18**
>
> 1. Weather:- Wet and stormy.
> 2. Battalion went on route march.
> 3. Lieut. A. McMullen reported Off Command Mytchett.
> 4. Lieut. W.O. Towers reported from Hospital.
>
> **8-9-18.**
>
> 1. Weather:- Wet and stormy.
> 2. Reinforcement for France. 8 Officers called for—"nil" return.
>
> **9-9-18.**
>
> 1. Weather:- Wet and stormy.
> 2. Between 10.00 and 11.00 p.m. a number of tents were blown down in a heavy wind & rain storm.
> 3. About 600 Other Ranks, who were on draft leave came back to camp about 11.00 p.m.
>
> **10-9-18.**
>
> 1. Weather:- Wet and stormy.
> 2. About 4.00 p.m. there was a very heavy rain and hail storm, which tested our drainage to its full capacity.
>
> **11-9-18**
>
> 1. Weather:- Wet.
> 2. Lieut. R.L. Meek On Command London.
> 3. The C.E.T.C. Baseball Team won the Baseball Championship in England, defeating Epsom in the final at Gilford. Score 2-0.
>
> **12-9-18**
>
> 1. Weather:- Changeable.
> 2. Col. T.V. Anderson, D.S.O. and Lt. Col. McKenzie, 1st. Divisional Engineers visited the lines.

>

>
> 1. Weather:- Fair. 50 O.R's were transferred to 2nd. C.E.R.B. to complete draft for France.
>
> **18-9-18**
>
> 1. Weather:- Fair.
> 2. Captain P.H. Lazenby On Command to Frensham Pond.

## Open questions / uncertain items (September 1918)
- Page 2 (September p. 2) is a filing sheet carrying only the handwritten reference "14-4-C"; it contains no diary text.
- Page 3 is a Form X 401 minute sheet, otherwise blank. The handwritten note at the right edge appears to read "292645 Still. W." and the second name in the Central Registry column appears to read "In. Kennedy J." — both are recorded with that reservation.
- 2 Sep: the initials before "C.S. Tyrrell" ("Capt. [blacked out] C.S. Tyrrell attached from C.A.P.C.") are blacked out in the original.
- 5 Sep: item 3 is numbered but blank in the original.
- 7 Sep: "Lieut. A. McMullen reported Off Command Mytchett" — "Mytchett" is as written.
- 9 Sep: "About 600 Other Ranks" is as written.
- 11 Sep: "defeating Epsom in the final at Gilford. Score 2-0." — "Gilford" is as written.
- 12 Sep: "Lt. Col. McKenzie, 1st. Divisional Engineers" is as written.
- 14 Sep: "Lieut. L.G. Davies reported Off Command T.W.S. Bexhill" — "T.W.S." is as written.
- 17 Sep: "50 O.R's were transferred to 2nd. C.E.R.B." — the word "were" appears overtyped in the original; the reading is as given.
- 19 Sep: "the prizes were presented to the men by Mrs Gunn the wife of the O.C." — "Mrs" appears to be a handwritten correction in the original.
- 23 Sep: "Col. J.A. Gunn, C.M.G., D.S.O., Headquarters, London" is as written — a different officer from Major J.D. Gunn, the battalion's temporary commander.
- 26 Sep: "Lieut. C.C. Stewart S.O.S. to C.E.R.D. on proceeding on command to C.T.S. Bexhill" — "C.E.R.D." and "C.T.S." are as written and not expanded.
- 29 Sep: "Lt-Col McDonald, A.D.C.S." is as written; "A.D.C.S." is not expanded.
- 30 Sep: "Capt. H.W. Yelland. M.O." is as written, but the same officer was entered on 3 Sep as "Capt. H.M. Yelland" — the initials differ between the two entries as written.
- The diary sheet for 13–19 September is headed "WAR DIARY or INTELLIGENCE SUMMARY." with the second line left standing, unlike the other sheets where it is blacked out.
- The signature at the foot of the September sheets is illegible; the signatory is given only as "Major, C.E., A/O.C. 3rd Canadian Engineers Res. Battn." and is not identified as Major J.D. Gunn, although Major Gunn held temporary command.
- Names read as: Lt. W.H. Stuart; Capt. C.S. Tyrrell; Capt. H.M. Yelland (3 Sep) / Capt. H.W. Yelland (30 Sep); Lieut. J.A. Rankin; Captain P.H. Lazenby; Lieut. W.H.A. Hamilton; Lieut. A. McMullen; Lieut. W.O. Towers; Lieut. R.L. Meek; Col. T.V. Anderson; Lt. Col. McKenzie; Lieut. W.F. Stevenson; Lieut. L.G. Davies; Lieut. R.H. Machin; Lieut. General R.E.W. Turner; Major A.M. Wright; Col. J.A. Gunn; Lieut. S.W. Bulman; Lieut. C.C. Stewart; Lieut. G.A. Campbell; Capt H.B. Boswell; Lt-Col McDonald; Capt Eager; Mrs Gunn (wife of the O.C.); Sgt. McKay J.D. (minute sheet).
- Casualties in September 1918: none recorded. No deaths, no wounded, no men admitted to hospital are mentioned in the September entries (several officers are noted as reporting from hospital, but no casualties in the battalion itself).
- Movements: the battalion stayed at Seaford all month. The first draft for France — 565 other ranks — left at midnight on 19 September; 50 other ranks were transferred to the 2nd C.E.R.B. on 17 September to complete the draft for France; a draft of 279 other ranks arrived from Frensham Pond on 4 September and a draft of 429 other ranks came in from Bourley on 23 September.
- No map coordinates (latitude/longitude) are given; the only places named are Seaford, Frensham Pond, Bourley, Bexhill (Trench Warfare School / C.T.S.), Mytchett, London, Gilford, Epsom, and Shorncliffe.
