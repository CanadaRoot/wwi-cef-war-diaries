# War Diary — 3rd Canadian Engineers Reserve Battalion — March 1919

- **Unit:** 3rd Canadian Engineers Reserve Battalion (3rd C.E.R.B.)
- **Month:** March 1919 — 1 March to 31 March 1919
- **Source:** Library and Archives Canada, Collection Search Item ID 2004925 (RG 9 III-D-3, vol. 5008, file 705, reel T-10856, finding aid 9-52)
- **Pages:** March 1919, p. 1 – p. 3 (image files page-0039 – page-0041)
- **March 1919 — COMPLETE** (pages 1–3): cover, diary text 1–31 March, no appendices. Transcribed 2026-10-27.

## Transcription conventions
Original spelling, punctuation and abbreviations kept. Diary text is typewritten throughout (Army Form C. 2118, "WAR DIARY or INTELLIGENCE SUMMARY" — neither heading is struck through; the printed note reads "(Erase heading not required.)"). `[uncertain]` marks words I could not read with confidence — never guessed. Unlike January and February, the March sheets write the place and date together in the Place column ("Seaford, Mar. 1st. 1919.") and the Date and Hour columns are blank throughout. Each March entry is initialled in ink at the end of the Summary column; the initials are not legibly readable and are not transcribed. The "Remarks and references to Appendices" column is blank throughout. "O.C." = Officer Commanding; "G.O.C." = General Officer Commanding; "Lieut." = Lieutenant; "Capt." = Captain; "Coy." = Company; "C.E.R.B." = Canadian Engineers Reserve Battalion; "C.E.T.C." = Canadian Engineers Training Centre; "Kinmel Park" is the camp at Rhyl, Wales. "D.O.W.E." (5 March) and "G.C.M." (6 March) are as written and are not expanded. The March sheets are printed on an older form: the printed footer reads "A6945 Wt. W11423/M1160 35,000 12/16 D. D. & L. Forms/C.2118/14." The diary says nothing about any disturbance or riot at Seaford in March 1919 — the entries record routine weather, parades, inspections and repatriation parties only.

## Open questions / uncertain items (March 1919)
- 5 March: "for work under D.O.W.E." is as written; the abbreviation is not expanded. "Capt. Duff, In Charge" is as written.
- 6 March: "G.C.M. Lieut. J.H. Clements." is as written; the abbreviation is not expanded and no further detail is given.
- 10 March: "1st. Check of Barracks Stores in C.E.T.C. Lines" is as written.
- 14 March: "Leave party of 130 Other Ranks proceeded." is as written — no destination is given.
- Party numbering jumps from No. 35 (13–14 March) to No. 37 (17–18 March); no Party No. 36 is mentioned in March.
- A small ink mark appears in the left margin beside the 19 March row; it is not read as text.
- Names read as: Capt. Duff; Lieut. J.H. Clements; Lieut. General Turner; Lieut.-Col. J. B. Dunbar, Commanding.
- Casualties in March 1919: none recorded — no deaths, no wounded, no hospital admissions. No map coordinates (latitude/longitude) are given; the only places named are Seaford, Kinmel Park (Rhyl), Newhaven, Bramshott, Norton, and Canada.
- The month continues the demobilisation pattern: repatriation parties No. 34–40 leave Seaford for Canada via Kinmel Park, a 225-man working party goes to Newhaven (returning 17 March), 100 men go on duty at Bramshott, 131 men of the 3rd Tunnelling Company arrive on 11 March, and a 130-man leave party proceeds on 14 March. No disturbance, riot, or disciplinary incident of any kind is recorded in the March diary.
