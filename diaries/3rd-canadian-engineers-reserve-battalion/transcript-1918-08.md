# War Diary — 3rd Canadian Engineers Reserve Battalion — August 1918

- **Unit:** 3rd Canadian Engineers Reserve Battalion (3rd C.E.R.B.)
- **Month:** August 1918 — 6 August to 31 August 1918
- **Source:** Library and Archives Canada, Collection Search Item ID 2004925 (RG 9 III-D-3, vol. 5008, file 705, reel T-10856, finding aid 9-52)
- **Pages:** August 1918, p. 1 – p. 6 (image files page-0001 – page-0006)
- **August 1918 — COMPLETE** (pages 1–6): false docket, title page, diary text 6–31 August, no appendices. Transcribed 2026-10-27.

## Transcription conventions
Original spelling, punctuation and abbreviations kept. Diary text is typewritten throughout (Army Form C. 2118, "WAR DIARY" — the second line of the printed heading, "or INTELLIGENCE SUMMARY", is blacked out on the August sheets). `[uncertain]` marks words I could not read with confidence — never guessed. `[blacked out]` marks words deliberately blacked out in the original. Place column reads "Seaford" throughout. The "Hour" column is blank throughout. The "Remarks and references to Appendices" column is blank except for a handwritten initial beside each entry. "C.E." = Canadian Engineers; "C.E.R.B." = Canadian Engineers Reserve Battalion; "C.E.T.C." = Canadian Engineers Training Centre; "C.S.M.E." = Canadian School of Military Engineering; "O.R." = other ranks; "O.C." = Officer Commanding; "D.S.O." = Distinguished Service Order; "M.C." = Military Cross; "C.M.G." = Companion of the Order of St Michael and St George; "K.C.B." = Knight Commander of the Order of the Bath; "K.C.M.G." = Knight Commander of the Order of St Michael and St George; "V.C." = Victoria Cross; "N.A.C.B." = Navy and Army Canteen Board; "Y.M.C.A." = Young Men's Christian Association; "R.C." = Roman Catholic; "C. of E." = Church of England; "R.F.A." = Royal Field Artillery; "M.O." = Medical Officer; "S.O.S." = struck off strength; "C.E.R.D.", "C.T.S.", "T.W.S." = as written (not expanded). The diary begins on 6 August 1918, the day the battalion was formed; there are no entries for 1–5 August.

## Pages 3–6 — Diary text (6–31 August 1918)

Army Form C. 2118, "WAR DIARY". The printed footer reads "D. D. & L., London, E.C. (2950) Wt W3300/7713 750,000 3/18 E & S Forms/C2118/16."

### Page 3 — 6–10 August 1918 (Sheet No. 1)

Place: Seaford.

> **6-8-18.**
>
> 1. Weather:- Wet.
> 2. The Formation of the 3rd Canadian Engineers Reserve Battalion is authorized in No 564 Daily Orders C.E.T.C. of 5/8/18.
> Major J.D. Gunn, C.E. takes [blacked out] temporary Command. Capt. J.B. McPhail, C.E. as Adjutant.
>
> **7-8-18.**
>
> 1. Weather:- Fine.
> 2. First issue of 3rd C.E.R.B. Daily Orders was issued.
> 3. The First Battalion Parade was held at 9.00 am. on the Battalion Parade and the men just released from Segregation was divided into four Companies viz. "C" Company under the Command of Major R.F.E. Wood. "D" Company, Captain H.E. Boswell. "F" Company, Lieut. J.H. Munro, C.E. "E" Company, Lieut. W.H. Stewart.
> "A" Company which will be the Draft Finding Company, has not yet been formed, but will be under Command of Lieut. Woodworth, C.E. M.C. "B" Company which will be composed of the cooks, training section and "B" category men, and will be under the Command of Lieut. W. F. Lees.
> 4. A double apron wire fence was put up between the Segregation Area and the 3rd. C.E.R.B. lines.
> 5. The men of the 3rd C.E.R.B. who were in segregation were released.
>
> **8-8-18**
>
> 1. Weather:- Fair.
> 2. Col. T.V. Anderson, D.S.O. visited the Battalion lines.
> 3. 250 Other Ranks were employed building a new road into the camp.
>
> **9-8-18**
>
> 1. Weather:- Fair.
> 2. 1500 Other Ranks paraded to the sea front in the afternoon on bathing parade.
>
> **10-8-18**
>
> 1. Weather:- Very warm.
> 2. Col. S.D. Gardner, D.S.O. M.C. Officer Commanding troops Seaford, visited the camp.
> 3. Battalion Muster Parade was held in the afternoon.
> 4. C.E.T.D. Baseball Team won from the 1st Reserve Battalion at baseball. Score— 9-7.

### Page 4 — 11–16 August 1918 (Sheet No. 2)

Place: Seaford.

> **11-8-18**
>
> 1. Weather:- Very warm.
> 2. Church Parade held at the Oval, South Camp. The C.E.T.C. paraded Officers and Other Ranks. The 3rd. C.E.R.B. did not send any men as they are not yet equipped.
> Sir Robert Borden, Prime Minister of Canada and General Sir R.E.W. Turner, V.C., K.C.M.G., K.C.B., D.S.O. were present and spoke to the troops and presented medals.
>
> **12-8-18**
>
> 1. Weather:- Very Warm.
> 2. The Battalion was divided into training parties by the Staff of Infantry Training Wing of the C.S.M.E. and commenced their training.
>
> **13-8-18**
>
> 1. Weather:- Very warm.
> 2. First Pay Parade for the Battalion was held at 6 p.m.
>
> **14-8-18**
>
> 1. Weather:- Very warm.
> 2. 3 Officers and 56 Other Ranks attended sports at Stanford Bridge.
> 3. The afternoon was spent in sports.
> 4. C.E.T.C. Baseball Team defeated the 11th Reserve Battalion, 11-5 in the baseball league.
>
> **15-8-18**
>
> 1. Weather:- Very warm.
> 2. Brigadier-General Roper, accompanied by Lieut. Col. Fall visited the lines, and men at training.
> 3. The O.C. and Adjutant attended a conference of O.C.'s and Adjutants at No. 3 Y.M.C.A. South Camp.
> 4. Major R.F.E. Wood and Lieut. H.L. Scott went on Command to Bourley Segregation Camp to look after Canadian Engineers who have arrived there.
> 5. The N.A.C.B. opened the wet canteen.
>
> **16-8-18**
>
> 1. Weather:- Very warm.
> 2. N.A.C.B. opened the dry canteen in the Battalion lines.

### Page 5 — 17–23 August 1918 (Sheet No. 3)

Place: Seaford.

> **17-8-18**
>
> 1. Weather:- Cloudy and cold. Wind blowing off the sea.
> 2. Three Companies of the Battalion, comprising 1093 Officers and Other Ranks, went out on their first route march, as a Battalion, one Company staying in Camp to complete the issuing of their equipment.
> 3. The C.E.T.C. Baseball Team defeated the 16th Reserve Battalion. 11-3 in the Baseball League.
>
> **18-8-18**
>
> 1. Weather:- Fair.
> 2. The Battalion marched out to Church Parade C. of E. to the Garrison Church, other denominations to the Arena, and R.C.'s. to the R.C. Church Hut.
>
> **19-8-18**
>
> 1. Weather:- Fine in the morning, and cold in afternoon.
>
> **20-8-18**
>
> 1. Weather, Fair.
> 2. Lieut. J.R. McNeil from 1st Reserve Battalion attached for duty.
>
> **21-8-18**
>
> 1. Weather:- Very warm.
> 2. Boxing in the Arena, which was well attended.
> 3. C.E.T.C. Baseball team lost to 11th Reserve Battn, 15 to 5 runs.
>
> **22-8-18**
>
> 1. Weather:- Very warm.
> 2. "A" Officers mess C.E.T.C. played "B" Officers mess at Push Ball, result draw.
> 3. Major J.M. Oxley reported from 12th C.E. Battn France, attached pending board.
>
> **23-8-18** — [no Place or Date entered in the original for this entry; the date is inferred from its position between the 22-8-18 and 24-8-18 entries]
>
> 1. Weather:- Fine.
> 2. C.E.T.C. Baseball Team played the 1st Reserve and lost, 19 to 4.
> 3. Hon. Capt. P.J. Dykes reported from Canadian Chaplain Service, for duty with this Battalion.

### Page 6 — 24–31 August 1918 (Sheet No. 4)

Place: Seaford.

> **24-8-18**
>
> 1. Weather:- Fine.
> 2. Lieut. L.G.B. Davis, went On Command to the Trench Warfare School, Bexhill.
> 3. Battalion route march, round through Alfriston.
> 4. Lieut. S Appleton, W.C. Milner and R.D.B. Roy, reported from Canada.
>
> **25-8-18**
>
> 1. Weather:- Fine in the morning, wet in afternoon.
>
> **26-8-18.**
>
> 1. Weather:- Fair.
> 2. Officers Reinforcements, 9 Officers called for, Nil return.
>
> **27-8-18**
>
> 1. Weather:- Cold and wet.
> 2. The representatives from Companies, held a meeting in Battalion Orderly Room and appointed a sports committee and representatives for each line of sport.
> 3. Draft of 2183 Other Ranks for C.E. Pool, called for.
> 4. Staff Captain L.A. Kennedy M.C., inspected the Battalion Ordely Room.
>
> **28-8-18**
>
> 1. Weather:- Fair.
> 2. Afternoon was spent in recreational sports. The principal event was a Lacrosse game between 2nd. C.E.R.Bn and 3rd.C.E.R.Bn, resulting in a draw: 4-4.
>
> **29-8-18**
>
> 1. Weather:- Fair.
> 2. Lieut. R.H. Meek reported from R.F.A. and taken on strength from 24-8-18.
>
> **30-8-18**
>
> 1. Weather:- Fine.
> 2. C.E.T.C. Baseball Team played a team from Cooden Beach and won: 5 to 3.
>
> **31-8-18**
>
> 1. Weather:- Fair.
> 2. The Battalion went out on a route march to the beach and bathed.
>
> [Signed, bottom right:] *[signature — illegible], Major, C.E., A/O.C. 3rd. C.E.R.Bn.*

## Open questions / uncertain items (August 1918)

- 6 Aug: a word after "takes" ("Major J.D. Gunn, C.E. takes [blacked out] temporary Command") is blacked out in the original.
- 6 Aug: the Place entry for the first row appears struck through; the Place column reads "Seaford" throughout the month.
- 7 Aug: "the men just released from Segregation was divided" and "First issue of 3rd C.E.R.B. Daily Orders was issued" are as written (grammatical slips in the original, not corrected).
- 10 Aug: "C.E.T.D. Baseball Team" is as written (elsewhere "C.E.T.C. Baseball Team").
- 14 Aug: "Stanford Bridge" is as written.
- 15 Aug: "Bourley Segregation Camp" is as written; "No. 3 Y.M.C.A. South Camp" is as written.
- 17 Aug: "comprising 1093 Officers and Other Ranks" is as written.
- 23 Aug: no Place or Date is entered in the original for this entry; the date is inferred from its position between the 22-8-18 and 24-8-18 entries.

- 27 Aug: "Draft of 2183 Other Ranks for C.E. Pool, called for" is as written; "Battalion Ordely Room" is as written (a slip for "Orderly Room").
