# War Diary — 3rd Canadian Engineers Reserve Battalion — October 1918

- **Unit:** 3rd Canadian Engineers Reserve Battalion (3rd C.E.R.B.)
- **Month:** October 1918 (Volume No. 3) — 1 October to 31 October 1918
- **Source:** Library and Archives Canada, Collection Search Item ID 2004925 (RG 9 III-D-3, vol. 5008, file 705, reel T-10856, finding aid 9-52)
- **Pages:** October 1918, p. 1 – p. 5 (image files page-0015 – page-0019)
- **October 1918 — COMPLETE** (pages 1–5): cover, diary text 1–31 October, no appendices. Transcribed 2026-10-27.

## Transcription conventions
Original spelling, punctuation and abbreviations kept. Diary text is typewritten throughout (Army Form C. 2118, "WAR DIARY or INTELLIGENCE SUMMARY" — "INTELLIGENCE SUMMARY" blacked out on the printed heading on most sheets; on October p. 4 it is printed unblacked). `[uncertain]` marks words I could not read with confidence — never guessed. Place column reads "Seaford." throughout. The "Hour" column is blank throughout. "O.C." = Officer Commanding; "G.O.C." = General Officer Commanding; "O.R." = other ranks; "Lieut." = Lieutenant; "Capt." = Captain; "A/Capt" = Acting Captain; "C.E.R.Bn." = Canadian Engineers Reserve Battalion; "C.E.R.B." = Canadian Engineers Reserve Battalion; "C.E.R.D." = Canadian Engineers Reserve Depot; "C.C.D." = as written (not expanded); "C.E.T.C." = Canadian Engineers Training Centre; "O.C.C." = as written (not expanded); "C.S.M.E." = as written (not expanded); "S.O.S." = struck off strength; "T.O.S." = taken on strength; "P.T." = Physical Training; "R.C." = Roman Catholic; "Y.M.C.A." = Young Men's Christian Association; "N.A.C.B." = as written (not expanded); "R.O.E." = as written (not expanded). The "Remarks and references to Appendices" column carries handwritten initials against each day's entry; they are not transcribed as names. Words split across line-ends in the typewriting ("T renches", "Occu-ational") have been joined. The printed footer on the diary sheets reads "D. D. & L., London, E.C. (29250) Wt W3200/P713 750,000 3/18 Forms/C2118/16."

## Pages 2–5 — Diary text (1–31 October 1918)

Army Form C. 2118, "WAR DIARY or INTELLIGENCE SUMMARY". The printed heading reads "Instructions regarding War Diaries and Intelligence Summaries are contained in F. S. Regs., Part II. and the Staff Manual respectively. Title pages will be prepared in manuscript."

### Page 2 — 1–8 October 1918

Place: Seaford.

> **1-10-18.** — 1. Weather:-Fine.
>
> **2-10-18.** — 1. Weather:-Fine. 2. The afternoon was spent in sports.
>
> **3-10-18.** — 1. Weather:-Fine. 2. 154 Other Ranks left on 6 days Landing Leave.
>
> **4-10-18.** — 1. Weather:-Fine.
>
> **5-10-18.** — 1. Weather:-Fine [struck through] Morning fair, afternoon wet. 2. Battalion went on route march. 3. Adjutant held preliminary inspection of Draft of 488 Other Ranks. (Draft No. 2.)
>
> **6-10-18.** — 1. Weather:-Fine. 2. O.C. inspected Draft #2.
>
> **7-10-18.** — 1. Weather:-Fair. 2. Inspection of #2 Draft by G.O.C. Seaford. 3. Lieut. W.F. Stevenson reported from Hospital. 4. Lieuts. A. Appleton, C. Millar and R.A. Roy taken on strength from 27-7-18.
>
> **8-10-18.** — 1. Weather:-Fine. 2. Lieut. G.A. Campbell taken on strength from Hospital [the word "Hospital" is handwritten in ink after the typed "from"]. 3. Lieut. M. Wilson reported from O.C.C. Bexhill.

### Page 3 — 9–15 October 1918

Place: Seaford.

> **9-10-18.** — 1. Weather:-Wet. 2. Draft #2 consisting of 486 Other Ranks left for France at 01.00. 3. Usual weekly half day, sports were called off owing to rain. 4. Lieut. E.I. Bolton reported from 1st. C.E.R.Bn.
>
> **10-10-18.** — 1. Weather:-Wet. 2. Capt. A.L. Cavanagh reported from 2nd. C.E.R.Bn. 3. The camp was quarantined for "Flue"
>
> **11-10-18.** — 1. Weather:-Wet. 2. Lieut. Col. Ammiot, Sanitary Officer, Headquarters, visited the camp.
>
> **12-10-18.** — 1. Weather:-Wet. 2. Route march called off owing to heavy rain. 3. The men in tents were moved into the Garrison Church, R.C. Church Hut No. 1 Lines, N.A.C.B., and The Green House convenient to the camp.
>
> **13-10-18.** — 1. Weather:-Fine and sunshine all day. 2. Church Parades were held in the Y.M.C.A, tent in lines.
>
> **14-10-18.** — 1. Weather:- Fair. 2. Leitu.Col. Dickson, from Headquarters, London, visited the Camp.
>
> **15-10-18.** — 1. Weather:- Fair. 2. The Battalion had P.T.Parade from 0900 to 1100 and Route March in afternoon.

### Page 4 — 16–23 October 1918

Place: Seaford.

> **16-10-18.** — 1. Weather:- Fair. 2. Afternoon spent in sports.
>
> **17-10-18.** — 1. Weather:- Fair. 2. P.T.Morning. Route March afternoon. All Concerts and congregating of men in buildings is dis-allowed until further orders.
>
> **18-10-18.** — 1. Weather:- Cold and Wet, 2. Route march afternoon. P.T.Morning. 3. Quarantine was lifted.
>
> **19-10-18.** — 1. Weather:- Fair. 2. Route march in morning. 3. Lieut. G.A.Campbell S.O.S. to C.S.M.E. 4. Lieut. R.L.Meek reported off command, London.
>
> **20-10-18.** — 1. Weather:- Wet. 2. Fire Picquet was called out, fire in Grain stack north of Camp.
>
> **21-10-18.** — 1. Weather:- Dull. 2. Lieut. W.H.Stewart went on command to Frensham. 3. A/Capt N.H.Clemes reported from 2nd C.E.R.Bn.
>
> **22-10-18.** — 1. Weather:-Fair. 2. Draft of 11 Other Ranks proceeded on draft. [The word "Party" is pencilled in below "Draft" at the start of item 2.]
>
> **23-10-18.** — 1. Weather:-Fine. 2. Sports afternoon carried on as usual.

>

## Open questions / uncertain items (October 1918)
- No dates are missing: every day 1–31 October 1918 has an entry.
- 3 Oct: "6 days Landing Leave" — "Landing Leave" as written; the diary uses the same phrase in December ("Landing Leave"), so it is kept as written.
- 5 Oct: the word "Fine" in the weather line is struck through and replaced by "Morning fair, afternoon wet."
- 8 Oct: the word "Hospital" in item 2 is handwritten in ink, not typed.
- 12 Oct: "N.A.C.B." is read as written; its meaning is not expanded.
- 14 Oct: "Leitu.Col." is as written — evidently a typist's error for "Lieut. Col."
- 17 Oct: "dis-allowed" is as written (hyphenated across the line-end in the original).
- 22 Oct: the word "Party" is pencilled in below "Draft" at the start of item 2, evidently a correction to "Draft Party of 11 Other Ranks proceeded on draft."
- 24 Oct: "Lieut. H.L. Scott returned Bourley." — "Bourley" as typed; no preposition is given and the word's meaning is unclear.
- The O.C.'s signature at the foot of the October sheets is an illegible flourish and is not read as a name.
- The October cover reads "3rd / C.E.R.B." with a small "55" beneath "3rd", "H.P." at left, "5. K. 3. / 768/3." handwritten at top left, and "ORIGINAL" stamped diagonally at top right.
- Names read as: Lieut. W.F. Stevenson; Lieut. A. Appleton; Lieut. C. Millar; Lieut. R.A. Roy; Lieut. G.A. Campbell; Lieut. M. Wilson; Lieut. E.I. Bolton; Capt. A.L. Cavanagh; Lieut. Col. Ammiot; Lieut. Col. Dickson; Lieut. R.L. Meek; Lieut. W.H. Stewart; A/Capt N.H. Clemes; Lieut. F.L. Mitchell; Lieut. H.L. Scott; Capt. Dykes; Capt. R.L. Junkin.
- Casualties in October 1918: none killed or wounded. One officer admitted to hospital sick — Lieut. F.L. Mitchell, Influenza (24 Oct). The camp was quarantined for "Flue" from 10 Oct, the quarantine being lifted 18 Oct; concerts and congregating of men in buildings were disallowed from 17 Oct.
- Movements in October 1918: Draft #2 (486 Other Ranks) left for France at 01.00 on 9 Oct; a draft party of 11 Other Ranks proceeded on draft 22 Oct; 154 Other Ranks left on 6 days Landing Leave 3 Oct and 139 Other Ranks proceeded on Draft Leave 29 Oct. The battalion remained at Seaford throughout.
- No map coordinates (latitude/longitude) are given; the place is Seaford, England, throughout.
