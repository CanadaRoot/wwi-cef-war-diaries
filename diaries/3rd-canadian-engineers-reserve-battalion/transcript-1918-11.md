# War Diary — 3rd Canadian Engineers Reserve Battalion — November 1918

- **Unit:** 3rd Canadian Engineers Reserve Battalion (3rd C.E. Res Battn)
- **Month:** November 1918 (Volume No. 4) — 1 November to 30 November 1918
- **Source:** Library and Archives Canada, Collection Search Item ID 2004925 (RG 9 III-D-3, vol. 5008, file 705, reel T-10856, finding aid 9-52)
- **Pages:** November 1918, p. 1 – p. 7 (image files page-0020 – page-0026)
- **November 1918 — COMPLETE** (pages 1–7): cover, back of cover (blank filing form), diary text 1–30 November, no appendices. Transcribed 2026-10-27.

## Transcription conventions
Original spelling, punctuation and abbreviations kept. Diary text is typewritten throughout (Army Form C. 2118, "WAR DIARY or INTELLIGENCE SUMMARY" — "INTELLIGENCE SUMMARY" blacked out on the printed heading). `[uncertain]` marks words I could not read with confidence — never guessed. Place column reads "Seaford." throughout. The "Hour" column is blank throughout. "O.C." = Officer Commanding; "G.O.C." = General Officer Commanding; "O.R." = other ranks; "Lieut." = Lieutenant; "Capt." = Captain; "C.E.R.B." = Canadian Engineers Reserve Battalion; "C.E.R.D." = Canadian Engineers Reserve Depot; "C.C.D." = as written (not expanded); "C.E.T.C." = Canadian Engineers Training Centre; "C.M.G.D." = as written (not expanded); "S.O.S." = struck off strength; "T.O.S." = taken on strength; "P.T." = Physical Training; "R.O.E." = as written (not expanded); "AIII" = medical category AIII; "Spr." = Sapper; "CERD" = Canadian Engineers Reserve Depot (as written, without stops). The "Remarks and references to Appendices" column carries handwritten initials against each day's entry; they are not transcribed as names. The printed footer on the diary sheets reads "D. D. & L., London, E.C. (29250) Wt W3200/P713 750,000 3/18 Forms/C2118/16."

## Pages 3–7 — Diary text (1–30 November 1918)

Army Form C. 2118, "WAR DIARY or INTELLIGENCE SUMMARY". The printed heading reads "Instructions regarding War Diaries and Intelligence Summaries are contained in F. S. Regs., Part II. and the Staff Manual respectively. Title pages will be prepared in manuscript."

### Page 3 — 1–8 November 1918

Place: Seaford.

> **1-11-18.** — 1. Weather:-Fair.
>
> **2-11-18.** — 1. Weather:-Fair. 2. No Route March. General inspection by O.C. of Quarters, Mess Tins, Etc.
>
> **3-11-18.** — 1. Weather:-Bad, very heavy rain.
>
> **4-11-18.** — 1. Weather:-Bad. 2. During the night the R.O.E. and the Messing Marquees were all blown down.
>
> **5-11-18.** — 1. Weather:-Fair. 2. Battalion Headquarters re-established in C.E.T.C. Headquarters. Considerable delay occasioned in routine.
>
> **6-11-18.** — 1. Weather:-Fine. 2. Sprots carried on in the afternoon as usual.
>
> **7-11-18.** — 1. Weather:-Fine. 2. G.O.C. Seaford inspected Draft #4 of 126 Other Ranks.
>
> **8-11-18.** — 1. Weather:-Good. 2. Draft #4 proceeded to France.

### Page 4 — 9–15 November 1918

Place: Seaford.

> **9-11-18.** — 1. Weather:-Fine. 2. Route March held in morning. 3. 51 O.Rs proceeded to [uncertain — words heavily overtyped] 1st. C.E.R.B. on transfer.
>
> **10-11-18.** — 1. Weather:-Fine. 2. Church service held in open.
>
> **11-11-18.** — 1. Weather:-Fair. 2. 112 Other Ranks transferred to C.M.G.D.
>
> **12-11-18.** — 1. Weather:-Fine. 2. 736 Other Ranks arrived from Frensham Pond and temporarily attached to 3rd. C.C.D. for quarters and rations.
>
> **13-11-18.** — 1. Weather:-Fine. 2. A Special Thanksgiving Service held in the Arena for the Engineer Brigade. 3. Training suspended for the day, Sports carried on as usual in the afternoon.
>
> **14-11-18.** — 1. Weather:-Fine. 2. 150 Other Ranks on Musketry firing.
>
> **15-11-18.** — 1. Weather:-Fine. 2. Arranging to send "B" Category men back to Canada.

### Page 5 — 16–23 November 1918

Place: Seaford.

> **16-11-18.** — 1. Weather:- Fine.
>
> **17-11-18.** — 1. Weather:-Fine. 2. Church parade held on Battalion Parade Ground.
>
> **18-11-18.** — 1. Weather:-Fine and cool. 2. Party of 300 Other Ranks employed filling in trenches near "D" Rifle Range. 3. Parade 10.00 a.m.
>
> **19-11-18.** — 1. Weather:-Fine and cool. 2. Parade 10.00 a.m.
>
> **20-11-18.** — 1. Weather:-Fine, misty in a.m. 2. Party of "B" Category Other Ranks proceeded to C.C.D. for return to Canada. 3. Sports carried on in the afternoon.
>
> **21-11-18.** — 1. Weather:-Fine. 2. Battalion moved from No. 1 Lines at 1300 o'clock to Nos. 4 & 5 Lines vacated by 3rd C.C.D. Move completed at 5.00 p.m. 3. Major A.M. Wright leaves Unit to return to Canada this morning.
>
> **22-11-18.** — 1. Weather:-Fine and cool. 2. Parades 10.00 a.m. and 1.30 p.m.
>
> **23-11-18.** — 1. Weather:-Fine.

### Page 6 — 23–29 November 1918

Place: Seaford.

> **23-11-18 (continued).** — 1. Weather:- [no weather word given] 2. 9.00 a.m. Muster Parade for All Ranks. 3. Pay Parades throughout the day.
>
> **24-11-18.** — 1. Weather dull, slight rain. 2. Church Parade All Ranks 9.00 a.m.
>
> **25-11-18.** — 1. Weather:-Mild. 2. Parades as usual. 300 Other Ranks filling in trenches at "D" Range.
>
> **26-11-18.** — 1. Weather:-Dull, threatening rain. 2. Parades as usual.
>
> **27-11-18.** — 1. Weather:-Wet and rainy. 2. Pay Parade. 3. Capt. J.E. Bell, Capt. W.G. Hardy, Lieut. J.R. McCall taken on strength from CERD. 4. Major A.M. Wright ceased to be attached from C.E.R.D. on proceeding to Canada.
>
> **28-11-18.** — 1. Weather:-Wet. 2. Parades as usual. 3. 42 AIII Men reported to Discharge Depot.
>
> **29-11-18.** — 1. Weather:-Fair. 2. Names of 14 AIII men submitted for return to Canada. 3. Spr. Scott, repatriated prisoner reported to the Battalion.

## Open questions / uncertain items (November 1918)
- No dates are missing: every day 1–30 November 1918 has an entry.
- **11 November 1918 (Armistice Day):** the entry records only "1. Weather:-Fair. 2. 112 Other Ranks transferred to C.M.G.D." — the Armistice is not mentioned anywhere in the day's entry.
- 4 Nov: "R.O.E." is read as written; its meaning is not expanded.
- 6 Nov: "Sprots" is as written — evidently a typist's error for "Sports".
- 9 Nov: the words between "proceeded to" and "1st. C.E.R.B." are heavily overtyped and cannot be read.
- 12 Nov: "Frensham Pond" and "736 Other Ranks" confirmed on close reading; the men were "temporarily attached to 3rd. C.C.D. for quarters and rations."
- 13 Nov: the Special Thanksgiving Service was held "in the Arena for the Engineer Brigade" — the first Thanksgiving noted in the diary, two days after the Armistice.
- 16 Nov: the Place cell reads "Seaford" with two lines of heavily overtyped text beneath it that cannot be read.
- 23 Nov: the entry is split across two sheets — the first sheet (p. 5) gives only item 1, "Weather:-Fine."; the continuation on p. 6 repeats the date and item 1 as "Weather:-" with no weather word, followed by items 2 and 3.
- The handwritten signature on the back of the November cover (p. 2) appears to read "Jas. Diamond. O." — recorded with that reservation, not as a certain name.
- The Commanding Officer's signature at the foot of the November sheets appears to read "J.B. Dunbar" — Lieut.-Colonel, C.E., Commanding 3rd C.E. Reserve Battalion — recorded with that reservation.
- Names read as: Major A.M. Wright; Capt. J.E. Bell; Capt. W.G. Hardy; Lieut. J.R. McCall; Spr. Scott (repatriated prisoner, surname only).
- Casualties in November 1918: none killed, wounded or sick recorded. The month is dominated by demobilisation: "B" Category men being sent back to Canada, AIII men reported to the Discharge Depot, and a repatriated prisoner (Spr. Scott) reporting to the battalion.
- Movements in November 1918: Draft #4 (126 Other Ranks) proceeded to France 8 Nov; 51 O.Rs proceeded to 1st C.E.R.B. on transfer 9 Nov; 112 Other Ranks transferred to C.M.G.D. 11 Nov; 736 Other Ranks arrived from Frensham Pond 12 Nov; a party of "B" Category Other Ranks proceeded to C.C.D. for return to Canada 20 Nov; the battalion moved within Seaford from No. 1 Lines to Nos. 4 & 5 Lines (vacated by 3rd C.C.D.) on 21 Nov, the move completed at 5.00 p.m.
- No map coordinates (latitude/longitude) are given; the place is Seaford, England, throughout.
