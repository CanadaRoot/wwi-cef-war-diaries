> OPERATION ORDER No. 115 1-5-18 Ref. Map sheet 36b.
>
> 1. In accordance with 8th C.I. Bde. S.C.-3/510 date 1-5-18 the 5th C.M.R. Battalion will vacate its present billets in GOUX SERVINS and march to DIEVAL area [uncertain] 1-5-18.
>
> 2. Companies will parade at 9.45 a.m. in column of Route, with Head of Column on the Road, facing North at approximately Q.36.c.7.9.
>
> 3. ORDER OF MARCH A Coy Brass-band, B. Coy, Bugle Band D Coy H.Qs. Coy. and Transport. 50 yards interval to be kept between Coys.
>
> 4. DRESS FULL MARCHING ORDER — Steel helmets to be carried on the packs.
>
> 5. ROUTE GRAND SERVINS – LE [uncertain] – REBREUVE – HOUDAIN – DIVION – OUR TON – DIEVAL.
>
> 6. TRANSPORT Transport will move with the Battalion.
>
> 7. HALTS Halts will be made at 10 mins to the hour and resume at the hour and at 5 mins to the half-hour, resume at the half hour. A Halt of One Hour will be made at 1.00 P.M. for dinner.
>
> 8. ADVANCE PARTY consisting of 1 Officer (Lieut. DIXON) and 2 N.C.Os. per Coy. (to include T.Q.M.S. [uncertain]) will proceed forward at 6.30 a.m. by Lorry. This Party will report to Bn. H.Qs., at 7.30 a.m.
>
> 9. Officers kits, etc., will be piled ready for loading in front of respective Coy. H.Qs. at 9.00 a.m. Transport Officer will make arrangements to collect same. The BAND's Packs and Rifles will be piled ready for loading outside Bn. H.Qs., by 9.00 a.m. to be conveyed to the new Area.
>
> 10. RATIONS, for consumption on 2nd inst will be delivered in the new Area.
>
> 11. SICK PARADE will be at 8.00 a.m.
>
> 12. REAR GUARD, consisting of Lieut. WALTON and 20 O.Rs., will be furnished by H.Qs. Coy., and will fall in in rear of the Battalion to pick up stragglers, if any, during the march.
>
> 13. Two Scouts from each Company will report to Lieut. A. W. LOGIE at 6 a.m. to reconnoitre rendezvous and will then report back to respective Coys.
>
> 14. All billets to be left in a clean and thoroughly sanitary condition Lieut. R. C. [uncertain] will obtain a clearance certificate from the Town Major to this effect, before the Battalion moves off.
>
> ACKNOWLEDGE Issued by Runner 4.30 a.m. [signed] J. Adamson Lieut A/Adjutant 5th C.M.R. Batt'n.

## Notes

- **Coverage:** Diary entries run 1–31 May 1918 with no date gaps. The contents sheet states "Diary Text 20 Pages", which matches the 20 diary-text pages (pages 4–23). Appendix 1–6 holds Operation Orders Nos. 115–120 plus Warning Order No. 9 (pages 24–30). Note the contents sheet calls the operation orders "C.O." (as written); the orders themselves are headed "OPERATION ORDER".
- **Officers named:** Lt J. Adamson (A/Adjutant; signs O.O. 115–118); Lt A. W. Logie (scouts); Lt Dixon (advance parties); Lt Walton (rear guard); Lt McLean (advance party); Lt Green (Special Composite Company demonstration, 4 May); Capt Nicholson, R.C. Chaplain of the unit (Mass, 12, 19 and 26 May); Capt Colwell, chaplain to the 2nd C.M.R. Battalion (Divine Service 12 May; lecture "Work after the War", 16 May); Capt Davis, chaplain to the 4th C.M.R. Battalion (Divine Service, 19 May); Lt-Col Draper D.S.O. (left 24 May to command 8th C.I. Brigade); Maj W. Rhoades M.C. (assumed command of the battalion, 24 May). The diary is signed on 31 May by "[uncertain] Major, O.C. 5th C.M.R. Bn."
- **Other ranks named:** None named in May.
- **Movements:** 1 May route march Gouy Servins to Dieval; 5 May march Dieval to Witterness (St. Hilaire area); 9 May march Witterness to Quarbeque, in reserve to 11th Corps; 28 May relieved by 2nd C.M.R. Battalion and marched Quarbeque to Fontes, taking over the 2nd's battle-position duties; 31 May relieved by the 52nd Canadian Infantry Battalion and marched Fontes to Bomy, ready to take part in the 3rd Canadian Division manoeuvres on 1 June.
- **Casualties:** None recorded in May 1918.
- **Battles/actions:** No front-line action in May — the month is entirely training, marches and reserve duties behind the lines: musketry, attack practice, bayonet fighting, route marches, gas-helmet drills, rifle-grenade and Stokes-bomb work, and a company elimination contest for the "Dumbell Badge" (30 May). The battalion stood to at 10 minutes' notice from 7 May, ready to man the trench line from Courant Brayle to Bas Hamel if attacked. A baseball game against the 2nd C.M.R. Battalion on 19 May ended 7–5 for the 2nd.
- **Anomalies:** (1) Two separate entries are both dated 19 May — the baseball game and the Sunday Divine Service/Mass; both appear to belong to Sunday 19 May. (2) The 4 May entry says the battalion would march to "WITTERNESS" while O.O. 116 says "Northern Portion of St. HILAIRE Area (WITTERNESS)". (3) The place name is spelled "QUARBEQUE" throughout the diary (also "GUARBEQUE" in the 12 May Mass entry). (4) The route line in O.O. 119 (page 29) is partly illegible. (5) The signature on O.O. 120 (page 30) and on Warning Order No. 9 (page 27) is not clearly legible.
