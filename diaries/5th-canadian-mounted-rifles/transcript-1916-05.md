### 1/5/16 — Camp "A"

> Wind S.W. Weather bright and warm. Battalion resting and cleaning up. Aeroplane guard with Lewis Gun detailed for duty.

### 2/5/16 — Camp "A"

> Wind S.W. Weather bright and warm, night quiet; no aeroplanes about. Battalion parade 9 a.m. for inspection by O.C. After inspection Companies carried on independently with gas helmet drill, fire control and bayonet fighting. G.O.C. inspected Transport and Transport lines, complimenting them on their work. Heavy rain and thunderstorm in afternoon.

### 3/5/16 — Camp "A"

> Wind N.W. weather dull but cleared early. Brigadier-General V.A.S. Williams, O.C. Brigade inspected Battalion in morning. Recreation for all ranks during afternoon.

### 4/5/16 — Camp "A"

> Wind N.W. weather bright and warm. Companies carried on independently. Nine other ranks arrived as reinforcements from Brigade Machine Gun Company.

### 5/5/16 — Camp "A"

> Wind N.W. weather bright and very warm. Companies carried on independently. Enemy shelled POPERINGHE during early morning. Working party last night of 158 men with Lieut. E. Gill in charge, worked on R.S.2 carrying material and revetting. Returned without casualty. Officers played Sergeants baseball. Result 26-14 in favor of Sergeants.

### 6/5/16 — Camp "A" (begins)

> Wind westerly. Weather still very warm. Companies carried on independently.

### 6/5/16 — Camp "A" (continued)

> Orders received for Battalion to go into Brigade Reserve at Camp "C" (M.14.a.) tomorrow, but enemy shelling this camp, causing several casualties, location of Brigade Reserve changed to Camp "F" (M.13.b.9.0). Working party of 148 other ranks under Lieut Barton returned to Camp owing to lack of transport. Working parties of 1st 42nd [uncertain] had 3 killed and 11 wounded by enemy shell fire. "A" Company played a Company of 2nd C.M.R. for Brigade Company Championship but lost 1-0. High wind spoiled what should have been a good game. Football.

### 7/5/16 (Sunday) — Camp "A"

> Wind N.E. Weather dull and squally. Battalion Church Parade in morning. Orders notifying transfer of Captain J.P. Goodall, M.C., to England received. Captain J. Mulvey who has been officiating for Captain Goodall relieved by Captain Leary, C.A.M.C. At 4.15 p.m. Battalion moves to "F" Camp (H.13.b.9.0). Relief completed at 8.30 p.m.

### 8/5/16 — Camp "F"

> Wind N.E. Weather dull and squally, raining. Battalion cleaning up camp. Lieutenant (late Sgt) Adam J. reports for duty from Cadet School. Working parties 150 men under Lieutenant Halling and 100 men under Lieutenant Rice. No casualties.

### 9/5/16 — Camp "F"

> Wind N.E.E. Weather dull and cold, raining. Companies carried on independently in morning. Recreation in afternoon. Working parties cancelled owing to rain.

### 10/5/16 — Camp "F"

> Wind N.W. Weather clear and bright. Enemy shelling roads north of Camp. Battalion cleaning up after rainy weather of yesterday. Working parties out at night. Returned without casualties.

### 11/5/16 — Camp "F"

> Wind N.W. by W. Weather fine and bright in morning, clouding later. Companies carried on independently. Enemy shelled vicinity of VLAMETINGHE and POPERINGHE. Working parties out at night. Returned without casualties.

### 12/5/16 — Camp "F"

> Wind W. Weather bright and fine. Companies carried on independently. Lieut. J. M. Macdonald and 100 other ranks, proceeded to CHATEAU BELGE (M.23.c.6.5) to be billeted there, and act as working party, to bury cable. Working party out at night, 150 men working on communication trench on North side of ZILLEBEKE LAKE. Returned, no casualties.

### 13/5/16 — Camp "F"

> Wind S.W. Rained in morning, clearing in afternoon. Companies carried on independently. Battalion football team played 42nd Canadian Battalion (R.A.C.) winning 4 goals to 2. Battalion Minstrel Troupe gave entertainment at Cinema, to a crowded house. Working party out at night 150 men. Returned without casualties.

### 14/5/16 (Sunday) — Camp "F"

> Wind N.W. Raining and cold. Church Parade in morning. Battalion cleaning up ready for move into front line tomorrow night. Thirty nine other ranks arrived as reinforcements from HAVRE, (10th Reserve Battalion). Working party out (150 other ranks) at night. Returned without casualties.

### 15/5/16 — Camp "F"

## Notes

- **Coverage:** Entries run 1–31 May 1916 with no date gaps. The contents sheet states "Diary Text 13 Pages", which matches the thirteen diary-text sheets (pages 3–15). The diary is Volume 8 of the battalion's war diary.
- **Officers named:** Brigadier-General V.A.S. Williams (O.C. Brigade, inspected the battalion 3 May); Lt E. Gill (working party 5 May; promoted to Captain, notice 18 May); Capt J.P. Goodall, M.C. (transferred to England, 7 May); Capt J. Mulvey (officiating for Goodall); Capt Leary, C.A.M.C.; Lt Barton; Lt (late Sgt) Adam J.; Lt Halling; Lt Rice; Lt J. M. Macdonald; Major H. J. Woodside (attached for instruction, shell shock 16 May); Capt J. D'A. Sneath (severe shell shock 16 May; promotion to Captain noticed 18 May); Major A. C. Fletcher and Lt H. M. Macdonald (both "B" Company, killed by shell 19 May); Major D. C. Draper; Lt P. H. Frade [uncertain]; Lt E. A. Chown; Lt H. A. Cotton; Lt H. Pines, Lt C. H. Lawson (promotions to Captain noticed 18 May); Rev. Hon. Capt C. Reagan (Chaplain, 3rd Brigade); Rev. Father Capt M. R. Tompkins; Rev. Capt McKinnon; Rev. Capt Robertson; Hon. Colonel Rev. J. Almond; Hon. Capt Father Thornton; Lt-Col E. M. Baker [uncertain] (O.C., signs the diary 31 May).
- **Other ranks named:** 41 named other ranks in the casualty lists, including Transport Sergt Duncan J. B. (killed 21 May), Corpl Hutchinson G. (wounded 15 May), Signalling Corporal Atkinson, Sgt Upton R., and Ptes Baskin, Kay, McDonough, Meakins, Rogerson, Murray, Rankin [uncertain], Campbell, Landsdell, Inman, Parr, Worsley, White, McGillivray, Keane, Stewart, Sykes, Prekett [uncertain], Reid, Mills, Hall, Brown, Grant, Smith, Johnson, Bennett, Paslow [uncertain], Cox, McQuaker, Price, Witherspoon, Stevens, Gagnon, Jackson, Christian, Anderson, and Gougon [uncertain].
- **Reinforcements:** Nine other ranks from the Brigade Machine Gun Company (4 May); 39 other ranks from Havre (10th Reserve Battalion, 14 May).
- **Movements:** Camp "A" to Camp "F" (7 May, after the planned move to Camp "C" was changed because Camp "A" was being shelled); into the front line the night of 15/16 May, relieving the 4th C.M.R. Battalion (trenches 63–66, Sanctuary Wood, Maple Copse sector); relieved by the 9th Battalion and back to Camp "A" (23 May); preparing on 31 May to relieve the 6th Battalion in Maple Copse, Zillebeke Bund and S.P.'s 13, 14, 16 and 17. Company dispositions in the line: "A" Coy Trench 63, "B" Coy Trenches 64, 65, 66, "D" Coy Duckboard trench, Bn H.Q. Sanctuary Wood; right sub-section of the section of defence.
- **Casualties:** The diary's own eight-day tour summary (16–23 May) totals 5 officers and 41 other ranks (46 all ranks): killed 2 officers and 3 other ranks; died of wounds 2 other ranks; wounded 3 officers and 36 other ranks. Named killed in action: Pte Baskin G. (16 May, rifle grenade, Trench 63); Pte Kay J. (17 May, gunshot wound to the head); Major A. C. Fletcher and Lt H. M. Macdonald (19 May, shell, 9.30 a.m.); Transport Sergt Duncan J. B. (night of 21 May, shell while bringing up rations); Pte Christian R. (22 May, machine gun fire in Maple Copse while on a ration party). Died of wounds: Pte Landsdell C. and Pte Worsley E. (both 19 May, died at the dressing station at 4.30 a.m.; Worsley was acting as a stretcher bearer carrying Landsdell). Three light draught horses were shot after being wounded by the same shell that killed Sergt Duncan. At Poperinghe on 29 May the diary records 3 soldiers killed and 5 civilians (not battalion casualties as such).
- **Burials:** Pte Baskin and Pte Kay in Maple Copse Cemetery (I.23.b.9.2); Major Fletcher and Lt Macdonald in Maple Copse Cemetery (buried 20 May); Ptes Landsdell and Worsley in Maple Copse Cemetery (funeral 19 May, 6.30 p.m.); Sergt Duncan in Vlamertinghe Military Cemetery (Row 4 No 14); Pte Christian in Maple Copse Cemetery (23 May).
- **Other activity:** Nightly working parties (cable burying, revetting, communication trench north of Zillebeke Lake, Reserve line); wiring parties in front of Trench 63; enemy mining sounds reported from Trench 66 (177th Tunnelling Company summoned, nothing found); snipers accounting for seven enemy periscopes (22 May); German aeroplane driven down east of the trenches (16 May); enemy aircraft active most days; football and baseball against other Canadian units (including beating the P.P.C.L.I. 3–1 at football on 28 May); Battalion Minstrel Troupe entertainment (13 May); baths at Poperinghe (25 May).
- **Anomalies:** (1) The 17 May entry breaks off mid-sentence at the foot of page 6 ("Killed, whilst with"); the casualty list resumes at the top of page 7 with Pte McDonough, and Pte Kay's burial is then recorded there. (2) Pte Baskin's regimental number is given as 415283 on 16 May but as 41528 on 17 May (final digit unclear on the 17th). (3) The 6 May entry reports working parties of "1st 42nd [uncertain]" with 3 killed and 11 wounded by shell fire — the unit designation is hard to read and it is not clear these were the battalion's own men. (4) The 19 May entry records Fletcher's and Macdonald's burial as "(20th)", i.e. the burial took place on 20 May but is entered under 19 May.
