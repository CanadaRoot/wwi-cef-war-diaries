### 1/10/18

> Attack on CAMBRAI renewed. Our troops on the left advanced their lines. No definite information. The 1st & 2nd C.M.R. Bns are holding WEST BANK of CANAL on their frontage. Instructions received that our boys would take over the MARCOING LINE SOUTH of 4th C.M.R. Bn. HQRS F.5.b.10.10. to Railway embankment F.17.b.30.90 Bn. HQrs. to remain in their present location. Boys moved off at 6.30 p.m. and took over their new locations. (APPENDIX I) Major J. J. E. Todd, 2nd in Command, Lieut Belding Ass't Adjt and Lieut Ingersoll Ass't Scout Officer proceeded forward and established Adv. Bn. H.Qrs at 1000 p.m. Lieut. ROBERTS. L.P.O. reported back from M. G. COURSE. The O.C. & Lieut ADAMSON proceeded to Adv. Bn. H.Qrs. at 9.00 A.M.

### 2/10/18

> Bn. H.Qrs. closed at F.9.c.70.30 and re-opened at F.11.b.10.30. at 11.30 A.M. The Bn. is responsible for the MAIN RESISTANCE LINE (MARCOING LINE) At 6 p.m. an S.O.S. went up on the left. Our artillery responded in 30 seconds. It appears that the HUN attempted a raid which was repulsed. One Officer & 7 O.Rs were taken prisoners and a large number Killed. At 1015 P.M. orders received that we would relieve the 1st C.M.R. Bn in the line to-morrow night 3rd/4th October 1918.

### 3/10/18

> We relieved the 1st C.M.R. Bn. in RIGHT FRONT LINE. Coys moved off in following order: D.C.B.A. HQ Coys at 8.30 p.m. Relief completed at 1220 A.M. (APPENDIX 2)

### 4/10/18

> Bn H.Q. at A.8.b.60.65 was closed at 0010 & re-opened at A.1.d.95.40. The enemy frequently shelled the town during the night. Isolated movements was observed in the outskirts of CAMBRAI. A successful shoot with 6" HOWS was carried out in the afternoon on the BARRACKS & WALL EASTY, BARRACKS in A.9.d.5.5. a large number of direct hits being observed. The Div'l COMMANDER Major-Gen. LOOMIS & Brig-Gen. D.C. DRAPER visited Bn. H.Q and then proceeded to the O.P. and watched the shoot with 6" HOWS. The enemy shelled the town during the night especially the EASTERN AREA near the CANAL.

### 5/10/18

> CASUALTY: Pte MARQUIS – KILLED. The enemy continued to straffe with his artillery almost continually during the night. He also bombed in the neighbourhood of BOURBON WOOD. Six BOMBS dropping in our TRANSPORT LINES Killed 5 O.Rs including Cpl DICKS who was proceeding on leave and receiving a direct hit on our BASE ORDERLY ROOM. The Orderly Room Staff (S/M. BRYENTON – Sgt WOODALL – Pte HART) were wounded and the Records destroyed. We were informed by Bde that we would be relieved in 3 or 4 days.

### 6/10/18

> Enemy shelled at considerable intensity of GAS throughout the evening. Enemy shelled the village with artillery throughout the day. Information received that we would not be relieved, but that the DIVISION would participate in another fight to capture CAMBRAI.

### 14/10/18

> Bn. Paraded on Bn. Alarm Post at 0930. The O.C. taking this opportunity to make a short resume of the Bn's figures since coming to FRANCE in October 1915. During his speech he paraded all originals of the 5th Cdn Bn & of the 60th Cdn. Inf. Bn; also all men who were through the 2nd of June 1916 show. He was surprised at the number of men who are still with the Battalion. All Officers & 100 Other Ranks attended the funeral of Major-General L. J. Lippett G.O.C. 4th Imperial Division, our late Divisional Commander who was killed in action on the 14th Every Bn & Unit in the Division was represented. The firing party being furnished by his old Battalion – the 6th Cdn. Inf. Bn. The Army loses a great General in his death.

### 15/10/18

> A Coy had use of RANGES at E.15.d. The remainder of Bn. carried on the usual training. Special attention being paid to Lewis Gun work. Lieut W.P. LOBIE proceeded on leave.

### 16/10/18

> Bn. Parade at 0730 & then proceeded to D.12.b. near INCHY EN ARTOIS where the Brigade was inspected by the Corps Commander Sir Arthur CURRIE. After the inspection the Brigade marched past. The Battalion returned to Camp at 1100. In the afternoon Officers & 300 men attended a concert given by the "Wye Emmas" at the Y.M.C.A. HUT INCHY, which was very good & much appreciated.

### 17/10/18

> Rain. Weather interfered with Training in the early morning but the weather clearing up about 1000 a.m. the O.C. inspected 'B' Coy. while the remaining Coys carried on with Lewis Gun & Rifle Grenade work. - **Equipment:** 6" howitzers; 18-pdr artillery; 3" trench mortars; Lewis guns and Lewis gun limbers; rifle grenades; smoke bombs and smoke grenades; bridging material (float bridge, small raft bridges, scaling ladders, infantry bridges, pontoon bridge); Stokes guns; field kitchens; G.S. limbers; mess cart; buses and a lorry for transport.
- **Movements:** 1 October the battalion (less Bn. H.Q.) took over the Marcoing Line south of 4th C.M.R. Bn.; 3–4 October relieved 1st C.M.R. Bn. in the right front line; 9 October crossed the Canal de l'Escaut into Cambrai, establishing bridgeheads at A.9.d.30.20 and A.9.d.50.90 and pushing outposts into the town; 10 October evacuated Cambrai to Inchy-en-Artois; 20 October embussed on the Pronville–Inchy road to Montigny; 21 October marched to Somain; 22 October to Helesmes; 23 October to Grand Bray; 26 October to Cataine; 27–28 October relieved 49th Cdn. Bn. in left support south of St Amand; 29 October 'D' Coy moved forward under 2nd C.M.R. Bn.; 30 October the battalion (less 'D' Coy) relieved 38th Cdn. Bn. in support.
- **Discipline:** None recorded in October 1918.
- **Casualties:** 2 October an enemy raid on the left was repulsed; one enemy officer and 7 enemy other ranks were taken prisoner and a large number killed. 5 October: Pte Marquis killed; six bombs in the transport lines killed 5 O.Rs including Cpl Dicks; S/M. Bryenton, Sgt Woodall and Pte Hart wounded; the Base Orderly Room received a direct hit and the Records were destroyed. 9 October: "Casualties NIL" after the Cambrai crossing, despite the town being shelled and burning. 25 October: three cases of 'Spanish Flu' in the battalion. 30 October: "No Casualties" on relieving 38th Cdn. Bn.
