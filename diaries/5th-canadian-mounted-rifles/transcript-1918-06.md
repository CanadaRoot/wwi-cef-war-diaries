### 1/6/18 — Bomy

> AM Weather Fine. The Battalion Participated in 3rd Canadian Division Tactical Scheme No 3 and in Conjunction with the other Battalions of the 8th Cdn. Infantry Brigade formed "RED FORCE". Battalions from 7th Cdn. Infantry Brigade comprised "BLUE FORCE". The 5th Cdn Mounted Rifles Battalion were ordered to attack Bomy and Petigny, which they did, and then pushed Northwards to Hill 140. This exercise was very beneficial to all Concerned. At the Conclusion of exercise a Conference of all mounted Officers was held at Farm on Hill 140.
>
> PM The Battalion returned to Billets at 4-30 P.M.

### 3/6/18 — Bomy (continued)

> BASSEE-BOULOGNE, Marthes and Ham. The attack on Hill 140. Commenced at 1.35 PM and progressed Satisfactorily, when information was received from Brigade that enemy was attacking in force at Petigny. The 5th C.M.R. Battalion were ordered to hold ground on Hill 140 detach 2 Companies and to attack enemy advancing on Petigny from North. Before this attack could be developed it was stopped by the 1st and 2nd C.M.R. Battalions. The 5th C.M.R. then proceeded to carry out the original orders, and were developing a strong attack on Equinegatte when the "Dismiss" was sounded at 5.20 PM and the Battalion then returned to Billets, with the exception of the Mounted Officers who attended a Conference at Farm on Hill 140.

> in connection with
> 3rd.Cdn.Div.Tactical Scheme No.4.
>
> 1. INFORMATION
> (a) Aeroplanes report enemy troops seen digging trenches today about THEROUANNE, ENQUINEGATTE and CUHEM. Enemy purpose [uncertain] appears to be -
>
> "B" of BERQUIGNY - HILL 106 - 5 crossroads on HILL 140 (5e.60.14.) DELETTE. If he can bring up sufficient troops in time he may possibly endeavour to regain the high ground along EAST of the River LYS.
>
> (b) 1. The [uncertain] Division will advance tomorrow to the line MAMETZ WITTERNESSE.
>
> The [uncertain] Division, on our left, will advance by the North bank of the LYS River to CLARQUES and REBECQ.
>
> The [uncertain] Division, on our right, will advance via BEAUMETZ les-Aire to the line LIETTRES - RELY.
>
> 2. The 8th.Cdn.Inf.Bde. Bde.C.F.A. less 3 Batteries and No. [uncertain] Squadron, C.L.H., will form the Advance Guard of [uncertain] Division
>
> The advanced guard ([uncertain] Division) will pass the outpost line ([uncertain] of RECLINGHEM - [uncertain]) at 11-30 a.m. Flanking Divisions cannot be ready to move earlier.
>
> If the enemy are met they will be vigorously attacked. If the opposition is such that it necessitates the use of the whole Division, the advanced guard will make good the high ground from BASSE - BOULOGNE to SERNY, both inclusive, the remainder of the Division being sent forward to assist as found necessary.
>
