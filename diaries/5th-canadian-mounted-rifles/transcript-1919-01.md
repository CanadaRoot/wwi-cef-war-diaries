## Page 4 — War Diary (Army Form C. 2118)

Heading: WAR DIARY or INTELLIGENCE SUMMARY ("INTELLIGENCE SUMMARY" struck through). Form imprint at foot: "A6945 Wt. W11422/M1180 350,000 12/16 D.D. & L. Forms/C.2118/14."

### 1.1.19 — LENNICK ST. QUENTIN

> Weather fine but cold. Battalion vacated billets in LENNICK ST QUENTIN at 0845 hours and proceeded by March Route to OPHASSELT. The Battalion marched into billets at 1010 hrs. Capt. T.D. JOHNSTON proceeded on leave to U.K.

Remarks column: "Appen I".

### 2.1.19 — OPHASSELT

> Morning again fine and cold, ideal weather for marching. The Battalion vacated billets in OPHASSELT at 0830 hrs. and proceeded by march route to RENAIX, a distance of 22 kilometres. The Battalion arrived in billets in RENAIX at 1550 hrs.

Remarks column: "Appen II".

### 3.1.19 — RENAIX

> The Battalion vacated billets in RENAIX at 0800 hours and proceeded by march Route to RUYEN and ESCANAFFLES area. Bn. Hqrs. and Transport being billeted in the latter town, the four Coys. in RUYEN. Lieut.-Col. W.R. RHOADES, DSO., MC., proceeded on leave to ENGLAND. Maj. J.S.E. TODD, MC assuming command of the Battalion. Lieut. K.H.M. MORRISON proceeded to Hospital. Lieut. G. HARVEY, MC. returns from leave.

Remarks column: "Appen III".

## Page 5 — War Diary (Army Form C. 2118), continued

### 4.1.19 — RUYEN

> Battalion completed the last stage of the march to the concentration area. Vacating billets in RUYEN and ESCANAFFLES, proceeding by March Route to DOTTIGNIES. The Companies are distributed over a large area, no billets were available for D Company, Transport and Quartermaster Stores. Billets were eventually secured for these details, at ESTAIMPUIS.

Remarks column: "Appen IV".

### 5.1.19 — DOTTIGNIES

> Battalion paraded for Divine Service in a small R.C. Church, at 1030 hours. Major Walker officiating. Mass was celebrated in the Parish Church, DOTTIGNIES for Roman Catholics at 1000 hours. Lieut. Milne returned from C.C.R.C.

### 6.1.19 — DOTTIGNIES

> Battalion carried on with clean up of equipment etc. Instructions received from Brigade that the Battalion less "D" Company and Transport would move to WATTRELOS tomorrow the 7th inst.
>
> HONOURS AND AWARDS: M.C. — Lieut. A/Capt. H.F.C. Cooks, MENTION in DESPATCHES: Lieut.-Colonel W. RHOADES, DSO., MC.; Capt. W.J. ATHERTON (deceased) Lieut. J. QUINLAN, and L/C DORION (deceased).

### 7.1.19 — DOTTIGNIES

> Company Commanders meeting at Battalion Headquarters at 0930 hours. Headquarters "A.B. and C. Companies proceeded independently to WATTRELOS area at 1300 hours.

Remarks column: "Appen V".

### 8.1.19 — WATTRELOS

> Coys. carried on independently with one hour Physical Training and cleaning up equipment.
>
> HONOURS & AWARDS: M.C. — Lieut. (a/Capt.) R.H. Curran, (D. Coy) Lieut. G.C. Dunham, Lieut. J.J. Harold (D. Coy). D.C.M. — Sgt. Powers, MM and Sgt. Hanlon, [uncertain] (D. Coy).

### 9.1.19 — WATTRELOS

> P.T. Parades, Coys. when not on Pay Parades carried on independently with one hours Physical Training.

Remarks column: "APPEN B".

### 10.1.19 — WATTRELOS

> The Bn. carried on with the usual training in the morning. The Education Officer Lieut. Savage recommended Education classes. P.M. — A football game was arranged between Bn. Hqrs. and 14th Battery R.C.A. which resulted in a win for the Btry by 3 goals to nil.

## Page 6 — War Diary (Army Form C. 2118), continued

### 11.1.19 — WATTRELOS

> Smartening up Drill and Education classes. P.M. — Recreation.

### 12.1.19 — WATTRELOS

> The Church Parade for C. of E. had to be cancelled on account of inclement weather. Mass was celebrated for R.Cs in the parish Church, HERSEAUX.

### 13.1.19 — WATTRELOS

> BATTALION PARADE. Inspection by the Officer Commanding. P.M. — Recreation for men.

### 14.1.19 — WATTRELOS

> The Bn. allotted Baths at TOURCOING. Authority received for all officers who served on any battlefront from 5th Aug. 1914 to 31st Dec. 1915, both dates inclusive, to wear the 1914–15 STAR Ribbon (Red, White and Blue, shaded watered). See Appendix "A" for Nominal Roll of officers and other ranks serving with this Unit who are entitled to wear the 1914–15 Riband.

Remarks column: "Appen 'A'".

### 15.1.19 — WATTRELOS

> The Bn. carried on with usual training and Education classes. All mounted officers proceeded to DOTTIGNIES to meet the Divl. Commander, Major-Gen. F.O.W. LOOMIS CB., CMG., DSO., to discuss the forthcoming demobilization of the Division.

### 16.1.19 — WATTRELOS

> Bn. was medically boarded prior to demobilization.

### 17.1.19 — WATTRELOS

> The Bn. carried on with the usual training and Education classes. P.M. — Recreation

### 18.1.19 — WATTRELOS

> Medical Board completed. P.M. — Battalion proceeded to DOTTIGNIES where the 8th Can. Inf. Bde. were inspected by the Corps Commander Lieut.-Gen. A.W. CURRIE, KCB., who briefly but explicitly outlined the procedure of demobilization for the 3rd Can. Division.

## Page 7 — War Diary (Army Form C. 2118), continued

### 19.1.19 — WATTRELOS

> Bn. paraded on the Bn. Alarm Post at 1100 hours for Divine Service. Capt. Colwell officiating. Mass was celebrated for the R.Cs at the parish Church, HERSEAUX. Lieut.-Col. W. RHOADES, DSO., MC., reported back from leave and assumed Command of the Bn.
>
> DESIGNATION OF UNIT. — The Hon. the Minister of the C.M.F. of C. has approved of the 5th C.M.R. Bn. being designated hereafter "5th Canadian Mounted Rifles (EASTERN TOWNSHIPS REGIMENT)" Authority: Can. Hqrs. London 34-1-1 d/4.1.19.
>
> HONOURS AND AWARDS: Belgian Croix de Guerre. — 110051 Sgt. BRAND J.J. 110594 Sgt. WOOD K.R.

### 20.1.19 — WATTRELOS

> Bn. was allotted Baths at TOURCOING.

### 21.1.19 — WATTRELOS

> Weather cold and fine. Bn. carried on with usual training and Education classes. Lieut. G. HARVEY, MC. proceeded to ENGLAND for demobilization.

### 22.1.19 — WATTRELOS

> Bn. paraded on the Bn. Alarm Post. The Bn. afterwards proceeded on a Route March. Confidential reports, Honours & Awards for PEACE DESPATCH, and Nominal Roll to which Dispersal Areas Officers and Other Ranks are proceeding to in CANADA (approx. 35 officers and 823 Other Ranks are returning with Unit to SHERBROOKE), were forwarded to Brigade Hqrs.

### 23.1.19 — WATTRELOS

> Bn. carried on with usual training in the morning. In the afternoon, the Brigade Commander, Brig.-Gen. D.C. DRAPER, CMG., DSO. addressed the Bn.

### 24.1.19 — WATTRELOS

> The Bn. paraded on the Bn. Alarm Post and afterwards proceeded on Route March. The following officers were despatched to ENGLAND in accordance with Cdn. Corps A-200 d/2.1.19. for demobilization: Capt. T.D. Johnston, Lieut. A.W. Thorne, Lieut. M.H. Rix; and Lieut. J. QUINLAN.

## Page 8 — War Diary (Army Form C. 2118), continued

### 25.1.19 — WATTRELOS

> Bn. carried on with usual training and Education classes.

### 26.1.19 — WATTRELOS

> Divine Service for Protestants had to be cancelled on account of the severe weather. Mass for R.Cs was celebrated in the Parish Church, HERSEAUX.

### 27.1.19 — WATTRELOS

> Pay Parade. When not on Pay Parades, Bn. carried on with usual training and Education classes. The following officers proceeded to ENGLAND for demobilization in accordance with Can. Corps A.200 d/2.1.19.: Lieut. J.S. Lindsay and Lieut. C.C. Dunham, MC.

### 28.1.19 — WATTRELOS

> Bn. parade and Route March.

### 29.1.19 — WATTRELOS

> Baths were allotted to the Bn. at TOURCOING. Coys when not at Baths carried on with Muster Parades and checking up of Coy. Nominal Roll.

### 30.1.19 — WATTRELOS

> Coys. carried on with the usual training and Education classes.

### 31.1.19 — WATTRELOS

> Capt. Colwell, MC. lectured to the Coys. on "Duties after return to Civil life" Coys. when not attending these Lectures carried on with the usual training.

## Page 9 — Appendix 1, Operation Order No. 187

Handwritten at top right: "APPEN 1". "SECRET. COPY ........" at top right; "Ref. Maps BRUSSELS & TOURNAI. 1/100000." at top left.

> 5th C.M.R. BATTALION.
>
> OPERATION ORDER No. 187.
>
> 30th Dec., 1918.
>
> 1. In accordance with 8th C.I.Bde. O.O.No. 37 dated 30th Dec. 1918; the 5th C.M.R. Battalion will vacate its present billets and proceed by march route to OPHASSELT tomorrow the 31st inst.
>
> 2. The Bn. will form up on the square, LENNICK ST. QUENTIN in close column of Coys. in line at 0840 hours tomorrow the 31st inst. The Transport to be parked in rear of C.Coy.
>
> 3. DRESS: FULL MARCHING ORDER. Steel Helmets to be carried on packs underneath kicking straps.
>
> 4. ORDER OF MARCH. Hqrs. — Band — D — A — B — C.Coys. — Transport.
>
> 5. DISTANCES. During the march the following distances will be maintained: Between Coys. — 10 yds. Between rear Coy. (C.Coy) & Transport — 10 yds. A distance of 30 yds. will be maintained behind every twelve vehicles.
>
> 6. ROUTE. Cross Roads 1/4 mile NORTH of first B in LENNICK ST QUENTIN — LOMBEKE ST. MARIE — Pt. 19 on HERZEELE-BRUSSELS Road — NINOVE — OPHASSELT.
>
> 7. HALTS. From 10 minutes to each clock hour, to each clock hour. The Bn. will halt for lunch from 1150 to 1300 hours.
>
> 8. ADVANCE PARTY, in accordance with our A.169 of date.
>
> 9. OFFICERS' KITS, ORDERLY ROOM BOXES, SIGNAL EQUIPMENT, CANTEEN STORES, BAND BOXES & BANDSMEN'S PACKS will be piled ready for loading outside respective Coy. Hqrs. by 0700 hours. The Transport Officer will arrange to collect same. The Transport Officer will send teams for Field Kitchens to respective Coys. at 0600 hours.
>
> 10. BLANKETS will be neatly rolled in bundles of ten, properly labelled and piled by each Coy. in front of respective Coy. Hqrs. by 0700 hours. Coy. Commanders will detail 1 O.R. per Coy. to guard blankets. These O.Rs will accompany lorry to destination.
>
> 11. REAR PARTY. The O.C. C.Coy. will detail a rear party of 1 officer and 10 men to follow on immediately in rear of C.Coy. to pick up any stragglers who may fall out during the march.
>
> 12. BILLETS will be left in a clean and sanitary condition and a certificate to this effect will be obtained by the Battalion Orderly Officer from the Town Major, LENNICK ST. QUENTIN, before the Battalion leave present billets. This certificate to be forwarded to Bn. Hqrs.
>
> 13. Upon arrival in OPHASSELT, Coy. Commanders, Transport Officer, and Quartermaster will forward to Bn. Hqrs. a complete list of billets occupied by men under their Command.
>
> 14. ATTENTION is drawn to Fourth Army "NOTES ON MARCH DISCIPLINE."
>
> Acknowledge.
>
> P. T. O.

## Notes

- **Coverage:** Entries run 1–31 January 1919 with no date gaps. The contents sheet states "Diary Text — 5 Pages", which matches the five diary-text pages (pages 4–8).
- **Movements:** A four-day march from Belgium into France, 31 December 1918–4 January 1919: Lennick St Quentin to Ophasselt (1 Jan), Ophasselt to Renaix, 22 km (2 Jan), Renaix to Ruyen and Escanaffles (3 Jan), Ruyen/Escanaffles to Dottignies (4 Jan), then to the Wattrelos area on 7 Jan (less "D" Company and Transport). The battalion remained at Wattrelos for the rest of the month: training, education classes, physical training, baths at Tourcoing, church parades at Herseaux.
- **Demobilization:** Medical boarding of the battalion on 16–18 January; the Divisional Commander (Maj.-Gen. F.O.W. Loomis) discussed demobilization with mounted officers on 15 January; Corps Commander Lt.-Gen. A.W. Currie outlined the demobilization procedure for the 3rd Canadian Division at Dottignies on 18 January. Officers were progressively despatched to England for demobilization from 21 January (Lt. G. Harvey, MC) and 24 and 27 January. About 35 officers and 823 other ranks were to return with the unit to Sherbrooke.
- **Designation:** On 19 January the battalion was redesignated "5th Canadian Mounted Rifles (Eastern Townships Regiment)" by authority of Canadian Headquarters London 34-1-1 dated 4.1.19.
