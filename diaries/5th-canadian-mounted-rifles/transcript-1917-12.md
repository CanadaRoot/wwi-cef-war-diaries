### 1/12/17 — FLECHIN

> Weather Fair. The morning was devoted to Musketry and Practice attacks by Platoons. Afternoon Recreation.

### 2/12/17 — FLECHIN

> Church Parade. Divine Service was held on the Battalion alarm post. Captain Colwell of the 2nd C. M. R. Battalion officiating.

### 3/12/17 — FLECHIN

> Weather Fine. The Battalion paraded on the Alarm Post at 9-00 AM for the Commanding Officer's inspection. After this Companies Carried on independently with Musketry and Gas Helmet Drill. The afternoon was devoted to Squad Drill and Musketry.

## Page 5 — War Diary (Army Form C. 2118), continued, diary page (2)

Place column: "FLECHIN" throughout.

### 4/12/17 — FLECHIN

> AM — Weather Fine. Companies Carried on independently with Specialists Training and Musketry.
>
> PM — Recreation

### 5/12/17 — FLECHIN

> Weather Fine. One full Platoon from each Company paraded on Battalion Alarm Post to participate in Tactical Scheme. Battalion was allotted Baths at FLECHINELLE, and on return Carried on with Specialists Training and Gas Helmet Drill.

### 6/12/17 — FLECHIN

> Battalion Paraded on Battalion Alarm Post for inspection by Officer Commanding, after which Companies Carried on independently with, Musketry, Platoon Drill, Specialist Training, and Gas Helmet Drill.

## Page 6 — War Diary (Army Form C. 2118), continued, diary page (3)

Place column: "FLECHIN" throughout.

### 7/12/17 — FLECHIN

> Battalion Paraded on the Battalion Alarm Post, after which Companies Carried on independently with Musketry, Practice of Platoons in the use of Opening fire during an advance, Fire orders and Fire Control. Firing practice was also Carried out whilst wearing Gas Respirators. Specialist Training and Platoon Drill were Carried on with. Two Companies had use of both miniature & large Ranges.

### 8/12/17 — FLECHIN

> AM — Battalion Paraded on the Battalion Alarm Post and proceeded on Route March.
>
> PM — The Afternoon was devoted to Recreation.

### 9/12/17 — FLECHIN

> Church Parade. Divine Service was held on Battalion Alarm Post. Captain Colwell MC of the 2nd C. M. R. Battalion officiating.

### 16/12/17 — FLECHIN (AM)

> Church Parade. Divine Service was held on Battalion Alarm Post at 11-00 AM. Captain Colwell MC officiating.
>
> PM — At 2 PM the Battalion had their Christmas Dinner. Three Companys sitting down at 2-00 PM, the remainder at 3-00 PM. The Dinner was followed by a Cinema Show. In the evening the Dumbell Concert Party entertained the Battalion and gave an excellent performance which was much enjoyed. Information was received that all Operation Orders re taking over line at MERICOURT were Cancelled.

### 17/12/17 — FLECHIN

> Rainy Weather. The Officer Commanding, 2nd in Command and Company Commanders witnessed Smoke Demonstration at Ferfay. Remainder of Battalion carried on with Patrol exercises.

## Page 11 — War Diary (Army Form C. 2118), continued, diary page (8)

### 17/12/17 — FLECHIN (PM)

> At 6-00 PM information was received that the Battalion would march to L'ECLEME at 8-00 AM next morning the 18th December. Preparations for move were immediately made.

### 18/12/17 — FLECHIN–L'ECLEME

> Weather very cold but fine. The Battalion moved off from the Battalion Alarm Post at FLECHIN at 8-00 AM. Marching was very difficult owing to the Slippery nature of the Roads. The Battalion arrived in Billets at L'ECLEME at 3-30 PM.
>
> APPEN. I

### 19/12/17 — L'ECLEME–FOUQUIERES

> Weather fine. The Battalion continued their march at 10-30 AM arriving in Billets at FOUQUIERES at 2-30 PM.
>
> APPEN. II

## Page 12 — War Diary (Army Form C. 2118), continued, diary page (9)

### 20/12/17 — FOUQUIERES–LES-BREBIS

> Weather Fine. Cold. The Battalion continued their march at 10-00 AM and arrived at LES BREBIS at 1-00 PM. In the afternoon preparations were made for taking over the front line at HILL 70 on the 21st and 22nd December.
>
> Appen. III

### 21/12/17 — LES-BREBIS–FRONT LINE

> The Battalion relieved Companies of the "8th DUKE OF WELLINGTONS" Regiment and the "5th DORSET" Battalion in the front line. Relief was completed at 8 P.M., and was very quiet. No Casualties. [underlined in the original]
>
> Appen. IV
>
> DISPOSITION STATE

### 22/12/17 — FRONT LINE

> Weather Fine. During the afternoon our Guns fired on the Enemys back areas. Enemy continuously swept our lines during the night with his Machine Guns. The Enemy aircraft was very active during the day, and movement could be seen at all times during the day behind the Enemy lines.

## Page 13 — War Diary (Army Form C. 2118), continued, diary page (10)

Place column: "FRONT LINE" throughout.

### 23/12/17 — FRONT LINE

> Snow. Enemy quiet, and was seen to be working hard on his defences, also movement could be seen behind his lines. Our front was patrolled during the night.

### 24/12/17 — FRONT LINE

> Weather Fine. At 12-00 noon a Patrol of two Officers (CAPTAIN WILSON and LIEUT. FITTO) went out of the Sap at N.2.b.37 to within fifteen yards of Enemys wire. His wire was found to be in good condition. No movement was heard at Enemys post and no attention was paid to the patrol. At 12-15 PM another daylight patrol Consisting of LIEUT. ADAMSON and one other Rank, Proceeded out of Sap. H. 32. d. 3-4 to Railway. Enemy wire no yards beyond the Railway was found to be in good Condition. No signs of Enemy were seen. — Inter Company Relief took place and was completed by 8-00 P.M. Relief movements were observed behind Enemy Lines.
>
> APPEN. V
>
> DISPOSITION STATE

## Page 14 — War Diary (Army Form C. 2118), continued, diary page (11)

Place column: "FRONT LINE" throughout.

### 25/12/17 — FRONT LINE

> Weather Fine. A Patrol of two Officers and one other rank under LIEUT. HANRATTY left front line at N.2.b.4.6. and proceeded along wire to N.2.b.25.50. The Enemy wire was Reported to be in very good Condition.

### 26/12/17 — FRONT LINE

> Weather Fine but Cold. Enemy very quiet.

### 27/12/17 — FRONT LINE

> Weather Fine. Movement observed in Enemy lines apparently this was a relief carried out during the night. Our Battalion was relieved by the 1st C.M.R. Battalion and relief was Completed by 9-30 PM and we then marched to Billets at MEZINGARBE arriving there at 1-00 AM. 28th/27th. This being the first time in the History of the Battalion, the Battalion Completed a tour in the front line without one Casualty.

## Notes

- **Coverage:** Entries run 1–31 December 1917 with no date gaps. The contents sheet states "Diary Text — 14 Pages", which matches the fourteen diary-text pages (file pages 4–17). The contents sheet lists six appendices, but the file holds seven appendix items: Operation Order No. 77 (26-12-17, file page 24) is not listed on the contents sheet, and the Distribution State appears twice (file pages 25 and 26).
- **Officers named:** Lt-Col D. C. Draper, D.S.O. (Commanding Officer; awarded a Bar to the D.S.O. for 30/31 October 1917 at Passchendaele); Major W. Rhoades (2nd in Command; presided at the officers' New Year dinner on 31 December; also signs the Distribution State as Major, for O.C.); Major Harbord (advance party, 18–19 December); Lt J. H. Cooke (Adjutant; signs all the operation orders and the diary); Captain Colwell, M.C., 2nd C.M.R. Battalion (officiated at Divine Service on 2, 9 and 16 December); Captain Wilson (daylight patrol, 24 December); Lieut. Fitto, H. L. Fitto, M.C. (daylight patrol, 24 December; Military Cross for 30/31 October 1917); Lieut. Adamson (daylight patrol, 24 December; advance party to Les Brebis, 20 December); Lieut. Hanratty (patrol, 25 December); Lieut. Atto (guides, Operation Order No. 77 — see Anomalies); Lieut. L. H. Millar, M.C.; Lieut. R. Andrews, M.C.; Lieut. H. R. Gifford, M.C.; Lieut. A. N. Logie, M.C. (all Military Cross for 30/31 October 1917); Captain Oliver (lecture on civil training, 31 December).
- **Other ranks named:** Military Medal, 10 December, for gallantry during the advance at Passchendaele: Sgt C. S. Rutherford (172458), Sgt D. Morgan (110364), Sgt E. L. Marshall (110383), Sgt F. I. Andrew (110008), Cpl Whyteman (202057), Cpl J. J. Nixon (110621), Pte W. Huntingford (832429), Pte A. Dunham (123126), Pte C. J. Gill (111539), Pte E. Burgess (111063), Pte L. Odell (111547), Pte R. Symons (408538), Pte W. O'Hearn (111401), Pte C. H. Carr (709898), Pte C. Buell (444438), Pte I. Walker (1110634 — see Anomalies). Distinguished Conduct Medal, 28 December, for 30/31 October 1917 at Passchendaele: Sgt J. H. Cameron (111070), Cpl G. Cruikshanks (110113), Cpl T. B. McAuley (748236), Pte S. L. Marquand (458392), Pte C. Benham (no number given). Military Medal, 28 December, for 30/31 October 1917 at Passchendaele: Pte J. Copperthwait (111015).
- **Equipment:** Lewis guns — the Distribution State lists gun positions for the companies in the line (two guns at Nuns Alley / Catapult, four guns in D Company positions). Gas helmets and gas respirators drilled 3, 5, 6, 7 December; firing practice carried out while wearing gas respirators, 7 December. Musketry on miniature and large ranges; bombing practice and bayonet fighting; night operations in wire cutting and covering fire (11–13 December); patrol exercises and smoke demonstration (17 December). Steel helmets, leather jerkins, haversack lunches, ground sheets and bandoliers on the marches (Operation Orders 73–75). Five lorries/trucks detailed for Lewis guns and surplus kit on the 27 December relief (Operation Order No. 77).
- **Movements:** The battalion was at Flechin 1–17 December, training (musketry, platoon attacks, specialist training, gas drill, night operations, a brigade scheme on 14 December). It marched 18 December Flechin to L'Ecleme (via Auchy au Bois, Borcq, Lillers, Cantrainne), 19 December L'Ecleme to Fouquieres (via Busnettes, Lehamel, Choques, Annezin, skirting south-west of Bethune), and 20 December Fouquieres to Les Brebis (via Vaudricourt, Noeux les Mines, Bracquemont, Petit Sains). On the night of 21/22 December it relieved companies of the 8th Duke of Wellington's Regiment and the 5th Dorset Battalion in the front line at Hill 70. An inter-company relief took place on the night of 24 December. On the night of 27/28 December the battalion was relieved by the 1st C.M.R. Battalion and marched to billets at Mazingarbe, arriving 1-00 AM on 28 December.
- **Discipline:** None recorded in December 1917.
- **Casualties:** None recorded in December 1917. The 21 December relief is noted as "very quiet. No Casualties." (underlined in the original), and the 27 December entry records that this was the first time in the history of the battalion that it completed a tour in the front line without one casualty.
- **Anomalies:** (1) The cover is marked "With appendices I – V" but the contents sheet lists six appendices, and the file in fact holds seven appendix items — Operation Order No. 77 (26-12-17) is not listed on the contents sheet at all, and the Distribution State is present in two copies (pages 25 and 26). (2) The two copies of the Distribution State disagree: the "A" and "B" company labels are swapped between them (the company at Nuns Alley / Catapult is "A" Coy. in copy 1 and "B" Coy. in copy 2, and vice versa for Nuns Alley / Noggin); the Bayonet Men figure on the first line of the Nuns Alley company reads 9 in copy 1 and 8 in copy 2; the D Company 13 Platoon and Coy. HQs trailing strength figures also differ (see the [uncertain] flags in the tables). (3) The Distribution State's own arithmetic does not reconcile: the printed column totals (21 officers; 46 bombers, 166 bayonet men, 55 rifle grenadiers, 92 Lewis gunners, 17 scouts, 23 S.B., 25 signallers, 21 runners) sum to 445 men, against the stated "Total Men 437"; the row figures as transcribed sum to 158 bayonet men, 54 rifle grenadiers and 18 signallers against the printed totals of 166, 55 and 25, so some faint figures in the table (most likely on the first "A" Coy. line) have probably been misread. (4) Spelling varies between pages as written: L'ECLUSE (contents sheet) vs L'ECLEME (diary and orders); LES BREGIS (contents sheet) vs LES BREBIS (diary and orders); MEZINGARBE (27 December entry) vs MAZINGARBE (diary place column 28–31 December and the orders); PASCHENDAEL (10 December) vs PASCHENDAELE (28 December); NELSON TRIL. (copy 1) vs NELSON TRN. (copy 2). (5) The officer named "Lieut. FITTO" in the diary (24 December) and in the Military Cross list (28 December) is named "Lieut. ATTO" in Operation Order No. 77. (6) Pte I. Walker's regimental number on 10 December appears to have seven digits (1110634), where every other number in the lists has six. (7) The 24 December entry reads, as written, "Enemy wire no yards beyond the Railway was found to be in good Condition." (8) The 27 December entry gives the arrival time as "1-00 AM. 28th/27th." (9) The 31 December entry has one word struck through before "enjoyable" ("A very [one word struck through] enjoyable evening was spent"). (10) Operation Order No. 76 of 23 December (Appendix 5) is headed "Inter-Battalion Relief" although the contents sheet describes it, correctly, as an inter-company relief. (11) The spelling "independantly" (12 December) and "Companys" (16 December) are as written in the original. (12) The operation orders re taking over the line at Mericourt were cancelled, per the 16 December entry; the battalion instead took over the Hill 70 line on 21/22 December. (13) The remarks-column initials on the diary pages are a monogram that could not be read with confidence; the diary is signed at the foot of page 17 by the Adjutant, Lt J. H. Cooke, for the O.C.
