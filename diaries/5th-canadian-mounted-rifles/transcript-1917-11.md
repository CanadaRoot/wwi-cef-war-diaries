### 1/11/17 — WATTOU AREA

> Battalion vacated Billetts at CAPRICORN KEEP and proceeded to Transport Lines where packs were collected, then the Battalion marched to the Asylum YPRES there embarking for MILLS CAMP (WATTOU AREA). On arrival of Battalion at MILLS CAMP it was found that no preparations had been made for the billeting of the men, who were very tired and worn after the heavy fighting.

### 2/11/17

> Weather Fine. Battalion engaged in erecting tents.

### 3/11/17

> Baths allotted to Battalion at POPERINGHE, and on return the erecting of tents and camouflaging was carried on with

### 4/11/17

> Weather Squally. Church Parade was held on the Battalion Alarm Post the Adjutant Officiating. Afternoon Recreation.

## Page 5 — War Diary (Army Form C. 2118), diary page (2)

Remarks column: each entry is initialled "H.H.C."

### 5/11/17

> Weather Squally. Companies Carried on Checking up equipment. During the day 65 reinforcements were received from C.C.R.C. these were chiefly old men who had left us at Les Brebis.

### 6/11/17

> Weather Showery. Battalion were allotted Baths, and the Companies not at Baths carried on with Musketry and Platoon drill.

### 7/11/17

> Weather Fair. Battalion paraded on 2nd C.M.R.s ground with 1st and 2nd C.M.R. Battalions and were inspected by the Corps Commander who afterwards made a speech in which he congratulated the whole of the 8th Canadian Infantry Brigade on the way they had behaved and worked in the recent heavy fighting at the PASCHENDAEL. He also made suitable reference to our Comrades who had made the supreme sacrifice. Three hearty Cheers were given at the conclusion of the Corps Commander's

## Page 6 — War Diary (Army Form C. 2118), diary page (3)

Remarks column: each entry is initialled "H.H.C."

> Speech. The Brigade then marched past Major General Sir A.W. Currie Corps Commander taking the Salute.

### 8/11/17

> Weather Showery. During the morning the Companies carried on with Gas Helmet and squad drill. The afternoon being devoted to musketry and practice attack on Pill Boxes by Platoons.

### 9/11/17

> The Divisional Commander inspected Battalion and personally congratulated the Officers and Other Ranks who had reached the final objectives (Vapour and Vales Farms.) He also thanked the Battalion for the splendid work they had done during the advance (PASSCHENDAEL)

### 10/11/17

> The morning was devoted to Gas Helmet and Squad Drill. In the afternoon practice attacks on Pill Boxes by platoons, were carried out.

## Page 7 — War Diary (Army Form C. 2118), diary page (4)

Remarks column: each entry is initialled "H.H.C."

### 11/11/17

> Church Parade. Divine Service was held on the Battalion Parade Ground. Capt'n Colwell, Chaplain to the 2nd C.M.R. Battalion officiated

### 12/11/17

> The Battalion vacated Mills Camp at 6-30 A.M. and proceeded by bus to "B" Camp in the Wieltje Area. The accommodation here was very poor. The afternoon was spent in cleaning up and making the Camp habitable. During the night the enemy shelled the Camp and one shell destroyed "D" Company's Field Kitchen.

### 13/11/17

> Orders were received this morning for Battalion to proceed to Camp 32 Brandhoek Area. The Battalion paraded at 1-30 P.M. and marched to G11b18 - Brandhoek Area where on our arrival it was found we had been given the wrong location, after considerable delay the Battalion arrived at G13b68 our destination, at 6.00 P.M.

### 14/11/17

> The day was devoted to the erection of tents and Camouflaging.

### 15/11/17

> Battalion was allotted Baths at Poperinghe and also carried on with Musketry.

> Weather Showery. Foot inspection was held in the morning by the Medical Officer, The checking up of Equipment was also carried on. The afternoon was devoted to Musketry.

### 17/11/17

> The Battalion carried on during the morning with Physical Training, Rifle and Squad drill The afternoon was spent making preparations to move The Battalion Transport proceeded to Caestre by road from 32 Camp, at 6.30 A.M. under the command of Transport Officer

## Page 9 — War Diary (Army Form C. 2118), diary page (6)

Remarks column: each entry is initialled "H.H.C."

### 18/11/17

> During the morning the Commanding Officer together with the Adjutant visited the graves of the late Lieut-Colonel Baker and Major Pilcher at the Cemetery Poperinghe The graves had been well kept and were in good order. At 11.30 A.M. the Battalion paraded on the Alarm Post and marched to H.18.C.0.5. Sheet 27 where it proceeded by bus to Haverskerque arriving 5-30 P.M. One night was spent in this village and the accommodation for both Officers and Other Ranks was very good. The Transport rejoined the Unit at this place. See Appen.

### 20/11/17

> Battalion fell in In Column of Route at Haverskerque at 8.45 A.M. and marched to Flechin arriving at 5-30 P.M. In spite of the long march of 28 kilometres, the whole of the Battalion arrived at the Billetts in Flechin in Splendid Condition See Appen.

> During the afternoon One Officer and eight other Ranks from the 1st Army Musketry School reported to the Battalion, also Six N.C.O. Instructors from the Divisional School, Ferfay.

### 26/11/17

> Weather Fine. The day was devoted to Musketry under the Instructors.

### 27/11/17

> The G.O.C. 8th Canadian Infantry Brigade, inspected the Training Area of unit. Battalion carried on as on the 26th. Lieut-Colonel D.C. Draper D.S.O. Officer Commanding, proceeded to the 9th Canadian Infantry Brigade & took over Command during the absence of Brigadier General F.W. Hill D.S.O. Major W. ### 28/11/17

> Battalion carried on with Musketry under the Instructors from 1st Army School. The Six N.C.O.s from Divisional School returned to their unit, their Services being much appreciated by the Battalion.

### 29/11/17

> Battalion carried on as on the 28th. A farewell dinner was given in the evening to the N.C.O. Instructors of the 1st Army School attended by the R.S.M. and Sergeants of the Battalion. The Battalion derived great benefit from the instruction received & their work was greatly appreciated by all.

### 30/11/17

> Two Companies had use of miniature Range on "A" Company's ground, the rest of Battalion carried on with usual training Major G.R. Pearkes proceeded to the 116th Battalion to take over duties as Senior Major of that Unit. Previous to his departure the Battalion Paraded and Lieut-Colonel D.C. Draper D.S.O.

## Notes

- **Coverage:** Entries run 1–30 November 1917. The contents sheet states "Diary Text — 10 Pages", which matches the ten diary-text pages (pages 4–13). There is no entry dated 19 November: the march to Flechin is entered under 20 November (page 9), and the following page (page 10) also opens on 20 November with a foot inspection at Flechin. Appendix 6 (Operation Order No. 79) orders the Flechin march for 19 November, so the diary's dating of the march to the 20th appears to be an error in the original.
- **Officers named:** Lt-Col D. C. Draper D.S.O., Officer Commanding (inspected 9 November; proceeded to 9th Canadian Infantry Brigade 27 November; congratulated Maj Pearkes 30 November). Maj W. Rhodes M.C. (assumed command of the battalion 27 November; signed the diary as Major, for O.C.). Maj G. R. Pearkes (proceeded to the 116th Battalion as Senior Major 30 November). Maj-Gen Sir A. W. Currie, Corps Commander (took the salute at the 7 November march past). Brig-Gen F. W. Hill D.S.O. (G.O.C. 9th Canadian Infantry Brigade, absent 27 November). Capt Colwell, chaplain to the 2nd C.M.R. Battalion (officiated 11 November). Capt Stewart of the 1st C.M.R. Battalion (officiated 25 November). Lt Winslow (in charge of lorries, Appendices 4 and 6). Lt Hanratty (in charge of "A" and "C" Coys on the Flechin march, Appendix 6). Lt Verney of the 1st Army School (lectured officers and N.C.O.s 27 November). Maj Harford (lorry parking instructions, Appendix 4). Lt H. H. Cooke, Adjutant (signed Operation Orders Nos. 66, 69, 70 and 79; his initials "H.H.C." appear in the diary remarks column). The late Lt-Col Baker and Maj Pilcher, whose graves at Poperinghe Cemetery the Commanding Officer and Adjutant visited on 18 November. The Divisional Commander (unnamed) inspected the battalion 9 November; the G.O.C. 8th Canadian Infantry Brigade (unnamed) inspected the training area 27 November.
- **Other ranks named:** None named by name. 65 reinforcements received from C.C.R.C. on 5 November, chiefly old men who had left the battalion at Les Brebis. One officer and eight other ranks from the 1st Army Musketry School and six N.C.O. instructors from the Divisional School, Ferfay, reported 25 November; the six Divisional School N.C.O.s returned to their unit 28 November. The R.S.M. and Sergeants attended the farewell dinner for the 1st Army School N.C.O. instructors on 29 November. Two other ranks accompanied Lt Winslow's lorry (Appendix 4).
- **Equipment:** Lewis guns and surplus baggage piled for loading (Appendix 5); four dixies per company for hot meals on the move (Appendix 5); gas helmets carried in the alert position and steel helmets worn on moves (Appendices 2, 3, 4, 6); practice attacks on pill boxes by platoons (8 and 10 November); a miniature range on "A" Company's ground (30 November); preparations for constructing a rifle range (23 November). "D" Company's field kitchen was destroyed by a shell on the night of 12 November.
- **Movements:** 1 November: Capricorn Keep to Transport Lines, then marched to the Asylum, Ypres, embussed for Mills Camp, Wattou Area. 12 November: by bus to "B" Camp, Wieltje Area. 13 November: marched to Camp 32, Brandhoek Area (first given the wrong location, G11b18, then reaching G13b68). 17 November: battalion transport marched by road to Caestre, billeted at Labouuo Farm. 18 November: battalion by bus to Haverskerque (via L.18.c.0.5., Sheet 27). 19/20 November: marched 28 kilometres to Flechin, arriving 5.30 p.m. The battalion spent the rest of the month training at Flechin.
- **Discipline:** None recorded in November 1917.
- **Casualties:** None recorded in the November diary text. The battalion was resting and refitting after the heavy fighting at Passchendaele; the only damage recorded is the destruction of "D" Company's field kitchen by shelling on the night of 12 November, with no casualties stated. The 18 November visit to the graves of Lt-Col Baker and Maj Pilcher at Poperinghe recalls earlier losses.
