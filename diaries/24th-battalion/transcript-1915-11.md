## Page 3 — Diary text (November 1915, p. 3)

Army Form C. 2118 — WAR DIARY or INTELLIGENCE SUMMARY. Each entry signed in the margin "C. J. Ritchie Capt."

### 3-11-15 — Near Kemmel

> Relieved the 25th Bn. Canadians in the Right Sector of 5th Can. Inf. Bde. taking over trenches from C.4 to J.11

### 4-11-15 to 8-11-15

> All available men used on repairing trenches damaged by rain, revetting the trenches, building drains and pumping out water.

[The dates 4.11.15 and 8.11.15 are bracketed together against this single entry.]

### 9-11-15

> Relieved by the 25th Bn. Canadians. Marched back to billets in LOCRE.

### 14-11-15 — Loore. [uncertain — probably Locre]

> Church Parade — Major Beattie conducted the service.

### 15-11-15 — Near Kemmel, 10.30 p.m.

> Marched out to trenches relieved the 25th Bn. CANADIANS, taking over the same trenches as on the 3rd inst.

### 19-11-15

> Relieved by the 25th Bn. Canadians. Marched back to LOCRE.

### 23-11-15

> Marched out from LOCRE and relieved the 25th Bn. Canadians, taking over the same trenches as on the 15th inst.,

### 27-11-15

> Relieved by the 25th Bn. Canadians. Marched back to LOCRE.

## Page 4 — Appendix 15: Operation Order No. 7, 15-11-15

> 24th BN. CANADIANS — OPERATION ORDER No. 7
>
> Copy No. 9.... 15-11-15.
>
> Ref. — Map Sheet 28, 1/40,000
>
> 1. During the day and night of the 15th inst., the 24th Bn. will relieve the 25th Bn. in the Right Sector, the 25th Bn. moving to LOCRE in Brigade Reserve.
>
> 2. The 24th Bn. will move from LOCRE to carry out the relief as follows:
>
> (a) "B" Coy. of the 24th Bn. will relieve the company of the 25th Bn. in support, by small parties. Relief to be completed before 2.30 p.m.
>
> (b) The Reserve Company of the 25th Bn. at SIEGE FARM will be relieved by "A" Coy. by 5 p.m. by small parties, via R.E. FARM.
>
> (c) "D" Coy. at present in billets at R.E. FARM will remain there until 9 p.m. when they will then move up and relieve the company of the 25th Bn. occupying the G. and H. trenches.
>
> (d) "C" Coy. of the 24th Bn. will move from billets in time to be at SIEGE FARM by 5 p.m. and will remain at SIEGE FARM until 9 p.m. when they will move up and relieve the company of the 25th Bn. occupying the J. trenches.
>
> (e) The Grenade Platoon will move from billets in time to be at R.E. FARM by 5.30 p.m. and will remain at R.E. FARM, under cover, until 9 p.m. when this platoon will move up and relieve the Grenade Platoon of the 25th Bn. in H.Q.
>
> (f) The M.G. Sec. of the 24th Bn. will relieve the M.G. Sec. of the 25th Bn. relief to be completed by 8 p.m.
>
> (g) The Sig. Sec. of the 24th Bn. will relieve the Sig. Sec. of the 25th Bn. by 8 p.m.
>
> 3. Between the hours of 4.30 p.m. and 9 p.m. there is to be no movement, and no transport will be on the road EAST of the DICKEBUSCH [uncertain] — LOCRE — BRANCOURT [uncertain] Road (inclusive)
>
> 4. The Qr. Mr. will arrange to have to-morrow's rations for "A" and "C" Coys. brought up to SIEGE FARM some time this morning. "C" Coy. will carry their rations from SIEGE FARM with them into the trenches. "B" Coy's rations for to-morrow will be taken to SIEGE FARM by the Qr. Mr. and "A" Coy. will furnish a carrying party to carry up those rations to-night. This carrying party will also take up water and will be at the Bn. Dump at 9 p.m. "D" Coy's rations will be sent out to them at R.E. FARM when they will then carry their own rations into the trenches. This also applies to the Grenade Platoon and M.G. Section.
>
> 5. (a) Usual certificates as to rifle inspection, and the receiving of billets and trenches in sanitary condition are to be taken and forwarded to Bn. H.Q.
>
> (b) When taking over trenches a most careful check is to be made of all trench stores.
>
> (c) O.C. relieving Coys. are responsible that the correct number of rubber boots, waders, etc., are handed over.
>
> 6. Water bottles are to be filled before leaving billets.
>
> 7. Co. Comdrs. will take every precaution for the safety of the men. Overland routes are only to be used in very small parties.
>
> 8. Completion of relief to be wired to Bn. H.Q. Co. Comdrs. will send down their reports by their own runners to reach Bn. H.Q. by 6 a.m. each morning.
>
> Issued at 10 a.m.
>
> [Signed] C. J. Ritchie Capt. Adjt. 24th Bn. (V.R)

## Page 5 — Appendix 22: Order No. 9, 22-11-15

> 24th BN. (VICTORIA RIFLES) CANADIANS — ORDER No. 9
>
> Copy No...... 22.11.15
>
> Ref. Map — Sheet 28 — 1/40,000
>
> 1. During the day and night of the 23rd inst., the 24th Bn. will relieve the 25th Bn. in the Right Sector, the 25th Bn. moving to LOCRE in Divisional Reserve.
>
> 2. The 24th Bn. will move from LOCRE to carry out the relief as follows:
>
> "B" Co. will relieve the company of the 25th Bn. at SIEGE FARM by small parties, via R.E. FARM. Relief to be completed before dark.
>
> 3. "A", "C" and "D" Companies will move from LOCRE at 4.15 p.m. Starting Point — LOCRE CHURCH.
>
> "A" Co. will garrison the G. and H. trenches.
>
> "C" Co. will garrison the J. trenches.
>
> "D" Co. will occupy supporting dugouts.
>
> Advance parties of 1 Officer and 4 N.C.O's per company ("A", "C" and "D" Cos.) will take over trench stores during the day in order to make an exact inventory of same and to expedite relief.
>
> 3. [numbered 3 again in the original] The machine guns of the 25th Bn. are to be relieved by the machine guns of the 24th Bn. by 12.30 p.m. on the 23rd inst.
>
> 4. The Signalling Section of the 25th Bn. is to be relieved by the Signalling Section of the 24th Bn. by 2.30 p.m. on the 23rd inst. The 25th Bn. will leave their operators with the 24th Bn. until the relief of their unit is completed.
>
> 5. The transport wagons will call [uncertain] for company stores at 3 p.m.
>
> 6. "B" Co. will supply a carrying party of 1 Officer and 60 men to report at the V.C. Road at an hour to be notified later.
>
> 7. The Grenade Platoon will relieve the Grenade Platoon of the 25th Bn. in H.Q., moving with the other three companies from LOCRE at 4.15 p.m.
>
> 8. Usual certificates as to rifle inspection, and the receiving of billets and trenches in sanitary condition are to be taken and forwarded to Bn. H.Q.
>
> 9. O.C. relieving Cos. are responsible that the correct number of rubber boots, waders, etc., are handed over.
>
> 10. Every precaution is to be taken for the safety of the men. Overland routes are only to be used by very small parties.
>
> 11. Completion of relief is to be wired to Bn. H.Q.
>
> [Signed] C. J. Ritchie Capt. Adjt. 24th Bn. (V.R.) Canadians.

## Open questions / uncertain items (November)
- The cover (p. 1) was first written for October ("From 3rd October to 27th October 1915, Volume 3") and corrected to November in pencil; the contents page gives the volume as "Volume 15-11". The diary text itself covers 3–27 November 1915.
- 14 November: the place column reads "Loore." [uncertain — probably Locre, spelled LOCRE elsewhere].
- Operation Order No. 7, para. 3: the road name reads "DICKEBUSCH — LOCRE — BRANCOURT Road" [uncertain] — the type is smudged.
- Order No. 9, para. 5: the verb before "for company stores" is read as "call" [uncertain]; para. 6: "V.C. Road" as read.
- Order No. 9 is numbered with two paragraphs numbered 3 (the machine-guns paragraph repeats the number) — as in the original.
- No casualties are recorded anywhere in the November diary text or appendices. No days between 28–30 November are covered; the diary resumes 6 December.
