## Pages 9–10 — Appendix 17: Order No. 11, 17 December 1915

> 24th BN. (VICTORIA RIFLES) CANADIANS — ORDER No. 11
>
> Copy No...... 17.12.15
>
> 1. The 25th Bn. will relieve the 24th Bn. on the day and night of the 18th December 1915. The 24th Bn. on relief will move into Divisional Reserve in billets at Locre.
>
> 2. Reserve Company. 1 company of the 25th Bn. will relieve "A" Co. 24th Bn. at SIEGE FARM, the relief to be completed by 4 p.m. On relief, "A" Co. will move by small parties via R.E. Farm and will rendezvous at road junction R.E. FARM — DICKEBUSCH — LA CLYTTE Road, and will move into Locre as a company, march discipline being maintained throughout.
>
> 3. Front line and Support Companies. 3 companies of the 25th Bn. will move from Locre at 3.45 p.m. and will relieve "B", "C" and "D" Cos. On relief each company will move by small parties via R.E. Farm and will rendezvous by companies at road junction R.E. FARM — DICKEBUSCH — LA CLYTTE Road, and will move into Locre as companies, march discipline being maintained throughout.
>
> 4. Machine Gun Sec. The M.G. Sec. will be relieved by 2 p.m., and will move via R.E. Farm as a unit under the command of the M.G. Officer.
>
> 5. Grenade Platoon. The Grenade Platoon on relief will move into Locre under the charge of the Grenade Officer, who will see that march discipline is kept.
>
> 6. Signals. The Sig. Sec. will be relieved by the Sig. Sec. of the 25th Bn. by 4 p.m. They will leave their operators with the 25th Bn. until the relief is completed. They will march back to Locre with the Hd. Qrs. sub Staff under the command of Lieut. Woolsey [uncertain].
>
> 7. Billets. 1 Officer per company will proceed to Locre and report to the Quartermaster at 11 a.m. for the purpose of taking over billets, and will also guide their companies to their respective billets.
>
> 8. Advance Parties. Advance parties consisting of cooks and Officers' servants will rendezvous at Siege Farm at 2 p.m. and will proceed to Locre under the charge of an Officer detailed by [uncertain] Coy.
>
> 9. Certificates. On arrival in billets, all rifles and ammunition to be examined and a certificate sent in to the Orderly Room to the effect that each man has the required number of rounds in his possession, and also reporting on the condition of the rifles and ammunition.
>
> 10. Trench Stores. Inventories of trench stores will be made out in duplicate and a receipt will be taken from the Officer of the 25th Bn. taking over these stores. The original receipt will be kept by the Officer of the 24th Bn. and will be handed in to the Adjutant before 9 a.m. Sunday, December 19th, copies being left with the Officer taking over. Care is to be exercised in preparing these receipts, and also as to the number of gum boots that are turned over. These receipts are to be made out neatly as they are to be forwarded to Hdqrs. Gum boots will be provided from Siege Farm for the men going into the front line trenches. All other gum boots will be left in the support trenches.
>
> 11. General. Every precaution is to be taken for the safety of the men. Overland routes are only to be used by very small parties.
>
> 12. Transport. The transport is to be South of Kemmel — La Clytte Road until 7 p.m. All company stores and baggage to be taken into Locre by the transport will be brought down and left at the Battalion Dump under the charge of 1 N.C.O. and 2 men per company who are to be held responsible for the delivery of the stores in their charge. These [a word struck through — uncertain] stores will NOT be brought to Bn. Hq. Qrs. The transport will be at the Battalion Dump at 8 p.m. After the transport has been loaded the details will be marched back to Locre under the senior N.C.O.
>
> 13. As soon as regimental units have been relieved they will immediately wire the hour of relief to Bn. Hdqrs.
>

## Open questions / uncertain items (December)
- The diary text (p. 3) covers 6–24 December, then two final entries whose dates are hidden under a dark stain in the date column: a visit by Maj. Gen. A.E.W. Turner to Bn. Headquarters at Rossignol Estaminet (margin ref. 7), and the final relief to Brigade Reserve at R.E. Farm (margin ref. 8). The final entry is dated 30 December on the strength of the cover ("From 6th December to 30th December 1915") and Operation Order No. 25 (29-12-15), which orders that relief for the day and night of 30 December; the Turner visit falls somewhere between 25 and 29 December and cannot be dated from these pages.
- 19 December: "Ordered to 'Stand To' on account of GAS attack, in the road." — the word "road" is as read [uncertain]; the entry gives no casualties.
- 24 December, 8 p.m.: a word after "5TH CAN. INF. BDE." could not be read [uncertain].
- The Turner visit entry writes the General's initials ambiguously — they look like "R.E.W." [uncertain]; every other entry gives "A.E.W. TURNER".
- The contents page (p. 2) has four appendix numbers clipped off at the left edge of the scan (Orders No. 10, 12 and 13, and Operation Order No. 23); the numbers used above are those visible on the appendix pages themselves where circled.
- The cover says "With appendices 1–8" while the contents lists nine appendices; the diary's margin references run 1–8. The cover volume is "Volume 4"; the contents page gives "Volume 15-12".
- Operation Order No. 21, para. 1: the typed "24th and 22nd Bns will relieve the 24th and 26th Bns" was corrected by hand to "25th and 22nd Bns will relieve the 24th and 26th Bns" — the correction is what is transcribed.
- Operation Order No. 23 is signed "Brigadier General E. Watson" [uncertain] — the initial may be D., as in Orders No. 21, 22, 24 and 25.
- Order No. 12 (25-12-15) has inconsistent company letters as typed ("B" Co. is given two different tasks; "C" Co. is said to relieve "B" Co. 24th Bn.) — transcribed as written.
- Order No. 11, para. 6: "Lieut. Woolsey" [uncertain]; para. 8: the company detailing the advance-party officer could not be read [uncertain].
- Order No. 21, para. (d): "B. 35. Nov 25th." [uncertain — probably "B.M. 35", as in Orders No. 22–24]; Copy No. 8 reads "4th" followed by an illegible word [uncertain].
- No casualties are recorded anywhere in the December diary text. The only enemy action noted is the counter-mine of 6 December and the gas-attack stand-to of 19 December.
- December p. 13 is a blank reverse with pencil notes ("Wagons, La Clytte 3.30" and an illegible first line); p. 17 is blank.
