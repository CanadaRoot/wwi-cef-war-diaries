- **Unit:** 24th Battalion (Victoria Rifles), 2nd Canadian Division
- **Source:** LAC Collection Search Item ID 1883241, "War diaries — 24th Canadian Infantry Battalion" (RG 9 III-D-3, vol. 4932, file 414; microfilm reels T-10733–T-10735)
- **Pages:** January 1916, p. 1 – p. 25 (image files page-0001 – page-0025 of item 1883241)
- **January 1916 — COMPLETE** (25 physical pages): cover, contents, diary text 1–29 January (three diary-text pages), Appendices 1–11: 5th Canadian Infantry Brigade Operation Orders Nos. 27, 28, 29, 30 and 31; 24th Bn. Orders Nos. 14, 15, 16, 17 and 18; and two syllabuses of inspections, training and carrying/working parties. Transcribed 2026-10-26.

## Conventions
Original spelling kept. `[uncertain]` marks words not read with confidence — never guessed. The diary text (pp. 3–5) is handwritten throughout, signed "C. T. Ritchie Capt." after each entry; the orders and syllabuses (pp. 6–25) are typed, with a few handwritten additions noted in brackets. Zoom crops were made of the hardest handwriting (pp. 3–5), the small print of the second syllabus (pp. 23–25) and several unclear typed lines, and re-read.

## Pages 3–5 — Diary text (January 1916, pp. 3–5)

Army Form C.2118 — WAR DIARY or INTELLIGENCE SUMMARY. Place column headed "N.15.C. SHEET 28 1-40,000" (ditto marks below). Remarks column carries appendix references I, II, III (p. 3); IV, V, VI, VII, VIII and one crossed-out mark (p. 4); X and XI (p. 5).

### 1 January

> Two hundred and fifty five men furnished to the Right Sector for working and carrying parties. C. T. Ritchie Capt.

### 2 January

> Two hundred and fifty five men furnished to the Right and Left Sectors for working and carrying parties. C. T. Ritchie Capt.

### 2 January, 11 P.M.

> The Battalion "Stood to" from 11 P.M. to 5 A.M. 3rd Jan. 1916 while the 25th Canadian Battalion endeavoured to carry out a special operation which was not successful. C. T. Ritchie Capt.

### 3 January

> Two hundred and fifty five men furnished to the Right and Left Sectors, the 25th and 27th Canadian Battalions respectively. C. T. Ritchie Capt.

### 4 January

> Two hundred and fifty men furnished to the Right and Left Sector for working and carrying parties to the 25th and 27th Canadian Battalion [uncertain — singular/plural not clear] respectively. C. T. Ritchie Capt.

### 4 January, 11 P.M.

> The Battalion "Stood to" from 11 P.M. to 5 A.M. 5th Jan. 1916 while the 25th Battalion endeavoured to carry out a special operation for the second time which again was unsuccessful. C. T. Ritchie Capt.

### 5 January

> The 24th Bn. (Victoria Rifles) Can. relieved the 25th Canadian Battalion in the Right Sector of the 5th Canadian Infantry Brigade's Sector. A. Company going into Reserve at SIEGE FARM. N C 16 x.7 [uncertain] Sheet 28. 1-40,000, "B." Company garrisoning the [one line struck through — illegible] "C." Company going into SUPPORT DUGOUTS at TURNERTOWN, N Y 36.2.5 [uncertain] Sheet 28 - 1/40000. "D" Company garrisoning the H and J. Trenches. C. T. Ritchie Capt.

### 10 January, 4 A.M.

> A brief bombardment was under way to the North of us. At the same time the Enemy Artillery shelled our trenches and supporting points with H.E. for half an hour. Our own Artillery retaliated when every thing quieted down. C. T. Ritchie Capt.

### 11 January

> Relieved by the 25th Canadian Battalion and moved into divisional Reserve at LOCRE. C. T. Ritchie Capt.

### 14 January, 5 P.M.

> Furnished a working party of 11 Officers and 500 other ranks to the 6th Canadian Infantry Brigade. C. T. Ritchie Capt.

## Pages 6–7 — Operation Order No. 27, 4 January 1916 (January 1916, pp. 6–7)

Typed. Headed "Operation Order No.27, by BRIGADIER GENERAL D. WATSON, Commdg. 5th Canadian Infantry Brigade. January 4th 1916." Ref. — Map, Sheet 28, 1/20,000. Marked "SECRET. Copy No. …2". A stamped mark at the top is crossed out [uncertain].

> 1. During the day and night of January 5th, the 24th and 26th Bns. will relieve the 25th and 22nd Bns. in the Right and Left Sectors respectively.
>
> The 25th Bn. on relief will be in Divisional Reserve, and will move to billets at LOCRE.
>
> The 22nd Bn. on relief will be in Brigade Reserve, and will move to billets in vicinity of R.E. FARM.
>
> 2. RIGHT SECTOR.
>
> The 24th Bn. will move from billets vicinity of R.E. FARM to carry out the relief as follows.
>
> (a) Reserve Company.
>
> One company of the 24th Bn. will relieve the company of the 25th Bn. at SIEGE FARM, by small parties, relief to be completed by 1 p.m.
>
> (b) Front Line and Support.
>
> The three companies of the 24th Bn. relieving the three companies of the 25th Bn. in the trenches, will start the relief at dusk, moving by platoons at 200 yards distance.
>
> (c) Machine Guns.
>
> The machine gun section of the 24th Bn. will relieve the machine gun section of the 25th Bn. by 11 a.m.
>
> (d) Signals.
>
> The signal section of the 24th Bn. will relieve the signal section of the 25th Bn. by 2 p.m. The 25th Bn. will leave their operators with the relieving Bn. until the relief is completed.
>
> (e) Move to Billets.
>
> The 25th Bn. on relief is to rendezvous by formations of strength of at least one company, to move to LOCRE.
>
> Advance parties to take over billets, company cooks, etc. will rendezvous and move to LOCRE under charge of an officer or N.C.O.
>
> 3. LEFT SECTOR.
>
> The 26th Bn. will move from billets at LOCRE to carry out the relief as follows.
>
> (a) Reserve Company.
>
> One company of the 26th Bn. will relieve the company of the 22nd Bn. at SANDBAG VILLA and YORK HOUSE, by small parties, via R.E. FARM. This company will not leave LOCRE until after 12 noon, and relief is to be completed by 4 p.m.
>
> (b) Front Line and Support.
>
> Three companies of the 26th Bn. will move from LOCRE at 3.45 p.m. Starting point — LOCRE CHURCH. On reaching the KEMMEL — LA CLYTTE Road barrier, companies will proceed by platoons at 200 yards interval to carry out the relief.
>
> (c) Machine Guns.
>
> The machine gun section of the 26th Bn. will relieve the machine gun section of the 22nd Bn. by 2 p.m.
>
> (d) Signals.
>
> The signal section of the 26th Bn. will relieve the signal section of the 22nd Bn. by 4 p.m. The 22nd Bn. will leave their operators with the relieving Bn. until the relief is completed.
>
> 4. TRANSPORT.
>
> All transport to be South of the KEMMEL — LA CLYTTE Road until 7 p.m.
>
> 5. DETAILS OF RELIEF.
>
> Battalion Commanders will mutually arrange details of relief, and O's.C. relieving Bns. are responsible that the correct number of rubber boots, waders, etc. are handed over.
>
> 6. GENERAL.
>
> Every precaution is to be taken for the safety of the men, and overland routes will only be used by very small parties.
>
> Usual certificates as to rifle inspection, and the receiving of billets and trenches in sanitary condition are to be taken and forwarded to Brigade Headquarters.
>
> 7. COMPLETION OF RELIEF.
>
> The completion of relief is to be wired to Brigade Headquarters.
>
> 8. BRIGADE RESERVE BATTALION.
>
> The Brigade Reserve Battalion, in accordance with Brigade Orders in Case of Attack (S.M.D.187) [uncertain], will render a certificate to this office, to the effect that all officers are conversant with the Brigade arrangements, and that platoons have been earmarked for the defence of rear line strong points as therein outlined.
>
> (Sgd.) A. G. Turner, Captain. for Brigade Major. 5th Can. Inf. Bde.
>
> Issued to Signals at 4 p.m.
>
> Copy No.1 — 2nd Can. Division. / 2 — 22nd Battalion. / 3 — 24th Battalion. / 4 — 25th Battalion. / 5 — 26th Battalion. / 6–8 — War Diary.

## Page 8 — 24th Bn. Order No. 14, 4 January 1916 (January 1916, p. 8)

Typed. Headed "24th BN. (VICTORIA RIFLES) CANADIANS — ORDER No. 14, Copy No. … 4.1.16." A stamped mark at the top is crossed out [uncertain]; handwritten circled "9" at top.

> 1. The 24th Bn. will move from Brigade reserve at R.E. FARM and relieve the 25th Bn. in the Right Sector during the day and night of January 5th, 1916, the relief to be carried out as follows:
>
> "A" Co. will move in small parties from billets and relieve one company of the 25th Bn. at SIEGE FARM, the whole relief to be completed by 1 p.m.
>
> "B" and "C" Cos. garrisons for the front line trenches only, will move from billets at 2 p.m. in small parties to SIEGE FARM, where they will put on their gum boots, and will remain at SIEGE FARM until it is dark, when they will move up to their trenches to complete the relief.
>
> The remainder of "B" and "C" Cos. will move as follows:
>
> "B" Co. will move at 4.30 p.m. in small parties at 200 yards distance to the K. trenches.
>
> "C" Co. will move at 4.45 p.m. in small parties at 200 yards distance to the J. trenches.
>
> "D" Co. will move at 5 p.m. in small parties at 200 yards distance and will relieve one company of the 25th Bn. in the support dugouts at TURNERTOWN.
>
> The M.G. Sec. will relieve the M.G. Sec. of the 25th Bn. by 11 a.m.
>
> The Grenade Platoon will move from billets at 3.15 p.m. and will relieve the Grenade Platoon of the 25th Bn. in H. 5.
>
> The Sig. Sec. will relieve the Sig. Sec. of the 25th Bn. at 2 p.m.
>
> The Headquarters Co. will move from billets at 5.30 p.m. and will relieve the Headquarters Co. of the 25th Bn. at ROSSIGNOL ESTAMINET.
>
> 2. Billets are to be thoroughly cleaned and in a sanitary condition when handed over to the relieving Battalion. O.C. Cos. will render a certificate to the Orderly Room before leaving to the effect that they have inspected their billets and that they have been left in a clean and sanitary condition. All straw will be cleaned up and buried.
>
> 3. A certificate will be rendered to the Orderly Room by O.C. Cos. and Regtl. Units before moving off, to the effect that all rifles, smoke helmets, goggles, iron rations, have been inspected, and report on the condition in which they were found. Also to the effect that all men have applied anti-frost bite grease and that the instructions in connection with the preventative against frost bite and trench feet have been complied with.
>
> 4. Advance parties of 1 Officer and 4 N.C.O.'s per company will take over trench stores during daylight so as to expedite the relief. O.C. Cos. and Regtl. Units taking over gum boots will wire to Bn. Hd. Qrs. the number of boots that have taken over.
>
> 5. "A" Co. will furnish a ration party of 1 Officer and 60 men to report at the V.C. ROAD DUMP at an hour to be notified later.
>
> 6. O.C. Cos. and Regtl. Units will wire Bn. Hd. Qrs. as soon as they have completed their relief.
>
> [Signed] C. T. Ritchie Capt. Adjt. 24th Bn. (Victoria Rifles) Canadians.

## Page 13 — 24th Bn. Order No. 15, 10 January 1916 (January 1916, p. 13)

Typed. Headed "24th BN. (VICTORIA RIFLES) CANADIANS. ORDER No. 15, Copy No. … 10.1.16." Stamped "V" at top.

> 1. The 25th Bn. will relieve the 24th Bn. in the Right Sector during the day and night of January 11th, 1916. On relief, the 24th Bn. will be in Divisional reserve in billets at LOCRE.
>
> 2. "A" Co. of the 24th Bn. at SIEGE FARM will be relieved by one company of the 25th Bn. by 4 p.m. On comp et.on [as printed — evidently "On completion"] "A" Co. will move from SIEGE FARM in small parties and will rendezvous and march back to LOCRE as a company. Parties crossing from Siege Farm to La Clytte – Kemmel Road are not to straggle. The men in each part are to keep together and N.C.O.'s will be held responsible that they keep away from the Artillery batteries and under cover as much as possible.
>
> 3. 3 Companies of the 25th Bn. will move from Locre at 4.30 p.m. and will relieve "A", "B" and "C" Cos. of the 24th Bn. On completion of the relief, companies will move by small parties and will rendezvous and march back to Locre by companies.
>
> 4. The M.G. Sec. of the 24th Bn. will be relieved by 2 p.m. On completion of the relief, they will rendezvous and march back to Locre as a section under the command of the Machine Gun Officer.
>
> 5. The Sig. Sec. of the 24th Bn. will be relieved by the Sig. Sec. of the 25th Bn. by 4 p.m. The operator of the 24th Bn. will remain with the relieving Battalion until the relief is completed. On completion of the relief, the Sig. Sec. together with the Headquarters Company will march back to Locre under the command of Lieut. Woolley.
>

## Open questions / uncertain items (January)
- Cover vs contents: the cover says "From 1st January to 31st January 1916" but the contents page says "From 1st to 29th January, 1916" and the diary text ends 29 January; a pencilled "29" appears below "31st" on the cover. No entries for 30–31 January appear in this month's diary.
- No diary-text entries for 6–9, 12–13, 15, 19–22 and 24 January; the battalion was in brigade reserve or in the line on those days, covered in outline by the two syllabuses (pp. 9–10, 23–25).
- Contents page dates O.O. No. 27 as "4-1-15" — an apparent typing error for 4-1-16.
- Appendix numbering: the cover says "With appendices 1–11"; the contents lists the appendices by the day of the month they relate to (5, 4, 10, 16, 21, 22, 28, 29); the pages themselves carry Roman-numeral stamps I–XI, several of them crossed out (the contents page notes the appendix numbers were changed into strictly chronological order). The crossed-out stamps on pp. 6, 8, 9, 20 and 23 are not legible.
- The diary-text remarks column on p. 4 ends with a sixth mark, crossed out [uncertain — appears to be "IX"].
- Map references in the diary text are hard to read exactly: "N C 16 x.7" (Siege Farm, 5 Jan), "N Y 36.2.5" (Turnertown, 5 Jan), "N. x 36 x.5" (Turnertown, 17 Jan) and "N [uncertain] 16.0.7" (Siege Farm, 17 Jan).
- 5 January diary: one line is struck through after "'B.' Company garrisoning the" and is illegible.
- 17 January diary: the words "'B' and 'D' Companies" are struck out before "'C' Company in Battalion Reserve at SIEGE FARM".
- 3 and 4 January: whether "Battalion" is singular or plural in "the 25th and 27th Canadian Battalion(s) respectively" is not clear in the hand.
- 16 January: "Major W H. BAYLEY Chaplain" — as written.
- 29 January diary: the closing signature reads "J. A. Gunn Lt Col" — the initials are hard to read with confidence.
- The signature on Orders No. 16, 17 and 18 and on the second syllabus reads "H. Sutherland" — a possible second initial between "H." and "Sutherland" is not legible.
- Brigade Orders in Case of Attack are cited as "(S.M.D.187)" in O.O. Nos. 27 and 28 but as "(S.M.L.167)" in O.O. Nos. 29 and 30 — as printed.
- Order No. 15, para 2: the typed text reads "On comp et.on 'A' Co." (evidently "On completion"). Para 10: "emergency rations at F.12 and S.P.13 Old." — the characters are hard to read. The copy list reads "10–11 — Orderly Room. 11–13 — War Diary." (ranges overlap, as printed).
- Order No. 16 is poorly typed with apparent errors reproduced as printed: the front-line garrisons are given as "B" and "D" Cos., then "A" and "B" Cos. and the remainder of "B" and "D" Cos.; "C" Co. is to "relieve the front line details 24th Bn."; the remainder of "B" Co. is ordered to move twice (4.35 p.m. to the J. trenches and 4.40 p.m. to the K. trenches); para 8's company letter is not legible; para 11's word before "examined" is not legible.
- Order No. 17, para 3: "3 companies of the 25th Bn. will move from billets at 4.30 p.m. and it is dusk and will relieve…" — garbled as printed. Para 5: "Billeting parties … will be over billets at 2 p.m." — as printed. A handwritten annotation at top left of p. 20 is illegible.
- Order No. 18, para 1: "'A' Co. will move at 4.45 p.m. … to the J. trenches" — Order No. 14 (4 Jan) assigns the J. trenches to "C" Co.; one word before "to the K. trenches" is struck out. Para 5: "Advance parties of 1 N.C.O. and 4 N.C.O.'s per company" — as printed (evidently "1 Officer").
- O.O. No. 28: the transport time "6.15 p.m." is handwritten over the typed "7 p.m."; the 4.30 p.m. move time in para 2(b) also appears handwritten over typed text. Its copy list reads "10 & 12 — War Diary" (copy 11 not listed).
