- **Pages:** August 1916, p. 1 – p. 15 (image files page-0193 – page-0207 of the item)

## Conventions
Original spelling kept. `[uncertain]` marks words not read with confidence — never guessed. The August diary text (pp. 3–5) is typed, not handwritten — unlike July; every entry is initialled in the right margin of the Summary column in the same hand, "R.O.A." [uncertain — the letters as read], presumably Major R. O. Alexander, who also signs the diary at the foot of p. 5. The diary pages are Army Form C.2118, "WAR DIARY or INTELLIGENCE SUMMARY" ("INTELLIGENCE SUMMARY" struck through). Form footers: "1875 Wt. W5931/826 1,000,000 4/15 J.B.C. & A. A.D.S.S./Forms/C. 2118." on pp. 3 and 5; p. 4 is a different printing of the form ("(73989) W4141—463. 400,000. 9/14. H.&J.Ltd. Forms/C. 2118/10.") with the combined column heading "Hour, Date, Place". The diary text runs continuously across the page break: the 20 August entry begins at the foot of p. 3 and continues at the top of p. 4. The appendices (pp. 6–15) are a mix of handwritten Army Form C. 2121 message forms (pp. 6, 8), a mostly blank page with a two-line note (p. 7), and typed brigade letters and battalion operation orders (pp. 9–15) with handwritten signatures noted in brackets. Zoom crops were made of the faint/handwritten passages and re-read; the crops are kept in this folder under `zoom-aug-p3-5/`, `zoom-aug-p6-10/`, `zoom-aug-p11-15/` and `zoom-aug-p14-15/`. The contents page lists appendices in renumbered chronological order; the diary pages carry the older appendix references in the Remarks column (Appendix A on 8 August, Appendix B on 16 August, Appendix "C" on 26 August). Place-names are rendered exactly as typed, including variant spellings (e.g. "BOLLARTBEEKE", "RENINGHELST", "VOORMEZEELE").
