- **Pages:** February 1916, p. 1 – p. 27 (image files page-0026 – page-0052 of the 1916–1917 diary)
- **February 1916 — COMPLETE** (pages 26–52 of the 1916–1917 diary, 27 physical pages): cover, contents, diary text 3–27 February (three diary-text pages), Order No. 19, two training syllabuses (5–10 February and 16–22 February), Order No. 1 with sketch map, Orders Nos. 20, 21, 22, 25 and 24, 5th Bde Operation Orders Nos. 33 and 34, 4th Bde Order No. 26, and 31st Bn. - January 1916 and March 1916 onwards — see other transcript files in this folder when available.

## Conventions
Original spelling kept, including "sieze", "SEIGE", "it's", "VERMORAL"/"Vermoral" and "Sylvas". `[uncertain]` marks words not read with confidence — never guessed. The diary text (pp. 3–5) is handwritten in one hand, signed "C. J. Ritchie" (the September transcript reads the adjutant's signature as "C. T. Ritchie" — the middle initial here reads as J; see open questions). The orders are typed, with a few handwritten additions and corrections noted in brackets. Zoom crops were made of the hardest handwriting and the faintest type and re-read. Dots (....) in the orders are blanks left unfilled in the original.

## Page 6 — Appendix 3: 24th Bn. Order No. 19, dated 3-2-16 (February 1916, p. 6)

Typed. Headed "24th. BN. (VICTORIA RIFLES) CANADIANS. ORDER No. 19. Copy No. ...... 3.2.16." Circled "3" at top.

> 1. The 25th. Bn. will relieve the 24th. Bn. in the Right Sector during the day and night of February 4th, 1916. On relief the 24th. Bn. will be in Divisional Reserve in billets at Locre. "A" Co. of the 24th. Bn. will be relieved by one company of the 25th. Bn. at Siege Farm, relief to be completed by 4.30 p.m. On relief, "A" Co. will move from SIEGE FARM in small parties, each party to be kept together and the men not allowed to straggle, and are not to move in the vicinity of the batteries. The Company will rendezvous and move into LOCRE as a company, march discipline being maintained throughout. "B" "C" and "D" Cos. and Grenade Platoon on being relieved will move by small parties and will rendezvous and march back to LOCRE in formations of not less than a company. There is to be no straggling, the men are to be kept closed up and march discipline is to be maintained throughout.
>
> 2. Billeting parties consisting of 1 Officer and 1 N.C.O. from each company and 1 N.C.O. from the Machine Gun Section and Grenade Platoon, will rendezvous at SIEGE FARM at 1 p.m. for the purpose of taking over billets and guiding their respective units into their billets.
>
> 3. Advance parties of Company Cooks and batmen will rendezvous at SIEGE FARM at 3.00 p.m. and will be marched into billets under the command of the senior N.C.O.
>
> 4. All surplus stores and baggage will be left at the Battalion Dump foot of the V.C. Road under the charge of 1 man per company who will act as loader and will accompany the transport into billets.
>
> 5. M.G. Section will be relieved by the M.G. Section of the 25th. Bn. by 3.00 p.m. The M.G. Sec. will move into billets under the command of the Machine Gun Officer.
>
> 6. Sig. Sec. of the 24th. Bn. will be relieved by the Sig. Sec. of the 25th. Bn. at 6.00 p.m. The operators will remain with the whole wire until the relief is completed.
>
> 7. Hd. Qrs. Co. on being relieved will move by small parties under the command of Lieut. Woolsey, and will rendezvous and march back to billets as a unit.
>
> 8. Gum Boots at SIEGE FARM are to be thoroughly cleaned and dried before being turned over.
>
> 9. Receipts for trench stores will be taken in duplicate. The original to be handed to the Adjutant at the orderly room as soon as arrival in Billets, great care being taken that all receipts are handed in, and a correct inventory of trench stores, especially Gum Boots, is to be carefully made.
>
> 10. O.C. Cos. and Regtl. Units will wire Bn. Hd. Qrs. before 4. p.m. the number of Gum Boots they are turning over.
>
> 11. The transport is to be SOUTH of the KEMMEL LA CLYTTE ROAD until 6 p.m.
>
> 12. O.C. Cos. and Regtl. Units will telephone Bn. Hd. Qrs as soon as they have been relieved.
>
> 13. On arrival in Billets, O.C. Cos. and Regtl. Units will inspect all Rifles and Ammunition, and will render a certificate to the orderly room immediately after this inspection, certifying that all rifles have been unloaded, that there are no clips or S.A.A. in the magazines, also that each man is in possession of the required number of rounds.
>
> 14. Certificates as to the sanitary condition of the trenches are to be taken and turned into the Adjutant on arrival in billets.
>
> 15. Every precaution is to be taken to ensure the safety of the men and overland routes will only be used by very small parties, care being exercised that no smoking is allowed during the movement of the troops until they are clear of the KEMMEL-VIERSTRAAT Road.
>
> [Signed] C. J. Ritchie Capt. Adjt. 24th. Bn. (Victoria Rifles) Canadians.

Typed. Headed "24th Bn. (Victoria Rifles) Canadians. Order No. 1. Copy No. 7 [uncertain — an '8' appears overtyped beside it]. 5-2-16." Reference — Map, Sheet 28, Wytschaete 1/10,000. and special Map, SPANBROEKMOLEN & PECKHAM 1-50. Circled "8" at the top of p. 9. Pages 10–12 headed "-2-", "-3-", "-4-".

> 1. (a) The 2nd Army on the frontage XXXXXX is to assault the enemy's position in the area XXXXX at 12.noon to-morrow.
>
> (b) The 2nd Canadian Division supported by their Artillery and an allotment of guns from the first Group H.A.R. will sieze the enemy's network of trenches from X to F (right of the position is off the enlarged map).
>
> (c) The enemy's trenches, strong points and entanglements along the entire front being attacked, having been subjected to an artillery bombardment for a period of six days from the 2nd to the 8th inclusive, will be subjected to a heavy bombardment from 6.00am to 12.noon on the 8th. At 11.45 am the artillery will increase their bombardment to it's greatest intensity possible until noon, when the artillery will lift the fire to the enemy's support trenches and the infantry assault of the 2nd Army will take place.
>
> (d) During the course of the artillery bombardment every effort must be made to secure information regarding the progress made by our artillery in cutting the enemy's wire. Any portion requiring special attention must be reported.
>
> (e) Trench mortars and Rifle Grenades will fire one salvo simultaneously with the attacking infantry leaving the trenches.
>
> (f) On the morning of the 8th inst. at 11.20 am Gas interspersed and flanked by smoke from smoke candles will be discharged at 12.noon. At this hour the gas will be shut off and the infantry assault will take place.
>
> (g) The 2nd Canadian Division is to primarily attack with the 4th and 5th Infantry Brigades. The 5th Canadian Infantry Brigade will assault the enemy's trenches in the area R.F.S.H. at noon on February 8th, on a two Battalion frontage.
>
> (h) The area of assembly for the assault of the 5th Canadian Infantry Bde., being the network of trenches T.B.U.D. and the Brigade will be formed up in position for the attack by 11.30 am.
>
> (i) The 4th Canadian Infantry Bde. has been allotted the network of trenches to the right, and the 7th Canadian Inf. Bde. the left of the 5th Canadian Inf. Bde. Each Brigade frontage for the attack is approximately 200 yards.
>
> (j) The 6th and 8th Canadian Infantry Brigades are being held as a Divisional Reserve in sheltered trenches in rear of the 4th and 5th Canadian Infantry Brigades.
>
> (k) The 22nd Bn., will assault the enemy's trenches on a half company frontage in the area R.E.S.Q.
>
> (l) The 26th and 25th Bns., will immediately follow the 22nd and 24th Bns., respectively.

In the first line there will be 15 wire cutters evenly distributed. >
> 15. Gas & Smoke. The gas and smoke discharge will last exactly 40 minutes. The last two minutes will be smoke only, so as to cover the deployment and give the gas time to get well ahead. The time of the assault will be taken from.................... all of whom have watches which will be carefully set. The 40 minutes are allotted as follows:- 12 minutes gas followed by 8 minutes smoke (4 candles) 12 minutes gas 8 minutes smoke (4 candles) At 11.58 am the smoke is to be thickened. In order that the gas may come as a complete surprise, no candles, phosphor bombs, phosphor grenades, or free phosphor should be lighted or thrown before 12 minutes after the discharge of gas begins. This procedure will be explained the day before the assault to the men taking part in it.
>

As few men as possible should be told off to take charge of prisoners who will be marched off in small groups to point AA [uncertain — overtyped]., where they will be handed over to Officer in charge of point who will dispose of same. All articles found on prisoners will be sent direct by orderly to Bn. Hqrs., without delay or waiting for the prisoners.

>
> 22. Flags. Three men per platoon throughout the Bn., will carry a yellow flag 2' X 2' on a 3' stick to mark the progress of his platoon in the attack, and one per bombing squad will carry a yellow flag 1' X 1' on a 5' stick to mark the progress of his squad. These flags will be carried, never stuck in the ground.
>
