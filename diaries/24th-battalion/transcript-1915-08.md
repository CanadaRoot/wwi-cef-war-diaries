# Transcript — 24th Battalion (Victoria Rifles) — August 1915

- **Unit:** 24th Battalion (Victoria Rifles), 2nd Canadian Division
- **Source:** LAC Collection Search Item ID 1883240, "War diaries — 24th Canadian Infantry Battalion (incomplete)" (RG 9 III-D-3, vol. 4931, file 414; microfilm reel T-10733)
- **Pages:** August 1915, p. 1 – p. 5 (image files page-0041 – page-0045 of the 94-page diary)
- **August 1915 — COMPLETE** (pages 41–45 of 94, 5 physical pages): cover, contents, diary text 1–31 August (two diary-text pages), recapitulation. No appendices listed. Transcribed 2026-10-26.
- September 1915 onwards — see transcript-1915-09.md and transcript-1915-10.md in this folder.

## Conventions
Original spelling kept. `[uncertain]` marks words not read with confidence — never guessed. Ditto marks (") in the diary tables mean "same as the line above". The diary text is a handwritten table (Army Form C.2118) in one hand, signed "C. T. Ritchie Capt. Adjt."; a close crop of the date column on p. 4 was used to settle the 27th (the figure can be misread as 24, but the recapitulation groups 23–26 as the Divisional Scheme and 27–31 as Battalion Parades).

## Open questions / uncertain items (August)
- 4 August, 3.30 p.m.: the activity after "Practice Loading & Firing Dummy Cartridges" is illegible — possibly "Close Order" [uncertain].
- No entry for 14 August; 24–26 August are covered only by the "4 Days" Divisional Scheme entry dated 23 August.
- 27 August: the date figure can be read as 24; settled as 27 by the recapitulation's grouping (23–26 scheme, 27–31 parades) and a close crop of the date column.
- The recapitulation groups 10–13 August as "Divisional" although the diary text has the 10th as a Brigade Scheme and the 11th–13th as the Divisional Scheme; its "16–20th Divisional Training" grouping likewise covers days the diary text lists as Battalion Parades. The diary text is treated as primary.
- The battalion was still training at East Sandling (Kent) for the whole month; it had not yet embarked for France (see September).
