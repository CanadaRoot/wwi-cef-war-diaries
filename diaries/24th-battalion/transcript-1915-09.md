Ritchie Capt. Adjt."); the orders (pp. 5–9) are typed, with a few handwritten additions noted in brackets. Zoom crops were made of the hardest handwriting (pp. 3–4) and re-read.

## Page 4 — Appendix A/1: first tour of first-line trench duty (September 1915, p. 4)

Army Form C.2118, headed "Appendix A/1". Stamped "24th INFY. BN. … Divn. OCT 10 1915 CANADA".

> Appendix of War Diary of September re tour of first line trench duty commencing Sept 28th at 9.00 P.M.
>
> The 24th Bn relieved the 25th Can Bn at 9. P.M. at WYTSCHAETE and occupied the ROSSIGNOL ESTAMINET as Bn H.Q. QRS. The relief took place in the rain and the change passed quietly although there were sudden bursts of machine gun fire and rifle fire. Snipers were very active all day of Sept 29th.

### 29 September

> Pte [uncertain] Bride No 12 Platoon C Coy was the first casualty, wounded in the left arm by a machine gun bullet while bring in rations. At 1.20 P.M. the enemy sent over several whizz-bangs but with no damage. From 9.30 to 10 P.M. they were again active but our supporting artillery opened and quickly quietened them. Our machine guns were not active. Enemy machine guns opened on our ration party. Snipers were very active. Pte Best claims to have brought down a sniper at Petit Bois. A working party of the enemy was dispersed by rifle fire from S.3L. One of our working parties was dispersed by enemy rifle fire but they afterwards returned and worked until 7.30 P.M.

### 30 September

> During the day our men broke several German periscopes while the enemy signalled hits or misses. The enemy are using green and red flares. They also fired one rocket which burst into green coloured stars. Four of the whizzy-bangs failed to explode. Smoke from the enemys line clearly visible from G.4 A at 4.30 P.M. The day was spent in making repairs to trenches, parapet and parados. Two flying traverses were erected in H.4. A dugout built in J.1 — a Dugout built and traverse made in communication trench J.10 to H.3 A. Fatigues of two officers and 50 men working on new trench from 8 P.M. to 2.30 A.M. No 65765 Cpl B.M. Paterson dangerously wounded in the head while firing over the parapet in G.4 A about 11. P.M.

## Pages 5–7 — Appendix A/2–4: 24th Bn. Order No. 1, dated 28-9-15 (September 1915, pp. 5–7)

Typed. Headed "24th BN. (Victoria Rifles) CANADIANS — ORDER No.1, Copy No. ……, 28-9-15". Ref. Map: BELGIUM YPRES No.28, 1/40,000; KEMMEL (Revised) 1/10,000.

> 1. Relief — The Bn. will relieve the 25th Bn. Canadians on the night of the 28-29th inst., in the right Sector of the 5th Canadian Inf. Bde. frontage.
>
> 2. Details of move — Bn. Parade Ground: "B" Co. Alarm Post. Hour of Bn. Parade: 5.50 p.m. Dress: Heavy Marching Order with rubber sheets. Blankets will NOT be carried. Hour of passing Starting Point: 6 p.m. Starting Point: LOCRE CHURCH. Route: M.23.c.d.b. to crossroads M.24.a.85 thence to road junction M.24.b.2. M.24.b. N.19.a.b. M.19.d. road junction N.14.a.6.2 thence to barrier on LA CLYTTE-KEMMEL Road at N.20.b.85. Order of March: Hd. Qrs. Bombers "B" Co. "C" Co. "D" Co. "A" Co. Transport.
>
> 3. "A" Co. — "A" Co. will relieve a company of the 25th Bn. (which when relieved will go into support of the Right Sector at SIEGE FARM) by parties of not more than 10. Pass Starting Point: 9 a.m. Starting Point: SIEGE FARM. The second and succeeding parties will leave at 10 minutes intervals, and all parties are to take every precaution not to draw shell fire.
>
> 4. Guides — 2 Guides per garrison from the 25th Bn. will meet the Bn. at the barrier on the LA CLYTTE-KEMMEL ROAD. Upon arrival there, the companies will proceed by platoons at 100 yards distance, led by their guides. In order to facilitate the location of Garrisons (see Appendix a) Co. Offrs. will see that the various Garrisons are detailed before leaving their billets.
>
> 5. M.G. — 12 Machine Gunners will augment the M.G. Sec. at SIEGE FARM at 2 p.m. to-day, and under the instructions of the M.G. Offr. will be led to their positions in the trenches, taking over the following emplacements: J.1, J.4, H.3, H.1.a, S.P.11, S.P.12. 2 guns of the 24th Bn. will be left with the 24th Bn. in the front line. 2 guns will be left at SIEGE FARM with 1 N.C.O. and 12 men from the second half of the 25th Bn. M.G. Reserve Sec.
>
> 6. Sig. Sec. — The Sig. Sec. will arrange to take over the communications of the Sector during the afternoon of the 28th inst. The Sig. Sec. of the 25th Bn. will stay at their stations until their unit is relieved.
>
> 7. Bombers — See Appendix (a).
>
> 8. Transport — Sufficient limbered wagons and Maltese Cart to convey the Hd. Qrs. baggage, stores, Medical Equipment and water cans will follow in rear of Bn. (See para 2.)
>
> 9. Water Supply — Water carts will be available at "B" Co's Alarm Post at 3 p.m. Bottles must be filled before the companies leave their billeting area. During the tour of duty in the trenches, water supply will be available nightly under arrangement of Qr. Mr.
>
> 10. Rations — Rations will be issued after the Bn. has taken over the Sector. The hour will be notified later.
>
> 11. Handing over Billets. — The Assistant Adjt. will remain behind to hand over the billets now occupied by the Bn. to the 25th Bn. and will take a receipt showing that they have been handed over in a sanitary condition. Co. Comdrs. will be held responsible for the cleanliness of their respective billeting areas.
>
> 12. Taking over trenches (Sanitation) — The Officers commanding Co's. will be responsible that the trenches are handed over to them in a sanitary condition and that a receipt to this effect is given to Co. Officer relieved.
>
> 13. Trench Stores — A receipt will be given for trench stores taken over in each Garrison, care being taken that a correct inventory is made. All receipts to be turned in to Hd. Qrs. the following day.
>
> 14. Reliefs. — Co. Comdrs. will report immediately by telephone to Bn. Hd. Qrs. when reliefs to various Sectors are completed, stating changes of Garrisons (if any).
>
> 15. Sandbags — Offrs. Comdg. Cos. will make sure that each man in his company is issued with 3 sandbags before going into the trenches.
>
> 16. Offrs. Baggage — Offrs. Surplus Baggage will be ready for collection for storing, outside their billets at 4.15 p.m. to-day.
>
> 17. Qr. Mr. Stores — The Qr. Mr. will be responsible for the safe keeping at Qr. Mr. Stores, of all Regimental stores and baggage.
>
> 18. Field Kitchens — Field Kitchens will be handed over to the Qr. Mr. after the evening meal, who will be responsible that they are thoroughly overhauled.
>
> 19. Bn. Hd. Qrs. — Bn. Hd. Qrs. in billets at LOCRE will close at 5 p.m. and open at ROSSIGNOL ESTAMINET at 9 p.m.

## Open questions / uncertain items (September)
- 18 September: no date is entered in the Date column; the date is inferred from sequence (between the 17th and 19th entries).
- 15 September: the ship is given only as "S.S. Queen" — no further name on the page.
- 29 September: the first casualty's name reads "Pte [uncertain] Bride, No. 12 Platoon, C Coy" — the word between "Pte" and "Bride" appears to read "hue" (possibly "Hugh") and is not read with confidence.
- Order No. 2 (p. 9): the date at the top right reads only "-15." — the day and month are not legible.
- Distribution of Garrison (p. 8): the "A" Co. fourth trench is printed "H.6" but the September narrative and Order No. 2 use H.3 for that garrison; the Co. column for the "D" Co. rows is printed "2D" [uncertain]. "L.P.", "C.P." and "C.T." appear as qualifiers in the Bombers column for J.1, J.3 new and J.3 right; their meaning is not stated in the document. The "W, Hd. Qrs." line reads literally as printed.
- No entries for 1, 3–6, 24–27 September in the diary text; the battalion was in transit or in billets on those days.
- The contents page lists the appendix as "Appendix 28" (the renumbered appendix set); the pages themselves are stamped "Appendix A/1" to "A/6".
