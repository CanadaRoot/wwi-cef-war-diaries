# Transcript — 24th Battalion (Victoria Rifles of Canada) — "October 1914" file (manifest pages 32–40)

- **Unit:** 24th Battalion, C.E.F. (24th Cdn. Inf. Battalion, 2nd Cdn. Division; Victoria Rifles of Canada)
- **Source:** LAC Collection Search, CEF war diaries (RG 9 III-D-3) — 24th Battalion diary, Volume 15-7. Manifest image files `1914-1915-page-0032` – `1914-1915-page-0040`.
- **Pages:** Manifest "October 1914" pp. 1–9 (9 physical pages): cover (p. 1), contents (p. 2), typed summary of the battalion's formation and embarkation (p. 3), typed recapitulation of July 1915 training (p. 4), and the typed daily training programme for 1–31 July 1915 at East Sandling (pp. 5–9).
- **October 1914 file — COMPLETE as far as these pages go** (9 physical pages). Transcribed 2026-10-26.
- **Manifest order note:** the LAC manifest labels this file "October 1914", but its contents are a typed summary covering October 1914 – May 1915 and the typed July 1915 training diary. The cover itself is corrected in manuscript: "From October 1914" is struck through and replaced with "July 1st 1915", to 31 July 1915. Transcribed in manifest order. Unlike the handwritten Medical Officer's diary in the "December 1914" file (manifest pages 1–31), this file is typewritten and fully legible.
- August 1915 onwards — not in this file; the diary continues in the next manifest files.

## Conventions
Original spelling kept. `[uncertain]` marks words not read with confidence — never guessed. Typewritten text is transcribed as printed, including its abbreviations ("Cos.", "P.T.", "A.M.", "P.M.", "&c.").

> **READABILITY NOTE:** This file is typewritten and legible throughout. A few company letters in the July training tables are faint or overprinted and are flagged [uncertain]. The marginal notes on p. 3 are handwritten and only partly legible.

## Page 3 — Typed summary (October 1914 – May 1915)

> WAR DIARY. 24th. Battalion, C.E.F.
>
> Victoria Rifles of Canada.
>
> Army Form C. 2118.

### Oct. 22nd. 1914

> Battalion authorized.

### Oct. 24th.

> Major J. A. Gunn from the 3rd. V.R.C. appointed Lieut. Colonel and to command the Unit.

### Oct. 26th.

> Started mobilization.

### Dec. 17th. 1914

> Inspection by Field Marshal His Royal Highness the Duke of Connaught, Hon-Colonel of the Battalion.

### 1915 Jan. 30th.

> All honours at Garrison Boxing Tournament carried off by members of this Battalion.

### May 10th.

> Embarked for England on S.S. Cameronian.

> [Marginal note, handwritten, left margin: "11/L. [uncertain]" and a note reading "Ent[?] [uncertain] [uncertain]".]
>
> Commanding Officer of this Unit advised that current War Diary is being compiled daily and at expiration of hostilities will be lodged with War Office, London — copy of same sent to Canada for record, if permitted.
>
> Of the above, 19 Officers and 376 other ranks enrolled from the 3rd. Regiment, Victoria Rifles of Canada.

## Open questions / uncertain items (October 1914 file)
- The cover (p. 1) is corrected in manuscript: "From October 1914" is struck through and replaced with "July 1st 1915", to 31 July 1915. The manifest's "October 1914" label follows the uncorrected heading.
- The contents page (p. 2) describes Volume 15-7, 1–31 July 1915, "Diary Text 7 Pages" — the seven typed diary pages are manifest pages 34–40.
- The typed summary (p. 3) duplicates the embarkation date (10 May 1915) already recorded in the Medical Officer's handwritten diary; the formation dates (22–26 October 1914, 17 December 1914, 30 January 1915) appear only here.
- The marginal handwritten note on p. 3 could not be read with confidence.
- The recapitulation (p. 4) refers to Appendices 1–5; no appendices are filed in this set of pages.
- The July training programme has no entries for 11, 16, 24 or 25 July 1915 in these pages; 11 and 25 July were Sundays.
- A few company letters in the July tables are faint or overprinted (notably p. 7, 9 July) and are flagged [uncertain].
